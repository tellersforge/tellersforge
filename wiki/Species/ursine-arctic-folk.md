# Arctic folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ursine](../Species_Families/ursine.md)*

Large, heavy, thick-furred people of frozen worlds, grouped with the bear-like builds for their shape rather than their ancestry. Their fur is white or grey, dense enough to shed snow, and their bodies hold heat like a banked stove. Some have many eyes, set to catch every movement on the glare of the ice; others carry great curved tusks.

They are made for cold and open ground, not for forest. Gentle herders drive their flocks across the tundra and sing to them in the dark months. Tusked hunters track prey across miles of drift and read a snowfield like a page. In warm lands they are miserable and slow, and most go home as soon as their business is done.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bear-folk](ursine-bear-folk.md)*, true bears of forest, mountain and ice; arctic folk are a different stock that only resembles them, built for tundra rather than trees.

## What sets this species apart

Thick-furred cold-world folk made for ice, not forest.
