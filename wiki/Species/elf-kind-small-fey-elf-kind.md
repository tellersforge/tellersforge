# Small fey elf-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

Little elves, often no taller than a child, with the pointed ears and bright eyes of their larger kin and something of the fey about them. Many clans have small wings folded at the shoulder, some good only for a leap or a glide, some strong enough for true flight. The smallest are sprite-sized and flit about like dragonflies, mending small hurts with a touch.

What marks them most is feeling. They sense each other's moods as clearly as speech, and some can share memories or dreams by touch, which makes lying among them nearly pointless. They are gentle, curious and often mischievous. Their history is full of loss: in several tellings they were hunted almost to nothing by a cruel overlord race, and the survivors still hide in forests and hollows, singing old songs about better days.

## Not to be confused with

*[fairy folk](fey-fairy-folk.md)*, small winged fey with no elven blood; these are elves in miniature, empathic and fey-touched.

## What sets this species apart

Small, fey-touched and empathic, with a bent toward wings. Flight is affinity only; winged Breeds/Races Set it.
