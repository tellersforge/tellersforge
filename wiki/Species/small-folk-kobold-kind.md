# Kobold-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Small folk](../Species_Families/small-folk.md)*

Small, timid burrowers of the dark places, often mistaken for goblins and deeply offended by it. They are scaly or leathery, with sharp little faces and quick, nervous eyes, and they live in warrens dug beneath hills and mines.

They are not brave, and they do not pretend to be. What they are is clever: a kobold warren is laced with pit-traps, tripwires, falling rocks and narrow crawlways that only a kobold can pass. Intruders who come looking for an easy fight find themselves bleeding, lost and chased by laughter in the dark. Kobolds do not welcome guests, and a guest who survives rarely comes back.

## Not to be confused with

*[Kobold](goblinoid-and-orc-kind-kobold.md)* is the goblinoid kind; kobold-kind are the small folk's own timid burrowers, known more for traps than for war.

## What sets this species apart

Timid burrowers of the underground, known for traps rather than hospitality.
