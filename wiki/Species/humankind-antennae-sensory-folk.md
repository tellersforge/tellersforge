# Antennae-sensory folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Pale blue near-humans from a world of ice, with a pair of slim antennae rising from the head. The antennae keep them balanced, catch faint currents in the air and feel what the eyes miss. They are also tender, and grabbing one is both an assault and a serious mistake. The people are short-tempered, proud and quick to settle quarrels with a blade, then quick to forget them.

A secretive kin line lives deep in caves and is born blind. They need no eyes: their antennae read the world so finely that they seem to sense thoughts as well as movement. Neither line likes crowds, heat or being touched on the head.

## Not to be confused with

the *[keen-sensed folk](humankind-keen-sensed-folk.md)*, whose sharp senses sit in eyes, ears or horns; these folk sense through a pair of antennae.

## What sets this species apart

Cold-world near-humans whose head antennae give balance and extra sensory input.
