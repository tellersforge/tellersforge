# Songbird & small-bird folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Small, bright and never still. Songbird and small-bird folk include parrot-kin with vivid plumage and crushing curved beaks, sweet-voiced finch and thrush people who sing as easily as they talk, and tiny hummingbird folk who hover in place on a blur of wings and must eat almost constantly to keep their hearts going. Every one of them truly flies, light as they are, and they flit through forests and market squares alike.

They live in flocks and dislike being alone. Their music is famous, their tempers quick, and their feuds over nesting ground or a flowering tree can be surprisingly fierce for people so small. Bigger folk tend to find them charming and to underestimate them, which songbirds have long since learned to use.

## Not to be confused with

*[Corvid folk](avian-corvid-folk.md)*, the clever black-feathered scavengers, who mimic voices for gain rather than for song.

## What sets this species apart

Small, light birds that all truly fly.
