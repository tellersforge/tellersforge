# Dangerous fey

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Not all fey are pretty, and not all are kind. The dangerous fey are the ones children are warned about. There are hag-folk of the swamps, grotesque witches who work dark magic in covens and lure travellers to their deaths. There are small, cruel goblin-things that haunt ruined castles and dye their caps in the blood of those they kill. There is a creature that sits at a banquet table with eyes in the palms of its hands, waiting for a child to break the rule.

Some are hardly people at all: swarms of tiny biting fey that strip flesh from bone in moments, or small water imps summoned by curses to torment a victim. They are predatory, cunning and delight in fear. The old defences still work, now and then: iron, salt, rowan wood, a clever answer, and running water between you and them.

## Not to be confused with

*[hag/witch-kind](undead-and-deathless-hag-witch-kind.md)*, the undead and witch family of hags; dangerous fey are predatory fairy folk, of which hags are only one kind.

## What sets this species apart

Predatory, grotesque fey who frighten rather than enchant.
