# Divine overseer-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

Beings charged with watching over an entire universe. Each overseer has one, and all its stars, worlds and peoples are, in a sense, theirs. They look like tall, calm humanoids, often in simple robes, and they carry themselves with the ease of those who have never been in real danger.

Their power is inner energy of almost limitless strength, cultivated over ages, and they can shatter planets with it if they choose. Most do not choose. They watch, balance, occasionally intervene, and spend a great deal of time on leisure, sparring and petty rivalries with overseers of other universes. Mortals who come to their attention are either very lucky or very unlucky, and it is hard to know which in advance.

## Not to be confused with

the *[multiversal overseer-kind](trans-dimensional-multiversal-overseer-kind.md)*, who watch over many realities at once, where each divine overseer guards a single universe.

## What sets this species apart

Near-godlike wardens of a whole universe, wielding immense inner energy. NPC-scale. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
