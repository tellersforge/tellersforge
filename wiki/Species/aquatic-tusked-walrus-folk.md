# Tusked walrus folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md)*

Big, heavy, thick-hided amphibians with long tusks and more eyes than most people. They breathe air, walk well on land and swim powerfully in cold seas. Their clans differ: some are small, gentle and four-eyed; some are larger, bad-tempered and heavy-jawed; one two-eyed line is treated by the others as outsiders, which it resents.

They fish, haul, bargain and fight, and are tougher than they look. Other peoples see a slow, lumbering creature and learn otherwise when a pair of tusks comes at them faster than expected.

## Not to be confused with

the *[aquatic folk](aquatic-aquatic-folk.md)*, most of whom live in the water; walrus folk breathe air and stay at home on land.

## What sets this species apart

Air-breathing, thick-hided, tusked folk who walk well on land.
