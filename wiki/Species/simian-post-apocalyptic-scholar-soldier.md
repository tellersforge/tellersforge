# Post-apocalyptic scholar-soldier

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md)*

Heavy, broad-shouldered ape-folk who rose from the ruins of the old world and built a nation of their own. They are strong enough to bend iron bars and disciplined enough not to. Their cities run on order: drilled soldiers, careful scientists, clear ranks and harsh rules, and a deep belief that chaos is what killed the world before.

They study the broken machines of the past and rebuild what they can. They train constantly. They have little patience for the ragged scavengers of the wastes and even less for the old human stories of dominion. Other peoples find them fair, rigid and very hard to bargain with. A single squad of their soldiers is worth an army of raiders.

## Not to be confused with

*[Great ape folk](simian-great-ape-folk.md)* live in all ages and cultures; the post-apocalyptic scholar-soldier belongs to the fallen world, and its drill and science mark it out.

## What sets this species apart

Heavy great-ape builds; their discipline and science are cultural and handled in the Culture step.
