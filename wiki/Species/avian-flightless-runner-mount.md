# Flightless runner mount

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

A tall, flightless bird built like a sprinter: long, powerful legs, a small head on a long neck, stubby useless wings and feet that hit the ground like hammers. Runner mounts are animals, not people, and they are ridden. Swamp and plains peoples raise them for speed on open ground, where they outpace most beasts over a short dash and kick hard enough to break a leg.

They are nervous, stupid and fiercely fast, prone to bolting, biting and sulking. Riders learn to sit light, keep the reins short and never stand behind one. A well-trained runner is a treasure; a badly trained one is a sprint straight into the nearest bog.

## Not to be confused with

*[Fowl-kind](avian-fowl-kind.md)*, the heavy ground and water birds, who are thinking people rather than mounts.

## What sets this species apart

Flightless running birds built for speed on the ground.
