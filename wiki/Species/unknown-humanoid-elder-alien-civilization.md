# Elder alien civilization

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md)*

They were here before anyone. Elder civilisations are ancient alien peoples who ruled, built and studied long before the younger races stood upright. Their bodies are strange beyond easy description: barrel-shaped trunks with starfish heads and folded wings, cones on clawed stalks, forms that seem designed by a mind with other priorities. Some may even have seeded life on young worlds, partly by accident.

Their cities are ruins now, or sealed vaults, or libraries that stretch through time itself, and some are said to send their minds across the ages to borrow other bodies. Each civilisation has its own powers and its own secrets. Scholars who study them come back with notes, sketches and a lasting reluctance to sleep in the dark.

## Not to be confused with

*[Precursor dimension-kind](trans-dimensional-precursor-dimension-kind.md)*, forerunners from beyond ordinary space; the elder civilisations are ancient aliens of this universe, older than the younger races but not from outside it.

## What sets this species apart

Ancient, varied NPC civilizations; their powers differ by Breed (e.g. mind projection for one), so no shared lines. NPC-scale; set key powers at Breed level.
