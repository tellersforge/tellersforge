# Astral elf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

Some elves look at the night sky and see beauty. These look and feel at home. Star-touched from birth, they have silvery skin that seems to hold a faint inner light, and eyes that pick out constellations through cloud. They know where the stars are without looking, the way others know which way is down.

That kinship pulls at them. Many spend their long lives drifting between planes of existence, following paths only they can sense, and they tend to treat the ordinary world as one stop among many. Others stay and map the heavens with obsessive care, reading omens and futures in the turning sky. They are unusually hard to wither with the magic of death and decay, as though something of the stars protects them. Other peoples find them dreamy and distant, and always slightly elsewhere.

## Not to be confused with

*[astral-plane native-kind](trans-dimensional-astral-plane-native-kind.md)*, beings born on that plane; astral elves are elves who feel its pull while living in the world.

## What sets this species apart

Star-touched: an inborn feel for the heavens and a pull toward other planes.
