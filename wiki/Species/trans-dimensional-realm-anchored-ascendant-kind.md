# Realm-anchored ascendant kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Their home was a small world, and it broke. A god died there, or shattered, and its pieces fell into the lives of the people who stayed. Some of those people rose. They look human, eat and sleep and quarrel like humans, but each carries a shard of that broken divinity and can draw on it, calling up powers that differ from one ascendant to the next.

The price is attachment. Their strength is bound to their home realm's fate, and when the realm suffers, they feel it in their bones. They are proud, often desperate, and fiercely protective of a place that others might think beyond saving. Outsiders see champions or fanatics, depending on which side of the border they stand.

## Not to be confused with

*[Divinely-ascended human](humankind-divinely-ascended-human.md)*, raised up by a living god's favour; these ascendants draw on a dead god's fragments and are tied to one shattered realm.

## What sets this species apart

Human-bodied exalts who channel power from their shattered home realm. The exact shard powers are set at breed level.
