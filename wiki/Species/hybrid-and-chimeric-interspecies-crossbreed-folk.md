# Interspecies crossbreed folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

The children of two very different parents. One parent may be human and the other something much more: a spirit, a demon, a being from another world, a stock of superhuman strength. The child inherits some of both, and fully belongs to neither. Most get a share of the stronger parent's toughness and some of its gifts, but not all, and age more like the weaker parent.

They often have to choose a side, and are rarely welcomed by either. A half-spirit child grows up hearing whispers from both villages. A warrior with demon blood in their veins fights every day to stay who they are, knowing that one day the other half may win. Some crossbreeds heal from terrible wounds. All of them carry the question of where they belong.

## Not to be confused with

*[hybrid bloodline human](humankind-hybrid-bloodline-human.md)*, humans with a distant trace of other blood; crossbreeds are first-generation children of two different peoples.

## What sets this species apart

Children of two bloodlines, often inheriting a share of a stronger parent's resilience. Healing Factor is affinity; lines that regenerate Set it at Breed/Race level. Parent-family lines are chosen per Breed/Race.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
