# Decay elemental-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

Where these walk, things rot. They are the living faces of decay itself: the great force of death, disease and corruption that answers every force of growth. Their bodies are not flesh but pestilence given form, heaps of mould, marsh-rot and old bone that somehow keep a shape. Flies attend them. Fruit spoils as they pass.

Their will reaches the dead. Swarms of rotting servants shamble at their word, and plague spreads in their wake like ripples on still water. They are not so much evil as inevitable, and they say so, with a terrible patience. As long as decay endures, they endure. Druids and healers oppose them, knowing the fight cannot be won, only held off for another season.

## Not to be confused with

*[True elemental](elemental-kind-true-elemental.md)*, living fire, stone, air or water; the decay elemental embodies rot and pestilence, and commands the dead.

## What sets this species apart

Living avatars of rot and plague who command the rotting dead. NPC-scale.
