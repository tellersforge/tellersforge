# Spirit-bound armor-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

A suit of armour that walks, speaks and remembers, and is completely empty. There is no body inside, only a will bound into the steel by enchantment. Look through the visor and you see the lining of the helm. The armour is the body: it cannot be taken off, it clanks at every step, and it weighs as much as the knight it might once have held.

They are long-lived, often very long, and many spend those years as scholars and warriors, patient and precise. They need no food or sleep. Water is their enemy: they sink, and must walk out along the bottom. Heard coming a hallway away, they make poor spies. Those who know them find them courteous, a little sad, and very good company on a night watch.

## Not to be confused with

*[Construct-kind](constructs-and-synthetics-construct-kind.md)*, which is built and animated as a whole; the haunted armour holds a living will that was bound into an empty suit.

## What sets this species apart

A bodiless will living in enchanted armour: armour is its body, heavy and loud, and it sinks.
