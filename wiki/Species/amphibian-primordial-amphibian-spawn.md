# Primordial amphibian spawn

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md)*

Monstrous broodlings of a primordial power that was sealed away before the world was finished. They are enormous, misshapen things, part frog, part squid, part nightmare, with grasping tentacles and mouths in the wrong places. They have no minds to speak of, only an urge to break and swallow. Each one that is loosed on the world is a sign that worse is coming.

They cannot be reasoned with or bargained with, and they do not stop. The few who have faced them and lived speak of swamps boiling and towns sinking into mud.

## Not to be confused with

the *[aquatic monster-kind](aquatic-aquatic-monster-kind.md)*, which are beasts of the water; these spawn are harbingers of ruin from before the world.

## What sets this species apart

Huge, mindless tentacled brood-monsters built for ruin rather than leaping. NPC-scale. Grasping tentacles; the lash is NPC-scale. No hands: held weapons unusable.
