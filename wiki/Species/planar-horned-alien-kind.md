# Horned alien-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

They look like every devil in every old painting: horned, taloned, with leathery wings and a long barbed tail. They are not devils. They are a people from another world, older and wiser than they appear, with a gift for sorcery and a mind that can reach into other minds.

Their appearance has cost them dearly. Zealots who took them for demons hunted them nearly to extinction, and the survivors are few, scattered and wary. Most hide, some wear disguises, a handful fight back. Among themselves they are scholarly, courteous and fond of long debate. Among strangers they are guarded, because experience has taught them that the first thing anyone sees is horns.

## Not to be confused with

the *[infernal/demonic-kind](planar-infernal-demonic-kind.md)*, true hellish outsiders, where these folk only look demonic and are mortal people of another world.

## What sets this species apart

Demonic-looking aliens rather than true divine beings: powerful sorcerers and psychics with talons.
