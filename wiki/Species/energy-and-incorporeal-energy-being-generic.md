# Energy being (generic)

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

Life without a body. These beings are knots of light and heat that hold together by some principle no scientist fully understands. They drift through space, cluster around stars and nebulae, or hang in the air of worlds where the conditions suit them. Up close they hum faintly and warm the skin like a fire in a hearth.

What they want is hard to say. Some seem curious and approach explorers like wild animals investigating a camp. Others ignore living things completely. A few feed on energy and drain ships, stations or people who stray too near. They communicate, if at all, through changes of colour, pattern and pulse. Exploration crews learn to treat them with the same caution they would give a storm: not evil, perhaps, but vast, and not to be taken lightly.

## Not to be confused with

*[plasma-bodied folk](amorphous-and-ooze-plasma-bodied-folk.md)*, beings whose bodies are made of plasma yet hold a shape and a society; energy beings are alien, formless lights.

## What sets this species apart

Bodiless beings of light and heat. Elemental Aura is heat.
