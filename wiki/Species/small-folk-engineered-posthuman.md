# Engineered posthuman

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Small folk](../Species_Families/small-folk.md)*

Humans made small on purpose. Generations ago, their ancestors were engineered to half the height and weight of ordinary people so that crowded space habitats could feed and shelter more of them. The result is a slim, quick, light-boned folk who look like perfect miniatures of their tall cousins.

They are agile and eat little, and they fit where others cannot. They are also fragile: a fall that would bruise a tall human can break their bones. They mostly live in stations and ships, and they tend to see planet-born humans as wasteful giants. Their tall cousins sometimes treat them as children, a mistake that generally lasts about one conversation.

## Not to be confused with

*[Tiny folk](humankind-tiny-folk.md)* are small by nature; engineered posthumans were made small by design, and are frailer for it.

## What sets this species apart

Humans engineered to half size; the difference is size and frailty, which are attributes.
