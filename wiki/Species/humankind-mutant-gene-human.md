# Mutant-gene human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Humans carrying a hidden gene that wakes without warning, usually around the time a child becomes an adult. What it brings is anyone's guess: fire from the hands, wings from the back, the voices of other minds, a body of stone or a touch that drains the life from others. Some can hide it. Many cannot, and live in tunnels under cities or in schools and compounds far from public eyes. A rare strain barely ages and heals from almost anything.

The world does not agree on what they are: the next step for humanity, a danger to be registered, a weapon to be recruited or a curse. Most mutants are simply trying to get through the week with a gift they never asked for.

## Not to be confused with

the *[mist-awakened human](humankind-mist-awakened-human.md)*, whose gift is unlocked by an outside trigger; this gene wakes on its own and follows no pattern.

## What sets this species apart

The latent gene expresses as a different power in each lineage or individual, so nothing is shared at species level. Powers are Set at Breed/Race level or chosen per individual.
