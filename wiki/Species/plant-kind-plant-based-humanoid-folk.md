# Plant-based humanoid folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md)*

Whole peoples whose bodies are green and growing rather than meat and bone. They look close enough to human at a distance: two arms, two legs, a face. Close up the differences show. Skin like a leaf, hair like roots or moss, a faint scent of sap, and a way of turning towards the sun without noticing.

Each people is its own. Some are gentle pacifist priests with blue skin and quiet psychic gifts. Some regrow lost limbs and lay their young like seeds. Some are mournful, root-haired folk who speak easily with the dead. Some can regrow an entire body from a single twig. They come from many worlds, and what they have in common is little more than the green.

## Not to be confused with

the *[vine/flower folk](plant-kind-vine-flower-folk.md)*, small garden-grown plant folk, where these are full-sized humanoid peoples from their own homeworlds.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference: these named plant races differ in regeneration, psychic gifts and homeworld, so each is handled at Breed/Race level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
