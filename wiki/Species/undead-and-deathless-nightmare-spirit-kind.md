# Nightmare spirit-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

You wake unable to move. The room is dark, the air is thick, and something small and heavy is sitting on your chest. This is the nightmare spirit's work. It creeps into bedrooms after midnight, settles on the sleeper, and pours terror into their dreams until they wake gasping, or do not wake at all for hours.

By day it is almost nothing: a shadow under the eaves, a cat that isn't quite a cat. By night it is patient and cruel, and it returns to the same victims night after night, feeding on their fear. Folk remedies are many: a knife under the pillow, shoes turned toward the door, a charm scratched on the bedpost. Some of them even work.

## Not to be confused with

*[Dream-realm natives](trans-dimensional-dream-realm-natives.md)*, who live inside the world of dreams; the nightmare spirit comes from outside, and reaches dreams only by sitting on the sleeper.

## What sets this species apart

A night spirit that creeps onto sleepers and fills their dreams with terror.
