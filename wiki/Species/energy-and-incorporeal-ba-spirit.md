# Ba spirit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

A bird with a human face: the soul's personality, given wings. In the beliefs of an ancient river kingdom, every person has more than one soul, and this one is the part that makes you who you are. When the body dies and is sealed in the tomb, the soul-bird does not stay there. It flies out by day, crossing into the world of the living to visit old haunts and loved ones, and returns to the body at night.

It is small, quiet and sad-eyed, about the size of a falcon, with the face its owner wore in life. Priests carve its likeness into tombs and paint it on coffins so it can find its way home. To the living it is a comfort and a warning: a sign that the dead are still near, still themselves, and still watching what is done with their name.

## Not to be confused with

*[harpy-kind](avian-harpy-kind.md)*, fierce bird-women of flesh and blood; the soul-bird is a gentle spirit of the dead.

## What sets this species apart

Winged soul-spirits that fly between the tomb and the world of the living.
