# Swamp-dwelling folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md)*

Humanlike people of the deep swamps, pale or mottled, with some damp amphibious traits: webbed fingers, slick skin, a tolerance for foul water that visitors cannot match. They live in stilt villages and hidden islands among the reeds, and they keep to themselves.

They are neither frogs nor fish, and they dislike being mistaken for either. Travellers who respect their silence may be guided safely through the mire. Those who do not tend to find the paths shifting under them.

## Not to be confused with

the *[swamp ogre-lizard folk](reptilian-swamp-ogre-lizard-folk.md)*, big reptilian brutes; these are quiet, humanlike marsh dwellers.

## What sets this species apart

Humanlike swamp people with only amphibious tendencies, lacking frog legs.
