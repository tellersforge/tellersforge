# Living-planet kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

Not a creature on a world but the world itself, awake. Its forests are its skin, its seas its blood, its mountains the slow bones of a body too large to see from anywhere but the sky. It cannot walk or reach out a hand. It does not need to. It raises ridges, opens valleys, turns rivers and coaxes new life from its soil, and it speaks directly into the minds of those who stand on it.

Such a being thinks in ages. It is patient beyond anything a mortal can understand, and most who visit it never realise they were being watched. Some are tended by small peoples who live on their surface as gardeners and priests. One kind can pull or loosen its own weight, so that a ship in orbit feels its mood.

## Not to be confused with

the *[cosmic space-whale kind](aquatic-cosmic-space-whale-kind.md)*, vast living beings that travel between worlds, where this one is a world and never goes anywhere.

## What sets this species apart

A whole living world: it shapes its own ground and speaks mind to mind, but cannot move like a creature. NPC-scale. Gravity Control is affinity only; one line manipulates its own gravity. Lifespan (ageless or immortal) is a lexicon detail, not a game number. A world, not a fighter: no stone fist and no family Brawling bonus. No hands: held weapons unusable.
