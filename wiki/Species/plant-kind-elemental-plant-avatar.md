# Elemental plant avatar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md)*

A huge shambling figure of moss, mud, vines and rotting leaves, with a face that is almost human and eyes that glow faintly green. It is an avatar of the green: the great force of growing life that runs through all plants everywhere. Some began when that force swallowed a dying person's memories and wove them into a body of swamp.

It can feel every root and leaf around it, and command them to grow, twist or strangle. Cut it down, burn it, scatter it, and it grows back from the nearest patch of weeds, in this forest or a forest on the other side of the world. The oldest of its kind sit rooted and silent in a council of ancient memory. Mortals find it grim, sad and fiercely protective of all that grows.

## Not to be confused with

the *[true elemental](elemental-kind-true-elemental.md)*, a being of raw fire, earth or water, where the avatar is the living voice of all growing plants.

## What sets this species apart

Avatars of the green elemental force: they command and grow plant life over vast distances and regrow from vegetation. The council elder breed is rooted and near-immobile; that is set at Breed/Race level. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
