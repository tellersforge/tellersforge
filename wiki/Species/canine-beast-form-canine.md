# Beast-form canine

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md)*

Hounds and wolves on four legs, without hands, though some of them think and even speak. Beast-form canines range from ordinary wolves to the great monsters of legend: many-headed hounds that guard the gates of the dead, spectral green hounds of the fairy hills, giant speaking wolves ridden by goblin raiders, scaly tracking hounds bred to hunt fugitives, and alien packs where one body is a dumb animal but several together become a person.

They run tirelessly, smell everything and bite hard. What they cannot do is grasp: no sword, no tool, no rein, no latch. Those that are intelligent rely on friends with hands, and they tend to choose those friends with great care.

## Not to be confused with

*[Canine folk](canine-canine-folk.md)*, the upright dog-people with hands, who walk on two legs and use tools like anyone else.

## What sets this species apart

Four-legged hounds and wolves with no hands, so fine handwork and riding are beyond the body; a quadruped gait runs a little better. No hands: held weapons unusable.
