# Asura-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

Demi-divine titans, taller and heavier than mortals, with hard skin, many-coloured eyes and in some tellings extra arms. They are neither heavenly nor hellish, but something between: powerful, proud and eternally at war with the radiant servants of the heavens, whom they regard as smug rivals who stole what should have been theirs.

They combine sorcery and martial skill in equal measure, and they take both seriously. An asura may spend a century mastering a spell and another century mastering a spear. Their courts are magnificent and their tempers short. Mortals who meet them find them impressive, generous to those who flatter them wisely, and deadly to those who confuse pride with foolishness.

## Not to be confused with

the *[celestial-kind](planar-celestial-kind.md)*, the radiant servants of order whom asuras envy and fight, and who lack their pride and hunger.

## What sets this species apart

Prideful demi-divine titans, combining magic with martial might and a hard, tough body.
