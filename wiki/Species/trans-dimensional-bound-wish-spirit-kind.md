# Bound wish-spirit-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Ancient spirits of smoke and fire bound, by old law or older ritual, to a lamp, a ring, a bottle or a jar. Whoever holds the vessel holds the spirit, and the spirit must grant that holder's wishes. Its power is vast; its freedom is not.

Every wish is bound by rules and wording, and the spirit knows those rules far better than any master. Some spirits serve with grace, some with wry resentment, and some twist each careless phrase into a lesson. They have lived for ages, served kings and beggars alike, and learned exactly how little most people understand about what they want. Many of them long for one thing only: to be set free.

## Not to be confused with

*[Genie-kind](planar-genie-kind.md)* are free planar spirits of fire and air; bound wish-spirit-kind are chained to a vessel and must grant wishes to whoever holds it.

## What sets this species apart

Ancient bound spirits whose nature is to grant wishes to whoever holds their vessel. Wish Granting works only for the vessel's holder and under the exact wording and binding rules. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
