# Rift and portal creatures

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Things that come through. When a rift tears open in a laboratory, a cellar or a forest clearing, these are the creatures that climb out: spiked hounds in packs, faceless stalkers whose heads open like flowers, tribal invaders from a blasted border-world, burning demons out of some furnace dimension, and above them all, vast minds and warlords who want the door kept open.

They are solid, hostile and all too real. Most hunt by sound, heat or scent, and many are driven by a single master's will. Wherever they emerge, panic follows: power fails, animals flee and survivors talk for years about the smell. Sealing the rift is the only lasting cure, and it is never as simple as it sounds.

## Not to be confused with

*[Far-realm and outer entities](trans-dimensional-far-realm-and-outer-entities.md)* are cosmic horrors whose presence unhinges minds; rift and portal creatures are solid invaders who force their way through tears in the world.

## What sets this species apart

Solid invaders that force their way through rifts and tears, frightening wherever they emerge.
