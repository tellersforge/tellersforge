# Astral-plane native-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

The astral sea is silver, endless and weightless, a luminous gulf that links the planes as water links islands. These folk were born there. Some live in quiet monasteries adrift on it, learning to leave their bodies behind and travel as pure thought on a silver thread. Others are nomads and raiders who sail its currents between worlds and fight with the mind as readily as the blade.

Time barely touches them out there, so they live long, and some age only when they return to a world of their own. Their gifts lean toward the psionic: steady minds, sharp wills, a talent for finding the way where there are no roads. On solid ground they seem a little distant, as if half listening to a sea no one else can hear.

## Not to be confused with

*[Astral elf](elf-kind-astral-elf.md)*, an elven people who settled the same silver sea; these natives are no elves, and their bent is the trained mind rather than elven magic.

## What sets this species apart

Long-lived natives of the weightless astral sea with a psionic bent. Lifespan (long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
