# Children-of-the-forest-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Small, ancient people of the deep woods, who lived in the land long before anyone else came to it. They are slight and brown as bark, with large dark eyes, and they move through the trees without disturbing a leaf. Their magic is rooted in the land itself: they sing to stone and root, and they dream green dreams in which past and future lie side by side like pages of an open book.

When the newer peoples came with axes and fire, the forest folk fought and lost, and retreated to the last old groves. It is said that in their desperation they once made a terrible weapon, and that its consequences are still being paid for. Few now see them. Those who do find them kind, sad and utterly unwilling to trust.

## Not to be confused with

*[small forest-bonded folk](plant-kind-small-forest-bonded-folk.md)*, the little woodland people who tend a patch of wood; the children of the forest are older, rarer, and carry the memory of the whole land.

## What sets this species apart

Small, ancient forest people with deep nature magic and prophetic green-dreams.
