# Dog-yokai

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md)*

Proud, beautiful and very old. Dog demons are canine spirits of great power who usually wear the form of tall, striking people, with only the ears, the markings or the golden eyes to betray the hound beneath. Pressed hard, they take their true shape: an enormous white dog with teeth like spears. They live for centuries, and their bodies hold a deep store of demonic energy that they can draw on in battle.

Their clans are aristocratic and dismissive of mortals, fond of lineage, honour and disdain. Humans who serve them are often ignored; those who cross them are not. A dog demon's respect is rare and hard-won, and once given, surprisingly lasting.

## Not to be confused with

*[Fox spirit](canine-fox-spirit.md)*, the other great shapeshifter of animal legend, who trades on trickery and charm rather than raw power and pedigree.

## What sets this species apart

Long-lived canine demons carrying a deep reserve of innate demonic energy. Store Power stands for the innate demonic-energy reserve. Lifespan (long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
