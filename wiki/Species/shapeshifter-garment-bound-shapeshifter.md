# Garment-bound shapeshifter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

Folk of two shapes who can move between them only by putting on, or taking off, a special garment: a sealskin, a cloak of white feathers, a pelt passed down from mother to child. With it on, they are a seal in the grey surf or a swan on the lake. With it off, they are men and women of uncommon grace, often with something of the sea or the sky in their eyes.

The garment is their life. Lose it, and they are trapped in human form; steal it, and you hold their freedom in your hands, which is why so many of the old tales begin with a thief hiding a skin. They are gentle, melancholy people, forever looking back toward the water.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lycanthrope](shapeshifter-lycanthrope.md)* shifts by curse or moon and fights a beast's temper; the garment-bound shapeshifter changes only through its skin or cloak and is helpless without it.

## What sets this species apart

They change only between human and one animal shape, and only by donning their skin or feather garment; they are not mimics. Animal Form works only while the garment is in their possession; if it is taken they cannot change.
