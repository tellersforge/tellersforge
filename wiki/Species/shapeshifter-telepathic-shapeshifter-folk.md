# Telepathic shapeshifter folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

A tall, grave people from a dying desert world, green or white of skin, with deep-set eyes and a stillness that unsettles others. They can reshape their bodies, hear the thoughts of those around them, and slip into invisibility or pass through walls at will. They live for centuries and remember everything.

Their history is a wound. Long ago the pale caste made war on the green, and a plague finished much of what the war began. The survivors are few. The green are meditative, empathic and gentle, the pale harsh and martial, and both share one terrible fear: fire, which burns them far worse than it burns anyone else. They are rarely seen, and even more rarely trusted.

## Not to be confused with

*[Psychic/telepathic human](humankind-psychic-telepathic-human.md)* reads minds but keeps one shape; telepathic shapeshifter folk change form, turn invisible and pass through walls as well.

## What sets this species apart

Shapeshifting telepaths who can also turn intangible or invisible at will. Vulnerable to fire (a flaw, handled outside this layer).
