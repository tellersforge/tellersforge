# Beasts of burden

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

The patient animals that carry civilisation on their backs. Hump-backed draft beasts, shaggy horned giants of the desert, long-necked pack animals that walk for days without water, foul-smelling cold-world mounts wrapped in thick pelts, and docile herd beasts raised for meat and hide. None of them think as people do, and none have hands; they are livestock and mounts, bred for strength, calm and endurance.

Their peoples know every one by name. A desert tribe may milk, ride, sleep against and sing to its great horned beasts, and weep when one dies. To outsiders they are loud, slow and smelly, until the outsiders have to cross a waste on foot and learn what the beasts are worth.

## Not to be confused with

*[Camelid folk](ungulate-camelid-folk.md)*, the thinking pack-carrying people; beasts of burden are true animals, bred and led by others.

## What sets this species apart

Non-sentient four-legged livestock and mounts with no hands, bred for hard hauling. Non-sentient beasts: social and craft skills do not apply. No hands: held weapons unusable.
