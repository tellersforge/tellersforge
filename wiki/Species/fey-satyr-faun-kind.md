# Satyr/faun-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Goat-legged fey with a man's chest, curling horns and a grin that promises trouble. They are springy and sure-footed, happy on a cliff path or a tavern table, and they never seem to tire of music, wine and company. Many carry pipes or drums and play them well enough to set a whole village dancing. Sweet words and glamours slide off them, which makes them very poor marks for a fey noble's charm and very good company at a fey noble's party.

A rarer, older kind keeps watch at the gates of hidden realms under the hills. These guardians are tall, slow-spoken and strange, and they set tests for the one mortal they have chosen. Their revelling cousins find them dull. Their chosen mortals find them terrifying.

## Not to be confused with

*[caprine folk](ungulate-caprine-folk.md)*, the goat-descended people of the ordinary animal line; satyrs and fauns are fey, made for revelry and old magic rather than for herding and trade.

## What sets this species apart

Goat-legged fey, springy and sure-footed, given to revelry.
