# Alien tinkerer ape-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md)*

Orange-furred alien apes with long arms, a short tail and an extraordinary knack for making machines work. Their speech is simple and their manners rough, but put scrap metal, wire and a broken engine in front of one and something useful appears, often a weapon, usually loud, and always slightly different from the last one.

They cannot explain how they do it. It is instinct, like a bird building a nest. Other species hire them, capture them or trade for their services, and treat them with the mix of respect and exasperation reserved for a genius who eats with his hands. Their workshops look like rubbish heaps. Their inventions do tend to go off, one way or another.

## Not to be confused with

*[Gnome-kind](small-folk-gnome-kind.md)* are small, clever inventors who plan every part; alien tinkerer ape-kind build by instinct, and rarely know why their devices work.

## What sets this species apart

Savant improvisers who can build working devices from scraps almost by instinct.
