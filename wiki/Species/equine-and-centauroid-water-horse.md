# Water horse

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Equine and centauroid](../Species_Families/equine-and-centauroid.md)*

A beautiful horse stands at the edge of a lake or river, dripping, its mane tangled with weed. It is gentle, and it seems to want a rider. Children reach out to stroke it, and travellers climb on to cross the ford. Then their hands stick fast to its hide, and it plunges into the deep water and does not come up again.

The water-horse is a shapeshifting spirit of lochs and rivers, a predator in equine form. It can walk as a handsome stranger at a dance, and only wet sand in its hair or hooves under its coat may give it away. Some say a bridle cast over its head binds it to serve. Some say iron or salt keeps it off. Everyone who lives near dark water knows the warning: never mount a horse you find by the shore, and never trust a stranger who smells of the river.

## Not to be confused with

*[water spirit-kind](aquatic-water-spirit-kind.md)*, the broader family of river and sea spirits; the water-horse is one predatory kind that hunts in equine form.

## What sets this species apart

Shapeshifting water-horses of lake and river that lure riders to drown. No hands in horse form: held weapons unusable.
