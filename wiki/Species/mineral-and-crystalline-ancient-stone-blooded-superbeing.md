# Ancient stone-blooded superbeing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

They look like tall, perfectly built men and women with skin like warm marble, and they are older than any human people. For ages they slept inside stone pillars, waiting. When one wakes it is agile, graceful and terrifying: its body mends from any wound in moments, and it can reshape its own flesh into blades, spines and hooks as easily as a person clenches a fist.

They regard ordinary people as livestock, or at best as curiosities. Their one great terror is the sun. Daylight turns them to stone and then to dust, so they move by night, sleep in the dark and build their plans around shadow. Those who have faced one describe it the same way: a beautiful face, a polite voice, and nothing that can kill it but the dawn.

## Not to be confused with

the *[vampire-kind](undead-and-deathless-vampire-kind.md)*, who also shun the sun and prey on the living, but are the risen dead, where these beings were never human at all.

## What sets this species apart

Flesh-like, agile ancient beings with near-unstoppable regeneration and bodies they can rebuild into weapons. NPC-scale. Destroyed by sunlight. The Running, Acrobatics and Swimming lines undo the family's heavy-stone penalties. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number. Flesh-like bodies, so no stone fist; the weapon forms it shapes from its body are its Self-Alteration power.
