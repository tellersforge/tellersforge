# Bovine folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

Big, horned and steady. Bovine folk carry the build of cattle, bison and oxen: deep chests, heavy shoulders, broad faces crowned with curved horns. They are made for endurance rather than speed. A bovine labourer can plough from dawn to dusk and still carry the plough home, but will lose any footrace to a child.

Most live in herds and clans, with strong family ties and a long memory for insults. Some are gentle farmers, some fierce highland clans, some plains warriors whose charge can break a shield wall. Shaggy northern kin circle together against wolves and weather. Among many peoples, bovine folk are the backbone of the harvest and the rearguard of the army, and they do not like being taken for granted.

## Not to be confused with

*[Minotaur-kind](ungulate-minotaur-kind.md)*, bull-headed fighters built for battle; bovine folk are herd people, farmers and clans first, and warriors only when they must be.

## What sets this species apart

Horned, hardy cattle builds: stronger on endurance, slower in a sprint.
