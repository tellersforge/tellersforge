# Equine folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Equine and centauroid](../Species_Families/equine-and-centauroid.md)*

Horse-kin who stand and walk on two legs. They have a horse's long face, mobile ears and swishing tail, a strong neck and powerful legs, but hands and an upright stance, so ladders, saddles and workbenches suit them as well as anyone. The breeds are as varied as real horses: sleek runners with endurance to spare, sturdy little ponies, striped savanna folk with a vicious kick, sure-footed donkey-folk who can carry a load all day, and massive draft-folk with feathered ankles and the patience of a mountain.

They value the herd. Family, village and caravan matter more to them than to most peoples, and they are uneasy alone. Temperaments follow build: the runners restless, the ponies canny, the donkeys stubborn. In some far-off tellings, true four-legged horses speak and rule a land of their own, regarding the rest of the world with calm contempt.

## Not to be confused with

*[centaur](equine-and-centauroid-centaur.md)*, whose horse body and human torso give them four legs and great speed; equine folk walk upright on two.

## What sets this species apart

Upright two-legged builds: ladders and saddles are within reach, unlike the four-legged family template. Four-legged Breeds/Races Set Riding back to Foreign Concept and Mod- Climbing. Two-legged: no centauroid charge, so the Lances bonus is cancelled.
