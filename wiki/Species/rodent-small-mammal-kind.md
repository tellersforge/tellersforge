# Small mammal-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md)*

A mixed crowd of small rodent folk, each shaped closely after its own animal. Beavers with flat tails and orange teeth, who build dams and lodges and are never idle. Capybaras, big, calm and sociable, happy in a pond with friends. Porcupines covered in quills. Chinchillas in impossibly soft fur. Cheek-stuffing hamsters and chipmunks, chattering guinea pigs, burrowing marmots who sleep through winter, and wrinkled, hairless mole-rats in their eusocial colonies.

They have little in common except size, teeth and a practical approach to life. Most are good-natured, hard-working and fond of their homes. Other peoples know them mostly as neighbours, craftsfolk and the reason the river now has a pond in it.

## Not to be confused with

the *[mouse and squirrel-kind](rodent-mouse-and-squirrel-kind.md)*, the quick, big-eared climbers, where this is a mixed crowd of other rodent shapes.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference: each breed follows its own real animal (beaver, porcupine, mole and so on), handled at Breed/Race level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
