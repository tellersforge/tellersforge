# Household spirit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

Every old house has one, if the family is lucky. The hearth spirit is tiny, rarely seen and never quite still: a flicker at the edge of the fire, a warmth in the storeroom on a cold night, a creak on the stair when no one is climbing it. It is bound to one home and one lineage, and it looks after both.

Families keep small shrines by the hearth, with a lamp, a pinch of salt or the first spoon of every meal. In return the spirit guards the stores against rot and mice, watches over sleeping children and keeps misfortune from crossing the threshold. It has no interest in strangers or in wider affairs. If a family moves, the wise ones carry embers from the old hearth to the new one, and hope the spirit follows. A house whose spirit has left feels cold, whatever the fire.

## Not to be confused with

*[household fey](fey-household-fey.md)*, small fey servants and pranksters who can be bribed or offended; the hearth spirit is an ancestral guardian bound to one family.

## What sets this species apart

Tiny hearth spirits bound to guard one home and its people.
