# Human culture folk

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Humans who are no different in body from anyone else, but whom the world knows by how they live. Caravan families who read the dunes and ration water as carefully as coin. Horse clans on open grassland who learn to ride before they walk. Ice hunters who wait at a breathing hole for half a day and think nothing of it. Sea nomads born, married and buried on lashed rafts that rarely touch land. Forest trackers with poisoned darts, island tribes who hunt beasts older than kingdoms, frontier settlers who remember a lost fleet in their songs.

Outsiders often mistake the way of life for the blood. A child taken from the steppe and raised in a harbour town grows up a sailor. What they carry is custom, craft and pride, and those are harder to take away than any birthright.

## Not to be confused with

the *[baseline human](humankind-baseline-human.md)*, which is the same stock described without any particular way of life attached.

## What sets this species apart

Physically baseline humans; their differences are cultural (desert, steppe, arctic, sea, tribal and so on) and are handled in the Culture step.
