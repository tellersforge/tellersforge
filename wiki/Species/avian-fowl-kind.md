# Fowl-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Heavy-bodied birds who gave up the sky for the ground or the water. Fowl-kind covers penguin folk, sleek swimmers who waddle on land and huddle together against the cold; long-necked runners who cannot fly but can kick a wolf senseless; web-footed waterfowl who honk, migrate and paddle; and the hardy barnyard fowl folk, sturdy foragers who raise large families.

Most fly a little or not at all, and on land they are rarely graceful. In water, the swimming lines are another matter entirely. Other peoples often cast fowl-kind as comic or humble, farmers and ferrymen rather than heroes, and fowl-kind stopped arguing about it long ago. They are practical, stubborn and very hard to starve out.

## Not to be confused with

*[Flightless runner mount](avian-flightless-runner-mount.md)*, a beast bred purely for speed and riding, where fowl-kind are thinking people.

## What sets this species apart

Heavier ground and water birds that fly little or not at all. Swimming breeds cancel the family's Swimming penalty at breed level.
