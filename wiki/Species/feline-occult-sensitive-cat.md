# Occult-sensitive cat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md)*

An ordinary cat, as far as anyone can tell: sleeping on the windowsill, knocking things off shelves, ignoring its owners' commands. But a cat sees what people cannot. It stares at the empty corner of the room because the corner is not empty. It hisses at a stranger because the stranger is wearing someone else's face. It refuses to enter one bedroom, and there is a reason.

These cats perceive the supernatural as clearly as they see mice. They are independent and secretive, and they have their own politics: an unspoken society of neighbourhood cats who watch over the unseen and share what they know only with each other. Sometimes they ally with the neighbourhood dogs against a common threat, sometimes they scorn them. Either way, a wise household pays attention when the cat starts staring, and does not argue when it wants to leave.

## Not to be confused with

*[occult-sensitive dog](canine-occult-sensitive-dog.md)*, the loyal canine counterpart; the cat sees the same unseen world, but keeps its own counsel.

## What sets this species apart

Ordinary four-legged cats who perceive the supernatural. No hands: held weapons unusable.
