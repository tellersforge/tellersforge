# Totem-bonded dimension-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Ordinary people, at first. A student, a dockhand, a pensioner, a creature from some other world entirely: the host can be anyone, of any kind, in any reality. What makes them one people is the bond. A totem force older than worlds, shaped like the spider, settles on them and stays. Afterwards they cling to walls and ceilings as easily as floors, feel a prickle at the back of the neck a moment before danger arrives, and carry strength out of all proportion to their size.

The bond does not change their face, their family or their rent. Most hide it and live double lives, which is harder than it sounds. Across the many worlds, hosts sometimes meet and recognise the same gift in strangers. They also learn, sooner or later, that something hunts them.

## Not to be confused with

*[Multiversal predator-kind](trans-dimensional-multiversal-predator-kind.md)*, the hunters who feed on hosts like these; and *[arachnid folk](insectoid-and-arachnid-arachnid-folk.md)*, who are born spider-bodied rather than bonded to a spider's power.

## What sets this species apart

Ordinary hosts bonded to a spider totem that grants wall-crawling, a danger sense and proportional strength.
