# Bred weapon organism folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

A killing machine grown in a vat and hardened by repetition. Its makers cloned it, killed it, and cloned it again, each time from the one that lasted longest, until what came out the other end could not be killed by anything they had. It is huge, grey and bony, and it barely thinks beyond the next blow. When it is harmed, it heals. When it is harmed the same way twice, it has already grown proof against it.

It was made to end battles, and it does. It does not stop when told to, does not tire and does not fear. Whoever finally faces one has to find something it has never suffered before, and hope that it works the first time, because there will not be a second.

## Not to be confused with

*[bio-engineered soldier folk](constructs-and-synthetics-bio-engineered-soldier-folk.md)*, synthetic soldiers built to follow orders; the weapon organism is a single monster bred to be unkillable and nothing else.

## What sets this species apart

A near-unkillable killing organism that regenerates and grows immune to whatever harmed it. NPC-scale. Invulnerability stands for adaptive immunity.
