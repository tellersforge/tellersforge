# Ancient mystic fey-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Old, slow, gentle sages who walk on four legs and speak without words. Their talk is a low humming that fills the head of the listener rather than the ears, and they seem to think in long circles, returning to a question years after it was asked. They have no hands and little interest in making things. What they do is watch, remember and consider.

Each one is bound mind to mind with a single counterpart, a creature of an opposite and often cruel nature, born of the same ancient stock. What one suffers the other feels. That bond is the great sorrow of their lives, and it is why they are so patient and so unwilling to act in haste. Visitors come for wisdom and usually leave with a riddle instead.

## Not to be confused with

*[noble glamour fey](fey-noble-glamour-fey.md)*, the scheming courtiers of the fairy realms; the mystic sages have none of the family's trickery and want only to be left to think.

## What sets this species apart

Gentle, contemplative sages without the family's trickery, speaking mind to mind and bound to a psychic counterpart. Mind Link is with the paired counterpart only. No hands: held weapons unusable.
