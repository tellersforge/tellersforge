# Exotic-build elf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

Elves who look like no one else's idea of an elf. One lineage is startlingly tall, with long upright ears like a hare's that swivel toward every sound, and a lean, springing build that makes its people superb archers and runners. Nearly all who leave their forest villages are women, and they are known as hunters and wandering mages. Another lineage is tall in a more stately way, broad-shouldered and grave, with a formal culture of titles, oaths and knightly service and a talent for both the sword and the spell.

What joins them to the rest of elf-kind is the long life, the grace and the sharp senses. What sets them apart is simply the frame they were born with. Other peoples notice their height first, and remember them for it.

## Not to be confused with

*[lagomorph folk](lagomorph-lagomorph-folk.md)*, true hare- and rabbit-kin; the long-eared elves only share the ears and are elves in every other respect.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference from the family: height is an attribute and size matter, and the lineages share nothing else beyond it.
