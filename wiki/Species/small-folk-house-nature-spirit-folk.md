# House/nature spirit folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Small folk](../Species_Families/small-folk.md)*

Little spirits of the home and the land: the old man under the stove, the woman in the cornfield, the builders in the forest who finish a bridge in a single night. They are tiny, rarely seen and deeply bound to a place, a farm or a family.

Treat them well, with a saucer of milk, a clean hearth, a place at the harvest feast, and they bring good fortune: lost things turn up, the cows give more milk, the grain stays dry. Neglect or insult them and the milk turns sour, tools go missing, the horses are ridden ragged at night. Wise families keep the old courtesies without asking why, and do not go looking for thanks.

## Not to be confused with

*[Household spirit](energy-and-incorporeal-household-spirit.md)* is an energy being tied to one home; house or nature spirit folk are small folk who bring luck or mischief to any house, field or wood.

## What sets this species apart

Tiny household and land spirits who bring luck to those who honour them and mischief to those who don't.
