# Earthborn giant

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Giant-kind](../Species_Families/giant-kind.md)*

Giants born straight out of the earth, where the blood of an ancient wound soaked into the soil. From the waist up they are vast, bearded warriors; from the hips down their legs end in huge coiling serpents, each with its own jaws. The serpents writhe and strike at anything that comes near, and a hero who closes in has to fight three enemies at once.

They are poor runners and clumsy leapers, since serpent coils make heavy going on rough ground. In the old tales they rose in rebellion against the gods of the sky and were hurled down beneath mountains and islands, which still smoke where they lie. Some believe they are only sleeping, and that every earthquake is one of them trying to wake.

## Not to be confused with

*[giant-kind](../Species_Families/giant-kind.md)*, the broad family of true giants who walk on legs; the earthborn are giants whose legs are living serpents.

## What sets this species apart

Serpent-tailed legs make running and leaping hard. The serpent legs coil and bite.
