# Deathless hero-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

Heroes who died, or should have, and came back changed. Death touched them and left a mark: a grey pallor, a cold hand, eyes that have seen the far shore. Some were blessed by death's own power and carry it like a lamp. Others were forged anew by accident or alchemy, and their bodies wither and renew on some strange cycle of their own.

They are attuned to death as a musician is to pitch. They sense the dying before anyone else, they hear the dead, and the arts of necromancy come to them as naturally as breathing. Most are tragic figures, walking the line between two worlds and belonging to neither. The living fear them, the dead distrust them, and nobody invites them to weddings.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lich-kind](undead-and-deathless-lich-kind.md)*, sorcerers who made themselves undead for power; the deathless hero was remade by death itself, and usually did not choose it.

## What sets this species apart

Mortals remade by death's power, attuned to death and necromancy.
