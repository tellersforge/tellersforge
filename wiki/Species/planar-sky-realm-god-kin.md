# Sky-realm god-kin

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

Folk from a realm high above the mortal world, joined to it by a bridge of light. They are tall, strong and hardy beyond any human, able to shrug off wounds that would kill an army. They live for thousands of years, long enough that the ancient peoples of the world below worshipped them as gods, and they have not entirely objected.

What mortals call magic, they treat as a craft, with smiths who forge enchanted hammers and scholars who study the patterns of fate the way engineers study load and stress. They are proud, warlike and fond of feasting, and their history is full of wars with neighbouring realms. Two rival tribes of them share the sky, bound by old treaties and older grudges.

## Not to be confused with

the *[cosmic god-race](planar-cosmic-god-race.md)*, near-immortal folk of two opposed homeworlds, where the sky-realm gods live in one realm and treat magic as their craft.

## What sets this species apart

Millennia-lived god-folk with superhuman strength and durability who treat magic as a craft. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
