# Dog-headed folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md)*

Small, upright folk with the heads of dogs: floppy or pricked ears, wet noses and a very good idea of what is cooking. Dog-headed folk live in close communities in the tunnels and towns that grow up around old ruins and dungeons, where their noses find the safe path, the fresh water and the thing that died last week.

They are practical, sociable and famously good cooks, turning whatever the deep places offer into something hot and edible. Adventurers who stop at their villages eat very well and learn not to ask what is in the stew. They are sometimes confused with small scaly folk of a similar name, which annoys them greatly.

## Not to be confused with

*[Kobold-kind](small-folk-kobold-kind.md)*, the small reptilian folk who share a similar name and burrows, but not the dog's nose or the cooking.

## What sets this species apart

No species-wide difference: the keen nose is already the family's, and the cooking is culture handled in the Culture step.
