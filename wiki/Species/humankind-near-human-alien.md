# Near-human alien

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

The great crowd of peoples who look almost like humans. A different skin, a ridged nose, a crest of hair, a spotted neck, a hammer-shaped head. Some differ only in culture: bureaucrats, pilgrims, pacifists, raiders, poets who talk entirely in stories. Some carry one surprising trick: a second heart for thin air, a body that dries out and waits for rain, glowing skin for talking in the dark, a companion creature living inside them with a thousand years of memories.

To a traveller on a busy station, they blur together into a single noisy, trading, arguing population, and that is how most of them live. Each people knows exactly how different it is from the others, and will gladly explain it at length.

## Not to be confused with

the *[baseline human](humankind-baseline-human.md)*, who has no such quirk at all; each near-human people carries its own small difference of body or custom.

## What sets this species apart

A catch-all of near-human aliens with no shared trait; every difference is set at Breed/Race level or is cultural.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
