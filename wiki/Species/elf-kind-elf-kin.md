# Elf-kin

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

In the old northern tales, elves come in two sorts, and the tales are careful not to confuse them. The light elves are radiant and beautiful, dwellers in a bright realm, linked to the sun, to fertile fields and to growing things. Farmers once left them offerings at harvest. The dark elves live under the earth, in halls of stone, and are the darker half of the same lineage. Storytellers often muddle them with dwarves, and the dark ones do not seem to mind.

What the two share is the elven line itself: long lives, keen senses and a sense of being older than the world around them. What divides them is where they chose to live and what that made of them. The one branch walks in daylight and the other shuns it, and neither much likes the other.

## Not to be confused with

*[dark elf](elf-kind-dark-elf.md)*, a separate cavern people bred for the deep, rather than the underground half of this paired lineage.

## What sets this species apart

No difference from the family at species level: its light and underground branches differ from each other, so those lines sit with each Breed/Race.
