# Corrupted thrall-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

Something vast and hungry lives beyond the stars, and it remakes whatever it touches. Its thralls were once other peoples: soldiers, colonists, crews of lost ships. Now they are twisted versions of themselves, flesh fused with chitin or metal, faces half remembered, voices that speak for someone else. Some were reanimated by a parasite and move with a feral, scuttling speed.

Every thrall is linked to the power that corrupted it. They feel its will, share its purpose, and act together without a word. That link is also a leash. When the master commands, the thrall obeys, whatever it once was, whatever it once loved. Those who fight them learn to aim for the leash, and to grieve for the faces they recognise.

## Not to be confused with

*[Corrupted/mutated folk](hybrid-and-chimeric-corrupted-mutated-folk.md)*, bodies changed by corruption but still free; the thrall is changed and owned, bound to the master that twisted it.

## What sets this species apart

Beings twisted by a cosmic corruption and linked to the power they serve. The link is to the corrupting master; it is also a weakness to its commands.
