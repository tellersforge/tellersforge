# Antelope-kin

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

Slender and fast, built for the open savanna. Antelope-kin have long, fine-boned legs, spiralled or ridged horns, and large eyes set wide to watch the horizon. They are quicker than their hooved cousins and even more watchful. A herd of antelope-kin at rest always has someone awake.

Gazelle-folk leap as if the ground were a spring. Giraffe-folk tower over everyone, browse from high branches and deliver a kick that can kill a lion. All of them prefer to see danger coming and outrun it rather than stand and fight. They live in herds, move with the rains, and distrust closed places. In a city they choose rooms with two doors and a window, and they keep the window open.

## Not to be confused with

*[Cervine folk](ungulate-cervine-folk.md)*, forest deer with antlers and keen ears; antelope-kin are horned grassland runners, faster and warier still.

## What sets this species apart

Slender savanna runners, even quicker and more watchful than the family.
