# World serpents

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md)*

Serpents too large to measure. World serpents are colossal, legless beings of myth and nature: the great snake coiled around the roots of the sea, the rainbow serpent who carved the rivers and brings the rain, the feathered serpent of wind and learning, the many-headed monster whose heads grow back when cut, the shy long-necked thing in the cold lake, the fiery guardian of the forest.

They care nothing for gold. Their concerns are the order of the world: chaos, creation, fertility, the end of days. Some can be prayed to; some can only be survived. Their coils can crush a ship or a fortress, and heroes who face them rarely do so twice.

## Not to be confused with

*[True dragons](draconic-true-dragons.md)*, the winged, clawed hoarders, where world serpents are legless forces of nature with no interest in treasure.

## What sets this species apart

Colossal legless serpents of myth and nature, not treasure-hoarders. Constricting coils. No hands: held weapons unusable.
