# Tiny warrior folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Small folk](../Species_Families/small-folk.md)*

A people no taller than a thumb, living on a warm island among giant flowers and fallen fruit. They are fierce, proud and very skilled with their hands, famed both as warriors and as makers of beautiful little toys, carvings and tools.

Their great secret is a special mushroom. Eat it, and for a while they grow to the size of a grown man, strong and startling, before shrinking back again. They keep the mushroom close and the secret closer. Strangers who stumble into their island are watched from the grass by hundreds of tiny eyes, and a careless foot is likely to meet a pin-sized spear.

## Not to be confused with

*[Tiny folk](humankind-tiny-folk.md)* are simply small humans; tiny warrior folk are thumb-sized island warriors who can grow for a time after eating their mushroom.

## What sets this species apart

A few centimetres tall, but able to grow to human size for a while. Growing works only after eating their special mushroom.
