# Psionic hive species

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md)*

A people who chose to become one. Somewhere in their history, through meditation, strange stars or sheer effort of will, they reached into each other's minds and never let go. Now a single shared consciousness runs through every member, and each individual is a thought in the mind of the whole. A child is born into the chorus and never knows silence.

They are gentle or terrible according to what the great mind wants. Their psychic power is formidable, whether turned to healing, persuasion or war. What they do not do is argue with themselves. Visitors find their calm unnerving, and their habit of answering a question put to one member with the voice of another more unnerving still. Some envy them. Most prefer their own solitary heads.

## Not to be confused with

*[psychic hive order](hive-and-collective-psychic-hive-order.md)*, communal orders of individuals linked by psionic power; the psionic hive species has given up individuality entirely for one shared mind.

## What sets this species apart

A single shared consciousness reached through psychic ascension, with strong psychic potential.
