# Colonial jelly folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

A single person that is really a crowd. What looks like one drifting creature is a colony of many small polyps, each with a job: some float, some feed, some breed and some sting. Together they form a bell and a trailing curtain of stinging threads, and they think together too, slowly and dreamily, as the current carries them. Some drift in flotillas, pulsing messages to one another in soft light.

They have no hands, and dry air kills them. Sailors give them a wide berth, because the threads trail farther than they seem and the sting lingers for days.

## Not to be confused with

the *[merging colonial folk](hive-and-collective-merging-colonial-folk.md)*, whose members join and part at will; the jelly colony has never been anything but one body.

## What sets this species apart

Drifting sea colonies of stinging polyps that cannot survive drying out. No hands: held weapons unusable. Venom is the Poison Touch power.
