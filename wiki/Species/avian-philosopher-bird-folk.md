# Philosopher bird-folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Winged humanoids from a distant world, with feathered bodies, keen faces and wings strong enough to lift them. Philosopher bird-folk fly as easily as others walk, but they are best known for how they think. Their culture prizes calm, duty and reasoned argument, and their warriors are also their thinkers: soldiers who read, debate and meditate between campaigns and meet danger without raising their voices.

To outsiders they can seem cold or smug, lecturing on patience while the room burns. Those who travel with them usually learn that the calm is real, and that a philosopher bird who finally loses their temper is worth stepping well away from.

## Not to be confused with

*[Garuda-folk](avian-garuda-folk.md)*, the hawk-headed fliers, who value personal freedom above all rather than duty and reason.

## What sets this species apart

Winged humanoids capable of true flight.
