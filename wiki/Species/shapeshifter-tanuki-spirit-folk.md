# Tanuki spirit folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

Round-bellied raccoon-dog spirits with a merry face, a bushy striped tail and a boundless appetite for mischief. They can take the shape of an animal, a monk, a teakettle or a passing merchant, though the disguise is rarely perfect: a tail peeks out, a leaf sits on the head, the laugh is too loud.

They love food, drink, song and above all a good prank. They drum on their bellies in the moonlight, pay for dinner with leaves that turn back in the morning and lead drunken travellers round in circles all night. For all that, they are thought lucky, and statues of them stand outside inns and shops to welcome customers. Few people truly hate a tanuki spirit. Many have been fooled by one.

## Not to be confused with

*[Fox spirit folk](shapeshifter-fox-spirit-folk.md)* are sly charmers who seduce and deceive; tanuki spirit folk are jovial pranksters whose tricks are more for laughter than for gain.

## What sets this species apart

Jovial raccoon-dog spirits who shift into animal form and play shape-illusion pranks.
