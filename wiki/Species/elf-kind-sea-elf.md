# Sea elf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

Elves who went into the water and did not come back out. Their bodies are made for it: long and supple, with strong limbs for swimming and lungs that take in water as easily as air, so they can dive as deep as they like and stay down as long as they please. Their hair drifts like weed, and their skin has the faint sheen of a fish's belly.

Some lines live in coral cities and kelp forests, singing to the tides and working a quiet sea magic that calms storms and calls fish. Others are raiders who come up from the shallows at night, board ships from below and are gone before the alarm is raised. Coastal folk leave gifts for the first kind and nail charms over their doors against the second. On land, all sea elves seem slightly uncomfortable, like guests who cannot wait to leave.

## Not to be confused with

*[merfolk](aquatic-merfolk.md)*, fish-tailed sea people; sea elves keep the elven legs and lineage and walk ashore when they choose.

## What sets this species apart

Sea-adapted bodies: strong swimmers and divers who can breathe water.
