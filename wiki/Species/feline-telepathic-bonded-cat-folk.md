# Telepathic bonded cat-folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md)*

A big cat that looks like a jungle hunter, but is something more. These cats were bred or engineered from ordinary stock, their senses sharpened and their minds opened, so that each one can link to a single partner. Once the bond forms it lasts for life: the cat and its partner share feelings, warnings and sometimes whole images across a distance, and fight together as though they were one creature with two bodies.

They go on four legs and have no hands, but they need none. They are silent, patient and deadly hunters, with noses that track a scent for days and ears that pick out a heartbeat in the dark. Scouts, rangers and commandos dream of such a companion. To the cat, the partner is everything, and there is no loyalty like it. The loss of a bonded partner can break a cat's heart.

## Not to be confused with

*[occult-sensitive cat](feline-occult-sensitive-cat.md)*, an ordinary cat that sees ghosts and spirits; the bonded cat is enhanced and linked mind to mind with one partner.

## What sets this species apart

Enhanced four-legged cats with sharpened senses and a mind bond to one partner. Mind Link is with the bonded partner only. No hands: held weapons unusable.
