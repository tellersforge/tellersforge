# Self-replicating machine kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md)*

A swarm that builds itself. Self-replicating machines are tiny mechanical or half-living units that consume metal, machines or living hosts and turn what they eat into more of themselves. Individually they are almost nothing: a speck of grey, a scuttling block, a probe drifting between stars. Together they assemble into insects, soldiers, humanoid shapes or clouds that strip a world to bare rock.

They serve a single directive, and they adapt: a weapon that works against them today may not work tomorrow. They cannot be bribed, frightened or persuaded. The only real defence is to stop them early, before there are enough of them to become a problem, which is always sooner than anyone thinks.

## Not to be confused with

*[Nanite-swarm folk](amorphous-and-ooze-nanite-swarm-folk.md)*, a people made of tiny machines, who are persons with their own minds rather than a spreading directive.

## What sets this species apart

Self-replicating machine swarms joined into a collective directive. Self-replication and adaptation are NPC-scale narrative traits.
