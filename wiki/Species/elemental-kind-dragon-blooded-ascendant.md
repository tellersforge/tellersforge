# Dragon-blooded ascendant

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md)*

They are born human, or nearly so. Somewhere in the family tree an elemental dragon left its mark, and in adolescence that mark wakes. A girl finds rain gathering around her when she weeps. A boy's forge fire flares to his temper. From that day on the youth is one of the exalted, bound to one of the great aspects: fire, water, earth, air or wood.

What sets them apart from other elemental scions is how close they stay to ordinary people. They marry among them, command them, keep their households and their feuds. Great families guard their bloodlines jealously and arrange matches to breed the gift true. Whole legions are built around them, each company led by an ascendant whose element shapes how it fights. To commoners they are nobility with a touch of the storm. To one another they are rivals in an endless game of houses.

## Not to be confused with

*[dragon-blooded folk](draconic-dragon-blooded-folk.md)*, the broader draconic kin with scales and breath; these ascendants look human and wield one elemental aspect.

## What sets this species apart

Human-descended elemental scions who wield an aspect element and are not distant from people. The Breed/Race names the aspect element.
