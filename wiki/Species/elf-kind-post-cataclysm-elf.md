# Post-cataclysm elf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

According to their own histories, these elves were once human. When the old world burned in a war of terrible weapons, some survivors fled into the forests, and over long ages the forest changed them: ears lengthened, lives stretched, senses sharpened. Now they are elves in all but memory, and their scholars argue about how much of that memory is true.

They are forest people through and through, reading trails, weather and the health of trees at a glance, and they are fine archers and woodsmen. Their relationship with magic is uneasy. In some ages they shun it, having seen what power did to the world before. In others, they are the chosen guardians of a great sacred tree whose seed holds back ancient evils. Humans who meet them find something oddly familiar in their faces, and cannot quite say why.

## Not to be confused with

*[woodland folk](elf-kind-woodland-folk.md)*, the common forest elves of old lineage; these elves believe they grew out of human survivors of a fallen world.

## What sets this species apart

Forest-dwelling elf-kind with a keen feel for woodland country.
