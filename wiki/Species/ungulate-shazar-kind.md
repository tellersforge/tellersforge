# Shazar-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

A nomad people of the high steppe, with small backswept horns, narrow faces and wind-browned skin. Their herds are their wealth and their calendar; they move with the grass, following routes their ancestors walked, and pitch felt tents where the wind is least cruel. Their bodies have been shaped by harsh country: they bear blistering summers and killing winters with the same weathered calm.

They are hospitable to guests and hard on thieves. Children ride before they walk, and every adult can read a sky for storm. Settled peoples see them as wanderers, raiders or traders, depending on the year. They see settled peoples as those who stayed behind, and pity them a little.

## Not to be confused with

*[Caprine folk](ungulate-caprine-folk.md)*, the goat- and ram-folk of cliffs and villages; these horned nomads are steppe herders, hardened to climate rather than to heights.

## What sets this species apart

Steppe nomads with bodies hardened to harsh climate.
