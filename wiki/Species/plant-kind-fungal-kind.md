# Fungal-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md)*

Small folk who lean towards the mushroom end of the green kingdom. Their skin is pale and soft or dark and ridged like bark, their heads sometimes capped, their hair sometimes a fringe of gills, and they smell faintly of damp earth after rain. Many can feed on light like a plant; most would rather live somewhere dim.

They are slow and deliberate, but very hard to wear down. Rot and decay trouble them little, and they recover from what would lay others low. Many can release a small puff of spores: a sneeze, a sleepy haze, a glowing cloud, a signal to kin. They live in cellars, caves and deep, damp forests, and other peoples find them odd, quiet and surprisingly good company once past the smell.

## Not to be confused with

the *[fungal folk](fungal-fungal-folk.md)*, true fungus peoples of their own family, where fungal-kind are plant folk that merely lean towards fungus.

## What sets this species apart

Fungus-leaning plant folk: resilient, at home in damp dark places, and resistant to rot. The spore abilities are minor and vary; Set at Breed/Race level.
