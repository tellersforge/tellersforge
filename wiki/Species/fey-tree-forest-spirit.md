# Tree/forest spirit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Spirits bound to trees, groves and wild places. The smallest are pale little things with rattling heads that sit on branches and watch; if they fade, the forest is dying. There are tree-bonded fey who share a tree's life and die if it is cut, antlered guardians of sacred groves who weave illusions, and spirits of wood and bark who swell to enormous size when the forest is threatened. Some are eternal children, never ageing, who have never heard of the world beyond their trees.

Others guard water, treasure or mountains. A one-legged sprite of the high passes startles travellers and answers their shouts with echoes. A tiny, kindly spirit is said to have taught people fire. They are at home in the wild, feel its health as their own, and punish those who harm it. To the respectful they can be generous, and to the careless they are merciless.

## Not to be confused with

*[tree/nature spirit kind](plant-kind-tree-nature-spirit-kind.md)*, the plant family's nature spirits; tree and forest spirits are fey, full of glamour, bargains and mischief.

## What sets this species apart

Spirits bound to trees and forests, at home in the wild and aware of its health. The mountain sprite Breed/Race swaps the forest line for mountain.
