# Elder-kin

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

A remnant, hidden. Long before the elves that humans know built their first halls, there was an older branch, and a little of it survives in enclaves no map shows: a valley that is never quite where the road says, a city behind a waterfall, a realm reached only through a gate that opens once a generation. The elder kin live there in near-endless lives, their blood gleaming faintly gold when they bleed.

Their mastery of magic runs deeper than any other elf-kind's, as natural to them as breathing. They are courteous, beautiful and utterly certain of their own superiority, and they regard even high elves as a younger, rather rustic cousin. They rarely meddle in the wider world. When they do, it is for reasons centuries in the making, and ordinary folk usually learn of it only when the plan is finished.

## Not to be confused with

*[high elf](elf-kind-high-elf.md)*, the proud but openly known noble branch; elder-kin are older, stronger in magic and deliberately hidden.

## What sets this species apart

An elder branch more deeply gifted in magic than ordinary elf-kind.
