# Small mammal folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mustelid and small mammal](../Species_Families/mustelid-and-small-mammal.md)*

Small, round, determined folk built more like a barrel than a ferret. Skunks with bold black-and-white coats and a defence nobody forgets; raccoons with clever black-masked faces and paws as nimble as hands; red-coated tree-climbers with ringed tails; stout moles who dig tunnels half-blind; hedgehogs who roll into a spiky ball when frightened.

They are mostly night folk, scavengers and tinkerers, farmers and small traders. What they lack in speed they make up in stubbornness and resourcefulness, and most have a single trick that makes bigger creatures think twice. Others treat them as harmless, a bit comic and slightly suspicious, often all at once, and a sensible guest counts the spoons after a raccoon has visited.

## Not to be confused with

the *[mustelid folk](mustelid-and-small-mammal-mustelid-folk.md)*, the long, slender weasel-kin who are quicker and fiercer but lack the round build and odd defences.

## What sets this species apart

Stouter, rounder small mammals (skunks, raccoons, hedgehogs, moles) that lack the slinky mustelid build.
