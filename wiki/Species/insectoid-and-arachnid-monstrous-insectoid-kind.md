# Monstrous insectoid kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md)*

The nightmare end of the insect family. These are not peoples but monsters: glossy, armed, fast and horribly determined. There are acid-blooded parasites that breed in living hosts and burst out when grown. There are winged fungal things from beyond the sky that carry off human brains in metal jars. There are insects the size of hills whose many eyes change colour with their moods, and endless, mindless swarms that defend their nests with suicidal fury.

They appear in the bestiary so the table knows what it is facing, not so anyone can play one. A few are intelligent, in a cold and alien way, but none can be bargained with. Most who meet them remember the sound: clicking, chittering, or a scrape on the ceiling.

## Not to be confused with

*[acid-blooded hunter folk](hybrid-and-chimeric-acid-blooded-hunter-folk.md)*, a single apex predator kind; monstrous insectoids are the whole bestiary of arthropod horrors.

## What sets this species apart

Bestial, armed and frightening arthropod monsters. NPC only.

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
