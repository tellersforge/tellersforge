# Hybrid stock insectoid folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md)*

The last stage of a long mixing between humans and an alien parasite stock. After enough generations there is almost nothing human left. They have a hard, chitin-plated body, a long skull, four arms ending in claws and a curling tail, and they move with the speed of a hunting insect. Here and there a flicker of something human shows through, in the shape of the eyes or a gesture of the hand.

They feel their parent hive always, as a wordless, feral pull at the back of the mind, and they cannot ignore it. They are rarely seen, living in the dark heart of their cults and appearing only when their masters call. Those who glimpse one do not always live to describe it.

## Not to be confused with

*[brood-parasite hybrid](goblinoid-and-orc-kind-brood-parasite-hybrid.md)*, which spans the whole road from near-human to monster; the hybrid stock insectoid is the far end, almost wholly alien.

## What sets this species apart

Human-born hybrids that have become almost wholly alien, with a feral link to the parent hive.
