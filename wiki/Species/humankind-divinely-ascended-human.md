# Divinely-ascended human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Ordinary mortals picked out by a god or by fate and made into something greater. A great sun patron chooses those it thinks worthy and gives them excellence in whatever they already do, from war to poetry, and a life that ends only by violence. Fate-weavers are chosen by the spinners of destiny and work in secret, tugging the threads of other lives. Lesser gods and spirits choose champions too, and their gifts are as odd as their patrons.

The chosen change their world, for good or ill. Other people rarely know until much later that the local hero was never quite mortal after all.

## Not to be confused with

the *[hybrid bloodline human](humankind-hybrid-bloodline-human.md)*, who inherited power from a parent; these mortals were chosen and changed during their own lives.

## What sets this species apart

Mortals raised by a god or by fate; each patron grants a different gift, so nothing is shared at species level. Patron powers (excellence, fate-weaving, idiosyncratic gifts) are Set at Breed/Race level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.

### No. 137 Solar-empowered ascendant folk *(genres: myth and legend, heavens, hells and other planes)*

- **Size:** medium · **Body:** biped
- **What it's like:** Mortals chosen by a solar power to become supreme masters of their craft. (looks like: human; good at: knowledge, powers and magic)
- **Attributes:** none
- **Powers:** [Instant Know-How](../Powers/instant-know-how.md) 2
- **For the Game Master only, this race as an NPC guard or soldier (not your character's numbers):** Attack 14 · Parry 11 · Dodge 8 · Damage longsword 1d6+2 · Armour 2 · Life 17 · Threat 1 ([Bestiary roles](../bestiary.md))
- *Why:* Chosen by a solar patron for supreme excellence; virtually immortal barring violence.
- *Note:* Instant Know-How stands for excellence in the chosen field. Carries out the species note. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
