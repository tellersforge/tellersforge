# Cervine folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

Deer-folk: slender, long-legged, with large dark eyes and ears that turn toward every sound. Stags bear branching antlers, and among the northern reindeer-folk both sexes do. They are forest people by nature, alert in the way of animals that have always been hunted, and they hear the snap of a twig long before anyone else knows there is a twig.

They are quick sprinters and gentle by temperament. The reindeer-folk are tougher, made for long migrations over snow, and the great elk-folk are solitary giants who swim rivers without a thought. In mixed cities, deer-folk surprise people by rising to lead; watchfulness, it turns out, is useful in council too. Hunters of other peoples make them uneasy, and they rarely hide it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Antelope-kin](ungulate-antelope-kin.md)*, savanna runners even quicker and more watchful; cervine folk are forest dwellers, antlered rather than horned.

## What sets this species apart

Alert woodland deer builds with keen ears.
