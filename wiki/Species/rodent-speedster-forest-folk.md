# Speedster forest folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md)*

Small, twitchy, furry forest folk who never, ever stop moving. They dart between trees faster than eyes can follow, chatter at a pitch that makes others wince, and have usually eaten half of anyone's supplies before anyone notices they arrived. Their speed goes beyond what any animal should manage.

They live in deep, wild forests, in the branches and hollows, and they have little use for the settled folk outside. They play tricks, steal shiny things, and treat strangers as a game. Most are more mischievous than cruel. Travellers learn to tie up their packs, keep an eye on their boots, and never try to catch one.

## Not to be confused with

the *[mouse and squirrel-kind](rodent-mouse-and-squirrel-kind.md)*, quick and watchful but nowhere near so fast, and far more settled.

## What sets this species apart

Hyperactive forest folk with supernatural speed, living in woods rather than ruins.
