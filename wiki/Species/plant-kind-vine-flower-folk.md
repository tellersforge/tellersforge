# Vine/flower folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md)*

Small plant folk shaped from the gentler kinds of greenery: vines, flowers, cacti and blossoming shrubs. Some have long flexible limbs that coil and twine; some wear crowns of petals and scatter pollen that makes the air sweet and sleepy; some are prickly desert folk who can go months without water; some glow faintly in the dark.

Most of them drink sunlight and grow back small injuries in time, and many follow the seasons closely, bright and busy in spring, quiet in winter. They are usually calm, empathic and a little dreamy, happiest in gardens, jungles and meadows. Others often find them charming and underestimate them, which is a mistake with the ones that eat meat.

## Not to be confused with

the *[plant-based humanoid folk](plant-kind-plant-based-humanoid-folk.md)*, distinct plant peoples from other worlds, where vine and flower folk are the small, garden-grown plant folk of the family.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference: the family already describes these small plant folk; photosynthesis, pollen, spines and desert life vary by breed.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
