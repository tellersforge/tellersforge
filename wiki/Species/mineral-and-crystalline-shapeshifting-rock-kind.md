# Shapeshifting rock-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

At rest they are an outcrop of rock on a barren plain: lumpy, still and unremarkable. Then one of them decides to talk, and the rock flows, cracks and reshapes itself into a person, a beast, or a figure from someone else's history, perfect in every detail. They can become anything that lives.

They are very old and very advanced, and they use their gift as a kind of study. They stage elaborate re-enactments, casting visitors against copies of heroes and villains from their own past, and watch closely to see what choices the visitors make. They rarely explain why. To travellers who stumble into one of their games, they are unsettling hosts: courteous, curious, and utterly detached from the fear they cause.

## Not to be confused with

the *[stone folk](mineral-and-crystalline-stone-folk.md)*, who are made of the same stuff but keep one body all their lives and cannot take another shape.

## What sets this species apart

Sentient rock formations that can take any living form to stage elaborate simulations. NPC-scale.
