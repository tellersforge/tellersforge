# Invader ooze

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

Formless organisms that come from outside to take. Some were servants of an elder race, shapeless labourers with many eyes that learned to imitate their masters and then turned on them. Some come from beyond the edge of the galaxy and hold a human shape only with effort, reverting to jelly when they sleep. Some slip into a host's body and ride its nerves. Some fall as spores from a neighbouring world and eat everything organic they touch. Some are plagues that know themselves, or tiny parasites that nest in sleeping minds.

They have little in common except intent. Wherever they appear, trust is the first casualty: every friend might be a mask, every cough the start of something.

## Not to be confused with

the *[symbiotic ooze](amorphous-and-ooze-symbiotic-ooze.md)*, which shares a body with a willing or bonded host; the invader takes and uses up.

## What sets this species apart

A loose grouping of hostile shapeshifters and parasites with no body trait common to nearly all of them beyond the family's. Shapeshifting, host-taking and spore traits are set per breed.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
