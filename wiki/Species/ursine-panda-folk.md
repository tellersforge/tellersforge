# Panda-folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ursine](../Species_Families/ursine.md)*

Round, calm and black-and-white, panda-folk look soft and are anything but. They live on bamboo, and their hands have an extra gripping thumb for stripping stalks, which also makes them surprisingly deft at brushwork, herb-cutting and holding a staff. They see poorly after dark and prefer to keep early hours.

Their temper is famously even. Most are quiet scholars, herbalists and gardeners, but a few take up the martial arts and prove astonishingly agile for their bulk. They have no taste for fishing, hunting or haste. Others underestimate them constantly, which panda-folk find restful. When roused at last, they move with a force that surprises everyone, and afterwards go back to their tea.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bear-folk](ursine-bear-folk.md)*, the true bears with a hunter's nose and claws; panda-folk are gentle bamboo-eaters, better with the brush than the kill.

## What sets this species apart

Calm bamboo specialists with an extra gripping thumb, not fishers.
