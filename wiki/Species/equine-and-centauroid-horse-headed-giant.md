# Horse-headed giant

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Equine and centauroid](../Species_Families/equine-and-centauroid.md)*

Taller than a man by a head and more, long-limbed and lean, with the head of a horse and feet that point backwards. It lives in the deep forest and guards it jealously. Travellers who cross its path find the trail twisting under their feet, the same tree coming round again and again, until they are hopelessly lost. Some wander for days. Some never come out.

The forest folk know how to deal with it: turn your shirt inside out, ask its pardon before passing, and above all be polite. It is a trickster more than a killer, though it has little patience for those who cut its trees or foul its streams. Legend says that anyone brave enough to leap on its back and pluck a hair from its mane wins its loyalty, and gains a powerful friend for the rest of their days.

## Not to be confused with

*[minotaur-kind](ungulate-minotaur-kind.md)*, bull-headed brutes of labyrinths; the horse-headed giant is a forest guardian and trickster.

## What sets this species apart

Two-legged forest guardians who lead travellers astray.
