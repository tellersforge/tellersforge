# Tree/nature spirit kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md)*

Small, shy spirits of trees and groves. Some take the shape of a slender woman with bark-brown skin and hair of leaves; some are tiny pale figures with round heads, rattling softly in the branches; some are faces made entirely of foliage peering from carved stonework; some are forest lords who grow as tall as the trees they walk among or as small as the grass.

They feel the life of their woods as a person feels their own body: a felled tree hurts them, a spring flowering makes them glad. Many are bound to one tree and die with it. They avoid mortals, mislead hunters who come to kill carelessly, and protect the creatures of the forest. A traveller who treats the woods kindly may find the path easier than it should be.

## Not to be confused with

the *[treant-kind](plant-kind-treant-kind.md)*, great walking tree-beings, where these spirits are small and live within or beside a tree rather than being one.

## What sets this species apart

Shy place-bound tree spirits who feel the life of their woods. Being bound to one tree (dryad, kodama) is a Breed/Race matter.
