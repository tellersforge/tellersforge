# Tiny fey-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

No taller than a hand, blue-skinned, painted with tattoos and wild-haired. These tiny fey are not pretty, and they have none of the glamour or grace of their larger cousins. What they have is strength far beyond their size, courage beyond all sense, and a boundless appetite for drink, song, theft and a good brawl.

They live in clan mounds and raid in swarming bands, carrying off sheep, cattle and anything shiny, and they will fight anything, a knight, a giant, a dragon, without a second thought. Their battle cries are loud and their loyalty fierce. They regard all laws except their own as a joke. Farmers who find their livestock gone and a small bottle empty on the doorstep know who to blame. Those who have fought beside them say there are no better allies, as long as you guard your ale.

## Not to be confused with

*[fairy folk](fey-fairy-folk.md)*, small winged fey of charm and mischief; tiny fey raiders are wingless brawlers with astonishing strength.

## What sets this species apart

Tiny, fearless raiders with strength far beyond their size, and none of the family's allure. Super Strength here means strength far beyond their tiny size, not beyond a large person's.
