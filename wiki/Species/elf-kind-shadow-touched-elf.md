# Shadow-touched elf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

Elves who were marked by another realm and never shook it off. The shadow-touched lines went into a grey plane of gloom and came back quieter, stealthier, with a melancholy that settles on them like dust. They see in darkness, move without being noticed, and shrug off magic that would rot the body or trouble the mind. They rarely laugh, and when they do it is brief.

The seasonal lines were touched instead by the wild realm of the fey. Their very bodies follow the year: bright and warm in summer, wistful in autumn, cold in winter, giddy in spring, with hair and mood changing to match. They can blink a few paces through the air as if the world had a hidden seam. Both kinds sit uneasily among ordinary elves, who admire them a little and trust them a little less.

## Not to be confused with

*[dark elf](elf-kind-dark-elf.md)*, which was shaped by life underground rather than by another realm, and which sunlight pains.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference from the family: the shadow and seasonal-fey lineages are touched in different ways, so those lines sit with each Breed/Race.
