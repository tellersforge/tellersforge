# Nature giant-kin folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Large, quiet forest fey, tall enough to look a bear in the eye and gentle enough to carry a fledgling back to its nest. Their skin is bark-brown or moss-green, and when they stand still among the trunks they simply vanish, so that a traveller may walk past one without ever knowing it was there. They coax seeds to sprout, heal blighted orchards and talk to old trees as if to grandparents.

They live a very long time and are in no hurry about anything. They avoid towns and dislike loud tools, but they will quietly help a farmer who treats his land well. Stories call them shy giants, and that is about right: slow to anger, hard to find, and dreadful to face when they finally decide a forest needs defending.

## Not to be confused with

*[giant-kind](../Species_Families/giant-kind.md)*, the true giants of mountain and hill, who are mortal, earthy and often quarrelsome; forest giant-kin are fey, and their size serves the woods rather than themselves.

## What sets this species apart

Gentle, large forest fey who blend into the woods and tend growing things.
