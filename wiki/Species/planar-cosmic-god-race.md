# Cosmic god-race

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

Two peoples of gods from two opposite worlds. One is a hellish, smoke-choked forge-planet ruled by a tyrant, where fire pits burn day and night and all are raised as weapons. The other is a paradise of floating cities, where godlike folk study, debate and build wonders of shining energy. They were sworn to peace once, by trade of their children, and have not been at peace for long since.

Both peoples are near-immortal, stronger than anything mortal and almost impossible to kill. Those of the dark world wield raw cosmic energy that can reshape matter; those of the bright world wield machines that do the same. To mortals caught in their wars, the difference between the two is often hard to see.

## Not to be confused with

the *[sky-realm god-kin](planar-sky-realm-god-kin.md)*, long-lived god-folk of a single realm who treat magic as craft, where this race is split between two warring homeworlds.

## What sets this species apart

Near-immortal god-folk of two opposed homeworlds with superhuman strength and durability. The dark breed adds matter-manipulating energy at Breed/Race level; the paradise breed's energy technology is gear. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
