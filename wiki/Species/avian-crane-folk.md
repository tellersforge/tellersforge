# Crane folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Tall, grey and white, with long legs, long necks and a stillness that is almost unnerving. Crane folk can stand on one leg in cold water for an hour without a tremble, and when they do move it is with a sudden, precise grace. They fly strongly, with slow, deep wingbeats, and dance in the open marshes at dawn.

Their civilised communities are known for discipline and for a fighting art built around balance, patient stances and leaping, wing-spread strikes. Not every crane is a fighter, but almost every one moves like one. Other peoples think of them as calm, elegant and a little aloof, and are generally right.

## Not to be confused with

*[Long-necked gentle giant folk](avian-long-necked-gentle-giant-folk.md)*, who share the long neck and the calm, but are huge, wingless and walk on four legs.

## What sets this species apart

Long-legged cranes that fly and hold a stance well. Their fighting style is training, handled in the Profession step.
