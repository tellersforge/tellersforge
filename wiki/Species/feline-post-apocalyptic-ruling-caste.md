# Post-apocalyptic ruling caste

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md)*

After the old world fell, the beasts rose. In the ruined lands that followed, the great cats walked upright and took power, and the tiger-lords and lion-lords now rule as a warrior aristocracy. They are enormous, heavily muscled and immensely strong, able to tear through steel doors or lift a cart. A lion-lord's mane shows his rank, and a tiger-lord's stripes are painted and tattooed with the record of his victories.

Their societies are rigid and militaristic, bound by codes of honour that value courage above all and mercy seldom. The degenerated humans of these lands serve them as labourers, servants and, in the darker tellings, livestock. The cat-lords do not think this cruel. They think it natural. To outsiders who stumble into their realms they are magnificent and terrifying, and the last thing many of them ever see.

## Not to be confused with

*[warrior cat-folk](feline-warrior-cat-folk.md)*, many feline peoples with fighting cultures; the ruling caste are giant cat-lords who hold a fallen world by strength.

## What sets this species apart

Huge predator lords with innate superhuman strength.
