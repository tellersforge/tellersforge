# Generic bird-humanoid folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Not one people but many. Across the stars and the old kingdoms alike there are bird peoples of every sort: feathered imperial courts with rigid ranks, crested zealots who worship fire, gentle scholars, cliff dynasties obsessed with lost glory, sky-nomads who never settle, mountain couriers who carry messages through the snow, slender fliers from light worlds who glide more than they fly, and vanished precursors known only by their ruins.

What unites them is the body plan: feathers, keen eyes, light bones and, usually, a beak. Whether they fly, how they rule themselves and what they believe varies with the breed and the culture. A traveller who has met one bird people has met exactly one.

## Not to be confused with

*[Civic bird folk](avian-civic-bird-folk.md)*, one particular people of city-dwelling birds, where this grouping gathers many distinct peoples under one roof.

## What sets this species apart

A grouping of distinct bird peoples; winged or not and their other differences are breed-level or cultural, so the family lines cover the species.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
