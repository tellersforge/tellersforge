# Engineered pest

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md)*

Small, furry vermin that nobody wants. Some are burrowing creatures bred, or bred themselves, to live in the walls and ducts of stations and ships, chewing wires and multiplying in the dark. Others are round, limbless balls of fur that purr and coo when stroked, charming for the first day, and then breed so fast they fill the storerooms within a week.

They think nothing beyond eating and multiplying, and cannot hold or use anything. They are rarely dangerous one at a time. In numbers, they are a disaster. Crews and colonists hunt them, trap them, curse them and, when all else fails, open the airlock.

## Not to be confused with

the *[tunneling pest folk](rodent-tunneling-pest-folk.md)*, small tunnel-dwelling people who think and talk, where these are mere vermin.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference beyond being non-sentient vermin; body oddities (the limbless furball) go at Breed/Race level. Non-sentient NPC pests. No hands: held weapons unusable.
