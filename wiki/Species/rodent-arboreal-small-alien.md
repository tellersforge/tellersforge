# Arboreal small alien

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md)*

Small squirrel-like aliens with bushy tails, bright black eyes and hooked claws that grip bark as easily as a hand grips a rail. Between wrist and ankle a fold of furred skin opens like a sail, so a leap from a high branch becomes a long, swooping glide. They live high in the canopies of their forest worlds and seem to spend more time in the air than on any floor.

They are quick, restless and playful, and braver than their size suggests; many a larger creature has been chased off by a furious, chattering ball of fur. Their lives are short, and they fill them to the brim. Other peoples find them charming and exhausting in about equal measure, and ship crews learn to stow anything loose.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mouse and squirrel-kind](rodent-mouse-and-squirrel-kind.md)* are the plainer rodent folk of any world; the arboreal small alien is an off-world people with a gliding membrane and a much shorter life.

## What sets this species apart

Squirrel-like tree aliens with climbing claws and a gift for gliding.
