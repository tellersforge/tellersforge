# Deathless judge-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

Tall, winged, stern beings who descend from high places to pass judgement on the guilty. They do not need a trial: they can sense wrongdoing as others sense heat, a stain on the soul that cannot be hidden. When they find it, they carry out the sentence themselves.

They are ancient and very rare, and they live far longer than any mortal. Their code is severe and entirely their own. No law, oath or king appointed them, and they are not moved by pleas, bribes or explanations. The innocent have nothing to fear from them, or so they say. Mortals, most of whom are not entirely innocent, tend to find this cold comfort.

## Not to be confused with

the *[celestial-kind](planar-celestial-kind.md)*, servants of heavenly order who act for a higher power, where the judges answer to no one but their own code.

## What sets this species apart

Ancient winged judges who sense guilt and outlive ages. Lifespan (long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
