# Zombie-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

Flesh that should have rotted in peace, set walking again. Some are mindless, driven by hunger alone, and a bite from one may spread the curse or the plague that raised them. Others kept their wits: undead who broke free of the necromancer's leash, cursed wanderers who wake again after each death a little less themselves, or stubborn souls who simply refused to stop working.

Whatever the mind, the body is the same. It is stiff, slow and clumsy, grey and loose at the joints, with a smell that arrives before it does. It feels little pain and never tires. Living folk treat zombies with horror, pity or, in stranger places, grudging respect for a colleague who still turns up despite being dead.

## Not to be confused with

*[Skeleton-kind](undead-and-deathless-skeleton-kind.md)*, the dead picked down to bare bone; and *[reanimated construct-kind](undead-and-deathless-reanimated-construct-kind.md)*, corpses stitched from many bodies into one powerful frame.

## What sets this species apart

Stiff, rotting reanimated flesh that is slow and clumsy.
