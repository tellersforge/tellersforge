# Wild giant hominid

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md)*

Huge, hair-covered wild folk of the deep forest and the high snows, seen once in a lifetime if at all. They stand head and shoulders over a tall man, with arms that could uproot a sapling and feet that leave prints the length of a forearm. The mountain kind is pale as old snow; the forest kind is dark and shaggy as a bear.

They live alone or in small families, far from roads and villages, and they have a gift for not being found. Hunters follow their trail for days and lose it at a stream. They rarely climb, and they do not care for tumbling about; they simply walk, steadily and very quietly. Whether they are shy, wise or merely indifferent, nobody can say.

## Not to be confused with

*[Giant-kind](../Species_Families/giant-kind.md)* are the true giants of myth and hall; the wild giant hominid is a reclusive, ape-like wildman who avoids people altogether.

## What sets this species apart

Huge, reclusive ground-dwelling hominids: poor climbers and tumblers, but famously hard to find.
