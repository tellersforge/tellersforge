# Adapted human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Humans shaped by where they live, slowly, over many generations, until the place is written into their bones. Desert folk waste no water, not even their tears, and can track a man across bare stone. Those raised among the stars grow tall and thin in low gravity, and find a planet's pull cruel. Heavy-world colonists grow short and broad and absurdly strong. Others have large pale eyes for sunless caverns, thick hides for cold, great frames for the open sea, or scales and odd eyes from a poisoned river.

Each line is fitted to one extreme and uneasy away from it. A void-born pilot aches in a proper gravity well; a sunless scout squints and weeps at noon. Home-world humans often call them strange. The adapted tend to call home-world humans soft.

## Not to be confused with

the *[bioengineered/cloned human](humankind-bioengineered-cloned-human.md)*, who was altered on purpose in a laboratory; the adapted were changed by long exposure to their homes.

## What sets this species apart

Each breed is adapted to a different extreme (desert, vacuum, gravity, cold, sea, darkness), so nothing is shared at species level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.

- **Size:** medium · **Body:** biped
- **What it's like:** Desert warriors who conserve every drop of water and track for days without rest. (looks like: human; good at: wilderness, toughness, fighting)
- **Attributes:** END +1
- **Skills:** [Survival: Desert](../Skills/survival-desert.md) +2
- **For the Game Master only, this race as an NPC guard or soldier (not your character's numbers):** Attack 14 · Parry 11 · Dodge 8 · Damage longsword 1d6+2 · Armour 2 · Life 18 · Threat 1 ([Bestiary roles](../bestiary.md))
- *Why:* Generations of deep-desert life make them hard to wear down in heat and drought.
