# Concealed-form folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md)*

No one has seen what they really look like. Concealed-form folk always appear sealed in suits, masks, cold-tanks or vessels, or send a probe and a voice in their place. Perhaps their bodies need surroundings no other people could share. Perhaps they are too strange for anyone else to look at. Perhaps they simply prefer secrecy, and have made a culture of it.

Some are soldiers behind blank helmets, some a vast trading house that speaks only through machines, some minds without bodies, kept alive in jars, who fight through hired champions. Each people works differently, and their suits are equipment, not flesh. What they share is the veil. Others trade with them, fight them and speculate about them endlessly, and never learn the answer.

## Not to be confused with

*[Template unknown folk](unknown-humanoid-template-unknown-folk.md)*, blank entries for peoples not yet decided; concealed-form folk are entirely real, and hide their bodies on purpose.

## What sets this species apart

True form is never shown, so nothing beyond the family's GM-chosen lines is known. Their suits and vessels are gear; any powers (such as remote control) are set per Breed.
