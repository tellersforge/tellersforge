# Cosmic celestial being

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

Beings so large that they stand among the planets. Their armoured shapes rise higher than mountains, some as large as a moon, and they move through space without ships. They are among the oldest things in existence, and they are silent in ways that suggest they simply have nothing to say to anyone small.

They go from world to world, planting life in some places and weighing it in others. What they want, and by what measure they judge, no mortal has ever fully understood. When one arrives in a system, the sky itself seems to hold still. Whole civilisations have risen as their gardens, and some have ended beneath their feet.

## Not to be confused with

the *[celestial progenitor-kind](planar-celestial-progenitor-kind.md)*, who reshape the life of worlds with deliberate experiments, where these colossal beings plant life and judge it from farther off.

## What sets this species apart

Colossal god-like beings who seed and judge life on whole worlds. NPC-scale. Size is handled by size class.
