# Transplanted-mind hybrid folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

An ordinary person woken up inside a body that is not theirs. The mind is human, with all its memories and habits, but the body is huge, grey and plated with something like rock. It was grown by alien makers for purposes of their own, and the human mind was set inside it to steer. It is enormously strong, very hard to injure and heavy enough to crack a floor.

The new body barely needs to breathe or eat, which makes it good for strange places, and it cannot have children, which makes it lonely. Those who live in it spend a lot of time trying to remember what it felt like to be small. Others see a monster. They see themselves, still, and wish others would look closer.

## Not to be confused with

*[stone folk](mineral-and-crystalline-stone-folk.md)*, true mineral people born of rock; transplanted minds are human souls housed in bodies made of something stone-like.

## What sets this species apart

A human mind in a huge rock-hided body: it takes the Mineral family's toughness and weight. Copied from the Mineral and crystalline family.
