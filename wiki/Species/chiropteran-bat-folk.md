# Bat folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Chiropteran](../Species_Families/chiropteran.md)*

Leathery-winged people of the night. Bat folk have arms that stretch into wide wings of skin, large ears, small sharp teeth and soft fur, and they fly well, with sudden turns and swoops. Their breeds vary: fruit-eating bat folk with big dark eyes; small agile cave-dwellers who find their way by calling out and listening to the echoes; blood-drinkers with heat-sensing noses; great flying foxes that glide on huge wings; and small, rapid-winged hoverers with a trader's streak.

They roost in colonies, sleep through the day upside down and wake at dusk. Other peoples tend to fear them, especially the blood-drinkers, and bat folk have learned to keep to caves, cliffs and the tops of old towers.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bat spirit](chiropteran-bat-spirit.md)*, the feared night spirits and bat-gods of legend, where bat folk are an ordinary living people.

## What sets this species apart

Winged bat-people who actually fly. A breed without working wings Sets Flight to 0.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
