# Shapeshifting human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Humans, or people close enough to pass, whose bodies are built to become something else. The beast-shifters of the high forests and cold northlands grow into bears or wolves when roused, with the strength and temper of the animal and the memory of the person underneath. Their neighbours trade with them readily and keep their children close to home at night. Far off among the stars, a green-skinned empire does the opposite: it takes on other peoples' faces so well that a friend can be replaced without anyone noticing, and it has conquered a great deal that way.

What unites them is a flesh that listens. Most of them learn early that others will fear the change more than anything they do while changed.

## Not to be confused with

the *[lycanthrope](shapeshifter-lycanthrope.md)*, a shapeshifter made by curse or bite; these people are born with the change in them.

## What sets this species apart

Human-derived peoples whose bodies are built to change shape. Affinity only. The beast-shifters Set Animal Form and the mimic empire Sets Self-Alteration or Disguise (power) at Breed/Race level.
