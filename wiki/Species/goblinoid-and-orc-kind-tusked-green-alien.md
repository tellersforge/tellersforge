# Tusked green alien

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Goblinoid and orc-kind](../Species_Families/goblinoid-and-orc-kind.md)*

A green-skinned, tusked people from somewhere out among the stars, big-hearted and built for trouble. They are broad and heavy-handed, with jutting lower tusks and a voice that fills a room. To anyone who expects the orcs of old stories they look like brutes. In truth they are warm, loyal and slow to take offence, and they make excellent friends for a starship crew that goes looking for trouble.

They fight with gusto, eat enormously and laugh at nearly everything. They do not abandon a companion, even when they should, and a bounty hunter or mercenary who has one at their side is fortunate. They find other peoples' fear of their faces funny, most of the time, and use it when it helps.

## Not to be confused with

*[orc](goblinoid-and-orc-kind-orc.md)*, the tusked warrior folk of fantasy; this tusked alien people only looks the part, and is famous for loyalty rather than cruelty.

## What sets this species apart

Hearty tusked aliens who fit the family as it stands.
