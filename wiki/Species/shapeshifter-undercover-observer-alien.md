# Undercover observer alien

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

Beings of pure energy from very far away, who have borrowed ordinary human bodies in order to study the strange creatures who live in them. From outside they look like anyone: a professor, a teenager, a quiet uncle. Inside, a small alien crew is trying very hard to understand why people cry at weddings and argue about the washing up.

They do not change shape; the borrowed body is their disguise, and they are stuck with it. They keep a strict chain of command and send earnest reports home. Most of their trouble comes from taking human sayings literally, misreading emotions and discovering, to their alarm, that bodies come with hunger, sleep and awkward feelings of their own.

## Not to be confused with

*[Energy being (generic)](energy-and-incorporeal-energy-being-generic.md)* is a free-floating being of energy; the undercover observer alien lives inside an ordinary human body and hides behind it.

## What sets this species apart

Energy beings living inside ordinary human bodies to study people: they pass as human without reshaping, and human ways still baffle them. Their disguise is the borrowed body itself, not a power.
