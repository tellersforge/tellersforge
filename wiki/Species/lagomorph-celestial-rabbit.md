# Celestial rabbit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Lagomorph](../Species_Families/lagomorph.md)*

A small, pale rabbit that stands on its hind legs and lives, so the old stories say, on the face of the moon. On clear nights people swear they can see it there, bent over a mortar, pounding away with a pestle as tall as itself. What it pounds is the elixir of long life, or rice for festival cakes, depending on who tells the tale.

Few have met one, and those who have describe a patient, soft-spoken creature with silver fur and eyes that seem older than the hills. It does not fight, does not hurry and does not bargain. It simply works. Pilgrims leave offerings in the hope of a single drop of what it makes, and emperors have sent expeditions to find it and come back empty-handed.

## Not to be confused with

the *[lagomorph folk](lagomorph-lagomorph-folk.md)*, the ordinary rabbit and hare peoples, who dig warrens and raise families on the ground rather than tending an elixir in the sky.

## What sets this species apart

A mythic moon-dwelling rabbit whose whole being is bound to pounding the elixir of life. Its elixir of long life is its work (a Great Work), not a power.
