# Soul-echo android

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md)*

A machine with a ghost inside. Soul-echo androids are mechanical bodies holding a fragment of a human soul: not a copied memory or a program, but a sliver of a real person, caught and housed in metal. The result thinks like a machine but feels like a person. It understands people, reads their faces and moods and knows what to say, far better than any ordinary robot.

They are often capable fighters and quiet philosophers, turning over the questions other machines never ask: who they were, whether they can die, and whether the missing part of them is waiting somewhere. Others find them strangely warm for machines, and sometimes strangely sad.

## Not to be confused with

*[Android/synthetic-kind](constructs-and-synthetics-android-synthetic-kind.md)*, artificial minds in humanlike bodies, which may imitate feeling but carry no piece of a human soul.

## What sets this species apart

Machine bodies holding a fragment of a human soul, so they read and reach people far better than other machines.
