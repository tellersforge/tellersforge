# Owl folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Round faces, forward-staring eyes and a head that turns much farther round than it should. Owl folk wake at dusk and go about their business while others sleep, gliding between trees on feathers so soft that a mouse hears nothing until the talons close. Their eyes gather the faintest light, and their ears, set lopsided in the skull, tell them exactly where a rustle came from.

Other peoples credit them with wisdom, partly because they are quiet and partly because they always seem to be watching. Some deserve the reputation: owl folk make patient scholars, night-watchmen and keepers of old records. Others are simply hunters who find daylight unbearable and conversation overrated. Villages near an owl roost learn to keep the lamps low and the chickens locked up.

## Not to be confused with

*[Raptor folk](avian-raptor-folk.md)*, the daytime birds of prey, who hunt by far sight in full sun and make no effort to be quiet about it.

## What sets this species apart

Silent night fliers with excellent dark vision.
