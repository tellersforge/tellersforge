# Warrior/combat near-human folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Near-humans known first for how they fight. Pale-haired masters of the empty hand whose reflexes make ordinary soldiers look slow. Secretive warrior societies who teach the blade in closed halls and never explain themselves. Scholar-duelists, often engineered, who study science in the morning and fence in the afternoon. Then the darker lines: warriors twisted by old alchemy into living weapons, and invaders from beyond the stars who grow their armour and ships and despise machines.

Their bodies differ; their reputations do not. Any town that hires one of them expects results and prays it will not be on the other side next time.

## Not to be confused with

the *[ridged warrior folk](humankind-ridged-warrior-folk.md)*, whose strength is in the hide and bone; these peoples win their fights by tradition and training.

## What sets this species apart

Their edge is a fighting tradition, which is culture or the weapon-group pass; bodies differ by breed.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
