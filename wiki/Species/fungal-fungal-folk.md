# Fungal folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md)*

The mushroom people of damp caves and deep forest floors. Some walk upright under broad caps, dusted with spores, feeding on rot and fallen timber. Some are lichen, fungus and green algae woven into one small, stubborn body that can survive frost, drought and poison and grows so slowly that its elders remember the last ice. Some are not bodies at all but vast webs of threads under the soil, putting up fruiting bodies as eyes and hands while the true mind spreads for miles.

They are patient, quiet and hard to read. Many speak to one another through the mycelium beneath their feet, and a few defend themselves with clouds of choking or dream-bringing spores. The worst of them, the mindless rots and moulds, are not people at all, only hunger.

## Not to be confused with

*[spore-born fungal folk](fungal-spore-born-fungal-folk.md)*, the violent spore-sprung warrior culture; the fungal folk are slow, patient growers of cave and forest, most of them peaceful.

## What sets this species apart

The mushroom, lichen and mycelial folk the family describes; no species-wide differences. Mycelial networking (Mind Link) and spore attacks are set per Breed/Race; not all have them.

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
