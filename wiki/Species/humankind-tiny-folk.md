# Tiny folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Humans in miniature. They look like anyone else and live like anyone else, only on a scale where a hen is a monster, a cat is a catastrophe and a kitchen is a country. Whole kingdoms of them build cities in hedgerows, fight wars over the width of a garden and send embassies to one another with great ceremony. Out among the stars, tiny engineers and pilots get into places no one else can reach and fix what no one else can see.

They ride what their size allows: mice, beetles, birds. Their tools and blades are exquisite and nearly useless to anyone larger. Big folk tend to find them charming, which they find insulting, or to forget them entirely, which they have learned to use.

## Not to be confused with

the *[diminutive folk](small-folk-diminutive-folk.md)*, a small people of their own kind; these are ordinary humans shrunk down in every proportion.

## What sets this species apart

Ordinary humans in miniature; size is handled in the attribute step, so no skill or power lines differ. Mounts, tools and weapons of their scale are a gear-fitting matter.
