# Clan wildcat folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md)*

From a distance, they are only cats, ordinary tabbies and gingers slinking through the forest. Up close, they are a people. They live in clans with territories, leaders, deputies, healers and apprentices, bound by a strict warrior code that governs hunting, fighting and mercy. They have no hands, and they need none. Claw and tooth serve them in the hunt and in the border skirmishes that shape clan life.

Their leaders are said to have many lives, granted by the stars. Around the clans live loners who follow no code, and the soft house cats who live with humans and are scorned by the clans as pampered pets. The clan cats see the forest as their whole world and humans as a distant, noisy weather. Humans, for their part, rarely suspect that the cats in the woods have a history, a religion and very long memories.

## Not to be confused with

*[wild feline predator](feline-wild-feline-predator.md)*, beasts that hunt by instinct; clan wildcats are ordinary-sized cats with a society and a warrior code.

## What sets this species apart

Ordinary-sized four-legged cats who hunt and fight with claw and tooth and have no hands. No hands: held weapons unusable.
