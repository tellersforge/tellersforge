# Brutish mutant ape folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md)*

Hulking ape-mutants with low brows, sloped shoulders and fists like hammers. Whatever changed them left them strong and simple, and they know it. Some have a strange knack for commanding animals; others just shout louder. They are not graceful, and they are certainly not diplomats.

They tend to serve a stronger master: a warlord, a mad scientist or a villainous faction that needs muscle. They make fine guards, poor planners and dreadful spies. Their camps smell, their tempers are short and their loyalty is simple: whoever feeds them and lets them fight. People who meet them on the road fear them, and are often right to, but a few prove kinder than anyone expected.

## Not to be confused with

*[Great ape folk](simian-great-ape-folk.md)* are thoughtful and dignified; brutish mutant ape folk were made by some twisted process into strong, menacing and not very bright servants.

## What sets this species apart

Brutish, heavy ape-mutants: menacing, not nimble.
