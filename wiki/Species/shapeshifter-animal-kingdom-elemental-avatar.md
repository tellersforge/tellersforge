# Animal-kingdom elemental avatar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

Champions chosen by the great web of life that runs through every animal on the earth. They feel it as a thrumming in the back of the mind, and through it they can reach any beast they know: the strength of a gorilla, the leap of a flea, the eyes of a hawk, the grip of a gecko. Their own shape stays, but its abilities change from moment to moment.

They are often wanderers and protectors, speaking for creatures who cannot speak for themselves. The link lengthens their lives and makes them strange; they tend to smell a lie, sense a storm and prefer the company of dogs. Other forces that feed on rot and death see them as enemies, and they are right.

## Not to be confused with

*[Beast-touched shifter](shapeshifter-beast-touched-shifter.md)* carries fixed animal traits; the animal-kingdom elemental avatar borrows whichever creature's gifts it needs through the web of life.

## What sets this species apart

Champions linked to all animal life who borrow any creature's traits instead of changing their whole shape.
