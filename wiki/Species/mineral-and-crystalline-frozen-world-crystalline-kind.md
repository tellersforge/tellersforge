# Frozen-world crystalline kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

Once they were human settlers on a world too cold to live on. To survive, they changed: their bodies became living crystal, clear and glittering, lit from within. They still have human faces and human hopes, and still tell stories of the warm world their ancestors left.

Their crystal bodies drink in energy. Heat, light and raw force pour into them and can be sent back out again, bent and focused, as a beam or a flare. On their frozen home this keeps them alive; off it, it makes them dangerous. They are generally warm-hearted people in cold bodies, and they tend to be proud of what they became. Others sometimes treat them as curiosities or as a lost branch of humankind, which they find tiresome.

## Not to be confused with

the *[crystalline folk](mineral-and-crystalline-crystalline-folk.md)*, who were always crystal, where these were once human and remember it.

## What sets this species apart

Former humans turned to living crystal to survive a frozen world; their bodies absorb and redirect energy.
