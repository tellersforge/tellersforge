# World-mind horror

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

A mind the size of a planet, lying asleep in a quiet corner of space. From orbit it looks like a strange, lovely world. Those who land find riches, wonders and a creeping sense of being watched. Then it wakes, and begins to eat: not bodies, but minds, taking each thought and memory and folding it into itself. It has been doing this for longer than anyone knows, and each race it swallows makes it cleverer.

It has no hands and no need of them. It works by lure, dream and whisper, and by the ruin of every expedition sent to find out what happened to the last one.

## Not to be confused with

the *[living-planet kind](mineral-and-crystalline-living-planet-kind.md)*, which is a world that lives; this is a mind that hunts other minds.

## What sets this species apart

A planet-sized mind that devours and absorbs the minds of those who find it. NPC-scale. No hands: held weapons unusable.
