# Surgically-altered snake-human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md)*

Humans who were cut, dosed and reshaped until they became part serpent. Their skin is scaled and cold to the touch, their eyes slitted, and a pair of hollow fangs folds back into an otherwise human mouth. Some kept their legs; others were given a long muscular tail in place of them. All of them bend in ways a human spine should not.

The change was made in laboratories and back-alley clinics, sometimes by choice and more often not. The venom it gave them is strong and the poisons of others barely touch them. Most end up as hired killers, enforcers or sideshow curiosities. Some are proud of what they became. Others remember their old faces and never forgive the people who took them.

## Not to be confused with

*[Snake folk](serpentine-snake-folk.md)* are born serpents; the surgically-altered snake-human was born human and made into something else, and bears the scars of the process.

## What sets this species apart

Altered humans with a stronger venomous bite, toxin resistance and unusual flexibility. Versions that keep human legs Mod Running and Jumping back at Breed/Race level. The retractable fangs sit in a human-sized mouth: a light bite; the venom is Poison Touch.
