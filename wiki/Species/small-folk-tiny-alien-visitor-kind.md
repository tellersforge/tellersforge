# Tiny alien-visitor kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Small folk](../Species_Families/small-folk.md)*

Alien explorers the size of an insect, landing in small craft that could sit on a thumbnail. They travel in tight, disciplined scouting parties, mapping, sampling and reporting home, and to them a garden is a jungle and a kitchen is a mountain range.

They are curious, careful and brave, as anyone that small must be to cross a world of giants. Most people never notice them. Those who do tend to swat first and ask questions later, which the visitors have learned to expect. Their reports home make lively reading: tales of rivers of spilt tea, sleeping titans and the terrible six-legged monsters of the lawn.

## Not to be confused with

*[Tiny folk](humankind-tiny-folk.md)* are small humans of the home world; tiny alien-visitor kind are insect-sized explorers from another star.

## What sets this species apart

Insect-sized alien scouts; their size is an attribute and their scouting is culture, so no further lines.
