# Mercantile trader folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Near-humans who live by the deal. Some have enormous ears and hear a bargain being whispered two tables away. Some are gaunt, tall and pale, counting-house folk who run banks for whole worlds and never raise their voices. Some trade in rumours and secrets and are rarely seen by the people who buy from them. Others are wandering caravaners, refugees and haulers of dangerous cargo, as warm or as gruff as the road has made them.

What unites them is custom, not body. They keep ledgers and oaths, honour a contract to the letter and sometimes only to the letter, and find other peoples' dislike of haggling faintly baffling. Nobody trusts them entirely. Everybody does business with them.

## Not to be confused with

the *[scavenger trader folk](rodent-scavenger-trader-folk.md)*, who trade in salvage and junk; these peoples trade in everything, and treat the deal as a way of life.

## What sets this species apart

Near-humans grouped by a trading way of life; the differences are cultural and are handled in the Culture step. Keen hearing of the large-eared breed is a Breed/Race line.
