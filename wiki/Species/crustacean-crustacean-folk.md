# Crustacean folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md)*

Armoured from birth. Crustacean folk are crab, lobster, shrimp and barnacle people, wrapped in hard shells and walking, scuttling or swimming as their breed allows. Crab-folk scurry sideways and snap with heavy pincers; lobster-folk carry one crushing claw and one cutting one; shrimp-folk flick away with a sudden snap of the tail; hermit crab-folk borrow shells and trade them; barnacles settle in one spot for life; and the dazzling mantis shrimp-folk strike faster than an eye can follow.

All of them grow by shedding their shells, and are soft and vulnerable for days afterwards, which they hide carefully. They tend to be gruff, practical and defensive, and very hard to crack in every sense.

## Not to be confused with

*[Monster crustacean](crustacean-monster-crustacean.md)*, the huge unthinking arena beast, where crustacean folk are people with homes and trades.

## What sets this species apart

No species-wide difference: this is the family template, and its breed lines carry the variation.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
