# Ascended energy being

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

Once they were flesh. At some point in a long history, a people learned to leave their bodies behind and became something else: light, thought, will, spread across a plane of existence that mortals cannot reach. Most who ascend lose interest in the world below. These did not. They want to be worshipped.

They cannot easily touch the physical world themselves, so they work through followers. Chosen mortals are filled with borrowed power and sent out as preachers and prophets, able to burn, heal and command with a gesture. Crusades follow. The ascended feed on the devotion that flows back to them, and grow stronger for it. To their faithful they are gods of fire and truth. To everyone else they are something colder: beings with the power of heaven and the vanity of a petty king, and no patience for those who refuse to kneel.

## Not to be confused with

*[divinely-ascended human](humankind-divinely-ascended-human.md)*, a mortal lifted to godhood; these beings are a whole bodiless people who crave worship and act through chosen servants.

## What sets this species apart

Ascended god-like beings who empower their mortal followers. NPC-scale.
