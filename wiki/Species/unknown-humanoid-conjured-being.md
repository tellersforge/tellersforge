# Conjured being

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md)*

A flash, a puff of blue-tinted smoke, and there stands a smiling blue figure, eager to help. Conjured beings are summoned servants, called out of nothing by a box, a circle or a spoken word to perform one task. Their bodies are ordinary and fixed: two arms, two legs, a cheerful face, nothing magical about them except their coming.

They exist for one purpose, and completing it is their only joy. When it is done, they vanish, content. When it is not, they suffer. The longer a task drags on, the more frantic and miserable they become, until the strain destroys them. Those who summon them are well advised to ask for something simple, and never for anything that cannot be finished.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bound wish-spirit-kind](trans-dimensional-bound-wish-spirit-kind.md)*, a spirit bound to grant its master's wishes; the conjured being serves one small task, then ends.

## What sets this species apart

A conjured humanoid servant with an ordinary fixed body, made for one task. Very short-lived by nature (a lifespan trait, not a skill line).
