# Acid-blooded hunter folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

Black, glistening predators with long ridged skulls, whip-like tails and a second set of jaws inside the first. Their bodies are hard and smooth as armour, they climb walls and ceilings with ease, and their blood is an acid that eats through steel. Wound one and you may regret it more than it does.

They do not hatch in any usual way. They grow inside a living host and burst out when ready, and each one takes something from the creature that bore it. Born from a beast, it may run on all fours, swift and low; born from another hunter, it may carry mandibles and a warrior's build. They have no culture, no language and no mercy. They are the end of the food chain, and they know it.

## Not to be confused with

*[monstrous insectoid kind](insectoid-and-arachnid-monstrous-insectoid-kind.md)*, a broad catalogue of arthropod monsters; the acid-blooded hunters are one specific apex predator, marked by host-born birth and acid blood.

## What sets this species apart

Exoskeletal apex predators with acid blood, inner jaws and tails. Climbing and Armour Skin copied from the Insectoid and arachnid family. Acid & Corrosion is the acid blood, which works when the body is wounded.
