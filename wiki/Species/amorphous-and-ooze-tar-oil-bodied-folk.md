# Tar/oil-bodied folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

Slow black creatures of living tar that lie in pits and pools and wait. Their bodies are thick, glossy and sticky, and whatever steps in stays in. They close around prey with endless patience and dissolve it over days. They move slowly, but they do not stop, and they can flow up a bank or across a floor while their victims are still pulling at a trapped boot.

They have minds, cunning and old, and some can be bargained with. They burn, and fiercely, and know it: the smell of a torch makes them sink deep and wait for another day.

## Not to be confused with

the *[monster ooze](amorphous-and-ooze-monster-ooze.md)*, which is mindless; tar folk are slow but cunning, and patient on purpose.

## What sets this species apart

Slow, sticky tar bodies that trap and dissolve prey. Flammable.
