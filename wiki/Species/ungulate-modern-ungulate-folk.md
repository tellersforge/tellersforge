# Modern ungulate folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

Sheep, gazelles, buffalo and their kin, in collared shirts and good shoes. Modern ungulate folk are ordinary citizens of a contemporary city: upright, two-legged, with hooves, horns or woolly heads, and full lives as clerks, teachers, constables and mayors. Their bodies differ little from the rest of the hooved family; horns in a crowded lift are a matter of etiquette rather than danger.

What makes them distinct is custom, not anatomy. A buffalo family and a gazelle family may share a street and nothing else, and old prejudices about predators and prey still shape who gets hired and who gets watched. For the most part they are simply people, and would like to be treated as such.

## Not to be confused with

*[Civic feline folk](feline-civic-feline-folk.md)*, their predator neighbours in the same cities; modern ungulate folk are hooved grazers, with the same civic lives and different old fears.

## What sets this species apart

Ordinary civic ungulate bodies; their differences are cultural and handled in the Culture step.
