# Bound child spirit-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

A small grey figure the size of a toddler, quick and silent, slipping through a gap under the door. It is the spirit of a dead child, caught and bound by a shaman or sorcerer and made to serve. Its master sends it into neighbours' houses to steal coins, trinkets and secrets, and it is very good at not being seen.

In return it must be fed, and regularly: milk, sweets, toys, and sometimes a drop of its keeper's own blood. Neglected, it turns spiteful and makes mischief of its own. People pity it as much as they fear it, for it never chose this. Those who suspect one at work scatter beads across the floor, because a child cannot resist stopping to play.

## Not to be confused with

*[Household spirit](energy-and-incorporeal-household-spirit.md)*, a being bound to a home's welfare; the bound child spirit belongs to its master, and is sent into other people's homes to steal.

## What sets this species apart

A tiny child-spirit made for sneaking and stealing.
