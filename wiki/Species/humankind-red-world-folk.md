# Red-world folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Near-humans of a dry, rust-coloured world with thin air, dead sea beds and the ruins of older cities. All of them hatch from eggs, which strikes visitors as strange and strikes them as perfectly ordinary. Three races share the world and very little else. The copper-skinned people build airships, keep courts of proud warrior-nobles and duel over points of honour. The green-skinned nomads are something else again: towering, tusked and four-armed, they cross the wastes in hordes, show little feeling, and leap startling distances in the light gravity. The pale folk hide behind a priesthood that has long pretended to be divine and has fed on the others for as long as anyone remembers.

Travellers from heavier worlds find they can jump like heroes here, and quickly learn that the locals have been fighting far longer.

## Not to be confused with

the *[multi-limbed folk](humankind-multi-limbed-folk.md)*, whose extra arms are shared by the whole people; among the red-world races only the green nomads have them.

## What sets this species apart

Egg-laying near-humans of a dry red world; the three races share no body trait that changes a skill or power, so their differences sit at Breed/Race level. Four arms, great size and low-gravity leaping belong to the green-skinned race only.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.

### Civilized red-skinned folk · White-skinned priesthood folk *(same numbers as a baseline human: play as one (Species rule 18))*

- **Size:** medium · **Body:** biped
- **Attributes:** none
- **For the Game Master only, this race as an NPC guard or soldier (not your character's numbers):** Attack 14 · Parry 11 · Dodge 8 · Damage longsword 1d6+2 · Armour 2 · Life 17 · Threat 1 ([Bestiary roles](../bestiary.md))
- *Civilized red-skinned folk:* Same as its species; red skin and a flying-ship civilisation differ only in appearance and culture.
- *White-skinned priesthood folk:* Same as its species; pale skin and a secretive god-posing priesthood are appearance and culture.

### No. 4 Green-skinned four-armed folk *(genres: pulp planets and lost worlds)*

- **Size:** large · **Body:** biped
- **What it's like:** Huge four-armed, tusked warriors who leap far and fight fiercely with their tusks. (looks like: alien; good at: strength, fighting, climbing)
- **Attributes:** STR +3 AGI −2
- **Skills:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md) +1, [Hand Tools](../Skills/hand-tools.md) +1, [Jumping](../Skills/jumping.md) +1
- **Weapon groups:** [Shields](../Weapon_Groups/shields.md) +1
- **Natural attacks:** tusks 1d6+3
- **For the Game Master only, this race as an NPC guard or soldier (not your character's numbers):** Attack 14 · Parry 11 · Dodge 5 · Damage tusks 2d6; longsword 1d6+5 · Armour 2 · Life 21 · Threat 2 ([Bestiary roles](../bestiary.md))
- *Why:* Four arms and tusks, with the leaping of a big body in low gravity, set this race apart from its kin.
- *Note:* Carries out the species note: the extra arms, great size and leaping are this race's alone. Size is set in the attribute step; the extra arms give Shields Mod+1, or a second weapon held in the spare hands.
