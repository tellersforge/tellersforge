# Parasitic star-shaped conqueror

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md)*

A giant, star-shaped thing from the far dark, with five great limbs and a single central eye. The parent body can grow to the size of a city. It does not fight with its own body, since it has no hands to hold a weapon. Instead it sends out small copies of itself, each the size of a dinner plate, which leap onto a victim's face and clamp tight. From that moment the victim is the conqueror's creature.

It thinks in terms of worlds, not people. A single spore on the right face can turn a guard, then a squad, then a city, and the parent grows vaster as its hosts multiply. Its enemies have learned to watch faces carefully, and to be very suspicious of anyone who wears a mask.

## Not to be confused with

*[parasitic hive-mind](hive-and-collective-parasitic-hive-mind.md)*, which spreads through many hosts as a shared infection; the star-shaped conqueror is one giant mind directing its hosts through spore copies.

## What sets this species apart

A giant telepathic parent body that enslaves hosts through small spore copies of itself. NPC-scale. Mind Control works through the spore copies clamped onto hosts. No hands: held weapons unusable.
