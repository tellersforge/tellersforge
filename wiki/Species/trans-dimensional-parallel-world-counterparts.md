# Parallel-world counterparts

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

People from the world next door. Their bodies are ordinary, often identical to someone the traveller already knows, but their history went another way: the empire won, the plague never came, the good king turned tyrant. What changed them is not biology but everything else.

Some come from harsher worlds and carry that harshness with them, some from worlds of opposite matter where even the laws of nature run backwards, and a few are dark doubles, twisted copies of real heroes and villains. On arrival they are strangers in the most unsettling way: they know the streets, the names, the faces, and get every one of them slightly wrong.

## Not to be confused with

*[Dimension-travellers](trans-dimensional-dimension-travellers.md)* move between worlds by gift or device; parallel-world counterparts are simply natives of another world who have ended up in ours.

## What sets this species apart

Baseline bodies from a divergent parallel world, so the family's otherworldly body traits are removed; only their strangeness to local ways remains. Doppelganger and undying breeds take their own powers at breed level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
