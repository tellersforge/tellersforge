# Predator raptor folk (non-sentient)

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Wild birds of prey, big enough to be a real danger to small folk. They wheel high over meadows and cliffs, drop without warning, and carry off whatever they can lift in their talons. To a mouse-sized people, a hawk's shadow on the grass is the most terrifying thing in the world, and lookouts are posted for nothing else.

These birds cannot be reasoned or bargained with. They are not cruel, only hungry and superbly built for it: eyes that catch a twitch in the grass from far above, a stoop faster than any arrow, and a grip that does not let go. Small folk learn to live under cover, in the hedges and beneath the trees.

## Not to be confused with

*[Raptor folk](avian-raptor-folk.md)*, the thinking birds of prey, who hunt by choice and can be spoken to.

## What sets this species apart

Wild birds of prey: flying hunters with keen eyes and talons.
