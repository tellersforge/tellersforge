# Insectoid hive folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md)*

Chitinous, many-limbed people who build vast burrows under the earth and live in them by castes: workers, soldiers, a queen, and in some colonies a class of subtle, patient diplomats. Some are six-limbed and radially built, able to face every direction at once. Their bodies are armoured with smooth plates, and their tunnels run for miles.

Each one is a real individual with its own thoughts and preferences, but their culture puts the colony first, always. To outsiders this looks like a hive mind; it is not, quite, and they are offended by the suggestion. Their diplomats are famously good at getting others to do what the colony wants while believing it was their own idea. Neighbours find them polite, alien and very hard to read.

## Not to be confused with

*[hive warrior folk](insectoid-and-arachnid-hive-warrior-folk.md)*, which are warrior castes bred for battle; insectoid hive folk are thinking individuals with a collective culture, not a single mind.

## What sets this species apart

Individually sentient chitinous burrowers with a caste culture that is collective but not a true hive-mind. Mind Link is removed because this people is not always a true hive-mind; caste and diplomat traits are culture.
