# Post-apocalyptic warrior caste

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ursine](../Species_Families/ursine.md)*

In the ruins of a fallen world, where animals rose to take the places of the vanished builders, one people grew into the wall of the army. They are bears: massive, shaggy, scarred, clad in scavenged plate. They are slow, and they do not care. In a battle line they walk forward and keep walking, soaking up blows that would fell a horse, until the enemy runs out of courage or out of soldiers.

Their soldiering is a matter of custom, taught from cubhood and honoured in song. Other castes rely on them and fear them in equal measure. Off the field they are quiet, slow to speak and devoted to their comrades, and they share out their rations first.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bear-folk](ursine-bear-folk.md)*, the ordinary true bears of every era; this caste is heavier and slower, and in a ruined world has become almost impossible to stop.

## What sets this species apart

Massive bears, slow but nearly unstoppable; their soldiering is cultural.
