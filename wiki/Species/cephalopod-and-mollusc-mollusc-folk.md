# Mollusc folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md)*

Slow, soft and patient. Mollusc folk are snail, slug and clam people. Snail-folk carry spiral shells they can retreat into; slug-folk go shell-less and rely on thick slime and a knack for healing; bivalve folk hide between hinged shells and barely move at all, filtering their food from the water and, now and then, growing a pearl. There are also shelled folk with pincers, secretive slug sorcerers of great age, and methodical communities that work almost as one.

They have none of the octopus's many strong arms, no beak, no colour-changing skin and no jet of speed. They make up for it in endurance, caution and time. A mollusc sage may take a year to finish an argument, and win it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Cephalopod folk](cephalopod-and-mollusc-cephalopod-folk.md)*, the octopus and squid peoples, who are fast, many-armed and colour-shifting where molluscs are slow and shelled.

## What sets this species apart

Slow crawling snails, slugs and bivalves: no colour-changing skin and no jet-propelled swimming. Snails, slugs and bivalves have neither the many strong arms nor the beak.
