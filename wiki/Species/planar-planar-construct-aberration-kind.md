# Planar construct/aberration-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

The strangest natives of the other realms, gathered together only because they fit nowhere else. At one end stand constructs of pure order: geometric beings of cubes and spheres who obey their rank without question. At the other end tumble the spawn of chaos: frog-like creatures that heal wounds almost at once and spread their young through the bodies of others. Beyond both lie aberrations whose shapes make the eye ache and the mind wander.

Among them are quieter folk too: wanderers who drift between the planes arguing philosophy, and shadow-born people who can step through darkness from one place to another. What they share is that they came from somewhere else, and that mortals rarely understand what they want.

## Not to be confused with

the *[far-realm and outer entities](trans-dimensional-far-realm-and-outer-entities.md)*, who come from beyond all known realms, where these beings are natives of the recognised planes.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference: order-constructs, chaos-spawn, aberrations and planewalkers vary too widely, so everything is carried at Breed/Race level.

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
