# Hive insect folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

Ant-people who grow by eating. The colony hunts other creatures, and when it feeds, it takes their traits: an ant that eats a wolf grows fangs and a sharp nose, one that eats a man grows a face and a tongue that can talk. Over generations the colony becomes a patchwork of whatever it has devoured, organised in rigid castes of workers and soldiers who drill together without a word.

At the top is the queen, and when she has eaten the strongest prey she can find, she bears a king. The king is something new, stronger, cleverer and harder to kill than any before, and his arrival is the kind of news that makes neighbouring kingdoms mobilise. Individuals can develop strange gifts of their own, and sometimes wonder who they are.

## Not to be confused with

*[insectoid hive folk](hive-and-collective-insectoid-hive-folk.md)*, whose hive culture is peaceful and burrowing; hive insect folk grow by devouring and taking the traits of their prey.

## What sets this species apart

Ant-based hive castes: they copy chitin from the Insectoid family and drill from the Hive family. Formation Fighting from Hive and collective; Pain Tolerance and Armour Skin from Insectoid and arachnid.
