# Bat spirit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Chiropteran](../Species_Families/chiropteran.md)*

Feared things of the night and the cave. Bat spirits are not people who happen to have wings: they are the shapes that stories give to darkness, death and hunger. Some are small and stealthy, slipping in at the window to feed on sleepers. Others are bat-headed gods of caves and sacrifice, served with dread by the peoples who live near them.

Their mere presence chills mortals. Dogs howl, candles gutter and children wake crying. Many can change their shape, fly on broad black wings or scatter into a swarm, though no two tales agree on how. They are feared everywhere they are known, and they seem to prefer it that way.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bat folk](chiropteran-bat-folk.md)*, the living bat-people who simply roost and hunt at night, rather than haunting it.

## What sets this species apart

Feared night spirits and bat-gods whose presence alarms mortals. Flight and shapeshifting are left to the Breed/Race layer.
