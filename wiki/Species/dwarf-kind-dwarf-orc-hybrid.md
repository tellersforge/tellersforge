# Dwarf-orc hybrid

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Dwarf-kind](../Species_Families/dwarf-kind.md)*

Bred, not born of love. Dwarf-orc hybrids were created by masters who wanted labourers with a dwarf's endurance and an orc's strength and menace. The result is thick-necked, broad, heavy-jawed and immensely strong, hardy in cold, heat and foul mine air, and unsettling to look at for both parent peoples.

They are rarely able to have children and seldom live long, which suited their makers well. Freed hybrids tend to be grim, blunt and fiercely protective of each other, since no one else will be. Dwarves see them as a crime against their blood; orcs see them as useful. The hybrids have their own opinion, and do not share it lightly.

## Not to be confused with

*[Dwarf](dwarf-kind-dwarf.md)*, the free stone-dwelling people, who share the stocky build but not the orcish menace or the history of bondage.

## What sets this species apart

Dwarf-orc hybrids who add orcish hardiness and menace to dwarf stock.
