# Monster ooze

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

Mindless oozes of dungeons, sewers and caverns. The classic kind is a clear, quivering mass that slides along the floor and ceiling, swallows whatever it touches and dissolves it slowly in acid. Cut it and you get two. Others are studded with crystals that grow like thorns and can be flung, or come as swarms of small blobs that together can swallow a dog, or rise as vast heaps of poisoned sludge. The strangest are what is left when a person or beast is overwhelmed by corruption: a heaving mass of limbs and mouths.

A few are tame. Pet oozes eat almost anything, follow their owners about and sulk when scolded. The rest are simply hunger with a shape, and the sensible response is fire, distance or both.

## Not to be confused with

the *[sentient slime folk](amorphous-and-ooze-sentient-slime-folk.md)*, which think and speak; monster oozes are beasts and hazards.

## What sets this species apart

Mindless bestiary oozes whose special traits (acid, crystal shards, swarming, bulk) differ breed by breed, so nothing is shared beyond the family. Acid, engulfing and splitting belong on the individual breeds.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
