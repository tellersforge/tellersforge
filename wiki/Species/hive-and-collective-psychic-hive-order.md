# Psychic hive order

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md)*

Tall, graceful humanoids with no mouths and no need of food, drink or sleep. They draw their strength from a shared psychic link that runs through their whole people, and from it they take a power that can split rock and shield armies. Their bodies are elegant but frail; they tire more from exertion than from want, and a wound that another would shrug off may lay one of them low.

They are organised in great orders of warriors, priests and judges, bound by ancient codes and a deep sense of shared purpose. Not all of them accept the link. An exiled offshoot, secretive and individualistic, cut itself away long ago and grew stronger alone. The two sides regard each other with a sorrowful, patient hostility that is older than most stars.

## Not to be confused with

*[psionic hive species](hive-and-collective-psionic-hive-species.md)*, which has merged into one mind; the psychic orders keep their individuals and share power, not identity.

## What sets this species apart

Communal orders of solid but frail humanoids joined by immense innate psionic power and free of ordinary bodily needs. The exiled offshoot order may cancel Mind Link at Breed/Race level.
