# Psychic/telepathic human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

People who hear more than words. In most ways they look and live like anyone else, but some inner sense lets them feel a mood across a room, share the eyes of a hunting hawk, speak mind to mind, or catch the shape of what is about to happen. Some are frontier scouts bonded to a beast partner. Some are quiet colonists on distant worlds who have almost forgotten how to talk aloud. Some were born and raised in space and feel its depth the way sailors feel weather. Others come in linked pairs, and when one twin dies the other is never whole again.

Ordinary folk treat them with a mixture of reliance and unease. It is useful to have a friend who knows when you are lying. It is less comfortable when that friend is sitting across the table from you.

## Not to be confused with

the *[logical ascetic folk](humankind-logical-ascetic-folk.md)*, whose mind gift is only a latent touch-bond; these people live by their inner sense from birth.

## What sets this species apart

Near-humans defined by an innate mental sense: they read people more easily and take readily to telepathy. Telepathy is affinity. The specific gift (animal bond, mindspeech, precognition, warp perception) is Set at Breed/Race level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.

### No. 17 Beast-bonded psychic folk *(genres: classic fantasy)*

- **Size:** medium · **Body:** biped

- **Powers:** [Beast Bonding](../Powers/beast-bonding.md) 2, [Animal Senses Link](../Powers/animal-senses-link.md) 1

- *Why:* Their gift is an innate mind-bond with an animal partner, sharing senses and feelings.
