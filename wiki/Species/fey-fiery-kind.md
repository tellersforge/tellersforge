# Fiery-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Loose-limbed, shaggy fey who live for noise, dancing and very bad jokes. Their bodies come apart and go back together at will: an arm can be thrown to a friend, a head tossed across a clearing and caught again, a leg left propped against a tree while its owner hops off in search of the next party. They find this endlessly funny, and are baffled when visitors do not.

They are not cruel, exactly. They simply cannot see why anyone would prefer to keep their own head on. Travellers who stumble into one of their revels are welcomed loudly, pressed to join in, and may find it hard to leave with all their belongings, or all their dignity. Wise ones laugh, dance a little, and slip away before the singing turns into a game.

## Not to be confused with

*[dangerous fey](fey-dangerous-fey.md)*, who hunt and harm the unwary; the limb-tossing fey are wild but not predatory, and only want someone to party with.

## What sets this species apart

Wild party fey who can pull off and reattach their own limbs and head. Self-Alteration covers detaching and reattaching body parts only.
