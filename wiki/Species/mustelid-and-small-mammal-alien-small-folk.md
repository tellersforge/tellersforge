# Alien small folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mustelid and small mammal](../Species_Families/mustelid-and-small-mammal.md)*

A tiny, furry, big-eyed creature with a round belly and a vacant, friendly expression, the sort of thing sold as a pet at a space market and happily fed scraps. It eats anything. It sleeps a great deal. It seems to understand very little.

None of that is true. Beneath the soft fur is one of the oldest peoples in the universe, far older and more powerful than any of those who pat them on the head. They watch the great events of the cosmos from the laps of the unwary, quietly guarding a balance that others don't know exists. What they can actually do, no one has fully learned. When one finally drops the act and speaks, in perfect calm tones, it is usually because the end of everything is close.

## Not to be confused with

the *[tiny alien-visitor kind](small-folk-tiny-alien-visitor-kind.md)*, small visitors who come openly from elsewhere, where these folk hide what they are behind a pet's face.

## What sets this species apart

A tiny, unassuming alien people who are secretly ancient and immensely powerful, hiding in plain sight. NPC-scale. The survey names no specific innate powers beyond great age, so the Game Master adds any others. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
