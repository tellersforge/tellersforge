# Night demon-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

A winged demon of the night, shaped like a woman with the feet and feathers of an owl. She rides the storm wind over villages, screeching, and where her shadow passes sickness follows. Most of all, the old stories say, she comes for newborn children, which is why mothers hang charms above the cradle and midwives paint signs on the doorway.

She is ancient, clever and utterly without pity. She prefers the dark, the storm and the sickroom, and she flees from the names of certain protectors and the light of the morning. Folk speak of her in whispers, and never on stormy nights.

## Not to be confused with

the *[hag/witch-kind](undead-and-deathless-hag-witch-kind.md)*, mortal or undead crones of dark magic, where the night demon is a winged spirit of storms and sickness.

## What sets this species apart

Winged night-demons of storms and sickness.
