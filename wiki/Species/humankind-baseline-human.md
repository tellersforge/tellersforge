# Baseline human

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Ordinary people: two arms, two legs, a fragile body and a restless mind. They are found in every age and on every shore, from cave mouths to the decks of starships, and they are nearly always the ones who got there first or arrived in the largest numbers. No gift of birth sets them apart. What they have instead is range. A human can learn to herd, to fight, to pray, to build or to steal, and can change trade halfway through a life.

Other peoples find them hard to sum up. They are short-lived, quarrelsome, curious and stubborn, and they breed fast enough to fill whatever room they are given. Elders among longer-lived folk tend to say humans never learn anything. Humans tend to say the same of their elders, and then go and do it anyway.

## Not to be confused with

the *[human culture folk](humankind-human-culture-folk.md)*, who are the same people in body and are named for a way of life, such as the caravan, the steppe or the ice.

## What sets this species apart

No different from the family: ordinary humans are the baseline, and their four free choices already express their versatility.
