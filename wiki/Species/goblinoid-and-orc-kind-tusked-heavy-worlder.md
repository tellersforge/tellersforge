# Tusked heavy-worlder

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Goblinoid and orc-kind](../Species_Families/goblinoid-and-orc-kind.md)*

Squat, heavy, tusked people from a world whose gravity would crush an ordinary visitor flat. They are short and nearly as broad as they are tall, with thick grey hide, dense bones and a strength that bends steel bars without meaning to. Blows that would break other people's ribs merely annoy them.

On their own world, everything has to be built strong or it falls down, and they are gifted engineers as a result: patient, stubborn and deeply practical. Away from home they move with a springy lightness that startles everyone around them, and they find doorways, chairs and handshakes alarmingly fragile. They are sought after as mechanics and feared as fighters, and they give up on nothing once they have started.

## Not to be confused with

*[tusked green alien](goblinoid-and-orc-kind-tusked-green-alien.md)*, the hearty tusked companion people; the heavy-worlders are shaped by crushing gravity, and are engineers first and fighters second.

## What sets this species apart

Heavy-gravity bodies with thick, damage-resistant hide.
