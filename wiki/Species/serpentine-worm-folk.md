# Worm folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md)*

Tiny, soft-bodied beings shaped like a worm with a face on the front end: round eyes, a wide mouth and a surprisingly expressive manner. They are small enough to sit on a table and they get about by wriggling, sliding and occasionally being carried. They have no venom, no coils worth fearing and no dangerous stare.

What they have is company. Worm folk live packed together in cheerful, noisy households and love to talk, drink and trade stories. They turn up in the back rooms of ports and offices, where they hear everything and repeat most of it. People who underestimate them tend to discover that the little wriggling crowd in the corner knew about their plans days ago.

## Not to be confused with

*[Snake folk](serpentine-snake-folk.md)* are sizeable, venomous serpents; worm folk are tiny, harmless and sociable, and belong to the family only by their long, limbless shape.

## What sets this species apart

Tiny worm-bodied folk with neither venom nor a hypnotic gaze. Too tiny to constrict or bite.
