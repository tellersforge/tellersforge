# Small forest-bonded folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md)*

Small, stocky, pig-snouted creatures with leaf-green skin, sharp tusks and bright little eyes, living in close-knit tribes in the heart of old forests. They look like beasts but think like people, and they are fierce, proud and loyal to their clan.

Their lives follow a cycle unknown to other peoples. When one of them dies, and their people perform the proper rite, they do not rot: they grow. Roots push from the body, bark closes over it, and within a season a young sacred tree stands where they fell, able to speak and remember. Their groves are made of their ancestors. Anyone who fells a tree in their forest is committing a murder, and the tribe will treat it as one.

## Not to be confused with

the *[treant-kind](plant-kind-treant-kind.md)*, walking giant trees, where these small folk are born as animals and only become trees in death.

## What sets this species apart

Plant-animal hybrids whose life cycle ends in rebirth as a sacred tree. Renewal at Death is the transformation into a living, speaking tree.
