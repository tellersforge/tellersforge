# Lower-dimensional beings

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Flat folk. Line folk. A lone point that believes itself the whole universe. These are beings of fewer dimensions than ours, living in worlds without any "up". The flat ones slide across their plane as polygons and circles, ranking their society by the number of their sides; the line ones know only forward and back.

They have no hands, cannot climb or leap out of their world, and see a visitor from ours only as a shifting line or a dot that grows and shrinks. They are not stupid, only limited, and some of their philosophers argue long and hard about the possibility of a direction they cannot imagine. A visitor from outside is, to them, a miracle or a madness.

## Not to be confused with

*[Higher-dimensional beings](trans-dimensional-higher-dimensional-beings.md)* see more of the world than we do; lower-dimensional beings see less, and experience our world only in slices.

## What sets this species apart

Flat, line or point beings with no 'up' in their world: they cannot climb or leap out of their plane and see a 3D world only in slices. Survey lists no innate powers for any breed. No hands: held weapons unusable.
