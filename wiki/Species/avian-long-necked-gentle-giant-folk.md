# Long-necked gentle giant folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Large, slow-moving and kind. Long-necked gentle giant folk walk on four sturdy legs, carry a long neck upright, and end it in a bird-like head with a mild, thoughtful expression. They have no wings and no pretence of flight; they could not glide off a roof. What they have is size, reach and an unhurried patience that makes smaller peoples feel oddly safe.

They browse, wander and talk at their own pace, and a question put to one may be answered next week. They are rarely angry and never in a hurry. Those who mistake that for weakness learn otherwise when one finally decides to stand in the way.

## Not to be confused with

*[Flightless runner mount](avian-flightless-runner-mount.md)*, another big wingless bird-beast, but a fast, unthinking animal bred for riding.

## What sets this species apart

Large, wingless, four-legged bird-headed folk with no gliding ability.
