# Dimension-travellers

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Wanderers between worlds. They look like anyone else, with ordinary bodies and ordinary faces, but they have been to places nobody else has: the world where the old empire never fell, the one where the seas are dry, the one where everyone speaks backwards. Some travel by an inborn gift, some by a humming device, some by the permission of a vast council that keeps order among the worlds.

They collect knowledge of countless other lives, and a certain detachment to go with it. A traveller has seen a hundred versions of every disaster and tends to shrug at the hundred-and-first. Their great fear is being stranded, with the door closed behind them and no way home.

## Not to be confused with

*[Parallel-world counterparts](trans-dimensional-parallel-world-counterparts.md)* belong to another world and stay there; dimension-travellers deliberately cross between worlds, by gift or by device.

## What sets this species apart

Ordinary humanoid bodies that travel between worlds by gift or device, so the family's otherworldly body traits do not apply. Device-based travel is gear, not a power; Plane Shift is affinity only.

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
