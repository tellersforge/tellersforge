# Mist-awakened human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Descendants of humans altered in the far past by powerful outsiders, whose gift sleeps until a strange mist or cloud touches them. Then they are wrapped in a shell like a cocoon and come out changed. One may breathe fire, another may walk through walls, another may grow a hide of stone or lose their face altogether. No two awakenings are the same, and the family is never sure who will step out.

They live in hidden cities and guard their mist jealously. Their society is split between the awakened and a bred servant caste who never go through the change, which is the source of most of their troubles.

## Not to be confused with

the *[mutant-gene human](humankind-mutant-gene-human.md)*, whose gift wakes on its own; this one needs an outside trigger to open it.

## What sets this species apart

An ancient gene alteration unlocks one random power per individual on exposure to a trigger, so no power is shared at species level. The awakened power is chosen per individual or Set at Breed/Race level; the bred servitor caste has none.
