# Celestial-engineered human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Near-immortal offshoots of the first humans, reshaped long ago by vast beings from beyond the sky. They do not age, rarely fall ill and can channel a cold, bright power for strength, flight, blasts of light and the slow remaking of matter. For ages they have lived apart, watching younger humanity and sometimes steering it. Their cities are hidden and their quarrels are older than most mountains.

Now and then one is born wrong: a throwback with a twisted face and a different set of powers, still far beyond any mortal. Such births are treated as shameful and hidden, and the hidden ones have their own long memory.

## Not to be confused with

the *[long-lived/ascended human](humankind-long-lived-ascended-human.md)*, whose long life came by other means; these were built by cosmic precursors to carry their power.

## What sets this species apart

Engineered, near-immortal offshoots of early humanity who channel cosmic energy for strength, flight, blasts and reshaping matter. The throwback breed has a different profile and resets these at Breed/Race level. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
