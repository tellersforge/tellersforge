# Horned winged folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

Winged people with horns, born to the high air. One kind has curling ram's horns and feathered wings, and a culture of spell-casters and soldiers. A rival kind wears branching horns like antlers, some with thin shimmering wings, and sorcery runs in their blood. The two have fought for so long that each defines itself by hating the other. A third, darker line is nearly gone: black-winged, demon-horned folk whose bodies can burst into flame and not burn.

All of them sense the wind and weather as a hawk does, and all feel at home far above the ground. Those who cannot yet fly still dream of it and lean into the wind. Ground-bound peoples tend to see them as beautiful, proud and a little dangerous, and they are right on all counts.

## Not to be confused with

*[winged sky human](avian-winged-sky-human.md)*, a winged branch of humankind; the horned winged folk are a distinct people, marked by horns and centuries of their own feuds.

## What sets this species apart

Winged humanoids: they copy the Avian family's air sense and flight affinity. Copied from the Avian family. Flight is affinity; lines that fly innately Set it at Breed/Race level.
