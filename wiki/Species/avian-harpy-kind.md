# Harpy-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Bird below, woman above. Harpy-kind have human heads and faces, arms that become or give way to wings, and legs ending in hooked talons. They fly fast and fight dirty, stooping on travellers, snatching food and goods, and leaving a foul trail behind them. In the older stories they are the storm-winds themselves: swift, filthy and quarrelsome.

Their kin, the sirens, perch on sea rocks and sing. The song is lovely, and it is meant to be followed, all the way onto the reef. Few peoples trust either kind. Sailors plug their ears with wax, shepherds guard the flock, and a village with harpies in the hills learns to eat indoors.

## Not to be confused with

*[Raptor folk](avian-raptor-folk.md)*, true birds of prey with beaked heads, where harpies keep a human face and a far worse temper.

## What sets this species apart

Taloned bird-women who fly. Human heads: no beak.
