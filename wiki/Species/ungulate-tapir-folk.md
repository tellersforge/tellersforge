# Tapir folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

Heavy, round-bodied folk with short trunks, small eyes and skin like boiled leather. Tapir folk are herbivores, placid by habit and hard to hurt: thorns, hail and clumsy blades glance off them. They live by herding.

What they herd is the uncomfortable part. In their oldest cities the livestock were human beings, kept in pens, fed, bred and led out to pasture, and the tapir folk saw nothing strange in it. Some still don't. To most other peoples this is a horror; to some tapir herders it is simply good husbandry. Travellers who meet them are advised to make it very clear, very early, that they are guests and not stock.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bovine folk](ungulate-bovine-folk.md)*, the horned cattle herders; tapir folk are trunked and hornless, and notorious for what they herd.

## What sets this species apart

Tough-bodied tapir herders.
