# Living-story folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

People made of story. Each was born, or formed, from a tale told often enough to take on flesh: the loyal squire, the wicked stepmother, the wandering fool. They look human, more or less, but their features drift with the part they play: a face grows more trustworthy when they are trusted, harder when they are feared.

They do not choose these changes, and they cannot stop them. What they can do is keep their minds; charms, glamours and mind-bending magic tend to slide off them, as if the story were holding their thoughts in place. They are drawn to tellers, libraries and firesides, and they often carry an uneasy sense that their story has an ending they have not yet reached.

## Not to be confused with

*[Changeling-kind](fey-changeling-kind.md)* take another's place by fey design; living-story folk shift only in small ways to fit a role, and never on purpose.

## What sets this species apart

Beings made of story: their features drift subtly to fit a role rather than change at will, and their minds resist tampering.
