# Pygmy folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Small folk](../Species_Families/small-folk.md)*

Small, lithe folk of the deep jungle, close to other humans in body but shorter and lighter, and far better suited to a world of vines, rivers and green shadow. They move quietly and live in close-knit bands under the canopy.

Other peoples know them mostly from rumour: hunters who strike from the leaves with silent darts, makers of powerful charms and grim trophies. The truth is less lurid and more impressive: they know every plant, animal and path of their forest, and they survive where outsiders sicken and die. They are wary of strangers, with good cause, and generous to those who earn their trust.

## Not to be confused with

*[Tiny folk](humankind-tiny-folk.md)* are small humans of any land; pygmy folk are near-human jungle dwellers whose forest craft is learned rather than born.

## What sets this species apart

Near-human small folk; their jungle hunting and charm-making are cultural and handled in the Culture step.
