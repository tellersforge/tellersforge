# True elemental

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md)*

Not flesh at all. A true elemental is living fire, water, stone, storm, metal, wood or raw primal matter, given shape and motion. A flame elemental roars and sets alight what it touches; a water elemental flows through cracks and drowns what it engulfs; a stone elemental grinds forward, slow and nearly unbreakable; a storm elemental is a whirl of wind and lightning, barely visible. They do not breathe or eat, and they shrug off their own element entirely.

They are rarely people in any sense a human would recognise. Most are summoned, bound or found in wild places where their element is strongest. They are dangerous to anger and impossible to bribe.

## Not to be confused with

*[Elemental spirit folk](elemental-kind-elemental-spirit-folk.md)*, humanlike spirits of the elements who can talk, bargain and love, where true elementals are raw forces.

## What sets this species apart

Pure elemental matter rather than flesh, near-immune to its own element. NPC-scale. The Breed/Race names the element.
