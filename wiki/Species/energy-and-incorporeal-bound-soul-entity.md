# Bound soul entity

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

They lost their bodies long ago, in a catastrophe their stories recount in hushed and bitter tones. What remains of each is a soul, and a soul cannot walk on its own. So they wrap themselves in a husk: a shape of bindings, cloth and enchanted bands, with gloves for hands and a hood over nothing at all. Faint light glows through the gaps where eyes should be.

The husk is their body. It lets them hold tools, carry goods and shake a trader's hand. Once they were a great civilisation. Now they are scattered wanderers, merchants and scavengers who drift between worlds and trade in magical wonders to survive. They are canny, polite and a little desperate, and they have learned to drive a hard bargain. Everyone suspects they are hoarding something. Everyone is probably right.

## Not to be confused with

*[spirit-bound armor-kind](undead-and-deathless-spirit-bound-armor-kind.md)*, a spirit trapped in an empty suit of armour; bound souls wear a husk of bindings by choice and live as a people.

## What sets this species apart

Bodiless souls held in a bound magical husk that can grip and carry things. The husk is their body, not gear. The husk has hands: held weapons are usable despite the family's no-hands note.
