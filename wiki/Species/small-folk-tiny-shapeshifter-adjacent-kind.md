# Tiny shapeshifter-adjacent kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Small folk](../Species_Families/small-folk.md)*

A small, gentle native people with round heads, huge eyes and soft voices. They are timid and peaceful and would sooner hide than fight, and on their home world they have little to fear but the strangers who come to it.

Yet among them, once in a while, is one who can change: who can take on the look of another, bend what others see, or twist the world around them in strange and unsettling ways. Such individuals are rare, prized and feared even by their own people. Outsiders hear of them and come looking, and the small folk have learned, painfully, to keep their gifted ones hidden.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mimic infiltrator folk](shapeshifter-mimic-infiltrator-folk.md)* are born to copy others; tiny shapeshifter-adjacent kind are timid natives among whom only a rare few ever show the gift.

## What sets this species apart

Timid small natives, a few of whom carry a latent talent for mimicry.
