# Desert elf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

Elves who took to the dry country and were shaped by it. Their skin is weathered brown and gold, their eyes narrowed against glare, and their bodies hold water and endure heat that would drop a forest elf in a morning. They know which rocks hide shade at noon and which dunes shift in the night.

There are two kinds of them. One is wild and half-feral, running in bands across wastelands where little else survives, and often gifted with strange powers of the mind that the harsh sun seems to draw out. The other is aristocratic and old, keeping courts in oasis cities and cliff palaces, with an arcane tradition as refined as any. The wild bands think the courtly ones soft, and the courtly ones think the wild bands barely elves at all. Both have outlived empires that tried to rule the sand.

## Not to be confused with

*[woodland folk](elf-kind-woodland-folk.md)*, the forest-dwelling elves; desert elves share their keen senses but traded the canopy for sun and stone.

## What sets this species apart

Heat-hardened elves at home in dry country. Resist Element is heat.
