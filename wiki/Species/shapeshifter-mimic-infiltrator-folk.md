# Mimic infiltrator folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

The most unnerving of all shapeshifters: beings who can become someone else exactly. Not a likeness, but a copy, down to the scar on the knuckle, the rasp in the voice and the way the person holds a cup. Some study their target for weeks; some must touch, absorb or even replace them first.

Their true form is often strange: mottled, liquid, rubbery or featureless. Most keep it hidden. They have been hunted and feared through history for the obvious reason, and many have become the spies, assassins and infiltrators that others always accused them of being. Others are refugees who only want to pass unnoticed. Either way, once people learn one is among them, nobody trusts anybody.

## Not to be confused with

*[Changeling-kind](fey-changeling-kind.md)* are fey children swapped in the cradle; mimic infiltrator folk can duplicate any grown person at will, voice and manner included.

## What sets this species apart

Perfect duplicators of other individuals, down to face, voice and manner.
