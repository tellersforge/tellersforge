# Formless primal folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

Beings of raw, unfinished substance from the ragged edge of creation, where the world has not yet decided what it will be. By nature they have no shape at all. Strong belief or strong will can press one into a form: a child's nightmare, a king's hope, a saint's vision. It holds that shape while the will holds, and softens back towards chaos when it fades.

They are rarely met and never understood. Scholars call them clay waiting for a potter; priests call them the leftover stuff of the first days. Anyone who deals with them learns fast to be careful what they expect.

## Not to be confused with

the *[sentient slime folk](amorphous-and-ooze-sentient-slime-folk.md)*, who have a nature of their own; primal folk have none until someone gives them one.

## What sets this species apart

Raw uncreated substance that takes new forms under strong will or belief. Their form answers to strong will, their own or another's.
