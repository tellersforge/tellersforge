# Corrupted/mutated folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

People and beasts that something has gone wrong with. A curse, a plague, a poisoned wasteland or a pact made in the dark has twisted them into new shapes: antlered, starving hunters of the winter woods, shambling masses that were once men, wasteland survivors with extra limbs and strange eyes, cultists who took on the strength of apes, cursed folk who become wolves under the moon. No two are alike.

The change usually makes them harder to hurt, since corruption tends to toughen hide and dull pain. It also makes them harder to like. Most are shunned or hunted by the peoples they came from, and many have lost a good part of their reason. Those who keep their minds live with the knowledge of what they were, and what they are becoming.

## Not to be confused with

*[chimeric folk](hybrid-and-chimeric-chimeric-folk.md)*, the fusion beings of legend; corrupted folk were once ordinary creatures, twisted by curse or poison.

## What sets this species apart

Bodies twisted by curse or corruption: harder to hurt, harder to like.
