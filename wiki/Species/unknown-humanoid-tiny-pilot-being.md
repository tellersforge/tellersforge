# Tiny pilot-being

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md)*

The giant at the negotiating table is not the one you are talking to. The real speaker is no bigger than a thumb, sitting in a chamber behind the eyes, working levers. Tiny pilot-beings are miniature aliens who live inside larger bodies: humanoid shells, armoured suits, lifelike androids, all driven from within like a cart steered by a clever mouse.

Their technology is extraordinary, as it must be for folk so small to cross the stars. Outside their machines, their bodies are frail and quick. Most prefer to be left alone and quietly enjoy the confusion they cause. When a hatch opens in a giant's forehead and a very small, very cross person climbs out, the conversation always changes.

## Not to be confused with

*[Tiny alien-visitor kind](small-folk-tiny-alien-visitor-kind.md)*, small aliens who travel openly in their own bodies; the pilot-being hides inside a larger one and speaks through it.

## What sets this species apart

Tiny beings who work through piloted bodies; the bodies are gear, so no lines beyond the family.
