# Rock-being kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

Giants of living rock, tall as a house and broad as a gatehouse, whose skin is rough boulder and whose fists land like falling cliffs. They move slowly and speak slowly, with voices like stones grinding in a riverbed. Almost nothing can hurt them, and almost nothing can stop them once they have decided to walk somewhere.

Most of their peoples have long warrior traditions: honour songs, duels, old wars remembered in carvings. Yet many of them are gentler than they look, patient and oddly good-humoured, happy to let smaller folk climb onto their shoulders for a better view. Others see them as the perfect bodyguard and the worst possible enemy, and prefer to meet them as the first.

## Not to be confused with

the *[stone folk](mineral-and-crystalline-stone-folk.md)*, who are made of the same rock at an ordinary size, where these are true giants.

## What sets this species apart

No different from the family: huge rock-bodied warriors that are the family baseline. Warrior traditions belong to the Culture step; size is an attribute matter.
