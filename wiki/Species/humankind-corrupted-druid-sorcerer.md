# Corrupted druid sorcerer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Humans who took up the old nature-priesthood and were turned, by ambition or by bargains with dark gods, into sorcerer-lords. They keep the robes, the sickle and the forest shrines, but the rites have changed: blood on the stones, sacrifice at the solstice, dead men walking the woods in their service. Their magic is old, cold and cruel, and it grows with every life they take.

Villagers fear them and sometimes serve them. Heroes hunt them. The honest druids they betrayed hate them most of all, and are often the ones who know how to end them.

## Not to be confused with

the *[witch-kind](fey-witch-kind.md)*, who draw their power from fey or folk craft; these were ordinary priests corrupted by dark gods.

## What sets this species apart

Humans corrupted by dark gods into sorcerer-lords with innate dark magic and command of the dead. Druidic learning is Culture/Profession, not species.
