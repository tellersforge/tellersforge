# Engineered hybrid folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

Beings made on purpose. Somewhere a laboratory, a gene-forge or a surgical theatre combined the traits of several creatures, or rebuilt one creature into something new. The result may be an amphibious worker who fits in anywhere, a small mammal given a human mind and a bitter memory of its making, a person whose blood swarms with healing machines, or a warrior dynasty that replaces its flesh with metal a little more each generation.

They share no single shape or temperament. Each is whatever its makers wanted, plus whatever they failed to predict. Many struggle with not knowing where they belong, and some resent their origins bitterly. Others make the best of it, and are quietly proud of being the only one of their kind.

## Not to be confused with

*[uplifted animal folk](hybrid-and-chimeric-uplifted-animal-folk.md)*, animals raised to sapience while keeping their base form; engineered hybrids are built from many sources or rebuilt entirely.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference: engineered hybrids vary with whatever went into them. Parent-family lines are chosen per Breed/Race. Cybernetics and nanites delivered by technology are gear. Attributes follow the parent families per Breed/Race.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
