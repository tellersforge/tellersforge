# Civic feline folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md)*

Cats in waistcoats. These are the feline citizens of busy towns and modern cities: lawyers, grocers, police officers, schoolteachers, bus drivers. They may look like lions, tigers or cheetahs, but centuries of city life have worn the wild away. The mane is groomed, the claws trimmed, the hunting instinct faded to a fondness for fish and an occasional twitch of the tail at a fluttering pigeon.

They have no special animal gift to speak of. They do not sprint across savannas or ambush from rivers. They pay rent, argue about politics and complain about the trains. What remains is a feline temperament: a certain dignity, a love of warm spots and naps, an independent streak that makes them poor committee members. They are, in short, as civilised as anyone, and more comfortable with it than most.

## Not to be confused with

*[feline folk](feline-feline-folk.md)*, cat people who keep their wild animal gifts; the civic folk have traded them for city life.

## What sets this species apart

Fully civilised city cats with no particular animal gift; the hunting instinct has faded.
