# Hive swarm horror

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md)*

Living weapons grown by the million. The swarm comes out of the dark between worlds, and everything it has is organic: claws that are also blades, ribs that are also armour, beasts that spit acid or fire living darts from their bellies. Nothing is forged. Everything is grown, and what is lost is grown again. Some are colonies of eel-like creatures packed into a single huge armoured shell, slow and appallingly strong.

The swarm has one mind and one appetite. It adapts as it fights, and each new world teaches it something. Behind the front lines, it strips planets to the rock and feeds the soup back into its breeding pools. There is no talking with it. Worlds in its path pray to be overlooked.

## Not to be confused with

*[hive mind swarm kind](insectoid-and-arachnid-hive-mind-swarm-kind.md)*, the insect-derived swarm organisms; the hive swarm horror is a vast living war machine, growing its weapons to order.

## What sets this species apart

Monstrous biological swarms with grown weapons and armoured bodies. NPC-scale. Rapid adaptation is a Game Master matter, not a fixed line. Its grown bio-weapons are permanent, so they are the talons attack.
