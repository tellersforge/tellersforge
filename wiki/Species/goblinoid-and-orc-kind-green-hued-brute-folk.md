# Green-hued brute folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Goblinoid and orc-kind](../Species_Families/goblinoid-and-orc-kind.md)*

Green-skinned folk of fungal descent, and more alike in spirit than in size. At one end are tiny scavengers that swarm across battlefields picking at the dead, near-mindless and barely worth a kick. In the middle are small, sly tinkerers who build explosive gadgets and cower behind them. At the far end are hulking brutes whose idea of a good day is a war.

The big ones are fearsomely tough and full of a rage that seems to spill out of them in some crude magic when enough of them gather and shout. Their war-bands are loud, crowded and cheerful, their gear crude but serviceable. Settled peoples see them as a disaster that comes back every few generations. They see themselves as the only people who know how to enjoy life.

## Not to be confused with

*[spore-born fungal folk](fungal-spore-born-fungal-folk.md)*, who are true fungi; the green-hued brutes are goblinoid kin with fungal ancestry, and range from tiny scavengers to towering brutes.

## What sets this species apart

Fungal-descended greenskins; their range from tiny scavengers to huge brutes leaves no shared difference from the family. Toughness and rage are set on the larger Breed/Races.
