# Sentient plant kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md)*

A mind that lives in plants. It can gather itself into a tall, humanoid shape of stalks and leaves, or it can spread itself thin, a presence drifting through a forest, a meadow or a whole world of green. Those who meet it in either form describe the same thing: a calm voice that speaks directly into the head, and a sense of being noticed by something very old.

These beings live for ages beyond count. They are patient, thoughtful, and inclined to look very far ahead, which can make them seem cold, or wise, or dangerous. Whole peoples have worshipped them, and some have been bred and shaped in their service. They rarely act quickly, but when they do, every leaf in sight moves at once.

## Not to be confused with

the *[elemental plant avatar](plant-kind-elemental-plant-avatar.md)*, which is the living voice of all plants in one body, where this is a whole people of plant minds.

## What sets this species apart

Telepathic, extremely long-lived plant minds that can hold a humanoid shape or spread out diffuse. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
