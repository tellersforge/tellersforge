# Wandering spirit folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

Spirits that do not belong where they are found. Some are native to a parallel spirit world that lies alongside our own, crossing over at certain places and times: a solstice night, an old shrine, a place where a great deal of blood was spilled. Their shapes are as varied as dreams. A great-eyed thing like a heron, a drifting face in the mist, a small creature that sits on the roof and watches.

Others have no fixed shape at all. One kind wears a pale mask over a shadowy body and takes on the qualities of whatever it eats, growing larger, louder and hungrier as it goes. It is lonely and easily led astray by greed, and becomes dangerous in the wrong company. Wandering spirits are unpredictable rather than wicked. Kindness is sometimes repaid, and insult is always remembered.

## Not to be confused with

*[incorporeal spirit](energy-and-incorporeal-incorporeal-spirit.md)*, the ghost of a dead mortal; wandering spirits were never alive in our world at all.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference from the family: these spirits vary too widely in form and gift, so those lines sit with each Breed/Race.
