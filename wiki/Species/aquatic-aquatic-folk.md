# Aquatic folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md)*

Sea creatures given human shape. Shark-folk with rows of teeth that grow back and a nose for blood. Dolphin-folk, clever and playful, who talk in clicks and hear the shape of the sea around them. Orca-folk who hunt in family pods. Seal-folk, graceful in water and ungainly on land, and sea lions who bark and argue in noisy colonies. Eels, anglers with glowing lures, upright seahorses, bright ornamental carp, whiskered catfish, shoaling piranha, and wide-winged rays.

Some breathe water and some must surface for air. What they share is that the sea is home, and the dry land a place to visit on business.

## Not to be confused with

the *[merfolk](aquatic-merfolk.md)*, who are human above and fish below; aquatic folk are sea creatures in upright human-like form.

## What sets this species apart

Anthro fish and sea-mammal folk; their differences (gills or lungs, echolocation, teeth) vary breed by breed, so nothing is shared beyond the family. Sea-mammal breeds cancel Water Breathing at breed level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
