# Colossal geometric invaders

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

They arrive without warning and they are the size of buildings. Some look like vast crystals turning slowly in the air; some like giant skeletal things with a bird's mask for a face; some like shapes that should not stand upright at all. Flesh and machinery run together in them, and their forms shift as they come, folding into new geometries.

Around each one hangs a barrier of force that turns aside every ordinary weapon. Shells burst harmlessly, rockets splash like rain. Only enormous power, or some way to tear the field open, gives defenders a chance. They seem drawn toward a single goal, usually something buried deep and carefully guarded, and they will raze a city to reach it. No one knows what they want with it, or what happens if they get there. No one has wanted to find out.

## Not to be confused with

*[faceless invader](energy-and-incorporeal-faceless-invader.md)*, human-sized conquerors who eat matter; the geometric invaders are colossal and shielded by a field of force.

## What sets this species apart

Colossal solid bio-mechanical beings shielded by a near-impenetrable energy field. NPC-scale. Solid, but no hands: held weapons unusable.
