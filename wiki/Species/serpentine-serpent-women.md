# Serpent women

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md)*

Figures of terror from the oldest tales: women with the scales, fangs and cold eyes of serpents. Some have a woman's body above a long coiling tail. Others stand on two legs but wear a crown of living, hissing snakes in place of hair. Wherever they walk, people draw back without knowing why, and brave warriors find their knees unsteady.

They tend to live alone or in small sisterhoods, in ruined temples, caves and lonely islands. Some are cursed and bitter, some are hunters who lure the unwary with a false and lovely face, and a few are said to turn the living to stone with a look. Heroes seek them out for glory. Few come back with more than a story.

## Not to be confused with

*[Snake folk](serpentine-snake-folk.md)* are a people with homes and families; serpent women are solitary, dreaded monsters of legend whose mere presence unsettles the bravest.

## What sets this species apart

Monstrous serpent-women whose very look inspires dread. The gorgon's petrifying gaze and the lamia's shapeshifting go at Breed/Race level; the gorgon has legs and Mods Running and Jumping back there.
