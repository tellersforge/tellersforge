# Beastfolk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

Huge, tusked, heavy-browed and heavier-fisted, beastfolk are an abhuman stock bred long ago for war and labour. Their faces are part man and part boar or bull; their shoulders fill doorways. They are enormously strong and very hard to kill, and they are not clever. Complex plans confuse them, crowds make them nervous, and manners are an effort.

What they have in abundance is loyalty. A beastfolk soldier will follow a trusted sergeant into anything, carry a wounded squadmate across a battlefield, and grieve openly when that squadmate dies. Other peoples treat them as brutes or tools, which hurts them more than they can say. They are neither herders nor wild things, but a people made for hard work who deserve better.

## Not to be confused with

*[Swine folk](ungulate-swine-folk.md)*, the free boar- and pig-folk; beastfolk were bred as soldiers and labourers, bigger and stronger, with little of the swine folk's wit.

## What sets this species apart

Huge abhuman soldiers and labourers, menacing and socially awkward, not herders.
