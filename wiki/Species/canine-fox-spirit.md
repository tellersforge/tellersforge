# Fox spirit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md)*

A fox that is not only a fox. Fox spirits are shapeshifters of old legend, able to take a beautiful human form while keeping a trace of the fox about them: a glint in the eye, a shadow with a tail, a wariness of hounds. They grow stronger with age, and their power shows in their tails, which multiply over the centuries until the greatest have many.

They weave illusions, charm and deceive, and they enjoy it. Some are sages who guard shrines and reward kindness. Others are predators who feed on human life to keep their stolen shape. Nobody is ever quite sure which kind they have met until it is far too late, or they have been very lucky.

## Not to be confused with

*[Wolf-shifter kind](canine-wolf-shifter-kind.md)*, humans who turn into wolves, where a fox spirit is a fox that learns to wear a human shape.

## What sets this species apart

Shapeshifting fox spirits that take human form and lean on charm and trickery. Self-Alteration covers the fox-to-human change; power grows with age and tails at the Breed or advancement level.
