# Hopping corpse-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

A corpse in the robes of an official, arms stretched stiffly ahead, bouncing down the road with both feet together. It would be funny if it were not coming for you. The hopping corpse is a body locked by death's stiffness, unable to bend knee or elbow, animated by a restless hunger. It cannot walk, so it hops, and it hops surprisingly fast.

It feeds on the life-breath of the living, drawing it from their mouths in a pale mist. It hunts by the sound of breathing, so a clever victim holds their breath and stands very still. Paper talismans, mirrors and the crowing of a cockerel are all said to stop it. Priests who know the rites are paid well.

## Not to be confused with

*[Zombie-kind](undead-and-deathless-zombie-kind.md)*, which shambles and eats flesh; the hopping corpse is too stiff to walk and drinks the breath of the living instead.

## What sets this species apart

A rigor-stiffened corpse that moves by hopping and drains life-breath.
