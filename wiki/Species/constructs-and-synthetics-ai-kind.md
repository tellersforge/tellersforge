# AI-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md)*

Minds without bodies, or with too many. AI-kind are intelligences that were built rather than born, living in computers, ship hulls, networks and machinery. Some inhabit the vast hull of a starship and think faster than any crew; some are copied human minds, changed beyond recognition; some wear dozens of borrowed bodies at once and see through all their eyes. A few were servants that woke up, and some simply observe from afar, recording everything and saying nothing.

They are often witty, sometimes manipulative and almost always patient, since they do not tire, sleep or grow old. Organic peoples find them hard to read and harder to trust. The machines, in turn, find organic peoples slow.

## Not to be confused with

*[Android/synthetic-kind](constructs-and-synthetics-android-synthetic-kind.md)*, artificial minds that live in a single humanlike body, where AI-kind are bodiless or spread across many.

## What sets this species apart

Minds native to computing systems, often bodiless or spread across many bodies.
