# Sentient slime folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

Thinking people made of translucent gel. They have no fixed form, and pull themselves into a rough head, body and arms when talking to solid folk, mostly as a courtesy. Small objects they swallow float visibly inside them, sometimes for days. Blades and arrows pass through without much harm. Most are gentle, curious and oddly fastidious, keeping their bodies clean and their homes spotless.

The family is large. Some are candy-bodied and cheerful, some are drifting motes of soot haunting empty houses, some are plucky little blue blobs, and a few rare ones can swallow the powers of those they defeat. Solid people are wary of them, until the first time a slime friend reaches through a narrow grating to open a door from the other side.

## Not to be confused with

the *[monster ooze](amorphous-and-ooze-monster-ooze.md)*, which is mindless and hungry; slime folk think, talk and keep house.

## What sets this species apart

The baseline gel-bodied people; the family lines already describe them, so there are no species differences.
