# Multi-limbed folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Near-humans with a second pair of arms below the first. They carry, climb, cook and repair twice as fast as anyone else, and in a scuffle they can hold a shield and a weapon and still have hands to spare. Some are great, genial giants who run eating houses and know everyone's business. Some are spindly nobles who play instruments no one else can manage. Some are ascetic desert nomads who walk the dunes in silent bands and take their discipline as seriously as their arms.

Tailors hate them. Everyone else tends to find them useful, and asks them to hold something.

## Not to be confused with

the *[red-world folk](humankind-red-world-folk.md)*, among whom only the green nomads have four arms; here the extra pair belongs to the whole people.

## What sets this species apart

An extra pair of arms gives more hands for work and grip. Extra arms: Shields Mod+1, or a second weapon held in the spare hands.
