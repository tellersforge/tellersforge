# Hybrid mounts and companions

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

The fused beasts that people ride, keep and love without ever mistaking them for people. There are flying mounts with a reptile's body and a bird's temper, bonded to their riders through a living link; round, furry companions with pleasant faces and several rows of teeth; chattering monkey-lizards kept by warlords to laugh at their guests. Each is an animal first, however strange its parts.

They are not sentient, but they are clever in the way animals are clever. They learn tricks, grow fond of their keepers and know when something is wrong. Owners are often surprised by what they inherited from the creatures they were fused from, whether a hunting instinct, a fear of water or a habit of biting strangers.

## Not to be confused with

*[reptilian beasts and mounts](reptilian-reptilian-beasts-and-mounts.md)*, which are reptiles through and through; hybrid mounts are fusion creatures whose traits come from several animals.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference: non-sentient fused animals whose lines come from their parent creatures. Parent-family lines are chosen per Breed/Race.
