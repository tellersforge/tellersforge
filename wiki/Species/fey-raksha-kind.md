# Raksha-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Fair folk born out of belief. They exist because mortals dreamed and feared them, and they are made of the same stuff as stories: fluid, bright and unstable. Their bodies are whatever they choose, and at their strongest they can reshape the world around them too, turning a field into a palace or a road into a river for as long as their will holds. They fight by rewriting the scene rather than by striking blows.

They have one great weakness. Cold iron burns them, and so does cold reason. A sworn oath binds them absolutely, a plain fact spoken plainly can unmake their illusions, and a mortal who refuses to believe in them is very hard for them to touch. They find such mortals fascinating, and hate them.

## Not to be confused with

*[noble glamour fey](fey-noble-glamour-fey.md)*, whose glamour hides and beguiles; this kind reshapes reality outright, but comes apart when faced with iron, oaths and cold logic.

## What sets this species apart

Belief-born fair folk who reshape their bodies and reality, and are undone by cold logic. Vulnerability to iron and oaths is a flaw, not a skill line.
