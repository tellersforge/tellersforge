# Great furred folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md)*

Towering folk covered in thick fur, with claws on their fingers and a roar that shakes loose leaves from the trees. They are immensely strong and live for centuries, and they carry that long life in a slow, steady manner, though their tempers can flare terribly when roused.

They are much more than brawn. Many are brilliant engineers who can repair a ship's engine with little more than their hands, and they bind themselves with strict codes of honour and life-debts. They are too big for tumbling and too heavy for most chairs. Others find them loyal beyond reason, frightening when crossed and surprisingly gentle with children. Losing an argument to one is safest.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bear-folk](ursine-bear-folk.md)* are ursine; great furred folk are simian, clawed and long-lived, known as much for their engineering and honour codes as for their roar.

## What sets this species apart

Tall, clawed, furred giants with a fearsome roar; too big for tumbling.
