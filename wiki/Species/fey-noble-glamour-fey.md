# Noble glamour fey

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

The lords and ladies of fairy, beautiful beyond mortal measure and ageless as the hills. Once, in the old tales, they were like gods and walked openly. Now they have withdrawn into mounds and hollow hills, and into seasonal courts where summer and winter each keep their own monarch. Time runs strangely there. A night of dancing may be a century outside.

Their chief power is glamour: magic that bends what mortals see and what they want. A noble fey can seem to be anyone, make a hovel look like a palace or a stone look like bread, and make a mortal fall hopelessly in love. They are bound by strict customs of bargain, hospitality and truth-telling, and they keep their word to the letter, never to the spirit. They are ruthless toward mortals and courteous about it. The wise never thank them, never eat their food and never tell them their true names.

## Not to be confused with

*[raksha-kind](fey-raksha-kind.md)*, fair-folk beings shaped by belief who reshape reality; the glamour fey are fairy nobility who bend perception and will.

## What sets this species apart

Ageless fey nobility whose glamour is the strongest of the family. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
