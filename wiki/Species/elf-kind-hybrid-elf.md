# Hybrid elf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

Children of two peoples, fully at home with neither. The commonest are the offspring of elves and humans, who live far longer than a human but nowhere near as long as an elf. They are a touch less keen-eyed and slight than their elven parent, taller or broader than the forest kin, and quick to learn the ways of both worlds without being fully welcome in either. Many become go-betweens, envoys and wanderers.

Rarer and more dramatic are those with giant blood in them, big-boned, hard to hurt and at home in harsh desert tribes where strength is the first thing anyone asks of you. Whatever the other half, hybrid elves share a sense of standing in a doorway. Pure-blooded elves see them as diluted. Everyone else sees them as elves. They often decide, sooner or later, to be only themselves.

## Not to be confused with

*[hybrid bloodline human](humankind-hybrid-bloodline-human.md)*, humans carrying a trace of other blood; hybrid elves are counted with elf-kind and live closer to its span.

## What sets this species apart

Mixed blood thins the elven keenness and slightness.
