# Surface-dwelling amphibious folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md)*

A rubbery-skinned amphibian people with large membrane ears, equally at home underwater and on land. They look clumsy, walk clumsily and talk too much, which leads others to underestimate them; they are in fact tough, adaptable and oddly lucky. Their surface clans build on shorelines and trade with landfolk. Their deep-water kin keep to the ocean cities and look down on surface cousins as flighty.

Many of them make good traders and go-betweens, trusted because they seem harmless. Sometimes they are.

## Not to be confused with

the *[swamp-dwelling folk](amphibian-swamp-dwelling-folk.md)*, a reclusive marsh people; these amphibians are a sociable, trading folk of both shore and sea.

## What sets this species apart

Close to the family baseline; the clan differences are cultural and handled in the Culture step.
