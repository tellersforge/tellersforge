# Ghoul-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

Eaters of the dead. Ghouls haunt graveyards, battlefields and the tunnels beneath old cities, and they can smell a corpse through earth and stone. Some were human once and were changed by what they ate: long-faced, clawed, doglike about the jaw, keeping crude societies in the dark below the tombs. Some are feral things whose touch leaves the living stiff and helpless. In desert lands they are said to take the shape of hyenas and lure travellers off the road.

There are gentler ghouls too, whose rotting bodies somehow endure and who keep their minds, their humour and their grievances. They are rarely welcome anywhere. Even the peaceful ones find that people watch their hands at dinner, and check the churchyard afterwards.

## Not to be confused with

*[Zombie-kind](undead-and-deathless-zombie-kind.md)*, reanimated corpses that hunger for the living; ghouls prefer what is already dead, and many were never dead themselves.

## What sets this species apart

Corpse-eaters with a nose for carrion and dead things.
