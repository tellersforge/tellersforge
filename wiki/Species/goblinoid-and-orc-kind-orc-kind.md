# Orc-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Goblinoid and orc-kind](../Species_Families/goblinoid-and-orc-kind.md)*

Tusked, broad-shouldered folk of medium height with a proud tribal tradition and a strong sense of honour. In some worlds they were ordinary people until a sudden, mysterious change swept through the population and left some families with tusks, heavy bones and shorter lives. In others they have always been this way, living in clans on the edges of the wild under chiefs who value keeping one's word above all.

Either way, they are strong, tough and used to being judged on their looks. Many people treat them as thugs and expect the worst. They answer in different ways: some with a chip on their shoulder, some with grim humour, some by proving the doubters wrong. They are not scavengers or raiders by nature, and resent anyone who says they are.

## Not to be confused with

*[orc](goblinoid-and-orc-kind-orc.md)*, the family's baseline warrior folk of old legend; orc-kind are proud tribal peoples whose tusks come with honour, not savagery.

## What sets this species apart

Tusked tribal folk with a strong honour tradition: tough, but not scavengers of ruins.
