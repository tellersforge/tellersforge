# Precursor dimension-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

The first gardeners. Long ago, before the known peoples woke, these beings seeded life across many worlds from a hidden realm outside ordinary space. They are enormous, ancient and dignified, and they have outlived ages the way others outlive winters.

Now they are mostly retired, keeping to their secret home and watching what grew. They left traces: vast monuments with no builders, carvings in no known language and patterns in the living world that scholars cannot explain. Some still meddle, gently or otherwise. Meeting one is like meeting a grandparent who is also a god, a scientist and a stranger, and who remembers exactly why you were made.

## Not to be confused with

*[Celestial progenitor-kind](planar-celestial-progenitor-kind.md)* are planar makers of life from the heavens; precursor dimension-kind engineered life from their hidden realm and have since withdrawn.

## What sets this species apart

Ancient founders and seeders of life who have outlived ages in their hidden realm. NPC-scale. Life-engineering technology is gear. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
