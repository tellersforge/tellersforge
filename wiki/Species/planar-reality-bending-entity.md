# Reality-bending entity

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

Beings who treat the laws of the world as suggestions. Some are tall, gaunt figures in dark suits whom no one can remember the moment they look away. Some are near-limitless tricksters with the temperament of a spoiled child, able to fill a room with illusions, rewrite a mind or send a ship across a galaxy on a whim. Some are imprisoned monsters in the depths of lost worlds, claiming to be the gods of ancient peoples.

Each bends reality in its own way, and none of them can be trusted to explain it. They are rarely evil in any simple sense; they are dangerous because they don't care. Mortals who meet one generally agree afterwards that the wisest course was to be very polite, very interesting, or very far away.

## Not to be confused with

the *[omnipotent trickster entity](energy-and-incorporeal-omnipotent-trickster-entity.md)*, a single all-powerful jester, where this is a loose kind of many unrelated reality-warpers.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference: each entity's reality-warping is its own, so it is Set at Breed/Race level. The smooth-skinned diplomat alien breed is not otherworldly and should cancel the family's planar lines at Breed/Race level. The god-like breeds are NPC-scale.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
