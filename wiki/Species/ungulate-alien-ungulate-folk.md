# Alien ungulate folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

Three legs, two long necks, and at the end of each neck a flat head that speaks in harmonies. These aliens are herbivores from a world with no mercy for the careless, and they have made caution their whole civilisation. Risk is sin. Courage is madness. A leader is the one who goes last, from the back of the herd.

This sounds comic until you meet their science. A people so determined to avoid all danger has spent ages learning to control everything that might hurt it, and its machines are far beyond most others. They make cautious allies, slow to commit and quick to withdraw. Their enemies discover that a coward with overwhelming power is very dangerous indeed.

## Not to be confused with

*[Radial alien kind](cephalopod-and-mollusc-radial-alien-kind.md)*, another body built far from any familiar pattern; these aliens are grazing herd-beasts at heart, and their nature is caution above all.

## What sets this species apart

Two-headed aliens whose whole nature is extreme risk-aversion.
