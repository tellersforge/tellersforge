# Draugr-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

A dead chieftain in his mound, sitting upright among his grave goods, waiting. Barrow-walkers are corpses that refused to lie down, and they guard what was buried with them against anyone foolish enough to dig. Their flesh is dark and swollen, their grip crushes bone, and they are far stronger than they were in life.

Stranger still, a barrow-walker can swell to the size of a giant when angered, and the sky answers its moods: fog rolls in, snow thickens, the wind turns against the grave-robber's boat. They do not wander far from home. They do not need to. Cold-country folk know which mounds to avoid, and those who must pass leave a gift, and never look back.

## Not to be confused with

*[Zombie-kind](undead-and-deathless-zombie-kind.md)*, the common walking dead; the barrow-walker is a single mighty guardian, bound to its grave and able to grow and call storms.

## What sets this species apart

Mound-guarding corpses of great strength that can swell to giant size and stir the weather.
