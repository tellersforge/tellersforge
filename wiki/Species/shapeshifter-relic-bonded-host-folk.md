# Relic-bonded host folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

Ordinary people chosen by something that is not ordinary: an ancient relic, alive and aware, that bonds itself to a single host. It might be a mask, a gauntlet or a stone set into flesh. When it wakes, the host is wrapped in living armour, sprouts blades, or starts bending the rules of the world around them.

None of that belongs to the host's body. Take away the relic and they are the same person as before, a little more tired and a lot more haunted. The bond changes them in other ways too: some find their temper wild and strange while it is active, and most feel the relic's will pressing against their own. Those who carry one rarely chose to.

## Not to be confused with

*[Artifact-bonded host-kind](planar-artifact-bonded-host-kind.md)* are the planar family's hosts of otherworldly objects; relic-bonded host folk are shapeshifters whose changes come only while the relic is worn.

## What sets this species apart

Ordinary humans whose changes come from a bonded relic, so the body itself has no innate shifting. The relic's armour, weapons and reality-bending are an item (gear or a Great Work), not species powers.
