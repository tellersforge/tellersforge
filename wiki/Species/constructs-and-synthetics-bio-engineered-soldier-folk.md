# Bio-engineered soldier folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md)*

Soldiers grown to order. Bio-engineered soldier folk are flesh-and-machine warriors produced in birthing vats and pits: some with leathery wings and grey insect hides, some armoured and armed, all built to charge in numbers and die without complaint. They have little will of their own. Many are linked to a central mind or mothership, and collapse when the link is cut.

Unlike true machines, they are still alive. They need food and rest, however little, and their engineered lives are often short. Their masters treat them as ammunition. Those who fight them find the waves endless, and those who capture one alive sometimes find, to their discomfort, something almost like a person.

## Not to be confused with

*[Cyborg-kind](constructs-and-synthetics-cyborg-kind.md)*, who began as free individuals before the machinery, where these soldiers were grown for war from the start.

## What sets this species apart

Grown flesh-and-machine soldiers bred for mass shock tactics who still need some food and rest.
