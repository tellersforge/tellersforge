# Riverine lizard-folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md)*

Long-tailed lizard-folk of the great rivers, as much at home in the water as on the bank. They swim with powerful sweeps of the tail, dive deep and stay down long, and come up with fish, pearls or the rope of a drifting barge. On land their tails give them a rolling, balanced gait.

They live off the water in every way: fishing, ferrying, and above all trading, moving goods and news up and down the river from one town to the next. They are gregarious, talkative and full of stories, which they tell at length and not always accurately. Most river towns have a few of them at the docks, and most river travellers have paid one of them more than they meant to.

## Not to be confused with

the *[lizardfolk](reptilian-lizardfolk.md)*, the broader scaled peoples, where the riverine kind are born swimmers who live on and by the water.

## What sets this species apart

Amphibious river lizards, strong swimmers and divers, living off the water.
