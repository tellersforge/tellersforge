# Animated stone guardian

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

A winged, clawed, grinning figure of carved stone, crouched on a roof ledge or a cathedral gutter with rain running out of its mouth. For most of its life it is exactly what it looks like: a statue. Then night falls, or an intruder climbs the wrong wall, and the statue turns its head.

These guardians were carved and woken to protect one building and whatever lies inside it. They are loyal to the stones they perch on rather than to any person, and they can wait unmoving for centuries. In some tellings they sleep by day and hunt by night, gliding down on heavy stone wings. Townsfolk leave them alone, masons speak of them with respect, and thieves who climb churches tend to learn the hard way which gargoyle was only decoration.

## Not to be confused with

the *[living-statue predator kind](mineral-and-crystalline-living-statue-predator-kind.md)*, which hunts whoever it finds and moves only when unseen, where the guardian protects one place and can be watched without effect.

## What sets this species apart

A carved winged stone figure that can pass for a statue and watches over a building. Flight is affinity only, as for the Avian family. Dormant by day in some tellings.
