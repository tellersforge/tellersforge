# Banshee-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

A woman at the edge of the field, or on the stair at midnight, or under the window, wailing. When the keening comes, someone in the house will die soon. That is all she does, and it is enough. She is a spirit tied to death as an omen is tied to what follows it, and many of her kind are bound to a single family, generation after generation.

Between her warnings she can pass for an ordinary person, a little pale and sad-eyed. When she wishes, she becomes almost weightless, thin as mist, and walls and doors mean little to her. Some are cruel, most are grieving, and all are unwelcome. Wise folk do not blame the messenger. They check the roof, and send for the priest.

## Not to be confused with

*[Ghost/spirit-kind](undead-and-deathless-ghost-spirit-kind.md)*, the restless dead in general; the keening spirit is not a lost soul but a herald whose cry foretells a death.

## What sets this species apart

A near-incorporeal death-omen spirit whose wail foretells a death.
