# Small woodland folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ursine](../Species_Families/ursine.md)*

Knee-high, round-bellied and furry, small woodland folk look like a child's toy bear until they start setting snares. They live in tribes deep in the forest, in villages built up in the branches or tucked among the roots, and they know their woods perfectly. Their senses are sharp, their fur blends with bark and leaf, and they can vanish into the undergrowth in a moment.

Some are peaceful builders and farmers; others are cunning trappers with clever primitive gear: deadfalls, rolling logs, gliders of hide and sticks. They are too small for a bear's crushing grip or heavy claws, and their bite is small too. Invaders who laugh at them tend to stop laughing somewhere around the second trap.

## Not to be confused with

*[Small forest-bonded folk](plant-kind-small-forest-bonded-folk.md)*, tiny peoples tied to the woods by spirit; small woodland folk are furry forest tribes with no such bond, only good traps.

## What sets this species apart

Small forest tribes: not big or frightening, but good at hiding in the trees. Too small for the bear hug or bear claws; a small bite only.
