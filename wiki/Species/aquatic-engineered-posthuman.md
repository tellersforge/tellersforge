# Engineered posthuman

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md)*

Humans redesigned to live under the sea: gills added at the neck, webbing between fingers and toes, lungs changed to take the pressure. They are otherwise quite ordinary, move easily on land, and can dive for hours without effort. Some variants struggle to breathe air for long and must return to the water to rest.

They build habitats on the seabed and farm kelp and fish. Surface humans see them as a triumph of science or as a warning, and they themselves mostly see the surface as a place they visit.

## Not to be confused with

the *[undersea humanoid](aquatic-undersea-humanoid.md)*, which is an old natural offshoot; engineered posthumans were designed by modern science.

## What sets this species apart

Human bodies with gills and webbing added, so not clumsy on land.
