# Subspace and hyperspace natives

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Not every other world is a separate universe. Some lie just underneath this one, in the layers that faster-than-light ships skim through: folded, fluid or crystalline spaces that most travellers see only as a blur. These are the peoples who live there. Some are patterns of pure energy that speak mind to mind and need no houses at all. Some are heat-loving crystalline castes, rigid and territorial. One kind drifts through a fluid otherspace and, when it surfaces, wears a vast living hull like a ship.

They cross between their layers and ordinary space with ease, and they often regard starships as clumsy trespassers churning through their home. More than one space-faring nation has learned, too late, that the shortcut it depended on ran through somebody's garden.

## Not to be confused with

*[Dimension-travellers](trans-dimensional-dimension-travellers.md)*, who cross between separate worlds by device or gift; these natives live in the layers beneath ordinary space itself.

## What sets this species apart

Natives of the layers beneath normal space, crossing between them and ordinary space.
