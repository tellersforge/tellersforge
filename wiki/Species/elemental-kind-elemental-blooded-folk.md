# Elemental-blooded folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md)*

People with a trace of the elements in their blood. Elemental-blooded folk look mostly human, but their heritage shows: hair that flickers like flame or drifts as if under water, skin with the sheen of stone or crystal, a breeze that follows them indoors. Each line is tied to one element and takes on its nature. Fire-touched folk run hot and passionate; water-touched folk breathe beneath the waves and keep their calm; earth-touched folk are tough and stubborn; air-touched folk are restless and light on their feet.

They are often the children of strange unions or planar accidents, and seldom entirely at home anywhere. Others find them striking, unpredictable and hard to forget.

## Not to be confused with

*[True elemental](elemental-kind-true-elemental.md)*, beings made wholly of their element, where elemental-blooded folk are flesh with a trace of it.

## What sets this species apart

No species-wide difference: each Breed/Race names its element and adds its element's lines.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
