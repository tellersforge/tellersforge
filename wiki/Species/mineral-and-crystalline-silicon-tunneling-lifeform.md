# Silicon tunneling lifeform

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

A low, heavy, shapeless creature with no hands and no face that anyone can find, its body a mottled mass of living silicon. It moves through solid rock the way a mole moves through soil, softening the stone ahead of it with a corrosive ooze and leaving smooth round tunnels behind. Miners hear it long before they see it.

It lives for thousands of years and thinks slowly, deeply and with great feeling. Most of what it does, it does for its eggs, which it hides in the deep rock and defends with terrifying devotion. Anyone who breaks into a nest learns what that acid does to flesh. Those patient enough to talk to it, though, find a reasonable being who will share the tunnels, and knows where the ore runs.

## Not to be confused with

the *[colossal desert worm](serpentine-colossal-desert-worm.md)*, a burrowing giant of sand and flesh that eats what it finds, where this creature is stone, thinking and far more careful.

## What sets this species apart

A handless silicon burrower that melts through solid rock with its own acid and lives for thousands of years. Lifespan (long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number. No hands: held weapons unusable. Its acid is the Acid & Corrosion power, not an attack.
