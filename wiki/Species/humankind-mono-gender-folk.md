# Mono-gender folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Near-humans whose people are, or appear to be, all of one sex. One such people is large, heavily built and stubbornly traditional, and treats any child born different as a problem to be solved, which causes more trouble than they like to admit. Another is blue-skinned, graceful and extraordinarily long-lived, able to join with almost any partner and faintly aware of the minds around them; its members are welcomed across the stars and trusted a little less than they would wish.

They have little else in common. Visitors tend to see one feature first and the rest of the person late, which is the thing both peoples complain about most.

## Not to be confused with

the *[near-human alien](humankind-near-human-alien.md)*, a broader grouping whose members differ in many small ways; these are defined by one striking fact about their people.

## What sets this species apart

Single-sex peoples whose two breeds share nothing that changes a skill or power; the differences sit at Breed/Race level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
