# Hobgoblin

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Goblinoid and orc-kind](../Species_Families/goblinoid-and-orc-kind.md)*

The soldiers of goblinkind. Hobgoblins are taller and heavier than goblins, with ruddy or grey skin, and they carry themselves like people who have been drilled since childhood, because they have. Their camps are laid out in neat lines, their weapons are sharp and well kept, and their war-bands march in step behind standards.

A hobgoblin feels at home in the ranks the way other people feel at home by a fire. Shoulder to shoulder, shields locked, they fight as one body, and they see perfectly well in the dark. They despise goblins for being cowardly and orcs for being disorderly. Their neighbours despise them for being the most dangerous of the lot: an army, not a mob.

## Not to be confused with

*[goblin](goblinoid-and-orc-kind-goblin.md)*, the small, sneaky cousin who runs when it can; hobgoblins are disciplined soldiers who hold the line.

## What sets this species apart

Disciplined goblinoids with an instinct for fighting in ranks.
