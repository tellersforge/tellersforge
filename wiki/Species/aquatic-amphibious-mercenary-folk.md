# Amphibious mercenary folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md)*

Amphibian mercenaries with sticky, clinging limbs that grip like tentacles. They move equally well underwater and in the weightlessness of space, which makes them sought after for boarding ships, sabotage and fighting where others flounder. On a hull or a sea wall they can hang by a limb and fight with the others.

They are paid professionals and treat every war as a contract. Employers value them and never quite turn their backs on them.

## Not to be confused with

the *[amphibious humanoid folk](aquatic-amphibious-humanoid-folk.md)*, a broad group of water-world peoples; these are specialists in fighting where there is no up and down.

## What sets this species apart

Adhesive-limbed amphibians equally at home underwater and in zero gravity. Wrestling & Grappling is the adhesive, tentacle-like grip.
