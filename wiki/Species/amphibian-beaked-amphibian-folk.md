# Beaked amphibian folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md)*

Amphibians with a hard beak or bill instead of lips. Some have turtle-like faces and bulging eyes and live half in water, half out. A river imp of old tales wears a shell and carries a dish of water in a hollow on top of its head; spill the water and it loses its strength, which makes it strangely polite. Duck-billed folk trade cautiously and hate any risk they cannot price.

None of them leap like frogs. They waddle, swim and wait, and outsiders tend to underestimate them until they bite.

## Not to be confused with

the *[turtlefolk](reptilian-turtlefolk.md)*, reptiles with shells; beaked amphibians have moist skin and are at home in water.

## What sets this species apart

Beaked, shell- or bill-faced amphibians without frog legs, so no leaping edge.
