# Monstrous ungulates

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

Great beasts on hooves, with no hands and no thought but their own. The single-horned white beast that lets only the pure of heart come near, and whose horn is said to draw poison out of a cup. The bull-beast that defends itself with a spray of scalding dung. The primeval behemoth, so vast its tail sways like a tree. Armoured, horned brutes bred to fight in arenas for the crowd.

None of them speak, bargain or keep herds of their own; they are animals, however strange. Hunters want their horns and hides, lords want them in menageries, and farmers want them to go somewhere else. They cannot hold a weapon, but a charge from one needs no weapon at all.

## Not to be confused with

*[Beasts of burden](ungulate-beasts-of-burden.md)*, the tame livestock and mounts of settled peoples; monstrous ungulates are wild, strange and dangerous, and nobody harnesses them for long.

## What sets this species apart

Non-sentient, horned four-legged beasts with no hands and no herding of their own. Non-sentient beasts: social and craft skills do not apply. No hands: held weapons unusable.
