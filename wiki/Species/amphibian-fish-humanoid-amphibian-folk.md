# Fish-humanoid amphibian folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md)*

Amphibians who look more like fish than frogs: smooth or scaled skin, gill slits at the neck, large eyes and wide mouths. They breathe water and air alike and move comfortably in both, though they need to wet their skin now and then on land. They walk rather than leap.

Their cultures vary. Some are gentle philosophers who abhor violence. Some are nervous diplomats who panic easily. An ancient telepathic line claims to be older than humanity, and a proud line of pilots is known for calm and honour. Other peoples find their stare unnerving, since they rarely blink.

## Not to be confused with

the *[aggressive fish-folk](aquatic-aggressive-fish-folk.md)*, who are predators and raiders; these fish-like amphibians are generally calm and thoughtful.

## What sets this species apart

Gilled fish-like humanoids that breathe water fully but lack frog legs.
