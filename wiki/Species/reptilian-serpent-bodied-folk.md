# Serpent-bodied folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md)*

Snakes, large ones, and snake-like beings with no legs at all. Some are giant constrictors with no more thought than hunger. Some are clever serpents who speak softly and hold their prey still with a steady, hypnotic stare. Some are guardian spirits of pools and springs, who can take a human form when it suits them.

With no hands, they cannot hold a weapon, and with no legs they cannot run or leap well. They do not need to. They climb anything, move in near silence, and their coils can crush the breath out of a grown warrior. Most live in forests, marshes and ruins where they can lie in wait. Most peoples fear them on sight, and very few of those fears are foolish.

## Not to be confused with

the *[snake folk](serpentine-snake-folk.md)*, the serpent-headed peoples who walk on legs and use tools, where these creatures have no limbs at all.

## What sets this species apart

Legless serpent bodies: good climbers, poor runners and leapers, and unable to ride. Wrestling & Grappling is the constricting coils. No hands: held weapons unusable.
