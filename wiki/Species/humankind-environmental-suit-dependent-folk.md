# Environmental-suit-dependent folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

People who cannot breathe the air everyone else breathes. Away from their home world they live in masks, breathing tubes, sealed helmets or full suits, and they have grown so used to this that the suit feels like skin. Some are small and round inside pressure suits that would crush if punctured. Some wear eye-slits and breathing grilles and are quietly formidable. Some wander in patched suits on endless fleets, and take them off only in rooms they trust with their lives.

Others often mistake the mask for the person, and never see the face beneath. The suit-wearers know better than anyone that a rip in the wrong place is a death, and they tend to be very calm and very careful around sharp things.

## Not to be confused with

the *[adapted human](humankind-adapted-human.md)*, whose bodies were changed to survive; these people survive by what they wear.

## What sets this species apart

They cannot breathe or survive unprotected away from home, so living in a mask or suit is second nature. The Era block still applies: in an era without suits this skill is Foreign Concept. The suit itself is gear.
