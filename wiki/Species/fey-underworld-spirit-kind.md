# Underworld spirit-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Spirits of the border between the living and the dead. They are not ghosts, for they never lived, but they walk the grey ground where souls cross over and know its roads the way ferrymen know a river. They feel death coming the way others feel rain. Some can step inside a living body and speak with its mouth; others simply whisper to the grieving, or carry a last message across.

Old and binding treaties limit what they may do, and they keep those bargains to the letter and no further. Mourners seek them out for news of the departed, necromancers for help, and soldiers on the night before a battle for a hint of who will still be standing. They are rarely unkind. They are never comforting.

## Not to be confused with

*[ghost/spirit-kind](undead-and-deathless-ghost-spirit-kind.md)*, the restless dead themselves; underworld spirits were never alive, and they serve the boundary rather than haunting it.

## What sets this species apart

Spirits of the boundary of death who sense it and can possess the living.
