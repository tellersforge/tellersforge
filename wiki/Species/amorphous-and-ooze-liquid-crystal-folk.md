# Liquid-crystal folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

People made of shimmering liquid crystal. Their bodies hold and bend light, and their colours ripple constantly with their moods: blue calm, red anger, flickering pale confusion. They talk in patterns of light across their skin, and learning to understand them is like learning to read weather. They cannot hide a feeling, and have never seen much reason to.

They are beautiful, and fragile in one particular way: a sudden hard freeze makes them brittle enough to shatter. They avoid cold places and dislike sudden shocks of any kind, and tend to be gentle hosts to anyone who brings a lamp.

## Not to be confused with

the *[crystalline folk](mineral-and-crystalline-crystalline-folk.md)*, who are solid crystal; these people flow and change colour as they move.

## What sets this species apart

Crystal bodies that store and show light, speaking in light patterns and wearing their moods openly. Brittle if suddenly frozen.
