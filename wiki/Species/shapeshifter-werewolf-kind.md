# Werewolf-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

Wolf-folk who change when they wish and hide from nobody. In one form they are lean, keen-eyed men and women; in the other, a great grey or black wolf with a nose that can read a street like a book. They choose the moment of the change, and the wolf is theirs, not a curse to be endured.

They live openly in their own neighbourhoods and villages, governed by their own councils and bound by pack loyalty stronger than blood. They are proud, clannish and quick to close ranks against outsiders. Other peoples have learned to deal with them as neighbours, with the usual mix of trade, grudges and marriages. Nobody, however, borrows a werewolf's dog.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lycanthrope](shapeshifter-lycanthrope.md)* changes by curse and struggles with the beast; werewolf-kind shift freely into the wolf alone and live openly as a people.

## What sets this species apart

Living wolf-human shifters with one animal shape rather than free reshaping; they live openly, so no disguise culture. Wolf form only; Animal Form is limited to the wolf.
