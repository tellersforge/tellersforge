# Gull-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Loud, greedy and impossible to discourage. Gull-kind are seabirds that fly, float and scavenge along every shore, harbour and fishing boat. Individually, a gull can be a sharp companion: talkative, cheeky and happy to scout ahead from the air or report what is going on down the coast, in exchange for a share of whatever is being eaten.

They sleep on the waves, ride the wind for hours and eat anything they can snatch, from fish heads to a sailor's lunch. Harbour folk curse them, fishermen tolerate them and lonely castaways sometimes befriend them. A gull ally is loyal in its way, as long as it is fed, and never quite trustworthy around food.

## Not to be confused with

*[Corvid folk](avian-corvid-folk.md)*, clever scavengers of the fields and towns, where gulls work the shoreline and the sea.

## What sets this species apart

Seabirds that fly, float and scavenge along the shore.
