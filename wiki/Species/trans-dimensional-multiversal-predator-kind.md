# Multiversal predator-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Tall, skeletal hunters with too many teeth and a patience measured in centuries. They are native to no single world. They walk the gaps between parallel realities, following a scent no one else can smell: the particular life of beings bonded to a totem power. When they find one, they feed, not on flesh but on the bright force that animates it, and every such meal buys them another long stretch of years.

They are vastly stronger than any mortal, and they know it. Their manners are those of aristocrats at a banquet, which is how they see the many worlds. Those who survive an encounter describe the same thing: a polite voice, a cold hand, and the sense of being tasted. They are met as villains, never as friends.

## Not to be confused with

*[Totem-bonded dimension-kind](trans-dimensional-totem-bonded-dimension-kind.md)*, their favourite prey, who carry the power these hunters crave; and *[vampire-kind](undead-and-deathless-vampire-kind.md)*, who drain blood or life but stay in one world.

## What sets this species apart

Superhuman hunters who track their prey across worlds and feed on its life-force to prolong their own. NPC-scale. Lifespan (long-lived while it keeps feeding) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
