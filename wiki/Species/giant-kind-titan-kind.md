# Titan-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Giant-kind](../Species_Families/giant-kind.md)*

The first giants, older than the gods who now rule, and nearly gods themselves. Each holds some great part of creation in its keeping: the sky, the deep sea, the turning of time, the roots of mountains. When a titan stirs, weather changes and coastlines move. Most were overthrown long ago and bound beneath the earth, in chains of adamant or sleep, and the tremors of their dreaming are felt in the world above.

Others never fell. They claim to have shaped the early world and promise to perfect it again, in return for worship and sacrifice, and there are always those willing to believe them. Mortals rarely meet a titan and never on equal terms. To stand before one is to stand before a mountain that remembers the dawn.

## Not to be confused with

*[elder god-kind](planar-elder-god-kind.md)*, alien powers from beyond the world; titans are giants of this world's own dawn, bound to its sky, sea and time.

## What sets this species apart

Primordial deific giants of cosmic power. NPC-scale. Each titan's domain (sky, sea, time) is a Breed/Race power.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
