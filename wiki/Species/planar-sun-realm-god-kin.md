# Sun-realm god-kin

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

The gods of a river-and-desert pantheon under a blazing sun. Many wear the heads of animals: a falcon, a jackal, a lioness, an ibis. Others appear as crowned kings and queens in white linen and gold. All of them are immortal, far stronger than any mortal, and hard to wound.

Each rules a domain: the sun, the dead, magic, war, justice, the flooding river. Their powers flow from it, and their worshippers organise whole lives around their favour. They are older and more formal than many pantheons, with long rituals, fixed hierarchies and a deep concern with balance and the passage of souls. Mortals approach them through priests, and with offerings of bread, beer and gold.

## Not to be confused with

the *[mountain-realm god-kin](planar-mountain-realm-god-kin.md)*, a different immortal pantheon, more quarrelsome and less concerned with ritual and the dead.

## What sets this species apart

Immortal pantheon deities with superhuman strength and durability and a personal domain. The domain power is Set at Breed/Race or character level. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
