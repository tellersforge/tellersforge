# Television-head robot folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md)*

Humanoid robots with old-fashioned television sets for heads. Their faces are the screens, and what the screens show is how they feel: a smile, a scowl, a storm of static when angry, a blank grey when stunned. They cannot help it. Lies come hard to a creature whose every flicker of emotion is broadcast to the room.

They live in a kingdom of their own, ruled by royalty with larger, grander screens, while a working caste with small, simple sets labours in factories and marches as conscript soldiers. Other peoples find them fascinating to talk to and very hard to play cards against. They, in turn, find other peoples' blank, unreadable faces rather rude.

## Not to be confused with

*[Robot-citizen-kind](constructs-and-synthetics-robot-citizen-kind.md)*, humanlike robots who can keep a straight face, where these robots show every feeling on a screen.

## What sets this species apart

Robots whose screen-faces broadcast their feelings, making deceit harder.
