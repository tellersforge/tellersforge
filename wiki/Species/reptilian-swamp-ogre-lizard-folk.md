# Swamp ogre-lizard folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md)*

Huge, hunched, one-eyed lizard-ogres living in the deepest, foggiest bogs, where the air is thick and the water black. Their single great eye is sharp enough in the murk and poor at judging distance anywhere else, and they know their swamps as a fish knows water.

They are an ancient people and a failing one. Their blood grows thinner and stranger with each generation, and they keep their line alive through dark magic and dreadful bargains. They wield massive weapons with ease, and most who meet them never see them coming until a shadow rises out of the fog. Travellers in the marshes speak of them in low voices, and keep lanterns lit.

## Not to be confused with

the *[lizardfolk](reptilian-lizardfolk.md)*, who also live in swamps but see with two eyes and walk into dry country without trouble.

## What sets this species apart

One-eyed swamp ogre-lizards: deeper at home in fog-bound bogs, with narrower sight.
