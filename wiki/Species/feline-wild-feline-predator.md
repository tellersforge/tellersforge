# Wild feline predator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md)*

Not people, but beasts: wild cats that go on four legs and hunt by instinct. The smaller ones are nimble pouncers that stalk birds and rabbits through long grass, freeze at a sound and spring faster than the eye can follow. The larger ones are nightmare hunters, some with extra eyes that glitter in the dark, spiked tails and fangs longer than a dagger.

They have no hands and no tools. Their weapons are the ones they were born with: claws, teeth and patience. Some are hunted for their pelts, and some are captured young and trained to fight in the arenas of cruel masters, where crowds pay to watch them tear at prisoners. Travellers who hear a low cough in the brush at night do well to build the fire higher. A wild cat will rarely attack a group, but it will follow one for miles, waiting for someone to stray.

## Not to be confused with

*[clan wildcat folk](feline-clan-wildcat-folk.md)*, small cats with a language and a code; wild feline predators are beasts of instinct, not people.

## What sets this species apart

Four-legged wild cats with no hands, fighting with claws and fangs. No hands: held weapons unusable.
