# Energy-draining pteranodon-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md)*

Once a man, now a winged reptile with a man's cunning. Energy-draining pteranodon-kind are humans transformed by a mystical or mutant process into leathery-winged, long-beaked reptilian hybrids, somewhere between a person and a creature from the age of great lizards. They fly, they rend with claws, and they think in a sly, primitive way.

The change must be fed. To keep their shape, they drain the life from others, leaving victims pale and weak. They have no love of gold and no dragon's pride, only hunger and the fear of losing what they have become. Most are solitary, hunted and very hard to reason with.

## Not to be confused with

*[Serum-mutated bat-human](chiropteran-serum-mutated-bat-human.md)*, another human turned into a winged creature, but by a laboratory serum and without the draining hunger.

## What sets this species apart

A flying reptilian mutate that sustains itself by draining life energy, with no hoarding instinct.
