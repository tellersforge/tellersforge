# Ancient amphibian progenitor race

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md)*

An ancient people of huge, slow, toad-like sages who moved between the stars long before younger races had fire. They were masters of the craft of life itself and shaped other peoples for their purposes, so that many younger species carry their marks without knowing it. Their bodies are vast and patient; their minds are deep and powerful enough to be felt as pressure behind the eyes.

Most have gone. The few that remain sleep, think and plan across centuries. Those who meet one find the encounter humbling, and are left uneasy about what they themselves were made for.

## Not to be confused with

the *[celestial progenitor-kind](planar-celestial-progenitor-kind.md)*, divine makers of peoples; these are mortal, if ancient, scientists of flesh.

## What sets this species apart

Slow, ancient, psychically potent precursors. NPC-scale. Their gene-craft is knowledge (Culture/Profession), not a species line. Very long-lived.
