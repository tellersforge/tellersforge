# Weather spirit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Winged spirits of wild country who ride the edge of the storm. They are seen as pale, lovely figures in the rain, dancing on the wind above a forest or sitting on a crag while lightning walks the valley below. They call up hail against woodcutters who fell too many trees, and fog to hide a hunted deer. The same hands that bring the storm can close a wound or break a fever, and a traveller who treats their woods with respect may wake from a fall to find the bone already set.

They are fiercely loyal to the forests they guard and have long memories for insults. Shepherds and charcoal-burners know to keep their fires small, to ask before they take, and to stay off the high meadows when the clouds pile up.

## Not to be confused with

*[elemental spirit-kind](fey-elemental-spirit-kind.md)*, bound to a single element; weather spirits command the whole sky and also heal, and they belong to a forest rather than to the storm.

## What sets this species apart

Winged nature spirits who ride storms, command weather and heal.
