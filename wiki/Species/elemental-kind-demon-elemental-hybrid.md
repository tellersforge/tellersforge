# Demon-elemental hybrid

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md)*

They look like anyone else. A broad-shouldered stranger at the inn, a quiet old retainer, a scholar who has somehow been at the same court for three generations. The disguise is not a trick. Their human face is real, and they can wear it for a lifetime, which in their case is a very long time.

Underneath is something older. Pushed far enough, in terror or in fury, the body splits open into a shape of heat and claws, the skin glowing like coals, the hands lengthening into hooks. Those who have seen the change rarely describe it the same way twice. Most do not live to describe it at all. The hybrids come from an old bloodline in which something infernal and something elemental were bred together long ago, and they keep to the shadows of power: advisers, bodyguards, hidden masters. They are feared by those who know, and unsuspected by everyone else.

## Not to be confused with

*[infernal/demonic-kind](planar-infernal-demonic-kind.md)*, true fiends who never pass as human; these hybrids live as people and reveal the fire only in extremity.

## What sets this species apart

Long-lived human-passing beings who can take a terrifying fire-and-claw form. Lifespan (long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
