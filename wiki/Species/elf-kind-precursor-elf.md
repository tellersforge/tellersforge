# Precursor elf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

Elves who came first, rose highest and are mostly gone. Their ruins are the strange ones in the deep forest: white towers with no doors, halls lit by stones that still glow, machines of brass and crystal that still turn after every hand that built them has turned to dust. Some of these peoples mastered sorcery; others built with gears and steam and prayer until one day they simply vanished, all at once.

The few that remain carry an air of decline. One line is psychic and fragile, sailing vast ancient ships between the stars and dreading the end of its own story. Another is descended from travellers whose star-ship crashed long ago, and keeps its old technology hidden while the rest of its kin forget. Raiding offshoots prowl the fringes. Those who study the precursors learn one lesson above all: nothing, however great, lasts.

## Not to be confused with

*[elder alien civilization](unknown-humanoid-elder-alien-civilization.md)*, a vanished culture of any kind; precursor elves are specifically elven peoples who built and fell.

## What sets this species apart

No shared bodily difference from the family: what unites them is a vanished advanced civilization, which is culture and handled in the Culture step.
