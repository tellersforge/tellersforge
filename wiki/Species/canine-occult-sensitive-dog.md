# Occult-sensitive dog

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md)*

An ordinary dog, as far as anyone can tell. It eats, sleeps in the sun, chases cats and barks at the post. It also sees what humans cannot: the thing standing behind the wardrobe, the cold spot on the stair, the neighbour who is not really a neighbour. And it does something about it.

Occult-sensitive dogs have four legs, no hands and no speech their owners understand, but they have sharp noses, loyal hearts and, in some tales, secret societies that meet by night to guard the neighbourhood. Their humans generally never know. A dog that growls at an empty corner may simply be silly. Then again, it may not.

## Not to be confused with

*[Occult-sensitive cat](feline-occult-sensitive-cat.md)*, the feline equivalent, which sees the same things and usually decides they are someone else's problem.

## What sets this species apart

Ordinary four-legged dogs with no hands who can perceive the supernatural. No hands: held weapons unusable.
