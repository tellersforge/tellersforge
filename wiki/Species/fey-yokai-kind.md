# Yokai-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

A wide, untidy family of spirit-creatures from the folklore of island empires and misty mountain provinces. No two look alike. One is a paper lantern with a lolling tongue, another an old umbrella that hops, another a long-necked woman who stretches up to peer through upstairs windows, another a gentle house-spirit who brings luck to a kind family. They are united only by being spirits, and by a certain sly delight in unsettling people.

Most are predatory, at least a little. They lure travellers off mountain roads, drown fishermen, eat the unwary or simply frighten them half to death. A few are benevolent, if well treated. Every village has its own, every temple its protective charms, and every grandmother a story about the thing she saw by the well when she was a girl.

## Not to be confused with

*[fox spirit folk](shapeshifter-fox-spirit-folk.md)*, one well-known kind of shapeshifting spirit; this family is a grab-bag of very different spirit-creatures that share little beyond their strangeness.

## What sets this species apart

A catch-all of spirit-creatures too varied to share lines beyond the family's. Each yokai's innate powers are set at Breed/Race level by the GM.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
