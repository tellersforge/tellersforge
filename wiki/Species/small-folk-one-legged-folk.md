# One-legged folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Small folk](../Species_Families/small-folk.md)*

Odd little folk with a single great leg ending in a single enormous foot. They hop, and hop surprisingly fast, but they cannot run or climb. When it rains they lie on their backs and raise the foot like an umbrella; when they reach a river, the foot becomes a boat.

They are invisible unless something reveals them, and they spend much of their time squabbling among themselves, boasting and getting into foolish trouble. Travellers sometimes hear a thump-thump-thump on the road behind them and see nothing at all. Anyone who manages to make them visible will find them rude, very funny and deeply embarrassed at being seen.

## Not to be confused with

*[Tiny folk](humankind-tiny-folk.md)* are simply small humans; one-legged folk are a strange hopping people who stay unseen and cannot run or climb.

## What sets this species apart

A single great foot: they hop instead of running or climbing, and stay unseen until revealed.
