# Star-empire honor guard kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

People from a world so dense that everything there weighs many times what it should. Their bodies grew to match: packed muscle, bones like iron and skin that turns blades and flame. Off their home world they are almost untouchable, strong enough to throw starships and tough enough to walk through fire.

A great star empire recruits them as its elite guard, and they are proud of the role, standing still for hours in gleaming armour and ending wars in a single afternoon. Outsiders see a wall that walks. The guards see themselves as loyal servants of an old throne, and rarely ask whether it deserves them.

## Not to be confused with

the *[augmented/enhanced human](humankind-augmented-enhanced-human.md)*, who was changed after birth; these are born strong by the pull of their world.

## What sets this species apart

Heavy-world people of immense strength and near-invulnerability. NPC-scale.
