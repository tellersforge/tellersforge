# Long-lived demon-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

They look like people and they talk like people, better than most people. They say kind words at the right moment, cry at the right story, laugh at the right joke. It is all learned. Behind the warmth there is nothing at all but hunger, and the soft voice is the lure that brings the prey close.

They live very long and study humankind the way a fisher studies a river. Some have horns hidden under hats; some look perfectly ordinary. They are strong in magic and patient in the hunt, and they can go years in a village before anyone notices the people who went missing. Those who fight them learn one rule: no matter what the creature says, it does not mean it.

## Not to be confused with

the *[infernal/demonic-kind](planar-infernal-demonic-kind.md)*, who deceive for bargains and schemes, where these demons mimic human feeling only to lure and eat their prey.

## What sets this species apart

Ancient predators that mimic human speech and warmth perfectly without feeling either. Lifespan (long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
