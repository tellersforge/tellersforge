# Gas-bodied folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

People made of mist, cloud or vapour. The playable kind is a pale, loosely human drift of fog that can thin itself to slip under a door or through a crack and slowly gather again on the far side. Solid things are a struggle for them: lifting a cup is hard work, and grappling a foe is out of the question. A stiff wind or a hot sun can scatter them for a day.

Larger kin float in the upper air of gas giants, feeding on what drifts past, or wander between the stars as vast, dark, thinking clouds that talk in pulses no ear can hear. Solid folk rarely notice the smaller ones until one speaks from the fog beside them.

## Not to be confused with

the *[incorporeal spirit](energy-and-incorporeal-incorporeal-spirit.md)*, which has no body; gas-bodied folk do have one, made of vapour.

## What sets this species apart

Bodies of gas or vapour that float but can barely carry or handle solid things. Strong wind disperses them. A body of gas cannot strike or hold a foe.
