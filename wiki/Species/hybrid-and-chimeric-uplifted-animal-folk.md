# Uplifted animal folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

Ordinary animals given minds, speech and a new upright shape. A mutagen spilled into a sewer, a secret research programme, a lab accident or a surgeon's knife has taken a turtle, a pig, a raccoon or a crow and turned it into a person who can read, argue and hold a sword. The result keeps the animal's instincts underneath the new intelligence.

An uplifted cat still stalks. An uplifted dog still wants a pack. An uplifted turtle may never quite get used to being hurried. There is no single uplifted people, since each was raised from a different animal, and many are the only one of their kind. They often band together with others like them for company, and spend a great deal of time explaining that they are not pets.

## Not to be confused with

*[beast-tribe folk](hybrid-and-chimeric-beast-tribe-folk.md)*, humans with animal features; uplifted animals began as beasts and were raised into people.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference: each keeps the instincts of the animal it was raised from. Copy the base animal's family lines at Breed/Race level.
