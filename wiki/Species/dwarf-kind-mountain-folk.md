# Mountain-folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Dwarf-kind](../Species_Families/dwarf-kind.md)*

An ancient people, older than the dwarves and nearly gone. Mountain-folk are short, dense and grave, with eyes like polished stone and voices that carry through rock. They shape stone by singing to it. A mountain-folk mason hums, and a wall rises smooth and seamless; she sings, and a cliff opens into a hall. Their greatest works still stand, though few living people know who made them.

They live so long that they measure time by the growth of stalactites. Most have withdrawn into hidden halls deep beneath the peaks, emerging rarely and briefly. Those who meet one come away feeling very young, very hasty and very loud.

## Not to be confused with

*[Dwarf](dwarf-kind-dwarf.md)*, the younger smith and miner folk, who work stone with hammers rather than song.

## What sets this species apart

An ancient, very long-lived people who shape stone by singing to it. Earth Shaping stands for innate stone-singing. Lifespan (long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
