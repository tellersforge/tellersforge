# Living-statue predator kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

A stone figure with wings folded and hands over its face, weeping, standing in a cemetery or a ruined garden. It looks like any other memorial. Look away, even to blink, and it is closer. Look away again, and it is at your throat.

These predators can only move when nobody sees them. Watched, they are nothing but stone, and cannot be hurt any more than a statue can. Unwatched, they move faster than thought. They do not kill in the ordinary way: a touch sends the victim into the past, stranded years or decades back, and the creature feeds on the life they would have had. People who know what they are keep their eyes open, walk backwards, and never, ever blink together.

## Not to be confused with

the *[animated stone guardian](mineral-and-crystalline-animated-stone-guardian.md)*, which watches over a building and moves freely, where this predator hunts anyone and freezes the moment it is seen.

## What sets this species apart

Stone predators that move with terrible speed whenever no one is watching. NPC-scale. Time Travel works only on a touched victim, sending them into the past. Frozen while observed (a flaw).
