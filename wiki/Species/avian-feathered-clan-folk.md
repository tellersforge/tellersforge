# Feathered clan folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Solid, golden-skinned people dusted with fine feathers, with none of the light hollow bones of true fliers. Feathered clan folk have no wings and do not miss them. They are sturdy, handsome and extremely conscious of it, because appearance, like everything else in their lives, reflects on their clan.

Their culture is a tangle of honour, obligation and old grudges. Every individual belongs to associations and rivalries that decide whom they may trade with, marry or insult, and a personal slight can become a political crisis. Their technology is advanced and closely guarded. Outsiders find them courteous, proud and almost impossible to read, and learn quickly never to confuse one clan with another.

## Not to be confused with

*[Generic bird-humanoid folk](avian-generic-bird-humanoid-folk.md)*, the broad cast of other bird peoples, most of whom still have wings or beaks.

## What sets this species apart

Feathered but wingless, solidly built humanoids with none of the hollow-boned frailty. Human-like faces: no beak.
