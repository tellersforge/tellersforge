# Higher-dimensional beings

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Beings from a place with more directions than ours. What we see of them is only a slice: a shape that swells and shrinks and changes colour as it moves through our world, like a hand pushed through the surface of a pond. They perceive the inside of a locked box as easily as its outside, and they walk through closed rooms as if every door stood open.

They have no hands in any sense we would recognise, and their thoughts run along directions we have no words for. Some are near-omnipotent, some are strange keepers of time, and all of them are nearly impossible to understand. Those who try often come away with headaches, nosebleeds or a new religion.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lower-dimensional beings](trans-dimensional-lower-dimensional-beings.md)* have fewer directions than we do and are baffled by our world; higher-dimensional beings have more, and find our walls meaningless.

## What sets this species apart

They perceive and move through extra dimensions, passing through closed spaces as if open. NPC-scale. Near-omnipotent breeds add their powers at breed level. No hands: held weapons unusable.
