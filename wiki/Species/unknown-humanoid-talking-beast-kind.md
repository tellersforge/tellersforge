# Talking-beast-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md)*

A fox who argues philosophy, a badger who keeps accounts, a horse with opinions about the road. Talking beasts are ordinary animals granted true speech and reason, by a creator's word, a shared law, or some blessing no one remembers. Their bodies are exactly what they seem: fur, feathers, hooves and paws, with all the strengths and troubles that come with them.

So are their instincts. A talking hare is still wary of hawks, a talking dog still loves to run, and a talking wolf still has to think hard about the sheep. They are persons, with names, families and grudges, living alongside their dumb kin and their two-legged neighbours. Most are proud of their speech, and very few will carry anyone's pack for free.

## Not to be confused with

*[Uplifted animal folk](hybrid-and-chimeric-uplifted-animal-folk.md)*, animals given reason by deliberate science; talking beasts were given speech by some older gift, and keep their plain animal bodies.

## What sets this species apart

Ordinary animals given speech; the body is whatever animal it is. At Breed level, copy lines from the matching animal family. Copy the matching animal's weapon lines and natural attacks too.
