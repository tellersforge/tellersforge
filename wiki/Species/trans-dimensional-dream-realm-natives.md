# Dream-realm natives

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Beings who live in the land of sleep. Their home is a shared dreamscape whose shape and laws bend with each dreamer's mind, and they move through it as fish move through water. From there, they can slip into any sleeper's dream, turn it sweet or turn it to nightmare.

Some are drifting, curious creatures of mist and colour. Others are predators that feed on fear, or hollow mimics that pose as loving parents to lure a child through a small door. The worst can drag a sleeper bodily into the dream, or step out of it themselves. People who know of them sleep with charms, lights and prayers, and teach children never to follow a smiling stranger in a dream.

## Not to be confused with

*[Nightmare spirit-kind](undead-and-deathless-nightmare-spirit-kind.md)* are undead spirits of dread; dream-realm natives are the living folk of the dream dimension, bound to dreams rather than death.

## What sets this species apart

Natives of the dream dimension who can enter sleepers' dreams and nightmares.
