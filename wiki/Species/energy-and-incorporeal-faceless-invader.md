# Faceless invader

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

Humanoid in shape, with arms, legs and a head, but nothing on that head. No eyes, no mouth, no nose, just a smooth, pale or grey surface that turns toward you as if it can see perfectly well. They are solid enough to grip a weapon and wear armour, and they fight in silent, relentless ranks.

They came, in the old accounts, from somewhere beyond, the vanguard of a formless host that conquers by consuming. What they touch they take into themselves: stone crumbles into their fingers, metal thins, flesh withers and is gone. Whole cities have simply been eaten. They do not negotiate or explain. Warriors who have faced them say that the worst part is the silence. There is no battle cry, no scream of pain, only the soft sound of the world being absorbed.

## Not to be confused with

*[energy being (generic)](energy-and-incorporeal-energy-being-generic.md)*, which has no body at all; faceless invaders are solid, handed flesh that devours matter by touch.

## What sets this species apart

Solid featureless humanoids who absorb and consume matter. Disintegrate is by touch, as consuming. Solid, with hands: held weapons are usable despite the family's no-hands note.
