# Nanite-swarm folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

A cloud of machines too small to see, trillions of them, acting as one. The swarm can take any shape it needs: a person to talk to, a bridge to cross, a hand to pick a lock, a tool, a wall. Scatter it and it drifts back together elsewhere. Its body is its only equipment and its only home.

Its mind lives in the whole, not in any part, and it thinks in patterns strange to fleshly people. Most folk find it unsettling, the way a room full of dust would be unsettling if the dust started to pay attention.

## Not to be confused with

the *[self-replicating machine kind](constructs-and-synthetics-self-replicating-machine-kind.md)*, whose members are separate machines that copy themselves; the swarm is one mind in many tiny parts.

## What sets this species apart

A swarm of tiny machine units that can take any shape, scatter and reassemble. The swarm is the body itself, not worn gear.
