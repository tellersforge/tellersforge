# Crystalline folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

People made of living crystal rather than rough stone. Their skin is translucent and faceted, with a cool glow running along veins beneath it, and when they step into sunlight they throw colour across the walls around them. Some can bend that light on purpose, shading themselves into a blur or painting a small false image in the air.

Crystal is hard but not tough. A blade struggles to cut them, poison has little to work on, and they live a long time on a slow, even pulse of life, but a heavy blow can crack them like glass. They are known as makers of beautiful things: architects, gem-cutters, sculptors and designers of halls that catch the dawn. Others find them lovely to look at and a little cold to talk to.

## Not to be confused with

the *[stone folk](mineral-and-crystalline-stone-folk.md)*, rock-bodied people who are dense, rough and hard to break, where the crystalline folk are sharper, brighter and more brittle.

## What sets this species apart

Faceted crystal rather than rough stone: their bodies catch and bend light. Minor Illusion is affinity for light refraction; lines that refract innately Set it at Breed/Race level.
