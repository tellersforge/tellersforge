# Wolf-shifter kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md)*

Born human, and mostly human. Wolf-shifter kind look like anyone else, with ordinary human hands, until something wakes the wolf: a full moon, a surge of rage, the scent of an old enemy. Then they change, into a huge wolf or a hulking half-wolf with claws and teeth, stronger and far keener-nosed than any man. In beast shape they track by scent across whole forests and heal wounds that would kill a human.

Some lines are hereditary and tribal, bound into packs that guard their lands. Others are cursed, the change spreading by a bite. Both are feared, both are hunted, and both know that their neighbours watch the moon closely.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lycanthrope](shapeshifter-lycanthrope.md)*, the wider shifter kind of any beast, where wolf-shifters belong to the dog family and change only into wolves.

## What sets this species apart

Human-born shifters with human hands in their normal form and an innate change into wolf or wolf-humanoid shape. The family's senses and tracking are at full strength in beast form; the GM may treat Enhanced Senses as 1 in human form.
