# Multiversal overseer-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Watchers from outside everything. Each of these tall, still beings stands beyond the edge of the multiverse and keeps watch over a single universe, the way a keeper tends a lighthouse. They are vastly powerful and almost entirely without feeling, and they speak of stars and nations as a farmer speaks of weather.

They rarely interfere. When they do, it is for reasons of balance that mortals seldom understand and never enjoy. Some of their kind have turned: counterparts from a dying opposite universe who devour others to save their own. The rest watch on, patient and cold, and anyone who meets one comes away feeling very small, and perhaps observed.

## Not to be confused with

*[Divine overseer-kind](planar-divine-overseer-kind.md)* are gods who watch over mortal faith; multiversal overseer-kind stand outside the multiverse altogether and watch whole universes without caring.

## What sets this species apart

Watchers from outside the multiverse who observe whole universes with near-total detachment. NPC-scale.
