# Goblin-bird mountain spirit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Mountain spirits with the look of men and the stamp of birds: some wear beaked, feathered faces, others a long red nose and a proud scowl. They fly on broad wings among the peaks and pine forests, know every ridge and crevasse, and treat the high places as their own household. Travellers who lose their way up there may be guided home, or led off a cliff, depending on their manners.

They are famous as swordsmen and teachers of fighting arts, taking on the occasional gifted, stubborn student from the valleys. They are also vain, touchy and quick to punish arrogance, which is ironic, since no one is prouder than they are.

## Not to be confused with

*[Crane folk](avian-crane-folk.md)*, who also teach a fighting art, but are mortal marsh birds rather than spirits of the peaks.

## What sets this species apart

Winged mountain spirits that fly and know the heights. Swordsmanship is training (Profession step and weapon groups).
