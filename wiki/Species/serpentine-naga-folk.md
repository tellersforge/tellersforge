# Naga folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md)*

Serpent guardians of deep water and hidden wealth. A naga may have a human head and shoulders above a long scaled tail, or be a great hooded serpent throughout, but either way it carries itself like royalty. They make their homes in lakes, springs, flooded temples and the wells of old cities, and they guard what lies there: treasure, sacred water or forgotten knowledge.

They live a very long time, and most are born with a little magic in them, enough to charm, confuse or ward a doorway. Some can take a fully human shape when they wish to walk among others. They are proud, patient and slow to forgive, and people who live near them leave offerings at the water's edge, just in case.

## Not to be confused with

*[Snake folk](serpentine-snake-folk.md)* are ordinary serpent people; naga folk are long-lived water guardians with a natural gift for magic and a regal manner to match.

## What sets this species apart

Long-lived serpent guardians of waters with innate spellcraft. The minor innate spellcasting is chosen at Breed/Race or character level. Lifespan (long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
