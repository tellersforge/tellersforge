# Long-lived/ascended human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Humans who were not given the usual span, or who stepped out of it. Some live for centuries and go silver-haired while still young. Some regrow a new body when the old one fails, with a new face and the same memories. Some are kept young by a strange light in their sky, and dare not change anything about their lives in case it fades. A few ancient peoples outgrew flesh altogether and left behind only their gates and ruins. A few others made themselves ageless by ritual and became cold judges or warm teachers of younger races.

Long life changes a people. They plan in generations, hold grudges for longer than empires last, and can seem patient, wise or insufferable depending on who is waiting for an answer. Their shorter-lived kin, including the ones whose years were cut rather than stretched, rarely forgive them for it.

## Not to be confused with

the *[celestial-engineered human](humankind-celestial-engineered-human.md)*, who is long-lived as well but was designed to channel cosmic power; these are simply humans who last.

## What sets this species apart

Humans whose lifespan is stretched far beyond the ordinary. Lifespan (long-lived; near-immortal in some breeds, shortened in the accelerated-aging splinter and the mortal precursor ancestors) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
