# Civic bird folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Birds in coats and hats, walking upright through rain-slick streets under flickering lamps. Civic bird folk are full citizens of crowded modern cities, where their feathers, beaks and bright eyes pass without comment among other animal peoples. They drink coffee, file paperwork, argue cases and take bribes like everyone else.

In the stories told about them, they tend to be clerks, lawyers, academics and shady officials: people of words and documents rather than muscle. Some are honest. Many are not. Their bodies are ordinary for bird folk; what sets them apart is the city that raised them, with its politics, its secrets and its long shadows.

## Not to be confused with

*[Generic bird-humanoid folk](avian-generic-bird-humanoid-folk.md)*, the many bird peoples of empires and wilderness, where civic bird folk are creatures of the modern city.

## What sets this species apart

Anthropomorphic city birds; their differences are cultural and handled in the Culture step.
