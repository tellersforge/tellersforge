# Winged sky human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Humans with wings. Winged sky humans are descended from ordinary people but carry a pair of working feathered wings on their backs, strong enough for real flight. They live on islands of cloud and stone high above the sea, where the ground ends at a sheer drop and every child learns to fly before it learns to swim.

Their cultures are tribal and warlike, shaped by scarce land and old enemies. Many guard the ruins of those who came before, and remember an older, more fully winged ancestor race that has mostly vanished into legend. To groundlings they look like angels. To each other, they look like neighbours with a grudge.

## Not to be confused with

*[Angel-winged alien kind](avian-angel-winged-alien-kind.md)*, who look much the same but are strangers from the stars, far stronger, and not human at all.

## What sets this species apart

Human-descended people with working feathered wings. Human heads: no beak.
