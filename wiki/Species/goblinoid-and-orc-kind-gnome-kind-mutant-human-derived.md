# Gnome-kind (mutant human-derived)

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Goblinoid and orc-kind](../Species_Families/goblinoid-and-orc-kind.md)*

Twisted human stock, bent out of shape by the poison of some ancient cataclysm. Generations after the world ended, they live in caves and wild places, short and gnarled, grey-green of skin and quick of hand. Some are cunning scavengers of the old ruins. Some band together as brutal raiders, carrying crude weapons and a fierce hatred of the settled peoples who drove them out.

They are tribal, superstitious and fiercely practical. Many hire out as mercenaries to whoever pays, since loyalty is a luxury. Their folk tales remember a time when their ancestors were ordinary people, though the tales disagree about who is to blame for what happened. Settled folk fear them and set dogs on them. The mutant gnomes remember every dog.

## Not to be confused with

*[gnome-kind](small-folk-gnome-kind.md)* of the Small folk family, the tinkering little people of burrow and hill; these mutant gnomes are post-cataclysm human stock, raiders rather than craftsmen.

## What sets this species apart

Mutated-human raiders who fit the family as it stands.
