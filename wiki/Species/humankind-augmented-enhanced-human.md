# Augmented/enhanced human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Humans pushed past human limits by something done to them or to their ancestors. Some were changed by alchemy as children and came out quicker, harder and colder, fit to hunt monsters and unwelcome in the villages they protect. Some come from warrior empires that bred themselves for greatness, or from peoples who draw astonishing strength from the light of a yellow sun and lose it under a red one. Others drink a village potion before a fight, or learn a way of breathing that burns their years for speed, or were soaked in radiation and now grow huge when angry.

What they share is a body primed for more than nature gave it. What they rarely share is peace: the enhanced are feared, recruited, envied and hunted in roughly equal measure, and many would trade their gift for a quiet life.

## Not to be confused with

the *[bioengineered/cloned human](humankind-bioengineered-cloned-human.md)*, which is built to a design from the start; the enhanced were ordinary stock that something later raised.

## What sets this species apart

Human stock altered by science, magic or alien biology, which leaves the body primed to take on superhuman strength. Affinity only. Each Breed/Race Sets its actual enhancements; some (red-sun colonists, the human-passing caste, potion villagers without the potion) have none.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
