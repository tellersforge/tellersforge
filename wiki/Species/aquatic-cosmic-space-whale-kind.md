# Cosmic space-whale kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md)*

Whales of the void: vast, slow, ancient beings that swim through the space between stars. They sing, and their songs carry across unimaginable distances and bend the way ahead, so that they can find paths no ship can find alone. They have no hands and need none. Their minds are old, strange and deep.

For this reason they have been hunted and enslaved, harnessed to ships and forced to sing the way for their captors. Free ones are wary and rarely approach other peoples. Sailors of the stars still speak of hearing their songs through the hull, and of the silence afterwards.

## Not to be confused with

the *[aquatic monster-kind](aquatic-aquatic-monster-kind.md)*, great beasts of earthly waters; space whales are thinking beings who swim the dark between stars.

## What sets this species apart

Vast whales of the void that cross the stars by psychic song. NPC-scale. Flight is through space. No hands: held weapons unusable.
