# Proto-dragon serpent

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md)*

A great serpent of rivers and deep caves, long as a longship and thick as an oak, waiting out the ages until it becomes a true dragon. Its scales are dark as wet slate, its eyes old and heavy-lidded. It has no hands and no venom, only strength, size and a very long memory.

Most of its life is spent coiled in a river pool or a flooded cavern, guarding the water and brooding over the day of its ascension. Some of these serpents are gentle and wise, others bitter at the waiting. Villagers downstream tell stories of a serpent who failed its rising and turned cruel, and they are careful to be polite to anything large moving under the river.

## Not to be confused with

*[True dragons](draconic-true-dragons.md)* have already made the change this serpent is still waiting for; the proto-dragon serpent is huge but wingless, with nothing of a dragon's fire or magic yet.

## What sets this species apart

Giant, very long-lived river serpents awaiting ascension; not venomous. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number. No hands: held weapons unusable.
