# Ancient manipulator energy being

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

Very old, very patient, and very rarely seen as they truly are. Beneath the elaborate suits they wear in the company of others, these beings are pure energy, a light so intense that those who glimpse it speak of angels. They choose that effect with care. Each people that meets them sees a different face: a winged messenger here, a figure from ancient myth there, always the image that race has been taught to revere.

They are the gardeners of younger civilizations, and like all gardeners they prune. Through prophecy, religion and cryptic advice they steer whole species along paths chosen ages ago, toward ends they rarely explain. Their words come in riddles and half-truths. Their allies are never sure whether they are being helped or used, and in the long run the answer is usually both.

## Not to be confused with

*[ascended energy being](energy-and-incorporeal-ascended-energy-being.md)*, which demands open worship; these manipulators hide behind masks and steer peoples quietly.

## What sets this species apart

Ancient energy beings who reach into minds and show each people a different face. Their encounter suits are gear. NPC-scale.
