# Beast-touched shifter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

Humans marked by the beast in a world where old magic came back. Some woke one morning with claws in place of nails, a pelt down the spine, ears that heard the neighbours' whispers, or a nose that could follow a scent across a city. They did not change shape and never will. The beast simply moved in and stayed.

Most are ordinary people trying to live with an unordinary body: hiding their hands in gloves, their eyes behind dark glasses, their sense of smell behind a polite smile. Some embrace it, working as trackers, guards or street fighters. Others face suspicion, prejudice and the constant question of what they will turn into next. The honest answer is nothing, but few believe it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lycanthrope](shapeshifter-lycanthrope.md)* changes into a full beast; the beast-touched shifter never changes at all, but lives every day with fixed animal traits.

## What sets this species apart

Humans carrying fixed beast traits (claws, keen senses) rather than true shape-changing.
