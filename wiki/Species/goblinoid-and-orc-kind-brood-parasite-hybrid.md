# Brood-parasite hybrid

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Goblinoid and orc-kind](../Species_Families/goblinoid-and-orc-kind.md)*

The children of a slow, hidden invasion. Some alien parasite stock breeds with humans, and the offspring are human in shape but not in loyalty. The first generation is nearly impossible to spot: a little pale, oddly bald, strangely devoted to a secret cult. Each generation after strays further. By the fourth there are extra arms, patches of chitin, clawed hands and a curling tail, and the creature has plainly stopped being a person.

They are strong, fast and fanatical. They hide in mines, slums and factories, pass for human as long as they can, and work toward a day when their true masters arrive from the stars. Governments that discover one of these cults tend to burn a whole district to be sure of it.

## Not to be confused with

*[hybrid stock insectoid folk](insectoid-and-arachnid-hybrid-stock-insectoid-folk.md)*, who are the final, almost wholly alien stage of the same kind of mixing; brood hybrids span the whole road from human to monster.

## What sets this species apart

Human-alien hybrids whose differences grow with each generation. Mutation degree (from passing as human to extra limbs and chitin) is set per Breed/Race.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
