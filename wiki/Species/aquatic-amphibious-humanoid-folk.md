# Amphibious humanoid folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md)*

A crowd of distinct water-world peoples who live between sea and shore. Salmon-coloured shipwrights with high domed heads and large eyes that see as well underwater as in air. Blunt shark-headed folk with grey hides and short tempers. Blue-skinned people whose head-tresses feel the water around them. Whale-bodied pilots who are surprisingly nimble for their size. Deep-ocean colonists with glowing patches on their skin, gentle river farmers with fish heads, cold-adapted tusked fisherfolk, and a vanished ancient people who left living machines on an ocean world.

They have little in common but water and the knowledge of how to live with it. Each people is certain its own way is the right one.

## Not to be confused with

the *[near-human alien](humankind-near-human-alien.md)*, which is a crowd of dry-land peoples; these are water-world peoples at home in both sea and air.

## What sets this species apart

A grouping of distinct water-world peoples; their differences are cultural or breed-level, so the family lines cover the species.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
