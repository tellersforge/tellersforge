# Herald-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

They were ordinary once, members of any race at all, until something vast chose them. A cosmic power that devours worlds needs a herald to find its meals, and it remakes that herald from the inside out. The flesh becomes something like living metal or light. The herald can fly between stars, survive the heart of a sun and unleash energy enough to crack a moon.

The price is service. A herald goes ahead of its master, finds worlds rich enough to feed on and announces their doom. Some serve willingly, having bargained to spare their own homeworld. Some serve in despair. A few rebel and are hunted for it. Their lives stretch on far beyond natural span, and most spend them alone, gleaming against the dark. Every culture that has seen one arrive has a word for it, and the word always means the end is near.

## Not to be confused with

*[cosmic celestial being](planar-cosmic-celestial-being.md)*, a vast power in its own right; the herald was a mortal remade to serve such a power.

## What sets this species apart

Solid bodies remade into living conduits of cosmic power: flight, vast energy and near-invulnerability. NPC-scale. Solid, with hands: held weapons are usable despite the family's no-hands note.
