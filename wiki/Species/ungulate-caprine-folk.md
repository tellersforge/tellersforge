# Caprine folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

Goat- and ram-folk, horned and bearded, with narrow hooves that find grip on ledges where a climber would need a rope. They are at home on cliffs and scree, hardy in body and hardier in stomach: a caprine traveller can live on thorns, bark and whatever the cook has thrown out. A lowered head and a sudden butt settle many an argument.

They are known for stubbornness, which they call conviction. Herders and mountain villagers make up most of their number, with thick wool against the cold and a strong sense of the flock. In the old woods live their merrier cousins, goat-legged pipers and revellers with a touch of wild magic. In wilder lands, goat-headed raiders come down from the hills to burn and plunder.

## Not to be confused with

*[Satyr/faun-kind](fey-satyr-faun-kind.md)*, the fey revellers of the woods; caprine folk are mortal goat- and ram-folk, and most keep flocks rather than feasts.

## What sets this species apart

Horned, sure-footed goats and rams at home on cliffs.
