# Serum-mutated bat-human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Chiropteran](../Species_Families/chiropteran.md)*

Once a human, now something else. A serum-mutated bat-human was made in a laboratory: a man or woman given an experimental drug, perhaps to cure deafness, perhaps to make a soldier, perhaps by accident. The result is a hulking, clawed creature with leathery wings, huge ears, a snout and senses so sharp it hears a heartbeat through a wall. It finds its way by echoes and hunts from the air.

What remains of the person inside depends on the dose and the luck. Some keep their memories and fight for control; others are little more than animals. All of them are alone, and none of them asked for it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bat folk](chiropteran-bat-folk.md)*, a born people with a culture of their own, where the bat-human is a single made thing of the laboratory.

## What sets this species apart

A human mutated into a clawed bat-creature with heightened senses, not a cave-born people.
