# Meteor-borne rock being

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

They arrive in fire. A stone falls out of the sky, cools in its crater, and a day later it cracks open like an egg and something climbs out. The thing is humanoid and huge, made of living rock, and whatever kind of stone it is made from decides what it can do. One of basalt burns, one of granite shakes the ground, one of crystal throws light, one of wet clay seeps and floods.

They come to conquer, and they have little personality beyond that. Each is a single-minded champion of its kind, and several may land at once in a kind of contest to see who takes the world first. Folk who find a warm, strange meteorite in a field would do well to leave it alone.

## Not to be confused with

the *[molten rock-being kind](mineral-and-crystalline-molten-rock-being-kind.md)*, which lives in the world's deep magma and is always of fire, where these invaders fall from the sky and share whatever element their stone holds.

## What sets this species apart

Rock invaders each tied to the element of the stone it is made from. NPC. The element follows the rock type; name it per individual.
