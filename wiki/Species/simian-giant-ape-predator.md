# Giant ape predator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md)*

A huge ape-shaped beast of the dense forests, covered in coarse fur, with long arms, a jaw full of fangs and a bellow that silences every bird for a mile. It is not a person. It does not speak, trade or make tools; it hunts.

Its favourite prey is the small folk who share its forest, and whole villages are built high in the trees, ringed with traps and warning drums, to keep it out. It is cunning in the way a predator is cunning: it learns paths, waits by water and remembers where the easy food is. Too heavy to leap or tumble, it simply smashes through whatever is in its way.

## Not to be confused with

*[Great ape folk](simian-great-ape-folk.md)* are thinking people; the giant ape predator is a mindless beast of the forest, far larger and entirely hungry.

## What sets this species apart

A huge non-sentient forest predator: a hunter and a terror, too heavy to tumble. Non-sentient beast: social and craft skills do not apply.
