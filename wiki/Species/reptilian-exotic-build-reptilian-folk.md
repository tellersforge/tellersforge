# Exotic-build reptilian folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md)*

Reptile peoples whose bodies follow no familiar plan. One walks on a single leg and gestures with four arms, its moods rippling across colour patches on its skin. One moves on six limbs and carries the pride of an ancient star lineage. One sits on a broad, skirted base that rolls like a wheel, waving tentacles, while another has a four-armed scholar's torso on the long body of a snake.

They have scales and cold blood in common, and not much else. Most come from distant worlds, belong to old and settled civilisations, and are used to other peoples staring. Their manners tend to be formal. They know they look strange, and they are usually far too polite to say the same about you.

## Not to be confused with

the *[multi-limbed folk](humankind-multi-limbed-folk.md)*, near-humans with extra arms, where these are scaled reptile peoples with wholly unfamiliar body plans.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference: each has its own unusual body plan (five limbs, six limbs, wheeled base, serpent lower body), handled at Breed/Race level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
