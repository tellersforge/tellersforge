# Uplifted great ape

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md)*

Gorillas raised to full, sharp intelligence, and then some. They are enormous and immensely strong, with the broad shoulders and gentle eyes of their wild cousins, but their minds are keen enough to run a city and argue philosophy. Their home is hidden, advanced beyond anything outsiders have seen and very private.

Many of them carry a latent gift for reaching other minds, a faint hum of thought that a few learn to turn into true telepathy. It makes them patient listeners and uncomfortable enemies. They are proud of what they have built, wary of the outside world and not above disguising their power. A visitor who mistakes them for beasts will not make that mistake twice.

## Not to be confused with

*[Great ape folk](simian-great-ape-folk.md)* are natural apes; the uplifted great ape was raised to its intellect and carries a hidden gift for telepathy.

## What sets this species apart

Uplifted gorillas: heavy great-ape build with a latent gift for telepathy. Latent psionics is affinity; the power itself comes by training or awakening.
