# Small burrow-dwelling kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md)*

Small, furry, round-faced folk who live in warm caves and burrows deep in the rock, reached by tunnels too narrow for anything bigger. Their fur comes in every colour, their tails are tufted, and their faces seem made for laughing. They sing a great deal, dance a great deal, and hold festivals for almost any reason.

Their underground homes are busy, communal places where everyone knows everyone and nobody locks a door. They share their caves with tiny builders and live uneasily near larger creatures beyond the tunnel mouth. They are curious, playful, and less timid than they look. Others find them joyful company, and sometimes wonder how any work gets done.

## Not to be confused with

the *[rat-kind](rodent-rat-kind.md)*, who scavenge sewers and city ruins, where these folk keep to their own cheerful cave burrows.

## What sets this species apart

Cave-burrow dwellers rather than ruin scavengers.
