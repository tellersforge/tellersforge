# Ritual/curse shapeshifter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

Beings whose changes come from curse, ritual or a dark spirit nature. One might wear the skin of a wolf, an owl or a black hare, and become it; another might take the face of a trusted neighbour to walk through a village unseen; the most dreadful take forms that are neither beast nor person, but something worse.

They hunt by night and keep their secrets well. Some are sorcerers who bought the power with unspeakable rites, some are demons that were never human at all, and a few are strange tricksters, as likely to help a lost traveller as to drown him. People speak of them in lowered voices, if at all, and guard their homes with salt, iron and holy things.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lycanthrope](shapeshifter-lycanthrope.md)* is locked to a single beast; the ritual or curse shapeshifter takes many animal and monstrous forms and wears human faces as disguises.

## What sets this species apart

Beings of curse, ritual or spirit nature who take animal and monstrous forms as well as human disguises.
