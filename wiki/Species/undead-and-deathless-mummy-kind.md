# Mummy-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

The dead who were prepared for eternity, and got it. Their bodies were dried, packed with salts and spices, wrapped in linen and laid in tombs with care. Now they walk again. Dry lands suit them: heat and thirst that would kill the living barely trouble their leathery flesh. Damp and fire are another matter.

Every mummy carries a curse, bound into it at burial or earned in the long dark since. Some are sorcerer-kings who never meant to give up their thrones and still seek youth and power. Some are silent guardians, slow and relentless, whose touch spreads rot and sickness to anyone who disturbs their rest. And some are whole peoples, calm and courteous, who embalm themselves by choice and honour ancestors who never quite left.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lich-kind](undead-and-deathless-lich-kind.md)*, sorcerers who chose undeath by ritual; the mummy's undeath comes from burial rites and the curse laid on its tomb.

## What sets this species apart

Dried, embalmed bodies that endure dry lands and carry a curse-bound nature.
