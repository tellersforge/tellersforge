# Water-bodied folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

Living water held in a shape by will. The playable kind stands like a clear blue person with rippling edges, walks into a lake and swims out the far side, pours itself under a door and stands up again. Scatter one and it gathers itself back together. Throw one at a fire and the fire loses. Frost makes them stiff and slow, and a hot, dry wind leaves them faint and shrunken.

There are stranger kin: storm-born drizzle-folk who step out of rain clouds and vanish in the sun, and whole oceans that think slowly and speak through their currents, conjuring the faces of lost loved ones from a visitor's memory. Solid peoples tend to find them soothing, and a little unnerving to share a bath with.

## Not to be confused with

the *[water spirit-kind](aquatic-water-spirit-kind.md)*, spirits of particular springs and rivers; water-bodied folk are made of water, wherever they happen to go.

## What sets this species apart

Bodies of living water: at home swimming, reform after being scattered, but dry heat evaporates them. Element is water. Vulnerable to freezing and evaporation.
