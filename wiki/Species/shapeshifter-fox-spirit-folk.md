# Fox spirit folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

Fox spirits who wear human faces. In one shape they are a russet fox with a white-tipped brush; in the other, a person of unsettling beauty and quick, amused eyes. As they grow older they grow more tails, and the oldest trail a fan of them behind like smoke, visible only to those who know how to look.

They are masters of illusion. A fox spirit can make leaves look like coins, a ruin look like a palace, and a stranger look like an old friend. Some use the gift to reward kindness and punish greed; others simply enjoy the game of charming and tricking mortals. Every village story about a too-perfect bride or a traveller who vanished at dawn has a fox spirit somewhere in it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Fox spirit](canine-fox-spirit.md)* is the canine family's fox-shaped spirit; fox spirit folk are the shapeshifter kin who live half their lives in human form and weave illusions as their chief art.

## What sets this species apart

Fox spirits who move between fox and human shape and weave illusions to charm and trick.
