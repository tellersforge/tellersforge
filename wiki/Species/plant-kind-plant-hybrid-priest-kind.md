# Plant-hybrid priest kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md)*

At first glance, ordinary people: priests in green robes, calm and soft-spoken. Look closer and something is off. Their skin has a faint green tinge, their veins run a little too dark, their hair is too much like fine moss, and they turn towards the light without noticing.

They were bred, or altered from birth, to share the nature of the plant minds they serve. Each is a living vessel: a body that such a mind can enter, fill and speak through. They are devoted, serene and utterly certain, and they give their bodies willingly. Other peoples find them unsettling, like a garden that is watching. When one of them suddenly speaks with a different voice, the wise listen carefully.

## Not to be confused with

the *[sentient plant kind](plant-kind-sentient-plant-kind.md)*, the plant minds themselves, where these are human priests grown to carry them.

## What sets this species apart

Altered humans with only subtle plant traits, bred as living vessels for a plant mind. Hosting a Spirit stands for being a vessel of the plant-sentience; the priesthood itself is Culture.
