# Non-corporeal ancient being

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

Minds without bodies, grown old beyond counting. Each is unique, and together they are less a people than a scattering of vast intellects across the dark between stars. Some dwell as drifting clouds of light or plasma, feeding on the energy of passing ships. Some live outside ordinary time in a hidden place and are worshipped as prophets by those nearby. Some embody a principle, order or chaos, rather than any living thing.

Most cannot or will not touch the world directly. They speak through dreams, visions or borrowed bodies, taking a host for an hour or a lifetime. A few are so alien that merely looking upon their true form drives a viewer mad. Their motives range from serene guardianship to cold hostility, and many are simply unknowable. Mortals who deal with them learn humility quickly, and learn to count what they have been promised very carefully.

## Not to be confused with

*[omnipotent trickster entity](energy-and-incorporeal-omnipotent-trickster-entity.md)*, a capricious god-like meddler; the ancient beings are each unique and mostly remote, working through hosts.

## What sets this species apart

Vast ancient bodiless minds; many reach others through thought or by taking hosts. Affinity only: the domain-specific powers of each being are Set at Breed/Race level, NPC-scale.
