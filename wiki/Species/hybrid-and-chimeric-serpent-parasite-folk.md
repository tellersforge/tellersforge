# Serpent parasite folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

Small, pale serpents that live inside other people. They enter through the back of the neck, wrap themselves around the spine and wake the host up as someone else. A host with a serpent inside heals faster, grows stronger and lives much longer than it otherwise would. Outside a host, the serpent is a finger-length thing with no hands, easily trodden on.

There are two strains. The dominant one takes over completely, silences the host and rules in its body, and has built an empire of false gods on that habit. The cooperative strain asks permission, shares the body and the mind with a willing partner, and has been at war with its cousins for as long as either can remember. Telling them apart from the outside is difficult, and usually matters a great deal.

## Not to be confused with

*[parasitic hive-mind](hive-and-collective-parasitic-hive-mind.md)*, whose countless tiny bodies share a single purpose; each serpent parasite is a full individual taking one host at a time.

## What sets this species apart

Tiny serpents that live inside a host body and strengthen and heal it. Skill lines copied from the Serpentine family, for the parasite outside a host. The cooperative strain shares control with a willing host. No hands outside a host: held weapons unusable.
