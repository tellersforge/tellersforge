# Reshaped faction folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md)*

A single ancestral people, divided long ago and remade in different directions by whoever held the power to do it. One faction looks almost human, cool and polished, and dominates the others politically. Another is slow, big-eyed and at home in the treetops, moving with a sloth's patience. Other factions went other ways again.

They share a history, a language and a great many grudges. Each faction believes the others went wrong, and each tells the story of the reshaping differently. Outsiders see a strange family of mismatched cousins; the folk themselves see nations. What unites them is the memory of having been one people, and the suspicion that someone meant them never to be one again.

## Not to be confused with

*[Great ape folk](simian-great-ape-folk.md)* are a natural family of apes; reshaped faction folk were deliberately divided into very different builds from one ancestor.

## What sets this species apart

The factions were reshaped in different directions (human-like and slow arboreal), so every difference sits at Breed level.
