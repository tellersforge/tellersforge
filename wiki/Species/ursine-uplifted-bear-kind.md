# Uplifted bear-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ursine](../Species_Families/ursine.md)*

Bears who were given speech. A patron people reshaped their ancestors, raised their minds and taught them to build, and in return the uplifted bears owe a debt that runs through their whole society. In body they are simply bears on two legs: huge, strong and thick-furred, with a bear's nose and claws.

Their thinking is steady rather than quick. They weigh things slowly and hate to be hurried, which others mistake for dullness at their peril. Loyalty to their patrons is the heart of their culture, a bond of gratitude and obligation that younger bears sometimes question. On colony worlds and starships they are valued crew: patient, reliable and very hard to knock down.

## Not to be confused with

*[Uplifted animal folk](hybrid-and-chimeric-uplifted-animal-folk.md)*, animals raised to reason by many patrons in many shapes; uplifted bear-kind are bears specifically, bound by their own client loyalty.

## What sets this species apart

Uplifted bears no different in body from the family; slower cognition is an attribute and client loyalty is culture.
