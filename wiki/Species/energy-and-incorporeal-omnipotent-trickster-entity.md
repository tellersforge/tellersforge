# Omnipotent trickster entity

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

Imagine a being who can do anything. Turn a starship into a garden, stop time in the middle of a sentence, appear as a judge, a jester or a grandmother, and then decide that humanity is on trial. These tricksters are that, or near enough. Within their home realm of existence nothing is beyond them, and outside it very little.

With limitless power comes limitless boredom. They are capricious, vain and endlessly curious about lesser creatures, whom they treat as pets, puzzles or entertainment. They set tests, pose riddles, and meddle in wars and love affairs simply to see what happens. Now and then their games teach something worth knowing. Ageless and deathless, they take the long view, which means they can be astonishingly patient and astonishingly petty. The only defence is wit. They respect nothing so much as a mortal who answers back.

## Not to be confused with

*[reality-bending entity](planar-reality-bending-entity.md)*, which warps the world by nature or accident; these tricksters remake it deliberately and for their own amusement.

## What sets this species apart

Near-omnipotent, ageless tricksters who remake form, space and time at will. NPC-scale. Lifespan (ageless or immortal) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
