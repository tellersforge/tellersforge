# Light spirit guide

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

A small, bright presence that appears when someone is lost. It might be a floating spark no larger than a firefly, or a glowing shape like a tiny creature with long ears and a trailing tail. It does not speak in words. It shows the way by moving ahead, warming when the traveller is on the right path, dimming when they stray, and sending pure feeling instead of sentences: reassurance, warning, grief, joy.

These guardian lights serve something larger, a great tree, a sacred spring, the forest itself, and their task is to protect what it cares for. They are gentle but not fragile. Faced with darkness or cruelty, a guide can flare into blinding brilliance. Travellers who have been led home by one tend to speak of it for the rest of their lives, and leave small lights in their windows in its honour.

## Not to be confused with

*[will-o-the-wisp](energy-and-incorporeal-will-o-the-wisp.md)*, a marsh light that lures travellers to their deaths; the light spirit guide leads them home.

## What sets this species apart

Small guardian spirits of pure light who guide the lost.
