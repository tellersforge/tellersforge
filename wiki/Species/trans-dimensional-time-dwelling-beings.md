# Time-dwelling beings

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Beings who live in time the way others live in a house. Some experience their whole lives at once, remembering tomorrow as clearly as yesterday. Some drift outside the flow of hours and collect specimens from every age. The most dreadful lurk in forgotten folds between minutes, waiting for the lost.

They have a feel for moments: they sense when something is about to happen, notice when something already has, and see the echoes of old events on a place. Many are calm to the point of fatalism; death, to them, is only one moment among many. Speaking with one is disorienting. They answer questions before they are asked, and sometimes the question they answer is next year's.

## Not to be confused with

*[Higher-dimensional beings](trans-dimensional-higher-dimensional-beings.md)* move through extra directions of space; time-dwelling beings live along the current of time itself.

## What sets this species apart

Their existence runs through time rather than space, giving them a feel for moments past and to come. Affinity only; whole-timeline perception is set per breed.
