# Artifact-bonded host-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

Ordinary mortals carrying something that is not ordinary at all. An ancient, malevolent force from beyond the world has fastened onto their bloodline, passing from parent to eldest child, and it lives in them now. It whispers. It hungers. And when it is called, it pours out of them as living darkness: whip-like tendrils, hooked claws, and small snarling shadow-demons that scuttle at their master's feet.

The bond lengthens their lives and grants great power, and it asks a great price. Hosts spend their days wrestling with a voice that wants blood and a temper that is not wholly their own. Some become monsters. Some fight it to the end. Those who know what they are tend to keep their distance, and to watch the host's shadow.

## Not to be confused with

the *[relic-bonded host folk](shapeshifter-relic-bonded-host-folk.md)*, who draw their power from a holy or ancient object, where these hosts carry a living evil in their blood.

## What sets this species apart

Mortal hosts of a malevolent force that grants shadow tendrils, claws and summoned shadow minions. Summon Creature covers the small shadow-demon minions. Natural Weapons stays because the tendrils and claws are grown at will from the bonded force, not a permanent body part.
