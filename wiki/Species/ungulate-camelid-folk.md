# Camelid folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

Camel-folk and their mountain cousins, built to go the long way and arrive. The desert breeds carry fat in humps on their backs, long lashes against blowing sand, and a patience with heat and thirst that astonishes other travellers. The mountain breeds wear thick wool, keep their footing on narrow paths, and spit with deadly accuracy when annoyed.

Both are made for burdens. They carry heavy loads over great distances without complaint, or at least without stopping, since the complaining never really ends. Caravan trade runs on their backs and on their knowledge of roads, wells and weather. They are proud, a little aloof, and remember every slight along with every waterhole.

## Not to be confused with

*[Beasts of burden](ungulate-beasts-of-burden.md)*, the unthinking livestock that haul for others; camelid folk carry loads by trade and choice, and set the price.

## What sets this species apart

Hardy pack-carrying builds made for long, hard journeys.
