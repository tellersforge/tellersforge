# Four-armed centauroid

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Equine and centauroid](../Species_Families/equine-and-centauroid.md)*

A centauroid built for war. The body is broad and heavy on four sturdy legs, and the upper torso carries not two arms but four, set one pair above the other. The upper arms wield weapons and gesture in conversation; the lower ones hold a shield, steady a rope, grip a ladder or carry gear. In a fight, one of them does the work of two soldiers.

Their societies are martial and orderly, organised into castes in which a person's rank and duty are fixed early and served for life. Warriors are the most honoured caste, but craftsmen and climbers find those extra hands just as useful. They are serious, loyal and hard to ruffle. Other peoples often mistake their gravity for dullness, until they see four blades moving at once, or a pack of them scaling a cliff that ordinary centaurs could never climb.

## Not to be confused with

*[centaur](equine-and-centauroid-centaur.md)*, the classic two-armed horse-bodied folk; the four-armed kind trade speed for a second pair of hands.

## What sets this species apart

A second pair of arms helps with climbing and handiwork. Second pair of arms: may carry a shield or a second weapon.
