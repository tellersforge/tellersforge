# Kobold

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Goblinoid and orc-kind](../Species_Families/goblinoid-and-orc-kind.md)*

Small, scaly, dog-snouted people who live in tunnels, mines and the forgotten corners of old ruins. A kobold is hardly knee-high to a man and fragile in a fight, and knows both facts well. What it lacks in strength it makes up for in numbers, patience and an extraordinary gift for traps: pits, deadfalls, swinging logs, tripwires and nastier things, laid in every corridor of the warren.

They work together like a pack of hounds, harrying larger foes from every side. They see in the dark, love shiny things and hold grudges for generations. Many revere dragons and claim distant kinship with them, which dragons find amusing. Adventurers who laugh at kobolds usually do it before entering the tunnels, not after.

## Not to be confused with

*[kobold-kind](small-folk-kobold-kind.md)* of the Small folk family, the household sprites of mine and hearth; these kobolds are reptilian pack folk who fight with traps.

## What sets this species apart

Small tunnel-dwelling reptilian pack folk: trap-makers, fragile in a fight.
