# Hive mind swarm kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md)*

Swarms that think. Each creature in them is small, simple and nearly mindless, but together they form a single distributed intelligence that plans, learns and remembers. Some began as ordinary ants, raised into a strange shared cleverness. Some are vast insect tides that strip whole worlds bare, guided by a single overmind. Some are titanic, shelled beings whose whole society thinks as one across a planet.

At the heart of many swarms is a queen-organism, huge, ancient and telepathic, who bears every worker and soldier and sees through their eyes. Others have no heart at all, only the swarm itself. None of them can be reasoned with in any ordinary way. Talking to one means talking to all of it, and it rarely has anything to say.

## Not to be confused with

*[hive swarm horror](hive-and-collective-hive-swarm-horror.md)*, the bio-engineered war swarms of grown weapons; the hive mind swarm kind is the family's broad range of collective insect minds.

## What sets this species apart

Barely individual swarm organisms that think as one distributed mind. Mostly NPC. Mind Link matches the value the Hive and collective family uses for a true hive-mind.
