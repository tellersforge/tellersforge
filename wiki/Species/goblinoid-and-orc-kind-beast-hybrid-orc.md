# Beast-hybrid orc

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Goblinoid and orc-kind](../Species_Families/goblinoid-and-orc-kind.md)*

Shock troops bred in dark places from human stock and the beasts of the field. They stand taller than any man, and each has something of an animal about the head: a wolf's jaws, a goat's horns, an eagle's hooked beak, a boar's tusks. They are savage, short-lived and always hungry, and they fight with an eagerness that frightens even their masters.

They are made to terrify, and they do. A line of them coming out of the mist is enough to break militia before a blow lands. Worse are their captains, pale, eyeless things bred from the same stock, who move with unnatural speed, are very hard to kill, and whose gaze freezes a brave man where he stands. Whoever breeds them is the real enemy.

## Not to be confused with

*[beast-tribe folk](hybrid-and-chimeric-beast-tribe-folk.md)*, people with animal features who live their own lives; beast-hybrid orcs are bred weapons, shaped only for war and fear.

## What sets this species apart

Bred shadow-troops whose look alone terrifies.
