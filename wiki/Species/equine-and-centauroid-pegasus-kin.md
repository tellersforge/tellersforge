# Pegasus-kin

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Equine and centauroid](../Species_Families/equine-and-centauroid.md)*

A horse with great feathered wings, sweeping over the mountains and landing on cliff-tops where nothing else could stand. The wings are no ornament: they carry a full-grown horse and a rider through the clouds, faster than any bird. Where a winged horse's hoof strikes the rock, the old tales say, a spring of clear water bursts out.

Most are proud beasts rather than people. They are clever, loyal and wilful, but they do not speak. They can be tamed, though never broken, and only a rider of honest heart is ever allowed to stay on their back. Kings and heroes dream of one. Stables built for them sit on high towers open to the wind. A winged horse that chooses a rider will carry them anywhere, and one that does not will simply fly away, and no rope will ever hold it again.

## Not to be confused with

*[unicorn-kin](equine-and-centauroid-unicorn-kin.md)*, horned healing horses of the forest; the winged horse is a creature of the sky and a mount of heroes.

## What sets this species apart

Winged horses that truly fly. No hands: held weapons unusable.
