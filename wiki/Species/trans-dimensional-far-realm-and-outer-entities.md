# Far-realm and outer entities

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Things from outside. They come from beyond the known dimensions, or from the gaps between them, and their shapes defy easy description: tentacles, eyes, angles, colours with no name, mouths in the wrong places. Some are vast sleeping gods and their spawn, some are hounds that hunt through sharp corners, some are seeds planted in a mind to hatch years later.

Their greatest weapon is simply what they are. Minds strain when they look at them; courage fails, sleep stops and reason slips. Cults worship them, scholars go mad studying them and wise folk burn the books. They care nothing for mortals, which is somehow worse than hatred.

## Not to be confused with

*[Elder god-kind](planar-elder-god-kind.md)* are vast divine beings with worshippers and domains; far-realm and outer entities are alien horrors from outside reality whose presence breaks minds.

## What sets this species apart

Cosmic horrors whose mere presence frightens and unhinges mortal minds. NPC-scale.
