# Reptilian beasts and mounts

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md)*

The scaled beasts that other peoples ride, drive and fear. Heavy desert lizards plod through the sand under packs and riders, sun-hardened and patient. Tall, long-legged six-limbed creatures carry whole caravans. Stocky bird-like reptiles haul carts with ill grace, and elephant-sized scaled haulers carry houses on their backs. In the wild, colossal armoured predators hunt, their hides healing almost as fast as they are wounded.

None of them thinks as people do. The tame ones are strong, steady and often short-tempered; the wild ones are simply dangerous. A few small flying reptiles can touch minds and be kept as pets. Riders who treat them well are carried far; riders who don't tend to find out how hard a tail can swing.

## Not to be confused with

the *[beasts of burden](ungulate-beasts-of-burden.md)*, the furred and hoofed working animals, where these are the scaled, cold-blooded mounts and predators.

## What sets this species apart

Non-sentient beasts of burden and predators whose bodies cannot ride another animal. Non-sentient NPC creatures. No hands: held weapons unusable.
