# Winter elf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

Elves of the snowfields and glaciers, pale as the land they live on. Cold that would kill other folk in a night is simply the weather to them. They walk on ice without slipping, sleep in snow without freezing, and hear a crevasse cracking under the drifts long before it opens. Their hair is white or silver from youth, and their breath barely fogs.

Their magic comes easily in the shapes of their country: frost, still air, the long silence of a winter night. Their settlements are hidden in high valleys or hollowed out of ice, and few outsiders ever see one. They are patient, reserved and hard to rouse, but slow anger among them lasts like permafrost. Lowlanders sometimes mistake them for spirits of the snow, and the winter elves see little reason to correct them.

## Not to be confused with

*[desert elf](elf-kind-desert-elf.md)*, the heat-hardened branch; winter elves are its mirror, built for ice and snow instead of sand.

## What sets this species apart

Cold-adapted bodies at home on snow and ice. Resist Element is cold.
