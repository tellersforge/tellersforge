# Template unknown folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md)*

The shrouded, the masked, the unseen, the veiled. Template unknown folk are peoples whose true nature has not been settled, and may never be. They are always glimpsed behind a covering: a featureless mask, a deep hood, a veil of cloth or light. They are mentioned in passing, known by report, or met only through intermediaries.

That is deliberate. They are the blank page of the bestiary, kept ready for any humanoid whose body and origin the table has yet to decide. A group may fill them in at leisure, discovering over many sessions what lies under the mask, or keep the mystery forever. Until then, they are exactly what they seem: someone standing in shadow, waiting to be known.

## Not to be confused with

*[Concealed-form folk](unknown-humanoid-concealed-form-folk.md)*, who hide real and definite bodies on purpose; template unknown folk have no settled body yet, and wait to be given one.
