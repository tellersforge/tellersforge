# Aardvark folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mustelid and small mammal](../Species_Families/mustelid-and-small-mammal.md)*

A heavy, grey-furred biped with a long pig-like snout, big ears and thick digging claws on hands that are equally happy tearing open a termite mound or closing around a sword hilt. They are strong, stocky and much tougher than they look, and much more intelligent than people expect from that face.

Few of them are left. The stories speak of a people who once were many and now are scattered to a handful, and those who remain tend to be loners: cynical, sardonic, fond of drink and slow to trust. More than one has turned up as a hired sword in some foreign war, and more than one has ended up running a city, usually against their own better judgement. People laugh at the snout, once.

## Not to be confused with

the *[mustelid folk](mustelid-and-small-mammal-mustelid-folk.md)*, slender quick hunters, where aardvark folk are heavy, long-snouted diggers.

## What sets this species apart

A heavier, long-snouted, clawed digger rather than a slinky small hunter. Burrowing is affinity only (clawed digging hands).
