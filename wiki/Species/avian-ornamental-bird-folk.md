# Ornamental bird folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Birds of display. Ornamental bird folk carry trains of shimmering tail feathers, crests, ruffs and eye-patterned fans that open like a painted screen, and they know exactly how good they look. Peacock-kin are the best known, but every line shares the same trouble: plumage that turns heads in a courtyard also turns them in the woods, where the wrong sort of head is watching.

They fly only in short, clumsy bursts. Their cultures revolve around courtship, rank and appearance, and their nobles can be insufferably vain, working their own shed feathers into fans, cloaks and even blades. Other peoples find them splendid at a feast and exhausting everywhere else, and ornamental folk rarely care what plainer birds think.

## Not to be confused with

*[Songbird & small-bird folk](avian-songbird-and-small-bird-folk.md)*, who are also brightly coloured, but small, strong fliers who value song over show.

## What sets this species apart

Display birds whose showy plumage draws eyes, for good and ill. Limited flight; not Set.
