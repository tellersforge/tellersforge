# Troll-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Dwarf-kind](../Species_Families/dwarf-kind.md)*

Not the lumbering brutes of the fireside tale. These trolls are an underground people of smiths and tunnellers, broad and heavy-handed, living in vast warrens dug deep beneath hills and forests. They work metal as well as any dwarf and dig faster, and their tunnels run for leagues under the feet of surface folk who never suspect a thing.

They are shrewd traders and old rivals of the elves, with whom they bargain, quarrel and sometimes fight. Their lives are about as long as a human's. Visitors who expect a stupid monster find a clever merchant instead, and generally leave the deal poorer than they came.

## Not to be confused with

*[Ogre-kind](giant-kind-ogre-kind.md)*, the huge, slow brutes that many tales mistake for trolls, where these trolls are clever underground smiths.

## What sets this species apart

No species-wide difference: underground smiths and tunnelers already match the family; trading is culture.
