# Salamander-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md)*

Tailed amphibians that walk rather than leap. They have long bodies, short legs, smooth damp skin and a remarkable gift: a lost limb grows back in a season, and a lost tail sooner. Many are slow and patient by nature, and in old tales they are thought to live in fire, which they find very funny.

They come in many kinds. Newt-folk begin life in the water and end it on land, and their skin is bitter with poison. Axolotl folk keep their feathery gills for life. Limbless kin burrow blind through tropical earth; giant wrinkled kin lurk on riverbeds; small gilled kin come out only at night. Others think them sleepy. It is a mistake to try to sneak past one.

## Not to be confused with

the *[frog-folk](amphibian-frog-folk.md)*, who leap and croak; salamanders crawl, walk and regrow what they lose.

## What sets this species apart

Tailed salamander bodies that regrow lost limbs and walk rather than leap.
