# Dream-realm construct-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

Somewhere beyond sleep lies a realm of dreams, and it has a ruler, and that ruler makes things. These beings are those things: shaped out of dream-stuff for a single purpose. One is a nightmare meant to hunt, one a comfort meant to soothe, one a riddle meant to be solved, one a door that should never be opened. Each wears whatever form its purpose needs, and those forms can be wildly strange.

Within dreams they move freely, slipping from one sleeper's mind to another's as easily as walking through a door. Their existence is bound to their task, and many have no life beyond it. When they cross into the waking world, as some rebellious ones do, they often start to fray, unravelling at the edges like mist in sunlight. A dream that runs away is a dangerous thing, and its maker always sends someone to fetch it.

## Not to be confused with

*[dream-realm natives](trans-dimensional-dream-realm-natives.md)*, beings born in the dreaming as a people; these constructs were deliberately made by the realm's ruler for a purpose.

## What sets this species apart

Beings of dream-stuff who move freely through dreams. Outside their dream-realm they may not hold together (a flaw).
