# Ascended dimension-lord-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Once they were mortal, or near enough. Then they climbed out of flesh altogether and became something that lives on a higher plane, looking down. Some ascended into pure light and keep a strict rule against meddling with those below. Others broke that rule and set themselves up as gods. The strangest were never mortal at all: capricious beings from a realm outside time who treat lesser peoples as puzzles, pets or pupils, and whose hidden courts dress their games in theatrical splendour.

They do not age and do not die in any sense that matters. When they choose to appear, they take whatever shape suits the lesson. Mortals who meet them rarely know whether they are being judged, helped or simply watched for sport, and the lords seldom say.

## Not to be confused with

*[Ascended energy being](energy-and-incorporeal-ascended-energy-being.md)*, which has risen into light but stays within reach of mortal affairs; these lords rule and watch from a plane apart.

## What sets this species apart

Beings raised beyond mortal form who watch and act from a higher plane. NPC-scale. Near-omnipotent continuum breeds add Reality Warping at breed level. Lifespan (ageless or immortal) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
