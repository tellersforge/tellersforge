# Shadow and dread-plane native-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

There is a world that lies behind this one like a reflection in a dirty window: the same hills and rivers, drained of colour and warmth. These are its natives. The common folk are slender and grey-tinged, quiet by habit, at ease in darkness that would blind anyone else. Bright sunlight hurts them, and they travel at dusk when they can. At the far edge of that gloom walk towering things closer to the dead than the living, whose gaze can wither a body in an instant.

The shade-folk are not wicked, only muted, and they bring back from their home a stillness others find unnerving. Traders value them as night guides. Villages near a thinning of the veil lock their doors when the fog rolls in, just in case.

## Not to be confused with

*[Shadow-touched elf](elf-kind-shadow-touched-elf.md)*, an elven people marked by shadow; these natives were born in the gloomy mirror-world itself and belong to no elven line.

## What sets this species apart

Natives of a gloomy mirror of the material world: at home in darkness, pained by bright light. Light sensitivity is a flaw, not a line here.
