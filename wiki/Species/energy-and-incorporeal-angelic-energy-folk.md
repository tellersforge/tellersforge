# Angelic energy folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

Beings of pure, soft radiance that drift on patterns of light shaped like great wings. They have no bodies in any ordinary sense, only a glowing presence that suggests a face, a figure, a lifted arm. Watching one is like watching the sun come through stained glass. Travellers who have met them describe a deep, bodily calm, as though the air itself had become kinder.

They rarely leave the place they call home: a particular shining moon, a nebula, a quiet corner of the void where they drift in slow, serene formation. Visitors who approach them with respect are welcomed and sometimes shown wonders. Those who come with violence find nothing to strike. Many peoples have mistaken them for angels and built religions around a brief encounter. The light-folk themselves seem unaware of this, or too gentle to correct anyone.

## Not to be confused with

*[celestial-kind](planar-celestial-kind.md)*, the heavenly servants of gods and higher powers; these beings of light serve no one and simply exist in peace.

## What sets this species apart

Serene beings of pure light that fly on wing-like light patterns.
