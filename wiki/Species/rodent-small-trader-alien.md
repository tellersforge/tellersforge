# Small trader alien

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md)*

Small, sharp-faced aliens with long whiskers, quick paws and eyes that never stop moving. They look like something between a rat and a weasel dressed in a travelling coat, and they are found wherever ships dock and gossip changes hands. What they sell is information: who is buying, who is hiding, which captain owes whom. They usually work in pairs or trios, finishing each other's sentences.

They are mischievous rather than cruel, and they enjoy a good bargain almost more than the profit it brings. Their prices are strange (a favour, a secret, a curious trinket) and they always seem to know more than they say. Spacers roll their eyes at them, pay them anyway, and check their pockets afterwards.

## Not to be confused with

*[Scavenger trader folk](rodent-scavenger-trader-folk.md)* collect and trade salvage; the small trader alien deals mainly in secrets, rumours and favours, and rarely carries any goods at all.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference: their information-broking is Culture, handled in the Culture step.
