# Dinosaur folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md)*

Peoples built on the great beasts of the deep past. Some are sickle-clawed raptor-folk, lean and feathered, who hunt in packs with frightening teamwork. Some are huge, plodding grazers with long necks and tails like whips. Some carry frills and horns, some plates and spiked clubs, some bear a skull so large their arms seem an afterthought. A few glide on leathery wings.

They have little in common beyond old bones and a certain sense of scale. Some are wild and tribal, some belong to hidden civilisations far older than humankind, and some roam the stars in fleets of their own, occasionally denying what they came from. Other peoples meet them with a primal, deep-seated alarm, and not always without reason.

## Not to be confused with

the *[lizardfolk](reptilian-lizardfolk.md)*, modern-built scaled folk, where dinosaur folk come from the great beasts of the ancient past.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference: raptors, grazers, fliers and saurian civilisations share too little beyond the family, so each is handled at Breed/Race level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
