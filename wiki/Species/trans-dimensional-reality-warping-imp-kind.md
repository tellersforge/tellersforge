# Reality-warping imp-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Small, grinning imps from realms where thought and matter are almost the same thing. They pop into being with a puff of smoke, a snap of the fingers or a small unsettling giggle, and pop out again somewhere else. Some can reshape the world around them on a whim, turning a sword to rubber or a street upside down.

They are pranksters to the core, hungry for attention and bored by anything serious. Most are not truly malicious, but their idea of fun can be ruinous. Every one of them is bound by some rule of its home: a word it must not be tricked into saying, a riddle it must not lose. The only sure way to be rid of one is to outwit it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Omnipotent trickster entity](energy-and-incorporeal-omnipotent-trickster-entity.md)* is an energy being of vast power; reality-warping imp-kind are small, mischievous imps who hop from place to place and live by strict loopholes.

## What sets this species apart

Small mischievous imps who pop about from place to place and delight in tricks. Fifth-dimensional reality reshaping is Set at breed level with its banishment weakness.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
