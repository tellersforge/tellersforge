# Witch-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Witches of a scale that makes ordinary witches look like village herbalists. Each is bound to an ancient sin or authority, a power older than kingdoms, and draws on it to bend the world: to stop a river, blight a nation's harvest, or wake the dead of a whole battlefield. They do not grow old in any way that matters. Some have outlived the languages they were born speaking.

They are rarely met and never forgotten. Their motives are their own, their moods are dangerous, and even their kindnesses tend to leave scars. Kings pay tribute to keep them away, and scholars spend lives studying the ruins they left. In a story, meeting one is not a fight. It is a negotiation with a storm that happens to have opinions.

## Not to be confused with

*[hag/witch-kind](undead-and-deathless-hag-witch-kind.md)*, the cursed undead crones of swamp and gibbet; the great witches are living fey, bound to an ancient power and far beyond any hag's reach.

## What sets this species apart

Functionally immortal witches of world-altering power. NPC-scale. Lifespan (ageless or immortal) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
