# Death-guide spirit-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

Guides from the world beyond death, who walk among the living in human shape and carry each departing soul to where it belongs. Each wears a sword or blade that cuts spirit rather than flesh, bound to its bearer and shaped by their nature: one a slim katana, another a scythe, another a blade of light.

They have duties as well as powers. Lost souls must be ushered on, and spirits turned hungry and monstrous by grief or corruption must be cut free and cleansed. They live very long lives, keep their own ranks and customs, and are often weary of the work. The living rarely see them; when they do, it is usually a sign that someone near is about to be needed on the other side.

## Not to be confused with

the *[underworld spirit-kind](fey-underworld-spirit-kind.md)*, native spirits of the realm of the dead, where these guides walk among the living with blades to lead souls there.

## What sets this species apart

Spirit-world guides who carry souls onward with a personal soul-cutting blade and cleanse corrupted spirits. Conjure Weapon & Armour stands for the personal soul-blade; its individual spirit power is chosen at Breed/Race or character level.
