# Undersea humanoid

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md)*

An old offshoot of humanity that went into the sea and stayed. They are much stronger and faster than their surface cousins, especially in water, and they live for centuries. Some can pass for surface folk, with legs and lungs; some are gilled and webbed, made for the crushing dark; some have true fish tails and cannot walk. A few lines went wrong: a feral, fanged people in the deepest trenches, and the descendants of exiled criminals who can hurl water like thrown spears.

Their kingdoms are proud, old and hidden beneath the waves. Surface folk tend to learn about them only when a ship goes down or a war comes up.

## Not to be confused with

the *[merfolk](aquatic-merfolk.md)*, sea-folk of legend with fish tails; undersea humanoids are human stock turned aquatic, with legs as well as tails.

## What sets this species apart

An offshoot of humanity with superhuman strength and speed in the water. Long-lived (centuries). Strength itself is set in the attribute step.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
