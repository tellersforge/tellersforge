# Vegetable curiosity

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md)*

A plant that bears a lamb. It grows low on the steppe, a stout green stalk topped with a pod, and when the pod splits a small, woolly lamb unfolds from it, alive and bleating, still fastened to the stem at its belly. It grazes the grass in a circle as far as the tether allows. When that grass is gone, both lamb and plant wither and die.

It thinks nothing, says nothing and goes nowhere. Travellers' tales describe its meat as tasting of fish and its blood as sweet as honey, and its fleece is said to be very fine. Scholars argue about whether it is animal or vegetable, and shepherds, who have to fence it, mostly consider it a nuisance.

## Not to be confused with

the *[vine/flower folk](plant-kind-vine-flower-folk.md)*, who are plant people who think and move, where this oddity is only a plant that happens to grow a lamb.

## What sets this species apart

A non-sentient plant whose lamb-fruit grazes on a stem tether and cannot travel or speak. Non-sentient NPC oddity. No hands: held weapons unusable.
