# Corrupted elf-kin

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

Elves who were changed by something that should never have touched them. The causes differ as much as the results. One line looked too long into the dark between realms and came back with purple skin and a hunger for that emptiness. Another drank deep of demonic sorcery and now glows a sickly green, powerful and never quite satisfied. A third fled underground so long ago that its people went blind, grew armour from insect husks and forgot they had ever been elves.

Others are stranger still: ancient princes who swore to take the world back from humankind, star-faring raiders who feed on others' pain, warriors fused with a substance that swells them to giants and eats away at what remains of them. What unites them is loss. Each was once something finer, and each remembers, or is reminded by its kin, what was given up.

## Not to be confused with

*[shadow-touched elf](elf-kind-shadow-touched-elf.md)*, reshaped by a plane of shadow or the fey seasons but not debased by it; corrupted elf-kin were twisted.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference from the family: each corruption changes the body in its own way, so all lines sit with each Breed/Race.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
