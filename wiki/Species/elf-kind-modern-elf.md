# Modern elf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

Elves in the age of glass towers and neon. In some tellings they appeared suddenly among humankind, children who began to change in a single strange season, and now make up a slice of every city's population. They have pointed ears, slender builds, eyes that see well in the half-light of a streetlamp, and a natural charm that opens doors, contracts and wallets.

They move easily through boardrooms and nightclubs alike. Many join the great corporations, where their long careers and good looks make them natural executives, spokesmen and fixers. Others turn their back on all that and run with street crews or activist cells, trading on the same charm for different ends. Humans envy them, court them, resent them and sometimes riot against them. The elves, for their part, are learning what every minority learns: being admired is not the same as being safe.

## Not to be confused with

*[high elf](elf-kind-high-elf.md)*, the arcane, courtly branch of older ages; modern elves are city-dwellers whose gift is presence, not sorcery.

## What sets this species apart

No difference from the family at the skill and power level: their charm is an attribute and their corporate life is culture.
