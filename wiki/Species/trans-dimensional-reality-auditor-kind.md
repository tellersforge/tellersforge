# Reality-auditor kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Somewhere outside the world, something keeps the books. These beings exist to check that reality follows its own rules, and to correct it when it does not. They know every fact there is to know: the weight of a star, the name of a stranger's grandmother, the exact moment a lie was told. What they cannot grasp is why anyone would feel strongly about any of it.

They dislike bodies. When they must take one, they choose the plainest form they can and wear it stiffly, like an inspector's coat in the rain. Life's mess offends them: grief, joy, mercy, the untidy exceptions people make for one another. Mortals who deal with them find them precise, humourless and impossible to bribe, and hope never to be the error they have come to correct.

## Not to be confused with

*[Multiversal overseer-kind](trans-dimensional-multiversal-overseer-kind.md)*, which watches over many worlds with its own agenda; the auditors have no agenda but the rules themselves.

## What sets this species apart

Form-averse enforcers of reality's rules who know facts perfectly and understand feelings not at all. NPC-scale.
