# Elder realm-bound folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

An ancient race older than most kingdoms, tall and dark-eyed, which long ago broke into three branches. Each branch bound itself to a different cosmic realm, and each has been shaped by it ever since. The children of light are disciplined and severe, keepers of a martial and magical tradition that brooks no doubt. The children of shadow are elegant and melancholy, master swordsmen and sorcerers who have grown weary across unimaginable spans of years. The children of storm are fractious and militant, quick to war and slow to forgive.

The three are kin, and they remember it mainly as a reason to hate one another. Their feuds are older than the mountains some of them have watched rise. When one of them walks among shorter-lived peoples, it is usually on an errand no one else understands, and they rarely explain.

## Not to be confused with

*[elder-kin](elf-kind-elder-kin.md)*, a single hidden remnant of the elven line; elder realm-bound folk are a divided race sworn to separate realms.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference from the family: each branch is shaped by its own realm (light, shadow, storm), so those lines sit with each Breed/Race.
