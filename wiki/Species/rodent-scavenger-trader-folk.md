# Scavenger trader folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md)*

Small, wiry folk with long tails and enormous ears that swivel independently, catching every rustle, clink and whisper for streets around. They hear more than anyone else in a room and, given half a chance, repeat it.

They are born scavengers. Broken machines, lost trinkets, the things others throw away all find their way into their pockets, then onto their market stalls, then back into the world at a profit. They are quick-witted, quick-tongued and endlessly haggling. Others find them charming for about as long as it takes to count their change, and then check their pockets.

## Not to be confused with

the *[rat-kind](rodent-rat-kind.md)*, who are known for a keen nose and life underground, where scavenger traders rely on their ears and their stalls.

## What sets this species apart

Big-eared scavengers who hear more than most. The trading habit is Culture.
