# Crystalline energy being

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

A large, sharp-edged being of faceted crystal that glows from within like a lantern. It does not build with its hands. It weaves. Threads of shining energy spin out from its body and harden into webs: nets across a passage, cages around a ship, walls strung between stars. A group of them working together can close off a region of space in an afternoon.

They are precise, territorial and famously prickly, and they treat any intruder as a trespasser first and a guest much later, if ever. Their weakness is heat. A crystal body tolerates cold and it tolerates warmth, but not a sudden swing from one to the other, and a sharp change can crack them through. They know it, and they guard their temperature as closely as their borders.

## Not to be confused with

the *[crystalline folk](mineral-and-crystalline-crystalline-folk.md)*, humanoid crystal people who bend light and live in cities, where these beings spin energy webs and keep to themselves.

## What sets this species apart

A crystal being that spins energy webs, and can shatter under sudden temperature change. Vulnerable to rapid heating or cooling.
