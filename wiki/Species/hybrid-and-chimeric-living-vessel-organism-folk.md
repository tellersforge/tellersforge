# Living-vessel organism folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

A ship that is alive. It was grown, not built, from something like a vast deep-sea creature or a star-swimming beast, and its crew lives inside it as a family lives in a house. Its corridors are warm and faintly breathing, its walls close and heal after damage, and its engines are muscles and organs doing their natural work. It has no hands and does nothing for itself except fly, grow and think.

It thinks a great deal. The vessel speaks with its crew mind to mind, knows each of them by feel, and takes a close interest in where they want to go. Some are cheerful, some shy, some old and a little sad. Their crews come to love them. Their enemies learn that every wound inflicted on the ship is also felt by her crew.

## Not to be confused with

*[cosmic space-whale kind](aquatic-cosmic-space-whale-kind.md)*, the great beasts that swim between stars in the wild; living vessels are grown ships, bonded to a crew they carry.

## What sets this species apart

A giant living starship that heals itself and speaks mind to mind with its crew. NPC-scale. No hands: held weapons unusable.
