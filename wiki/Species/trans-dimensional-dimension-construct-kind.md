# Dimension-construct kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Made things, or things remade. Some were built in squads of identical drones, half metal and half grown tissue. Some were living people once, before a swarm-mind swallowed them. Some are self-copying machines that pour from world to world like a tide of grey sand, and some are guardians called into being at the threshold of a door between realms, existing only while that door stands open.

What joins them is the bond to a purpose or a network larger than any one of them. They do not tire, they do not argue, and they move together as if one mind worked all their limbs. They are also cold. Pleading, grief and fear mean nothing to them. To other peoples they are less a nation than a weather front: something to shelter from, or to break.

## Not to be confused with

*[Network/hive-mind machine kind](constructs-and-synthetics-network-hive-mind-machine-kind.md)*, ordinary machines linked by built equipment; these constructs are joined by an innate collective bond that crosses realms.

## What sets this species apart

Made or remade things tied to a network or purpose: tireless, coordinated and cold to others' feelings. Networked tech is gear; the link here is the innate collective bond.
