# Mutant rodent-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md)*

Rodent folk twisted into shape by the aftermath of ruin. Their ancestors were rats, mice or rabbits in the rubble of a fallen world, and radiation changed them: taller, cleverer, able to speak and use tools, and each with some strange gift that no two of them share.

They shrug off the poisoned air and glowing dust that kill other peoples, and they have made the ruins their home. They scavenge, barter and fight over scrap, clean water and old machines. Every family is different: one may see in the dark, another may shock anything that touches them, another may read thoughts. Others treat them with wary curiosity, which is fair, because nobody knows what the next one will be able to do.

## Not to be confused with

the *[rat-kind](rodent-rat-kind.md)*, ordinary rodent peoples of settled lands, where mutant rodents were changed by radiation and live in the ruins.

## What sets this species apart

Radiation-mutated rodent folk who shrug off radiation. Resist Element is radiation. The random mutation power is Set at Breed/Race or character level.
