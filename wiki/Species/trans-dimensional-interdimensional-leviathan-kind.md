# Interdimensional leviathan-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Creatures the size of mountains, swimming the empty dark between universes. They drift and glide where there is neither space nor time, moving with slow flicks of vast fins or flukes, and their passing makes whole realities shiver. They have no hands, no cities and no interest in any single world.

They live almost forever and pay little attention to smaller beings. Travellers between worlds sometimes hunt them for their bodies, follow them like sailors following whales, or even ride them across the void. Every so often one brushes too close to a world, and the people there see strange lights in the sky and feel a pressure in the air, like a storm that never breaks.

## Not to be confused with

*[Cosmic space-whale kind](aquatic-cosmic-space-whale-kind.md)* swim between the stars of one universe; interdimensional leviathan-kind swim the empty void between universes themselves.

## What sets this species apart

Colossal creatures that swim the empty void between universes. NPC-scale. No hands: held weapons unusable.
