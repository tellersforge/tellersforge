# Liquid-metal folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

People made of living quicksilver. They are dense, cool to the touch and very heavy, and sink in water like dropped anvils. When cut or shot, they close around the wound and flow on. The deadliest can copy any face and voice, and harden their hands into blades; they make perfect assassins, and are feared accordingly. Smaller kin are quick, darting messengers and tricksters who squeeze through keyholes and are poison to anyone foolish enough to swallow one.

Intense cold stiffens them and a strong magnet drags them helplessly, and their enemies know it. They know it too, and avoid ice-bound places and forges with the same care.

## Not to be confused with

the *[nanite-swarm folk](amorphous-and-ooze-nanite-swarm-folk.md)*, a crowd of tiny machines; liquid-metal folk are a single living body of metal.

## What sets this species apart

Dense living metal that sinks like a stone and flows back together after wounds. Vulnerable to extreme cold and strong magnetism.
