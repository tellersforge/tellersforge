# Plasma-bodied folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

Beings of living star-fire, held together in a restless, blazing shape. They are born in the outer layers of suns and other places of terrible heat, and they glow so brightly that looking at one hurts. Whatever they touch burns. Holding a tool is impossible; approaching them without protection is a short and final lesson.

Few mortals ever meet one. Those who do usually find them curious rather than cruel, fascinated by cold, solid things they can never touch. Their curiosity is dangerous all the same, since a friendly embrace would leave nothing but ash.

## Not to be confused with

the *[true elemental](elemental-kind-true-elemental.md)*, a being of pure fire from its own realm; plasma folk are born in the heat of stars.

## What sets this species apart

Bodies of contained star-hot plasma that blaze with light and burn whatever they touch. NPC-scale. Element is fire/heat. No hands: held weapons unusable.
