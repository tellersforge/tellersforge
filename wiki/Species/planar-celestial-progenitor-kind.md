# Celestial progenitor-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

Colossal armoured figures, taller than towers, who come down from the stars once in an age to look at a world's life and change it. They are among the oldest beings in existence, and they think of peoples the way a gardener thinks of seedlings: as things to be tended, pruned and tested.

Long ago, the first of them visited young worlds and reshaped the creatures they found, splitting single peoples into many and planting hidden potential in their blood. They sometimes return to see how the experiment is going. When a special caste of them arrives to pass judgement, whole worlds hold their breath, because a failed test ends in fire. Mortals know them as distant, silent and entirely beyond appeal.

## Not to be confused with

the *[cosmic celestial being](planar-cosmic-celestial-being.md)*, a colossal god-like wanderer that seeds life, where the progenitors deliberately reshape peoples and return to judge them.

## What sets this species apart

Vast, ancient cosmic experimenters who reshape the life of whole worlds. NPC-scale. The judge caste's world-ending judgment is Set at Breed/Race level. Lifespan (ageless or immortal) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
