# Monkey folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md)*

Quick, chattering monkey-folk with long tails, clever fingers and more energy than anyone knows what to do with. They are small or middling in size and built for the treetops, with a balance that lets them run along a rope or a roof ridge without a second thought. Some have tails that grip like a third hand.

They live in noisy troops ruled by gossip and shifting alliances. Some lines are traders, porters and go-betweens; some are tricksters of legend; some are fierce, loud and quick to show their teeth. Big-eyed night kinds leap between branches in the dark. Others treat them as charming nuisances, until the day a monkey-folk thief walks off with the crown jewels in broad daylight.

## Not to be confused with

*[Great ape folk](simian-great-ape-folk.md)* are heavy and tailless; monkey folk are small, tailed and remarkably sure-footed, with a gift for balance the bigger apes lack.

## What sets this species apart

Smaller, mostly tailed monkey builds with extra balance.
