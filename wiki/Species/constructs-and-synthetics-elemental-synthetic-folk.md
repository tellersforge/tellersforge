# Elemental synthetic folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md)*

Machines built to hold an element. Elemental synthetic folk are android bodies designed around a core that channels a single natural force. Some are made of living metal that flows, stretches and hardens at will; others wear a human-passing skin over a frame that can turn to wind, drift through walls or call up a local storm.

They share the worries of all synthetic beings, and then some. Is the element theirs, or were they built to carry it for someone else? Many serve as heroes or as weapons, and many struggle with questions of identity and purpose. Their makers tend to be brilliant, secretive and rarely around to answer questions.

## Not to be confused with

*[Elemental-blooded folk](elemental-kind-elemental-blooded-folk.md)*, born people with a trace of elemental heritage, where these are made machines.

## What sets this species apart

Androids built to channel an element. The specific elemental powers (metal flow, intangibility, wind) are Set at the Breed/Race layer.
