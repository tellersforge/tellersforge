# Dragon shapeshifter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

True dragons who have learned to walk as people. In human form they are tall, striking and hard to look away from, with eyes the wrong colour and a voice that fills a room. The dragon never quite leaves: they see in the dark, they shrug off the heat or cold of their own element, and they have a hoarder's eye for anything fine.

They take human shape to trade, to learn, to rule quietly behind a throne or simply to be curious. They live for centuries and plan accordingly, and they find the hurry of short-lived peoples faintly amusing. A clever scholar can sometimes spot one by the collection: every book, every coin, every gem arranged just so, and never, ever lent out.

## Not to be confused with

*[Dragon-blooded folk](draconic-dragon-blooded-folk.md)* are mortals with a trace of dragon ancestry; the dragon shapeshifter is a true dragon wearing a human body as a garment.

## What sets this species apart

True dragons wearing human form: they keep a dragon's presence, senses, hoard instinct and elemental hardiness. Resist Element is the dragon's own element; Fire Blast affinity stands in for any breath.
