# Post-apocalyptic underclass

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md)*

Rat-folk of the ruined world: small, tailed, grey-brown and wiry, with busy hands and noses that twitch at every smell. They are everywhere in the broken cities, because they breed quickly and eat almost anything, and they do the work nobody else will: hauling rubble, clearing drains, carrying loads for masters who look down on them.

Larger predator-folk treat them as labour at best and a pest at worst, and the rat-folk have learned to keep their heads low and their eyes open. They are quick, resourceful and good at vanishing into holes when trouble comes. Among themselves they are loyal to family and burrow, and their songs are full of jokes at the expense of the powerful.

## Not to be confused with

*[Rat-kind](rodent-rat-kind.md)* covers rat-folk in general; the post-apocalyptic underclass are the rat-folk of the fallen world, marked less by body than by the hard place they hold beneath larger peoples.

## What sets this species apart

No shared difference: the family covers these rat-folk; their underclass status is cultural and handled in the Culture step.
