# Alluring bird-fey kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

She seems to be a beautiful woman, perhaps a little wild, with a lilting voice and a gaze that is hard to look away from. That allure is not chance. These fey are daughters of an old bird-spirit lineage, and a natural charm hangs about them like scent. Men lose their heads, and occasionally their lives, pursuing them.

When angered or threatened, they change. Great wings burst from their shoulders, their hands and feet become talons, their faces sharpen into something beaked and terrible. In that winged, harpy-like form they are fierce hunters, and few who see it forget it. Most live quietly among humans, marrying and keeping homes. They make loyal friends and devoted lovers, but betrayal can bring out the bird, and a man who has broken his word to one should look up before he steps outside.

## Not to be confused with

*[harpy-kind](avian-harpy-kind.md)*, monstrous bird-women who always wear that shape; the bird-fey pass as human and take the winged form only in fury.

## What sets this species apart

Human-passing bird-fey with innate allure and a fierce winged form. Monstrous Form is the winged harpy-like shape.
