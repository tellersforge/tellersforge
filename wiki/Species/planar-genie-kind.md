# Genie-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

Beings made not of flesh but of a single primal substance: smokeless fire, rolling water, or some other raw stuff of creation. In their own form they are tall, proud and shifting, with bodies that flicker at the edges. They can go unseen at will, wear any shape they like, and slip into a lamp, a bottle or a ring if bound there.

Their fame comes from wishes. A genie who is bound or indebted may grant one, and the stories agree that it will be granted exactly as spoken, which is rarely how it was meant. They can be pious or wicked, helpful or spiteful, and they are almost always proud. Fire-genies are counted the most fearsome, water-genies the hardest to bind. Mortals approach them politely and word their requests with great care.

## Not to be confused with

the *[bound wish-spirit-kind](trans-dimensional-bound-wish-spirit-kind.md)*, spirits chained to grant wishes, where genies are proud, free beings of primal element who only sometimes deign to.

## What sets this species apart

Beings made of a primal element (smokeless fire, water) who can reshape themselves and bargain in wishes. The element (fire, water) is named per breed.

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
