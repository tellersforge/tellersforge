# Beast-tribe folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

Humans with the marks of animals on them: pointed ears high on the head, a tail, a wet nose, fur along the arms. Their senses are sharper than a human's, especially hearing and smell, and they tend to move with a restless animal grace. Some live as a quiet minority in human kingdoms, putting up with suspicion and unfair laws. Some are bred servants and soldiers with the heads of beasts, raised for obedience. Some are feral raiders with horns and hooves who fight for whoever feeds them.

The hyena-headed clans are a people of their own: pack-minded, loud, strong-jawed and hard to shake off a trail. Whatever their shape, beast-tribe folk share one thing, which is that other people rarely see past the ears.

## Not to be confused with

*[uplifted animal folk](hybrid-and-chimeric-uplifted-animal-folk.md)*, animals raised to sapience; beast-tribe folk are human stock with animal features, not animals made human.

## What sets this species apart

Humans with animal features and sharper animal senses.
