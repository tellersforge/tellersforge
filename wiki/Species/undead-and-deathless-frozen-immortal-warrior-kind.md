# Frozen immortal warrior-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

An ancient warrior people who made a bargain with the cold. By a rite no one living remembers, they bound themselves into bodies preserved like meat in a glacier: pale, hard, rimed with frost at the edges. They do not tire. They do not starve. Cut them down and, as often as not, they stand back up.

They remember everything, which is the trouble. Grudges older than kingdoms, oaths to rulers long turned to dust, battles no historian recorded: all of it is fresh to them. Cold cannot touch them, and they march through blizzards in silence. When a frozen legion wakes, the lands around it learn quickly that there is no bargaining with people who have waited a thousand winters for their chance.

## Not to be confused with

*[Ice sovereign undead-kind](undead-and-deathless-ice-sovereign-undead-kind.md)*, the winter lords who raise and command the dead; these warriors keep their own minds and fight in person.

## What sets this species apart

Ice-preserved immortal warriors, utterly tireless and nearly unkillable. Resist Element is cold.
