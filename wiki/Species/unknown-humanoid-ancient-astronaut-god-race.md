# Ancient astronaut god-race

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md)*

Long ago, visitors came down from the stars in fire and thunder, and the people who saw them fell on their faces. These are those visitors. They are mortal aliens, humanoid and long-lived, and every miracle ever credited to them was a machine: lightning from a staff, healing in a golden chamber, a chariot that climbed the sky.

Some of them encouraged the mistake, and ruled as gods. Some left and were remembered in carvings and myths. A few are still around, patient, aloof and very well armed, and their wonders are devices rather than any gift of their bodies. Scholars who trace old gods back to them are laughed at, until one of the old gods turns up.

## Not to be confused with

*[Divine overseer-kind](planar-divine-overseer-kind.md)*, true divine beings who watch over mortals; these ancient visitors are mortal aliens whose godhood is all technology.

## What sets this species apart

Mortal aliens whose 'divinity' is advanced technology. Their god-like abilities are gear, not powers.
