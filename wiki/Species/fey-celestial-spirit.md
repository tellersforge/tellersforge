# Celestial spirit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Spirits of the heavenly courts, sent down on errands the gods would rather not run themselves. Some are dancers of the high halls, who take whatever shape will best tempt a holy man away from his vows, and report back on how well he held out. Others walk the earth on bright wings, cast out of paradise for some old fault and working their way home by guarding mortals who never learn their names.

They are beautiful in a way that makes people forget what they came to say. They are at ease in temples, shrines and sacred groves, and a priest who finds one kneeling beside him is never sure whether he is being blessed or examined. Villages that know of them leave flowers at crossroads and hope to be judged kindly.

## Not to be confused with

*[celestial-kind](planar-celestial-kind.md)*, the planar servants of the heavens themselves; celestial spirits are fey of the divine courts, closer to the mortal world and far more playful.

## What sets this species apart

Heavenly spirits of divine courts, alluring and at ease with the sacred.
