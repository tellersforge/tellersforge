# Insatiable giant-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

It was a person once, before the winter closed in and the food ran out and they did the thing that must never be done. Now it is a giant: impossibly tall and gaunt, ribs showing through grey skin, antlers branching from its skull, lips gnawed to rags. It is always hungry, and the more it eats, the hungrier it becomes.

It hunts in the deep cold, among snowbound forests where no one will hear. Frost and bitter wind do not trouble it at all. It is said that simply being near one sours the mind, and that those it touches begin to dream of meat. The peoples of the northern woods speak of it quietly, and never by name after dark.

## Not to be confused with

*[Giant-kind](../Species_Families/giant-kind.md)*, the living giants of the world; this one is a starved, cursed thing of the dead winter, and it never stops eating.

## What sets this species apart

A gaunt winter hunter driven by endless hunger. Resist Element is cold.
