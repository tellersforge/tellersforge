# Spacefaring canine

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md)*

Dog-faced humanoids from a distant world, with floppy ears, a wagging tail and an expression of perpetual enthusiasm. Spacefaring canines serve aboard starships and in the ports of many worlds, and they are famous for two things. The first is that they are loyal and brave to a fault, the kind of crewmate who will run back into a burning corridor for a friend.

The second is that they are dreadfully clumsy. They trip over cables, knock over drinks, lean on the wrong control and apologise with their whole body. Crews learn to keep them away from delicate machinery and very close in a fight, where their courage and sharp noses more than make up for the broken cups.

## Not to be confused with

*[Canine folk](canine-canine-folk.md)*, the wider family of dog-people, most of whom are rather more graceful.

## What sets this species apart

Dog-faced humanoid aliens who are notably clumsy and accident-prone.
