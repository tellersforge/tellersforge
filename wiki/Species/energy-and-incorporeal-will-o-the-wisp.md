# Will-o-the-wisp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md)*

A pale light floating over the marsh at night, bobbing like a lantern held by someone walking just ahead. Travellers lost in the fog see it and follow, relieved, thinking it a guide or a cottage window. The light moves away as they approach, always a little farther on. Then the ground gives way beneath them, and the bog takes them.

The wisps are tiny spirits of light, and nobody is sure whether they are cruel, playful or simply hungry. Some say they are the souls of those who drowned in the fens, doomed to lure others. Others say they feed on fear. Country folk give the same advice everywhere: never follow a light you can't reach, stay on the path, and if you must cross the marsh at night, turn your coat inside out and walk with your eyes on your feet.

## Not to be confused with

*[light spirit guide](energy-and-incorporeal-light-spirit-guide.md)*, a kindly spirit of light that leads the lost to safety; the wisp leads them to their deaths.

## What sets this species apart

Glowing marsh lights that lure travellers off safe paths.
