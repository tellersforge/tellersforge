# Keen-sensed folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Near-humans with an unusual eye, ear or organ that tells them more than most people know. Some carry eyes on stalks or set wide apart, and can watch the whole room without turning their heads. Some have four eyes and a pilot's reflexes. Some have huge dark eyes for sunless worlds and move gracefully in the gloom. Some grow horns that feel sound, emotion or the shape of what comes next. A domed, earless people hear through their bones and make music no one else can follow.

Whatever their gift, they are hard to sneak up on and hard to lie to. The rest of the world thinks them twitchy. They think the rest of the world is half deaf and half blind.

## Not to be confused with

the *[antennae-sensory folk](humankind-antennae-sensory-folk.md)*, who sense through antennae; these peoples each carry a different extra eye, ear or horn.

## What sets this species apart

Each breed carries an unusual eye, ear or head organ, which gives all of them sharper senses. The specific sense (wide vision, dark sight, horn-sense, bone hearing) is Set at Breed/Race level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
