# Pocket-realm dwellers

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Folk whose home is a small, folded place tucked outside ordinary space: a single valley that loops back on itself, a tower that is bigger inside, a hidden village that appears one day in a hundred. Their bodies are ordinary enough, but they carry a feel for folds and seams, and many can open a small pocket of their own or step into one.

Some are playful little tricksters, some grave caretakers of a linking realm, some sleepy hermits who have half forgotten their own names, and some descendants of a lost colony practising the folk magic of their ancestors. Visitors to their realms learn quickly that the rules there are the dweller's rules, and that leaving is harder than arriving.

## Not to be confused with

*[Dimension-travellers](trans-dimensional-dimension-travellers.md)* roam across many worlds; pocket-realm dwellers are natives of one small folded realm and masters of its seams.

## What sets this species apart

Natives of small folded realms, with a knack for shaping and entering such pockets. Affinity only; a hermit's power over its own domain is a breed-level line.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
