# Alternate-dimension native-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Ordinary peoples of extraordinary places. Each evolved in its own alternate dimension: a hostile zone of opposite matter, a kingdom at the scale of an atom, the dark domain of a tyrant lord, a realm of living shadow or a nexus of mystic courts. At home, they are simply people with ordinary bodies, farms, wars and families.

They vary a great deal: armoured insect hives, tusked brutes of immense strength, human-like courtiers, shadow beings and energy-thieves. What unites them is where they come from and how strange that place looks to outsiders. When they cross into other worlds they find the rules different, and the locals find them just as odd in return.

## Not to be confused with

*[Parallel-world counterparts](trans-dimensional-parallel-world-counterparts.md)* are copies of known folk from another version of this world; alternate-dimension native-kind are wholly separate peoples from wholly different dimensions.

## What sets this species apart

Physical peoples native to their own alternate dimensions, with ordinary bodies there, so the family's otherworldly body traits do not apply. Chitin, strength and energy-draining breeds add their own lines.
