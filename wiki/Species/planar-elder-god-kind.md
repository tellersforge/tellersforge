# Elder god-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

The gods before the gods. They ruled when the world was young and raw, long before any pantheon mortals remember, and their power was of a different order: they raised continents, split the seas and shaped the first living things. Then came wars no history records, and most of them fell.

The survivors sleep. Some lie beneath mountains, some at the bottom of the sea, some in the spaces between the stars. They are vast, strange and indifferent to mortal concerns, and their dreams leak into the world as omens, cults and madness. The younger gods fear them. Wise mortals pray that they keep sleeping, and fools try to wake them.

## Not to be confused with

the *[mountain-realm god-kin](planar-mountain-realm-god-kin.md)* and the other later pantheons, who rule and walk the world today, where the elder gods came before them and mostly sleep.

## What sets this species apart

Primordial pre-pantheon deities of world-shaping power, mostly dormant. NPC-scale. Lifespan (ageless or immortal) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
