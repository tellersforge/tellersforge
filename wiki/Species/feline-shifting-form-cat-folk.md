# Shifting-form cat-folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md)*

One people, many bodies. A litter of these cat-folk may include a child who grows up small and quick, one who looks almost human with only slit eyes and faint fur, one who becomes a sturdy digitigrade trader, one who swells into a hulking warrior, and one who drops to all fours and grows to the size of a war-horse. Which form a child will take depends on the phase of the moon at their birth, or so their priests say.

Despite all that, they are one culture, and they know it. The small ones scout and steal, the standard ones trade and bargain, the near-human ones deal with outsiders, the great ones fight or serve as mounts for their kin, and the tiniest, housecat-sized ones are kept as beloved companions. Outsiders find the arrangement bewildering. The cat-folk find it natural, and gently mock anyone born in only one shape.

## Not to be confused with

*[warrior cat-folk](feline-warrior-cat-folk.md)*, many separate feline peoples with fighting cultures; the shifting-form folk are one people born into many different bodies.

## What sets this species apart

One people born into very different body forms; the forms differ from each other, not the people as a whole from the family. Each body form (tiny, standard, near-human, war-mount) sets its own lines at Breed/Race level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
