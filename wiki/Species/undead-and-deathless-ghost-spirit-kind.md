# Ghost/spirit-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

The dead who did not leave. A ghost may be a revered ancestor who blesses the household that remembers it, or a lost soul tied to a place and a piece of business it cannot finish. Some slip inside the living to borrow their hands; some inhabit corpses; some are drowned women singing by the river, or bottomless hungers with a throat too narrow to swallow.

Most are hard to notice. They are cold spots, flickers at the edge of sight, a door that closes by itself. Walls and flesh are no great obstacle to them. Priests, exorcists and mediums make their living from them, and nearly every people has rules for keeping the dead content: offerings, prayers, and the name spoken gently, never in anger.

## Not to be confused with

*[Incorporeal spirit](energy-and-incorporeal-incorporeal-spirit.md)*, any bodiless being of spirit; ghost/spirit-kind are specifically the dead, and their ties to the living shape everything they do.

## What sets this species apart

Spirits of the dead: hard to notice and inclined to pass through things or into the living. Truly incorporeal breeds Set Phasing at breed level.

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
