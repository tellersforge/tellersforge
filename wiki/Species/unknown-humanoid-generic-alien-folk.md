# Generic alien folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md)*

The crowd in the spaceport bar. The crew of the freighter at the next berth. The clerk at the customs desk with feathers, or scales, or three eyes, or a face like a hammer. Generic alien folk are the countless peoples of a busy galaxy who fit no neat family and need no deep study: citizens of many shapes going about ordinary lives.

Some are small, round and cheerful, with one great eye. Some are squat, grey and obsessed with forms in triplicate, write terrible poetry, and will demolish a house for a road. Most are simply there, filling the stations, docks and markets. They share nothing but being alien, and being, in their own eyes, perfectly normal.

## Not to be confused with

*[Near-human alien](humankind-near-human-alien.md)*, strangers who pass easily for human; generic alien folk come in every shape, and share only their strangeness.

## What sets this species apart

Miscellaneous aliens with nothing shared beyond the family's GM-chosen lines.
