# Headless rider-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

Hoofbeats on an empty road at night, and then the horse: black, foam-flecked, eyes like lanterns. Its rider has no head on his shoulders. He carries it under one arm, or holds it high like a lamp, and the grin on it is the worst thing about him. Where he stops, someone dies.

The headless rider is a harbinger, and he and his steed are one creature in two bodies. The black horse obeys him as no living mount obeys anyone, while other beasts shy and bolt at his scent. Doors do not keep him out, and he is said to blind anyone who watches him pass. Travellers who hear the hooves are advised to get off the road, close their eyes, and pray.

## Not to be confused with

*[Banshee-kind](undead-and-deathless-banshee-kind.md)*, another omen of death, who wails rather than rides; the headless rider arrives in person, on his black steed.

## What sets this species apart

A headless death-harbinger bound to its black steed. Its own steed is exempt from the family's Animal Handling penalty in play.
