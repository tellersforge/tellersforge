# Parasitic hive-mind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md)*

Tiny organisms, worm-like or cellular, that have no bodies worth speaking of and do not need them. They burrow into a host, wind through its nerves and take over, and from then on the host walks, talks and fights for them. One infected person becomes several, then a village, then a spreading mass of bodies all moving to the same purpose.

Outside a host they are nothing but a creeping film or a cloud of spores, and they have no hands of their own. Inside, they have whatever the host had: strength, training, a trusted face. Whether they truly think is a matter of dispute. What is not disputed is that they spread, adapt and do not stop, and that the only certain cure has always been fire.

## Not to be confused with

*[serpent parasite folk](hybrid-and-chimeric-serpent-parasite-folk.md)*, where a single thinking creature takes one host at a time; the parasitic hive-mind spreads through many bodies and shares one purpose among them.

## What sets this species apart

Tiny organisms that act by seizing host bodies, sharing one purpose across the infection. NPC-scale. While in a host, the host body's physical skills apply. No hands of its own: held weapons usable only through a host.
