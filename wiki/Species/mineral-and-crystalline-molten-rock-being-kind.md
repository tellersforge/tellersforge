# Molten rock-being kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

People of living lava, from the deep molten layers far beneath the crust. Their bodies glow orange and red at the joints, cracked with dark cooling rock like the skin of a lava flow, and the air around them shimmers. Wood smoulders where they stand. Stone softens under their hands.

They are immensely strong and at home in heat that would kill anyone else. What harms them is cold, and above all sudden cold: a flood of water or a blast of freezing wind can harden them in place and crack them apart. They rarely come to the surface and are not fond of those who live there, whom they regard as fragile and noisy. When they do rise, usually through a new vent or a mine dug too deep, everyone within miles knows about it.

## Not to be confused with

the *[meteor-borne rock being](mineral-and-crystalline-meteor-borne-rock-being.md)*, whose element depends on its stone and which falls from the sky, where these beings rise from the deep earth and are always fire.

## What sets this species apart

Living molten rock from deep magma layers: radiates heat, but rapid cooling harms it. NPC. Element is fire. Vulnerable to rapid cooling.
