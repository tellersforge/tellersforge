# Elemental spirit-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Fey whose nature is fused with one of the great elements. Some are made of air: light, swift and changeable, dancing on the wind, bringing storms or dispelling them. Others are made of fire, wreathed in flame, quick to anger and immune to burning. Their element hangs about them as a shield, turning aside blows and scorching those who strike, and in a moment of anger or need it bursts from them like a gale or a blaze.

The best known are spirits of wind and fire bound, in many stories, to lamps, rings or pacts, and obliged to grant wishes. They grant them with a sly literalness that makes most wishes regretted. They are shape-changers and tricksters, proud and capricious, and they are never truly servants. A wise master uses one sparingly and phrases every request with care.

## Not to be confused with

*[elemental spirit folk](elemental-kind-elemental-spirit-folk.md)*, elemental beings that simply are their element; elemental spirit-kind are fey bound to one element, with fey cunning and bargains.

## What sets this species apart

Fey bound to one element, which shields them and can burst from them. The Breed/Race names the element (air or fire).
