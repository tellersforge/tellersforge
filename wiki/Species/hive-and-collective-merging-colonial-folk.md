# Merging colonial folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md)*

A people made of many who can become one. Alone, each is a small, soft-bodied creature with a modest mind. When they choose, they come together, press into one another and merge, and the result is a single larger being with a single, much larger mind. The merged body is stronger, cleverer and more patient than any of its parts, and it thinks thoughts none of them could.

When the merged being comes apart, each small body remembers something of the whole, and misses it. Their society is built around choosing when to join and when to separate, and who joins with whom. Outsiders are often unsure which one they are talking to, and are sometimes told, a little impatiently, that the question does not quite make sense.

## Not to be confused with

*[colonial jelly folk](amorphous-and-ooze-colonial-jelly-folk.md)*, whose bodies are permanently colonies; the merging folk are separate people who choose to fuse, and can part again.

## What sets this species apart

Individuals who can physically fuse into one larger being with a shared mind. Hive Mind applies only while merged; the physical fusion itself is a descriptive trait.
