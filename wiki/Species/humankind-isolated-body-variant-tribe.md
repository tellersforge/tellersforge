# Isolated body-variant tribe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Small, cut-off human tribes that each grew one feature to extremes. On one island, arms long enough to pick fruit from the high branches and pull a fish from deep water. On the next, legs so long that the villagers stride through the surf and see over the trees. Elsewhere, a neck that bends and stretches to look round corners. A near-vanished line born with a third eye can read a script no one else remembers, and is hunted for it. A sky-island people carry small nubs of wings from ancestors who once flew.

They are otherwise quite ordinary, and rather tired of being stared at. Travellers who mock them usually find out how far a long arm can reach.

## Not to be confused with

the *[multi-limbed folk](humankind-multi-limbed-folk.md)*, who have more limbs; these tribes have the usual number, stretched or changed in one striking way.

## What sets this species apart

Each tribe evolved a different body exaggeration (arms, legs, neck, third eye, vestigial wings), so nothing is shared at species level.
