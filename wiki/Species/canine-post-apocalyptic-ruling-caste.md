# Post-apocalyptic ruling caste

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md)*

Wolves in uniform. In the ruined lands after the fall of the old world, a nation of animal peoples rose, and at its head stands a caste of upright wolves who rule through discipline and force. They march in tight ranks, salute their officers and hunt in coordinated packs, and their keen noses and ears make them excellent sentries and trackers.

Their bodies are those of ordinary wolf-folk; what sets them apart is the training and the order they impose. Lesser peoples of the nation serve under them, some willingly, many not. To outsiders they are efficient, frightening and certain of their right to rule, and very few of them have ever been asked to explain why.

## Not to be confused with

*[Canine folk](canine-canine-folk.md)*, whose wolf-folk share the same body, without the uniform, the drill or the conviction that they were born to command.

## What sets this species apart

No species-wide difference in body: the soldier discipline and pack tactics are cultural and handled in the Culture step.
