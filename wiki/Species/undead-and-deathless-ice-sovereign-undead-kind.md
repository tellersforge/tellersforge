# Ice sovereign undead-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

Pale lords of the long winter, with eyes the blue of glacier ice and armour that looks carved from frost. They are ancient and terribly clever, and they do not fight their wars alone. Behind them march the dead, raised in their thousands, mindless and obedient, through storms that would freeze a living army where it stood.

Cold is their element; they walk in blizzards as others walk in spring rain. Their weakness is famous and rarely useful: struck by the right weapon, a sovereign shatters like a dropped goblet. Finding that weapon, and a hand steady enough to hold it, is the business of legends. Most who face them see only the endless ranks of the dead, and the pale figure watching from the ridge.

## Not to be confused with

*[Frozen immortal warrior-kind](undead-and-deathless-frozen-immortal-warrior-kind.md)*, ice-preserved soldiers who fight in person; the sovereign commands, and fills its ranks with the reanimated dead.

## What sets this species apart

Ancient ice-bodied undead lords of winter who command the reanimated dead. Resist Element is cold. Shattering to the right weapon is a flaw.
