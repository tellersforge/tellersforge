# Witch-touched folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Near-humans born with a mystic power in the blood: the knack of moving things, reading minds or bending the world by will alone. Horned, pale or red-skinned clans keep witch traditions handed down from mother to daughter and fight in brotherhoods beside them. Sharp-toothed people with ridged skulls bred the gift into their lines for so long that it runs in almost all of them. A pale folk hear thoughts easily, and a tiny, wrinkled green people produce sages of frightening age and calm.

Other peoples court them and watch them. A witch-touched child is a treasure to its family and a matter of concern to its neighbours, and more than one old order has tried to take such children young and raise them its own way.

## Not to be confused with

the *[mutant-gene human](humankind-mutant-gene-human.md)*, whose power is a random accident of the blood; here it is a steady inheritance kept by clan and custom.

## What sets this species apart

Bloodlines carrying an innate mystic power that moves things by will. Affinity only. Breeds with a strong innate gift Set their powers at Breed/Race level; the tough-hided horned breed has none.

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
