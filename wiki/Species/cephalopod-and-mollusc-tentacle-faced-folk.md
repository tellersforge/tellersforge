# Tentacle-faced folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md)*

People with tentacles where a beard might be. Tentacle-faced folk walk upright on two legs, with bones, hands and ordinary gaits, but their faces bristle with soft tentacles that twitch as they speak and taste the air. Some lines have large round eyes and a squid's aspect; others trail sensitive head-tendrils that catch scents and moods; still others are gentle, glowing diplomats, or quiet telepaths who share one mind among many.

Most swim well and hold their breath for a very long time, and many breathe water outright. Their tentacles are for feeling, not gripping. Other peoples tend to be wary of them at first sight, and ashamed of that wariness soon after.

## Not to be confused with

*[Tentacled overlord kind](cephalopod-and-mollusc-tentacled-overlord-kind.md)*, the mind-eating horrors with similar faces, where tentacle-faced folk are ordinary people.

## What sets this species apart

Boned humanoids with facial tentacles: they walk normally, have no colour-changing skin and are not boneless escape artists. Water Breathing stays at species level; a breed that cannot breathe water Sets it to 0. Facial tentacles are not grasping arms: the family grappling bonus is cancelled.
