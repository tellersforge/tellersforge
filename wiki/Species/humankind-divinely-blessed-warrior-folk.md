# Divinely-blessed warrior folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Warrior lineages given strength, speed and toughness by gods who wanted champions. The best known are an island of women trained from girlhood with spear, sword and shield, who do not age while they keep their vigil and die only in battle. An exiled sisterhood of theirs holds a desert fortress and has picked up harder habits and newer weapons. A brother tribe, split from them long ago, herds and fights with the same gifts in far-off hills.

They treat their blessing as a duty. Other peoples see them as heroes, invaders or legends, depending on which side of the shore they meet them.

## Not to be confused with

the *[divinely-ascended human](humankind-divinely-ascended-human.md)*, a single mortal lifted by a patron; these are whole peoples blessed from birth.

## What sets this species apart

God-blessed lineages with superhuman strength, speed and durability and very long lives. Arms training and warrior society are culture. Lifespan (long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
