# Garuda-folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Tall, winged humanoids with the heads of great birds of prey: hooked beaks, fierce eyes and crests of bright feathers. Garuda-folk are among the strongest fliers of any people, able to climb above storms and cover a day's march before breakfast. On the ground they stride with a hunter's confidence.

Their culture is fiercely individualist. They value personal freedom so highly that restraining another person without cause counts among their worst crimes, and a cage offends them more than a knife. They make restless allies and unwilling subjects. Serpent peoples have old reasons to fear them, and most other peoples simply give them room.

## Not to be confused with

*[Raptor folk](avian-raptor-folk.md)*, the wider kind of birds of prey, most of whom lack the garuda's size, strength and powerful flight.

## What sets this species apart

Bird-of-prey-headed humanoids with powerful flight.
