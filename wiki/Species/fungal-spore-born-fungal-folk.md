# Spore-born fungal folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md)*

A warrior people that grows from spores wherever they land. Where one of them falls, others spring up, already hungry for a fight, and within a season a patch of wasteland can be full of them. They do not court or marry. They simply sprout, and the strongest take charge. Their hides are thick and rubbery, and their big warriors hardly seem to notice wounds that would drop anyone else.

They love battle the way other peoples love feasting. Their tools are crude but they work, and their war-bands are loud, cheerful and appalling. Smaller, weaker kin serve them as runners and fodder, cringing and sly, and they breed fungal beasts for meat and mounts. Settled peoples see them as a plague. They see everyone else as the next good scrap.

## Not to be confused with

*[green-hued brute folk](goblinoid-and-orc-kind-green-hued-brute-folk.md)*, the green-skinned brutes of goblinoid stock; the spore-born folk are true fungi, and their whole culture grows from the spore.

## What sets this species apart

Spore-born warrior stock that springs up anywhere, not slow damp-dwelling growths tied to caves. The warrior's toughness and pain indifference sit at Breed/Race level; the underlings lack them.
