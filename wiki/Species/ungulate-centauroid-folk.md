# Centauroid folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

The body of a heavy grazing beast, and rising from its shoulders the torso, arms and head of a thinking being. Centauroid folk are herbivores and herd creatures to the bone. They live packed close, trust the herd over any stranger, and treat their own territory as the only safe ground in the world.

Open space frightens them. Not darkness, not heights, but the wide, unclaimed plain where no herd has set its mark. They cross it fast, in tight formation, and do not linger. They are wary of outsiders and slow to make alliances. Yet a centauroid's charge is its whole body, and a lance couched on that body strikes like a falling tree.

## Not to be confused with

*[Centaur](equine-and-centauroid-centaur.md)*, the free-roaming horse-bodied people of the plains; centauroid folk are herd-bound and fear the open ground centaurs love.

## What sets this species apart

Centaur bodies that fear open, unclaimed space. A centauroid's charge is its own body, so Lances comes easy.
