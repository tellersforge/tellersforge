# Corvid folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Black-feathered, bright-eyed and forever curious. Corvid folk are crow and raven people, and they are among the cleverest of the bird-kinds: tool-users, puzzle-solvers and collectors of anything shiny, useful or left unattended. Many can mimic any voice they have heard, which makes them gifted messengers, linguists and liars. They eat almost anything, find food where others see rubbish, and live in noisy, gossiping flocks that remember a grudge for years.

Some lines fly strongly; others have lost the knack and make up for it with nimble feet. Everywhere, they carry the trickster's reputation. Farmers curse them, thieves' guilds recruit them, and old tales set them on battlefields and gallows, waiting. Most corvid folk find this unfair, and also quite funny.

## Not to be confused with

*[Songbird & small-bird folk](avian-songbird-and-small-bird-folk.md)*, who also mimic and sing, but are smaller, lighter and far less interested in other people's belongings.

## What sets this species apart

Clever scavenger birds and gifted mimics. Flight varies by breed and is Set there.
