# Elemental spirit folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md)*

These are not people with a trace of fire or water in the blood. They are the element itself, gathered into a shape that walks and speaks. A flame-bodied figure crackles as it crosses a room and leaves scorch marks where it rests its hand. A snow-bodied one frosts the window it stands beside. Water spirits move like a current in a woman's form, and small lizard-shaped things are said to bask inside a blacksmith's forge without harm.

Their bodies spill their element around them, and their moods follow it: the fire-folk are quick and volatile, the ice-folk cool and slow to warm. Each kind carries its own frailty. A flame can be doused, and snow melts beside a hearth. Villagers treat them as weather with a will of its own. The greatest of them, bound by ancient pacts to mages, can wear the shape of a small animal and still hold the power to flatten a valley.

## Not to be confused with

*[elemental-blooded folk](elemental-kind-elemental-blooded-folk.md)*, who are mortal people with an element in their ancestry; elemental spirit folk are the element given a body.

## What sets this species apart

Spirit beings whose bodies give off their element around them. Elemental weaknesses (flame put out by water, ice melting) belong to the Breed/Race layer.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
