# Angel-winged alien kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

Tall, beautiful and terrible, with great white-feathered wings and the faces of men and women. Angel-winged aliens are visitors from another star who came to younger worlds long ago, walked among primitive peoples and left behind the stories of angels. They fly with ease and are far stronger than their graceful build suggests, able to lift a loaded cart or break a gate.

Their motives are old and not easily read. Some interfered to guide, some to rule, some simply to study. Those who meet one today usually feel an echo of the old awe, and rarely know whether they are being protected, tested or used.

## Not to be confused with

*[Winged sky human](avian-winged-sky-human.md)*, winged people of human descent who live on sky islands and have neither the strength nor the history.

## What sets this species apart

Great feathered-winged humanoids with true flight and superhuman strength. Human heads: no beak.
