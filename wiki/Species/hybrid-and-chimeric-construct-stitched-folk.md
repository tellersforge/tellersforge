# Construct/stitched folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

People who were assembled rather than born. Their bodies are made of borrowed pieces sewn, grafted or fused together, and the seams usually show. Some are patchwork servants built by an obsessive surgeon, who swap organs with one another when something wears out. Some are strange, childlike creatures made from what a curse left behind of a human body, who carry fragments of someone else's memories.

No animal went into their making. What they have instead is a body that shrugs off ordinary injury, since what was never alive in the ordinary way does not die in the ordinary way. They are often gentle and eager to please, often lonely, and endlessly puzzled by questions of who they really are and whether their old parts remember them.

## Not to be confused with

*[construct-kind](constructs-and-synthetics-construct-kind.md)*, the golems and machines built from clay or metal; stitched folk are made from living flesh, even if it came from elsewhere.

## What sets this species apart

Assembled rather than born, with no animal in the mix; bodies shrug off ordinary injury. Pain Tolerance copied from the Constructs and synthetics family.
