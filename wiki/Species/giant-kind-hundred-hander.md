# Hundred-hander

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Giant-kind](../Species_Families/giant-kind.md)*

Storm giants beyond the ordinary, each with a hundred arms and fifty heads. Every head keeps watch in a different direction, so nothing creeps up on them. Every hand can grasp a boulder, and when they hurl them all at once the hills shake as if in an earthquake. They can hold a shield in one set of arms and swing weapons with several more.

In the old stories they were the jailers of the fallen titans, set to guard the prison at the bottom of the world, and they are said to be there still. Some believe that earthquakes are the hundred-handers shifting at their posts, and thunder their many voices arguing. They are seldom seen. When they are, it is usually because something dreadful has escaped.

## Not to be confused with

*[titan-kind](giant-kind-titan-kind.md)*, the primordial giants whom the hundred-handers once guarded; the hundred-handers are wardens and weapons, not lords of creation.

## What sets this species apart

Fifty heads watch everywhere and a hundred arms throw and grasp; storm and earthquake giants. NPC-scale. A hundred arms: may wield a second weapon alongside a shield.
