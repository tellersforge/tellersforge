# Tunneling pest folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md)*

Small, twitchy diggers who live in warrens of their own making under hills, mines and old ruins. They are furred and blunt-snouted, with strong little hands for scraping and eyes that squint at daylight. Each tunnel tribe keeps a candle-lit maze of passages, and every passage has a bolt-hole at the far end.

Alone, one of them is no threat to anybody, and they know it. They run from a raised boot and hide from a lantern. In numbers they are another matter: a mine that fills with them fills quickly, and they defend their holes with sharpened sticks, thrown stones and a great deal of shrieking. Miners treat them as vermin, and the tunnel folk return the favour, stealing candles, tools and anything else left lying about below ground.

## Not to be confused with

*[Kobold-kind](small-folk-kobold-kind.md)*, the small-folk burrowers, are scaly trap-makers; tunneling pest folk are furred rodents who rely on numbers and flight rather than cunning snares.

## What sets this species apart

Small tunnel-dwellers, at home underground.
