# Alien warlord race

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Near-human aliens with soft, slug-like faces, damp skin and an air of permanent calculation. Their bodies are nothing special: no stronger, faster or tougher than any other soldier. What makes them feared is their thinking. They plan wars like games, hire other people to fight them, and keep mind-controlling devices and clever gadgets in every pocket.

They tend to appear as warlords, crime bosses and scheming generals, always one move ahead and always with a way out. Those who deal with them count their fingers afterwards, and then check again.

## Not to be confused with

the *[tentacle-faced folk](cephalopod-and-mollusc-tentacle-faced-folk.md)*, whose features are cephalopod rather than slug; these warlords rely on cunning, not body.

## What sets this species apart

Near-human aliens with slug-like features and ordinary physical ability; their edge is cunning, which is culture and training. Mind-control devices are gear, not powers.
