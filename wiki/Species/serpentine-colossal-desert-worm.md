# Colossal desert worm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md)*

A worm the size of a hill, moving beneath the sand the way a whale moves under the sea. It has no eyes, no limbs and no venom, only a vast ringed body and a round mouth lined with teeth like a quarry's worth of broken stone. A dune ripples, the ground hums, and then the whole desert seems to open.

It does not think in any way a person would recognise. It follows vibration and swallows whatever makes it, whether a caravan, a harvester or a careless camp. Desert peoples revere it all the same, because its passage leaves behind a precious substance they gather and trade. Their lives are shaped by it: how they walk, how they build and who they pray to.

## Not to be confused with

*[World serpents](draconic-world-serpents.md)* are mythic serpents large enough to circle a world; the colossal desert worm is an eyeless burrowing animal, vast but mindless and bound to the sand.

## What sets this species apart

Colossal eyeless desert worms that tunnel through sand; not venomous or hypnotic, and cannot climb. NPC-scale, non-sentient. It swallows rather than constricts; the bite is NPC-scale. No hands: held weapons unusable.
