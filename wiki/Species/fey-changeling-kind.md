# Changeling-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

The thing left in the cradle when the fey take a child. Sometimes it is a fey infant, sometimes an ancient fey dressed in a baby's shape, and either way it wears the stolen child's face well enough to fool a tired mother. It fails at the details. It is sickly and pale, it cries too much or never at all, it eats like a grown man and looks at the family with eyes much too old.

Folk tales are full of tricks for unmasking one, and most of them are cruel. A changeling who is not found out grows up in the house, learning to pass among the people it replaced. Some never stop resenting it. A few come to love their borrowed families, and they are the ones who suffer most when the truth comes out.

## Not to be confused with

*[fey-blooded adventurer-kind](fey-fey-blooded-adventurer-kind.md)*, who are mixed-blood mortals with a trace of the fey; a changeling is fey through and through, only wearing a human child's face.

## What sets this species apart

Fey substitutes for stolen children: sickly and off-putting, but able to pass as the child.
