# Giant slug-bodied folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md)*

An enormous mound of slug, glistening and heavy, with small arms, a wide mouth and heavy-lidded eyes that miss nothing. Giant slug-bodied folk barely move. They cannot run, leap or hide, and they do not try. They are carried, or they sit, or they have the world brought to them. They live for centuries, digest almost anything and shrug off poisons that would kill a crew of lesser beings.

Their culture is one of wealth, patience and quiet menace. They run smuggling empires, gambling dens and protection rackets from vast chambers, surrounded by guards and flatterers. They do not need to be fast. By the time a rival realises what has happened, the slug has already won.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mollusc folk](cephalopod-and-mollusc-mollusc-folk.md)*, the ordinary snail and slug peoples, who are slow and small rather than huge, ancient and in charge.

## What sets this species apart

Huge, near-sessile land slugs: they can't run, leap or hide, and shrug off most toxins. Lifespan (long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
