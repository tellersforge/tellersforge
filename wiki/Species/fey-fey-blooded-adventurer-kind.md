# Fey-blooded adventurer-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Folk with a thread of fey blood in them, usually from a grandparent no one likes to talk about. The mixture thins the old glamour down to something a person can live with: a knack for small illusions and charms, a feeling for what others are feeling, a face that can shift a little when it needs to. Some are small, quick and capricious, and outlive the friends they make. Some have soft, dusty moth wings that lift them a short way, and an embarrassing habit of drifting towards lamps.

They belong fully to neither world. The fey find them clumsy and mortal; mortals find them unsettling and hard to pin down. Many take to the road for exactly that reason, and make good company for anyone who can live with a friend who never quite explains herself.

## Not to be confused with

*[changeling-kind](fey-changeling-kind.md)*, who are true fey left in a stolen child's place; fey-blooded folk are born of mixed parents and grow up knowing, more or less, what they are.

## What sets this species apart

Part-fey folk whose mixed blood thins the family's glamour.
