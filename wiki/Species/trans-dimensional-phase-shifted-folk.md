# Phase-shifted folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Beings who live half a step to one side of the solid world. With a shiver and a blur they slip out of phase and become thin as mist: walls pass through them, blades pass through them, and they watch the ordinary world as if through frosted glass. Some kinds blink instead, vanishing in one place and reappearing a few strides away.

They include hit-and-run raiders, patient warrior races, ambushing hunters that come through walls, and grim exiles trapped forever out of phase. Most are long-lived, and the longest-lived grow cold and strange from watching a world they can barely touch. Soldiers hate fighting them. Locks mean nothing, and the blow that should have landed never does.

## Not to be confused with

*[Incorporeal spirit](energy-and-incorporeal-incorporeal-spirit.md)* is a ghostly being with no body at all; phase-shifted folk are solid people who can slip out of phase with matter when they choose.

## What sets this species apart

They slip out of phase with solid matter, so their otherworldly insubstantiality is stronger than the family's. Breeds that blink rather than phase Set Short Teleport at breed level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
