# Shaggy forest folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md)*

Tall, shaggy tribes of the deep forest, covered head to foot in thick hair. Some are broad and heavy, others gangly and long-limbed, but all are built for the cold under the canopy and the long climb up giant trees. They live in villages lashed high among the branches or tucked among the roots.

They are tribal, territorial and quick to defend their patch of forest, whether against outsiders or the neighbouring tribe. Warriors carry clubs and spears; elders carry long memories of every feud. Visitors who come with gifts and patience are sometimes adopted into the village, which is a great honour and a tremendous amount of hugging. Those who come with axes are not.

## Not to be confused with

*[Wild giant hominid](simian-wild-giant-hominid.md)* is a reclusive wanderer who avoids others; shaggy forest folk live in tribes and fiercely hold their own stretch of woodland.

## What sets this species apart

Shaggy tribes bred to deep forest.
