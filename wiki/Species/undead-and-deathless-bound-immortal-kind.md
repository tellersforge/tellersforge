# Bound immortal-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

A house at the edge of the world, or a door that opens onto many roads, and someone who keeps it. The keeper has kept it for a very long time. They are bound there by an old curse or an older duty, and death does not release them. Stab them, burn them, throw them from the tower: sooner or later they return, weary, and pick up the broom.

The binding holds only while they stay. Should they leave the threshold, the gift fails, and a keeper who wanders far is as mortal as anyone. They have met every traveller who ever passed their door, and they remember the stories. Ask politely and they will tell one. Ask for their own, and they will change the subject.

## Not to be confused with

*[Deathless judge-kind](planar-deathless-judge-kind.md)*, who weigh souls at a threshold between worlds; the bound immortal only keeps a house, and cannot leave it without losing its undying gift.

## What sets this species apart

Cursed keepers of a threshold who always return after death and hold many stories. Renewal at Death holds only while bound to its house.
