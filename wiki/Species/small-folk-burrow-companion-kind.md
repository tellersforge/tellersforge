# Burrow companion-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Small folk](../Species_Families/small-folk.md)*

Small four-legged diggers, something like a dog crossed with a mole, with blunt claws, a twitchy nose and big dark eyes. They have no hands and no speech, though they understand more than their size suggests, and they dig tunnels of their own through soft earth and old rubble.

They turn up in ruins, crypts and ancient cellars, and are friendly rather than dangerous. Adventurers who share a crust with one sometimes find it trotting after them for good, pointing out loose stones, sniffing out hidden passages and whining at the approach of danger. They are loyal to a fault, and very hard to leave behind.

## Not to be confused with

*[Small burrow-dwelling kind](rodent-small-burrow-dwelling-kind.md)* are furred rodent folk with hands and speech; burrow companion-kind are four-legged diggers with no hands at all.

## What sets this species apart

Small four-legged diggers with no hands. No hands: held weapons unusable.
