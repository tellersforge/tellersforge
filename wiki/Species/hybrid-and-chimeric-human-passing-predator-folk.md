# Human-passing predator folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md)*

Predators that look exactly like the people they hunt. They walk down the street, sit in cafes and hold jobs, and nothing marks them out until they are hungry. Then something comes out: a blade of bone from the back, a tail, a mouth where no mouth should be, a spiral of powers from a curse in the blood. Most must eat people to live, and many hate themselves for it.

They heal from wounds that would kill anyone, and they are faster and stronger than they let on. Some shun the sun, and some fear a special ore or metal. Some are former spirits who tore off their masks to become human-shaped. All of them live in two worlds at once, and neither one would forgive them if it knew.

## Not to be confused with

*[vampire-kind](undead-and-deathless-vampire-kind.md)*, who are undead drinkers of blood; these predators are living beings with hunger and a hidden weapon inside.

## What sets this species apart

Predators who look fully human and heal fast; they carry no animal features. Disguise Set 0 cancels the family's penalty, since they pass as human. Most must feed on people to survive.
