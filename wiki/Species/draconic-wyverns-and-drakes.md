# Wyverns and drakes

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md)*

Dragons without the minds. Wyverns and drakes are the feral cousins of the great dragons: smaller, fierce, territorial beasts driven by hunger and instinct. Wyverns walk on two legs with their wings joined to their forelimbs and a venomous sting at the end of the tail. Drakes go on four legs, some wingless, some breathing a feeble flame or frost. Others guard temples with two heads, stalk deserts in armoured hides or climb sheer cliffs.

They do not hoard, speak or plan, and few will ever carry a rider. They can be hunted, trapped and sometimes tamed as guard beasts, though their handlers tend to be short of fingers.

## Not to be confused with

*[True dragons](draconic-true-dragons.md)*, the vast, thinking and very long-lived kind, which these beasts resemble only in teeth and temper.

## What sets this species apart

Feral non-sentient dragon-beasts: no hoarding eye or lore, and no body for riding. Flight and breath are Set per breed. No hands: held weapons unusable.
