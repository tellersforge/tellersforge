# Stone folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

The rock-bodied peoples in their plainest form. Their skin is mottled stone, grey or brown or veined with ore, and their bodies are heavy with it. They move slowly, speak slowly and think things through before they act, and they live long enough that most other peoples seem to them like hurrying children.

They are immensely strong and very hard to hurt, but they sink in water and struggle on soft ground. Some lines eat minerals and grind gravel between flat teeth; some grow as large as a house; some are famous musicians who play deep, resonant instruments no one else can lift. Other peoples see them as dependable, stubborn and a little dull, until they are needed to hold a wall or carry a bridge, and then no one complains.

## Not to be confused with

the *[rock-being kind](mineral-and-crystalline-rock-being-kind.md)*, the huge warrior giants of the same stone, where the stone folk are a whole range of peoples of ordinary size.

## What sets this species apart

No different from the family: the rock-bodied peoples are the family's baseline. Mineral-eating, great size and musical cultures differ by Breed/Race or Culture.
