# Mountain-realm god-kin

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

The gods of a classical mountain pantheon: tall, radiant, beautiful and quarrelsome, living in marble halls above the clouds and meddling ceaselessly in mortal affairs. Each is bound to a domain that is part of their very being: war, the sea, love, wisdom, the hunt, messages and swift travel. Their powers grow from that domain, and so does their temper.

They do not age or die, though they can be wounded, imprisoned or forgotten. They remember every slight for centuries and every kindness for rather less. They love heroes, curse fools, father children with mortals and fight among themselves over trifles. Mortals worship them for protection and fear them for exactly the same reason.

## Not to be confused with

the *[sun-realm god-kin](planar-sun-realm-god-kin.md)*, a different pantheon of immortal deities, with their own customs, domains and courts.

## What sets this species apart

Immortal pantheon deities: durable, undying, each empowered by a personal domain. The domain power (war, speed, wisdom and so on) is Set at Breed/Race or character level. Lifespan (very long-lived) is a lexicon detail, not a game number.
