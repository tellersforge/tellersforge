# Avian guardian shapeshifter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

A guardian spirit with two faces: a woman of calm, watchful dignity and a falcon riding the high winds. She changes between them at will, and only in her bird shape does she fly. She wears no other disguise and borrows no other face.

Such guardians are bound to a place, usually a magical land, an old castle or a secret valley, and they watch over it and those who shelter there. They are wise, somewhat aloof and fond of speaking in hints. Heroes who earn the favour of one gain an ally with sharp eyes and a long memory. Those who threaten her domain find that a falcon can strike from the sun before they see her coming.

## Not to be confused with

*[Raptor folk](avian-raptor-folk.md)* are birds of prey by birth; the avian guardian shapeshifter is a spirit who takes falcon form only when she chooses and is bound to protect one land.

## What sets this species apart

A guardian spirit with a single falcon form, not a mimic of other people. Flight only in falcon form.
