# Bio-armored invader

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md)*

What steps out of the rift is tall, grey and wrapped in layered, living armour, plates of hardened flesh grown over one another like the scales of a pine cone. The thing inside is smaller. It rides at the heart of that shell, soft and hidden, and it never willingly comes out.

These invaders come from a fluid dimension beyond normal space and regard every other realm as an infection to be burned away. They are hostile to all contact and answer any incursion into their home with total war. Their armour can take a terrible beating, and it grows back. Those who have broken one open and seen the true body inside rarely describe it the same way twice.

## Not to be confused with

*[Invader kind](cephalopod-and-mollusc-invader-kind.md)*, the tentacled conquerors of other worlds; this invader comes from a fluid dimension, and hides a small body inside living armour.

## What sets this species apart

A small true body inside layered organic armour.
