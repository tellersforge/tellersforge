# Bugbear

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Goblinoid and orc-kind](../Species_Families/goblinoid-and-orc-kind.md)*

Big, hairy goblinoids with long arms and surprisingly soft feet. A bugbear is taller than a man, heavy-shouldered and covered in coarse fur, with a flat nose and yellow eyes that see well in the dark. It looks like it should crash through the undergrowth. Instead it moves like a cat, and the first sign most people have of a bugbear is the long arm reaching out of the shadows behind them.

They are ambushers by preference, patient in hiding and sudden when they strike. Goblin tribes hire or bully them as enforcers, and stories in the hill villages use them to frighten children who wander after dark. They are strong, sly and lazy, and they like best a fight that is over before the other side knows it has begun.

## Not to be confused with

*[hobgoblin](goblinoid-and-orc-kind-hobgoblin.md)*, the disciplined soldiers of goblinkind; bugbears are lone ambushers whose strength comes with a surprising quiet.

## What sets this species apart

Long-limbed brutes who move with surprising stealth.
