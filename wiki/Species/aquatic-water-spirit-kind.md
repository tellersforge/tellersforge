# Water spirit-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md)*

Spirits of springs, rivers, lakes and the sea. Some are gentle nymphs who bless a well or calm a storm. Many are not. A shapeshifter may appear as a fine horse or a young fiddler by the ford, and those who follow it are found drowned. An old, frog-faced man in the millpond punishes anyone who fouls his water. Dark things drag swimmers under at the river bend or pull children through holes in the ice.

Others change skins: seals who become men and women on the shore, river dolphins who come to dance at festivals and slip back into the water before dawn. People who live by water leave gifts, speak politely and never swim at dusk.

## Not to be confused with

the *[water-bodied folk](amorphous-and-ooze-water-bodied-folk.md)*, whose bodies are made of water; water spirits belong to a place and take any shape they like.

## What sets this species apart

Water spirits that lure, charm or drag mortals into their waters.
