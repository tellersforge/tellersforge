# Linked-realm bridge-folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Their homeland lies on a seam. On one side of it is one world, on the other a second, and the bridge-folk live across both. A child steps out to fetch water and returns with a bucket from the other realm, not quite sure which well she used. Their houses have doors that open onto two skies. Their songs, food and law mix both sides so thoroughly that no one remembers which came from where.

They step between the two realms almost without thinking, but only between those two; the wider multiverse is as closed to them as to anyone. Merchants hire them as couriers, spies prize them, and every border guard on either side has learned to ask, politely, which country a bridge-folk traveller thinks she is in today.

## Not to be confused with

*[Dimension-travellers](trans-dimensional-dimension-travellers.md)*, who roam between many worlds by effort or device; the bridge-folk cross only between their own two linked realms, and do it without trying.

## What sets this species apart

A people native to the seam between two linked realms who step between them almost without noticing. Plane Shift reaches only the two linked realms.
