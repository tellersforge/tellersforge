# Household fey

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Small, strange folk who live in a house alongside its family, whether that family knows it or not. The commonest kind comes out at night and does the chores: sweeping, churning, mending, tending the animals. In return it expects a bowl of cream or porridge by the hearth, and above all respect. Insult one, or try to pay it with new clothes, and it may leave in a huff or turn into a noisy, destructive nuisance.

They are found in farmhouses, mines, mills and palaces in many lands, each with its own name and habits. Some are bound to a family by old and powerful magic and serve whether they want to or not, working their own odd spells without a wand. Some are little dragon-spirits that bring their keepers stolen grain and gold, and must be paid in eggs. All of them are best treated politely.

## Not to be confused with

*[household spirit](energy-and-incorporeal-household-spirit.md)*, an ancestral hearth-guardian of one family; household fey are fairy helpers who work for payment and respect.

## What sets this species apart

Hearth-bound spirits whose nature is to serve, guard and keep out of sight in a home.
