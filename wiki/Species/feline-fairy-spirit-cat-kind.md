# Fairy/spirit cat-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md)*

Some cats are more than cats. In the old tales of the highlands, a large black cat with a single white spot on its chest prowls the moors, and the wise know it for a fairy, or a witch in disguise. In the far east, a cat that lives long enough grows a second tail, learns to speak and to change its shape, and the oldest of them can even make the dead rise and dance. A house cat that is cruelly treated may grow monstrous and cunning, and take its revenge.

These spirit cats walk on four legs and deal with the unseen world as easily as ordinary cats deal with mice. They keep bargains strictly and grudges forever. Villagers leave out milk for them on certain nights and speak of them politely, and wise folk never kick a cat they do not know.

## Not to be confused with

*[fox spirit](canine-fox-spirit.md)*, the shapeshifting fox of eastern tales; spirit cats are a similar breed of the uncanny in feline form.

## What sets this species apart

Spirit cats that take other shapes and deal with the unseen world. Raising the dead belongs to the elder spirit breed only. No hands: held weapons unusable.
