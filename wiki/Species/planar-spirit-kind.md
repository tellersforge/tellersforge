# Spirit-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md)*

Spirits that are neither heavenly nor hellish: loose, drifting beings with little or no fixed body, and the folk who have grown close to them. Some are gentle wisps drawn to strong feeling or to magic. Some are dark, faceless, floating things that feed on joy and leave a chill behind them. Some are winged furies who hunt oath-breakers without rest.

They are more at home in the spirit world than in this one, and they sense its moods the way sailors sense weather. A few can slip inside a person and ride them for a while; others can touch minds or feed on souls. Some mortal peoples bond with a kindly spirit from birth, and are said to be almost impossible to possess. Ordinary folk light candles, hang charms, and try not to be noticed by them.

## Not to be confused with

the *[incorporeal spirit](energy-and-incorporeal-incorporeal-spirit.md)*, a lone ghost with no body at all, where spirit-kind are a whole host of planar spirits and spirit-bonded folk.

## What sets this species apart

Loosely embodied spirits or spirit-bonded folk, naturally attuned to the spirit world. Possession, soul-feeding and telepathy differ by breed and go at Breed/Race level.
