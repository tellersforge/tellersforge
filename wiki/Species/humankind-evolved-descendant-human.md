# Evolved-descendant human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

What humans may become if they are left alone long enough. Given ages of separation, a people splits and drifts, and the drift rarely goes somewhere comfortable. Far in the future, the children of labourers have become pale, stooped and afraid of daylight, and they come up at night to feed on the soft, pretty, childlike descendants of their old masters. Elsewhere, savage human-shaped brutes are kept as livestock by a people wiser than they are. In another line of history, heavy-browed kin of an older humanity have taken the worlds that should have been ours.

To baseline humans they are a mirror and a warning. Travellers who meet them usually come home quieter than they went.

## Not to be confused with

the *[adapted human](humankind-adapted-human.md)*, shaped by one harsh place; these are humanity's far descendants, drifted apart by time rather than by climate.

## What sets this species apart

Offshoots evolved in opposite directions (underground, pastoral, savage, conquering), so nothing is shared at species level.
