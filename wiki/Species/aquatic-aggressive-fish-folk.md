# Aggressive fish-folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md)*

Predatory fish-folk who live by hunting and raiding. Sharp teeth, cold eyes that see well in the murk, and a taste for fresh meat. Some are deep-sea raiders who come up in the dark to strike coastal villages. Some are hugely strong amphibious people, once enslaved and now proud and dangerous. Some are slippery cave dwellers who worship gods at the bottom of the sea and are weak and blinking in the light.

Other peoples generally fear them, which they find convenient. Not all of them are cruel, but even the kind ones smile with a lot of teeth.

## Not to be confused with

the *[aquatic folk](aquatic-aquatic-folk.md)*, a broad and varied sea people; these fish-folk are hunters and raiders by nature.

## What sets this species apart

Predatory fish-folk with teeth and hunting instinct.
