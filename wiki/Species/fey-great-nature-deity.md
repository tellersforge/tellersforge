# Great nature deity

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

Behind the ordinary forest spirits stand the great ones, and they are gods in all but name. One walks by day as a deer with a strange, human-like face, and where it steps flowers bloom and wither in a single breath. At dusk it rises into a colossal, translucent shape that strides over the treetops, and it gives life and takes it as easily as breathing.

Another is a huge, furry, gentle guardian, bigger than a house, that sleeps in the hollow of a great tree. Few adults ever see it. Children sometimes do, and ride with it through the night sky. These deities are rarely hostile, but they are vast and alien, and their power over the living wood is total. Kings who try to cut their forests down learn, too late, that some things cannot be conquered, only respected.

## Not to be confused with

*[tree/forest spirit](fey-tree-forest-spirit.md)*, the smaller spirits of trees and groves; the great nature deities are forest gods on a vast scale.

## What sets this species apart

Vast forest deities whose power over the living wood is near-godlike. NPC-scale.
