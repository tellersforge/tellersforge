# Dwarf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Small folk](../Species_Families/small-folk.md)*

Short, stocky and gruff, with jaws strong enough to chew stone. These dwarves quite literally eat their way through rock, grinding it with great flat teeth and passing it out behind them, which makes them unrivalled tunnellers and uncomfortable company in a small room.

They work in digging crews, drive tunnels and mines for anyone who pays, and treat surface folk with brusque impatience. Their humour is coarse, their loyalty solid and their bite, when it comes to it, is very bad news for whoever gets in the way. Many peoples hire them, few invite them to dinner, and nobody who has watched one dig ever forgets it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Dwarf-kind](../Species_Families/dwarf-kind.md)* is the family of stout mountain folk and smiths; this dwarf is the small-folk tunneller who eats through rock with its jaw.

## What sets this species apart

Gruff tunnellers who literally eat through rock. The rock-eating jaw gives a strong bite.
