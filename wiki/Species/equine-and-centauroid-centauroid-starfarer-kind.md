# Centauroid starfarer kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Equine and centauroid](../Species_Families/equine-and-centauroid.md)*

Horse-like folk with long faces, strong four-legged bodies and a fierce intelligence behind their dark eyes. Most races who see them for the first time expect beasts of burden, and are embarrassed when the strangers repair their ship. These are engineers, pilots and scientists, heirs to a civilisation that built vessels to cross the stars while others were still learning to plough.

Their technology is shaped around their bodies: consoles set at chest height, doorways wide and tall, manipulator tools that extend their reach. They are courteous, precise and deeply proud of their achievements, sometimes to the point of condescension. Their ships range far, trading knowledge and machines. Those who treat them as animals discover that they have long memories, excellent weapons and very little patience for fools.

## Not to be confused with

*[centaur](equine-and-centauroid-centaur.md)*, the horse-bodied folk of myth and forest; the starfarers are a technological spacefaring people with a similar body.

## What sets this species apart

No bodily difference from the family: their technical skill and spacefaring life are attributes and culture.
