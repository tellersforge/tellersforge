# Swine folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md)*

Boar-, pig- and warthog-folk: broad, bristled and thick-skinned, with snouts that miss nothing. A swine-folk nose can find truffles under a forest floor, a hidden cellar under a floorboard, or a lie under a merchant's perfume. Many carry tusks, and a lowered head and a rush can knock a warrior flat.

Temperament varies more than bodies do. Wild boar-folk are territorial raiders, quick to fight. Others are stout, industrious workers, hard-bargaining mercenaries, or blunt, combative diplomats who treat argument as sport. Outsiders mock them as greedy or dirty, which says more about the outsiders. Swine folk shrug it off. They are tough, they are clever, and they eat very well.

## Not to be confused with

*[Beastfolk](ungulate-beastfolk.md)*, huge bred labourers and soldiers with porcine features; swine folk are a free people, keener of nose and far more varied in temper.

## What sets this species apart

Rooting, keen-nosed, tough-hided swine rather than herd grazers.
