# Advanced shapeshifting race

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

A refined, far-travelled people whose civilisation blends magic and science so smoothly that outsiders cannot tell which is which. They are human-shaped and handsome, marked by delicate lines on the face that vary by family, and they can alter their looks in small ways: the colour of the hair, the set of the jaw, a little height.

The stronger gifts are rare. A few among them can take entirely new shapes or mend flesh through a subtle alchemy, and those few are honoured, envied and closely watched. The rest simply live long, study hard and carry their history with pride. Other peoples find them polite, reserved and very difficult to read.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mimic infiltrator folk](shapeshifter-mimic-infiltrator-folk.md)* copy others perfectly as a species gift; the advanced shapeshifting race changes only a little, and its great shifters are rare individuals.

## What sets this species apart

Their natural shape-changing is modest; stronger shifting and healing alchemy belong to gifted individuals.
