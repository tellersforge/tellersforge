# Predatory tracker folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md)*

Humanoid at first glance: two legs, two hands, a face that could pass in a crowd. Look again and the eyes are gold with slit pupils, the teeth are sharp, and there is a stillness about them that belongs to something that waits in the dark. Their ancestors were night predators, and something of that survives. They see well by starlight and read a trail with uncanny ease: a bent grass stem, a fleck of blood, a scent on the wind.

They have made a trade of it. Many work as trackers, scouts and bounty hunters, and are in high demand wherever someone needs finding. They keep their own counsel and their own schedules, sleeping late and working at night. Other folk hire them readily enough, but are glad when they leave, and never quite comfortable when those gold eyes rest on them for too long.

## Not to be confused with

*[feline folk](feline-feline-folk.md)*, upright cat people with fur and tails; predatory tracker folk look nearly human and keep only the eyes and teeth.

## What sets this species apart

Nocturnal predator-descended humanoids with a hunter's eye for spoor. Humanoid hands without claws; only the sharp teeth remain, a small bite.
