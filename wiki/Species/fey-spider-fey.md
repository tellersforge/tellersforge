# Spider-fey

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

A fey witch who is a woman above the waist and a great spider below, living in a cave so thick with webs that a visitor must cut a path to reach her. She knows things no one should: the hour of a death, the fate of a lost child, the name a king whispered on his wedding night. She will share what she knows, for a price, and she is always the one who sets the terms.

Those who keep their side of the bargain leave with the truth, which is not always a gift. Those who fail are wrapped in silk and eaten at her leisure. Heroes come to her when every other road is closed, and old stories agree on one piece of advice: read the bargain twice, and never ask a question you are not ready to hear answered.

## Not to be confused with

*[arachnid folk](insectoid-and-arachnid-arachnid-folk.md)*, the spider and scorpion peoples of the ordinary world; the spider-fey is a lone fey witch whose webs catch prophecy as well as prey.

## What sets this species apart

A spider-legged, web-dwelling fey witch with prophetic sight.
