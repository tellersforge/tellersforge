# Silent aquatic race

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md)*

A gentle, retiring sea people who almost never make a sound. They speak mind to mind, softly and only a little, sharing feelings and pictures rather than words. Among themselves this is enough; with outsiders it is maddening, and conversations are slow and full of misunderstanding.

They keep to quiet waters and avoid conflict, and most other peoples know them only as pale shapes watching from under the surface. Those who spend time among them come home speaking less, and listening more.

## Not to be confused with

the *[fish-humanoid amphibian folk](amphibian-fish-humanoid-amphibian-folk.md)*, whose telepathic line also talks aloud; these people rarely make any sound at all.

## What sets this species apart

A near-silent people who speak by limited telepathy.
