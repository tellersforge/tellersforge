# Sand-bodied folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

Desert people made of loosely bound sand and dust, held in a human shape by will. A blow scatters them; they gather themselves back grain by grain. A sandstorm is a pleasant stroll to them, and they can lie in a dune for a week and look like nothing at all. Water is their terror. Soaked through, they slump into mud and set hard when they dry, and it takes a long while to shake themselves loose again.

They trade, guide caravans and keep old desert roads, and their humour is as dry as their homes. Visitors notice that they never stand close to a well.

## Not to be confused with

the *[stone folk](mineral-and-crystalline-stone-folk.md)*, who have solid bodies of rock; sand folk are loose grains held together by will.

## What sets this species apart

Bodies of loose animate sand: desert dwellers that reform after being scattered but turn to mud in water. Element is earth (sand).
