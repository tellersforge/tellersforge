# Rift-touched entities

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md)*

Beneath reality lies a turbulent sea of raw feeling: rage, terror and longing, churning without bodies. Some beings are born there. Flame-shaped spirits cross into the world wearing half-solid human forms; shapeless mind-eaters slip through the thin places and leave empty husks behind; other spirits seize a body or bend stone for a few terrible moments before they are pulled back.

And some were once ordinary mortals who glimpsed that sea and came back changed. They see a little too much, hear thoughts at the edges of rooms, and are never entirely steady again. All of them share one talent: reaching into other minds and taking hold. Priests, mind-healers and the psychically gifted fear them most, because those are the minds that shine brightest to a hungry thing.

## Not to be confused with

*[Rift and portal creatures](trans-dimensional-rift-and-portal-creatures.md)*, which come through tears between worlds to hunt like beasts; rift-touched entities are born of a psychic depth and prey on minds.

## What sets this species apart

Born of or scarred by a turbulent psychic dimension, they reach into and seize other minds.
