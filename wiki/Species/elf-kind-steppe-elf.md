# Steppe elf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

Elves of the open grass, where the sky is enormous and the horizon never ends. They trade the forest's closeness for distance and speed. Their eyes are made for far horizons, picking out a rider or a wolf on the skyline long before anyone else, and their bodies are lean and weathered by wind and sun.

They live in clans that follow the herds and the seasons, carrying their homes with them. Their riders and horse-archers are the stuff of legend among settled peoples, who have seen a steppe elf band appear from nowhere, loose a storm of arrows and vanish before the town gates could close. Among themselves they are communal, generous and bound by kinship. They find forest elves stuffy and city folk incomprehensible, and they are baffled by anyone who would choose a roof over a sky.

## Not to be confused with

*[woodland folk](elf-kind-woodland-folk.md)*, the forest-dwelling elves; steppe elves are the same long-lived stock at home in open grassland instead.

## What sets this species apart

Elf-kind at home in open grassland rather than forest. Their riding and mounted archery are culture, handled in the Culture step.
