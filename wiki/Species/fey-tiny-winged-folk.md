# Tiny winged folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Fey](../Species_Families/fey.md)*

A people no bigger than a hand, with quick bright wings that blur like a hummingbird's. They dart through gardens and hedgerows, investigate everything, and are gone before anyone can decide whether they were really there. Curiosity rules them. They pry into pockets, taste strange foods, follow travellers for miles simply to see where they are going, and forget to be afraid until it is much too late.

They are fast and easily overlooked, which keeps them alive. Cats, owls and children with jars are their great enemies. Despite some old confusion they have nothing to do with bats, whatever their leathery-winged kin might suggest. Most people who meet one are charmed; most who try to keep one discover how clever a small, determined prisoner can be.

## Not to be confused with

*[fairy folk](fey-fairy-folk.md)*, the family's classic small winged fey with their charms and illusions; the tiny winged folk rely on speed and curiosity rather than magic.

## What sets this species apart

Tiny, quick winged fae who fly and slip past notice.
