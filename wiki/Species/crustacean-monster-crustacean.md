# Monster crustacean

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md)*

A clattering horror of legs and shell, as big as a wagon. Monster crustaceans are arena predators: many-legged, heavy-shelled beasts with long piercing claws that stab through plate and flesh alike. They are not people and have no minds to speak of, only hunger, rage and very fast reflexes.

They are bred and kept by those who enjoy blood sport, and set loose in pits and arenas against prisoners, gladiators and each other. Outside the arena they are rare, which is fortunate. Those who must fight one learn to stay out of reach of the claws and to look for the joints in the shell, where even the thickest carapace has to bend.

## Not to be confused with

*[Crustacean folk](crustacean-crustacean-folk.md)*, the thinking crab and lobster peoples, who carry claws but not a monster's size or hunger.

## What sets this species apart

Huge arena predators whose piercing claws are serious weapons. Non-sentient NPC beast. Its piercing claws are the family pincers attack. No hands: held weapons unusable.
