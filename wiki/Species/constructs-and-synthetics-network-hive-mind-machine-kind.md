# Network/hive-mind machine kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md)*

Many machines, one mind. Network machine kind are built intelligences that think together, pooling their thoughts across hundreds or thousands of bodies. A single unit may be small, frail and simple, little more than a thinking spark on legs. A gathering of them is something else: a consensus that reasons, plans and remembers as one.

Some are ancient machine races of cold logic and undying metal. Some consume and absorb other technology into themselves. A few are gentle, floating engineers that repair whatever they find and speak by gesture. All of them find single beings a little strange, and a little inefficient, and they seldom understand why anyone would want to be alone.

## Not to be confused with

*[Self-replicating machine kind](constructs-and-synthetics-self-replicating-machine-kind.md)*, collectives that spread by building copies of themselves, where these simply think together.

## What sets this species apart

Machine minds that think as a networked collective.
