# Reclusive temple amphibian folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md)*

Small, robed amphibian folk who tend an ancient holy place. They fish, mend the stones, sweep the stairs and speak little to visitors, whom they tolerate rather than welcome. Their loyalty is to the site, not to any priesthood or pilgrim, and they have outlasted many of both.

A related people hide in shallow coves far from anyone, pacifist and water-breathing, living long, quiet lives by choice. Both kinds share a love of stillness and a deep suspicion of anyone who arrives in a hurry.

## Not to be confused with

the *[surface-dwelling amphibious folk](amphibian-surface-dwelling-amphibious-folk.md)*, who trade and travel; these folk stay at home and keep a place.

## What sets this species apart

Close to the family baseline; their fishing and temple-keeping are cultural and handled in the Culture step.
