# Alien ursine folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Ursine](../Species_Families/ursine.md)*

Large, stocky, furred aliens with a bear's shape and a banker's calm. They come from a world far from any forest, and nothing in their history involves catching fish or raiding hives. What they are known for is presence: an ursine envoy fills a room, speaks slowly and waits, and more often than not the other side starts making concessions just to fill the silence.

Their people have built a great deal of interstellar trade and diplomacy, and are widely respected for it, though that skill comes from their culture rather than their bodies. They are patient to a fault, hard to rattle, and dangerous when patience finally runs out.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bear-folk](ursine-bear-folk.md)*, true bears born of forests and ice; alien ursine folk only look bear-like, and share none of their fishing or foraging past.

## What sets this species apart

Calm, bear-like aliens with no fishing heritage; their trading is cultural.
