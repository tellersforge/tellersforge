# Diminutive folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Small folk](../Species_Families/small-folk.md)*

A loose collection of small peoples who share little besides their size. Some are round, big-eyed hermits who study magic and summon spirits; some are quick, disease-proof folk with fast hearts; some are childlike in looks but old in mind; some are furred, frost-touched or mountain herders. Each has its own homeland, customs and troubles.

What they have in common is how the world treats them: as children, pets or servants, until one of them does something extraordinary. Many of them are resilient survivors from homelands that have suffered disaster after disaster. Some are timid, some are fierce, and all of them are tired of being patted on the head.

## Not to be confused with

*[Tiny folk](humankind-tiny-folk.md)* are small humans of one kind; diminutive folk are a varied collection of small peoples, each different from the next.

## What sets this species apart

A loose group of small races that differ from each other more than from the family; differences sit at Breed level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
