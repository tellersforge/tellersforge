# Astral exile folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elf-kind](../Species_Families/elf-kind.md)*

Lean, sharp-featured wanderers of the planes, filed among elf-kind by convention though their own histories say otherwise: they tell of ancestors who were human slaves of a mind-eating tyranny, and who rose and broke free. Whatever their origin, the long bondage left them with minds that reach out into the world. Small objects shift at their will, and their thoughts can strike like a blade.

The escape split them in two. One people became raiders, red-skinned and martial, sailing between planes on astral ships and taking what they want. The other became ascetics, pale and disciplined, living in monasteries within the chaos and perfecting mind and body. The two hate one another bitterly. Neither has any of the elven bond with forests and growing things, and neither much cares for ordinary elves, who return the feeling.

## Not to be confused with

*[astral elf](elf-kind-astral-elf.md)*, star-touched true elves drawn to the planes; the exile folk are mind-gifted strangers with no elven kinship with nature.

## What sets this species apart

Psionic planar folk filed with elf-kind by convention: innate minor telekinesis, and none of the elven bond with growing things.
