# Hag/witch-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

The old woman at the crossroads who offers a meal. The beauty on the lonely road at dusk. The kind neighbour whose neck, at night, grows long enough to look in at the upstairs window. Every land has its version, and every version is a trap: a monstrous true form hidden under a human seeming, used to bring the victim close.

Some freeze travellers with a breath of winter. Some haunt particular trees, or split at the waist and fly by night. Some eat children, some eat men, and a few, in older tales, help heroes polite enough to deserve it. They are predators first and foremost, patient and clever. Their disguises are good, but rarely perfect. There is always a tell: a metal leg, a shadow wrong for its owner.

## Not to be confused with

*[Witch-kind](fey-witch-kind.md)*, the fey's folk of cunning craft; hag/witch-kind are deathly predators whose human face is only a lure.

## What sets this species apart

Predators who hide a monstrous true form behind a human seeming to lure victims.
