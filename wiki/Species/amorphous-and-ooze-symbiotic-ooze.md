# Symbiotic ooze

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md)*

Living ooze that needs a partner. It flows over or into a host, joins its nerves, and the two become something neither could be alone: stronger, quicker, able to grow claws or grasping tendrils from the skin. The ooze gains legs and a life; the host gains power and a voice in the back of the head that is not their own. Bonds can be kind or dreadful, and the rare violent strains make them dreadful. Most fear loud, ringing sound and fire.

A different kind is a small worm that lives in a host's belly for centuries, passing from body to body and carrying every past host's memories. Those who carry one are never quite alone, and sometimes forget where they end and it begins.

## Not to be confused with

the *[invader ooze](amorphous-and-ooze-invader-ooze.md)*, which takes a host by force; the symbiont bonds and shares.

## What sets this species apart

Lives by bonding with a host, sharing mind and body with it. Mind Meld stands for the host bond; used on a willing or held host.
