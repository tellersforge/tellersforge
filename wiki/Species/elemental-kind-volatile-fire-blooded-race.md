# Volatile fire-blooded race

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md)*

A people whose blood runs hot, and not only in the figurative sense. Their veins carry something closer to liquid flame than to anything a surgeon would recognise. Cut one and the wound hisses. Their skin is warm to the touch in any weather, and a crowd of them raises the temperature of a hall.

They are built for heat. Furnace worlds, volcanic badlands and sun-scoured deserts that would kill other folk are simply home. They can send fire out of their hands in a gout or a spray, and they shrug off burns that would cripple anyone else. What they cannot do well is stay calm. Temper and power are the same thing in them: the angrier they get, the hotter they burn. Their fighters learn to feed that rage and aim it. Their diplomats learn to count slowly, and other peoples learn to keep a bucket of sand within reach.

## Not to be confused with

*[elemental-blooded folk](elemental-kind-elemental-blooded-folk.md)*, whose fire-touched line merely resists heat; these people carry living fire in their veins and throw it.

## What sets this species apart

Fire-blooded, hot-tempered people who project flame and thrive in heat. Resist Element is fire.
