# Hive construct

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md)*

A cold army of metal bodies that has waited in stone tombs for longer than most civilisations have existed. When it wakes, it marches in silent, identical rows, with no fear, no fatigue and no mercy. Its warriors feel nothing, want nothing but the orders of the collective, and remember an empire that ruled the stars when others were still in caves.

They are terrifyingly hard to destroy. Damaged bodies slowly repair themselves, and a warrior blown apart may simply flicker out of sight and reappear, whole, somewhere else. Victory against them is never certain until the tomb is sealed or the whole host is broken. Many who fought them claim that the worst part was the silence.

## Not to be confused with

*[self-replicating machine kind](constructs-and-synthetics-self-replicating-machine-kind.md)*, which builds copies of itself from raw matter; the hive construct is an ancient, fixed army that repairs and rises again.

## What sets this species apart

An emotionless, self-repairing robot collective that rises again after destruction. NPC-scale. Healing Factor stands for self-repair and Cheat Death for phase-out reanimation.
