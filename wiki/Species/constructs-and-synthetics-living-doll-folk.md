# Living-doll folk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md)*

Small, soft and awake. Living-doll folk are stuffed toys, rag dolls and cloth animals brought to life by the sheer force of someone's love, often a child's. They are stitched, button-eyed and floppy, light enough to be carried and untroubled by poison or illness, since there is nothing inside them to poison but stuffing.

They are brave far beyond their size, loyal to the ones who made them real, and quietly anxious about fire, dogs, scissors and damp. A tear can be mended; a burn is much harder. Most try to stay near their people, keeping watch at night. Some have outlived their children and wander, looking for someone new to love.

## Not to be confused with

*[Construct-kind](constructs-and-synthetics-construct-kind.md)*, bodies animated by deliberate magic or ritual, where living dolls are woken by love.

## What sets this species apart

Magically animated cloth dolls, immune to poison and disease but not computing machines. Vulnerable to fire and tearing.
