# Cosmic-barrier collective

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md)*

At the very edge of all that exists stands a wall, and the wall is made of those who tried to pass it. Every one of them came seeking what lies beyond: gods, scholars, conquerors, madmen. Every one was caught, turned to living stone and set into the barrier alongside the rest. Their faces stare out from the rock in countless numbers, frozen at the moment of failure.

They are not dead. Somewhere inside the stone a flicker of mind remains, and those who come close enough can feel it: a murmur of regret, a warning, sometimes a plea. They cannot move, cannot die and cannot be freed by any ordinary means. Travellers who reach the wall treat it as a monument and a threat at once, and most of them turn back.

## Not to be confused with

the *[living-statue predator kind](mineral-and-crystalline-living-statue-predator-kind.md)*, which only pretends to be a statue, where these beings truly are imprisoned in stone and cannot move at all.

## What sets this species apart

Beings trapped as living stone in a wall at the edge of existence: conscious but unable to move. NPC only; immobile. Lifespan (ageless or immortal) is a lexicon detail, not a game number. No hands: held weapons unusable.
