# Wise cosmic bird

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md)*

A single vast and ancient bird, older than kingdoms, that nests at the crown of the great tree holding up the world. Its wings, when spread, darken whole valleys, and its feathers are said to heal any wound they are laid upon. It knows a great deal, has seen more, and is kind in the slow, distant way of something that has outlived empires.

Heroes climb for weeks to ask it a question. Most never find it. Those who do come back with an answer, sometimes a feather, and often the feeling that they were expected. It is not a people or a tribe but one being, met perhaps once in a lifetime and never forgotten.

## Not to be confused with

*[Phoenix-kind](avian-phoenix-kind.md)*, radiant firebirds of renewal, where the wise cosmic bird is one ancient being of wisdom and healing.

## What sets this species apart

A vast, ancient bird of great wisdom and healing power. NPC-scale.
