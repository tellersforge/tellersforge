# Reanimated construct-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md)*

Sewn together from many dead: an arm from the gallows, a heart from the charnel house, a face that belonged to someone else. The pieces never quite match. Science, alchemy or a storm-lit ritual set them going, and the result is a single powerful body that should not exist. It is huge, scarred and strong enough to bend iron, and it shrugs off wounds that would drop a soldier.

It is also slow, and awkward in its borrowed limbs. Most have only a dim, instinctive mind, though a few wake into full awareness and understand exactly what was done to them. They are more often tragic than cruel. Villagers with torches rarely stop to ask which kind they have found, and the creature learns quickly to fear fire.

## Not to be confused with

*[Construct/stitched folk](hybrid-and-chimeric-construct-stitched-folk.md)*, a living people assembled from grafts; the reanimated construct is dead flesh, stitched and set walking.

## What sets this species apart

Stitched-together corpses of great strength and durability, slow and mismatched.
