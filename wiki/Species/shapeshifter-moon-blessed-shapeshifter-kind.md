# Moon-blessed shapeshifter kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Shapeshifter](../Species_Families/shapeshifter.md)*

Heroes chosen by the moon itself. They were once ordinary mortals, until a divine hand set its mark on them and gave them the shapes of beasts: a wolf, a hawk, a great cat, a bear, whatever the moment needs. With the shapes came strength beyond any mortal's and a fierce, wild sense of purpose.

Their power waxes and wanes with the moon. At its full they are nearly unstoppable; at its dark they are closer to the people they were. They wander the borders of the world, guarding wild places and hunting what threatens them. Townsfolk see them as saints, monsters or both, and hardly anyone can tell which of the creatures in the forest is really a hero.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lycanthrope](shapeshifter-lycanthrope.md)* is cursed into one beast and fights it; moon-blessed shapeshifter kind were chosen by a divine power and take many animal forms by right.

## What sets this species apart

Divinely exalted heroes whose shifting is chiefly into animal forms, backed by divine strength. Tied to the lunar cycle.
