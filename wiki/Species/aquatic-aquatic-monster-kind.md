# Aquatic monster-kind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md)*

The great beasts of the water. A strait where a many-headed horror reaches down from a cave. A river monster, part crocodile and part elephant, that serves as steed to the river gods. Serpentine guardians who protect or devour, depending on how they are treated. A thing in the billabong that bellows at night. An otter-like beast with a hand on the end of its tail, which drowns its victims and takes their eyes. In some ages, even machines built in the shape of sea predators.

They are huge, hungry and ancient, and they cannot hold tools. Sailors tell stories about them, and chart their waters carefully.

## Not to be confused with

the *[primordial amphibian spawn](amphibian-primordial-amphibian-spawn.md)*, which are mindless harbingers of ruin; these monsters are the beasts and guardians of particular waters.

## What sets this species apart

Large, fearsome water monsters with teeth, claws or coils. NPC-scale. No hands: held weapons unusable.
