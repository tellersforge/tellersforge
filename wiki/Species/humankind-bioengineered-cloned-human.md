# Bioengineered/cloned human

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Family: [Humankind](../Species_Families/humankind.md)*

Humans made rather than merely born. Some are grown in rows from a single template, drilled from the moment they open their eyes and spent like ammunition, their short lives shorter still. Some were redesigned to breathe poison air or to live in vacuum or water, and carry the stigma of looking useful. Others are stitched together from mismatched parts by surgeon-cults and go on replacing pieces of themselves for the rest of their lives, courteous and uncomplaining. A few are the last of vanished golden-age peoples, or operatives changed by exposure to the empty dark between worlds.

The question everyone asks them, sooner or later, is who owns them. The answer they give is the whole of their story.

## Not to be confused with

the *[augmented/enhanced human](humankind-augmented-enhanced-human.md)*, who was ordinary stock raised by later change; these were built to a plan from the beginning.

## What sets this species apart

Engineered, cloned or void-altered humans whose alterations differ by breed; cybernetics are gear, so nothing is shared at species level.

## Breeds and races

Each block below is one set of numbers; breeds that share every number share a block (Species rules, rule 9). Attributes are final modifiers, the size trade-off included. Skills, powers and weapon groups are final levels after the family, species and breed layers.
