# Axes, Maces & Clubs

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Weight on the end of a handle. A club is the first weapon anyone picks up; an axe is a tool that learned to fight; a mace is a club that met a smith. Together they make the commonest arms of farmers, raiders and men-at-arms who cannot afford a sword. They do not guard well and they do not thrust, but what they hit stays hit. Against armour they are often better than blades, because a dent or a broken bone does not care how good the rings are.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **STR**/DEX/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** one-handed chopping and crushing weapons

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Hook the Shield

The beard of the axe catches the rim of a shield and drags it down.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **STR**/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Axes, Maces & Clubs level 2
- **Effect (draft):** With an axe or hooked head, against a shield. On a pass, the shield is pulled aside: the target gets no shield Parry against your next Attack this round. On a failure, your weapon is caught and your next Parry is at −2.
- **Not to be confused with:** Shield Bash, which uses a shield instead of attacking one.

### Bone-Breaker

A heavy overhand blow that ignores the armour and breaks what is underneath.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **STR**/END/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Axes, Maces & Clubs level 3
- **Effect (draft):** With a mace or club. On a hit and a pass, the target's armour counts as two less against this blow. You can't Parry this round.
- **Not to be confused with:** Through the Gap, which slips past armour instead of crushing through it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Great Weapons](great-weapons.md)*, which are the same idea at two-handed size, slower but crushing.
