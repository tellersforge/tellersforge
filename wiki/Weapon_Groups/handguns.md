# Handguns

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Pistols and revolvers from the age of steam onward: small enough for one hand, a coat pocket or a holster, loaded with cartridges and ready to fire again at once. They are the weapons of lawmen, officers, gamblers and anyone who expects trouble at close quarters and doesn't want to be seen carrying it. The family teaches the draw, the grip, the stance and the habit of counting shots. A handgun is inaccurate at any real distance, and deadly across a room.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **PER**/DEX/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** pistols, revolvers (steam)

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Quick Draw

The hand drops, the pistol clears the holster, and the shot is fired before the other man's hand has moved.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Handguns level 2
- **Effect (draft):** At the start of a fight, roll the check. On a pass, you act first in the first round with your handgun drawn, whatever the usual order. On a fail, you act in the usual order.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Draw and Throw*, which does the same with a knife and a throw.

### Two-Gun Fire

A pistol in each hand, fired in turn, keeping the enemy's head down.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **DEX**/DEX/COU · **Prerequisite:** Handguns level 4
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, you fire both handguns this round, each Attack at −3. On a fail, you fire only one, at −3. You can't Dodge that round.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Rapid Volley*, a bow technique; two guns fire at the price of both hands.

### Fan the Hammer

The revolver is held low and the hammer slapped back again and again with the flat of the other hand.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Handguns level 3
- **Effect (draft):** Revolvers only. On a pass, you fire three shots this round, each at −4, at one target or at up to three close together. The cylinder is then empty.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Automatic Weapons*, whose mechanism does the same work far faster and more steadily.

## Not to be confused with
