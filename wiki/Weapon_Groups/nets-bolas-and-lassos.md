# Nets, Bolas & Lassos

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Weapons that catch rather than kill: weighted nets, bolas of stones on cords that wrap the legs, and the loop of rope thrown by herders from the saddle. Hunters, fishermen, cattle drivers, arena fighters and slave-takers all learned them, and so do those who want a prisoner alive. The family teaches the throw, the spread, the timing of the pull and how to keep hold of a struggling creature at the far end. A good throw ends a fight without a drop of blood. A bad one leaves the thrower tangled and unarmed.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **DEX**/PER/AGI (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** entangling missiles

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Drag Down

The lasso or net is taut, and one hard heave pulls the victim off their feet.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **STR**/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Nets, Bolas & Lassos level 3
- **Effect (draft):** After you have entangled a foe, roll the check instead of Attacking. On a pass, the foe falls prone and must spend their next round getting up. On a fail, they keep their footing and can try to pull free at +2.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Wrestling & Grappling*, which takes a foe down with the hands, at arm's length.

### Rope from the Saddle

The mounted herder throws the loop on the move and turns the horse so that its weight does the pulling.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **DEX**/WIS/STR · **Prerequisite:** Nets, Bolas & Lassos level 4
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, you can throw a lasso or bola while riding at full pace without the usual penalty, and a foe you catch is dragged along with your mount. On a fail, you miss and need a round to gather the rope.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Drag Down*, which pulls a foe over from a standstill.

## Not to be confused with

*[Whips](whips.md)*, which strike and snare at close range; these missiles fly to their target and bind it.
