# Flails & Chains

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A weight on a chain, or a chain on its own, swung round and let fly. The flail began as a farm tool for beating grain and was never quite forgiven for it. Chain weapons are awkward, unpredictable and hard to parry, because the head curls around a guard or over the top of a shield. They are hard to master and dangerous to the careless, who tend to learn by hitting themselves. In skilled hands they whirl and snap with a rhythm that unsettles anyone facing them.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **STR**/AGI/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** flails, chain weapons

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Over the Shield

The head swings over the rim of a raised shield and strikes the man behind it.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Flails & Chains level 2
- **Effect (draft):** Against a shield. On a pass, the target can't use the shield to Parry your next Attack this round. On a failure, the chain tangles and your next Attack is at −2.
- **Not to be confused with:** Hook the Shield, which drags the shield aside instead of going around it.

### Wrap and Pull

The chain winds around a weapon, arm or leg, and a sharp tug does the rest.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **DEX**/STR/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Flails & Chains level 3
- **Effect (draft):** Instead of attacking. On a pass, choose: the target drops their weapon, or falls. On a failure, they may pull your weapon from you with a Strength contest.
- **Not to be confused with:** a Whip's Lash and Snare, which is lighter and works at longer range.

## Not to be confused with

*[Whips](whips.md)*, which lash and wrap but carry no striking weight; and *[Axes, Maces & Clubs](axes-maces-and-clubs.md)*, whose heads are fixed and more controllable.
