# Wrestling & Grappling

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The oldest sport there is, and the ugliest way to lose a fight. Wrestling is holds, locks, trips, throws and pins: getting hold of an enemy and keeping him where you want him. Farm boys learn it at fairs, soldiers learn it for the moment a spear breaks, and every guard learns it for the drunk who won't come quietly. It rewards weight, stamina and a patient grip. A wrestler who gets close enough has made the other man's sword almost useless.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **STR**/AGI/END (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** holds, throws, pins

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Hip Throw

The wrestler turns his back into the other man, loads him across a hip, and pulls.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **STR**/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Wrestling & Grappling level 2
- **Effect (draft):** Once you have a grip, roll the check. On a pass, the target falls, takes unarmed damage, and must spend their next round getting up. On a failure, you both go down.
- **Not to be confused with:** a trip, which only unbalances; a throw lands the whole body.

### Choke Hold

An arm around the neck from behind, squeezing until the struggling stops.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **STR**/END/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Wrestling & Grappling level 3
- **Effect (draft):** From a hold behind the target. Each round you keep the hold and pass, the target loses Life as from unarmed damage and can only try to break free. Armour on the neck halves it. Anyone can hit you while you hold on.
- **Not to be confused with:** a pin, which keeps someone down without hurting them.

### Disarming Grip

A hand on the wrist, a twist, and the weapon clatters to the floor.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Wrestling & Grappling level 4
- **Effect (draft):** Instead of attacking, when an enemy with a weapon is within reach. On a pass, they drop it. On a failure, they get a free Attack on you.
- **Not to be confused with:** Binding the Blade, a sword technique that disarms with steel rather than bare hands.

## Not to be confused with

*[Martial Arts](martial-arts.md)*, which strikes and redirects; wrestling seizes and holds.
