# Two-Handed Swords

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Greatswords are as tall as their bearers, heavy in the hand and heavier in reputation. They were made for open ground: to clear space around a banner, to break a line of pikes, to hold a bridge. The two-handed swordsman gives up the shield and trusts in reach and momentum. Swung, the blade keeps a whole ring of enemies at bay; gripped by the long cross or the blunt stretch near the hilt, it becomes a short spear or a lever. It is tiring, loud and hard to ignore.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **STR**/DEX/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** greatswords

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Sweeping Arc

The blade swings in a wide circle and everyone within its reach has to step back or be hit.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **STR**/DEX/END · **Prerequisite:** Two-Handed Swords level 3
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, make one Attack against every enemy within reach, each at −2. You can't Parry until your next turn. On a failure, you are left open: your next Dodge is at −2.
- **Not to be confused with:** a single heavy cut, which hurts one target a great deal.

### Grip the Ricasso

One hand slides onto the blunt stretch of blade above the guard, and the long weapon becomes a short one.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Two-Handed Swords level 2
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, you can fight at arm's length this round without penalty and your thrusts gain +1 damage. You lose the extra reach until you shift your grip back.
- **Not to be confused with:** Half-Swording, which aims the point precisely; this simply shortens the weapon.

## Not to be confused with

*[Swords](swords.md)*, which are lighter and used with a shield; and *[Great Weapons](great-weapons.md)*, which put all the weight in a head rather than along a blade.
