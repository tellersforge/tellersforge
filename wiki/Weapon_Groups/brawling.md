# Brawling

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Fists, knees, elbows, foreheads and whatever else is attached. Brawling is the fighting nobody teaches and everybody learns, in schoolyards, taverns, barracks and back alleys. It has no stances and no manners. A good brawler keeps his chin down, hits first, and knows that the fight usually ends when someone's nose breaks. Sailors, drovers, dockhands and bouncers are rarely elegant, but they are hard to put down, and they fight dirtiest when they are losing.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **STR**/AGI/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** punching, kicking, scrapping; everyone has some

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Dirty Trick

Sand in the eyes, a stamp on the instep, a drink thrown in the face. Anything to buy a moment.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/CHA · **Prerequisite:** Brawling level 2
- **Effect (draft):** Instead of attacking, roll the check. On a pass, your opponent can't Parry or Dodge your next Attack this round. On a failure, you have wasted your action and they know what you tried.
- **Not to be confused with:** a feint, which is a trained deception with a weapon rather than a cheap shot.

### Headbutt

The brawler steps in close and drives his forehead into the other man's face.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **END**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Brawling level 3
- **Effect (draft):** Only when grappling or at arm's length. On a pass, your unarmed damage this round is doubled and the target loses their next Parry. You take a quarter of the damage you deal yourself, and a helmet on the target makes it useless.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary punch, which hurts nobody but the one who is hit.

### Roll With It

An old hand turns his head with the punch and lets the blow slide off.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **END**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Brawling level 4
- **Effect (draft):** Once per round, after being hit by an unarmed blow or a club, roll the check. On a pass, halve the damage. You lose your next Attack as you recover your footing.
- **Not to be confused with:** a Dodge, which avoids the blow altogether instead of soaking it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Martial Arts](martial-arts.md)*, which is a trained, disciplined style; brawling is instinct, habit and a willingness to get hurt.
