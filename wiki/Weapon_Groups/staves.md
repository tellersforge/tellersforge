# Staves

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A length of hard wood, shod perhaps with iron, held in both hands. The staff is the traveller's weapon, the pilgrim's companion and the monk's discipline. It costs nothing, raises no suspicion at a gate and doubles as a walking stick. In trained hands both ends strike, the middle guards, and the fighter moves in circles, keeping several opponents busy at once. It rarely kills. It bruises, breaks fingers, and sends people home thinking hard about their choices.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **AGI**/DEX/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** quarterstaffs, fighting sticks

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Both Ends

The staff strikes low with one end and high with the other before the target can guard both.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Staves level 2
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass after a hit, make a second Attack this round at −2 against the same or an adjacent target.
- **Not to be confused with:** Flowing Strike, the unarmed version of the same rhythm.

### Spinning Guard

The staff turns in a blur before the fighter, catching whatever comes.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **DEX**/WIS/END · **Prerequisite:** Staves level 3
- **Effect (draft):** Instead of attacking. On a pass, you may Parry every Attack against you this round, including from several enemies, without the usual penalty for extra parries.
- **Not to be confused with:** Haft Guard, which blocks one heavy blow rather than many light ones.

### Leg Sweep

Low and fast, the staff clips an ankle and the world tilts.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Staves level 2
- **Effect (draft):** Instead of attacking. On a pass, the target falls and must spend their next turn getting up.
- **Not to be confused with:** Shaft Sweep, a Spears technique that does the same with the butt of a spear.

## Not to be confused with

*[Spears](spears.md)*, which have a point and fight to kill; a staff strikes with both ends and fights to stop.
