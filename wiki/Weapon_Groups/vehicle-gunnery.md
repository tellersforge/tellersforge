# Vehicle Gunnery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The guns of tanks, armoured cars, gunboats, fighters and starships: turrets that traverse with a whine, sights with rangefinders, fire that must allow for the gunner's own movement as well as the target's. The family teaches reading the sight, working with a driver or pilot, choosing the right round and knowing a gun's limits of arc. It is teamwork as much as marksmanship. A good gunner calls out corrections before the driver asks, and knows the difference between a hull hit and a kill.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **PER**/INT/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** turrets, ship and vehicle guns

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Fire on the Move

The gunner rides the vehicle's pitch and roll and fires at the top of the sway.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Vehicle Gunnery level 3
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, you ignore the usual penalty for your own vehicle moving this round. On a fail, the penalty applies as normal.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Shot on the Move*, a bow technique for an archer on foot or horseback.

### Aim for the Tracks

The gun is laid not at the thick armour but at wheels, tracks, engines or thrusters.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **WIS**/INT/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Vehicle Gunnery level 4
- **Effect (draft):** Declare before the Attack at −4. On a hit and a pass, the target vehicle is slowed to half speed or can't manoeuvre until repaired, in addition to the damage.
- **Not to be confused with:** a weak-point strike, which looks for a gap in armour rather than for what makes the vehicle move.

## Not to be confused with

*[Heavy Weapons & Launchers](heavy-weapons-and-launchers.md)*, which are carried and fired by infantry; vehicle gunnery works guns built into a vehicle.
