# Bows

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The stave, the string and the arrow: the oldest killing machine that works at a distance, known wherever there is springy wood and something to hunt. Hunters, herders, steppe riders and whole villages of trained archers have all carried bows. The family teaches the draw, the anchor at the cheek, the release, and the far harder art of judging wind, drop and distance by feel. A bow is quiet and fast. It asks for strength in the back and patience in the head, and it pays back in the kill before the enemy is close.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **PER**/DEX/STR (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** short and long bows

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Rapid Volley

The archer holds arrows in the bow hand and looses as fast as the string comes back to the cheek.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **DEX**/STR/END · **Prerequisite:** Bows level 3
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, you loose two arrows this round instead of one, each with its own Attack at −2. On a fail, you loose only one arrow, at −2. You can't Dodge in the round you volley.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary shot, which takes the full draw and anchor each time.

### Arcing Shot

The arrow goes high, into the sky, and comes down steeply on a target behind a wall or a line of shields.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **WIS**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Bows level 4
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, you can shoot at a target at medium range or farther that you can't see directly but whose place you know, ignoring cover from walls or shields in front. The Attack is at −4 and the arrow takes a round to fall; the target can Dodge if warned.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Artillery & Siege Engines*, whose indirect fire lobs far heavier loads at buildings and crowds.

### Shot on the Move

The archer shoots at a run or from the saddle, loosing in the moment when every foot is off the ground.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Bows level 5
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, you can move your full distance and still shoot this round without the usual penalty for moving. On a fail, the shot is lost.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Rapid Volley*, which trades accuracy for speed while standing still.

## Not to be confused with

*[Crossbows](crossbows.md)*, which store the draw in a mechanism: easier to learn and harder-hitting, but slower to reload.
