# Shields

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A board of wood, a hoop of hide or a plate of steel, strapped to the arm or gripped in the fist. The shield is the most useful thing a warrior carries: it stops arrows and blades, it lets a line of fighters become a wall, and its rim and boss are weapons too. Fighting with a shield is about angles and patience, giving the enemy nothing to aim at and then punishing the moment they overreach. Shields break, and are replaced, which is a good trade for the arm behind them.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **STR**/AGI/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** fighting and blocking with a shield

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Shield Bash

The boss or rim slams into the enemy's face or chest.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **STR**/END · **Prerequisite:** Shields level 2
- **Effect (draft):** Instead of your weapon Attack. On a pass and a hit, the target takes unarmed damage +1 and loses their next Attack as they stagger. You can't Parry with the shield this round.
- **Not to be confused with:** Hook the Shield, which attacks the other man's shield instead of using your own.

### Shield Wall

Rims overlap, shoulders brace, and a line of fighters becomes a single wall.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **COU**/END · **Prerequisite:** Shields level 3
- **Effect (draft):** With an ally beside you, both with shields. On a pass, you and that ally each get +2 to Parry against arrows and charges this round. You can't move.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary block, which protects only the one who makes it.

### Cover a Friend

The shield swings across to catch a blow meant for someone else.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Shields level 3
- **Effect (draft):** Once per round, when an ally next to you is attacked. On a pass, you may Parry that Attack with your shield. Your own next Parry is at −2.
- **Not to be confused with:** Second Rank, which strikes past an ally rather than guarding one.

### Deflect the Missile

The shield tilts just enough that the arrow glances away instead of sticking.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **DEX**/WIS/END · **Prerequisite:** Shields level 4
- **Effect (draft):** Against an arrow, bolt or thrown weapon you can see coming. On a pass, the missile is turned aside entirely and doesn't damage the shield.
- **Not to be confused with:** Shield Wall, which protects a group but not as surely.
