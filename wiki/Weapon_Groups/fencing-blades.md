# Fencing Blades

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Long, slender blades built for the point: rapiers, smallswords and their cousins. They belong to towns and courts rather than battlefields, to duels at dawn and quarrels over honour, worn by people who expect to meet other swordsmen and not men in armour. The fencer fights in a line, measuring distance to the width of a finger, and wins with a single thrust nobody saw coming. It is geometry, nerve and footwork, and it is taught in fencing halls by masters who write books about each other's errors.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **DEX**/AGI/PER (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** rapiers and light thrusting blades

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Lunge

The back leg straightens, the body flies forward, and the point arrives from further away than anyone thought possible.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Fencing Blades level 2
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, your next Attack this round reaches one step further and the target's Parry against it is at −2. If it misses, you are overextended: your next Parry is at −2.
- **Not to be confused with:** a charge, which throws the whole body; a lunge snaps back.

### Feint in Time

A threat to the face, a flicker to the knee, and the real thrust goes where the guard just was.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **WIS**/DEX/CHA · **Prerequisite:** Fencing Blades level 3
- **Effect (draft):** Before your Attack. On a pass, the target can't Parry that Attack (they may still Dodge). On a failure, your Attack is at −2.
- **Not to be confused with:** Dirty Trick, a Brawling technique that cheats rather than deceives.

### Riposte

Parry and answer in one motion, the blade never stopping.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **DEX**/WIS/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Fencing Blades level 4
- **Effect (draft):** After a successful Parry, roll the check. On a pass, make an immediate Attack against that opponent, outside your normal turn. Once per round.
- **Not to be confused with:** Binding the Blade, which controls the other blade rather than striking back at once.

## Not to be confused with

*[Swords](swords.md)*, which cut as well as thrust and are built to survive a battlefield; a fencing blade is faster but fragile against armour.
