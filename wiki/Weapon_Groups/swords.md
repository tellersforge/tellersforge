# Swords

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The sword was never the most practical weapon on a battlefield, but it has always been the most admired. It is made only for fighting, costs more steel than a spear, and takes years to learn. In return it does everything: it cuts, thrusts, guards, and hangs at the hip until needed. This family covers arming swords, sabres and hand-and-a-half blades. Its fighting teaches distance, timing and the conversation between two blades, and its masters are proud, expensive and slightly insufferable.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **DEX**/AGI/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** one-handed swords, sabres, hand-and-a-half swords

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Half-Swording

The swordsman grips the middle of his own blade with his free hand and drives the point at a gap in the armour.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Swords level 3
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, your next weak-point or vital-gap strike this round takes half the usual Attack penalty (−2 instead of −4, −4 instead of −8). You can't parry with the sword that round.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary weak-point strike, which anyone can try at the full penalty.

### Binding the Blade

Blade meets blade and stays there. The swordsman feels the other man's pressure and turns it aside, then steps in.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **WIS**/DEX/STR · **Prerequisite:** Swords level 4
- **Effect (draft):** After a successful parry, roll the check. On a pass, the attacker loses their next parry, or you disarm them (your choice).
- **Not to be confused with:** a plain parry, which only stops the blow.

### Draw and Cut

The blade leaves the scabbard already moving, and the first cut lands before the other man is ready.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Swords level 2
- **Effect (draft):** At the start of a fight, with the sword sheathed. On a pass, you draw and Attack in one action, and the target's Parry against that Attack is at −2. On a failure, you fumble the draw and lose your first Attack.
- **Not to be confused with:** a quick draw with a pistol, which is a Handguns matter.

## Not to be confused with
