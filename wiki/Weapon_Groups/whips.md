# Whips

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A lash of braided leather or cord, tapering to a cracker that breaks the air with a sound like a shot. The whip is a drover's tool and a slaver's shame, and in a fight it is more trick than weapon. It rarely kills. It stings, cuts skin, wraps ankles and snatches things from hands at a distance no sword can match. Its users are showmen as often as fighters, and they know that the crack alone makes horses bolt and men flinch.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **DEX**/AGI/PER (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** whips and lashing weapons

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Lash and Snare

The tip wraps around a wrist, an ankle or a weapon hilt, and a pull tightens it.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Whips level 2
- **Effect (draft):** Instead of attacking, at a whip's reach. On a pass, choose: the target drops what they hold, or falls. On a failure, the lash misses and the whip must be recovered.
- **Not to be confused with:** Wrap and Pull, which does the same with a heavy chain at close range.

### Crack of Warning

A crack an inch from someone's face is more persuasive than any threat.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **CHA**/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Whips level 2
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, one animal or frightened person nearby backs off or bolts this round. It doesn't work on the determined or the trained.
- **Not to be confused with:** an Intimidation attempt, which uses words and presence rather than sound.

### Flick to the Eyes

The very tip, snapped at the face.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **DEX**/DEX/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Whips level 4
- **Effect (draft):** Attack a weak point (−4). On a hit and a pass, the target is blinded until the end of their next round instead of taking damage.
- **Not to be confused with:** Dirty Trick, which blinds with sand rather than skill.

## Not to be confused with

*[Flails & Chains](flails-and-chains.md)*, whose heavy heads break bones; a whip is light and works by speed and reach.
