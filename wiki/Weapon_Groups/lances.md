# Lances

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A long, heavy shaft tucked under the arm of a rider at full gallop. The lance is not really swung or thrust: the horse does the work, and the rider aims. The impact of a charging horse and rider behind one point can lift a man from the saddle or go clean through a shield. Knights train for years at rings and targets, and at tournaments where the crowd cheers the splinters. Once the charge is spent, most riders drop the lance and draw something shorter.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **STR**/AGI/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** couched charges from horseback

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### True Aim

The point settles on a shield boss or a helm, steady even at the gallop.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Lances level 2
- **Effect (draft):** During a charge. On a pass, your Attack ignores the usual penalty for striking from a moving mount, and a weak-point strike takes −2 instead of −4.
- **Not to be confused with:** Half-Swording, which steadies a sword's point on foot.

### Unseating Strike

The lance takes the other rider in the shield and keeps going until he leaves the saddle.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **STR**/DEX/COU · **Prerequisite:** Lances level 3
- **Effect (draft):** Against a mounted target, during a charge. On a hit and a pass, the target is thrown from their mount and takes damage as from a short fall. The lance breaks on a failure.
- **Not to be confused with:** Unhorse, which drags a rider down from the ground with a polearm.

## Not to be confused with

*[Spears](spears.md)*, which are used on foot or thrown; a lance only makes sense at the charge.
