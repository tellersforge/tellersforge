# Slings

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Two cords and a pouch, a stone from the riverbed, and a whirl above the head: the cheapest weapon in the world and one of the most underrated. Shepherds carry slings to drive off wolves, and island peoples have raised whole companies of slingers who could break a helmet at a distance. The family covers the plain sling and the staff-sling that throws heavier stones in an arc. It teaches timing, rhythm and a wrist that knows the release point without thinking. A sling fits in a pocket; ammunition lies on every road.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **DEX**/PER/AGI (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** slings and staff-slings

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Stunning Stone

The stone is aimed at the head, to drop a man rather than kill him.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Slings level 3
- **Effect (draft):** Declare before the Attack at −2. On a hit and a pass against a head zone, the target is dazed and loses their next action instead of the usual damage beyond armour, which is halved.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary sling shot, which aims for the body and the wound.

### Slinger's Rhythm

The sling never stops turning; the next stone is in the pouch before the last has landed.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Slings level 4
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, reloading costs you nothing this round and you gain +1 to your shot. On a fail, the cord tangles; you can't shoot until the end of your next round.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Rapid Volley*, a bow technique that looses more than one missile in a round.

## Not to be confused with

*[Thrown Weapons](thrown-weapons.md)*, which leave the hand directly; a sling hurls much farther but only stones and shot.
