# Knives & Daggers

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The short blade is the most common weapon in the world, because it is also a tool. It cuts bread, rope and leather, and it cuts people just as easily. Fighting with a knife is close, fast and frightening: there is no reach to hide behind, and both fighters usually bleed. Assassins favour it because it hides in a sleeve, knights because it fits through the gaps in plate. Anyone who has seen a real knife fight will tell you the same thing: run.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **DEX**/AGI/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** short blades

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Inside the Guard

The knife fighter slips past the point of a longer weapon and is suddenly chest to chest.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Knives & Daggers level 2
- **Effect (draft):** Against an opponent with a longer weapon. On a pass, you close to arm's length: until they step back, their Parry is at −2 and your Attacks are not.
- **Not to be confused with:** a Dodge, which avoids the blow but doesn't close the distance.

### Through the Gap

A thin point slid under an armpit, through a visor or between plates.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **DEX**/INT/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Knives & Daggers level 3
- **Effect (draft):** At arm's length or in a grapple. On a pass, your next weak-point or vital-gap Attack this round ignores half the armour of that spot. You can't Parry with the knife this round.
- **Not to be confused with:** Half-Swording, which does the same job with a sword at greater effort.

### Concealed Draw

A blade that appears from nowhere: a boot, a sleeve, the small of the back.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/CHA · **Prerequisite:** Knives & Daggers level 2
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, you draw and Attack in the same action, and a target who didn't know you were armed can't Parry that Attack. Works once per fight against the same target.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ambush, which is about where you stand rather than what you hold.

## Not to be confused with

*[Swords](swords.md)*, which fight at a longer distance and can guard; a dagger fights inside the other man's reach.
