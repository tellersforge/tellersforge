# Automatic Weapons

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Submachine guns and assault rifles from the age of electricity onward: weapons that keep firing while the trigger is held, from a magazine that empties alarmingly fast. They made the lone soldier as dangerous as a whole line of musketeers, and they rewarded control over courage. The family teaches short bursts, holding the muzzle down against recoil, changing magazines by feel and moving between cover. Commandos, police raiders and infantry everywhere carry them. Undisciplined fire wastes ammunition and hits the ceiling.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **PER**/STR/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** submachine guns, assault rifles (electricity)

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Controlled Burst

Three or four rounds, a pause, the muzzle brought back on target, again.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Automatic Weapons level 2
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, a burst at a single target gains +2 and uses only half the usual ammunition. On a fail, the burst climbs; −2 and full ammunition used.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary burst, which spends more rounds for less effect.

### Suppressing Fire

A long stream of fire along a wall or a line of cover, not at anyone in particular.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **COU**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Automatic Weapons level 3
- **Effect (draft):** Spend a round firing at an area. On a pass, anyone there who leaves cover or acts other than keeping down before your next turn is Attacked by you at −2. Uses a large share of your magazine.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Wall of Shot*, a shotgun's spread; suppression hits no one unless they move.

## Not to be confused with

*[Heavy Weapons & Launchers](heavy-weapons-and-launchers.md)*, which fire belt-fed and explosive loads from a mount or the shoulder; automatic weapons stay in a single soldier's hands.
