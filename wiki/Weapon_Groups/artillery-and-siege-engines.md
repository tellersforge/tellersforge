# Artillery & Siege Engines

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Engines that throw great weights over great distances: torsion bolt-throwers, counterweight engines, field cannon and the guns and mortars of later ages. They are worked by crews, not heroes, and aimed with arithmetic as much as eyesight. The family teaches reckoning range and angle, reading where the last shot fell, and correcting the next. Walls fall to it, and so do packed ranks and parked vehicles. It is slow, loud and pitiless, and the target rarely sees who fired.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **INT**/PER/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** catapults, cannon, indirect fire

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Bracket the Target

One shot long, one shot short, and the third lands in the middle.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **INT**/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Artillery & Siege Engines level 2
- **Effect (draft):** After a miss against a stationary target, roll the check. On a pass, your next shot at the same target gains +3. Each further pass against it adds +1 more, up to +5.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Aimed Shot*, a rifle technique that aims one shot rather than walking in a battery.

### Indirect Fire

The crew fire over a hill or a wall at a target they cannot see, guided by a map, a spotter or a signal.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **INT**/WIS/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Artillery & Siege Engines level 4
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, you can fire at a target out of sight if a spotter tells you where it is. The Attack is at −4 unless the spotter can see the fall of shot. On a fail, the shot falls wide, somewhere the Game Master chooses.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Arcing Shot*, a bow technique; this technique throws far heavier loads much farther.

## Not to be confused with

*[Vehicle Gunnery](vehicle-gunnery.md)*, which aims guns mounted on a moving vehicle directly at what the gunner can see.
