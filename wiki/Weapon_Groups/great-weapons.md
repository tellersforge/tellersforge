# Great Weapons

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Great axes, mauls and long war hammers: two-handed arms with all their weight in the head. They are the answer to a man in plate or a beast with a thick hide. Every blow is committed, and every miss leaves the wielder open, so those who carry them learn to strike first and hard. They are the weapons of champions, executioners and very large people. A great weapon swung in anger makes a sound that ends many fights before they begin.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **STR**/END/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** great axes, mauls, war hammers

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Overhead Smash

The weapon rises high and comes down with everything behind it.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **STR**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Great Weapons level 2
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, your next Attack this round deals +2 damage. If it misses, you lose your next Parry and Dodge.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary blow, which keeps something back for defence.

### Shattering Blow

The hammer hits the shield, the helmet or the blade, and the thing it hits gives way.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **STR**/STR/END · **Prerequisite:** Great Weapons level 4
- **Effect (draft):** Aim at a shield, weapon or armour piece. On a hit and a pass, the item loses wear as if it took double damage; a wooden shield or weapon may break outright. The wielder takes normal damage only if the blow was not stopped.
- **Not to be confused with:** Bone-Breaker, which crushes through armour to hurt the wearer rather than the gear.

### Haft Guard

The long handle comes across the body to catch a blow, both hands wide.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Great Weapons level 3
- **Effect (draft):** Great weapons normally parry badly. On a pass, your next Parry this round is made without the usual difficulty for a heavy weapon. You can't Attack that round.
- **Not to be confused with:** a Shield block, which is the better guard but needs a free arm.
