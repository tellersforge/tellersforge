# Blowguns

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A long, straight tube of cane or hollowed wood, a sliver of a dart, and a breath. The blowgun makes almost no sound and very little damage; everything depends on what is on the dart's tip. Forest hunters use it to bring down birds and monkeys from the high canopy, and assassins value it for the same quiet. The family teaches breath control, a steady aim and, above all, the patience to wait for bare skin. Against armour it is nearly useless. Against a sleeping guard it is terribly good.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **PER**/DEX/END (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** blowpipes

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Silent Dart

The dart leaves the tube with a faint puff, and the target slaps at what feels like an insect.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Blowguns level 2
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, a hit from hiding doesn't reveal your position, and the target doesn't at first realise they have been shot. On a fail, the puff or movement is noticed.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary blowgun shot, which is quiet but not unseen.

### Find Bare Skin

A long breath, a still moment, and the dart finds the neck, the wrist or the gap under the helmet.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **WIS**/DEX/END · **Prerequisite:** Blowguns level 4
- **Effect (draft):** Declare before the Attack. On a pass, a dart aimed at a weak point takes −2 instead of −4, and a hit there delivers the dart's poison or drug even through otherwise armoured zones.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Half-Swording*, a sword technique that also halves the weak-point penalty but needs a blade in both hands.

## Not to be confused with

*[Thrown Weapons](thrown-weapons.md)*, whose darts are thrown by hand and strike harder but fly nowhere near as silently or truly.
