# Energy Blades

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Swords of a spacefaring age: a hilt that hums, and a blade of shaped field, charged edge or burning light that sheds heat and a faint smell like a storm. They cut through things older blades glance off, and they punish the careless owner as readily as the enemy. The family teaches a light, fast style with little weight behind it, since the edge does the work. Duelling orders, ship-boarding marines and cold-eyed bodyguards favour it. A drawn energy blade lights the room and silences it.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **DEX**/AGI/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** powered or energy swords (spaceflight)

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Burning Parry

The glowing blade meets the incoming weapon edge-first and cuts into it.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Energy Blades level 3
- **Effect (draft):** After a successful Parry against a metal or wooden melee weapon, roll the check. On a pass, the attacker's weapon is damaged and does half damage until repaired (or is cut through if it is small). On a fail, your blade flickers and you lose your next Parry.
- **Not to be confused with:** a plain Parry, which only stops the blow.

### Through the Plate

The wielder holds the blade still against armour for one terrible moment until it burns through.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **DEX**/DEX/COU · **Prerequisite:** Energy Blades level 4
- **Effect (draft):** Declare before the Attack. On a hit and a pass, the target's armour counts as half on that zone, and that zone's armour is left with a hole until repaired. You can't Parry or Dodge that round.
- **Not to be confused with:** a weak-point strike, which finds a gap; this one makes a gap.

## Not to be confused with

*[Swords](swords.md)*, whose steel has weight and bite of its own; an energy blade weighs almost nothing and cuts by power, not by force.
