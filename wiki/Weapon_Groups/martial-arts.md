# Martial Arts

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Unarmed fighting refined into a discipline: stances, footwork, breathing and years of drills repeated until the body answers before the mind asks. Every culture with monasteries, barracks or wrestling yards grows its own style, and each insists it is the true one. A trained fighter strikes with the whole body, turns an enemy's weight against him and knows where the soft places are. The art is taught by masters to students, often with rules about when it may be used, and rarely with the promise that it will be easy.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **AGI**/DEX/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** trained unarmed styles

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Flowing Strike

One blow turns into the next without a pause, the hands and feet taking turns.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Martial Arts level 2
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass after landing an unarmed Attack, make one extra unarmed Attack this round at −2. On a failure, you overbalance and lose your next Dodge.
- **Not to be confused with:** a flurry of wild punches, which is Brawling and hits nothing in particular.

### Redirect the Blow

The fighter meets the attack with an open hand, guides it past, and helps the attacker on his way.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **WIS**/DEX/COU · **Prerequisite:** Martial Arts level 3
- **Effect (draft):** After a successful unarmed Parry or Dodge, roll the check. On a pass, the attacker falls or stumbles, and loses their next Attack. Doesn't work against a much larger creature.
- **Not to be confused with:** a Wrestling throw, which needs a grip first.

### Striking the Nerve

A precise finger or knuckle to a point the master knows by heart.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **INT**/DEX/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Martial Arts level 4
- **Effect (draft):** Declare against an unarmoured spot and take the weak-point penalty (−4). On a hit and a pass, the target loses the use of one arm or leg for two rounds instead of taking full damage (you deal half).
- **Not to be confused with:** a vital-gap strike, which aims to kill, not to disable.

## Not to be confused with

*[Brawling](brawling.md)*, which is untrained scrapping; and *[Wrestling & Grappling](wrestling-and-grappling.md)*, which holds and pins rather than striking.
