# Long Guns

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Rifles and shotguns from the age of steam onward: two hands, a stock against the shoulder, cartridges fed from the breech or a magazine. The rifle is the marksman's weapon, patient and far-reaching; the shotgun is the brutal answer to close quarters, doors and anything that flies. The family teaches breathing, stance, the trigger squeeze and reading distance and wind. Hunters, soldiers, sheriffs and farmers alike carry long guns, and more wars have been decided by them than by any blade.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **PER**/DEX/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** rifles, shotguns (steam)

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Aimed Shot

The rifleman settles, breathes out, and squeezes the trigger between heartbeats.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **WIS**/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Long Guns level 2
- **Effect (draft):** Spend a full round aiming without moving. On a pass, your next shot at that target gains +3, or halves the weak-point or vital-gap penalty (−2 instead of −4, −4 instead of −8). If you are struck or forced to move, the aim is lost.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Snap Shot*, which gives up accuracy to fire at once.

### Snap Shot

The rifle comes up and fires in one movement, at a target that has only just appeared.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Long Guns level 3
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, you can shoot at a foe who appears or moves into view during their turn, interrupting them, with a −2 Attack. On a fail, the moment is gone. Once per round.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Aimed Shot*, which waits for the perfect moment instead of taking the first one.

### Wall of Shot

The shotgun is swept across a doorway or a crowd, and the pellets spread.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **STR**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Long Guns level 3
- **Effect (draft):** Shotguns only, at point-blank or short range. On a pass, the shot hits the target and one other foe next to them, each taking half damage. On a fail, only the main target can be hit.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Automatic Weapons*' sweeping fire, which covers more ground but burns through ammunition.

## Not to be confused with

*[Handguns](handguns.md)*, one-handed pistols and revolvers that are quicker to draw but short-ranged and less accurate.
