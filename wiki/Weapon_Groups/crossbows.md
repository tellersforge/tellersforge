# Crossbows

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A bow laid on its side on a wooden stock, with a trigger to hold it drawn until the moment is right. It is the weapon that let a townsman kill a knight after a few weeks of training, and lords have tried to ban it for exactly that reason. The family teaches loading with lever, crank or foot-stirrup, aiming down the stock, and the discipline of shooting once and making it count. Crossbowmen fight from behind big shields and castle walls, where the long reload doesn't matter as much.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **PER**/DEX/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** crossbows of all kinds

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Held at the Ready

The loaded crossbow is levelled at a doorway, a gap or a man, and the finger rests on the trigger.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **WIS**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Crossbows level 2
- **Effect (draft):** Spend a round aiming at one place or one foe. On a pass, if the foe moves, draws or strikes before your next turn, you shoot first, before their action, at +2. On a fail, you shoot on your turn as usual.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary aimed shot, which waits for its own turn.

### Quick Span

Foot in the stirrup, hands on the string, a practised heave: the reload that drill makes fast.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Crossbows level 3
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, your reload time for this crossbow is one round shorter (never less than one round). On a fail, the reload takes its normal time and the string slips; lose your next Dodge.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Rapid Volley*, a bow technique; no drill makes a crossbow as quick as a bow.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bows](bows.md)*, which shoot far faster but need years of training and a strong back to use well.
