# Thrown Weapons

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Anything made to leave the hand and hit hard: knives balanced for the throw, axes that turn end over end, javelins, darts, throwing stars and, in later ages, the grenade. The family teaches judging distance by eye, the right spin for each weapon and the knack of throwing from an awkward stance. Warriors of every era have opened a battle with a volley of spears before closing in. The weakness is plain: once it is thrown, it is gone, and the enemy may throw it back.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **DEX**/STR/PER (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** knives, axes, javelins, grenades

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Draw and Throw

The knife comes off the belt and is in the air in one movement.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Thrown Weapons level 2
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, drawing and throwing a knife, dart or small axe take a single action, and you throw at +1. On a fail, the throw happens but the draw costs you your next Dodge.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary throw, which starts with the weapon already in hand.

### Pinning Throw

The javelin or knife strikes cloth and wood, nailing a sleeve or cloak to a door or the ground.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **DEX**/WIS/STR · **Prerequisite:** Thrown Weapons level 4
- **Effect (draft):** Declare before the Attack at −2. On a hit and a pass, you deal no damage, but the target is pinned and must spend their next round freeing themselves.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Nets, Bolas & Lassos*, which are made to entangle; a pinning throw borrows the wall to do it.

### Grenade Cook

The thrower pulls the pin, counts, and lets go when there is no time left to throw it back.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **COU**/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Thrown Weapons level 4
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, a thrown grenade bursts on landing and can't be thrown back or escaped by leaving its area in time. On a fail, it bursts in the air short of the target. Spectacular failure: it bursts close to you.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary grenade throw, which gives the enemy a moment to react.

## Not to be confused with

*[Slings](slings.md)*, which hurl a stone with a strap and reach much farther; thrown weapons leave the hand itself.
