# Polearms

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Halberds, glaives, pikes and all the ingenious cousins: a blade, spike, hook or hammer on a shaft longer than a man is tall. They were made for foot soldiers facing horsemen and armoured knights, and they make ordinary people dangerous in groups. A polearm can thrust, chop, hook a rider from the saddle, or trip a horse. It is clumsy alone and in close quarters, and formidable in a disciplined line where each fighter covers his neighbour.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **STR**/AGI/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** halberds, glaives, pikes

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Unhorse

The hook catches a rider's belt or collar and drags him down to where the footmen are waiting.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **STR**/DEX/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Polearms level 3
- **Effect (draft):** With a hooked polearm, against a mounted enemy. On a pass, the rider falls and takes damage as from a short fall. On a failure, you are dragged a few steps and lose your next Parry.
- **Not to be confused with:** Set Against the Charge, which stops the charge rather than the rider.

### Second Rank

From behind a friend's shoulder, the long blade reaches past and strikes.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **WIS**/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Polearms level 2
- **Effect (draft):** When an ally stands between you and the enemy. On a pass, you Attack as if you were in front, and the enemy can't strike back at you this round.
- **Not to be confused with:** Keep at Bay, which holds the enemy off rather than striking over an ally.

### Hook and Pull

The back of the head catches an ankle, a shield rim or a weapon and yanks.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Polearms level 4
- **Effect (draft):** Instead of attacking. On a pass, choose: trip the target, pull their shield aside for your next Attack, or drag them a step towards you.
- **Not to be confused with:** Hook the Shield, an axe technique that works at arm's length only.

## Not to be confused with

*[Spears](spears.md)*, which are shorter and simpler; and *[Great Weapons](great-weapons.md)*, which are heavier-headed but lack the reach.
