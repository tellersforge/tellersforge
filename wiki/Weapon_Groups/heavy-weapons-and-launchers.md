# Heavy Weapons & Launchers

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Machine guns on bipods and tripods, rocket and grenade launchers, flamethrowers: weapons that turn a soldier into a small army, at the price of weight, ammunition and very little subtlety. Crews carry, feed and fire them; one drops the belts in while the other aims. The family teaches setting up, clearing jams, leading moving vehicles, and the calm needed when every enemy on the field is looking for you. Heavy weapons break assaults, burn out bunkers and stop vehicles. They also draw fire like nothing else.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **STR**/PER/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** machine guns, launchers, flamers (electricity)

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Set the Gun

Bipod down, belt fed, arcs of fire marked on the ground: the gun is ready and waiting.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **INT**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Heavy Weapons & Launchers level 2
- **Effect (draft):** Spend a round setting up a heavy weapon on a mount or firm ground. On a pass, all your shots from it gain +2 until you move it. On a fail, the setup takes another round.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Held at the Ready*, a crossbow technique for one shot, not a whole field of fire.

### Leading the Target

The gunner fires not where the vehicle is, but where it is about to be.

- **Check:** 3 dice, **WIS**/INT/COU · **Prerequisite:** Heavy Weapons & Launchers level 4
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, you ignore the usual penalty for a fast-moving target this round. On a fail, the rocket or burst lands behind it and warns the crew.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Vehicle Gunnery*, which fires guns mounted on a vehicle; this technique shoots at one.

## Not to be confused with

*[Automatic Weapons](automatic-weapons.md)*, lighter rifles and submachine guns one person can carry and fire on the move.
