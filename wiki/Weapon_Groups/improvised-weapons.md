# Improvised Weapons

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Whatever is to hand: a chair, a bottle, a lamp, a spade, the leg of a table that was a table a moment ago. Nobody sets out to master this family, yet innkeepers, dockhands and old campaigners all seem to have. It teaches the eye to see a weapon in every room and the hand to judge weight, balance and breaking point in a heartbeat. The things rarely last beyond a few blows, and they rarely need to. Most fights won this way are won by surprise, and by the other man's disbelief.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **STR**/DEX/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** chairs, bottles, tools

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Whatever Comes Handy

A glance around the room, and the fighter's hand closes on the best thing in reach.

- **Check:** 1 die, **WIS** · **Prerequisite:** Improvised Weapons level 2
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, you snatch up an improvised weapon without losing any time this round, and it doesn't count as breaking on its first blow. On a fail, you grab something useless and lose your next Parry.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary weapon draw, which needs the weapon to be on you already.

### Shatter in the Face

The bottle, the jug or the chair breaks over the enemy's head on purpose, and the splinters do the rest.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Improvised Weapons level 3
- **Effect (draft):** Declare before the Attack. On a hit and a pass, the target also loses their next Parry or Dodge, dazed and blinking. The improvised weapon is destroyed whether you pass or not.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary improvised blow, which hopes the weapon survives.

### Table Between Us

The fighter heaves a bench, a cart or a table into the enemy's path and fights around it.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **WIS**/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Improvised Weapons level 4
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, one foe in reach can't close on you or strike you in melee this round without first spending the round to get past the obstacle. You can't Attack that round yourself.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Shields*, which are carried and can block blows; a table only gets in the way.

## Not to be confused with

*[Brawling](brawling.md)*, which fights with fists, knees and elbows alone; improvised fighting borrows the furniture.
