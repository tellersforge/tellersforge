# Energy Weapons

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Lasers, plasma throwers, particle beams and the other weapons of a spacefaring age: no recoil, no powder, often no sound but a crack or a hum. Power cells replace magazines, and heat replaces smoke. The family teaches managing charge, keeping cooling vents clear, aiming a beam that arrives at once, and the sense to stop firing before the weapon overheats. Soldiers, security officers and void pirates all carry them. On a world where armour is built for beams, a plain bullet can still be a nasty surprise.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **PER**/DEX/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** lasers, plasma, beams (spaceflight)

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Overcharge

The safety limiter is thumbed off and the next shot takes everything the cell can give.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **INT**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Energy Weapons level 3
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, your next shot does +3 damage but uses several times the usual charge. On a fail, the extra damage still applies but the weapon overheats and can't fire next round. Spectacular failure: it burns your hand, or worse.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Controlled Burst*, which saves ammunition rather than spending it.

### Sweeping Beam

The beam is held on and dragged across a line of foes like a blade of light.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/WIS · **Prerequisite:** Energy Weapons level 4
- **Effect (draft):** Beam weapons only. On a pass, make one Attack at −2 against each of up to three foes standing close together. Uses triple the usual charge.
- **Not to be confused with:** *Suppressing Fire*, which pins foes in cover rather than cutting across them.

## Not to be confused with

*[Long Guns](long-guns.md)*, which fire solid rounds with recoil and a slight drop over distance; energy weapons strike instantly and in a straight line.
