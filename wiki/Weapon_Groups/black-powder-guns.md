# Black-Powder Guns

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Muskets, early pistols, blunderbusses and the like: a tube of iron, a charge of powder rammed down the muzzle, a ball, and fire from a match, a wheel or a flint. They are loud, smoky, slow and unreliable in the rain, and they changed the world anyway. The family teaches the drill of loading, the care of powder and flint, and the nerve to stand in a line and wait for the order to fire. A single shot is usually all there is before the smoke clears and the blades come out.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **PER**/DEX/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** muskets, early pistols (gunpowder)

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Drilled Reload

Bite the cartridge, prime, pour, ram, present: the steps done without looking, even under fire.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Black-Powder Guns level 3
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, reloading a muzzle-loaded weapon takes one round less than normal (never less than one round). On a fail, it takes its usual time. Spectacular failure: a double charge or a lost ramrod, and the gun can't fire until fixed.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary reload, which each gun's record gives.

### Point-Blank Discharge

The gun is fired almost in the enemy's face, where the smoke and flash do as much as the ball.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **COU**/DEX · **Prerequisite:** Black-Powder Guns level 3
- **Effect (draft):** At point-blank range, on a hit and a pass, the target also loses their next Parry or Dodge, blinded and deafened for a moment. You can't use this in the same round you reload.
- **Not to be confused with:** an ordinary point-blank shot, which hurts but doesn't stun.

## Not to be confused with

*[Long Guns](long-guns.md)*, later rifles and shotguns loaded from the breech with ready-made cartridges, far faster and more accurate.
