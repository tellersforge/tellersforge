# Spears

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A sharp point on a long stick. The spear is older than farming, cheap to make and easy to teach, which is why most armies in history have been armies of spearmen. It keeps the enemy at the end of the shaft, where a sword can't reach. Held in one hand with a shield or in two with the full weight behind it, it thrusts, feints and wards. Hunters use it on boar and bear, warriors in tight rows. A spear wall is a hard thing to walk into.

## Game block

- **Attributes:** **STR**/AGI/COU (a weapon group is written on the sheet like a skill; Base = the average of these three, rounded down; Attack = Base + Lvl/Mod; Parry = Attack − 3 for close-combat groups)
- **Covers:** thrusting spears, one or two hands

## Techniques (draft)

Each technique is its own skill with the group as a prerequisite. Effects are first drafts until play-tested.

### Keep at Bay

The point weaves in the enemy's face, and every step forward runs onto it.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/COU · **Prerequisite:** Spears level 2
- **Effect (draft):** On a pass, an enemy with a shorter weapon who tries to close with you this round takes a free Attack from you first. On a failure, they close with you as normal.
- **Not to be confused with:** Inside the Guard, the knife fighter's answer to exactly this.

### Set Against the Charge

The butt goes into the ground, the point comes up, and the charging enemy does the work.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **COU**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Spears level 3
- **Effect (draft):** You must spend your turn braced. On a pass, a charging enemy or mount that reaches you takes your spear's damage plus their own charge bonus before they strike.
- **Not to be confused with:** a Lance charge, which delivers the same force from the other end.

### Shaft Sweep

The butt end swings low and takes the legs out from under the enemy.

- **Check:** 2 dice, **DEX**/STR · **Prerequisite:** Spears level 3
- **Effect (draft):** Instead of attacking. On a pass, the target falls and must spend their next turn getting up. On a failure, your next Parry is at −2.
- **Not to be confused with:** a Staves sweep, which is the same move from a fighter trained to use both ends.

## Not to be confused with

*[Polearms](polearms.md)*, which are longer and carry axe blades, hooks or hammers; and *[Lances](lances.md)*, which are for charging from horseback.
