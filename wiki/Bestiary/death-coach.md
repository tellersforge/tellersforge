# Death coach

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · large other · fast runner · myth, horror, historical, fantasy*

On certain nights the sound of wheels comes down the lane: iron rims on stone, harness jangling, no hoofbeats. A black coach drawn by black horses, all of them headless, the driver hunched and hooded or with no head at all. It stops outside one door, and within the week someone in that house will die.

It does not wait long, and it does not swerve. Anyone who stands in its road is run down; anyone who opens the door or tries to stop it is taken along. It fights to the death, if a thing already dead can be said to die. Country folk bar the shutters and do not look out. Some say throwing a handful of earth under the wheels will make it pass by.
