# Long-legged racing beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Equine and centauroid](../Species_Families/equine-and-centauroid.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · sci-fi*

A lanky, gentle runner on four long legs, with a narrow head, long drooping ears and a loping stride that eats up ground. In rich casino towns they are bred and trained for racing, and huge fortunes change hands on them. The best are pampered like royalty; the losers end up worked to exhaustion or worse.

They are timid by nature and flee at the first wound. Freed from their stables, whole herds panic and stampede through streets, trampling stalls, gamblers and anyone in their way. Those who care for them say they are clever and affectionate, and some rebels have freed them as much out of pity as to cause chaos.
