# Lightning beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth*

A wolf- or cat-shaped beast wreathed in crackling blue lightning, which falls to earth with the thunderbolt in a storm. It leaps from tree to tree and scorches the ground where it lands. It moves faster than the eye can follow.

It bites and claws with lightning in its teeth, and it flees when badly hurt. Some say it sleeps in people's navels during storms, so parents make their children cover their bellies. Trees split by lightning are said to bear its claw marks.
