# Jet-flying fire turtle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · gigantic quadruped · flyer, walk · modern, sci-fi, superhero*

A giant turtle with a domed shell, tusks jutting from its lower jaw and a hearty appetite for fire. It eats flames, fuel, burning oil and even the heat of volcanoes, and it grows stronger the more it swallows.

When it wants to fly, it pulls its head and legs into its shell and blasts jets of flame from the holes, spinning through the sky like a flaming disc. It can land, walk and fight upright too, and its shell turns shells and bombs. It is not always the enemy, and some say it has a soft spot for children, but it fights to the death once it has decided to fight.
