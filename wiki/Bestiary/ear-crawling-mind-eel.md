# Ear-crawling mind eel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · tiny serpentine · slow · sci-fi, horror*

A small, pale grub in a coat of overlapping armour plates, with tiny pincers at its front end and a body slick with something oily. On its own it is almost comically slow. The trouble begins when someone drops it into a victim's ear, or when the victim falls asleep near its nest.

It burrows inward and coils around the brain. The host becomes deeply suggestible, answering every question and obeying every order, and any attempt to resist brings blinding pain. Over the following days the mind frays, and the host goes mad and dies. Tyrants and interrogators keep them in sealed jars. The grub has no mind of its own and never flees, and getting one out without killing the host is a surgeon's nightmare.
