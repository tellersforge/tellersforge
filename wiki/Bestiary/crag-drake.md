# Crag drake

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · large quadruped · climber · fantasy*

A wingless drake the colour of wet slate, its back and flanks shingled with plates so like the rock around it that climbers often put a hand on one before they see the eye. It spends warm days stretched across a ledge, soaking up the sun, and it treats the whole cliff as its own.

It does not chase. It waits for climbers to reach the awkward part of the face, then sweeps them off with its tail or claws loose stone down onto them in a clattering slide. Near its nest it will not give ground at all, and shepherds who lose goats on the heights learn to take the long way round rather than go up after them.

## Not to be confused with
