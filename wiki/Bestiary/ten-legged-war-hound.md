# Ten-legged war hound

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium many-legged · fast runner · sci-fi*

A squat, frog-mouthed watchdog on ten short legs, low to the ground and broad as a barrel. Its mouth splits nearly the width of its head and is lined with three rows of teeth. It is ugly by any standard, and its masters adore it.

It is fast, tireless and utterly loyal. It guards its master's house, sleeps across the doorway and runs down anything that flees, however far it has to go. It fights to the death for its master. Travellers on dry worlds learn to stand very still when one approaches, and to wait until its master calls it off. Its affection, once won, is embarrassingly slobbery.
