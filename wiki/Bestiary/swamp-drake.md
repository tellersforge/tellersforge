# Swamp drake

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · large quadruped · walk, swimmer · fantasy, horror*

A heavy, mud-coloured drake with no wings, a broad, flat head and a long, paddle-like tail. It lies submerged in bog water for hours, only its eyes and nostrils showing among the reeds, and drags waders and drinking animals under.

When it surfaces to fight, it spits caustic bile that pits armour and scalds flesh. Poison and disease do not touch it, which is fortunate, given where it lives. It flees when badly hurt, sliding back into the murk. Marsh folk probe dark pools with long poles before crossing, and they leave the stillest water well alone.
