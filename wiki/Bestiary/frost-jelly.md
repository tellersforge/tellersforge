# Frost jelly

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · small amorphous · slow · fantasy*

A pale blue jelly the size of a dog, found in frozen caverns and snowfields. It hides under snow or in cracks in the ice, almost invisible.

It clings to whatever it touches, numbing and freezing flesh until it turns white and brittle. It is mindless and never flees. Fire harms it, while cold only makes it stronger. Mountain travellers and ice miners learn to probe the snow ahead, and to recognise the faint blue glow that betrays it.
