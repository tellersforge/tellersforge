# Giant snapping turtle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · large quadruped · swimmer · fantasy*

A mossy, ridged turtle as big as a rowing boat, lying in the shallows so still it looks like a rock. Weed grows on its shell. Its head is the size of a barrel, and its hooked beak can snap an oar in half or take a leg off at the knee. It does not chase; it waits, and bites whatever comes close enough.

It fights to the death, and even after death the jaws may close. Fishers and ferrymen learn where the old turtles lie and steer around them. Its shell is prized for shields and roofing, and river peoples sometimes honour a very old one as a guardian of the crossing, feeding it the first catch of the season to keep it content.
