# River jaguar

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · medium quadruped · climber · fantasy, historical, modern*

A stocky, heavy-headed cat with a rosetted gold coat, built for power rather than speed. Jaguars haunt rainforest rivers and swamps, where they swim readily and hunt turtles, caimans, fish and anything that comes to drink. Their bite is the strongest of any cat, and they often kill by driving their teeth straight through the skull or shell.

Jaguars are solitary and seldom seen, heard more often as a rasping cough at night. Forest peoples hold them in awe as masters of the river and the dark, and warriors wear their skins. They rarely trouble people, and one that is badly hurt will vanish back into the undergrowth.
