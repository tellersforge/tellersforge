# Fire-spitting hive grub

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · small many-legged · walk · sci-fi*

A small, pale hive insect about the size of a cat, soft-bodied, with a pair of sharp mandibles and a swollen gland at its rear. It breeds fast, and nests in colonies that grow under outbuildings, in abandoned mines and in the walls of settlements.

It spits a sticky, burning slime at intruders, and a swarm can set a building alight in a moment. They fight as long as the swarm fights. Colonists burn out the nests on sight, because an unchecked colony can overrun a settlement within a season. Some scrape the slime from dead grubs for fuel, though it has a way of catching at the wrong moment.
