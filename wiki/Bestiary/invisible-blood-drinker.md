# Invisible blood-drinker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · large other · flyer · horror*

An unseen thing that drifts through the air, tittering softly like a delighted child. Its victims feel only a sudden cold, then the sharp prick of many sucking mouths. As it drinks, it becomes visible: first a red smear hanging in the air, then a shape of veins and tentacles, filling out with the victim's blood.

It fights to the death, and it is terrible to face. Those who have survived say the laughter is the worst part. Throwing flour, dust or paint into the air shows it, and fire hurts it. Once it has fed, it can be struck like anything else.
