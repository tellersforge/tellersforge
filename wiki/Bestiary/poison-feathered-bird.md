# Poison-feathered bird

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · small flyer · flyer · fantasy, myth, historical*

A small, purple-green bird with a sharp beak that eats venomous snakes and grows poisonous from them. Its feathers are so venomous that a single one dipped in a cup of wine makes a deadly draught.

It flees at the first wound. Assassins and poisoners prize its feathers above gold, and rulers keep tasters for fear of them. Its nests are dangerous to touch, and fledglings are poisonous from birth.
