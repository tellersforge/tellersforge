# Small raptor

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · small biped · fast runner · prehistoric, fantasy*

A turkey-sized feathered hunter, fast and nervous, with a sickle claw on each foot and bright, darting eyes. It hunts in small packs, dashing at lizards, small mammals and the eggs of larger dinosaurs, and it harries stragglers and wounded beasts until they fall.

Alone, it is no threat to a grown person, and it flees at the first wound. In numbers it is dangerous, especially to the young or the injured. Camps of that age keep fires going and food hung high, because small raptors will follow a band for days, waiting for someone to fall behind.
