# Ash elemental

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md) · medium biped · walk · fantasy, post-apocalyptic*

A grey, crumbling figure of hot ash, shaped roughly like a man, left behind by great fires and eruptions. It walks slowly, leaving a trail of grey dust, and the air around it tastes of burnt wood.

When struck, it collapses into a drift of ash, then reforms a moment later, whole again. It is mindless and never flees. It smothers and burns with hot hands. Wind scatters it for a time, and water turns it to paste that takes a long while to pull itself back together. It is often found wandering the ruins of burned cities, as if searching for something it lost.
