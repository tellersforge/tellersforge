# Spined hunting hound

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · sci-fi, horror*

A quill-backed, long-jawed tracking beast, lean and muscular, with a row of spines down its back and a mouth full of teeth. Alien trophy hunters raise them to flush quarry toward their masters.

Packs drive prey into ambushes, and never give up a trail. They fight as long as the pack fights. Those who are hunted by them rarely escape, and survivors speak of the sound of their baying through the night. The hunters who keep them are feared across many worlds, and the hounds are feared almost as much.
