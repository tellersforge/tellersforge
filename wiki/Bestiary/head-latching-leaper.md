# Head-latching leaper

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md) · small many-legged · walk · sci-fi, horror*

A fleshy, pale disc the size of a dinner plate, on four short legs, with a hooked beak in the middle of its belly. It lurks in dark corners and vents and springs at heads with a wet slap. Once it clamps on, the beak goes in and the body below belongs to it. The host lurches to its feet and shambles on, arms reaching, the disc riding on top like a hat.

It is mindless and never flees. Leapers breed wherever there are bodies to take, and a quiet station or ship can fill with shambling puppets in days. Survivors wear helmets, watch the ceiling, and learn that what's left of a friend underneath can't be saved.
