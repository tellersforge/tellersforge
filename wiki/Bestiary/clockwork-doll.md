# Clockwork doll

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · tiny biped · walk · fantasy, horror*

A porcelain-faced wind-up doll with painted cheeks, lace clothes and a key in its back. It plays at being a toy: it curtseys, turns its head, sits on a shelf. At night it climbs down, and a thin blade slides out of its hand. It stabs sleepers and climbs back to its shelf before morning.

It fights to the end. Its one flaw is the ticking. In a silent room, it can be heard coming. Those who suspect a doll lie awake and listen. Its makers are usually assassins or jealous toymakers, and the dolls are sent as gifts to children of rich households, which makes them all the more hated.
