# Vampire bat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Chiropteran](../Species_Families/chiropteran.md) · tiny flyer · flyer · fantasy, historical, modern, horror*

A small brown bat with a pug face and razor-sharp front teeth. Vampire bats live in dark caves and hollow trees in warm lands and come out at night to feed on blood. They land near a sleeping cow, horse or person, crawl up on all fours, make a tiny painless cut and lap the blood that flows.

One feeding is not much, but the wounds bleed long, fester in the heat and can carry fever and madness. Herders lose cattle slowly to them, and travellers sleeping in the open wake with a stiff, bloodied toe. The legends of blood-drinking monsters owe something to this bat, which flees at the first wound.
