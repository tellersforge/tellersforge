# Magma ooze

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · medium amorphous · slow · fantasy*

A glowing, bubbling ooze of molten slag, orange and red, found near volcanic vents and foundries. It is too hot to approach, and the air shimmers above it.

It flows slowly from vents, melting metal and setting fire to anything it crosses. It is mindless and never flees. Weapons melt against it. Water turns to steam, but a great deal of cold water cools it into stone. Smiths near volcanic regions keep their forges far from vents, and watch for glowing trails in the dark.
