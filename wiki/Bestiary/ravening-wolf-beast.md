# Ravening wolf-beast

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth, horror, historical*

A wolf-like beast as large as a calf, with a wide chest, a long tail, reddish fur and a black stripe down its back. It stalks shepherds and herd children in remote uplands, and it kills for pleasure, leaving far more dead than it could ever eat.

It flees when badly hurt, but it always comes back. Whole regions have been terrorised by a single one, and rulers have sent companies of hunters, dogs and soldiers against it. Most came home with an ordinary wolf and a story. Some say it can only be killed by blessed silver.
