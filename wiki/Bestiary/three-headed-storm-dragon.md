# Three-headed storm dragon

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · gigantic winged biped · flyer · modern, sci-fi, fantasy*

A dragon of colossal size with three heads on long, twisting necks, two great bat-like wings and a body scaled in gleaming gold. It came down from the stars, and wherever it lands the sky above it darkens and fills with thunder.

Each head spits forked lightning, and together they burn cities to ash in a single sweep. It shows no sign of reason and no purpose except destruction. It has no fear and never flees. It can be driven off only by other giants, and the peoples who have faced it often say the only hope was that something bigger decided to fight it.
