# Walking brain beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · small quadruped · walk · sentient · fantasy, horror*

A living brain the size of a dog, on four stubby clawed legs, with a hard beak underneath. It can stun a mind with a flash of thought, leaving its victim blank and swaying. Then it climbs up, opens the skull, eats the brain and settles into the empty space. The body stands up again and walks away, with the brain-beast riding inside.

The puppet moves a little stiffly and speaks little, but friends often don't notice until it is too late. Brain-beasts serve their masters as scouts and spies. They flee when badly hurt, and always look for a new body first.
