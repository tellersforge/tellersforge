# Spore-jumping tardigrade

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · huge many-legged · walk · sci-fi*

A water-bear grown to the size of a house: a plump, armoured body on eight stubby, clawed legs, with a round, tube-shaped mouth and small, wet eyes. It survives vacuum, burning cold and killing heat, and its wounds close almost as fast as they are made.

It travels between stars along an invisible web of spores, vanishing from one place and appearing in another in an instant. Left free, it is calm and mild, and wanders peacefully. Caged, it becomes a thrashing fury that fights to the death and tears apart the walls that hold it. More than one research station has learned this. Navigators whisper that one could guide a ship anywhere, if anyone could persuade it.
