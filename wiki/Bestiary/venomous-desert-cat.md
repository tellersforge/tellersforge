# Venomous desert cat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · sci-fi*

A lion-sized cat of scorching deserts, lean and sand-coloured, with a heavy tufted tail and claws that drip a clear poison. It stalks along desert ridges at dusk and dawn, following herds and caravans until something falls behind.

A scratch poisons and needs medicine quickly; a bite kills. It is a patient hunter and prefers to wound and follow rather than fight. Once badly hurt, it slinks away. Desert travellers carry antidotes and keep fires burning, and anyone with a deep scratch is watched carefully through the night.
