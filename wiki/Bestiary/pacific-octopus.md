# Pacific octopus

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · medium other · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

The largest octopus of all, red-brown and wrinkled, with arms long enough to span a rowing boat and suckers the size of saucers. These giants live in cold coastal waters, lurking in rocky dens and hunting crabs, fish and even small sharks. Like their smaller cousins they change colour at will, squirt ink and slip through impossibly narrow gaps.

They rarely trouble people, but a diver who disturbs one can find an arm wrapped around a leg and the octopus dragging them against the rocks. Its beak can crack shells and bite through a wetsuit. Tales of monster octopuses owe a great deal to this animal; it flees when badly hurt.
