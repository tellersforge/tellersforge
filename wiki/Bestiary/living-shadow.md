# Living shadow

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · medium other · hover · sentient · fantasy, horror*

A shadow that moves on its own, slipping from dark corners and creeping along walls. It has no body, only a shape of darkness, and it is cold to the touch.

It drains strength from its victims, leaving them weak and cold, and then withdraws into the dark. It flees bright light, and a lantern or a fire is the best defence against it. Those who have been drained by it often feel cold for the rest of their lives.
