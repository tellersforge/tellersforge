# Bottlenose dolphin

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · medium swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A sleek grey small whale with a short beak and a permanent curving smile. Dolphins live in chattering pods along coasts and in open seas, hunting fish together by herding them into balls or driving them onto mud banks. They find their way and their prey by clicks and echoes, and they seem to take a real delight in play: riding bow waves, leaping and tossing seaweed.

Sailors' tales are full of dolphins that guided lost ships or pushed drowning swimmers to shore, and some of those tales are true. They are also tough fighters that will ram a shark to death with their snouts. A dolphin that is badly hurt swims away.
