# Burrowing alien antlion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium many-legged · burrower · sci-fi*

A spike-legged bug about the size of a dog, with a sandy, ridged shell and a pair of short, stiff wings folded along its back. It lives in hives that stretch for miles beneath beaches and deserts, and it feels the tread of a boot through the sand from far away.

When something walks overhead, they erupt from the sand in dozens, buzzing in short, clumsy flights and stabbing with their spiked legs. They fight as long as the swarm fights. The locals know which sands are hollow and cross only on rock or at low tide. Strangers who stroll along the empty beach are the reason the locals know.
