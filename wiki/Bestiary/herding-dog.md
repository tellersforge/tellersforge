# Herding dog

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, historical, modern*

A quick, medium-sized working dog with bright eyes and a restless mind. Herding dogs are bred to move sheep and cattle, and they do it by stare, circle and a well-timed nip at the heels. A good one reads the flock better than the shepherd and answers whistled commands across a whole hillside.

They are loyal, tireless and easily bored, and a herding dog without work will herd children, chickens or guests instead. In a fight they bite fast and dart away, and they flee when badly hurt. Their real value lies in the watching: nothing crosses a pasture at night without the dog knowing, and the whole farm hearing about it.
