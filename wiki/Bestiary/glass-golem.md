# Glass golem

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium biped · walk · fantasy*

A figure of faceted coloured glass, from ruby to deep blue, jointed like a stained-glass window come to life. When it moves it throws rainbows across the walls, and when the light is right it blazes so brightly that foes throw up their hands to shield their eyes. Its forearms are ground into long, cruel edges.

It is mindless and never retreats. It is also brittle: a hammer or mace can crack it, and when it breaks it bursts in a storm of razor shards that cuts anyone standing close. Glass golems guard temples, jewellers' vaults and the towers of wizards who like to be looked at. Fighting one in darkness removes its best trick.
