# Cave bear

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ursine](../Species_Families/ursine.md) · large quadruped · walk · prehistoric, fantasy, historical*

A massive ice-age bear, heavier than any living kind, with a domed skull and a thick brown pelt. It dens in deep caves and does not share them. When a cave bear rears up at the mouth of its den, it fills the opening, and its smashing claws can break ribs through furs.

It eats mostly plants and roots, but it defends its cave with complete ferocity and fights to the death. Stone-age peoples revere it. Its skulls are arranged in shrines deep in caves, and hunters who kill one take on its name. Those who shelter in caves learn to check for bear sign first: scratches on the walls, bones on the floor and that deep, musky smell.

## Not to be confused with

the *[brown bear](brown-bear.md)*, the ordinary great bear of forests and mountains; the cave bear is larger, older and guards its den to the death.
