# Hunting cheetah

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · medium quadruped · sprinter · fantasy, historical, modern*

A slender, long-legged cat with a small head, black tear-lines down its face and a spotted coat, built like a greyhound. The cheetah is the fastest runner on land, but only for a short burst. It stalks as close as it can across open plains, then explodes into a sprint, trips its prey with a swipe of the paw and holds it by the throat.

The effort leaves it exhausted, and lions, hyenas and even vultures often steal the kill before it can eat. A cheetah will not fight for it and flees at the first wound. Princes of hot lands once kept tame cheetahs, hooded like falcons, and took them hunting on horseback.
