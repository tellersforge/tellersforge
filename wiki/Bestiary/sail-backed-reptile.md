# Sail-backed reptile

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · large quadruped · walk · prehistoric, fantasy*

A sprawling predator from before the dinosaurs, as long as a horse, with a great sail of skin stretched on spines along its back. It is often mistaken for a dinosaur, but it is older and closer to the ancestors of mammals. It basks in the morning sun to warm its body, turning its sail toward the light, and then it hunts.

It lunges at prey from cover with a sudden burst of speed, biting with a mouth full of teeth of different sizes. It flees when badly hurt. Its sail flushes red and blue when it is warm and ready, and people who know its signs keep away at those times.
