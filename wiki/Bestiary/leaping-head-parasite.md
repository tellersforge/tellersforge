# Leaping head parasite

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md) · small many-legged · walk · sci-fi, horror*

A flat, shelled crawler about the size of a dinner plate, ringed with short, twitching legs and hiding a hooked beak underneath. It lurks in vents, ducts and dark corners, and it can jump a startling distance.

It leaps at the head of anything that passes, clamps on and drives its beak into the skull. The host goes limp for a moment, then gets up again, staggering and blank-eyed, a husk steered by the thing on its head. It has no mind of its own beyond the urge to ride, and never flees. Crews that have fought them wear helmets at all times, and burn every body afterwards to be sure.
