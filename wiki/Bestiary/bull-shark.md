# Bull shark

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · medium swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A stocky, blunt-nosed grey shark with small eyes and a notoriously bad temper. Bull sharks can live in fresh water as well as salt, and they swim far up great rivers and into lakes, hunting fish, turtles, dogs and anything else in the murky shallows where bathers and washerwomen go.

They are aggressive and bite first, often in water so muddy that neither the shark nor its victim can see. River peoples far from any sea have learned to fear them without always knowing what they are. A bull shark that is badly hurt will turn away, but by then the damage is usually done.
