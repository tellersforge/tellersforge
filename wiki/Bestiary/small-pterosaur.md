# Small pterosaur

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · small flyer · flyer · prehistoric, fantasy*

A leathery-winged flying reptile about the size of a crow, with a long beak, a furry body and wings of skin stretched from a single long finger. It nests in noisy colonies on sea cliffs and crags, thousands of them crowded together, wheeling and shrieking over the surf and diving for fish.

Alone, it is a nuisance. At the colony, it is a storm: intruders are mobbed by a cloud of pecking beaks and beating wings, and climbers have been knocked from ledges by the sheer mass of them. Each bird flees at the first wound, but there is always another. Egg-gatherers climb the cliffs anyway, because the eggs are rich and the droppings make fine fertiliser.
