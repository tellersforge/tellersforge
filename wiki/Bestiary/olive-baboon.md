# Olive baboon

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md) · medium quadruped · climber · fantasy, historical, modern*

A heavy-set, dog-faced monkey with olive-grey fur, a long snout and canine teeth like daggers. Baboons live in large, noisy troops on savannah and rocky hills, sleeping on cliffs or in trees and spending the day foraging on the ground for grass, roots, insects and whatever else they can find.

They raid crops and camps with bold efficiency, stealing food, tearing open bags and screeching at anyone who tries to stop them. The big males fight off leopards, and the whole troop mobs anything that threatens them. Farmers keep dogs and boys with slings in the fields to drive them off.
