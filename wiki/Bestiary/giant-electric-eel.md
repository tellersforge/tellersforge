# Giant electric eel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · large serpentine · swimmer · fantasy*

A river eel as thick as a man's body and long as a canoe, grey-brown and slow-moving, lurking in murky tropical waters. It lives in muddy channels and flooded forests, and when anything wades in, it sends a burst of current through the water that stuns every fish, beast and person nearby. Then it bites the ones that stopped moving.

The shock is felt as a hammer-blow to the whole body, and those caught standing in the water often drown before they can recover. River folk cross its haunts in boats with dry bottoms and drive cattle across first, hoping the eel spends its charge on them. It flees when badly hurt, trailing a faint crackle through the water.
