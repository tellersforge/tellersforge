# Walking kaiju crab

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md) · gigantic many-legged · walk, swimmer · modern, sci-fi*

A lobster-like crustacean the size of a city block, with a spiny shell, long whipping feelers and two enormous claws. It lives in the deep sea and wades ashore at night, dripping with weed and trailing a smell of salt and rot.

It feeds by prying open buildings as if they were shellfish, snipping off roofs and walls with its claws and plucking out what is inside. Harbours, piers and whole waterfronts are crushed in a single visit. It has no fear and never flees. Coastal cities that know its habits turn out every searchlight they have and pray it chooses another shore.
