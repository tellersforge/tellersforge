# Armoured jaw fish

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · huge swimmer · swimmer · prehistoric, fantasy*

A heavy armoured fish from before the dinosaurs, as long as a river barge, its head and shoulders sheathed in thick bony plates. It has no true teeth. Instead its jaws carry sharp-edged plates of bone that grind against each other as it bites and keep themselves keen, so they close on prey like a pair of shears.

It prowls warm, shallow seas, slow to turn but quick to strike, and it slices through sharks, other armoured fish and anything else it catches. It fights to the death. Sailors who find themselves in its age speak of the sound more than the sight: a heavy crunch in the water, like a gate slamming.
