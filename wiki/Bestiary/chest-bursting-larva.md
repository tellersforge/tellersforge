# Chest-bursting larva

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · tiny serpentine · fast runner · sci-fi, horror*

A toothy, eyeless worm, pale and slick with its host's blood, that tears its way out of a living chest with a shriek. At first it is no longer than a forearm. It does not stay that size for long.

Once free, it flees at once, darting into ducts and dark corners to hide and grow, shedding its skin as it goes. It flees at the first wound, and its blood burns like acid, so hunting it is dangerous work. Crews who find a burst body know they have a short time to catch the larva before it becomes something much larger, and they rarely manage it.
