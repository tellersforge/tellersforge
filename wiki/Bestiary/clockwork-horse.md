# Clockwork horse

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, historical*

A wind-up steed of brass and steel, with a mane of wire and hooves that ring on cobbles. It runs without rest, food or water, as long as its spring is wound, and it does not shy at fire or noise. A rider need only turn a key in its chest each morning.

It kicks when commanded and carries its rider into the thickest fight without complaint. It is mindless and never flees. It is also tiring to own: it needs oiling, repairs and a trusted smith, and a bent gear can leave it standing like a statue in the middle of a road. Rich messengers, eccentric knights and mechanical cavalry use them.
