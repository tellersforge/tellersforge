# Storm wyvern

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · large winged biped · fast flyer · fantasy, myth*

A lean, blue-grey wyvern with long, narrow wings built for speed, that lives inside thunderheads and rides them for days without landing. Sailors and herders see it only as a dark shape moving against the flicker of the clouds.

It hunts from the storm itself, spitting crackling bolts at the ground to stun or kill, then folding its wings and stooping with its talons to snatch whatever fell. It flees when badly hurt, climbing back into the cloud. People who live under stormy skies grow wary of lightning that strikes the same field twice, and shepherds bring their flocks in when a storm lingers too long over one valley.
