# Faceless winged gaunt

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md) · medium winged biped · flyer · horror, fantasy*

Thin, black and rubbery, with inward-curving horns, barbed tails and wide bat-like wings, the gaunts have no faces at all. They make no sound in flight. They roost in the high crags of lonely mountains and on the edges of dreams, and they come out at night in silent groups.

A gaunt seizes a traveller or a sleeper from behind and carries them up into the dark, tickling them with its clawed fingers until they cannot resist, then drops them somewhere far away or delivers them to whatever it serves. They are not brave. A few hard blows and they let go and flap off. Mountain folk sleep indoors, keep a fire lit, and never climb the high peaks alone.
