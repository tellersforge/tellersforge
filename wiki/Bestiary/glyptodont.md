# Glyptodont

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mustelid and small mammal](../Species_Families/mustelid-and-small-mammal.md) · large quadruped · slow · prehistoric, fantasy*

An armadillo the size of a small cart, with a domed shell of fused bony plates like a turtle's and a thick tail ringed with spikes or ending in a club. It shuffles across grassland and scrub, grazing, and when threatened it hunkers down under its shell and swings its tail at anything that comes close.

Its shell is nearly impossible to break, and it fights while it can. Hunters of its age flip it over or wait for it to tire. Some peoples use its empty shells as shelters, sleeping under them like tents, and a band that finds one has a roof for the season.
