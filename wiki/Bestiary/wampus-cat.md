# Wampus cat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, horror, modern*

A big, yellow-eyed cat of wooded hills, sleek and dark, that sometimes rises and walks upright like a person. Its yowl carries at night across the valleys, a sound somewhere between a woman's scream and a cat fight. Hunters who hear it pack up their camp early.

Worse than its claws is its look. Meeting its eyes, even briefly, fills the heart with terror, and some who saw it clearly never recovered their wits. It flees when badly hurt, but it is rarely hurt at all. Old hill folk say it was once a woman who spied on a forbidden rite and was cursed to wear a cat's skin forever.
