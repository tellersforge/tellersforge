# Common chimpanzee

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md) · medium biped · climber · fantasy, historical, modern*

A black-haired ape with a pale face, expressive eyes and long, powerful arms. Chimpanzees live in forest troops, sleeping in nests high in the trees and foraging for fruit, leaves and insects. They use sticks to fish for termites and stones to crack nuts, and they hunt monkeys as a team.

They are far stronger than they look. Troop males patrol their borders and wage war on neighbouring troops, and they throw sticks, stones and dung at intruders. A chimpanzee that decides a stranger is a threat can maul them savagely, and the troop fights as one.
