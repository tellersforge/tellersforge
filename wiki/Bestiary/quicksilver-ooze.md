# Quicksilver ooze

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · medium amorphous · fast runner · fantasy, sci-fi*

A mirror-bright pool of living liquid metal, gleaming silver, rippling as it moves. It flows quickly across floors and walls, as fast as a running man, and it reflects its surroundings so well it can almost vanish.

It reshapes itself as it strikes, forming spikes, blades and grasping hands. It is mindless and never flees. Blades pass through it and it reforms. Cold slows it, and strong magnetism is said to hold it still. It is sometimes found in the ruins of alchemists' laboratories or the vaults of forgotten machines.
