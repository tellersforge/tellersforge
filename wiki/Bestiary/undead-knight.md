# Undead knight

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium biped · walk · sentient · fantasy, horror*

A fallen knight bound in black plate by its oath or its crime, with a cold light burning behind its visor. It rides at the head of undead armies, a black sword in its hand, and it still carries itself with the pride and skill of a champion.

It leads skeletons into battle and duels champions, and it fights to the death. It is the most dangerous kind of undead: it thinks, it plans and it remembers. Some say it can be freed by fulfilling its oath or atoning for its crime. Others say it can only be destroyed.
