# Bedroom bogey

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · medium other · climber · myth, horror, modern, fantasy*

Every child knows it is there. It lives in the gap under the bed, behind the half-open wardrobe door, in the corner the candle does not reach. Grown-ups never see it, which is how it likes things. When it does show itself it is all long grey fingers, too many knuckles and a pair of eyes that catch the light.

The bogey feeds on fear more than flesh, drawing out a child's terror night after night until the house is sick with it. Its claws are real enough when cornered. It hates light above everything and flees from a lamp, an open curtain or a flung-open door. Old remedies are simple: a candle left burning, a sprig of something bitter under the pillow, and a parent who believes the child.
