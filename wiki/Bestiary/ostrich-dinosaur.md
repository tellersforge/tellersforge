# Ostrich dinosaur

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · medium biped · fast runner · prehistoric, fantasy*

A long-legged, long-necked dinosaur with a small, toothless beak, built like an ostrich and faster than a horse. It lives in flocks on open plains, grazing on plants, seeds, insects and small animals, and at any sign of danger the whole flock bolts in unison, a stampede of long legs and dust.

It is not dangerous unless you are in its way. Cornered, it kicks hard with clawed feet. It flees at the first wound. Hunters of its age find it hard to catch, and they drive flocks into narrow valleys or ambush them at water holes.
