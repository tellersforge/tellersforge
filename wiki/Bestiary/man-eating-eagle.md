# Man-eating eagle

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · large flyer · flyer · fantasy, myth, prehistoric*

A giant eagle with a wingspan wider than a house, which nests on mountain crags. It swoops from the sky and snatches people, carrying them back to its nest to feed its young.

It flees when badly hurt. Mountain villages build shelters along paths and keep a lookout on the sky. Heroes climb to its eyrie to rescue the taken, and some come back.
