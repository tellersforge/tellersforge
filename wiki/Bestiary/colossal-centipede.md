# Colossal centipede

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · huge many-legged · climber · fantasy, horror*

A centipede longer than a river barge, with plated segments as broad as a cartwheel and legs like curved spears. It hunts the deep jungle and the black tunnels beneath the earth, slithering over fallen trunks and along cave roofs, and when it finds prey it coils its whole length around it and bites again and again, pumping venom until the struggling stops.

Nothing short of a monster can fight it in the open. It does not flee, does not tire, and its plates shrug off spears. Tribes of the deep forest carve its image on their gates as a guardian. Delvers who see its shed skins, hollow and man-high, in a tunnel ahead of them usually decide the tunnel goes nowhere interesting.
