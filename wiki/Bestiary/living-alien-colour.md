# Living alien colour

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · large amorphous · hover · horror, sci-fi*

It came down from the sky in a stone that shrank and vanished, and something went into the well. The colour is not a colour anyone has a name for. It glows faintly at night in the water, in the grass, in the eyes of animals. At first the orchards bear huge fruit that tastes of ash. Then everything greys, crumbles and dies.

It does not hunt in any ordinary way. It settles into a place and drinks the life of it: crops, livestock, and then the minds and bodies of the people who stay. It never leaves on its own. Families who should flee stay on, strangely unwilling to go. The only cure anyone has found is abandoning the land entirely, and even then nothing grows there again.
