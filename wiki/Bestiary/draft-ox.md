# Draft ox

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · slow · fantasy, historical, modern*

A massive, castrated bull, yoked in pairs to pull ploughs, carts and heavy wagons. Oxen are slow, steady and immensely strong, and they will plod on through mud and over stony ground where horses would balk. They are cheaper to feed than horses and, at the end of their working lives, they are eaten.

Oxen are placid and patient, but their horns can still gore. They shrug off blows and pull loads that seem impossible. Many civilisations have been built on their backs, and in some lands a farmer's wealth is still counted by the number of his oxen.
