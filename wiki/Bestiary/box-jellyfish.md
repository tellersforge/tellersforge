# Box jellyfish

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · small other · drifter · fantasy, historical, modern*

A pale, box-shaped bell no bigger than a bucket, almost invisible in clear water, trailing long thin tentacles armed with countless stinging cells. Box jellyfish drift in warm coastal shallows, and unlike most jellyfish they swim deliberately and even have crude eyes.

They have no mind to speak of and never flee. Anyone who brushes a tentacle is stung at once, with pain like a burning whip, and a heavy sting can stop the heart within minutes. Coastal peoples keep away from the water in the season when they drift in, and swimmers who must go in wear thin cloth over every inch of skin.
