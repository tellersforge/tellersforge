# Marsupial lion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Marsupial](../Species_Families/marsupial.md) · medium quadruped · climber · prehistoric, fantasy*

A thick-limbed hunter the size of a leopard, with powerful forearms, a hooked claw on each thumb and teeth that work like shears. It is not a true lion but a pouched animal of the ancient southern lands, and it hunts from the trees, dropping onto large prey from above.

Its bite is among the strongest of any beast its size. It can drag prey much larger than itself up into the branches to eat in safety. It flees when badly hurt. Travellers watch the canopy as much as the ground, and they learn the warning cry of birds that have spotted one.
