# Rock-backed desert crab

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md) · large many-legged · walk · sci-fi*

A heavy desert crab, as broad as a table, whose shell is lumpy, grey and pitted, and looks exactly like weathered rock. It squats among real boulders, perfectly still, for days at a time.

When something passes within reach, it snaps with huge claws, crushing whatever it catches. It flees when badly hurt, scuttling sideways surprisingly fast. Desert travellers learn to watch the rocks around waterholes, and to throw a stone at any boulder that looks a little too convenient to sit on. Some desert tribes use its shell as armour.
