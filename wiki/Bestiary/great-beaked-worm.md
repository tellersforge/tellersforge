# Great beaked worm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · large serpentine · climber · fantasy*

The swollen matriarch of a nest of beaked worms, as long as a longboat and thick enough to swallow a small person whole. Her head splits into four great barbed tentacles around a beak like a plough blade. She lies coiled in the heart of her nest, surrounded by her lesser brood, and rarely moves unless the nest is threatened.

When it is, she fights to the death. She guards her eggs fiercely, and her young swarm around her. Cave-dwellers who find a nest usually seal it off and leave. Destroying one takes fire, many spears and a great deal of nerve.
