# Sword wraith

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · medium other · hover · sentient · fantasy, horror*

The ghost of a warrior, still clutching a spectral blade and wearing ghostly armour. It stands guard over the battlefields where it fell, and it challenges anyone who draws steel near it.

Its blade cuts both flesh and courage, and those it wounds feel their nerve fail. It fights to the death, and it is a skilled and deadly duellist. Some say it can be laid to rest by defeating it in a fair fight, or by burying its bones with honour.

## Not to be confused with
