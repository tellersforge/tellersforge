# Brass golem

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · large biped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A figure of hammered brass plates, polished bright or gone green with age, with a furnace burning in its belly. It hisses with every step, and its skin is too hot to touch. It spends its quiet hours feeding coal into the grate in its chest, as a smith stokes a forge.

When enemies gather, it opens its chest and vents a roaring sheet of flame over them. Blades heat and blister the hands that hold them. It is mindless and never flees. The best tactic is to starve its fire: wet it down, flood the room, or let it burn its fuel before closing in. It was made to guard workshops, foundries and the halls of fire-priests.
