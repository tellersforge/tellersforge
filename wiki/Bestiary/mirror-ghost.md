# Mirror ghost

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · medium other · teleports · sentient · horror, modern, fantasy*

You see it first as something wrong in the glass: a face over your shoulder that is not there when you turn, a figure standing in a reflected doorway. It lives on the far side of mirrors, polished shields and still dark water, and it has learned to be patient. Anyone who stares too long into the glass draws it closer.

It steps out of one reflection and appears behind the victim in another, then reaches with cold hands and pulls them back into the glass with it. Those taken are sometimes glimpsed later, hammering on the other side. When hurt it retreats into its mirror. Houses that know of it cover every glass with cloth at night, and smashing the right mirror is said to end it.
