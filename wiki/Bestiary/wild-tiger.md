# Wild tiger

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern, myth*

The largest of the great cats, orange and cream striped with black so that it vanishes into reeds, grass and dappled shade. Tigers live alone across jungle, reed-bed and cold northern forest, each claiming a vast range. They hunt by stealth, creeping close and striking from behind with a bite to the neck. Unlike most cats, they love water and swim well.

A tiger that has learned people are easy prey is the most feared animal in the forest. Villagers wear masks on the backs of their heads, walk in groups and light fires at night. Yet most tigers avoid people, and a badly hurt one will slip away to lick its wounds rather than fight on.
