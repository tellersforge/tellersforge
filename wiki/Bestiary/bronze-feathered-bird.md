# Bronze-feathered bird

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · medium flyer · flyer · fantasy, myth*

A marsh bird the size of a heron, with feathers of sharp, gleaming bronze that rattle as it moves. It lives in flocks over marshes and lakes, and when threatened, it flings its feathers down in rattling showers that cut like knives. It also bites and scratches, and it eats people.

It fights while the flock fights. Its feathers can pierce armour and shields. Loud noise scatters the flock: the beating of pans, the clash of cymbals or the blast of a horn sends them into the air in panic. The heroes of old tales who drove them away did it with noise, and then shot them as they flew.
