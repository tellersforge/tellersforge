# Spine-backed brute

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, modern*

A heavy, horned brute of northern swamps and thickets, with a squat frog-like face, a row of spines along its back and the stench of rotting meat and woodsmoke. It lurks in dense undergrowth, often downwind of the trail, and the smell is usually the only warning.

It charges out without warning, goring with its horns and raking with its spines. It fights when cornered and otherwise lumbers away through the brush. Woodsmen who smell rot in the thickets turn back, and dogs refuse to follow its trail. Its tracks are wide, clawed and oddly human.
