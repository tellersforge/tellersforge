# Great tunnel worm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · huge serpentine · burrower · fantasy*

A colossal worm ringed in bands of violet and dull grey, thick enough that a mounted rider could stand in its mouth. It bores through soil and bedrock alike, chewing stone with rings of grinding teeth, and it leaves behind tunnels that miners later find and fear.

It is blind and hunts by feeling vibrations through the ground. It turns towards footsteps, hoofbeats and the ring of picks, and bursts upward to swallow its prey whole, armour and all. It has no mind, no fear and never flees. Those crossing its ground move softly and in short bursts, and some tunnel towns keep scouts listening at the walls.
