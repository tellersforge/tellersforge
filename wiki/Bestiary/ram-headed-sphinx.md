# Ram-headed sphinx

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A lion-bodied beast with a ram's curled horns and heavy woolly head, carved in avenues outside temples and, more rarely, found alive in the same places. It lies still for days before temple doors, watching. Then an intruder comes, and it rises and charges, horns first.

It butts like a battering ram and claws anyone knocked down. It never leaves its post and fights to the death. Priests who tend it bring it food and speak softly around it. Grave-robbers who hear about it try to come at night, and learn that it sees quite well in the dark.
