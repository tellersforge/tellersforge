# Sabre-toothed pre-mammal

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · large quadruped · walk · prehistoric, fantasy*

A bear-sized hunting creature from before the dinosaurs, with a heavy skull, powerful jaws and two long upper fangs like daggers. It is half reptile, half mammal, with sprawling legs and a thick body. It hunts by ambush, waiting in the undergrowth and lunging to bite with its fangs.

It fights to the death. Its fangs can pierce tough hides and stab deep into flesh, and it holds on until its prey stops moving. Wanderers who stray into its time, if any, learn to watch the undergrowth closely, and they learn that the creature's patience is longer than their own.
