# Mastodon

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Pachyderm](../Species_Families/pachyderm.md) · huge quadruped · walk · prehistoric, fantasy, historical*

A stocky cousin of the mammoth, lower and heavier, with coarse brown hair, a long flat skull and straight tusks that jut forward like spears. It lives in forests and swamps rather than open steppe, browsing on branches, shrubs and water plants, and it moves alone or in small family groups.

It is peaceful until cornered, and then it charges, tusks first, crashing through undergrowth that stops everything else. It flees when badly hurt. Hunters of the old forests track it by broken branches and trampled paths, and they hunt it with pits and fire rather than meeting it head on.

## Not to be confused with

the *[woolly mammoth](woolly-mammoth.md)*, the taller, curling-tusked steppe elephant; the mastodon is stockier, lives in forests and has straight tusks.
