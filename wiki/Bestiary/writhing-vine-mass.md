# Writhing vine mass

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · large plant · slow · fantasy, horror, sci-fi*

A seething knot of thorny tendrils that has made a ruin its body. It fills cellars, halls and collapsed towers from wall to wall, and when something living steps inside, every vine in the room moves at once. There is no front to it and no head to strike. Cut a tendril away and two more come creeping back to fill the gap within a day.

It lashes, tangles and drags, and whatever it catches ends up as mulch at its roots. It cannot be frightened and cannot be reasoned with, only starved, burned or dug out root by root. Villages near old ruins sometimes seal the doorways with stone and leave it to the dark rather than try.
