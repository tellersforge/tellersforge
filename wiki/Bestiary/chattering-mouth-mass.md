# Chattering mouth mass

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · medium amorphous · slow · fantasy, horror*

A heaving blob of flesh the size of a cart, covered in eyes and mouths that open and close at random. Every mouth babbles at once, a flood of nonsense, laughter, whispers and screams, and the noise scrambles the mind of anyone who hears it. It crawls slowly toward the living, spits blinding goo from its mouths, and engulfs anyone too stunned to run.

It has no mind of its own and never flees. People who survive it describe a headache that lasts for days, and a habit of hearing voices in the silence afterward. Wax in the ears helps. Distance helps more.
