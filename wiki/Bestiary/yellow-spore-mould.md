# Yellow spore mould

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md) · tiny plant · rooted · fantasy*

A furry yellow patch of mould spreading across damp stone, old timber and forgotten stores. It looks like nothing, the kind of growth a housekeeper scrubs away without thinking. But when disturbed, by a hand, a boot or a careless torch, it coughs out a cloud of fine yellow spores that sting the lungs and poison the blood.

Tomb robbers and cellar-clearers fear it more than traps. It is often found on the doors and chests of sealed vaults, where it has had a long time to grow. Fire kills it, but a torch thrust into it releases one last, deadly cloud first.
