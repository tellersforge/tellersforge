# Mud elemental

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md) · large amorphous · slow · fantasy*

A heaving mass of wet earth and marsh water, brown and black, with reeds and roots trailing from it. It lies still as a patch of bog, and waders cross it without a thought. Then it rises into a lumpish, dripping shape.

It sucks at legs and holds them fast, dragging people down into itself. It is mindless and never flees. Blades pass in and out of it with little effect. Drying it out is the best tactic: fire, sun or strong wind leave it crumbling and brittle. Marsh folk probe the ground ahead with long poles and never walk alone.
