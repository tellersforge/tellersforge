# Shield-eating tube

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · medium other · slow · fantasy*

A slow, fleshy tube as tall as a man, pale and ringed like a worm, standing upright on its base. It slurps up anyone in reach, swallowing them whole. Inside, it digests their shield or armour, and then spits them out, dazed and unharmed, but without their gear.

It is mindless and never flees. It is more embarrassing than dangerous, but losing your armour in the wrong place can be deadly. Warriors learn to keep their distance, and to leave their shields behind if they must go near.
