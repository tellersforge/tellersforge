# Blizzard elemental

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md) · huge other · flyer · fantasy*

A towering whirl of snow, ice and freezing wind, taller than a castle tower, with a hollow roar at its heart. It brings a storm wherever it goes, and the cold around it freezes eyelashes and cracks stone. From a distance it looks like a single white column moving across the mountains.

It howls down high passes and buries travellers in drifts. It is mindless and never flees. There is no fighting it in the usual sense. Mountain folk watch the sky, retreat to caves and huts, and wait for it to pass. Some summoners bind them to guard their fortresses, and those fortresses stand in endless winter.

## Not to be confused with

The *[Living tornado](living-tornado.md)*, a funnel of wind that tears across open ground; the blizzard elemental is a storm of snow and cold that buries.
