# Southern cassowary

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · large biped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern*

A huge, flightless forest bird as tall as a man, with shaggy black plumage, a bare blue and red neck and a tall horny crest on its head. Its three-toed feet end in claws, and the inner one is a long dagger that can slice open a belly with a single kick.

Cassowaries live in thick rainforest, eating fallen fruit and walking quiet trails. They are shy, but a cassowary guarding a chick or cornered on a path may charge, leaping and kicking forward with both feet. Forest peoples respect and hunt them, and never turn their backs on one. A badly hurt cassowary crashes away into the undergrowth.
