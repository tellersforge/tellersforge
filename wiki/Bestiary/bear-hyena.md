# Bear-hyena

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ursine](../Species_Families/ursine.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, modern*

A shaggy, high-shouldered night predator with a hyena's sloping back and heavy head and a bear's powerful paws. It raids villages after dark, breaking into huts and dragging people off, and it is said to eat the brains of its victims first.

Its eyes shine in torchlight, and it sees clearly in pitch darkness. It avoids fighting in the open and prefers to take the weak and sleeping, but when cornered it fights hard with claws and a crushing bite. Villagers light fires at night and build thorn fences around their homes when it is about.
