# Giant sea scorpion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · large many-legged · swimmer · prehistoric, fantasy*

An armoured sea arthropod as long as a man, with a segmented body, a broad flat tail and two grasping forelimbs lined with spines. It lives in the shallow seas and estuaries of ancient ages, half buried in the mud, waiting for fish or anything else that comes within reach.

It grabs, holds and tears, and it fights while it can. Its armour is thin but tough, and its claws can hold a struggling swimmer long enough to drown them. People who find themselves wading its shallows learn to watch the mud for a pair of small eyes and stay out of its reach.

## Not to be confused with

the *[giant scorpion](giant-scorpion.md)*, the land-dwelling desert beast with a killing sting; the sea scorpion lives in water and grabs with its claws.
