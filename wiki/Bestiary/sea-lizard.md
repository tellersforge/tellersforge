# Sea lizard

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · gigantic swimmer · swimmer · prehistoric, fantasy*

A vast, scaly sea lizard with a long body, flipper-like limbs and a tail with a shark-like fin at its end. It hunts near the surface, breaching to snatch birds, fish, turtles and swimmers, and it is fast and powerful, capable of leaping clear of the water.

It fights to the death. Its jaws have a second row of teeth on the roof of its mouth, so prey that goes in rarely comes out. Sailors of its age fear it more than storms. It is often seen near the coast, hunting in shallow bays and river mouths.
