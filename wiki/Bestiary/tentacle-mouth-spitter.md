# Tentacle-mouth spitter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md) · medium biped · walk · sci-fi*

A squat, two-legged swamp beast with a broad, frog-like body, thick hind legs and a heavy, muscular tail. Where its mouth should be is a ring of writhing tentacles around a gullet that gurgles when it breathes.

It prefers to fight at a distance, lobbing gobbets of acid spit that sizzle on armour and blister skin. Anything that closes in gets a crack from the tail. When badly hurt, it waddles into the water and vanishes. Swamp settlers treat it as a dangerous pest and carry shields against the acid. Its tentacles are said to be edible, for those who can face them.
