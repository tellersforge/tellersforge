# Silicon rock grub

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md) · small amorphous · burrower · sci-fi*

A small, stone-bodied larva that looks like a smooth, grey pebble with a mouth at one end. It dissolves rock with an acid it secretes, and leaves behind smooth, round tunnels as it eats its way through the stone.

Miners find them in clutches by the thousand, and they can damage tunnels and equipment. When found, it curls into a ball and flees at the first wound. Its body is worth something to collectors and scientists, and some mining colonies farm them to dig tunnels for them. Stepping on one is a mistake: the acid burns through boots.
