# Sky bull

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · huge quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A bull as big as a hill, sent down from the heavens to punish a city. Its horns are the length of ship's masts. Each time it snorts, the ground splits open into pits wide enough to swallow a hundred men, and each time it walks, walls come down.

It tramples streets, drinks rivers dry and does not stop until it is killed. In the stories only the greatest heroes, working together, bring one down, grabbing its horns and tail while another strikes. Its hide is thicker than a gatehouse door. Nobody asks what the city did to deserve it; everyone asks how to make sure it never happens to theirs.
