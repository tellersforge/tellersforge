# Winged hound

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium winged quadruped · flyer · fantasy, myth*

A dog with the long legs of a hunting hound and a pair of falcon's wings, grey-barred and quick. It watches over fields, seedbeds and young orchards, circling above them on the wind like a hawk and dropping on any animal or thief that comes to steal. Where it has passed, crops grow a little greener, and bruised shoots mend.

Farmers in some places set out a bowl of milk at the edge of the field to keep it happy. It is loyal to the land rather than to any person, and it flees if badly hurt. Hunting one is said to bring blight, which is reason enough for most people to leave them alone.
