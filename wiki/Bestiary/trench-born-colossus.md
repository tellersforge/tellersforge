# Trench-born colossus

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · gigantic biped · walk, swimmer · sci-fi, modern, horror*

A giant beast that crawls up out of a rift in the floor of the ocean, and looks more like a weapon than an animal. Its body is armoured in heavy plates, its limbs end in blade-like claws, and it bleeds a bright blue fluid that eats through steel and poisons the ground where it falls.

It walks out of the sea and marches straight for the nearest city, as if something sent it. Nothing seems to turn it aside or make it afraid, and it never flees. More follow it from the same rift, each larger than the last. Those who study them fear that someone on the other side is testing us.
