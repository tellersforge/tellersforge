# Giant pitcher plant

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · large plant · rooted · fantasy, sci-fi*

A vast jug of a plant, taller than a man, its lip waxy and slippery and its throat full of a sweet, still liquid. The nectar smell drifts far on warm evenings, and animals, birds and the occasional careless traveller follow it to the brim. The walls inside are smooth as glass and slope inward. Whatever falls in can't climb out, and slowly drowns in a soup that dissolves flesh and softens bone.

It never moves and never hunts. It simply waits, and the jungle feeds it. Hunters cut the drained husks for water carriers, and more than one explorer has been hauled out by the ankles, stinging and half digested, by friends with a rope.
