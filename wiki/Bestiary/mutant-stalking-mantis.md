# Mutant stalking mantis

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium many-legged · walk · post-apocalyptic*

A praying mantis as tall as a man, the colour of dead grass and dry sticks. It stands perfectly still for hours among brittle stalks, swaying gently with the wind, until something walks within reach. Then the folded forelimbs snap out, scythe-edged and quick as a thrown knife.

Scrubland hunters learn to read the places where a mantis would stand: along game trails, beside water, in the shade of fallen signs. It is a lone ambusher and has no stomach for a long fight. Once badly cut it springs away through the grass on its long legs. Its forearms, dried and sharpened, make fine blades, which is why some scavengers go looking for them.
