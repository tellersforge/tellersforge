# Burning wicker giant

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · huge biped · walk · fantasy, horror, historical*

A towering figure of woven wicker and willow, with a hollow cage for a chest and a bonfire roaring inside it. It walks through the night, lighting up the hillsides, and though flames pour from every gap, the wicker never burns away. It is the stuff of old harvest rites and darker bargains.

It reaches down with long wicker hands, seizes victims and thrusts them into the cage of its chest, where they burn. It is mindless and never flees. Water hisses on it and does little. People who live where it walks keep to stone houses on the night it comes and leave offerings at the edge of the fields, hoping they will be enough.
