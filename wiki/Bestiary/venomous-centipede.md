# Venomous centipede

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · tiny many-legged · fast crawler · fantasy, historical, modern, horror*

A long, flattened, many-legged creature with a reddish or brown body and a pair of venomous claws just behind its head. Centipedes live in damp places, under logs, stones and leaves, and hunt insects, spiders and even small lizards at night. They are fast and agile.

A centipede bites when disturbed. The bite is fiercely painful and can cause swelling and fever, but it is rarely fatal. Centipedes are common in houses in warm lands, and many people are bitten when they reach into a dark place. A hurt centipede flees at once.
