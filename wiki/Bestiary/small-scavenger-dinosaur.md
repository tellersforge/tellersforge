# Small scavenger dinosaur

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · tiny biped · fast runner · prehistoric, fantasy*

A chicken-sized dinosaur, two-legged and quick, with a long neck, a small head and a mouth of tiny teeth. It lives in flocks that swarm around carcasses and camps, darting in to snatch scraps, nipping at hands and ankles, and scattering at any threat.

Its bite carries a mild venom that numbs and swells, and a dozen bites can bring a weakened person down. It flees at the first wound. Wanderers who stray into its time find it a pest rather than a danger, a noisy, thieving plague that follows them everywhere, but they learn not to sleep on the ground where the flock is watching.
