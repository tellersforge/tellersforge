# Rolling fanged furball

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · tiny quadruped · fast runner · horror, sci-fi*

A round, spiky ball of fur the size of a cat, with no neck, tiny red eyes and a mouth that splits nearly the whole body in two. It rolls about in hungry packs, bouncing and tumbling, and eats absolutely everything: crops, stores, furniture, livestock and the occasional farmer. When cornered, it rolls up and fires poison quills that put their target to sleep.

The pack fights together and falls apart when it is broken. Outlying farms suffer most. Settlers who have dealt with them seal their stores in metal, shoot from rooftops, and count the pack twice, because they breed very quickly.
