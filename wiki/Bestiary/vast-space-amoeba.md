# Vast space amoeba

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · gigantic amorphous · drifts · sci-fi, horror*

A single living cell thousands of kilometres long, drifting through space like a pale, dim cloud. Somewhere in its depths is a nucleus as large as a small moon. From outside it looks like a patch of fog in the stars; from inside there is only a grey, featureless glow.

It drains the energy of anything near it, ships, stations and in time even stars. Ships that drift inside find their power ebbing away, their engines fading and their lights dimming, until the crew freezes in the dark. It has no mind, feels no fear and survives cold and emptiness that would kill anything else. Those few who have escaped did it by finding the nucleus and cutting their way out.
