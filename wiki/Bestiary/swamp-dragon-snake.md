# Swamp dragon-snake

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · huge serpentine · swimmer · sci-fi*

A long, thick serpent of swamps and murky lakes, with a flat, heavy head and a mouth full of backward-curved teeth. It lies hidden under black water for days, and only a glimpse of its back ever breaks the surface before it strikes.

It seizes waders by the leg, coils around them and drags them under to drown. Lake folk tell stories of it and warn their children not to swim at dusk. For all its size, it will let go and sink away when badly hurt. Hunters who want one bait it with meat on a line and wait with harpoons ready.
