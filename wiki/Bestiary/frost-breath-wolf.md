# Frost-breath wolf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth*

A great white wolf of the far north, heavier than any grey wolf, with a dense double coat, pale eyes and frost forever crusted on its muzzle. It hunts in blizzards, when prey cannot see or smell it coming, and its pack moves through the whiteout in silent single file.

When it closes, it breathes a freezing wind that numbs limbs and stops hearts, then bites. Cold does not trouble it at all. The pack fights while the pack fights, and scatters together into the snow. Northern peoples keep fires high when the blizzards come, and hunters who find frozen deer standing upright in a drift turn for home.
