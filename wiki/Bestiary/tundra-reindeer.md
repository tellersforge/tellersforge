# Tundra reindeer

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, historical, modern*

A sturdy, grey-brown deer with a pale neck, broad hooves and antlers carried by both males and females. Reindeer live in the cold tundra and northern forests, digging through snow for lichen and migrating in great herds between summer and winter grounds.

Northern peoples have herded them for thousands of years. They are milked, ridden, harnessed to sledges and eaten, and their hides and antlers provide clothing and tools. Reindeer are calm, but bulls spar in the rut and can gore with their antlers. Their thick coats shrug off cold that would kill a horse.
