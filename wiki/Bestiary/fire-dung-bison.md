# Fire-dung bison

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, historical*

A shaggy, bull-maned bison with curled horns that turn inward on themselves and are almost useless as weapons. Nature, or some unkind god, gave it another defence. When chased, it lifts its tail and sprays scalding dung behind it over a great distance, and wherever the stuff lands the grass smokes and bursts into flame.

Hunters who know the beast approach from the front or not at all. It flees at the first wound, and a whole herd running together can leave a burning trail across a plain. Some say its dung, once dried, burns hotter than charcoal. Few have been willing to gather enough to find out.
