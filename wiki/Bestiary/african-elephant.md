# African elephant

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Pachyderm](../Species_Families/pachyderm.md) · huge quadruped · walk · fantasy, historical, modern*

The largest animal that walks on land, grey and wrinkled, with huge fan-shaped ears, a long, supple trunk and curving ivory tusks. Elephants live in family herds led by an old female who remembers every waterhole and path across the savannah and forest. They are clever, long-lived and mourn their dead.

Most of the time an elephant is peaceful, but bulls in musth attack anything, and a mother protecting her calf is terrifying. An elephant can crush a man, a hut or a wagon. Its thick hide shrugs off most blows. A badly hurt elephant usually flees, but some turn and charge.
