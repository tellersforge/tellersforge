# Fire lizard

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · tiny quadruped · climber · fantasy*

A lizard no longer than a hand, with bright orange-red skin and eyes like beads of glowing glass. It runs so hot that it leaves little scorch marks on wood and loves nothing better than to curl up in the embers of a hearth or the warm cracks around a volcanic vent.

It is harmless enough if left alone, but startle one and it bolts, setting light to straw, tinder and curtains as it goes. More than one barn fire has been blamed on a fire lizard that got in for the warmth. Hurt, it flees at once. Some smiths keep one in the forge as a pet and swear it keeps the coals lit.
