# Tunnel-boring beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · huge many-legged · burrower · fantasy, sci-fi*

An armoured beast built like a battering ram, with a heavy plated body, short powerful legs and jaws that turn and grind like the head of a drill. It tears through rock faster than any crew of miners with picks, and it leaves smooth round tunnels behind it.

It digs for minerals it can eat, and it does not care what is in the way. It grinds through mine galleries, collapses shafts and tunnels up under walls and cellars. Miners who meet one either run or fight, and it fights to the death. Some mining companies pay handsomely for its hide, which turns picks, and a few try to tame the young.

## Not to be confused with

the *[great tunnel worm](great-tunnel-worm.md)*, a blind swallowing worm; the boring beast is armoured, legged and grinds through rock with its jaws.
