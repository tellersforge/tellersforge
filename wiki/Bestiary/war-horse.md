# War horse

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern, myth*

A heavy, powerful horse, bred for strength and trained for battle. War horses carry armoured riders into charges, trample foot soldiers and kick and bite on command. They are taught to stand firm among noise, fire and blood that would send an ordinary horse bolting.

A war horse fights while its rider fights, and a knight's horse is often his most valuable possession. It takes years of training to make one, and many lords prize their horses above their men. In the chaos of battle, a war horse is as dangerous as the rider on its back.
