# Tunnelling hive worker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium many-legged · burrower · sci-fi*

A blind digger caste of an alien hive, with a hard, pale shell, powerful mandibles and short, thick legs. It chews tunnels through rock and soil, endlessly expanding the hive in a maze that can stretch for miles.

They are placid while they work, ignoring anything that does not interfere. But if the nest is disturbed, they pour out in their hundreds, swarming to defend the queen. They fight as long as the swarm fights. Colonists who find a hive nearby learn to leave it alone, and miners who break into their tunnels are advised to leave quickly.
