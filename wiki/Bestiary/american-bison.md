# American bison

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern*

A huge, shaggy grazer with a humped shoulder, a massive head and short curved horns. Bison live in thundering herds on open plains, migrating across great distances and grazing the grass down to the roots. They are faster and more agile than they look, and a herd in stampede can flatten anything in its way.

Plains peoples lived by hunting bison, using their meat, hides, bones and sinew for almost everything. Bulls charge if pressed, and their thick hides shrug off blows. A badly hurt bison flees with the herd.
