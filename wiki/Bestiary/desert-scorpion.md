# Desert scorpion

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · tiny many-legged · walk · fantasy, historical, modern, horror*

A pale, straw-coloured scorpion with slender pincers and a thin tail ending in a curved sting. Desert scorpions hide under stones, in cracks and in boots and bedding by day and hunt insects at night. They glow faintly under certain lights, and they can see in the dark.

A desert scorpion stings when touched or trapped. Its venom is painful and can kill a child or a weakened traveller. Desert travellers learn to shake out their clothes and boots each morning.
