# Fire mote

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md) · tiny flyer · flyer · fantasy*

A fist-sized spark of living flame that flits about like a firefly or a moth. It is playful and curious, drawn to lanterns, candles and hearths, and it bobs about them like a child at a fair. It is also dangerous, because anything it touches it sets alight.

It ignites straw, curtains and hair without meaning any particular harm. When hurt, it flees. Farmers fear them near barns in dry weather. Some people keep one in a stone lantern for light and warmth, feeding it oil and wood chips, and treat it much like a pet. It rarely ends well.

## Not to be confused with
