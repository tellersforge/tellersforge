# Giant dragonfly

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium flyer · flyer · fantasy, prehistoric*

A dragonfly whose four glassy wings span wider than a man is tall, its long body shining metallic blue or green in the sun. It darts over marshes and lakes with a dry rattle, changing direction in an instant, and snatches small birds, frogs and lizards out of the air or off the reeds. Its huge eyes cover most of its head and miss very little.

It is no real threat to people, though it will buzz a boat out of curiosity and its jaws can open a finger to the bone. It flees at the first wound. Children in marsh villages chase them with nets, and the wings, dried and pressed, are sold as fancy windows and lantern shades.
