# Nine-headed bird

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · large flyer · flyer · fantasy, myth, horror*

A large, dark bird with nine heads on long, snaky necks, one of which is always dripping blood. It flies at night over towns and villages, and wherever its blood falls on a roof, misfortune follows.

Its cry is terrible and brings ill luck to those who hear it. It flees when badly hurt. People bark like dogs or shout to scare it away, and a house with blood on its roof is scrubbed and blessed at once.
