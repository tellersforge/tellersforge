# Sand drake

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · large quadruped · burrower · fantasy*

A tan, flat-bodied drake with a wedge-shaped head and short, strong legs, built to swim through loose sand the way an eel swims through mud. Its colour matches the dunes so well that it is invisible even when half-exposed, and heat, thirst and blowing grit do not trouble it.

It waits under the sand along caravan routes and bursts up beneath the passing line, seizing camels and people and dragging them down. It flees when badly hurt, diving back under. Caravan guides watch for faint ripples and avoid soft dunes, and a guide who knows the drakes' grounds is worth twice the fee.
