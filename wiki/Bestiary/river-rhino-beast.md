# River rhino-beast

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · huge quadruped · swimmer · fantasy, myth, modern*

A huge, semi-aquatic beast of deep jungle rivers, heavier than any hippopotamus, with a single long horn, thick grey hide and a heavy tail. It spends most of its life in murky water, grazing on river plants and surfacing with a snort.

It is fiercely territorial, and any elephant or boat that enters its stretch of river is attacked. It gores elephants from below and overturns canoes, and fights to the death to hold its water. River peoples know where its pools are, and they go the long way around.
