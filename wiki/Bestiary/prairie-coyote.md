# Prairie coyote

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · small quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, historical, modern, post-apocalyptic, horror*

A slight, sandy dog of scrub country and open range, all ears, muzzle and bushy tail. Coyotes are survivors. They eat mice, fruit, carrion, chickens and whatever a careless camp leaves out, and they live happily at the edge of farms and towns. At dusk they sing: a burst of yips and wavering howls that makes two animals sound like twenty.

Alone, a coyote is a nuisance rather than a threat, and it skulks off the moment it is hurt. In pairs they grow bolder, one drawing the dogs away while the other slips into the henhouse. Ranchers curse them, trappers chase them, and neither ever quite gets rid of them.
