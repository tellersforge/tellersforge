# Imp swarm

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md) · medium swarm · flyer · fantasy, horror, myth*

A cackling cloud of hell imps, each no bigger than a cat, with bat wings, needle teeth and grabbing fingers. They pour down out of a chimney or a storm cloud and are everywhere at once: pulling hair, biting ankles, knocking over lamps, setting fire to hay and snatching anything that glints.

The swarm is more nuisance than murderer, but a nuisance that burns a farmhouse down is bad enough. Imps are cowards. The first real wound scatters them, laughing and swearing, and they flee with whatever they stole. Bells, salt and cold iron are said to drive them off, and so is a stout broom used vigorously.
