# Rat swarm

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md) · medium swarm · walk · fantasy, historical, modern, horror, post-apocalyptic*

A squealing, seething tide of rats, driven out of walls and sewers by fire, flood or hunger. A swarm moves as one mass across floors, up walls and over bodies, and it feeds on anything that cannot get away: grain, carrion, the sleeping and the fallen.

One rat is a nuisance. A swarm can overwhelm a wounded soldier, a drunk or a baby, and the bites carry disease. Swarms rise in sieges, famines and flooded cities, and they are a staple of dark tales about cursed castles. Fire and open space break them; a swarm that is badly hurt scatters back into the walls.
