# Clockwork hound

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, historical*

A hunting dog of brass and steel, driven by springs and gears, with glass eyes and a ticking heart. Its handler feeds a scent-card into its jaw, a strip of cloth or leather marked with the quarry's smell, and sets it on the trail. It runs without tiring and without stopping to eat or drink.

It cannot be called off by anyone but its maker, and sometimes not even then. It never gives up and never flees. Fugitives cross running water, walk through smoke, or double back along their own tracks to confuse it. Workshops that build them are the property of rich lords and secret police, and their clatter at night is feared in every alley.
