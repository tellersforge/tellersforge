# Skeleton archer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium biped · walk · fantasy, horror*

A skeleton strung together by old magic, a bow in its bony hands and a quiver of arrows on its back. It still keeps the discipline it had in life: it stands, draws, aims and looses with the patience of a veteran, and it never tires or trembles.

Skeleton archers are set on walls, in towers and on hilltops, where they have a good view of anyone coming. They keep shooting until the living close in, then fight with whatever is at hand. They have no minds of their own and never flee. Mobs of the living usually rush them, as arrows do little to bones.
