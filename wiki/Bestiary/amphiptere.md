# Amphiptere

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · medium serpentine · flyer · fantasy, myth*

A slim serpent with a pair of wings and no legs at all, gaudy as a parrot. Some have feathered wings in greens and blues, others have leathery ones veined with red, and all of them flash in the light as they whip between the branches. They live in noisy flocks in the high canopy of hot forests.

An amphiptere hunts by diving, biting and climbing away again before its prey knows what struck it. The bite carries a weak venom that numbs and swells. Travellers learn to cover their heads and keep moving. Badly hurt, an amphiptere simply flies off, and the flock goes with it.
