# Red kangaroo

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Marsupial](../Species_Families/marsupial.md) · medium biped · hopper · fantasy, historical, modern*

A tall, russet grazer that stands upright on huge hind legs and balances on a thick tail. Red kangaroos live in mobs on dry open plains, grazing at dawn and dusk and resting in the shade during the heat. They move in great bounds, covering ground faster than a horse and clearing fences with ease.

Male kangaroos box rivals, standing tall and grappling with their forearms before leaning back on their tails to deliver a two-footed kick. The clawed feet can disembowel a dog. People who corner one learn this lesson painfully. A badly hurt kangaroo bounds away.
