# Floating beaked brain

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · medium other · hover · fantasy, horror*

A pale, floating brain the size of a keg, with a sharp, hooked beak on its underside and a skirt of long barbed tentacles hanging below. It drifts silently through dark tunnels and ruined halls. When a group passes beneath it, it waits for the last one, the straggler, and drops its tentacles. The barbs numb the flesh, and the victim is lifted up to the beak without a sound.

It is patient, and it flees when badly hurt. Parties that travel the deep halls count their number at every turn, keep someone watching the ceiling, and never let one walker drift behind.
