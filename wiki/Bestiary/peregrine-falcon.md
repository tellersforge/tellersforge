# Peregrine falcon

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · tiny flyer · fast flyer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A slate-backed, long-winged falcon with a dark hood like a helmet. Peregrines nest on cliffs, towers and cathedral ledges and hunt other birds in open sky. A peregrine climbs high above its target, then folds its wings and drops in a stoop so fast that it seems to vanish, striking its prey in mid-air with a blow of its talons.

It is the fastest diver alive and, in many lands, the bird reserved for nobles. Falconers spend years training them. Messengers sometimes fear them, for a trained peregrine will strike down a carrier pigeon. A falcon that is hurt flees at once.
