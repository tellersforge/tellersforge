# Snout-tentacled toad-thing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md) · large biped · walk · sentient · fantasy, horror*

A greyish-white, toad-like creature taller than a man, with no eyes and a mass of pink, twitching tentacles where its snout should be. Toad-things live in dark cities on distant shores, and they sail black galleys to trade slaves and plunder with the evil peoples of the world.

They are cruel, cunning and fond of torture. They fight while their band fights, and flee when it breaks. Coastal folk hear tales of black ships that come in the night and leave villages empty. Their tentacles sense the world better than eyes, so darkness doesn't hide you from them.
