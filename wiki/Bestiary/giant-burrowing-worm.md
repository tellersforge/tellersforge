# Giant burrowing worm

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · large serpentine · burrower · fantasy, horror, sci-fi*

A pale, rubbery worm as long as a horse, living in loose soil, sand and the soft beds of old rivers. Its skin is slick and ringed, and its blunt head carries a round, toothed mouth that can clamp onto a leg and hold.

It cannot see, but it feels footsteps through the ground from a long way off. It rises beneath its prey, lunges from the soil and drags whatever it has caught back down. It has no fear and never flees. Farmers in its country learn to plough with a heavy stone dragged behind to draw it to the wrong place, and children are told never to play barefoot in the fields.
