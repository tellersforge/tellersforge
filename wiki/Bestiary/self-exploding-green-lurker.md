# Self-exploding green lurker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy, modern*

A mottled green creature the height of a man, on four stubby legs, with a long stalk of a body and a small frowning face near the top. It creeps up silently on its target, making no sound at all. When it is close, it hisses, and then it blows itself apart in a great blast.

It is mindless and never flees. It has no reason to. Those who know the danger listen for the hiss and run, or try to kill it from a distance before it gets close. A crater and a missing wall are its usual signature.
