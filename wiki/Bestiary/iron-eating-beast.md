# Iron-eating beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · huge quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A beast that eats iron: weapons, tools, nails, hinges, chains. Each time it eats, it grows larger, and its hide grows harder. At first it is small enough to hold in a hand. Given time, it becomes a colossus that blades break on and walls cannot hold.

It is mindless and never flees. The only way to stop it, the stories say, is to starve it, or to trick it into eating something that disagrees with it. Blacksmiths fear it more than fire.
