# Short-necked sea reptile

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · gigantic swimmer · swimmer · prehistoric, fantasy*

A huge-headed marine predator with a short neck, a thick body and four powerful flippers, its jaws longer than a man and full of teeth like railway spikes. It is the apex hunter of the ancient ocean, bursting up from the depths to seize prey, and it can bite a boat in two.

It fights to the death. Sea monsters of old tales often borrow its shape. Sailors of its age keep to the shallows and the coast, and they tell of ships found in pieces, with teeth embedded in their timbers.
