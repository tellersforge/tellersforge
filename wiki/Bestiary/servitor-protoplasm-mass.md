# Servitor protoplasm mass

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · huge amorphous · slow · horror, sci-fi*

A bubbling, heaving black mass as big as a house, covered in eyes that form and fade, and mouths that open and close. It was bred long ago as a slave by elder beings, and when they fell, it was left behind. It forms limbs, jaws and tools as it needs them, and it rolls down tunnels like a runaway train, crushing everything in its path.

As it goes, it pipes eerily, mimicking the voices of its lost masters. It is mindless and never flees. There is no fighting it in a tunnel. The only escape is to get out of its way, and it is very fast.
