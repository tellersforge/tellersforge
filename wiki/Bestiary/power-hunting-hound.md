# Power-hunting hound

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · sci-fi*

A lean, grey pack hunter with a narrow, eyeless-looking head and a whip-like tail tipped with a stinging barb. It can sense supernatural power the way other hounds smell blood, and it hunts those who wield it above all other prey.

Packs cross great distances to find a single psychic or mystic, ignoring ordinary travellers along the way. They surround their quarry and bite, and the tail's poison clouds the mind. They keep fighting as long as the pack fights. Those with powers treat any sign of them as a reason to move on, and some tyrants breed them to hunt down gifted rebels.

How to read this block: [Bestiary](../bestiary.md).

## Not to be confused with

the *[power-nullifying lizard](power-nullifying-lizard.md)*, which suppresses power around it but never hunts; the hound actively tracks down those who wield power.
