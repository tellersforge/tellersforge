# Drifting spore orb

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md) · medium other · hover · fantasy, horror*

A grey, papery fungal balloon, as big as a man's torso, with markings on its skin that look very like a great eye and a ring of dangling stalks. From a distance it can pass for a floating eye orb, and it drifts slowly toward warmth, bobbing on air currents in caves and ruins. It has no mind at all.

Strike it, and it bursts in a violent cloud of spores that choke the lungs and settle in the flesh, where they may take root. Those who know the difference keep their distance and pop it with an arrow. Those who don't learn it the hard way.
