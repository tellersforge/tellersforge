# Sand elemental

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md) · large other · burrower · fantasy, myth*

A whirling figure of desert sand, shaped roughly like a tall man but always shifting, grains pouring off it and being drawn back in. It can sink into a dune in moments and rise behind travellers far away, and it moves beneath the sand as a fish moves through water.

It attacks by scouring, blasting sand into eyes and skin until both are raw. It is mindless and never flees. Caravans cross the desert in tight groups and keep eyes and faces covered. Some desert peoples say that water poured into it turns it to mud, which is easier to fight. They also say that rarely helps, since water is precious out there.

## Not to be confused with

The *[Dust devil](dust-devil.md)*, a small whirlwind that plagues camps; the sand elemental is a larger living spirit of sand that hunts beneath the dunes.
