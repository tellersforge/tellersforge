# Giant millipede

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · large many-legged · walk · prehistoric, fantasy*

A millipede as long as a canoe, from the ancient coal forests, black and glossy with orange bands, with hundreds of legs rippling in waves along its body. It crawls across the forest floor eating rotting plants, and it is harmless unless handled.

When disturbed, it coils into a tight ball and oozes a stinging fluid that burns skin and eyes and smells like bitter almonds. If pressed, it bites with small jaws. It fights while it can, though mostly by doing nothing. People who find themselves in its forests learn to step around it and never to pick it up.
