# Hunting hawk

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · tiny flyer · flyer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A compact, short-winged hawk with barred breast feathers and fierce orange eyes. It hunts in woods and hedgerows, flying fast and low, weaving between trunks to snatch pigeons, pheasants and rabbits. Its short wings give it sudden speed and sharp turns, not endurance.

Falconers prize these hawks as working birds. Carried on a gloved fist, one can be flown at a flushed bird and will return to the fist when called. They are high-strung, easily upset and need daily attention. A hawk that takes a wound will leave and may not come back.
