# Dragon-maned lion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · large winged quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A lion with a mane of brazen, overlapping scales and a pair of small dragon wings. It guards lairs in desert ruins, lying in the shade of broken pillars.

Its roar is so loud that it knocks the breath out of foes and staggers them. It fights to the death with bites and claws, and it can take short, gliding flights. Desert travellers give its ruins a wide berth.
