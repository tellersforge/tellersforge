# Angle-hunting hound

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · medium quadruped · teleports · horror, sci-fi, fantasy*

Lean and blue-grey, dripping a thick ichor, with a long curling tongue, the hound comes from somewhere outside ordinary time, where everything is made of angles. It cannot pass through a curve. It emerges from the sharp corners of a room, a crease in a wall, the point where two beams meet, and smoke pours out before it.

It hunts those who have looked where they should not, through time and dream, and once it has the scent it never stops. It kills and does not retreat. Those who know the lore plaster every corner of their rooms smooth, living in curved chambers like the inside of an egg, and hope they missed none. The rest simply wait for the smell of smoke.
