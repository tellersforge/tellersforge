# Sewer tentacle lurker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · large swimmer · swimmer · sci-fi*

A squat, many-armed scavenger that lives in garbage pits, sewers and the flooded holds of wrecked ships. Its body stays submerged in filth; only a single eyestalk rises above the surface to watch what comes near. Its colouring is exactly the brown and grey of rot.

It feeds on refuse, but it is always glad of something fresh. The curious lean in to look at the stalk, and a tentacle wraps round an ankle and drags them under. Its mouth is full of filth, and its bite festers. Station workers who work near the refuse compactors learn to keep a pole handy. When badly hurt, it lets go and sinks out of sight.
