# American alligator

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · large quadruped · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A broad-snouted, dark-backed reptile of warm swamps, slow rivers and marshes, armoured in ridged plates. Alligators spend long hours basking on banks or floating like logs, and they bellow in a deep, rumbling chorus during the breeding season. Females build mounded nests of rotting plants and guard them fiercely.

They hunt at the waterline, lunging at fish, birds and animals that come too close, and they are slower to go for large prey than their crocodile cousins. Still, a careless dog or a wader who treads near a nest can lose a leg. Swamp folk know to keep children and pets away from the edge, and that a hurt alligator usually sinks away rather than fights.
