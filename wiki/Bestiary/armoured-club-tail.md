# Armoured club-tail

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · huge quadruped · slow · prehistoric, fantasy*

A tank-like dinosaur, low and wide, covered in bony plates and spikes, with a heavy club at the end of its tail. It moves slowly across forests and plains, grazing low plants, and it does not run.

When threatened, it crouches low, protecting its soft belly, and swings its tail club. The club can shatter a shin, break a jaw or crack a skull. It fights to the death. Predators leave it alone, and anyone who meets it learn that its armour is hard to break and its tail is always ready.
