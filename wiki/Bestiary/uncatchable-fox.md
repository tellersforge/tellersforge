# Uncatchable fox

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · small quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth*

A fox of ordinary russet colour and extraordinary luck. It has been fated never to be caught, and the fate holds. It raids flocks and henhouses in plain sight, and when the hounds are loosed it simply runs, always a little faster than them, always a step ahead of every trap, net and arrow. It seems to know where danger will be before danger arrives.

A region plagued by one learns to live with the losses, because hunting it is a way to ruin good dogs. It flees at the first scratch, though scratches are rare. Old tales say that only something equally fated, a hound that always catches its prey, could end the chase, and then the chase would never end.
