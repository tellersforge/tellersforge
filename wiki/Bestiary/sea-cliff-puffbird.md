# Sea-cliff puffbird

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · tiny flyer · flyer · sci-fi*

A round, fluffy seabird with a stubby beak, huge dark eyes and a curious disposition that no amount of shooing can cure. It nests in huge colonies on rocky islands, and roosts in any shelter going, including the holds of ships, the cockpits of parked aircraft and the pockets of sleeping travellers.

It begs, squawks, gets underfoot and steals scraps, but it is harmless. It flees at the first sign of real trouble, only to come back a moment later. Sailors find them a nuisance or good luck depending on who you ask, and few can bring themselves to eat one after looking at those eyes.
