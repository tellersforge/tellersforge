# Giant eagle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · large flyer · flyer · fantasy*

A mountain eagle big enough to carry off a sheep, dark brown with a golden nape and a hooked beak the size of a hand. It nests on the highest crags, in eyries piled with bones, and hunts by circling on the winds for hours, watching with eyes that can pick out a hare from beyond sight. Then it stoops, wings folded, and strikes with a force like a falling rock.

Mountain herders hate it and admire it at once. Some peoples tell tales of eagles that carry heroes, speak or keep ancient bargains; whatever the truth, an eagle is no fool, and it breaks off when badly hurt. Its feathers fetch high prices from fletchers and chieftains alike.
