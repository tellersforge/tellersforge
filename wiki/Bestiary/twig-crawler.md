# Twig crawler

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · small plant · walk · fantasy, horror*

A small bundle of dry twigs and bark bound into a crouching body, with thin clawed limbs and nothing that could be called a face. Lying still, it is deadwood. Twig crawlers gather in scattered groups among fallen branches and brush along forest paths, and when a traveller passes, they rise together and swarm, scratching and clawing at legs and faces.

They are weak one by one, and brittle enough to snap underfoot, but they never stop coming while any of them can move. Foresters clear the brush from the edges of their roads for this reason, and a crackle in the undergrowth on a windless day is reason enough to walk faster.
