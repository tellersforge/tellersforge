# Cursed doll

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · tiny biped · walk · sentient · horror, modern*

A porcelain doll with a cracked cheek, a rag doll with button eyes, a painted puppet with its strings long gone. It sits on a shelf or in a pram and does nothing at all while anyone is watching. Look away and it has moved: a head turned, a hand raised, a place further down the hall. Something clever and spiteful lives inside it.

The doll kills quietly, in the dark and when its owner is alone. It can slip its spirit into a sleeping child or a pet, and it lays curses on those who try to be rid of it. Burning or burying rarely works; it comes back to the shelf. When badly hurt it hides and waits. Those who deal with it keep it watched at all times until the spirit inside can be driven out.
