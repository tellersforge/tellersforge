# Flame-hooved steed

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Equine and centauroid](../Species_Families/equine-and-centauroid.md) · large quadruped · flyer · fantasy, myth, horror*

A black horse with a mane and tail of fire and hooves that burn with every step, leaving smoking hoofprints on stone and scorch marks on grass. It runs on air and smoke as easily as on the ground.

It carries dark riders, knights of shadow and demons. It fights to the death, biting and striking with burning hooves, and it can spit fire. Villages that see burning hoofprints on the road bar their doors and pray.
