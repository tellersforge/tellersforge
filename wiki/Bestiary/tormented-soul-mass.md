# Tormented soul mass

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md) · medium amorphous · slow · fantasy, horror*

The damned are not always given faces. Some are melted together into lumps of pale, weeping flesh with half-formed hands and mouths that never stop wailing. These soul masses crawl across the floors of the lower realms in slow, sobbing herds, driven on by whips.

They have no mind left, only a dull craving for anything alive. They crawl towards the living, clutch, drag and smother. Demon captains use them as fodder, sending waves of them ahead to soak up arrows and clog trenches before the real assault. Killing them feels like a mercy to some and a horror to others. Either way, there are always more.
