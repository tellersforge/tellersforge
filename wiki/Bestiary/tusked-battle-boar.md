# Tusked battle boar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · walk · sci-fi*

A bristly, spined boar with a thick neck, short legs and tusks that curve up past its snout. Warrior peoples raise them as hunting beasts, war pets and guards, and a warrior's boar is often treated as a member of the family.

It charges without warning, head down, and gores with its tusks. It fights to the death. Its meat is rich and its heart is prized as a delicacy and a source of courage; eating the heart of a boar you have killed yourself is an honour among those peoples. Outsiders find them terrifying, and the smell unforgettable.
