# Belly-mouthed sloth

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · large biped · slow · fantasy, myth, horror*

A giant, shaggy ground-sloth of the deep rainforest, standing taller than a man, with a single eye in its forehead and a second, gaping mouth in its belly. Its hide is so thick that arrows and spears bounce off it, and its claws can smash a tree trunk.

It screams in the rainforest, a noise that deafens and stuns, and its stench is so strong that hunters faint. It fights to the death. Forest peoples fear it more than any jaguar, and they say only a strike to the belly-mouth can wound it.
