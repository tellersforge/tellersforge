# Lurking mycelium mat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md) · large plant · slow · fantasy, horror, sci-fi*

A carpet of fine white threads spread over a cave floor, thin as spider silk and wide as a hall. It feels every footstep that crosses it. Most walkers never notice, but anyone who falls, sleeps or lies wounded is slowly wrapped in threads that tighten and sink into the skin, feeding while the victim is still alive.

It is slow and weak and patient. It lets others do the killing. It grows thickest where predators leave their scraps and where travellers rest. Old cavers sleep standing or on bare rock, and scrape a floor clean with a torch before they set down their packs.
