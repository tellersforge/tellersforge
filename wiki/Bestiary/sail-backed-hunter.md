# Sail-backed hunter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · gigantic biped · swimmer · prehistoric, fantasy*

A long-snouted river predator with a great sail of skin along its back, taller than a man at the spine. Its jaws are narrow and lined with conical teeth, made for gripping slippery fish, and its hands carry heavy hooked claws. It wades through rivers and estuaries after giant fish, and it hunts on land as well as in water.

It fights to the death. On the riverbanks it is as dangerous as any tyrant, and in the water it is worse. Travellers of its age find it hard to escape, since neither land nor river is safe. The sail flushes with colour when it is roused, a warning few live to understand.
