# Hoofed tentacle tree

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · huge other · walk · fantasy, horror*

A lumbering mass of black, ropy tentacles as big as an oak, standing on goat-like hooves and dripping with mouths. From a distance, in the dark, it looks like a dead tree. It shambles out of old groves where dark gods are worshipped, and it seizes and crushes the enemies of its worshippers, stuffing them into its mouths.

It is mindless and never flees. Cultists summon it with drums and blood. The only true defence is to find the grove and burn it, and to deal with the worshippers before they call it.
