# Snake-headed desert coyote

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · post-apocalyptic*

Somebody once thought a coyote would be improved by a rattlesnake, and the wastes have been paying for it ever since. The thing that came out of the laboratory has a dog's legs and lungs, a narrow head crowned with scales, and a thick, banded tail that buzzes a dry warning when it is too late to heed it. Its hide shifts colour with the ground, so a resting pair is nearly invisible among stones and scrub.

They hunt in pairs. One shows itself and draws the eye; the other slides out of the heat haze and bites, and the venom does the rest. Caravan guards learn to watch where nothing seems to be, and to treat the sound of a rattle on flat, empty ground as a call to arms.
