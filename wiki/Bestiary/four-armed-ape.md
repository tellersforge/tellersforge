# Four-armed ape

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md) · large biped · climber · fantasy, myth*

A huge white ape with four arms, living in jungle canopies. It ambushes prey from above, dropping down and rending with all four hands.

It fights to the death. Jungle travellers keep watch on the treetops and carry spears and torches. Its roar echoes through the jungle, and its white fur makes it visible at night, if you know to look up.
