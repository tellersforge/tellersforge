# Crested porcupine

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md) · small quadruped · slow · fantasy, historical, modern*

A large, slow rodent covered in long black and white quills, with a crest of stiff spines on its head and neck. Porcupines live in burrows and caves in scrub and rocky hills and forage at night for roots, bark and fallen fruit. They are peaceful and short-sighted and would rather be left alone.

Threatened, a porcupine raises its quills, rattles the hollow ones on its tail and stamps its feet. If that fails, it runs backwards into its attacker, driving the quills deep. They break off, work their way in and fester. Leopards and dogs learn the lesson once. A cornered porcupine fights; otherwise it waddles away.
