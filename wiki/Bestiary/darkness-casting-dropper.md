# Darkness-casting dropper

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · small other · flyer · fantasy*

A small, squid-like cave creature, grey as stone, that hangs from the roof and looks like a stalactite. When prey passes below, it drops, wraps its tentacles around the head, and casts a cloud of magical darkness around itself. The victim is blinded and gripped at once, and friends cannot see to help.

It is small, but it can squeeze hard. It flees when badly hurt, flying off into the dark. Veteran explorers carry a shielded lamp and a second light, and learn the sound of something damp landing on a helmet.
