# Irradiated mole rat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md) · small quadruped · burrower · post-apocalyptic*

A hairless, bucktoothed rodent grown to the size of a dog, pink-grey and wrinkled, with tiny eyes and huge yellow teeth that never stop growing. Mole rats dig warrens under ruins and old tunnels, and they hunt in packs. They burst out of the ground around their prey, bite, and dive back into their holes before anyone can strike.

They flee when badly hurt. They are a steady danger for scavengers, who learn to watch for fresh mounds of earth and to fight with their backs to rock. Their meat is tough and gamey, but it is meat, and many a wasteland stew is made from it.
