# Carrier pigeon

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · tiny flyer · flyer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A plain, grey-blue pigeon with an iridescent neck and an astonishing gift: take it far from home, release it, and it will fly straight back to its loft, sometimes across hundreds of miles. Small messages tied to its leg have carried news of battles, prices and births far faster than any rider.

Armies, merchants and spies keep lofts of them, and the birds are carefully bred for speed and endurance. A pigeon has no fight at all and flees every threat. Hawks, falcons and bad weather are its enemies, which is why important messages are always sent by more than one bird.
