# Fused-flesh crawler

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · medium many-legged · slow · post-apocalyptic, horror*

What happens when a mutagen reaches a crowd. Several bodies have melted into one lump of flesh, and it moves on whatever limbs happened to end up on the outside: an arm here, a leg there, something that might once have been a tail. Faces surface and sink in the mass. It drags itself along slowly, leaving a glistening trail.

It has no mind left to speak of and no fear at all. When something living comes near, it lashes out with ropy limbs and hawks up a gout of bile that glows faintly green, eats through cloth and leaves a sickness in the bones. It will not run, and it will not stop until it is burned or cut apart. Scavengers who find one in a basement usually just seal the door.
