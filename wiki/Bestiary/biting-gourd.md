# Biting gourd

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · small plant · slow · fantasy, horror*

A fat, ripe-looking fruit, pumpkin-orange or melon-green, lying among its vines like any other. Then a split opens along its side, lined with rows of hard seed-teeth, and it rolls. Biting gourds grow in patches in neglected gardens and haunted fields, and they defend their patch like a pack of small, stupid dogs, snapping at ankles and bowling over anyone who stumbles.

One alone is a nuisance. A patch of them is a real danger to a lone thief after dark. Farmers who have them fence them in, keep geese to warn of their movement, and never, ever carve the wrong pumpkin at harvest time.
