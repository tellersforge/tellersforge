# Haunted household object

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · small other · hover · myth, fantasy, horror*

Keep a thing long enough and it may start to keep you. An umbrella a hundred years old, a paper lantern, a worn sandal or a tea kettle can wake with a spirit of its own, sprouting a single eye, a long tongue or a hopping foot. Most are pranksters rather than killers. They hide keys, tip over teacups, spook the cat and float about the house at night.

Their great grievance is neglect. Throw one out on the rubbish heap and it bites, sulks and haunts the family for years. Treat it with a little respect, mend it and give it a place on the shelf, and it may even bring luck. When badly hurt it flees rather than fight, and it can usually be found again under the eaves.
