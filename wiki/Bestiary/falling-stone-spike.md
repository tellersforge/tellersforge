# Falling stone spike

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · small other · slow · fantasy*

A slug living inside a hard, stone-like shell shaped exactly like a stalactite. It hangs among real stalactites on cave roofs, waiting. When something warm passes beneath it, it lets go, and falls spike first. A good hit can kill a man outright. Then it slowly crawls back up the wall to find a new place to hang.

It has no mind and never flees. Where there is one, there are often a dozen. Experienced cavers throw stones at the ceiling and listen for the difference between rock and shell, or simply walk along the walls instead of the middle.
