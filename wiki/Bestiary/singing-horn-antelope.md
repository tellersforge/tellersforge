# Singing-horn antelope

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth*

A graceful, pale antelope with a single long horn, hollow and pierced by many small holes like a flute. When the wind blows through it, or when the beast runs, the horn plays music. Soft tunes lull hunters to sleep or fill them with longing; high, harsh notes make listeners weep, rage or run in fear.

It lives in open grassland and upland forest and is timid, fleeing at the first wound. Its horn, taken whole, is one of the most prized instruments in the world, and a single one has started wars between princes. Wise musicians say the horn only plays true while the beast lives.
