# Acid-spitting zombie

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium biped · walk · horror, modern, post-apocalyptic, fantasy*

A zombie with a grotesquely swollen throat, its neck bulging like a toad's. It hangs back behind the other dead, and when prey comes into view it hawks up a gob of burning yellow bile and hurls it with surprising accuracy.

The bile sizzles on skin, eats through cloth and leather and blinds anyone it hits in the face. The spitter keeps its distance and lets the others close in. It has no mind and never flees. Survivors learn to pick it out of the crowd and kill it first, before it can soften them up for the rest.
