# Flying bloodshot eye

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · tiny flyer · flyer · fantasy, horror*

A single veined eyeball, about the size of a plum, trailing a tangle of nerves and flapping through the air on nothing at all. After dark they come out in numbers, drifting over graveyards and marshes, and when they find a living thing, they ram it, nibble with tiny unseen mouths and swarm around the face.

One is disgusting. A cloud of them, at night, is terrifying. They are mindless and never flee, but they are fragile, and a torch or a quick blade pops them. Folk who live where they come out shutter their windows at dusk and keep a lamp burning.
