# Lashing violet fungus

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md) · medium plant · slow · fantasy*

A purple, many-stemmed cave fungus that shuffles slowly across the floor on a mat of fleshy roots. From its crown hang several limp, rubbery tendrils, and when anything warm passes within reach, they whip out. Where they touch, skin blackens and flesh begins to rot, as if the wound were weeks old.

It often grows among shrieking mushrooms, and the two together make a cave passage very dangerous: one calls, the other lashes. Miners with good sense carry a long pole to probe ahead, and treat any bright purple in the lantern light as a warning.
