# Grave mist

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · large amorphous · hover · fantasy, horror*

A cold, grey fog that seeps from graves and rolls over graveyards at night. It moves slowly, drifting with the wind, and it smells of damp earth and decay.

It smothers anything warm, drawing the heat and breath from anyone caught in it. It has no mind and never flees. Those who cross graveyards at night carry torches and keep to the high ground, where the mist is thinnest.
