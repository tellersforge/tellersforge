# Giant mantis

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · large many-legged · walk · fantasy*

A green mantis as tall as a mounted man, standing so still among cane and tall grass that it looks like a plant. Its forearms are folded up under its narrow head as if in prayer. They are lined with hooked spines and snap out faster than any eye can follow, catching and cutting in one motion, and then the mantis begins to eat.

It waits rather than hunts. It can stand motionless for half a day, turning its triangular head to follow movement, and it strikes the moment something steps within reach. It fights on while it can. Martial schools in some lands claim to have learned a style by watching one, and they keep their students well back from the real thing.
