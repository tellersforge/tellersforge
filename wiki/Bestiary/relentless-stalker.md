# Relentless stalker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium biped · walk · horror, modern*

A figure in a blank mask and dull work clothes, carrying a blade or a tool, saying nothing. It walks. It never runs. And yet whenever the victim stops, turns, or looks out of a window, it is there, closer than before. Shoot it, stab it, run it over with a car or throw it from a roof, and it lies still for a moment, then gets up again.

The stalker is less a creature than a rule of the story: it picks its prey, usually a group of young people somewhere isolated, and it comes for them one by one. It feels no pain and never gives up. Survivors describe the only things that seemed to help: fire, daylight, and never, ever splitting up.
