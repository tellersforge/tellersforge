# Giant wolverine

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mustelid and small mammal](../Species_Families/mustelid-and-small-mammal.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy*

A weasel the size of a bear, brown-black with a pale band along its flanks, low to the ground and thick with muscle. It lives in the northern forests and snowfields, and it fears nothing. It will attack a wolf pack, a bear, a moose or a hunting party with equal fury, biting and clawing in a frenzy that does not stop until one side is dead.

Trappers hate it. It raids caches, destroys traps, fouls what it cannot eat and follows hunters for days. Northern peoples respect it as the fiercest thing in the forest and tell children that a wolverine will fight the wind if the wind annoys it. Nobody who has met one laughs.
