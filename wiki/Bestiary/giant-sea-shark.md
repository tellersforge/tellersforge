# Giant sea shark

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · huge swimmer · fast swimmer · fantasy, modern, horror*

A monstrous shark, grey above and pale beneath, big enough to bite a fishing boat in half. It can smell blood in the water from miles away, and the struggling of a hooked fish or a wounded swimmer draws it faster than anything else.

It hunts in a pattern every sailor learns to dread: first a fin circling, closer each time, then the rush from below and the crash as it rams. Once blood flows, it will not stop until it or its prey is dead. Fishing fleets carry harpoons and bait barrels to throw overboard as distractions, and no one trails a bleeding hand in the water.
