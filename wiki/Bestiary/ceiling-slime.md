# Ceiling slime

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · tiny amorphous · slow · fantasy*

A patch of bright green slime, no bigger than a hand, clinging to the ceiling of a cave or a dungeon. It looks like moss or damp. It waits for someone to pass beneath.

It drops onto its victim and slowly turns their flesh into more green slime. It is mindless and never flees. It must be scraped off at once, burned off, or cut away with the flesh it touches. Fire, cold and certain holy waters kill it. Delvers carry torches and look up, and a companion who stops talking and starts scratching is checked immediately.

## Not to be confused with

The *[Wall-crawling jelly](wall-crawling-jelly.md)*, a larger ooze that hunts along walls; the ceiling slime is small, drops from above and turns flesh into itself.
