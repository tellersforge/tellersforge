# Tentacled rolling devourer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · large amorphous · rolling · sci-fi, horror*

A ball of grasping tentacles around a single ring-shaped mouth, rolling along the ground like a boulder with a grudge. It has no eyes anyone can find and no front or back; it simply tumbles towards whatever is warm and moving. Groups of them roll together and fan out to surround prey.

A tentacle snatches, several more follow, and the victim is reeled into the mouth and chewed. They fight as long as the group fights. Smugglers sometimes haul them as cargo for collectors, and nearly always regret it when a cage door fails. They are hard to stop once rolling; the wise shut a heavy door and let them batter it.
