# Griffon vulture

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · small flyer · soaring flyer · fantasy, historical, modern, post-apocalyptic*

A huge, pale-brown carrion bird with a bare, pinkish head on a long neck, a ruff of white down and wings like a sail. Griffon vultures soar in lazy circles high above mountains and plains, riding warm air for hours and watching for death below. When one descends, the others follow, and a carcass is stripped to the bone in minutes.

Their stomachs laugh at rot and plague, and they gather wherever there has been a battle, a drought or a massacre. Some mountain peoples leave their dead for them in sky burial; others see them as grim omens. A vulture has no fight in it and flees at the first wound.
