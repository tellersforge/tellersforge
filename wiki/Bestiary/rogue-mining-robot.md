# Rogue mining robot

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · large other · slow · sci-fi, post-apocalyptic*

An industrial digging robot, heavy and low-slung, with a spinning drill for one arm and a massive claw for the other. It was built to clear rock in deep tunnels, and something has gone wrong with it: now it treats anything that moves as rock to be cleared.

It grinds forward slowly through the dark, drilling and crushing, and it never retreats. It is tough and hard to hurt, but also slow and easy to outpace in open tunnels. Miners who meet one flee, block the shaft behind them and send for engineers. The engineers try to cut its power, find its maintenance hatch, or collapse a tunnel on it.
