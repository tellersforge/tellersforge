# Scrap golem

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · large biped · walk · fantasy, post-apocalyptic, sci-fi*

A shambling heap of rusted panels, pipes, springs, cables and whatever else was lying about when it was made. It has no set shape, only something like arms and legs, and it clanks and screeches wherever it goes. Its makers are usually tinkers, junk dealers or desperate folk in ruined places.

After every fight it pulls the broken weapons and armour of its foes onto itself, bolting and twisting them in, so it grows larger and more dangerous with every victory. It never retreats. Dismantling one takes crowbars, cutters and time. Some wasteland settlements keep one as a gate guard and feed it their rubbish, and hope it does not decide they are rubbish too.
