# Drowned revenant

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium biped · swimmer · fantasy, horror, historical, myth*

A bloated sailor's corpse, draped in weed and trailing water. It walks along the seabed, climbs up anchor chains and hauls itself aboard ships in the fog.

It rises from the shallows to drag sailors under, gripping their legs and pulling them down to join it. It has no mind and never flees. Sailors in its waters keep their lanterns lit, their weapons close and their prayers ready, and they never go swimming at night.
