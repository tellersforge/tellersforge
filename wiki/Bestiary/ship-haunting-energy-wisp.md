# Ship-haunting energy wisp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · small other · hover · sci-fi*

A crackling knot of living energy, the size of a fist, that glows blue-white and hums faintly. It slips aboard ships through their power grids and follows power lines, feeding on reactors and burning out circuits.

It drains systems, causing lights to flicker, engines to fail and computers to crash. It shocks anyone who interferes, and flees at the first wound. Crews who find one aboard try to trap it in an empty battery or lure it off the ship with a stronger power source. Superstitious spacers call them ghosts, and some say they carry the souls of dead engineers.
