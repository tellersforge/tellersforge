# Tree-hanging web stalker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · large many-legged · climber · sci-fi, horror*

A long-limbed alien arachnid, dark and hairy, with a bulbous body and a cluster of eyes that gleam in the gloom. It strings nets across jungle canopies, high above the ground, and waits.

When something passes below, it drops on a line of silk, bites, and climbs back up. Its bite paralyses prey, and it wraps the helpless body in silk to eat later. It flees when badly hurt. Jungle travellers watch the canopy, carry blades to cut the webs, and check each other's backs.
