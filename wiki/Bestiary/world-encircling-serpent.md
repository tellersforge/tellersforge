# World-encircling serpent

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · gigantic serpentine · swimmer · myth, fantasy*

A serpent so long that its body rings the entire world, lying coiled in the deepest ocean with its own tail held in its mouth. Its scales are the size of islands, and sailors who have seen a stretch of it rolling in the distance mistook it for a mountain range.

It is almost always still. When it stirs, the land shakes, tides surge and whole coasts are swamped. Its venom is said to be so strong that if it ever lets go of its tail and breathes, the sky itself will be poisoned. It has no fear and never flees. In most stories, its waking means the end of everything.
