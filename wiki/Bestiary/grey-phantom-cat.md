# Grey phantom cat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, modern*

A huge, grey-striped cat, larger than a leopard, with long legs and pale eyes. It is seen rarely and never clearly, a grey shape at the edge of lamplight, a set of prints in the morning mud. Coastal villages blame it for night killings of goats, dogs and, now and then, people.

It kills with a crushing bite to the neck and drags its prey into thick cover. It flees when badly hurt, and nobody has ever produced a body. Hunters say it walks on the sea wind. Villages post watchers and light fires on moonless nights.
