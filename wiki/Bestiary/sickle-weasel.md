# Sickle weasel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mustelid and small mammal](../Species_Families/mustelid-and-small-mammal.md) · small quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth*

A small, quick weasel with long claws curved like sickle blades. It rides on whirlwinds and gusts of wind, and moves so fast that it is only a blur. It slashes travellers' shins as it passes, and they barely feel the cut until they see the blood.

The wounds bleed but do not hurt, which makes them strange and frightening. It flees at the first wound. Travellers in windy mountain passes wrap their legs and walk in groups. Some say three of them work together: one knocks you down, one cuts and one heals.
