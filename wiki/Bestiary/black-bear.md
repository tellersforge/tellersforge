# Black bear

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ursine](../Species_Families/ursine.md) · medium quadruped · climber · fantasy, historical, modern*

A smaller, glossy-coated forest bear, black, brown or cinnamon, with a straight face and short curved claws made for climbing. Black bears eat nuts, berries, insects and anything people leave lying about, and they have become expert camp thieves: they open coolers, tear through tents and learn exactly which cabins keep food.

A black bear confronted usually huffs, slaps the ground and makes a bluffing rush before turning away. Most will climb a tree rather than fight. The dangerous ones are few, but they exist, and a bear that has grown too used to people is no longer afraid of them. Shouting, standing tall and throwing things normally send it off.
