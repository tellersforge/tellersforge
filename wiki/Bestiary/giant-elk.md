# Giant elk

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · prehistoric, fantasy, historical*

A great deer with antlers wider than a man's arm span, palmate and heavy as a cartwheel. It roams open woodland and grassy valleys, grazing and browsing, and it is built to run: long legs, a deep chest and a nervous temper. It flees predators and hunters at the first sign of danger.

Only in the autumn rut do the stags turn dangerous. They bellow, clash antlers and charge anything that approaches, including hunters. Stone-age peoples prize its antlers as tools, trophies and ritual crowns. It flees at the first wound, but a stag in rut does not always read the signs.
