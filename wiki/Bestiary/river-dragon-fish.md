# River dragon-fish

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · large serpentine · swimmer · fantasy, myth*

A carp-like fish as long as a canoe, scaled in shimmering gold, with long whiskered feelers and a crest along its back that seems to grow a little each year. River folk say it is halfway to becoming a dragon.

It leaps up rapids and waterfalls with great bounds, flashing in the sun. It is not cruel, but it is proud and quick to anger, and when it thrashes in the shallows it floods fords and overturns boats. Fishermen leave it alone and pilgrims come to watch it leap. Badly hurt, it sinks to the bottom of the deepest pool and waits to heal.
