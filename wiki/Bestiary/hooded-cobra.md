# Hooded cobra

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · medium serpentine · walk · fantasy, historical, modern*

A long, olive or black snake that can rear up a third of its length and flare the ribs of its neck into a broad hood, often marked on the back with a pattern like eyes or spectacles. Cobras hunt rats, frogs and other snakes in fields, forests and the edges of villages, and they are drawn to granaries where rodents gather.

Threatened, a cobra rears, spreads its hood and hisses, giving a clear warning before it strikes. Its venom kills by paralysis. Snake charmers keep them in baskets, temples honour them, and farmers step carefully in the tall grain. A badly hurt cobra flees; the wise traveller gives it the room to do so.
