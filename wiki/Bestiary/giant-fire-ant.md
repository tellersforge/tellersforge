# Giant fire ant

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium many-legged · fast runner · post-apocalyptic*

An ant grown to the size of a dog, with a red-black shell, jaws like shears and a swollen gland in its abdomen that spits sticky, burning fluid. Colonies dig their mounds beneath ruined towns, and a single nest can hold hundreds of them, tending eggs, dragging carcasses underground and fighting anything that treads too close.

A scout that finds food brings the rest, and they come boiling out of cracks in the pavement in a stream of clicking legs. Each one bites and sprays, and the ground behind them smokes. They keep fighting as long as the swarm fights. Settlements beside an old mound learn to leave it well alone, or to flood it with fuel on a still day and keep a long way back.
