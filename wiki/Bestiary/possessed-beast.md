# Possessed beast

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, horror, modern, myth*

A dog, a bull or a wolf that has a demon riding it. It looks almost like itself, but not quite. The eyes are wrong, too bright or too still, and it moves in jerks and lurches like a puppet on bad strings. It does not flinch at blows, does not whimper at wounds, and keeps coming on broken legs.

The demon inside cares nothing for its host. When the body finally fails, the spirit leaps to the nearest animal, or the nearest person, and the fight begins again. Exorcists, not hunters, are the ones to send for. Where none are to hand, farmers kill the beast in an open place with no other animals near.

## Not to be confused with

The *[Feral dog](feral-dog.md)*, a wild animal that can be scared off with fire and noise; a possessed beast feels nothing, and its rider moves on when the body dies.
