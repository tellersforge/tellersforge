# Steppe pony

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern*

A short, shaggy, stocky horse with a big head, a thick mane and endless stamina. Steppe ponies live half wild in great herds on open grassland and mountain pastures, foraging on almost nothing through bitter winters. Nomad herders ride them from childhood, and a rider with several ponies can travel for days without stopping.

They are hardy, sure-footed and tough, and entire empires have been built on their backs. A steppe pony is not beautiful and it is not fast over a short distance, but it will still be going long after finer horses have broken down. A badly hurt pony flees.
