# Cape buffalo

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern*

A massive, dark buffalo with a heavy boss of horn across its forehead and horns that sweep down and then up to sharp points. Cape buffalo live in herds on savannah and swamp, grazing and wallowing in mud. A herd mobs any predator that comes near, and even lions hesitate to take them on.

Hunters fear them more than any other animal. A wounded bull will circle back and wait in thick cover to ambush the hunter who follows its trail, then fight to the death. Its thick hide and heavy bones shrug off blows. Old hunters call it the black death.
