# Yali

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A temple guardian with a lion's body and an elephant's trunk and tusks, or in some places a horse's head, carved on pillars and gateposts and sometimes found alive before temple doors. It stands in pairs or rows and watches all who enter.

When an intruder comes, it rears up, trumpets and charges, seizing enemies with its trunk and biting with its great jaws. It fights to the death and is said to be stronger than any elephant. Priests revere it, and pilgrims touch its carved feet for luck.
