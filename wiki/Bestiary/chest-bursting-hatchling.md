# Chest-bursting hatchling

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md) · tiny serpentine · fast runner · sci-fi, horror*

A pale, eel-like newborn as long as a forearm, slick with blood, with a blunt head and rows of steel-grey teeth. It grows unseen inside its host for a day or so, and then, with no warning, it tears out through the ribs. The host dies screaming. The hatchling shrieks once and scurries into the dark to hide.

It flees at the first wound and is weak at first. But it grows fast, shedding its skin, and within days it is something much worse. Crews that find a broken body with its chest opened from the inside know they have very little time, and start searching every vent and crawlspace at once.
