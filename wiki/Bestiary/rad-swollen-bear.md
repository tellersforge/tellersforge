# Rad-swollen bear

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ursine](../Species_Families/ursine.md) · huge quadruped · walk · post-apocalyptic*

A bear bigger than any natural bear, hairless in patches, its skin knotted with tumours and glowing faintly with sickness. Whatever poisoned it drove it mad. It lurks in ruins and thick forest at the edge of dead zones, and it ambushes from cover, charging with a roar and mauling until its prey stops moving, and often long after.

It fights to the death, and it is extremely hard to kill. Its meat is poison and its hide falls apart. There is nothing to gain from it and a great deal to lose. Wasteland folk give its territory a wide berth and mark trees near its den with scratched warnings.
