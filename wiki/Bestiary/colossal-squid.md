# Colossal squid

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · gigantic swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A deep-sea squid longer than a longship, pale and translucent in darkness, with eyes the size of shields and ten arms lined with toothed suckers that leave ring-shaped scars on whales. It rises from the depths at night, drawn by lanterns, and plucks sailors off decks or wraps its arms around a hull to feel what is inside.

When wounded it releases a black cloud and vanishes into the dark water. Sailors' tales of it are many and none of them are cheerful. Whalers find its beak in the stomachs of the great whales, and some sailors carry one as a charm. Night watches on dangerous seas are kept with lanterns shuttered and axes close to hand.
