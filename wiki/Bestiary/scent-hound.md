# Scent hound

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern, post-apocalyptic*

A deep-chested dog with long, soft ears, loose skin and a mournful face, built around a nose that is sharper than any other sense a hunter can borrow. A scent hound can pick up a trail that is a day old and follow it across fields, through streams and over roads, baying all the while so the huntsmen can follow.

Hounds are not fighters. They find, follow and corner, and leave the killing to others. Hunting lords keep them in packs; gaolers and bounty hunters keep one or two for escaped prisoners. A fugitive who hears that slow, belling voice behind them knows they have perhaps an hour, and that water, pepper and luck are their only friends.
