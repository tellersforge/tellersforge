# Colossal hermit crab

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md) · gigantic many-legged · walk, swimmer · fantasy, modern*

A crab the size of a castle that has outgrown every shell the sea could offer and now wears a sunken fortress, a ruined tower or the hulk of a warship on its back. From a distance it looks like a building walking out of the surf.

It wades ashore to feed, crushing sea walls, docks and whole streets beneath its legs, and its claws can snap a stone tower in two. When it outgrows its shell, it goes looking for a bigger one, and coastal lords guard their keeps anxiously. It fights to the death. Those living inside its old shells rarely survive the move.
