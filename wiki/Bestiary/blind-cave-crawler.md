# Blind cave crawler

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md) · medium biped · climber · horror*

A pale, eyeless, wiry predator the size of a man, all long limbs, knotted joints and grey skin stretched over bone. It moves on all fours through tunnels and over rock faces, clicking and shrieking in the dark to find its way. It hunts in packs, surrounds a group, and drags victims one by one into side tunnels to feed.

It fights while the pack fights. In its face, now and then, is something that looks almost human, and some say the crawlers were once people who went underground and never came out. Explorers carry bright lights, which hurt their pale skin, and never go down alone.
