# Giant hornless rhino

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · gigantic quadruped · walk · prehistoric, fantasy*

A hornless rhinoceros taller than a house, with long legs, a long neck and a small head it lifts into the treetops to strip leaves and branches. It is the largest land mammal that ever lived, and it moves through open woodland like a slow grey hill, browsing in silence.

It is not aggressive, but it does not need to be. Anything that annoys it, it tramples, and nothing living is large enough to stop it. It flees when badly hurt, which few ever manage. Travellers in its age watch it the way one watches a storm: from a respectful distance, and with nothing between them and it except good sense.
