# Cave lion

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · prehistoric, fantasy, historical*

A maneless lion bigger than any living cat, with a pale, thick coat for the cold and faint stripes on its legs. It hunts in prides across the cold steppes and open woodland, working together to run down horses, deer and young bison. It is fast, powerful and patient, and it rests in caves and rock shelters in bad weather.

Cave paintings show it in lines of lionesses stalking together, the same way hunters see them now. It flees when badly hurt. Stone-age hunters treat it as a rival and a teacher, watching how the pride circles and drives prey, and some bands use the same method against the same herds.
