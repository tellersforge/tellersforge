# Voice-mimicking tiger

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, horror*

A great white tiger of the mountains, with pale stripes and ice-blue eyes. It calls from the woods in a child's voice, or the voice of a loved one, drawing people into the forest where it waits.

It can also cast illusions, so that its victims see what they hope to see. It flees when badly hurt. Mountain villages never answer voices from the forest at night, however familiar, and keep their doors barred until dawn.
