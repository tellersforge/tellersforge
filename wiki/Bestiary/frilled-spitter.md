# Frilled spitter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · small biped · fast runner · prehistoric, fantasy*

A slender two-legged dinosaur about the size of a large dog, with a thin neck and a wide, brightly coloured frill that flares from its head when it is threatened. Before it bites, it spits: a spray of venom that blinds and burns the eyes. Then, while its victim claws at their face, it darts in.

It flees when badly hurt. Its frill is a warning of red, yellow and blue, and those who know its colours back away. Hunters of its age cover their eyes and faces with leather and move in pairs, since a blinded hunter alone is soon a dead one.
