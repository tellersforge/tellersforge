# Ember swarm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md) · small swarm · flyer · fantasy*

A drifting cloud of living sparks and cinders, glowing orange, riding the wind up from a great fire. Each ember is tiny and short-lived, but together they form something with a will: to spread. The cloud drifts over roofs and fields and settles wherever it finds dry wood or grass.

Wherever it settles, new fires start. It is mindless and never flees. Fighting it with weapons is useless. Wet blankets, water and firebreaks are the tools. Communities in dry country fear it more than any beast, since a single ember swarm can turn a small fire into a wildfire that burns for weeks.

## Not to be confused with

The *[Fire mote](fire-mote.md)*, a single playful spark of living flame; the ember swarm is a whole cloud of cinders that spreads wildfire.
