# Afanc

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mustelid and small mammal](../Species_Families/mustelid-and-small-mammal.md) · large swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, myth*

A great, dark beast of deep lakes, seen as a huge beaver by some and a crocodile by others, with a flat tail, a broad head and a mouth full of long, pale teeth. It lives in lairs dug beneath the water and breathes lake water as easily as air. Swimmers and fishermen who stray too close are seized and dragged down.

When it is angered, its thrashing churns the lake until it bursts its banks and floods the fields around it. Villages have dragged afancs out of lakes with teams of oxen and chains, and heroes have fought them in the shallows. It fights to the death, and it is never easy to reach.
