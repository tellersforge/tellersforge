# Golden-fleeced ram

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · flyer · fantasy, myth*

A ram whose fleece is not golden in colour but golden in fact: every curl is soft, warm metal that rings faintly when the animal shakes itself. It can fly, running up the air as if it were a hillside, and in the old stories it carries a chosen child or a hunted exile across the sea to safety.

It is shy of crowds and noise and will not land where people gather. Its horns are sharp and it defends itself when caught, but it flees once badly hurt. Its fleece is worth more than a kingdom's treasury, which is why most tales about it end badly for everyone. The ram itself seems not to understand greed at all.
