# Rift tentacle mass

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · huge other · slow · fantasy, horror*

A tear hanging in the air, jagged and dark, as tall as a house. Through it reaches a forest of long grey tentacles, groping, curling and seizing whatever comes close. What they drag into the tear is never seen again. Nobody has ever seen what they belong to, and those who have looked into the tear say it goes much farther than it should.

It is mindless and never flees. It cannot be killed by cutting the tentacles, because more come through. The only answer is to close the tear, and the ritual for that is rare, costly and dangerous. Most people build walls around it and hope.
