# Giant snake

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · gigantic serpentine · swimmer · prehistoric, fantasy*

A snake as long as a coach and heavy as a cow, olive and black, with a body as thick as a man. It hunts in steaming swamps and rivers of ancient ages, lying in the water with only its eyes and nostrils showing. When prey comes, it strikes, grabs with its teeth and coils around it, crushing until it stops breathing.

It hunts crocodiles, turtles and anything else it can find. It fights while it can. People who find themselves in its swamps learn to fear the water and to watch for the ripple of its body. Its skin is prized as a trophy, and its bones as a charm.
