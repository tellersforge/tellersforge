# Lightning hawk

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · small flyer · flyer · fantasy, myth, horror*

A small, sharp-winged hawk with black-and-white plumage, bound as a servant to a witch or cunning-woman. In the sky it can become lightning, striking down onto its mistress's enemies in a flash and a crack. It is also a bird of blood, and drinks from sleepers through a tiny cut they barely notice until morning.

It is loyal to its mistress and nobody else, and it flees when badly hurt. Villages that suspect one sleep with lights burning and keep iron by their beds. Where lightning strikes a house on a clear night, neighbours look for a witch.
