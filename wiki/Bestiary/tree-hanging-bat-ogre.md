# Tree-hanging bat-ogre

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Chiropteran](../Species_Families/chiropteran.md) · large winged biped · climber · fantasy, myth, horror*

A long-limbed forest horror, man-sized but thin as rope, with iron-hard teeth, leathery skin and hooked feet like a bat's. It hangs from high branches in thick forest, perfectly still, waiting for hunters and gatherers to pass below.

When they do, it reaches down with its long arms and hooks them upward, hauling them into the canopy. It fights hard when cornered, with claws and fangs. Forest peoples look up before they walk under great trees and carry spears to poke into the leaves.
