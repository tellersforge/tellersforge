# Spotted hyena

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern*

A heavy-shouldered, sloping animal with a spotted coat, round ears and jaws that can crack a thigh bone like a nut. Spotted hyenas live in large clans ruled by their females, and they are far better hunters than their reputation allows. A clan can run down a zebra, and a mob of them will harass a lone lion off its kill with whooping, cackling and sheer numbers.

Nothing is wasted: hooves, horns and bones all go down. Herders fear them for the livestock they take, and in many lands they are said to dig up graves, carry witches on their backs or mimic the voices of the dying. Face a clan and it fights as one; break its nerve and it scatters into the night.
