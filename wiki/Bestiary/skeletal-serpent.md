# Skeletal serpent

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · large serpentine · slow · fantasy, horror*

A long spine of linked vertebrae ending in a fanged skull, animated to guard a sunken vault or a pit of bones. It lies coiled in the dark, its ribs rattling faintly as it moves.

It waits in ossuaries and treasure pits, and when anyone steps inside it rises and wraps them in its coils. Its bones grind as it squeezes, and its fangs, though empty of venom, are still sharp. It has no mind and never flees. The best way to deal with one is to smash the skull, which is harder than it sounds when the rest of the body is squeezing.
