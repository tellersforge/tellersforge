# Oath-bound revenant

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium biped · walk · sentient · fantasy, horror, myth*

A corpse risen from the grave for one purpose: to find and kill its murderer. It walks without rest, following a trail only it can sense, and it always knows exactly where its killer is.

It ignores everyone else, walking past guards and through crowds as if they were not there. But anyone who stands between it and its killer will be struck down. It fights to the death, and it cannot be stopped until its purpose is done. When it finally catches its murderer, it falls dead again.

## Not to be confused with

the *[undead knight](undead-knight.md)*, bound by an oath of loyalty; the revenant is bound by vengeance and hunts only its murderer.
