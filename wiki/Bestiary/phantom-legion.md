# Phantom legion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · huge swarm · hover · fantasy, horror*

Once a year, on the anniversary of an old battle, a mist rises on the old route and a whole army marches out of it. Ghostly soldiers in the gear of a long-gone war, spears, banners and drums, march in silent ranks and fight the battle again. Their shapes blur together into a moving fog of faces.

They are not hunting anyone. They simply march, and anyone caught on their road is cut down by blades that should not be solid. Nothing turns them aside. Locals know the date and keep clear. Scholars who want to study them watch from a hillside. Now and then a descendant of one of the soldiers tries to speak to them, and is sometimes answered.
