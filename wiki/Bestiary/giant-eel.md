# Giant eel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · large serpentine · swimmer · fantasy*

An eel as long as a canoe, mottled brown and yellow, with a mouth full of backward-curving teeth. It lives in wrecks, reef holes and sea caves, where only its head shows, jaws opening and closing as it breathes. When a diver or swimmer passes close enough, it darts out, bites and drags its prey back into the hole.

It rarely leaves its lair. Divers learn to read the reefs, avoiding dark holes and never reaching into gaps. Fishers sometimes catch one on a baited line and are lucky to keep their boat. It flees when badly hurt, back into its hole, which means following it is always a bad idea.
