# Spore-bloated beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, horror*

A cow, elk or great hog that fed where it shouldn't have. The fungus has grown through it, swelling its flanks with grey spore sacs and crusting its hide with soft shelves of growth. It still walks with its herd, stupid and restless, and it spreads rot to every animal that grazes beside it. When struck or startled, the sacs burst in thick, choking clouds.

Herders fear it more than wolves. One sick beast can empty a valley of healthy stock in a season. The only cure is to put it down from a distance, burn the carcass where it falls, and move the herd far upwind.
