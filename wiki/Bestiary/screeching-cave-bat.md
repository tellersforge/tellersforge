# Screeching cave bat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Chiropteran](../Species_Families/chiropteran.md) · small flyer · flyer · fantasy, horror*

A bat the size of a dog, with leathery wings, huge ears and a face mostly made of teeth. It lives in deep caverns, hanging in clusters from the roof, and finds its way through total darkness by its own shrill cries. Every cave and every dungeon has them. Most adventurers meet their first one within a minute of lighting a torch.

It flutters erratically, screeches in the faces of torch-bearers, nips at anything warm and wheels away before it can be struck. It is mostly a nuisance and a startler, and it flees the moment it is hurt. The real danger is the ledge a startled explorer steps off while flailing at it.
