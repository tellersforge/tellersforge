# Abyssal hook leviathan

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · gigantic swimmer · swimmer · sci-fi, horror*

A vast, hook-jawed sea horror of the deep ocean, dark and scarred, with a huge head, mandibles like hooks and a body that stretches away into the gloom. It patrols its territory, roaring loudly enough to shake the water and rattle submarine hulls.

It hunts by echo, and divers hear it long before they see it. It grabs submarines in its mandibles and crushes them. It fights to the death. Deep-sea explorers avoid its territory, and those who venture there listen carefully for its roar, and hope they hear it from far away.
