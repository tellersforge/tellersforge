# Glow beetle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · small many-legged · walk · fantasy*

A beetle the size of a dog that wanders mine tunnels and cave systems, casting a soft green-gold glow from two glands behind its head and one on its tail. It eats fungus, lichen and whatever it stumbles over, and it is peaceful unless stepped on, when it bites hard enough to break a toe.

Miners love it. The glands keep shining for days after the beetle dies, a cold light that will not set fire to mine gas and cannot be blown out, and a pair of them hung on a helmet is worth a week's wages. Where glow-beetles wander, the air is good enough to breathe. Where they are found dead, people turn back.
