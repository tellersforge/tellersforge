# Animated marionette

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · small biped · climber · fantasy, horror*

A jointed wooden puppet, painted with a fixed smile, moving without anyone holding its strings. It dances jerkily, clattering and twitching, often in time to music no one else can hear. The strings trail from its limbs and head, and they are the danger.

It skips close and loops them around ankles, wrists and throats, tripping and binding its victims, then climbs up them to strike. It fights to the end. Old theatres, puppet-makers' shops and the playrooms of cursed houses are where it turns up. Cutting the strings slows it; burning the puppet is the usual cure, though some believe the puppeteer who once owned it must be found first.
