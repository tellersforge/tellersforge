# Arena horned cat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · sci-fi*

A great spiny-tailed cat of hot jungles, with a crown of short horns, four eyes that see the heat of living bodies, and a head that splits wide open at the jaw to bite. Its stripes blend into dappled light, and its tail can whip a spine into anything following too close.

It hunts by leaping down from branches or high ledges onto prey below, then mauling at the throat. At night it can see what others cannot. Hunters trap them for arenas, where their speed and ferocity draw crowds. For all that, it is a hunter, not a fighter: once badly hurt, it retreats to lick its wounds.
