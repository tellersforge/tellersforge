# Silverback gorilla

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md) · large biped · climber · fantasy, historical, modern*

A massive adult male gorilla, with a broad chest, huge arms and a saddle of silver hair across his back. Silverbacks lead family groups of females and young through mountain forests, eating leaves, stems and fruit. For all their bulk they are gentle, patient and peaceable.

Threatened, the silverback rises on his legs, hoots, beats his chest and charges, crashing through the undergrowth. Most charges are bluffs. If the threat does not back down, he fights with terrifying strength and will die for his family. Those who lie down and look away are usually spared.
