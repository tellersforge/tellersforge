# Sail-membrane glider

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · medium flyer · flyer · sci-fi*

A light-boned creature with vast, skin-like sails stretched between its limbs, and a narrow beak. It glides for hours in thin, low-gravity air, riding thermals and feeding on insects.

It flees shadows, which to it mean predators, and flees at the first wound. Low-gravity colonists watch them for signs of weather, and some tame them as pets. They are fragile and easily injured, but beautiful in flight, and their sails glow in the sunlight like stained glass.
