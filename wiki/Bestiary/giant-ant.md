# Giant ant

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium many-legged · walk · fantasy, modern, post-apocalyptic*

An ant the size of a dog, shelled in dull black or red plates as hard as iron, with jaws that can snip through a boot. It is never alone. Where one is seen, a hundred are near, all following the same invisible scent trails between the nest and whatever they have found to eat. A single ant is a nuisance; the colony is a small, tireless army.

Near the nest mound they swarm anything that comes close, and they fight while their sisters fight. Wise travellers step off the trail, since the ants rarely stray from it, and never kill one near the others: the smell of a dead ant brings the rest. Miners and farmers dread a colony that settles near them.
