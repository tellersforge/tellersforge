# Burrowing landshark

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · huge quadruped · burrower · fantasy, myth*

A huge, armour-plated beast with a shark-like head, a crest of bony fins and powerful digging claws. It swims through soil and loose rock as a shark swims through water, and senses footfalls from far away.

It erupts from below with jaws open, seizing prey and dragging it down. It fights to the death. Farmers and travellers in its territory walk softly and keep to stony ground. When the fins break the surface, everyone runs for the rocks.
