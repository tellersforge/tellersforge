# Tentacled swamp toad

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md) · huge quadruped · swimmer · fantasy, horror*

A bloated toad as large as a barn, mottled green and brown, with four long tentacles that coil beneath the swamp water, and a single eye on a stalk that peers above the surface. It lies submerged for hours. When something comes near, the tentacles seize it and drag it to a mouth wide enough to swallow a horse.

It fights to the death, and it is very hard to kill. Swamp people who know it pole their boats well clear of any eye-stalk that looks like a reed. Its croak, heard once at night, is enough to clear a village.
