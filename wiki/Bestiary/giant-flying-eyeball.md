# Giant flying eyeball

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · large flyer · flyer · fantasy, horror*

A bloodshot eye as big as a wagon, veined and wet, flying low over the ground at night. It hunts by charging again and again at whatever it sees, and as it flies it sheds a spray of smaller eyes that swarm around it like gnats. It never blinks, and it never stops staring.

Wounded, it becomes worse. The eye tears open down the middle to reveal a ring of teeth, and it keeps coming until it or its prey is dead. Those who survive it tell of the silence before the charge, and of the sound the eye makes when it splits.
