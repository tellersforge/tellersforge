# Winged hopper bug

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium flyer · flyer · sci-fi*

A hive bug with long, veined wings folded along its back, a narrow body and bladed forelimbs held close like a mantis. It roosts on rock spires and cliffs in its thousands, clinging to the stone like a crust of dried leaves until something moves below.

Then the flock drops. They dive, slash and snatch soldiers off their feet, carrying them high into the air and letting them fall. They are mindless and never flee, and a single thought from the hive seems to move them all at once. Troops crossing cliff country march in close order with their weapons pointed upward, and rarely feel safe until they reach open ground.
