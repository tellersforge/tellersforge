# Thunder eagle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · huge flyer · flyer · fantasy, myth*

A colossal eagle, so vast that its wingbeats are thunder and its eyes flash lightning. It lives in the mountains and flaps up storms when it flies. It hunts whales in the sea and horned serpents in lakes, carrying them back to the mountains to eat.

It fights to the death and strikes with lightning as well as talons. Coastal and mountain peoples revere it and carve it on posts. It is not evil, but it is dangerous to be near when it hunts.
