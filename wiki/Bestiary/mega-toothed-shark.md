# Mega-toothed shark

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · gigantic swimmer · swimmer · prehistoric, fantasy, modern*

A shark as long as a whale, with a massive jaw lined with triangular teeth the size of a hand. It hunts the open ocean, following blood trails for miles, and it rams boats and whales from below before biting.

It fights to the death and is terribly hard to hurt, with a tough hide and a thick body. Its bite can split ships. Sailors of its age tell of boats found with holes in their hulls and teeth embedded in the wood. Some coastal peoples worship it as a sea god; others hunt it with great harpoons and whole fleets.
