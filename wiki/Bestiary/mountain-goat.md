# Mountain goat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · climber · fantasy, historical, modern*

A shaggy white goat with a beard, short black horns and hooves built for gripping rock. Mountain goats live on sheer cliffs and high ridges, where they climb and leap with confidence along ledges no wider than a hand. They graze on alpine grasses and lick salt from the rocks.

They are not aggressive, but a goat that meets a climber on a narrow ledge may lower its head and butt them off. Many hunters and climbers have fallen that way. A badly hurt goat flees up the cliff, where nothing can follow.
