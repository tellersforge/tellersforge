# Army ant column

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium swarm · walk · fantasy, historical, modern*

A river of ants a stride wide and a field long, flowing across the forest floor with a dry, rustling hiss. Scouts run ahead, soldiers with hooked jaws flank the edges, and the column swallows everything too slow or too stupid to get out of the way: beetles, lizards, nestlings, a tethered goat, a drunk asleep under a tree. What is left behind is bone and silence.

There is no fighting a column, only moving. Villagers in the deep forest simply leave when one comes through, taking the children and the chickens, and return to find their huts cleaner than they left them. Fire and running water turn it; nothing else does, because there is no single ant to kill and no mind to frighten.
