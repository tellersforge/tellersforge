# Bloated mutant fly

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · small flyer · flyer · post-apocalyptic*

A fat, bristly fly the size of a dog, with bulging red eyes, glassy wings and a swollen, green-grey belly. Its droning can be heard from a long way off. It lives around carrion and waste heaps, laying eggs in the dead, and when something comes near its feeding ground, it swings its belly round and squirts a spray of wriggling maggots.

The maggots burrow into skin and eat. It flees when badly hurt, but comes back to its carrion. It is revolting more than deadly, but its maggots cause wounds that fester and spread sickness. Scavengers burn its feeding grounds and shoot it on sight.
