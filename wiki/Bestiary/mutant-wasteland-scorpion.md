# Mutant wasteland scorpion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · large many-legged · burrower · post-apocalyptic*

A radioactive scorpion as big as a cart, with a pitted, dust-coloured shell, crushing pincers and a tail thick as a man's leg. It buries itself in sand and dust with only the tips of its claws showing, and it can wait for days. When something walks close, it erupts from the ground, grabs with both pincers and stings.

The poison burns in the blood for hours. It fights to the death. Wasteland travellers walk around unexplained ridges of sand, and prod ahead with long poles. Its shell makes good armour plates, and its venom sacs sell for a fortune to healers who know how to use them.
