# Sea snake

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · small serpentine · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A banded, flattened snake with a paddle-shaped tail, at home among reefs and warm coastal waters and almost helpless on land. Sea snakes hunt eels and small fish in crevices, surfacing to breathe, and sometimes gather in great drifting mats on the ocean surface.

They are curious and sometimes swim right up to divers to investigate. Most are reluctant to bite, but fishermen who haul them up in nets, or step on them in the shallows, may be struck. The venom is among the strongest of any snake, and a bite that barely hurts at first can kill within hours. A sea snake that is hurt swims away.
