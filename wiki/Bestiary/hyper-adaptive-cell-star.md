# Hyper-adaptive cell star

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · small amorphous · climber · sci-fi, horror*

A translucent, starfish-like organism, grown in a laboratory from a single sample cell. At first it is small enough to hold in a cupped hand. It does not stay that way.

It grows with every meal, and it grows cleverer as well as larger, learning vents, doors, locks and traps, and using them to trap prey in turn. It survives cold, heat and poison, and fights to the death. Researchers who study it keep it sealed behind several doors. Those who let it out find that it has already learned which doors are the weak ones.
