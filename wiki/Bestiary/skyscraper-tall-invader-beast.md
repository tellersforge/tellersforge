# Skyscraper-tall invader beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · gigantic biped · walk · sci-fi, horror*

A towering, long-armed monster from the deep, taller than a city tower, with grey, scarred hide, a long, thrashing tail and a small head that seems too small for its body. It rises from the sea or the earth, and it walks straight into cities.

It does not seem to hunt; it seems to panic, smashing buildings and crushing everything in its way. As it goes, it sheds swarms of crab-like lice that bite and make their victims swell and burst. It has no mind and never flees. Cities that survive its passage are left in ruins, and the lice are almost worse than the beast itself.
