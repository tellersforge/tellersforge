# Giant slime

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · large amorphous · slow · fantasy*

An oversized wobbling slime the size of a cart, translucent and jelly-like, with things suspended inside it: bones, coins, the occasional helmet. It moves by bouncing, landing on its foes with a wet slap.

When struck hard, it breaks into a crowd of smaller slimes that bounce about and attack independently. It is mindless and never flees. Fire and cold harm it, while blades only make more slimes. Adventurers treat them as more of a nuisance than a threat, until one lands on them.
