# Brain-eating larva

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · tiny swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, sci-fi, horror*

A finger-long, grey tadpole with a fringe of short tentacles around its mouth, bred in brine vats by a hidden people of mind-eaters. They keep them as seeds of their own kind. Placed near a sleeper, the larva wriggles into an ear or the corner of an eye and burrows inward. If it reaches the skull, it eats the brain and grows into it, and the victim slowly becomes its puppet, then its body.

It is weak and afraid, and flees at the first wound. Anyone who has seen one dropped from a vat keeps a sleeping guard with a lamp on every watch, and stuffs their ears with wax.
