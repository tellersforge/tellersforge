# Mist-borne tentacle horror

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · gigantic other · walk · horror*

Something vast walks inside a creeping mist from another world. No one has ever seen all of it. Only its tentacles and immense legs, like pillars, are ever glimpsed, looming in the fog far above. The tentacles pluck at anything moving, and pull it up into the mist.

It is mindless and never flees. It cannot be fought. The only defence is to stay inside, shutter the windows, and wait for the mist to pass. Towns caught in the mist are often never the same.
