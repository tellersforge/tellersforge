# Flesh-eating horse

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Equine and centauroid](../Species_Families/equine-and-centauroid.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth, horror*

A horse that has been fed on human meat until it wants nothing else. It is heavier in the jaw than any honest horse, with a thick neck, flat grinding teeth gone sharp at the front, and eyes that roll white at the smell of blood. A herd of them is kept by cruel kings as a punishment, or runs wild on plains where no one dares to settle.

Strangers who come among the herd are torn apart by teeth and hooves together, and the dead are eaten where they fall. The horses fight as long as the herd fights; break the herd and each one bolts. Only iron chains and fire will hold them, and even their grooms carry scars.
