# Hive-swarm colossus

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md) · gigantic other · walk · sci-fi, horror*

A towering shape that walks and fights like a single giant, but is made of millions of smaller creatures clinging together: insects, rats, crabs or stranger things. Its surface seethes and ripples as they crawl over each other.

When it is struck, the blow sends a cloud of creatures scattering, and they flow back together as soon as it has passed. Shells and bullets pass through it and do almost nothing. It has no single mind that anyone has found, and it never flees. The only ways to fight it are fire, poison or finding whatever holds it together and breaking that.
