# Crying infant spirit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · tiny other · hover · myth, horror, fantasy*

On a dark road, far from any house, a baby is crying. It lies alone in the grass or on a stone, wrapped in rags, pitiful and cold. Any decent traveller stops. When they lift it, it clings. Then it grows heavier. It grows heavier than a sack of grain, than a man, than a stone slab, and it will not let go.

Its victims are found crushed flat in the road. The spirit fights to the end and cannot be pried off. Tales disagree on the defence: some say a prayer said with the child in your arms breaks it, others that the only safe thing is to walk past, however badly your heart wants to stop.
