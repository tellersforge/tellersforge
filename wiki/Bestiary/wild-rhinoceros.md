# Wild rhinoceros

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Pachyderm](../Species_Families/pachyderm.md) · large quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern*

A heavy, thick-skinned grazer with one or two horns on its snout, small eyes and a huge body. Rhinos live in grasslands and bush, browsing on leaves and grass. They are nearsighted, and they charge blindly at anything they hear or smell and do not like.

A charging rhino can flatten a hut or overturn a wagon, and its horn can gore a horse. Its hide shrugs off blows. Most charges are short, and a rhino often loses interest if its target steps aside. A badly hurt rhino flees.
