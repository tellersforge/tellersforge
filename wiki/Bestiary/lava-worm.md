# Lava worm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · huge serpentine · burrower · fantasy*

A glowing worm armoured in plates of cooled rock, swimming through molten stone the way an eel swims through water. Between its plates it shines an angry orange, and the air around it shimmers with heat.

It lives deep in volcanic ground and rarely comes up. When it does, it surfaces through cracks and eruptions, leaving smoking channels of cooling rock and burning everything nearby. It has no mind worth the name and never flees. The only real protection is distance. Mountain folk living near fire peaks learn the signs of its rising, a faint tremor and a hot smell, and get out of the way.
