# Wax golem

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium biped · walk · fantasy, horror*

A lifelike figure of moulded wax, sculpted to look exactly like a real person, down to the eyelashes and a mole on the cheek. At a distance and at rest it passes perfectly. Up close, the skin is too smooth, and it smells faintly of candles. Its makers use it as a double, a spy or a decoy.

It fights when ordered and never flees. In heat, it softens, sags and runs like a candle, but as it cools it pulls itself back into shape, so a torch only buys time. Cold, sharp blows do more harm. People who suspect a wax double hold a candle close to its face and watch whether the cheek begins to drip.
