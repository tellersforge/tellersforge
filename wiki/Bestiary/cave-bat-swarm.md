# Cave bat swarm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Chiropteran](../Species_Families/chiropteran.md) · large swarm · flyer · fantasy, horror*

Hundreds of cave bats pouring out of a crevice at once, a shrieking black cloud that fills a passage from wall to wall. Wings slap faces and snuff torches, claws catch in hair, and small teeth nip at every patch of bare skin. The noise alone is enough to make grown warriors drop to the floor with their hands over their ears.

The swarm is not really hunting: it has been disturbed, and it wants out. It finds its way by sound and pays no heed to darkness, so the explorers are the ones left blind and deafened in the rush. Once badly hurt, it breaks up and flows away down the tunnels. Experienced delvers cover their lamps and lie flat until it passes.
