# Sled dog

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern*

A thick-furred, wolfish dog with a plumed tail curled over its back and pale eyes that seem made for glare off snow. Sled dogs are bred for the far north, where they haul loaded sledges in long teams across ice and tundra for days on end, eating little and sleeping curled in the drifts.

They are pack animals through and through: they howl together, squabble over rank and fight as a team if one of them is threatened. A good lead dog finds the safe ice and the buried trail when its driver cannot. To the peoples of the cold, a team of dogs is wealth, transport and sometimes the difference between reaching home and freezing on the way.
