# Blighted walking tree

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · huge plant · slow · fantasy, horror*

A rotting tree, its bark black and peeling, its leaves long gone, pulled up by its own roots and driven to rage by blight or a curse. It walks slowly, dragging its roots, and the stench of rot precedes it.

It rages at woodcutters and fire, crushing camps and battering anyone with an axe. It fights to the death. Fire harms it, but its rotten wood is damp. Woodcutters avoid forests where one walks, and some say that lifting the curse or cleansing the blight will return it to rest.

## Not to be confused with

The *[Blood-drinking tree](blood-drinking-tree.md)*, which stays rooted and feeds on sleepers; the blighted walking tree walks and rages at those who harm the forest.
