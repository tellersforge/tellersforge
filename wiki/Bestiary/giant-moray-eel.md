# Giant moray eel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · large serpentine · swimmer · fantasy, modern, horror*

A thick, spotted eel as long as a longboat, with a narrow head, cold eyes and a mouth that never quite closes over its teeth. It lives in holes in the reef and in the broken hulls of wrecks, with only its head showing, breathing slowly as it waits.

When a swimmer passes close, it strikes like a spring and seizes whatever it can reach, then drags back into its hole. Its grip is hard to break and the teeth slant backwards. Pearl divers learn which holes to avoid and never put a hand into a dark gap. Badly hurt, it releases its prey and slides deep into the wreck.
