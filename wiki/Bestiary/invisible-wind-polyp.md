# Invisible wind polyp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · huge other · hover · horror*

A half-visible, polyp-like being as large as a hall, that flickers in and out of sight like heat haze. It is very old, older than any people, and it lives in deep places underground, where its whistling can be heard for miles. It fights with blasts of howling wind, battering foes against walls and tearing the breath from their lungs.

It fights to the death, and it cannot be seen well enough to hit easily. Explorers who hear the whistling in the deep stop, go back, and seal the passage. Scholars say it once ruled whole worlds.
