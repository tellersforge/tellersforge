# Diesel war crawler

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · huge other · slow · post-apocalyptic, sci-fi, historical*

A war machine of riveted plate on grinding tracks, belching black smoke from its stacks, with no crew inside. It crawls forward over rubble, wire and bodies, and nozzles on its hull spray burning fuel across anything in its way. The noise and the stink arrive long before it does.

It is slow but hard to stop, mindless and never retreats. Bullets spark off its armour. Soldiers dig tank traps, set mines, or wait for it to run out of fuel. Its tracks are its weakness, and a broken track leaves it spinning in place and spitting fire at everything nearby.
