# Locust swarm

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · gigantic swarm · flyer · fantasy, historical, modern, myth*

First a smudge on the horizon, then a roar like rain, then the sun goes out. A locust swarm is a cloud of grasshoppers so vast it takes a day to pass over, and it lands on green fields and rises again from brown earth. Grain, orchards, thatch, even the linen left out to dry vanish into countless small jaws. No one is bitten. They are simply left with nothing to eat.

Swarms are the stuff of scripture and chronicle: a punishment sent on proud kings, the start of a famine, the reason a village sold its daughters or went to war with its neighbours. People beat pans, light smoke fires and dig trenches for the young hoppers. Mostly they pray, and count what is left.
