# Dome-headed dinosaur

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · medium biped · walk · prehistoric, fantasy*

A thick-skulled dinosaur, man-sized and two-legged, with a domed head like a helmet ringed with bony knobs. It lives in small herds in hills and forests, browsing plants, and it settles quarrels by ramming rivals and intruders with its head.

When threatened, it lowers its dome and charges at a run. The blow can break ribs and knock a person senseless. It flees when badly hurt. Those who share its world learn to step aside and never stand in front of one, since it seems to consider everything a rival.
