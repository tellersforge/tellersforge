# Giant monitor lizard

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · huge quadruped · walk · prehistoric, fantasy*

A monitor lizard as long as a small boat, heavy and scaly, with a forked tongue that tastes the air for blood and a mouth full of serrated teeth. It hunts the dry woodlands and open plains of the ice age, and it bites prey once and follows it, waiting for the festering venom and sickness to do their work.

It fights while it can. Its bite is not quick to kill, but it rarely fails. Scavenging bands learn to tend any bite at once with fire and herbs, and to keep moving, since the lizard follows its victims for days.
