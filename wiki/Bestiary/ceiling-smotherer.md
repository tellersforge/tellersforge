# Ceiling smotherer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · large amorphous · slow · fantasy*

A flat, grey sheet of muscle as wide as a rug, lying along a cave roof or floor and looking exactly like rock. It can wait for weeks without moving. When something walks across or beneath it, it drops or folds up around its prey like a closing fist, and squeezes until the breath is gone.

It fights to the death once it has a victim. Cutting a friend out from inside it is grim work, and fast work, because there isn't long. Cavers poke floors and ceilings with spears and listen for the dull, fleshy sound that is not stone.
