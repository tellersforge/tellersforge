# Kelp strangler

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · large plant · swimmer · fantasy, modern*

A broad mat of slick, brown-green seaweed that drifts among ordinary kelp beds and looks exactly like them. When a swimmer or diver brushes through, the fronds tighten. They wrap around legs, arms and throat, and the whole mass sinks, dragging its catch down to the rocks below and holding it there until it stops struggling.

Pearl divers and sponge gatherers know the danger and carry short hooked knives on their wrists. Fishermen tell of nets pulled from their hands and small boats tipped over in calm water. A diver who feels the weed tighten has only a few heartbeats to cut free before panic sets in.
