# Doorway lurker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · large amorphous · slow · fantasy*

A mimic that has grown too big to pass as a chest. It takes the shape of doors, pillars, wall panels or archways in old ruins, and it is very convincing: the grain of the wood, the stain of the stone, even a handle or knocker. It lies still for weeks at a time.

Whoever touches it sticks to its gluey skin, and the whole wall opens into a mouth. It swallows its prey slowly. It is sluggish and prefers not to move far, and when badly hurt it will peel away and crawl off to heal. Old delvers tap walls and doors with a spear butt before going through, and note the doors that seem slightly warm.
