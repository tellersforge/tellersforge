# Diamondback rattlesnake

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · small serpentine · slow · fantasy, historical, modern*

A thick-bodied pit viper patterned with dark diamonds edged in pale scales, with a broad head and a string of hollow rattles at the tip of its tail. Rattlesnakes live in deserts, rocky hills and dry grassland. Pits on their faces sense warmth, letting them strike a mouse in total darkness.

Before it bites, the rattlesnake shakes its tail in a dry, furious buzz that every ranch hand learns to freeze at. Most bites happen to people who stepped too close or reached into a crevice. The venom swells and rots the flesh around the wound and can kill. A rattlesnake that is hurt tries to slide away into the rocks.
