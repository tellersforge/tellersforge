# Wyvern whelp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · small winged biped · flyer · fantasy*

A young wyvern about the size of a hunting dog, all neck, elbows and appetite, with soft scales that have not yet hardened and wings it has only just learned to use. Whelps hatch in clutches and squabble constantly, hissing, shoving and snapping at anything that comes near the nest, including one another.

A single whelp is a nuisance rather than a danger, and it flees at the first wound, screaming for its parent. That is the real danger, because the parent is rarely far away. Adventurers who stumble on a nest should be quick, quiet and already leaving. Trained young, a whelp can be raised, but few keepers keep all their fingers.
