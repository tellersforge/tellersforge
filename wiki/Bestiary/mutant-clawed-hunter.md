# Mutant clawed hunter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · large biped · fast runner · post-apocalyptic*

A horned, lizard-like apex predator of the wastes, taller than a man, with scaly hide, a long jaw and claws the length of a forearm. It was bred long ago from chameleon stock for war, and when its makers were gone it went wild and bred on its own. It stalks ruins and gullies, then charges in a blur and tears its prey open.

It is utterly fearless and fights to the death. Its hide turns light blades, and it heals and adapts quickly. Wastelanders mark its lairs with warning signs and go around them, and a hunter who brings one down is rich and famous for a season.
