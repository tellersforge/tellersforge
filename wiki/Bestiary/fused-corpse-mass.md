# Fused corpse mass

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · large amorphous · slow · fantasy, horror*

Dozens of bodies melted into one heaving mass of arms, legs, heads and mouths. It moves slowly, rolling and dragging itself along, and the mouths moan and gibber as it goes.

It engulfs victims, pulling them into itself, and they become part of the mass. It has no mind and never flees. It is hard to kill: cutting off an arm or a head does little. Fire is the best weapon, and even then it takes a long time to burn.
