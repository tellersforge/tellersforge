# Fire wyvern

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · large winged biped · flyer · fantasy*

A red-scaled wyvern of volcanic peaks, with a long neck, black-edged wings and a throat that glows orange, then yellow, just before it spits flame. It nests in craters and lava caves where no one else can bear the heat, and the heat does not trouble it at all.

It hunts herds from above, scorching them first and landing among the burned and fleeing to feed. Far from home it flees when badly hurt, but near its nest it fights to the death. Herders on volcanic slopes move their flocks at night and watch for a glow in the sky that is not the mountain.
