# Northern wolverine

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mustelid and small mammal](../Species_Families/mustelid-and-small-mammal.md) · small quadruped · walk · fantasy, historical, modern*

A squat, broad-headed animal like a small bear with a bushy tail, dark brown with a pale band along its sides. Wolverines roam the snowbound forests and tundra of the far north, covering huge distances in search of carrion and prey, and they fear almost nothing.

A wolverine will drive a bear or a pack of wolves off a kill, and it raids traplines, stealing bait and caught animals and fouling what it cannot carry. Once it decides to fight, it goes into a snarling fury and fights to the death. Trappers regard it with a mixture of hatred and grudging respect.
