# Shrieking zombie

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium biped · walk · horror, modern, post-apocalyptic, fantasy*

A thin, wasted corpse with its jaw torn open and its throat raw. When it sees the living, it throws back its head and screams, a high, piercing howl that carries for a mile and draws every dead thing in earshot.

It does not fight much itself. It shrieks, and the horde comes running. It has no mind and never flees. Survivors kill it first, before it can raise the alarm, and some carry bows and crossbows just for that purpose.
