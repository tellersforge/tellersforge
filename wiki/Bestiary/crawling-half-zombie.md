# Crawling half-zombie

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · small other · slow · horror, modern, post-apocalyptic, fantasy*

The top half of a corpse, severed at the waist, dragging itself along by its arms. Its guts trail behind it, and it moves slowly, with a wet, scraping sound.

It is easy to miss. It lies hidden in long grass, under rubble or behind furniture, and when someone walks past, it grabs their ankle and bites. It has no mind and never flees. Survivors learn to watch the ground as carefully as the horizon, and to finish off anything that looks dead.

## Not to be confused with
