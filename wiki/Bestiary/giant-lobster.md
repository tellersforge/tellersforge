# Giant lobster

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md) · huge many-legged · swimmer · fantasy, modern*

A deep-water crustacean as long as a ship's boat, armoured in plates of dark red and blue like a fortress wall. It lurks on the sea floor among wrecks and rocks, antennae waving in the currents, and when something floats overhead it rises and seizes the hull with claws that can crack timber. Small boats are dragged under.

It fights to the death and is terribly hard to hurt. Divers who hunt pearls and sunken treasure watch for its antennae poking from a wreck. Coastal lords sometimes offer a reward for one, and the feast that follows is long remembered, for those who survive to attend it.

## Not to be confused with

the *[giant crab](giant-crab.md)*, which comes ashore to hunt; the lobster stays on the sea floor and is far larger and harder to kill.
