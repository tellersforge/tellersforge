# Giant wolf spider

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · large many-legged · fast runner · fantasy, horror*

A great, hairy hunting spider the size of a pony, grey and brown like dry earth, with two huge eyes that shine green in lamplight. It spins no web. It runs. Across open ground it can match a horse, and it sees in darkness as well as by day, so a traveller walking at night may notice nothing until the pounce.

Plainsfolk know its signs: silk-lined burrows in the hillsides, carcasses of sheep with two small punctures, the eyeshine that stays too still at the edge of the firelight. A camp with a good fire and a watchful sentry is usually safe. A lone rider who stops to rest in the long grass is not. Badly hurt, it breaks off and bolts back to its hole.
