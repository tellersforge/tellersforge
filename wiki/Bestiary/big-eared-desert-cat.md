# Big-eared desert cat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · small quadruped · fast runner · sci-fi*

A small, sandy wild cat with a patterned coat, a bushy tail and enormous ears that swivel at every sound. It prowls the grasslands and scrub around frontier farms, catching rodents and lizards, and is common enough that most farm children have seen one.

It hisses and swats when cornered and flees at the first real wound. It is no danger to anyone with a stick. Some frontier folk say they gather where strange power flows, and follow those who have it, and a few hermits and mystics keep one as a companion. Whether that is true or just a story is anyone's guess.
