# Spectre

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · medium other · hover · sentient · fantasy, horror, myth*

A pale, cold phantom, shaped like the person it was in life, but thin and translucent, with eyes like dark holes. It haunts the place where it died, and it hates all living warmth.

It wants others to be as dead as it is, and it drains the life from anyone it touches. It fights to the death, and it can pass through walls and doors. Those who deal with it use holy symbols and prayers, and some try to lay it to rest by finding out how it died.

## Not to be confused with

the *[wraith](wraith.md)*, a hooded shape that drains sleepers; the spectre is the pale ghost of a person, bound to where it died.
