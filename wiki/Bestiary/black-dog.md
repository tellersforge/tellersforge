# Black dog

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth, horror, historical*

A huge black hound, bigger than any mastiff, with a shaggy coat and eyes that glow like coals. It is met on lonely roads, at crossroads and at the edges of churchyards, usually at night, often walking silently alongside a traveller before vanishing. To see it is to know that a death is coming, sometimes the watcher's own.

It rarely fights. It simply looks, and the looking fills people with dread. When it does bite, it bites hard, but it retreats when badly hurt and seems to melt into the dark. Some villages treat it as a guardian of the dead; others lock their doors when it is seen and pray.
