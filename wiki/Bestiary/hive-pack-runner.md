# Hive pack runner

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md) · small quadruped · fast runner · sci-fi*

A dog-sized, scythe-clawed runner of a devouring swarm, lean, low and armoured along the spine. Alone, it is fragile and not very dangerous. It is never alone.

The swarm breeds them in vast numbers and throws them forward in dozens, flooding in fast, surrounding prey and shredding it. Wounds close almost as fast as they are made, and the dead are simply replaced. They are mindless and never flee; the will of the swarm drives them on. Soldiers facing them hold tight formations and keep firing, because once the line breaks, it is already too late.
