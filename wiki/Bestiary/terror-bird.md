# Terror bird

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · large biped · fast runner · prehistoric, fantasy*

A flightless hunting bird taller than a man, with long legs, stubby wings and a hooked beak like an axe head. It sprints across open grassland faster than a horse, runs down prey and hammers it with its beak, striking downwards again and again. A kick from its taloned feet can break bone.

It hunts alone or in pairs and flees when badly hurt. Its shrieking cry echoes across the plains. Hunters learn to watch for the bobbing crest in the grass and to stand their ground in groups, since it prefers lone prey. Some tribes tell stories of it as a demon bird that runs down the souls of the dead.
