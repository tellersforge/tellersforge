# Guardian lion

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, historical*

A curly-maned beast halfway between a lion and a great dog, with a broad face, bulging eyes and a thick coat of tight curls. They always come in pairs, male and female, one with a paw on a ball, the other with a paw on a cub. They stand at the gates of palaces and temples, and they never leave their posts.

Most are stone. Some are not. A living pair sees intruders at once and attacks with teeth and claws, fighting to the death rather than yield the threshold. They let pass anyone who comes in good faith. Keepers feed them and brush their manes.
