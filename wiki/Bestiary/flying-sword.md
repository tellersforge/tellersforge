# Flying sword

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · tiny flyer · flyer · fantasy*

A bare blade that rises from its rack or its heap of treasure and flies at the nearest foe, hilt first or point first, as a swordsman would wield it. It turns in the air, darts, feints and fences. It catches incoming blows on its edge and knocks them aside, then slides past a guard to cut.

It is mindless and never flees. There is rarely only one: a dragon's hoard or a wizard's armoury may hold dozens, and they rise together in a glittering, ringing cloud. The best answer is a sturdy shield and a heavy hand to knock them down and pin them. Once on the ground, a flying sword is just a sword, and worth money.

## Not to be confused with

The *[Sword wraith](sword-wraith.md)*, the ghost of a swordsman still wielding his blade; the flying sword has no wielder at all, only a spell.
