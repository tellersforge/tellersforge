# Electric eel

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · medium swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A long, dark, eel-shaped fish of warm, muddy rivers, with a flat head and a body that is mostly a living battery. It breathes air, rising to gulp at the surface, and it hunts fish in water too murky to see through by sensing the faint electrical fields around it.

When it strikes, the eel unleashes a burst of electricity through the water that stuns every fish nearby, and can knock a wading horse or a man off their feet. Several shocks in a row can leave a person senseless and drowning in the shallows. Fishermen learn to avoid still pools with a telltale shape lying in the mud.
