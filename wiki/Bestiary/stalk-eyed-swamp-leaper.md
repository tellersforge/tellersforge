# Stalk-eyed swamp leaper

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · sci-fi*

A long-legged amphibian predator, slick and mottled green, with a wide, flat mouth and a pair of swivelling stalk eyes that turn independently. It lives in reed beds and marshes, lying low among the stems and watching.

When something comes near, it leaps from cover and lassoes the prey with a long, sticky, venomous tongue, dragging it in to bite. It flees when badly hurt, bounding off through the reeds. Swamp dwellers know its croaking call and watch the reeds carefully. Its venom is collected by some for arrows and blades.
