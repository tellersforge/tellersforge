# Energy-draining jellyfish

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · small other · hover · sci-fi, horror*

A floating creature with a translucent, domed membrane, trailing tendrils and a ring of fangs beneath. It drifts silently through the air, rising and falling like a thing underwater, always drifting towards warmth and life.

When it finds prey, it drops onto the head and clamps on with its fangs, and the victim's strength begins to drain away. Pulling at it only tightens its grip; only cold makes it let go. It has no mind and never flees. Freezing is the surest way to kill it, and those who travel where they live carry freezing sprays, cold packs, or at least a bucket of ice.
