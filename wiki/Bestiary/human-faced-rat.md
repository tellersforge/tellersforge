# Human-faced rat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md) · tiny quadruped · walk · fantasy, horror*

A rat with tiny, pink human hands and a small, wizened human face, like an old man's. It squeaks in a thin, whispering voice. Often it serves some old witch as a familiar, spying for her, stealing things, and carrying messages. It nibbles sleepers at night, whispers mockery in their ears, and scurries away.

It is small and weak and flees at the first wound. It is nasty rather than dangerous. Villagers who see one know there's a witch nearby, and they know the rat will tell her everything they say.
