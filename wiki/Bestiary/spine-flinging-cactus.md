# Spine-flinging cactus

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · medium plant · slow · fantasy, sci-fi*

A squat, ribbed column of grey-green flesh, bristling with barbed needles and shuffling across the sand on a skirt of shallow roots. From a distance it is just another cactus. Up close it is the only one that turns to face you. When anything warm comes near, the ribs clench and a spray of spines flies out with a dry crack, and the barbs work deeper with every step the victim takes.

It has no mind to speak of, only a thirst. In the dry season it drifts slowly toward wells, oases and river beds, which is exactly where travellers stop. Desert folk learn to count the cacti around a waterhole each evening and to worry when the count goes up. Burning works, if you can get close enough to light it.
