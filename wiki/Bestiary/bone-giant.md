# Bone giant

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · huge biped · walk · fantasy, horror, myth*

The raised skeleton of a giant, as tall as three grown men, with long bones thick as tree trunks and a skull the size of a barrel. It walks with heavy, grinding steps and swings fists of bare bone.

Necromancers use bone giants to smash gates and break ranks. Arrows pass between its ribs, and blades glance off its bones. It has no mind, no fear and never flees. Soldiers facing one learn to aim for the knees and ankles with heavy weapons, and to drop it where it stands.
