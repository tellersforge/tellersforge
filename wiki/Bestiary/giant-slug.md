# Giant slug

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · huge serpentine · slow · fantasy*

A slug as long as a wagon, grey-brown and glistening, leaving a trail of slime a stride wide that dries to a glassy crust. It moves very slowly and cannot be stopped. When anything comes near, it rears its front end and spits a stream of acid that eats through leather, hisses on steel and pits stone, then crawls over what is left and grinds it down.

It lives in caves, sewers and sodden forests, and it fights to the death because it has no notion of doing anything else. Blades sink into it and come out corroded. Salt makes it writhe, fire makes it shrink, and old dungeon-delvers say the best tactic is to walk briskly in another direction, since it will never catch you.
