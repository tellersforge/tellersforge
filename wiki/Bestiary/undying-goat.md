# Undying goat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A sturdy, sharp-horned goat bred to pull a war chariot for a great storm-lord. It has a remarkable gift. At night it can be slaughtered, roasted and eaten by a hungry company, and so long as the hide is kept whole and the bones are laid back on it unbroken, a blessing at dawn raises the goat again, alive and well, ready to be driven.

If a single bone is cracked for its marrow, the goat rises lame, and its master's anger is legendary. In a fight it butts with its horns and runs once badly hurt, so the trick is to keep it alive in the first place. Travellers who hear of it should mind their manners at supper.
