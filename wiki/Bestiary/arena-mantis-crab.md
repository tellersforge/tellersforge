# Arena mantis-crab

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md) · huge many-legged · fast runner · sci-fi*

A long-legged, shelled beast with a pair of scythe-edged forelimbs that it uses to parry blows and impale opponents. Its shell is bone-coloured and ridged, its legs thin and quick, and its small head sits low between the blades. It is at home in shallow water and on mudflats, but it is best known in the fighting pits.

Owners of arenas pay well for them because they are fierce, unpredictable and fight to the death. It stabs with a forelimb, pins the victim to the ground, and bites. Gladiators learn to get inside the reach of the blades, where the creature is clumsy, or to cripple a leg. Captured wild, it is almost impossible to tame.
