# War mastiff

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern, post-apocalyptic*

A broad, heavy-headed dog as big as a small man, with a chest like a barrel and jowls that swing when it walks. War mastiffs are bred and trained for one purpose: to guard their master and to seize anything that threatens him. They go to battle in spiked collars and leather coats, and they are taught to clamp onto an arm or a horse's leg and drag it down.

A mastiff does not bark much and does not retreat. Once set on a target it holds on until the target stops moving or the mastiff dies. Lords keep them at their gates, jailers keep them in their yards, and anyone planning to climb a wall at night learns to ask about the dogs first.
