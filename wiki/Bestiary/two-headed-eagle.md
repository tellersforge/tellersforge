# Two-headed eagle

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · huge flyer · flyer · fantasy, myth, historical*

A giant eagle with two heads on two strong necks, one facing each horizon, so that it never has a blind side. Its wings span a field and its claws can carry off an elephant. It nests on the highest peaks and takes whatever it wants from the plains below.

It is proud and fierce and fights to the death. Empires put it on their banners to show they watch in every direction. Real two-headed eagles take elephants and cattle, and sometimes whole carts, and their nests are littered with bones and broken harness.
