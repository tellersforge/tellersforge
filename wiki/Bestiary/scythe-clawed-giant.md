# Scythe-clawed giant

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · huge biped · slow · prehistoric, fantasy*

A pot-bellied, feathered giant with a small head, a long neck and hand claws as long as swords, curved like scythes. It is a plant-eater, rearing up to pull branches down with those claws and strip them bare, and it moves slowly through forests and river valleys, browsing.

It is harmless until provoked. When threatened, it rears up, spreads its arms and slashes, and the claws can cut a hunter in half. It fights while it can. Other hunters of its age leave it alone, and people learn to give it room and never come between it and its young.
