# Smoke elemental

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md) · medium other · flyer · fantasy*

An oily, drifting cloud of soot and smoke, darker than night, with a faint shape that might be arms and a head. It moves silently, seeping under doors and through keyholes, and it smells of burnt tar. It is born in great fires and furnaces, and it seeks out the living.

It pours into sleeping lungs and chokes its victims from within. It is mindless and never flees. Blades pass through it. Strong wind scatters it, and fresh water and open windows help. Some cities with many furnaces are plagued by them, and householders seal their doors with wet cloth at night.
