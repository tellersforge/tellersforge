# Forked-tail jackal

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth, historical*

A lean desert canine with long, square-tipped ears and a stiff tail forked at the end like a snake's tongue. Its coat is the colour of sand, and its eyes are narrow and knowing. It trails caravans across wastelands at a distance, and when it howls, the wind rises and sandstorms roll in behind it.

It fights while the pack fights and scatters when the pack does. Desert peoples consider it a bringer of trouble, chaos and bad weather, and some say it serves a storm god. Caravan masters pay handsomely for guards who can shoot straight in bad light.
