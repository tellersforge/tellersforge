# Great dragon-worm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · huge serpentine · slow · fantasy, myth*

A limbless dragon so long that it can lie coiled three times around a hill, with its head resting on its own tail. Its hide is scarred and thick as a castle door, poison does not trouble it, and its breath is a hot, foul fume that sours wells and kills the grass for a mile downwind.

It sleeps for years at a time on whatever treasure it has gathered, and a region can forget it exists. When it wakes, it is hungry. It takes cattle first, then demands them, and the stories of maidens offered to worms begin here. It fights until it dies, and it takes a long time to die.
