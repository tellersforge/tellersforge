# Fish-toothed war hound

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · sci-fi*

A scaly, fish-jawed hound, lean and muscular, with a wide, lipless mouth crammed with needle teeth and eyes set far apart on its head. It smells faintly of brine, even far from water.

Alien mercenaries breed and train them as war beasts, and set them on enemies in packs. They hunt together and fight as long as the pack fights. Packs that escaped or were abandoned have overrun empty colonies, where they live in the ruins and hunt anything that comes near. Settlers treat them as a dangerous pest. Mercenaries value a well-trained hound more than a new recruit, and say so.
