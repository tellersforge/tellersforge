# Smothering rug

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium other · slow · fantasy, horror*

A fine carpet or tapestry, richly woven, lying flat on the floor of a hall or hanging on a wall. Nothing about it seems strange until someone steps onto it. Then it rolls itself up with terrible speed around its victim, tighter and tighter, until they cannot breathe.

It is slow to move across a room but fast when it strikes, and it is mindless and never lets go. Friends must cut or tear the victim free before they suffocate, taking care not to cut them too. It can also be set to guard a passage, where it lies waiting. Thieves who know about them walk around the edges of rugs, or throw a sack of grain on first.
