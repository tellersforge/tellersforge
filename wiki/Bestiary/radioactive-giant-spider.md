# Radioactive giant spider

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · gigantic many-legged · walk, climber · modern, sci-fi, horror*

A spider grown to the height of a hill by some atomic mishap in the desert or the laboratory. Its body is hairy and swollen, its legs bend like cranes, and its many eyes glitter above jaws that drip a venom strong enough to dissolve a cow.

It strings cables of silk across valleys and between towers, thick as rope and strong as steel. Tanks and trucks caught in them stop dead, and the crews are paralysed by the bite before being wrapped. It is mindless and never flees. Fire and heavy guns are the only answer, and even they need to be many.
