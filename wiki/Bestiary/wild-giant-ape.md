# Wild giant ape

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md) · large biped · walk · fantasy, myth, modern*

A tall, shaggy ape that walks upright on two legs, standing head and shoulders above a tall man. It lives in deep forests and snowy mountains, far from people, and avoids them whenever it can. It is rarely seen and never caught, and it leaves only huge footprints in mud or snow.

When disturbed, it throws stones and branches, and it may slam or bite if it cannot get away, but it flees when badly hurt. Its passage leaves no trace except footprints. Hunters and scholars have spent their lives searching for it. Most have found nothing but prints and rumours.
