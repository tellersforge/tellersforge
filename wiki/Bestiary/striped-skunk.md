# Striped skunk

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mustelid and small mammal](../Species_Families/mustelid-and-small-mammal.md) · tiny quadruped · walk · fantasy, historical, modern*

A small, black animal with bold white stripes down its back and a great plumed tail. Skunks forage at night for insects, grubs, eggs and scraps, trundling along with little fear, because they carry a weapon nothing wants to face.

When threatened, a skunk stamps its feet, raises its tail and hisses. If that does not work, it turns and sprays a foul musk that can blind for a time, sicken anyone nearby and cling to clothes for days. Dogs and curious children learn this lesson quickly. After spraying, the skunk ambles off without hurrying.
