# One-legged thunder ox

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large other · hops · fantasy, myth*

A great grey ox, hornless, with a single leg that glows like the sun and moon. It emerges from the sea and hops across the land, and wherever it goes, storms follow. Its bellow is like thunder, and lightning flashes from its eyes.

It fights to the death. A legendary emperor is said to have killed one and made a war drum from its hide, whose sound could be heard for miles and struck fear into enemies. Few have hunted one since.
