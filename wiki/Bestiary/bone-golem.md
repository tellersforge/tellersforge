# Bone golem

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · large biped · walk · fantasy, horror*

A tall lattice of fused bones taken from many creatures, men and horses and beasts no one can name, bound together into one many-armed guardian. It rattles and clacks loudly as it moves, so there is no sneaking up on anyone, and no need: it has arms reaching in every direction.

It lashes out with all of them at once, clubbing and clawing everything around it. It is mindless and never flees. Blunt weapons shatter its joints far better than blades. Necromancers build them from the leftovers of their other work to guard their crypts, and the grim rattling in the dark is often the first sign of such a place.

## Not to be confused with

The *[Bone giant](bone-giant.md)*, a single huge skeleton raised as undead; the bone golem is a construct built from the bones of many creatures.
