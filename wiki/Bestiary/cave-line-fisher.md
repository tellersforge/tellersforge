# Cave line fisher

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium many-legged · climber · fantasy*

A crab-like cave dweller the size of a large dog, perched on high ledges in the dark. It fishes. From its body it fires a thin, sticky filament, which it lets hang or shoots down at passing prey. When the line touches something, the fisher reels it in, hand over claw, and snips it apart with its pincers up on the ledge.

Its filament is prized for bowstrings and fine rope, and some cave-dwellers hunt it for that. It flees when badly hurt. Explorers who feel something sticky across their face in a dark tunnel should cut first and look up second.
