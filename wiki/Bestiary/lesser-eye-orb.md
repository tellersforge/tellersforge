# Lesser eye orb

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · small other · hover · fantasy*

A fist-sized ball of rubbery flesh with one bulging eye, ringed by a few twitching stalks that each end in a smaller eye, and a slit of a mouth full of needle teeth. It floats at head height with no wings and no sound. Lesser orbs pester lone travellers like spiteful wasps: they dart in, stare, bite, and dart away again. Each little stalk throws a gaze that stings the skin like nettles.

They are cowardly and flee when badly hurt, but they remember faces and come back. Most are spawn from a much larger mother orb, and a scholar who finds several should look for the lair.
