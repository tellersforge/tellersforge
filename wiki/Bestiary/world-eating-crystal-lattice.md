# World-eating crystal lattice

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md) · gigantic flyer · flyer · sci-fi*

A vast crystal shaped like a snowflake, glittering in starlight, big enough to shade a city. It drifts through space from world to world, and it is beautiful right up to the moment it arrives.

It hangs above a living world and feeds on the living tissue of everything on its surface. Beams of light lance down from its points, and forests crumble to grey dust, herds fall where they stand, and the seas go still. It has no mind to bargain with and never turns back. Worlds that see it coming have one choice, to leave, and the empty planets it leaves behind are its only record.
