# Short-faced bear

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ursine](../Species_Families/ursine.md) · huge quadruped · fast runner · prehistoric, fantasy, historical*

A giant, long-limbed bear of the ice-age plains, towering over a horse when it rears, with a short, broad face and legs built for long strides. It does not creep; it roams, covering vast distances, and when it finds a kill made by wolves, cats or hunters, it simply walks in and takes it. Few beasts argue.

It is fast for its size and fights to the death. Hunters of the old plains fear it more than any cat, because it cannot be scared from a carcass and it can outrun a man. Some say it hunts too, running down horses and deer, but its favourite meal is someone else's work.
