# Radioactive sludge

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · medium amorphous · slow · post-apocalyptic, sci-fi, superhero*

A glowing green sludge, bubbling and oozing, spilled from broken drums and waste pits into sewers and craters. It hums faintly, and plants wither near it. It is not truly alive in the usual sense, but it moves, slowly, towards warmth.

It pools in low places and poisons everyone who comes near, sickening them over hours or days. It is mindless and never flees. Weapons do little. Survivors avoid it, cover it with earth and concrete, or seal it in lead. Scavengers learn to recognise its green glow and the dead ground around it.
