# Giant fire salamander

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A long, low lizard wrapped in a sheath of living flame, as big as a crocodile and a good deal more dangerous. Its skin is black and crusted where the fire gutters, and it moves through the lava tubes of volcanic mountains leaving scorched stone behind.

Anything that touches it burns: blades grow hot, shields blister, and a wrestler's grip is suicide. It lairs in the hottest caves and treats anyone who enters as prey. It fights to the death, and fire and heat do nothing to it. Those who must hunt one bring water, long spears and a very good reason.
