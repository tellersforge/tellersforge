# Sharabha

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · huge many-legged · leaps · fantasy, myth*

A monstrous hunter mightier than lions and elephants together, with eight powerful legs, heavy claws, great wings and a hooked raptor's head. It leaps over valleys in a single bound, landing on its prey with crushing weight.

It hunts lions for sport and fights them to the death. It is very hard to wound, and its hide turns spears. Some sages say it was made by a god to tame an even greater beast. Kings who see it in the sky send gifts to the temples and pray it passes by.
