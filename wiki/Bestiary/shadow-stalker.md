# Shadow stalker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · medium biped · walk · fantasy, horror*

A tall, thin thing made of darkness, with long arms and clawed hands. It only moves in darkness, and outside the light it is invisible.

It takes lone watchmen, sentries and travellers, killing them without a sound. It flees from light, and the best defence against it is to keep a fire or a lantern burning and never be alone.
