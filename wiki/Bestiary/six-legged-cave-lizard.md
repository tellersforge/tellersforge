# Six-legged cave-lizard

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · large many-legged · walk · fantasy, myth, horror*

A huge lizard with six legs and a frog's wide head and mouth. It lives in caves in riverbanks and crawls out at night to hunt. It swallows people whole, and nobody who is swallowed comes back.

It fights to the death. River peoples know its caves and avoid them, especially at night. Some brave warriors have killed one by luring it out with bait and attacking from above. It is slow on land but fast in the water.
