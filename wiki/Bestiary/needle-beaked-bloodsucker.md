# Needle-beaked bloodsucker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · tiny flyer · flyer · fantasy, horror*

A crow-sized pest with leathery bat wings, a furry body and a long, needle-like snout like a mosquito's. It lives in caves and hunts at dusk. It lands on a victim, latches on with its claws, pushes its snout in, and drinks blood until it can barely fly. Then it drops off and flutters clumsily away to digest.

It is a nuisance alone, and a real danger in groups. It flees when badly hurt. The wounds bleed long after, and often fester. Farmers near caves lose cattle to them, and travellers learn to sleep with their faces covered.
