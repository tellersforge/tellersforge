# Dim-witted dread beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · large quadruped · walk · sci-fi*

A ravenous, heavy-bodied predator with a huge mouth, stubby legs and tiny, suspicious eyes. It will eat anything, including its own kind, and never seems full. Its jaws are strong enough to crush a cart.

It is also astonishingly stupid. It believes that anything it cannot see also cannot see it, so a traveller who wraps a cloth around their own head becomes invisible to it, and it will wander off in confusion. It fights to the death if it does find something to fight. The trick is known across its world, and the peoples there tie a hood to every child's coat.
