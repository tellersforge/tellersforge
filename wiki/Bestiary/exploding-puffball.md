# Exploding puffball

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md) · small plant · rooted · fantasy*

A pale, swollen fungus the size of a large melon, thin-skinned and full of dark powder. It grows on damp cave floors and in rotting logs. The slightest touch, a boot, a spear-butt, a falling stone, bursts it in a thick, choking cloud of spores that blinds the eyes and fills the throat.

It does not hunt, but it doesn't need to. Clusters of puffballs line tunnels where other creatures wait for blinded, coughing prey. Miners throw stones ahead of them in unknown galleries, and cover their faces with wet cloth when they hear the soft thump of a burst.
