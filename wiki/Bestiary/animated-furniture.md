# Animated furniture

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium other · walk · fantasy, horror*

Wardrobes, chairs, tables and chests of drawers that wake up. A spell or a haunting stirs them, and they lurch across the floor on stiff legs, doors banging open and shut. Their favourite trick is to trap intruders: sliding across doorways, pinning people against walls, closing in from every side of a room.

They crush and batter rather than bite. They are mindless and never flee, and a heavy wardrobe is surprisingly hard to hurt. An axe does good work. Old houses, abandoned inns and wizards' towers are where they are found. Sometimes a single house wakes all at once, and the whole place becomes a trap.
