# Giant ground sloth

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md) · huge quadruped · slow · prehistoric, fantasy*

An elephant-sized sloth, shaggy and slow, with a heavy tail and enormous curved claws on its forelimbs. It walks on the sides of its feet with a rolling, shuffling gait and rears up on its hind legs, braced on its tail, to strip leaves and branches from the treetops. Its hide is studded with small bony nodules that make it tough to wound.

It is a placid browser and harmless unless pushed. When cornered, it rakes with those great claws, and the blow can open a horse from shoulder to hip. It flees when badly hurt. Hunters of its age find it an easy target and a huge reward, and they tend to forget that it can fight back.
