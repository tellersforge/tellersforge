# Bombardier beetle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium many-legged · walk · fantasy*

A beetle as big as a hound, with a bright orange and black shell and a rear end like a cannon. When menaced it swings about and fires a boiling, stinking spray with a loud crack, and the spray scalds skin, pits steel and blinds the unwary. Then it scurries away, since it would rather not fight at all.

It lives in dry woodland and among stones, eating smaller insects. Alchemists think its twin glands a marvel, since they mix two harmless liquids inside it to make something explosive, and many a laboratory fire began with someone trying to copy the trick. Hunters approach from the front, with shields raised, and try very hard not to go round the back.
