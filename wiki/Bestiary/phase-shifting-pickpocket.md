# Phase-shifting pickpocket

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · small many-legged · teleports · fantasy*

A spindly, many-legged creature the size of a cat, grey and half transparent, that lives with one foot in another world. It steps out of nowhere, snatches whatever shiny thing catches its eye, a ring, a coin, a jewelled dagger, and fades away again before anyone can grab it. It does not bite or fight if it can help it.

It flees at the first wound. Merchants and nobles tell of lost jewellery, and thieves use it as an excuse. Some wizards keep them as pets, and hunt out their hoards in the other world. Tying valuables down is the only real defence.
