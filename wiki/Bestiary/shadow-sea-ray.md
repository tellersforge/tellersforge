# Shadow sea ray

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · huge swimmer · swimmer, glider · fantasy, sci-fi*

A dark, wing-finned ray as broad as a ship's mainsail, velvet black above and pale beneath. It glides through the sea at night, so smooth and silent that it passes under ships without disturbing the water, and it can leap clear of the waves and sail through the air a short way.

It uses that leap to hunt. A deckhand on the night watch hears a soft rush, and something dark folds over them and slides back into the sea. By the time the alarm is raised the ray is gone. Crews in its waters keep lanterns burning and watch in pairs. Badly hurt, the ray glides off into the dark.
