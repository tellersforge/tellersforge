# Void crystal grazer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md) · large other · hover · sci-fi*

A slow, glowing cluster of living crystal, big as a cart, that floats through vacuum and soaks up starlight and radiation. It drifts across asteroid fields, turning slowly, its facets flashing as they catch the light.

It is harmless unless disturbed, but it rams intruders that get too close, and its crystal body is hard as stone. It has no mind and never flees. Miners prize its shed shards, which glow for years and fetch high prices. Some say it is the seed of a living world, and some mining crews follow them hoping to find a new vein.
