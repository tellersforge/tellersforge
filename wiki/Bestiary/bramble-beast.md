# Bramble beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy*

A wolf-sized tangle of briars and thorny stems that has knotted itself into the shape of an animal: four legs, a long body, a head of hooked branches with no eyes at all. It prowls overgrown ruins and old hedges, guarding a thicket as if it had been told to and has forgotten why. It moves with the creak and rustle of dry wood, and it rakes with thorns that leave long, ragged, stinging wounds.

It does not flee and cannot be scared off, but it rarely strays beyond its thicket. Woodcutters give it a wide berth, and a good fire, set on a dry day with the wind behind you, is the usual answer.
