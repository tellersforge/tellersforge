# Poison dart frog

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md) · tiny quadruped · hopper · fantasy, historical, modern*

A frog no bigger than a thumb, painted in colours no sensible animal would wear: jewel blue, flame orange, lemon yellow barred with black. The colours are a warning, and every bird and snake in the rainforest knows it. The frog's damp skin sweats a poison strong enough to kill whatever eats it, and it hops about in plain daylight with nothing to fear.

It is harmless unless handled. Forest hunters catch them with leaves wrapped round their hands, rub the tips of their blowgun darts across the frog's back, and let it go again. A traveller who picks one up out of curiosity and then rubs his eyes learns a hard lesson, if he lives to learn it.
