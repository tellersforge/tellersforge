# Corpse swarm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium swarm · walk · fantasy, horror*

A carpet of dead rats, crows, beetles and other vermin, moving as one hungry tide. It pours over the ground like a dark liquid, and wherever it passes, nothing is left but bones.

It swarms over the fallen, stripping flesh in minutes, and it will attack the living if they are wounded or trapped. It has no mind and never flees. Swords are useless against it. Fire, water and running are the best defences.
