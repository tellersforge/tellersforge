# Desert horned dragon

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · gigantic quadruped · burrower · sci-fi*

A colossal horned lizard of the deep desert that swims through sand the way a fish swims through water. Only a ripple in the dunes shows it coming. When it surfaces, it bursts up beneath a herd or caravan in a spray of sand, horns first, and the ground collapses into its gullet.

It sprays a caustic venom that stuns and softens prey before it swallows it whole. It fights to the death and is almost impossible to wound through its hide. Desert tribes hunt them anyway, because hard, lustrous pearls form in their gizzards and fetch fortunes in the cities. Most hunting parties never find one; the unlucky ones are found by one.
