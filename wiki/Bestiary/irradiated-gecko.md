# Irradiated gecko

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · small biped · fast runner · post-apocalyptic*

A lizard that learned to stand up. It reaches about to a grown man's knee, runs on two springy hind legs with its tail held out for balance, and chirps constantly to its packmates as it hops. Its skin is blotched with pale, sickly patches and it glows faintly in the dark after feeding near old reactors.

Packs bounce towards travellers from all sides, nipping and clawing at legs, more annoying than deadly on their own. They lose heart quickly, though: once a couple of their number go down, the rest scatter into the rubble, chirping indignantly. Waste-rangers treat them as vermin and a nuisance, and hungry ones as dinner, since the meat is edible if well cooked.
