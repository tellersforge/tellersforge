# Giant squid

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · huge swimmer · fast swimmer · fantasy, modern, historical*

A deep-sea squid far larger than a ship's boat, with eyes as wide as dinner plates and long feeding arms tipped with hooked, toothed suckers. It lives in the cold dark water where sunlight never reaches, and it rises at night to hunt.

It is fast and fierce. It battles whales in the deep, leaving round scars on their hides, and it will seize a small boat and try to drag it down. Its body is soft and its hold is strong, so the best defence is a sharp blade and a willingness to hack at the arms. Badly hurt, it jets away into the dark trailing ink.
