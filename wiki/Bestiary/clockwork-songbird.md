# Clockwork songbird

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · tiny flyer · flyer · fantasy, historical*

A jewelled mechanical bird the size of a finch, with enamelled feathers and ruby eyes, sitting on a perch in a golden cage. It sings beautifully. It also listens, and remembers every word spoken near it, and can repeat them later in the speaker's own voice.

Its owners set them in rivals' parlours as gifts, or send them flying out through windows to eavesdrop. At the first sign of a hand reaching for it, or the first scratch, it flees. Sensible courtiers keep quiet in rooms with pretty birds, and some keep a cat, which does not care whether a bird is made of gears.

## Not to be confused with
