# Magma basking lizard

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · huge quadruped · slow · fantasy*

A huge lizard crusted over with cooled rock, so that it looks like a boulder half sunk in the glow of a lava lake. It sleeps there for weeks, soaking in molten stone the way other beasts soak in a warm pool, and its breath comes out in slow, sulphurous puffs.

It is slow to wake and slow to move, but it wakes angry. Disturbed, it lifts its head and sprays molten rock across the cavern, then drags itself after whatever disturbed it. It does not give up. Miners and treasure hunters who go near the fire lakes learn to walk softly and to go around rocks that breathe.
