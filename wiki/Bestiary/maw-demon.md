# Maw demon

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md) · large other · slow · fantasy, horror, sci-fi*

A lump of reddish hide on stubby legs, and almost all of it is mouth. The jaws split the body nearly in two, with teeth like broken paving stones and a tongue that lolls out on the floor. It rolls and waddles forward, slowly, eating anything in reach: bodies, furniture, weapons, other demons.

It has no thought beyond hunger and never stops or flees. Its slowness is its weakness, and a nimble foe can dance around it. Its hide is thick and it swallows blades whole. In the lower realms maw demons are used as living rubbish pits and as guards set at narrow doors, since nothing gets past a mouth that fills the corridor.
