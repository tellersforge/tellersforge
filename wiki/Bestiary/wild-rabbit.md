# Wild rabbit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Lagomorph](../Species_Families/lagomorph.md) · tiny quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, historical, modern*

A small, grey-brown burrowing grazer with long ears, large eyes and a white scut that flashes as it runs. Rabbits live in warrens dug into grassy banks and hillsides, grazing at dawn and dusk and breeding with legendary speed. They are the commonest game of snare, ferret and hawk, and a staple in every poor family's pot.

At the first sight of danger a rabbit freezes, then bolts in a zigzag for the nearest burrow. It has no fight in it at all. Where rabbits multiply unchecked they can strip a country bare, and farmers wage long wars against them. In folk tales they are tricksters, messengers and bringers of luck.
