# One-eyed secret-eater

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md) · medium biped · walk · sentient · fantasy, horror*

A hunched, one-eyed wreck of what was once a spellcaster, twisted by a curse into something grey, withered and rotting. Its single eye is huge and red-rimmed, and its gaze tears secrets out of minds: names, passwords, hidden places, the things people most want to keep. It feeds on them. It prowls ruined libraries and abandoned towers, always hungry for more.

It can be bargained with. It trades secrets it has stolen for secrets it hasn't, and many scholars have gone to it for that. It flees when badly hurt. Those who meet its eye should expect it to know more about them afterward than their friends do.
