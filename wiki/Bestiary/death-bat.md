# Death bat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Chiropteran](../Species_Families/chiropteran.md) · large winged biped · flyer · fantasy, myth, horror*

A bat the size of a man, with a blade-like leaf-nose, leathery wings and long, hooked fangs. It lives in the caves of the underworld and in the dark hollows that lead down to it. It flies in darkness, and brings more darkness with it, snuffing out torches as it swoops.

It beheads intruders with a slash of its nose-blade and drinks their blood. It fights to the death in its own caves. Explorers who go into deep caverns carry many lights and keep their heads low. Some peoples make offerings to it before entering the underworld, hoping to pass unharmed.
