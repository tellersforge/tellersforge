# Night-hunting bone flyer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · medium winged biped · flyer · sci-fi, horror*

A pale, winged hunter with bone-white skin, leathery wings and a long, blade-edged head like a hatchet. It sleeps underground in huge colonies until darkness falls, and on worlds where night is rare it waits for eclipses.

When darkness comes, they swarm out of the ground in their thousands, hunting by echo and cutting down anything in the open. In darkness, they fight to the death. Bright light burns and scatters them, and the peoples of their world keep fires, lamps and flares ready for every nightfall. A settlement that runs out of light before dawn is rarely found again.
