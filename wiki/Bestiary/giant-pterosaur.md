# Giant pterosaur

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · huge flyer · flyer · prehistoric, fantasy*

A flying reptile as tall as a giraffe when it stands on the ground, with wings wider than a small ship's sails and a beak like a spear. It flies rarely and for long distances, but it hunts on foot, stalking across plains and riverbanks on all four limbs, folded wings used as front legs, and it swallows small prey whole.

Wanderers who stray into its time are small prey. It is terrifying on the ground, because it moves quickly and its beak reaches far, and it flees when badly hurt only after a hard fight. Its bones are hollow and light, and its wing skin is tough enough for sails. Those who hunt it need nets, many spears and a good hiding place.
