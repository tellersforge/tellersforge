# Common raven

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · tiny flyer · flyer · fantasy, historical, modern, horror, myth*

A big, glossy black bird with a heavy beak, shaggy throat feathers and a deep croaking voice. Ravens live everywhere from deserts to arctic cliffs, and they are among the cleverest of birds, solving puzzles, remembering faces and holding grudges. They follow wolves, hunters and armies, waiting to feast on what is left.

Ravens can mimic voices, barking dogs and even human words, which does nothing to help their reputation. Many peoples see them as messengers of gods, spies of witches or heralds of battle. They are wary and quick, and flee at the first wound, but they remember who hurt them.
