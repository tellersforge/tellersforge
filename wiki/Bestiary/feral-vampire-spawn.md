# Feral vampire spawn

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium biped · climber · fantasy, horror*

A bloodsucker turned without a master's care, bestial and half-mad with hunger. Its skin is grey and stretched tight over its bones, its eyes are red and its teeth are long and sharp.

It swarms from ceilings and drinks the wounded, and packs of them can overwhelm small villages. It flees from fire and sunlight, and hides in caves, cellars and ruins by day. Hunters who know its ways carry torches and stakes, and they strike at dawn.
