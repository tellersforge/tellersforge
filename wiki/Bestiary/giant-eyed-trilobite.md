# Giant eyed trilobite

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · gigantic many-legged · walk · sci-fi, post-apocalyptic*

A colossal armoured insect, big as a hill, with a segmented shell, rows of eyes along its front and countless legs beneath. It is peaceful, grazing on plants and fungi in vast, slow herds, and its eyes glow a calm blue.

When its young are hurt, the eyes turn red, and it stampedes in rage. It fights to the death when enraged, and herds can flatten cities when roused. It can also sense the thoughts of others. The peoples who live near them learn to respect them, and some say they can be calmed by someone who listens.
