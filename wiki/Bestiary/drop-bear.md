# Drop bear

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Marsupial](../Species_Families/marsupial.md) · small quadruped · climber · fantasy, myth, modern*

A heavy, carnivorous marsupial, like a koala but bigger, stockier and with a mouth full of sharp teeth. It lives in gum trees and waits in the branches for travellers to pass below. Then it drops onto their heads, biting and clawing.

It flees when badly hurt and scrambles back up the tree. Locals warn visitors to watch out for them, and recommend smearing paste behind the ears or speaking with a local accent to keep them away. Visitors who believe this are much loved by locals.
