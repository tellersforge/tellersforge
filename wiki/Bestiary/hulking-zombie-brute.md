# Hulking zombie brute

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · large biped · walk · horror, modern, post-apocalyptic, fantasy*

A mutated corpse swollen with muscle, twice the size of a man and big enough to overturn a cart. One arm is often grotesquely larger than the other, and its skin is stretched over lumps of growth that should not be there.

It charges the strongest fighter in any group, knocking them flat, and it hurls rubble, barrels and even bodies at anyone out of reach. It has no mind and never flees. It takes a lot of killing, and the best hope is to scatter, keep it busy with several fighters and hit it from behind.

## Not to be confused with

the *[zombie ogre](zombie-ogre.md)*, the risen corpse of a real ogre or troll; the zombie brute is a human corpse mutated into a giant.
