# Giant tarantula

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · huge many-legged · walk · fantasy, modern, horror*

A hairy spider as big as a haywain, which belongs to the drive-in horror tales of irradiated deserts and military men shouting into radios. It is slow and heavy, and it does not need to be quick. When something approaches it rubs its back legs and flings off clouds of barbed hairs that sting eyes and throats and set skin burning, then lumbers forward to crush and bite.

Its body is so huge that ordinary weapons seem to go in and do little. Towns in those stories deal with it by the truckload: fuel, dynamite, planes. Anyone with less is best advised to get upwind, keep their eyes shut against the hairs and drive very fast. It will not stop coming while it lives.
