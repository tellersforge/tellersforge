# Sidehill gouger

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, modern*

A goat-like hill beast with shaggy grey fur and legs noticeably shorter on one side than the other. This suits it perfectly on steep ground, where it trots round and round a hillside in one direction, wearing deep paths into the slope. It cannot turn about, and on flat ground it topples over and lies helplessly bleating.

There are said to be left-handed and right-handed gougers, and when two meet on the same path one must go all the way round the hill backwards. It is harmless unless startled, when it may butt with its horns, and it flees at the first wound. Hill farmers point to terraced slopes as proof.
