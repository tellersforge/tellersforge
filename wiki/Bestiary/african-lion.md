# African lion

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern, myth*

A great tawny cat of the grasslands, the males crowned with heavy manes of gold or black. Lions are the only truly social cats: a pride of related females and their cubs hunts together, fanning out through the long grass to drive a herd towards others lying in ambush. The males guard the territory, roar across the plain at dusk and eat first.

Lions spend most of the day asleep in the shade, which makes them look lazier than they are. Cornered, a lion fights with terrible power; otherwise, once badly hurt, it turns away. Herders build thorn fences and keep fires burning, and kings everywhere have borrowed the lion for their banners.
