# Reticulated python

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · large serpentine · slow · fantasy, historical, modern*

A huge, patterned constrictor, longer than a boat and patterned in a net of gold, black and brown that disappears into leaf litter. Reticulated pythons lie in wait near water and game trails in hot forests, sometimes for weeks, and they are also at home in drains and rafters near villages, where rats and chickens abound.

When prey passes, the python strikes with backward-curving teeth, then throws coil after coil around it and squeezes until the heart stops. It swallows its meal whole, and the largest can take a deer or, rarely, a person. A python that is badly hurt slides away; it has no venom and relies entirely on surprise.
