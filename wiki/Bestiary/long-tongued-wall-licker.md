# Long-tongued wall licker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md) · medium quadruped · climber · sci-fi, horror*

A skinless, mutated thing the size of a man, with red, raw muscle, an exposed brain bulging from its skull, huge hooked claws, and a whip-like tongue as long as a spear. It crawls on walls and ceilings as easily as on floors. It is blind, and hunts by sound, turning its head toward every footstep and breath.

It fights to the death. The tongue lashes out and pierces or grabs from far away, and the claws finish the job. Those who survive it learn to walk slowly, breathe quietly, and never make a sound when one is near.
