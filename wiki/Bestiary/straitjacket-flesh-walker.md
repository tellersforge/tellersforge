# Straitjacket flesh walker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md) · medium biped · walk · horror*

An armless, faceless body, its own skin wrapped around it like a straitjacket, the seams pulled tight. It staggers and lurches as it walks, drawn to light. When it reaches its victim, its chest splits open and it sprays a burning, caustic mist.

It is mindless and never flees. It is slow, and it can be outpaced, but it keeps coming. Those who have fought one say it is hard to look at, and harder to forget. The best defence is to put out the lights and stay very still.

## Not to be confused with
