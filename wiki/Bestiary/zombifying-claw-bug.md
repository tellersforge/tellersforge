# Zombifying claw bug

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium many-legged · fast runner · sci-fi, horror*

A fast, many-limbed bug about the size of a large dog, with a dark, glossy shell and long, bladed forelimbs. It races out of cover, slashes at its prey and, in the same stroke, injects its young into the wound.

Within hours, victims rise as mindless, shambling husks, wandering aimlessly until they burst open to release a new brood. The bug has no mind and never flees. Colonies infested with them are usually burned, and any survivor with a fresh slash is watched closely by friends who keep their weapons near. Rumours of a cure circulate on every frontier. None has yet been proved.
