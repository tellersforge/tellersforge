# Acid-spitting swamp squid

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · medium quadruped · walk · sci-fi, horror*

A squat, heavy-bodied swamp beast that walks on two thick legs, with a head like a squid's: a fringe of short tentacles hanging over a hidden mouth, and small, wet eyes. It wades through bogs and flooded ruins, and when intruders come near, it gobs a thick stream of acid at them from a distance. If they keep coming, it closes and whips them with its long, heavy tail.

It is territorial rather than hungry, and flees when badly hurt. Its acid pits metal and burns skin. Swamp folk learn to hear its gurgling croak, and to back away slowly rather than run.
