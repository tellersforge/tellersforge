# Leathery wasteland flyer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Chiropteran](../Species_Families/chiropteran.md) · small flyer · flyer · sci-fi, post-apocalyptic*

A pale, eyeless, bat-like thing with leathery wings as wide as a man's arms and a small hooked mouth. It roosts by the hundred in desert cliffs and ruined towers, hanging in dark crevices through the heat of the day. At dusk the flocks pour out, screeching, and dive on anything weak, wounded or slow, picking and tearing and flapping away again.

One alone flees at the first wound and is little threat. A flock is different. Caravans keep the sick, the old and the young in the middle, and fire into the flock to scatter it. Their droppings, scraped from cliff bases, are sold as fertiliser and for making powder.
