# Giant ape

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md) · large biped · walk · prehistoric, fantasy, modern*

A great ape half again the size of a gorilla, with dark fur, a massive chest and arms that can snap saplings. It lives in the deepest bamboo forests and mountain jungles, moving in small family groups led by an old male, feeding on shoots, leaves and fruit.

When intruders come, the male rises, roars and beats his chest in warning, and if that fails he charges, slamming and biting. It flees when badly hurt. People of the mountain forests leave it offerings and speak of it as a forest guardian, and some say it can be reasoned with, if you know how to sit quietly and look at the ground.
