# Faceless chaos-beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · large other · walk · fantasy, myth*

A plump, sack-like body, red as fire and yellow as a harvest moon, with six legs, four wings and no face at all. It has no eyes, ears, nose or mouth, yet it dances and sings without purpose, turning in circles in wild, empty places. It embodies the chaos that came before the world had order.

It shuns the virtuous and drifts towards the wicked, whom it seems to find good company. Its presence confuses the mind, and those who stand near it forget what they came to do. It is mindless, never flees and is very hard to hurt, so most people simply walk away.
