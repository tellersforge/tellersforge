# Spore-ridden zombie

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md) · medium biped · walk · horror, post-apocalyptic, sci-fi, fantasy*

A body overgrown by a fungus that splits its face open and hardens into plates across its head, steered by the mould growing inside it. It no longer sees, but it hunts by sound, making a clicking noise that echoes off walls and helps it find its prey.

When it dies, it releases a cloud of spores that can infect anyone who breathes them. It has no mind and never flees. Survivors move quietly, cover their mouths and burn the bodies. Those who are infected are rarely told what is happening to them until it is too late.
