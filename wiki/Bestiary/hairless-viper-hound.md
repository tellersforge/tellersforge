# Hairless viper hound

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · climber · sci-fi*

A six-legged, hairless pack hunter with slick grey skin, needle teeth and grasping, almost hand-like paws. It lives in dense jungle and hunts by night, seeing clearly in the dark.

Packs circle their prey, climb trees to strike from above, and attack from all sides at once. They fight as long as the pack fights. Jungle travellers keep fires burning through the night and sleep in shifts. Their howls, high and wavering, are the sound of nightfall in the deep forest.
