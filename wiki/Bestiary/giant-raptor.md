# Giant raptor

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · large biped · fast runner · prehistoric, fantasy*

A sickle-clawed hunter as long as a horse, heavy-bodied and powerful, with killing claws the size of daggers on its feet. It leaps onto large prey, grips with its hands and rakes with its feet, opening flanks and bellies. It is heavy enough to bring down beasts many times its own size.

It hunts in packs and fights while the pack fights. Among its kind it is the heavy fighter, trading cleverness for weight and power. Stone-age bands find it harder to outrun than a tyrant and harder to outfight than a small raptor, and nowhere safe from it but high ground and deep water.
