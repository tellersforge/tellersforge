# Giant bat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Chiropteran](../Species_Families/chiropteran.md) · large flyer · flyer · fantasy, horror*

A cave bat with a wingspan wider than a cart, furred in brown or grey, with leathery wings, huge ears and a pushed-up snout. At dusk whole flocks pour out of cave mouths like smoke. They hunt by sound in total darkness, crying out and listening to the echoes, and they bite and flit away before anyone can strike.

Most feed on large insects, fruit and birds, but a giant bat will not turn down a sheep or a sleeping traveller. Their caves are knee-deep in droppings, which gunpowder makers dig out by the cartload. People who must pass through such caves go at noon, when the bats sleep, with cloths over their faces and very quiet feet.
