# Snapping turtle

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · small quadruped · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A heavy, mud-coloured turtle with a rough shell, a long neck, a thick tail and a hooked beak like a pair of shears. Snapping turtles lie half buried in the mud of ponds and slow rivers, sometimes with moss growing on their backs, waiting for fish, frogs or ducklings to come within reach.

They are not aggressive in the water and usually sink away from swimmers. On land, or when trodden on, they turn and snap with startling reach and speed, and that beak can take a finger or a toe. Children who wade in the shallows learn quickly to shuffle their feet. People who catch them for soup learn to hold them by the tail.
