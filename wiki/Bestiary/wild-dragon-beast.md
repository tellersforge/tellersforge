# Wild dragon beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · huge winged quadruped · flyer · fantasy*

A full-grown dragon with the body of a legend and the mind of an animal. It is as large and strong as any of the great, speaking dragons, but there is nothing behind its eyes except hunger and the fierce sense that everything it can see belongs to it. It claims a valley, a mountain or a stretch of coast and burns whatever challenges it.

It cannot be bargained with or flattered. It can only be avoided, driven off or killed. It sleeps on a hoard gathered for no reason it understands, and near that hoard it fights to the death. Kingdoms raise armies against a wild dragon and still count it a good year if half come home.
