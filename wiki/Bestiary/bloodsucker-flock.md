# Bloodsucker flock

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · large swarm · flyer · fantasy, horror*

A whole cave colony of needle-beaked bloodsuckers rising at once, a screeching, flapping cloud of wings and snouts. The flock settles on one target at a time, covers it completely, and drains it. A man disappears under a heaving blanket of bodies. A horse falls in the time it takes to saddle another.

It flees when badly hurt, but it is hard to hurt. Fire, smoke and noise scatter it, and cave-dwellers keep smudge fires going at dusk. Those caught in the open throw a cloak over their head and run for water.
