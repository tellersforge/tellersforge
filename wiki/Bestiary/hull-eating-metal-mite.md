# Hull-eating metal mite

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · tiny swarm · climber · sci-fi*

A swarm of tiny, shelled creatures, each smaller than a grain of rice, that live in vacuum and feed on starship alloys. They cluster on hulls like a spreading patch of rust, secreting acid and chewing through the softened metal.

An infested ship can lose its hull within days, and crews often do not notice until the air starts to leak. The swarm is mindless and never flees; it simply keeps eating. Spacers inspect their hulls after passing through asteroid fields and burn off any suspect patches with torches. Some dock masters turn away any ship that has been near an infested wreck.
