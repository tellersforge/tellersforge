# Weeping fire jelly

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · huge flyer · flyer · fantasy*

A great white jelly shaped like a floating cube, big as a house, drifting high over the fire-lands with long tendrils trailing beneath it. On one face is something like a child's face, eyes shut and mouth open, and it weeps and wails without end. The sound carries for miles over the ash plains and gets under the skin like a toothache.

When it notices something moving below, it stops crying just long enough to spit a ball of fire, then drifts on, sobbing. It fights from afar and flees when badly hurt, rising out of reach. Travellers in the burning country cross in the open only when they can't hear it, and take cover behind rock when they can.
