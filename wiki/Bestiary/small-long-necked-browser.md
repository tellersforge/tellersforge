# Small long-necked browser

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · huge quadruped · walk · prehistoric, fantasy*

A long-necked dinosaur smaller and earlier than the great giants, but still as heavy as several elephants, with a thick neck and a tail that ends in a whip. It moves in herds through forests and river valleys, browsing leaves and ferns.

It flees from predators, since its size is no longer enough to keep it safe. When caught, it whips its tail, and the blow can break bones. It flees when badly hurt. Predators of its age hunt it by separating stragglers, and people follow herds to scavenge the kills.
