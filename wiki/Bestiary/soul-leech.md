# Soul leech

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md) · small serpentine · hover · fantasy, horror*

A translucent, eel-like thing as long as an arm, with a round sucking mouth and no eyes, drifting through the air as if through water. Its body glows a faint sickly blue, and the glow brightens as it feeds. It comes from the lower planes and is drawn to death the way flies are drawn to rot.

It fastens onto the dying and sucks at the soul itself, leaving nothing to pass on and nothing to raise. Wounded soldiers on a battlefield are its favourite meal. It has no fear and no sense; it will stay latched on until killed. Field healers who know of them keep a torch and a knife ready, and check every fallen body before walking on.
