# Livestock-eating waterbird

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · large flyer · swimmer · fantasy, myth*

A diving bird the size of a rowing boat, with a long neck, a heavy dagger of a beak and slick black-and-white plumage. It lives on highland lochs and cold lakes, and its booming, wailing cry carries for miles over the water at dusk. It hunts from the water's edge, surging out of the shallows to seize sheep, calves and dogs and drag them under.

Shepherds keep their flocks well back from the shore and listen for the cry. It can fly, though clumsily, and prefers to swim. Once badly hurt, it dives and does not come up where anyone is looking. Its feathers are prized as waterproof cloak-trim, when anyone can get them.
