# Polar bear

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ursine](../Species_Families/ursine.md) · large quadruped · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A vast white bear of sea ice and frozen coasts, the largest meat-eater that walks on land. Its fur hides black skin, its paws are broad as shovels, and it swims for days between floes. The polar bear hunts seals with enormous patience, waiting motionless beside a breathing hole in the ice for hours until a head appears.

Unlike most bears, it will hunt people. To a polar bear a traveller on the ice is simply meat that has not yet noticed it. Its fur and fat shrug off both cold and blows, and a starving one fights to the death. Northern peoples keep dogs to warn of its approach and treat it with a respect close to worship.
