# Plasma-spitting hive bug

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · huge many-legged · slow · sci-fi*

A slow, bloated artillery beast of the hive, huge and swollen, its back a glowing blister of chemicals. It barely moves, squatting deep in the hive's defences and stamping at anything that strays near its legs.

Its purpose is the sky. It hunches, strains and hurls a searing bolt of plasma upward, high enough to strike ships in orbit. A field of them can close the heavens over a hive world. It is mindless and never flees, and its hide shrugs off small arms. Commanders who want to land troops send bombers after them first, and pilots count every one they see.
