# Duck-billed dinosaur

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · huge biped · walk · prehistoric, fantasy*

A large, crested plant-eater with a flat, duck-like beak, strong hind legs and a hollow crest on its head. It moves in herds through forests and river plains, grazing and browsing, and it honks warnings through its crest, a deep booming sound that carries for miles.

When danger comes, it bellows alarm calls and stampedes. The sound can deafen and stun those too close. It flees at the first wound. Travellers learn its calls, since they warn of predators, and they learn to get out of the way when a herd begins to run.
