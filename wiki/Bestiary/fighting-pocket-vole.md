# Fighting pocket vole

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md) · tiny quadruped · climber · sci-fi*

A small, bristly rodent no bigger than a fist, with stiff grey fur, bright black eyes and teeth that never stop growing. It breeds in wall spaces and ducts of stations and ships, and gnaws through wiring, insulation and food stores with equal enthusiasm.

It is timid alone and flees when badly hurt, but it bites hard when cornered. Gamblers on rough stations catch the fiercest ones and pit them against each other in small arenas, and a champion vole can make its owner rich. Engineers loathe them, because a single breeding pair can bring down a ship's systems within a season.
