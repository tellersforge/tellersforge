# Infernal boar

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, horror, myth*

A boar the size of a draught horse, with iron bristles that glow red at the tips and tusks like blackened scythe blades. Its breath scorches grass, its hooves leave smoking prints, and a burning smell hangs over everywhere it has been. It comes up from the pit through cracks in the earth, often in the wake of a demonic invasion.

It charges through flame without flinching, rooting up fields and wrecking villages, and it fights to the death. Hunting one is a matter of spears, nets, and a long ditch of water between the hunters and the boar. The tusks and bristles are prized by smiths who work with fire, and the hide makes coats that never burn.
