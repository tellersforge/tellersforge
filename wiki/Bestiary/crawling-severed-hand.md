# Crawling severed hand

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · tiny other · climber · fantasy, horror*

A dead hand, cut off at the wrist, that scuttles about on its fingers like a pale spider. It is silent and quick, and it can climb walls, crawl under doors and hide in the smallest spaces.

It lurks under beds, in drawers and behind curtains, and when the house is asleep it creeps up to a sleeper and grips their throat until they stop breathing. It has no mind and never flees. The only defence is to find it before it finds you, and the best way to destroy it is to burn it. Some say it seeks the rest of its body.
