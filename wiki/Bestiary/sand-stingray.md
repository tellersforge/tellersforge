# Sand stingray

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · medium swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A flat, round-winged ray that lies buried in the sand of warm shallows with only its eyes showing. Stingrays feed on shellfish and worms and want nothing to do with people. The danger is stepping on one: the ray lashes up its tail, driving a serrated, venomous barb into the foot or leg.

The sting is savagely painful, and the barb can break off in the wound and fester. Beachcombers and fishermen learn to shuffle their feet as they wade so that the rays feel them coming and glide away. A hurt stingray flees at once into deeper water.
