# Killer whale

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · huge swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A huge black-and-white toothed whale with a towering dorsal fin, the greatest hunter in the sea. Killer whales live in close family pods led by old females, and each pod has its own calls, habits and favourite prey. Some hunt fish; others hunt seals, sharks and even great whales.

Their methods are cunning. A pod will swim together to raise a wave that washes a seal off an ice floe, or take turns exhausting a whale calf. They find prey in the dark by clicking and listening to the echoes. Sailors call them wolves of the sea, and a pod that has turned on a small boat fights as one. Mostly, though, they ignore people entirely.
