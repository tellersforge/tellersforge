# World-shambling hunter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · large biped · teleports · fantasy, horror*

A gaunt, ape-like hunter taller than a man, with loose, wrinkled skin and long claws. It walks between worlds. It steps out of nothing, grabs someone, and steps back out, and the victim vanishes with it in mid-scream. Where they go, no one knows.

It flees when badly hurt, back into the gaps. Some sorcerers can summon it and bind it to hunt their enemies, but it is a dangerous servant and turns on masters who show weakness. Those who have seen it describe a smell of something burned, and a grey space behind it that isn't there.
