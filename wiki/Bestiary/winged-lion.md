# Winged lion

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large winged quadruped · flyer · fantasy, myth, historical*

A great maned lion with a pair of eagle's wings rising from its shoulders, broad enough to shade a courtyard. It hunts from the sky, dropping onto deer and cattle with its claws spread, and it is also a guardian: it keeps watch over temples, tombs and holy mountains, and drives off anyone who comes there without the right.

Cities carve it over their gates and stitch it on their banners as a sign of watchfulness and strength. Real winged lions are rarer than their statues and more dangerous, but they are not reckless; one badly hurt will beat its wings and climb away. Priests sometimes claim to speak with them. The lions are not known to answer.
