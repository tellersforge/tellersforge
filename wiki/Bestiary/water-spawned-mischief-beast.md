# Water-spawned mischief beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · small biped · walk · horror, modern*

A scaly, big-eared little monster the size of a child, all grin and claw and wicked red eyes. Where it came from, no one agrees. What is known is that water makes it multiply: splash one and it buds off copies in a boiling of wet lumps. Feed one after dark and it turns vicious. Bright light burns its skin like fire.

It loves to wreck things, machines most of all, and it cackles while it works. A town with a few of them can be in ruins by morning. It flees when badly hurt. Those who have dealt with them keep water covered, lights bright, and never, ever give them food at night.
