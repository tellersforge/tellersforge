# Fungal colossus

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md) · huge biped · slow · fantasy, horror*

A towering mass of fused mushrooms, shelf fungus and thick white mycelium, shaped roughly like a giant and walking the deep caverns. Its steps shake loose dust and spores from the ceiling, and it leaves a trail of fresh growth behind it wherever it goes. It has no face, only a crown of pale caps swaying high above.

It is not cruel and not hungry in any way a person would recognise. It simply moves, and crushes what is in the way. Underground peoples treat it like weather, clearing its paths and waiting for it to pass. Some worship it, and plant their dead in its trail.
