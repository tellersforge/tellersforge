# Giant mutant salamander

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md) · gigantic quadruped · walk, swimmer · modern, sci-fi, post-apocalyptic*

A slimy, black-spotted salamander grown as long as a city block, bred from the chemical sludge and poisoned water of an industrial river. Its skin oozes, its wide mouth gapes, and wherever it crawls it leaves a trail of stinking slime.

It crawls out of polluted rivers and wanders through towns, eating whatever it can catch. It is almost impossible to kill: legs lost to shellfire grow back in hours, and wounds close as soon as they are made. It has no fear and never flees. The only lasting answer anyone has found is to clean the river, and that takes years.
