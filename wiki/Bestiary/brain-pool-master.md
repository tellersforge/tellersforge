# Brain pool master

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · huge amorphous · slow · sentient · fantasy, sci-fi, horror*

A vast, wrinkled brain lying in a pool of dark, briny slime deep beneath the earth. It never moves. It doesn't need to. It is the centre and memory of a whole colony of mind-eaters, and its thoughts reach for miles through the rock, speaking in every head at once and holding the minds of hundreds of thralls.

It is patient, proud and very old. It bargains with those who come to it, trading knowledge for minds and service, and it always knows more than it says. It fights to the death when threatened, through every mind it holds. Killing it is a victory that leaves a colony screaming.
