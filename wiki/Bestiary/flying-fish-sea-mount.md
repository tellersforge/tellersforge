# Flying-fish sea mount

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · large swimmer · swimmer · sci-fi*

A fin-winged sea beast, sleek and silver-blue, with a powerful tail and long, wing-like fins. It skims the waves at speed and glides in short hops over the water, carrying a rider on its back.

It is fierce and hard to break, and only the bravest riders can tame one. It bites if mishandled, and flees when badly hurt. Sea peoples ride them in races and battles, and a tamed one is a prize worth fighting for. Those who fall from one at speed rarely forget it.
