# Ox-headed spider

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · huge many-legged · walk · fantasy, myth, horror*

A giant beast with an ox's horned head set on the body of a monstrous spider, grey and dripping with seawater. It haunts rocky shores and rises from the surf to seize fishermen and drag them down onto the rocks, where it devours them.

Its bite is venomous and its hide is hard to cut. It fights to the death. Fishing villages keep watch at the tide line, and when it appears, everyone runs. Some say it serves a sea woman who lures men to the water's edge. Either way, it is best not to fish alone.
