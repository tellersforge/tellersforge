# Dromedary camel

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, historical, modern*

A tall, long-legged desert beast with a humped back, a curved neck and thick lips. Camels can go days without water and can drink enormous amounts when they find it. They carry heavy loads across deserts that would kill a horse, and nomads ride them, milk them and weave their hair.

Camels are stubborn and bad-tempered. They spit, bite and kick when annoyed, and a bite can crush an arm. Their broad feet walk easily on sand, and their bushy lashes and closing nostrils keep out the dust. A badly hurt camel flees.
