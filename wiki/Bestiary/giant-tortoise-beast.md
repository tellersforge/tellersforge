# Giant tortoise-beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · huge quadruped · slow · prehistoric, fantasy*

A land tortoise the size of a small hut, with a high domed shell, a horned head like a bull's and a spiked club at the end of its tail. It moves slowly across grasslands and scrub, grazing low plants, and it withdraws into its shell when threatened.

When cornered, it bites with its heavy beak and swings its tail. It fights while it can, but mostly it waits for the danger to go away. Explorers hunt it for meat and use its shell as a shelter or a boat.
