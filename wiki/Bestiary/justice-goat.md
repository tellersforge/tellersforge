# Justice goat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A one-horned goat, or a goat with something of a lion about its mane, that lives in courts of law. When two parties bring a dispute before a judge, it looks at each in turn, then lowers its horn and gores the guilty one. It is never wrong.

Judges keep it as an honour and a threat. It is calm and patient until it charges, and it fights to the death if attacked. Liars are nervous in its presence, and many cases are settled before the goat is even brought in. Its image is embroidered on the robes of judges to this day.
