# Leech swarm

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · small swarm · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern, post-apocalyptic, horror*

A writhing mass of dark, slimy worms that lives in stagnant ponds, flooded ditches and jungle swamps. Each leech has a sucker at either end and a mouth that numbs the skin as it bites, so waders rarely feel them. They cling to legs, slip under clothing and fill themselves with blood before dropping off.

The swarm has no fear and no mind; it simply keeps feeding. A single leech is disgusting but harmless; dozens can leave a person faint and pale, and the wounds they leave bleed for hours and often fester. Soldiers crossing swamps check each other at every halt and burn the leeches off with coals or salt.
