# Bull moose

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, historical, modern*

A towering, long-legged deer with a humped shoulder, a drooping nose, a dewlap of skin under its throat and huge palm-shaped antlers. Moose live in northern forests and bogs, wading into lakes to eat water plants and browsing willow and birch. They are solitary and seem lazy, but they are strong swimmers and can run faster than a horse through deep snow.

A moose is more dangerous than most bears. In the rut, or when hungry, harried or guarding a calf, it charges without warning, stamping with its front hooves. Its thick hide shrugs off blows. People in moose country give them a wide berth, and dogs make them worse.
