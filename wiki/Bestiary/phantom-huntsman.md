# Phantom huntsman

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · large other · flyer · sentient · myth, fantasy, horror*

A ghostly rider leading a hunt across the storm clouds, with a pack of spectral hounds baying at his heels. His horse is black, his cloak streams behind him, and his horn sounds like thunder.

He rides on stormy nights, and he sweeps up anyone caught outdoors, carrying them off to join his hunt. He fights to the death. Those who hear his horn should get indoors and bar the door, and never look up.
