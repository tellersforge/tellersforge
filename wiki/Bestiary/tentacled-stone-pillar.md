# Tentacled stone pillar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md) · large other · slow · fantasy*

What looks like a stalagmite rising from the cave floor, damp and grey. Then a single eye opens, and a jagged maw splits the stone below it. From its sides whip out long, sticky strands that catch whatever they touch. Wherever the strands cling, strength drains away, and the victim is dragged slowly into the mouth.

It fights to the death, and is very hard to kill. Many travellers have leaned against one to rest. Old cavers say to tap every stalagmite with a spear-butt before sitting down, and to cut the strands, not pull them.
