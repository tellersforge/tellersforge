# Colossal sun moth

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · gigantic flyer · flyer · modern, sci-fi, myth*

A moth so large that its wings could shade a city district, patterned in brilliant gold, orange and deep brown. When it flies, the beat of its wings raises gales that strip roofs and toss vehicles, and a glittering dust of scales falls behind it.

That dust stings. It burns the skin, the eyes and the lungs, and whole armies have been driven back by a single pass overhead. Many who live in its lands believe it guards them, and it does fight other giant beasts that threaten the country. It does not want to kill people, but it will not hold back. Badly hurt, it rises and flies away.
