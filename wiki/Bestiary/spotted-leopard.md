# Spotted leopard

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · medium quadruped · climber · fantasy, historical, modern*

A lithe, spotted cat with a long tail and pale eyes, strong enough to carry a kill heavier than itself up a tree. Leopards live almost everywhere there is cover: forest, rocky hills, scrub and the edges of villages. They are secretive and nocturnal, and their rosetted coat makes them nearly invisible in dappled shade.

A leopard stalks to within a few paces, pounces, and then drags its prey into the branches, where lions and hyenas cannot follow. Herders lose goats and dogs to them; now and then an old or injured leopard turns to people. Most never see one at all, only the half-eaten kill wedged in a fork above the path.
