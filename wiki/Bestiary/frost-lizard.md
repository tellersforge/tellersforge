# Frost lizard

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy*

A lean, white-scaled lizard the size of a large dog, built for the glaciers. Its blood never freezes, its feet grip ice like spikes, and its breath is a bitter mist that numbs whatever it touches and glazes skin and fur in frost.

It hunts on the ice floes and along frozen shores, breathing over fish holes and seal pups until they are stiff, then eating at leisure. People of the far north treat it as they would a wolf: a real danger to the lost and the lone, not a monster to go looking for. Badly hurt, it scrambles away across the ice faster than anyone can follow.

## Not to be confused with
