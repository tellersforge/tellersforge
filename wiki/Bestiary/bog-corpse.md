# Bog corpse

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium biped · slow · fantasy, horror, historical, myth*

A body tanned black and leathery by the peat of the marsh, its skin wrinkled and its hair a dull red. It lies under the surface for years, waiting, preserved by the acid of the bog.

When the living come close, it rises and grabs them, pulling them down into the peat and holding them there until they stop struggling. It has no mind and never flees. Those crossing the marsh keep to the known paths, test the ground with a pole and never walk alone.
