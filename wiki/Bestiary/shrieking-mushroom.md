# Shrieking mushroom

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md) · small plant · rooted · fantasy*

A pale, man-high cave fungus with a thick stalk and a drooping, fleshy cap. It has no eyes and no teeth. It simply screams. When light or movement comes near, it gives out a piercing, rising shriek that carries far through the tunnels and does not stop until the disturbance is gone.

It harms no one on its own, but everything that hunts in the dark knows the sound means food. Underground peoples sometimes plant it at the mouths of their halls as a living alarm. Explorers who meet one either smash it at once or retreat and cover their lanterns.
