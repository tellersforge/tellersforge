# Backwards shadow-goat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · small quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, horror*

A small beast, something like a dog or a goat, that walks backwards with its head hanging between its hind legs. It creeps into houses at night, unseen, and feeds on the blood of sleepers by sucking it from their shadows.

It is invisible to those who do not know to look for it, and it flees when badly hurt. Villagers who wake up weak and pale check their shadows. Some keep a lamp burning all night so that their shadows are always small.
