# Voice-mimic hyena

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth, horror*

A lean, hunch-shouldered beast like a hyena, but with ridges of bare bone where its teeth should be. Its real voice is a low, wet chuckle. The voice it uses at night is yours, or your mother's, or the child you left at home. It circles camps in the dark, listening, and then calls out a traveller by name from just beyond the firelight.

Those who walk out to answer are rarely found again. A camp that knows the trick answers no voices, however familiar, until dawn. The hyena is a coward in the open and runs once badly hurt, so the best defence is a bright fire and people who stay beside it.
