# Red deer

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, historical, modern*

A tall, reddish-brown deer of woods and moorland, with a pale rump and large ears. The stags grow great branching antlers every summer and shed them each winter. In the autumn rut they roar across the hills and fight fiercely for control of the hinds, locking antlers and pushing until one gives way.

Most of the year red deer are shy and bolt at the first scent of people. A rutting stag is a different matter and may charge anything that comes too close. Lords have hunted them for centuries, and in many lands they are the royal game, protected by harsh laws. A wounded deer flees at once.
