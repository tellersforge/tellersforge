# Honey badger

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mustelid and small mammal](../Species_Families/mustelid-and-small-mammal.md) · small quadruped · burrower · fantasy, historical, modern*

A low, broad badger with a black body, a silvery-white back and skin so thick and loose that it can twist inside it and bite whatever has hold of it. Honey badgers live in dry grassland, scrub and forest, digging burrows and hunting rodents, snakes, scorpions and anything else they find.

They are famous for their fearlessness. A honey badger will tear open a beehive and shrug off the stings, eat a venomous snake and wake up from its bite, or attack a lion that comes too close. Once it decides to fight, it fights to the death. Wise travellers let it pass.
