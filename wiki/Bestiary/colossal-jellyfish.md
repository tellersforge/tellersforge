# Colossal jellyfish

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · gigantic amorphous · drifting swimmer · fantasy, horror*

A glassy, trembling bell as broad as a harbour, so clear that it can pass beneath a ship unseen. Beneath it trail curtains of stinging threads a mile long, drifting in the current like a fine, deadly rain.

It has no mind and does not hunt. It simply drifts, and whatever swims into its threads is stung and paralysed, then slowly drawn up into the bell. Whole shoals of fish vanish this way, and so do swimmers, divers and the crews of boats that capsize nearby. It never flees. Coastal peoples watch for the eerie calm and dead fish that warn of one close by.
