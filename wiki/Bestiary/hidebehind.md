# Hidebehind

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mustelid and small mammal](../Species_Families/mustelid-and-small-mammal.md) · medium biped · climber · fantasy, myth, horror, modern*

A lanky, dark-furred forest thing that nobody has ever seen straight on. However fast a logger turns, it is already behind a tree, or behind him. Its long arms end in hooked claws, and it can fold its thin body flat behind a trunk no wider than a man's thigh. Lone loggers and trappers vanish from deep timber, and it is always blamed.

It is as good as invisible and relies on never being looked at. If someone does catch it in plain sight, it flees at once. Logging camps work in pairs and never send a man alone into the trees, and old hands claim that strong drink repels it, which is at least a comforting excuse.
