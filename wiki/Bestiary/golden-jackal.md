# Golden jackal

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · small quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, historical, modern*

A lanky, long-legged dog of dry grassland and scrub, its coat a dusty gold that blends into the stubble. Jackals live in small family bands that keep in touch with high, eerie yipping after dark. They hunt small game, eat fruit and insects, and above all follow larger hunters, waiting at a respectful distance for the lions or the wolves to finish so they can dart in for scraps.

They are bold around the dead and dying, and a wounded straggler or an unguarded carcass draws them quickly. A healthy traveller is in little danger: a jackal that is badly hurt runs. In many desert cultures the jackal haunts graveyards and stands for death, a reputation it has done everything to earn.
