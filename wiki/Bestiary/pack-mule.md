# Pack mule

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy, historical, modern*

A sturdy cross between a horse and a donkey, with long ears, a stubborn streak and remarkable surefootedness. Mules carry heavy loads over mountain trails and desert tracks where horses would stumble and fall. They need less food and water than horses and are tougher, but they cannot breed.

A mule refuses to step onto unsafe footing no matter how it is beaten, which has saved many a traveller. It is also known for kicking backwards at anyone who comes too close behind it. Armies, merchants and miners have relied on them for centuries.
