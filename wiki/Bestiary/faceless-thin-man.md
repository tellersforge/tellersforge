# Faceless thin man

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · large biped · teleports · sentient · horror, modern*

Far too tall and far too thin, in a dark suit with long arms, and where the face should be, smooth pale skin. It appears first at the edge of a wood or the far end of a playing field, standing still and watching. Each night it is a little nearer. Photographs show it where no one saw it, and people who notice it begin to lose time.

It does not walk towards anyone. It is simply somewhere else each time the eye blinks. Its victims are found days later, or not at all. Those who try to corner it find nothing but an empty clearing. There is no known way to fight it. The advice among those who believe is grim: do not look for it, do not write about it, and do not go into the trees.
