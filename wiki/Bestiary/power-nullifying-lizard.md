# Power-nullifying lizard

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · tiny quadruped · slow · sci-fi*

A small, sluggish lizard that spends its life clinging to branches in humid forests, eating leaves and blinking slowly. It is utterly harmless, has no teeth worth the name, and can barely hurry. Yet around it, in a wide bubble, supernatural power simply stops working.

No one quite knows why. Perhaps it evolved to hide from predators that sense the gifted. Whatever the reason, those who fear psychics and mystics prize them highly, and carry them in wooden frames on their backs. Hunters of the gifted bring them into battle. It never flees, but it cannot run, so capturing one is easy. Keeping it alive in a dry place is much harder.

## Not to be confused with

the *[power-hunting hound](power-hunting-hound.md)*, which seeks out those who wield power; the lizard hunts nobody and simply switches power off around itself.
