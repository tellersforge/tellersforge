# Great barracuda

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · medium swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A long, silvery, pike-like fish with a jutting lower jaw and a mouth full of fang-like teeth. Barracudas hang motionless near reefs and wrecks, watching with cold eyes, and then strike at incredible speed at anything that flashes: a fish, a lure, a ring or a knife blade on a diver's belt.

They are not man-eaters. A barracuda makes a single slashing bite and swims away, often confused by what it has hit. That one bite can take a chunk of flesh the size of a fist. Pearl divers learn to cover jewellery and bright metal, and a barracuda that is hurt flees at once.
