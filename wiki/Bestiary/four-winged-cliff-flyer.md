# Four-winged cliff flyer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · large winged quadruped · flyer · sci-fi*

A bright-crested, four-winged flyer, lean and long-tailed, with scales in vivid blues, reds and yellows. It roosts on floating crags and mountain peaks, and its cries echo across the valleys.

Each accepts one rider in its life, and only after a fight: the rider must climb to its nest and wrestle it into submission. Once bonded, the pair are loyal until death. It fights with talons and bite, and flees when badly hurt. Those who ride them are honoured by their people, and those who fail the trial rarely survive the fall.
