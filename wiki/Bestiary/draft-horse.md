# Draft horse

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, historical, modern*

A huge, heavy-boned horse with feathered hooves, a broad back and a patient temper. Draft horses pull ploughs, wagons, barges and brewers' drays, and a pair of them can move loads that would defeat a dozen men. They are slow, calm and remarkably gentle.

They are not fighters. A draft horse will stand stolidly through commotion that would panic a riding horse, and its size and strength let it shrug off blows. Farms and towns depended on them for centuries, and many still do. A badly hurt draft horse will try to get away.
