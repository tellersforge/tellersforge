# Long-armed strangler

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md) · small biped · climber · fantasy, horror*

A skinny, rubbery little horror, no taller than a child, with thin arms twice as long as its body that bend like tentacles. It climbs into the dark corners of halls and ceilings and waits. When someone passes below, the arms drop and wrap around the neck, and it throttles them from above.

It is cowardly and flees when badly hurt. But it is patient and quiet, and it chooses lone victims in dark places. Guards in old castles check the rafters with a torch before they settle in for the night, and never walk a corridor alone.
