# Rogue war mech

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · huge biped · walk · sci-fi*

A walking war machine several storeys tall, armoured and bristling with guns, built to carry a pilot into battle. This one has none. Its orders have gone corrupt: a war long over, a target that no longer exists, an enemy that now means everyone.

It stalks the battlefield or the city on its own, flattening buildings to reach targets it can see through walls. It is mindless and never retreats. It is also running on whatever fuel and ammunition it had when things went wrong, so some hunters simply wait. Others aim for the knees, the sensors or the hatch, where the pilot once sat and where the controls might still answer.
