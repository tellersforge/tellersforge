# Giant bison

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · walk · prehistoric, fantasy, historical*

A long-horned ice-age bison, far heavier than its modern kin, with a massive humped shoulder and horns that spread as wide as a man's outstretched arms. It moves in vast herds across open plains, grazing and wandering, and a herd on the move shakes the ground for miles.

The bulls charge together when threatened, a wall of horn and muscle that tramples anything in its path. It flees when badly hurt. Stone-age hunters live on it: a single kill feeds a band for weeks. Hunts are organised, dangerous affairs, driving the herd over a cliff or into a bog, and the bulls do not always go where they are driven.
