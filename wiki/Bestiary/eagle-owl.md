# Eagle owl

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · small flyer · silent flyer · fantasy, historical, modern, horror*

A great tawny owl with blazing orange eyes and tall feathered horns, large enough to kill hares, foxes and other birds of prey. Eagle owls nest on cliffs, in quarries and in old forests, and they hunt at night on feathers so soft that they make no sound at all.

Its victims never hear it coming. It strikes from behind, driving its talons into the head or neck. People have been knocked down or clawed by one guarding a nest. Its deep, booming hoot carries far in the dark, and in many cultures it is an omen of death. A badly hurt owl simply vanishes into the night.
