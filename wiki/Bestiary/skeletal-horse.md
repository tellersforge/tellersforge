# Skeletal horse

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, horror*

The bare bones of a warhorse, held together by magic and clattering along with a hollow, rattling sound. It needs no fodder, no water and no rest, and it carries its rider as fast and as far as it is asked.

Undead riders use them as mounts, and a troop of skeletal horsemen can cross a kingdom in a night. The horse tramples whatever stands in its way, and it does not shy from spears or fire. It has no mind and never flees. Living horses scream and bolt at the sight of one, which is often the greatest danger it poses.

## Not to be confused with

the *[war horse](war-horse.md)*, a living mount trained for battle; the skeletal horse is dead bones that never tire.
