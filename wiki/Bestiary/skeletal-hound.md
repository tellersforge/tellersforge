# Skeletal hound

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, horror*

The skeleton of a dog, still following a scent it can no longer smell. It moves with a quick, silent lope, its jaws open, its empty eye sockets fixed on its prey.

Skeletal hounds run in silent packs at the command of their master, and they are used to chase down fugitives, guard gates and harry the living. They never bark or howl, and the first warning is usually the click of bones on stone. They have no minds and never flee. A blow that breaks one's spine is enough to scatter it, but there are always more.
