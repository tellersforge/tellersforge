# River otter

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mustelid and small mammal](../Species_Families/mustelid-and-small-mammal.md) · small quadruped · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A sleek, brown, long-bodied animal with webbed feet, a thick tapering tail and a whiskered face. Otters live along rivers, lakes and coasts, building dens in banks and hunting fish, crayfish and frogs underwater. They are playful and curious, sliding down muddy banks and chasing one another for the fun of it.

Fishermen resent them for stealing fish from traps. Otters are shy and seldom seen, but they bite hard if trapped or handled. An otter that is hurt flees into the water at once. In many stories it is a shape-shifter, a trickster or a friend of drowned souls.
