# Reforming bone heap

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium amorphous · slow · fantasy, horror*

A pile of loose bones scattered on the floor of a crypt or a battlefield, looking like nothing more than the remains of the dead. But when the living come close, the bones rattle, rise and snap together into whatever shape suits them: a man, a beast, something in between.

When struck, it falls apart into a heap, and a moment later it snaps back together. It has no mind and never flees. The best way to destroy it is to scatter the bones widely, crush them or burn them, before they can reform.

## Not to be confused with

the *[bone giant](bone-giant.md)*, a single raised skeleton of great size; the bone heap is a loose pile that reshapes itself.
