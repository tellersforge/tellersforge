# Moon-eating dog

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · large quadruped · flyer · fantasy, myth*

A black celestial dog that runs across the sky chasing the moon, and sometimes the sun. When it catches up, it bites, and the light goes dark in an eclipse.

It flees when badly hurt, and it hates loud noise. People bang pots, beat drums and set off fireworks to scare it off and free the moon. It is not really cruel, only hungry, but a world without light would be a bad place.
