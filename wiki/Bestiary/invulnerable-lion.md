# Invulnerable lion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth*

A golden lion larger than any natural cat, with a mane like fire and a hide that turns every blade, spear and arrow. It lairs in a cave with two mouths in the hills above herding country, and it comes out to take sheep, cattle and the herders who guard them.

It fights to the death. Weapons simply glance off it, and hunters who went after it with ordinary gear were never seen again. The heroes who defeat it in the old tales do it with their bare hands, strangling it in its lair, or by trapping it, or with some cunning trick. Its skin, once taken, makes armour that nothing can pierce.
