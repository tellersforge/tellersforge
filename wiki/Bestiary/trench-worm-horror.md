# Trench worm horror

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · huge serpentine · burrower, swimmer · fantasy, horror*

A pallid, segmented worm from the deepest trenches of the sea, long as a ship and ringed with pale feelers around a throat lined with rows of inward-pointing teeth. It lives in the dark around hot vents on the ocean floor, where nothing ever sees it.

It erupts from the vents when anything comes near, feelers groping for heat and motion. Deep-diving craft and their crews are dragged down into the trench and never seen again. It has no fear and no sense beyond hunger, and it never flees. Explorers of the deep go down in strong vessels and with orders not to go near the vents.
