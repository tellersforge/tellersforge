# Horned mountain-beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Pachyderm](../Species_Families/pachyderm.md) · huge quadruped · burrower · fantasy, myth*

A colossal beast with a body like a bull elephant's, thick grey hide, and one great horn rising from the brow. It is called the king of beasts in the old tales. It travels through the rock beneath mountains the way a fish swims through water, and where it pokes its horn up through the ground, springs burst out and streams begin.

It is not aggressive and prefers to go around trouble, under it, or through it. Cornered, it fights with horn and bulk and is terribly hard to hurt. Miners who feel the deep rock shiver whisper that it is passing by. Its shed horn, if ever found, is said to purify any water it touches.
