# Dust devil

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md) · medium other · flyer · fantasy, myth*

A small spinning whirlwind of grit, dust and dry leaves, about as tall as a man. It dances across desert camps and dusty trails, apparently at random, and it seems to enjoy making trouble.

It whirls through camps, blinding travellers with dust and scattering tents, fires and gear. It is mindless and never flees, but it is more nuisance than killer. Desert folk cover their eyes and hold down their belongings until it passes. Some say it is the spirit of a traveller who died of thirst, still wandering.

## Not to be confused with

The *[Sand elemental](sand-elemental.md)*, a larger spirit of sand that hunts beneath dunes; the dust devil is a small, mischievous whirlwind.
