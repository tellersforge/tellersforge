# Beaked cave worm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · medium serpentine · climber · fantasy*

A stone-grey worm the length of a man, coiled among rocks on cave ledges where it is nearly impossible to see. When prey passes, its head opens: four barbed tentacles unfold like the petals of a flower around a snapping beak, and they seize and drag the victim close.

It waits motionless for days. Beaked worms live in nests, and where there is one there are usually several, and perhaps a great matriarch somewhere deeper. They flee when badly hurt. Their shed skins are used by some cave peoples as leather.
