# Tidal surge elemental

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md) · huge swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, myth*

A wave that stands up and walks. It rises from the sea, taller than a ship's mast, green and white with foam, and moves against the wind and the tide. Fish, weed and wreckage churn inside it.

It breaks over ships and quays, swamping and drowning whatever it crashes down upon. It is mindless and never flees. Sailors see it coming as an impossible wave on a calm sea, and try to turn into it or run for shore. Coastal towns build sea walls and leave offerings to the waters. Weapons are useless, but some believe it can be calmed by the right song.
