# Island devil-cat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A monstrous cat, as big as a bull, that came out of the sea one stormy night and made an island its own. It is grey-green like wet stone, with flat ears, a heavy, wet coat that never quite dries and a hiss like breaking surf. It kills whatever lands on its shore, and it has killed warriors by the score.

It does not flee and does not bargain. War bands have landed with spears and shields and been found in pieces on the shingle. In every telling a hero finally comes and fights it, usually alone, and usually at great cost. Until that hero arrives, sailors simply give the island a wide berth.
