# Giant mosquito

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · small flyer · flyer · fantasy, horror*

A bloodsucker as big as a crow, with a striped body, long dangling legs and a proboscis like a knitting needle. It whines in clouds over marshes and flooded forests, and when it finds warm flesh it lands, stabs and drinks. The bite leaves a weeping hole, and many victims come down with marsh fever days later.

They are fragile and flee at the first wound, but there are always more. Travellers wear heavy cloaks and veils, light smoky fires and camp on high, windy ground. Some marsh folk keep bats or birds near their homes to eat them. Soldiers on campaign sometimes say the worst enemy they ever faced was a swamp.
