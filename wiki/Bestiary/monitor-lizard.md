# Monitor lizard

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy, historical, modern*

A long, heavy lizard with a forked tongue that flickers constantly, a thick tail and a slow, swaggering walk. Monitors live in scrub, forest and island grasslands, where they eat eggs, carrion and anything they can overpower. Their bite is both venomous and filthy, and a wound from one sickens and festers.

The largest monitors do not need to finish a kill. They bite a deer or a goat, then follow it for days, tasting the air, until the poison and the rot bring it down. They are dangerous to anyone who corners them, and their lashing tail can knock a child flat. A monitor that is badly hurt will usually lumber off into the brush.
