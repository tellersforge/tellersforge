# Split-jawed wasteland hound

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · sci-fi, post-apocalyptic*

An eyeless, dog-sized scavenger of the desert wastes, lean and grey-skinned, with ribs showing through its hide. Its head ends in a blunt snout that splits four ways when it bites, opening like a flower full of teeth. It hunts by smell and sound, and it hunts in packs, loping after travellers across the open sand until the slowest falls behind.

It fights while the pack fights, and scatters when the pack breaks. Caravans travel with watchmen and fires at night and learn the sound of the pack's yelping. Killing the biggest hound often drives the rest away, and wagons are safer than tents.
