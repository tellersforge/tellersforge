# Deep-crawling abyss horror

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · gigantic many-legged · walk, swimmer · horror, fantasy, sci-fi*

A many-armed crawling mass from the abyssal plain, part squid and part crab, with jointed legs, trailing tentacles and a body studded with eyes that do not blink in unison. It crawls slowly across the ocean floor in total darkness.

Anything that sinks, wrecks, divers, submersibles, whales, is seized, broken and pulled into it. The few who have seen it and come back are rarely the same: they wake screaming, talk to themselves or refuse to go near the water again. It has no fear and never flees. Deep-sea crews keep a careful watch on their instruments and pray for good luck.
