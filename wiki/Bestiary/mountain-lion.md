# Mountain lion

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · medium quadruped · climber · fantasy, historical, modern*

A tawny, long-tailed cat with no spots and no mane, lean and muscular, at home on crags, canyons and wooded hills. Mountain lions are silent hunters. They stalk deer along the ridges, then leap from a rock or a bank onto the back of their prey. Their jump is prodigious: a mountain lion can clear a gully or land on a ledge high above a man's head.

They are solitary and secretive, and most people who live among them never see one. Now and then one stalks a lone hiker or a child, following for a long time before it strikes. Standing tall, shouting and fighting back usually drives it off.
