# Bat swarm

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Chiropteran](../Species_Families/chiropteran.md) · medium swarm · flyer · fantasy, historical, modern, horror*

A shrieking cloud of hundreds or thousands of small bats, pouring from a cave mouth, a ruined tower or an old mine when something disturbs them. They find their way by echoes and can fly through tunnels in total darkness, but a swarm in panic becomes a whirling chaos of leathery wings, claws and teeth.

The bats rarely mean harm; they are simply trying to get out, and anyone in the way is in the way. The noise, the beating wings in the face and the bites are terrifying, and a torch can be snuffed out in moments. Explorers learn to enter caves quietly and to throw themselves flat if the air begins to squeal.
