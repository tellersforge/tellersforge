# Weeping warty beast

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · small quadruped · slow · fantasy, myth, modern*

A small, pig-like beast with loose grey skin covered in warts and moles, so ashamed of its looks that it weeps constantly. It hides in deep hemlock woods, and on quiet evenings hunters follow the sound of sobbing to find it. It does not fight and has no weapons worth the name.

If it is cornered or trapped, it dissolves into a puddle of tears and bubbles, leaving the hunter holding an empty sack. It flees at the first wound, if it is not already gone. Some find it comic; others find it the saddest creature in the woods, and leave it to cry in peace.
