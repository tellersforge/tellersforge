# Tasmanian devil

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Marsupial](../Species_Families/marsupial.md) · small quadruped · walk · fantasy, historical, modern*

A stocky, black scavenger about the size of a small dog, with a white stripe across its chest, pink ears and jaws that seem too large for its head. Devils live on cold, wild islands, sheltering in dens by day and prowling at night for carrion and small prey.

They are famous for their bloodcurdling screeches, which early settlers took for demons. At a carcass several devils squabble, snarl and scream, and they eat everything: bones, fur and all. They rarely attack people, but a cornered devil bites hard enough to crack bone.
