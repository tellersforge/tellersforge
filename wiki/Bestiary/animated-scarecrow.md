# Animated scarecrow

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium biped · walk · fantasy, horror*

A field guardian of rags, straw and old clothes, with a carved turnip or sackcloth head and long fingers like sickle blades. A curse woke it. Now it guards the crops it was set in, and it takes its task far too seriously. It walks the rows at dusk, and anyone found among the ripe grain is in terrible danger.

Its stare is the worst of it. Those who meet its painted eyes often find themselves rooted to the spot, unable to run, while it closes in. It fights to the death. Farmers who inherit such a field harvest by day only, in large groups, and some leave a portion of the crop standing for it every year.
