# Strangler vine

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · large plant · slow · fantasy, horror*

A mass of creeping vines, green and brown, hanging among branches in forests and jungles. It looks harmless, like any other creeper, until it moves.

It coils around necks and limbs, lifting its victims off their feet and strangling them. It is mindless and never flees. Blades cut it, but it has many strands. Fire is effective, but dangerous in a forest. Travellers learn to watch the branches above them and to carry a sharp knife.

## Not to be confused with

The *[Writhing vine mass](writhing-vine-mass.md)*, a tangle of vines on the ground; the strangler vine hangs from branches and lifts its prey.
