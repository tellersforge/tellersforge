# Healing white bird

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · small flyer · flyer · fantasy, myth, historical*

A small bird with plumage as white as fresh snow and a calm, unblinking eye. Physicians and wealthy households keep one in a cage by the sickbed. If the bird looks at the patient, it draws the illness into itself, then flies up towards the sun and burns the sickness away. If it turns its face to the wall, there is nothing to be done, and the family sends for a priest.

The bird is gentle and frightened of rough handling, fleeing at the first wound. Selling a false one, a whitewashed dove, is an old swindle. The real bird is so valuable that thieves have been known to steal it from a king's bedside.
