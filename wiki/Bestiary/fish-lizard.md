# Fish lizard

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · large swimmer · swimmer · prehistoric, fantasy*

A dolphin-shaped sea reptile with a long, toothy snout, a crescent tail and huge eyes, the largest of any animal for its size. It is built for speed in open water, hunting squid and fish in fast pods, and it dives deep into the dark to chase them.

It is not aggressive toward people, but it is curious, and a pod may circle a boat or bump swimmers. It flees when badly hurt. Sailors of its age take it as a good omen, since it hunts where fish are plentiful, and some tell of it guiding lost boats home.
