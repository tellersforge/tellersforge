# Swivel-horned goat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, historical*

A long-legged goat-antelope with a pair of horns it can turn at will, each one swinging independently on its skull. In a fight it lowers one horn forward to stab and keeps the other swept back, safe and in reserve. If the fighting horn is cracked or blunted, it swings the fresh one forward and carries on.

Herders find it in rough uplands, grazing among ordinary wild goats and standing out only when it squares off with a rival. Its horns are prized as curiosities and as knife handles. It is a stubborn fighter but not a foolish one, and it breaks away once badly hurt. Scholars have argued for generations about how the horns are fixed.
