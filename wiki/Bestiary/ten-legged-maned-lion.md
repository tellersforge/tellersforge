# Ten-legged maned lion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large many-legged · fast runner · sci-fi*

A hairless, tawny hunter on ten legs, long and low, with a thick mane around its neck and jaws that split open all the way to the ears. It roams the hills and dry grasslands in small prides, sunning itself on rocks during the heat of the day.

It roars to scatter prey, then pounces on whatever runs the wrong way. Its many legs give it a strange, rippling gait and surprising speed over broken ground. It flees when badly hurt. Herders keep fires and watchdogs, and the roar echoing over the hills at dusk sends every flock scurrying into its pen.
