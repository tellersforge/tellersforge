# Star-winged steed

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · large winged biped · flyer · fantasy, horror*

A rotting thing that seems stitched together from a crow, a bat, an ant and a corpse, with vast leathery wings that can carry riders across the gulf between the stars. It flies through the emptiness itself, where there is no air and no warmth, and riders must protect themselves with charms or potions to survive the journey.

It can be summoned with the right words and a whistle, and it will serve as a mount. It is not loyal, and it flees when badly hurt. Those who ride it return with strange eyes and stranger stories, if they return at all.
