# Mutant mongrel hound

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · post-apocalyptic*

A dog that the end of the world did no favours. Its hair is gone, its skin is a patchwork of sores and old scars, and its ribs show through no matter how much it eats. Nothing about it is pretty and nothing about it is kind. It is faster than it looks, and far hungrier.

Raiders keep them chained at the gates of their camps and let them loose on anyone who approaches uninvited. The ones that slipped their chains, or whose masters died, run in feral packs through the ruins, scavenging when they must and killing when they can. A pack presses on as long as the others are fighting, and scatters only when the pack itself breaks. Settlers shoot them on sight and nobody mourns them.
