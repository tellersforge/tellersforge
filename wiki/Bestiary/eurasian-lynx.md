# Eurasian lynx

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · medium quadruped · climber · fantasy, historical, modern*

A long-legged forest cat with a short, black-tipped tail, a ruff of fur around its face and black tufts rising from its ears. Its great padded feet work like snowshoes, letting it hunt across deep drifts where deer and hares flounder. The lynx waits in ambush beside a trail, then rushes the last few bounds.

Lynxes shun people so thoroughly that whole villages live beside them without knowing. They see well in the dark and move without sound, which has earned them a reputation in folk tales for seeing through walls and keeping secrets. A lynx that is hurt runs at once; it has no reason to fight what it can avoid.
