# Black mamba

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · medium serpentine · fast crawler · fantasy, historical, modern*

A long, slim, grey-brown snake with a coffin-shaped head and a mouth that is inky black inside, shown as a warning when it gapes. Black mambas live in savannah and rocky scrub, and they are among the fastest snakes alive, able to race across the ground with their heads held high.

They are nervous rather than aggressive, and prefer to escape. Cornered, though, a mamba strikes repeatedly, and its venom can kill a grown man within the hour. Stories claim it chases people down; in truth it is usually fleeing to a burrow the person happens to be standing near. Knowing that is little comfort when it comes your way.
