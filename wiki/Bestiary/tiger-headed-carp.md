# Tiger-headed carp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · large swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, myth*

A large carp-bodied fish with a tiger's striped head and a ridge of sharp spines along its back. It lives in rivers and lakes and can summon rain and control water, spraying jets from its mouth. Carvings of it are set on the roof ridges of castles and temples to protect them from fire.

It fights when cornered, biting with its tiger's jaws and slashing with its spines. When a building catches fire, some say the carving comes alive and puts out the flames. Fishermen who catch one throw it back, and quickly.
