# Winged hell fiend

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md) · medium winged biped · flyer · fantasy, horror*

Wiry, leathery-winged fiends the size of a man, with barbed tails and hot coal eyes. They come in shrieking flocks, circling high over a road or a caravan before dropping all at once. Each one spits gobbets of ember that set cloaks and wagon covers burning, and then they stoop with claws and tails.

They are cruel but not brave. A hard blow sends one flapping off to lick its wounds, and a flock that loses several members may break and scatter. Travellers through blasted lands keep wet blankets on the wagons, archers watching the sky, and plenty of torches, since the fiends prefer prey that cannot see them coming.
