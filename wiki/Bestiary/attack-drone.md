# Attack drone

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · small flyer · flyer · sci-fi*

A hovering gun drone with four rotors and a small weapon pod slung underneath. It is cheap, fast and hard to hit, and it usually flies in pairs. It swoops over rooftops and down streets, strafing anyone who does not carry the right signal.

It prefers unarmoured targets and ignores cover it cannot see past. It is mindless and never flees. Soldiers who face them learn to keep under roofs and trees, carry jamming gear, and shoot at the rotors rather than the body. In wartime, the sky above some cities is thick with them, and people learn to listen for the whine before they cross an open square.
