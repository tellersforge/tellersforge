# Talking parrot

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · tiny flyer · flyer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A bright, long-lived bird with a hooked beak, clever eyes and plumage in reds, greens, blues or greys. Parrots come from warm forests, but sailors and merchants have carried them everywhere as pets, and a parrot can easily outlive its first owner.

What makes them famous is their voice. They repeat words, songs and sounds they have heard, often at the worst possible moment: a secret whispered in the kitchen shrieked across a dinner table, a dead captain's curses repeated years later. They bite hard when annoyed and flee at the first wound, and are loved and cursed in about equal measure.
