# Wild boar

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern, post-apocalyptic*

A bristly, dark forest pig with a massive head, small eyes and curving tusks that grow sharper with age. Wild boars live in family groups in woodland and marsh, rooting through the soil for acorns, roots and grubs, and they will raid crops overnight, leaving fields ploughed and ruined.

Hunters fear them more than wolves. A boar that is wounded turns and charges, slashing at legs with its tusks, and it is hard to stop. It fights to the death when hurt, and a spear needs a crossbar to keep a speared boar from running up the shaft. Boar hunts are a test of courage.
