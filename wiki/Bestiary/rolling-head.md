# Rolling head

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · small other · fast runner · myth, horror, fantasy*

A severed head with wild hair and a wide, wet grin, bounding down a dark road faster than a horse can gallop. It rolls, leaps and bounces off stones, snapping at heels and laughing a high, breathless laugh that does not stop. Tales say it belonged to someone murdered on that road, or to a sorcerer who sent it out hungry and never called it back.

It chases lone travellers at night, clamps its teeth on a leg or a throat and will not let go. It knows no fear and has nothing left to lose. Village advice is plain: never walk that road alone after dark, and if you hear laughter behind you, get off the road and over running water before it catches up.
