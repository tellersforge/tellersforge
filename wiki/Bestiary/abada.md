# Abada

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A small, stubborn beast like a donkey, with a boar's short tail and two twisted horns rising from its brow. It grazes on open bush and dry grasslands, and is often seen alone or in pairs, shy and suspicious of people.

Its horns are its misfortune. Traders prize them as an antidote to poison, and ground horn is sold in tiny, expensive pinches. It fights with those horns when trapped and flees when badly hurt. Most horn sold as abada horn is from goats, but the real thing does work, which keeps the hunters coming.
