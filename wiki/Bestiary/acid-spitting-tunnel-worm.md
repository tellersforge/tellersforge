# Acid-spitting tunnel worm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · gigantic serpentine · burrower · sci-fi*

A colossal tunnelling worm, its body as thick as a house, its front end a gaping maw ringed with tentacles. It burrows deep beneath the ground, feeling the tremor of engines and footsteps from far away.

When it strikes, it bursts up beneath vehicles and prey, drenching them in acid and swallowing what is left. It has no mind and never flees. Some warrior peoples hunt them as a rite of passage, with spears, charges and a great deal of luck; most who try do not come home. Travellers in its territory go on foot, walk without rhythm, and keep to the rocks.
