# Cat-headed lizard

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · small quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, historical*

A stubby, thick-bodied lizard as long as a forearm, with a round, whiskered face like a cat's and a short tail. It lives in cracks and crevices among high pastures and scree, and herders have told stories about it for centuries. It hisses like a kettle, then lunges out of its hole with a venomous bite that swells the flesh and can kill a sheep.

It is seldom seen, and few who describe it agree on the details, but alpine folk still carry a stick to probe rock crevices before reaching into them. It flees when badly hurt, back into its hole. A dried specimen shown at fairs is almost always a fake.
