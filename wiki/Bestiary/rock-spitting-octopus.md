# Rock-spitting octopus

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · small many-legged · walk · fantasy*

A little land-walking octopus, knee high, with a tube-like snout. It hides in long grass and pops up to spit rocks at passers-by, then ducks back down. It is more of a nuisance than a danger, but a well-aimed rock can still bruise, bloody or knock someone off balance at the worst moment.

It flees at the first wound, scurrying away on its eight legs. Children in its country throw rocks back, and some keep them as pets. Travellers learn to watch the grass for a sudden snout.
