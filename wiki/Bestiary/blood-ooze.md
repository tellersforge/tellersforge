# Blood ooze

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · medium amorphous · slow · horror, fantasy*

A pulsing red jelly that seeps from old battlefields and slaughterhouses, where the ground has drunk too much blood. It is the colour of fresh blood and smells of iron.

It smells fresh wounds from far off and flows towards them, fastening on the injured and draining their blood, growing larger with each victim. It is mindless and never flees. Fire and salt harm it. Soldiers bind their wounds tightly and keep the injured off the ground, and healers keep a wary eye on the edges of the field.
