# Mutant swamp mosquito

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · small flyer · flyer · post-apocalyptic*

A mosquito as big as a crow, with a needle as long as a dagger and a thin whine that can be heard across a bayou. Its body is dull grey until it feeds, and then its swollen belly fills and glows a deep, lamp-like red. Swamp folk say you can count a hunter's kills by the red lights drifting home at dusk.

It lands, stabs and drinks, and it keeps drinking until either it is full or its victim is too weak to swat it. Once hurt, though, it lurches off into the reeds. They breed in stagnant pools among rusting machinery. Nets, smoke and plenty of mud on bare skin are the usual defences.
