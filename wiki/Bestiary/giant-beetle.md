# Giant beetle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · large many-legged · walk · fantasy*

A beetle as big as an ox, its domed back shining like lacquered armour in dull green, bronze or black. It is slow to anger and slower to stop. The giant beetle does not so much attack as blunder into things, snapping its heavy mandibles at whatever moves near it and pushing on regardless of blows that glance off its shell.

It lives in rotting forests, dung plains and deep caverns, feeding on dead wood and carrion. Most of the time it ignores people. Its shell is prized by armourers and shield-makers, which is why people keep going looking for one. The trick is to turn it over or find the soft joints under the plates, and not to stand in front of it.
