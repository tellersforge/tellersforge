# Steam elemental

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Elemental-kind](../Species_Families/elemental-kind.md) · medium other · flyer · fantasy*

A billowing figure of hot white vapour, hissing and dripping, shapeless except for a hint of a head and arms. It comes from hot springs, great boilers and the places where lava meets the sea. It moves within its own fog, so its victims see only white mist until it is too late.

It wraps its prey in scalding vapour, burning and blinding them. It is mindless and never flees. Blades pass through it. Cold wind thins it, and ice or frost makes it condense and fall as rain. Engineers in steam-driven cities sometimes find one lurking in the pipes after a burst boiler, and send for a wizard before anyone goes in.
