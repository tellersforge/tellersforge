# Clockwork spider

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · large many-legged · climber · fantasy, historical*

A great many-legged machine of iron plates, gears and pistons, the size of a pony, built to guard vaults and treasure rooms. It walks up walls and across ceilings on clawed feet that grip stone, ticking and clicking softly. Its eyes are lenses that catch the lamplight.

It waits overhead, then drops onto intruders from above, pinning them under its weight and striking with sharp forelegs. It is mindless and never flees. Its joints are its weakness: a spike in the gears stops a leg, and enough spikes stop the spider. Its makers are master engineers who like to hide their best work where no one looks up.
