# Bull walrus

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · large swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A huge, wrinkled, cinnamon-brown seal of arctic coasts, with a bristling moustache and a pair of long ivory tusks. Walruses haul out in great noisy herds on ice floes and beaches, grunting, bellowing and jostling for space. They use their tusks to heave themselves onto the ice and to fight rivals, and they feed on shellfish from the sea floor.

In the herd they are peaceable, but a bull that feels threatened will charge a small boat, hook its tusks over the side and tip it. Their thick blubber shrugs off both cold and harpoons. Northern hunters prize their ivory, hide and fat, and approach them with great care.
