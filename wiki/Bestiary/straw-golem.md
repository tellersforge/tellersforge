# Straw golem

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium biped · walk · fantasy*

A bundle of straw and sacking tied into a human shape with twine, a turnip or sack for a head. It is the cheapest golem to make and looks it: light, rustling and loose at the seams. It stands still in a field or a barn, and when someone passes close enough to touch, it moves.

It is quick for its size and flails with stiff arms, but it is flimsy, and a torch will end it in moments, often taking the barn with it. It is mindless and never retreats. Hedge-witches and country magicians make them in batches to guard fields and stores, and more than one farmer has burned a genuine scarecrow by mistake.
