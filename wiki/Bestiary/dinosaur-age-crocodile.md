# Dinosaur-age crocodile

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · gigantic quadruped · swimmer · prehistoric, fantasy*

A crocodile as long as a war galley, with a broad, heavy skull and teeth like tent pegs. It lurks in rivers and estuaries in the age of dinosaurs, waiting at crossings for anything that comes down to drink, and it feeds on dinosaurs themselves, dragging them under, rolling and drowning them.

It fights to the death and is armoured like a fortress. Nothing in its waters is safe, not even the great hunters on land. Travellers of that age cross rivers far upstream, or at night when the crocodiles are sluggish, or not at all. The bones of its victims pile up on the riverbanks.

## Not to be confused with

the *[giant crocodile](giant-crocodile.md)*, a river beast big enough to pull down a horse; the dinosaur-age crocodile is larger still and feeds on dinosaurs.
