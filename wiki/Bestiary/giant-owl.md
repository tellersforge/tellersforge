# Giant owl

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · large flyer · flyer · fantasy*

A great night bird with the wingspan of a small boat, dappled grey and brown like bark, with a pale disc of a face and eyes like lanterns. It hunts in total silence. Its feathers are so soft that no rush of air is heard, and the first sign of it is talons closing on the back of the neck.

It sees in the dark as well as a person sees by day, and it hunts from behind. Foresters say it takes deer and wolves, and on bad winters, a woodcutter who stays out too late. Some folk think it wise, some think it an omen of death, and all agree it should be left alone. It retreats when badly hurt.
