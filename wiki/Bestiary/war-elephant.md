# War elephant

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Pachyderm](../Species_Families/pachyderm.md) · huge quadruped · walk · fantasy, historical, myth*

A huge elephant trained for battle, often armoured with plates of leather or iron and carrying a tower of archers on its back. War elephants charge enemy lines, trample foot soldiers and panic horses that have never seen one before. A driver sits on its neck with a hook and a hammer and chisel, ready to kill the beast if it panics.

It fights while its driver lives. If he falls, or if it is wounded badly, it may panic and run amok through its own lines. Armies that used war elephants often lost as many men to their own beasts as they killed.
