# Sentry robot

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium biped · walk · sci-fi*

A guard robot with a sensor head that swivels constantly and a gun mounted on each arm. It walks a fixed patrol route around a building, a gate or a compound, day and night, exactly the same each time. Its voice is flat and polite.

When it finds an intruder it gives one warning, and then it fires. It follows its last programmed orders forever, even long after its owners are dead, and it never flees. Clever intruders learn its route and its timing and slip past between laps. Hackers try to change its orders. The rest simply wait behind cover for it to run out of ammunition.
