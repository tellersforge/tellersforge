# Four-armed cave brute

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md) · medium biped · climber · sci-fi*

A squat, heavily muscled cave dweller with four arms, a flat, snarling face and strength out of all proportion to its size. It is not much taller than a child, but it can tear a door off its hinges and throw a grown man across a cave.

It lives alone or in small families in caves, which it defends with blind fury. It pummels with all four arms at once and fights to the death. Picking a fight with one is a byword for folly on the worlds where they live, and anyone who stumbles into its cave is well advised to back out slowly and apologise.
