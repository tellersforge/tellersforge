# Brood-mother eye orb

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · large other · hover · sentient · fantasy*

A bloated sphere of flesh as wide as a cart, with a great central eye and a crown of twisting stalks. From its underside, lesser orbs bud and drop away, and it rules them as a queen rules a hive. It hides deep in caves and ruins, sends its spawn out first to test intruders, and watches through their eyes. Its central eye dulls all magic that falls in its gaze.

It is proud, greedy and very clever. Cornered, it bargains, offering secrets, treasure or the lives of others in exchange for its own. Many adventurers have left its lair richer, and later found out what the price really was.
