# Colossal war golem

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · gigantic biped · slow · fantasy*

A siege engine that walks. Built from stone, iron and timber, as tall as a castle tower, it was made to crack fortresses. Its footsteps shake the ground for a mile around, and its fists bring down walls in a few blows. It moves slowly, but it does not stop.

It is mindless and never retreats. Soldiers below it are simply trampled, and arrows do nothing. Armies that face one dig deep trenches across its path, try to topple it on soft ground, or send small, brave bands to climb it and find the heart that drives it. Building one takes a kingdom's wealth and years of work, so few exist, and those are remembered in songs.
