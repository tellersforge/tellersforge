# Wish-giving cow

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A sacred cow, gentle and milk-white, whose milk never runs dry and who can give whatever her keeper truly needs. She grazes near the huts of sages and follows those who treat her kindly.

Kings covet her and have waged wars to steal her, but she will not serve a cruel master. She flees at the first wound and returns only to the gentle. Those who care for her find their larders never empty. Those who try to force her find nothing but trouble.
