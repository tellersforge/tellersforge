# Coconut crab

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md) · small many-legged · climber · fantasy, historical, modern*

A giant land crab with a body the size of a small dog, blue or orange armour and a pair of massive claws. Coconut crabs live on tropical islands, sheltering in burrows and rock cracks by day and roaming at night. They climb trees with ease and can crack open coconuts with their pincers.

They eat fruit, carrion, birds and anything a camp leaves out, and they will happily steal pots, knives and shiny objects. Their grip is crushing and they do not let go easily, but they are not hunters of people. Castaways learn to lock up their food; islanders learn that the crabs make a rich meal, once caught.
