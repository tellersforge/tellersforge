# Blood-hungry vapour

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · large amorphous · hover · sci-fi, horror*

A living cloud of faintly reddish gas that smells, strangely, of honey. It drifts through rooms and corridors with no hurry at all, drawn by the scent of rich blood, and settles over sleepers like a warm fog. When it lifts, the body beneath has been drained white.

It feeds on the red cells in blood and nothing else. It slips through walls, vents and hulls, and can cross the empty space between worlds on its own. Blades and bullets pass through it, but it can be hurt, and when badly hurt it flees. Crews who have met it learn to dread a sweet smell aboard ship, and more than one captain has vented a whole deck into space rather than share it.
