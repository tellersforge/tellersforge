# Three-legged crow

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · small flyer · flyer · fantasy, myth*

A glossy black crow with three legs, which lives in the sun and flies out from it at dawn and home again at dusk. Its feathers catch the light and glow like embers, and it can light a path through the dark.

It guides lost travellers and heroes, leading them over mountains and through forests to where they need to be. It flees when badly hurt. Kings claim descent from it, and its image appears on banners and sun-disks. People who see one in the sky follow it, with good reason.
