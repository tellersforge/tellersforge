# Colossal sea titan

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · gigantic biped · swimmer, walk · modern, sci-fi, superhero*

A finned, fish-headed colossus that walks upright out of the ocean. Its scales glitter like wet armour, its eyes are round and staring, and its mouth is full of needle teeth. It comes ashore in typhoons, bringing the storm-surge with it.

It drags harbour defences into the sea, tears down piers and smashes ships at anchor. The waves rise with it, so that a city fights both the titan and the flood at once. It has no fear and never flees. Some say it is a god of the deep, and some coastal peoples still sacrifice to it in the hope that it will stay under the water.
