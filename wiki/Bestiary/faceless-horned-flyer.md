# Faceless horned flyer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · medium winged biped · flyer · fantasy, horror*

A thin, black, rubbery flyer the size of a man, with horns curling from a blank, smooth head, wide bat wings and a barbed tail. It has no face at all, and makes no sound. It comes at night and snatches sleepers from their beds or camps, carrying them high into the dark. It tickles them with its claws until they are helpless, then drops them far away, sometimes gently and sometimes not.

It flees when badly hurt. Nobody knows why it does what it does. Those who have been taken do not like to speak of it, and the lucky ones are found alive in strange places.
