# Widow spider

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · tiny many-legged · climber · fantasy, historical, modern, horror*

A small, glossy black spider with a round belly, often marked with a red hourglass underneath. Widow spiders hide in dark corners, woodpiles, outhouses and old boots, spinning tangled webs to catch insects. They are shy and bite only when pressed or trapped against skin.

The bite itself is a pinprick, but the venom brings agonising cramps, sweating, and a pain that spreads through the belly and back. It is rarely fatal to a healthy adult but can kill a child or an old man. Everyone learns to shake out their boots.
