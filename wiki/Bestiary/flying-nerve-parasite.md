# Flying nerve parasite

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md) · tiny flyer · flyer · sci-fi, horror*

A flat, jelly-like flyer, translucent and about the size of a hand, that flaps through the air with a faint wet sound. It stings, and once inside it spreads along the host's nerves like frost across a window, until every limb answers to it and the host feels pain whenever it disobeys.

Every parasite in a colony shares one mind, so every host is part of one will. Hosts are driven to carry more parasites to new victims, and a whole colony or ship can fall in a few days. The creatures are mindless in themselves and never flee. Their one weakness is intense light, which kills them outright, and those who have fought them fill every corridor with glaring lamps.
