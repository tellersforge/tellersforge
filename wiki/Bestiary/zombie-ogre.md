# Zombie ogre

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · large biped · slow · fantasy, horror*

The raised corpse of an ogre or troll, slow and rotting, but still enormously strong. Its skin hangs in loose flaps, its eyes are clouded, and it lumbers forward with a heavy, dragging gait.

It swings its fists or a club and ignores any wound that does not destroy it outright. Arrows sprout from its back, and it does not notice. It has no mind and never flees. Necromancers use them as shock troops, and the living learn to bring fire and heavy weapons to take them down.

## Not to be confused with
