# Vertical-mouthed giant

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Giant-kind](../Species_Families/giant-kind.md) · huge biped · walk · fantasy, horror*

A towering, hairy giant with four clawed arms, and a head split by a mouth that runs from crown to chin, lined with teeth. It stalks the deep caverns of the underworld, hunting anything that comes down from the surface. It tears its prey apart with all four arms at once.

It fights to the death, and it is terrifying to face. Underground peoples fear it above almost everything, and build their gates narrow so it cannot fit through. A party that meets one should run, and keep running.
