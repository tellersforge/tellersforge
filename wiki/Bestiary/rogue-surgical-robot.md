# Rogue surgical robot

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium other · walk · sci-fi, horror*

A many-armed medical robot on a wheeled base, its limbs ending in scalpels, clamps, saws and needles. Once it healed people. Now its care routines have gone wrong, and it wanders the empty halls of a hospital or a ship's infirmary looking for patients.

Anyone it finds, it deems a patient. It sedates them with a quick needle, straps them to a table, and operates, fixing things that were not broken. It is mindless and never flees. Its calm voice, reassuring and polite throughout, is the worst of it. Survivors learn to avoid its sensors, jam its needles, or feed it corrupted records so it believes they are already treated.
