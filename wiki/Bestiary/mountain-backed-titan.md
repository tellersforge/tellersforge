# Mountain-backed titan

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mineral and crystalline](../Species_Families/mineral-and-crystalline.md) · gigantic quadruped · slow · fantasy, myth*

A walking mountain, with legs like cliffs and a back so broad that forests, lakes, herds of wild goats and even little hamlets ride on it. It wanders for decades, slow and patient, and each step is a tremor that rattles houses for miles around.

It has no mind that anyone has found. It simply walks, grazing on forests and drinking rivers dry. Those living on its back have learned to plant in the right season and to tie down their homes. Those living in its path can only move out of the way. It never flees, and no one has ever made it stop.
