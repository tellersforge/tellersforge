# Spine-hurling hive serpent

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md) · large serpentine · walk · sci-fi*

A long, hooded swarm-beast that moves like a snake but rears up on its tail to fight. Its back bristles with needle spines, and its forearms end in long scythe blades. A crest of armour flares around its head like a cobra's hood.

It hurls needle spines from range, flexing its back to fling a volley, then closes to slash with its scythes. Wounds heal quickly, and the swarm's will drives it on; it is mindless and never flees. Troops learn to take cover when the hood flares, and to keep it at a distance, but not so far that the spines reach them.
