# Ceiling tongue-lurker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md) · medium other · immobile · sci-fi, horror*

A shelled mouth, like a great barnacle, fixed to the ceilings of caves, ruins and derelict ships. It never moves from its spot. From its lip it lowers a long, sticky tongue that hangs almost invisible in the gloom, like a fishing line waiting for a bite.

Anything that brushes the tongue sticks fast and is reeled upward, kicking, to be chewed. It has no mind and never flees; it cannot go anywhere anyway. Explorers who know of it carry their lamps high and sweep the ceiling before every step. Those who don't learn about it when their companion rises silently into the dark.
