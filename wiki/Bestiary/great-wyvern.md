# Great wyvern

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · huge winged biped · flyer · fantasy*

An ancient wyvern grown vast, its wings wide enough to shadow a village. Its wingbeats flatten crops and its roar empties villages. It lords over a valley, taking cattle, riders and small boats.

It is very hard to wound, and it terrifies all who see it. It flees when badly hurt. Valley folk pay it tribute in cattle, and heroes who try to kill it are remembered in songs.
