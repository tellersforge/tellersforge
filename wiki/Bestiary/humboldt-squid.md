# Humboldt squid

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · medium other · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A muscular, red-brown squid as long as a man, with ten sucker-lined arms, a hooked beak and great staring eyes. These squid live in the deep by day and rise to hunt in enormous shoals at night, flashing red and white as they move, and fishermen call them red devils for good reason.

They are aggressive and attracted to light. A diver with a lamp in the dark may be mobbed by dozens at once, grabbed by arms, bitten by beaks and dragged down. They squirt ink when threatened and fight as long as the shoal fights. Fishing villages pull them up by the thousand, but no one swims among them willingly.
