# Clicking fungal zombie

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md) · medium biped · walk · horror, post-apocalyptic*

A host that has carried the fungus for a long time. Its head has split and flowered into ridged, plate-like growths that cover the eyes completely. Blind, it hunts by sound: it makes a wet, rattling click and listens for the echo, turning its ruined head slowly back and forth. When it hears breathing or a footstep, it lunges, and what it grabs, it does not let go.

Those who survive among the infected learn that crouching and silence are the best armour. A single clicker can be avoided. Several in a narrow place are a death sentence, and survivors mark such buildings and never go back.
