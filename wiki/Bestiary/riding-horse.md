# Riding horse

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, historical, modern, post-apocalyptic*

A light, long-legged horse bred for speed and stamina on the road. Riding horses carry messengers, travellers, nobles and scouts, and a good one can cover a long distance in a day without breaking down. They are intelligent, sensitive and easily frightened.

A horse shies at sudden noises, strange smells and flapping cloth, and a spooked horse may kick, bite or bolt. It has no love of fighting and flees at the first wound. Good horsemanship lies as much in calming the animal as in riding it. A fine riding horse is worth more than many houses.
