# Pincered tentacle crab

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md) · large many-legged · swimmer · fantasy*

A lobster-like creature the size of a horse, with a heavy dark shell, two crushing claws and a cluster of short, writhing tentacles around its mouth. It lives in flooded ruins, sunken temples and drowned cellars, and treats them as its home. It grips its prey with a claw, pulls it close, and the tentacles paralyse it before it is eaten.

It guards its ruins to the death. Divers and treasure-seekers who explore sunken places carry spears long enough to keep the claws at a distance. The shell is hard to crack. The joints are softer, if you can reach them.
