# Shadow hound

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, horror*

A black hound that runs through darkness as if through a door. It steps into one shadow and out of another, appearing behind its prey without warning.

It hunts in packs, and it fights while the pack fights. Its bite is cold and its eyes glow faintly. Those who hunt it use light to cut off its escape routes and fight in the open, where there are few shadows to hide in.

## Not to be confused with
