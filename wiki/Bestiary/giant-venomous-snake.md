# Giant venomous snake

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · large serpentine · walk · fantasy*

A viper as long as three men, with a thick, patterned body, a broad arrow-shaped head and fangs as long as fingers. It lies coiled in cover, in tall grass, among rocks or beneath fallen leaves, and strikes faster than a whip. Its venom can kill a horse, and a person bitten has little time to find help.

It hunts by warmth and scent and prefers not to meet anything it cannot eat. If disturbed it hisses, rattles or flattens its neck in warning, and it will flee when badly hurt. Snake-charmers boast of handling them. Travellers in their country carry a staff to beat the grass ahead and walk loudly, so the snake has time to leave.
