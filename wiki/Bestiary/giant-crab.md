# Giant crab

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md) · large many-legged · walk · fantasy, modern*

A shore crab the size of a cart, its shell mottled green and grey like weed-covered rocks. It scuttles sideways out of the surf with frightening speed, its eyes raised on stalks, and seizes prey with two massive claws that can snap a spear shaft or crush a helmet. It breathes water and air alike and roams the tideline at night.

Fishing villages keep watch on the beach during the full moon, when giant crabs come ashore to lay eggs. Its meat feeds a village for a week, and its claws make fine tools and ornaments. The trick, say the old fishers, is to come at it from behind and flip it over. It fights while it can.
