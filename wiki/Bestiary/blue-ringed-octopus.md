# Blue-ringed octopus

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · tiny other · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A tiny octopus that could sit in the palm of a hand, sandy yellow and unremarkable until it is disturbed. Then dozens of electric blue rings flash across its skin, a warning that it carries one of the deadliest venoms in the sea. It lives in rockpools and shallow reefs, hunting small crabs.

The bite is painless and often goes unnoticed. Minutes later the victim's lips and tongue grow numb, then breathing stops while the mind stays awake. There is no cure but keeping the victim breathing until the poison passes. Children who pick up pretty creatures from rockpools are its usual victims. Handled gently and left alone, it flees.
