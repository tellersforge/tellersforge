# Great sea serpent

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · gigantic serpentine · swimmer · fantasy, myth*

A serpent so vast that its coils can ring a galleon from bow to stern with room to spare. Its scales are the size of shields and scarred by old harpoons, and when its head rises beside a ship the crew can look straight into an eye as wide as a cartwheel.

It does not pick at sailors. It wraps a ship, squeezes until the timbers burst, and drags the wreck under in a single pull. Sometimes it follows whole fleets for days, taking the stragglers. It fights to the death, and sinking it is a feat that earns a captain a song for the rest of their life.

## Not to be confused with

the *[sea serpent](sea-serpent.md)*, its smaller cousin that plucks sailors from decks; the great sea serpent crushes and sinks whole ships.
