# Funnel-web spider

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · tiny many-legged · burrower · fantasy, historical, modern, horror*

A large, glossy, dark spider with thick legs and fangs that can pierce a fingernail. Funnel-webs live in burrows lined with silk, in damp soil, under rocks and logs. The males wander at night looking for mates and often end up in houses, shoes and swimming pools.

Threatened, a funnel-web rears up, showing its fangs, and bites repeatedly. Its venom is fast-acting and deadly: a victim can die within the hour. It fights when cornered and does not back down.
