# Scaled dragon hound

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · fantasy*

Lean, long-legged and plated in small dull scales, the dragon hound looks like a hunting dog that something has gone badly wrong with. It carries its nose low and its tail straight, and it can follow a trail over bare stone that would baffle any ordinary hound. When it corners its quarry it opens its jaws and coughs out a short, hot gout of flame.

Dragon hounds hunt in pairs and are bred and kept by cults that worship the great dragons, who use them as guard beasts at their shrines. A pair will fight as long as the other is fighting. Kill one, and the survivor often loses its nerve.
