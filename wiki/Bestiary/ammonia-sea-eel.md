# Ammonia-sea eel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · large serpentine · swimmer · sci-fi*

A long electric eel of frozen, poisonous oceans on cold moons, dark and slick, with rows of pale, glowing spots along its flanks. It coils in icy seas beneath the frozen crust, feeding on whatever swims past.

It stuns prey with a crackling electric discharge, then bites. It flees when badly hurt. Divers on cold moons wear insulated suits and watch for the glow of its spots in the dark water. Its flesh is inedible to most, but its glowing organs are prized by collectors.
