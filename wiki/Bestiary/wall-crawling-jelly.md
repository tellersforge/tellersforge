# Wall-crawling jelly

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · large amorphous · climber · fantasy*

A yellow-brown jelly the size of a large animal, flowing along walls, across ceilings and under doors in search of flesh. It slides through cracks and narrow gaps and creeps up behind its prey.

It clings to what it touches and dissolves it slowly. It is mindless and never flees. Fire and cold harm it, while weapons pass through. It prefers dark, damp places such as sewers and deep dungeons. Delvers check the ceilings as well as the floors, and learn to recognise the faint smell of rot that heralds it.

## Not to be confused with

The *[Ceiling slime](ceiling-slime.md)*, a small patch that drops from above and turns flesh into more slime; the wall-crawling jelly is larger and hunts along walls.
