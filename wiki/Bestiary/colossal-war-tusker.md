# Colossal war-tusker

![Colossal war-tusker](../Images/BE-697-bestiary-colossal-war-tusker.jpg){.illus}

*Catalogue: BE-697*
*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Pachyderm](../Species_Families/pachyderm.md) · gigantic quadruped · walk · fantasy, historical, myth*

A mountain of muscle and ivory, bred and armoured for war: thick grey hide plated with iron, four curving tusks capped in bronze, and a wooden fighting tower lashed across its back for archers and spearmen. Where an ordinary war elephant carries a handful of soldiers, this beast carries a small garrison into the enemy line.

It fights by trampling, goring and simply walking through whatever stands in its way; a charging herd of them can break a battle line before the first arrow lands. Drivers guide it from a seat behind its skull, but a maddened or badly hurt tusker turns on friend and foe alike, and veteran armies keep archers ready to bring one down from beneath rather than be crushed by it.

## Not to be confused with

the *[War elephant](war-elephant.md)*, a trained but ordinary-sized battle elephant that carries a few archers; the colossal war-tusker is bred to siege scale and fielded only by the wealthiest armies. Nor the *[Many-tusked elephant](many-tusked-elephant.md)*, a peaceful, many-trunked rain-bringer of myth rather than a war-beast.
