# Eight-legged saddle beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Equine and centauroid](../Species_Families/equine-and-centauroid.md) · large many-legged · fast runner · sci-fi*

A tall, eight-legged mount with broad, padded feet, bred for crossing the dust of dead sea bottoms on dry worlds. It has a long neck, a small, sour-looking head and a hide the colour of old leather.

It is ill-tempered, obeys firm riders and bites clumsy ones. It flees when badly hurt, often leaving its rider behind. Riders on dry worlds prize them for their endurance, and a good one can carry a rider across the desert for days without water. Its temper is a small price to pay.
