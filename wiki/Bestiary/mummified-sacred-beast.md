# Mummified sacred beast

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, horror, myth, historical*

A temple cat, jackal or baboon, wrapped in bandages and raised to watch over holy ground. It prowls the halls and courtyards of ancient temples, moving swiftly and silently, its eyes glowing faintly in the dark.

It attacks anyone who desecrates the temple, and those who harm it are said to be cursed. It has no mind and never flees. Priests and scholars treat it with respect, and tomb robbers who know the stories leave its temples alone.
