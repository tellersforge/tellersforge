# Mutant stinging wasp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · small flyer · flyer · post-apocalyptic*

A mutant wasp the size of a hawk, striped black and rust-red, with a sting as long as a finger and venom strong enough to kill a big man or a two-headed cow. It flies fast and erratically, darting and zigzagging, and it is almost impossible to aim at. Swarms nest in ruins and rock crevices and defend their nests fiercely.

They are the terror of the desert. They fight together and scatter only when the swarm is broken. Travellers who hear the buzz run for water or cover, and settlers burn nests at night with oil and fire. Antivenom is worth more than gold in wasp country.
