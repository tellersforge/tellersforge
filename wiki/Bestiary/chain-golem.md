# Chain golem

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium biped · walk · fantasy, horror*

A figure made of wound, hooked chains, wrapped over and over into a man-shaped mass. It rattles constantly, even when standing still, a noise that carries through a whole dungeon. When it fights, lengths of chain uncoil from its arms and lash out, hooks first.

It snags a victim and drags them close, then wraps them tight. It is mindless and never flees. Cutting through it takes a heavy axe and a lot of patience, and every broken link seems to leave another hook. It is a favourite guardian of prisons, torture halls and the lairs of warlords who like the sound of chains.

## Not to be confused with

The *[Rope golem](rope-golem.md)*, a quiet construct that binds and strangles; the chain golem is loud, heavy, and tears flesh with its hooks.
