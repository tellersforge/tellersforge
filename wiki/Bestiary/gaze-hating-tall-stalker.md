# Gaze-hating tall stalker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Trans-dimensional](../Species_Families/trans-dimensional.md) · medium biped · teleports · fantasy, horror*

A gangly black figure far taller than a man, thin as a rake, with long dangling arms and eyes that glow a faint purple. It wanders the land on business of its own, picking up blocks of earth and stone and setting them down elsewhere, and now and then it simply vanishes and reappears a little way off. Left alone, it is odd but harmless, and it ignores travellers entirely.

Meet its gaze, though, and it shrieks and comes for you, blinking from place to place, and it does not stop until one of you is dead. People who live near its haunts learn to keep their eyes on the ground and their hat brims low. Children are taught never to stare at strangers for a reason.
