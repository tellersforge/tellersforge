# Giant beaver

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md) · medium quadruped · swimmer · prehistoric, fantasy*

A beaver as large as a bear, with thick brown fur, a flat tail like a paddle and front teeth as long as a man's hand. It lives in ice-age lakes and marshes, gnawing trees and building dams that flood whole valleys, and it spends most of its life in the water.

It is shy and flees at the first wound. When trapped, though, it bites, and those teeth can take off a finger or open a leg to the bone. Stone-age peoples hunt it for fur, meat and teeth, which make fine chisels. A lake where the giant beavers build is a place of plenty, full of fish, birds and game.
