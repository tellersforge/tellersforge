# Glutton beast

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, horror*

A horned, goat-bodied beast with a heavy human-like face sunk in its armpits and a great mouth that never stops chewing. It eats grass, trees, livestock, carts and people, and when there is nothing left it begins to eat its own body, starting with the tail. It knows no fear and never flees.

It is hard to wound, and harder to stop, because it simply keeps coming towards the next meal. Its image is carved on bronze cauldrons as a warning against greed. Those who meet it are advised to leave it something large to eat and run the other way.
