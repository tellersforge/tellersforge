# Common octopus

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · small other · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A soft, eight-armed mollusc with a bulbous head, clever eyes and skin that can change colour and texture in an instant, from sandy speckle to rock-grey to warning red. Octopuses live in dens among rocks and reefs, hunting crabs and shellfish, and they can squeeze their boneless bodies through any gap wider than their beak.

They are astonishingly clever, able to open jars, solve puzzles and slip out of fishermen's pots. Threatened, an octopus squirts a cloud of dark ink and jets away, leaving an attacker grasping at nothing. Its beak can give a painful bite, but it flees at the first real wound.
