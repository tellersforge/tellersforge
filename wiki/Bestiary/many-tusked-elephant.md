# Many-tusked elephant

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Pachyderm](../Species_Families/pachyderm.md) · huge quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A white elephant of enormous size, with many tusks and many trunks, that bears gods on its back. It lives near the edge of the world, where it draws water up from the underworld with its trunks and blows it into the sky to make rain clouds.

It is peaceful, and fights only when cornered, when it tramples and gores. Its hide is thick and almost impossible to wound. Farmers pray to it for rain, and a sighting is taken as a sign of good harvests. Kings who own white elephants claim a share of its blessing.
