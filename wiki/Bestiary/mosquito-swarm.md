# Mosquito swarm

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium swarm · flyer · fantasy, historical, modern, post-apocalyptic*

At dusk the marsh begins to hum. A mosquito swarm rises from the reeds in a grey, shifting haze, and every uncovered wrist and neck is found within moments. One bite is nothing. A hundred bites leave a traveller swollen, sleepless and scratching, and some bites carry the shaking fevers that empty river towns every wet season.

No blade helps. Travellers light smoky fires of green wood, sleep under gauze, smear themselves with mud or rancid grease and keep moving until they are clear of the standing water. Soldiers who camp by marshes lose more men to the fever than to the enemy, and old campaigners learn to pick high, windy ground.
