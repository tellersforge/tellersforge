# Whirlpool maw

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · gigantic other · stationary · fantasy, myth*

A vast mouth in the seabed, ringed with teeth like broken columns, that opens a few times a day and drinks the sea. As it swallows, the water above turns and spins into a great whirlpool, and anything floating nearby is drawn round and down into its throat.

It does not move and has no mind that anyone can find. It simply feeds. Ships caught at the edge of the spiral may row free with enough strength and luck, but those that drift too close are lost. Sailors who know the strait time their passage by the tides of the maw, and pilots who can do this are paid very well.
