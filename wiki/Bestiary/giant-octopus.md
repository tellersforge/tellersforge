# Giant octopus

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · large swimmer · swimmer, climber · fantasy, modern, horror*

An octopus as large as a rowboat, soft-bodied and clever, able to change the colour and texture of its skin to match rock, weed or rotten timber. It can squeeze its whole bulk through a gap no wider than a man's head, and it lives in the holds and cabins of sunken ships.

It hunts by ambush. A diver reaching into a wreck feels an arm curl around their wrist, then another around their waist, and is dragged into a crevice where the beak waits. Out of water it can climb and crawl for a short while. Badly hurt, it squirts ink and vanishes into the smallest hole it can find.
