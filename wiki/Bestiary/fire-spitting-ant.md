# Fire-spitting ant

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium many-legged · walk · fantasy, post-apocalyptic*

A giant ant the colour of hot coals, with a swollen, glossy abdomen that holds a reservoir of burning liquid. When threatened it curls its body under and squirts a stream that ignites grass, blisters skin and eats into leather. Then, while its target is beating out the flames, it rushes in with its jaws. Where one attacks, the pack attacks with it.

Its nests are marked by rings of scorched ground and the smell of acrid smoke. Alchemists and fire-priests pay well for the fluid glands, carefully removed, and some armies have tried to tame colonies for siege work, with results that are mostly remembered by the survivors. Water and wet cloaks help; being elsewhere helps more.
