# Winged stag

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium winged quadruped · flyer · fantasy, myth*

A stag with a full rack of antlers, the body of a deer and the wings and feathers of a great bird of prey, dark green and glossy. Flocks of them roost on sea cliffs and in high forests. They swoop on travellers from above, raking with their antlers, and they seem to hate people with a particular, personal hatred.

The strangest thing about them is their shadow. It falls on the ground in the shape of a man, and scholars say they are the restless dead of those lost far from home, who will not rest until they have killed. They fight as long as the flock fights, then scatter together. A lone traveller who sees a human shadow with no human above it should find cover.
