# Golden eagle

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · small flyer · flyer · fantasy, historical, modern, myth*

A great brown eagle with a golden nape and wings wide enough to shadow a child. Golden eagles nest on cliffs and hunt over open mountains and plains, soaring for hours before folding their wings and stooping onto prey. They take hares, foxes, marmots and lambs, and can drive a wolf into submission.

Steppe hunters capture them as chicks and train them to hunt from horseback, sending them against foxes and even wolves in the winter. The birds are proud, heavy on the arm and fiercely loyal to their handlers. Shepherds resent them. Kings put them on banners. A badly hurt eagle takes to the air and does not return.
