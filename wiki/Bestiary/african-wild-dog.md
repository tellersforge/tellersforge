# African wild dog

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, historical, modern*

A leggy, big-eared dog with a coat splashed in black, tan and white as if someone had spilled paint on it, no two alike. These dogs live in tight, affectionate packs on open savannah and light woodland, greeting one another with chirps and twittering calls before every hunt.

Their method is endurance. The pack picks out an antelope and runs it, mile after mile, taking turns at the front, until the prey stumbles from sheer exhaustion. Few hunters are more successful, and none share a kill more fairly, feeding the pups and the injured first. Their pack loyalty makes them stubborn fighters, but they have little interest in people and usually trot away from a traveller on foot.
