# Animated statue

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · large biped · slow · fantasy, myth*

A carved statue of a hero, a god or a king, stone or bronze, set upon a plinth in a temple, a square or a tomb. It holds its pose for centuries. Then someone trespasses: they steal from the shrine, break an oath beneath it, or enter the forbidden chamber. The statue steps down.

It advances slowly and without pause, grinding across the floor, and strikes with fists or a carved weapon. It is mindless and never flees. It is very hard to damage and very hard to outlast, since it neither tires nor sleeps. The usual escape is to leave its ground and make amends. Priests sometimes have the words to still it.
