# Giant toad

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md) · large quadruped · hopper · fantasy*

A warty marsh toad as big as an ox, the colour of mud and moss, with golden eyes and a mouth that stretches from one side of its head to the other. It sits among the reeds so still that people walk past, until its sticky tongue shoots out and drags something small into its gullet. Children, dogs and ducks are swallowed whole.

Its skin sweats a bitter poison that numbs the lips and tongue of any hunter who tries to eat it, and burns in open cuts. It flees when badly hurt, with enormous, splashing hops. Marsh folk keep children away from the water at dusk, when the toads sing, and some keep one penned as a guardian of the village well.
