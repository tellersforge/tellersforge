# Stinging walker plant

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · medium plant · slow · sci-fi, post-apocalyptic, horror*

A tall, bare-stemmed plant that walks on three thick roots, swaying like a drunkard, topped by a cluster of leaves and a long whip-like stinger coiled beneath them. It has no eyes but is drawn to noise, and shuffles toward voices, engines and hammering with a slow, patient gait. When something comes within reach, the stinger lashes at the face, and its poison can blind.

Once a curiosity grown for its rich oil, it proved hard to fence and harder to kill. Farmers who keep it wear masks and face shields in the fields. Where it has escaped, a quiet stand of tall stalks at the edge of the woods is a warning.
