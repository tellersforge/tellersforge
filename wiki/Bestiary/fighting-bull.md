# Fighting bull

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern, myth*

A bred or wild bull of great temper, black or dark brown, with massive shoulders and long, lowered horns. It is raised for its aggression and its fighting spirit, and it charges anything that moves: a rider, a cloak, a fence or a wagon.

Its charge tosses and tramples, and it fights to the death. Some cultures pit it against men in the arena; others simply keep it away from the road. Herders drive fighting bulls with long poles and a great deal of respect. A fighting bull does not know how to give up.
