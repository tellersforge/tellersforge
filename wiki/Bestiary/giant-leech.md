# Giant leech

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · medium serpentine · swimmer · fantasy, horror*

A slick, olive-black leech as long as a man's leg, with a sucker at each end and a mouthful of tiny saw-teeth. It waits in warm, still water: swamps, flooded rice fields, the slow brown pools of jungle rivers. When something wades in, it swims with a smooth ripple, fastens on below the waterline and drinks until it is full, which can take a long time and a great deal of blood.

Its bite numbs the skin, so many victims feel nothing until they see it. Salt, fire and vinegar make it let go. Physicians in some lands keep a small one in a jar for bleeding patients, and in others that same practice is thought a crime.
