# Giant short-faced kangaroo

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Marsupial](../Species_Families/marsupial.md) · large biped · hopper · prehistoric, fantasy*

A heavy browsing kangaroo taller than a man, with a short, blunt face, strong forearms and one long toe on each hind foot. It does not hop so much as stride, walking upright on its toes, and it reaches up with long arms to pull branches down to its mouth.

It is shy and bounds away at the first sign of danger. When cornered, it rears, grabs with its forelimbs and kicks with its hind legs, and the kick can gut a hunter. It flees at the first wound. Hunters of the ancient southern lands know to approach from the side and never from the front.
