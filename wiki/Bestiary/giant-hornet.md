# Giant hornet

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · small flyer · flyer · fantasy, modern*

A hornet as big as a hawk, striped yellow and black, with a stinger like a nail. A single sting swells a limb until it is useless for the rest of the day, and several can kill. They nest in hollow trees, abandoned towers and old ruins, building papery hives the size of a cart.

One alone is dangerous; a nest is a disaster. Disturb it and the whole colony pours out in a furious swarm, and they keep coming as long as the swarm holds together. Woodcutters mark hornet trees with a red rag and never go near them in summer. The only sensible way to deal with a nest is smoke and fire at night, when the insects are sluggish.
