# Great lone wolf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · huge quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth*

A gigantic grey wolf, as tall at the shoulder as a horse, with a grizzled muzzle and old scars across its face. Unlike its smaller kin it hunts alone, prowling forests and mountains at night, and it has a particular hatred for those who hunt after dark.

It kills night hunters, and it kills the hunters sent after it. Its hide turns blades and it fights to the death. Villages warn their trappers to be home by sunset. Some say it is the ghost of a wolf killed unfairly; others, that it is the forest itself, punishing greed.
