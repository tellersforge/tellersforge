# Four-finned sea steed

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · large swimmer · swimmer · sci-fi*

A long-necked, four-flippered reef swimmer, sleek and blue-grey, with a gentle face and a playful manner. It swims the reefs and shallows of an ocean world, and is curious and quick to befriend.

It lets riders bond with it, carrying them through reefs, playing with them and protecting them from predators. It flees at the first wound. Reef peoples ride them as companions and mounts, and children are introduced to them early. A bond with a sea steed is said to be for life.
