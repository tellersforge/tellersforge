# Flying fox

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Chiropteran](../Species_Families/chiropteran.md) · small flyer · flyer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A large fruit bat with a fox-like face, big dark eyes, reddish fur and leathery wings wider than a man's arms. Flying foxes roost in noisy colonies of thousands in tall trees, hanging upside down, squabbling and fanning themselves in the heat. At dusk they stream out across the sky to feed.

They eat fruit, nectar and blossoms, and they can strip an orchard in a single night, which makes them the enemy of every fruit grower. They are harmless to people unless handled, when they bite. Some peoples hunt them for food, and their colonies are a spectacle nobody forgets. A hurt flying fox flees.
