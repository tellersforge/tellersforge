# River hippopotamus

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Pachyderm](../Species_Families/pachyderm.md) · huge quadruped · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A huge, barrel-bodied animal with thick grey skin, a massive head and jaws that can open wide enough to swallow a child. Hippos spend the day in rivers and lakes, their eyes and nostrils peeping above the water, and come out at night to graze on the banks.

They are one of the deadliest animals to boatmen. A hippo defends its stretch of water fiercely and will capsize a canoe and bite a man in half. It fights to the death in its water. Its thick hide shrugs off blows. River people give hippos a wide berth.
