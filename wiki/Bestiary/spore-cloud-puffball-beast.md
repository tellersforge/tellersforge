# Spore-cloud puffball beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md) · medium other · slow · sci-fi, horror*

A lumbering fungal animal, round and pale as a mushroom, with stubby legs and a soft, spongy body that dimples when touched. It waddles slowly towards warmth, and its surface is covered with small puffball sacs.

When touched, it bursts clouds of spores. Those who breathe them wander in confusion, forgetting where they are and who they are with, and later sprout fungus from their skin. It has no mind and never flees. Explorers who meet one wear masks and burn it from a distance, and keep an eye on each other for days afterwards.
