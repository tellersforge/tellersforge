# Bloated brain bug

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · large other · slow · sci-fi, horror*

A pulpy, sagging hive-ruler, big as a wagon, its body little more than a swollen sac of thought on stubby legs. It lives deep in the hive, surrounded by guards, and directs the whole swarm with its mind. Its mouth is a single long spike.

It feeds on captives' thoughts, sucking their minds out through that spike and learning all they knew. It can reach into a mind from a distance and speak without words. For all its power, it is a coward: it flees at the first wound and dreads being caught. Capturing one alive is the prize every war against the hive is fought for.
