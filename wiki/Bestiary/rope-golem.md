# Rope golem

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium biped · walk · fantasy*

A knotted human shape of hemp and cord, all splices, loops and hanging ends. It moves quietly, swaying like a rope bridge. Its trick is to come apart: an arm unwinds into a snare, a leg becomes a lasso, and in a moment a victim is bound hand and foot.

It strangles one foe at a time, holding the others off with whipping lines. It is mindless and never flees. Blades can cut it, but it re-ties itself as fast as it is severed unless the cords are burned. Sailors, bandits and hangmen are said to make them from old ropes that have held something heavy. Some guard ships' holds and gallows yards.
