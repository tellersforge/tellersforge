# Energy-draining jelly

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · small flyer · hover · sci-fi*

A floating, translucent jelly the size of a head, with a dark, clawed membrane at its heart. It drifts slowly through ruins and wastes, and when it finds a victim, it clamps onto the head with small fangs and sucks the life-energy out. The victim weakens and pales, and falls if it is not torn away.

It is mindless and never flees. It can be cut off, but carefully. The best weapon is cold: ice, frost or freezing water makes it let go and drift away. Those who travel where they are found carry flasks of chilled water.
