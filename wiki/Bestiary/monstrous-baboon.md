# Monstrous baboon

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md) · large biped · climber · fantasy, myth, horror*

An old, giant baboon of the mountains, grizzled and cunning, with long fangs and a cruel intelligence. It laughs as it seizes its prey, a sound that chills the blood. It demands a maiden from each village as tribute, and villages that refuse suffer.

It fights hard when cornered, with bites and claws. In the old tales, a hero, usually with a faithful dog, finally kills it. Until then, villages pay the tribute and hide their daughters.
