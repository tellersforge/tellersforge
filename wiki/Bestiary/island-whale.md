# Island whale

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · gigantic swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, myth*

A whale so enormous that it lies on the surface for years at a time, grown over with sand, shells, gulls and scrubby bushes, until sailors lower their boats and land on it, thinking they have found an island. They walk its back, gather driftwood and light fires for their supper.

The whale does not care. It does not notice them at all until it decides, for reasons of its own, to dive. When it goes down it sucks in the sea around it and swallows anything floating nearby, ships included. There is no fighting it. The stories are full of sailors living in its belly afterwards.
