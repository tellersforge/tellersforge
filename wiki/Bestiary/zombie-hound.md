# Zombie hound

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · horror, modern, post-apocalyptic, fantasy*

A dead dog with its hide peeling away in strips, showing muscle and bone beneath. It is still fast, still hungry, and still knows how to hunt.

Zombie hounds burst through windows and doors, and they hunt in packs, running down anyone who tries to flee. They do not bark or growl, only pant and snap. They have no minds and never flee. They are hard to hit and harder to outrun, and survivors learn to barricade the ground floor and fight from above.

## Not to be confused with

the *[skeletal hound](skeletal-hound.md)*, bare bones that hunt in silence; the zombie hound is a rotting dead dog, fast and hungry.
