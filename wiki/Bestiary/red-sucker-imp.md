# Red sucker-imp

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amphibian](../Species_Families/amphibian.md) · small biped · climber · fantasy, myth, horror*

A small, red, frog-like creature with sucker pads on its long fingers. It waits in fig trees until someone sits in the shade below, then drops down, clamps on and drains their blood through its fingers.

When it has drunk, it swallows the victim whole and spits them out again, alive but a little shorter. It flees when badly hurt. People who sit under fig trees keep watch on the branches, and anyone who has shrunk is suspected of having met one.
