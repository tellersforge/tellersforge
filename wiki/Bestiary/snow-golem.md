# Snow golem

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium biped · walk · fantasy*

Packed snow and ice shaped into a squat, broad guardian, with eyes of black stone and arms like snowdrifts. It stands in high passes and at the gates of northern halls, where it can go unnoticed for a whole winter as just another heap of snow.

It hurls packed ice with deadly force and batters foes with heavy fists. It is mindless and never flees. But it is a creature of the cold, and in a thaw it grows sluggish and soft, sagging until it barely moves at all. Travellers caught by one light fires, and those who plan ahead wait for spring. In the deep of winter, nothing gets past it.
