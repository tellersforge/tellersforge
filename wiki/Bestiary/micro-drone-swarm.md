# Micro-drone swarm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium swarm · flyer · sci-fi*

A buzzing cloud of insect-sized drones, thousands of them, acting as one. From a distance it looks like smoke or a swarm of bees. Up close, each one carries a tiny blade or sting, and together they flay a target in moments.

The swarm envelops its prey, slicing and stinging from every side, and armour offers little protection against something that finds every gap. It is mindless and never flees. Swatting individual drones is hopeless. Fire, water, electrical pulses or sealed rooms are the usual defences, and some soldiers carry smoke to confuse its sensors. Its makers are always far away, watching through the swarm's eyes.
