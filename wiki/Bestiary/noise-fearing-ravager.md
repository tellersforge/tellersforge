# Noise-fearing ravager

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth*

A horned beast with a long lion-like mane and a great mouth, which rises from the sea or comes down from the mountains every new year. It devours crops, livestock and villagers, and whole families once hid in their houses until it left.

It is very hard to kill, but it has a weakness. It dreads the colour red and loud bangs, and flees from both. So at every new year villages hang red banners, paint their doors red and set off firecrackers all night. The beast has not been seen in some places for generations, and nobody intends to stop the celebrations.
