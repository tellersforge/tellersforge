# Confusing-gaze burrower

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · large biped · burrower · fantasy*

A hulking, beetle-bodied creature, taller than a man, with huge claws that dig through stone like mud. It tunnels through the deep rock, and bursts out of walls in a spray of rubble. Its face has four large, dark, glittering eyes, and anyone who looks into them loses their sense of where they are and what they meant to do.

It is a hunter, but not a mindless one, and it flees when badly hurt. Those who have fought one learn to look at its feet and claws, never its face. Miners who hear the grinding of rock in an empty gallery down tools and run.
