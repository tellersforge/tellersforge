# All-knowing white beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · large quadruped · walk · sentient · fantasy, myth*

A white, horned beast, something like an ox or a lion, with calm eyes and a deep voice. It appears only to wise rulers, and it speaks every tongue. It knows the names, natures and weaknesses of every spirit and monster in the world, and it once told them all to a king, who had them written down.

It is a bringer of knowledge rather than a fighter, though it will butt an attacker with its horns and flee when badly hurt. Its image is hung over doorways to keep evil away. Scholars would give anything for one conversation with it. So far, it prefers kings.
