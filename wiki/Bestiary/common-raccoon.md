# Common raccoon

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Mustelid and small mammal](../Species_Families/mustelid-and-small-mammal.md) · small quadruped · climber · fantasy, historical, modern*

A stocky, grey animal with a black mask across its eyes, a ringed tail and nimble hands that can open latches, lids and knots. Raccoons live in forests near water and, more and more, in towns, where they raid bins, gardens and pantries at night.

They are clever and curious, and a camp that is not locked down will be raided by morning. A raccoon avoids a fight and flees at the first wound, but a cornered one bites and scratches, and it can carry sickness. Some people find them charming; people with gardens rarely do.
