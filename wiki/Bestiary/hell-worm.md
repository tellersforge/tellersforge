# Hell worm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Planar](../Species_Families/planar.md) · huge serpentine · burrower · fantasy, horror*

A segmented worm as wide as a castle gate, plated in cooling black rock with red fire glowing through the seams. It swims through molten stone in the fire pits the way an eel swims through mud. When demon hosts go to war, hell worms are driven up beneath the enemy lines.

The ground heaves, splits and glows, and the worm bursts up through it, trailing lava and swallowing whole ranks. It is mindless and never retreats, and there is little point in running once the earth cracks. Commanders who fight demons learn to watch for warm ground and rising smoke, and to keep their soldiers on bare stone well away from any fissure.
