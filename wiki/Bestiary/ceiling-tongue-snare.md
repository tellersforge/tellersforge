# Ceiling tongue snare

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md) · small other · slow · sci-fi, horror*

A fleshy sac the size of a barrel clinging to a high ceiling, grey-pink and pulsing slowly. From it hangs a long, sticky tongue that reaches down to the floor and looks, in bad light, like a rope, a vine or a dangling cable. It does nothing at all until something touches it.

Then the tongue sticks fast and snaps upward, reeling the victim toward a round mouth full of teeth. It is mindless and never flees. A sharp blade can cut the tongue if someone is quick, and fire makes the sac let go. Those who work in dark halls learn a simple rule: never pull on a rope you didn't hang.
