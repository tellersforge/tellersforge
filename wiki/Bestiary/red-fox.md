# Red fox

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · small quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, historical, modern*

A small russet hunter with a white-tipped brush, black stockings and a sharp, knowing face. Foxes live along hedgerows, woodland edges and, increasingly, the back alleys of towns, where they den under sheds and eat whatever the bins offer. They hunt mice with a high, arching pounce and raid poultry at night, sometimes killing far more than they can carry.

A fox wants nothing to do with people and bolts at the first wound or the first shout. That caution has made it the hero of a thousand folk tales, always the clever one who talks its way out of the trap. Farmers are less charmed, and in some lands riders in bright coats still chase it across the fields with hounds.
