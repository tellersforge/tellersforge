# Bloated zombie

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · medium biped · slow · horror, modern, post-apocalyptic, fantasy*

A corpse swollen to bursting with gas and bile, its skin stretched tight and mottled green and purple. It lurches along slowly, gurgling, and a sour smell of rot rolls ahead of it.

It does not need to bite. When it reaches its prey, or when anyone strikes it, it splits open in a spray of rotten fluid that burns the eyes, sickens the stomach and spreads disease. It has no mind and never flees. Those who know its ways kill it from a distance, with arrows, spears or thrown rocks, and stay well clear of the burst.
