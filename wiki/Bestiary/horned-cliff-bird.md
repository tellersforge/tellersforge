# Horned cliff-bird

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hybrid and chimeric](../Species_Families/hybrid-and-chimeric.md) · large winged quadruped · flyer · fantasy, myth*

A winged, scaled beast with a bearded human-like face, antlers, a long tail and talons. It nests on river bluffs and carries off people. Its image is painted on cliffs as a warning.

It fights to the death. River peoples paint the cliffs where it nests and keep away. Some say it was killed long ago by a brave chief; others say it is still up there.
