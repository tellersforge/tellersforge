# Bone colossus

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Undead and deathless](../Species_Families/undead-and-deathless.md) · gigantic biped · slow · fantasy, horror*

A towering shape built from thousands of fused skeletons. Its legs are braided from thigh-bones, its arms are made of spines and ribs, and its head is a great mass of skulls. Every bone twitches and clicks, as if each dead one was trying to get free.

It moves slowly, but with each step it crushes buildings and walls, and it can tear the roof off a house. It has no mind and never flees. Necromancers raise it as a siege engine, and whole towns have been flattened by one. The only hope is to break the magic that binds it, or to hack it apart one bone at a time.
