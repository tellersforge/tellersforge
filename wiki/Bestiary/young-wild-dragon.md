# Young wild dragon

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · large winged quadruped · flyer · fantasy*

A dragon in its awkward years: bigger than a horse, strong enough to fly, and not yet cunning. It has no speech and no patience. It hunts by seeing something large and flying at it, and it tests itself against anything that looks like a challenge, from a herd of bulls to a column of armed riders.

This recklessness is its weakness. A young dragon will pick a fight it cannot win, and it has not yet learned that spears can hurt. When it is badly wounded it breaks off, screaming, and flies home to lick its burns. It remembers, though, and it comes back bigger.
