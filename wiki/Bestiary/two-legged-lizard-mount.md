# Two-legged lizard mount

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · large biped · walk · sci-fi*

A squat, big-jawed lizard that walks on two thick hind legs and carries riders across rough country, deserts and rocky hills. It has a heavy tail for balance, tiny forearms and a mouthful of blunt teeth. It can go for days without water, and its scaly hide shrugs off sun and sand.

It is famously ill-tempered. It bucks unfamiliar riders, bites if spurred and sulks for hours if rushed. Those who ride them say that one it respects is worth a dozen horses, but it takes weeks of stubbornness to earn that respect. When badly hurt, it bolts, rider or no.
