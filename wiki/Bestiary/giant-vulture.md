# Giant vulture

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · large flyer · flyer · fantasy, post-apocalyptic*

A bald, reeking scavenger bird as big as a man, with a naked red neck, a hooked beak and a ruff of dirty feathers. Giant vultures follow armies and caravans across dry country, circling high overhead in slow patient spirals. When something falls behind, they come down.

They prefer the dead, but they are not choosy about how dead. A flock will mob a wounded man or a lame horse, hissing and tearing, and they carry sicknesses from the carrion they eat. Yet they are cowards: one good wound sends a bird flapping heavily away. Desert peoples call them the patient ones, and soldiers say that when the vultures gather over a camp, someone there already knows he is dying.
