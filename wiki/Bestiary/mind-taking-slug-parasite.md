# Mind-taking slug parasite

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · tiny serpentine · slow · horror, sci-fi*

A dark, leech-like slug the length of a finger, wet and ridged. It waits in shadowed places and flings itself at an open mouth, slithering down the throat before the victim can spit. Within moments it has wrapped itself around the brain stem, and the host is no longer the host.

The taken keep walking and talking, and are only a little too calm. They are joined, all of them, in a spreading hive that thinks as one. They share every slug they carry with their friends, and a town can fall in a week. The slugs are mindless and never flee. Survivors keep their mouths covered, watch for flat voices, and test each other with heat or salt.
