# Piranha shoal

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · medium swarm · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A shoal of small, deep-bodied river fish with red bellies and interlocking razor teeth. Alone, a piranha is a timid scavenger. Together, in low dry-season rivers where food is short, they become a single boiling mass that can strip an injured animal to the bone faster than a man can climb out.

They are drawn by splashing and above all by blood. River folk bathe in the rapids, where shoals do not gather, and never wade with an open wound. A shoal keeps feeding as long as blood is in the water, then scatters as quickly as it came.
