# Desert burrow rat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md) · small quadruped · fast runner · sci-fi*

A lean, dog-sized rodent with sandy fur, big ears and teeth for gnawing through anything. It nests in canyon walls and comes out at dusk to raid camps and farms for food, water and anything shiny. On desert worlds, it is as common as dust.

A pack will circle a camp at a distance, then dart in when backs are turned. Individually they are cowardly and flee at the first real wound. When starving, though, they grow bold. Farmers keep dogs and traps, and young pilots and hunters practise their aim on them. In a bad year, a plague of them can ruin a harvest.
