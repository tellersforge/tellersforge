# Hornet swarm

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium swarm · flyer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A hornet nest is quiet until someone steps on it. Then the ground or the eaves erupt, and a furious cloud of wasps as long as a thumb pours out, droning like a sawmill. Each sting burns like a hot nail. A few are agony; a few hundred can stop a heart, and the swarm does not give up once it has a target. It follows a running man across fields and through hedges, and it waits above a pond for him to come up for air.

Farmers burn nests at night, when the hornets are slow and home. Soldiers have been known to toss a nest over a wall, and armies have learned to fear the old trick of hurling one into a trench or a tunnel.
