# Large theropod

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · huge biped · fast runner · prehistoric, fantasy*

A big, agile two-legged meat-eater, somewhat smaller than the tyrants but faster and lighter, with a narrow head full of blade teeth and strong three-fingered hands. It often hunts in pairs, ambushing large plant-eaters from cover, slashing with its claws and biting deep.

It fights to the death. Where tyrants crush, this hunter cuts, leaving long wounds that bleed its prey out. It is the terror of forest edges and river valleys. Those who share its world dread the sight of two of them working together, since one drives and the other waits.
