# Sound-hunting armoured stalker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Unknown humanoid](../Species_Families/unknown-humanoid.md) · large biped · fast runner · sci-fi, horror*

An eyeless predator as tall as a man, plated in dark armour like a beetle's shell, on long spindly legs built for speed. Its head opens like the petals of a flower to show great folds of ear. It hears a whisper across a field and a heartbeat through a wall, and at any sound it charges, reaching its prey in moments.

It fights to the death, and blades and bullets glance off its plates. The only defence is silence. Survivors learn to go barefoot on sand paths, speak with their hands, and muffle everything that clinks. A dropped pot is a death sentence. Loud noise elsewhere draws it away, and the soft flesh inside the open head is its weakness.
