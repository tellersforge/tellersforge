# House cat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · tiny quadruped · climber · fantasy, historical, modern, sci-fi, post-apocalyptic*

A small, sleek hunter that chose to live with people on its own terms. Cats keep granaries, barns and ships free of rats and mice, and in return they take the warm spot by the fire and ignore whoever feeds them. They come in every colour, see well in near darkness and move with a silent, deliberate grace.

A cat is more trouble than danger: it hisses, scratches and flees at the first real hurt. Sailors will not leave port without one, and many cultures hold them sacred, lucky or suspicious depending on their colour. A witch's cat, a temple cat, a ship's cat: the animal is the same, only the story changes.
