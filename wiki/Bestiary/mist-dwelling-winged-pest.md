# Mist-dwelling winged pest

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · small flyer · flyer · horror*

A dog-sized flying insect that comes out of a strange mist, pale and many-legged, with a soft, pulpy body, gauzy wings and a long curved sting. It is drawn to light. On foggy nights they gather in buzzing, bumping clouds at lit windows and lamps, and when they break through, they sting whatever is there. The flesh swells, burns and blisters.

They are mindless and never flee. Anything larger in the mist eats them eagerly, and a lit window can draw worse things behind them. People caught in the fog learn to put out their lights, stuff the gaps around their shutters, and keep a torch ready to burn any that get in.
