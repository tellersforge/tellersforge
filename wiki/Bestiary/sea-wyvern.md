# Sea wyvern

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Draconic](../Species_Families/draconic.md) · large winged biped · flyer, swimmer · fantasy*

A sleek wyvern with blue-green scales and wings webbed and ribbed like a fish's fins. It hunts by folding those wings tight and plunging into the sea like a diving gull, then swimming after fish with strong strokes of its tail, as at home under water as in the air.

It harries ships at sea, circling mastheads and snatching men from the rigging or the deck, and it can drag a small boat over with its weight. It flees when badly hurt, diving deep. Sailors keep harpoons and nets ready when one follows in their wake. It nests on bare rock islands, in colonies loud with squabbling.
