# Haunted armour

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · medium biped · walk · fantasy, horror*

An empty suit of plate standing in a castle gallery, dusty, dented and apparently harmless. At night it walks. Its joints grate, its gauntlets clank, and the sound of it carries through the whole keep. The ghost that moves it was once a knight, a guard or a lord who died unwilling to give up his post.

It guards what it guarded in life, usually a hoard or a tomb, and anyone who touches the treasure is struck down by heavy iron fists. There is no mind left to reason with, and it never retreats. The ghost inside can slip out of the steel and pass through a wall when the armour is smashed. The surest cure is to find the old grievance and lay it to rest.
