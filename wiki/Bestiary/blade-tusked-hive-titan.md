# Blade-tusked hive titan

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md) · huge quadruped · walk · sci-fi*

A heavily plated swarm beast, big as a house, on four pillar-like legs. Its head carries a pair of enormous tusk-blades, curved and sharp, that sweep side to side as it walks.

It tramples through lines of soldiers and scythes everything in front of it. Its plates turn most weapons, and its wounds knit together as it fights. It is mindless and never flees. Soldiers facing one aim for its legs or its eyes, and pray for heavy weapons. Where it walks, the swarm follows through the gap it leaves.
