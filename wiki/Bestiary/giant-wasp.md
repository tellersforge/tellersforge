# Giant wasp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium flyer · flyer · fantasy*

A wasp as big as a hunting dog, striped yellow and black, with wings that thrum like a drum roll. It dives out of the sky, stings to paralyse, and flies off with its victim in its legs, back to a paper nest the size of a house hanging from a cliff or a dead tree. There the prey is kept alive, still and aware, as food for the young.

That last part is what people tell each other about at night. Giant wasps hunt in small groups and fight while the others fight, so a traveller who swats one soon has three. Herders cover their flocks with nets. Heroes who go into a nest to bring back a lost child come out talking very little about it.
