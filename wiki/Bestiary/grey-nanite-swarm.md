# Grey nanite swarm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · medium swarm · slow · sci-fi, horror*

A shimmering grey cloud of machines too small to see, drifting like fog or smoke. Everything it touches it takes apart: metal, stone, flesh, plastic, bone. It uses what it strips to build more of itself, and so the cloud grows with every meal.

It moves slowly, pouring across ground and flowing through vents, and it is utterly mindless. It never retreats. Where it has passed, only fine dust remains. Those who face it try sealed rooms, electrical pulses, fire hot enough to melt its parts, or a code to shut it down. The fear that one unchecked swarm could eat the world keeps its makers awake.
