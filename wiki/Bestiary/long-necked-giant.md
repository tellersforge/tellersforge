# Long-necked giant

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · gigantic quadruped · slow · prehistoric, fantasy*

A colossal plant-eater with a neck as long as a ship, a tail even longer and a body like a hill. It grazes the treetops of forests and plains, moving in herds, and its tread shakes the ground. When grown, it is too big for anything to hunt.

When threatened, it whips its tail, which cracks like thunder, and it tramples anything it can reach. It flees when badly hurt, which few manage. Scavenging bands follow its herds, since the herds clear paths through forests and leave clearings behind them, and they keep their distance.
