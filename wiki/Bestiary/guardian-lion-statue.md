# Guardian lion statue

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · large quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, historical*

A carved stone lion, or lion-dog, with a curled mane and a ball or cub beneath one paw. They stand in pairs at temple gates and palace doors, one with mouth open and one closed. For most who pass, they are only statues.

Those with ill intent find otherwise. The lions wake at the threshold, leap down and maul the intruder with stone jaws and claws. They are mindless and never flee, and they cannot be bribed or fooled by disguise, since they judge the heart. Honest visitors walk past untroubled. Temple keepers tend them with offerings, and thieves who study them try to break one before the other can wake.
