# Pack llama

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy, historical, modern*

A long-necked, woolly animal with a calm face, banana-shaped ears and a haughty expression. Llamas are the pack animals of high mountain peoples, carrying light loads along steep trails where the air is thin. They are sure-footed, need little food and provide wool and meat.

Llamas are gentle and calm, but they spit when annoyed, and a well-aimed gob of half-digested grass is hard to forget. A llama that is overloaded will lie down and refuse to move. They are guard animals too: a llama will chase a fox or a dog away from a flock of sheep.
