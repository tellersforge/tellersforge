# Hand-tailed water-dog

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · small swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, myth, horror*

A sleek, black water dog about the size of an otter, with a human hand at the tip of its tail. It lurks in deep pools and rivers, and when someone wades in, it grabs them with its tail-hand and drags them under to drown.

It bites and flees when badly hurt. People who live by rivers keep their children away from deep water and avoid wading alone. Drowned bodies with missing eyes, teeth and nails are blamed on it.
