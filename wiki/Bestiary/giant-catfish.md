# Giant catfish

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · large swimmer · swimmer · fantasy*

A whiskered river giant as long as a boat, with a flat head, tiny eyes and a mouth wide enough to swallow a calf. It lies in the mud at the bottom of deep, slow pools, sensing movement through its long barbels, and when something falls or swims into its water it rises and gulps. Swimmers, dogs and careless fishermen vanish in a swirl of brown water.

River peoples tell of old catfish that have lived in the same pool for generations and are treated almost as spirits, given offerings of meat to keep them fed. Others hunt them with great hooks and buffalo lines. It flees when badly hurt, back into the mud.
