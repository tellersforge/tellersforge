# Moray eel

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · small serpentine · swimmer · fantasy, historical, modern*

A thick, muscular eel with mottled or speckled skin, a wide gaping mouth and backward-curving teeth. Morays live in holes and crevices in reefs and rocky coasts, their heads poking out and their jaws slowly opening and closing as they breathe, which makes them look far fiercer than they are.

A moray will not chase anyone. But reach into its crevice after a lobster or a shell, and it bites hard and holds on, and those teeth are not easily shaken off. Divers learn to look before they reach. A cornered moray fights; otherwise it stays in its hole and watches.
