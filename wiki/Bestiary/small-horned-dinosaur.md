# Small horned dinosaur

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · medium quadruped · walk · prehistoric, fantasy*

A sheep-sized plant-eater with a parrot-like beak, a small bony frill and sometimes a short horn. It lives in herds in deserts and dry scrub, cropping tough plants and digging for roots, and it is the prey of choice for desert raptors and larger hunters.

It flees when threatened, since it has little to fight with. When cornered, it bites with its beak, and the bite can break fingers. It flees when badly hurt. Anyone caught in its country hunt it for meat and hides, and they learn where its herds water.
