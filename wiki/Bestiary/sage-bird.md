# Sage bird

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · huge flyer · flyer · sentient · fantasy, myth*

An immense bird of great age, with feathers of every colour dulled to bronze and gold, that nests atop the tree at the centre of the world. Its wings can cover a valley. It raises lost children as its own, speaks with the wisdom of centuries, and gives counsel to kings who are wise enough to climb to it.

Those it favours receive a feather. Burned in need, the feather summons the bird, and its smoke heals wounds that no physician could close. It fights only in defence of its nest and its children, and it flies away when badly hurt, which is almost never. To offend it is to lose the world's best adviser.
