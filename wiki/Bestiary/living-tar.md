# Living tar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · medium amorphous · slow · fantasy, horror, post-apocalyptic*

A black, stinking pool of sticky tar, bubbling faintly, lying in a pit or in the shadows of a tunnel. It looks like a natural seep until it moves.

It clings to whatever touches it and drags it under, slowly, into its sticky body. It is mindless and never flees. It is very hard to pull free from once stuck. It burns fiercely when lit, which is both a weapon and a danger. People who have to cross its territory lay planks and throw sand over it, and carry fire with care.
