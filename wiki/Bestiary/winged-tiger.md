# Winged tiger

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · large winged quadruped · flyer · fantasy, myth*

A tiger with a pair of feathered wings, striped in black and orange, that flies over mountains and forests. It is a beast of topsy-turvy justice: it devours the honest and the virtuous, and brings gifts of game to the wicked.

It fights to the death, with bites and claws. It is a terror to good people and an ally of thieves and tyrants. Villages that see it overhead wonder which of their neighbours is secretly a villain.
