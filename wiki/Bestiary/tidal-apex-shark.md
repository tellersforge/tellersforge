# Tidal apex shark

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Aquatic](../Species_Families/aquatic.md) · huge swimmer · swimmer · sci-fi*

A giant, armour-plated shark of an ocean world, grey-blue and scarred, with a mouth full of teeth like knives. It hunts the reefs and shallows, and is the terror of reef villages.

It bites through boats to reach the riders inside, and it fights to the death. Reef peoples mark its territory with coloured buoys and avoid it, and hunters who want its teeth go in numbers and with spears. Legend says it cannot be killed, only driven off, and the scars it carries suggest many have tried.
