# Grey stone ooze

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · medium amorphous · slow · fantasy*

A slick grey ooze that looks exactly like damp stone, lying in hollows on cave floors or along the walls of old tunnels. It could be mistaken for a puddle of wet rock until it moves.

It lashes out at passing feet with a sudden pseudopod, burning through boots and skin. It is mindless and never flees. It moves slowly and prefers to wait. Delvers learn to probe suspicious patches of floor with a staff, and to notice when a patch of stone seems to shift. It is also known to eat metal, and a careless adventurer can lose a good sword in it.
