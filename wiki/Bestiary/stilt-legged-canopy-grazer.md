# Stilt-legged canopy grazer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · huge quadruped · walk · sci-fi*

A towering, long-legged browser of jungle worlds, taller than most trees, with a long neck and a small head that feeds on the canopy. It walks slowly and calmly through the forest, rarely noticing anything below.

When startled, it stampedes, and its steps can crush a camp flat. It flees when badly hurt. Travellers in the jungle keep their camps away from its paths, and hunters know that its meat can feed a village for weeks. Some peoples ride them, perched in baskets on their backs.
