# Giant sea turtle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · large swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, modern, myth*

A sea turtle big enough to carry a rider, its shell crusted with barnacles and trailing weed. It swims slowly and steadily across warm seas, grazes on sea-grass meadows and hauls itself up on quiet beaches to lay its eggs in the sand.

It is placid and hard to anger. Swimmers can hold onto its shell, and some coastal peoples tame them to carry messages or pull small rafts. The one sure way to provoke it is to go near its nest. Then it turns, rams boats and bites hard with a beak that can crush a leg. Badly hurt, it swims off, and its shell makes it hard to hurt at all.
