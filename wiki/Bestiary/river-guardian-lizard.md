# River guardian-lizard

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · huge swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, myth*

A giant lizard, or sometimes a great shark-like fish, that guards a river pool, a harbour or a stretch of coast. It protects some families and peoples and is the bane of others. It breathes water and commands it, raising waves and whirlpools.

It drowns those who show disrespect, and it fights to the death to defend its waters. Its hide is thick and hard to wound. Those it protects bring it gifts and speak of it with respect. Those it hates stay away from the water.
