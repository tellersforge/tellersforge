# Foxfire fox

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · small quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth*

A small fox with many tails, each one sparking pale, cold fire at the tip. At night it runs through forests and marshes with its tails glowing, and it conjures ghost-lights and small illusions to lead travellers astray.

It is a trickster rather than a killer. It bites and flees at the first wound. People who see ghost-lights in the marshes stay on the path, and those who follow the lights often end up in bogs or lost in the woods.
