# Irradiated shore crab

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Crustacean](../Species_Families/crustacean.md) · medium biped · swimmer · post-apocalyptic*

A mutant crab the height of a man that walks upright on its back legs, with a thick, mottled shell, heavy pincers and small stalked eyes. It lives on the flooded coasts and in shallow lagoons around drowned cities, half buried in mud and debris, and it rises from the water to snap at anyone who wanders near.

Its shell is hard enough to turn bullets. Only the face, between the eyes and above the mouth, can be cracked. It fights to the death. Shore folk hunt it with spears aimed at the face, and its meat, carefully cooked, feeds a whole family for days.
