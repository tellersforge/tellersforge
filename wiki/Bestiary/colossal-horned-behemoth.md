# Colossal horned behemoth

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · gigantic quadruped · walk · fantasy, sci-fi*

An armoured beast as large as any long-necked giant of the ancient world, with a massive horned head, a hide like overlapping iron plates and legs as thick as tower columns. It walks with a heavy, rolling gait that shakes the ground.

When it charges, nothing stops it. It lowers its head and runs at walls, gates and fortresses, and flattens them in one blow. It has no mind to speak of and never flees. Armies can only try to turn it aside, dig trenches in its path or get out of the way. Once it starts running, the only hope is that it runs somewhere else.
