# Gaze-killing catfox

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Feline](../Species_Families/feline.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy, myth, horror*

A wiry beast born of an unlucky pairing between a wildcat and a fox, with a cat's flat face, a fox's brush and eyes the colour of old brass. It lurks near farms and sheepfolds, and when it looks at livestock with ill intent, the animals sicken and die. Some say its stare can kill a person, too.

It is shy and flees once badly hurt, but it is also very hard to kill. Ordinary shot passes through it, and folk wisdom says only a blessed bullet will do, or showing it its own reflection so that its glare turns back on itself. Farmers who suspect one hang polished mirrors in their barns.
