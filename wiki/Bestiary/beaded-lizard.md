# Beaded lizard

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · small quadruped · slow · fantasy, historical, modern*

A slow, thick-bodied desert lizard whose skin looks like it is sewn with black and pink or orange beads. Beaded lizards spend most of their lives underground, coming out at dusk to raid nests for eggs and baby animals. They are shy and sluggish and would much rather hide than fight.

Pick one up, though, and it clamps its jaws and chews, grinding venom into the wound. The bite is excruciating and hard to pry loose. Few people die of it, but many wish they had. Desert herders know to leave these lizards well alone, and there are old tales that their breath alone can kill, which is not true.
