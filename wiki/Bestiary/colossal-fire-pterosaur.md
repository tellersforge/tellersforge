# Colossal fire pterosaur

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · gigantic winged biped · fast flyer · modern, sci-fi, prehistoric*

A pterosaur so large that it can lift a locomotive or a small ship in its claws, with vast leathery wings, a long crested head and a glowing, heated breast. It flies faster than sound, and its passing is a thunderclap that shatters windows across a city.

It hunts for its meals among trains, trucks and boats, snatching them up and carrying them to its nest in a volcano. Buildings beneath its path are flattened by the shock of its flight alone. It is hard to shoot down and harder to hit. Badly hurt, it climbs away on the winds and heals in the fire of its mountain.
