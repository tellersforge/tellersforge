# Woolly rhinoceros

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · walk · prehistoric, fantasy, historical*

A shaggy rhinoceros of the ice-age tundra, heavy-shouldered and low-headed, with a thick coat of reddish hair and two horns, the front one long and flattened like a blade. It sweeps snow aside with that horn to reach grass beneath. It is near-sighted, short-tempered and startles easily.

When something startles it, it charges, and it keeps charging while it can. Hunters say you can step aside if you keep your nerve, since it cannot turn quickly, but few people have the nerve. Cave paintings show it with its head down and its horn forward, which is how most people saw it last.

## Not to be confused with
