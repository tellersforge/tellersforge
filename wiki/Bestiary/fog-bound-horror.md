# Fog-bound horror

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · huge other · walk · horror, modern, fantasy*

A thick grey fog rolls in off the sea or up from a valley and does not lift. Somewhere inside it, something enormous moves. People glimpse fragments: a limb as tall as a church tower, a wall of skin, a tentacle sliding past a streetlamp. Sound travels strangely in the fog, and screams seem to come from everywhere.

The horror hunts only inside its fog and is never seen whole. It strikes without warning, takes whoever strays from shelter, and cannot be driven off. Smaller things often travel in its mist, feeding on its leavings. Towns caught in it board their windows, stay indoors and wait. Those who try to cross the fog on foot rarely reach the other side.
