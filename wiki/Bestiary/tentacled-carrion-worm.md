# Tentacled carrion worm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · large many-legged · climber · fantasy, horror*

A many-legged grey worm the size of a horse, crowned with a cluster of twitching tentacles around its mouth. It crawls along walls and ceilings of tombs, sewers and battlefields, eating the dead. But it does not ignore the living. Its tentacles paralyse whatever they brush, and a paralysed man is carrion soon enough.

It is a scavenger at heart and flees when badly hurt. Grave-robbers and tomb explorers meet it often, and learn to look up. The worst thing about it is how it waits, very still, while a paralysed victim watches it come closer.
