# Spectral black dog

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · large quadruped · fast runner · myth, horror, historical, fantasy*

A huge black hound with burning red eyes, padding along old roads and lanes at night. It is silent, and it leaves no footprints.

It guards lanes, crossroads and churchyards, and it is said that anyone who sees it will die within the year. It fights to the death if attacked, but most people who see it simply run. Some say it is a guardian, protecting travellers from worse things. Others say it is death itself.
