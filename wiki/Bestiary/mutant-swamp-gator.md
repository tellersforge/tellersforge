# Mutant swamp gator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · large quadruped · swimmer · post-apocalyptic*

An alligator from the irradiated bayous, bloated to twice its forebears' size, its hide bubbled with tumours and its eyes glowing a sickly green at night. The water around its den tingles on the skin. It is slower to tire than any natural beast, and a great deal harder to kill.

It lies still in the shallows like a floating log until something wades close. Then the jaws close, the tail thrashes, and the prey is dragged under and rolled until it stops struggling. It fights to the death. Swamp folk steer their boats well clear of any log that seems a little too wide, and hunters who want its hide go in numbers, with long spears and a great deal of patience.
