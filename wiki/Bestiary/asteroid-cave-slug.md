# Asteroid cave slug

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · gigantic serpentine · slow · sci-fi*

A worm big enough to hide a starship in its gullet, living in the tunnels of dead asteroids. It needs no air, no warmth and almost no light. It feeds on the minerals of the rock it lies in and on whatever flies into its mouth, which it can mistake for a cave. Pilots who shelter in the wrong crater find the ground turning soft beneath them.

It moves slowly and thinks not at all. Once a ship is inside, the jaws close and the walls begin to squeeze. Escape means flying out of its mouth before the teeth close, or doing enough damage from the inside that it spits the ship back out. Prospectors listen for a faint, rhythmic tremor in asteroid rock and leave at once.
