# Heat-draining mould

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md) · tiny plant · rooted · fantasy*

A dull brown mould that grows on cold stone in deep places, spreading in slow patches toward any warmth. Walk close and the air turns icy. Touch it, and the heat is drawn out of the body so fast that fingers go numb and lips turn blue. Those who sleep beside it are found stiff and frosted in the morning.

Its great trick is that fire feeds it. A torch thrust into the patch makes it swell and spread across the floor in moments. Those who know it keep flame away and use ice, cold or simply a wide berth. Scholars have tried to cultivate it for cold stores, rarely for long.
