# Giant tick

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · small many-legged · climber · fantasy, horror*

A tick the size of a cat, flat and leathery when hungry, swollen and grape-purple when it has fed. It waits on overhanging branches along game trails and drops onto anything warm that passes beneath, burying its barbed head in the flesh and drinking. As it drinks it swells, and it passes on fevers that linger long after the bite has healed.

Foresters wear wide hats and check each other at every rest. A tick that has latched on must not be torn off, or the head stays in and festers; old woodsmen burn it loose or smother it with grease. It flees when badly hurt, but badly hurt usually means already full.
