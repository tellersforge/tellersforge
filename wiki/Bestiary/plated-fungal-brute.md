# Plated fungal brute

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Fungal](../Species_Families/fungal.md) · large biped · slow · horror, post-apocalyptic*

A host that has been dead and growing for years. The fungus has swollen it to twice a man's bulk and covered it in thick, bark-like plates that turn blades and bullets alike. It moves slowly, heavily, swinging arms like tree trunks, and it smashes through doors and walls to reach what it hears. Soft sacs on its body burst when struck, spilling choking spores into the air.

It cannot be outrun in a tight space and cannot be beaten up close. Survivors fight it from a distance, with fire if they have it, and aim for the soft places between the plates. Most simply run.
