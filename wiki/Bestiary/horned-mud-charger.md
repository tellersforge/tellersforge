# Horned mud charger

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · walk · sci-fi*

A thick-hided, single-horned brute of swampy lowlands, built like a barrel on stumpy legs. It digs a burrow deep in the mud, where the female lays a single large egg and guards it with blind rage. Its skin is caked in dried mud, which protects it from biting insects.

It charges anything that comes near its burrow, head down and horn first, and tosses intruders into the air. Near its young it fights to the death. Swampland villagers know the signs of a burrow and give it a wide berth, and hunters who want its horn go in numbers and always check the wind.
