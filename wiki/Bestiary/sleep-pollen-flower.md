# Sleep-pollen flower

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · medium plant · rooted · fantasy, myth*

A huge bloom of heavy, velvet petals, its colour shifting from cream to rose as the light changes, its scent like honey and warm bread. It puffs out a golden pollen that settles on the skin and in the lungs, and brings a slow, sweet drowsiness and dreams of home. Those who lie down beside it rarely get up again. Pale tendrils creep from the soil around the stem and feed on the sleepers, gently, over many days.

Meadows where it grows are strangely quiet and strewn with old bones in the grass. Wise travellers cross such fields with wet cloth over their faces, and keep one companion walking and talking the whole time.
