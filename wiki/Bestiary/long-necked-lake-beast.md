# Long-necked lake beast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · huge swimmer · swimmer · fantasy, modern, myth*

A shy creature of cold, deep lakes, with a small head at the end of a long neck, a heavy body and four broad flippers. It is glimpsed far more often than met: a dark shape at dusk, a head and neck rising out of the mist, a wake where no boat is.

It avoids people and prefers deep water. It becomes dangerous only when chased, and boats that pursue it too closely are overturned by a sudden heave from below. At the first wound it dives and vanishes, sometimes for years. Lakeside towns tell stories about it, sell carvings of it and quietly hope no one ever catches it.
