# Body-copying pod

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Plant-kind](../Species_Families/plant-kind.md) · medium plant · rooted · sci-fi, horror*

A leathery seed pod the size of a man, fallen from somewhere beyond the sky and put down among ordinary plants. At night it swells and splits, and inside grows a pale, wet copy of whoever is sleeping nearby: the same face, the same scar, the same voice when it wakes. By morning the sleeper is gone, crumbled to grey dust, and the copy walks into their life.

The replacement is calm, polite and utterly empty of warmth. It remembers everything and feels nothing. Whole towns are said to have been taken this way, one quiet night at a time. The only defence is not to sleep near a pod, and to burn every one you find.
