# Feral dog

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · runner · fantasy, historical, modern, post-apocalyptic, horror, sci-fi*

A dog that has forgotten its master, or never had one. Feral dogs are thin, scarred and mangy, all ribs and teeth, and they live wherever people leave scraps: rubbish tips, ruined towns, the edges of camps and slums. By day they slink and scavenge. After dark they grow bold, and a pack will follow a lone walker, circling closer as their courage rises.

They carry every sickness of the gutter, and a bite that looks trivial can fester. What makes them dangerous is numbers and hunger; one dog is easily driven off, but a pack fights on as long as its fellows do. In fallen cities they are often the first predators to return, and the last to leave.
