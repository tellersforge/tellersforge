# Seven-headed horse

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Equine and centauroid](../Species_Families/equine-and-centauroid.md) · large quadruped · flyer · fantasy, myth*

A shining white horse with seven heads, each with a flowing mane, that rose from the churning ocean when the gods stirred it. It is the king of horses and flies ahead of the gods' chariots, faster than the sun.

It is proud and beautiful, and it fights with hooves and bites, but it flees when badly hurt. Kings dream of owning one; a few claim to. It is said that a ruler who rides one will never lose a race or a war.
