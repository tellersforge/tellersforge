# Golden-horned hind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · fantasy, myth*

A sacred deer, slender and swift, with golden antlers that shine in the sunlight and bronze hooves that ring on stone. It outruns any hunter and any arrow, and it can run for a year without tiring. It lives in the wild places of sacred groves and high mountains, and it belongs to a divine keeper.

It flees at the first wound, but wounding it is the trouble. Killing one angers its keeper, and harming it brings curses, bad luck or worse. Heroes in the old tales captured it alive after chasing it for a year, and then had to face the keeper who came looking for it.
