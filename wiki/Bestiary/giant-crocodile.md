# Giant crocodile

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · huge quadruped · swimmer · fantasy, prehistoric*

A river crocodile longer than the width of a war galley, its back ridged like a fallen log, its jaws long enough to close around a horse. It waits submerged in muddy rivers with only its eyes and nostrils above the surface, patient for hours, and when prey comes to drink it bursts out, clamps on and drags it under, rolling to tear and drown.

It fights to the death and is terribly hard to hurt, with a hide like armour and a skull like stone. River peoples know its haunts and cross only at guarded fords. Some worship it; others hunt it with barbed spears and many boats. Its hide makes fine armour and its teeth are prized as charms.
