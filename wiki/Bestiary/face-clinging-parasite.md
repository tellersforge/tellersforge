# Face-clinging parasite

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · small many-legged · fast runner · sci-fi, horror*

A pale, bony crawler with long finger-like legs and a coiling, muscular tail. It waits inside a leathery egg until something warm leans over it, then springs out faster than the eye can follow and clamps itself over the victim's face, winding its tail around their throat.

The victim sinks into a deep sleep while the parasite plants an embryo inside them. Its blood is a burning acid, so cutting it loose can eat through the deck and the victim alike. It has no mind and never lets go of its own accord. Eventually it drops off, dead, and the victim wakes with a sore throat and no idea what is growing in their chest.
