# Fanged pet bear

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ursine](../Species_Families/ursine.md) · large quadruped · walk · sci-fi*

A heavy, shaggy beast with a round, friendly face, small eyes and a pair of long, curving fangs. It is kept as a family pet and children's guardian on its home world, and is a gentle, placid, rather lazy creature that loves to be scratched and fed sweets.

Threaten its household, though, and it becomes a savage fighter. It bites with those fangs, mauls with its claws and fights to the death for its family, especially the children. Visitors to such homes learn to move slowly and never raise a voice to a child. It is said that more burglars are stopped by these beasts than by locks.
