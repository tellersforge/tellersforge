# Phase-hunting spider

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · large many-legged · teleports · fantasy, horror*

A pale, long-legged spider the colour of old wax, which never bothers to stalk. It is simply somewhere else, and then it is beside you. The phase-hunter steps out of thin air, bites, and is gone before its victim can turn, reappearing a stone's throw away to wait for the poison to work. Those who survive speak of a faint pop, a smell like a snuffed candle and a pale shape at the corner of the eye.

Wizards prize its body for studies of travel through space, and hunters who take one are well paid. Taking one is the problem. Fighters learn to stand back to back and strike at the air where it appears, and they learn that a badly hurt phase-hunter blinks away for good.
