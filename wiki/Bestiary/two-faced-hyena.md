# Two-faced hyena

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Canine](../Species_Families/canine.md) · medium quadruped · fast runner · sentient · fantasy, myth, horror*

A demon that wears a handsome human face at the front of its head and hides a hyena's slavering jaws at the back, under a fall of long hair. It can pass for a charming stranger at a feast or a helpful guide on the road, laughing and joking, and it lures the careless away from company.

Once alone with its victim, it turns its head. It flees when badly hurt, but it can wear a new disguise within a day. Wise hosts look behind a guest's hair, and wise travellers never follow a stranger away from the fire.
