# Babbling flesh mass

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Amorphous and ooze](../Species_Families/amorphous-and-ooze.md) · medium amorphous · slow · fantasy, horror*

A heap of shifting, glistening flesh covered in eyes and mouths that open and close as it moves. The mouths never stop talking. They gibber, mutter, whisper and shriek in a hundred voices, none of them in any tongue, and the sound works its way into the head until thinking straight becomes impossible.

It oozes slowly across the floors of deep places and ruined vaults. Its victims stand dazed and slack-jawed, and the mass flows over them, its mouths biting as it pulls them down into itself. It has no mind to frighten and does not retreat. Survivors say the trick is to stop listening: wax in the ears, singing aloud, anything to drown out the babble while the others strike.
