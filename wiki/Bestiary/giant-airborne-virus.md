# Giant airborne virus

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Hive and collective](../Species_Families/hive-and-collective.md) · tiny flyer · flyer · sci-fi, horror*

A sickness grown monstrous: a virus the size of an insect, a spiked, glistening sphere that drifts and buzzes through the air on stubby, twitching filaments. It prefers the still air of ships and stations, where it swarms through the ducts in a faint grey haze.

Its sting injects a sickness that turns the victim's own body into a breeding ground. Within days, the sick cough out the next swarm. It has no mind and never flees. Crews that find it seal the vents, burn the filters and quarantine anyone who has been stung, and more than one station has been abandoned rather than cleaned.
