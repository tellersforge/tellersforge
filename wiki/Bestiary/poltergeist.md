# Poltergeist

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Energy and incorporeal](../Species_Families/energy-and-incorporeal.md) · small other · hover · sentient · horror, modern, fantasy, historical*

An unseen, spiteful presence that throws crockery, slams doors, tips sleepers from their beds and rattles houses for its own amusement. It is never seen, but its work is everywhere: broken plates, overturned furniture, scratches on the walls.

It is rarely deadly, but it can drive a family mad or out of their home. It fades away when exorcised by a priest or a wise woman. Some say it is drawn to children, or to houses where there is anger and grief.
