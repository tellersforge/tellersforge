# Surveillance drone

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Constructs and synthetics](../Species_Families/constructs-and-synthetics.md) · tiny flyer · hover · sci-fi*

A small hovering camera drone, no bigger than a bird, with whirring rotors and a lens that swivels. It drifts high over streets, rooftops and fences, or slips indoors, and tails anyone who looks out of place. It watches, records and sends everything home.

It does not fight. At the first sign of harm it darts away. Its danger is who it calls: armed guards, police, or attack drones arrive soon after it sees you. Thieves and rebels shoot them down, net them, or blind their lenses with paint, and learn to move under cover. In a watched city, the soft hum of rotors is a sound everyone learns to hate.
