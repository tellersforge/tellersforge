# Frost worm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Serpentine](../Species_Families/serpentine.md) · huge serpentine · burrower · fantasy*

A blue-white worm as long as a river barge that tunnels through glaciers and packed snow. Its hide is slick with frost, and ridges along its back glow faintly when it is hungry. Where it passes, the ice is left grooved and polished.

It hunts with sound. It surfaces near prey and gives a high, piercing trill that seizes the limbs of anyone who hears it, freezing them in place, and then it swallows them whole. It has no fear and never flees. Travellers in its country plug their ears with wax and cloth, and tell each other to keep moving, whatever they hear.
