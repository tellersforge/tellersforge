# One-legged fire crane

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Avian](../Species_Families/avian.md) · medium flyer · flyer · fantasy, myth*

A crane with red-spotted plumage and a single leg, which it hops on in a strange, jerking dance. Strange fires follow wherever it lands: haystacks smoulder, thatch bursts into flame and hearths flare up without warning.

It flees when badly hurt and flies off with a cry. People keep a close watch on it, because its fires spread fast. Some say it is a spirit of fire itself, and others that it only likes to watch things burn.
