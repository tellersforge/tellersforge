# Six-legged pack lizard

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · medium many-legged · fast runner · sci-fi*

A lean, six-legged raptor-lizard of alien grasslands, fast and agile, with a long neck, sharp teeth and hooked claws on all its feet. Its scales are the colour of dry grass, and it runs with a strange, rippling stride.

It hunts in shrieking packs across open plains. The packs run prey down in coordinated hunts, surrounding it and attacking from all sides. They fight as long as the pack fights. Plains travellers keep watch for them, circle their wagons at night, and learn to recognise the shrieks that carry for miles across the grass.
