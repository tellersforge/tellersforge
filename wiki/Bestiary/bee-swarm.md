# Bee swarm

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Insectoid and arachnid](../Species_Families/insectoid-and-arachnid.md) · medium swarm · flyer · fantasy, historical, modern*

An angry, humming cloud of honeybees defending their hive. Honeybees live in colonies of thousands in hollow trees, rock crevices and hives kept by people, and they work together like a single mind. When the hive is threatened, they pour out, stinging anything that moves.

A single sting is painful; hundreds can kill. Bees chase intruders over long distances and wait above water for them to come up for air. People who keep bees wear veils and use smoke to calm them. A swarm fights while the hive is threatened, then drifts back.
