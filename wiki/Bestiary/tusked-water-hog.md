# Tusked water-hog

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Ungulate](../Species_Families/ungulate.md) · large quadruped · walk · sci-fi*

A heavy, grey, thick-skinned beast with a pair of stubby tusks, broad feet and a great love of mud and water. It plods along at its own pace, grunting contentedly, and is almost impossible to hurry. Farmers on many worlds keep them as beasts of burden, to haul carts, drag ploughs and pull stumps.

It is placid and good-natured, and will wallow for hours if given the chance. Harass it, though, and it lowers its head and gores with those tusks. Once badly hurt, it heads for the nearest water. Its meat is edible if not very good, and a family's hog is often counted as part of the household.
