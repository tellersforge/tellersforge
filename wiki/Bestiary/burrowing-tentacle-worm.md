# Burrowing tentacle worm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Cephalopod and mollusc](../Species_Families/cephalopod-and-mollusc.md) · huge serpentine · burrower · sentient · horror, sci-fi*

A vast worm with a squid-like head, thick as a castle tower, that bores through the deepest rock. It burns its way through stone with heat, and it can shake the ground to call up earthquakes, burying its foes in rubble. It is ancient, clever and patient, and it never forgets a slight.

It fights to the death, and it hunts anyone who steals one of its eggs, however long it takes. Underground peoples treat it with the fear due to a god. The tremors it leaves behind are felt in cities far above.
