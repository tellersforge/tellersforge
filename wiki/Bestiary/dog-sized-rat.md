# Dog-sized rat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md) · medium quadruped · walk · fantasy, sci-fi, post-apocalyptic*

A rat as large as a wolf, whether bred by mutation, sorcery or something in the water. Its fur is patchy, its eyes are pink and clever, and it has learned that people are meat. Packs of them den in ruins, collapsed tunnels and the dead quarters of cities, and they hunt the way wolves do: watching, waiting, cutting a straggler off from the group.

They fight while the pack fights. Scavengers in broken lands travel together, keep a fire going and hang their food high, and they burn what the rats have touched, because the bite carries disease. A pack that loses its boldest members will slink off and try again another night.
