# Horned white ape

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Simian](../Species_Families/simian.md) · large biped · climber · sci-fi*

A white-maned ape of cold highland forests, taller than a man when it stands, with a single curved horn on its brow and long, yellowed fangs. It stalks from rocks and branches, moving silently for something so large, and drops on its prey from above.

Its bite carries a poison that kills within hours unless treated, and many who survive the attack die on the way home. When badly hurt, it retreats up into the rocks. Highland villagers keep antidotes and burn the forests' edges to keep them away. Its mane and horn are valued as trophies, and some warriors wear them to show courage.
