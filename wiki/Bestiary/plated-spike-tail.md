# Plated spike-tail

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Reptilian](../Species_Families/reptilian.md) · huge quadruped · slow · prehistoric, fantasy*

A back-plated dinosaur with a small head, a hunched back and a row of tall bony plates along its spine. Its tail ends in four long spikes, and it swings them with lethal force. It moves slowly through forests and plains, browsing low plants.

When danger comes, it turns its back toward it, spikes ready, and swings. The spikes can impale a hunter or tear open a predator. It fights while it can. Hunters treat it as a slow, stupid, dangerous thing, and they give it plenty of room.
