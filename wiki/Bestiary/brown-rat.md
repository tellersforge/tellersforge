# Brown rat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*[Rodent](../Species_Families/rodent.md) · tiny quadruped · climber · fantasy, historical, modern, sci-fi, horror, post-apocalyptic*

A grey-brown rodent with a naked tail, sharp yellow teeth and an endless appetite. Rats live wherever people live: sewers, ships, granaries, cellars, rubbish tips and walls. They gnaw through wood, lead and sacks, eat grain, candles and each other, and breed faster than any trap or cat can keep up with.

A single rat flees light, noise and the first wound. The danger is in what they carry: fleas, filth and plague, which have killed more people than any war. Cities and ships wage a constant, losing war against them. Where rats have spread, sickness is never far behind.
