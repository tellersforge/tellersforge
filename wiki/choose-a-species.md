# Choose a species


How to find your character's species without reading hundreds of entries ([Rules, chapter 3, step 1](Rules/03-making-a-character.md)). Every playable race has a **number**; each link goes to its species page, where its breed block shows the same number and all its numbers.

1. **Starters first.** Plain, iconic choices, each with one line on what it's like. Most players find their pick here.
2. **Finding lists.** Pick a genre that sounds like your game, then a group within it. A race can appear in more than one list.
3. **Genres are for finding, not fencing.** Any race the Game Master agrees to can join any game; talking animals turn up in the most classic fantasy.
4. The Game Master may give a short list of numbers for their world. **HP** marks high-power races, which need the Game Master's approval.
