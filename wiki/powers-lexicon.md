# Powers lexicon


Every power in Teller's Forge. Powers use the same dice machinery as skills. **Minor → major** shows a power's range; **costumes** show how the same power looks when it comes from arcane study, a god, psionics, technology, mutation, spirits, a pact, inner discipline, folk magic or legend. Cost is the table's choice.
