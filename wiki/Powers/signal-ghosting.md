# Signal Ghosting

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Being there to the eye and nowhere to the machine. Cameras record an empty corridor, motion sensors sleep, heat scanners see only a smear of background warmth. The user walks past the guard post in plain sight, and the only thing that notices is the guard, if he happens to look up from his screens.

It is the chosen gift of infiltrators in ages that trust machines more than people. At its faintest the user is a blur, a glitch the operator blames on dust. At its strongest they are simply absent from every record. It does nothing at all against a dog, a nosy clerk or an old-fashioned tripwire, which is why careful guards keep all three.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** hide from sensors and cameras
- **Minor → major:** a blur → electronically invisible
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The sensors catch a flicker and raise a quiet alert. Spectacular failure: the user shows up loud and clear, sometimes in several places at once.

## Not to be confused with

*[Nondetection](nondetection.md)*, which hides from magical senses and divination; Signal Ghosting fools cameras and sensors and nothing else.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a cloak woven with emitters or an implant that feeds false data to every sensor in range.
- **Arcane:** a charm of the unseen, turning lenses aside the way old wards turned aside the evil eye.
