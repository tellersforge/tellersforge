# Plant Animation

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The forest wakes and walks. Vines coil and strike like snakes, hedges shuffle to close a gap, and old trees pull their roots from the earth with a groan of wood and soil. The awakened plants move slowly but hit terribly, and nothing short of fire or axes will stop them.

At its smallest the gift sends a few grasping tendrils against an enemy's ankles. At its greatest a whole grove marches on a fortress and tears it down. It is a rare, ancient gift, most often held by forest guardians and the eldest of the green peoples. The trees remember who woke them, and they are not always pleased afterwards.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/END/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** awaken trees and plants to move and fight
- **Minor → major:** grasping vines → marching trees
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The plants stir and settle back. Spectacular failure: they wake angry and act on their own, trampling friend and foe.

## Not to be confused with

*[Animate Object](animate-object.md)*, which gives motion to lifeless things; Plant Animation wakes living plants that already have a slow will of their own.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** the voice of the forest's spirit, calling the trees to walk.
- **Arcane:** a sigil of green fire drawn in the air, and the bark cracks as the tree stands.
