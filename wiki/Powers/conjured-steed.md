# Conjured Steed

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A call into the dark, and something answers with hoofbeats. The summoner whistles or speaks, and a mount appears: a grey horse that leaves no prints, a great hound fit to carry a rider, a sleigh that needs no reindeer. At its smallest it is a pale, silent horse good for one night's ride. At its greatest it is a chariot drawn through the clouds, or a vessel that sails on nothing at all.

The steed is loyal and tireless, but strange. Animals shy from it, it does not eat, and it fades at dawn or when dismissed. Messengers and knights errant value it above gold, and in old tales it carries the hero to places no living horse could go.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** call or create a mount or vehicle
- **Minor → major:** phantom horse → flying chariot
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing comes, or the steed is thin and wavering and fades after a short way. Spectacular failure: it arrives wild, or bolts with the rider still aboard.

## Not to be confused with

*[Summon Creature](summon-creature.md)*, which calls a real beast to fight or help; the Conjured Steed is made to carry you and does little else.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a circle traced in the air, and a horse of smoke and silver steps out of it.
- **Divine:** a white steed sent from above, shining faintly and patient as a saint.
- **Spirit:** a spirit horse from the other side, with eyes like wet stones and no sound to its hooves.
