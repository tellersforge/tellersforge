# Reflex Precognition

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The next heartbeat, shown in advance. In a fight, the bearer sees the blow before it is thrown, and so it never lands where it should. At first it simply means they are never surprised. At its highest, they step out of the way of arrows and even bullets, moving with an eerie calm.

It is the gift of legendary duellists and bodyguards. It shows only the next moment, not the day or the year, and only in the heat of the fight. Against many foes at once, it can show too much, and the bearer freezes between too many futures.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/AGI · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** see a heartbeat ahead in a fight
- **Minor → major:** never surprised → dodge bullets
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No warning; the bearer fights as anyone would. Spectacular failure: a false glimpse sends them moving into the blow.

## Not to be confused with

*[Danger Sense](../Skills/danger-sense.md)*, which only warns that trouble is near; reflex precognition shows each move in a fight.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a flicker of the next moment behind the eyes, like a double image.
- **Inner energy:** a master's emptied mind, moving before thought.
- **Technology:** a combat implant predicting movement from muscle signals.
