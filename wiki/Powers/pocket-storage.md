# Pocket Storage

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A bag that is bigger inside than out. A satchel that swallows a spear, a chest that swallows a cart, a tiny box that swallows a warehouse. The mouth opens onto a space that should not fit, and the weight somehow doesn't come along.

Merchants dream of it, quartermasters can't live without it, smugglers use it to hide cargo in plain sight. It has its dangers: things get lost at the back, living creatures don't always come out, and a torn bag can spill its contents across the landscape at an awkward time.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** a container that is bigger inside
- **Minor → major:** a bag → a warehouse
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The bag holds what a bag holds. Spectacular failure: the pocket tears, spilling everything or swallowing the owner's arm.

## Not to be confused with

*[Pocket Realm](pocket-realm.md)*, which makes a hidden world to live in; Pocket Storage is only a container, however large.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a satchel stitched with runes on the inner lining, opening onto grey darkness.
- **Technology:** a compressed-space container with a glowing seal, humming when its lid is closed.
