# Geas

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A binding duty or taboo laid on someone by a higher power, or by a person with the right to lay it. Never refuse hospitality. Never eat the flesh of a hound. Always go to help anyone who calls your name at a ford. Keep the rule and the geas protects and honours. Break it and doom follows, often swiftly, sometimes with a strange appropriateness.

Heroes in old tales are laid under many geasa at birth, and the stories are about the day they are forced to break one. Small ones are nearly harmless. The greatest are fatal, and the most dangerous thing an enemy can learn is a rival's geas.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/COU/WIS/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** lay a binding duty or taboo that brings doom if broken
- **Minor → major:** a minor rule → a fatal constraint
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The rule is spoken but it does not bind. Spectacular failure: the geas binds the one who laid it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Oath-Binding](oath-binding.md)*, which enforces a promise freely sworn; a Geas is laid on someone whether they agree or not.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a god's command given through a priest at a naming, binding for life.
- **Spirit:** a fae lady's rule, laid on a hero with a kiss at a ford.
- **Arcane:** a ritual that writes a command into the target's name.
