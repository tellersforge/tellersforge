# Veil of Concealment

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Not invisibility but forgetfulness. A person, a door or a whole valley under this veil is simply overlooked: eyes slide over it, maps leave it out, and those who have seen it cannot quite remember where it was. Searching powers and scrying find only mist. The smallest use makes a man easy to ignore in a crowd; the greatest hides an island that sailors pass for generations without seeing.

It is a quiet, patient power, favoured by hermits, hidden peoples and those who guard secret places. Its weakness is the one who already knows exactly what they seek, and walks straight to it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** hide a person or place from notice, memory and magic
- **Minor → major:** overlooked → an island no one can find
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The thing remains plain to see. Spectacular failure: the veil draws attention instead, like a blank space on a map.

## Not to be confused with

*[Invisibility](invisibility.md)*, which hides from sight alone; the veil hides from notice, memory and searching magic as well.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a ring of warding runes around the hidden place, renewed each season.
- **Spirit:** a mist raised by fae or land spirits; travellers turn aside without knowing why.
- **Technology:** sensor jammers and data scrubbers that erase the place from every record.

- **Close to:** [Invisibility](invisibility.md), [Nondetection](nondetection.md)
