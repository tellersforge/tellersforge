# Reality Veil

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The deepest of all illusions. The victim falls under a false world so complete that they live in it, eating and sleeping and working, making friends and enemies who are not there. A short veil steals a false moment; a long one steals years, and the victim wakes old, with a lifetime of memories that never happened.

It is a feared and cruel power, used by vengeful sorcerers, wicked queens and prison-keepers who want no walls. Breaking it from the inside takes a sharp mind that notices small wrongs: a clock that never changes, a face without a name.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/CHA/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** an illusion so deep the victim lives a false life
- **Minor → major:** a false moment → a false lifetime
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The victim wakes almost at once, uneasy and suspicious. Spectacular failure: the caster falls into their own false world.

## Not to be confused with

*[Major Illusion](major-illusion.md)*, which fools the senses for a while; the reality veil replaces a whole life.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a great enchantment sealed with a sleeping draught or a pricked finger.
- **Occult:** a pact that hands the victim's mind to something that dreams them a life.
