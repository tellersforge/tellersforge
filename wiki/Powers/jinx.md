# Jinx

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A snap of bad luck at exactly the right moment. The sword slips from a sweaty hand, the key breaks in the lock, the rope gives just as the thief reaches the window. A jinx doesn't last and doesn't linger. It picks one moment and spoils it.

Gamblers fear it, duellists swear by it, and nobody likes to be near someone who has it. Small jinxes look like clumsiness. Great ones look like the world briefly turning against a single person: a whole bridge collapsing under one rider, a whole machine failing at once. Those who jinx often learn to keep a straight face, because the only evidence is a grin at the wrong time.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/LUCK · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** make a target's action fail
- **Minor → major:** clumsiness → catastrophe
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The action succeeds as normal. Spectacular failure: the jinx rebounds, and it is the caster who fumbles.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bane](bane.md)*, which weighs on an enemy through a whole fight; a Jinx spoils one single action, sharply and completely.

## Costumes

- **Folk magic:** crossed fingers, a spat word, and a quick glance at the target's hands.
- **Technology:** a pocket device that scrambles the target's electronics at the key moment, or nudges a machine into failure.
