# Power Backlash

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Turning another's power against them. The target reaches for their gift, and the backlasher pushes: not blocking it, but overfilling it, twisting it, sending it in the wrong direction. The fireball goes off in the caster's hand, the healing touch burns, the mind-reader hears a scream.

It is a feared and dishonourable gift, favoured by assassins and duellists who like to watch an opponent's pride become their downfall. At its mildest, the power simply misfires. At its worst, the target's gift is scorched from them, perhaps for a time, perhaps for good.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/COU/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** overload another's power so it misfires
- **Minor → major:** a misfire → the power lost
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The target's power works as intended. Spectacular failure: the backlash turns on the user's own powers instead.

## Not to be confused with

*[Power Negation](power-negation.md)*, which quietly stops powers from working; Power Backlash makes them misfire and hurt their owner.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a jolt of will that twists the target's thought as they reach for the gift.
- **Occult:** a curse whispered into the target's shadow, turning their power into poison.

- **Close to:** [Power Negation](power-negation.md)
