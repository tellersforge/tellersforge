# Disintegrate

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Matter simply stops. A lock becomes a drift of grey dust. A door gains a neat round hole. At its most terrible, a building folds into a cloud of fine powder that drifts on the wind, and nothing that was in it is ever found. There is no fire and little sound, only a hiss and a faint, dry smell.

It is the last word in destruction and it is feared everywhere it is known. Few things resist it: some rare metals, some wards, very old stone. Those who have it are watched closely, and many have learned to keep it hidden, because nobody sleeps easily next to a person who could erase them.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/COU/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** unmake matter
- **Minor → major:** erase a lock → annihilate a building
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A patch of surface turns to dust and the rest remains. Spectacular failure: the unmaking reaches something the caster needed, such as the floor.

## Not to be confused with

*[Shatter](shatter.md)*, which breaks things into pieces; Disintegrate leaves no pieces at all.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a pale green ray from a pointing finger, and a whisper like sand falling.
- **Technology:** a heavy beam weapon that unbinds matter, with a whine that rises to a scream.
- **Occult:** a word of the void, taught by things that hate the world, which leaves a hungry chill.
