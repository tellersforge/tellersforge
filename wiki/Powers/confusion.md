# Confusion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Thoughts that were in a neat row suddenly are not. The target forgets what they were saying, reaches for a sword and picks up a cup, looks at a friend and sees a stranger. At its weakest the power is a moment's daze, a fogginess that clears in a breath. At its strongest, a band of soldiers turns on itself in the belief that the enemy is already among them.

It leaves no mark and little memory, which makes it a favourite of tricksters and ambushers. It is less useful against those who were already muddled, and least useful against the very simple, who have fewer thoughts to scramble.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** scramble thoughts; turn foes on each other
- **Minor → major:** a daze → allies attack allies
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The target blinks and carries on. Spectacular failure: the muddle spreads to the caster's own side, or to the caster.

## Not to be confused with

*[Madness](madness.md)*, which breaks the mind for good. Confusion only scrambles it for a while, and it passes.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a spiralling gesture and a word that sounds different to every ear.
- **Psionic:** a burst of static across the target's thoughts.
- **Occult:** a mark drawn in ash that makes the eye slide off the world.
- **Technology:** a strobing light and a sound pitched to rattle the brain.
