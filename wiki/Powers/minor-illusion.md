# Minor Illusion

*Set: Core · Basic power*

*aka: ______________________________*

A footstep in the empty corridor. A candle that seems to burn on a bare table. A voice calling from the next room. The minor illusion is a single false image or sound, simple and still, conjured out of nothing. It doesn't move on its own and doesn't answer back, but it doesn't need to: a guard who hears a noise elsewhere will usually go to look.

It is the tool of pranksters, tricksters and patient thieves. The best users keep their illusions small and plausible. A detailed scene can fool a watcher for a while, but touch or a thrown stone passes straight through it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** a simple false image or sound
- **Minor → major:** a noise elsewhere → a detailed still scene
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **Version I, as in Basic:** One still image, person-sized or smaller, or one simple sound (a footstep, a few spoken words, a door slam), up to 10 metres away, for about a minute. It does not move or react. Anyone who studies it closely can tell it is false with a Perception check. It cannot hurt anyone.
- **What failure looks like:** The illusion is plainly false, flickering or silent. Spectacular failure: it shows or says something that gives the caster away.

## Not to be confused with

*[Major Illusion](major-illusion.md)*, which fills every sense and moves and reacts; the minor illusion is one simple, still sight or sound.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a flick of the fingers and a muttered phrase; the image forms like breath on glass.
- **Psionic:** a nudge to the watcher's senses so they see or hear what is not there.
- **Technology:** a hidden projector or speaker planted in the room.
