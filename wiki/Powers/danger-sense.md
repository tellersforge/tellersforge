# Danger Sense

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A prickle at the back of the neck, a sudden need to step left. Danger sense warns its bearer that something is about to go wrong before there is any reason to think so. Sometimes it is only a twinge and a moment's unease. At its sharpest, the bearer is never taken by surprise: they turn before the ambush and step aside before the trap.

It gives no details. It says only that danger is near and roughly where. Soldiers, thieves and bodyguards who have it often seem nervous or superstitious, and their friends stop laughing once they've been saved a few times.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** feel threats coming
- **Minor → major:** a twinge → never surprised
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No warning comes. Spectacular failure: a false alarm sends the bearer running from the wrong danger.

## Not to be confused with

*[Reflex Precognition](reflex-precognition.md)*, which shows the next moment in a fight; danger sense only warns that trouble is near.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a sharp pressure behind the eyes whenever hostile intent comes near.
- **Inner energy:** a trained awareness so calm that any disturbance ripples through it.
- **Mutation:** hair that stands on end and a racing pulse before any threat.
