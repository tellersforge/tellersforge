# Petrification

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Skin greys, joints stiffen, the heartbeat slows to nothing, and where a person stood there is a statue with a look of surprise on its face. Gardens of such figures are the classic warning outside the lairs of certain monsters and witches. Some are kept for decoration, some as trophies, some in the hope that they may one day be released.

The smallest use stiffens a limb for a moment. The greatest makes a statue that will outlast its city. Whether the victim is aware inside the stone is a question nobody wants answered. Chipping or breaking a statue before it is restored is the kind of accident that ends feuds, or starts them.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **COU**/INT/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** turn flesh to stone
- **Minor → major:** a stiff limb → a statue forever
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** a creeping chill that wears off. Spectacular failure: the stone creeps back up the caster's own arm.

## Not to be confused with

*[Transform Others](transform-others.md)*, which can change a victim into anything; Petrification only makes stone, but makes it very well.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a grey powder blown from the palm and a single hard word.
- **Occult:** a gaze lent by some old horror, which the caster must pay to borrow.
- **Mutation:** eyes that turn flesh to stone, kept behind a veil or blindfold.
