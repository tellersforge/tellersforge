# Second Sight

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The bearer sees what others cannot: the grey shape by the well, the small folk dancing at the edge of the wood, the coloured haze of feeling that hangs around every living person. Second sight opens the eye to spirits, fae, ghosts and auras. At first there is only a flicker in the corner of the eye. At its fullest, the whole Otherworld lies over the ordinary one like a second landscape.

It is inherited in some families and feared in most. What is seen can also see back, and those with the gift learn not to stare.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** perceive spirits, fae, ghosts and auras
- **Minor → major:** a flicker → the whole Otherworld
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The bearer sees nothing unusual. Spectacular failure: something on the other side notices the bearer and takes an interest.

## Not to be confused with

*[True Sight](true-sight.md)*, which sees through illusion and disguise; second sight sees spirits and hidden worlds.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a gift of the fae or the ancestors; the eyes turn grey or silver while it is open.
- **Folk magic:** looking through a holed stone, or over the left shoulder on a certain night.
