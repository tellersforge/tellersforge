# Limb Regrowth

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Lost flesh grows back. A missing fingertip sprouts again, a severed hand buds and unfurls, and at its greatest a whole leg, an eye, or a failing organ is rebuilt from nothing. It is slow at first and then very fast, and no one who has watched it happen forgets the sight.

The new part is not always a perfect match. It may be a slightly different colour, stiffer or more sensitive than the old. For soldiers maimed in war, for the victims of accidents and cruel punishments, it is the difference between a life and a half-life.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** regrow lost body parts
- **Minor → major:** a fingertip → limbs and organs
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing grows. Spectacular failure: something grows, but it is not what was lost.

## Not to be confused with

*[Healing Factor](healing-factor.md)*, which heals wounds quickly but cannot rebuild what is gone. Limb Regrowth rebuilds it.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** a stump that bulges and buds like a lizard's tail.
- **Arcane:** a long ritual, candles and wax shaped into the missing part.
- **Divine:** a pilgrim's miracle, prayed for at a shrine and granted overnight.
