# Power Theft

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Taking another's gift and leaving them empty. The thief touches the victim, or drinks their breath, or catches their shadow, and the power leaves its owner and settles into the thief. The victim feels it go: a sudden hollowness, as if a limb had been taken in their sleep.

At its mildest the theft is a loan, the power returning when the thief's hold fades. At its greatest the gift is kept for good, and the thief grows stronger with every victim. It is one of the most hated powers in any world, and those known to have it are hunted by everyone with anything to lose.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **COU**/END/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** take a power from its owner
- **Minor → major:** borrow it → keep it
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing passes, and the victim knows what was tried. Spectacular failure: the thief's own power flows into the victim instead.

## Not to be confused with

*[Power Mimicry](power-mimicry.md)*, which copies a gift and leaves the owner whole; Power Theft takes it away. *[Luck-Stealing](luck-stealing.md)* takes only fortune.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** a hungry touch that drains a gift out of anyone it grips, leaving the thief's hand glowing.
- **Occult:** a pact with a devouring power, sealed by the victim's blood.
