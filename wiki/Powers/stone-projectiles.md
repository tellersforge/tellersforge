# Stone Projectiles

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The ground gives up its stones. At the caster's gesture, pebbles lift and fly like shot from a sling, or sharp spikes of rock tear out of the earth and hurl themselves forward. At its greatest, a hillside's worth of boulders rises and crashes down on a column of soldiers, and anyone watching understands exactly why walls get built.

It needs stone to work with, and the more loose rock, the better. On a cobbled street it is terrible; on a boat, nearly useless. It is noisy, dusty and bruising, and it tends to leave a mess of broken rock and cratered ground behind. Few who have it are called subtle.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **STR**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** hurl rocks or spikes
- **Minor → major:** pebbles → a boulder barrage
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A few pebbles skitter across the ground. Spectacular failure: the stones fly wide or fall back on the caster.

## Not to be confused with

*[Kinetic Push & Pull](kinetic-push-and-pull.md)*, which shoves with raw force; Stone Projectiles throws rock and spike from the ground itself.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a stamp and a gesture; rock splinters loose and flies at the pointing hand.
- **Mutation:** stony growths that the body sheds and flings, or spines shot from the back.
