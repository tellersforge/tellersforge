# Gender & Age Shift

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The face in the mirror changes and the soul behind it does not. A young man grows grey and stooped, an old woman smooths into a girl, a husband becomes a wife, or someone discovers at last the body they always felt they had. At its gentlest it is a trick of years, adding a few lines or taking them away. At its fullest the change is complete, down to the voice and the walk.

Old stories are full of it: heroes who spent seven years as the other sex, gods who walked as beggars, crones who were maidens by moonlight. It serves disguise, escape and punishment, and sometimes it is simply the answer to a prayer.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** change your sex or apparent age
- **Minor → major:** look older → fully another
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the change will not come, or comes only as a passing shimmer. Spectacular failure: it goes too far, or sticks, and the way back is not obvious.

## Not to be confused with

*[Self-Alteration](self-alteration.md)*, which reshapes features and build freely; this power changes only sex and apparent age, but changes them completely.

## Costumes

- **Heroic legend:** a curse or a gift from the old stories, laid on by a spring, a goddess or a crossed serpent.
- **Arcane:** a carefully brewed draught, bitter and slow, taken with a counting spell.
