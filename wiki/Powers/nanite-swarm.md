# Nanite Swarm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A cloud of machines too small to see, obeying a single will. It looks like smoke, or dust caught in sunlight, or a silver sheen on a surface that ought to be dull. Sent to heal, it knits torn metal and flesh. Sent to build, it lays down walls grain by grain. Sent to consume, it eats: first paint, then steel, then anything at all.

It is the most feared tool of the far future, and rightly so. A careful master uses it to mend a cracked hull in minutes or to shape a shelter from a pile of rubble. A careless one leaves a grey tide spreading across the landscape. Some worlds forbid the gift entirely, and burn any device suspected of carrying it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** command microscopic machines to repair, build or consume
- **Minor → major:** fix a tool → eat a city
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The swarm drifts and does nothing useful. Spectacular failure: it misreads its orders and starts to dismantle the wrong thing, possibly the user.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mend](mend.md)*, which repairs a single broken thing; *[Matter Synthesis](matter-synthesis.md)* creates material outright. Nanite Swarm repairs, builds or consumes, grain by grain.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a vial or wrist-port that releases a shimmering haze, humming faintly as it goes to work.
