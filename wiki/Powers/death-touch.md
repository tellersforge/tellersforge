# Death Touch

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

One touch, and it is over. The heart stops, the breath fails, and the body falls without a mark. Death Touch is the most feared of powers. At first it only weakens and chills. At full strength it kills instantly, and nothing is left to show how.

Those who have it rarely advertise it. Assassins, dark priests and certain kinds of monsters use it. Its bearers learn to keep their hands to themselves, and those around them learn never to accept a handshake. Some stories say the power has a price, and that each death takes a little of the bearer's own life too.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **COU**/INT/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** kill outright
- **Minor → major:** weakness → instant death
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** a chill and nothing more. Spectacular failure: the death flows back up the arm.

## Not to be confused with

*[Harm Touch](harm-touch.md)*, which wounds and sickens; Death Touch kills.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a mark of the grave on the palm, cold as the tomb.
- **Arcane:** a forbidden word breathed into the victim's face.
- **Technology:** a nerve-disruptor glove that stops the heart.
