# Truth-Sensing

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The lie sounds wrong. Perhaps it rings flat like a cracked bell, or leaves a sour taste, or makes a small light flicker at the edge of sight. Truth-sensing tells its bearer when someone is lying. At first it is a gut feeling that can be doubted; at its best, it is certainty.

Judges, merchants, interrogators and suspicious spouses have wanted it for as long as there have been lies. It knows only whether the speaker believes what they say, not whether it is true, and a clever liar learns to speak in careful half-truths.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** know when someone lies
- **Minor → major:** a gut feeling → certainty
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No feeling either way. Spectacular failure: the bearer is sure of a lie that was the truth.

## Not to be confused with

*[Compel Truth](compel-truth.md)*, which forces a person to speak honestly; truth-sensing only knows whether they have.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a sacred bell only the priest can hear, which rings sour at every lie.
- **Psionic:** a flicker of the speaker's own doubt picked up by the mind.
- **Technology:** a voice and pulse analyser that flashes red at stress.
