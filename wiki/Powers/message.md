# Message

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A few spoken words carried across distance. The sender speaks, low and clear, and the words are heard by the one intended, in the sender's own voice, as if they stood at the listener's elbow. A murmur across a crowded room goes unnoticed by anyone else; at full strength the message crosses miles of hills and walls.

It is short and one-way. The listener hears, but must find their own way to answer. Scouts, lovers, spies and quartermasters all find uses for it, and in places where it is common people learn not to startle when a friend's voice speaks suddenly out of empty air.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **INT** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** send a short spoken message across distance
- **Minor → major:** a murmur across a room → across miles
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The words are lost on the wind. Spectacular failure: the message is heard by everyone nearby, or by exactly the wrong person.

## Not to be confused with

*[Sending](sending.md)*, which reaches anywhere and can carry replies. Message is spoken, short, and travels only so far.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** words whispered into a cupped hand, then released like a bird.
- **Technology:** a small receiver in the ear; the voice arrives with a faint crackle.
- **Psionic:** a phrase pushed across the distance in the sender's own inner voice.
