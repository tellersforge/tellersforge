# Identify

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The worker lays a hand on a strange sword and knows at once what it is: who made it, what it was made for, whether it is cursed. Identify learns the nature of an object or creature, from its basic properties to its full history and its weaknesses. A small use tells what a potion is; a great one tells what a monster fears.

It is essential to adventurers who find things in tombs, and to merchants who are offered them. It takes quiet study, and some things resist it on purpose.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** learn an object's or creature's properties
- **Minor → major:** basic nature → full history and weaknesses
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The thing remains a mystery. Spectacular failure: the reading is confidently wrong, and a cursed item is declared safe.

## Not to be confused with

*[Object Reading](object-reading.md)*, which sees an object's past through feelings and scenes; Identify learns what the object is and what it does.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a close look through a crystal lens and a quiet chant; the answers come as knowledge.
- **Technology:** an analysis device that scans the object and lists its properties.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Object Reading](object-reading.md), [Appraisal (skill)](../Skills/appraisal.md)
