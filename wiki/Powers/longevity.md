# Longevity

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Time loosens its grip. The years pass and the bearer's face stays the same, or ages at a crawl, while friends go grey and children grow old. At its lightest, the gift adds a few decades of vigour. At its strongest, it stops ageing altogether.

It does not protect against the sword, the plague or the fall. The long-lived die as easily as anyone else, only not of age. And it is lonely: the long-lived outlast their loves, their homelands and their languages, and many become strange and cold with the centuries.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS/INT/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** slow or stop your own ageing
- **Minor → major:** added decades → immortality
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The years go on as usual. Spectacular failure: the ageing catches up, all at once.

## Not to be confused with

*[Ageing & Youth](ageing-and-youth.md)*, which changes one's own or another's age. Longevity only slows or stops the years.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a lifelong study of an elixir brewed once a year.
- **Inner energy:** breathing exercises and a diet so disciplined that the body barely changes.
- **Technology:** telomere therapy, replacement organs and cell repair.
- **Vampiric:** the drinking of others' life keeps the years at bay.
