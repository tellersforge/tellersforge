# Density & Weight

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The body grows heavy as a statue or light as a leaf. At first it is only a little extra weight, hard to push over, hard to lift. At its greatest the one who has it becomes immovable, rooted to the floor like an iron pillar while a charging horse breaks against them, or so light they are blown along by a breath of wind and fall without harm.

Heavy, the body hardens too: fists land like hammers and blades glance off skin. Light, it floats in water and drifts across rooftops. Neither extreme is comfortable for long. Floors crack under the heavy form, and the light one is at the mercy of the weather. Old stories tell of heroes no giant could lift.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS/STR · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** change your own mass and hardness
- **Minor → major:** heavier → immovable, or feather-light
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The body's weight wavers and settles back to normal. Spectacular failure: it lurches to the wrong extreme, going light in a gale or heavy on a rotten floor.

## Not to be confused with

*[Armour Skin](armour-skin.md)*, which only hardens the surface; Density & Weight changes the whole body's mass.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** a body that can pack itself dense as iron, the skin taking on a dull grey sheen.
- **Arcane:** a spell of stone or feather spoken over the heart.
- **Heroic legend:** the hero no one can lift from the ground, or who walks on the tips of the grass.
