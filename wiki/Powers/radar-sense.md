# Radar Sense

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Sight without eyes. The bearer feels the room around them: the shape of each wall, the man behind them, the arrow in flight. It may come as clicks and echoes, as ripples in the air or as a map that simply appears in the mind. At first it only gives a rough idea of the room; at its best it gives full awareness in every direction at once.

It serves blind warriors, cave-dwellers and those who fight in smoke. Fog, darkness and blindfolds mean nothing. Its limits are noise and distance: a roaring crowd can drown the picture, and nothing far off shows at all.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** sense your surroundings without sight
- **Minor → major:** feel the room → full all-round awareness
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Only a blurred sense of the space. Spectacular failure: the echoes confuse the bearer, who walks confidently into the wall.

## Not to be confused with

*[Danger Sense](../Skills/danger-sense.md)*, which only warns of threats; radar sense shows the shape of everything around the bearer.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** the bearer clicks softly, and listens like a bat.
- **Technology:** a sonar collar or motion tracker feeding a map to the ear or eye.
- **Inner energy:** a blind master's calm, feeling every movement in the air.
