# Far Seeing (Clairvoyance)

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The eyes close, and the bearer sees another place: the next room, the enemy camp, a harbour across the sea. Far seeing sees a distant place without mirror, bowl or screen. At first it reaches only a short way. At its greatest, it crosses the world.

It is prized by generals, spies and worried parents. It shows only what the bearer would see if they stood there, no more. Darkness still hides things, and far places are hard to find without having seen them first.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **PER**/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** see a distant place without tools
- **Minor → major:** the next room → across the world
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Only darkness or a blurred scene. Spectacular failure: the bearer sees the wrong place and trusts it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Scrying](scrying.md)*, which needs water, a mirror or smoke to look through; far seeing needs nothing.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** the mind drifting out of the body and seeing through the distance.
- **Divine:** a god's eye lent to the faithful for a moment.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Scrying](scrying.md)
