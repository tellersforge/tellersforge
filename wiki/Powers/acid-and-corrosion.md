# Acid & Corrosion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Everything eaten away. The one who has this gift spits, sprays or touches, and armour pits and blackens, leather bubbles, ropes fray and fall apart, and stone weeps and softens. At its gentlest it etches a mark on glass or a coin. At its worst it dissolves a steel door, or flesh, in a stinking cloud of vapour.

It is a messy, frightening power, and it ruins good gear. Those who carry it rarely win friends, and innkeepers dislike them. Rust and rot follow them, and they learn to keep their own tools in glass or clay.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** melt armour, gear or flesh
- **Minor → major:** etch → dissolve
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A faint fizz, and the target barely scarred. Spectacular failure: the acid splashes back, or eats through something of the caster's own.

## Not to be confused with

*[Poison Touch](poison-touch.md)*, which harms living bodies from within; Acid & Corrosion dissolves whatever it touches, flesh or iron alike.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** glands that spit or sweat acid, and a mouth lined with scar tissue.
- **Technology:** a sprayer or wrist nozzle with a canister of corrosive gel.
- **Alchemical:** a flask of green fuming liquid flung or poured, with gloves and a mask to handle it.
