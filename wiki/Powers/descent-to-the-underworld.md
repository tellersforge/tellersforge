# Descent to the Underworld

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The oldest journey in any story: to walk into the country of the dead while still breathing, and to walk out again. The way is never easy to find. It lies behind a cave mouth that exhales cold air, at the bottom of a lake, down a road that only appears at dusk. The traveller passes grey fields and silent crowds, gatekeepers who ask questions, rivers that must not be drunk from.

Most who make the descent go for someone: a lost wife, a murdered brother, a secret only the dead still keep. The dead are rarely glad to see the living, and the living come back changed. They are quieter, colder to the touch, and they never again eat a meal without counting what they owe.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **COU**/WIS/END/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** enter the land of the dead alive and return
- **Minor → major:** a glimpse → rescue someone from it
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The way down stays shut, or the traveller turns back at the gate. Spectacular failure: they arrive, but the dead will not let them leave empty-handed, and something of theirs stays behind.

## Not to be confused with

*[Plane Shift](plane-shift.md)*, which crosses into any other reality; the Descent goes only to the land of the dead, and its whole art is getting back out.

## Costumes

- **Heroic legend:** a hero's road through a cave at the world's edge, paid for with courage and a promise the dead will hold them to.
- **Spirit:** a shaman's drum carries the soul down the roots of the world tree while the body lies cold beside the fire.
