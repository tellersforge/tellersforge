# Entangle

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The ground reaches up and holds on. Roots break the earth, vines whip around ankles, grass knots itself into cords as tough as rope. A fleeing man stumbles, a charging horse goes down, a whole company finds itself stuck fast in a thicket that wasn't there a heartbeat before.

It is the gift of forest guardians, druids and anyone who fights where things grow. It does little on bare stone or city streets, and a sharp blade or a torch will free a captive in time. But time is usually what the caster wanted.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** roots and vines bind targets
- **Minor → major:** snag a foot → a thicket prison
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The plants twitch and fall still. Spectacular failure: they seize the caster or their friends instead.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bind](bind.md)*, which holds a target with invisible force or chains; Entangle uses living roots and vines, and needs something growing nearby.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a whispered plea to the roots, and the earth answers like an old friend.
- **Arcane:** a sweeping gesture and a word of binding; the vines glow faintly green.
- **Technology:** a canister of fast-growing gel fibres that sprout and tangle on contact.
