# Beast Speech

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The power to talk with animals, and to understand what they say back. At first only moods come through: the fox is wary, the ox is weary, the dog is certain of something. With skill, the speaker can hold real conversations: asking a raven what it saw, bargaining with a bear, or thanking a horse after a hard ride.

Animals are not people. They care about food, safety, territory and each other, and have little interest in human troubles. Still, a crow sees much from a rooftop, and a cat remembers who came through the garden. Hermits, hunters and some odd children have the gift, and none of them are ever lonely.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** talk with animals
- **Minor → major:** moods → conversations
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The animal only stares, or wanders off. Spectacular failure: the creature takes offence, or tells every other animal about the rude stranger.

## Not to be confused with

*[Beast Command](beast-command.md)*, which orders animals to obey. Beast Speech only lets both sides talk.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** the speaker's totem animal translates, whispering in each ear.
- **Mutation:** extra senses and throat structures that understand chirps and growls.
- **Folk magic:** a charm of bird-bones and beeswax worn under the tongue.
