# Spirit Allies

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A voice at the shoulder, a warning before an ambush, a brightness in the corner of the eye. The spirit ally is a guardian that has chosen to walk with its human: an ancestor, a totem beast, a household god. It guides, scouts, warns and, in the end, fights. At first it is only a whisper. At its fullest it stands visible beside its companion, and enemies see what they are facing.

The bond is a relationship, not a leash. Spirits have wishes, moods and taboos, and an ally that is neglected or insulted may simply leave. Those who treat theirs well are rarely alone and seldom surprised.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** a bonded helper spirit that guides, scouts and fights
- **Minor → major:** a whisper → a guardian
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the spirit is silent. Spectacular failure: the spirit is offended and withdraws for some time.

## Not to be confused with

*[Familiar](familiar.md)*, which is a bonded animal; Spirit Allies are guardians of the unseen world.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a guardian angel or saint who walks beside the faithful.
