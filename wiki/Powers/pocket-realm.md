# Pocket Realm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A private world, folded away from all the others. At its smallest it is a hut in a mist that only its maker can find. At its greatest it is a whole land inside a gourd, a pearl or a painted scroll, with its own sky and seasons.

Sorcerers keep their libraries there, hermits their gardens, fugitives their families. The maker sets the rules: when the sun rises, what grows, who may enter. Visitors are often astonished how much can fit in a walnut shell. The danger is that pocket realms depend on their maker, and when the maker dies, the realm tends to go with them.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** create a hidden private world
- **Minor → major:** a hut → a world in a gourd
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The fold won't open. Spectacular failure: the realm opens but won't let its maker out.

## Not to be confused with

*[Pocket Storage](pocket-storage.md)*, which is only a container; a Pocket Realm is a world, with land and sky and room to live.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a study behind a painted door that only opens for its painter.
- **Occult:** a realm bought from a thing beyond the world, with its own strange price.
- **Heroic legend:** a sage's gourd, which holds mountains and rivers and a kingdom inside.
