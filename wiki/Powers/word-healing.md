# Word-Healing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Old words that close wounds. The healer mutters a verse over a bleeding cut, lays a thumb on a broken bone, or spits and speaks, and the body obeys. Blood slows, flesh draws together, bone grinds back into place. The smallest charm stops a nosebleed; the strongest knits a broken leg overnight.

The words are passed from grandmother to grandchild, rarely written down, and each village has its own. They sound like nonsense to outsiders and often rhyme. Churches in some lands frown on them, and village healers keep using them regardless, because they work.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** spoken charms that staunch blood and set bones
- **Minor → major:** stop a nosebleed → heal a broken limb
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The words are spoken and nothing changes. Spectacular failure: the charm twists, and the wound opens wider.

## Not to be confused with

*[Healing Touch](healing-touch.md)*, which heals by touch or prayer. Word-Healing uses spoken charms and folk verses.

## Costumes

- **Folk magic:** a muttered charm passed down through generations, with a thumb pressed on the wound.
- **Song:** a slow healing chant, the same few lines sung over and over.
