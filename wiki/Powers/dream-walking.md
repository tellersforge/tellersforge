# Dream Walking

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

When the target sleeps, the dream walker comes. Stepping into another's dream, they can speak, listen, watch or reshape what is there. A message delivered in a dream feels more urgent than any letter. A threat felt in a nightmare follows the dreamer into the day. At full strength the walker can trap a sleeper inside a dream that never ends.

It is patient work, done at night, often from far away. Dreams are unreliable country: the walker may find the dreamer's fears waiting for them, or lose the way back. Shamans, cult priests and trained mystics use it, and those who have met one in a dream never quite sleep easy again.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** enter, shape or speak through dreams
- **Minor → major:** send a message → trap the dreamer
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The dream is too thick to enter, or the dreamer wakes. Spectacular failure: the walker is caught in the dream and can't get out without help.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mind Illusion](mind-illusion.md)*, which fools a waking mind; and *[Dream Prophecy](dream-prophecy.md)*, which reads the future in one's own dreams. Dream Walking enters another's sleep.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** the soul leaves the body by night on a thread of smoke.
- **Psionic:** a trance deep enough to drift from one sleeping mind to another.
- **Occult:** a name written backwards and placed under the walker's own pillow.
