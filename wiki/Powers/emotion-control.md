# Emotion Control

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The power to reach into the weather of another heart and change it. A grieving widow finds her tears drying. A sullen guard begins to smile. A calm meeting, for no reason anyone can name, turns sharp and bitter. The one who works it rarely looks like much: a quiet figure at the edge of the room, watching faces the way a sailor watches clouds.

It is a subtle art and an easily abused one. A mood that was put there can be felt as false by anyone who stops to think about it, so the best practitioners push only in the direction a heart was already leaning. Crowds are easier than individuals, and anger is always the easiest of all.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** calm, enrage, sadden or inspire
- **Minor → major:** shift a mood → a riot, or a peace
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The mood stays as it was. Spectacular failure: the feeling lands on the wrong people, or turns into its opposite at the worst moment.

## Not to be confused with

*[Fear](fear.md)*, which only terrifies; and *[Courage-Giving](courage-giving.md)*, which only steadies allies. Emotion Control can push any feeling, friend or foe, in any direction.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a slow pressure behind the eyes; the empath breathes in time with the target before shifting them.
- **Divine:** a blessing of peace, or a curse of wrath, given with open hands.
- **Arcane:** a murmured cantrip and a pinch of coloured powder scattered in the air.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Fear](fear.md), [Courage-Giving](courage-giving.md)
