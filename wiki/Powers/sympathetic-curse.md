# Sympathetic Curse

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Like touches like. A lock of hair, a nail paring, a drop of blood or a crude wax image stands in for the person, and what is done to the stand-in is felt by the body. A pin in the doll's arm, and the victim winces at dinner. A doll held over the fire, and the victim burns with fever three valleys away.

It is patient, intimate magic. The worker needs something of the target, and getting it is half the craft: a comb stolen from a washstand, a bandage from a sickroom, a portrait. Wise people burn their hair clippings and bury their nails. Its greatest workings kill at any distance, and leave nothing to show how.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/COU/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** affect someone through their hair, blood or image
- **Minor → major:** a pinprick → death at a distance
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The link doesn't hold, and the doll is just a doll. Spectacular failure: the link runs both ways, and the worker feels every pin.

## Not to be confused with

*[Curse](curse.md)*, which is declared on its target and follows them; a Sympathetic Curse works through a stolen piece of them, at any distance.

## Costumes

- **Folk magic:** a rag doll stuffed with the victim's hair and pricked with thorns by candlelight.
- **Occult:** a wax image baptised with the victim's name in a black rite, then slowly melted.
