# Swift Travel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The road shortens under the traveller's feet. A walk that should take a week takes a day; a day's march takes an hour. The walker does not seem to run. They stride along at an easy pace, and yet the milestones flick past like fence posts, and those left behind see only a figure dwindling far too quickly into the distance.

It works best over open roads and long journeys, and poorly in a crowded street or a fight. Couriers and pilgrims with the gift are known across whole kingdoms, and stories are told of messengers who arrived before the news they carried. Travellers who use it too often report that the land blurs, and that they sometimes miss what they came to see.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** cover vast distances unnaturally fast
- **Minor → major:** double pace → a week's road in an hour
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The journey goes at an ordinary pace. Spectacular failure: the traveller arrives much later, or somewhere else entirely, having lost a stretch of road and time.

## Not to be confused with

*[Super Speed](super-speed.md)*, which makes you fast in the moment; and *[Long Teleport](long-teleport.md)*, which crosses the distance at once. Swift Travel still walks every step, only quicker.

## Costumes

- **Inner energy:** a long-distance runner's trance, breath and stride in perfect rhythm.
- **Arcane:** boots stitched with runes, or a charm spoken at the first milestone.
- **Divine:** a pilgrim's blessing; the road itself seems to carry the faithful forward.
