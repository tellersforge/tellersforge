# Deflect & Reflect

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The arrow meets an open palm and turns aside. The thrown dagger spins back at the thrower. The fireball bends, hesitates, and goes home. This power doesn't stop harm so much as redirect it, and it needs quick reflexes and nerve to use. The beginner turns aside one arrow in a dozen. A master sends spells back to their casters, to the great satisfaction of anyone watching.

It is a showy power and a popular one among duellists. The risk is obvious: misjudge the angle and the attack still lands, perhaps on a friend.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** turn attacks or powers aside, or back at the sender
- **Minor → major:** parry an arrow → reflect a spell
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the attack comes through untouched. Spectacular failure: it deflects onto a friend or bystander.

## Not to be confused with

*[Energy Absorption](energy-absorption.md)*, which soaks up an attack and stores it; Deflect & Reflect sends the attack somewhere else.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a mirror word spoken fast, a flash of silver at the palm.
- **Psionic:** a flick of will that nudges each missile off its line.
- **Inner energy:** a martial master's circling hands that bend each strike aside.
