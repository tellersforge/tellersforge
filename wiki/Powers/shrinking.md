# Shrinking

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The body folds down into itself until a grown woman stands no higher than a table, then no higher than a boot, then nothing at all that a careless eye would see. Clothes shrink with the wearer if the power is well made, and fall in a heap if it is not. The small can go where the large cannot: under doors, through keyholes, into the pocket of a man who never knew he carried a spy.

The world becomes enormous and hostile. Cats turn into monsters, rain into a pounding flood, and a draught into a gale. The shrunken are hard to hit and easy to step on, and a wise one never forgets which of those matters more.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/AGI · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** become very small
- **Minor → major:** knee-high → insect-sized
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the body stays stubbornly its own size. Spectacular failure: the shrinking stops halfway, or will not reverse for some time.

## Not to be confused with

*[Growing](growing.md)*, which is the same trick run the other way: bigger, stronger and impossible to hide.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a pinch of dust from a crushed beetle shell, a word, and the room swells around the caster.
- **Technology:** a belt or suit that compresses the wearer, with a thin whine that rises as they fall away.
- **Mutation:** an inborn knack, discovered in a child who kept vanishing into the grass.
