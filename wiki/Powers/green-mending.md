# Green Mending

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Healing that looks like nothing much. Herbs steeped and cooled, a hand held on the brow through the night, a poultice changed with care. The healer calls it craft, and humans watching see only skilled nursing. But fevers break by morning, and wounds human healers had given up on close cleanly and fully.

It is slow. It needs rest, quiet, green things and a healer who will stay. Its keepers are an old and patient people who have never thought it was magic at all, and are mildly puzzled when others insist that it is.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** speeds the body's own slow mending through herbs, hands and patience; the healer calls it craft
- **Minor → major:** a fever broken overnight → wounds human healers call finished, made whole
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The patient recovers only at the usual pace. Spectacular failure: the herbs are wrong, and the fever rises instead.

## Not to be confused with

*[Healing Touch](healing-touch.md)*, which heals at once; and *[Remedies & Pharmacy](../Skills/remedies-and-pharmacy.md)*, the skill, which prepares medicines. Green Mending uses both, and patience, to do what neither can.

## Costumes

- **Elf-craft:** gentle hands, sweet steam and old songs half-hummed at the bedside.
- **Folk magic:** a grandmother's herb garden and a pot always simmering on the stove.
- **Spirit:** the spirits of the green things themselves coaxed to share their strength.
