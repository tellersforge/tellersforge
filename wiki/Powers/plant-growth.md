# Plant Growth

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Green things answering a call. A seed splits and pushes out a shoot, a dry stick puts out leaves, a bramble thickens until it is a wall. Flowers open out of season and fruit ripens overnight. Those watching hear the creak of stems and the rustle of leaves unfurling faster than any breeze could move them.

Farmers and gardeners treasure the gift, and so do armies: a road can be made impassable, a wall overgrown until it crumbles, a besieged town fed from its own window-boxes. At its greatest, bare ground becomes forest between dusk and dawn. The growth is real and stays; it simply arrives all at once, and the soil feels the cost afterwards.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** make plants grow, bloom and fruit
- **Minor → major:** a bloom → a forest overnight
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The plants shiver and do nothing. Spectacular failure: they grow wild and wrong, choking the crop, the path or the user's feet.

## Not to be confused with

*[Crop Blessing & Blight](crop-blessing-and-blight.md)*, which blesses or curses a whole harvest over a season; Plant Growth makes plants grow on the spot.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a song to the green spirits, and moss creeping along the singer's footprints.
- **Divine:** the blessing of a harvest god, with a scent of rain and bread.
- **Arcane:** a formula of quickening chalked on the soil; the earth trembles as roots stir.
