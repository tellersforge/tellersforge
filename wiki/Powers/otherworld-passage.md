# Otherworld Passage

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The doors are always there, if you know how to see them. A gap between two standing stones, a mirror in a certain light, a well that goes down too far, a path through a mist that falls only on holy nights. This power finds such doors and knows how to pass them, into the lands of the fae, the spirits or the gods, and back.

At first it works only at the thin times: midsummer, midwinter, the eve of the dead. Masters can find a door wherever they stand. The otherworlds have their own rules, and wise travellers learn them: eat nothing, give no names, thank every host and leave before the music stops.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/COU/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** find and cross hidden doors into fae, spirit or divine realms
- **Minor → major:** a door on a holy night → at will
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No door shows itself. Spectacular failure: a door opens, but it leads somewhere the traveller did not mean to go.

## Not to be confused with

*[Plane Shift](plane-shift.md)*, which crosses into any reality directly; Otherworld Passage finds the hidden doors between this world and the lands next door.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a spirit guide shows the way through a gap in a hedge or a ring of stones.
- **Heroic legend:** a hero follows a white stag into the mist, and emerges in the land beyond.
