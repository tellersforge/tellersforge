# Satire

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The poet's revenge. A well-made verse, sung in the hall where the target must hear it, can raise blotches on a face, curdle a reputation and bring a proud man lower than any sword could. In old courts the satirist was feared more than the champion, because the champion only killed you. The satirist made you ridiculous for a hundred years.

A small satire brings a flush of shame and some sniggering at the next feast. A great one follows its victim from hall to hall, is sung by children in the street, and has toppled kings who could no longer hold their followers' respect. Wise lords feed their poets well.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** verse that shames, blemishes or ruins
- **Minor → major:** a flush of shame → a king's downfall
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The verse falls flat, and people laugh at the poet instead. Spectacular failure: the satire turns and sticks to its maker for years.

## Not to be confused with

*[Wit & Mockery](../Skills/wit-and-mockery.md)*, the skill, which wins laughs in the moment; Satire is a made verse that leaves a real mark on body and name.

## Costumes

- **Bardic:** a poet's composed verse, sung with harp at a feast, its rhymes landing like blows and leaving red marks on the target's cheek.
