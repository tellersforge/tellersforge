# Elemental Body

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The body stops being flesh and becomes one of the great elements. A burning figure walks through a gatehouse, leaving scorch marks at every step. A man of rough stone shrugs off arrows. A woman of water flows round a sword and closes behind it. Lightning in human shape crackles along a wire and is gone.

At first only a hand or an arm changes. Mastery brings the whole body, and at the peak, something closer to weather than a person: a living storm, a moving hillside. Each element has its enemies. Fire fears water, stone is slow, water can be boiled. Coming back to flesh is always harder than leaving it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS/COU/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** become fire, stone, water or lightning
- **Minor → major:** one limb → a living storm
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the element flickers over the skin and fades. Spectacular failure: the change goes wrong, burning or chilling the body it came from.

## Not to be confused with

*[Elemental Aura](elemental-aura.md)*, which wraps the body in an element; Elemental Body becomes it.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** long study of one element until the mage and the element can no longer be told apart.
- **Mutation:** a born elemental, whose skin smokes or ripples when angry.
- **Spirit:** an elemental spirit invited in, which takes the body as its own for a while.
