# Fertility Blessing & Curse

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The power over birth: to open a womb that has stayed closed, to guard a mother through a hard labour, to bring a child safely into the world. And the reverse, which is spoken of more quietly: to close a womb, to turn a pregnancy against its bearer, to leave a house without heirs.

Midwives and village wise-women hold most of it. Temples of mother goddesses hold the rest, and are visited by queens and farmers' wives alike. Few powers are more sought or more feared. A house that has ended without heirs will often blame someone, and in many places the blessing and the curse are thought to come from the same hands.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** grant or prevent conception and safe birth
- **Minor → major:** good odds → the barren made fruitful
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing changes, and nature takes its course. Spectacular failure: the blessing lands elsewhere, or the curse on the wrong house.

## Not to be confused with

*[Blessing](blessing.md)*, which grants general favour and protection; this power works only on conception and birth.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a mother goddess's blessing, with an offering at her shrine and a charm tied on the wrist.
- **Folk magic:** an egg, a red thread and a midwife's murmured words over the bed.
