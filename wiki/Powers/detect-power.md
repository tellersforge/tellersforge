# Detect Power

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A prickle in the fingertips near a charmed ring. A hum in the teeth when someone gifted walks into the room. Detect power senses magic, psionics, charged machines and other workings, and often who holds them. The simple version says only 'something here'. A skilled one names the effect, its source and its strength.

Magistrates, inquisitors, relic hunters and suspicious guards have need of it. It makes hidden gifts hard to hide, which is why the gifted fear those who have it, and why so many try to keep it secret themselves.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** sense magic, psionics or charged tech, and who holds it
- **Minor → major:** "something here" → exact effect, source and strength
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing is sensed, or only a vague prickle. Spectacular failure: the bearer senses power everywhere, and trusts the wrong source.

## Not to be confused with

*[Identify](identify.md)*, which studies a known object in detail; detect power finds workings the bearer didn't know were there.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a sweep of the hand and a glance; charmed things glow faintly.
- **Divine:** a holy symbol that grows warm near sorcery.
- **Psionic:** a mind sensitive to the hum of other gifted minds.
- **Technology:** a meter or wand that clicks faster near charged power sources.
