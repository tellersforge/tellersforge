# Store Power

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A working bottled for later. The user pours a power into a vessel: a ring, a stone, a sealed jar, a battery humming on a shelf. The power waits there, patient, until someone breaks the seal or speaks the word, and then it happens as if cast that very moment.

It is how wise mages prepare for danger and how poor ones make a living, selling bottled light and sealed healing to those without gifts. At its greatest, a vessel becomes a reservoir holding many workings or one vast one. Such vessels are prized, stolen and occasionally broken by accident, with results that make the news.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** store a power in a vessel for later
- **Minor → major:** one use → a reservoir
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The power drains out of the vessel and is lost. Spectacular failure: it goes off at once, in the user's hands.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lasting Enchantment](lasting-enchantment.md)*, which makes a permanent magic item; Store Power holds one working until it is used.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a crystal sphere sealed with wax and a spoken word, glowing faintly on the shelf.
- **Technology:** a capacitor cell charged with raw power, humming and warm to the touch.
- **Occult:** a bone vessel sealed with blood, whispering until it is opened.
