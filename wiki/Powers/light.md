# Light

*Set: Core · Basic power*

*aka: ______________________________*

A small, kind power. A fingertip glows, a staff head brightens, a ball of pale light bobs overhead like a lantern that needs no oil. The strongest workers can flood a whole field with daylight at midnight, turning a battlefield or a search into something you can actually see.

It is the first working many learn, and one that never goes out of use. Miners, night watchmen, explorers of ruins and anyone who has lost their only torch down a well come to value it above flashier gifts. It gives no heat and burns nothing, which makes it safe for libraries and dangerous for anyone hoping to stay hidden.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **WIS** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** create light
- **Minor → major:** a glowing fingertip → daylight over a field
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **Version I, as in Basic:** A ball of white light the size of a fist, as bright as a torch. It lights a room and reaches about 10 metres. It floats above your open hand, or you can send it up to 10 metres away and leave it there. No heat, no damage. Lasts about an hour, or until you put it out.
- **What failure looks like:** A feeble glow that gutters out. Spectacular failure: the light flares at the worst moment and shows everyone exactly where the caster is.

## Not to be confused with

*[Blinding Flash](blinding-flash.md)*, which is a sudden burst meant to dazzle; Light is steady and simply lets people see.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a word breathed onto a stone or a staff tip, which then shines with cool light.
- **Divine:** a gentle holy radiance around the priest's hand or holy symbol.
- **Technology:** a lamp or bright emitter built into a glove, collar or drone.
