# Memory Editing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The power to change what someone remembers. A minute can be erased, a face replaced with another, an event added that never happened. The subject goes on with their life never knowing the difference. The smallest use is a forgotten minute; the greatest is a whole life rewritten, with a new name, new family and new past.

It is a delicate and ugly art. Memories are knotted together, and pulling one tends to loosen others, so careless editors leave gaps, contradictions and dreams that don't fit. Spy services, cults and alien visitors in old stories all make use of it, and those who suspect they have been edited are rarely able to prove it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** erase, alter or implant memories
- **Minor → major:** forget a minute → rewrite a life
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The memory stays. Spectacular failure: the edit tears, and the subject remembers both what happened and that someone tried to change it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Deep Mind Probe](deep-mind-probe.md)*, which reads memory without changing it. Memory Editing rewrites, removes or adds.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a spell woven around a lock of the subject's hair, snipped at the end.
- **Psionic:** patient work in the subject's mind, smoothing over what was there.
- **Occult:** a draught of dark water from a forgotten well.
- **Technology:** a flash device or neural machine that overwrites stored memories.
