# Shadow Shaping

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Shadows that obey. The shaper's own shadow stretches across the floor and does something the body isn't doing: reaches, grasps, stands up. A shadow can become a rope, a blade, a cage or a clutching hand, dark and cold and solid as long as the will holds it. At the start it only moves; at its strongest it makes real, heavy constructs that can bind or strike.

It needs light to cast shadows, and fails in total dark as surely as under a noon sun with nothing to shade. Its users are rarely trusted. People who have seen their own shadow pulled away from them tend not to forget it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** shape shadows into tools, bonds and attacks
- **Minor → major:** animate a shadow → solid shadow constructs
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The shadow twitches and lies still. Spectacular failure: it takes a shape of its own and turns on the shaper.

## Not to be confused with

*[Darkness](darkness.md)*, which floods an area with black; shadow shaping moulds existing shadows into tools and weapons.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a dark bargain lets the shaper command shadows; each one whispers faintly while in use.
- **Inner energy:** inner energy sent into one's own shadow, which then moves with the martial artist's forms.
- **Arcane:** sigils drawn in soot, and shadows peeling off the walls to answer.
