# Madness

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The power to crack a mind open. A glimpse of something wrong, a truth too large to hold, a whisper that will not stop: the target's grip on sense slips. Mild uses leave them shaken and jumpy, starting at shadows for a day. Severe ones break something that does not mend: fears that never leave, voices that never go quiet, a person who is never quite themselves again.

Those who deal in it are rarely sane themselves. It is the currency of forbidden cults and alien things, and the kind of power that is spoken of in lowered voices, if at all. A caster who uses it lightly is seldom trusted again.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/INT/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** shatter sanity
- **Minor → major:** shaken → lasting insanity
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The target is unnerved for a moment and shakes it off. Spectacular failure: the caster glimpses what they meant to show, and carries it away.

## Not to be confused with

*[Confusion](confusion.md)*, which only scrambles thoughts for a short while. Madness does lasting damage.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a flood of alien thought poured into a mind too small for it.
