# Tongues

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Every language opens. The speaker hears foreign words and understands them, opens their mouth and speaks back fluently. At the lightest, only the gist comes through, enough to barter or find the inn. At the strongest, all speech, all script and all codes yield to the one who holds the gift, even the chirps and flickers of beings that were never born on any world the speaker knows.

Traders, missionaries, ambassadors and scholars would kill for it, and sometimes have. It does not give tact, however: a speaker may know exactly what was said and still misunderstand what was meant.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** understand and speak any language, and read any code
- **Minor → major:** the gist → fluent in all, even alien
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The words stay foreign. Spectacular failure: the speaker understands perfectly but can only reply in the wrong tongue, or something rude.

## Not to be confused with

*[Language (per language)](../Skills/language-per-language.md)*, the skill, which is learned one tongue at a time. Tongues opens every language at once, without study.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a flame that seems to touch the tongue; the words come as a gift.
- **Technology:** a translation earpiece and a small speaker at the collar.
- **Arcane:** a spell of understanding cast on eyes and ears together.

## Relations
