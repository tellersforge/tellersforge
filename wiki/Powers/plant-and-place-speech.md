# Plant & Place Speech

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The oak remembers who carved into its bark. The river knows who drowned upstream, the standing stone has watched a thousand dawns, and the hearth has heard every family quarrel. This power lets the speaker ask them. At first only impressions come back: a feeling, a colour, a weight. With practice, the place gives full testimony, in its own slow way.

Plants and stones are patient and strange. They speak slowly, reckon time in seasons, and care little for names. Druids, hermits and wandering mystics use the gift to learn what the land has seen. Investigators with it are rare and very much sought after.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** talk with plants, stones, rivers and the spirits of places
- **Minor → major:** impressions → full testimony
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Only silence, or the rustle of leaves. Spectacular failure: the place answers, and it is angry, or will not let go of the listener.

## Not to be confused with

*[Spirit Speech](spirit-speech.md)*, which talks with the spirits of the dead and the unseen. Plant & Place Speech talks with living things, stones and the land itself.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** palms on bark or stone, and a long quiet listening.
- **Arcane:** a small circle of salt and leaves, spoken into as if into a well.
