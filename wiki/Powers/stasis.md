# Stasis

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A bubble outside of time. What lies in it doesn't change: a wound stops bleeding, a poison stops spreading, a body stops ageing. Light sits oddly on its surface, and dust doesn't settle on it.

Physicians of strange schools use it to keep the dying alive until help can come. Sorcerers use it to hold an enemy out of the world for a century. Old tales tell of princesses asleep for ages in towers wrapped in briars, and of sleeping armies waiting under hills for the day they are needed. The greatest stasis is a sleep of ages, and those who wake from it face a world that no longer remembers them.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** freeze a target in time
- **Minor → major:** pause a wound → a sleep of ages
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The moment moves on unchanged. Spectacular failure: the bubble catches the caster as well, until someone thinks to break it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Sleep](sleep.md)*, which only makes someone sleep; Stasis stops time for them entirely, and they wake unaged.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a circle of runes around the body, and a faint silver shimmer like ice on still water.
- **Technology:** a cryogenic pod or a field projector, with frosted glass and a steady hum.
