# Conjure Object

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Something from nothing. A rope when the climb needs one, a lamp in the dark, a key for the door. The caster pictures the thing and it forms in the hand, whole and working. The things are plain and useful, as a rule, rather than beautiful.

Small workings make a tool or a torch. Great ones make a building's worth of stone, timber and iron. Conjured things tend to last a while and then fade, though some traditions claim a master's work can be made permanent. Merchants don't care for it much, and it is rarely welcome at a market.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** create an object from nothing
- **Minor → major:** a tool → a building's worth
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing forms, or the thing is useless. Spectacular failure: the object appears, then crumbles in the hand at the worst moment.

## Not to be confused with

*[Matter Synthesis](matter-synthesis.md)*, which builds items from raw material atom by atom; Conjure Object makes them from nothing at all.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a gesture and a word, and the object assembles in a glitter of light.
- **Divine:** the god provides what is needed, falling into an open hand.
- **Technology:** a pocket fabricator prints the object in a few moments.
