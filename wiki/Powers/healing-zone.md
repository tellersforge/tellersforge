# Healing Zone

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Healing spread through a place. Everyone within reach feels warmth, pain eases, and wounds begin to close. The weakest form is a gentle trickle across a sickroom. At its strongest, an entire battlefield's worth of wounded soldiers rises again.

It is rarely precise. Friend and foe alike are healed if they stand within it, and the healer cannot choose who is spared. Some armies bring such healers to battle; others hunt them first.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/END/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** heal everyone in an area
- **Minor → major:** a trickle → an army restored
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Only a faint warmth. Spectacular failure: the zone drains the healer instead, or heals the enemy most.

## Not to be confused with

*[Healing Touch](healing-touch.md)*, which heals one person by touch. Healing Zone heals everyone in an area at once.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a holy relic raised high and a hymn sung over the ranks.
- **Spirit:** a great spirit summoned to breathe over the whole field.
- **Technology:** a projector that fills the air with a healing mist of tiny machines.
