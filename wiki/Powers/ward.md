# Ward

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A place that knows when it is being entered. Glyphs traced over a doorway, marks carved beneath the floor, a thread strung across a gate: the ward is a guardian that never sleeps. The simplest ring a chime in the owner's ear when someone crosses. Stronger ones lock doors, blind intruders or burn them where they stand.

Wards take time to lay and care to keep. They can be found by those who know what to look for, and broken by those who know how. Every thief who has studied them knows the most dangerous kind is the one you did not see.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** protect a place with alarms, glyphs and seals
- **Minor → major:** a chime → deadly seals
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the ward doesn't hold, or doesn't notice. Spectacular failure: it goes off against its own maker, or at the wrong moment.

## Not to be confused with

*[Warding Circle](warding-circle.md)*, a quick barrier against spirits or foes; and *[Rune & Sigil Magic](rune-and-sigil-magic.md)*, the broader art of powers bound into written marks.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** glyphs drawn in chalk and ink, glowing faintly when disturbed.
- **Divine:** holy symbols painted above doors, blessed with oil.
- **Technology:** sensors, locks and hidden turrets wired to a central alarm.
- **Folk magic:** bells, threads and bottles buried under the doorstep.
