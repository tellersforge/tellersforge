# Oath-Binding

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A promise made with this power attached cannot be broken lightly. At its mildest, the oath-taker feels a tightening in the throat when lying, a sour taste when tempted. At its strongest, breaking the oath means sickness, madness or death, and everyone at the swearing knows it.

Kings use it for treaties, merchants for contracts, clans for peace-making after a feud. A binder is a precious thing at a negotiation, because words backed by this power are worth more than any hostage. The cautious read every clause twice and argue over each word, since the binding holds to the letter, not the spirit, and oathbreakers have been undone by a single forgotten comma.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** enforce sworn oaths and bargains
- **Minor → major:** discomfort when lying → death on breaking
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The oath is sworn, but nothing holds it but honour. Spectacular failure: the binding catches the binder, too.

## Not to be confused with

*[Geas](geas.md)*, which lays a duty or taboo on someone by decree; Oath-Binding enforces promises freely sworn.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** an oath sworn on the altar with a god as witness, the flame flaring once as it is spoken.
- **Folk magic:** palms cut and pressed together over a spring, and a rhyme recited by both.
