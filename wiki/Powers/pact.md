# Pact

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Something always answers if you ask the right way and offer the right price. The pact-maker makes deals with beings of power: demons, fae, the spirits of old places. A few years of their life, their firstborn, their voice, their shadow. In return they receive a gift: power, luck, knowledge, revenge. At its least a pact buys a small favour. At its most it binds a lifetime, and sometimes beyond.

Pacts are legal, precise and merciless. The being keeps to the letter, and the letter is never quite what the mortal expected. The wise read every word, and the wiser don't sign.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/COU/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** seal a bargain with a being for power, at a price
- **Minor → major:** a small favour → a lifelong pact
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the being refuses or doesn't come. Spectacular failure: the bargain is sealed, but on the being's terms.

## Not to be confused with

*[Otherworldly Dealings](../Skills/otherworldly-dealings.md)*, the skill of negotiating with such beings; Pact is the power to seal the bargain.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a gift left at a crossroads or a fairy ring, and a promise spoken to the wind.
