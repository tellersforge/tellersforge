# Fate-Weaving

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Destiny is a thread, and a weaver with this power can reach into the loom. At its smallest, it nudges one outcome: the arrow that should have killed turns slightly, the messenger arrives an hour sooner. At its greatest it rewrites a life, making a beggar a king or a prophesied hero an ordinary farmer.

Fate-weavers are rare and feared, even by gods. The old stories agree that fate resists: pull one thread, and others tighten elsewhere, often in ways no one predicted. The wise weaver changes as little as possible, and prefers a light touch to a heavy hand.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/WIS/CHA/INT/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** alter destiny itself
- **Minor → major:** nudge an outcome → rewrite a destiny
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The thread won't move. Spectacular failure: the weave tangles, and the change takes a shape nobody wanted.

## Not to be confused with

*[Fate-Reading](fate-reading.md)*, which only reads what destiny holds; Fate-Weaving reaches in and changes it.
