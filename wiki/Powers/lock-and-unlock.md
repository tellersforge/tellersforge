# Lock & Unlock

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A small, quiet power with a large reputation. A latch lifts, a bolt slides back, a padlock opens in the hand. Or the reverse: a door closes, and no key or crowbar will open it until the caster allows. Burglars dream of it, gaolers dread it, and cautious lords pay to have their strongrooms guarded against it.

It is a power of closures of every kind: doors, chests, shackles, sluice gates, the clasp of a book. At its smallest it pops a simple latch. At its greatest, a vault is sealed so thoroughly that an army with a battering ram would give up first.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **INT** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** seal or open closures
- **Minor → major:** pop a latch → seal a vault
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The lock holds. Spectacular failure: it jams for everyone, the caster included.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lockpicking](../Skills/lockpicking.md)*, the skill, which opens a lock by hand and with tools, and which Mage-Sight can't detect.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a whispered word pressed into the keyhole.
- **Psionic:** feeling the pins and pushing them with the mind.
- **Technology:** a handheld device that talks electronic locks open.
