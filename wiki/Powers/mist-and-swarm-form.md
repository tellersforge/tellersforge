# Mist & Swarm Form

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Flesh unravels into something that cannot be held: a grey fog that seeps under a door, a column of smoke, a shadow on the wall that moves on its own, a whirring cloud of flies or bats. Blades pass through, ropes fall slack and walls become only suggestions. The traveller drifts where a draught might go and gathers again on the other side.

It is a haunting power, and those who use it are rarely trusted. A mist does not speak, a swarm does not open a door politely, and people who see the change tend to reach for fire. In that shape the self is scattered, and a strong wind or a blaze can do real damage.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** become mist, smoke, shadow or a swarm
- **Minor → major:** slip under a door → travel as fog
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the body blurs at the edges and stays solid. Spectacular failure: part of it scatters and part does not, or the pieces are slow to find each other again.

## Not to be confused with

*[Phasing](phasing.md)*, which passes through solid matter while keeping a body; and *[Elemental Body](elemental-body.md)*, which becomes fire, stone or storm rather than drifting mist.

## Costumes

- **Vampiric:** the undead's old trick, a creeping fog that thickens at the foot of a bed.
- **Spirit:** a spirit-walker who unbinds into wind and swirling leaves.
- **Technology:** a body made of tiny machines that disperses into a buzzing grey cloud.
