# Mind Meld

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Two minds become, for a while, one. The practitioner lays hands on the other's face, or forehead to forehead, and the boundary between them dissolves. Each knows what the other knows, feels what the other feels, and remembers what the other remembers. At its lightest it passes a single thought. At its fullest the two can hardly tell afterwards whose childhood was whose.

It needs touch and it needs consent, or at least no struggle, and it is never one-sided: whatever is taken, something is given back. That makes it intimate and dangerous. Those who meld too often or too deeply begin to lose the edges of themselves.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** two minds merge fully by touch
- **Minor → major:** share a thought → fully merged
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The minds brush and slide apart. Spectacular failure: a memory or fear crosses over and will not leave.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mind Link](mind-link.md)*, which lets minds talk across a distance while staying separate. Mind Meld needs touch and merges them fully.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** fingertips on the other's temples and a long, slow breath shared between them.
