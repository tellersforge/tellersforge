# Weaken

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A draining of strength that leaves the victim puzzled first and frightened after. Arms feel heavy, the sword seems to have doubled its weight, a familiar stair becomes a climb. The effect creeps in rather than striking, and most victims blame fever or age until it is too late.

At its mildest, Weaken takes the edge off a warrior for a single fight. At its worst it leaves a strong man as helpless as an infant, unable to lift his own head from the pillow. It can also take other gifts: a scholar's clear thinking, a singer's voice. Sorcerers favour it because it is quiet, and because weakened enemies can still be made to talk.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **COU**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** sap strength or ability
- **Minor → major:** tired arms → helpless as a child
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The target feels a moment's weariness, then nothing. Spectacular failure: the drain runs backwards, and the caster is left shaking.

## Not to be confused with

*[Harm Touch](harm-touch.md)*, which wounds the body outright; Weaken leaves the flesh whole and takes away what it can do.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a grey mist drawn from the target and caught in the caster's closed fist.
- **Occult:** a thread from the victim's clothing, knotted and left in a bowl of stale water.
- **Technology:** a nerve-dampening dart or field that slackens the muscles.
