# Bind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Invisible cords wrap tight around the victim. The tongue freezes, the limbs lock, the spirit cannot move. Bind holds a person or being still, silent or powerless for as long as the binder can keep it. Beginners stop a tongue for a moment. The greatest bindings in story hold gods beneath mountains for a thousand years.

It is a strained, sweating power. Every binding pulls against the binder, and the stronger the victim the harder it pulls. A bond that slips often leaves the victim very angry indeed, and the binder in the worst possible place to deal with it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/WIS/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** restrain a person or spirit from acting, speaking or moving
- **Minor → major:** silence a tongue → bind a god
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the bonds don't hold. Spectacular failure: they snap back and bind the binder.

## Not to be confused with

*[Imprison](imprison.md)*, which seals a being away in a place; *[Geas](geas.md)*, which binds by oath; and *[Hold](hold.md)*, which simply freezes the body. Bind restrains a being where it stands.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** glowing chains of words wrapped around the target.
- **Occult:** a knotted cord pulled tight over the victim's name.
- **Folk magic:** a charm of hair and thread and a muttered binding.
- **Technology:** restraining fields and paralysing charges.
