# Transmutation

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The old dream: lead into gold. The transmuter touches a thing and it becomes another: lead goes to iron, wood to stone, water to wine, and, at the peak of the art, grey stone gleams into gold. Small changes are cheap and useful, like a rusted chain made fresh iron. Great ones can make a fortune, or ruin a kingdom's coinage.

What changes is the substance, not the shape. A lead cup becomes a gold cup. The work is exacting and sometimes unstable: a change may slip back after a time, and some materials resist or refuse. Merchants, lords and treasuries fear transmuters more than thieves, and many transmuters live in quiet, well-guarded towers.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** change one substance into another
- **Minor → major:** lead to iron → stone to gold
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The substance changes only at the surface, or reverts quickly. Spectacular failure: it becomes something else entirely, often poisonous or explosive.

## Not to be confused with

*[Matter Synthesis](matter-synthesis.md)*, which makes new matter from nothing; and *[Alchemy](../Skills/alchemy.md)*, the skill of refining and mixing substances by craft. Transmutation turns one thing into another directly.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a formula of correspondences spoken over the object; its colour drains and refills.
- **Occult:** a secret learned at a price; the change leaves a faint smell of burnt hair.
- **Alchemical:** powders, reagents and a crucible; the work bubbles, smokes and settles into something new.
