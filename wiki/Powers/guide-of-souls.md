# Guide of Souls

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The dying are frightened, and the dead are lost. The guide walks with them. With a lamp, a song or simply a quiet presence, the guide leads souls across the river, down the road, to whatever rest awaits them. At its simplest it eases a hard passing. At its greatest the guide can walk into the land of the dead and bring someone back out.

The guide lives with one foot in each world. They are welcome at deathbeds and uneasy at weddings. The dead trust them. The gods of the underworld tolerate them, most of the time.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** lead the dead to rest, or travel beside them
- **Minor → major:** ease a passing → bring someone back out
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the soul stays lost. Spectacular failure: the guide is pulled along the road, too far.

## Not to be confused with

*[Descent to the Underworld](descent-to-the-underworld.md)*, which is travelling to the land of the dead; Guide of Souls leads the dead on their way.
