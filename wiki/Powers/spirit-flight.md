# Spirit Flight

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The body lies still on the mat, and its owner is somewhere else entirely. Spirit flight frees the self to wander: up through the roof, across the valley, into the other worlds where spirits and gods keep their houses. At first it is only a moment of peeking out. At its fullest the traveller can roam the spirit realms for days.

The body left behind is helpless and must be guarded. The spirit can be lost, chased, trapped or eaten. Those who travel far often come back changed, and stories warn of the one who flew too far and returned to find someone else at home.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** leave your body as a spirit
- **Minor → major:** peek out → roam the spirit worlds
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the spirit will not leave the body. Spectacular failure: it gets out and cannot find its way back.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bilocation](bilocation.md)*, which is being in two places at once in the body; and *[Descent to the Underworld](descent-to-the-underworld.md)*, the journey into the land of the dead.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a shaman's drum and a long night's trance.
- **Psionic:** a projected mind that floats above the body.
- **Occult:** a flying ointment rubbed on the skin, and dreams of dark sabbaths.
- **Technology:** a virtual avatar in a data world, or a sensory projection device.
