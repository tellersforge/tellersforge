# Luck-Stealing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The thief of fortune. A handshake, a borrowed coin, a shared cup, and the luck leaves one person and settles on another. The victim notices nothing at first. Then the cards stop falling, the horse goes lame, the letters go astray. Meanwhile someone nearby is having a remarkably good year.

Folk tradition is full of it: the neighbour who asks to borrow salt on a feast day, the stranger who wants to shake the bride's hand. Small thefts give a winning streak. Great ones strip a family's fortune for generations and carry it off to another house, which prospers until someone works out why.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/COU/LUCK · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** take someone's luck for yourself
- **Minor → major:** a winning streak → drain a family's fortune
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No luck moves. Spectacular failure: the thief's own luck goes the other way, and the victim has a fine week.

## Not to be confused with

*[Luck-Granting](luck-granting.md)*, which gives fortune freely; Luck-Stealing takes it from someone else, and they pay for every lucky break.

## Costumes

- **Folk magic:** a borrowed pinch of salt or a coin never returned, carrying the household's luck out the door.
