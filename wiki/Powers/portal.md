# Portal

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A doorway cut into the air. Its edges burn, or ripple, or shine like water, and on the other side lies somewhere else: a room in a distant city, a hillside on another continent, another world entirely. Anyone can step through while it stays open.

Small portals flicker for a moment and let one person pass. Great ones stand for years, carrying trade and armies between worlds. In the far future, starships fold space and pass through gates of the same kind. Every portal is a door both ways, and those who open them learn to be careful what might be waiting to come through from the far side.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** open a doorway others can pass through, including folded-space drives
- **Minor → major:** a brief door → a lasting gate between worlds
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No doorway opens. Spectacular failure: it opens in the wrong place, or something comes through before anyone goes.

## Not to be confused with

*[Long Teleport](long-teleport.md)*, which carries the caster alone and in an instant; a Portal is a door others can walk through too.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a ring of fire traced in the air with a staff.
- **Technology:** a ring of machinery, or a folded-space drive in a ship's heart.
- **Occult:** a blood-drawn doorway that opens onto somewhere dark.
- **Divine:** a gate of light the god opens for their chosen.
