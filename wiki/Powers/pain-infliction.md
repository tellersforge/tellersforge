# Pain Infliction

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Agony with no wound to show for it. The target doubles over, gasping, while nothing touches them and no blood is spilled. A sting on the skin, a burning in the joints, a white-hot knife behind the eyes: this power produces them all, and leaves the body whole afterwards. That is exactly why interrogators and cruel masters want it, and why those who use it openly are hated.

It does no lasting harm, which is not the same as doing no harm. Pain breaks concentration and resolve, stops a spell in the throat, and brings brave people to their knees. The worst of those who hold it learn how much a body can bear, and stay just short of it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **COU**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** cause agony without injury
- **Minor → major:** a sting → torture
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The target winces and recovers. Spectacular failure: the pain comes home to the caster, sharp and whole.

## Not to be confused with

*[Harm Touch](harm-touch.md)*, which does real injury. Pain Infliction leaves no wound at all; it only hurts.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a baton or collar that sends a searing charge through the nerves.
