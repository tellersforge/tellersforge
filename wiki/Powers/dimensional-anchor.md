# Dimensional Anchor

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Space grows heavy. The blink of a teleporter falters, the doorway to another world refuses to open, and anyone trying to slip away between one step and the next finds they have to use their legs like everyone else. This power pins things to where they are. The beginner hinders a single jump. The master locks down a whole region, so that nothing arrives or leaves except the slow way.

Fortresses, prisons and treasuries set such anchors to keep out raiders who do not use doors. Hunters of otherworldly beings rely on it to keep their prey from fleeing home.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** block teleporting and world-crossing
- **Minor → major:** hinder one blink → lock down a region
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the escape slips through. Spectacular failure: the anchor catches the caster's own friends, or the caster, at the wrong moment.

## Not to be confused with

*[Power Negation](power-negation.md)*, which blocks all powers; Dimensional Anchor stops only travel between places and worlds.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a heavy iron spike driven into the ground and inscribed with binding runes.
- **Divine:** a god's stillness laid on the land, a place where nothing may flee.
- **Technology:** a field generator that stabilises space and fouls jump drives.
