# Wind Control

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The air becomes a weapon and a servant. A gust snuffs a candle across the room, a steady breeze fills a becalmed sail, a gale bowls soldiers off a wall. The more skilled can spin a whirlwind that lifts dust and debris, or shape winds so fine and fast they cut like blades. At its greatest, a tornado twists down out of a darkening sky.

The wind is hard to aim and harder to stop. Arrows veer, smoke flees, and loose tiles, sand and gravel become missiles. Sailors pay dearly for a wind-worker and fear one just as much, since the power that fills sails can also tear them.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** gusts, gales, whirlwinds, cutting winds
- **Minor → major:** snuff a candle → a tornado
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A puff of air and nothing more. Spectacular failure: the wind swings round and flings the caster or friends off their feet.

## Not to be confused with

*[Weather-Working](weather-working.md)*, which brings whole storms and seasons; Wind Control commands the air directly, here and now.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a sweeping arm and a whistled note, and the air rushes to follow.
- **Spirit:** the wind spirits called by name, who come howling and playful.
- **Divine:** the breath of heaven, a strong clean wind that smells of rain.
- **Inner energy:** a sharp exhale and a palm strike, sending a blade of air across the field.
