# Radiant Smite

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Holy light made into a weapon. The smiter's blow lands with a flash, and the thing struck burns without flame. Living foes feel a hard, clean blow. The walking dead, the corrupted and the creatures of the dark feel it far worse, as though light itself were poison to them. At its greatest it calls down a pillar of radiance that leaves nothing unholy standing.

It belongs to champions of faith: temple knights, wandering exorcists, grim hunters of the unquiet dead. It asks for courage as much as belief, because it works best at arm's length from the horror it is meant to destroy.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/WIS/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** pure or holy energy, strongest against the undead and the dark
- **Minor → major:** a blessed blow → a pillar of light
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The blow lands as an ordinary blow, with no light behind it. Spectacular failure: the light fails at the worst moment and the dark thing knows it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Turn Undead](turn-undead.md)*, which drives the dead back or away; Radiant Smite burns them where they stand.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a prayer spoken as the blow falls, and the weapon blazes with its god's light.
