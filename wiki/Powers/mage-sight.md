# Mage-Sight

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The mage looks at an empty road and reads it like a page: the lie of the land, the intent of those who passed, the faint marks left by old spells. Mage-sight reads the traces in a place. At first there is only a faint trace; at its best, a full reading of what happened there.

It shows only what was, never what will be. It is the working tool of a travelling sorcerer, a hunter of wicked mages or a guardian of old sites, and it rewards patience. Those who practise it walk slowly and look at nothing in particular.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/PER/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** read the traces left in a place: the lie of the land, the intent of those who passed, the residue of past workings; shows only what was, never what will be
- **Minor → major:** a faint trace → a full reading of what happened here
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing but an ordinary road or room. Spectacular failure: the mage misreads old traces as fresh ones.

## Not to be confused with

*[Postcognition](postcognition.md)*, which sees the past as a vision; Mage-Sight reads its traces like tracks.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a mage's focused gaze, with eyes half closed and a whispered word.
