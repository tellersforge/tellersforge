# Super Strength

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Strength far beyond anything a human body should hold. A door torn from its hinges, a cart lifted out of a ditch with one hand, a wall pushed over with a shoulder. The strong one's own body often looks ordinary, which surprises everyone the first time.

At its smallest it is the strength of three men. At its greatest the bearer lifts a house, a ship, or a building. The world is fragile to such people: they break cups, door handles and handshakes, and learn to be gentle or learn to be alone. Heroes of legend have it, and so do the monsters they fight.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **STR**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** strength far beyond human
- **Minor → major:** lift a door → lift a building
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The load doesn't move. Spectacular failure: something gives, and it is the bearer's own back, or a floor collapses beneath them.

## Not to be confused with

*[Growing](growing.md)*, which makes the body huge and strong with it; Super Strength keeps the ordinary size and adds only the might.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** an inborn power in muscle and bone, first noticed when a child lifts the family cart.
- **Technology:** an exoskeleton or synthetic muscles, whirring faintly under the skin.
- **Arcane:** a spell that lends the strength of an ox or a giant for a while.
- **Inner energy:** inner energy gathered in the belly and loosed through the limbs in a shout.
