# Attribute Boost

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A brief surge of excellence in one quality of body or mind. The scholar's thoughts sharpen until every page makes sense. The runner's legs find a second wind. The shy clerk finds, for an hour, the voice of a king. It is the power to be more than one is, briefly.

A small boost is an edge, the sort that goes unremarked. A great one makes the subject superhuman for a time: a strongman out of a scribe, a genius out of a farmhand. When it wears off, the gap feels larger than before, and some people chase that feeling for the rest of their lives.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** temporarily raise one attribute
- **Minor → major:** a brief edge → superhuman
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The surge never comes. Spectacular failure: the boost comes and goes too fast, and leaves the subject weaker than before.

## Not to be confused with

*[Power Surge](power-surge.md)*, which drives a power harder for a moment; this boosts a quality of the body or mind itself.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a potion or a brief incantation that makes the eyes shine brighter.
- **Divine:** a god's favour granted for a holy task.
- **Technology:** a neural booster or a stimulant injection that burns through the blood.
