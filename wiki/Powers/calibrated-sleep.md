# Calibrated Sleep

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Sleep exactly as deep as needed. The practitioner lays hands on one person and chooses: light rest to let a fevered child finally close their eyes, or a stillness so deep that a surgeon can cut and stitch without a flinch. The subject wakes when released, refreshed and with no memory of the time in between.

It is a healer's art, and a precise one. It works on one person at a time and needs touch and calm. In places without anaesthetic it is worth its weight in gold, and physicians with the gift are courted by armies and noble houses alike. In the wrong hands it is a very tidy way to kidnap someone.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** put one person into exactly the depth of sleep wanted, from light rest to deep stillness for surgery; leaves no memory
- **Minor → major:** a short rest → surgery-deep sleep
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The subject only dozes and wakes at the first cut. Spectacular failure: the sleep goes too deep, and the subject can't be woken for far too long.

## Not to be confused with

*[Sleep](sleep.md)*, which drowses anyone nearby but can't be controlled. Calibrated Sleep takes one person to exactly the depth wanted.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a gentle hand on the brow, a counted breath, and a word that sets the depth like a dial.
