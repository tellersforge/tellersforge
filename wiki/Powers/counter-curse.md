# Counter-Curse

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Every curse has a thread, and the counter-curser finds it and pulls. The evil eye loses its sting, the hex on the cow is lifted, the spell on the sleeping princess unravels. The humblest practitioners are village cunning folk with a bag of charms. The greatest can grab a curse in mid-flight and send it back to whoever cast it, with interest.

It is careful work. A clumsy attempt can spread a curse rather than lift it. Those who do it often become targets themselves, since curse-makers do not like being undone, and so they learn to protect their own homes first.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** break or reverse spells and curses
- **Minor → major:** lift a jinx → return a curse to its sender
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the curse holds. Spectacular failure: it spreads to the counter-curser.

## Not to be confused with

*[Dispel](dispel.md)*, which ends any working power; and *[Purification](purification.md)*, which cleanses a person or place of general pollution.

## Costumes

- **Folk magic:** egg-rolling, poured lead, a thread burnt in a candle flame.
- **Arcane:** a formal unbinding read backwards from an old book.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Dispel](dispel.md), [Purification](purification.md)
