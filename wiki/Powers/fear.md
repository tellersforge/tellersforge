# Fear

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The cold in the stomach, the prickle along the neck, the sudden certainty that something is wrong and coming closer. This power pours that feeling into others. At its gentlest it is unease: a traveller who hurries past a lane without knowing why. At its strongest, whole companies of hardened soldiers throw down their weapons and run, and never agree afterwards about what they saw.

It is the favourite gift of tyrants, cults and things that live under bridges. It works best in the dark and on the tired, and worst on those who have already lost everything. It also makes enemies for life, because nobody forgives the one who made them run.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **COU**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** instil terror
- **Minor → major:** unease → an army flees
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A shiver and nothing more. Spectacular failure: the dread rebounds, and the caster is the one who flees.

## Not to be confused with

*[Presence](presence.md)*, which makes others feel small but keeps them in place; and *[Emotion Control](emotion-control.md)*, which can stir any feeling. Fear only terrifies, and scatters.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a whispered name from a forbidden book; shadows lengthen toward the target.
- **Arcane:** a gesture that makes the caster seem suddenly tall, with eyes like coals.
- **Psionic:** a scream no ear hears, pressed straight into the mind.
- **Mutation:** a smell, a sound or a glimpse of the body that stirs an ancient animal horror.
