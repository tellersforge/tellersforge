# Water-Spirit Allure

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A song across the water, a pale face beneath the surface, a promise of something lovely just a little further out. Those caught by the allure walk towards the river or the lake without thinking, smiling, and keep walking when the water reaches their knees, their waist, their chin.

This is the ancient gift of river spirits, lake maidens and the lonely beings of the deep, and those mortals who learn it are rarely trusted. At its gentlest it draws someone to the water's edge for a conversation, or turns their head for a season. At its cruellest it drowns. Old wisdom warns travellers never to answer a voice from the water, and never to look too long at their own reflection in a still pool.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** lure people into water to drown or enchant them
- **Minor → major:** a lure → drowning
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The target hesitates on the shore, uneasy but free. Spectacular failure: the lure draws the wrong person, or the user, into the water.

## Not to be confused with

*[Charm](charm.md)*, which makes someone well disposed towards the user; Water-Spirit Allure draws them bodily into the water.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a voice rising from a pool at twilight, sweet and cold, with ripples spreading from nowhere.
- **Heroic legend:** the lake maiden of the old tales, combing her hair on a stone while the hero drifts closer.
