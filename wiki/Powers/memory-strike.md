# Memory Strike

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

One person is struck out of the world's memory. Their friends forget their face, their family forgets their name, and the tavern keeper cannot recall who ran up that tab. At its smallest, a single room forgets someone was ever there. At its greatest, an entire city wakes without any memory that a certain person ever lived.

It is a void art and a cruel one. Letters, portraits and records remain, which only makes things stranger for those who find them. The target remembers everything, and walks among people who look straight through them. Few are ever restored; most simply start again somewhere else.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/CHA/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** strike one person out of the memory of everyone for miles around (dark)
- **Minor → major:** a room forgets them → a city forgets they existed
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A few people blink, then remember. Spectacular failure: the forgetting takes the caster's own name instead.

## Not to be confused with

*[Memory Editing](memory-editing.md)*, which changes what one person remembers. Memory Strike erases one person from everyone's memory at once.
