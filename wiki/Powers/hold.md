# Hold

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The target stops. Mid-stride, mid-swing, mid-word, the muscles lock and the body stands like a statue while the eyes still move. It is a frightening thing to see and a worse thing to feel. Those who have been held describe a cold pressure around the whole body, as if frozen in clear ice.

A brief hold buys a moment to escape or to strike. A strong one lasts until it is released, and in the old tales captives stand for years in some sorcerer's garden. Temple guards use it to stop fighting without bloodshed, and a band of kidnappers can do terrible things with it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/WIS/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** freeze a creature in place
- **Minor → major:** brief stiffness → indefinitely
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The target stiffens and shakes it off at once. Spectacular failure: the hold fastens on the wrong person, perhaps a friend.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bind](bind.md)*, which ties up with conjured bonds that can be cut or broken; Hold locks the body from within.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a sharp word and a hand closing into a fist; the victim's limbs seize.
- **Divine:** a god's command to be still, spoken with a raised staff.
- **Psionic:** the mind grips the nerves and holds them fixed.
- **Technology:** a paralysis beam or a hack into the target's motor implants.
