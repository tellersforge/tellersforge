# Detect Life & Minds

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The heartbeats in the dark. The bearer feels living things around them: a hidden guard, a rat in the wall, a sleeping child upstairs. At greater reach, and with minds rather than bodies, the gift maps whole streets, then a city full of minds, bright points of thought in the dark.

It is invaluable for search and rescue, for hunters and for guards, and dreadful for anyone trying to hide. The undead, machines and the truly thoughtless may not show at all, which the wise treat as a warning in itself.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** sense living beings or thinking minds
- **Minor → major:** a nearby heartbeat → map a city's minds
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The bearer senses no one. Spectacular failure: the bearer feels a crowd where there is none, or misses the one who matters.

## Not to be confused with

*[Nature Sense](nature-sense.md)*, which reads the living land as a whole; this power looks for beings and minds.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a warmth in the chest for every living soul near the priest.
- **Psionic:** the mind picking up the murmur of every nearby thought.
- **Spirit:** the spirits of the place whispering who has come in.
- **Technology:** a life-sign scanner with a blinking map of bodies.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Nature Sense](nature-sense.md)
