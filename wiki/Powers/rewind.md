# Rewind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A few moments taken back. The glass shatters, the caster frowns, and the glass is whole again on the table. The wrong word is said, and then it hasn't been. Everyone else goes on as though nothing happened, but the caster remembers the first time and can try again.

It is a small power at first, one action undone. At its greatest it relives a whole day, over and over, until the day comes out right. It is also a burden: the caster remembers the versions no one else does, and some grow strange carrying so many unlived hours.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** undo recent moments
- **Minor → major:** one action → a whole day relived
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Time won't step back. Spectacular failure: the rewind skips, and the caster loses a memory of something that did happen.

## Not to be confused with

*[Future Seeing (Precognition)](future-seeing-precognition.md)*, which shows what may come; Rewind undoes what has come already.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a spell spoken backwards, while the world blurs and resets like a wave pulling back.
- **Technology:** a device on the wrist that dials the local moment back, with a rushing sound.
- **Divine:** a saint's mercy, granting a second chance to a sinner who prays for it.
