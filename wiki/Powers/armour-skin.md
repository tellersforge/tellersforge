# Armour Skin

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Skin that turns to something harder. It may be grey stone, a dull metal sheen, or a glittering crust of crystal that chimes when struck. At first only patches change: the forearm raised to ward off a blow, the back of the neck. Masters become living statues in full, able to stand in a hail of arrows and walk forward through it.

The change is heavy. Movement slows, swimming is out of the question, and touch dulls to almost nothing. Some who keep the change too long find their skin slow to soften again. In a fight, though, few sights are as discouraging as a sword blade bending on bare flesh.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** skin turns to stone, steel or crystal
- **Minor → major:** patches → full living metal
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** a patch of the skin greys and softens back. Spectacular failure: the body locks stiff, hardened in the wrong places.

## Not to be confused with

*[Natural Weapons](natural-weapons.md)*, which grows claws and blades; and *[Invulnerability](invulnerability.md)*, which makes the body untouchable except for one weak spot.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a spell of earth and iron, the skin taking on the colour of the chosen metal.
- **Mutation:** a hide that crusts over with stone or crystal when threatened.
- **Technology:** subdermal plates or nanite armour that spreads across the skin like frost.
