# Pain Transfer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The wound does not vanish; it moves. The healer takes a sick child's fever into their own body, or presses a bruise into a stone, or passes a broken leg into a sacrificial goat. The sufferer rises well. The bearer, or the stone, or the goat, pays.

It is old and quietly frightening. The lightest use moves a bruise; the strongest takes on a death, and the one who carries it must survive it or not. Village cunning folk use it with great care, and some families keep an old dog or a pebble on the mantel for the purpose.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** take another's wounds or sickness onto yourself, an object or an animal
- **Minor → major:** a bruise → a death
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The wound stays where it was. Spectacular failure: it lands in the wrong place, or on the wrong person.

## Not to be confused with

*[Transfer Health](transfer-health.md)*, which moves life force from one creature to another. Pain Transfer moves the injury or sickness itself.

## Costumes

- **Folk magic:** a charm spoken while the wound is rubbed onto an egg, a stone or a knotted string.
- **Spirit:** a spirit carries the sickness from one body to another.
