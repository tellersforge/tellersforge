# Transform Others

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The oldest threat in the fairy tale: annoy the wrong person and you end the day as a frog, a toad, a stone lion or a swan. This power forces change on another body, from a harmless joke, such as donkey's ears or blue hair, to a complete remaking that may never be undone. The victim keeps their mind, which is the cruelty of it.

It is feared everywhere and rarely admired. Kings keep counter-magicians just in case. Stories insist there is always a way back, often a kiss, a name spoken aloud or a year of silence, but stories are kind. A great practitioner can make the change last until the world ends.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **COU**/INT/WIS/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** force a transformation on someone else
- **Minor → major:** a cosmetic change → a prince into a frog forever
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the target shivers, perhaps sprouts a feather, and stays themselves. Spectacular failure: the change rebounds on the caster, or strikes someone else.

## Not to be confused with

*[Petrification](petrification.md)*, which only turns flesh to stone; Transform Others can make anything of anyone.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a pointed finger and a precise formula that names what the victim will become.
- **Occult:** a curse muttered over a lock of hair, with a price the caster will pay later.
- **Spirit:** the fae's revenge, laid on with a glance for a broken promise.
