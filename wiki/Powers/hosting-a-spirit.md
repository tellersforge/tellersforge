# Hosting a Spirit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The medium goes still, eyes roll back, and when they speak the voice is someone else's. Hosting lets a spirit, ancestor or god borrow the body: to deliver a message, to give a blessing, to dance at a festival or fight in the host's place. At first only a whisper comes through. At full depth the host steps aside entirely, and whatever is inside wields the body with its own strength.

It takes trust and discipline. A host who is careless may open the door to something they did not invite. Afterwards they often remember nothing, and wake tired, hungry and with bruises they cannot explain.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** let a spirit or deity act through your body
- **Minor → major:** a whispered message → full embodiment
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the spirit does not come. Spectacular failure: the wrong spirit comes, or the right one won't leave.

## Not to be confused with

*[Possession](possession.md)*, which is one being taking over another by force; and *[Trance & Spirit Work](../Skills/trance-and-spirit-work.md)*, the skill of entering trance without power.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** an oracle's chamber, sacred smoke and the god speaking through the priestess.
