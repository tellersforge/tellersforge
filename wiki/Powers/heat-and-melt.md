# Heat & Melt

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Heat without flame. The one who has this gift lays a hand on a cup and it steams; looks at a pot and it boils; stares at a lock and the iron glows a dull red. At its fiercest, a soldier drops a sword that has gone white-hot in his hand, and the blade sags and drips onto the floor.

It is quieter than fire and in some ways worse, because it works on what the enemy is already holding. Water, wax and soft metal go first. Armour grows hot, then hotter, and those inside it learn to strip it off in a hurry. It is also a comfort on a winter march: a cold camp, a frozen stream, a bowl of warm porridge from nothing.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** superheat objects or liquids
- **Minor → major:** warm a cup → melt a sword in its owner's hand
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The object warms a little and cools again. Spectacular failure: the heat blooms in the caster's own hand, or in something they were carrying.

## Not to be confused with

*[Fire Blast](fire-blast.md)*, which throws flame outward; Heat & Melt heats a thing directly with no fire at all.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** the fire spirit's breath passed through the hands, felt as a pulse of warmth.
- **Arcane:** a formula of heat spoken into the object, which begins to tick and creak.
- **Psionic:** the mind stirs the object from within; the air shimmers above it.
