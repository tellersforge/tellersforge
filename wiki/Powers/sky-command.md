# Sky Command

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Holding the sun still. The one who wields this gift raises a hand at dusk and the light lingers; they speak a word at midnight and the dawn comes early. The moon can be called up in daylight or sent below the horizon, and the sky obeys the wishes of one voice.

It belongs to legends and to gods: the heroes of the oldest tales stopped the sun to finish a battle or hastened the dawn to scatter the creatures of the night. At its mildest, the gift steals an hour of light. At its greatest, it halts the heavens. The world notices when the sun stands still, and the powers that set it moving in the first place are rarely pleased.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/WIS/CHA/COU/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** hold or hasten sun and moon, call the dawn
- **Minor → major:** an extra hour of daylight → halt the sun
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The sky ignores the command. Spectacular failure: the heavens shift the wrong way, bringing darkness at noon or a long, cold night.

## Not to be confused with

*[Weather-Working](weather-working.md)*, which commands wind, rain and storm; Sky Command moves the sun and moon. *[Time Stop](time-stop.md)* halts time itself.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a priest-king's prayer, answered by the sun-god holding the chariot in place.
- **Heroic legend:** a hero's command shouted from a hilltop, and the sun, out of respect, obeys.
