# Time Stop

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The world halts. Raindrops hang in the air, a bird is caught mid-wingbeat, a blade stops a finger's width from a throat. Only the caster moves through the stillness, and the silence is total.

It lasts a heartbeat, at first. The greatest hold it for minutes. In that time a great deal can be done: a prisoner freed, a thief away with the prize, a fight settled before the enemy knows it has started. It is exhausting, and dangerous in its own way. Those who use it too often say the stopped world is lonely, and that some have lingered in it and not come back.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/END/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** freeze time
- **Minor → major:** a heartbeat → minutes
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Time wavers and moves on. Spectacular failure: the caster is the one frozen while the world goes on.

## Not to be confused with

*[Haste & Slow](haste-and-slow.md)*, which changes the pace for one body; Time Stop halts everything but the caster.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a snapped finger and a cracked word; the colour drains from the world.
- **Technology:** a wrist device that cuts the caster out of time's current, with a grey shimmer all round.
