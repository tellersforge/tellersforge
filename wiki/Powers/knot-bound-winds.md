# Knot-Bound Winds

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Weather tied up in string. The worker of this gift catches a wind as it blows and binds it into a knot on a cord. Later, far from land or deep in a calm, the knot is untied and the wind comes out, whole and fresh. One knot is a fair breeze. Three knots in a row are said to be a storm, and nobody sensible unties the last one.

Seafarers buy these cords from old women on windy coasts and treat them with great respect. A cord can be traded, stolen or left to a grandchild, and a good one lasts for years. What it cannot do is change what kind of wind it holds.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** tie winds or weather into a cord to release later
- **Minor → major:** one fair wind → a gale in a knot
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The knot slips and the wind blows away before it is caught, or the untied knot releases only a sigh. Spectacular failure: the wrong knot comes undone.

## Not to be confused with

*[Wind Control](wind-control.md)*, which shapes the wind in the moment; and *[Store Power](store-power.md)*, which holds any power for later. Knot-Bound Winds catches weather in a cord.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** the wind is thanked and asked to wait in the knot until it is needed.
- **Folk magic:** a coastal wise woman's trade, done with three knots in a red cord and a coin.
