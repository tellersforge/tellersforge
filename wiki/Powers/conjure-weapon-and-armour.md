# Conjure Weapon & Armour

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A blade that forms from light in an empty hand, a shield that shimmers into being on the arm, a coat of mail that appears on the body as if poured. The gear is real enough to cut and to turn a blow, and it vanishes when its purpose is done or its maker lets it go.

War priests use it to arm the faithful in an ambush. Psychic knights shape swords from thought. At its smallest it is a glowing knife. At its greatest it is a full panoply, helm to greaves, gleaming on a warrior who a moment before stood in a nightshirt.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **COU**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** manifest gear from nothing
- **Minor → major:** a glowing blade → a full panoply
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The weapon flickers and won't hold. Spectacular failure: it shatters mid-fight, or fades just as it is needed.

## Not to be confused with

*[Force Constructs](force-constructs.md)*, which shapes walls, bridges and cages of solid force; this power makes weapons and armour only, sized for a fighter.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a sword of blue fire drawn from the air with a gesture.
- **Divine:** armour of light, bright as the god's favour.
- **Psionic:** a blade of pure will, shaped by the mind and shimmering at the edges.
- **Technology:** nanite gear that assembles on the body in a rush of glittering motes.
