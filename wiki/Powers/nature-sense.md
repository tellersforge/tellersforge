# Nature Sense

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Feeling the land the way others feel the weather. The user stands in a field and knows the soil is sour, walks into a wood and senses that something is wrong with the water upstream, or lies on the grass and feels the whole valley breathing. At first it is only unease, a prickle that says something is not right. With skill, it becomes a living map.

Shepherds, foresters and wandering priests of the green have it, and so do those whose task is guarding a region. It tells of sickness, drought, fire, over-hunting and the passage of large creatures. It says nothing of towns or machines, which to most who have the gift feel like cold, quiet holes in the world.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** sense the life and health of the land
- **Minor → major:** unease → a map of a region
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The land says nothing, or only something vague. Spectacular failure: the sense is badly wrong, pointing to health where there is rot, or danger where there is none.

## Not to be confused with

*[Detect Life & Minds](detect-life-and-minds.md)*, which finds individual living things and thinkers; Nature Sense reads the health of a whole landscape.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** hands pressed to the earth, listening to the land's spirit as it speaks.
- **Technology:** a network of scattered sensors feeding soil, air and water readings straight to the user's mind.
