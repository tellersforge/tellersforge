# Mend

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Broken things made whole. A torn cloak knits itself along the rip, a cracked pot draws its pieces together with a faint click, a snapped sword runs back into one bright length. Small mending is a household blessing that saves a thrifty family a great deal. Great mending rebuilds a fallen wall stone by stone, or restores a wrecked cart to the road.

It repairs what was, not what could be: a broken lamp becomes the same lamp again, not a better one. Pieces that are missing must usually be found first. Crafters have mixed feelings about it, since it can undo a week's careful work or save it, and some old artisans insist a mended blade is never quite as true.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **WIS** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** restore broken objects
- **Minor → major:** fix a torn cloak → rebuild a wall
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The pieces refuse to join, or join weakly and fall apart. Spectacular failure: the object is mended wrong, with parts in the wrong places.

## Not to be confused with

*[Nanite Swarm](nanite-swarm.md)*, which rebuilds and alters things with tiny machines; and *[Mechanics](../Skills/mechanics.md)*, the skill of repair by hand. Mend simply restores the object as it was.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a stroke of the hand along the break, and the edges crawl back together.
- **Divine:** a prayer over the broken thing, which is made whole as if it had never been harmed.
- **Technology:** a repair wand spraying fine filament, or a field that fuses the break seamlessly.
