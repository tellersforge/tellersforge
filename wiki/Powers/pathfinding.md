# Pathfinding

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The certainty of the way. A wayfinder at a crossroads simply knows which road to take, even in fog, even in a city never visited, even in a forest that swallows ordinary travellers. At its smallest it means never being lost. At its greatest, it finds roads no one else can see: the hidden stair, the trail that only opens at dusk, the door in the hillside that leads out of this world and into another.

Guides and scouts with this gift are prized, and some grow uncanny about it, walking with their eyes half closed. It tells the way, not what waits along it. Many a wayfinder has led a party unerringly to exactly where it wanted to go, and straight into trouble.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** always know the way, or find a hidden road
- **Minor → major:** never lost → find the door between worlds
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The sense of direction goes quiet and the wayfinder must navigate like anyone else. Spectacular failure: the way feels certain and is completely wrong.

## Not to be confused with

*[Locate](locate.md)*, which finds a particular thing or person; and *[Wayfinding](../Skills/wayfinding.md)*, the skill of reading stars, maps and land. Pathfinding simply knows the road.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a spirit guide seen from the corner of the eye, a fox or a bird always a little ahead.
- **Divine:** a gentle tug of the heart toward the right road, felt as a small warmth.
- **Psionic:** an inner map that unrolls behind the eyes, with the path traced in light.
- **Technology:** a navigation implant or device that whispers bearings and paints the route on a lens.
