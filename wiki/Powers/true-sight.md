# True Sight

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The eyes open, and the world is exactly what it is. Illusions become thin mist, disguises show the faces beneath, the invisible thief becomes plain in the corner. True sight sees through every false seeming. At first there is only a shimmer where something is wrong; at its fullest, all things show their true shape.

It makes a bearer hard to fool and terrible to deceive. The price is that nothing is ever quite beautiful again. The ruler's fine robes, the painted courtesan's face, even a friend's kind smile show all their flaws.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **PER**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** see through illusion, disguise and invisibility
- **Minor → major:** a shimmer → all things as they truly are
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The illusion holds. Spectacular failure: the bearer sees through the truth and believes the lie.

## Not to be confused with

*[Second Sight](second-sight.md)*, which sees spirits and hidden worlds; true sight strips away illusion and disguise.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** eyes blessed by a god of truth, glowing faintly gold.
- **Arcane:** a salve or a crystal lens that dissolves seeming.
- **Psionic:** a mind that refuses every false impression pressed upon it.
