# Machine Disruption

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A pulse of silence for machines. Lights die, screens go black, engines cough and stop, and the only sounds left are breathing and the tick of cooling metal. The effect is invisible, but everyone in the area feels it: the moment when every clever device around them becomes a lump of dead weight.

Small uses are surgical: a single camera blinks off, a lock stops listening, a pistol's sight goes dark. Large ones darken a whole street. It is a favourite of saboteurs and a terror to anyone whose life depends on a pump, a pacemaker or an aircraft. Most machines recover once the effect fades; the most delicate never do.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** shut down electronics in an area
- **Minor → major:** one device → a city block
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The devices flicker and carry on. Spectacular failure: the pulse knocks out the user's own gear, or a friend's life-support, and leaves the enemy's untouched.

## Not to be confused with

*[Power Negation](power-negation.md)*, which smothers powers and gifts; Machine Disruption kills electronics and leaves magic alone.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a pulse grenade or shoulder-mounted emitter, with a sharp crack and a smell of ozone.
- **Arcane:** a hex of rust and stillness spoken over the machine; frost creeps across its casing.
- **Psionic:** a scream of static from the mind that only machines can hear.
