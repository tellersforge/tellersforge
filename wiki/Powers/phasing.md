# Phasing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Walls stop being walls. The ghost-walker leans into solid stone and slides through it like a hand through mist, stepping out on the other side with no mark on either. A curtain or a thin door is easy. A fortress wall, many paces thick, is the work of a master, and a long, strange walk through grey darkness.

While phased, blades and arrows pass harmlessly through, but so does everything the walker tries to hold. Many learn this the hard way when a dropped key falls through the floor. The great fear is stopping halfway. Stories tell of bodies found fused into walls, and prudent walkers never linger inside anything solid.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** pass through solid matter
- **Minor → major:** a curtain → a fortress wall
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The body meets the wall like anyone else's. Spectacular failure: the phase ends while part of the body is still inside something.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mist & Swarm Form](mist-and-swarm-form.md)*, which breaks the body into cloud or creatures; and *[Etherealness](etherealness.md)*, which steps partly out of the world altogether. Phasing stays whole and simply passes through.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** a flickering, half-real body, the edges blurring as the walker passes into the wall.
- **Psionic:** the mind loosens the body's grip on the world; a faint shimmer and a ringing in the ears.
- **Spirit:** a ghost's gift, borrowed or earned; the walker goes pale and cold as they walk through.
- **Technology:** a phase belt that hums and makes the wearer's outline stutter like a bad image.
