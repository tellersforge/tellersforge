# Life Drain

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Touch, and the victim sags; the drainer straightens. Life Drain pulls vitality out of others to heal and strengthen oneself. At first it is only a tiring touch that brings a little colour to the drainer's cheeks. At its worst the victim is drained to a husk, and the drainer glows with borrowed youth.

It is a hungry power. The more it is used, the more it is wanted. Those who have it live longer, heal faster and are hated wherever they are found out. Stories agree that the hunger never fades, only the reasons for resisting it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **END**/COU/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** steal vitality to heal yourself
- **Minor → major:** a tiring touch → drained to death
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the life will not come. Spectacular failure: the flow runs the wrong way.

## Not to be confused with

*[Transfer Health](transfer-health.md)*, which gives or swaps vitality with consent; and *[Harm Touch](harm-touch.md)*, which wounds without feeding. Life Drain steals.

## Costumes

- **Vampiric:** a bite at the neck or a kiss that leaves the victim pale.
- **Occult:** a withered hand and a hungry curse.
- **Technology:** a gauntlet that draws off the target's bioelectric energy.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Transfer Health](transfer-health.md), [Harm Touch](harm-touch.md)
