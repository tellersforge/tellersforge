# Blood Control

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The blood answers. A wound that should gush slows to a trickle, a fever flush drains away, a pulse steadies. Turned darker, the same power makes a man's limbs jerk and his body walk where he does not want to go, a puppet on strings of his own blood, or makes his heart race until it stops.

It is one of the most feared powers there is, and one of the most whispered about. It needs a living body within reach of the caster's sight, and it is said to work best under a full moon. Many who have it keep to healing, and many who do not are hunted.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/COU/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** move the fluids inside living bodies
- **Minor → major:** slow a bleed → puppet a body
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The blood stirs and settles back. Spectacular failure: the caster's own blood runs wild, bursting from the nose or ears.

## Not to be confused with

*[Water Control](water-control.md)*, which moves water outside bodies; and *[Body Control](body-control.md)*, which governs one's own body. Blood Control reaches into other living bodies.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a forbidden art of blood, learned in a pact and marked by scars.
- **Arcane:** a formula of vessels and humours, spoken with a finger drawing the path of the flow.
