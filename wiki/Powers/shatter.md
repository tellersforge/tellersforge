# Shatter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Things break. A glance, a word or a clenched fist, and a window cracks from corner to corner, a clay jar bursts, a sword snaps at the guard. At its greatest the power finds the weakness in stone and mortar and makes whole structures fail: a gatehouse arch drops its keystone, a tower groans and leans and comes down in a roar of dust.

It is a destroyer's gift and a sapper's, and it is nothing like subtle. Brittle things go easily; tough, flexible ones, leather and green wood and good steel, resist. Those who use it near buildings learn the hard lesson about what falls and where. A singer who can crack glass with a note is a party trick; one who can crack a wall is a weapon.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** break objects and structures
- **Minor → major:** crack glass → topple a tower
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A faint crack and nothing more. Spectacular failure: something breaks that was holding up the ceiling above the caster.

## Not to be confused with

*[Disintegrate](disintegrate.md)*, which unmakes matter into nothing; Shatter only breaks things into pieces.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a sharp, discordant word that sets the object ringing until it bursts.
- **Psionic:** the mind finds the fault in the thing and pushes along it.
- **Technology:** a resonance projector tuned to the target's frequency, whining higher until it breaks.
