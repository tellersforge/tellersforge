# Shared Soul

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

One life in two bodies. The user's soul is bound to an animal double: a wolf, a hawk, a bear, a hare. At first they only sense each other across the distance, dreaming each other's dreams. As the bond deepens, they become one life: what one feels, the other feels, and what wounds one wounds both.

In many old traditions, every person has such a double somewhere, and only the gifted ever meet theirs. It is a precious and dangerous link. The double can scout, fight and carry messages, and a hunter who kills a certain wolf may find a village mourning the next morning. Wise people guard their other self carefully, and never tell strangers what shape it wears.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** your life is bound to an animal double; harm to one harms the other
- **Minor → major:** sense through it → share one life
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The link goes silent, leaving only a nagging sense of absence. Spectacular failure: a wound, a fear or a sickness passes along the bond into the other body.

## Not to be confused with

*[Beast Bonding](beast-bonding.md)*, a partnership with a creature that remains a separate life; Shared Soul binds the two lives into one.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** the shaman's animal double, met in a trance and bound by an old rite.
- **Heroic legend:** the hero's twin-beast, born on the same night and fated to die on the same day.
