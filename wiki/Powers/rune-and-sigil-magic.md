# Rune & Sigil Magic

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Power written down. The carver cuts a sign into wood, stone, skin or metal, and the sign holds its meaning like a vessel holds water. A rune on a doorframe keeps out ill luck; a sigil on a floor waits for the wrong foot to step on it; a seal on a tomb holds shut what is inside.

It is patient, careful work. Each sign must be made precisely, with the right tool, at the right time, and a careless stroke can undo it or twist its meaning. At its greatest, a single seal can bind a demon for a thousand years. Scholars of the craft are often recognisable by their ink-stained fingers and their habit of reading every inscription they pass.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** inscribe signs that hold power, including glyph traps
- **Minor → major:** a luck rune → a demon-binding seal
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The sign is only a sign. Spectacular failure: it takes a different meaning than intended, and springs on its maker.

## Not to be confused with

*[Ward](ward.md)*, which is a protective barrier however it is made; Rune & Sigil Magic writes power into signs. *[Folk Magic](../Skills/folk-magic.md)*, the skill, is the humbler village craft.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** precise geometric seals drawn in silver ink, glowing faintly in darkness.
- **Divine:** holy symbols carved at sacred sites, blessed by a priest's hand.
- **Folk magic:** marks scratched on a barn door or cradle with a nail, as grandmother taught.
