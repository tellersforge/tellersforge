# Resist Element

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Fire licks the skin and does not bite. Frost gathers in the eyebrows and the blood stays warm. Acid runs off like rain from oilcloth. This power guards against one kind of harm, chosen when it is learnt or when it is called up, and it does that one thing well. At its smallest it takes the sting out of the blow. At its greatest the fire is simply irrelevant.

Smiths, sailors, miners and firewalkers have always wanted it. Travellers who expect a desert or a frozen pass take it along as others take a coat. It is humble, reliable and often the difference between a bad day and a funeral.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **END** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** resist one kind of harm
- **Minor → major:** take less → immune
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the protection doesn't take, and the element hurts as it normally would. Spectacular failure: the body becomes more vulnerable to it for a while.

## Not to be confused with

*[Environmental Immunity](environmental-immunity.md)*, which lets the body ignore a whole hostile place such as deep water or thin air; Resist Element guards against one harm.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a sigil traced on the skin with ash, cold water or salt, according to the danger.
- **Divine:** a prayer of protection, the saint's hand held over the head.
- **Mutation:** hide that was always tough against fire, cold or poison.
- **Technology:** a coating, a treated suit or a skin implant rated for one hazard.
