# Mental Blast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A blow that lands behind the eyes. There is no light and no sound, only the target reeling, clutching their head, perhaps bleeding from the nose. The lightest touch is a sudden headache that ruins an aim or a spell. The strongest drops a victim senseless and leaves them lying for days, or longer.

It is a crude weapon for a subtle power. Armour does nothing against it, and neither do walls if the attacker can see through a window. Those who use it tend to be feared by their own side as much as the enemy's, since nobody quite knows how much control they have.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/COU/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** strike the mind directly
- **Minor → major:** a headache → a coma
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A dull ache and nothing more. Spectacular failure: the blow echoes back, and the attacker drops to their knees.

## Not to be confused with

*[Madness](madness.md)*, which cracks sanity; and *[Neural Hack](neural-hack.md)*, which breaks into a machine-linked brain. Mental Blast is simple force against the mind.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a clenched jaw, a pulse of will, and the target's head snaps back.
- **Occult:** a dark syllable that splits the listener's skull with pain.
- **Technology:** a pistol-shaped emitter that fires a pulse tuned to the brain.
