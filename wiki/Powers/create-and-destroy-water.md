# Create & Destroy Water

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Water from empty air, or water gone to nothing. A cupped hand fills and overflows; a dry trough brims; a cellar floods to the steps. Reversed, a puddle vanishes, wet clothes dry in a moment, and a pond shrinks to cracked mud. The water made is clean, cool and plain, and tastes of nothing at all.

In deserts and sieges it is worth more than any sword, and in many dry lands those who have it are treated as holy. Great floods and great droughts are the far end of the power. Farmers learn to fear someone who can dry a well, and anyone who makes water in a closed room learns to keep an eye on the door.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **WIS** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** produce or remove water
- **Minor → major:** fill a cup → flood a room, or dry a pond
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A few drops, or a damp patch where there should be none. Spectacular failure: far too much water, or all the water, including the drink in everyone's cups.

## Not to be confused with

*[Water Control](water-control.md)*, which moves and shapes water already there; Create & Destroy Water makes it or unmakes it.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a blessing on an empty vessel, which fills slowly with sweet water.
- **Spirit:** the rain spirit's gift; a small cloud gathers overhead and weeps into the jar.
- **Arcane:** a spoken formula and a turning hand, drawing moisture from the air itself.
