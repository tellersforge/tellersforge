# Weapon Empowerment

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The blade burns, the hammer rings, the fist strikes like a battering ram. For a short time a weapon, or the arm behind it, is more than it should be. Edges bite deeper, arrows fly truer, and blows carry weight that no human arm can give.

Small workings sharpen an edge for a single fight. Great ones deliver the one strike that fells a giant or breaks a castle gate. Knights of holy orders pray for it before battle. Martial artists gather it in the breath before the blow. It fades once the fight is over, and those who rely on it learn to finish quickly.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **COU**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** empower your strikes or weapon briefly
- **Minor → major:** a sharper blade → a strike that fells giants
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The weapon strikes as it would have. Spectacular failure: the power bursts at the wrong moment and breaks the weapon.

## Not to be confused with

*[Temporary Enchantment](temporary-enchantment.md)*, which lays a lasting charm on an item for a while; Weapon Empowerment is a quick surge in the strike itself.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** runes flaring along the blade as the caster runs a hand down it.
- **Divine:** a holy light on the weapon, called down with a battle prayer.
- **Inner energy:** inner energy gathered and driven through the fist or the weapon in a shout.
