# Swarm Control

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A thousand small bodies moving as one. Flies gather into a humming cloud, rats pour out of the drains in a tide, ants form a black carpet across the floor. The controller does not command each creature; they command the swarm, and it flows where it is sent like smoke with teeth.

It is a horrifying gift to witness. A few flies in a sleeper's ear are a nuisance; a plague of locusts can strip a province bare. Swarm masters are rarely welcome anywhere, and many wear the marks of their trade: a buzzing that follows them indoors, a rat or two always in a pocket. Cities fear them, farmers hate them, and generals pay them well.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** control insects or vermin as one
- **Minor → major:** a few flies → a plague
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The swarm scatters or ignores the call. Spectacular failure: it turns on the controller, or on the nearest crowd.

## Not to be confused with

*[Beast Command](beast-command.md)*, which commands individual animals; Swarm Control commands insects and vermin as a single mass.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a humming song that the spirits of small things answer.
- **Occult:** a pact with a lord of flies, sealed in rotting meat.
- **Mutation:** a body that is partly swarm already, shedding insects when it moves.
- **Technology:** a cloud of insect-sized drones steered from a wrist console.
