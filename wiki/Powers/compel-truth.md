# Compel Truth

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The lie will not come. The target opens their mouth to deny it, and the truth falls out instead, with all the colour draining from their face. At its gentlest the power only makes lying uncomfortable, a pricking of conscience that most honest people barely notice. At its strongest it lays a charm on a room, a hall or a court, so that no falsehood can be spoken inside it at all.

Judges and priests of justice value it greatly, and so do suspicious kings. It forces no one to speak, however: a clever witness can stay silent, or answer only what is asked. That is why good interrogators learn to ask very carefully.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** force someone to speak truly
- **Minor → major:** uneasy honesty → a zone where no lie can be told
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The lie comes out smoothly. Spectacular failure: the caster finds they cannot lie either, for rather longer than they would like.

## Not to be confused with

*[Truth-Sensing](truth-sensing.md)*, which only tells whether a lie was told; and *[Oath-Binding](oath-binding.md)*, which enforces promises. Compel Truth stops the lie being spoken at all.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a holy relic placed on the table, before which no falsehood can be uttered.
- **Arcane:** a circle chalked on the floor, glowing faint blue when anyone in it lies.
