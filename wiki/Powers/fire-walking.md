# Fire Walking

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

To walk through fire and come out unburnt. At its simplest it is the old festival feat: bare feet across a bed of glowing coals, while the crowd holds its breath. At its greatest the fire-walker strides into a burning house, through smoke and falling timbers, and returns carrying a child, their clothes scorched and their skin untouched.

The power protects the walker, not what they carry, and the smoke is still thick in the lungs. Many who have it are priests or ascetics who treat it as a test of faith, and they are rarely proud about it. Others are simply lucky, and learn early why children cry when their fingers go near a candle.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **END** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** walk through or endure fire
- **Minor → major:** hot coals → a burning house
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The heat bites and the walker must pull back or be burned. Spectacular failure: the protection fails halfway across.

## Not to be confused with

*[Resist Element](resist-element.md)*, which protects against one element's harm in general; Fire Walking is endurance of flame and heat, crossing through it.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a pilgrim's faith; the coals feel like cool stones underfoot.
- **Inner energy:** calm breath and an unbroken focus that keeps the heat outside the skin.
- **Mutation:** a heat-proof hide, dark and faintly scaled, that barely warms in a fire.
