# Sonic Scream

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A mouth opened wide, and the world flinches. The sonic scream turns the voice into a weapon: a shriek that rings in the ears, rattles teeth and shatters every glass on the table. At full strength it is a wall of sound that knocks people flat, cracks stone and brings down plaster from the ceiling.

It is loud in every sense, and there is no hiding who did it. The screamer's throat pays the price afterwards, and allies learn to cover their ears at the first deep breath. In a small room it is devastating; in the open, sound spreads thin and the effect fades quickly with distance.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** use your voice as a weapon
- **Minor → major:** an ear-ringing shriek → a shattering shockwave
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A hoarse croak and a sore throat. Spectacular failure: the voice cracks and the scream echoes back, deafening the screamer.

## Not to be confused with

*[Sound Control](sound-control.md)*, which bends and shapes sounds from any source; the sonic scream only hurls noise out of the user's own throat.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** a throat with strange extra folds and a jaw that opens too wide.
- **Spirit:** the keening wail of a grieving spirit borrowed for one terrible moment.
- **Technology:** a collar amplifier that turns a shout into a shockwave.
