# Camouflage

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The bearer goes still and fades into the wall behind them, their skin and clothes shifting to the grey of the stone or the dapple of the leaves. It isn't true invisibility. A careful watcher who knows where to look may still see the outline. But in a hurry, at a distance or in poor light, the bearer is simply part of the scenery.

Hunters, scouts, spies and ambushers treasure it. It works best while the bearer keeps still, and grows thin once they move quickly. At its fullest, the bearer can stand in an open square and let a whole crowd walk past without a glance.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/AGI · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** blend into your surroundings
- **Minor → major:** harder to spot → vanish in plain sight
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The blending is patchy and the outline clear. Spectacular failure: the bearer takes the wrong colour entirely and stands out like a painted sign.

## Not to be confused with

*[Invisibility](invisibility.md)*, which hides the bearer from sight entirely; Camouflage only blends them into what is around them.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** skin that shifts colour and texture like a cuttlefish or a lizard.
- **Technology:** a cloak or suit of adaptive fabric that copies whatever is behind it.
- **Spirit:** a forest spirit's blessing that makes the eye slide past the bearer.
