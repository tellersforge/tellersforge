# Fog & Mist

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Grey swallows everything. A thin haze drifts up out of nothing, thickens, and in moments the next person is a shadow and the one after that is gone. Small uses hide a face or a doorway. At its greatest, a whole valley, a harbour or a battlefield vanishes beneath a sea of fog, and armies stumble about by the sound of each other's voices.

The fog is cold and damp, muffles sounds, and does not care who is inside it: the caster sees no better than anyone else unless some other gift helps. Smugglers, raiders and retreating armies bless it. Sailors and sentries curse it. In old stories, fogs that come from nowhere are rarely good news.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** make obscuring mist
- **Minor → major:** haze → miles of fog
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A wisp of mist that lifts on the breeze. Spectacular failure: the fog is thick exactly where the caster needs to see.

## Not to be confused with

*[Veil of Concealment](veil-of-concealment.md)*, which hides particular people or places from sight; Fog & Mist simply blinds everyone in the area.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** breath blown over a cupped hand, and the mist spills out and rolls away.
- **Spirit:** the marsh spirits rise at a call and bring their grey cloaks.
- **Occult:** an unnatural, clammy fog that smells of graves and whispers at the edge of hearing.
