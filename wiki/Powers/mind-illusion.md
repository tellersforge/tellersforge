# Mind Illusion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

An illusion that no one else can see, because it exists only inside one head. The victim sees a spider on their hand, hears a dead friend's voice, feels a knife slide between their ribs. Nobody else notices a thing. At its mildest, it is an unsettling image; at its most terrible, the pain it causes is so real that the body gives up and dies.

It is intimate and cruel, and it leaves no trace. Interrogators, torturers and haunting spirits use it. The only defences are a strong will, a friend who says 'there's nothing there', and the stubborn refusal to believe one's own eyes.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/CHA/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** an illusion inside one mind
- **Minor → major:** a haunting image → phantom pain that kills
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The victim senses a false note and shrugs it off. Spectacular failure: the illusion leaks back into the caster's own mind.

## Not to be confused with

*[Major Illusion](major-illusion.md)*, which everyone present can see and hear; mind illusion lives only in one victim's head.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a quiet push into the victim's mind; the caster stares, unblinking.
- **Occult:** a whispered curse lets a haunting presence ride the victim's senses.
- **Arcane:** a hex pressed into the victim's shadow with a painted finger.
