# Guardian Projection

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Out of the user's body steps a figure made of light, smoke or will: a warrior, a beast, a hulking guardian that moves when its maker wills it. At first it is only a glowing mist that hovers and startles. With mastery it becomes a fighting spirit-beast, a great armoured shape that strikes, shields and guards while its creator stands behind it.

The projection is part of the user. What hurts it, they feel, and if it is torn apart, they are shaken or wounded. It can't go far from its source. Most who have it give it a name, and some say it has a character of its own.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/CHA/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** project a protector spirit-form that acts for you
- **Minor → major:** a glowing mist → a fighting spirit-beast
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** a wisp of light that fades before it can act. Spectacular failure: the projection lashes out on its own, or collapses and takes its maker down with it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Spirit Allies](spirit-allies.md)*, which are separate beings that choose to help; a Guardian Projection is part of its maker.
