# Future Seeing (Precognition)

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Visions of what might be. The seer glimpses possible futures: a burning city, a wedding, a battlefield. At first there is only a vague omen, a sense of dread or hope. At its greatest, the seer maps the paths of fate and knows which choices lead where.

It is the most coveted and the most troubling of gifts. The future is never fixed, and every vision is only one road. Seers are forever trying to change what they have seen, and often find that the attempt is what makes it happen.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/WIS/INT/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** visions of possible futures
- **Minor → major:** a vague omen → mapping the paths of fate
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No vision, or one too vague to use. Spectacular failure: the seer trusts a false future and leads everyone towards it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Reflex Precognition](reflex-precognition.md)*, which shows only the next moment in a fight; future seeing shows the roads ahead.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a god's vision granted in trance or ecstasy.
- **Psionic:** the mind reaching forward, catching glimpses of what is coming.
- **Occult:** a forbidden bargain with a power that sees all time.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Reflex Precognition](reflex-precognition.md), [Augury](augury.md)
