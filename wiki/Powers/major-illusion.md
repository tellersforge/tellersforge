# Major Illusion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A phantom that walks, speaks and answers back. The major illusion fills every sense: its soldiers clank, its fires crackle and smell of smoke, its dogs bark when approached. A small working makes one figure convincing enough to hold a conversation; a great one raises an illusory army on a hill or a whole town where there is only empty field.

The illusion can't truly harm anyone, but people who believe in it act as if it can: they flee, surrender or fight shadows until dawn. Its danger is a clear head and a thrown stone that passes straight through.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** full-sense illusions that move and react
- **Minor → major:** a phantom → an illusory army or town
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The illusion is flat and wrong, fooling no one. Spectacular failure: it acts on its own and gives the whole game away.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mind Illusion](mind-illusion.md)*, which is felt by one mind alone; a major illusion is there for everyone to see and hear.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a long spell drawn in the air, painting sound and colour into shape.
- **Psionic:** the minds of all onlookers bent together into one shared false scene.
- **Occult:** shades and phantoms from beyond called up to play their parts.
