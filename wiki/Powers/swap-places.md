# Swap Places

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Two things trade places in a heartbeat. The one who has this gift picks a target, a comrade, an enemy, a sack of grain, and in the blink of an eye each stands where the other stood. The comrade is suddenly out of the trap; the enemy is suddenly in it. At its smallest it works with something close by and in plain sight. At its greatest it reaches across a whole city to a person or thing the swapper knows well.

It is a trickster's power and a rescuer's, and it plays havoc with lines of battle. It needs something to swap with: in an empty room it does nothing. Those swapped unwillingly are often badly disoriented, and sometimes very angry.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** trade places with a person or object
- **Minor → major:** within sight → across a city
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing moves. Spectacular failure: the swap happens with the wrong person or object, often one that does not belong where the swapper was standing.

## Not to be confused with

*[Short Teleport](short-teleport.md)*, which moves only you to an empty place; Swap Places needs a partner and moves both.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** two points tapped in the air, one near and one far, and a crack as the space folds.
- **Psionic:** the mind holds both bodies at once and simply exchanges them.
- **Technology:** a pair of linked beacons, or a tag stuck on the target, that trade their positions on command.
