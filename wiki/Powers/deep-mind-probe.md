# Deep Mind Probe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Where telepathy skims the surface, this power digs. The prober reaches down past the target's present thoughts into their memory, hunting for a face, a password, a buried night years ago. The light form catches a flash, a fragment rising to the surface. The full search is a ransacking of the whole mind, room by room, and it leaves damage: lost memories, nightmares and gaps that never heal.

It is slow and obvious. The target nearly always knows something is happening, and fights it if they can. Secret police and inquisitions keep people who have the gift, and many a prisoner has talked just to avoid it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/COU/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** dig out hidden memories
- **Minor → major:** a flash of memory → a full search that leaves damage
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing but the target's present thoughts. Spectacular failure: the prober gets lost in the memories and comes back with some of them as their own.

## Not to be confused with

*[Telepathy](telepathy.md)*, which reads only surface thoughts; and *[Memory Editing](memory-editing.md)*, which changes memories rather than reading them.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a long, hard stare, both parties sweating, fingers gripping the chair.
- **Arcane:** a silver needle laid on the brow while a spell unpicks the mind.
- **Technology:** an interrogation chair and a crown of wires, readouts scrolling beside it.
