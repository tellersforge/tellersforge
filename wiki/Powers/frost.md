# Frost

*Set: Core · Basic power*

*aka: ______________________________*

Cold that creeps. A breath clouds the air, a window blooms with white ferns, a puddle skins over with ice. The one who has this gift can chill a room, freeze a water jug solid, or glaze a road so smooth that a charging horse goes sliding. At its greatest, a whole lake goes still in a heartbeat, white and hard enough to walk on.

It is slow and quiet compared to fire, and more useful than it looks. Food keeps, fevers cool, fleeing enemies slip and fall on stone steps, and a river crossing appears where there was none. Those who use it a great deal tend to feel the cold less, and are said to be cold company.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** chill, freeze water, make ice slick
- **Minor → major:** frost a pane → flash-freeze a lake
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **Version I, as in Basic:** A burst of freezing cold at one target in sight, up to 10 metres away. 1d6 damage. If the target takes any damage, ice crusts its limbs and it loses its next action. The target may Dodge. A shield-holder may Parry instead (half damage).
- **What failure looks like:** A light frost, gone in moments. Spectacular failure: the ice forms underfoot, or creeps up the caster's own arms.

## Not to be confused with

*[Ice Shaping](ice-shaping.md)*, which builds walls and bridges out of ice; Frost chills and freezes, but does not sculpt.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a breath of winter spoken over the water; blue sparkles drift from the lips.
- **Spirit:** the winter spirit's touch, a whisper of frost that follows the hand.
- **Psionic:** the mind drains the heat from things, leaving them pale and brittle.
- **Technology:** a refrigerant sprayer or cryo-coil that hisses a cold white mist.
