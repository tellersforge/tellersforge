# Earthquake

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The world shakes. A low rumble, the rattling of cups on a shelf, dust falling from the beams, and the ground shivers underfoot. At the far end of the power, buildings crack and fall, walls crumble, and a whole city is laid flat in the time it takes to say a prayer.

Nothing is safe near it, including the one who calls it. Stone does not choose whose house it falls on. In many lands it is thought of as the anger of the earth itself, and those who can call it are prayed to, bribed or sent away. The rest of the time it is a siege-breaker, and the last resort of desperate people.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END/STR/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** shake the ground
- **Minor → major:** a tremor → level a city
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The ground trembles faintly and settles. Spectacular failure: the quake centres on the caster's own position.

## Not to be confused with

*[Earth Shaping](earth-shaping.md)*, which moves soil and stone with care; Earthquake only shakes, and shakes everything.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** the wrath of the earth's god, called down by a priest pressing both hands to the ground.
- **Spirit:** the great spirits of the deep stone woken and angry.
- **Arcane:** a long chant and a staff driven into the ground, cracks spreading out from where it strikes.
