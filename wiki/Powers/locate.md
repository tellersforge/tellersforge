# Locate

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A pull in the chest, a pointing finger, a needle that turns and settles. Locate finds a particular thing or person, whether a lost ring, a missing child or a stolen relic. At first it gives only a rough direction, and only if the thing is close. At its greatest, it names the exact place anywhere in the world.

It works best with a link: something that belonged to the missing person, a blood-stained cloth, a fragment of the stolen thing. Kidnappers and thieves who know of it take care to break every link and hide behind running water.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** find a specific thing or person
- **Minor → major:** a direction nearby → the exact place anywhere
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No sense of direction at all. Spectacular failure: it points firmly towards the wrong place.

## Not to be confused with

*[Scrying](scrying.md)*, which shows a distant scene; Locate only tells where a thing is.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a pendulum or needle held over a map until it settles.
- **Divine:** a prayer to a patron of lost things, answered by a sense of direction.
- **Psionic:** the mind reaching out for a remembered presence.
