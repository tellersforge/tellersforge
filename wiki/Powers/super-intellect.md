# Super Intellect

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A mind that works like a counting engine with a poet's reach. The possessor multiplies long numbers before the question is finished, reads a ledger once and remembers every line, and sees in a handful of facts the shape of what will follow. Conversation with them is unnerving: they answer the next three questions before the first is done.

At its height it becomes something close to prophecy by arithmetic. Markets, wars and harvests become patterns to be read, and whole peoples look, to such a mind, as predictable as tides. The price is often loneliness. Few can keep up, fewer want to, and the brilliant are rarely thanked for being right too early.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** compute and reason like a machine
- **Minor → major:** fast arithmetic → predicting whole societies
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The reasoning stalls, or reaches an answer that is neat and wrong. Spectacular failure: a small error at the start grows into a grand, confident conclusion that leads everyone astray.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mathematics](../Skills/mathematics.md)*, the skill, which is learned and worked through by hand; Super Intellect is a mind that simply outruns the pencil.

## Costumes

- **Trained:** years of memory palaces, drills and discipline, producing a mind honed like a blade.
- **Technology:** a cortical coprocessor humming at the base of the skull, feeding calculations straight to thought.
- **Mutation:** an oversized brow and a stare that never quite blinks; the gift was there from the cradle.
