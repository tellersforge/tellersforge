# Toughness

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A body that won't give. Punches are shrugged off, pain is felt as a dull ache, and cold and fatigue that would drop others do little more than annoy. At its extreme, the bearer walks through gunfire and comes out bruised.

Some have it from birth: thick-hided folk who could never feel much. Others have it from years of hard living, or from machines under the skin, or from a monk's discipline that shuts pain out. It doesn't make them invulnerable, but it does make them very hard to stop, and they often don't know how badly they're hurt until it's far too late.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** resist damage, fatigue and pain
- **Minor → major:** shrug off a punch → bulletproof
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The blow lands as it would on anyone. Spectacular failure: the bearer ignores a real wound until it drops them.

## Not to be confused with

*[Armour Skin](armour-skin.md)*, which hardens the skin into a shell; Toughness is the whole body enduring, and looks like nothing at all.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** dense bones and thick skin, or an inborn indifference to pain.
- **Technology:** sub-dermal plating and pain dampeners, or a medical cocktail.
- **Inner energy:** iron-body training that hardens flesh by breath and discipline.
