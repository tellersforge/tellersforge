# Weather-Working

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The sky is asked, and answers. Clouds gather on a clear morning and rain falls on thirsty fields; a storm that threatened a harvest breaks and passes; snow comes early, or late, or not at all. At its greatest, the weather of a whole region changes for days: a great storm raised over a sea to scatter a fleet, or a drought ended across a kingdom.

It is slow and broad. Nothing happens in a heartbeat, and nothing stays exactly where it is put. Farmers and sailors love and fear it in equal measure, since good weather for one is ruin for another. Old stories are full of rainmakers who were paid well, then blamed for the floods.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/WIS/END/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** call or stop rain, storm, snow or drought
- **Minor → major:** clear a sky → a region-wide storm
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The sky stays as it was. Spectacular failure: the weather comes, but worse, or somewhere else, or the opposite of what was asked.

## Not to be confused with

*[Wind Control](wind-control.md)*, which commands the air here and now; and *[Call Lightning](call-lightning.md)*, which draws bolts down. Weather-Working changes the weather itself.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a rain dance, a drum and an offering to the sky spirits.
- **Divine:** a procession and a prayer, answered in the days that follow.
- **Arcane:** a long ritual with a map of the heavens and chalked signs for wind and rain.
- **Technology:** cloud-seeding rockets, weather satellites or a climate engine humming on a hill.
