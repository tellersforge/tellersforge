# Love Magic

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The oldest and most troublesome of charms. It kindles longing, or binds an existing affection so tightly it can't slip. A glance lingers, a heart quickens, and a name is suddenly on someone's lips at all hours. The mild form is a nudge toward attraction; the strong form becomes obsession that eats sleep and sense.

Village wise women sell it in small packets and warn against using it; court sorcerers sell it in silver phials and don't. Stories about it rarely end well. Love made by magic is shallow unless the true feeling grows under it, and when the spell lifts, the one who was bound tends to remember everything.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** arouse or bind love or desire
- **Minor → major:** attraction → obsession
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No spark, or a fleeting fancy. Spectacular failure: the wrong person is smitten, often with violence in them.

## Not to be confused with

*[Charm](charm.md)*, which makes someone friendly and trusting. Love Magic kindles desire and devotion, far stronger and far more dangerous.

## Costumes

- **Folk magic:** a knot of hair and thread tucked under a pillow, or an apple cut at midnight.
- **Arcane:** a rose-coloured philtre mixed by the light of a full moon.
