# Matter Synthesis

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Build it from the bottom up. Raw stuff goes in: scrap iron, sand, water, ash. What comes out is exactly what the maker wants, a copy of a tool, a gear, a drug, a gem. Nothing is created from nothing. It is rearranged, grain by grain, into something new.

Alchemists have dreamed of it for as long as there has been alchemy. Far-future workshops do it daily. At its smallest it copies a simple tool. At its greatest it makes anything at all, as long as enough raw matter is fed in. Where it is common, the value of things drops, and the value of raw materials rises.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/DEX/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** build items atom by atom from raw material
- **Minor → major:** copy a tool → anything, given the mass
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The raw matter sits there, or comes out as sludge. Spectacular failure: the process runs wild and eats what it should not.

## Not to be confused with

*[Conjure Object](conjure-object.md)*, which makes things from nothing; Matter Synthesis needs raw material and rebuilds it.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a fabricator fed with raw stock, which hums and glows and delivers the finished piece.
- **Alchemical:** a crucible, a furnace and a long night's work, the raw matter bubbling into its new form.
