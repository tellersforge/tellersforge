# Radiation

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

An invisible poison that pours out of the one who has it. At first it is a sickly aura: nausea, headaches, hair falling out in clumps among those who stay too close. At its worst it is a zone of slow death, where plants wither, animals die, and people burn from the inside without ever seeing a flame.

It is a dreadful power, and one hard to live with. Its bearers are often shunned, forced to live apart, or kept by rulers who want a weapon and not a neighbour. Some can glow faintly in the dark. The damage it does can last long after the bearer has gone, and some places stay dangerous for years.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **END**/INT/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** emit harmful or mutating energy
- **Minor → major:** a sickness aura → a lethal zone
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A faint warmth and nothing more. Spectacular failure: the power leaks out unbidden, harming allies or the bearer's own body.

## Not to be confused with

*[Energy Bolt](energy-bolt.md)*, which throws a single blast of energy; Radiation poisons a whole area slowly.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** a glowing body, skin faintly luminous, that harms everything around it.
- **Technology:** a reactor core, isotope emitter or radiation projector, clicking warning counters in the background.
