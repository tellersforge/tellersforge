# Levitation

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

To rise from the ground and hang there. The body goes up as if drawn by a thread, a hand's breadth at first, then over heads, then to the rafters, and stays there as long as the will holds. It can come down just as slowly. It is not flight: there is little going sideways except by pushing off walls or being pulled along a rope, but up and down are entirely the levitator's to choose.

Hermits are said to float while they pray, and witnesses swear to it. Thieves use it to reach high windows, and a levitator can cross a chasm by rising, being hauled over, and settling on the far side. Wind is the enemy, and a strong gust can carry a floating body where it does not want to go.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** float up and down
- **Minor → major:** a few inches → hang over a chasm
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The body lifts a little and sinks back. Spectacular failure: the levitator rises and cannot stop, or drops suddenly from the full height.

## Not to be confused with

*[Flight](flight.md)*, which moves freely and fast through the air; and *[Telekinesis](telekinesis.md)*, which lifts other things. Levitation raises only yourself, straight up and down.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** rising in prayer, eyes closed, with a faint glow at the hem of the robe.
- **Psionic:** a steady inward pull of concentration; the levitator's hair drifts upward first.
- **Arcane:** a slow circling gesture and a murmured count; dust lifts from the floor with the caster.
- **Inner energy:** seated breathing so deep and light that the body leaves the mat.
