# Self-Alteration

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The body is clay. The bearer can change their own face, eyes, hair and skin, lengthen their bones or soften their features, even change their build and shape. The simplest change is eye colour or hair; the greatest is a new face and body altogether.

It is the perfect gift for spies and the hunted. It is also unsettling: those who change too often can lose track of their true face. Friends learn secret words to tell them from their doubles.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** change your own features
- **Minor → major:** eye colour → a new face and body
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The change is partial or awkward. Spectacular failure: the body gets stuck in a half-changed, ugly state.

## Not to be confused with

*[Disguise](../Skills/disguise.md)*, the skill, which uses makeup and clothes; and *[Mimicry](../Skills/mimicry.md)*, the skill, which copies manners. Self-Alteration truly changes the body itself.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** skin and bone that shift and settle like soft clay.
- **Arcane:** a mirror spell and a long, painful hour.
- **Technology:** nanite reshaping, or a flesh-changing surgery done at home.
