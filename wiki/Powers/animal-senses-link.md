# Animal Senses Link

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Borrowing the eyes of a hawk and the ears of a fox. The user touches the mind of a nearby creature and, for a while, sees what it sees and hears what it hears. The world tilts strangely: colours shift, smells become maps, and the ground rushes past at the height of a dog's nose. The user's own body sits still, eyes half-closed, as if dreaming.

Scouts and hunters prize it; spies love it, since nobody minds a crow on the windowsill. At its greatest, a whole flock becomes a web of eyes spread across the sky. The animal is not commanded, only ridden, and it goes about its own business, which is not always the user's.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** see and hear through an animal
- **Minor → major:** one bird → a whole flock
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The link won't form, or brings only a blur of animal feeling. Spectacular failure: the user takes on the animal's panic, hunger or fear for hours afterwards.

## Not to be confused with

*[Familiar](familiar.md)*, a bonded companion creature that lives beside the user; Animal Senses Link borrows any animal's senses for a while.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** the shaman's spirit flies out on the wind to sit behind the creature's eyes.
- **Arcane:** a feather or tuft of fur bound to a charm, then held to the brow.
- **Psionic:** a trained mind slipping gently into a simpler one.
