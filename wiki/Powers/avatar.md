# Avatar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

For a few moments, the mortal becomes something more. The body blazes, grows, crowns itself in light or storm or fire, and a voice that is not quite theirs fills the air. Enemies falter and friends kneel. Whatever the source, a god, a perfected inner power or a machine at full output, the result is the same: a brief, overwhelming form that can turn the course of a battle.

It never lasts. When it ends the vessel collapses, drained and shaking, sometimes for days. Those who call it too often grow thin, strange and distant, and some stories say they eventually fail to come back at all.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **COU**/END/CHA/WIS/STR · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** a brief transformation into a godlike form
- **Minor → major:** a surge → a battle-changing super form
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** a glow and a rush of strength that fades before it can be used. Spectacular failure: the form arrives and cannot be held, leaving its bearer wrung out and helpless.

## Not to be confused with

*[Power Surge](power-surge.md)*, which boosts one power for a moment; Avatar transforms the whole person.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a god descends into the faithful, and a halo or storm-crown burns over them.
- **Inner energy:** inner energy released all at once, the hair lifting and the body glowing.
- **Technology:** a battle suit's overdrive mode, every system running past its limits.
