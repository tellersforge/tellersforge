# Kinetic Charging

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A playing card, a pebble, a coin, a spoon. In the right hand any small thing becomes a bomb. The charger fills the object with restless energy until it glows, then throws it, and it bursts on landing. The small version pops like a firecracker; the great one takes a door off its hinges and the wall with it.

It rewards a good throwing arm and a steady nerve, since the charged object is dangerous for as long as it is held. Practitioners tend to carry pockets full of junk and have a showman's habit of flicking it about. Nobody enjoys a card game with them.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** charge a thrown object with explosive energy
- **Minor → major:** a glowing card → a blasting charge
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The object stays dull and lands harmlessly. Spectacular failure: it goes off in the hand or at the thrower's feet.

## Not to be confused with

*[Energy Bolt](energy-bolt.md)*, which throws raw force directly; kinetic charging needs something to hold and throw, and the blast comes where it lands.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** fingertips that glow and spark with a charge the body can't keep in.
- **Inner energy:** inner strength poured into the object with one focused breath before the throw.
