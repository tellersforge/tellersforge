# Enhanced Senses

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The world turned up. With this gift, a whisper across the street is clear, a trail days old still smells fresh, and a colour change on a far hillside stands out like a flag. At its keenest the bearer hears heartbeats across a room and tastes lies in the sweat of a nervous man.

It is a quiet, useful power loved by hunters, trackers, guards and investigators. It has a cost: loud noises hurt, strong smells sicken and bright light stings. Bearers learn to live with cotton in their ears and a hood over their eyes in crowded places.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **PER** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** sharpen your natural senses
- **Minor → major:** keen ears → hear heartbeats across a room
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing more than ordinary senses would catch. Spectacular failure: a sudden blast of noise or light overwhelms the bearer.

## Not to be confused with

*Perception (attribute)*, the skill, which is ordinary awareness; enhanced senses go far beyond what any normal body can notice.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** wide pupils, pointed ears, or a nose that twitches like a hound's.
- **Technology:** implanted ears and eyes, or a visor with amplified sound and sight.
- **Inner energy:** a calm mind and a trained body, attention spread to every sense.
- **Spirit:** an animal spirit lends its keen nose or ears for a while.
