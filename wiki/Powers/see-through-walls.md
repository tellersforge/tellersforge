# See Through Walls

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Stone and wood become smoke-grey, then glass. The bearer of this gift looks at a wall and sees the room beyond: first only outlines, then clear shapes, then every thread and bone. Locked chests give up their contents; hidden men behind curtains are no longer hidden.

It is the watchman's dream and the burglar's greatest weapon. It usually has limits: lead, thick earth, or enchanted walls may block it, and very close things blur. Many who have it keep it secret, because people behave very differently around someone who can see through their door.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** perceive behind cover
- **Minor → major:** outlines → full x-ray sight
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The wall stays a wall. Spectacular failure: sight blurs for a while and the bearer sees nothing clearly.

## Not to be confused with

*[Far Seeing (Clairvoyance)](far-seeing-clairvoyance.md)*, which sees a distant place; see through walls only looks past what stands in the way.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** goggles or scanner that paint the hidden room in pale outlines.
- **Psionic:** the mind reaching past the wall and feeling the shapes beyond.
- **Mutation:** eyes that cloud grey when looking through things.

## Relations

- **Close to:** Clairvoyance
