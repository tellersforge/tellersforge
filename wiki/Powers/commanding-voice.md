# Commanding Voice

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A voice that must be obeyed. Spoken plainly, with no gesture or trick, a single word stops a man in his tracks: Halt. Drop it. Kneel. The listener does it before they have decided anything at all. With mastery the voice carries over a crowd, and the whole square does as it is told.

Commands must be short, clear and possible. An order to walk off a cliff, or to betray a child, tends to break the spell rather than the listener. Drill sergeants, holy orators and certain old nobles are all said to have the gift, and nobody who has heard it forgets the feeling of their own legs moving without asking.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/COU/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** speak words that must be obeyed
- **Minor → major:** one word obeyed → a crowd obeys
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The listener hesitates, then does as they please. Spectacular failure: the voice cracks, and everyone within earshot knows who tried to bully them.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mind Control](mind-control.md)*, which steers a body for as long as it lasts. Commanding Voice wins only the brief obedience of a single spoken order.

## Costumes

- **Trained:** years of parade-ground drill; the voice drops, sharpens and cuts through any noise.
- **Divine:** a word spoken in the name of a higher power that ears cannot refuse.
- **Spirit:** a voice that carries the echo of the ancestors speaking with it.
