# Etherealness

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A shift sideways, into a thin, grey layer of the world that lies over this one like a veil. Walls become shadows, people become ghosts, and the traveller drifts among them, unseen and unfelt, able to pass through stone and steel as through fog.

A brief slip lets a fugitive walk through a locked door. A master can dwell there, and some come to prefer it: quiet, cold and free of every trouble of the flesh. It has its own dangers. Things live in the grey that notice travellers, and those who linger too long find it hard to remember how to be solid.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** shift into a parallel layer of the world
- **Minor → major:** briefly intangible → dwell there
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The caster stays solid. Spectacular failure: they shift halfway, and are stuck there until someone helps.

## Not to be confused with

*[Phasing](phasing.md)*, which lets solid matter pass through solid matter; Etherealness steps out of the world into a layer alongside it.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a word and a pass of the hand, and the caster fades like smoke.
- **Occult:** a pact with the things of the grey, that lets the caster walk among them.
- **Mutation:** an inborn ability to slip half out of the world, often first discovered in a panic.
