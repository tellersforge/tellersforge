# Wish Granting

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Ask, and it shall be given, but read the fine print. The granter hears a wish and makes it come true: gold in the chest, a lost love returned, an enemy fallen. The wisher's heart leaps. Then, often, the catch arrives. The gold is cursed, the love is not quite the same, the enemy's brother comes looking for revenge.

Wishes are bound by rules as old as the gift. Most granters can grant only so many, must obey the letter of the words and cannot refuse once a wish is spoken. At the greatest scale, a single wish reshapes the world. Wise folk who meet a wish-granter think very carefully, and some simply walk away.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/INT/WIS/COU/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** grant wishes, with rules and catches
- **Minor → major:** a small wish → reshape the world
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The wish goes unanswered. Spectacular failure: it is granted exactly as spoken, with the most disastrous reading possible.

## Not to be confused with

*[Reality Warping](reality-warping.md)*, which changes the world at the user's own will; Wish Granting changes it at someone else's, under strict rules.

## Costumes

- **Heroic legend:** a spirit of lamp or well, bound by old laws to grant what is asked.
- **Occult:** a devil's bargain, sealed with a signature and a price paid later.
