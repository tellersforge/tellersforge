# Neural Hack

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The cruellest trick of an age that puts machines inside people. The hacker finds the implant behind an eye, the chip in a spine, the booster wired into a soldier's reflexes, and turns it against its owner. The victim's sight fills with snow, their legs refuse to move, or their hands obey someone else.

At its mildest it is a flicker, a stumble, a word that comes out wrong. At its worst it cooks the brain around the implant. Those who carry a lot of machinery fear this power more than any gun, and the rich pay fortunes for firewalls in their heads. Those with no implants at all simply shrug, and in some circles going unaltered has become a quiet form of armour.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/COU/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** hurt, blind or control people through their implants
- **Minor → major:** a glitch → a fried brain
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The implant resists, or glitches harmlessly. Spectacular failure: the feedback runs backwards into the hacker's own implants.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mental Blast](mental-blast.md)*, which strikes any mind directly; Neural Hack works only through implants, but needs no mental power at all.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a targeting app that locks onto a victim's implant signature, then a burst of malicious code.
