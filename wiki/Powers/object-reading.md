# Object Reading

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The bearer touches a knife and feels the rage of the hand that last held it. A ring gives up a flicker of a wedding day; a letter shows the tear that fell on it. Object reading senses an object's past through touch. At first it gives only a flash of emotion; at its fullest, a full replay of scenes in which the object was present.

It is a gift of investigators, historians and grieving families. It is not always welcome: old objects carry old pain, and bearers learn to wear gloves in pawnshops and museums.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/PER/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** read an object's past by touch
- **Minor → major:** a flicker of emotion → a full replay
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing but the feel of the object. Spectacular failure: a flood of old emotion overwhelms the bearer.

## Not to be confused with

*[Postcognition](postcognition.md)*, which sees the past at a place; object reading reads it from a single held object.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** fingertips on the object, eyes closed, a stream of feeling.
- **Occult:** the spirits bound to an object compelled to speak of what they saw.
- **Technology:** a trace analyser that reconstructs past handling from residue.
