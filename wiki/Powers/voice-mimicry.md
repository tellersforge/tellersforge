# Voice Mimicry

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A voice from nowhere, or a voice that belongs to someone else. The mimic can throw sound into a corner, a chimney or a passing cart, and can copy anyone they have heard: a guard captain's bark, a mother's call, a king's slow drawl. At first it is only a trick for parties. With mastery it becomes a perfect impersonation that fools even close friends.

Thieves, spies and pranksters love it. So do certain birds and monsters, which lure travellers with the cries of lost children. It works only on the ear: a mimic who is seen talking gives the game away at once.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **CHA** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** throw your voice or mimic anyone
- **Minor → major:** a voice from a corner → a perfect impersonation
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The voice sounds almost right, and almost is enough to raise suspicion. Spectacular failure: the mimic's own voice breaks through mid-sentence.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mimicry](../Skills/mimicry.md)*, the skill, which copies voices and manners by practice alone. Voice Mimicry is uncanny, can throw sound, and needs no rehearsal.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a word breathed into a hollow reed, the sound appearing elsewhere.
- **Mutation:** an odd throat and flexible vocal cords, like a songbird's.
- **Spirit:** a mocking spirit that repeats any voice it is asked to.
- **Technology:** a throat implant or speaker that samples and replays voices.
