# Water Control

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Water rises, turns and flows at command. A ripple crosses a still pond against the wind; a stream bends out of its bed to fill a ditch; a wave lifts a boat off a sandbank. At its greatest, a river parts to let an army cross dry-shod, or a harbour draws back and returns as a wall of water that sweeps away the quays.

Water is heavy and stubborn, and moving much of it is exhausting. The controller does not make water, only commands what is there, so the power is small in a desert and immense at sea. Fishing villages, river towns and navies all have stories of those who could command the waves, and not all of the stories end well.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** shape and move water, part rivers
- **Minor → major:** a ripple → part a sea, raise a tidal wave
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The water ripples and settles. Spectacular failure: it surges in the wrong direction, often over the caster.

## Not to be confused with

*[Weather-Working](weather-working.md)*, which brings rain and storm; Water Control moves water that is already there.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** sweeping gestures and a chant, the water rising in shapes that follow the hands.
- **Divine:** a prayer answered; the sea draws back as if bowing.
- **Spirit:** the river spirit's favour, felt as a cool presence and seen in the swirling current.
- **Mutation:** an inborn bond with water, with webbed hands and eyes that shine sea-green.
