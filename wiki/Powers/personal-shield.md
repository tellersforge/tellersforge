# Personal Shield

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A shimmer around the body, faint as heat haze, that hardens when something comes too close. An arrow skids off a cheek; a sword stops a finger's breadth short. The shield is shaped to its maker and moves with them, so it can't be lent. At first it catches only the odd blow. At its strongest little gets through at all.

Holding it takes concentration, and the harder it is struck the more it costs. Veterans learn that a shield is not a wall: it buys time, and the wise spend that time getting out of the way. Some shields glow when hit, which is useful for knowing when they are close to breaking.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** protective energy around yourself
- **Minor → major:** deflect a blow → near-invulnerable
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the shimmer appears and is gone before the blow lands. Spectacular failure: the shield collapses with a crack that leaves the user stunned.

## Not to be confused with

*[Force Field](force-field.md)*, which protects others or an area; Personal Shield is worn by one person only.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a word of warding and a sphere of pale runes.
