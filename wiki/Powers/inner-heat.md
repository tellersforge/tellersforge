# Inner Heat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Warmth from within. The practitioner sits in the snow, breathes a certain way, and the cold stops mattering. Wet clothes steam dry. Frostbitten fingers turn pink again. At full strength the snow melts in a circle around them, and the ice beneath softens.

It is an old discipline of mountain hermits and monastic orders who live in high, cold places. It takes years of practice and great stillness, and it will not keep a body warm in a howling storm without effort. For those who master it, winter holds no terror, and bare feet on snow become a kind of prayer.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **END** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** generate heat against cold
- **Minor → major:** stay warm → melt snow around you
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The cold still bites, and the breathing only makes the practitioner dizzy. Spectacular failure: the body overheats and faints, in the snow.

## Not to be confused with

*[Fire Walking](fire-walking.md)*, which protects against flame and burning coals. Inner Heat protects against cold by warming from within.
