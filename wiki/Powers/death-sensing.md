# Death-Sensing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A chill in a warm room, a smell of cold earth, a bird on the sill. Death-sensing is the old gift of knowing when death is near: for a sick neighbour, a stranger in the street, or oneself. At first it is a vague shiver. At its strongest, the bearer knows the day and the hour.

It is a burden more than a gift, and those who have it are often feared and avoided. Village wise women, gravediggers and the seventh child of a seventh child are said to carry it. Whether it can be used to cheat death is a question every bearer eventually asks.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** know when death is near
- **Minor → major:** a chill → the day and hour
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No sign comes. Spectacular failure: a false sign sends the bearer into grief or panic for someone who is fine.

## Not to be confused with

*[Future Seeing (Precognition)](future-seeing-precognition.md)*, which shows many possible futures; death-sensing only warns of death.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** the dead gather quietly around the dying, and the bearer sees them waiting.
- **Folk magic:** old signs read in dreams, candle flames and the behaviour of birds and dogs.
