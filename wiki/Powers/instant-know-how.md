# Instant Know-How

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Knowledge poured in all at once, like water into a jar. The user reaches for a skill they never learned and finds it waiting: the grip of an unfamiliar sword, the grammar of a foreign tongue, the right way to set a broken leg. For a while the hands know what the head never studied. Then it drains away, leaving only a faint memory of having been, briefly, someone else.

It is a favourite of spies, troubleshooters and anyone sent into a situation nobody planned for. The borrowed knowledge is real but shallow at the edges. It has no habits, no scars and no instinct for when the textbook answer is the wrong one, and old masters can often tell, from a single movement, that the stranger in front of them is wearing their craft like a borrowed coat.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** temporarily gain a skill or knowledge, including weapon mastery
- **Minor → major:** a small edge → master of any skill for a scene
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing comes, or it comes muddled and half-remembered. Spectacular failure: the wrong knowledge arrives with total confidence, and the user acts on it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Attribute Boost](attribute-boost.md)*, which sharpens a body or mind that is already there; Instant Know-How gives a skill the user never had.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a skill-chip slotted behind the ear, or a download streamed into a neural implant; the eyes flicker as the data settles.
- **Arcane:** a pinch of ash from a burned book, swallowed with a word, and the author's learning rises for an hour.
- **Divine:** a quick prayer to a patron of craft, answered by a sure and steady hand that isn't quite one's own.
