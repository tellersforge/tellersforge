# Growing

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The body swells in every direction at once, and keeps going. Shoulders scrape ceilings, then roofs, and the world below turns into toys. At its smallest the power makes a person as big as an ogre. At its greatest they wade through rivers and peer over hills, and armies scatter from their feet.

Size solves many problems and creates others. A giant is easy to see, hard to hide and impossible to feed. Floors give way, bridges crack and friends get trodden on. Clothes rarely keep up. Stories of giants who were once ordinary folk may explain why so many of them seem lonely.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **END**/STR/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** become gigantic
- **Minor → major:** ogre-sized → mountain-high
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the body grows a little and then settles back. Spectacular failure: it grows lopsided, too fast, or will not stop when told.

## Not to be confused with

*[Shrinking](shrinking.md)*, the same power in reverse, which trades strength and reach for stealth.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a cake, a draught or a word from an old grimoire, and the walls begin to creak.
- **Technology:** a mass-gathering field that swells the wearer, with a low throb felt through the ground.
- **Mutation:** giant blood that wakes in a crisis, bursting seams and ceilings.
