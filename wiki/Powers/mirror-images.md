# Mirror Images

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Where there was one figure there are suddenly three, or seven, each moving in step and each as solid-looking as the real one. Mirror images are decoys: blurred copies of the user that shift and weave, so that an enemy's blow lands on nothing more often than not. A weak casting gives no more than a shimmer at the edges; a strong one fills the room with twins.

The images can't touch or speak, and they vanish when struck. That is enough. In a fight, every swing at a ghost is a swing wasted, and the real target may already be behind the swinger.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** decoy copies of yourself confuse attackers
- **Minor → major:** one blur → many images
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A faint doubled outline that fools no one. Spectacular failure: the images move out of step and show clearly which one is real.

## Not to be confused with

*[Duplication](duplication.md)*, which makes real copies that can act; mirror images are only false pictures that vanish when touched.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a spin and a word, and the air behind the caster fills with reflections.
- **Psionic:** attackers' eyes made to see the target in several places at once.
- **Technology:** a belt projector casting flickering holograms around the wearer.
