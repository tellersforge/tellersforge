# Harm Touch

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A hand on the shoulder, and the victim goes pale. Harm Touch wounds, sickens or rots with the lightest contact. At its weakest it brings on nausea, a fever or a sudden ache. At its worst the flesh blackens and spoils beneath the fingers.

It is a secret power, hard to see and easy to deny, and those who have it are rarely trusted once found out. Some have it as a curse, others as a punishment from a god, others as a gift of dark bargains. Even friends think twice before shaking hands.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **COU**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** wound or sicken by touch
- **Minor → major:** nausea → rot
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the touch does nothing. Spectacular failure: the sickness spreads to the toucher as well.

## Not to be confused with

*[Death Touch](death-touch.md)*, which kills outright; Harm Touch wounds and sickens.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a blackened palm and a hissed curse, the fingers cold as ice.
- **Divine:** the wrath of a god of plague or judgement, laid on by the hand of the faithful.

- **Close to:** [Death Touch](death-touch.md)
