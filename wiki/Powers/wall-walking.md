# Wall Walking

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Hands and feet that stick. The wall-walker goes up a brick chimney as easily as up a staircase, crosses a ceiling upside down with cloak hanging, and waits motionless in the corner of a hall where nobody thinks to look. At its smallest it is a grip on rough stone that no one else could hold. At its greatest, the walker runs across polished walls and ceilings as though down were only a suggestion.

Thieves covet it, assassins earn a reputation with it, and guards learn to look up. It does not help with balance on a rope or a ledge, and it gives no strength to haul anything else along. Wet, greasy or crumbling surfaces are a problem, as is anything that someone has thought to cover in oil.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** cling to and walk on walls and ceilings
- **Minor → major:** grip rough stone → run upside down
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The grip slips and the climber must hold on the ordinary way or drop. Spectacular failure: the hold gives way on the ceiling, directly above the people who must not know.

## Not to be confused with

*[Water Walking](water-walking.md)*, which lets you stand on surfaces too weak to bear you; Wall Walking lets you stand on surfaces at any angle.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** tiny hooked hairs on palms and soles, like a lizard's, that leave faint prints on glass.
- **Arcane:** a spider traced in ash on each palm before the climb.
- **Inner energy:** a discipline of balance and breath that lets the body rest its weight wherever it touches.
- **Technology:** gloves and boot soles that grip by suction or clinging fibres, with a soft kiss at each step.
