# Mark

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A sign placed on someone that only the marker, or the marker's allies, can see or feel. It burns like a brand in the mind's eye, faint as a glow-worm at dusk or bright as a torch at noon. The marked can be followed across a city or a continent, and often never know they carry anything.

Hunters use it to track prey. Assassins' guilds use it to name their next target. Priests use it to set a judgement on someone, so that every servant of their god knows the enemy on sight. The greatest marks are permanent and cannot be scrubbed off, and those who bear them live out their lives looking over their shoulders.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** mark a target to track or doom
- **Minor → major:** a faint sigil → a doom-mark forever
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The sign fades before it settles. Spectacular failure: the target feels the mark and knows who placed it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Locate](locate.md)*, which finds a thing or person now; Mark sets a lasting sign on them, for tracking, for others or for doom.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a sigil drawn in the air that sinks into the victim's back, cold as a coin.
- **Divine:** a god's brand, visible only to the faithful, glowing on the brow of the accursed.
- **Technology:** a tracker tag, fired or slipped into a pocket, that pings a network.
