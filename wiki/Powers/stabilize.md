# Stabilize

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The dying stop dying. Bleeding slows and stops, a failing heart finds its rhythm again, and the fading patient stays on the right side of the threshold. It does not heal, not truly: the wound is still there, the fever still burning. What it buys is time, the most precious thing a healer can offer.

Battlefield surgeons would trade their knives for it. At its deepest, the power suspends death entirely, holding a body in a strange grey stillness until proper help can come. Those who have been held there speak of a long hallway with no doors.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **WIS** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** keep the dying alive
- **Minor → major:** staunch bleeding → suspend death
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The patient keeps slipping. Spectacular failure: the power jolts the body and hastens the end instead.

## Not to be confused with

*[Healing Touch](healing-touch.md)*, which mends wounds; and *[First Aid](../Skills/first-aid.md)*, the skill, which stops bleeding with hands and bandages. Stabilize only holds off death.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a prayer spoken over the patient, the healer's hand pressed to the wound.
- **Technology:** a small field unit that clamps, seals and holds the body in stasis.
