# Postcognition

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The bearer stands in an empty hall and sees it full: the feast, the quarrel, the knife, the blood on the floor. Postcognition shows past events at a place. At first it is only an echo, a faint sound or shadow of what happened. At its greatest, it shows whole eras, the rise and fall of a city seen as if in a single afternoon.

It is the gift of detectives, historians and ghost-hunters. It shows what happened but not why, and old, violent places tend to shout louder than ordinary days.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** see past events at a place
- **Minor → major:** an echo → a whole era
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing, or only a vague feeling. Spectacular failure: the bearer is caught in the vision and cannot come back out of it for a time.

## Not to be confused with

*[Object Reading](object-reading.md)*, which reads the past from one held object; postcognition reads the whole place.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** the bearer stands very still and the scene rises around them like fog.
- **Occult:** the ghosts of the place are called to re-enact their deeds.
- **Arcane:** a circle of chalk and dust, and the past drawn up into it.
