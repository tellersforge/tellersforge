# Hollowing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The darkest of the mind arts. The hollower pours despair into another, drop by drop, until the will has nothing left to swim in. What comes out the far side is obedient, flat-eyed, and hollow. At first the target only grows listless and easy to persuade. At the last they become a servant who will do anything, because nothing matters any more.

It draws on the void itself, the cold empty space between the stars, and those who practise it are said to lose something of themselves with each use. Victims can sometimes be brought back, but slowly, and they never speak of what the hollowness was like.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **COU**/CHA/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** pour despair and emptiness into a mind until the will drowns into obedience (dark)
- **Minor → major:** dull a will → a hollow servant
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The despair lifts after a moment. Spectacular failure: the emptiness flows back up the channel into the hollower's own heart.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mind Control](mind-control.md)*, which seizes the will; and *[Madness](madness.md)*, which breaks sanity. Hollowing empties a mind of everything but obedience.

## Costumes

- **Occ (Void):** a cold breath blown into the victim's mouth, drawn from the silence between stars.
