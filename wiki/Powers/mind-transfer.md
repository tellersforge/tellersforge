# Mind Transfer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The self moved out of one body and into another, or into a machine. A dying scholar is carried into a young host; two enemies wake up in each other's skin; an old ruler copies her mind into a crystal archive to rule forever. The lightest form is a backup, a stored copy of a mind. The fullest is moving freely from body to body without end.

Each move costs something. Memories frayed, habits lost, a new body's instincts that won't quite listen. And the question always hangs over it: is the new self the same person, or only a copy who believes they are?

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/END/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** move or copy a mind into another body or a machine; body swapping
- **Minor → major:** a backup copy → moving between bodies forever
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The transfer won't take, and the mind stays where it was. Spectacular failure: part of the mind is lost in transit, or the minds end up in the wrong bodies.

## Not to be confused with

*[Possession](possession.md)*, which borrows a body for a time; and *[Hosting a Spirit](hosting-a-spirit.md)*, where a guest shares one's body. Mind Transfer moves or copies the whole self.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a pair of reclining couches wired together, and a long whine of machines.
- **Occult:** a ritual of swapped blood, performed at midnight in a circle of salt.
- **Curse:** a witch's spite; two people wake one morning in the wrong skins.
