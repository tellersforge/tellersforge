# Space Warp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Space made to bend. A corridor grows longer the further one walks. A room becomes larger inside than out. A path loops back on itself, and a traveller who sets out north keeps arriving at the same crossroads from the south.

It is a defensive power as much as anything. Fortresses have been guarded by it, whole armies lost in woods that shouldn't be that big. At its greatest it makes an endless maze, and the unwary can wander in it for the rest of their lives. The skilled can also shrink distance, bringing a far door within reach.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** fold, stretch or loop space
- **Minor → major:** a longer corridor → an endless maze
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The space stays where it was. Spectacular failure: the fold snaps, and everyone near is thrown somewhere unpleasant.

## Not to be confused with

*[Pocket Realm](pocket-realm.md)*, which makes a hidden world; Space Warp bends the ordinary space of this one.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a design traced on the floor that makes the corridor stretch out ahead.
- **Occult:** angles that should not exist, drawn in a crooked hand, that make rooms swallow themselves.
- **Technology:** a spatial distortion field that folds a building's interior.
