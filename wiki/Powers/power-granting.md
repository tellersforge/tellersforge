# Power Granting

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Waking the gift in someone who never had it. The granter lays hands on a farmer's child, and the child's eyes blaze; speaks a word over a dying soldier, and the soldier rises with strength beyond any mortal. The recipient feels a door open inside them that they never knew was there.

It is the rarest and most dangerous of powers. Given for a day, a gift is a blessing. Given forever, it changes a person's fate, and not always for the better. Gods, heroes of legend and certain strange laboratories are said to have it. Those who receive powers from another often find that the giver still has some claim on them.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/WIS/END/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** awaken or give powers to others
- **Minor → major:** temporary → permanent
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The spark fades without catching. Spectacular failure: the gift takes hold wrongly, twisting the recipient or burning through them.

## Not to be confused with

*[Blessing](blessing.md)*, which grants luck or protection for a time; Power Granting gives a true power.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a god's touch passed through a chosen priest; the recipient's eyes shine with holy light.
- **Heroic legend:** a hero's gift given to a worthy follower, sworn over a sacred flame.
- **Technology:** a serum or implant that rewrites the body to carry new abilities.
