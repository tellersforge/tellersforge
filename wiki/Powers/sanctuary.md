# Sanctuary

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The protected person stands in the open and no one strikes. It is not a wall or a light, only a feeling that grows in anyone who means them harm: that this would be wrong, that there are other targets, that the hand will not quite move. At its weakest it buys a heartbeat of hesitation. At its strongest nobody at all can bring themselves to hurt the one under its grace.

Priests and pilgrims use it to cross battlefields; temples use it on those who seek refuge. It usually breaks when the protected one strikes out, which is part of its meaning. Those who abuse it tend to lose it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** enemies hesitate to harm the protected
- **Minor → major:** hesitation → none can harm
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the enemy pauses briefly, frowns and attacks anyway. Spectacular failure: the sanctuary is broken so harshly that the enemy's anger is doubled.

## Not to be confused with

*[Protective Aura](protective-aura.md)*, which shields everyone nearby from harm; Sanctuary stays the hand that would do harm.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a blessing spoken over the head, a sign drawn in the air, and the faint sense of a presence.
