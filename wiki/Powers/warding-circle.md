# Warding Circle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A ring on the ground, drawn in chalk, salt, blood or the edge of a staff, and nothing may cross it. The summoner stands inside while what was summoned paces just beyond, or the priest stands guard while the ghosts press at the line. Beginners keep out small spirits and restless dead. Masters hold back demons and worse.

The circle is strong but fragile. A smudge, a gap or a stray foot breaking the line is enough. Stories are full of apprentices who swept the floor at the wrong moment. Those who rely on circles learn to draw them fast, check them twice and never, ever step out.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/COU/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** a boundary spirits or foes cannot cross
- **Minor → major:** keep out small spirits → hold back demons
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the circle is drawn but gives no protection. Spectacular failure: it holds something in with the caster rather than out.

## Not to be confused with

*[Ward](ward.md)*, which protects a whole place and lasts; Warding Circle is a single quick boundary.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a precise ring of symbols in chalk and silver dust.
- **Divine:** a circle of salt and holy water blessed in the name of a god.
- **Folk magic:** iron filings, ash and a charm muttered while walking round.
