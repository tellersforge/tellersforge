# Luck-Granting

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A kindness to chance. The friend's throw comes up well, the coin falls the right way, the loose stone holds just long enough. Luck-Granting doesn't guarantee anything, but it tilts the table, and people who carry it tend to notice after a while that things go their way.

At its smallest it is a lucky break when one is needed. At its greatest it makes someone fortune's darling for a season, winning at cards, escaping every fall and walking away from wrecks. Those who grant it are loved, courted and sometimes kidnapped. The old stories add a warning: luck borrowed from the world is luck the world will ask back.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/LUCK · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** improve someone's fortune
- **Minor → major:** a lucky break → fortune's favourite
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The luck stays where it was. Spectacular failure: the good fortune goes astray and lands on the target's worst enemy.

## Not to be confused with

*[Luck-Stealing](luck-stealing.md)*, which takes fortune from one person for another; Luck-Granting gives it freely and robs nobody.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a saint's medal pressed into the palm with a quick prayer.
- **Folk magic:** a four-leaved plant sewn into a collar, or a spit and a wink before the throw.
- **Technology:** a probability engine that nudges tiny events in the user's favour.
