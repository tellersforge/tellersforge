# Exorcism

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The possessed thrash, the room grows cold, and objects fly. The exorcist stands firm with book, bell, candle or drum, and orders the spirit out of the body or the house. Beginners calm a restless ghost and send it on its way. Masters cast out whole legions from a single screaming host.

It is a grim, exhausting rite. The spirit fights, lies, begs and threatens, and it knows things the exorcist would prefer it did not say. Victims can be hurt in the struggle. Afterwards everyone needs a long sleep and a good meal.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/CHA/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** drive spirits out of people or places
- **Minor → major:** calm a ghost → cast out a legion
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the spirit stays. Spectacular failure: it leaps into someone else, maybe the exorcist.

## Not to be confused with

*[Banish](banish.md)*, which exiles a being to its own world; and *[Exorcism & Curse-Breaking](../Skills/exorcism-and-curse-breaking.md)*, the skill, which is the knowledge of rites without the power itself.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** holy water, a sacred text read aloud, and a raised symbol.
- **Spirit:** a shaman's drumming and a song that coaxes the spirit out.
