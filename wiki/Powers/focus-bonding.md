# Focus Bonding

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A staff, a wand, a crystal, a ring: an object that becomes part of the user's gift. Through long use and a patient ritual, the two are joined, and the focus begins to steady and strengthen every power that passes through it. In the hand it feels warm and alive, like an old friend's handshake.

Most mages and priests carry one, and most would rather lose a finger. A bonded focus can make a modest caster formidable, and losing it can leave them fumbling like an apprentice. At its greatest, a focus multiplies its owner's powers, and the pair become legendary together.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** bond with a focus (staff, wand, crystal) that strengthens your powers
- **Minor → major:** a slight boost → power multiplied
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The focus stays cold, lending nothing. Spectacular failure: it rejects the user, and the gift misfires through it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bound Item Recall](bound-item-recall.md)*, which only brings an item back to the hand; Focus Bonding makes the item strengthen the user's powers.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a carved staff soaked in years of spellwork, its runes glowing when the power flows.
- **Divine:** a holy symbol worn smooth by prayer, warm against the chest.
- **Psionic:** a crystal attuned to the mind, pulsing with every thought.
