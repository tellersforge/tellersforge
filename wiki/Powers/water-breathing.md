# Water Breathing

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Lungs that forget they need the sky. The one who has it slips beneath the surface and simply keeps breathing, drawing water in and out as easily as air, and moves through the green dark with no rush to come back up. A pearl diver with this gift stays down all afternoon. A drowned prisoner comes up smiling.

At its greatest it is not a visit but a way of life: the cold and crushing weight of the deep sea stop mattering, and a whole world of trenches, wrecks and slow-moving things opens up. It does not make anyone a better swimmer, and it says nothing about what lives down there. Harbour folk tell stories about those who went down with this gift and liked it too much to return.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **END** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** breathe and move freely underwater
- **Minor → major:** minutes → life at abyssal depth
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The breath comes short and the swimmer must surface. Spectacular failure: the gift fails at depth, with a long way to go and no air in the lungs.

## Not to be confused with

*[Environmental Immunity](environmental-immunity.md)*, which shrugs off heat, cold, poison air and vacuum alike; Water Breathing is only about life under water.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** gill slits along the neck or ribs that open and flutter the moment the head goes under.
- **Arcane:** a mouthful of charmed seawater swallowed before the dive, tasting of salt and copper.
- **Technology:** a rebreather mask or implant that pulls air from the water and hums against the jaw.
- **Spirit:** a bargain with the water spirits, sealed with an offering thrown into the waves.
