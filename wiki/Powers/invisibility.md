# Invisibility

*Set: Core · Basic power*

*aka: ______________________________*

The bearer is there, and then they aren't. Invisibility bends the light, or the watcher's mind, until nothing shows where a body should be. At first it is only a blur, a ripple in the air like heat over a road. At its fullest, the bearer can't be seen, heard, smelled or felt by any ordinary sense.

It is the oldest dream of thieves and spies, and it is not as simple as it sounds. Footprints still show in snow and mud, rain outlines a head, and dogs are often not fooled. The invisible soon learn to move slowly, breathe quietly and never sneeze.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** become unseen
- **Minor → major:** a blur → invisible to all senses
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **Version I, as in Basic:** You, or one person you touch, fade from sight for about ten minutes. Sounds, smells and footprints remain: anyone searching must roll Perception at &minus;4 to notice a sign. It ends at once if the invisible person attacks, casts a power or takes damage.
- **What failure looks like:** The bearer fades only to a visible shimmer. Spectacular failure: they think they are hidden, and they are not.

## Not to be confused with

*[Camouflage](../Skills/camouflage.md)*, which only blends the bearer into the background; Invisibility takes them out of sight altogether.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a ring, a cloak or a whispered formula that folds the light around the body.
- **Psionic:** every nearby mind persuaded that no one is there.
- **Technology:** a light-bending suit that shimmers faintly at its seams.
- **Mutation:** a body that turns glass-clear when frightened or holding its breath.
- **Inner energy:** stillness so perfect that the eye passes over the master without catching.
