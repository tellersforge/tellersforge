# Telepathic Speech

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Speaking without sound, mind to mind. The telepath forms words, and the listener hears them in their head as clearly as if they had been said aloud. It crosses languages if the listener can grasp the meaning, and it passes through walls, noise and gags. The simplest form reaches someone close by; the strongest crosses the gulf between worlds.

It is a practical gift. Soldiers coordinate silently, prisoners plan escapes in plain sight, and lovers talk across crowded rooms. Those who are unused to it find it deeply unnerving, and more than one listener has spun round, looking for a speaker who isn't there.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** speak mind to mind
- **Minor → major:** someone nearby → across worlds
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The words don't arrive, or arrive as mush. Spectacular failure: the thought is broadcast to every mind in range.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mind Link](mind-link.md)*, which joins minds for constant talk. Telepathic Speech sends one mind's words to another, a sentence at a time.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a slight frown as the words are formed and thrown.
- **Arcane:** a paired charm; the words travel between two amulets and into the wearers' heads.
- **Technology:** a neural link or implant that sends words as signals.
