# Monstrous Form

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The body swells, darkens and breaks out of its clothes. Muscles bunch, the jaw juts, the voice drops to a roar, and whatever was a person a moment ago is now a hulking brute that wants to smash something. The form is still recognisably the same individual, only wrong, as if the worst part of them had been given room to grow.

Most who have this power can't fully steer it. The monster is strong and difficult to hurt, but it is also stupid, loud and dangerous to friends. Afterwards come exhaustion, torn clothes, bruises that ache for days, and the question of what exactly happened while they weren't there.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **END**/STR/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** become a larger, wilder, stronger self
- **Minor → major:** swollen muscles → a rampaging giant
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** a snarl, a tremor and nothing more. Spectacular failure: the monster comes out and will not listen, turning on whoever is nearest.

## Not to be confused with

*[Battle Fury](battle-fury.md)*, a rage of the mind in an unchanged body; and *[Growing](growing.md)*, which only makes the body bigger without the beast.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** a chemical or inherited change that bursts out under stress.
- **Occult:** a bargain with something below, which lends its shape for a price.
- **Spirit:** an ancestor or beast spirit that rides the body and wears it like armour.
