# Possession

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The self leaves its own body and moves into another's. The possessor looks out through borrowed eyes, speaks with a borrowed mouth, and walks among the other's friends unrecognised, until someone notices the new way the body moves. At its lightest the possessor merely rides along, seeing and hearing. At its fullest, they take the reins entirely.

The possessor's own body lies empty meanwhile, vulnerable and still. The host is not gone, only pushed aside, and a strong host may fight back from within. Spirits, demons, cult masters and certain machines are said to do it, and exorcists earn their living in the aftermath.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/COU/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** take over another's body
- **Minor → major:** ride along → full control
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The host holds firm and the possessor snaps back. Spectacular failure: the possessor is pushed out and can't find their way home, or finds the host in their own body instead.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mind Control](mind-control.md)*, which steers from outside; and *[Mind Transfer](mind-transfer.md)*, which moves a mind for good. Possession borrows a body for a time.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a pact with something that loans its skill for a price in souls.
- **Spirit:** the shaman's spirit leaves on the drumbeat and enters the host's breath.
- **Psionic:** a mind reaching across and pushing the other self aside.
- **Technology:** a remote link into the host's implants, riding the nerves like wires.
