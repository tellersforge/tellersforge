# Kinetic Push & Pull

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A single blow of force with no hand behind it. A door slams open ahead of the caster, a charging man is thrown back as if he had run into a wall, a dropped weapon leaps into an open palm. It is crude, fast and loud. There is no holding or placing, only the shove or the yank, but in a fight a moment of force at the right time is worth more than a lifetime of careful lifting.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** shove or yank with force
- **Minor → major:** knock one down → blow away a crowd
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A gust and nothing more. Spectacular failure: the shove goes the wrong way, and the caster ends up on the floor.

## Not to be confused with

*[Telekinesis](telekinesis.md)*, which moves things with control but is slower and harder.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a sharp thrust of will; the air ripples.
- **Arcane:** a barked word of command and an open palm.
- **Inner energy:** a martial artist's strike that lands without contact.
- **Technology:** a wrist-mounted pulse emitter.

- **Close to:** [Telekinesis](telekinesis.md)
