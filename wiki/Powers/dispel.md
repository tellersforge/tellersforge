# Dispel

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Unpicking a working already in force. The shimmering ward fades, the enchanted blade goes dull, the cursed man stops shaking and sits up blinking. The dispeller studies the power like a knot, finds the loose end and pulls.

Small effects snuff out with a word. Great workings fight back, and taking them apart can be slow, dangerous and exhausting: ancient seals, curses of generations, the protections on a fortress. It is the craft of exorcists, curse-breakers and anyone who clears up after other people's magic. Most practitioners agree that it is harder to undo a thing than to do it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** cancel an active power
- **Minor → major:** snuff a small effect → unravel a great working
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The power holds as if nothing had been tried. Spectacular failure: the working lashes back at the dispeller, or unravels the wrong thing.

## Not to be confused with

*[Counterspell](counterspell.md)*, which stops a power in the moment of casting; Dispel cancels one already in force. *[Power Negation](power-negation.md)* shuts down all powers in an area.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a patient unweaving, fingers tracing the pattern backwards until it falls apart.
- **Divine:** a holy word spoken with authority; the unclean working flees like a shadow from dawn.
- **Technology:** a field disruptor tuned to the working's frequency, hissing as the power fails.
