# Technopathy

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Machines that listen when the mind speaks. A glance switches on a light, a thought opens a lift door, a quiet word in the head sends a car rolling down the street with no one at the wheel. Technopaths describe it as hearing the hum of every device near them, and learning to sing in tune with it.

At its greatest it reaches beyond single machines to the webs that connect them: traffic lights, water pumps, power grids, whole cities obeying one thinker. It is a lonely gift in a modern world, because the technopath can never stop hearing. Some go half mad from the noise of a busy city and flee to places where the nearest machine is a windmill.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** control machines and computers with the mind
- **Minor → major:** turn on a light → seize a city grid
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The machine ignores the call, or obeys only in part. Spectacular failure: it does the opposite, loudly and at the worst moment.

## Not to be confused with

*[Remote Hack](remote-hack.md)*, which breaks into devices through their weaknesses; Technopathy commands them directly by thought, and *[Machine Speech](machine-speech.md)* only talks to them.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a trained mind reaching into circuits; screens ripple and static crawls at the edge of hearing.
- **Mutation:** an inborn gift that shows in a child whose toys come to life around them.
- **Technology:** a brain woven with wireless implants that speak to every device in range.
