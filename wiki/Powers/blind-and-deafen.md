# Blind & Deafen

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The world goes dark, or silent, or both. Light fades to grey and then to nothing. Voices blur into a dull roar and then stop. Victims describe a cold film over the eyes, a pressure in the ears like deep water. It is one of the most frightening harms a caster can inflict, because it leaves the mind clear and takes away everything it needs.

A brief blurring is a battlefield trick: a sentry who can't see the intruders, an archer who can't find a target. A great working lasts for life, and many a tyrant has used it on rivals he did not quite dare to kill. Gods in old tales strike the impious blind, and priests still claim the right.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **COU**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** take away senses
- **Minor → major:** blurred → permanent
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The senses flicker and return. Spectacular failure: the dark or the silence falls on the caster instead.

## Not to be confused with

*[Blinding Flash](blinding-flash.md)*, which dazzles everyone near with a burst of light; Blind & Deafen reaches into one target's senses and shuts them off.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a pinch of ash thrown in the target's face and a word that darkens the eyes.
- **Divine:** a god's judgement, a finger raised against the blasphemer.
- **Technology:** an optic scrambler, a sonic burst, or a hack that shuts off a cybernetic eye.
