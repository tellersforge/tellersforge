# Spirit Speech

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Everything has a voice for those who know how to listen: the old oak at the crossroads, the well that has seen three hundred weddings, the sword that remembers the hands that held it. The spirit speaker hears them. At first it is only a mood, a sense that a place is sad or angry or waiting. Later come words, and eventually full conversations, though spirits are not people and rarely see the world as people do.

It is a quiet and patient gift. Spirits can be vain, sleepy, frightened or cruel. They know a great deal about their own small corner of the world and nothing about anything else.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** speak with the spirits of places and things
- **Minor → major:** sense a mood → full conversation
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** silence, or only an impression. Spectacular failure: the spirit takes offence.

## Not to be confused with

*[Second Sight](second-sight.md)*, which sees the unseen without speaking to it; and *[Commune](commune.md)*, which asks questions of a deity or great power.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a shaman kneels, lays a hand on bark or stone, and listens with eyes closed.
