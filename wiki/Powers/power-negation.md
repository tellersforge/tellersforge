# Power Negation

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Where this gift reaches, wonders stop. Flames go out, flying figures drop, illusions vanish like breath on a mirror. The negator stands in a zone of dull, ordinary reality, and every gifted person near them feels suddenly heavy and small, as if a sense they had never noticed had been switched off.

At its mildest, it stops one power user in their tracks. At its greatest, it creates a null zone where no miracle, spell or strange science can function. It is loved by prison-keepers and feared by every mage. The negator is often a lonely figure, unable to share in the wonders around them, and many hunters of the gifted have come from their ranks.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **COU**/WIS/END/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** an area where powers fail
- **Minor → major:** one user → a null zone
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The powers flicker but keep working. Spectacular failure: the negation falls on the user's allies and spares the enemy.

## Not to be confused with

*[Dispel](dispel.md)*, which cancels one active power; Power Negation stops all powers from working in an area.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a circle of iron and salt; inside, magic simply fails.
- **Mutation:** an inborn stillness, a person around whom wonders never quite happen.
- **Technology:** a dampening field generator, humming and draining power from the air.
- **Divine:** a god's refusal; within the sacred bounds, no other power may act.
