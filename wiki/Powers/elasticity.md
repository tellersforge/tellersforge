# Elasticity

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Flesh that forgets it has bones. An arm reaches across a room to catch a falling cup, a neck stretches round a corner to look, a body pours itself through a gap no wider than a letterbox. At its fullest the stretcher is less a person than a substance, able to become a rope, a net, a spring or a soft wall that swallows a punch and gives it back.

It looks ridiculous, and those who have it are often laughed at until they are needed. It is also unsettling. There is something wrong about a smile that keeps widening, and children who watch it happen rarely forget it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** stretch and reshape the body
- **Minor → major:** long arms → any shape
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the body stretches a little and snaps back. Spectacular failure: it stretches too far, tangles or tears, and takes a long time to gather itself together again.

## Not to be confused with

*[Self-Alteration](self-alteration.md)*, which changes looks and features; Elasticity changes shape and reach but still looks like its owner.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** rubbery flesh and loose joints from birth, or from some strange accident; the skin has a faint sheen.
