# Leap

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A single bound that should not be possible. The leaper crouches, and then is simply somewhere else: on top of the wall, across the river, landing on the far rooftop with a crack of tiles. Small feats look like a lucky jump. Great ones look like a stone flung from a sling, a figure arcing over a whole street and coming down in the square beyond.

It is not flight. Once the feet leave the ground, the path is set, and what goes up comes down where it was aimed. Good leapers learn to judge distance by eye and to land rolling. Bad ones learn about awnings, chimneys and hay carts. In a chase there are few better gifts, and in a siege few more alarming ones.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **STR**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** jump far beyond normal ability
- **Minor → major:** clear a wall → bound over buildings
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The jump falls short and the leaper lands badly, or not where intended. Spectacular failure: a mighty bound in exactly the wrong direction.

## Not to be confused with

*[Super Speed](super-speed.md)*, which covers ground by running faster; and *[Flight](flight.md)*, which can turn and stay aloft. Leap is one great arc and a landing.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** legs that bend the wrong way at the knee, or thighs corded like a hare's.
- **Technology:** spring-loaded boots or leg braces that hiss and clank on the push-off.
- **Inner energy:** breath gathered low, a shout, and the body lifts as if the ground had pushed it.
- **Arcane:** a rune chalked on each boot heel, glowing briefly as the leaper springs.
