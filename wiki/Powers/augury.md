# Augury

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Should we sail tomorrow? Will this marriage be happy? The augur asks, and the world answers: a bird turns left, the entrails are dark, the coin falls on its edge. Augury asks whether a plan will go well. At its simplest it gives yes or no; at its best, detailed counsel on what to do and what to avoid.

Kings and captains have always wanted it, and priests have always charged for it. It judges plans, not the whole future. It says nothing about how things will go if the plan is changed, and a lazy asker learns little.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** ask whether a plan will go well
- **Minor → major:** a yes/no omen → detailed counsel
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The omen is unclear or contradictory. Spectacular failure: the omen gives a firm answer that is wrong.

## Not to be confused with

*[Future Seeing (Precognition)](future-seeing-precognition.md)*, which shows visions of possible futures; augury only judges a plan.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** the flight of birds or the entrails of a sacrifice read by a priest.
- **Spirit:** the ancestors answer through smoke, bones or a dream.
- **Technology:** a predictive engine offering a probability and a list of risks.

- **Close to:** Precognition, [Divination (skill)](../Skills/divination.md)
