# Command by True Name

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Every being has a name beneath its name, and whoever knows it can command. Speak it aloud and the spirit must stop, the dragon must listen, and even a god must hear. The power lies not only in knowing the name but in speaking it with the right force: a beginner compels only small spirits, a master can bind a deity.

True names are the most jealously guarded secrets of all. Beings kill to protect theirs, and hunters of names spend lifetimes on research and trickery. Many people keep their own true name hidden, just in case.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/INT/INT/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** rule a being whose true name you know
- **Minor → major:** compel a minor spirit → bind a god
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the name is spoken, but the being resists. Spectacular failure: the name is wrong, and the being now knows someone is hunting it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bind](bind.md)*, which holds a being by force; and *[Word of Creation](word-of-creation.md)*, which speaks things into being. True Name commands through knowledge.

## Costumes

- **Heroic legend:** the hero who guessed the imp's name and so owned him.
- **Arcane:** a scholar who spent years in libraries, and now speaks a name in the old tongue.
