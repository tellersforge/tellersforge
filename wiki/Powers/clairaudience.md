# Clairaudience

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A whisper in the ear from far away. Clairaudience lets the bearer hear what happens elsewhere: a conversation in the next room, a council behind stone walls, secret words spoken in a distant city. At its most extraordinary, it hears the grass grow and the worms turning the soil.

It is a gift of the eavesdropper and the watchful. Hearing without seeing has its limits, and more than one listener has mistaken the speaker or the meaning. It is also tiring: the bearer's own surroundings fade away while they listen elsewhere.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** hear far away, or hear secret speech
- **Minor → major:** the next room → hear grass grow
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing but a hiss. Spectacular failure: the bearer hears a false conversation, or loud sound that leaves them deafened.

## Not to be confused with

*[Far Seeing (Clairvoyance)](far-seeing-clairvoyance.md)*, which sees a distant place; clairaudience only hears it.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a god's messenger carries the voices to the priest's ear.
- **Psionic:** the mind tuned like a harp string to a distant sound.

## Relations

- **Close to:** Clairvoyance
