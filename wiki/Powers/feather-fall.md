# Feather Fall

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The power to fall gently. A climber loses her grip and drifts down the cliff face like a seed on the wind; a man thrown from a tower lands on the cobbles as lightly as a cat. At its smallest it saves a twisted ankle on the stairs. At its greatest, someone steps out of a cloud and walks away from the landing.

It does nothing going up, and very little sideways. It only argues with the ground, and it only wins at the last moment, which is why those who trust it learn to keep their nerve until the very end. Sailors, steeplejacks and burglars would all pay handsomely for it. Soldiers who have it tend to volunteer for the jobs that start with a long drop.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **WIS** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** slow or stop a fall
- **Minor → major:** soften a stumble → drop from the clouds unhurt
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The fall is only a little slower than it should be. Spectacular failure: the power takes hold halfway down and lets go just above the ground, at the worst possible height.

## Not to be confused with

*[Levitation](levitation.md)*, which lifts and holds you in the air at will; Feather Fall only slows a descent that has already begun.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a single word spoken as the feet leave the ledge, and the robes billow as if underwater.
- **Psionic:** a flash of focus in the instant of falling; the air seems to thicken beneath the body.
- **Technology:** a harness that fires a burst of braking thrust or snaps open a canopy near the ground.
- **Inner energy:** the body goes loose and light, breath held low in the belly, and the landing is a soft step.
