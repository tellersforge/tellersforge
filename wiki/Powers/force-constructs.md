# Force Constructs

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Shapes made of nothing and solid as stone. A glowing platform appears under falling feet, a wall springs up across a corridor, a cage drops over a snarling beast. At its simplest the construct is a flat plane that bears weight. At its greatest the maker builds barriers, bridges, prisons and great clenched fists that punch through doors.

The shapes shimmer faintly, often in the maker's favourite colour, and they last only as long as the maker holds them in mind. Complex things are hard; moving parts are harder. A clever maker thinks like a carpenter and a siege engineer at once, and a tired one watches the bridge flicker under the party's feet.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** make solid shapes of force or hard light
- **Minor → major:** a platform → walls, cages, giant fists
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The shape forms thin and wavering, then fades. Spectacular failure: it vanishes at the worst moment, or appears somewhere it traps the maker instead.

## Not to be confused with

*[Force Field](force-field.md)*, which is a protective bubble around a person; Force Constructs are shapes built for any purpose.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** shapes of pure will, faintly glowing, that ring like glass when struck.
- **Technology:** hard-light projectors drawing planes and cages from a gauntlet or drone.
- **Arcane:** sigils traced in the air that swell into shining walls and steps.
