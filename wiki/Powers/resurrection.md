# Resurrection

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

To call someone back from death and set them breathing again. It is the greatest of the healing arts and the one most hedged about with rules, prices and fear. The fresh dead come back most easily, still warm and still half in the world. The long dead come back slowly, if at all, and the dust must be gathered and the name remembered.

No culture treats it lightly. Temples demand offerings the size of a harvest, spirits demand bargains, and laboratories demand the body frozen within the hour. Those who return rarely talk about where they were. Some are grateful. Some are angry. A few are not entirely the person who died, and their families spend a long time not mentioning it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/WIS/CHA/END/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** return the dead to life
- **Minor → major:** the just-dead → the long-dead
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The body stays still and the soul does not answer. Spectacular failure: something answers, and it is not the person who died.

## Not to be confused with

*[Renewal at Death](renewal-at-death.md)*, which is the character's own return in a new life; Resurrection is worked by another hand on someone already dead.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a priest's long prayer over the bier, the temple bells ringing until the chest rises.
- **Spirit:** a shaman crosses into the land of the dead, bargains with its guardians and leads the soul home by the hand.
- **Technology:** a medical bay restarts the heart and repairs the brain before the damage sets, beeping steadily through the night.
