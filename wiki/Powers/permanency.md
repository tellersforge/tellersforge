# Permanency

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Making another working last forever. A glow that should have faded by dawn burns on for centuries, a ward that should have fallen at the next storm stands for generations, a change of shape becomes the new shape for good. The caster takes something temporary and fixes it into the world like a nail into oak.

It is the gift of the great masters, and it rarely comes free. Some pay in years of their life, some in a piece of their own power, some in a price that the world itself names. Those who seek it for the wrong reasons often find that they, too, have been made permanent in a way they did not expect.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/END/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** make another working hold forever; the Game Master may set a price
- **Minor → major:** a charm that lasts → an enchantment that can never fade
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The working fades on schedule. Spectacular failure: the wrong thing is made permanent, perhaps the caster's own weariness, or a curse.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lasting Enchantment](lasting-enchantment.md)*, which creates a new magic item; Permanency makes an existing working last forever.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a final word spoken over an existing spell, sealing it with a drop of the caster's blood.
- **Divine:** a god's decree that what has been done shall never be undone.
