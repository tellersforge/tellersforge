# Haste & Slow

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A trick played on the pace of time for one body. Hastened, the caster moves as if everyone else were wading through honey: two steps for their one, a blow landed before the other blade has finished rising. Slowed, a foe struggles like a sleeper in a nightmare, every gesture late and heavy.

A small working gives an edge that onlookers barely notice. A great one leaves an enemy nearly frozen, eyes moving in a face that can't catch up. Hastened bodies tire fast and eat like wolves afterwards, and those who use it often age a little ahead of their friends.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/AGI/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** speed yourself up or slow others down
- **Minor → major:** a little faster → a near-frozen foe
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The pace of the moment stays the same. Spectacular failure: the caster is the one slowed, or a foe is hastened.

## Not to be confused with

*[Time Stop](time-stop.md)*, which freezes all of time around the caster; Haste & Slow alters the pace for single bodies only.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a flick of the wrist and a whispered count; the world's sounds drop or rise in pitch.
- **Psionic:** the mind races, and the body follows, or the target's thoughts are dragged to a crawl.
- **Technology:** a stimulant injector or a nerve-booster implant, or a projector that jams a foe's reflexes.
