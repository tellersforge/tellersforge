# Lightning

*Set: Core · Basic power*

*aka: ______________________________*

Thunder in the hand. A crack, a blue-white flash, and a bolt of electricity leaps from the fingertips to strike the target. Then, as the power grows, it jumps: from the first soldier to the one beside him, from a sword to the next sword, down a line of armoured men like a spark down a chain.

It is fast, bright and loud, and it leaves the smell of scorched air behind. Metal draws it, water spreads it, and the caster's hair stands on end before it strikes. Small uses make a sharp shock or a spark. Large ones leave smoking bodies and stunned silence. Those who wear heavy armour against a lightning-caster usually regret it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** discharge electricity that chains between targets
- **Minor → major:** a static shock → chain lightning
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **Version I, as in Basic:** A jagged arc of electricity leaps to one target in sight, up to 20 metres away. 2d6 damage in steps: full, half, quarter, none. The target takes full; everyone within 2 metres of it takes half, rounded down (friends too). It cannot be avoided, but a successful Dodge moves a person one step down (full to half, half to quarter). So does standing on or wearing something that does not conduct (dry wood, rubber, glass). Armour and shields do not help.
- **What failure looks like:** A crackle of static and nothing more. Spectacular failure: the bolt jumps to the nearest metal, which may be a friend's.

## Not to be confused with

*[Call Lightning](call-lightning.md)*, which draws bolts down from a storm; Lightning comes from the caster's own hands.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a thrust of the fingers and a bolt that forks from them, crackling blue.
- **Psionic:** sparks gathering around the head, then a bolt from the eyes or palms.
- **Mutation:** a body that stores charge like an eel, crackling at the touch.
- **Technology:** a gauntlet coil or shock projector, with a whine and a hot tang in the air.
- **Divine:** the sky's own fire given to the faithful, white and gold.
