# Duplication

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

One person steps forward and two step back. The copies are real: they can open doors, carry loads, fight and bleed. At first the double lasts only a few breaths and thinks slowly. Masters send out whole companies of themselves, each doing a separate job, and gather them back at dusk with all their memories.

It is exhausting and strange. Copies sometimes argue with each other. Some disagree about who the original was. Hurting one hurts the others a little, and losing one leaves a gap in the mind that takes time to heal. In battle, though, an army made of a single warrior is a terrifying thing to face.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **END**/INT/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** split into real copies of yourself
- **Minor → major:** one short-lived double → an army
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** a flickering double that fades before it can act. Spectacular failure: the copy won't obey, or refuses to go back.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mirror Images](mirror-images.md)*, which are illusions that cannot touch anything; and *[Bilocation](bilocation.md)*, which puts one self in two places rather than making many.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** a body that splits along a seam of light and becomes two.
- **Inner energy:** a martial artist's shadow selves, born of perfect breath and focus.
- **Technology:** cloned bodies or printed shells, each loaded with the same mind.
- **Arcane:** a mirror spell cast so strongly that the reflection steps out.
