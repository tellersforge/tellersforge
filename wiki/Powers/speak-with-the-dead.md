# Speak with the Dead

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The dead remember. With the right power they can be made to speak: a few words from a fresh corpse, a ghost who can be asked questions, a skull on the table giving answers. At first they only mutter scraps. Masters conduct long interrogations, drawing out names, places and old secrets.

The dead are not always honest, not always clear, and not always happy to be called. Some are confused, some are vengeful, some ask for things in return. It is a powerful tool for detectives and for those who want to know where the treasure was buried, but it leaves a cold feeling that does not quickly pass.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** question the dead
- **Minor → major:** a few words → a full interrogation
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** silence, or only noise. Spectacular failure: the dead one answers with anger, or something else answers in its place.

## Not to be confused with

*[Spirit Speech](spirit-speech.md)*, which talks to the spirits of places and things; Speak with the Dead talks only to the dead.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a prayer to a god of death for leave to question the departed.
- **Occult:** a candle of human fat and the corpse's hand in the necromancer's own.
- **Spirit:** a medium's trance in which the ancestors speak.
- **Technology:** a machine that reads the last memories stored in the brain.
