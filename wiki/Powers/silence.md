# Silence

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A bubble of stillness in a noisy world. Inside it, footsteps make no sound, breaking glass is mute, and shouts die on the lips. A small use hushes only the caster's own steps; a larger one covers a room, a courtyard or a whole camp where nothing can be spoken aloud.

It serves the thief and the assassin, but also the monk and the peacemaker: a silent room can stop a quarrel or protect a council from eavesdroppers. Its hardest use is against those who work their powers with words, for a caster who can't speak often can't cast at all.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** a zone where no sound carries
- **Minor → major:** your own steps → a zone where nothing can be spoken
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Sounds are muffled but carry anyway. Spectacular failure: the silence falls only on the caster's friends, or only lifts at the loudest moment.

## Not to be confused with

*[Sound Control](sound-control.md)*, which shapes and moves sounds; Silence only takes them away.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a hush of reverence spreading from a raised holy symbol.
- **Arcane:** a finger laid on the lips and a whispered word that drowns all others.
- **Technology:** a disc that emits cancelling waves and makes the air feel thick.
