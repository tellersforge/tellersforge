# Power Amplification

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Making everyone else stronger. The amplifier lays a hand on a friend's shoulder, and the friend's flames burn hotter, their visions clear, their healing touch reaches further. In a group of gifted fighters, the amplifier is the quiet centre that everything else revolves around.

The gift is a lens rather than a fire: it creates nothing, only makes what is there more. At its greatest, a whole band of heroes blazes with borrowed brilliance, but the amplifier pays for it in fatigue, and the boosted powers are harder to control. Enemies learn quickly which one to strike first.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** boost other people's powers
- **Minor → major:** a small boost → a huge surge
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The boost fizzles and nothing changes. Spectacular failure: the power swells out of control, hurting its owner or those nearby.

## Not to be confused with

*[Power Surge](power-surge.md)*, which boosts only the user's own gifts; Power Amplification boosts other people's.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** an inborn aura that brightens every gift near it; the user's skin glows faintly when it works.
- **Technology:** a resonance amplifier worn as a harness, linking with the powers of those around it.
- **Divine:** a blessing laid on the faithful, filling them with holy strength.
