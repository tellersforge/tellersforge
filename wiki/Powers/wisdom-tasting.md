# Wisdom-Tasting

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A single sip from a sacred well, a bite of an enchanted fish, a drop of blood from a wise beast. Wisdom-tasting brings sudden knowledge by eating or drinking something sacred. At first it grants one flash of insight. At its fullest, it gives all knowledge of a thing at once, and a sage for life.

It is the stuff of heroic tales: the kitchen boy who burned his thumb on the magic salmon, the wanderer who drank from the spring of memory. Sacred sources are rare and guarded. A hero who knows the trick still has to find them, and win them.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** gain sudden knowledge by eating or drinking a sacred source
- **Minor → major:** one insight → all knowledge of a thing
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The taste gives nothing but a full stomach. Spectacular failure: the knowledge comes too fast, and the mind is left dazed or broken.

## Not to be confused with

*[Cosmic Awareness](cosmic-awareness.md)*, which intuits truths without any source; wisdom-tasting needs something sacred to eat or drink.

## Costumes

- **Heroic legend:** the hero tastes a sacred salmon, hazelnut, honey or spring water, and suddenly knows.
