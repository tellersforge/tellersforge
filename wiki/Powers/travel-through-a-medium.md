# Travel Through a Medium

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Step into an oak and come out of another in a distant forest. Step into a shadow, a hearth fire, a mirror or a still pool, and step out of its kin far away. Each traveller has a chosen medium, and the way is always through it: bark closes around the body, darkness swallows it, flames lick over it without burning. At its smallest it links two trees in the same wood. At its greatest, a shadow in one land opens onto a shadow on the far side of a continent.

It is quiet and hard to follow, which makes it prized by spies, smugglers and old forest wardens. It does little in a place without the medium, and a well-lit room is a prison to a shadow walker.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** step into trees, shadows, fire, mirrors or water and out elsewhere
- **Minor → major:** tree to tree → across a continent through shadow
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The medium stays solid and the traveller stays put. Spectacular failure: they come out somewhere unexpected, or linger too long in the in-between.

## Not to be confused with

*[Portal](portal.md)*, which opens a doorway anyone can walk through; this power carries only the traveller, and only through their chosen medium.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** the old kinship of tree and fire and water; the medium opens like a friend.
- **Arcane:** a word written on the surface of the mirror, pool or trunk, and the traveller steps into it.
- **Occult:** shadow-walking learned from things that live in the dark, which may notice the traveller passing.
