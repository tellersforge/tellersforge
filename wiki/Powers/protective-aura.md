# Protective Aura

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Stand near the protector and blows seem to land a little softer. Wounds are shallower, fire burns less fiercely, and falls end in bruises instead of broken bones. It is not a wall but a presence, a field of calm around one person that shelters everyone within a few paces. At its greatest, nothing within the zone can be harmed at all, and those outside watch in frustration as their weapons fail.

Armies love it. Healers value it for keeping the wounded alive until help arrives. Those who have it learn to stand in the middle of their friends, and to be the first target the enemy seeks out.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** shield everyone near you
- **Minor → major:** a small edge → a zone immune to harm
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the aura glows faintly and gives no shelter. Spectacular failure: it gutters out suddenly, leaving everyone near exposed and shaken.

## Not to be confused with

*[Force Field](force-field.md)*, a visible wall or dome that stops blows outright; Protective Aura softens harm to all who stand close.

## Costumes
