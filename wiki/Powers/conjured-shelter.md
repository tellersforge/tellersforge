# Conjured Shelter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A roof in the wilderness, raised with a word. At its simplest, a tent of woven light that keeps off the rain for a night. At its most impressive, a stone hall with a hearth, beds and a barred door, standing on a bare hillside where there was nothing an hour before, and gone again by morning.

Travelling priests use it to shelter pilgrims, and wizards to avoid inns. Soldiers who have the gift in their company are envied by every other. The shelter keeps out the weather and most of what prowls in the dark, though old campaigners never quite trust walls that were not there at sunset.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** instant shelter
- **Minor → major:** a tent → a fortified hall
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No roof rises, and the night is spent in the open. Spectacular failure: the shelter vanishes at midnight, in the rain.

## Not to be confused with

*[Conjure Object](conjure-object.md)*, which makes any single thing; Conjured Shelter makes a place to live in for a night or two.
