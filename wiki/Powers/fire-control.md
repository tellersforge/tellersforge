# Fire Control

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Fire already burning obeys. A candle flame leans, stretches and dances into the shape of a bird. A campfire leaps into a wall between the party and the wolves. A blaze in a granary is coaxed back from the roof and smothered in its own ashes. At its greatest, a forest fire is steered across a valley like a flock of sheep.

The controller does not make fire, only commands what is there, so they carry a lantern, a torch or a tinderbox. It is a power of great danger and great responsibility: a fire told where to go does not always stay told. Firefighters and arsonists alike would give a great deal for it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** shape and steer existing fire
- **Minor → major:** bend a flame → steer a forest fire
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The flames flicker and ignore the caster. Spectacular failure: the fire does exactly what it wants, spreading toward the caster.

## Not to be confused with

*[Fire Blast](fire-blast.md)*, which makes flame and throws it; Fire Control only shapes fire already burning.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** commanding gestures that the flames echo, like a conductor and an orchestra.
- **Spirit:** a bargain with the fire spirit, which listens but has its own appetite.
- **Psionic:** the mind grips the heat and bends it, eyes reflecting the glow.
- **Inner energy:** a martial form that the flame follows, swirling around the body like a ribbon.
