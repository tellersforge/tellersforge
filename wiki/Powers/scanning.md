# Scanning

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A beam of light sweeps across a stranger, and a screen fills with numbers. Scanning is the power to analyse matter, life and energy: what something is made of, whether it is alive, what it carries, what might harm it. A simple reading gives a rough outline. A thorough one maps a body or machine down to its smallest parts.

It belongs to later ages: surgeons, engineers, explorers of strange worlds and soldiers entering a building. It sees much and understands little. A scanner can list every substance in a potion and still not know what the potion does.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** analyse matter, life and energy
- **Minor → major:** a basic readout → a molecular map
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The reading is garbled or empty. Spectacular failure: the device gives a confident, completely wrong answer.

## Not to be confused with

*[Identify](identify.md)*, which learns what an object or creature is and does; scanning measures what it is made of.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a hand-held scanner, a visor overlay or a drone that hums and projects a fan of light.
