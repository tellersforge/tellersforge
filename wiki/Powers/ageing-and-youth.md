# Ageing & Youth

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Time turned inside out for one person or one thing. A grey hair sprouts, a wrinkle deepens, a sword rusts in its scabbard. Or the reverse: lines smooth, joints loosen, a faded tapestry comes back to colour. At its extremes, a man is turned to dust in the chair where he sat, or an old woman becomes an infant squalling in her own clothes.

It is sought by the vain and the dying, and used as a weapon by those who dare. A good deal of old gossip about ageless queens and wizened young rivals comes down to this. Legends say the stolen years must go somewhere, and the careful worker asks where.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **COU**/INT/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** age or rejuvenate a person or object
- **Minor → major:** a grey hair → dust, or an infant
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The years stay put. Spectacular failure: the ageing or the youth lands on the caster.

## Not to be confused with

*[Longevity](longevity.md)*, which slows ageing over a whole life; Ageing & Youth moves the years quickly, backwards or forwards.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a glass filled with sand turned in the hand, and the target's hair greying or darkening as it runs.
- **Occult:** years drawn from a victim through a bitten apple, and worn by the thief.
- **Technology:** a gene treatment or cellular regeneration bath, or a weapon that speeds decay.
