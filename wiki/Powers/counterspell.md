# Counterspell

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Stopping a working in the instant it begins. The rival caster raises a hand and speaks, and the counter-caster cuts across the words with a sharper word of their own. The gathering light gutters, the half-formed fireball collapses into sparks, and everyone smells singed air.

It is a duellist's gift, all timing and nerve. The counter-caster must see the power coming, know what it is and answer in a heartbeat. Weak powers are easy to smother; great ones need great skill. Among wizards, a well-timed counterspell is considered the height of elegance, and a slow one is fatal.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** stop a power as it is used
- **Minor → major:** disrupt a weak one → a major one
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The rival's power goes off as intended. Spectacular failure: the counter feeds the working, making it stronger.

## Not to be confused with

*[Dispel](dispel.md)*, which unravels a power that is already in force; Counterspell stops one in the moment it is cast.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a sharp word and a slicing gesture; the rival's spell collapses in a shower of sparks.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Dispel](dispel.md)
