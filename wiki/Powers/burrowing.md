# Burrowing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The ground opens and takes the digger in. Soil parts like water, roots are pushed aside, and a trail of heaved earth marks where someone has gone beneath the field. At the small end it is quick, mole-like digging through soft loam. At the great end, the burrower slides through bedrock as a fish slides through a river, leaving no tunnel at all or a clean round passage behind.

Miners envy it, sappers fear it, and castle builders who know of it sink their foundations deeper. Underground there is no light and little air, and a burrower who loses direction may surface somewhere unwelcome. Some cultures bury their dead with care in lands where burrowers are known.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **STR**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** tunnel through earth
- **Minor → major:** soft soil → swim through bedrock
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The earth resists and the digging is slow and exhausting. Spectacular failure: the tunnel collapses behind, or the burrower breaks out into a cellar full of people.

## Not to be confused with

*[Earth Shaping](earth-shaping.md)*, which moves soil and stone from outside; Burrowing moves you through it.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** spade-like claws and a hard, ridged skull, and a body that can squeeze through a narrow hole.
- **Arcane:** a spell that makes the earth yield like dough and close again behind.
- **Technology:** a drill-nosed suit or a small boring machine that grinds and shudders forward.
