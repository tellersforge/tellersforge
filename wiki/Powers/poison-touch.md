# Poison Touch

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Venom from the fingertips. The caster's hand brushes a wrist, a cheek, a cup, and something foul is left behind. At the least it brings nausea and a sweat, enough to put someone off their dinner or their duel. At its worst it spreads as a yellow cloud from open palms, and everything in it chokes.

Some have it in their blood, like a spider or a toad, and have to be careful whom they touch. Others learn it as a craft of poisoners' guilds, or carry it in a ring or glove that injects. It is a feared power in courts, because it leaves the killer's hands clean and the victim's cup innocent.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** inflict toxin
- **Minor → major:** nausea → a lethal cloud
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The touch leaves only a faint sting. Spectacular failure: the poison seeps into the caster's own skin.

## Not to be confused with

*[Acid & Corrosion](acid-and-corrosion.md)*, which burns and eats away flesh and metal; Poison Touch sickens from inside and leaves the skin whole.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** glands in the palms that sweat venom, leaving a faint sheen on everything touched.
- **Arcane:** a greenish glow on the fingertips and a word that turns the caster's touch foul.
- **Technology:** a glove with hidden needles, or a skin patch that doses on contact.
