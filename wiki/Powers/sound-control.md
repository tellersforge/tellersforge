# Sound Control

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Sound becomes clay. A whisper is carried across a crowded hall to one ear alone. A voice comes from an empty corner. A drum grows loud enough to shake the dust from the rafters. Or everything simply goes quiet, footsteps and all, and the guard never hears the door. At its greatest, the noise is enough to deafen a crowd.

It is a trickster's and a spy's gift more often than a warrior's. Musicians with it are uncanny, and heralds are heard in every corner of the square. Those who throw voices often enjoy it too much, and a room of people turning to stare at an empty chair is a joke that rarely wears thin.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** make, amplify, throw or silence sound
- **Minor → major:** whisper across a room → deafening
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The sound comes out wrong or not at all. Spectacular failure: it booms out at full volume in the worst possible place.

## Not to be confused with

*[Silence](silence.md)*, which blankets an area in stillness; and *[Sonic Scream](sonic-scream.md)*, a destructive blast of sound. Sound Control shapes, throws and adjusts sound.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a gesture that scoops the noise from the air and sends it where it's pointed.
- **Psionic:** the mind shapes the vibration of the air; there is a faint pressure in the ears.
- **Technology:** directional speakers and noise-cancelling fields hidden in a collar or drone.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Silence](silence.md), [Sonic Scream](sonic-scream.md)
