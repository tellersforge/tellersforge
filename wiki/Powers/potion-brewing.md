# Potion Brewing

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Cauldrons, stills and bottles of coloured liquid that do real things. The brewer grinds roots, distils essences, simmers mixtures for days and seals the result in glass. One potion heals a wound overnight, another lets the drinker breathe underwater, a third puts a guard to sleep in the middle of a sentence.

It is slow, exacting and smelly work. Ingredients must be fresh, measures exact and timing perfect, and a failed brew can poison or explode. At its greatest the craft produces legendary elixirs that restore youth or bring back the dying. Brewers are respected, often feared, and almost always suspected of poisonings in their town.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** brew potions, elixirs and salves with real effects
- **Minor → major:** a weak salve → an elixir of life
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The brew goes flat and does nothing. Spectacular failure: it does something else entirely, usually unpleasant, and usually to whoever drinks first.

## Not to be confused with

*[Alchemy](../Skills/alchemy.md)*, the skill, which studies and transforms substances without any power; Potion Brewing puts real power into the draught.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a mage's laboratory, runes on the cauldron and spells whispered over the steam.
- **Alchemical:** retorts, furnaces and patient distillation, the power drawn from the substances themselves.
