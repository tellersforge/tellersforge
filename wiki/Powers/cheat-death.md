# Cheat Death

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The sorcerer who hid his heart in an egg, inside a duck, inside a chest at the bottom of the sea. The machine mind that keeps a copy of itself in a vault three worlds away. Cheat Death is the art of putting your life somewhere other than your body, so that when the body falls, you do not.

It always leaves a thread to pull. Somewhere there is a vessel: a jar, a ring, a stone, a server, a sleeping twin. While it lasts, the owner can be struck down, burned or buried and still come back. Find the vessel and break it, and the deathless die like anyone else. This is why such people grow careful, secretive and very rich, and why heroes spend so much time looking for eggs.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **END**/COU/WIS/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** survive a death that should be final: a hidden heart, a soul vessel, a backup
- **Minor → major:** one last chance → deathless until the vessel is found
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The death sticks: there is no vessel, or it was never properly bound. Spectacular failure: the vessel works, but it is found, and now someone else holds the owner's life in their hand.

## Not to be confused with

*[Renewal at Death](renewal-at-death.md)*, which is a new life begun after dying; Cheat Death refuses the death itself and hangs everything on a hidden vessel.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a heart cut out in a forbidden rite and sealed in a jar, still beating faintly under wax and lead.
- **Technology:** a mind backed up to a hidden store, ready to wake in a grown body when the old one stops.
- **Heroic legend:** a soul hidden in a needle, in an egg, in a duck, in a chest on an island no map shows.
