# Overcharge

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Asking a machine for more than it was built to give, and getting it. A pistol fires a shot that punches through a wall, a failing engine roars back to life, a lamp blazes like a small sun. For a moment the device outperforms its makers' wildest claims. Then, very often, something melts.

Engineers hate it and soldiers love it, which says much about both. At its mildest it squeezes a little extra from tired machinery. At its fiercest it turns any device into a bomb with a short and unpredictable fuse. Old hands learn to tell, from the pitch of the whine, how long they have left, and to throw the thing before that moment arrives.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** push a device beyond its limits
- **Minor → major:** a small boost → explosive power
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The device stutters and gives nothing extra. Spectacular failure: it bursts in the user's hands, burning, shocking or blinding them.

## Not to be confused with

*[Power Surge](power-surge.md)*, which pushes the user's own gifts to their limit; Overcharge pushes a machine.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a bypass cable and a disabled safety, with warning lights flashing red across the casing.
- **Psionic:** the mind pours raw energy into circuits until they glow and hum.
