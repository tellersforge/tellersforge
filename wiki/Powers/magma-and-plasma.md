# Magma & Plasma

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Stone that runs like syrup and air that burns white. This power commands matter at the edge of ruin: molten rock welling up through a cracked floor, or a glowing thread of plasma that parts steel like cheese. At its gentlest the worker's palm simply glows, warm enough to light a pipe or soften a lock. At its fullest, the ground splits and a slow red river rolls downhill, taking houses with it.

It is a heavy, slow gift that demands nerve. Molten stone does not hurry and does not forgive, and the heat reaches the worker as surely as anyone else. Those who use it well tend to be patient, scarred and very careful about where they stand.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END/COU/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** control molten rock or plasma
- **Minor → major:** a glowing hand → a lava flood
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The rock stays cold, or glows and sets again uselessly. Spectacular failure: the melt spreads the wrong way and the caster is standing in it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Fire Control](fire-control.md)*, which shapes flame that is already burning; Magma & Plasma makes the ground itself flow and burn.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a long incantation over a brazier of hot coals; the ground answers with a dull red glow.
- **Mutation:** blood that runs hot as a forge, and skin that smokes when the gift wakes.
- **Technology:** a gauntlet with a plasma lance, venting heat through fins along the forearm.
