# Plane Shift

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

To leave this reality entirely and arrive in another: a realm of fire, a world of mirrors, a heaven, a hell, a universe next door where history went otherwise. The shift is a lurch, a moment of falling, and then the air tastes different.

At first it is a glimpse, a look into another reality before being snapped back. Masters can carry a whole company across. Every plane has its own rules and its own rulers, and a traveller who arrives unannounced should be ready to explain themselves. The way back is not always the same as the way in.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/COU/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** travel to another reality
- **Minor → major:** a glimpse → a group crossing
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The caster stays in this reality. Spectacular failure: they arrive somewhere else entirely, and not alone.

## Not to be confused with

*[Portal](portal.md)*, which opens a door for others; Plane Shift moves the caster, and anyone they bring, directly.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a spell of great complexity, cast in a chalked circle, that drops the caster through the floor of the world.
- **Divine:** a god lifts the faithful into the heavens.
- **Occult:** a summoning that trades places with a creature from beyond.
- **Technology:** a dimensional transporter that shifts matter between realities.
