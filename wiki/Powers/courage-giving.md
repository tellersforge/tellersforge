# Courage-Giving

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A hand on the shoulder, a word in the line before the charge, and the shaking stops. This power takes fear away and puts resolve in its place. The frightened recruit grips the spear again. The villagers stop barring their doors and pick up pitchforks. The one who gives it is usually standing in front, where everyone can see, because courage is catching and needs someone to catch it from.

It does not make anyone stronger or more skilled, only willing. That is often enough. Many battles are lost by the side that breaks first, and a company that will not break has already won half of its fight. Priests, captains and bards all claim the gift, and all are sure theirs is the true kind.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** remove fear and inspire allies
- **Minor → major:** steady a friend → rally an army
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The words fall flat and the fear stays. Spectacular failure: the speaker's own voice cracks, and doubt spreads faster than it would have alone.

## Not to be confused with

*[Emotion Control](emotion-control.md)*, which can push any mood in anyone; and *[Blessing](blessing.md)*, which grants good fortune. Courage-Giving only drives out fear and lifts the heart of allies.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a sign made over the troops and a prayer shouted above the noise.
- **Song:** a marching song or war chant; each verse steadies the line a little more.
