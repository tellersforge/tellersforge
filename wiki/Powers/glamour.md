# Glamour

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The oldest trick of the hidden folk. With glamour, leaves pass as gold coins, a hovel as a lit hall, a plain face as unbearable beauty. It changes not the thing but how it seems, and it can cover anything: an object, a place, a person, the caster's own form. Minor workings make a trinket pass as treasure; great ones raise a false castle or show the caster in the shape of a god.

Glamours tend to fail at dawn, over running water or at the touch of iron, and when they fail they fail completely. Whoever has been paid in glamoured gold will be very angry in the morning.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** make things seem other than they are: leaves as gold, hovels as halls, dazzling beauty
- **Minor → major:** a trinket passes as gold → a false castle or a divine form
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The seeming is thin and plainly false. Spectacular failure: it drops away at the worst moment, in full view.

## Not to be confused with

*[Disguise](../Skills/disguise.md)*, which changes only the caster's own face and form into another person's; glamour makes anything seem other than it is.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a fae gift, sweet-smelling and lovely; it fades with the dew.
- **Arcane:** a delicate weaving of seeming laid over a thing like a veil.
