# Crop Blessing & Blight

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The oldest magic in any farming land: to make the fields bear, and its dark twin, to make them fail. A good worker walks the furrows in spring and the barley stands thick by summer. A bad neighbour walks them at night, and the grain comes up black and the cows give blood instead of milk.

Small workings mean a better harvest or a spoiled churn. Great ones end famines or cause them, and are remembered for generations. Villages hire the worker they trust and whisper about the one they don't. Stolen harvests are part of it too: the cream that vanishes from one dairy and shows up thick in another, the crop that moves overnight from one field to the next.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** make land fruitful or barren; steal or spoil harvests and milk
- **Minor → major:** a good harvest → end a famine, or ruin a village
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The season goes as it would have. Spectacular failure: the blessing falls on weeds and pests, or the blight spreads to the worker's own fields.

## Not to be confused with

*[Plant Growth](plant-growth.md)*, which makes plants grow fast and wild; Crop Blessing & Blight works on the fruitfulness of the land and its yield.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a harvest god's rite, with sacrifice and procession round the field boundaries.
- **Spirit:** offerings to the spirits of the earth, which reward or punish the farm.
- **Folk magic:** a bundle of straw burned at the corner of the field, or a charm buried in the furrow.
