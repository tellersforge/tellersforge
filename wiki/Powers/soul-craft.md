# Soul Craft

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The soul is a thing, and things can be shaped. The soul-crafter senses the spirit behind the eyes, marks it, splits it, traps it or binds it in a jewel, a phylactery or a doll. At first they can only see and mark a soul. At full mastery a soul is held in a gem, used as a battery, a hostage or a key.

It is dreadful, precise work, and forbidden almost everywhere. The dead do not rest when their souls are held. The crafter's own soul is also at risk, since those who handle souls often lose track of their own.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/COU/WIS/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** sense, trap, split or bind souls
- **Minor → major:** mark a soul → a soul held in a gem
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the soul slips through the fingers. Spectacular failure: the crafter's own soul is caught.

## Not to be confused with

*[Cheat Death](cheat-death.md)*, which escapes one's own death; Soul Craft shapes and traps souls.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a black gem, a rite at midnight and a soul pulled from the dying.
- **Arcane:** a vessel etched with runes that draw and hold a spirit.
