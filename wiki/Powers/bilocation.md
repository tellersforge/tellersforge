# Bilocation

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

To stand in two places at once, both of them real. A holy woman is seen praying in her cell and, at the same hour, comforting a dying man in a village two valleys away. Both bodies are warm, both speak, both can be touched. At first the second presence is brief and faint, a figure glimpsed at a bedside that fades when someone reaches out. At its greatest, the bilocator lives fully in both places, talking, working and acting as two.

The gift is rare, and it is heavy on the mind. Two sets of sights and sounds pour in together, and many who have it seem distracted or distant. Stories of it are nearly always stories of saints and hermits, and those who meet the second self rarely forget it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/WIS/INT/END/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** be bodily present in two places at once
- **Minor → major:** a brief apparition → acting fully in both
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The second presence never forms, or appears only as a faint shape. Spectacular failure: the mind splits badly, and neither self knows clearly where it is.

## Not to be confused with

*[Duplication](duplication.md)*, which makes extra copies of the body; and *[Spirit Flight](spirit-flight.md)*, which sends the soul out while the body sleeps. Bilocation is one person, wholly present twice.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a saint's grace; the second self appears in a gentle light and is gone when the need is met.
- **Inner energy:** a master's meditation so deep that the self is no longer held in one place.
