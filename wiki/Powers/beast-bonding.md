# Beast Bonding

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A friendship that goes all the way down. The user and one creature choose each other, and from then on they are partners: each feels the other's moods, each lends the other strength, and each knows without looking where the other is. A hound fights harder with its master at its side; the master runs longer with the hound beside them.

The bond is for life and does not easily move. Many bonded companions are ordinary animals made extraordinary by the bond: the wisest horse, the most loyal falcon. In the old tales, a rare few bond with something far greater, a great winged serpent or a sea beast, and the two change the fate of kingdoms together.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** a lifelong bond with one creature, sharing strength
- **Minor → major:** a trusted companion → a dragon partner
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The creature holds back, wary or distracted. Spectacular failure: the bond frays, and the partner turns away for a time, hurt.

## Not to be confused with

*[Familiar](familiar.md)*, a magical servant tied to a caster's craft; Beast Bonding is a partnership of equals. *[Shared Soul](shared-soul.md)* goes further, binding two lives into one.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a rite under the stars, the partners sharing breath and blood, and the spirits witnessing the bond.
- **Heroic legend:** a hero chosen by a legendary beast, bound by an oath older than kingdoms.
