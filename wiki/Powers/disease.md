# Disease

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Sickness sent on purpose. A cough that won't clear, a fever that comes at night, a rash that spreads. Small workings pass as bad luck or bad water. Great ones empty towns, and are remembered for centuries under names people still whisper.

It is among the most hated powers in any tradition, because it does not choose between guilty and innocent once it starts. Some priesthoods claim it as their god's judgement. Some cults serve a pestilence as a god. Some laboratories grow it in sealed rooms and give it a number. Those who work it tend to keep their own remedies close and their neighbours closer.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/INT/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** inflict sickness
- **Minor → major:** a cough → a plague
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The target feels unwell for an hour and recovers. Spectacular failure: the sickness takes the caster, or spreads well beyond its target.

## Not to be confused with

*[Cure](cure.md)*, the power that drives sickness out; Disease is its dark twin, and puts it in.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a rag from a sickbed buried with a name written on it.
- **Divine:** a plague sent by an angry god, falling on the house that offended.
- **Technology:** an engineered pathogen released from a vial or a sprayer.
