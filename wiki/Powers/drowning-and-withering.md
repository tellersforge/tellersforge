# Drowning & Withering

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A cruel and quiet killing. The one who has this gift looks at a person, and water begins to gather in their lungs where none could have come from. At the start it is only a cough, a gurgle, a sudden gasping. At its end, a man drowns on dry land. Reversed, the power draws water out of a body, and the victim shrivels, cracks and withers like fruit left in the sun.

Few who see it forget it, and few who use it are trusted again. It needs the target to be close and in sight, and it takes time; interrupted, the victim coughs and recovers. In many lands its use is a hanging crime, and it is whispered about in the same breath as poison and the evil eye.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/WIS/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** fill lungs with water or draw moisture from a body
- **Minor → major:** a cough → death
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The target coughs and clears their throat, unharmed. Spectacular failure: the water rises in the caster's own chest.

## Not to be confused with

*[Blood Control](blood-control.md)*, which moves the fluids inside a body; Drowning & Withering only floods or dries it.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a forbidden word learned from drowned things, which bubbles in the caster's throat as they speak it.
- **Arcane:** a slow closing of the hand, the air grown heavy and damp around the victim.
