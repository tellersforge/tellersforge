# Neural Interface

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The mind as a plug and the network as a room to walk into. The user closes their eyes and the flow of data opens before them: files as shelves, traffic as crowded streets, locked vaults as doors with shapes that can be learned. Reading a message becomes as natural as reading a face.

The deeper users stop seeing a network and start living in one. They move through it faster than any keyboard allows, speak with its programs as if with servants, and feel an attack on the system as a blow to the body. Their bodies lie slack while they are away, which is why old hands never dive alone, and why the unwary sometimes come back to find that someone else has been using their limbs.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** plug your mind into networks
- **Minor → major:** read data → live inside the network
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The connection won't take, or the data comes as noise. Spectacular failure: the network pushes back, flooding the mind with pain or leaving the user trapped inside until someone pulls the plug.

## Not to be confused with

*[Remote Hack](remote-hack.md)*, which reaches out to seize or disable a device; Neural Interface brings the user's own mind inside the network.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a jack at the temple or wireless implant behind the eye; the pupils dart as the world of data opens.
