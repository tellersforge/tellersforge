# Cosmic Awareness

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Truth arrives without asking. The bearer suddenly knows where the key is, that the ship will sink, that the stranger is a king. Cosmic awareness intuits truths beyond all senses. At first it is only a lucky insight; at its greatest, near-omniscience, a vast calm knowing that makes the bearer seem more than human.

It is rare and fearsome. Those who carry it are often distant and strange, answering questions no one asked and ignoring ones they did. The mind was not made to hold the whole world, and bearers must learn to let most of it go.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/WIS/INT/INT/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** intuit truths beyond all senses
- **Minor → major:** a lucky insight → near-omniscience
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No insight comes. Spectacular failure: the bearer is overwhelmed by too much knowledge and loses themselves for a while.

## Not to be confused with

*[Future Seeing (Precognition)](future-seeing-precognition.md)*, which shows visions of the future; cosmic awareness simply knows, past, present and future.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** the god's own knowledge flowing into a chosen vessel.
- **Occult:** a mind opened to the vast cold cosmos, at a price.
