# Summon Otherworldly Being

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The circle is drawn, the words are spoken, the candle gutters, and something steps through that has no right to be here. Spirits, demons and angels do not live in the ordinary world, but the summoner can open a way. A beginner calls up an imp that sneers and bargains. A master calls down a heavenly host or a prince of hell.

Such beings do not serve out of kindness. They come because they must, they obey only as far as they are held, and they remember everything. Summoners who survive long learn caution, contracts and the value of a very good circle.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/COU/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** call a spirit, demon or angel from another plane
- **Minor → major:** an imp → an archfiend or a heavenly host
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** nothing comes. Spectacular failure: something comes that was not called, or the right thing arrives unbound.

## Not to be confused with

*[Pact](pact.md)*, which bargains with a being for favour; and *[Bind](bind.md)*, which holds a being still. Summoning brings the being here.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a pentacle in chalk and blood, a black candle and a forbidden name.
- **Divine:** prayer and fasting until a radiant messenger descends.
- **Arcane:** a precise ritual of seals, sigils and calculated hours.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Pact](pact.md), [Bind](bind.md)
