# Power Surge

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Everything, all at once, and then the bill. The user opens every door inside themselves and lets the power flood through: flames burn hotter, blows land heavier, the mind reaches farther than it has ever reached. Witnesses see a figure that seems to grow brighter and louder, as if more of them had suddenly arrived.

It cannot last, and everyone who uses it knows that. When the surge ebbs, it takes more than it gave. The user may shake, bleed from the nose, sleep for a day or find their gifts silent until they recover. Wise heroes keep it for the moment when there will be no afterwards if they don't, and trust friends to carry them home when it ends.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/END/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** temporarily multiply your powers, with a crash afterwards
- **Minor → major:** a brief boost → a huge surge
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The surge sputters and dies, and the crash comes anyway. Spectacular failure: the power runs wild for a moment, hurting friends and the user alike.

## Not to be confused with

*[Battle Fury](battle-fury.md)*, which is a rage of the body and fighting spirit; Power Surge swells the user's powers themselves, and the *[Avatar](avatar.md)* becomes something else entirely.

## Costumes

- **Inner energy:** the fighter draws breath from the belly and releases every seal at once; the air around them shimmers with heat.
- **Technology:** a limiter switched off with a warning chime, cooling fans screaming as the rig runs past safe limits.
- **Mutation:** veins darken and glow as an inborn gift, usually held in check, is let off its leash.
