# Energy Absorption

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Fire splashes against the bearer and vanishes. A bolt of lightning strikes them and fades into their skin. The energy absorber drinks in force and energy, turning harm into stored power, and can later spend it again as a burst of their own. The smallest use blunts a spark; the greatest swallows a blast and sends it straight back where it came from.

It is a defensive gift with a dangerous edge. What is taken in must go somewhere, and bearers who hold too much for too long become restless, bright-eyed and twitchy, their hands humming like a hive.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** soak up energy or force and release it
- **Minor → major:** blunt a spark → send an explosion back
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The energy lands as it would on anyone. Spectacular failure: the bearer takes it in, then loses control of it on the spot.

## Not to be confused with

*[Deflect & Reflect](deflect-and-reflect.md)*, which turns an attack aside at once; absorption stores it and can release it later.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** a body that glows faintly after every hit it swallows.
- **Technology:** a harness of capacitors or a shield that drinks energy and hums with it.
- **Arcane:** a warding sigil on the chest that eats spells and holds them for later.
