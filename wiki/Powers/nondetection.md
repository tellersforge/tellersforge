# Nondetection

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Seers look and see only fog. The crystal clouds, the bones fall meaningless, the mind that reaches out finds nothing to grip. This power hides its user from scrying, divination, far-sight and every clever way of finding someone who does not want to be found. At its weakest it blurs the picture. At its strongest the user is simply absent from every vision.

It is valued by spies, fugitives and anyone whose enemies have seers. Its great weakness is that it does nothing against ordinary eyes. A person hidden from every oracle can still be recognised by a baker.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** hide from scrying and detection
- **Minor → major:** a blur → invisible to all divination
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the seer sees through it. Spectacular failure: the user shows up brighter than before, like a beacon.

## Not to be confused with

*[Veil of Concealment](veil-of-concealment.md)*, which hides from sight and senses; and *[Mind Shield](mind-shield.md)*, which guards thoughts. Nondetection hides from powers that seek.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a smoky amulet worn against the skin.
- **Psionic:** a mind trained to show a blank face to anyone who searches for it.
- **Technology:** a jammer that scrambles tracking signals, scans and satellite eyes.
