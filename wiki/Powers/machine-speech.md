# Machine Speech

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Engines have moods. The speaker hears them: a lift that is tired, a lock that is spiteful, a ship's engine that is afraid. At first there is only a feeling that comes through the hand on the housing. At its full reach, the speaker holds a whole conversation with a machine, asking what happened, persuading it to try harder, or bargaining for a door to open.

To everyone else it looks like talking to a machine, which it is. The machines themselves are often grateful, and remember those who spoke to them kindly. Engineers with the gift are legendary and are rarely believed.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** talk with machines as living beings
- **Minor → major:** a mood → full dialogue
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The machine only hums. Spectacular failure: the machine takes offence, and stops working for anyone nearby.

## Not to be confused with

*[Technopathy](technopathy.md)*, which commands machines by thought. Machine Speech treats them as living beings and talks with them.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** an interface plug and a small speaker that turns engine noise into speech.
- **Psionic:** a hand laid on the casing; the machine's mood seeps into the mind.
