# Forest-Guarding

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The woods have a keeper, and the keeper does not want you here. Paths bend back on themselves, landmarks move, the same crooked oak appears three times in an hour. Brambles close behind intruders, mists rise from nowhere, and a clear stream becomes a bog. At its height, the whole forest turns: branches lash, roots trip, and the trees themselves seem to watch.

It is the gift of hermits, fae guardians and forest peoples who know the woods better than any map. Most who use it would rather turn a traveller around than harm them, and a polite visitor with a gift for the spirits is often let through. Loggers, hunters for sport and anyone carrying an axe are rarely so lucky.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** lead intruders astray, hide paths, turn the woods hostile
- **Minor → major:** confuse a path → the forest turns on them
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The woods stay ordinary and intruders find their way. Spectacular failure: the guardian loses their own bearings, lost in the forest they meant to protect.

## Not to be confused with

*[Pathfinding](pathfinding.md)*, which finds the way through wild country; Forest-Guarding hides it from others.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a song hummed to the trees; the mist thickens and the paths quietly shift.
