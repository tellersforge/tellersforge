# Water Walking

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

To step off the jetty and keep walking. The surface of a lake gives a little underfoot, like a stretched hide, and the walker crosses it dry-shod while the boats row after. Small uses are a puddle that does not soak the boots, or a bog crossed without sinking. Great ones are a figure striding over open sea in a storm, or across a river of molten rock that would swallow a horse.

It is a quiet power that looks like a miracle, and many who have it are treated as holy whether they wish it or not. It gives no grip on steep ground and no help if the walker trips and goes under. Waves still move, so a rough sea is a long and tiring walk.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/AGI · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** walk on water or surfaces that can't hold weight
- **Minor → major:** a puddle → the open sea, or lava
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The surface softens and the walker sinks in to the knees, then deeper. Spectacular failure: it gives way entirely in the middle of the crossing.

## Not to be confused with

*[Wall Walking](wall-walking.md)*, which clings to walls and ceilings; Water Walking stands on things too soft to hold a person.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** calm faith; each step lands with a faint ring of light on the water.
- **Inner energy:** a lightness of body so complete that the surface barely feels the weight.
- **Arcane:** a spoken charm, and the water stiffens underfoot like cold glass.
