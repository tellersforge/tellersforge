# Remote Hack

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Reaching into a machine from across the room, or across the city, and making it do something else. A door that should stay locked opens, a camera loops the same empty minute, a guard robot forgets who it belongs to. The work is fast, silent and invisible except to the hacker, who sees the machine's defences as walls and doors to be picked.

The small uses are daily business for thieves and corporate spies. The great ones decide wars: a single operator seizes a fortress's guns and turns them on its own courtyard. Every system has its defenders, though, and some of them hack back.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** take over or disable electronics from a distance
- **Minor → major:** open a door → take a facility
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The defences hold, or the device resets before it can be taken. Spectacular failure: the intrusion is traced, alarms sound and the defenders now know exactly where the hacker is.

## Not to be confused with

*[Hacking](../Skills/hacking.md)*, the skill, which does the same work slowly with a keyboard and a connection; Remote Hack reaches in from a distance without either.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a slim deck or implant firing invisible code through the air, the hacker's fingers twitching as they type on nothing.
- **Psionic:** the mind slides into circuits like a key into a lock.
