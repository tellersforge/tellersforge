# Healing Factor

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The body mends itself, fast. Cuts close as they are watched, bruises fade within hours, bones knit overnight. The gentlest form heals what would take a week in a matter of hours. At its strongest, a wound closes before the blade that made it is drawn back out.

The gift asks a price in hunger: the body must be fed to rebuild itself. Those who have it are often reckless, having learned that little harm lasts. Enemies who know about it learn to hit hard and not stop, or to use fire, which the body finds harder to forgive.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** heal yourself fast and unaided
- **Minor → major:** hours → seconds
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The wound heals only at the usual pace. Spectacular failure: the flesh knits wrong, sealing a blade inside or twisting a bone.

## Not to be confused with

*[Limb Regrowth](limb-regrowth.md)*, which regrows what was lost entirely. Healing Factor mends wounds quickly but cannot rebuild a missing part.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** flesh that crawls closed, visibly and a little horribly.
- **Technology:** a swarm of tiny repair machines under the skin.
- **Vampiric:** wounds that close as fresh blood is drunk.
