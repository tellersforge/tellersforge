# Charged Blast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Power gathered like a held breath. The user stands still, drawing energy in until the air trembles and the light goes strange, then lets it all go at once. A small charge makes a punch that throws a man across a courtyard. A long one can flatten forests and send a wave rolling through a city.

Its weakness is time. The charging is slow and obvious, and a canny enemy will strike while the glow builds. For this reason it is favoured by those with loyal friends to guard them, or by those who strike first from hiding. Everyone who sees it remembers it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/END/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** build power over time for one huge release
- **Minor → major:** a charged punch → a city-scale wave
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The gathered power seeps away with nothing to show. Spectacular failure: it releases too early and in every direction at once.

## Not to be confused with

*[Energy Bolt](energy-bolt.md)*, which is fast and repeatable; the charged blast is one slow, huge release.

## Costumes

- **Inner energy:** a long stance and deep breathing, energy visibly pooling in the cupped hands.
- **Mutation:** the body swelling with light until it spills out in one burst.
- **Arcane:** a lengthening chant, each verse adding to a sphere of gathering force.
