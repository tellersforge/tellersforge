# Cure

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Sickness driven out. The poison leaves the blood, the fever cools, the rash fades, the curse loosens its grip. At the lightest touch, symptoms ease and the patient can sleep. At its strongest, a whole plague lifts from a city in a single morning, and the bells ring.

It is a precious gift and a hard one. It cures the illness but not the damage done, and some sicknesses fight back. Plague years make heroes of those who have it, and martyrs of those who try too many times.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** remove poison, disease or afflictions
- **Minor → major:** ease symptoms → end a plague
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The sickness lingers. Spectacular failure: the curer takes the illness on themselves, or spreads it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Purification](purification.md)*, which cleanses food, water, places and people of taint. Cure removes poison and disease from the body.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a prayer and a sign made over the sufferer's heart.
- **Spirit:** a spirit sucked out of the body by the shaman's lips and spat into the fire.
- **Technology:** an injection of engineered medicine that hunts the sickness.
