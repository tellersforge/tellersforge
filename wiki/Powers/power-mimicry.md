# Power Mimicry

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Seeing a power and doing it too. The mimic watches a rival throw fire and, a moment later, throws fire back. A touch on a healer's hand teaches them to heal; a glance at a flier teaches them to fly. At first the copy is weak and brief, a pale echo that fades within moments. With mastery the mimic keeps what they have taken, and becomes a collection of other people's gifts.

It is a feared gift and an envied one. Mimics have no powers of their own and every power at once, but they only ever have what they have seen. Clever enemies keep their best gifts hidden until the mimic is out of sight.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/END/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** copy powers you see or touch
- **Minor → major:** a weak copy briefly → keep them
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The copy doesn't take, or produces only a faint shimmer. Spectacular failure: the mimic gets the power wrong, and it turns against them.

## Not to be confused with

*[Power Theft](power-theft.md)*, which takes a power from its owner; Power Mimicry copies it and leaves the original. *[Mimicry](../Skills/mimicry.md)* only imitates voices and appearances.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** an inborn gift that reshapes the body to echo whatever power it touches.
- **Arcane:** a mage who reads the pattern of any spell and weaves it back at once.
