# Night & Spectrum Sight

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Eyes that do not need the sun. With this gift the bearer sees in near darkness, then in heat, then in lights that human eyes were never built for. A warm body glows behind a curtain, a cold stove is dark, footprints show as fading smudges on the floor. At its greatest, the bearer looks straight through thin walls and into the bones of the living.

It is prized by scouts, hunters, guards and anyone who works at night. It has its oddities: a torch is dazzling, fine print is hard, and faces lose their colour.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **PER** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** see in darkness, heat or other spectra
- **Minor → major:** low light → thermal and x-ray
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The dark stays dark. Spectacular failure: the bearer is dazzled and blind for a while.

## Not to be confused with

*[See Through Walls](see-through-walls.md)*, which is about piercing cover; night and spectrum sight lets the bearer see in darkness and strange light.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** wide cat's eyes that shine in lamplight.
- **Technology:** goggles or eye implants with thermal and low-light modes.
- **Arcane:** a salve rubbed on the eyelids that opens hidden colours.
