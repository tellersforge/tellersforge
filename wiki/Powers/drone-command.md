# Drone Command

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Many machines, one will. The commander sends out drones, turrets or a cloud of tiny flyers and moves them as easily as moving their own fingers. One drone becomes an eye around a corner; a dozen become a hunting pack; a swarm becomes a weather front of buzzing metal that fills a room and leaves it empty.

It looks effortless and is not. Every unit must be kept in mind, and a commander stretched too thin loses track of a few, then of many. Soldiers of the far future fear it the way earlier soldiers feared cavalry: it comes fast, it comes from above, and it never tires. The commander, meanwhile, stands very still behind cover, with eyes that seem to be looking at nothing.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** control drones, turrets or a swarm
- **Minor → major:** one → a swarm
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The units drift, hover in place or return home. Spectacular failure: the swarm loses its orders and turns on whatever is nearest, the commander included.

## Not to be confused with

*[Drone Operation](../Skills/drone-operation.md)*, the skill, which flies one machine by hand with a controller; Drone Command directs many at once as easily as thinking. *[Spirit Servants](spirit-servants.md)* do the same with spirits.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a control rig on the wrist and a headset; the drones answer in bright chirps and move as one.
- **Psionic:** a mind spread thin across many metal bodies, feeling through each at once.
