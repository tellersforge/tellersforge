# Reality Warping

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The world as clay. The warper looks at something and decides it is otherwise: the wall becomes a doorway, the stone becomes bread, the storm never happened. There is no spell, no gesture, just a moment when the world adjusts itself to a new truth, and everyone present feels a lurch like missing a step on the stairs.

At its mildest, it produces small miracles: a lucky coin, a coincidence, a wound that was never quite as deep. At its greatest, it rewrites the laws of nature, and the warper becomes something like a god. It is a gift that frightens its owners as much as their enemies, because reality does not like being bent, and it tends to snap back.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/CHA/COU/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** rewrite reality
- **Minor → major:** a small miracle → godlike rewriting
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Reality holds firm. Spectacular failure: the world warps in an unintended direction, and the change may be hard to undo.

## Not to be confused with

*[Wish Granting](wish-granting.md)*, which reshapes the world on someone else's behalf under strict rules; Reality Warping is the warper's own unbounded will.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a god's hand reaching through a mortal vessel to reshape creation.
- **Occult:** forbidden knowledge of the flaws in reality, pried open by a whispered formula.
