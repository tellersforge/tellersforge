# Metal Control

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Iron listens. The one who has this gift reaches out and metal bends to their will: a spoon curls, a lock twists open, a sword tugs itself from its owner's hand. At its greatest, girders buckle, gates tear from their hinges and the iron hull of a ship peels open like the lid of a tin.

It is a power that grows more terrible as the world grows more metal. Against a village of wood and leather it is a curiosity. Against an army in plate, or a city of steel, it is a catastrophe. Those who have it often feel metal as a hum or a taste, and many find it hard to sleep in a room full of nails. Soldiers who know of it learn to fight with bronze, bone or wood.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** move and reshape metal
- **Minor → major:** bend a spoon → tear apart a ship
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The metal quivers and stays put. Spectacular failure: it moves wildly, toward the one who called it or into a friend's hand.

## Not to be confused with

*[Shape Material](shape-material.md)*, which reshapes stone, wood or metal slowly by touch; Metal Control moves and wrenches metal only, and from a distance.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** an inborn magnetism; iron filings stand on end and compass needles swing toward the one who has it.
- **Arcane:** the old iron-binding words, with the caster's hand closing as the metal folds.
