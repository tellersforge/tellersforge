# Evil Eye

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Harm carried by a look. The neighbour admires the calf, and the calf sickens. The stranger stares too long at the baby, and the baby won't stop crying. Some who have the evil eye use it on purpose, with a hard, flat stare. Many have it and wish they didn't, and cannot praise anything without it wilting.

Whole traditions of charms exist against it: blue beads, painted eyes on boats, spitting to one side when a child is praised, horns hung over doors. Small harms are the usual work: milk that spoils, bread that won't rise. The great ones wither a herd in a week or drive a family from its farm. People with the gift learn to keep their eyes down, and their neighbours learn not to ask them to look.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **COU**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** harm with a look (can also be an involuntary trait or flaw)
- **Minor → major:** spoiled milk → a withered herd
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The look goes unnoticed and does no harm. Spectacular failure: the glance lands on something the looker loves.

## Not to be confused with

*[Curse](curse.md)*, which is declared aloud and follows its target; the Evil Eye needs no words, and is often cast without meaning to.

## Costumes

- **Folk magic:** a long, envious stare and a remark that is a little too admiring.
- **Mutation:** an inborn trait, eyes of an odd colour that spoil whatever they rest on, whether the owner wishes it or not.
