# Gravity Control

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The pull of the earth becomes a thing to bargain with. The one who has this gift can make a heavy load light, so that a child lifts a sack of millstones, or make the ground so heavy that people drop to their knees and cannot rise. At its greatest they tip the world sideways: men fall toward a wall, rocks tumble up into the air, and a whole area is pressed flat beneath a crushing weight.

It is a disorienting power to be near. Water behaves oddly, dust hangs, and anyone not braced loses their footing. Those who use it carelessly find out that gravity, once disturbed, tends to take everything with it, including them.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** make gravity heavier, lighter or point elsewhere
- **Minor → major:** lighten a load → crush an area
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Weight shifts a little and settles back. Spectacular failure: the pull goes the wrong way, and the caster and friends are the ones flattened or thrown.

## Not to be confused with

*[Telekinesis](telekinesis.md)*, which moves particular things; Gravity Control changes the weight of everything in an area.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a heavy chant and a hand pressed palm down; the air grows thick and slow.
- **Psionic:** the mind grips the world's weight; the psion's ears pop and nose may bleed.
- **Technology:** a graviton projector, humming low, bending the pull around a target point.
- **Occult:** a borrowed weight from a dead star or a buried thing, and it leaves a cold ache behind.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Telekinesis](telekinesis.md), gravity skills
