# Mind Shield

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A wall around the self. The one who has it cannot easily be read, charmed, frightened, commanded or fooled by anything that works on the mind. Those who try describe it as reaching for a door and finding smooth stone, or as shouting into fog. At its simplest it is a steady will that shrugs off a little nudging. At its strongest, even the most skilled mind-reader finds nothing at all, not even a mood.

Spies prize it, and so do the servants of any court where such powers are practised. It is quiet, defensive and invisible, which suits the kind of people who seek it. It does have a cost: some bearers find the wall hard to lower, even for friends.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** resist mental intrusion
- **Minor → major:** a steady will → an unreadable mind
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The intrusion gets through. Spectacular failure: the shield cracks in a way that lets the intruder see exactly what was being hidden.

## Not to be confused with

*[Nondetection](nondetection.md)*, which hides the whole person from being found or scried. Mind Shield hides only the thoughts, and the bearer is still plain to see.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a practised inner discipline; the eyes go flat and calm.
- **Divine:** a saint's medal worn close, and a short prayer against temptation.
- **Technology:** a lining of fine mesh in the hat, or an implant that jams incoming signals.
