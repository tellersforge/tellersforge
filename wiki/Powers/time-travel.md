# Time Travel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

To step outside the flow of years and walk into the past or the future. At its most modest it slips a few hours backwards or forwards. At its greatest it reaches the dawn of the world or its last evening.

The past resists being changed, and the future doesn't wait to be understood. Travellers find themselves strangers everywhere, speaking outdated tongues and carrying useless coins. Some traditions say the past can't be altered at all; others that every change opens another branch; others that the whole weave will snap if pushed too hard. Nobody who travels far comes home quite the same, and some don't come home at all.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/INT/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** travel into the past or future
- **Minor → major:** hours → all of history
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The traveller stays in their own time. Spectacular failure: they arrive somewhere, or somewhen, that they did not intend.

## Not to be confused with

*[Plane Shift](plane-shift.md)*, which reaches other realities; Time Travel stays in this world and moves through its years.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a circle chalked with star signs and a long incantation, and a rushing of years like wind.
- **Technology:** a machine of dials and coils that tears open the moment and steps through.
- **Occult:** a bargain with something that lives outside time, paid for with a memory.
