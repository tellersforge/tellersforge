# Detect Intent

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A face smiles and something underneath it growls. Detect intent lets the bearer sense what a person truly wants: hostility, corruption, kindness, fear. At first it is a vague unease or a warm feeling. At its deepest, it reads the soul itself and knows what kind of creature stands before them.

Paladins, judges, and temple doorkeepers value it. It sees purpose, not facts: it warns of an assassin but not of his poison. The bearer must also face uncomfortable truths about friends, and sometimes about themselves.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** sense hostility, corruption or goodness
- **Minor → major:** vague unease → read a soul
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No impression, or a vague and useless one. Spectacular failure: the bearer reads the exact opposite of the truth.

## Not to be confused with

*[Truth-Sensing](truth-sensing.md)*, which only tells whether words are lies; detect intent reads what a person means to do.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a warmth or chill from the holy symbol when the heart is good or wicked.
- **Psionic:** a taste of the other person's feelings, sweet, sour or bitter.

- **Close to:** [Truth-Sensing](truth-sensing.md)
