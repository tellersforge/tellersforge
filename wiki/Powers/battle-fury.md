# Battle Fury

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The red haze. The one who has it stops thinking, stops fearing and, for a while, stops feeling. Wounds that would drop a man are shrugged off, weapons are swung harder than any sane arm would dare, and the sound that comes out of the throat is barely human. Some fall into it on their own; others can wake it in companions, or in whole warbands before a charge.

The fury does not tell friend from foe very well, and it does not end gently. When it drains away, the pain arrives all at once, along with exhaustion and, often, horror at what was done. Old warriors who carry the gift are quiet people. They have to be.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **COU**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** enter or induce a rage that ignores pain
- **Minor → major:** aggression → a near-invulnerable frenzy
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The anger comes but not the numbness; pain still bites. Spectacular failure: the rage takes hold and will not choose sides.

## Not to be confused with

*[Monstrous Form](monstrous-form.md)*, which changes the body itself; and *[Power Surge](power-surge.md)*, which boosts strength or a power for a moment. Battle Fury changes the mind, and the body follows.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a beast spirit rides the warrior; the eyes go animal and the teeth are bared.
- **Mutation:** a gland floods the blood; veins stand out and the skin flushes dark.
- **Technology:** a combat injector hisses at the neck and the pupils blow wide.
