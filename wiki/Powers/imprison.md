# Imprison

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A lamp, a bottle, a stone, a mirror or a whole mountain: anything can become a prison. The imprisoner seals a being away, in an object, a place or a pocket of nowhere, so that it can do no more harm. The beginner holds a creature for a few breaths. The master seals it for eternity, and the seal waits for some fool to break it.

Imprisoning is how the ancient world dealt with the things it could not kill. The earth is full of sealed jars, buried coffins and locked towers. A good many adventures begin when someone opens one.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/COU/WIS/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** seal a being away
- **Minor → major:** hold briefly → sealed for eternity
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the being slips free. Spectacular failure: the prison seals the wrong occupant, possibly its maker.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bind](bind.md)*, which restrains a being where it stands; and *[Banish](banish.md)*, which sends it home. Imprison seals it away.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a seal on a lamp or bottle, stamped with the being's name.
- **Occult:** a cursed object bound in iron chains and written sigils.
- **Technology:** a containment cell or a data-prison for a digital being.
