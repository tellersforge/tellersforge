# Kindle & Quench

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The smallest fire-magic, and the most used. A snap of the fingers and a candle lights; a breath and it goes out. Damp kindling catches on a wet evening, a pipe glows without a taper, a hearth that has died in the night springs back to life. The reverse is just as handy: a spark on the thatch smothered, a lamp put out across the room to plunge it into darkness.

It never makes a great fire, only starts or stops small ones. In a cold world it is priceless, and many village wise folk, hearth-keepers and soldiers on campaign have it. Thieves like the quench more than the kindle.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **WIS** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** light or put out small fires
- **Minor → major:** a candle → a hearth
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A wisp of smoke and nothing more. Spectacular failure: the fire starts in the wrong place, often on the caster's own sleeve.

## Not to be confused with

*[Fire Control](fire-control.md)*, which shapes and steers fires already burning; Kindle & Quench only starts and ends small ones.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a snap of the fingers or a single spoken syllable.
- **Spirit:** a word of friendship to the hearth spirit, who wakes or sleeps as asked.
- **Psionic:** a flick of attention; the wick glows and catches.
