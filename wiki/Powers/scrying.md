# Scrying

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A still bowl of water, a black mirror, a crystal, a drift of smoke, or in later ages a flickering screen. The scryer gazes into it and sees something far away: a person, a place, an event. At first there is only a blurred glimpse. With mastery, the scryer can watch what is happening now, clearly and for as long as they can hold the gaze.

It is the classic gift of witches, court sorcerers and wise women. It needs a medium, and it is known to work both ways: some scryers have looked into the water and seen someone looking back.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** see distant things through a medium: water, mirror, crystal, smoke, screens
- **Minor → major:** a blurred glimpse → watch live
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The medium shows only the scryer's reflection. Spectacular failure: something on the other side notices, and looks back.

## Not to be confused with

*[Far Seeing (Clairvoyance)](far-seeing-clairvoyance.md)*, which sees a far place with no medium at all; scrying needs water, mirror, crystal or smoke.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a polished crystal or black mirror, and a spell that stirs mist inside it.
- **Occult:** a bowl of blood or ink, and a whispered bargain with what watches through it.
- **Folk magic:** a basin of well water under the moon, or smoke from a hearth fire.

## Relations

- **Close to:** Far Seeing
