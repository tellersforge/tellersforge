# Natural Weapons

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The body arms itself. Nails harden and curve into hooks, teeth lengthen, bone pushes out through the knuckles as blades, and the skin of the back grows ridged plates like a beast's hide. It is never pretty and rarely quiet: there is a crack of bone, a sharp breath, and then a person who no longer needs a sword.

Those born to it learn early to hide their hands. Those who gained it later find they cannot always choose when it happens, and a fright or a rage can bring the claws out at a dinner table. In a fight, though, there is no weapon to drop, no blade to lose, and nothing for a guard to confiscate at the gate.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/STR · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** grow claws, fangs, spikes or plates
- **Minor → major:** sharp nails → bone blades and a carapace
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** a prickle under the skin and nothing more. Spectacular failure: the weapons come out wrong, half-formed or turned inward, and hurt their owner.

## Not to be confused with

*[Armour Skin](armour-skin.md)*, which hardens the whole body to turn blows; Natural Weapons grows edges and points meant to deal them.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** bone and horn that grow from the body itself, sore for days afterwards.
- **Technology:** retractable blades and plates under synthetic skin, sliding out with a click.
- **Vampiric:** fangs and long black nails that come with hunger, and go when it is fed.
