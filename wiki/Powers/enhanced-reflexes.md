# Enhanced Reflexes

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The body reacts before the mind has finished noticing. A cup knocked off the table is caught before it falls far. An arrow is swayed away from, a blade slipped past, a thrown knife snatched out of the air. Others see a stillness broken by a flash of movement.

Small gifts give a quick draw and quick hands. At their greatest, the bearer steps aside from gunfire. It is the gift of master duellists, of certain monks, of soldiers wired with machinery to react faster than thought. Those who have it often find sleep hard, because every creak in the dark sets them moving.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** react and dodge unnaturally well
- **Minor → major:** a quick draw → dodge bullets
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The reaction comes a heartbeat too late. Spectacular failure: the bearer flinches the wrong way, straight into the blow.

## Not to be confused with

*[Reflex Precognition](reflex-precognition.md)*, which glimpses the danger a moment ahead; Enhanced Reflexes simply reacts to it faster than anyone else.

## Costumes

- **Technology:** a wired nervous system with accelerated nerves, and a faint click when it engages.
- **Inner energy:** a disciplined emptiness of mind that lets the body move before thought.
- **Mutation:** inhuman nerves, often paired with slit pupils or an unblinking stare.
