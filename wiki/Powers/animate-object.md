# Animate Object

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A broom sweeps by itself, a chair walks across a room, a suit of armour rises from its stand and draws its sword. Objects given false life obey their maker without question and without thought. They don't tire, don't eat and don't argue.

A small working makes a helpful household tool. A great one marches an army of stone statues out of a temple. Animated things are only as clever as their instructions, and many tales turn on the apprentice who knew how to start them and not how to stop. They fall still again when the maker lets them go, or when broken badly enough.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** bring objects to life to serve
- **Minor → major:** a dancing broom → an army of statues
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The object twitches and lies still. Spectacular failure: it comes to life but obeys no one.

## Not to be confused with

*[Life Bestowal](life-bestowal.md)*, which creates true living or thinking beings; an animated object only moves and obeys, with no mind of its own.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a rune painted on the object and a command spoken to it.
- **Occult:** a spirit bound into the object against its will, grinding and creaking.
- **Technology:** remote servos or nanite swarms that make ordinary things move.
