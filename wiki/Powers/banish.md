# Banish

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Get out. The word carries weight, and the being hears it. Demon, ghost, fairy or stray god, whatever does not belong is sent back where it came from. At first the banisher only drives it away for a while. At full strength, the exile is permanent and the door closed behind it.

Banishing is combat of a kind, will against will, and the being has every reason to resist. Those who do it for a living carry the marks: burns, grey hair, a limp, stories they do not tell. Those who do it badly often find the thing came back, and brought company.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/CHA/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** send a being back where it came from, or into exile
- **Minor → major:** push it away → exile forever
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the being stays. Spectacular failure: it stays and grows stronger, or turns on the banisher.

## Not to be confused with

*[Exorcism](exorcism.md)*, which drives spirits out of a person or a place; Banish sends a being back to its own world.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a raised holy symbol and a word of command in the god's name.
- **Arcane:** a written seal and a formula that names the being's home.
- **Technology:** a field that forces extradimensional matter out of phase with this world.
