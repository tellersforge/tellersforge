# Beast Command

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The voice that animals obey. A snarling dog sits and wags its tail, a runaway horse stops dead, a pack of wolves falls in behind the caller like soldiers. The user may summon creatures from the hills, soothe a frightened herd or send a bear to clear a road. Animals feel the command as a certainty rather than a word: this is what is going to happen.

It is an ancient gift, feared as much as admired. Shepherds and beast-tamers use it gently. Warlords use it to send hounds at an enemy's throat, and some hungry nobles of the night keep whole packs of wolves bound to their will. The animals remain themselves, though: they obey while the will holds, and some remember how they were treated.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** summon, calm or command animals
- **Minor → major:** calm a dog → lead a wolf pack
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The animals ignore the call, or wander off confused. Spectacular failure: they take the command as a threat and turn on the caller.

## Not to be confused with

*[Beast Speech](beast-speech.md)*, which only talks with animals; Beast Command orders them. *[Swarm Control](swarm-control.md)* rules insects and vermin as one mass.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a call in the old tongue of beasts, answered by the spirit of the animal's kind.
- **Psionic:** a firm pressure of will against a simpler mind.
- **Vampiric:** a stare and a thought from a creature of the night; wolves and bats gather at its feet.
- **Mutation:** a scent or voice born into the user's blood that animals simply follow.
