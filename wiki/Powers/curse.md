# Curse

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A declared harm, spoken aloud, that follows its target wherever they go. The curser names the victim and the doom: may your fields burn, may your sons die before you, may no roof keep out the rain. And then it happens, slowly or all at once.

A small curse is a nuisance: bad weather on every journey, spoiled food at every feast. A great curse hangs on a family for generations and becomes part of its story. Curses can often be lifted, by an apology, a quest, a stronger hand or the death of the one who spoke it, which is why cursers do not live peacefully. Many traditions hold that every curse carries a price for the one who lays it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **COU**/CHA/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** a declared harm that follows its target
- **Minor → major:** a nuisance → generational doom
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The words are spoken and nothing follows. Spectacular failure: the doom lands on the curser, or on their own household.

## Not to be confused with

*[Minor Hex](minor-hex.md)*, which is small and spiteful; a Curse is declared, can kill, and can follow a family down the years.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a name written backwards on lead and nailed into a grave.
- **Folk magic:** a widow's curse screamed at a landlord's door, with a handful of earth thrown after.
- **Divine:** a priest's anathema from the steps of the temple, and the god's judgement falling after.
