# Threshold Guarding

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A home is a boundary, and boundaries have always had power. Salt on the sill, an iron nail above the door, a sprig of rowan, a blessing said at dusk: this is the old knowledge of keeping out what has not been asked in. At its humblest it keeps mice from the pantry and bad luck from the hearth. At its strongest nothing with ill intent can cross, not a thief, a hungry spirit or a walking corpse.

It is the magic of grandmothers and village priests. It asks nothing grand, only care, habit and a door that is kept. It fails the moment the owner invites the wrong visitor in.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** seal a home so evil can't enter uninvited
- **Minor → major:** keep out pests → seal against any intruder
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the charm is set but something small slips through. Spectacular failure: the threshold lets in exactly what it was meant to keep out, and seals the household in with it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Ward](ward.md)*, which can guard any place with alarms and seals; Threshold Guarding protects only a dwelling, and only against the uninvited.

## Costumes

- **Folk magic:** salt, iron and rowan at the door, a charm said at every lock.
- **Divine:** a house blessing with holy water, a sign chalked above the lintel.
