# Temporary Enchantment

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Magic lent to an ordinary thing for a short while. A sword blazes with blue light, a shield hums with a protective charge, a cloak turns the rain. The effect is real but borrowed: when it fades, the item is plain again, sometimes a little scorched.

It is the everyday work of war-mages and temple priests before a battle. At its smallest, a blade glows enough to read by. At its greatest, it becomes a weapon of fire or holiness, feared by creatures that shrug off ordinary steel. Blacksmiths grumble that it spoils honest work, but they rarely turn down the extra trade.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** imbue an item briefly
- **Minor → major:** a glowing blade → a weapon of fire or holiness
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The item stays plain. Spectacular failure: the enchantment takes badly, and the item cracks, burns or turns against its bearer.

## Not to be confused with

*[Weapon Empowerment](weapon-empowerment.md)*, which strengthens weapons only; Temporary Enchantment lends any item a brief power.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** runes traced along the blade with a fingertip, glowing and fading as the power wanes.
- **Divine:** a blessing spoken over the steel; it shines with a clean white light.
- **Inner energy:** inner strength channelled through the grip until the weapon hums with the wielder's breath.
- **Technology:** a power cell clipped to the hilt, charging the edge with a crackling field.
