# Blessing

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A hand on the head, a word over the threshold, a cup raised to a journey. Blessing is the gentle art of wishing someone well and having the wish take. The blessed walk a little more lightly: rain holds off until they reach the inn, the horse doesn't go lame, the nervous suitor finds the right words. At its greatest, a blessing sits on a family for a generation, and the house prospers while its neighbours struggle.

Priests give it at weddings, grandmothers at doorways, wandering holy folk in return for bread. It is freely asked and, in most places, freely given, though old stories warn that a blessing withdrawn from a household that has offended can feel very like a curse.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** grant favour, protection or success
- **Minor → major:** a good day → a family's prosperity
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The words are said and nothing follows. Spectacular failure: the blessing sours, and the good luck falls on the wrong person entirely.

## Not to be confused with

*[Luck-Granting](luck-granting.md)*, which tilts chance itself; Blessing is favour and protection laid on a person or a house, broader and slower.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a priest's raised hand and the name of a god, felt as warmth on the brow.
- **Folk magic:** salt on the doorstep, bread broken over a cradle, and an old woman's words said three times.
