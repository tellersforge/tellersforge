# Fate-Reading

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The fate-reader looks at a newborn, or a stranger at the crossroads, and sees the thread of their life stretching ahead. At first there is only a hint: a great voyage, an early grave, a crown. At its fullest, the whole span of a life lies open, from birth to death.

It is a heavy gift. Mothers beg to hear good news and kings order prophets killed for bad. The wise say that fate shows the road, not every step on it, and that those who know their fate often walk straight into it trying to avoid it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** know a person's destined path
- **Minor → major:** a hint → the full span of a life
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The thread is tangled and unreadable. Spectacular failure: the reader sees another person's fate and gives it to the wrong one.

## Not to be confused with

*[Fate-Weaving](fate-weaving.md)*, which changes the threads of destiny; fate-reading only reads them.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** the fate-goddesses show the thread to their priest.
- **Spirit:** the spirits of the ancestors whisper what is written for the child.
