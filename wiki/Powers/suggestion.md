# Suggestion

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The gentlest way to steer a mind. An idea is slipped in so softly that the target never notices it arrive. They simply find they have decided to take the left road, or to trust the stranger, or that the guard post doesn't really need watching tonight. It feels like their own thought, and they will defend it as their own.

The light form is a nudge: a small wish, easily set aside. The strong form rewrites a belief that the target will hold for years. It cannot make anyone do something they find unthinkable, but most people's line is further off than they would like to admit.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** plant an idea that seems the target's own
- **Minor → major:** a nudge → a rewritten belief
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The idea slides off. Spectacular failure: the target senses the tampering and holds hard to the opposite view.

## Not to be confused with

*[Charm](charm.md)*, which makes someone warm toward the caster; and *[Mind Control](mind-control.md)*, which takes the wheel outright. Suggestion leaves the target sure the idea was theirs.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a word planted in ordinary talk, with a fingertip tap on the table.
- **Psionic:** a whisper placed in the mind in the target's own inner voice.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Charm](charm.md), [Mind Control](mind-control.md)
