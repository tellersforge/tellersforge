# Dream Prophecy

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The dreamer lies down in a holy place and wakes with an answer. Dream prophecy brings knowledge in sleep: at first a cryptic dream that must be puzzled over, at its clearest a plain message spoken by a god or ancestor. It often needs a special place, a sacred cave or temple, and a ritual of fasting, washing and sleeping on the skin of an offering.

It is an old practice, and pilgrims have always come to sleep in the right temples. Dreams are slippery. What seemed plain at night may be hard to remember by breakfast.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** receive answers in sleep, often at a holy site
- **Minor → major:** a cryptic dream → a clear message
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A restless night and ordinary dreams. Spectacular failure: a false dream, confidently believed.

## Not to be confused with

*[Dream Walking](dream-walking.md)*, which enters the dreams of others; dream prophecy receives answers in one's own sleep.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a night in the temple of a healing or oracular god, and the god speaks.
- **Spirit:** ancestors or animal spirits visit the dreamer and show the way.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Dream Walking](dream-walking.md), Precognition
