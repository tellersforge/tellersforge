# Machine Genius

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A mind that looks at a broken engine and simply sees it whole. Where others read manuals, the machine genius lays a hand on the casing and knows where the fault is, how the thing was built and three ways it could have been built better. Tools become extensions of their hands; spare parts become opportunities.

At the height of the gift, the genius works beyond their time. A blacksmith's apprentice builds a clockwork arm, a stranded pilot makes a radio from wire and a tin cup, a scavenger coaxes a working reactor out of a scrap heap. Such wonders are often temperamental and rarely work for anyone else, which is one reason geniuses are hard to replace.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** understand and fix any machine at once; build beyond your era
- **Minor → major:** a quick fix → wonders from junk
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The fix doesn't hold, or the device refuses to start. Spectacular failure: the contraption works brilliantly, just long enough to fail in spectacular and dangerous fashion.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mechanics](../Skills/mechanics.md)*, the skill, which repairs machines through training and effort; Machine Genius understands them at a glance and builds beyond its era.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** a born tinkerer whose fingers find loose screws in the dark; toys took apart easily from the cradle.
- **Technology:** a diagnostic implant that overlays schematics on everything the eye sees.
