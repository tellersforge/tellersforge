# Renewal at Death

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Death is not the end. When the body dies, it rebuilds itself, often with a new face, a new voice, a new temper, but the same mind underneath. Some rise from the ashes of their pyre, some wake in a new body elsewhere, some are found as children who remember everything. At first it may work only once. At its fullest, it is an endless cycle of lives.

Each renewal changes something. Friends may not recognise the one who comes back, and enemies may not either, which can be useful. Those who have lived many times grow patient and distant, playing very long games.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **END**/END/WIS/COU/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** rebuild the whole body at death, often with a new face
- **Minor → major:** survive once → a cycle of lives
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Death takes hold as it should. Spectacular failure: the renewal goes wrong, and something else comes back.

## Not to be confused with

*[Cheat Death](cheat-death.md)*, which survives a single death by luck. Renewal at Death rebuilds the whole body after death.

- **Mutation:** a chrysalis grown from the dying body, splitting open days later.
- **Heroic legend:** a hero's legend: they always return when their people need them.
