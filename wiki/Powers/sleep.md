# Sleep

*Set: Core · Basic power*

*aka: ______________________________*

Eyelids grow heavy, the fire seems warmer, and the voice of whoever is speaking becomes very soft and very far away. This power makes others sleep. It is the gentle enemy of every sentry, and the reason sensible guards stand their watches in pairs. The lightest form leaves a yawning, clumsy target. The deepest, in old tales, holds a whole court asleep behind a wall of thorns while generations pass outside.

It is a kind power, as powers go: nobody is hurt, and most wake refreshed. It fails against the frightened and the furious, whose blood runs too hot for dreaming, and a sleeper can always be shaken awake.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** put others to sleep
- **Minor → major:** drowsiness → sleep for a century
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **Version I, as in Basic:** Point at one person or beast in sight, up to 10 metres away. It rolls the d20 against its Courage. Courage or lower: nothing happens. Higher: it falls into a deep sleep for about ten minutes. Taking damage, a shake or a shout wakes it. It cannot be Dodged or Parried. It has no effect on constructs or anything else that does not sleep.
- **What failure looks like:** A yawn, no more. Spectacular failure: the caster nods off instead, or everyone sleeps but the one who mattered.

## Not to be confused with

*[Stasis](stasis.md)*, which freezes a body in place and time; a sleeper still breathes, dreams, and can be woken.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a pinch of fine sand blown from the palm and a slow counting word.
- **Spirit:** a small night spirit sent to sit on the target's chest and hum.
- **Psionic:** a quiet push that slows the target's thoughts to a crawl.
- **Song:** a lullaby, low and repeating, that the target can't stop hearing.
