# Spirit Servants

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Invisible hands that sweep the floor, fold the laundry, carry the tray up the stairs. The spirit servant is a small, simple being called into service, or a thing made of will that obeys and then fades. In a rich house nobody sees them, and only the dust and cobwebs that never appear give them away. At their greatest they are a host of unseen soldiers that can fight a battle.

They are obedient but not clever. Give an unclear order and the result is a flooded kitchen or a door opened to the enemy. Masters learn to be very precise.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** create or command minor spirits or unseen servants
- **Minor → major:** tidy a room → fight a battle
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** nothing answers the call. Spectacular failure: the servants do exactly what was asked, which is not what was meant.

## Not to be confused with

*[Summon Creature](summon-creature.md)*, which calls one real creature to serve; Spirit Servants are many small, unseen helpers.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a ring snapped with the fingers, and a floating broom gets to work.
- **Spirit:** household spirits fed with a saucer of milk and a kind word.
- **Technology:** tiny drones and household robots coordinated by a single command.
