# Flight

*Set: Core · Basic power*

*aka: ______________________________*

True flight, free as a bird and sometimes faster. The flier leaves the ground, turns, climbs, stoops and glides, going wherever the will points. At its least it is a wobbling hover above the heads of a crowd. At its most it is a streak across the sky that outruns the sound of its own passing, leaving a thunderclap behind.

Few powers change a life so completely. Walls, rivers and mountains stop mattering, and the whole world becomes a map seen from above. Fliers are envied and watched with suspicion, and every archer, gunner and air-defence crew in the land learns to track them. Cold, wind and exhaustion are the real limits, and a tired flier over the sea has few places to rest.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END/AGI · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** sustained free flight
- **Minor → major:** hover → faster than sound
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **Version I, as in Basic:** You rise and fly at about running pace for about five minutes. Land before it ends: if it ends while you are in the air, you fall, and the Game Master decides the damage. You carry your own gear, but not another person. You can still cast other powers while you fly.
- **What failure looks like:** The flier cannot get off the ground, or lurches and sinks. Spectacular failure: control is lost at height, in a tumble that will end on the ground.

## Not to be confused with

*[Levitation](levitation.md)*, which only rises and sinks; Flight moves in every direction and at speed.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a flowing mantle that lifts the wearer, spell-woven and crackling at the edges.
- **Mutation:** great feathered, leathery or insect wings unfolding from the back.
- **Technology:** a jet pack, rotor harness or gravity belt, loud and bright in the sky.
- **Psionic:** the mind pushes the body through the air; the flier's eyes glow faintly with effort.
- **Divine:** carried aloft by an unseen grace, a soft rushing sound like many wings.
