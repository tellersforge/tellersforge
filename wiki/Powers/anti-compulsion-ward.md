# Anti-Compulsion Ward

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A lock on the will. The seductive voice slides off, the commanding stare meets a wall, and the whisper that tells the guard to open the gate finds nobody listening. This ward protects a person or a place against charm, compulsion and outright domination. At its weakest it makes a gentle push easier to ignore. At its strongest not even a master of minds can take hold.

Rulers commission it for their councils; spymasters wear it; temples seal their inner halls with it. Its great weakness is that honest persuasion still works, and it cannot tell the difference between a friend's good advice and a clever lie.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/COU/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** protect a person or place against compulsion and mind control
- **Minor → major:** shrug off a nudge → proof against domination
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the compulsion gets through. Spectacular failure: the ward shatters and the victim is left more open to control than before.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mind Shield](mind-shield.md)*, which guards against all mental intrusion; and *[Ward](ward.md)*, which guards a place against intruders of every kind.
