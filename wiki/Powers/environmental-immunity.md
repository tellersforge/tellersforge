# Environmental Immunity

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The world's harshest places lose their teeth. Blistering heat and bitter cold both go unfelt. Crushing ocean depths leave the body whole, and the airless void between worlds does no harm. At its lightest, a storm is only mildly uncomfortable. At its strongest, the bearer can stand in vacuum unhurt.

Explorers, sailors and the priests of harsh places value it most. It keeps the body alive, not comfortable, and it does nothing about thirst or hunger. The bearer also has to remember that the companions beside them are not so lucky.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** survive heat, cold, pressure, vacuum
- **Minor → major:** comfortable in a storm → unharmed in vacuum
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The heat or cold bites as it would for anyone. Spectacular failure: the protection flickers and fails mid-exposure.

## Not to be confused with

*[Air Supply](air-supply.md)*, which provides breath; and *[Resist Element](resist-element.md)*, which guards against one danger. Environmental Immunity keeps the body whole in any conditions.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a shimmering skin of air cast close around the body.
- **Divine:** a blessing that the world itself cannot harm the faithful.
- **Mutation:** hardened skin, strange lungs and blood that neither boils nor freezes.
- **Technology:** a sealed suit, or implants that regulate the body from within.
