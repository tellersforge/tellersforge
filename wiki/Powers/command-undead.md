# Command Undead

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Every undead thing hears the command. The skeleton halts, the ghoul kneels, the wraith turns aside. This power takes control of undead that already walk, whoever raised them and whatever they want. Beginners stop one shambling corpse. Masters bend even a vampire lord to their will.

It is a strange and ambiguous gift. Priests use it to hold the dead at bay, and necromancers to steal each other's servants. The undead resent being ruled, and the most powerful of them remember who did it, and wait.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/CHA/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** control existing undead
- **Minor → major:** halt one → command a vampire lord
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the undead ignore the command. Spectacular failure: they turn on the commander.

## Not to be confused with

*[Turn Undead](turn-undead.md)*, which drives the dead away or destroys them; Command Undead makes them obey.

## Costumes

- **Occult:** a word in a dead language and a sign made with bone.
- **Divine:** a god of death's authority, carried by a priest in black.
