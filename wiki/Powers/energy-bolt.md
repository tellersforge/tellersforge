# Energy Bolt

*Set: Core · Basic power*

*aka: ______________________________*

A flash from an outstretched hand and something across the room is suddenly on fire, or on the floor. The energy bolt is the plainest of attacks: no flame, no frost, no lightning, just raw force thrown in a crackling line. The small version stings like a slapped cheek. The great one tears through walls and leaves a smoking trench behind it.

It is the first offensive gift many learn and the last one they abandon. It is quick, it needs no tools, and it works on nearly anything. Soldiers who face it learn to fear the raised hand more than the drawn sword.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **COU**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** fire a blast of raw energy
- **Minor → major:** a sting → a building-levelling beam
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **Version I, as in Basic:** A glowing pulse of force the size of a fist hits one target in sight, up to 20 metres away, like a hard thrown stone. 1d6+1 damage. The target may Dodge. A shield-holder may Parry instead (half damage).
- **What failure looks like:** The bolt sputters out or flies wide. Spectacular failure: it bursts in the caster's own hand.

## Not to be confused with

*[Charged Blast](charged-blast.md)*, which gathers power slowly for one enormous release; the energy bolt is quick and can be thrown again and again.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a snapped word and a pointed finger; a bright dart of force leaps from the fingertip.
- **Inner energy:** breath drawn deep and pushed out through the palm in a visible shimmer.
- **Technology:** a wrist projector or pistol-grip emitter that whines as it charges.
- **Mutation:** eyes or hands that glow before a searing beam bursts out.
- **Divine:** a prayer of wrath answered by a lance of light from above.
