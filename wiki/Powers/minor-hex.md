# Minor Hex

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The small, spiteful magic of village feuds. Milk that curdles in one house and nowhere else. A cart wheel that loses its pin every market day. A man who stubs his toe on every step he climbs, for a month. A minor hex is not deadly, and it isn't meant to be. It is meant to be felt, and to be understood.

Hexes are laid with a word, a knot or a pin, and lifted by apology, by payment, or by a cleverer hexer. At their worst they dog a person for life, never quite enough to kill but always enough to ruin a good day. In most places people are polite to the old woman at the end of the lane, and this is why.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** a small lasting misfortune
- **Minor → major:** a stubbed toe → a lifetime of mishaps
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing sticks, and the target never notices. Spectacular failure: the hex bounces, and the hexer spends the week tripping over things.

## Not to be confused with

*[Curse](curse.md)*, which is a declared doom that can kill or ruin a line; a Minor Hex only irritates, though for a very long time.

## Costumes

- **Folk magic:** a knot tied in a thread from the victim's coat, and a muttered rhyme.
- **Occult:** a scratched sign and a drop of the hexer's own blood on the victim's doorstep.
- **Spirit:** a small spirit set on someone's heels, to pinch and tangle and hide things.
