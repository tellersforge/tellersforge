# Steadying

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A quiet calm spreads outward from the one who works it, so softly that no one notices. The trembling hand grows still, the panicked breathing slows, the man about to run finds that he doesn't, after all. People near the steadier simply seem to keep their heads better than they should. They never know why.

It calms fear, but it cannot create courage. A steadied company holds its ground; it does not charge. Healers use it at the sickbed, officers use it on the line, and the best practitioners are never suspected at all, which is exactly how they prefer it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** quietly dampen panic in people nearby, without their knowing; calms fear but can't create courage
- **Minor → major:** steady one frightened person → keep a whole company from breaking
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The panic goes on as before. Spectacular failure: the calm lands too heavily, and people grow sluggish and careless just when they need to move.

## Not to be confused with

*[Emotion Control](emotion-control.md)*, which can push any mood openly; and *[Courage-Giving](courage-giving.md)*, which inspires. Steadying only dampens panic, and no one feels it happening.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a slow, silent spell held behind the teeth, the caster breathing deliberately for everyone nearby.
