# Hive Mind

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Many minds become one. Each member still has a body and eyes of their own, but thoughts flow between them like water between pools, and in time there is no telling where one ends and the next begins. At first it is only a shared sense: a sound heard by one is heard by all. At its fullest, a single mind lives in a hundred bodies and moves them as one creature.

It is astonishingly efficient. Such a group never argues, never misunderstands orders, never sleeps all at once. It is also frightening, both to outsiders and to any member who remembers being a single person. Some colonies of insects and some machine cultures work this way naturally.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/CHA/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** many minds become one network
- **Minor → major:** a shared sense → one mind in many bodies
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The link is patchy and the minds fall back to themselves. Spectacular failure: a single member's panic or pain floods the whole network.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mind Link](mind-link.md)*, which lets separate minds talk. Hive Mind merges many into one self.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a constant hum at the back of every member's thoughts.
- **Technology:** an implant network, each node glowing faintly as it shares.
