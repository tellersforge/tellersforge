# Cold Blast

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A bolt of pure winter. The caster points and a ray of white cold streaks across the room, frosting everything it touches. A chill touch numbs a hand; a stronger burst freezes a foe where they stand, encased in blue ice with only their eyes moving.

Cold is slower to kill than fire but harder to escape. Limbs stiffen, weapons fall from frozen fingers, and the floor turns treacherous around the target. The power works best in dry air, and it is at its most frightening in winter, when the cold it makes simply stays. Ice-bound foes can be chipped free, if someone gets there in time.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** a ray or burst of freezing cold
- **Minor → major:** chill touch → encase a foe in ice
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A breath of chill air and nothing more. Spectacular failure: the frost blooms back along the caster's arm.

## Not to be confused with

*[Frost](frost.md)*, which chills and freezes an area slowly; Cold Blast is a sudden ray or burst aimed at a target.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a pointed finger and a shard of blue light that crackles through the air.
- **Psionic:** a stare that drains the warmth from the target's body.
- **Technology:** a cryo-projector rifle with a frost-rimmed barrel.
