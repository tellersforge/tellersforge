# Darkness

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A pool of black that no lamp can pierce. The darkness spreads out from the caster like spilled ink, swallowing candlelight and moonlight alike. At its least it dims a room enough to slip by unseen; at its greatest, the sun seems to go out over a valley and people fall silent in fear.

It is not merely the absence of light but something heavier, cold and still, and animals hate it. Thieves, ambushers and those who serve darker powers make the most use of it. Those caught inside tend to panic long before anything else happens to them.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** create unnatural darkness
- **Minor → major:** dim a room → blot out the sun locally
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A thin gloom that barely hides anything. Spectacular failure: the dark falls on the caster alone, or on the wrong place entirely.

## Not to be confused with

*[Shadow Shaping](shadow-shaping.md)*, which moulds shadows into things; Darkness simply drowns an area in black.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** an inverted lantern sigil and a whispered word that snuffs every light.
- **Occult:** a borrowed piece of the void called up through a pact; the air grows cold and wrong.
- **Mutation:** ink-black mist that seeps from the skin when the bearer is afraid or angry.
