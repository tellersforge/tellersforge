# Charm

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A smile, a glance, a word, and the stranger becomes a friend. Charm makes a person friendly and trusting towards the bearer. At first it gives only goodwill and an open door. At its strongest, it becomes devotion: the charmed will fight, lie and risk much for the bearer's sake.

It is a dangerous gift. Those who find out that they have been charmed are rarely forgiving. It is also a lonely one, since the bearer can never be quite sure whether anyone likes them for themselves.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** make someone friendly and trusting
- **Minor → major:** goodwill → devotion
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No warmth; the person is unmoved. Spectacular failure: the charm is noticed, and the target becomes an enemy.

## Not to be confused with

*[Suggestion](suggestion.md)*, which plants a single idea or command; charm changes how a person feels about the bearer.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a whispered spell and a lingering look.
- **Spirit:** a fae charm like a sweet scent in the air.
- **Mutation:** a natural glamour, pheromones or a voice that soothes.
- **Vampiric:** the predator's locked gaze that leaves the victim willing.
