# Telekinetic Crush

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

An invisible hand that closes. The victim feels a pressure at the throat or the chest, then a squeeze that grows and grows. At its least it leaves bruises and a sense of being held. At its worst it dents a breastplate inward, cracks ribs beneath it, and chokes a strong man from across a hall while the one who does it does not even raise a hand.

Few powers are so feared, or so plainly cruel. It needs sight and concentration, and it does nothing subtle: everyone in the room sees someone clutching at nothing, face darkening. Its users are rarely trusted and often hunted, and many tell themselves they only use it to make a point.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/COU/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** squeeze or choke from range
- **Minor → major:** bruise → crush armour
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The grip slips and the target feels only a passing tightness. Spectacular failure: the squeeze turns inward, or catches the wrong person.

## Not to be confused with

*[Telekinesis](telekinesis.md)*, which moves and holds objects with care; Telekinetic Crush does nothing but squeeze.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a slowly closing fist and a cold stare; the air around the victim seems to thicken.
- **Occult:** a strangling grip lent by something dark, which enjoys the work a little too much.
