# Mind Link

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Thoughts pass between friends without words. With a mind link, a willing ally hears the bearer's thoughts and answers in kind: a warning, a plan, a joke no one else hears. At first it links two minds; at its greatest, a whole group shares one silent conversation.

It is prized by soldiers, thieves, spies and lovers. It requires trust. A link lets in more than words, and linked friends soon know each other's moods, fears and secrets better than they might like.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** share thoughts with willing allies
- **Minor → major:** two minds → a linked group
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No link forms, or only static. Spectacular failure: the link carries the wrong thoughts to the wrong friend.

## Not to be confused with

*[Telepathic Speech](telepathic-speech.md)*, which sends words to one mind; the mind link joins minds together.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a silent reach towards another mind, and a click as the link forms.
- **Technology:** neural implants or earpieces connecting a team's thoughts.
- **Arcane:** a shared drop of blood and a spoken bond that joins minds.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Telepathic Speech](telepathic-speech.md), [Mind Meld](mind-meld.md)
