# Long Teleport

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

To be home in an instant. The teleporter fixes a place firmly in mind, somewhere known and remembered, and is simply there: across town, across the sea, across the world. There is no journey. One moment the room of the inn, the next the courtyard of the family house a month's ride away.

The power depends on knowing the destination. A place seen once is a gamble; a place known well is safe; a place that has changed, a burned house or a flooded valley, may be a nasty surprise. Rulers pay fortunes for teleporters and fear them in equal measure, since walls mean little to someone who can arrive inside them.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** travel instantly to a known distant place
- **Minor → major:** across town → across the world
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The teleporter stays where they are, drained. Spectacular failure: they arrive somewhere similar but wrong, or far off course.

## Not to be confused with

*[Portal](portal.md)*, which opens a lasting doorway others can use; *[Short Teleport](short-teleport.md)*, which covers only a short distance in sight.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a long spell and a diagram chalked on the floor, the circle flaring white as the mage vanishes.
- **Divine:** carried home by grace; a column of light, then an empty space.
- **Occult:** a debt to something that knows every road, paid a little each time.
- **Technology:** a matter transmitter or beam station, with a hum, a shimmer and a coded destination.
