# Bound Item Recall

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The item always comes back. A thrown hammer returns to the hand, a dropped sword leaps up from the mud, a stolen amulet slips from a thief's purse and flies home. The bond between user and object is a quiet thing most of the time, but it is always there, like a thread pulled tight.

It is a warrior's gift, and a traveller's. No bonded weapon can be lost for long, and disarming such a fighter is only a delay. At its strongest, the item comes through doors, walls and locked chests, leaving holes behind. Thieves learn to fear it, and to check what they steal.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **WIS** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** a bonded item returns to your hand
- **Minor → major:** returns when called → through walls
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The item doesn't move, or falls short. Spectacular failure: it comes back hard and fast, and the user fumbles the catch painfully.

## Not to be confused with

*[Focus Bonding](focus-bonding.md)*, which bonds with an object to strengthen powers; Bound Item Recall only brings the object back to the hand.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a rune carved into the item's hilt and the user's palm, drawn together like magnets.
- **Heroic legend:** a hero's weapon that answers no other hand and always flies home.
- **Technology:** a magnetic tether built into the handle and a gauntlet that calls it back.
