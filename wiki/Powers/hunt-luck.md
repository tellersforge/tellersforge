# Hunt-Luck

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Luck that comes when a hunter calls. Tracks show clearer, the wind turns just in time, the deer steps out of the thicket into an open shot. At its greatest, herds drift toward the hunter's camp as if called, and the season's hunger is over in a day. The reverse is possible too, and feared: a curse that sends game fleeing from a rival's snares for a whole season.

It belongs to hunting peoples of every age, who understand it as a bargain with the animals themselves. The gift is honoured with thanks, fair killing and a portion left for the spirits. Those who take too much, or boast, often find the luck turning against them.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/LUCK · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** call game to hunters, or drive it away
- **Minor → major:** better tracking → herds come to you
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The hunt goes as hunts usually do, which is to say badly. Spectacular failure: the game vanishes from the region, and the spirits take offence.

## Not to be confused with

*[Beast Command](beast-command.md)*, which summons and orders animals directly; Hunt-Luck only tilts chance, bringing game within reach or driving it away.
