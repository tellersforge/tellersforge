# Void Drawing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

There is a place, or a not-place, beneath all things, where there is truly nothing: no light, no heat, no time, no name. Void Drawing reaches into it and pulls a little of that nothing out. It gathers like frost on the skin, drinks warmth from the room and sound from the air. Poured into a vessel, it makes a cold that eats. Poured into a person, it makes a hollow that never fills.

Its practitioners are few and unwelcome. They grow thin and quiet, and lamps gutter as they pass. The greatest of them forge vessels that drain the world around them, blade or crown or jar, and leave dead ground behind. Every tradition that knows of this art forbids it, and every such tradition keeps a copy of the forbidding text.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **COU**/INT/WIS/END/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** draw the substance of the Void, real emptiness, into a vessel or a person (dark)
- **Minor → major:** a chill of nothing → a vessel that drains the world
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Nothing comes, only a passing chill. Spectacular failure: the emptiness comes through freely and starts on the one who called it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Soul Craft](soul-craft.md)*, which shapes the stuff of souls; Void Drawing brings in true emptiness, and everything it touches is made less.
