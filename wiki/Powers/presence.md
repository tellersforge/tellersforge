# Presence

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Some people fill a room simply by entering it. Those with this power fill a hall, a square, a battlefield. Conversation falters, heads turn, and the bravest in the crowd find themselves standing a little straighter or looking at the floor. Nothing is said and nothing is asked. The feeling arrives on its own: here is someone greater than you.

Kings have been crowned on the strength of it, and saints followed barefoot through the snow. It is a blunt gift. It does not make anyone like the bearer, only feel small beside them, and people who have been made to feel small have long memories. Wise bearers use it sparingly, and never on the same crowd too often.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** radiate awe or majesty
- **Minor → major:** striking → crowds kneel
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Heads turn, then turn away again; the moment passes unremarked. Spectacular failure: the crowd senses the effort and laughs, and the bearer looks smaller than before.

## Not to be confused with

*[Charm](charm.md)*, which makes one person friendly and willing; Presence makes everyone feel lesser, liked or not. *[Fear](fear.md)* drives people off, where Presence holds them in place.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a light that seems to fall on the bearer alone, as if heaven had picked them out of the crowd.
- **Spirit:** the ancestors stand at the bearer's back, and everyone present feels them watching.
- **Vampiric:** a cold, old stillness; the eyes hold the crowd the way a snake holds a bird.
