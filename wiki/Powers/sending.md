# Sending

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A message delivered anywhere at all. The sender forms the words, and they travel: by dream, by spirit messenger, by a bird that seems to know the way, or by a small bright light that flies faster than any horse. The simplest form carries only a short image. The greatest opens two-way talk across seas, mountains and even worlds.

It is used rarely and carefully. The sender must know the one who receives it, and sending too often leaves the sender drained. Kings, high priests and distant lovers use it when nothing else will do, and many messages end the same way: come home.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** deliver a message anywhere, by dream, spirit or envoy
- **Minor → major:** a short image → two-way talk across worlds
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The message never arrives. Spectacular failure: it reaches the wrong person, or arrives as a nightmare.

## Not to be confused with

*[Message](message.md)*, which is short, spoken and limited by distance; and *[Dream Walking](dream-walking.md)*, which enters the dream itself. Sending crosses any distance.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a sealed letter burned in a brazier, the words rising with the smoke.
- **Divine:** an angel or saint appearing briefly to speak the words aloud.
- **Spirit:** a spirit bird sent from the shaman's hand.
