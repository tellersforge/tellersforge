# Telepathy

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The power to hear what others are thinking. Not their secrets, not their memories, but the surface: the words forming just before they speak, the worry chewing at them, the flicker of recognition they hoped to hide. At the lightest touch it is only a mood, a colour. With practice, a telepath can follow a train of thought as easily as a conversation.

It is a noisy gift in a crowd, and many who have it seek out empty places. It is also a lonely one. Friends grow careful around a telepath, and lovers rarely stay. People who know about the power quickly learn to think about the weather.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** read surface thoughts
- **Minor → major:** a mood → clear thoughts
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Only noise, or a mood too faint to read. Spectacular failure: the target feels the intrusion and knows exactly who is listening.

## Not to be confused with

*[Deep Mind Probe](deep-mind-probe.md)*, which digs into hidden memory; and *[Mind Link](mind-link.md)*, which joins willing minds to talk. Telepathy only reads what is on the surface.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** a quiet tilt of the head, as if listening to a far-off tune.
- **Arcane:** a spell of hearing cast on the caster's own ear, with fingers at the temple.
- **Technology:** a headset tuned to the faint electric chatter of a nearby brain.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Deep Mind Probe](deep-mind-probe.md), [Mind Link](mind-link.md)
