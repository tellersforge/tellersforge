# Turning Blades

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Old soldiers swear by it and most will not say who taught them. The weapon that should have struck home glances aside, snaps, catches in a cloak, or simply fails to cut, as if the steel had lost interest. At first it is one lucky arrow. At its strongest no edge or point will pass the skin, and armies have been known to break when their best shots all go wide.

The gift is usually tied to a charm, a prayer, a relic sewn into a shirt, or the blessing of a mother. It rarely protects against clubs, falls or fire, which only confirms, say its users, that it knows what it is for.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** make weapons miss, break or fail to cut
- **Minor → major:** turn one arrow → no weapon can touch you
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the blade still bites. Spectacular failure: the protection is exhausted or broken and will not return soon.

## Not to be confused with

*[Deflect & Reflect](deflect-and-reflect.md)*, which actively turns attacks aside with skill; Turning Blades works through charm and grace without any movement.

## Costumes

- **Folk magic:** a knotted cord, a stitched charm or a coin carried in the boot.
- **Divine:** a saint's medal or a psalm sung before battle.
