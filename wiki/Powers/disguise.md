# Disguise

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A face that isn't one's own. With this power the user takes on another appearance: a change of hair or eyes at first, then a different age, build or voice, and at the height of the gift a perfect double of someone else, down to the scars. It is only the look of things. The mind behind the face is the same, and the user must still act the part.

Spies, assassins, runaways and confidence tricksters prize it. So do bored nobles who wish to walk the markets unseen. Its danger is the slip: an old friend's question, a forgotten habit, a mirror that shows the truth.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** appear as another person
- **Minor → major:** change your hair → a perfect double
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The change is poor and fools no one who looks twice. Spectacular failure: it fades in company, halfway through the lie.

## Not to be confused with

*[Glamour](glamour.md)*, which can make anything seem other than it is; Disguise changes only the user's own appearance, into another person.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a mask of woven light laid over the face with a murmured word.
- **Psionic:** watchers' minds persuaded to see a stranger where the user stands.
- **Technology:** a holographic shroud or a mask of smart material that reshapes itself.
- **Mutation:** flesh that slides and settles into new features, painfully and slowly.
