# Body Control

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Mastery of the body's quiet machinery. The heart slows at will, breath stills, digestion halts, blood pressure drops. The practitioner can sweat or stop sweating, speed up or slow down their body's work, and in extremes neutralise poison by stopping it spreading. The simplest use slows the heart to calm a panic; the deepest makes a living body look dead to any doctor.

It takes years of practice in stillness. Hermits, monks, contemplatives and certain spies learn it. Its danger is its own perfection: a body told to stop too thoroughly may forget to start again.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** control your heartbeat, metabolism and body chemistry
- **Minor → major:** slow the heart → feign death, neutralise poison
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The heart keeps its own beat. Spectacular failure: the body obeys too well, and the practitioner collapses.
