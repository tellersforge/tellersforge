# Endurance Without Need

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The body stops asking. Hunger goes quiet, thirst fades, sleep becomes a choice, and at the far edge even breath stops being necessary. At first the gift only makes a skipped meal unnoticeable. At its greatest, the bearer can wait in a sealed tomb or a flooded passage for as long as it takes.

Holy hermits have lived on it for years on pillars and in caves. Soldiers and explorers with it outlast everyone else. There is something lonely about it: food and sleep are how most people share their days, and one who needs neither drifts slowly out of step with them.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** go without food, water, sleep or air
- **Minor → major:** skip a meal → no bodily needs at all
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The hunger and weariness arrive as usual. Spectacular failure: all the skipped needs come due at once.

## Not to be confused with

*[Body Control](body-control.md)*, which directs the body's workings. Endurance Without Need removes the need for food, water, sleep or air.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a fast blessed by a higher power, the body sustained by faith.
- **Inner energy:** breath and discipline replacing bread and water.
- **Mutation:** a body that feeds on sunlight or stores months of fat.
- **Technology:** implants and nutrient packs that keep the body running.
