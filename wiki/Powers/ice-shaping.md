# Ice Shaping

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Ice raised and carved at will. A frozen slick spreads across a floor, then thickens, then rises into a waist-high wall, a stair, a narrow bridge over a torrent. The shaper can make blades and spears of ice, or pillars that hold up a collapsing roof. At its greatest, a whole fortress rises glittering from a frozen lake, with towers and gates that last as long as the cold does.

Ice is strong but brittle, and it melts. In summer the power is a fleeting thing; in the deep north, it builds towns. Those who shape it carefully learn the grain of ice as a carpenter learns wood.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** build walls, bridges and weapons of ice
- **Minor → major:** a slick → a glacier fortress
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The ice forms thin and cracks at once. Spectacular failure: it grows where it should not, freezing a friend in place or sealing the only door.

## Not to be confused with

*[Earth Shaping](earth-shaping.md)*, which moves soil and stone; Ice Shaping builds with ice and does not last outside the cold.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a rising hand, and blue-white ice climbs out of the ground after it.
- **Spirit:** the frost spirits carve at the shaper's request, leaving delicate patterns.
