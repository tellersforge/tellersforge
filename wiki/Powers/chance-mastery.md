# Chance-Mastery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The gambler's gift. Knucklebones, lots, cards, spinning wheels and drawn straws all seem to go the way this person needs. Some of it is feel, some sleight, and some something stranger. Onlookers call it luck. Opponents call it cheating and watch the hands very carefully, but they never find anything.

A little of it gives an edge over the course of an evening. A great deal of it means never losing at all, and those who have it learn quickly to lose on purpose, now and then, to stay welcome at the table and alive in the alley afterwards. Trickster tales are full of heroes who wagered with gods and walked away with the prize.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/LUCK · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** master games of chance, dice and lots
- **Minor → major:** an edge → never lose
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The throw goes like any other throw. Spectacular failure: the loss is huge, and the table is suddenly sure it was cheated.
