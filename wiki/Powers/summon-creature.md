# Summon Creature

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A call goes out and something answers. A wolf trots out of the trees; a hawk drops from the sky; a great bear rises from the river bank. The summoned beast serves for a while, then goes back where it came from. Beginners call small, useful creatures: messengers, guards and scouts. Masters bring war-beasts, giant serpents and the kind of thing that ends a siege.

The creature is real and has its own nature. It may not like its task. It certainly doesn't like being hurt. A careless summoner who pushes too hard may find the beast turns round and remembers who called it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** call a creature to serve
- **Minor → major:** a small helper → a great beast
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** nothing comes. Spectacular failure: something comes, but it is hungry and angry.

## Not to be confused with

*[Summon Otherworldly Being](summon-otherworldly-being.md)*, which calls spirits, demons or angels from another plane; Summon Creature calls a living beast.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a circle and a spoken name, and the creature steps from a flash of light.
- **Spirit:** a drum and a howling song to the animal spirits.
- **Divine:** a prayer answered by a sacred beast of the god.
- **Technology:** a signal to a biological or robotic creature waiting in storage.
