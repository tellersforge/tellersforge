# Elemental Aura

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The fighter's fists catch fire. A sword rimes over with frost, or crackles with blue sparks along its edge. The elemental aura wraps a body or a weapon in an element and lets it burn, freeze or shock whatever it touches. At its weakest it is a tingle on the skin and a smell of ozone; at its strongest it is a field that kills anyone who comes near.

It is a gift for the close fight. Warriors who carry it are terrible up close, and wise foes keep their distance. Clothes, hair and scabbards tend to suffer, so bearers often go about singed, damp or faintly frosted.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** wreathe your body or weapon in fire, frost or lightning
- **Minor → major:** a tingle → a lethal field
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A flicker around the hands that fades at once. Spectacular failure: the element turns inward, burning, freezing or shocking the bearer.

## Not to be confused with

*[Elemental Body](elemental-body.md)*, which turns the whole body into the element; the aura only wraps its surface or a held weapon.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** runes traced along a blade that light up with the chosen element.
- **Technology:** heating coils, cryo vents or charged plates built into armour or weapon.
- **Mutation:** skin that bursts into flame or frost when the bearer's temper rises.
