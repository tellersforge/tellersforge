# Blinding Flash

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A sudden white burst, and every eye in the room is full of spots. The blinding flash is light turned against sight: a dazzle that makes guards stagger, archers miss and pursuers lose their quarry. A short use buys a heartbeat of confusion. A stronger one leaves its victims groping in blindness for a long while afterwards.

It is a favourite of the careful and the outnumbered, because it wins escapes rather than fights. The trick is to close one's own eyes in time, and the caster's friends learn quickly to look away when a hand is raised.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** dazzle or blind
- **Minor → major:** a moment's dazzle → lasting blindness
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** A flicker that makes people blink and nothing more. Spectacular failure: the caster's own eyes take the full glare.

## Not to be confused with

*[Blind & Deafen](blind-and-deafen.md)*, which strikes the senses directly and can take hearing as well; the blinding flash works only through a burst of light.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a thrown pinch of powder and a sharp word, answered by a white flare.
- **Divine:** a holy symbol raised, and a burst of sacred radiance too bright to bear.
- **Technology:** a flash grenade or a strobe built into a helmet or visor.
