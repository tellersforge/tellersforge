# Commune

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The priest kneels, the shaman drums, the sorcerer speaks the words at midnight, and something vast answers. Commune asks questions of a god, a spirit or the cosmos itself. At first the answer is a simple yes or no; at its fullest, an extended revelation that may last all night.

It is a great and dangerous honour. The powers do not like to be asked trivial questions, and their answers are often in their own terms rather than human ones. Those who commune too often grow strange, distant and a little holy.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** ask questions of a god, spirit or the cosmos
- **Minor → major:** a yes/no → extended revelation
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No answer comes. Spectacular failure: something answers that was not asked.

## Not to be confused with

*[Spirit Speech](spirit-speech.md)*, which talks with the spirits close at hand; commune calls on great powers far above.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a long prayer answered by a god's voice, or silence.
- **Spirit:** a shaman's trance journey to the great spirits.
- **Occult:** a forbidden summoning, and an answer from something vast and hungry.
