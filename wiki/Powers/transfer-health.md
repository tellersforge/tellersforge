# Transfer Health

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Life poured from one vessel into another. A healthy person holds hands with a dying one, and colour drains from one face as it returns to the other. It can be given freely, or taken by force. The gentlest form moves a little strength; the strongest makes a full swap, the dying rising and the healthy falling in their place.

It is simple and brutal. It creates nothing: every bit of life given is taken from somewhere else. Parents use it to save children, lovers to save each other, and the wicked to steal the youth of others.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** move life force between two creatures
- **Minor → major:** a little → a full swap
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** No strength moves. Spectacular failure: it flows the wrong way.

## Not to be confused with

*[Life Drain](life-drain.md)*, which only takes; and *[Pain Transfer](pain-transfer.md)*, which moves wounds rather than vitality. Transfer Health moves life force itself.

## Costumes

- **Folk magic:** clasped hands, a red thread tied around both wrists and a whispered verse.
- **Technology:** a machine of tubes and pumps linking the two bodies.
