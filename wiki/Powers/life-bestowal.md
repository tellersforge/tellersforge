# Life Bestowal

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The greatest and most dangerous of making arts: to create a being that lives and thinks. A clay man, a homunculus in a jar, a thinking machine, an entirely new kind of creature. The work is long and the results are unpredictable, and every tradition has its stories of creations that turned on their makers.

It starts small, with a hand-high thing that squeaks and fetches. At its greatest, it brings a new people into the world. What the created owe their creator, and what their creator owes them, is a question that has started wars.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/INT/CHA/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** create living or thinking beings: golems, homunculi, new species
- **Minor → major:** a homunculus → a new species
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The creature never quickens. Spectacular failure: it lives, and hates its maker.

## Not to be confused with

*[Animate Object](animate-object.md)*, which makes objects move and obey; Life Bestowal creates beings with minds of their own.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a clay body inscribed with a secret name that wakes it.
- **Occult:** a homunculus grown in a sealed jar from forbidden ingredients.
- **Technology:** an artificial mind in an engineered body, or a species grown in a lab.
- **Divine:** a god breathes life into a figure shaped from river mud.
