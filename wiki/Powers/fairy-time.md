# Fairy Time

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A place where time runs differently. An hour at the dance under the hill, and a century passes outside. A night in the enchanted wood, and the travellers come home to find their village grey and their names forgotten. Or the reverse: years spent in one room while outside a single evening goes by.

This power makes such a place, or makes an existing place behave so. It is a subtle and dangerous gift, and the old tales are full of its victims: young men who return to find their sweethearts in the churchyard, and prisoners who emerge unaged into a world that has moved on.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/CHA/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** make time pass differently in a place
- **Minor → major:** an hour lost → centuries in a night
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Time runs as normal. Spectacular failure: the drift catches the caster and their friends, and nobody knows for how long.

## Not to be confused with

*[Pocket Realm](pocket-realm.md)*, which makes a hidden world; Fairy Time changes how fast time passes in a place, whether hidden or not.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a ring of mushrooms or a hollow hill where the fae folk dance, and the music never ends.
- **Arcane:** a warded chamber in which the sorcerer studies a year while the world outside waits a night.
