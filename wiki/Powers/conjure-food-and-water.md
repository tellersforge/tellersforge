# Conjure Food & Water

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Nourishment from nothing. Bread appears on an empty plate, water fills a dry jug, a bowl of stew steams where there was only a stone. It is plain food, as a rule: filling rather than fine, the kind a traveller is glad of at the end of the road.

A small working feeds one hungry person. A great one feeds a besieged city or a crowd on a hillside. Temples use it in famine years, and are loved for it. Armies that carry it travel light and fast. The food is real, and gone once eaten, and few who have lived on it for a long while want to taste it again.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** create nourishment
- **Minor → major:** a meal → a feast for thousands
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The plate stays empty. Spectacular failure: the food appears but is spoiled, or it vanishes once in the stomach.

## Not to be confused with

*[Abundance](abundance.md)*, which makes a supply that never runs out; Conjure Food & Water makes a meal, and then it is gone.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a prayer over empty baskets that come up full.
- **Technology:** a synthesiser that assembles meals from base nutrients.
- **Spirit:** a spirit's gift of fish in the net and water from the rock.
