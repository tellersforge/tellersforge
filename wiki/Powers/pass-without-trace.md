# Pass Without Trace

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The wild covers for its own. Grass springs back after the user's feet, branches part and close again, rain arrives to wash away a trail. Birds keep singing as the traveller passes, and hounds lose the scent at the edge of a stream. Hunters from the town find nothing and turn back, muttering.

At its greatest, nature does more than hide the user: brambles close across a path behind them, a swarm of bees rises against the pursuit, fallen trees block a road. It asks respect in return. Those who break branches carelessly or leave fires burning find the forest less forgiving next time.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/AGI · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** nature hides and aids you
- **Minor → major:** leave no trace → nature defends you
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The trail stays, as clear as any other. Spectacular failure: the land takes offence and marks the user's path for every hunter to follow.

## Not to be confused with

*[Camouflage](../Skills/camouflage.md)*, which blends the body into its surroundings; Pass Without Trace hides the path and turns nature itself into an ally.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a quiet word with the spirits of the place, and a small offering left at the roots of a tree.
- **Divine:** the gift of a wild god who shelters travellers; the grass bows as the pilgrim passes.
