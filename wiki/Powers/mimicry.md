# Mimicry

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Face, voice, walk, the little cough before speaking: all copied until even a mother would be fooled. The mimic studies the original, sometimes touches them, and then simply becomes a second one. At its weakest the likeness is only good from a distance or in poor light. At its best it is perfect, down to the scar on the thumb.

It is the favourite power of spies, assassins and impostors, and it makes whole courts paranoid. Guards learn to ask questions only the true person would know. Mimics learn to answer them. The copy is of the body only: memories, skills and secrets stay with the original.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **END**/CHA/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** copy another's exact look and voice
- **Minor → major:** a resemblance → a perfect double
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** a passing likeness that wouldn't fool a stranger. Spectacular failure: the copy slips at the worst moment, a voice changing mid-sentence or a face sliding back.

## Not to be confused with

*[Disguise](../Skills/disguise.md)*, the skill, which uses makeup and props; and *[Power Mimicry](power-mimicry.md)*, which copies another's powers, not their looks.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** a changeling's soft face that shapes itself to what it watches.
- **Arcane:** a stolen hair or nail burnt in a candle while the caster speaks the victim's name.
