# Poison & Disease Immunity

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Poison doesn't take. The cup is drained, the adder bites, the plague spreads through the city, and this one person walks through it untouched. At first the gift only holds off a single dose, a single infection. At its greatest, no venom and no fever can touch the body at all.

It is a lonely immunity. Those who have it watch others sicken around them and can do nothing, and suspicion follows: why did they survive? Courtiers who fear poison value it enormously. So do plague doctors, who are the ones most often suspected of spreading what they themselves never catch.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **END** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** resist poison, venom and plague
- **Minor → major:** one dose → immune to all
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The poison takes hold as it would for anyone. Spectacular failure: the body overreacts, and the sickness hits harder than it should.

## Not to be confused with

*[Cure](cure.md)*, which removes poison or disease once it has taken hold. Poison & Disease Immunity stops it taking hold at all.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a saint's protection, sometimes shown by a small scar or birthmark.
- **Mutation:** strange blood that breaks down venom like a snake's own.
- **Technology:** filters in the blood, or a regular course of engineered antidotes.
