# Bane

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The opposite of a blessing, aimed at an enemy on the field. The foe's grip slips, his footing goes, his arrows fly wide and his courage drains away at the worst moment. It isn't a lasting curse. It is a weight hung on someone's shoulders for as long as the fight lasts, and it tends to be spoken loudly, where everyone can hear.

War priests call it down before a charge. Duelling wizards use it to take the edge off a rival. Its greatest workings mark a foe as doomed for the day, so that everything he does goes wrong, and his own men start to edge away from him.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** hinder enemies' actions
- **Minor → major:** a slight hindrance → doomed to fail
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The enemy shrugs it off and fights on as before. Spectacular failure: the hindrance lands on the caster's own side.

## Not to be confused with

*[Jinx](jinx.md)*, which spoils one action at one moment; Bane hangs on the foe for the whole fight, and blunts everything they try.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a war-priest's curse called down from the front of the line, heavy as a hand on the neck.
- **Arcane:** a sharp word and a pointed finger; a grey haze clings to the foe's limbs.

## Relations

- **Close to:** [Jinx](jinx.md), [Blessing](blessing.md)
