# Talisman Making

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Luck and protection tied into a small, humble object. A knot of red thread, a stone with a hole in it, a scrap of parchment folded around a pinch of salt. The maker works slowly, with prayers or songs, and the finished charm is given to a child, a soldier or a traveller setting out.

The small ones ward off bad luck, fevers and the evil eye. The great ones are relics, passed down through families and fought over by armies. Every village has someone who makes them, and every sensible household keeps one above the door.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** make protective or empowering charms
- **Minor → major:** a lucky charm → a mighty relic
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The charm is only an object, and does nothing. Spectacular failure: it draws the very thing it was meant to keep away.

## Not to be confused with

*[Rune & Sigil Magic](rune-and-sigil-magic.md)*, which inscribes signs of power on any surface; Talisman Making creates small charms to be carried.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a medallion blessed at the altar, pressed into a pilgrim's hand.
- **Folk magic:** a bundle of herbs, thread and hair knotted by a grandmother while she hums.
