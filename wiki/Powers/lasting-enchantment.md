# Lasting Enchantment

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Magic that never fades. The enchanter works for weeks or years, weaving power into a sword, a ring, a cloak or a door, until it holds forever. The finished piece carries its gift for generations: a blade that never dulls, a lamp that never goes out, a cloak that hides its wearer from every eye.

It is the rarest of crafts and the most prized. A small enchanted trinket can buy a farm; a legendary artefact can decide a war. Most great enchanters are old, obsessive and short of friends, and many leave a piece of themselves in their greatest works, which is why some of those works seem to have opinions.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/DEX/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** craft permanent magic items
- **Minor → major:** a trinket → a legendary artefact
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The power won't take, and the materials are wasted. Spectacular failure: the enchantment takes wrongly, creating a cursed item that clings to its owner.

## Not to be confused with

*[Rune & Sigil Magic](rune-and-sigil-magic.md)*, which inscribes signs that hold power; Lasting Enchantment creates a whole magical item.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** years of patient spellwork in a tower workshop, the power woven into metal thread by thread.
- **Divine:** a relic forged in a temple and blessed by the faith, glowing with holy light.
- **Occult:** an item quenched in blood and sealed with a pact; it hungers.
