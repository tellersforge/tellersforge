# Familiar

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A black cat on the windowsill, a raven on the shoulder, a toad in the pocket. The familiar is bonded to its master in a way that goes deeper than training: it shares their senses at need, runs their errands, and understands far more than an animal should. A beginner's familiar is a spy and a messenger. A master's can think, choose and act alone.

The bond is precious and fragile. Hurting the familiar hurts the master, and losing one is a grief that can take years to heal. Witch-hunters have always known to kill the cat first.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** a bonded animal or spirit companion
- **Minor → major:** a spy → an agent that acts alone
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the familiar does not respond, or misunderstands. Spectacular failure: it bolts, sulks or fights its master for days.

## Not to be confused with

*[Beast Bonding](beast-bonding.md)*, which makes friends of ordinary animals; a Familiar is a single companion, magically joined.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a creature bound in a long ritual, with a drop of the wizard's blood.
- **Spirit:** an animal spirit who chose the shaman, and not the other way round.
- **Technology:** a drone or robotic pet linked by a wireless neural bond.
