# Imaginary Constructs

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Imagination made solid. The caster pictures a thing, a sword, a staircase, a wall, a horse, a castle, and it appears, bright and a little strange, bearing the marks of the maker's mind. A child's wooden sword comes out with painted stars. A frightened man's wall has spikes on it.

Novices make crude, wobbling shapes that fade when their concentration slips. Masters build detailed scenes: rooms full of furniture, crowds of figures, forests of crystal trees. Everything lasts as long as the maker keeps believing in it, and the moment attention slips, it goes.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** imagination becomes solid
- **Minor → major:** a crude shape → a detailed scene
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The shape flickers and fails. Spectacular failure: it forms from the caster's fears rather than their plans.

## Not to be confused with

*[Force Constructs](force-constructs.md)*, which makes plain barriers of force; Imaginary Constructs take any shape the mind can picture.

## Costumes

- **Psionic:** the mind's eye projects the image, and it hardens into matter that shines at the edges.
- **Arcane:** a dreamer's spell that makes a painting walk out of its frame.
