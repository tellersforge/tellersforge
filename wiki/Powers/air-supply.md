# Air Supply

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A breath where there should be none. The one who has this gift can draw clean air in a room full of smoke, a mine thick with bad damp, or the choking fumes of a marsh. At first it is a single long breath, enough to cross a burning hall. Given more strength, a bubble of fresh air forms around the head, or around a whole party, and holds even at the bottom of a lake or in the airless dark between the stars.

It is a lifesaver more than a weapon. Miners, firefighters and divers would give much for it, and a sealed tomb or a poisoned well is far less frightening with one of these along. The bubble is fragile: a hard blow or a moment's lost concentration can burst it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **END** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **What it does:** breathe in bad air, make air bubbles
- **Minor → major:** one breath → a party survives vacuum
- **Cost:** 2 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The air is thin and stale, enough for a gasp but not for long. Spectacular failure: the bubble bursts in the worst possible place.

## Not to be confused with

*[Water Breathing](water-breathing.md)*, which lets the body breathe water itself; Air Supply brings air with you, anywhere.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a shimmering globe of air conjured around the head, faintly glowing at the edges.
- **Divine:** the breath of life granted by a blessing, sweet and cool however foul the surroundings.
- **Technology:** a compact breather or field generator that clips to a collar and hums.
