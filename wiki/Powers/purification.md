# Purification

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Water, smoke, light, song: every faith has its way of washing the world clean. Purification lifts pollution from people and places, drives off lingering spirits, breaks lesser curses, and returns the land to health. At its simplest it is a blessing wash for a sick child or a newly built house. At its greatest it cleanses a whole haunted valley, and the birds return.

The rite takes care and time, and it always costs the purifier something: weariness, fasting or a burden they carry away with them. It is gentle and thorough rather than fierce, and does its best work before trouble becomes a fight.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** cleanse people and places of pollution, spirits or curses
- **Minor → major:** a blessing wash → a haunted region cleansed
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** the stain lingers. Spectacular failure: the pollution clings to the purifier instead.

## Not to be confused with

*[Counter-Curse](counter-curse.md)*, which breaks one spell or curse outright; and *[Exorcism](exorcism.md)*, which drives a particular spirit out of a person or place.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** holy water, incense and prayers sung until the air feels light again.
- **Spirit:** smoke of sacred herbs and a shaman's chant to the spirits of the place.
