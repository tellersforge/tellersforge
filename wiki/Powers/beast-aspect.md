# Beast Aspect

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

One trait of an animal, borrowed. The bearer takes a wolf's nose, a hawk's eyes, a cat's night sight, a bear's strength, or a lizard's grip on stone. The rest of the body stays as it was. The gentlest version gives only a sharpened sense; the strongest gives full animal power in one part of the body.

It is practical and flexible. Hunters, scouts and warriors choose their aspect by need. It is also visible: a bearer with a bear's arms or a cat's eyes rarely passes unnoticed.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** take on one animal trait
- **Minor → major:** a keen nose → a bear's strength
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The trait doesn't come. Spectacular failure: the wrong trait comes, or the animal's instincts along with it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Animal Form](animal-form.md)*, which turns the whole body into an animal. Beast Aspect borrows only one trait.

## Costumes

- **Spirit:** a totem animal lends its gift through a feather, claw or tooth.
- **Mutation:** an inborn animal trait that the bearer can bring forth at will.
- **Technology:** a cybernetic sense or limb modelled on an animal's.
