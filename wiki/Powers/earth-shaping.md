# Earth Shaping

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Soil and stone heave at a word. A mound rises in the field, a ditch opens along a line drawn by the heel, a boulder rolls aside from a cave mouth. At its greatest the ground splits into a chasm wide enough to swallow a wagon, or rises into a rampart before an advancing army.

It is a builder's power as much as a warrior's. Farmers terrace hillsides with it, sappers undermine walls, and hermits raise stone huts in an afternoon. The earth is slow and heavy, and moving much of it is exhausting, but what is moved stays moved. Those who have it often feel the land as a slow, deep hum under their feet.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/STR/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** move soil and stone
- **Minor → major:** a mound → open a chasm
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The ground shivers and settles. Spectacular failure: it moves wrong, opening beneath the caster or collapsing a friend's footing.

## Not to be confused with

*[Shape Material](shape-material.md)*, which reshapes stone, wood or metal finely by touch; and *[Earthquake](earthquake.md)*, which shakes the ground. Earth Shaping moves soil and stone in bulk.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a spoken command and a stamping heel, and the earth rolls like a wave.
- **Spirit:** the land spirits asked for help, the soil moving as if something vast shifts beneath it.
- **Inner energy:** a martial stance, rooted and heavy, and the ground rises with the lifted palms.
