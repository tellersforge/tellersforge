# Shape Material

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Stone turns soft as clay under the hand, wood flows like wax, and metal can be pressed and pulled into new forms. The shaper runs a palm along a cracked lintel and it is whole and smooth. Given time and skill, a shaper sculpts a statue, carves a doorway where there was none, or raises a slender bridge across a gorge.

It is patient, careful work, closer to a craft than a weapon. The material must be touched, and the shaper must know what they want, because the power does not supply taste or skill. The best shapers are masons and carpenters first. The worst leave behind lopsided doors and chairs that fall apart when someone sits down.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/DEX/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** reshape stone, wood or metal by touch
- **Minor → major:** smooth a crack → sculpt a bridge
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The material stiffens back before the work is done, leaving a half-formed lump. Spectacular failure: it shapes into something dangerous, or sets hard around the shaper's hand.

## Not to be confused with

*[Earth Shaping](earth-shaping.md)*, which moves loose soil and stone on a large scale; and *[Transmutation](transmutation.md)*, which changes what a thing is made of. Shape Material changes only its form.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a craftsman's spell; the fingers sink into stone as though it were bread dough.
- **Spirit:** asking the stone or tree to change, and waiting patiently for it to agree.
- **Divine:** a builder-saint's blessing on the work, the material flowing warm and willing.
