# Awaken Object

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Giving a thing a mind. A sword begins to whisper advice, a door refuses to open for strangers, a ship's figurehead turns to watch the horizon. The awakened object thinks, remembers and wants things, although what it wants may not be what its maker had in mind.

At its smallest the gift produces a voice in a blade, a companion that grumbles about the weather. At its greatest it creates a sentient artefact with its own will and history, loyal, clever and sometimes bitterly resentful. Such objects outlive their makers, and the stories they tell about them are rarely kind.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/INT/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **What it does:** give an item a mind and will
- **Minor → major:** a whispering blade → a sentient artefact
- **Cost:** 6 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The object stays silent and inert. Spectacular failure: it wakes, and immediately decides it dislikes its maker.

## Not to be confused with

*[Life Bestowal](life-bestowal.md)*, which makes an object into a living creature; Awaken Object gives it a mind without a living body.

## Costumes

- **Arcane:** a spark of the mage's own mind breathed into the object; its eyes open, if it has any.
- **Occult:** a bound spirit sealed inside, whispering at night.
- **Technology:** an artificial mind installed in a weapon or tool, speaking through a small speaker.
