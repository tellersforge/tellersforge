# Super Speed

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Movement so fast that the world seems to stand still. The speedster crosses a room between one blink and the next, snatches a knife from an attacker's hand, reads a book in the time it takes to lift a cup. To everyone else, there is a gust of wind and a blur.

A little of it is enough to outrun a horse, or a car. At its greatest, the bearer is gone before anyone knows they were there. It costs: speedsters eat enormously, wear out shoes in days, and find ordinary conversation painfully slow. Friction burns, and a stumble at full pace can be fatal.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **What it does:** move and act at extreme speed
- **Minor → major:** outrun a car → a blur
- **Cost:** 3 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** Only an ordinary sprint. Spectacular failure: a fall at full pace, or a crash into a wall the bearer didn't see in time.

## Not to be confused with

*[Haste & Slow](haste-and-slow.md)*, which alters the pace of time for a body; Super Speed is the body itself moving faster, with no magic on time at all.

## Costumes

- **Mutation:** legs and nerves that burn with inborn speed, leaving scorch marks on the floor.
- **Technology:** a stimulant-boosted nervous system or powered boots.
- **Inner energy:** a monk's discipline, the breath held and the body moving like a thrown stone.
