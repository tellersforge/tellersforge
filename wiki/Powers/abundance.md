# Abundance

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The jar of oil that never empties, the purse that is always full, the pot that goes on feeding whoever sits down. Abundance doesn't create a meal once. It makes a supply that simply doesn't run out.

It is a gift of saints and legendary heroes, and a common thread in tales of hospitality rewarded: the poor widow who shares her last bread, and whose bin is full every morning after. A small working feeds a family through a winter. A great one feeds a city. Those who hold such a treasure must guard it, because the whole world will want it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **What it does:** make a supply that never runs out
- **Minor → major:** feed a family → feed thousands
- **Cost:** 4 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The supply runs out as normal. Spectacular failure: it overflows and won't stop, and the kitchen fills with porridge.

## Not to be confused with

*[Conjure Food & Water](conjure-food-and-water.md)*, which makes a single meal; Abundance makes a store that never empties.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a saint's blessing on the widow's jar, filled again every dawn.
- **Heroic legend:** a hero's cauldron from which no one goes hungry, however many sit at the table.
