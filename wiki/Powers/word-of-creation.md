# Word of Creation

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Speaking a thing and having it be. The speaker names a cup, and there it stands on the table; names a bridge, and it spans the river; names a mountain, and the earth heaves up to meet the word. The things created are real and lasting, and they carry some trace of the voice that called them.

This is the gift of creator-gods and the eldest legends, the power that, in many old tales, made the world itself. Mortals who glimpse it use it sparingly and in whispers, because every word spoken this way costs something, and a careless phrase cannot be taken back.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/INT/INT/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **What it does:** bring things into being by speech
- **Minor → major:** small objects → worlds
- **Cost:** 8 Energy (version I)
- **What failure looks like:** The words fall into silence, and nothing comes. Spectacular failure: something comes, but not what was named, and it has its own ideas.

## Not to be confused with

*[Conjure Object](conjure-object.md)*, which summons an item for a short time; Word of Creation makes things truly and permanently real. *[Command by True Name](command-by-true-name.md)* rules what already exists.

## Costumes

- **Divine:** a sacred word in the tongue of the gods, the air ringing like a struck bell.
- **Heroic legend:** the first word, spoken over the void, and the world takes shape.
