# Matches

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A small box of wooden sticks tipped with chemicals that flare when struck against a rough strip. They made fire easy for everyone in the industrial cities, and they fit in any pocket. They are wonderful when dry and nearly useless when damp, and a gust of wind snuffs them before they catch.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Fire-Making dry conditions
- **Penalty:** −1 Fire-Making wet or windy
- **Used for:** [Fire-Making](../Skills/fire-making.md)
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 1 C
- **Also known as:** friction matches, lucifers

## Not to be confused with

*[Storm matches](storm-matches.md)*, coated to burn through wind and rain.
