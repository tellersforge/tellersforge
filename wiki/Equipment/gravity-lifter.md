# Gravity lifter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Fields and far-future devices · Needs: far future · far future*

A handheld disc that clamps onto a heavy load and cancels most of its weight, so that a crate of stone or an engine block can be lifted by one person. Dockworkers and builders use them every day. When the charge runs out, the weight comes back all at once, which has ended a few careers and many toes.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Lifting & Carrying heavy loads; +1 Cargo Handling moving awkward loads
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Cargo Handling](../Skills/cargo-handling.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 20 S
- **Also known as:** lift disc

## Not to be confused with

the *[Anti-Grav Sled](anti-grav-sled.md)*, which carries a whole load on a floating pallet rather than lightening it by hand.
