# Luminous paint

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: electricity · modern*

Paint that soaks up light by day and glows faintly through the night. Workers mark stair edges, exits, tool handles and trail blazes with it, and soldiers use a dab to find their gear in the dark. It is not bright enough to read by, but it shows the way.
