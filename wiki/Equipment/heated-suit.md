# Heated suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: computing · modern*

Clothing with thin heating wires woven into the lining, fed from a battery pack at the hip or a cable plugged into a vehicle. Motorcyclists, pilots and workers in freezing stores wear it, and so do explorers who can carry the batteries. While the power flows, the wearer stays warm in cold that would numb anyone else. When the battery dies, the thin suit gives less warmth than an ordinary coat.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 END against cold while charged
- **Penalty:** −1 END if the battery fails in cold
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** battery-heated jacket, electric suit

## Not to be confused with

*[Thermal underwear](thermal-underwear.md)*, which traps body heat without power; the heated suit makes its own warmth, but only while charged.
