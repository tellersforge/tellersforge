# Ice axe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: iron · mountain folk*

A short axe with a pointed pick on one side of the head, a flat adze on the other and a spike at the foot of the shaft. The climber cuts steps in ice with it, drives it in as an anchor, and, above all, throws their weight onto it to stop a slide down a snow slope before it becomes a fall. Mountaineers learn to self-arrest before anything else.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing on ice; +1 Mountaineering arresting a slide
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Mountaineering](../Skills/mountaineering.md), [Snow & Ice Travel](../Skills/snow-and-ice-travel.md)
- **Weight:** 0.8 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** ice pick

## Not to be confused with

*[Camp Axe](camp-axe.md)*, which splits wood; the ice axe is for ice, snow and saving the climber from sliding off the mountain.
