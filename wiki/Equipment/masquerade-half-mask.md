# Masquerade half-mask

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: printing · western kingdoms*

An elegant mask of silk, velvet or lacquered leather that covers the eyes and upper face, tied with ribbons or held on a stick. At a masked ball it lets courtiers whisper and plot behind a pretence of anonymity. It hides enough to deny, not enough to deceive a close friend. Outside a masked event it draws suspicious looks.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +1 Intrigue & Politics at a masked ball; +1 Disguise hiding identity at a ball
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA outside a masked event
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** domino mask, ball mask

## Not to be confused with

*[Masquerade mask](masquerade-mask.md)*, a fuller, more ornate carnival mask that hides the whole face and invites mischief.
