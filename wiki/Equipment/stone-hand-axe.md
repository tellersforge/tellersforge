# Stone hand axe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: stone tools · any*

A lump of flint or hard stone, knapped on both faces into a teardrop shape with a sharp edge all round. It fits the palm and does everything: chops branches, butchers carcasses, scrapes hides and cracks bones for marrow. It is the oldest tool that people have made, and for a very long time it was the only one they needed.
