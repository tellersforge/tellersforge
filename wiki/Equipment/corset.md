# Corset

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: printing · western courts*

A bodice stiffened with whalebone, reed or steel strips and laced tight at the back or front, shaping the waist, lifting the chest and straightening the spine into the fashion of the day. At balls and at court it is what elegance looks like. It also makes deep breathing difficult and bending impossible, and nobody has ever turned a cartwheel in one.
