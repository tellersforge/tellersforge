# Tactical light

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: computing · modern*

A small, very bright light in a tough metal body, made to be carried on a belt or fixed to a weapon. It can strobe, and a sudden blaze of flickering light to the eyes at close range leaves an opponent blinking and half-blind for a moment. Police and soldiers carry them.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Intimidation dazzling someone at close range
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** weapon light, strobe light

*[Flashlight](flashlight.md)*, the everyday kind, cheaper and far dimmer.
