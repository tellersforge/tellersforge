# Shipboard jumpsuit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A snug one-piece suit of fire-resistant cloth, with no loose flaps, laces or trailing ends to snag in machinery, grip patches on knees and palms, and a dozen sealed pockets so nothing floats away. It is the everyday dress of starship crews, and it helps them move and brace themselves when gravity fails. On a planet's fashionable streets, it marks the wearer as a mere spacer.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Low & Zero Gravity while worn
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA in planetary high society
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.8 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** ship suit, flight jumpsuit

## Not to be confused with

*[Pressure suit](pressure-suit.md)*, which is sealed and keeps the wearer alive in vacuum; the jumpsuit is only everyday clothing aboard ship.
