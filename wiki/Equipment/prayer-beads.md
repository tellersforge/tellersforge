# Prayer beads

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: writing · any*

A loop of beads of wood, seed, stone or bone, often with a larger bead or tassel to mark the start. The faithful pass them one by one through the fingers, saying a prayer or a holy name with each, so the mind can rest while the count keeps itself. Pilgrims, monks and grandmothers carry them, and the gentle click of beads is a sound of patience in many faiths.
