# Camouflage fatigues

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · modern*

Field clothing printed in blotches of green, brown and black, or sand and khaki for the desert, cut loose with many pockets. The pattern breaks up the human outline so the eye slides past a soldier lying in scrub or woodland. In matching country it hides the wearer well. In the wrong terrain or a city street it stands out badly, and civilians who fear soldiers keep their distance.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Stealth in matching terrain; +1 Camouflage in matching terrain
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth in the wrong terrain or a city; −1 CHA among civilians wary of soldiers
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** camo uniform, disruptive-pattern uniform

## Not to be confused with

*[Ghillie suit](ghillie-suit.md)*, which covers the wearer in rags and real plants for total concealment while still; camouflage fatigues are ordinary field clothing with a pattern.
