# Bugle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: gunpowder · any*

A plain brass horn without valves, looped once to be carried on a cord. It plays only a handful of notes, but those notes are arranged into set calls that every soldier knows by heart: wake up, eat, form up, charge, fall back, lights out. In battle, when no one can hear shouted orders, the bugle is the commander's voice. Unfortunately, it is also a clear sign to the enemy of where the commander is.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Leadership ordering troops in battle
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth once blown
- **Used for:** [Signals](../Skills/signals.md), [Instrument (per instrument)](../Skills/instrument-per-instrument.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** gunpowder · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** army trumpet

## Not to be confused with

*[Signal Horn](signal-horn.md)*, which gives a single blast; the bugle plays a whole set of recognised military calls.
