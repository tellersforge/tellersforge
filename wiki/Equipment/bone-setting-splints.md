# Bone-setting splints

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: stone tools · any*

A bundle of straight slats of wood or stiff bark, with strips of hide or cloth to bind them. Once a broken arm or leg has been pulled straight, the splints go on either side and are tied tight to hold it still while it knits. Every people that has broken bones has invented them. A well-set limb heals straight; a badly set one heals crooked and stays that way for life.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 First Aid setting broken limbs
- **Penalty:** 0

*[Plaster Cast Kit](plaster-cast-kit.md)*, which sets a hard shell round the break; splints are quicker to fit and easier to carry.
