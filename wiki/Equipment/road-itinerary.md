# Road itinerary

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: wheel and roads · western kingdoms*

A long strip of parchment or paper, rolled on a stick, listing the towns, crossroads, rivers, inns and stages along a road, with the distances between them. It does not care about the shape of the land; it cares only about the order of stops. Couriers, merchants and quartermasters of armies plan their marches by it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Wayfinding on roads; +1 Logistics planning a journey
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Literacy (per script)](../Skills/literacy-per-script.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** wheel and roads · **Price:** 8 C
- **Also known as:** route scroll, waystation list

## Not to be confused with

*[Printed Map](printed-map.md)*, which shows the shape of the land; the itinerary is simply the list of stops along one road.
