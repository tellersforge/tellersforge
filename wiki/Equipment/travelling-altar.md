# Travelling altar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: iron · any*

A wooden box that folds open to become a small shrine, with painted panels, a clean cloth, a little cup and plate, candles and the holy images of a faith. Chaplains carry them to war, missionaries to strange lands, and pilgrims to places without temples. Wherever it is unfolded, the rite can be held properly.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Ritual & Liturgy holding rites away from a temple
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Ritual & Liturgy](../Skills/ritual-and-liturgy.md)
- **Weight:** 5 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** portable shrine, folding altar

## Not to be confused with

*[Prayer Rug](prayer-rug.md)*, which gives a clean place to kneel but none of the vessels or images a full rite needs.
