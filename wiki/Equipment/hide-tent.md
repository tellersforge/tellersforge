# Hide tent

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: stone tools · plains peoples*

A cone of long poles tied together at the top and wrapped in hides sewn edge to edge, with a flap for a door and a smoke hole at the peak. A small fire in the middle keeps a family warm through wind and cold, and the smoke goes up and out if the flaps are set right to the wind. It comes down in the morning and is dragged to the next camp. The inside always smells of smoke and fur, which is to say, of home.
