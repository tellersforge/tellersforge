# Shell bead necklace

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: stone tools · any*

Small shells pierced with a flint point and strung on sinew, fibre or leather cord. They are among the oldest ornaments people have made, found in graves older than farming. Coastal and island peoples still wear them as a sign of the sea and of who they are. Among them a shell necklace is recognised and respected. Elsewhere it is simply pretty and cheap.
