# Portable forge

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: iron · any*

A small iron hearth on legs, with a hand bellows, a fuel box and a stubby anvil, all made to be loaded onto a wagon. Armies drag them along so that horseshoes, wagon tyres and broken weapons can be mended at the end of each day's march. It takes an hour to set up and a strong back to move, but it saves many a campaign from grinding to a halt.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Blacksmithing repairs on the march
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Blacksmithing](../Skills/blacksmithing.md), [Weaponsmithing & Armouring](../Skills/weaponsmithing-and-armouring.md)
- **Weight:** 40 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 20 G
- **Also known as:** field forge
