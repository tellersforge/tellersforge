# Immersion headset

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: computing · modern*

A padded visor and earphones that shut out the real world completely and replace it with a simulated one: a battlefield, a hospital ward, a mountain peak or a cockpit. It is used for games, and increasingly for training, since mistakes made inside it cost nothing. While it is worn, the wearer is blind and deaf to the room around them.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Teaching simulated training
- **Penalty:** −3 Perception while worn
- **Used for:** [Computer Use](../Skills/computer-use.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** virtual reality headset

## Not to be confused with

*[Hologram Theatre](hologram-theatre.md)*, which fills a real room with images that everyone present can see.
