# Climbing gloves

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: iron · any*

Rough leather gloves, reinforced across the palm, that save the hands from rope burn when sliding or hauling, and from the scrape of stone. Sailors, riggers and soldiers going down a rope all wear them. They make the hands tough and slow: picking pockets, working locks or anything delicate is hard until they come off.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing on rope
- **Penalty:** −1 Sleight of Hand any
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** rope gloves

## Not to be confused with

*[Grip Gloves](grip-gloves.md)*, which cling to smooth surfaces; climbing gloves only protect the hands on rope and rock.
