# Butcher's kit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: stone tools · any*

A roll of heavy knives, a cleaver, a sharpening steel, iron hooks and a sack of coarse salt. With it a butcher turns a carcass into cuts, sausages and salted meat that keeps. Hunters and herders who can't butcher properly waste half of every kill. The work is bloody, but a skilled butcher is quick, clean and wastes almost nothing.
