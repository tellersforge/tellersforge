# Yoke

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: agriculture · any*

A shaped pole laid across the shoulders with a load hung from each end: buckets of water, baskets of fish, bundles of cloth. Water carriers and street sellers walk miles with one, swaying in a steady rhythm that keeps the weight balanced. Loaded, the wearer can do little else with their body but walk.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Lifting & Carrying paired loads such as water buckets
- **Penalty:** −1 DEX while loaded
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** carrying pole, shoulder pole

## Not to be confused with

*[Carrying yoke](carrying-yoke.md)*, the heavier version for porters; the ordinary yoke is for everyday paired loads like water.
