# Sea bag

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: iron · any*

A tall canvas cylinder closed at the top with a cord, packed tight with everything a sailor owns and slung over one shoulder. It fits a narrow bunk or a corner of the hold and survives spray and rough handling. Anything needed in a hurry, of course, is always at the very bottom, and finding it means emptying the lot onto the deck.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** 0
- **Penalty:** −1 DEX getting anything from the bottom
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** duffel bag, kit bag

## Not to be confused with

*[Dry bag](dry-bag.md)*, which truly keeps water out; a sea bag only resists it.
