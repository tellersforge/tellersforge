# Glow stick

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: computing · modern*

A plastic tube that glows green, red or blue for hours once its inner glass vial is bent and cracked. It needs no fire or battery, is safe near fuel and water, and can be dropped to mark a path or waved to signal. Divers, soldiers and partygoers all use them.
