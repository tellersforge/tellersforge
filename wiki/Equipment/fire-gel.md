# Fire gel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: electricity · modern*

A thick burning paste or a solid fuel tablet that catches easily and holds a steady flame for many minutes. Laid under damp twigs, it keeps burning until the wood dries and catches. Hikers, soldiers and campers keep some for the wet evenings when nothing else will light.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Fire-Making wet tinder
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** fire paste, solid fuel tablets

## Not to be confused with

*[Storm matches](storm-matches.md)*, which make the first flame; fire gel feeds it long enough for the wood to catch.
