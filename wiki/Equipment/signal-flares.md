# Signal flares

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: steam · any*

A bundle of flares: some to be struck and held up, burning with a brilliant red or white flame, others fired into the sky from a pistol or tube to hang and drift over the landscape. They mark a landing site, show where a ship is sinking or call for help from anyone who can see them. Once lit, they also tell everyone else where the party is.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Signals calling help at night
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth once lit
- **Used for:** [Signals](../Skills/signals.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** hand flares, flare pistol

## Not to be confused with

*[Signal Fire Kit](signal-fire-kit.md)*, which builds a fire that can be tended and shaped; flares burn hot and bright but only for a moment.
