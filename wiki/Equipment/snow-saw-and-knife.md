# Snow saw and knife

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

A long flat blade of bone, antler or metal, used to carve blocks from wind-packed snow and shape them into a spiralling dome. With it and some skill, a snow house can be raised in an hour that is warmer than any tent, where a small lamp makes the inside almost comfortable. Without the skill, a person produces only a snow wall and a sore back.
