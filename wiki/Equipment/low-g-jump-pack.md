# Low-g jump pack

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A belt with small thrusters that fire at the push of a button, turning a hop in low gravity into a long, controlled bound across the ground. Explorers on small moons and asteroids use it to cover distance fast and to land without tumbling. Each thrust flares brightly, so anyone watching can follow its user's path.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Jumping long bounds in low gravity
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth visible jets
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 8 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 20 S

the *[Manoeuvring Pack](manoeuvring-pack.md)*, which lets a suited person fly freely in open space rather than bound over a surface.
