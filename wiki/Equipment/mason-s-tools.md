# Mason's tools

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: bronze · any*

Chisels, points, a pitching tool, a heavy mallet, a plumb line and an iron square, all carried in a stout bag. Masons use them to split stone, dress it to a clean face and set it true in a wall. Their work outlasts every other trade: cathedrals, bridges and city walls stand centuries after their builders are forgotten. Masons guard their secrets and their marks jealously.
