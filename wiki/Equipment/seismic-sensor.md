# Seismic sensor

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: computing · modern*

A metal spike with a small box on top, hammered into the ground beside a trail or a fence and left behind. It feels the earth. The tread of boots, the rumble of an engine and the thud of hooves travel through the soil to it, and it quietly radios a warning to someone far away. Border patrols and cautious camps scatter them along the approaches. Heavy rain and passing herds send false alarms.

- **Bonus:** +2 Shadowing & Surveillance detecting footsteps and vehicles; +1 Danger Sense approaching movement

the *[Motion Sensor Kit](motion-sensor-kit.md)*, which watches the air of a room or doorway; the seismic sensor listens through the ground outdoors.
