# Cupping set

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: pottery · any*

A set of small cups of clay, glass or horn. The healer warms the air inside with a flame and presses each cup to the skin, where it cools, clings and draws the flesh up into a round, red bruise. Patients say it eases aches and draws out bad blood. Whether or not it cures, it comforts people who trust the old ways, and a healer who knows that has half the battle won.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Bedside Manner with patients who trust the old ways
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Remedies & Pharmacy](../Skills/remedies-and-pharmacy.md)
- **Weight:** 0.8 kg · **Needs:** pottery · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** cupping glasses, horn cups

## Not to be confused with

*[Acupuncture Needles](acupuncture-needles.md)*, which treat pain by placing needles in the skin; cupping draws the skin up with heated cups.
