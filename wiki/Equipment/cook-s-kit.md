# Cook's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: pottery · any*

A sturdy bag of knives, a nesting set of pots, ladles, a tinder pouch and a clutch of little packets of salt, spice and dried herbs. A good cook with this kit turns rough trail fare into something people look forward to at the end of the day. Armies, caravans and adventuring parties all run on their stomachs, and the cook is the most popular person in camp.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Cooking on the road
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Cooking](../Skills/cooking.md), [Professional Cookery](../Skills/professional-cookery.md)
- **Weight:** 6 kg · **Needs:** pottery · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** kitchen kit, chef's roll

## Not to be confused with

the *[Mess Kit](mess-kit.md)*, a single traveller's plate, cup and spoon rather than a kit for cooking for many.
