# Gold ring

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: copper · any*

A plain band or a gem-set ring of gold or silver, worn on the finger. It speaks quietly of wealth and taste, and merchants and nobles notice it at once when a hand is offered. It is also easy to see and easy to slip off a finger, so in poor streets and crowded markets it draws thieves like flies.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 CHA among merchants and nobles
- **Penalty:** −1 Streetwise draws thieves
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.01 kg · **Needs:** copper · **Price:** 25 S
- **Also known as:** finger ring, band

## Not to be confused with

*[Signet ring](signet-ring.md)*, which bears a carved seal and proves identity; the gold ring is an ornament and a store of wealth.
