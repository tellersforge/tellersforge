# Henna kit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: agriculture · desert peoples*

A pouch of crushed dried leaves, a paste mixer and a fine cone of rolled paper or leaf for piping the paste onto skin. Left overnight, it stains the hands and feet in lasting patterns of red-brown lace and vines. In the desert peoples' weddings and festivals, a bride's hands are painted by her friends, and every guest admires the work.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 CHA at weddings and festivals
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Tattooing & Body Art](../Skills/tattooing-and-body-art.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** mehndi, henna paste

## Not to be confused with

*[Tattoo kit](tattoo-kit.md)*, which marks the skin forever with needles; henna stains lightly and fades within a few weeks.
