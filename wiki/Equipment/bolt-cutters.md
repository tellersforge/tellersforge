# Bolt cutters

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: steam · any*

Two long handles and a short pair of hardened jaws, joined so that every ounce of a two-handed squeeze becomes a crushing bite at the tip. Chains, padlock shackles, iron bars and wire fences part with a crack. Fire crews, railway gangs and burglars all carry them, and none of them bother with subtlety. They are heavy, awkward and obvious, but a strong pair of arms will cut through almost anything a small lock is hung on.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 STR cutting chains and padlocks
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 4 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 3 S

## Not to be confused with

*[Lockpicks](lockpicks.md)*, which open a lock intact and leave no sign, where bolt cutters simply destroy whatever holds it.
