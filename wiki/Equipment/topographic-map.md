# Topographic map

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A survey sheet crowded with fine brown lines, each one tracing a single height across the land. Close lines mean a steep slope, wide ones gentle ground, and a tight ring of them a hilltop. To the untrained it looks like a thumbprint. To a hunter, a soldier or a surveyor it shows where water runs, where a column can march unseen, and which valley ends in a cliff. Industrial states drew them for their armies first and their walkers later.
