# Pocket calculator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: computing · modern*

A small plastic device with a keypad and a narrow screen, powered by a tiny battery or a strip of solar cells. It adds, subtracts, multiplies and divides as fast as a thumb can press. Shopkeepers, students and engineers use it daily. It never gets bored, but it only calculates what it is told; a wrong number in means a wrong answer out.
