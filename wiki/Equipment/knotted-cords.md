# Knotted cords

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: agriculture · mountain empires*

A thick main cord with many coloured strings hanging from it, each knotted in clusters that stand for numbers. The colours mark what is counted, the position of each knot gives its value, and a trained reader runs the strings through their fingers like a page. Whole empires have kept their censuses, tributes and granary stocks this way. Untrained eyes see only a tangle of string.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Bookkeeping tallies and censuses
- **Penalty:** 0

## Not to be confused with

*[Tally Stick](tally-stick.md)*, which records a single count or debt in notches; the cords can hold a whole ledger.
