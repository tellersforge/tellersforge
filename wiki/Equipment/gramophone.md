# Gramophone

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A wooden cabinet with a crank handle, a turntable, a needle arm and a great flaring brass horn. Wind it up, lower the needle onto a spinning disc, and the scratchy voice of a singer, a dance band or a speech fills the room. Parties begin when it starts to play. It needs rewinding every few minutes and a supply of records that break if dropped.
