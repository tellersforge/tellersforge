# Spirit drum

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

A large, flat frame drum with a hide head painted with spirits, animals and stars, beaten with a single padded stick. Arctic shamans use its steady, rolling beat to leave their bodies and travel to the spirit world, and the drum itself is said to be the vessel that carries them. A spirit drum is made once, kept for life and treated as a living thing.
