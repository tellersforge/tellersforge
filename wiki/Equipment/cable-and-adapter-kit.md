# Cable and adapter kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A rolled canvas pouch full of cables, plugs, clips and odd little adapters, each one meant to join something that was never meant to be joined. With it, an old radio can talk to a new battery, a foreign charger can fit a local socket, and a desperate engineer can wire almost anything to anything else. It is always tangled, and the right adapter is always at the bottom.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Jury-Rigging connecting mismatched gear
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Electrical Work](../Skills/electrical-work.md)
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** patch kit

## Not to be confused with

the *[Electronics Toolkit](electronics-toolkit.md)*, which holds tools and spare parts for repairs, not connectors.
