# Climate blanket

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: far future · far future*

A thin, soft blanket threaded with fine elements that warm or cool to whatever comfort the sleeper has set. In a snowfield it glows faintly warm to the touch; in a jungle it stays cool as water. It folds small and weighs little. It needs charging, and those who rely on it for years find sleeping under an ordinary blanket a hardship.
