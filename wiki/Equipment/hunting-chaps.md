# Hunting chaps

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · herders and riders*

Leg coverings of heavy leather, belted at the waist and strapped over ordinary trousers, open behind the thigh. Herders and riders wear them through thorny scrub and brush that would rip cloth and skin to tatters. They make the legs almost as tough as the horse's. On foot they are stiff and slow.

## Game block

- **Slot:** legs
- **Bonus:** +1 armour against thorns and brush; +1 Riding through scrub
- **Penalty:** −1 Running stiff leather
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** leather leggings

## Not to be confused with

*[Riding Trousers](riding-trousers.md)*, fitted trousers for gripping a horse on long rides, not for pushing through thorns.
