# Archer's bracer

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: bronze · archers*

A stiff guard strapped to the inside of the forearm of the bow arm, made of leather, bone, horn or polished stone. When the string is loosed it snaps against the bracer instead of the arm, and a loose sleeve is held out of the way. Archers who forget theirs soon have a bruise from wrist to elbow and a reason never to forget again.
