# Flint and pyrite

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: stone tools · any*

A lump of flint struck against a nodule of iron pyrite, throwing small sparks onto dry fungus or tinder. It is the stone-age way of making fire by spark rather than friction, and some of the oldest travellers carried the pair together in a pouch. The sparks are weak, so the tinder must be very good.
