# Gourd rattle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: agriculture · any*

A dried gourd, its seeds rattling loose inside, sometimes netted with beads or shells on the outside and fitted with a wooden handle. Shaken, it hisses and clatters in time with a song or a chant. Farmers make them from the harvest, children steal them for play, and healers and priests shake them in rites to drive away sickness or summon the rain.
