# Fountain pen

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A slim pen with a metal nib and its own reservoir of ink, filled from a bottle and able to write pages without dipping. It glides across the paper in a clean, flowing line. Clerks, writers and people of standing carry one in a waistcoat pocket, and a fine one is often a gift that marks an achievement. It leaks at altitude and, sooner or later, in a shirt pocket.
