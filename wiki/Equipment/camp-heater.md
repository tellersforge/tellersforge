# Camp heater

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: electricity · modern*

A small heater that burns fuel on a catalytic plate without a flame, or runs from a battery or a line. Set in the corner of a tent or a cabin, it takes the edge off a freezing night and dries wet socks. Careful users crack a vent, because the fuel kind eats the air as well as warming it, and every winter someone forgets.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 END against cold inside a shelter
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** tent heater

## Not to be confused with

*[Camp Stove](camp-stove.md)*, which is for cooking; the heater is for keeping a shelter warm.
