# Felted coat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: pottery · steppe riders*

A thick coat of wool pressed and rolled into felt rather than woven, the everyday coat and half the armour of the steppe. It turns wind and rain, and its dense layers can stop a spent arrow or blunt a sword stroke that only grazes. It is stiff, it smells of sheep and horses, and it is far too hot when the wind drops in summer.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +2 END against cold; +1 armour against arrows and slashes
- **Penalty:** −1 END in heat; −1 Acrobatics stiff and thick
