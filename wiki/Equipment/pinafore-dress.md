# Pinafore dress

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · northern villages*

A woollen overdress, like an open tube held up by two shoulder straps, fastened at the front with a pair of bronze brooches. It is worn over a long linen shift, and a string of beads hangs between the brooches. Among northern villagers, the brooches and the beads announce that the wearer is a married woman and the mistress of her household.
