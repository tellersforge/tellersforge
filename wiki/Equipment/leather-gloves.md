# Leather gloves

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · any*

Close-fitting gloves of soft leather, deer or goat or sheepskin, for riding, travelling and cold days. They keep the hands warm and protect them from reins and the wind. Rich people have them embroidered at the cuff. Anyone who needs deft, secret fingers pulls them off first.

## Game block

- **Slot:** hands
- **Bonus:** +1 END against cold
- **Penalty:** −1 Sleight of Hand while worn
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** gloves

*[Fine Silk Gloves](fine-silk-gloves.md)*, the thin court gloves worn for manners rather than for warmth.
