# Pilot's flight suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A one-piece coverall zipped up the front, with pockets on the thighs and sleeves for maps, pencils and charts, often lined with fleece for the cold of high flight. Aircrew wear it because nothing snags on levers and switches, and it keeps them warm in an open or thin-walled cockpit. It is stained with oil and sweat, and it looks entirely wrong at a formal gathering.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 END against cold at altitude
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA at a formal gathering
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** flying suit, flight coverall
