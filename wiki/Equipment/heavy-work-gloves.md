# Heavy work gloves

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · craftsmen*

Thick gauntlets of stiff leather, often doubled at the palm, worn for handling thorny hedges, hot iron, quicklime, raw hides and rough loads. They save the hands from cuts, burns and blisters. They also turn every fine task, from threading a needle to picking a lock, into a clumsy fumble.

## Game block

- **Slot:** hands
- **Bonus:** +1 armour against thorns, hides and hot metal
- **Penalty:** −2 DEX fine handwork
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.4 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** tanner's gloves, hedging gloves

## Not to be confused with

*[Leather Gloves](leather-gloves.md)*, thin, close-fitting gloves for warmth and riding, which protect far less but leave the fingers nimble.
