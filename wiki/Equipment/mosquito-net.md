# Mosquito net

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: printing · any*

A fine mesh of cotton or gauze, hung from a hook or a frame over a bed and tucked in beneath the mattress. It lets the air through and keeps the biting insects out, and with them the fevers they carry. In hot, wet lands it is the difference between a traveller who stays healthy and one who shakes with fever for weeks. It works only if there are no holes, and there are always holes.

- **Bonus:** +2 END against insect-borne fever
