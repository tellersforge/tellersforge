# Ceremonial headdress

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · plains tribes*

A tall crown of eagle feathers mounted on a leather band, sweeping down the back in a long trail, each feather granted for a deed of courage or generosity. It is worn only by those whose people have honoured them, and only on great occasions: a council, a treaty, a dance. The wearer can be seen from very far away.
