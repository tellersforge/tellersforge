# Jeweller's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: bronze · any*

A small wooden box of tiny hammers, files, gravers, pliers, a jeweller's lens and a set of delicate scales. With it a jeweller sets stones, repairs chains and engraves rings, and just as often tells real gold from gilded lead or a true gem from coloured glass. Many a merchant hires a jeweller before a deal, and many a thief after.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Appraisal gems and precious metal
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Jewellery & Gem Cutting](../Skills/jewellery-and-gem-cutting.md), [Appraisal](../Skills/appraisal.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 20 S
- **Also known as:** goldsmith's tools

## Not to be confused with

the *[Magnifying Lens](magnifying-lens.md)*, a single glass that shows fine detail but can't weigh, test or work the metal.
