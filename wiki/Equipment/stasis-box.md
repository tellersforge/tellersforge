# Stasis box

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Fields and far-future devices · Needs: far future · far future*

A small case that holds time still inside it. Food, samples, evidence or organs placed within stay exactly as they were sealed, however long they wait. Explorers, doctors and investigators prize them. Opening one after years feels strange, as everything inside is exactly as fresh as the day it was closed.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +3 Butchery & Preserving keeping anything unchanged; +2 Field Medicine preserving samples or organs
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 4 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 80 S
- **Also known as:** time-lock case

the *[Stasis Pouch](stasis-pouch.md)*, which is smaller and portable but holds less.
