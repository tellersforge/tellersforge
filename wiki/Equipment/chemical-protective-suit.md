# Chemical protective suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: computing · modern*

A sealed coverall of coated fabric with a hood, gloves and boots, the joins wrapped in tape so nothing can creep in. Spill cleaners, chemical workers and emergency crews wear it where acids splash and poisonous dust drifts. Inside it the wearer is safe and miserable: sweat pools, the gloves make fingers clumsy, and the hood muffles sound and cuts off everything to the side.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 END against chemical splashes and dust
- **Penalty:** −2 END in heat or hard work; −1 DEX fine handwork; −1 Perception hearing and side vision
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** hazmat suit, splash suit

## Not to be confused with

*[Hazmat suit](hazmat-suit.md)*, a heavier fully enclosed suit with its own air; the chemical protective suit guards the skin but relies on the wearer's breathing mask.
