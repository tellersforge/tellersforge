# Hobbles

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: agriculture · any*

Short straps of leather or rope fastened round a horse's forelegs so that it can graze but can't wander far or bolt. Herders and travellers use them each night on the open plains, where there are no fences and no trees to tie to. A hobbled horse shuffles and grumbles, but it is still there in the morning.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Herding & Husbandry keeping mounts near camp
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Herding & Husbandry](../Skills/herding-and-husbandry.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** leg ties

## Not to be confused with

the *[Leash and Collar](leash-and-collar.md)*, which leads an animal rather than stopping it from straying.
