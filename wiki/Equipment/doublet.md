# Doublet

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · western kingdoms*

A close-fitting jacket, padded and stitched through, buttoned or laced down the front, worn over a shirt and with hose. It shapes the chest and shoulders into the fashion of the day. Gentry wear them in silk and velvet, tradesmen in wool, and everyone looks respectable. The stiff padding makes tumbling or twisting awkward.
