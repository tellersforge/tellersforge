# Ear spools

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: stone tools · any*

Round plugs of stone, bone, jade or later metal, worn in earlobes that have been stretched a little at a time over many seasons. The bigger the spool, the longer the patience behind it, and among the wearer's own people that patience earns quiet respect. Jade spools belong to lords and priests; bone ones to everyone else. Taken out, they leave a loop of skin that tells its own story.
