# Trench coat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A belted, double-breasted raincoat of tightly woven cotton twill, with storm flaps at the shoulder and back and deep pockets inside and out. It was first made for officers in the trenches and soon became the dress of detectives, reporters and spies. It keeps off rain and wind, and it hides a great deal: a folded newspaper, a bottle, a camera or something with a trigger.
