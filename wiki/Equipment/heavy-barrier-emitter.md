# Heavy barrier emitter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Fields and far-future devices · Needs: far future · far future*

A crate-sized generator that hums loudly and raises a wide, strong wall of force, enough to shelter a whole squad. It is carried by several people or hauled on a sled, and set up to hold a position for as long as its power lasts. It glows brightly and hums like a beehive, making it the most obvious thing on any battlefield.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +3 Dodge behind the barrier against shots
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth glowing and humming
- **Used for:** [Fortification & Siegecraft](../Skills/fortification-and-siegecraft.md)
- **Weight:** 40 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 150 G
- **Also known as:** fortress field

## Not to be confused with

the *[Barrier Projector](barrier-projector.md)*, which is portable but smaller and weaker.
