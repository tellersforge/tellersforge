# Sunglasses

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: electricity · modern*

Tinted lenses in a light frame, perched on the nose to cut the glare of sun, snow, water and chrome. They save the eyes on long days outdoors, and they do something else too: nobody can see where the wearer is looking. Card players, bodyguards and people pretending not to stare all appreciate that. In a dark corridor or at dusk, they turn the world into murky shadow.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +1 END against glare; +1 Insight hiding where you look
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception in dim light
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** dark glasses, shades

## Not to be confused with

*[Snow Goggles](snow-goggles.md)*, which do the same job with slits in bone and a far narrower view.
