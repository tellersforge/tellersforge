# Mountain tent

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: computing · modern*

A low, tough tent with a heavy frame of many poles, a double skin, snow flaps round the base and a vestibule for boots and stove. It is made to be pitched on a ledge or a glacier and to stand through a night of screaming wind and falling snow. It is heavy to carry and cramped inside, and those who have spent a storm in one never complain about the weight again.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 END against blizzard and high wind
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Survival: Mountain](../Skills/survival-mountain.md)
- **Weight:** 4 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 25 S
- **Also known as:** four-season tent, expedition tent

## Not to be confused with

*[Dome Tent](dome-tent.md)*, which is lighter and roomier but would flatten in a mountain blizzard.
