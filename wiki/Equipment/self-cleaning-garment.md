# Self-cleaning garment

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: far future · far future*

A garment woven from fibres that break down dirt, sweat, stains and smells, so that it always looks freshly laundered. Travellers, diplomats and spacers on long journeys prize them. After days on the road, a wearer can step out looking as if they had just dressed. It asks for no care, no washing and no ironing, and never offends a host.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Grooming & Style on a long journey; +1 CHA after days of travel
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** nanite fabric, clean cloth

## Not to be confused with

*[Colour-shifting garment](colour-shifting-garment.md)*, which changes its look on command; the self-cleaning garment only keeps itself fresh.
