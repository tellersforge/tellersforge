# Marine chronometer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: steam · sea peoples*

A precise clock in a brass drum, hung in gimbals inside a wooden box so it stays upright however the ship rolls. It keeps the time of the home port to within a few seconds over months at sea. Compared with local noon, found by sextant, it tells the navigator how far east or west the ship has sailed. It was once one of the costliest objects aboard, and it is wound every day without fail.
