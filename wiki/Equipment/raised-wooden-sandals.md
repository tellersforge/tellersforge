# Raised wooden sandals

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · eastern empire*

Flat wooden soles raised on two blocks or teeth beneath, held on by a cloth thong between the toes. They lift the wearer and the hems of their robe clear of mud, puddles and rain-soaked streets. They clack on paving stones and make any running a clumsy, teetering business.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 END keeping feet above mud and rain
- **Penalty:** −1 Running tall wooden teeth; −1 Stealth they clack
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.8 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** platform sandals, stilt clogs

## Not to be confused with

*[Wooden Clogs](wooden-clogs.md)*, the carved wooden shoes of western farms, which enclose the foot rather than lifting it high.
