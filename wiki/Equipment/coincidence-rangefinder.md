# Coincidence rangefinder

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A long tube with a window at each end and an eyepiece in the middle, showing two images of the same target, split or overlapping. The observer turns a dial until the two images line up, and the dial shows the distance. Gunners and surveyors use it to find the range to a target or a landmark without walking there.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Artillery & Siege Engines judging range to a target
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Surveying](../Skills/surveying.md)
- **Weight:** 6 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 25 S
- **Also known as:** optical rangefinder

## Not to be confused with

*[Laser Rangefinder](laser-rangefinder.md)*, which measures distance in an instant with a beam rather than by lining up two images.
