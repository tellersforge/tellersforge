# Smoke helmet

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A hood of oiled leather or riveted copper that goes over the whole head, with a small glass window at the front and a long hose running back to a bellows. Someone outside works the bellows while the wearer goes into a smoke-filled cellar or a burning mine gallery. Fresh air arrives in puffs, the window fogs, and the hose drags and snags on everything. It is the first step toward breathing where no one can breathe.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +2 END in smoke
- **Penalty:** −1 DEX tethered to an air hose; −1 Perception through the small window
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 4 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** firefighter's hood

## Not to be confused with

the *[Breathing Apparatus](breathing-apparatus.md)*, which carries its own air on the back and needs no hose or partner working a bellows.
