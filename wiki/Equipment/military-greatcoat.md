# Military greatcoat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · standing armies*

A long, heavy coat of thick wool with a short cape over the shoulders and brass buttons down the front, handed out to soldiers, sentries and anyone who must stand all night in the rain. It keeps its wearer alive on a winter march. Its long skirts slap against the legs and tangle when there is running to do.

- **Penalty:** −1 Running long heavy skirts
