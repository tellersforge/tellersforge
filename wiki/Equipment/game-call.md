# Game call

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: stone tools · any*

A cut reed, a hollow bone or a small horn that imitates a bird's cry, a doe's bleat or a stag's challenge. In the right mouth it brings curious animals closer to find out who is calling. It takes practice: a bad call scatters the game, and a good one can bring a rival stag crashing through the brush looking for a fight. Hunters from every age have carried something like it, often carved by their own hand.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Hunting drawing game
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Hunting](../Skills/hunting.md), [Mimicry](../Skills/mimicry.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** decoy call

## Not to be confused with

*[Scent Lure](scent-lure.md)*, which draws game by smell; the call brings animals by sound and only while someone is calling.
