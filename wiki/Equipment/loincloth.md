# Loincloth

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: — · any*

A strip of hide or woven cloth passed between the legs and held up by a cord at the waist, and in hot lands sometimes the whole of a person's clothing. It is cheap, cool and easy to swim in. In cities where people cover more of themselves, it marks the wearer as a labourer, a savage or a madman, depending on who is doing the looking.
