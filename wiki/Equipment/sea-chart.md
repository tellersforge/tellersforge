# Sea chart

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: printing · sea peoples*

A drawn or printed chart of coasts, harbour mouths, sounded depths, rocks, sandbanks and wrecks, criss-crossed with compass lines for steering. A navigator lays a course on it with rulers and dividers, then checks it against the coast as it appears. Every captain keeps charts dry and up to date, because an old chart is a trap.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Sea Navigation charted waters
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Sea Navigation](../Skills/sea-navigation.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** portolan, nautical chart

## Not to be confused with

*[Printed Map](printed-map.md)*, which shows land; the sea chart shows the waters, their depths and their dangers.
