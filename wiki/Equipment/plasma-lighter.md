# Plasma lighter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: computing · modern*

A small rechargeable lighter that makes an electric arc between two points instead of a flame. The arc is fiercely hot and wind cannot blow it out. It lights tinder, candles and fuses in a gale. When the battery runs flat, however, it is only a small box.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Fire-Making in wind
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Fire-Making](../Skills/fire-making.md)
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** arc lighter

## Not to be confused with

*[Pocket lighter](pocket-lighter.md)*, which burns fuel for a flame that wind can snuff.
