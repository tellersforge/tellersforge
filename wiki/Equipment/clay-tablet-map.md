# Clay tablet map

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: writing · river kingdoms*

A slab of baked clay, flat enough to hold in two hands, with a city plan, field boundaries or a district's canals incised into it before firing. It shows walls, gates, temples and streets, often with the names of districts written small. It is too heavy and fragile to carry on a journey, but a scribe or a surveyor keeps it for reference and argument.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Local Lore mapped city or district
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Literacy (per script)](../Skills/literacy-per-script.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** tablet plan

## Not to be confused with

*[Hide Map](hide-map.md)*, which shows a wide land roughly; the clay tablet map shows a city or its fields in precise detail.
