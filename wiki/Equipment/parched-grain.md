# Parched grain

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: agriculture · any*

Grain toasted in a pan or on hot stones, then carried whole or ground into meal. It can be eaten dry, stirred into water or mixed with a little fat. Marching armies and pilgrims have lived on it for weeks. It is dull, filling and very light.
