# Riding kaftan

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · steppe riders*

A long coat cut to close across the chest, one front over the other, with slits at the back or sides so the skirts fall on either side of the saddle. It keeps the wind off the rider's legs and back and does not bunch or chafe after a day at the gallop. Nobles wear it in brocade, herders in plain wool, and the cut is the same.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +1 Riding on long rides; +1 END against wind
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
