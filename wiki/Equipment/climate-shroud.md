# Climate shroud

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Fields and far-future devices · Needs: far future · far future*

A slim belt with a small emitter at the buckle that wraps its wearer in a thin, invisible layer of comfortable, breathable air. Heat, cold and bad air stay outside, and the wearer walks through a desert or a blizzard in perfect comfort. It is the most luxurious travelling device ever made, and those who own one often forget how harsh the world really is.
