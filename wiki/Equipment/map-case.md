# Map case

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: printing · any*

A stiff tube of oiled leather or waxed canvas with a tight cap, slung over the shoulder or strapped to a saddle. Inside, maps and charts stay rolled, dry and uncreased through rain, river crossings and careless hands. It does nothing to help anyone read them. Couriers and officers carry one out of habit, and thieves know that a well-guarded map case often holds more than maps.
