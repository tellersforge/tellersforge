# Pheromone scent

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: computing · modern*

A perfume blended to be almost unnoticed, working on the other person at close range before they know why they like the wearer. It is sold in small bottles with suggestive names. Some find it cloying and grow cool without quite knowing why. Animals are not fooled at all, and a hunter or a guard dog will smell the wearer long before seeing them.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Charm & Seduction in close company
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA with those who find it cloying; −2 Stealth against animals by scent
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** attraction perfume

## Not to be confused with

*[Perfume](perfume.md)*, which is meant to be noticed and admired; pheromone scent works quietly and up close.
