# Dome tent

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: computing · modern*

A light shell of nylon on two or three crossing flexible poles that bend into a dome and hold the tent up on their own, without guy lines or a tree. With a waterproof flysheet over the top it keeps out rain and wind well. The whole thing packs down into a bag the size of a loaf. Pegs still help, as anyone who has watched one roll away across a hillside will say.
