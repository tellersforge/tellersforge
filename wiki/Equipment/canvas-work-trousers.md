# Canvas work trousers

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

Trousers or bib overalls of heavy cotton canvas, with seams held by copper rivets and pockets for hammers, rules and nails. Dockers, miners, builders and factory hands wear them until they are more patch than cloth. They shrug off splinters, grease and hard kneeling, and they last years. They are also unmistakably the clothes of a labourer, and a man who walks into a formal dinner in them will be shown out.

## Game block

- **Slot:** legs
- **Bonus:** +1 END against scrapes and grime at hard labour
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA at a formal gathering
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** dungarees, bib overalls

## Not to be confused with

*[Mechanic's coveralls](mechanic-s-coveralls.md)*, a one-piece suit that covers the whole body; canvas work trousers protect the legs and hold tools, and are worn with any shirt.
