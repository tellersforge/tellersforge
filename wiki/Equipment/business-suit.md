# Business suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A matched jacket and trousers or skirt in grey, navy or black, worn with a pressed shirt and tie or a neat blouse. It is the uniform of offices, banks, ministries and boardrooms, and a well-cut one says the wearer means business and can be trusted with money. On a rough street corner it marks an outsider, and out in the woods it is quickly ruined and very little help.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 CHA in business or government settings; +1 Trading & Business in a formal meeting
- **Penalty:** −1 Streetwise in rough neighbourhoods; −1 Survival: Forest (Woodcraft) in the wild
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.8 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** lounge suit, office suit

## Not to be confused with

*[Frock coat](frock-coat.md)*, the older formal dress of the steam age; the business suit is its shorter, plainer modern successor.
