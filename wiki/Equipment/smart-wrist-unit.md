# Smart wrist unit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: computing · modern*

A slim screen on a wrist strap that tells the time, shows messages, counts steps and heartbeats, and knows where its wearer is. It stays in touch with a network and with the owner's other devices, and can guide a traveller turn by turn through an unfamiliar city. It needs charging, it needs a signal for most of its tricks, and it records a great deal that someone else might like to read.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Counting & Measuring exact timing; +1 Wayfinding with network signal
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Computer Use](../Skills/computer-use.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** wrist computer

the *[Wristwatch](wristwatch.md)*, which only tells time but never needs a network.
