# Patched rags

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: — · any*

Clothing worn past any sensible use, patched with scraps of other clothing, tied together with string where the seams have gone. It is the dress of beggars, drifters and the desperate. Anyone wearing it is ignored by the respectable, trusted by the poor, and welcomed nowhere with a clean floor. It does little against a winter wind.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Disguise passing as a beggar; +1 Streetwise among the poor
- **Penalty:** −2 Etiquette anywhere respectable; −1 END against cold
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** — · **Price:** 1 C
- **Also known as:** beggar's rags, tatters

## Not to be confused with

*[Homespun Clothes](homespun-clothes.md)*, which are plain but whole and decent, the dress of an honest farmer rather than a beggar.
