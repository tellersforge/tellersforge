# Gut rain parka

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

A smock made from the dried intestines of seals or walrus, slit, flattened and sewn in long strips with sinew so fine that the seams do not leak. The result is pale, crackling and almost see-through, like a lantern made of skin. Kayakers and fishers wear it over their furs against spray, rain and the endless wet of the sea. On its own it is thin as paper and useless against cold.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +2 END against rain and sea spray
- **Penalty:** −1 END against cold if worn alone
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 4 S
- **Also known as:** seal-gut parka, intestine parka

## Not to be confused with

*[Fur Parka](fur-parka.md)*, the heavy coat it is worn over: the parka keeps the cold out, the gut smock keeps the water off.
