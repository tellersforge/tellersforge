# Starched ruff

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: printing · western courts*

A wide collar of fine linen pleated into dozens of figure-of-eight folds, stiffened with starch and set with hot irons into a white wheel around the neck. At court it is the height of refinement; the larger, the grander. It also makes it almost impossible to look down at one's own feet, and eating soup in one is an art.
