# Prayer wheel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: iron · mountain monasteries*

A hand-held cylinder of metal or wood on a spindle, packed with rolled paper covered in written prayers, and weighted by a small chain so it spins easily with a turn of the wrist. Each spin sends the prayers out into the world. Mountain monks and pilgrims spin them while walking, talking or waiting, and many find the steady whirl settles the mind.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Meditation & Concentration while spinning it
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Ritual & Liturgy](../Skills/ritual-and-liturgy.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** hand prayer wheel

## Not to be confused with

*[Prayer Beads](prayer-beads.md)*, which count prayers through the fingers instead of sending them out by spinning.
