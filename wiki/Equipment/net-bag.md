# Net bag

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

An open-meshed bag knotted from twisted plant fibre, light enough to roll into a fist and able to stretch around almost any load. Forest and coastal peoples carry them to gather fruit, nuts and shellfish, because the mesh lets sand and water drain away. It holds nothing small, and a sharp twig will ruin it.
