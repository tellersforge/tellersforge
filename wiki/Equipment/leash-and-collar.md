# Leash and collar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: agriculture · any*

A collar of leather or woven rope around the animal's neck, with a long lead held by the handler. It keeps a dog close in crowds, stops it chasing game and lets the handler pull it back from trouble. Hunters, herders and guards all use one. A strong dog can still drag a careless handler into a ditch.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Animal Handling controlling a dog
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Animal Handling](../Skills/animal-handling.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** lead

## Not to be confused with

the *[Muzzle](muzzle.md)*, which stops an animal biting rather than wandering.
