# White ritual robe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: pottery · temple faiths*

A robe of spotless white linen or cotton, worn only for rites of purification, initiation and passage, and washed after each use. Wearing it means entering a state apart from the everyday. The priest who wears it must keep it immaculate. Its brightness glows in the dark, which makes it a poor choice for a night-time errand.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Ritual & Liturgy purification rites
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth shows up at night
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** pottery · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** purity robe

*[Priest's Vestments](priest-s-vestments.md)*, the embroidered layered robes of ordinary worship, rather than a garment of purity.
