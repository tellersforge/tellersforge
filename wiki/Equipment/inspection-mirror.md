# Inspection mirror

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: printing · any*

A small round mirror on a long, jointed rod, sometimes with a little lamp. It lets the user see under a cart, behind a boiler, round a corner or inside a machine without crawling in. Mechanics use it for repairs, guards for checking under wagons at a gate, and cautious types for looking round doors before they open them.

- **Penalty:** 0
