# Felt yurt kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: bronze · steppe riders*

The whole of a round house broken down for travel: folding lattice walls, a wooden roof ring, a sheaf of curved roof poles, felt covers and binding bands, all loaded onto a string of pack animals. Raised by a family in an afternoon, it is warm in the worst winter of the open plains and cool in summer with the felts rolled up. Inside, every place has a meaning: where the elders sit, where guests sit, where the stove stands.
