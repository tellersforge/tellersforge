# Shaman's costume

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

A long coat of hide hung with iron charms shaped like animals and bones, bells, mirrors, ribbons, fringes and the feathers or claws of spirit helpers. It is not decoration but equipment: each piece has a meaning and a purpose on the journey between worlds. When the shaman drums and dances, the whole coat clatters and rings. Anyone uninitiated watching is afraid.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 Trance & Spirit Work in ritual; +1 Intimidation before the uninitiated
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth bells and rattles
- **Used for:** [Trance & Spirit Work](../Skills/trance-and-spirit-work.md)
- **Weight:** 4 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** spirit coat

## Not to be confused with

*[Ceremonial Headdress](ceremonial-headdress.md)*, which honours a leader's deeds, where the shaman's coat is a tool for travelling among spirits.
