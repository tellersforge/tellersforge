# Bandolier

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: gunpowder · any*

A broad leather belt worn diagonally across the chest, with loops or small pockets for cartridges, powder charges, vials or tools. It keeps what a fighter needs next right under the hand, so reloading or drawing a small item is quick and sure. It looks fierce, which its wearers rarely mind.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Quick-Draw reloading or drawing small items
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** gunpowder · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** cartridge belt

## Not to be confused with

*[Webbing harness](webbing-harness.md)*, a full system of straps and pouches around the torso rather than a single belt.
