# Beaked plague mask

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: printing · western kingdoms*

A leather mask covering the whole head, with glass eyeholes and a long curved beak packed with dried herbs, flowers and spices. Doctors wear it when pestilence comes, believing the herbs shield them from the poisoned air. At the very least, they cover the stench of the sickroom. The patients see a great black bird bending over them, and some of them scream.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +1 END against foul air and smells
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA frightens patients; −1 Perception narrow eyeholes
