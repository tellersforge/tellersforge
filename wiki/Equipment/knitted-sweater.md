# Knitted sweater

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · fishing villages*

A heavy sweater knitted tight from wool that still has the sheep's natural oil in it. Fishing families knit them in patterns that belong to a single village or clan. Such a sweater is warm when dry and stays warm when soaked through by waves, which is exactly why sailors and fishermen wear nothing else in winter.

- **Slot:** torso
- **Bonus:** +2 END against cold even when wet
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —

- **Also known as:** jumper, fisher's jersey
