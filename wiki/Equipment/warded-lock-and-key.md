# Warded lock and key

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: iron · western kingdoms*

An iron lock box on a door or chest, with metal ridges called wards inside that block any key without matching cuts. Its key is heavy and often ornate, hanging at a steward's belt with a dozen others. It is the lock of castles, manor houses and prison doors. A thief with a skeleton key can often get past the wards, which is why the rich keep a dog as well.

*[Padlock](padlock.md)*, which is portable and hangs on a hasp; the warded lock is fixed into a door or chest.
