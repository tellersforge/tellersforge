# Music player

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: computing · modern*

A slim pocket device and a pair of earphones, holding more songs than a person could listen to in a year. It makes long dull work easier: sorting, walking, waiting, sweeping. It also makes the listener deaf to the world, so footsteps, warnings and shouting all go unheard until they are too close.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Endurance long dull tasks
- **Penalty:** −2 Perception with earphones in
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** pocket music player, earbuds
