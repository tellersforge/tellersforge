# Surgeon's instrument roll

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: bronze · river kingdoms*

A roll of soft leather that opens flat to show rows of slim tools in stitched pockets: scalpels, probes, forceps, hooks, needles and a small saw, in bronze or later in polished steel. It is the surgeon's whole trade in one bundle, tied with a thong. Owning a good set says the owner has studied; knowing when not to use it says rather more. The tools are only as clean as the last person who washed them.
