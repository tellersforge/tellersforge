# Paracord hank

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: computing · modern*

A neat bundle of thin, strong synthetic cord with a woven sheath and a core of loose inner strands. Pulled apart, the inner strands become fishing line, sewing thread or snare wire. The whole cord lashes shelters, hangs food from bears, replaces bootlaces and repairs packs. Soldiers and campers braid it into bracelets so they never go without.

- **Bonus:** +1 Survival: Forest (Woodcraft) improvising shelter or snares
- **Penalty:** 0

- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 1 S

## Not to be confused with

*[Nylon Climbing Rope](nylon-climbing-rope.md)*, which holds a falling climber; paracord ties and snares but won't bear a body.
