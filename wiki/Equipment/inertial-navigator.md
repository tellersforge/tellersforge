# Inertial navigator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: computing · modern*

A sealed box of spinning gyroscopes and sensitive accelerometers that feels every turn, climb and step and adds them up into a position. It needs no stars, no radio and no satellites, which is why submarines, deep miners and missiles carry one. It is always a little wrong, and the error grows with time, so a careful user corrects it against a known landmark whenever one appears.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Wayfinding without outside signals
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Wayfinding](../Skills/wayfinding.md), [Sea Navigation](../Skills/sea-navigation.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** dead-reckoning unit

## Not to be confused with

the *[Satellite Locator](satellite-locator.md)*, which is more accurate but useless underground, underwater or when the signal is jammed.
