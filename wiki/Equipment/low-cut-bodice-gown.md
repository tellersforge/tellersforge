# Low-cut bodice gown

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · western courts*

A fashionable gown with a tight, stiffened bodice cut low across the breast, full skirts and trimmed sleeves, the dress of balls, card parties and evening salons. It draws attention and conversation, and a dance partner rarely forgets it. In front of a priest or a gathering of generals, it causes quiet scandal. It gives little warmth.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 Charm & Seduction at a ball or salon; +1 Carousing at a party
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA before clergy or at a war council; −1 END against cold
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** gunpowder · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** evening gown

## Not to be confused with

*[Velvet Court Gown](velvet-court-gown.md)*, the heavy formal gown required by protocol, far more respectable and far less charming.
