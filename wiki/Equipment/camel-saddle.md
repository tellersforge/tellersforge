# Camel saddle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · desert peoples*

A wooden frame padded with blankets and cushions, set in front of, over or behind the camel's hump depending on the local style. Desert peoples ride long distances on them, rocking with the camel's strange rolling gait. Riders unused to a camel find it a seasick experience until they learn to sway with it. Saddlebags and water skins hang from the frame.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Riding camel riding
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Riding](../Skills/riding.md)
- **Weight:** 8 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 25 S
- **Also known as:** riding saddle

## Not to be confused with

the *[Pack Saddle](pack-saddle.md)*, which carries loads rather than a rider.
