# Headlamp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: computing · modern*

A small light on an elastic band around the head, shining wherever the wearer looks and leaving both hands free. Cavers, climbers, miners and mechanics swear by it, because climbing a rock face or fixing an engine in the dark needs two hands and eyes that can see.
