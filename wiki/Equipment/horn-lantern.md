# Horn lantern

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: iron · western kingdoms*

A candle set in a metal frame paned with sheets of horn scraped thin enough to glow. It keeps the flame safe from wind and rain, and a small shutter lets the bearer hide the light or aim it. Watchmen, stable hands and burglars all find a use for that shutter.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Stealth closing the shutter
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** shuttered lantern, dark lantern

## Not to be confused with

*[Oil lantern](oil-lantern.md)*, the later glass-chimneyed kind, much brighter but harder to hide.
