# Breathing apparatus

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: computing · modern*

A full face mask joined by a hose to a steel or composite tank strapped on the back. It brings its own air, so it works in smoke, in poison, in a sealed vault where the air has long since gone bad. Firefighters and rescue crews wear them into burning buildings, watching the gauge and counting minutes. The tank is heavy and awkward, and every breath comes with a mechanical hiss that can be heard from the next room.
