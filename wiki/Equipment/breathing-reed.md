# Breathing reed

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: stone tools · any*

A hollow reed, a length of cane or a thin tube, held between the lips while the rest of its owner lies flat beneath the surface of a pond or a slow river. Only the tip shows, and it looks like any other stalk among the rushes. Fugitives, ambushers and children playing at being fugitives all know the trick. It works only just under the surface, in calm water, and it needs a steady nerve when searchers wade past.
