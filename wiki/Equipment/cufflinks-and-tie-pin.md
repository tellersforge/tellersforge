# Cufflinks and tie pin

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: steam · modern*

Small matched fittings of gold, enamel, silver or gems, used to fasten the cuffs of a formal shirt and pin a tie in place. They are the finishing touch of formal dress, and at a dinner, a wedding or an awards night, a knowing eye notices them. They are small, but they show that the wearer knows how things are done.
