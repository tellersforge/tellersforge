# Mechanical calculator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A heavy desk machine of brass gears, keys and a side crank, clanking and whirring as it works. Its operator sets the numbers, turns the crank, and reads the answer in a row of little wheels. It never makes a mistake and never tires, which made clerks and accountants love it. It is too heavy to carry anywhere, and it fills an office with a steady mechanical clatter.
