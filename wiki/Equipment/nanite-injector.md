# Nanite injector

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: far future · far future*

A slender injector holding a dose of microscopic machines in a silver fluid. Once in the blood, they spread through the body, find torn vessels, broken bone and damaged organs, and repair them from within. The patient feels warm and heavy while it happens. The machines break down and pass out of the body afterwards, or so the makers promise, and most people choose to believe them.
