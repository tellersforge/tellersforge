# Fur hat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · steppe riders*

A thick cap of fox, wolf or sheepskin fur, with long flaps that tie down over the ears and under the chin when the wind begins to bite. On the steppe in winter, it is the difference between keeping one's ears and losing them. With the flaps down, the world goes quiet and muffled.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +2 END against cold
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception ear flaps down
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.4 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** fur cap, ear-flap hat

## Not to be confused with

*[Felt Cap](felt-cap.md)*, a lighter cap of pressed wool for milder weather, without the fur and ear flaps.
