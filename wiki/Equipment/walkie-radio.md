# Walkie radio

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: electricity · modern*

A handheld two-way radio with a stubby aerial and a button to press and talk. It lets a small team keep in contact across a building, a construction site or a stretch of hills. Security guards, film crews and soldiers carry them on their belts. They hiss, crackle and squawk at unfortunate moments, which is why careful users turn the volume down before creeping anywhere.
