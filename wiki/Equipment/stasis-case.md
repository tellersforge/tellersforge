# Stasis case

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: far future · far future*

A case whose interior is wrapped in a field that slows time almost to a stop. Fresh food stays fresh, samples do not decay, and a wounded limb or an organ for transplant stays exactly as it was when the lid closed. It is precious and costly, and doctors, explorers and the very rich rely on it.
