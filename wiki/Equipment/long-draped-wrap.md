# Long draped wrap

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · river kingdoms*

A single uncut length of cloth, many paces long, wrapped around the waist, pleated at the front and thrown over one shoulder. It is cool, graceful and endlessly adaptable, and the way it is draped can tell a knowing eye where the wearer comes from. It is the proper dress for family feasts and temple days. Running in it means hitching it up first.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 END in heat; +1 Etiquette at family and temple occasions
- **Penalty:** −1 Running unless tucked up
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.8 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** sari, draped robe
