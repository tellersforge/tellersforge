# Notebook

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: printing · any*

A small bound book of blank or ruled pages, with a pencil tucked into the spine or a strap to keep it shut. Travellers keep diaries in it, artists sketches, and investigators names, times, places and the odd little detail that later turns out to matter. It fits in a coat pocket. Losing it is a disaster; having it read by the wrong person can be worse.

## Game block

- **Penalty:** 0

*[Account Ledger](account-ledger.md)*, which is a formal book of accounts; the notebook is for private notes, sketches and clues.
