# Woven sandals

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: agriculture · any*

Cheap, simple sandals woven from straw, reed, palm leaf or rope, tied on with cords. They are cool and comfortable, cost nothing, and wear out in a few days of travel, so travellers carry spares on the belt and leave old pairs by the roadside. On sharp rock, they are thin and slippery.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 END in heat
- **Penalty:** −1 Climbing on rough rock
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** straw sandals, rope sandals, reed sandals

## Not to be confused with

*[Leather Sandals](leather-sandals.md)*, which last far longer and are the common shoe of warm lands with good roads.
