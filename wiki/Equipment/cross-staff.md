# Cross staff

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: printing · sea peoples*

A long wooden rod marked with scales, with a crosspiece that slides along it. The navigator puts one end to his cheek, moves the crosspiece until one tip touches the horizon and the other the sun or a star, and reads off the height. It is cheap, sturdy and plain, and every ship's mate can make one. Sighting straight into the noon sun blinds more than a few old navigators in one eye.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Sea Navigation finding latitude
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Sea Navigation](../Skills/sea-navigation.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** jacob's staff, kamal

## Not to be confused with

the *[Sextant](sextant.md)*, which uses mirrors and does the same job far more precisely, without staring into the sun.
