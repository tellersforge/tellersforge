# Leg wrappings

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · any*

Long strips of wool or linen wound in a spiral from ankle to knee, sometimes held by a garter or crossed thongs. Soldiers, herdsmen and pilgrims swear by them: they support tired calves, keep grit and stones out of the shoes, and stop brambles tearing the shins. After a long day's march, a good wrap is the difference between walking and limping.

- **Slot:** legs
- **Bonus:** +1 Endurance on long marches
- **Penalty:** 0
