# Pop-up shelter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: computing · modern*

A tent sewn around a coiled spring frame that is kept twisted in a round flat bag. Pull it out and let go, and it leaps into shape with a noise like a startled bird. It keeps off rain and is loved by the tired and the lazy. It is less loved in strong wind, which it catches like a sail and tries to carry off, and packing it away again is a puzzle few solve on the first try.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 END against rain
- **Penalty:** −1 END in strong wind
