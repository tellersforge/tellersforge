# Multimeter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A small box with a dial, a readout and two long probes on cables, red and black. Touch the probes to a circuit and it tells how much current flows, how much force pushes it, and whether a wire is whole or broken. Electricians and repairers use it to find faults quickly. It will not fix anything, but it saves hours of guessing.
