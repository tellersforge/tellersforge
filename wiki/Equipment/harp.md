# Harp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: bronze · any*

A tall frame of carved and polished wood, strung with rows of gut or wire strings from the curved neck down to the sound box. The harper sits with the instrument leaning back on a shoulder and plays with both hands, drawing out clear, rippling chords. It is expensive, heavy and slow to tune, and it belongs in halls and temples more than on the road.
