# Cipher wheel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: printing · any*

Two flat rings of wood, brass or card, one inside the other, each marked with the alphabet round its edge. Turning the inner ring to an agreed setting lines up each letter with its secret substitute, and writing a message in code becomes a matter of reading across. Spies, soldiers and schoolchildren passing notes have all used them. It is simple to use and simple, for a patient expert, to break.
