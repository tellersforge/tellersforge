# Bosun's pipe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: gunpowder · sailors*

A small curved pipe of silver or brass with a ball at one end, held in the hand and blown with the fingers opening and closing over the hole. Its high, wavering trills cut through wind, waves and shouting on a crowded deck, and every call has its meaning: all hands, hoist away, pipe down. Sailors learn the calls as they learn the ropes. It is also played to welcome important visitors aboard.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Sailing & Seamanship giving orders aboard
- **Penalty:** 0

## Not to be confused with

*[Whistle](whistle.md)*, which gives a single sharp blast; the bosun's pipe plays a set of trills that mean particular orders at sea.
