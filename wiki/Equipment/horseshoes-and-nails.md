# Horseshoes and nails

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · any*

A set of iron shoes shaped to a horse's hooves, with square nails and a rasp for trimming and fitting. Shod horses can travel on hard, stony roads that would soon split and lame an unshod hoof. Any rider on a long journey carries a spare set, because a lost shoe on a rocky road means walking.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Endurance mount on stony roads
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Blacksmithing](../Skills/blacksmithing.md), [Veterinary Care](../Skills/veterinary-care.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** farrier's kit

## Not to be confused with

the *[Grooming Kit](grooming-kit.md)*, which keeps the horse clean and calm rather than protecting its hooves.
