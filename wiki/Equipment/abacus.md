# Abacus

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: writing · any*

A wooden frame strung with rods, each carrying a row of beads that slide back and forth. In skilled hands it clicks and rattles through sums faster than most people can write them down. Merchants, tax collectors and shopkeepers have used it for centuries. It never runs out of power and never lies, though it only remembers the answer until someone knocks it.
