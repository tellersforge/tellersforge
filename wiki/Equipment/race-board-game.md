# Race board game

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: writing · any*

A board carved, painted or scratched into a slab, with a track of squares or holes from start to finish and a set of tokens for each player. Throwing sticks or small cubes decide how far each piece moves, and landing on an opponent sends them back. It is a game of luck with a little cunning, played in palaces, taverns and on the lids of coffins.
