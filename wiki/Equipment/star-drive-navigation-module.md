# Star-drive navigation module

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: far future · far future*

A sealed computer core, often the most guarded part of a starship, that knows the shape of the space between stars and calculates jumps across it. A jump plotted badly can end inside a planet, a sun or nowhere at all, so the module checks and rechecks, drawing on charts that are themselves precious. Crews treat it with the reverence older sailors gave their charts, and pirates value a captured one above the cargo.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +3 Astrogation plotting faster-than-light jumps
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Astrogation](../Skills/astrogation.md)
- **Weight:** 20 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 60 S
- **Also known as:** jump computer

## Not to be confused with

the *[Orbital Nav Computer](orbital-nav-computer.md)*, which handles orbits and burns within one star system but can't plot a jump between stars.
