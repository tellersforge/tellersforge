# Gravity-grip pads

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: far future · far future*

Pads worn on the palms and the soles of the feet, each with a tiny gravity projector inside. Switched on, they pull the wearer toward whatever surface they touch, so that a wall becomes a floor and a ceiling becomes a place to stand. It takes practice to walk up a tower without feeling sick. The power cells drain faster than expected, usually at the worst possible height.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +3 Climbing any surface
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 40 S
- **Also known as:** wall walkers

## Not to be confused with

*[Grip Gloves](grip-gloves.md)*, which let the hands cling; gravity-grip pads let the whole body walk up walls.
