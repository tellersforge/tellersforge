# Festival mask

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: stone tools · any*

A painted mask of carved wood, papier-mache or leather, often with horns, feathers or bright teeth, worn in dances, carnivals and processions. In the festival the wearer becomes the spirit, demon or hero the mask portrays, and nobody asks who is behind it. The eyeholes cut off the sides of the world. Worn outside the festival, it is frightening or ridiculous.

- **Slot:** face
