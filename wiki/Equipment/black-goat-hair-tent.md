# Black goat-hair tent

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: iron · desert peoples*

A long, low tent of dark cloth woven from goat hair, stretched over rows of short poles and guyed down with ropes. In the heat, the loose weave lets the air through and the shade is deep. When rain comes, the hair swells and the cloth closes tight. It shrugs off sun and blowing sand, and the herding families who live in it add a new strip each year and cut away the oldest.
