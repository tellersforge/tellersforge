# Hair ornaments

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · eastern empire*

Decorative pins, combs and sticks of lacquer, jade, silver or tortoiseshell pushed into dressed hair, often dangling with beads or tiny chains. At court, at a tea gathering or at a formal visit, they show refinement and good upbringing. In old stories, a long hairpin can be drawn and used as a slim dagger in a pinch.

## Not to be confused with

*[Diadem](diadem.md)*, which is worn across the brow as a badge of rank; hair ornaments are set in the hair and show refinement.
