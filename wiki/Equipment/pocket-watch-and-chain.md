# Pocket watch and chain

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A gold or silver watch in a hinged case, hung on a chain draped across the waistcoat. Businessmen, bankers and railway officials consult it with a flourish, and it marks the owner as a man of affairs who values time and has money. It also marks him as worth robbing, and pickpockets watch the chain with interest.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 CHA among businessmen
- **Penalty:** −1 Streetwise draws pickpockets
- **Used for:** —
