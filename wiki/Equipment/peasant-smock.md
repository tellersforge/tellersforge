# Peasant smock

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · farming villages*

A long, loose smock of coarse linen, gathered in tight rows across the chest and shoulders so it hangs full and never binds. Farmers wear it in the field to keep the dirt off the clothes beneath, and it goes on over everything in the barn and the yard. It is honest work clothing, and wholly out of place at a lord's table.
