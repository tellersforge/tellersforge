# Pencil

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: printing · any*

A thin stick of graphite set in a casing of wood, sharpened with a knife. It writes on almost anything, in rain and cold, needs no ink, and its marks can be rubbed out. Surveyors, soldiers, reporters and artists keep one behind the ear. It is cheap enough to lose without grief, which is just as well, since everyone loses them all the time.
