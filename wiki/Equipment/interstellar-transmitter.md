# Interstellar transmitter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: far future · far future*

A great installation of dishes, coils and humming machinery, big enough to fill a hall or the hold of a ship, that sends messages between the stars faster than light. It links colonies to homeworlds and fleets to their admirals. Messages are costly and precious, and often queue for days. Whoever controls the transmitter controls what news reaches a whole star system.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Communications & Sensors between star systems
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Communications & Sensors](../Skills/communications-and-sensors.md), [Signals](../Skills/signals.md)
- **Weight:** 20 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 500 G
- **Also known as:** star-relay set, faster-than-light radio

## Not to be confused with

*[Radio Set](radio-set.md)*, which reaches only across a battlefield or planet; the interstellar transmitter reaches between star systems.
