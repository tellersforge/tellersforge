# Snakebite kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: steam · any*

A flat tin case holding a rubber suction cup, a small blade, a length of cord and a vial or tablets of antivenom. Surveyors, soldiers in the tropics and farmers in snake country carry one and hope never to open it. The instructions are printed inside the lid, in case the owner is too frightened to remember them. What really saves the bitten is calm, speed and knowing which snake it was.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Poisons & Antidotes against snake and insect venom
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [First Aid](../Skills/first-aid.md), [Poisons & Antidotes](../Skills/poisons-and-antidotes.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** venom kit

## Not to be confused with

*[Antidote Kit](antidote-kit.md)*, which treats swallowed poisons; the snakebite kit is made for venom from bites and stings.
