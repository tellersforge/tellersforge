# Wok

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: iron · eastern empire*

A wide, thin iron pan with a rounded bottom and a pair of handles or a long one, made to sit in the mouth of a fierce fire. Food goes in, is tossed and stirred with a ladle and comes out seconds later, crisp and smoky. The same pan boils noodles, deep-fries dumplings and, with a lid and a rack, steams fish. It uses little fuel and cooks for many, which is why street cooks and army kitchens alike love it.
