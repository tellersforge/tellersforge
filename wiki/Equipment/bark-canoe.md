# Bark canoe

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

A long, narrow canoe of sheets of bark sewn together over ribs and gunwales of split wood, the seams sealed with pitch. It is light enough for two people to carry between rivers and lakes on their shoulders, and fast and steady in skilled hands. Trappers, traders and whole families travel great distances in it. A sharp rock can open its side.
