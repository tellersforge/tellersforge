# Leather sandals

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: bronze · any*

A sole of thick leather tied to the foot with thongs between the toes and around the ankle, the common shoe of warm lands for thousands of years. They are cool, light, easy to walk and run in, and last a long time on roads and hard ground. When the frost comes, they are cold comfort.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 Running on roads and hard ground
- **Penalty:** −1 END against cold
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** thong sandals

## Not to be confused with

*[Hobnailed Sandals](hobnailed-sandals.md)*, heavy marching sandals with iron studs, made for soldiers on the road rather than for everyday wear.
