# Cheese wheel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: agriculture · any*

A great round hard cheese sealed in its own rind, or in wax or cloth. It keeps for months on a journey and grows richer as it ages. Travellers carve slices off with a knife as they go. It is heavy, but good food on the road is worth its weight.
