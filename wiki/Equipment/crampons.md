# Crampons

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: iron · mountain folk*

Frames of steel spikes strapped beneath the boots, some pointing down, some jutting forward from the toe. On ice and hard snow they give grip where a boot would slide, and they let a climber kick into a frozen wall and stand. On bare rock they scrape and skitter, and they clank loudly across stone floors. Running in them is awkward and dangerous.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Climbing on ice; +1 Balance on ice
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth on stone floors; −1 Running on bare ground
- **Used for:** [Mountaineering](../Skills/mountaineering.md), [Snow & Ice Travel](../Skills/snow-and-ice-travel.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** ice spikes

## Not to be confused with

*[Snowshoes](snowshoes.md)*, which spread weight on soft snow; crampons bite into ice and hard-packed snow.
