# Hammock

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: iron · island peoples*

A net of knotted cord or a length of stout cloth, slung between two trees, posts or ship's beams. It lifts the sleeper above the mud, the flood, the snakes and the ants of the swamp and the jungle. Sailors sleep in hammocks at sea because they swing with the ship. On a cold night, however, the wind blows through underneath, and the sleeper freezes from the back up.
