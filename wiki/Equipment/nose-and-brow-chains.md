# Nose and brow chains

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · desert peoples*

Fine chains of silver or gold running from a ring in the nose to the ear, the hairline or an ornament at the brow. Desert brides wear them on their wedding day, and dancers at festivals set them swinging. They are lovely in firelight and faintly musical. That music is the trouble: nobody wearing them moves quietly.
