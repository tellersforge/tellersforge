# Pump water filter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: computing · modern*

A hand pump with an intake hose, or a thick sipping straw, with a cartridge of very fine filter inside. Drop the hose in a stream, work the handle and clean water trickles out the other end, free of grit and of the tiny living things that turn a traveller's gut to water. It works fast and tastes of nothing. The cartridge clogs in muddy water and has to be scrubbed or swapped.
