# Pack cart

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: wheel and roads · any*

A light two-wheeled cart with shafts, pulled by hand or pushed, for goods too heavy or bulky to carry. Pedlars, farmers and removers use them on roads, market streets and flat country, where one person can move what would take several on foot. On a mountain path or a stair, it is simply a thing to be left behind.
