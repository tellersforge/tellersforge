# Shovel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: iron · any*

A broad blade of wood or iron on a long handle, for digging, shifting earth and clearing snow. Soldiers dig trenches with it, farmers dig ditches, miners shift rubble and gravediggers do what gravediggers do. It is the least glamorous tool in any army and possibly the most important. A soldier who complains about digging usually changes his mind when the shooting starts.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Construction digging
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Hand Tools](../Skills/hand-tools.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** spade

## Not to be confused with

the *[Digging Stick](digging-stick.md)*, which digs up roots rather than moving earth.
