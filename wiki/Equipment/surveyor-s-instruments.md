# Surveyor's instruments

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A theodolite on a wooden tripod, a measuring chain, a spirit tube for finding the horizontal and a tall marked staff. The surveyor uses them to measure distances, angles and heights across the land, and from those numbers come maps, boundaries, roads and railway lines. Surveyors are often the first people to arrive in a place, and the least welcome, because a surveyor usually means someone is about to claim it.
