# Servant's livery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · noble households*

Clothing in a great house's colours, sometimes with its badge on the sleeve, issued to the footmen, grooms and maids who serve it. In that house it grants the right to come and go by the back stairs; outside it, it passes the wearer through many doors as one more servant nobody looks at twice. The same coat, among the master's equals, commands no respect whatsoever.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Service & Hospitality in the house that issued it; +1 Disguise passing as staff
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA among equals of your master
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** livery, house colours

## Not to be confused with

*[Service Livery](service-livery.md)*, the tidy uniform of later hotels and great houses, which marks staff in the same way in a more modern cut.
