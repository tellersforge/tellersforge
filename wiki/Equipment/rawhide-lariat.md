# Rawhide lariat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: agriculture · steppe riders*

A long rope braided from strips of rawhide, with a small eye at one end through which the rope runs to make a sliding noose. Herders swing it overhead and throw it over the horns of a steer or the neck of a runaway horse. It is heavy enough to fly true and stiff enough to hold its loop open, and a skilled roper makes it look like a living thing.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Nets, Bolas & Lassos for throwing loops
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Nets, Bolas & Lassos](../Weapon_Groups/nets-bolas-and-lassos.md), [Herding & Husbandry](../Skills/herding-and-husbandry.md)
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** riata, braided rawhide rope

## Not to be confused with

*[Hemp Rope](hemp-rope.md)*, which ties and hauls; the lariat is built to be thrown.
