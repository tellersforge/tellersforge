# Sounding line

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: bronze · sea peoples*

A long line knotted or marked with cloth at regular lengths, weighted with a lead plummet whose hollow base is packed with tallow. Cast ahead of a ship, it tells how deep the water runs. Hauled back, the tallow brings up sand, shell or mud, and an old pilot can tell from a pinch of it where on the coast he is. In fog or unknown shallows, the leadsman's chant is the most important voice aboard.

## Not to be confused with

the *[Chip Log](chip-log.md)*, another marked line, which measures a ship's speed rather than the depth below it.
