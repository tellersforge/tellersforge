# Swimsuit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: steam · modern*

A close-fitting garment of knitted wool, then later of stretch fabric, made for bathing and swimming. It lets the wearer move through water freely and without the drag of clothes. On a beach or at a pool it is perfectly ordinary. Anywhere else it causes a stir, and once out of the water the wearer quickly shivers with nothing between their skin and the wind.

## Game block

- **Slot:** torso
- **Bonus:** +1 Swimming while worn
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA away from the beach; −2 END against cold out of the water
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** bathing costume, swim trunks

## Not to be confused with

*[Wetsuit](wetsuit.md)*, which is made of foam rubber and keeps a swimmer warm in cold water; a swimsuit gives no warmth at all.
