# Falcon hood

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · steppe riders*

A small, snug cap of stitched leather, often topped with a plume or tassel, that slips over a hawk's head and covers its eyes. In darkness the bird calms at once, sitting quietly while it is carried through crowds or across country. The falconer removes it at the moment of the hunt, and the bird sees its prey all at once.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Falconry keeping a bird calm
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Falconry](../Skills/falconry.md)
- **Weight:** — · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** hawk hood

## Not to be confused with

the *[Falconry Lure](falconry-lure.md)*, which calls the bird back rather than calming it.
