# Rebreather

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: electricity · modern*

A compact set of bags, canisters and valves strapped to the chest, that takes each breath the diver lets out, scrubs away the stale part, adds a little fresh gas, and hands it back. Almost nothing escapes. A diver wearing one leaves no trail of bubbles on the surface and can stay down far longer than a tank allows. Combat swimmers and cave divers use them, though the set demands care and punishes mistakes.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Stealth underwater, no bubbles; +1 Diving long dives
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Diving](../Skills/diving.md)
- **Weight:** 12 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 25 S

## Not to be confused with

the *[Scuba Set](scuba-set.md)*, which is simpler and cheaper but spills a telltale stream of bubbles with every breath.
