# Signal flare pistol

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: electricity · modern*

A stubby, wide-barrelled pistol that fires a bright flare high into the air, where it hangs or drifts down burning for many seconds. Ships in trouble, lost climbers and downed pilots use them to call for rescue. It is not a weapon, though it can still hurt someone at close range.

*[Road flare](road-flare.md)*, which burns on the ground to warn traffic rather than rising into the sky.
