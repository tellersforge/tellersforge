# Paper lantern

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: writing · eastern empire*

A collapsing frame of bamboo and paper around a candle, casting a soft, wide, warm glow. In the eastern empire they hang in rows at festivals, teahouses and gates. Folded flat, they pack into nothing. In wind or a careless hand, the paper catches and the lantern goes up in a moment.
