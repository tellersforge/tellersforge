# Evening dress

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · modern*

Black-tie finery: a dinner jacket and bow tie, or a long gown with gloves and jewellery, worn for formal dinners, galas, opera and casino nights. It makes the wearer look at home among the rich and gives an easy opening at any party. Worn at a war council or in a muddy field camp it is badly out of place, and no one runs well in patent shoes and a floor-length hem.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 Etiquette at a formal evening function; +1 Charm & Seduction at a party or private meeting
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA at a war council, workshop or field camp; −1 Running while worn
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** tuxedo, dinner jacket, cocktail dress

## Not to be confused with

*[Seductive dress](seductive-dress.md)*, which is cut to attract rather than to meet a formal dress code; evening dress is correct first and alluring second.
