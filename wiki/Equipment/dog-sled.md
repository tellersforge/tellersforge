# Dog sled

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

A long, light sled of wood and bone, lashed with sinew and runners of bone or wood, drawn by a team of dogs in harness. The driver stands on the back runners or runs alongside, shouting orders to the lead dog. On good snow a team covers huge distances in a day, carrying people, food and goods across frozen land where nothing else can go.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Snow & Ice Travel covering distance
- **Penalty:** 0

- **Weight:** 25 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 3 S
