# Sleeve guards

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · scribes and clerks*

A pair of simple tubes of cloth or thin leather pulled up over the forearms from wrist to elbow. Scribes, clerks and copyists wear them at the desk to keep ink, sand and wax from staining their sleeves, and to stop the cuffs from smearing the page. They are unheroic and indispensable.

## Game block

- **Slot:** hands
- **Bonus:** +1 Calligraphy & Scribing keeping ink off sleeves
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** oversleeves

## Not to be confused with

*[Archer's Bracer](archer-s-bracer.md)*, a stiff guard for one forearm worn to stop a bowstring, not to protect the sleeve.
