# Hobnailed sandals

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · imperial legions*

Heavy open boots of thick leather, laced up the ankle, their soles studded with dozens of iron nails. They are made for marching: legions wear them across entire empires, and the nails give grip on dirt roads and long life to the soles. On paving stone they ring like a hammer and slip like ice.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 Endurance on long marches
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth noisy on stone; −1 Balance on smooth stone
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** marching sandals

## Not to be confused with

*[Leather Sandals](leather-sandals.md)*, lighter everyday sandals, quiet and quick but made for a stroll rather than a march.
