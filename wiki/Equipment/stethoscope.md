# Stethoscope

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: steam · any*

A pair of earpieces joined by rubber tubes to a flat chest-piece. Pressed to the body, it lets a doctor hear the heart's rhythm, the rattle in a chest and the gurgle of a troubled gut. Worn round the neck, it is the universal sign of the physician. It tells a trained ear a great deal and an untrained ear almost nothing at all.
