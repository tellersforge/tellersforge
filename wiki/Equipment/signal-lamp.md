# Signal lamp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: electricity · any*

A heavy electric lamp with a shutter of slats that open and close, sending flashes of light in coded patterns. Ships use it to talk to each other at night without breaking radio silence, and posts use it to talk across valleys. A skilled operator sends and reads quickly. The beam is narrow, so only those it is aimed at can see the message, though anyone watching knows someone is signalling.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Signals at night
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Signals](../Skills/signals.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** shuttered lamp

## Not to be confused with

*[Signal Flags](signal-flags.md)*, which work only in daylight; the signal lamp is at its best at night.
