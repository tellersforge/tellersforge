# Incense clock

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: writing · eastern empire*

A long, winding trail of powdered incense laid in a groove of ash, or a thick candle marked with rings. It burns at a known pace, and the smouldering point shows how much time has passed. In an eastern empire's temples and scholars' studies it measured meditation, study and sleep. Some had threads with small weights that dropped as the fire reached them, ringing a bell. Anyone nearby can smell it burning.
