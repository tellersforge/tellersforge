# Clay cooking pot

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: pottery · any*

A round-bellied pot of fired clay, blackened on the outside from a lifetime in the coals. It is set among the embers or on three stones, filled with grain, beans, roots and whatever the hunt provided, and left to simmer for hours. Stews taste better in it, and every household believes theirs is the best. It is heavy for its size and breaks if dropped, so travellers leave it at home and village cooks treasure it.
