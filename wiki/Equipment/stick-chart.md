# Stick chart

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: stone tools · island peoples*

A lattice of thin sticks lashed together, with small shells tied at certain crossings. The curves and straight lines show how ocean swells bend around islands, and the shells mark the islands themselves. It stays on shore; the navigator memorises it and then reads the swells through the hull at sea. To anyone else it looks like a craft piece.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Sea Navigation between known islands
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Wayfinding](../Skills/wayfinding.md), [Sea Navigation](../Skills/sea-navigation.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** swell chart

## Not to be confused with

*[Sea Chart](sea-chart.md)*, which is drawn and printed and shows coasts and depths rather than the patterns of the swell.
