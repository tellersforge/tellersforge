# Locket

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: printing · any*

A small hinged pendant, usually on a chain, holding a painted portrait, a photograph, a curl of hair or a folded note. It is opened in private and closed quickly. Soldiers carry them to war, sailors carry them to sea, and prisoners are sometimes allowed to keep them when everything else is taken. A glance inside steadies the hand and reminds the wearer what they are trying to get home to.
