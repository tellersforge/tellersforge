# Folding stove

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: electricity · modern*

A little stamped-metal stand that folds flat into a pocket, with a hollow for a tablet of solid fuel. Lit with a match, the tablet burns with a pale, almost invisible flame and a faint chemical smell. It is enough to heat a mug of tea or warm a tin of rations for one, not to cook a proper meal. Soldiers carry it because it weighs nothing and needs nothing but its tablets.
