# Pilgrim's cloak

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · any faith*

A plain, broad cloak of undyed wool, sometimes with a short shoulder cape, sewn with the badge of a shrine: a shell, a key, a palm leaf. It says the wearer is on a holy journey, and in many lands that means a bowl of soup and a place in the barn from people who would slam the door on any other stranger. It keeps off the rain on the long road.
