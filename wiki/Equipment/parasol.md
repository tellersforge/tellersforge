# Parasol

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · imperial courts*

A light umbrella of painted silk or oiled paper on bamboo ribs, carried over the head against the sun. In imperial courts the colour and number of tiers marks the owner's rank as much as it shades them, and servants carry it for great lords. In high society a lady or gentleman would never go out without one. Opened, it is hard to miss.
