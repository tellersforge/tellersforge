# Data spike

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A small, sharp-edged plug no bigger than a finger, loaded with a single piece of hostile code. The moment it touches a data port, it runs its program: unlocking a door, copying records or shutting down a camera. It is quick, quiet and requires almost no skill. If a system has no open port to plug into, the spike is just a small piece of useless metal.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Hacking through a physical port
- **Penalty:** −1 Hacking against systems with no open port
- **Used for:** [Hacking](../Skills/hacking.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** port spike

## Not to be confused with

the *[Hacking Deck](hacking-deck.md)*, a full portable workshop for attacking any system, not just one through a port.
