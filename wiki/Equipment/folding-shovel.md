# Folding shovel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: steam · modern*

A short spade whose steel blade folds flat against its handle or locks at a right angle to make a pick. It digs latrines, fire pits, drainage ditches round a tent and trenches when the shooting starts. Soldiers carry it on the belt, and some sharpen the edge. It is also good for digging a stuck wheel out of mud, which is what it spends most of its life doing.
