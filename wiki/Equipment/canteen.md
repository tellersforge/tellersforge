# Canteen

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: iron · any*

A flattened bottle with a stopper and a shoulder strap, made of tin, turned wood, stitched leather or a covered metal flask. It holds a day's careful drinking and rides at the hip, where it sloshes and bangs against everything. Soldiers, pilgrims and caravan guards all carry one, and all learn the same lesson: fill it at every well, whether you are thirsty or not.
