# Straw rain cape

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: agriculture · eastern villages*

A cape of layered straw, rice stalks or palm fibre, woven at the neck and hanging loose below like the thatch of a roof. Rain runs off it and never reaches the wearer beneath. Farmers work in paddies through the wet season wrapped in them. It rustles with every step, which is no concern in a rice field.

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +2 END against rain
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth rustles
