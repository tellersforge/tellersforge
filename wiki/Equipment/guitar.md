# Guitar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: printing · any*

A flat-backed, waisted wooden body with a long fretted neck and six strings of gut or steel. It is strummed for chords or plucked for melody, and it goes everywhere: street corners, taverns, campfires, and under balconies at midnight. It is easier to learn than most instruments and harder to master. A player with a guitar and a good voice has a reliable way into many hearts.
