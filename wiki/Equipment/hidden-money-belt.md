# Hidden money belt

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: writing · any*

A flat cloth or leather pouch worn under the clothes against the skin, holding coins, papers or gems where no pickpocket can reach them. Travellers, pilgrims and smugglers all trust it. Getting at the money means a trip behind a tree or a closed door, which is rather the point.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Smuggling & Concealment hiding coin on the body
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** body pouch

## Not to be confused with

*[Belt pouch](belt-pouch.md)*, which keeps money handy on the outside of the clothes and is much easier to steal.
