# Apothecary chest

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: gunpowder · any*

A wooden chest opening into rows of labelled jars and drawers: powders, pills, tinctures and dried leaves, with a small brass balance and a mortar and pestle fitted into the lid. It is a dispensary that travels, carried by physicians on campaigns and by apothecaries to country fairs. With everything to hand, the maker can weigh out and compound almost any remedy of the day. It is heavy, fragile and a very tempting thing to steal.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Remedies & Pharmacy with the full chest to hand
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Remedies & Pharmacy](../Skills/remedies-and-pharmacy.md), [Medicine](../Skills/medicine.md)
- **Weight:** 8 kg · **Needs:** gunpowder · **Price:** 25 S
- **Also known as:** medicine chest, dispensary box

## Not to be confused with

*[Herb Pouch](herb-pouch.md)*, which carries a few local plants; the chest is a whole shop of prepared medicines.
