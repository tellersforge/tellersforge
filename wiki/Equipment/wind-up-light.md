# Wind-up light

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: computing · modern*

A lantern or torch that charges from a hand crank or a small sun panel, needing no batteries at all. A minute of winding gives a good while of light. Relief workers, remote villages and preppers keep them, knowing they will work long after every other light has died.
