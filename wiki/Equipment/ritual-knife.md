# Ritual knife

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: bronze · any*

A consecrated blade of bronze, iron or stone, often with a carved handle, used only for cutting herbs, slicing offerings and drawing circles on the ground or in the air. It is never used to fight or to cut food. Priests and hedge witches keep it wrapped and apart from ordinary blades, and the taboo against using it for anything else is fiercely kept.
