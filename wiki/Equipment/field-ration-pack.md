# Field ration pack

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: electricity · modern*

A sealed packet holding a day's meals for a soldier: main dishes, biscuits, sweets, drink powder and a flameless heater that warms food with a splash of water. It is not fine cooking, but it is hot, filling and safe wherever the soldier happens to be. Trading the good bits is a camp tradition.
