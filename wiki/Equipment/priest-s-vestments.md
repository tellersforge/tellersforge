# Priest's vestments

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · temple faiths*

Layered robes prescribed by a faith for those who lead worship: an under-robe, an embroidered over-robe, stoles or collars in the colours of the season, all heavy with thread of gold or silver. When the priest wears them, the rite begins and the faithful fall quiet. They are hard to move in and impossible to mistake for anything else.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 Ritual & Liturgy leading rites; +1 CHA before the faithful
- **Penalty:** −1 DEX heavy layers; −2 Disguise unmistakable
- **Used for:** [Ritual & Liturgy](../Skills/ritual-and-liturgy.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** vestments, liturgical robes

## Not to be confused with

*[Monk's Habit](monk-s-habit.md)*, the plain everyday robe of a religious order, worn for a life of prayer rather than for leading rites.
