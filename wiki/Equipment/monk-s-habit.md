# Monk's habit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · monastic orders*

A plain robe of undyed wool, with a deep hood and a rope cord tied at the waist, sometimes knotted in the vows the wearer has taken. It is the everyday dress of the monastery, made for prayer, labour and silence. On the road, it makes its wearer a familiar and unremarkable figure. In a tavern, every drinker notices it.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Meditation & Concentration in retreat; +1 Disguise passing unremarked as clergy
- **Penalty:** −1 Carousing in taverns
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** cassock, robe and cowl

## Not to be confused with

*[Mendicant's Robe](mendicant-s-robe.md)*, the patched robe of monks who wander and beg, rather than living within a cloister.
