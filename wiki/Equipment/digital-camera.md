# Digital camera

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: computing · modern*

A compact camera with a screen on the back, storing thousands of images on a memory card and showing each one the moment it is taken. The photographer can shoot a hundred frames of a single moment and keep the best. Reporters, tourists and investigators carry one everywhere. It needs charged batteries; when they die, the camera is only a fancy paperweight.

- **Penalty:** 0

*[Film Camera](film-camera.md)*, which records on film to be developed later; the digital camera shows its pictures at once.
