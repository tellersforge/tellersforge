# Laser communicator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A compact unit that sends messages along a narrow beam of invisible light to a matching receiver, ship to ship or post to post. Nothing leaks sideways, so a listener would have to stand in the beam itself to catch a word. Military vessels and secret posts depend on it. Anything in the way, from a moon to a cloud of dust, breaks the line and the message with it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Cryptography messages hard to intercept
- **Penalty:** −2 Communications & Sensors without line of sight
- **Used for:** [Communications & Sensors](../Skills/communications-and-sensors.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 20 S
- **Also known as:** light link

## Not to be confused with

*[Entangled Message Pair](entangled-message-pair.md)*, which works at any distance without a line of sight; the laser communicator needs a clear straight path.
