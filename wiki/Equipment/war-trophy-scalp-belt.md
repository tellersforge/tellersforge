# War trophy scalp belt

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

A belt hung with trophies taken from defeated enemies: scalps, teeth, braids or small tokens, each with a story the wearer is ready to tell. Among raiding peoples it is a record of courage, and enemies who see it know exactly who they face. In a peaceful village or a city street it is horrifying, and hosts may not let the wearer through the door.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Intimidation over enemies
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA among peaceful peoples or cities
