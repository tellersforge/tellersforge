# Howdah

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · river kingdoms*

A carriage or covered platform strapped onto an elephant's back, with room for several people. On state occasions it is gilded, curtained and hung with bells. In war it is plain, sturdy and bristling with archers or spearmen who shoot down from a height no one on the ground can reach. The mahout rides on the elephant's neck below and does the steering.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Mounted Combat shooting from elephant-back
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Riding](../Skills/riding.md)
- **Weight:** 80 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 60 S
- **Also known as:** elephant castle
