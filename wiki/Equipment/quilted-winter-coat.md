# Quilted winter coat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · eastern empire*

A long coat made of two layers of cloth with cotton wadding or silk floss stitched between them in rows, the winter coat of an empire's cities and villages alike. It is warm without fur, and the quilting keeps the stuffing from sliding to the hem. The sleeves are fat and loose, which keeps the hands warm inside them but makes any fiddly task awkward.
