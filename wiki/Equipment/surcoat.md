# Surcoat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · knightly orders*

A long sleeveless coat of linen or silk worn over mail, painted or embroidered with its owner's coat of arms. It keeps the sun from turning armour into an oven and the rain from rusting it. More than that, it tells friend and foe who is inside the steel, and it gives followers a banner to rally round. Nobody in bright heraldry hides anywhere.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +1 END keeps sun off armour; +1 Leadership showing your colours in battle
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth bright heraldry
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** tabard coat
