# False-bottom case

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: printing · any*

An honest-looking trunk, violin case or travelling box with a second floor beneath the lining, just deep enough for papers, coins, a pistol or a little packet of something illegal. The best are built so the outside and inside measure the same to a careless eye. Smugglers, spies and nervous travellers pay well for good work. The case earns its price the first time a customs officer lifts the lid, rummages, and waves it through.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Smuggling & Concealment hiding small items
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Smuggling & Concealment](../Skills/smuggling-and-concealment.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 5 S
