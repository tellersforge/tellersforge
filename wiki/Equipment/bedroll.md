# Bedroll

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: iron · any*

A blanket or two rolled up inside a waterproof sheet of oiled canvas and tied with straps, carried across the top of a pack or behind a saddle. At night it unrolls into a bed that keeps the ground's damp away and the cold off. It is the whole bedroom of cowhands, drovers and campaign soldiers. Old hands roll a spare shirt and socks into it to keep them dry.
