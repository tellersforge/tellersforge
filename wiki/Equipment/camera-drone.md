# Camera drone

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: computing · modern*

A small flying machine with four spinning rotors and a camera slung underneath, flown from a handheld controller. It rises quickly over fields, rooftops and forests, sending back a bird's-eye view. Scouts, reporters and surveyors use it to see what lies beyond the next hill. Its rotors buzz like an angry swarm of bees, so anyone below usually knows it is there.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Shadowing & Surveillance watching from the air; +1 Searching searching open ground
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth buzzing rotors
- **Used for:** [Drone Operation](../Skills/drone-operation.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** scout drone

## Not to be confused with

the *[Insect Drone](insect-drone.md)*, which is tiny and silent, made for spying indoors rather than scanning open ground.
