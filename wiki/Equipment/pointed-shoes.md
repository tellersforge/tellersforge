# Pointed shoes

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · western courts*

Soft leather shoes whose toes run out to an absurd point, sometimes as long as the foot itself, stuffed with moss or wool to keep their shape. The longer the point, the grander the wearer, and fashionable gentry compete to outdo one another. Among their own kind they are the mark of good taste. Anywhere else they catch on steps and roots, and a wearer who runs is likely to end on their face.

- **Penalty:** −1 Running toes catch and trip
