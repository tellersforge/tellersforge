# Down sleeping bag

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: electricity · modern*

A hooded bag of thin, strong cloth stitched into baffles and stuffed with the soft underfeathers of ducks and geese. It weighs almost nothing and packs into a stuff sack, and yet it keeps a climber warm on a glacier at night far below freezing. It has one terrible weakness: once soaked, the down clumps into sodden lumps and holds no warmth at all. Careful owners guard it from rain like a sick child.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 END sleeping in severe cold
- **Penalty:** −1 END when soaked
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.2 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** mummy bag, feather bag

## Not to be confused with

*[Synthetic Sleeping Bag](synthetic-sleeping-bag.md)*, which is heavier and less warm but still keeps its heat when damp.
