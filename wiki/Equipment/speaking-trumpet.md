# Speaking trumpet

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: gunpowder · any*

A long cone of tin, brass or wood, wide at one end and narrow at the mouthpiece, which throws a speaker's voice across a deck, a crowd or a battlefield. Ship's captains hail other vessels with it; fire chiefs shout through it over the roar of flames. Later versions have a battery and loudspeaker inside. It makes a voice louder, but not clearer, and a mumbled order becomes a louder mumble.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Leadership shouting orders over noise
- **Penalty:** 0

## Not to be confused with

*[Bugle](bugle.md)*, which gives set musical calls; the speaking trumpet carries the speaker's actual words.
