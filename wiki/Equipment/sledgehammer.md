# Sledgehammer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: iron · any*

A heavy iron head on a long wooden handle, swung with both hands and the whole body. It breaks stone, drives stakes, demolishes walls and smashes doors that won't open any other way. It is exhausting to use and impossible to use quietly. A strong worker with a sledgehammer can go through a wall faster than a locksmith through a lock.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 STR breaking walls or doors
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth while in use
- **Used for:** [Hand Tools](../Skills/hand-tools.md)
- **Weight:** 5 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** maul

## Not to be confused with

the *[Crowbar](crowbar.md)*, which pries doors open rather than breaking them apart.
