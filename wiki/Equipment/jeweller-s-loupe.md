# Jeweller's loupe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: printing · any*

A small, stubby tube with a powerful lens at one end, held in the eye socket by a squint of the brow, leaving both hands free. Jewellers, gem cutters, coin dealers and engravers use it to judge flaws, cuts, fakes and fine work. A glance through a loupe tells an expert in moments what a stone is worth, and whether it is really a stone at all.
