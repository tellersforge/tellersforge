# Laser rangefinder

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: computing · modern*

A small handheld unit with an eyepiece and a button. Point it at a target, press, and it bounces an invisible beam off it and shows the distance, down to the step. Surveyors, hunters and marksmen use it, because knowing the exact range makes a long shot far more likely to hit.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Counting & Measuring measuring distance; +1 Long Guns long shots with known range
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Surveying](../Skills/surveying.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 10 S

## Not to be confused with

*[Coincidence Rangefinder](coincidence-rangefinder.md)*, the older optical instrument that finds distance by lining up two images.
