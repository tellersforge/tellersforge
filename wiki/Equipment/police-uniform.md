# Police uniform

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: steam · modern*

A dark tunic or shirt with a shining badge, a peaked cap or helmet, and a duty belt hung with whistle, cuffs and truncheon. The uniform is the law made visible. Honest citizens straighten up and answer questions when it approaches, and a suspect in a small room feels its weight. Criminals, on the other hand, fall silent and melt away, and there is no crowd in which it blends.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 Intimidation over law-abiding civilians; +1 Interrogation while on duty
- **Penalty:** −2 Streetwise among criminals; −2 Stealth in a crowd
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** constable's uniform, patrol uniform

## Not to be confused with

*[Military service uniform](military-service-uniform.md)*, which marks a soldier and commands troops; the police uniform marks the keeper of civil order among ordinary people.
