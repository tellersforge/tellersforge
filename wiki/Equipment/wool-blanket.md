# Wool blanket

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: agriculture · any*

A thick, heavy blanket of woven or fulled wool, often striped at the ends with the colours of a weaver or an army. Wool stays warm even when wet, which is why soldiers, shepherds and sailors all trust it. It doubles as a cloak, a litter, a bundle wrap and a saddle pad. It also itches, smells of sheep in the rain and takes days to dry.
