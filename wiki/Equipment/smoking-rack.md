# Smoking rack

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: fire · any*

A frame of green poles lashed together over a low, smouldering fire of damp wood, hung with strips of meat or split fish. Over a day or several, smoke and slow heat dry the flesh and keep flies away, and the result keeps for weeks. Hunting camps and fishing villages smell of it all season. It must be watched: a flare-up cooks the meat instead of smoking it, and the rack itself can catch fire.
