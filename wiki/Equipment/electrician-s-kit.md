# Electrician's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: electricity · modern*

A pouch of insulated screwdrivers, pliers, wire strippers, crimpers, rolls of wire and a meter for testing current. With it an electrician wires buildings, fixes circuits and traces faults. Without it, touching live wiring is a fast way to find out why electricians are careful people. The tools are rubber-handled for good reason.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** 0
- **Penalty:** −1 Electrical Work without it on live wiring
- **Used for:** [Electrical Work](../Skills/electrical-work.md), [Electronics](../Skills/electronics.md)
- **Weight:** 5 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 8 S
