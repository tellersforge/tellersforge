# Sleeping mat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: agriculture · any*

A mat of woven reeds, rushes, palm leaf or straw, rolled up by day and spread out at night. It keeps a sleeper off the damp, the dust and the worst of the crawling things, and in hot lands it is all the bed anyone needs. Guests are offered the best mat in the house. The old mat, worn soft, is what the dog sleeps on.
