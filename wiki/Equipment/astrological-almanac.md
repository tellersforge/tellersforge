# Astrological almanac

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: printing · any*

A yearly book of star and planet positions, moon phases, eclipses, and tables of lucky and unlucky days for weddings, voyages, planting and war. Farmers keep one on a nail by the door, sailors in a chest, and kings in their libraries. It mixes real sky lore with a great deal of wishful thinking, and people follow it anyway.

- **Bonus:** +1 Astronomy & Calendars finding lucky days
