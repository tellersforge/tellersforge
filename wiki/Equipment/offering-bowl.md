# Offering bowl

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: pottery · any*

A plain or decorated bowl of clay, wood or metal, set out at a shrine, at a crossroads or on a grave and filled with rice, milk, wine, honey or incense. It feeds the gods, the ancestors or the spirits of a place, so they will listen kindly. Those who deal with the unseen find that a gift offered first makes every conversation easier.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Otherworldly Dealings when offerings are made
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Otherworldly Dealings](../Skills/otherworldly-dealings.md), [Ritual & Liturgy](../Skills/ritual-and-liturgy.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** pottery · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** spirit bowl

## Not to be confused with

*[Scrying Bowl](scrying-bowl.md)*, which is gazed into for visions rather than filled with gifts.
