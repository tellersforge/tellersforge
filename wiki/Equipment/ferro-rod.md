# Ferro rod

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: computing · modern*

A rod of special spark metal scraped hard with a blade or striker, throwing a shower of sparks far hotter than flint and steel ever could. It works soaked, frozen or at altitude, and one rod lasts for thousands of fires. Survivalists and soldiers carry one on their keys.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Fire-Making in wet conditions
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Fire-Making](../Skills/fire-making.md)
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** ferrocerium rod, metal match

## Not to be confused with

*[Flint and steel](flint-and-steel.md)*, the older version, whose cooler sparks need dry tinder.
