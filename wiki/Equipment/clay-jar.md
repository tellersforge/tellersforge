# Clay jar

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: pottery · any*

A fired pot with a narrow neck and often a pointed or round base, sealed with a stopper or wax. Clay jars hold oil, wine, grain and water in storerooms and ships' holds the world over. They keep contents cool and safe from mice. They also break, and a dropped jar is a spill and a loss in one.
