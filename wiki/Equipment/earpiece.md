# Earpiece

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: computing · modern*

A tiny radio receiver that sits almost invisibly in the ear, with a hidden microphone clipped inside a collar or cuff. Bodyguards, undercover officers and stage crews use it to stay in quiet touch with a team. The wearer can receive instructions without anyone around them knowing. A trained eye watches for the tell: the little pause and the hand drifting to the ear.
