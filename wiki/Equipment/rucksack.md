# Rucksack

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: printing · any*

A soft pack of canvas or leather on two shoulder straps, holding a traveller's everyday load: spare clothes, food, a blanket, odds and ends. It sits high on the back and leaves the hands free. Hikers, pedlars, students and soldiers off duty all carry one, and it goes wherever its owner does.
