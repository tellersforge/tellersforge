# Mechanic's coveralls

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A one-piece work suit of tough cotton or blended cloth, zipped or buttoned up the front, with pockets on the chest, thighs and legs for tools and rags. Mechanics, engineers and repair crews pull it on over their own clothes so the grease stays on the coveralls. It is practical, faded, often embroidered with a name, and very obviously not something to wear to a formal dinner.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 END against oil, grease and grime
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA at a formal gathering
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.2 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** boiler suit, overalls

## Not to be confused with

*[Canvas work trousers](canvas-work-trousers.md)*, which protect only the legs; coveralls cover the body from collar to ankle and keep the clothes underneath clean.
