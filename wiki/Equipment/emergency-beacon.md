# Emergency beacon

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: computing · modern*

A sealed, brightly coloured transmitter the size of a flask, with a pull tab or button. Once switched on, it sends a distress signal and its position to rescuers on the other end, and keeps sending for days. Sailors, pilots and explorers carry one and hope never to use it. It says only one thing, over and over: here, now, help.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Signals calling rescue
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Signals](../Skills/signals.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** distress beacon

## Not to be confused with

*[Signal Flares](signal-flares.md)*, which call help only while they burn and someone sees them; the beacon calls for days and gives its position.
