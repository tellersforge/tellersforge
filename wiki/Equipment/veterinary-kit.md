# Veterinary kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: steam · any*

A heavy case holding the tools of animal medicine: oversized syringes, a drenching horn for pouring medicine down a throat, a hoof knife, clippers, strong ointments and bottles of animal drugs. Farm vets carry it in the boot of a car or on a saddle. Most of their patients kick, bite or sit on them, so the kit also usually contains a great many bandages for the vet.
