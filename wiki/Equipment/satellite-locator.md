# Satellite locator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: spaceflight · modern*

A handheld receiver the size of a deck of cards that listens to signals from satellites high overhead and shows the user's position to within a few paces. It makes getting lost almost impossible, which is exactly the danger. A traveller who never learnt to read a map follows the glowing arrow happily until the battery dies, the canyon walls block the sky or someone jams the signal, and then has no idea where they are.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +3 Wayfinding with satellite signal
- **Penalty:** −1 Wayfinding relying on it when signal fails
- **Used for:** [Wayfinding](../Skills/wayfinding.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** position finder, navigation receiver

## Not to be confused with

the *[Inertial Navigator](inertial-navigator.md)*, which keeps track of position without any outside signal at all.
