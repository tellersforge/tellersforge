# Fleece layer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: computing · modern*

A soft jacket or pullover of synthetic pile, light and fluffy, worn between a shirt and an outer shell. It stays warm even when damp, dries within an hour by a stove, and can be stuffed into any pack. It keeps out little wind or rain on its own, but underneath a shell it is the hiker's favourite garment, and the one they are still wearing in the pub afterwards.
