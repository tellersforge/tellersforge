# Desktop computer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: computing · modern*

A box, a screen and a keyboard on a desk, humming quietly to itself. It holds libraries of records, letters and figures, and finds any of them in moments when asked the right way. Offices, libraries and homes all keep one. It needs steady power and a table, and it is far too heavy to take anywhere.
