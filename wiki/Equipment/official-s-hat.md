# Official's hat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · imperial courts*

A stiff black hat of lacquered gauze on a frame, with two wings sticking out at the sides whose length and angle announce the wearer's grade in the imperial service. Everyone at court can read it at a glance, and it is a sign of hard-won rank. Nobody wearing it can pretend to be anyone else.

- **Penalty:** −2 Disguise marks your rank
