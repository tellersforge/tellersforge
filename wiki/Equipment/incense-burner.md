# Incense burner

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: pottery · any*

A small clay pot, a bronze stand or a censer on chains swung by a priest, with a pouch of resin, sandalwood or dried herb bundles. Lit coals send a thick fragrant smoke rising, filling a shrine with a scent that marks the space as holy. The smoke carries on the breeze, though, and anyone trying to hide nearby is betrayed by the smell.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Ritual & Liturgy in rites
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth its scent carries
- **Used for:** [Ritual & Liturgy](../Skills/ritual-and-liturgy.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** pottery · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** censer, thurible, smudge bundle

## Not to be confused with

*[Smudge Pot](smudge-pot.md)*, a smoky camp fire pot for driving off insects rather than honouring the gods.
