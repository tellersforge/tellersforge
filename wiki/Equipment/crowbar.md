# Crowbar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: iron · any*

A heavy iron bar, flattened and bent at one end into a hooked claw. Wedged into a gap and leaned on, it forces doors, lifts lids, pries boards and shifts stones that no one could move by hand. It is the burglar's friend and the rescuer's tool. It is loud, splintering work, and nobody who hears it mistakes it for anything else.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 STR forcing doors
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth forcing doors
- **Used for:** [Hand Tools](../Skills/hand-tools.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** pry bar, jemmy

the *[Sledgehammer](sledgehammer.md)*, which smashes through rather than prying open.
