# Clay tablet and stylus

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: writing · river kingdoms*

A slab of damp clay, small enough to hold in one palm, and a cut reed whose tip presses wedge-shaped marks into the surface. When the writing is done, the tablet dries in the sun or is fired in a kiln and lasts for thousands of years. Temple scribes, merchants and tax collectors all used them. They are heavy, they shatter when dropped, and one tablet holds only a letter's worth of writing.
