# Carrier-bird kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: writing · any*

A wicker basket holding a few homing birds, with tiny tubes of bone or metal to tie to their legs and slips of thin paper for the messages. Released far from home, each bird flies straight back to its own loft, carrying whatever was written. Armies, merchants and besieged towns have relied on them. Storms blow them off course, hawks hunt them, and a bird only ever flies home, never onward.
