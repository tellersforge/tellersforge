# Protective amulet

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: stone tools · any*

A small charm of carved stone, bone, metal or knotted cord, worn at the neck or wrist to keep away curses, the evil eye and bad luck. Every culture has its own: an eye, a hand, a knot, a tooth. Whether it works or not, it gives the wearer courage when facing things that should not be, and that alone is worth something.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 COU against fear of the uncanny
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Folk Magic](../Skills/folk-magic.md)
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** charm, talisman, evil-eye bead

## Not to be confused with

*[Holy Symbol](holy-symbol.md)*, the emblem of a particular faith, where the amulet belongs to folk magic and old custom.
