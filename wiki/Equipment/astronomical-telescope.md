# Astronomical telescope

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: printing · any*

A long tube of lenses or mirrors mounted on a heavy stand or tripod, pointed at the night sky. Through it, the moon shows craters, the planets become discs with moons of their own, and faint stars appear by the thousand. Astronomers spend whole nights at the eyepiece, making charts and arguing about what they see.
