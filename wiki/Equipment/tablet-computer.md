# Tablet computer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: computing · modern*

A thin, flat computer with a glass touch screen, about the size of a book. It holds maps, manuals, records and whole libraries, and with a network link it can look up almost anything within moments. Travellers read on it, officials fill in forms on it and children play on it. Dropped, it cracks; left in the sun, it overheats; without power, it is a slab of glass.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Research with a network link
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Computer Use](../Skills/computer-use.md), [Research](../Skills/research.md)
- **Weight:** 0.6 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 6 S
- **Also known as:** slate computer, reader tablet

## Not to be confused with

*[Mobile Phone](mobile-phone.md)*, which is smaller and made for calls; the tablet has a larger screen for reading and work.
