# Honey salve jar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: agriculture · any*

A small sealed jar of honey thickened with pine resin or beeswax, sometimes with crushed herbs stirred in. Smeared on a cut and covered with a clean cloth, it keeps the flesh from rotting and helps the edges knit. Healers have trusted it since before anyone knew why it worked. It keeps for years in a cool place, and it attracts every wasp within a mile once opened.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 First Aid against wound rot
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [First Aid](../Skills/first-aid.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** wound honey, balm pot

## Not to be confused with

*[Poultice Kit](poultice-kit.md)*, which draws out swelling with a hot pack; the salve keeps a wound clean while it closes.
