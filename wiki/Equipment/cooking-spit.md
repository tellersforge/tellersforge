# Cooking spit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: fire · any*

A green stick sharpened at both ends, or a long iron rod with a crank, pushed through a joint of meat and turned slowly over the coals. It is the oldest cooking tool after the fire itself. Someone has to sit beside it and turn, which is why the youngest in camp learns the job first. Done well, the fat drips and spits and the crust goes brown; done carelessly, one side burns while the other stays raw.
