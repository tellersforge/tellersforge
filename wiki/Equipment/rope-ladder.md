# Rope ladder

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: iron · any*

Two ropes with wooden rungs knotted between them at regular intervals, rolled up for carrying and hung from a hook or a rail. Sailors, besiegers, firefighters and lovers eloping from upper windows have all used it. Once hung, it makes a wall easy to climb, though it sways and twists under the feet and swings away from the wall with every step.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing once hung
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md)
- **Weight:** 4 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** jacob's ladder

## Not to be confused with

*[Grappling Hook](grappling-hook.md)*, which hangs the rope; the rope ladder is what makes the climb itself easier.
