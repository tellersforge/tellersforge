# Engineer's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A flat case of drawing instruments, a slide rule, gauges, callipers and a thick book of reference tables. The engineer uses it to design bridges, calculate loads, plan mines and test whether a boiler will burst. It is a scholar's kit for a practical trade, and the confident engineer is often the only person on a building site who can say why something will fall down.
