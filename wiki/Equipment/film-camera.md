# Film camera

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: steam · any*

A box of wood, leather or metal with a lens at the front and glass plates or rolls of film inside, which record an image when the shutter opens. The pictures must be developed in a dark room before anyone sees them. Early cameras needed the subject to sit perfectly still; later ones could snap a moment as it happened. It sees what the eye misses, and remembers it for good.
