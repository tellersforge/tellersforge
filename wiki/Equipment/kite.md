# Kite

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: iron · eastern empire*

A light frame of bamboo or thin wood, covered in paper or silk painted as a bird, a fish or a dragon, and flown on a long line. In eastern empires kites fill the sky at festivals, some with little whistles that hum in the wind. They have been used to measure distance, send signals and carry lines across rivers, but mostly they are flown for joy.
