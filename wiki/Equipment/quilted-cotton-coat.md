# Quilted cotton coat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: pottery · jungle kingdoms*

A heavy coat of cotton padding, quilted thick and soaked in brine until it dries stiff as board. Warriors of the jungle kingdoms wear it into battle, because it stops blowgun darts and slows arrows and clubs better than many metal coats, at a fraction of the weight. It is hot to wear in the steaming forest, and a warrior who falls into a river finds it soaks up water and drags him down.

## Game block

- **Slot:** torso
- **Bonus:** +1 armour against arrows, darts and clubs
- **Penalty:** −1 Swimming waterlogged; −1 END in heat
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** pottery · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** salt-cotton coat

## Not to be confused with

*[Quilted jacket](quilted-jacket.md)*, the plain linen padding of poorer soldiers elsewhere, lighter and cooler but softer against darts and arrows.
