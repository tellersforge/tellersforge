# Royal robe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: bronze · any*

A ruler's robe of the richest cloth a kingdom can make, purple or gold or scarlet, lined with ermine or leopard, with a train that pages must carry. It is made to be seen from the far end of a great hall, and when the ruler stands in it their people remember who commands. Nobody wearing it goes anywhere unnoticed, or quickly.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 CHA before your subjects; +1 Leadership commanding your own people
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth unmistakable; −1 DEX heavy train
- **Used for:** —
