# Camp fire grate

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: iron · any*

An iron grid on short legs, or a tripod of iron rods with a hook and chain, set over an open fire. It holds pots and pans steady at the right height above the flames and keeps them from tipping into the coals. It is heavy and blackened, and carried in a wagon rather than on a back. With it, a campfire becomes a kitchen.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** 0
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Cooking](../Skills/cooking.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S

## Not to be confused with

*[Cooking Spit](cooking-spit.md)*, which roasts meat directly over the fire; the grate holds pots and pans.
