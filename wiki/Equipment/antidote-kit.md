# Antidote kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A hard case with padded slots for vials, a bottle of emetic, a tin of activated charcoal and a card listing common poisons and what to give for each. Hospitals, ships' doctors and cautious rulers keep one close. It works best when the poison is known: a sniff of the cup, a label on the bottle, a symptom recognised in time. Against the unknown, it is a lottery of guesses.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Poisons & Antidotes treating a known poison
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Poisons & Antidotes](../Skills/poisons-and-antidotes.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** poison kit

## Not to be confused with

*[Snakebite Kit](snakebite-kit.md)*, which is for venom; the antidote kit is for poisons eaten, drunk or breathed.
