# Grip gloves

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

Thin, tight gloves whose palms and fingers carry pads of countless microscopic bristles. Pressed against glass, polished metal or a ship's hull, they cling as a gecko's foot clings, and peel away with a rolling motion. In low gravity they let a spacer hold on to anything. They also make every fine movement of the fingers clumsy and fumbled, and nobody palms a coin while wearing them.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Climbing smooth surfaces; +1 Low & Zero Gravity holding on
- **Penalty:** −1 Sleight of Hand any
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** gecko gloves, adhesion gloves

## Not to be confused with

*[Gravity-Grip Pads](gravity-grip-pads.md)*, which let the wearer walk up any wall; grip gloves only give the hands a hold.
