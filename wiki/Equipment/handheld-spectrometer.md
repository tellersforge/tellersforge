# Handheld spectrometer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: computing · modern*

A pistol-shaped analyser with a small window at the muzzle. Press it to a coin, a ring or a crumbling rock, pull the trigger, and a flash of light or a quiet buzz is followed by a list on the screen: this much gold, this much copper, a worrying trace of something else. Assayers, customs officers and scrap dealers love it. Forgers hate it, because it never gets tired or impressed by a good story.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Appraisal identifying metals and minerals; +1 Physical Sciences identifying substances
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Physical Sciences](../Skills/physical-sciences.md), [Geology & Prospecting](../Skills/geology-and-prospecting.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 20 S
- **Also known as:** material analyser

## Not to be confused with

the *[Handheld Scanner](handheld-scanner.md)*, which sweeps a whole scene for many kinds of reading; the spectrometer does one thing, naming what a sample is made of.
