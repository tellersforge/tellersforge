# Sundial

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: writing · any*

A flat or curved face of stone or bronze with hour lines carved across it and an angled blade, the gnomon, casting a shadow onto them. It stands in temple courts, market squares and the gardens of the rich, and tells the hour to anyone who walks past. It needs sun, it is wrong at night, and priests often argue with it on feast days. Many bear a gloomy motto about time.
