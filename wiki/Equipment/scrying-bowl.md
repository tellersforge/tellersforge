# Scrying bowl

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: bronze · any*

A dark bowl of still water, ink or oil, a polished black mirror, or a ball of clear crystal. The seer gazes into it in dim light, lets the eyes lose focus, and waits for images to rise from the depths. Some see faces, some see places, some see nothing. In old stories, what looks back from the bowl is not always the future.
