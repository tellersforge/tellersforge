# Folding kayak

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: electricity · modern*

A hull of tough canvas and rubber over a jointed frame of wood or alloy that comes apart into pieces and packs into two large bags. Assembled on a beach in under an hour, it is a seaworthy boat for one or two paddlers, and it has carried raiders, explorers and island travellers along long coasts. Parts get lost in the sand.

- **Bonus:** +1 Rowing & Paddling any
