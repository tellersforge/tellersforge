# Eating utensils

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: stone tools · any*

A personal set of whatever a culture eats with: a horn spoon and a small knife, a pair of lacquered sticks, a fork, a scoop of shell, rolled in a cloth or hung in a case at the belt. Guests in many places are expected to bring their own. The set often outlives its owner, is handed down with care and says more than it seems about where someone comes from and how they were raised.
