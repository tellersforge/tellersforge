# Accordion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: steam · any*

A boxy instrument of pleated leather bellows between two wooden ends set with buttons or piano keys. Squeezed and pulled, it pumps air through metal reeds to give melody, chords and bass all at once. It is heavy, loud and cheerful, and a single player can carry a wedding, a harbour tavern or a village dance. It is also a ready excuse for everyone to get up and move.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Carousing at a dance
- **Penalty:** 0

## Not to be confused with

*[Mouth Organ](mouth-organ.md)*, a pocket reed instrument blown by the player rather than pumped by bellows.
