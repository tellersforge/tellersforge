# Collapsible bucket

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: steam · any*

A tarred canvas or plastic pail on a wire rim that folds flat into a pack and springs open when it is needed. It hauls water from a stream to camp, puts out a cooking fire, waters horses and washes socks. It sags a little when full and leaks a little when old. Every army and survey party has one, and every one is misplaced at least once a week.
