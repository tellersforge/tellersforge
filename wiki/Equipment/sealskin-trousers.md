# Sealskin trousers

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

Trousers of sealskin, fur side outward, sewn with sinew into seams that do not let water through. Arctic hunters wear them on the sea ice and in kayaks, where wet means death. They keep the legs warm and dry through spray and slush. Anywhere warm they are stifling.

## Game block

- **Slot:** legs
- **Bonus:** +2 END against cold and wet
- **Penalty:** −1 END in heat
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** hide trousers

## Not to be confused with

*[Fur Boots](fur-boots.md)*, which keep the feet warm on snow, where the sealskin trousers keep the legs warm and dry.
