# Assistant intelligence

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: far future · far future*

A small smooth core, often worn as a pendant or kept in a pocket, holding a thinking program that listens, remembers and answers. It can find any fact, summarise a long report and remind its owner of a name from a meeting years ago. Some owners grow fond of their assistant and give it a name; some come to rely on it far too much. It is helpful, patient and entirely without judgement about why it is being asked.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Research finding and summing up information; +1 Memory Training recalling anything once recorded
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Computer Use](../Skills/computer-use.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 60 S
- **Also known as:** pocket intelligence, personal assistant core

## Not to be confused with

the *[Handheld Computer](handheld-computer.md)*, which only finds what you look for; the assistant listens, remembers and answers on its own.
