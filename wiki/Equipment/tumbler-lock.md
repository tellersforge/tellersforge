# Tumbler lock

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

The lock of industrial cities: a brass cylinder set in the door, holding a row of tiny spring-loaded pins that must all be lifted to exactly the right height before the plug will turn. Each key is cut to its own pattern, and factories turn out thousands, each a little different. Old picks and bent nails that opened a warded lock in a moment scrape uselessly here. Burglars had to learn a new, far finer craft.
