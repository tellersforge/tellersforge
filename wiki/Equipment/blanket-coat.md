# Blanket coat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · frontier trappers*

A hooded coat cut from a thick wool trade blanket, often cream with bright bands of colour across it. Trappers and traders wear it through the northern winter, and at night the same thick cloth becomes bedding under the pines. Those cheerful stripes are handsome at a trading post and a nuisance when trying to stay unseen in the snow.
