# Radiation pills

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: computing · modern*

A small bottle of plain white tablets, taken with water before walking into a contaminated zone. They fill the body with harmless salt so that the poisonous kind has nowhere to settle. They do not stop radiation, only one of its dangers, and they must be taken in time to help. Emergency kits in the shadow of reactors carry them, and survivors of a disaster trade them like precious coins.
