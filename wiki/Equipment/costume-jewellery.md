# Costume jewellery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: steam · modern*

Glass, paste and plated metal cut and polished to pass for diamonds, pearls and gold. At a glance, across a crowded room, it works wonderfully, and a clever impostor can walk into a party dressed like an heiress. Up close, under the eye of someone who owns the real thing, it fails badly, and being caught wearing fakes among the rich is worse than wearing nothing at all.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 CHA at parties at a glance; +1 Disguise passing as rich
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA if spotted as fake by the wealthy
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** paste jewels, imitation jewellery

## Not to be confused with

*[Gaudy jewels](gaudy-jewels.md)*, which are genuinely valuable though vulgar; costume jewellery is cheap pretending to be precious.
