# Loose desert robe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · desert peoples*

A loose, pale robe falling to the ankles, with wide sleeves and a simple open neck, worn by the peoples of the deep desert. It seems too much clothing for such heat, but it keeps the sun off every patch of skin and lets air move freely beneath. The long hem gets under running feet.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 END in desert heat
- **Penalty:** −1 Running long hem
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** ankle-length shirt, thawb

## Not to be confused with

*[Hooded Desert Cloak](hooded-desert-cloak.md)*, the heavier cloak worn over the robe against sandstorm and the cold of the desert night.
