# Account ledger

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: printing · any*

A heavy book with ruled columns for dates, names, sums owed and sums paid. Merchants, stewards and shopkeepers write every sale and purchase in it, and add up the pages at the end of each month. A well-kept ledger shows exactly where every coin went. A badly kept one ruins businesses, and a cleverly kept false one has made more than one fortune.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Bookkeeping keeping accounts over time
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Bookkeeping](../Skills/bookkeeping.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** daybook

## Not to be confused with

*[Notebook](notebook.md)*, which is for personal notes; the ledger is a formal record of accounts, debts and stock.
