# Day pack

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: computing · modern*

A small, light pack with zips and pockets, big enough for a day's water, lunch, a map and a spare layer. It is what walkers, students and city travellers carry, and it barely registers on the back. It is not meant for nights in the wild, and those who try soon find out why.
