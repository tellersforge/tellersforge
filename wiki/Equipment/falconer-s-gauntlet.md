# Falconer's gauntlet

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · noble hunters*

A long, thick glove of stiff leather reaching halfway to the elbow, worn on the arm that carries the hawk. The bird grips it with its talons, which could pierce bare flesh to the bone, and the falconer holds the jesses between gloved fingers. Nobles have them embroidered. The stiff leather makes fine work with that hand clumsy.

## Game block

- **Slot:** hands
- **Bonus:** +2 armour against talons
- **Penalty:** −1 DEX fine handwork
- **Used for:** [Falconry](../Skills/falconry.md)
- **Weight:** 0.4 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** hawking glove

## Not to be confused with

*[Archer's Bracer](archer-s-bracer.md)*, a guard on the inner forearm that stops a bowstring rather than a bird's talons.
