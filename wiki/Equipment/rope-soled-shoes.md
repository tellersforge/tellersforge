# Rope-soled shoes

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · coastal villages*

Light canvas shoes on soles made from coils of tarred rope or plaited grass, stitched tight into a flat pad. Fishermen, sailors and cliff-foot villagers wear them, because rope soles cling to wet rock, rigging and a heaving deck where leather skids. They are cheap, cool and quiet. They are also never truly dry in a wet season, and cold feet are the price of a sure step.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing on rigging and rock
- **Penalty:** −1 END in the wet
- **Used for:** —

## Not to be confused with

*[Split-toe boots](split-toe-boots.md)*, which grip with the toes themselves and suit creeping; rope-soled shoes grip with the sole and suit decks and rigging.
