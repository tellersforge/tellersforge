# Panpipes

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: agriculture · mountain peoples*

A row of reed or bamboo tubes, each cut a different length and bound side by side with cord or wax, closed at the bottom. The player blows across the open tops, sliding from pipe to pipe, and gets a breathy, airy scale. Mountain shepherds carry them, and the sound of panpipes drifting across a high pasture is as old as the flocks. They are cheap to make and hard to play well.
