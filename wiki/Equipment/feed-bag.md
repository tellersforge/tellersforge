# Feed bag

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: agriculture · any*

A canvas or leather bag of grain, hooked over a horse's or mule's head with a strap so it can eat on a halt without spilling a mouthful. Cavalry and carters carry one for every animal, because grass does not grow on roads and a hungry mount slows and sickens. The animal nods and snorts into it contentedly. Put it on for too long in the heat, and the dust inside makes it cough.
