# Smudge pot

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: pottery · any*

A clay pot of smouldering dung, green leaves and damp wood, set upwind of a camp or carried on a cord. It gives off thick, sour smoke that insects hate and people barely tolerate. On a still summer evening by a marsh it is the only thing that makes camp bearable. The smell soaks into clothes and hair, and anyone downwind knows a camp is there long before they see it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 END against biting insects
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth smoke smell
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** pottery · **Price:** 2 C
- **Also known as:** smoke pot

## Not to be confused with

*[Insect Repellent](insect-repellent.md)*, which goes on the skin; the smudge pot drives insects from a whole camp with smoke.
