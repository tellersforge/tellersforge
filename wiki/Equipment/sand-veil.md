# Sand veil

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: agriculture · desert peoples*

A long strip of cloth, often indigo or undyed, wound around the head and drawn across the face until only the eyes show. It keeps blowing sand out of the mouth, the sun off the neck and the worst of the dust out of the lungs. Desert travellers wind it with a practised twist that strangers take days to learn. In towns where an honest person is expected to show their face, a veiled traveller earns suspicious looks.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +1 END in sandstorms and dust
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA where showing the face is expected
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** face veil, mouth cloth
