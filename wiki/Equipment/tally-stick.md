# Tally stick

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: stone tools · any*

A length of wood or bone scored with notches, each one a day, a sheep, a measure of grain or a coin owed. Often the stick is notched across and split lengthwise, so lender and borrower each keep half; when the debt is settled, the halves are fitted together and the notches must match. It is the oldest account book there is, and it cannot be quietly altered by either side.
