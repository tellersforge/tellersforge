# 3d printer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: computing · modern*

A boxy machine that builds objects layer by thin layer from melted plastic or fused metal powder, following a stored design. Give it the pattern and some hours, and it produces a bracket, a gear, a replacement knob or a small tool. It needs power, feedstock and patience, and a flawed design produces a flawed part. Anything larger than a loaf of bread is beyond it.
