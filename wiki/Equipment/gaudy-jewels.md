# Gaudy jewels

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: iron · any*

Heavy chains, fat rings and glittering pendants chosen for size rather than grace. Gaudy jewels are a boast worn on the body: they say the wearer has more money than sense and does not care who knows. In a rowdy tavern or a loud club they impress and intimidate. Among thieves they are an invitation, and among families with old money they are quietly, devastatingly laughed at.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 Intimidation showing wealth; +1 Carousing in taverns and clubs
- **Penalty:** −2 Streetwise among thieves; −1 Etiquette among old money
