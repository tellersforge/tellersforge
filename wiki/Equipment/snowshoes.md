# Snowshoes

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

Wide oval or teardrop frames of bent wood, laced with a web of rawhide, strapped to the boots. They spread the walker's weight over deep powder so that they sink a hand's depth instead of to the waist. Walking in them is a wide, rolling shuffle; running is impossible. On hard ground they clatter and scrape and are just a nuisance.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Snow & Ice Travel deep snow
- **Penalty:** −1 Running any; −1 Stealth on hard ground
- **Used for:** [Snow & Ice Travel](../Skills/snow-and-ice-travel.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** bear-paw shoes

## Not to be confused with

*[Wooden Skis](wooden-skis.md)*, which glide swiftly over open snow; snowshoes trudge but go anywhere.
