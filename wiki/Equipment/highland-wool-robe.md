# Highland wool robe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · mountain peoples*

A huge, voluminous robe of wool or sheepskin, belted high at the waist so the fabric above forms a great pouch for carrying things. When the mountain sun is out, the wearer shrugs one shoulder and arm free; at night, the belt comes off and the robe becomes a bed. Its bulky sleeves make careful work awkward.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 END against mountain cold; +1 Survival: Mountain doubles as bedding
- **Penalty:** −1 DEX bulky sleeves
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 6 S
- **Also known as:** sheepskin robe
