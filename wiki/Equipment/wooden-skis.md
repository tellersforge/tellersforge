# Wooden skis

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

Long, narrow boards of wood, turned up at the front, with strips of fur or a coat of wax on the underside so they slide forward but grip going back. The traveller strides and glides across open snowfields, pushing with a single long pole. On the flat they cover great distances. On steep or wooded slopes, where turning and stopping matter, they become terrifying.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Snow & Ice Travel open snowfields
- **Penalty:** −1 Balance steep or wooded slopes
- **Used for:** [Snow & Ice Travel](../Skills/snow-and-ice-travel.md)
- **Weight:** 4 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** runners

## Not to be confused with

*[Snowshoes](snowshoes.md)*, which are slower but steady on any ground; wooden skis are fast on open snow.
