# Leather work apron

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A long bib apron of thick, stiff leather hung from the neck and tied at the waist, often with a split skirt so the wearer can straddle an anvil or a bench. Smiths, welders and foundry hands swear by it. Sparks bounce off, molten spatter rolls down, and a sharp edge slips aside instead of opening the belly. It is heavy and stiff, though, and nobody tumbles or leaps well with one flapping at the knees.

## Game block

- **Slot:** torso
- **Bonus:** +1 armour against sparks, splashes and hot metal
- **Penalty:** −1 Acrobatics while worn
- **Used for:** [Blacksmithing](../Skills/blacksmithing.md), [Machining & Welding](../Skills/machining-and-welding.md)
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** smith's apron, shop apron

## Not to be confused with

*[Smith's apron](smith-s-apron.md)*, the older, simpler apron of the village forge; the leather work apron is the heavier, full-length industrial version.
