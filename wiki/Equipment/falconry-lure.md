# Falconry lure

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · any*

A bundle of feathers or a pair of wings tied to a leather pad, with meat attached and swung round on a long cord. The falconer whirls it overhead, and the bird comes back to it from the sky. It is used in training and to recall a hawk that has missed its prey. Birds that learn the lure well come back; those that don't are soon lost.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Animal Training birds of prey
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Falconry](../Skills/falconry.md), [Animal Training](../Skills/animal-training.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** swing lure

## Not to be confused with

the *[Falcon Hood](falcon-hood.md)*, which calms a hawk rather than calling it back.
