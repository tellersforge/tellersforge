# Gemstone brooch

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: iron · any*

A decorated pin of gold or silver set with gems or enamel, strong enough to fasten a heavy cloak at the shoulder or to sit at the breast of a gown. Nobles judge one another by such things, and a well-chosen brooch tells them the wearer has taste as well as money. It also tells every cutpurse in the market the same thing, so the prudent wear it under a hood in the lower streets.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 CHA among nobles; +1 Appraisal as a mark of taste
- **Penalty:** −1 Streetwise draws thieves
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 15 S
