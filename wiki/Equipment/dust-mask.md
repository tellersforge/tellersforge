# Dust mask

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: electricity · modern*

A folded pad of cloth, sometimes with a layer of charcoal or wet moss inside, tied over the nose and mouth. It keeps out the worst of the dust in a mine, the smoke from a burning field and the spores of a mouldy cellar. It is cheap, simple and far better than nothing, though it does nothing at all against real poison gas, and it grows stiff and foul by the end of a long day.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +1 END against dust, smoke and spores
- **Penalty:** 0

## Not to be confused with

the *[Gas Mask](gas-mask.md)*, which seals the face and filters true poison; the dust mask only strains out dust, smoke and spores.
