# Gnomon stick

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: stone tools · any*

A straight stick pushed upright into flat ground, and nothing more. Its shadow tells the story: shortest at noon, and pointing north at that moment for anyone in the northern lands. Mark the shadow's tip at intervals and a line appears running east to west. Herders, hunters and early builders used it to find the seasons and lay out their fields. On a cloudy day it is just a stick.
