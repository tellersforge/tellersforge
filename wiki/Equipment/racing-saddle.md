# Racing saddle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: steam · modern*

A tiny, feather-light pad of leather that barely covers the horse's back, with very short stirrups. Jockeys crouch in it, balanced over the horse's shoulders, so the animal can run as fast as possible with almost no weight. It is perfect for a race and miserable for anything else. After a long ride in one, the rider can barely walk.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Riding racing
- **Penalty:** −1 Riding long rides
- **Used for:** [Riding](../Skills/riding.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 20 S
- **Also known as:** jockey saddle

## Not to be confused with

the *[Stock Saddle](stock-saddle.md)*, which is made for comfort through long days rather than speed.
