# Radio direction finder

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: electricity · modern*

A receiver with a loop aerial that the operator turns by hand, listening through headphones as a signal swells and fades. Where it goes quietest, the transmitter lies along that line. Aircraft use it to home on beacons, ships to find harbours in fog, and hunters of spies to track down a hidden transmitter street by street. Two bearings from two places cross on the map and give the answer.
