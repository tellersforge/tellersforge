# Pocket lighter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: electricity · modern*

A small metal or plastic case with a flint wheel and a reservoir of fuel, giving a steady flame at the flick of a thumb. Refill it and it lasts for years. It lights pipes, candles, campfires and fuses, and wherever there is air it will light something.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Fire-Making anywhere with air
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Fire-Making](../Skills/fire-making.md)
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** petrol lighter, gas lighter

## Not to be confused with

*[Plasma lighter](plasma-lighter.md)*, which uses an electric arc and has no flame for wind to blow out.
