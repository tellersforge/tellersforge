# Codebook

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: printing · any*

A small book listing hundreds of words, phrases, names and places, each matched to a group of letters or numbers used in secret messages. With it, a whole order can be sent in a few short groups that mean nothing to anyone without the book. Navies, embassies and spy rings guard their codebooks with their lives, and capturing the enemy's is worth more than a battle won.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Cryptography messages in its code
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Cryptography](../Skills/cryptography.md), [Signals](../Skills/signals.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** code list

## Not to be confused with

*[Cipher Wheel](cipher-wheel.md)*, which scrambles letters one at a time; the codebook replaces whole words and is far harder to break without a copy.
