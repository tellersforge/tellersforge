# Beekeeper's veil

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · farming villages*

A broad-brimmed hat with a curtain of fine netting hanging from its edge, tucked into the collar so no bee can crawl in. Beekeepers wear it when they open a hive and lift out the combs. Through it the world is a little dimmer and slightly blurred, which is a small price for a face without stings.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +2 armour against stings
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception seeing through mesh
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** bee hood

## Not to be confused with

*[Face Veil](face-veil.md)*, a cloth wound across nose and mouth against sand and prying eyes, not against insects.
