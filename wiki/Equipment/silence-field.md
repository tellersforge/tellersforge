# Silence field

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Fields and far-future devices · Needs: far future · far future*

A small disc set on a table or the floor that swallows every sound inside a narrow circle. People inside can speak freely; anyone outside hears nothing but a faint hush. Diplomats, spies and plotters use it for private talks in crowded rooms. It hides voices, but not faces; lips can still be read.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Stealth against hearing; +1 Intrigue & Politics private talks
- **Penalty:** 0

- **Also known as:** hush field

## Not to be confused with

the *[Cloaking Field Emitter](cloaking-field-emitter.md)*, which hides the wearer from sight rather than hiding sound.
