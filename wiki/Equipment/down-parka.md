# Down parka

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: computing · modern*

A thick, hooded coat filled with down or synthetic fill, quilted into baffles so the stuffing cannot bunch, with a fur-trimmed hood and heavy mittens clipped to the sleeves. It is made for polar expeditions and high mountains, where lesser coats simply fail. It turns a killing cold into a merely uncomfortable one. In mild weather it is stifling, and the mittens make any fine handwork a fumbling struggle.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +3 END against cold
- **Penalty:** −2 END in heat; −1 DEX fine handwork in the mittens
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** puffer jacket, expedition parka

## Not to be confused with

*[Fur parka](fur-parka.md)*, the traditional hunter's coat of pelts; the down parka is lighter, warmer for its weight and made in factories.
