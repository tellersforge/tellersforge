# Dark close-fitting clothes

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · thieves and scouts*

Clothes chosen for one purpose: to go unseen. The cloth is dark and dull, never true black, which shows in moonlight, but charcoal, brown and deep blue. Nothing flaps, rustles, jingles or shines. Scouts wear such garb at night, and so do thieves, spies and anyone climbing a wall they shouldn't. The same clothes that vanish in the dark look shifty in a sunlit street, and wise wearers carry something to throw over them.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Stealth at night
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA in daylight society (suspicious)
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** night clothes

## Not to be confused with

*[Camouflage fatigues](camouflage-fatigues.md)*, which break up the outline against trees and grass by day; dark close-fitting clothes are for moving unseen at night.
