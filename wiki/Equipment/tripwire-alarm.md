# Tripwire alarm

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: bronze · any*

A cord pegged low across a path or around a camp, hung with bells, shells, bone rattles or old pots. Anyone who blunders through it in the dark makes a racket, and sleepers wake with a weapon in hand. It is simple, cheap and older than walls. A careful intruder who spots the cord steps over it. A careless one, or a hungry animal, announces itself to everyone.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Perception noticing intruders at camp
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Traps](../Skills/traps.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** bell line, camp alarm

## Not to be confused with

*[Bell Tripwire](bell-tripwire.md)*, a ready-made camp set with bells and stakes; this is the general idea, strung from whatever is at hand.
