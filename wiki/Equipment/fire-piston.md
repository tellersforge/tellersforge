# Fire piston

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: iron · island peoples*

A sealed tube of wood, horn or bone with a close-fitting plunger. A pinch of tinder goes in the tip, the plunger is slammed home, and the squeezed air grows hot enough to light it. Island peoples invented it, and travellers who see it work think it is magic. It works in wind and damp where sparks fail.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Fire-Making in wind or damp
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Fire-Making](../Skills/fire-making.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** fire syringe

## Not to be confused with

*[Flint and steel](flint-and-steel.md)*, which strikes sparks onto tinder and struggles in wet or windy weather.
