# Field matter printer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Fields and far-future devices · Needs: far future · far future*

A suitcase-sized box that builds tools, spare parts and small devices from a store of feedstock, layer by layer, over several hours. Explorers and engineers far from any workshop depend on it. It can make almost anything it has a pattern for, but slowly. When the feedstock runs out, it is just a heavy box.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Jury-Rigging printing spare parts; +1 Mechanics replacing broken parts
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Machining & Welding](../Skills/machining-and-welding.md)
- **Weight:** 15 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 100 G
- **Also known as:** matter printer, field fabricator

## Not to be confused with

the *[Repair Swarm](repair-swarm.md)*, which mends broken equipment directly rather than printing new parts.
