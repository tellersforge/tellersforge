# Inflated skin float

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: bronze · river kingdoms*

A whole goat or sheepskin, sewn shut at the legs and neck, then blown up through a hollow leg until it is round and taut as a drum. A traveller hugs it under the chest and kicks across a river too deep to ford, pack tied on top. Armies of old crossed great rivers this way, whole regiments bobbing like ducks. It needs one arm to hold, and a thorn or an arrow ends the crossing badly.
