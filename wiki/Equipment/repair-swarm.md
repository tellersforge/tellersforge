# Repair swarm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Fields and far-future devices · Needs: far future · far future*

A small canister that, when opened, releases a cloud of tiny machines. They crawl over broken equipment, sealing cracks, joining wires and knitting metal whole. Engineers use them on damaged engines, vehicles and hull breaches. The swarm is fast and thorough, but it cannot tell a machine from anything else, so its users keep their hands well away.
