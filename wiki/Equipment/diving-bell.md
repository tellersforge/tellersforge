# Diving bell

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: printing · western kingdoms*

A great open-bottomed bell of wood or iron, weighted with lead and lowered from a ship on chains. Water pushes up into it but cannot fill it, so a pocket of air stays trapped inside. Divers swim out from its lower edge to work on the seabed, then come back to breathe, talk and rest. Salvagers use it to reach sunken cargo, and bridge-builders to lay foundations deep beneath rivers.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Diving working on the seabed
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Diving](../Skills/diving.md)
