# Float bladders

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: stone tools · any*

The inflated skins or bladders of goats, sheep or cattle, tied shut and held under the arms or lashed to the body or a raft. A swimmer clinging to one crosses a deep, fast river that would otherwise drown them, and soldiers of old armies crossed whole rivers this way. A thorn or a spear point lets the air out.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Swimming crossing rivers
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Swimming](../Skills/swimming.md)
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** inflated skins, swim bladders

## Not to be confused with

*[Inflated Skin Float](inflated-skin-float.md)*, a single larger float for one person; the bladders are a bundle of smaller floats.
