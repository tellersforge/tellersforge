# Thumb piano

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: iron · forest tribes*

A small wooden board or hollow box with a row of flattened metal tongues fixed across it at different lengths. The player holds it in both hands and plucks the tongues with the thumbs, making a bright, tinkling, bell-like pattern. Forest peoples play it walking, sitting by the fire, or during ceremonies to speak with ancestors. Some fix bottle caps or shells to it for a buzzing edge.
