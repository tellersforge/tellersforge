# Court slippers

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · imperial courts*

Soft slippers of embroidered cloth or silk, with thin soles meant for polished floors, lacquered halls and carpets in the palace. Courtiers wear them to pad silently through ceremonies and to show they never need to walk anywhere rough. Indoors they mark the wearer as someone who belongs. Out in the courtyard gravel, or worse on a muddy road, they are torn and useless within the hour, and running in them is a comedy.
