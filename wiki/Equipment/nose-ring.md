# Nose ring

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · any*

A small ring or stud worn through the side of the nostril or the thin wall between them. In many lands it is a mark of marriage, adulthood or belonging, given at a ceremony and never taken off. Among the wearer's own people it is simply handsome. In stiff, conservative company it is noticed first and judged quickly, and the wearer may find doors a little slower to open.
