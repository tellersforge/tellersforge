# Cleanroom suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: computing · modern*

A white, lint-free coverall with a hood, face mask and overshoes, often worn with gloves and a second pair of shoe covers. It is not there to protect the wearer. It protects the work: delicate machinery, pure chemicals or living cultures that a single hair or flake of skin would ruin. Inside, the wearer hears less and looks anonymous. Outside the lab it looks like a costume.
