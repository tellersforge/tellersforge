# Bio-scanner

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A handheld sampler with a needle probe, a swab slot and a small chamber for leaves, blood or scraped skin. It reads the stuff of life: what species this is, whether it is sick, what toxin runs in its veins, and whether the berry in your hand will kill you. Naturalists, colony doctors and survey crews on strange worlds carry one everywhere, and the most useful thing it says is often simply 'do not eat this'.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Life Sciences identifying species, toxins and disease; +1 Poisons & Antidotes identifying a poison
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Life Sciences](../Skills/life-sciences.md), [Biotechnology](../Skills/biotechnology.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 20 S
- **Also known as:** biological analyser

## Not to be confused with

the *[Medical Scanner](medical-scanner.md)*, which reads the state of a patient's body; the bio-scanner identifies organisms, toxins and disease in samples.
