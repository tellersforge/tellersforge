# Folding solar panel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: computing · modern*

A roll or fold of dark glassy cells, spread out on a rock or tied to a pack, which drinks in sunlight and turns it into electricity for batteries. It is light, silent and needs no fuel, only daylight and patience. Hikers, soldiers and scouts on long journeys depend on it. Its shiny surface catches the sun and can flash for a great distance.
