# Cooling vest

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: computing · modern*

A vest lined with thin tubes carrying chilled water, or with pockets holding packs of cooling gel that draw heat away as they melt. Firefighters, soldiers in hot countries and workers in sweltering engine rooms wear one under their gear. It keeps the body from overheating in conditions that would otherwise fell a person within the hour. The packs need refreezing or the pump needs power, but otherwise it asks for nothing.
