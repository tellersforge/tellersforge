# Gaming tiles

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: printing · eastern empire*

A lacquered box of small carved tiles of bone, bamboo or ivory, painted with characters, dots, flowers and winds. Players draw and discard, hunting for matching sets and runs, and the clack of the tiles being shuffled carries through the evening. In eastern empires the game is played by everyone from courtiers to dockhands, and gambling on it is a vice no law has ever managed to stamp out.
