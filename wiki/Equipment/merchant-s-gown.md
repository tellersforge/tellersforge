# Merchant's gown

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · trading cities*

A long gown of good dyed wool, trimmed at collar and cuffs with fur, cut generously so it hangs in heavy folds. It says, before a word is spoken, that its wearer has money and credit and does not need to make a quick sale. Other merchants take the wearer seriously. In the slums, the same gown marks them as the richest purse on the street.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Haggling showing solid means; +1 Trading & Business among merchants
- **Penalty:** −1 Streetwise marks you as a mark in the slums
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 20 S
- **Also known as:** burgher's gown

*[Scholar's Gown](scholar-s-gown.md)*, a dark, wide-sleeved robe that marks learning rather than wealth.
