# Synthetic sleeping bag

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: computing · modern*

A quilted, hooded bag filled with spun artificial fibre instead of feathers. It is bulkier and heavier than a down bag of the same warmth, but it keeps most of its heat even when damp from rain, sweat or a leaky tent. It dries quickly, washes easily and costs less. Soldiers, cavers and anyone camping somewhere wet usually choose it over down.
