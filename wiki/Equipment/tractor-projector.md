# Tractor projector

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Fields and far-future devices · Needs: far future · far future*

A handheld device with a glowing lens that throws a beam able to grip objects from a few paces away. It pulls crates, pushes boulders and holds heavy parts in place while its operator works. Dockworkers and engineers use it to move loads without touching them. It is slow and steady, not quick or forceful.
