# Holographic disguise

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Fields and far-future devices · Needs: far future · far future*

A collar with a small emitter at the throat that paints a false face, hair and clothes over its wearer. To a casual glance, they are someone else entirely. Spies and con artists find it invaluable. A touch reveals the real face beneath the image, and a scanner sees through it at once.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Disguise changing apparent face and clothes
- **Penalty:** −2 Disguise if touched or scanned
- **Used for:** [Disguise](../Skills/disguise.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 25 S
- **Also known as:** holo-mask

## Not to be confused with

the *[Cloaking Field Emitter](cloaking-field-emitter.md)*, which hides the wearer rather than making them look like someone else.
