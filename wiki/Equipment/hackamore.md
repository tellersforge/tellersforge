# Hackamore

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: agriculture · any*

A bitless bridle with a noseband of rawhide, rope or leather, which works by gentle pressure on the horse's nose and jaw. Horse trainers often use it on young animals, and some riders prefer it for good. It is kinder to the horse, but a spirited or excitable animal can ignore it when it truly wants to run.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** 0
- **Penalty:** −1 Riding control of a hot-blooded mount

the *[Bridle and Bit](bridle-and-bit.md)*, which gives firmer control through the horse's mouth.
