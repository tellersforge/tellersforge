# Frock coat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A fitted coat reaching to the knee, worn with a waistcoat, stiff collar and cravat. It is the uniform of the respectable man of the industrial age: bankers, doctors, lawyers and factory owners. In drawing rooms and boardrooms it opens doors and ensures a hearing. In a dockside tavern it marks the wearer as a mark or an enemy of the working man, and nobody vaults a fence well in its tails.
