# Chip log

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: printing · sea peoples*

A flat wooden board, weighted along one edge, tied to a long line knotted at even spacing. A sailor throws it over the stern, where it sits upright in the water, and counts the knots running out through his fingers while a small sandglass empties. That count is the ship's speed. With speed, heading and time, a navigator can mark his position on the chart even when no star shows.
