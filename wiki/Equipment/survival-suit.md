# Survival suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: computing · modern*

A baggy, bright-coloured suit of thick insulated rubber, pulled on over clothes and boots and zipped to the chin, with a hood and built-in mittens. It floats its wearer and keeps out the killing cold of northern seas for hours instead of minutes. Crews on trawlers and oil platforms practise climbing into them in the dark. Wearing one, a person moves like a child in a snowsuit, and can barely hold a rope.
