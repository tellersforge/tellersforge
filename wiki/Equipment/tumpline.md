# Tumpline

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

A broad strap of leather or woven fibre passed across the forehead or top of the head, with its ends tied to a heavy load on the back. The neck and spine take the weight straight down, and porters carry astonishing loads up mountain trails with it. The bearer leans forward, eyes on the path, and sees little else.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Lifting & Carrying heavy back loads
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception while hauling
- **Used for:** [Lifting & Carrying](../Skills/lifting-and-carrying.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 2 C
- **Also known as:** forehead strap

## Not to be confused with

*[Carrying Frame](carrying-frame.md)*, which puts a load on the shoulders and hips; the tumpline hangs it from the head.
