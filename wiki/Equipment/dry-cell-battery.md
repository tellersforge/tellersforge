# Dry-cell battery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A small sealed cylinder of metal and paste that makes electricity, fitted into torches, radios, toys and clocks. It is cheap, sealed and safe to carry in a pocket. Travellers keep spares, and every drawer in every house seems to hold a few that may or may not still work. It runs down steadily and gives no warning before it dies.
