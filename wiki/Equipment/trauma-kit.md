# Trauma kit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: computing · modern*

A compact pouch worn on the belt or chest, holding clotting gauze, chest seals, pressure bandages and a quick-release tourniquet. Soldiers, police and rescue crews carry it to keep someone alive through the first minutes after a gunshot or blast. Everything is laid out so it can be found by touch, in the dark, with shaking hands. It buys time, and time is what a surgeon needs.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Field Medicine gunshot and blast wounds
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Field Medicine](../Skills/field-medicine.md), [First Aid](../Skills/first-aid.md)
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** individual trauma kit, blowout kit

## Not to be confused with

*[First Aid Kit](first-aid-kit.md)*, which handles everyday cuts and sprains; the trauma kit is made for bullets and blasts.
