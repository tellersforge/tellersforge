# Pain-easing tea

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: agriculture · any*

A folded paper packet of bitter willow bark, poppy heads and dried flowers, steeped in hot water to make a draught that takes the edge off pain. Soldiers drink it after a battle, old people before bed and the injured whenever they can get it. It works slowly and gently. It also blurs the world a little, so the drinker is best kept away from sentry duty for a while.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Pain Tolerance for a few hours after drinking
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception for an hour after drinking
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 2 C
- **Also known as:** willow-bark tea, poppy tea
