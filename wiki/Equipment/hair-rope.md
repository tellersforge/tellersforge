# Hair rope

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: agriculture · steppe riders*

Rope plaited from the long hair of horses, yaks or goats, dark, coarse and bristly. It never rots, shrugs off rain and lasts for generations in the tents of herders who make it. It is also as prickly as a thistle: climbing it hand over hand without gloves is a misery, and a slip leaves the palms full of splinter-like hairs.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** 0
- **Penalty:** −1 DEX bare-handed climbing
- **Used for:** [Knots & Rope Work](../Skills/knots-and-rope-work.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** horsehair rope, yak-hair rope

## Not to be confused with

*[Hemp Rope](hemp-rope.md)*, which is smoother on the hands but rots if left damp.
