# Hologram jewellery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: far future · far future*

A set of tiny projectors, worn as clips, rings or a slender band, that dresses the wearer in shimmering crowns, drifting motes and gems that are not there. At a far-future court it is expected, and the most elaborate displays are a language in themselves. The look can be changed in a heartbeat, which is useful for disguise, but the light gives the wearer away unless it is switched off.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +2 CHA at a far-future court; +1 Disguise quickly changing ornaments
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth unless switched off
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.01 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 15 S
