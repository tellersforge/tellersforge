# Steppe saddle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · steppe riders*

A light saddle on a wooden frame, with short stirrups that let the rider stand up in them. Standing, the rider can twist, lean and shoot a bow in any direction while the horse gallops, and the steppe peoples who invented it built empires on that skill. It is quick to fit and quick to mount, and it asks a great deal of the rider's knees.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Mounted Combat archery from horseback
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Riding](../Skills/riding.md)
- **Weight:** 6 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 25 S
- **Also known as:** wooden-tree saddle

## Not to be confused with

the *[War Saddle](war-saddle.md)*, which locks the rider in for a lance charge rather than freeing them to turn and shoot.
