# Bronze cauldron

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: bronze · any*

A great metal pot with a rim and a pair of ring handles, hung over the fire on a chain or a tripod. It feeds a whole household, a war band or a feast, and it is valuable enough to be counted among a chief's treasures and buried with the dead. Scoured with sand it shines like gold. It takes two people to lift when full, and a good one lasts longer than the family that owns it.
