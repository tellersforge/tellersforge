# Nutrient paste

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A complete balanced food squeezed from a tube or a bulb, made to be eaten in weightless conditions without crumbs drifting into the machinery. It feeds the body perfectly and the spirit not at all. After weeks of it, crews dream of bread and quarrel over nothing.
