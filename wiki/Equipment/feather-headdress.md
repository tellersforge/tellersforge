# Feather headdress

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: stone tools · plains peoples*

A crown of feathers from eagles, hawks or bright tropical birds, each feather earned by a deed of courage or leadership. Among the peoples who make them, a full headdress marks a war leader or respected elder, and the wearer's people follow it gladly. Enemies know it too, and many dread it. It is impossible to sneak anywhere wearing one.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +2 Leadership among the wearer's people; +1 Intimidation against enemies
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth while worn
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.8 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** war bonnet, feather crown

## Not to be confused with

*[Feather cloak](feather-cloak.md)*, a garment of feathers worn over the body; the feather headdress crowns the head and records deeds.
