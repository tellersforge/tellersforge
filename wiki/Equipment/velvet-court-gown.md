# Velvet court gown

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · western kingdoms*

A heavy gown of velvet and brocade over stiff underskirts and hoops, with sleeves slashed to show silk beneath and jewels sewn into the bodice. It is designed for the court and the ballroom, where it commands attention and follows every rule. It weighs as much as a small child, is stifling in summer, and makes running or climbing a comedy.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 Court Etiquette & Protocol at court; +1 Charm & Seduction at a ball
- **Penalty:** −2 Running heavy skirts; −1 Climbing heavy skirts; −1 END in heat
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 5 kg · **Needs:** gunpowder · **Price:** 40 S
- **Also known as:** court dress

## Not to be confused with

*[Low-Cut Bodice Gown](low-cut-bodice-gown.md)*, the fashionable evening gown made to charm at a salon rather than to satisfy court protocol.
