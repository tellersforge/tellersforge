# Wooden barrel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: iron · western kingdoms*

Bent staves of oak or pine bound with hoops of wood or iron into a round, bulging drum that can be rolled by one person and stacked by many. Barrels carry wine, ale, water, salt meat, nails and gunpowder, and the cooper who makes them is respected in any port. A sound barrel lasts for years.
