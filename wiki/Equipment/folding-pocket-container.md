# Folding pocket container

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: far future · far future*

A small pouch whose inside is folded through space so that it holds far more than its outer size shows, perhaps a suitcase's worth in something the size of a wallet. It is a smuggler's dream and a customs officer's nightmare, since nothing about the outside suggests what might be packed within.
