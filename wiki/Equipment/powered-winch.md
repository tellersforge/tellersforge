# Powered winch

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: electricity · modern*

A drum of steel cable turned by a motor, bolted to the front of a vehicle or staked down on a portable frame. It drags a mired truck out of a bog, hoists an engine, pulls a fallen tree off a road or hauls a boat up a ramp. It is tireless and strong. It is also loud, and the growl of its motor carries across a quiet landscape.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Lifting & Carrying dragging or hoisting
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth while running
- **Used for:** [Cargo Handling](../Skills/cargo-handling.md), [Heavy Machinery](../Skills/heavy-machinery.md)
- **Weight:** 12 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 25 S
- **Also known as:** cable winch

## Not to be confused with

*[Block and Tackle](block-and-tackle.md)*, which multiplies human muscle; the winch replaces it with a motor.
