# Bagpipes

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: iron · highland clans*

A leather or hide bag held under the arm, with a blowpipe, a chanter for the melody and a row of drones for the endless steady note beneath. The piper blows, squeezes and the whole thing shrieks into life. It is piercingly loud and was meant to be: highland clans march behind it into battle, and enemies hear them coming. No one has ever played the pipes quietly.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Intimidation before battle
- **Penalty:** −3 Stealth while playing
- **Used for:** [Instrument (per instrument)](../Skills/instrument-per-instrument.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 5 S
