# Toe rings and anklets

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · any*

Chains of silver or brass worn about the ankle, often hung with tiny bells, together with rings on the toes. Dancers wear them to add the music of their own movement to a performance, and they are worn every day in many hot countries. They make every step ring out, which delights an audience and betrays a sneaking wearer.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 Dance during a performance
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth bells or chains jingle
- **Used for:** —

- **Also known as:** anklet, foot jewellery

## Not to be confused with

*[Copper bangles](copper-bangles.md)*, which are worn on the wrist and forearm; toe rings and anklets are worn on the feet.
