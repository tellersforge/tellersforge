# Fur mittens

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

Thick mittens of fur with the hair turned inward, joined by a long cord that runs through the sleeves or around the neck, so they can be shaken off in a hurry and never lost in the snow. They keep fingers alive in cold that would kill them in an hour. Nothing fine can be done in them.

## Game block

- **Slot:** hands
- **Bonus:** +2 END against cold
- **Penalty:** −2 DEX fine handwork
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** mitts

## Not to be confused with

*[Leather Gloves](leather-gloves.md)*, fitted gloves that leave the fingers free but are no match for polar cold.
