# Marked cards

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: printing · any*

A deck that looks perfectly ordinary to anyone who does not know where to look. Tiny changes to the pattern on the backs, a dot here, a curl of a vine there, tell the cheat every card in an opponent's hand. It is a steady living for a patient card-sharp. The first player who finds the marks will never trust them again, and neither will anyone else at the table.
