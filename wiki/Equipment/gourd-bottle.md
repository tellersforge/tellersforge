# Gourd bottle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: agriculture · any*

A gourd grown to shape, hollowed out, dried hard and stoppered with a plug of wood or wax. It holds water, beer or milk and weighs almost nothing. Farmers carry one to the fields and pilgrims hang one from a staff. A cracked gourd is replaced from the next harvest.
