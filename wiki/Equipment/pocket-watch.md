# Pocket watch

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A small round clock of brass, silver or gold, wound by a key or a crown and carried on a chain across the waistcoat. The lid snaps open, the owner frowns, the lid snaps shut. For an age it was the sign of a respectable person with somewhere to be. Railway guards, doctors and officers depend on one. A fine example is an heirloom, and pawnshops are full of the less fine.
