# Locksmith's pick set

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: steam · any*

A fitted case of picks, rakes, tension wrenches and extractors, each ground from spring steel and polished by a master of the trade. Nothing here is improvised. There is a tool for every shape of keyway and a rake for every habit of a manufacturer. Locksmiths use it to open doors whose owners lost the key; others use it for doors whose owners kept theirs. On mechanical locks, it makes hard work pleasantly easy.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Lockpicking mechanical locks
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Lockpicking](../Skills/lockpicking.md), [Locksmithing](../Skills/locksmithing.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 5 S
