# Pick gun

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: electricity · modern*

A pistol-shaped spring tool with a thin steel blade for a barrel. Pushed into a pin-tumbler lock and snapped, it flicks every pin upward at once; a turn of the wrench at the right instant and the plug spins free. It is quick on the sort of lock it was built for. It is also loud, a sharp metallic clack that carries down a quiet corridor, so the user should hope the building is empty.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Lockpicking pin tumbler locks
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth while using it
- **Used for:** [Lockpicking](../Skills/lockpicking.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** snap gun

## Not to be confused with

*[Lockpicks](lockpicks.md)*, slow and silent hand tools that lift pins one by one and work on older locks as well.
