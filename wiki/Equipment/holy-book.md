# Holy book

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: writing · any*

A copy of a faith's scriptures, hymns or prayers, handwritten on vellum or printed on thin paper, and often bound in leather with clasps or kept in a cloth wrapping. Priests preach from it, scholars argue over its every word, and the faithful swear oaths upon it. A quotation from it at the right moment settles more arguments than a sword.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Religion & Theology citing scripture
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Religion & Theology](../Skills/religion-and-theology.md), [Ritual & Liturgy](../Skills/ritual-and-liturgy.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 10 G
- **Also known as:** scripture, prayer book

## Not to be confused with

*[Spellbook](spellbook.md)*, a book of rites and sigils for working magic, rather than the scriptures of a faith.
