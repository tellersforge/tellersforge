# Survival blanket

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: spaceflight · modern*

A sheet of thin, silvered plastic foil that unfolds from a packet the size of a wallet into a cover big enough to wrap a person. It reflects body heat back inward and keeps a cold or injured person from sliding further into shock. It rips easily, and it crinkles and flashes in the light with every movement, which makes the wearer easy to find, whether they want to be found or not.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 END against cold or shock
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth rustles and glints
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** space blanket, foil blanket

## Not to be confused with

*[Foil Blanket](foil-blanket.md)*, the heavier reusable cousin; the survival blanket is the emergency sheet that lives in a pocket.
