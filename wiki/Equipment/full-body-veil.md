# Full-body veil

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · desert cities*

A large piece of cloth, dark or pale, draped over the head and falling to the feet, drawn close with one hand when out of the house. In the desert cities it is worn in public, and it shields from sun and from the gaze of strangers alike. The wearer is almost impossible to identify. They also see less to the side, and the long hem trips a hurried step.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 Disguise hiding face and figure; +1 END against sun
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception narrow view; −1 Running long hem
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** enveloping veil, chador

## Not to be confused with

*[Face Veil](face-veil.md)*, which covers only the face and leaves the body in ordinary clothes.
