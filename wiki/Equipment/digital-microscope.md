# Digital microscope

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: computing · modern*

A camera and lens on a stand or a handheld wand, showing magnified images on a screen and saving them to be shared or studied later. Forensic scientists use it to record traces, electronics technicians to check circuit boards, and biologists to film tiny life. Everything seen can be kept, measured and compared.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Forensics examining and recording traces; +1 Electronics inspecting circuit boards
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Forensics](../Skills/forensics.md), [Life Sciences](../Skills/life-sciences.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 15 S

## Not to be confused with

*[Microscope](microscope.md)*, the older optical instrument, seen through an eyepiece and recording nothing.
