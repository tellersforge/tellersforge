# Deep-ground scanner

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A heavy sled or tracked unit that sends pulses deep into the earth and maps the echoes into a picture of rock layers, voids, ore bodies and buried structures far below the surface. Prospectors use it to find mines, and archaeologists to map whole buried cities without lifting a spade. It is slow, heavy and expensive, but it sees through a mountain.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Geology & Prospecting finding ore and cavities; +2 Archaeology mapping buried ruins
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Geology & Prospecting](../Skills/geology-and-prospecting.md), [Mining & Quarrying](../Skills/mining-and-quarrying.md)
- **Weight:** 8 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 40 S
- **Also known as:** ground-penetrating scanner

## Not to be confused with

*[Metal Detector](metal-detector.md)*, which finds only metal close to the surface.
