# Burglar alarm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: electricity · modern*

A network of little switches fixed to doors and window frames, wired to a battery and a brass bell on the outside wall. Open anything without first turning the key in the control box and the bell rings until someone comes. Shops and townhouses fit them, and a sticker in the window warning of the alarm deters half the burglars on its own. The other half learn to find the wires.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Perception noticing break-ins
- **Penalty:** 0
