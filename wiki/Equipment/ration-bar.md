# Ration bar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: computing · modern*

A dense block of pressed grain, fat and sugar wrapped in foil, packing a day's energy into something the size of a pocket notebook. It keeps for years and can be eaten while walking. It is edible, and that is about the most anyone says for it.
