# All-language translator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Fields and far-future devices · Needs: far future · far future*

A slim device that listens to any spoken language and within minutes learns it well enough to translate both ways. It lets explorers speak to peoples they have never met. It is remarkable, but not perfect: idioms, gestures and customs pass it by, and speakers sometimes find themselves saying the right words the wrong way.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +3 Language (per language) speaking any tongue it has heard
- **Penalty:** −1 Cultures & Customs misses idioms and gestures
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** — · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** translator implant, speech converter

## Not to be confused with

the *[Electronic Translator](electronic-translator.md)*, which knows only a fixed set of languages and speaks slowly and flatly.
