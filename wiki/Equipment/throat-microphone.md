# Throat microphone

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: electricity · modern*

A thin collar with small microphones that press against the throat and pick up the vibrations of speech directly. Paired with a radio and earpiece, it lets the wearer talk clearly in whispers or through gunfire, engine roar and wind. Pilots, tank crews and special forces all use them. The voice it produces sounds flat and strange, but perfectly understandable.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Stealth talking on a radio quietly
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Communications & Sensors](../Skills/communications-and-sensors.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** neck mic

## Not to be confused with

*[Earpiece](earpiece.md)*, which is a hidden receiver with a small microphone; the throat microphone picks up speech from the throat itself.
