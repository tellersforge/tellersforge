# Floating compass

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: iron · any*

A sewing needle, stroked with a lodestone, pushed through a straw or a scrap of cork and set to float in a bowl of water. It turns slowly and settles on north. It is cheap, simple and surprisingly reliable on land when the sky is overcast. On a ship it is another matter: every roll of the deck slops the water and sets the needle wandering, and a helmsman learns to trust it only in calm weather.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Wayfinding overcast skies
- **Penalty:** −1 Wayfinding on a rolling deck
- **Used for:** [Wayfinding](../Skills/wayfinding.md), [Sea Navigation](../Skills/sea-navigation.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** wet compass, needle bowl

## Not to be confused with

the *[Dry Compass](dry-compass.md)*, a needle or card pivoted in a closed box, which stays steady at sea.
