# Wooden toy

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: stone tools · any*

A small carved thing for small hands: a spinning top, a doll with painted eyes, a horse on wheels to be dragged along on a string. Parents carve them by the fire, and pedlars sell them at fairs. Given to a frightened or restless child, a toy can do what no amount of stern words manages, and many a crying infant has been quieted with one.
