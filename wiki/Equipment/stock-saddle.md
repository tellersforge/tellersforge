# Stock saddle

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: gunpowder · plains peoples*

A deep leather saddle with a high horn at the front and wide fenders over the stirrups. Herders tie a rope to the horn to hold a roped steer, and the deep seat keeps them comfortable through long days in the saddle. It is heavy, sturdy and practical, and a herder often owns a saddle worth more than the horse under it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Herding & Husbandry roping and herding; +1 Endurance long rides
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Riding](../Skills/riding.md)
- **Weight:** 15 kg · **Needs:** gunpowder · **Price:** 30 S
- **Also known as:** herder's saddle, cattle saddle

## Not to be confused with

the *[War Saddle](war-saddle.md)*, built to hold a rider firm in a charge rather than through long days of herding.
