# Mourning garments

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: pottery · any*

Clothes in the colour a people keeps for grief, black in some lands, white or undyed in others, worn for weeks or years after a death. They tell everyone the wearer has lost someone. Strangers speak gently to them and give way. At a feast or a flirtation, the same clothes make every bit of merriment feel like disrespect.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Etiquette at funerals and in mourning; +1 Persuasion appeals for sympathy
- **Penalty:** −2 Carousing at feasts; −1 Charm & Seduction while in mourning
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** pottery · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** mourning clothes, widow's weeds

## Not to be confused with

*[Sackcloth](sackcloth.md)*, the rough garment of penance and grief worn before the gods rather than before the family.
