# Coin headdress

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: iron · steppe riders*

A cap or veil hung with rows of silver coins, sometimes chains and charms among them, which chime and flash when the wearer moves. Among the steppe riders a woman's headdress is both her jewellery and her savings, added to over a lifetime. It is glorious in a dance and delightful at a festival. It is useless for creeping anywhere.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +1 Dance during a performance; +1 CHA at festivals
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth clinks when moving
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.6 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 40 S
- **Also known as:** coin veil, coin cap
