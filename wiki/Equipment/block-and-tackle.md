# Block and tackle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: iron · any*

Two wooden or iron pulley blocks, each with several wheels, threaded with a long rope. One block is hung from a beam or tree, the other hooked to the load, and a steady pull on the free end raises what a crowd couldn't lift. Ships, builders and warehouses depend on it. It needs a solid anchor above, and a great deal of rope passes through the hands for a little rise.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Lifting & Carrying hoisting with a fixed anchor
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Cargo Handling](../Skills/cargo-handling.md), [Construction](../Skills/construction.md)
- **Weight:** 5 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** pulley set, rope hoist

## Not to be confused with

*[Single Pulley](single-pulley.md)*, which only turns the direction of a pull; the block and tackle multiplies it.
