# Synthesizer keyboard

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: computing · modern*

A plastic and metal keyboard full of electronics, able to sound like strings, horns, drums or things no one has heard before. It can record a track, loop it, add another on top, and build a whole piece of music in a small room. Composers use it to sketch pieces before an orchestra plays them. Beginners use it to make one finger sound like a symphony.
