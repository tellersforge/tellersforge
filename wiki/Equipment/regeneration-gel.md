# Regeneration gel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: far future · far future*

A soft tube of pale, faintly warm gel that is alive in its own way. Spread over a torn wound and left under a dressing, it slowly regrows the missing flesh and skin over the next few hours, leaving at most a faint pink mark. It must be kept cool and used before it spoils. The sensation of new skin forming is, those who have felt it agree, extremely itchy.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Life restores Life when spread on wounds (Quick Heal)
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [First Aid](../Skills/first-aid.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 30 S
- **Also known as:** regrowth gel

## Not to be confused with

*[Wound-Sealing Spray](wound-sealing-spray.md)*, which only seals a wound; the gel rebuilds the flesh beneath it.
