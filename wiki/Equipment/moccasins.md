# Moccasins

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

Soft shoes made from a single piece of deer or elk hide gathered and stitched over the top of the foot, sometimes embroidered with quills or beads. The wearer feels every twig through the sole and learns to step around it, and so moves silently through the woods. In wet snow they soak through and freeze.

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 Stealth moving quietly
- **Penalty:** −1 END in snow once wet
