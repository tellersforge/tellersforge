# Court robe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · imperial courts*

Layer upon layer of silk robes, the outermost embroidered on breast and back with a square badge showing the wearer's rank: a crane, a tiger, a cloud. At an imperial audience nothing else is permitted, and every detail of colour and stitch is read by the officials present. The sleeves are heavy, the silk rustles at every step, and in a back alley the robe announces a rich victim.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 Court Etiquette & Protocol at court; +1 CHA before officials
- **Penalty:** −1 DEX heavy sleeves and layers; −2 Stealth rustling silk and bright colour; −1 Streetwise marks you as rich
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 4 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 20 S
- **Also known as:** embroidered robe, dragon robe

## Not to be confused with

*[Wrapped Robe](wrapped-robe.md)*, the everyday robe of the same empire, worn at home and on calls, carrying no badge of rank.
