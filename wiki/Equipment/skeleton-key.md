# Skeleton key

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: iron · any*

An ordinary iron key with most of its bit filed away, leaving only a thin stem and a hooked tip. Old warded locks guard themselves with fixed rings and plates meant to stop the wrong key; this one slips past them all and turns the bolt. Gaolers, landlords and thieves have all carried one. Against the pin-tumbler locks of the industrial age it is almost useless, a relic rattling in a modern keyhole.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Lockpicking simple warded locks
- **Penalty:** −2 Lockpicking against tumbler locks
- **Used for:** [Lockpicking](../Skills/lockpicking.md)
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** master key, passe-partout

## Not to be confused with

*[Lockpicks](lockpicks.md)*, which pick a lock one ward or pin at a time and work on far more kinds of lock, slowly.
