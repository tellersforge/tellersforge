# Painkiller injector

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: computing · modern*

A single-use tube with a spring-loaded needle and a dose of strong painkiller. Pressed against the thigh, even through cloth, it clicks and fires. Within minutes the wounded can move, talk and sometimes keep fighting. It is standard issue for soldiers and rescue teams. The drug that stops the pain also clouds the mind, and those who have used it remember very little of the hour that follows.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Pain Tolerance for an hour after injecting
- **Penalty:** −1 INT for an hour after injecting
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** morphine syrette

## Not to be confused with

*[Medical Injector](medical-injector.md)*, which also heals wounds; the painkiller injector only masks the pain.
