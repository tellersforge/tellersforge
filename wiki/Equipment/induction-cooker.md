# Induction cooker

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: computing · modern*

A flat glass-topped plate, sometimes folding into a case, that heats any iron or steel pot set on it by a hidden field, while staying cool to the touch itself. There is no flame, no smoke and no smell but the cooking. It needs a steady supply of electricity, which makes it the stove of kitchens, vehicles, boats and ships rather than of the trail. Aluminium and clay pots sit on it and stay stubbornly cold.
