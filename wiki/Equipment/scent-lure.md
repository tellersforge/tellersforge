# Scent lure

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

A small stoppered bottle of musk, gland oil or rotted bait, dabbed on a post, a trap or a hunter's own boots to draw game from far off. The smell is rich and foul to a person and irresistible to the right animal. Trappers guard their recipes like family secrets. The trouble is that it clings: anything with a good nose can follow the wearer as easily as the wearer hoped to follow it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Hunting drawing game; +1 Trapping any
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth against animals with a keen nose
- **Used for:** [Hunting](../Skills/hunting.md), [Trapping](../Skills/trapping.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** musk lure

## Not to be confused with

*[Game Call](game-call.md)*, which draws animals with sound; the lure works by smell and keeps working after the hunter has gone.
