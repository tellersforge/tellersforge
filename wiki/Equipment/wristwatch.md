# Wristwatch

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: electricity · modern*

A small clock on a leather or metal strap, worn at the wrist where a glance is enough. Soldiers took to them first, because pulling out a pocket watch in a trench is awkward. They run on a wound spring, a battery or light, and range from cheap tin to jewelled heirlooms. A watch face can glint at the wrong moment, and a dead one is only a bracelet.
