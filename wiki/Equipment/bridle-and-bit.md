# Bridle and bit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: bronze · any*

A headstall of leather straps with a metal or bone bit that sits in the horse's mouth, joined to reins in the rider's hands. Pressure on the bit tells the horse to turn, slow or stop. It gives the rider fine control, but a heavy hand can hurt the animal and spoil its mouth. Riders judge each other by how gently they use it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Riding control
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Riding](../Skills/riding.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** headstall, reins

## Not to be confused with

the *[Hackamore](hackamore.md)*, a bitless bridle that works on the nose instead.
