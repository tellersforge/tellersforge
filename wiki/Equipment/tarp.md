# Tarp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: electricity · any*

A plain rectangle of waxed cloth or plastic sheet with metal eyes or tie loops along its edges and middle. Strung between two trees it is a roof; pegged at one side it is a lean-to; folded under a sleeper it is a groundsheet; spread on the grass it is a table or a rain catcher. Experienced travellers prefer it to any tent. It keeps off rain from above, if not the wind from the side.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 END against rain
- **Penalty:** 0
