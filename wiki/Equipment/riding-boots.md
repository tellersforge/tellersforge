# Riding boots

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · steppe riders*

Tall boots rising to the knee with soft, wrinkled tops that let the leg bend at a gallop and a firm heel that locks into the stirrup and will not slip through. Riders of the steppe live in them. On foot, the stiff soles and heels make the wearer walk with the rolling gait of someone who belongs on a horse.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 Riding in the stirrup
- **Penalty:** −1 Running stiff soles and heels
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** horse boots

*[Thigh Boots](thigh-boots.md)*, taller boots reaching over the knee, worn by cavaliers and fishermen rather than steppe riders.
