# Memory stick

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: computing · modern*

A thumb-sized stick of plastic or metal with a plug at one end, holding a great quantity of computer files. It carries documents, pictures and programs from one machine to another, or hides them in a pocket, a shoe or a sealed envelope. Its smallness is its strength and its weakness: stolen easily, lost more easily still, and occasionally put through a washing machine.
