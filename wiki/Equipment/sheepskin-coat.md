# Sheepskin coat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: agriculture · steppe riders*

A long coat of tanned sheepskin, leather outside and fleece within, the winter coat of shepherds and horse nomads on the open grasslands. It keeps out the steppe wind that cuts through wool, and a rider can sleep in it on the ground. It is bulky enough to turn every scramble and tumble into hard work, and in summer it is unbearable.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 END against cold
- **Penalty:** −1 END in heat; −1 Acrobatics bulky
