# Body paint kit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: stone tools · any*

Little pots of ochre, white clay, charcoal and plant dyes, with brushes of chewed twig or feather, for painting the face and body. Warriors paint themselves before battle to look fearsome and to call on the strength of their ancestors, and priests and dancers use it in ceremony. Painted, the wearer is a figure of power. In a city among strangers, the paint marks them as outlandish and wild.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Intimidation in battle when applied; +1 Ritual & Liturgy for ceremonies
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA among strangers or in a city
- **Used for:** [Tattooing & Body Art](../Skills/tattooing-and-body-art.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 2 C
- **Also known as:** ochre paints, war paint

## Not to be confused with

*[Ritual body paint](ritual-body-paint.md)*, the paint itself prepared for a single rite; the body paint kit is the reusable set of pigments and tools.
