# Bell tripwire

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: stone tools · any*

A length of cord strung round a camp at ankle height, tied off to stakes or bushes and hung with little bells, shells, bones or empty tins. Anything that brushes it rattles, and the sentry turns. It is an ancient trick and it still works, though animals find it as often as enemies, and a clever intruder steps carefully over it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Danger Sense against intruders at camp
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Traps](../Skills/traps.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** rattle line

## Not to be confused with

*[Tripwire Alarm](tripwire-alarm.md)*, which is fixed to a building or door; the bell tripwire is a portable camp version made from cord and rattles.
