# Microscope

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: printing · any*

A heavy brass instrument of stacked lenses, a stage for a glass slide and a mirror to catch the light. Through it, a drop of pond water teems with life, a fleck of blood shows its cells, and a thread from a crime scene reveals its fibres. Natural philosophers, physicians and investigators have all changed their minds after looking through one.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Life Sciences studying tiny organisms; +1 Forensics examining traces
