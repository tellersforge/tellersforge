# Ritual body paint

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: stone tools · any*

Pots and pouches of red and yellow ochre, white clay, charcoal and ash, mixed with fat or water and painted on skin in patterns handed down from elders. For rites, the paint makes the wearer a vessel of something greater. For war, it makes them a thing to be feared. In a foreign court, it makes them a strange savage in the eyes of the courtiers.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Ritual & Liturgy painted for rites; +1 Intimidation as war paint
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA in foreign courts
- **Used for:** [Tattooing & Body Art](../Skills/tattooing-and-body-art.md), [Ritual & Liturgy](../Skills/ritual-and-liturgy.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** war paint, ochre pots

## Not to be confused with

*[Body Paint Kit](body-paint-kit.md)*, which is used for decoration and disguise rather than for rites and war.
