# Falconry glove

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · steppe riders*

A thick, stiff glove of heavy leather that covers the hand and much of the forearm. The falconer's bird perches on it, gripping with talons that would tear bare skin to the bone. Falconers wear it on the off hand, keep it oiled and supple, and never lend it to anyone. A good glove lasts a lifetime and carries the scratches of every bird it has held.
