# Radar set

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: electricity · modern*

A folding dish that turns and turns on its mast, and a round scope where a green line sweeps and leaves glowing dots behind it. Each dot is something far away: an aircraft over the hills, a ship beyond the horizon, the ragged edge of a coming storm. Coast watchers, ship crews and forward outposts trust it more than their eyes. Its signal, however, is loud to anyone with the right receiver, and a radar that is switched on is a radar that can be found.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Perception spotting aircraft, ships and storms at range
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth its emissions can be traced
- **Used for:** [Communications & Sensors](../Skills/communications-and-sensors.md)
- **Weight:** 150 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 60 S

## Not to be confused with

*[Binoculars](binoculars.md)*, which see far only in daylight and in clear weather, but never betray the watcher.
