# Hand drum

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: stone tools · any*

An animal skin stretched tight over a wooden hoop, a hollow log or a clay pot, and tied down with sinew or cord. It is played with the palms and fingertips, and everyone can make a sound on it while few can make it talk. It keeps time for dancers and marchers, gathers people to a fire, and in some hands carries signals from village to village.
