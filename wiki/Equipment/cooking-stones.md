# Cooking stones

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: fire · any*

A handful of fist-sized river stones, heated in the fire until they glow and then dropped with tongs or forked sticks into a hide bag, a tight basket or a pit of water. The water hisses and churns and, stone after stone, comes to the boil. It is how people cooked stews before they had pots, and how clever travellers still cook when the pot is lost. Wet stones from a stream can crack in the fire, sometimes violently.
