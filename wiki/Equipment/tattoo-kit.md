# Tattoo kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: stone tools · any*

A roll of needles, bone combs, pots of ink made from soot and plant juice, and a small mallet for tapping the comb into the skin. In many lands tattoos tell stories: rank, family, deeds, protection or shame. The tattooist is often a respected figure, trusted with meaning as much as skill. The work hurts and the ink is permanent.
