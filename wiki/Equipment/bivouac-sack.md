# Bivouac sack

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: computing · modern*

A waterproof, breathable bag just big enough to hold a person in a sleeping bag, with a hood or a zip across the face. There is no pitching, only climbing in. It keeps off rain, wind and spindrift on a ledge where no tent would fit. Inside, it is like sleeping in a coffin with a view of the stars, and condensation slowly damps everything by morning.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 END in rain or wind
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** bivvy bag

## Not to be confused with

*[Tarp](tarp.md)*, which gives a roof to sit under; the bivouac sack encloses the sleeper and nothing else.
