# Fluid bladder

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: computing · modern*

A soft reservoir of flexible plastic slipped inside a pack, with a long tube running over the shoulder to a bite valve. Runners, hikers and soldiers sip from it on the move without stopping or reaching back. Small, steady drinks keep the body going far longer than long, rare ones.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Endurance sipping while on the march
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** hydration bladder, drinking reservoir

## Not to be confused with

*[Canteen](canteen.md)*, which must be unclipped and opened, so the walker drinks less often.
