# Hooded desert cloak

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · desert peoples*

A loose woollen cloak with a deep hood, pale enough to throw back the sun at noon and thick enough to hold warmth when the desert night turns cold. When the sand blows, the wearer turns their back to the wind and waits inside it like a tent. The hood that keeps off sun and grit also shuts out everything to either side.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +2 END against desert sun and sandstorm
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception hood limits side view
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 4 S
- **Also known as:** burnous

## Not to be confused with

*[Loose Desert Robe](loose-desert-robe.md)*, the garment worn beneath: the robe keeps the body cool, the cloak keeps the weather off.
