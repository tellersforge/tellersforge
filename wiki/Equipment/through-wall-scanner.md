# Through-wall scanner

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A flat, dark panel the size of a serving tray, pressed against a wall or held a hand's breadth from a door. Its face fills with grey ghosts: a crouching shape behind a table, two figures by a window, the slow pulse of someone trying very hard to hold still. Boarding crews and rescue teams carry them for the same reason, which is that the worst moment of any room is the moment before the door opens. Thick metal and dense stone blur the picture to mush.

- **Bonus:** +2 Searching seeing movement behind walls; +1 Military Tactics planning a room entry
- **Penalty:** 0

## Not to be confused with

the *[Deep-Ground Scanner](deep-ground-scanner.md)*, which looks down through soil and rock for buried things; this panel looks sideways through walls for people.
