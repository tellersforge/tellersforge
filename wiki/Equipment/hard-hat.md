# Hard hat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · industrial cities*

A light shell of pressed fibre, metal or moulded plastic that sits on a webbing harness rather than directly on the head, so a blow spreads out before it reaches the skull. On building sites and in shipyards and mines, no one goes under the scaffolding without one. It turns a dropped spanner or a shower of brick into a bad moment rather than a funeral. Worn anywhere polite, it looks faintly ridiculous.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +1 armour against falling debris
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA in polite society
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.4 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** safety helmet, hard helmet
