# Parchment roll

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: iron · western kingdoms*

Sheets of animal skin, soaked, scraped, stretched and dried until thin and pale, stitched into a roll or cut into pages. It is tough, it takes ink cleanly, and errors can be scraped away with a knife. Written parchment can survive for centuries. It is costly, since each sheet is a calf or sheep, so old texts are often scraped clean and written over by a later hand.
