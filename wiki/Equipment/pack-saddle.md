# Pack saddle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: bronze · any*

A wooden frame with padding, fitted over the back of a horse, mule, donkey or camel so that loads can be hung on both sides. With good packing, an animal can carry heavy loads over rough country where no cart could go. Caravan masters judge a packer by how evenly the load sits, because a sore back ends an animal's usefulness.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Logistics animal loads
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Herding & Husbandry](../Skills/herding-and-husbandry.md), [Logistics](../Skills/logistics.md)
- **Weight:** 8 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** sawbuck, pack frame

*[Saddlebags](saddlebags.md)*, which carry a rider's gear rather than a full pack load.
