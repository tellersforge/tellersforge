# Tape recorder

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: electricity · modern*

A box with two reels of brown magnetic tape, a microphone and a speaker. It records voices, music or any sound and plays it back, and the tape can be wound back, erased and used again. Journalists, musicians and secret police all find uses for it. The machines are heavy, and the tapes stretch, tangle and spill out of their reels at the worst possible moment.
