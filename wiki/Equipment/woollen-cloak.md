# Woollen cloak

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: pottery · any*

A broad half-circle or rectangle of heavy woven wool, fulled until it sheds water, with a hood, and pinned at the shoulder with a brooch or a thorn. It is the travelling coat of half the world for thousands of years. By day it keeps off rain and wind, by night it is a bed, and with the hood drawn up after dark the wearer is only a shape in the street. In deep water it drinks the river and drags its owner down.
