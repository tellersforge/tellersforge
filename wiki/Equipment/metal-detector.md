# Metal detector

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: electricity · modern*

A flat disc on the end of a pole, connected to a box with a dial and a pair of headphones. Swept slowly over the ground, it whines and hums when it passes over buried metal. Treasure hunters, archaeologists, prospectors and soldiers clearing mines use it. The whine and the slow sweeping make it impossible to go unnoticed while using one.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Searching finding buried or hidden metal
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth its whine and sweep draw notice
- **Used for:** [Archaeology](../Skills/archaeology.md), [Geology & Prospecting](../Skills/geology-and-prospecting.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 8 S

## Not to be confused with

*[Dowsing Rod](dowsing-rod.md)*, a twig or pendulum said to find hidden things, with nothing but faith behind it.
