# Nylon climbing rope

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: computing · modern*

A long rope with a smooth, woven outer sheath over a core of stretchy synthetic fibre, coiled in bright colours. When a climber falls, it stretches and absorbs the shock, so the stop is sharp rather than bone-breaking. It is light, wears slowly and is washed and inspected with care. Every serious climber knows the history of their rope, and retires it after one bad fall.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing when a fall is held
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md), [Mountaineering](../Skills/mountaineering.md), [Knots & Rope Work](../Skills/knots-and-rope-work.md)
- **Weight:** 3.5 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** kernmantle rope, dynamic rope

## Not to be confused with

*[Hemp Rope](hemp-rope.md)*, which holds a load but stops a falling body like a wall.
