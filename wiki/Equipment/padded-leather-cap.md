# Padded leather cap

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · miners and workers*

A close-fitting cap of thick leather, stuffed with wool or horsehair and stitched in rows. Miners, masons and carpenters wear it to soften the knock of a low beam, a dropped tool or a stone falling from above. It is not a war helmet, but underground, where the ceiling is always too low, it saves many a skull.
