# Desert sandals

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · desert peoples*

Sandals with wide, flat soles that spread the wearer's weight across the sand, like the feet of a camel. The foot stays on top of the dunes rather than sinking in at every step, and a day's walk becomes far less exhausting. They also keep the soles of the feet off sand hot enough to burn.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 Survival: Desert walking on sand
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —

- **Also known as:** broad-soled sandals
