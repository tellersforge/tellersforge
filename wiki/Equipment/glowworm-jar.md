# Glowworm jar

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

A jar of glowing insects or luminous fungus that gives off a faint, cold, greenish light. Forest peoples use it to find their way along a path at night or to watch over a sleeping child. It will not light a room or a book, but it needs no fire, and the insects can be let go at dawn.
