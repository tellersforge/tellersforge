# Orbital nav computer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A ship's computer that turns the pull of planets and moons into numbers a pilot can fly. It plots burns, predicts orbits, calculates intercepts and guides a docking approach to within a hand's breadth. Pilots fly by it all day and argue with it constantly. It knows the physics perfectly and nothing at all about the station commander who has just changed the docking bay.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Astrogation in-system courses; +1 Spacecraft Piloting docking and orbits
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Astrogation](../Skills/astrogation.md), [Spacecraft Piloting](../Skills/spacecraft-piloting.md)
- **Weight:** 10 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 30 S
- **Also known as:** flight computer

## Not to be confused with

the *[Star-Drive Navigation Module](star-drive-navigation-module.md)*, which plots jumps between star systems rather than courses within one.
