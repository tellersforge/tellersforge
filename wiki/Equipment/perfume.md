# Perfume

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · any*

A small vial of scented oil or alcohol, blended from flowers, resins, spices and musk. Worn on the wrists and throat, it leaves a trail in every room and lingers after the wearer has gone. In refined society and close company it charms. It also tells every dog, tracker and wild beast exactly where the wearer is, and any hunter who wears it has already failed.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Charm & Seduction in close company; +1 CHA in refined society
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth against animals or trackers by scent; −1 Hunting while worn
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** scent, attar, cologne

## Not to be confused with

*[Scented oil](scented-oil.md)*, which is milder and rubbed into skin and hair; perfume is stronger and carries farther.
