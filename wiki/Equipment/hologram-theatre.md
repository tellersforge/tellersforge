# Hologram theatre

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: far future · far future*

A projector no bigger than a trunk that fills a room with lifelike moving figures and scenery: a battle, a storm at sea, a dancing crowd. Actors can step among the images, and audiences gasp as a dragon or a ship passes overhead. Storytellers and far-future broadcasters use it for shows, lessons and propaganda alike.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Storytelling & Conversation staged shows
- **Penalty:** 0

- **Also known as:** sim projector

*[Holographic Projector](holographic-projector.md)*, a portable device for showing a single image or message, not staging a whole show.
