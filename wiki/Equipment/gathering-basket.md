# Gathering basket

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

An open woven basket carried on the arm or hip, used by forest peoples to collect berries, roots, nuts and herbs. Its weave is tight enough for small seeds and its shape lets the gatherer drop things in without looking. Children learn to fill one before they learn much else.
