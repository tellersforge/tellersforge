# Breathing-mask hood

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A clear, soft hood that pulls over the whole head and seals at the neck, with a filter at the mouth or a small tank of air. Ship crews keep them near every hatch for fires; colonists pull them on in dust storms and on worlds where the air is thin. It keeps the wearer breathing where smoke or dust would kill. The hood narrows side vision, and voices sound muffled through it.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +2 END against smoke, dust and thin air
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception side vision; −1 CHA speech is muffled
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.6 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** filter hood, smoke hood

## Not to be confused with

*[Breather mask](breather-mask.md)*, which covers only the mouth and nose; the breathing-mask hood encloses the whole head.
