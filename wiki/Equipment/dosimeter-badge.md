# Dosimeter badge

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: computing · modern*

A small film or crystal badge pinned to the chest, blank and harmless-looking. It does not warn; it remembers. Every trace of radiation that passes through its wearer darkens it a little, and at the end of a shift or a mission it is read to learn how much of the day's poison the body has taken on. Workers in reactors, hospitals and wastelands wear them, and some keep the old badges as grim souvenirs of bad weeks.

the *[Radiation Counter](radiation-counter.md)*, which clicks a live warning; the badge only tells you afterwards how much you have already soaked up.
