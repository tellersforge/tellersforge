# Flintknapping kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: stone tools · any*

A roll of soft hide holding the stone toolmaker's gear: a round hammerstone for the heavy blows, a length of antler for the finer ones, a pointed bone for pressing off tiny flakes, and a thick pad to protect the thigh. With it and a good nodule of flint or obsidian, a skilled knapper makes knives, spearheads and scrapers in an afternoon. The unskilled make sharp gravel and bleeding hands.
