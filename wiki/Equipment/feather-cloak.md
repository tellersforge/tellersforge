# Feather cloak

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: pottery · island peoples*

A chief's cloak covered in thousands of tiny bright feathers, red and yellow and black, each knotted by hand to a fine fibre net in patterns handed down for generations. Birds were caught, plucked of a few feathers and released for years to make one. When the chief wears it, everyone knows who leads. Rain ruins the feathers.
