# Calendar stick

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: stone tools · any*

A length of wood or bone notched once for each day, with deeper cuts for new moons and marks for feasts, hunts and plantings. Some peoples use knotted cords instead, with colours for seasons. It is a memory held in the hand, and the one who keeps it is often the one who decides when to plant and when to celebrate. Lose it and the village argues about what day it is.

## Not to be confused with

the *[Gnomon Stick](gnomon-stick.md)*, which finds the time of day and season from the sun rather than counting the days.
