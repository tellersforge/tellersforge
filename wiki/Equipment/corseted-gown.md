# Corseted gown

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A bodice laced tight over a stiff corset, above wide skirts held out by hoops or petticoats, trimmed with lace, ribbons and silk flowers. It is the height of fashion for balls and salons, and a woman wearing one well commands attention across the room. It is also nearly impossible to run, climb or even breathe deeply in, and a long evening in it is an ordeal of endurance.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 Etiquette at a ball or salon; +1 Charm & Seduction at a society event
- **Penalty:** −2 Running while worn; −2 Climbing while worn; −1 END sustained exertion
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 4 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** ball gown, crinoline gown

## Not to be confused with

*[Corset](corset.md)*, the stiffened undergarment alone; the corseted gown is the complete evening dress built over it.
