# Telegraph key

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A small brass key on a wooden base, a clicking sounder and a heavy battery, packed in a case. Wired into a line, it taps out coded messages of long and short pulses that arrive at the far end in an instant. Railway stations, army camps and newspapers depended on it. A skilled operator's touch is so distinctive that others on the line can recognise who is sending by rhythm alone.
