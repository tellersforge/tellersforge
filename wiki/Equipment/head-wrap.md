# Head wrap

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: bronze · desert peoples*

A long length of cotton cloth wound around the head in turns, shading the skull from the sun and keeping sand out of the hair. The loose end can be drawn across the face as a veil, and unwound, the whole cloth makes a rope, a bandage, a water filter or a sling. Desert travellers never go without one.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +1 END against sun and sand
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** turban, head cloth
