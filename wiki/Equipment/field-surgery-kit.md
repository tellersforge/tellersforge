# Field surgery kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: gunpowder · western kingdoms*

A wooden case with brass corners that opens to show bone saws, bullet forceps, long probes, tourniquets, silk ligatures and knives, each nested in its own velvet slot. It belongs to the army surgeon who works in a tent behind the lines with the guns still firing. The kit is built for speed: finding the ball, stopping the bleeding, taking off what cannot be saved. Its owners rarely sleep, and they are rarely thanked.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Surgery gunshot and blade wounds
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Surgery](../Skills/surgery.md), [Field Medicine](../Skills/field-medicine.md)
- **Weight:** 5 kg · **Needs:** gunpowder · **Price:** 30 S
- **Also known as:** amputation kit, bone saw case

## Not to be confused with

*[Surgeon's Instrument Roll](surgeon-s-instrument-roll.md)*, which is a general set; the field kit is made for gunshot and sword wounds under fire.
