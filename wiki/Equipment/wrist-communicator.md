# Wrist communicator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A slim band worn on the wrist, with a small speaker and microphone, linking the wearer to crewmates across a ship, a station or the surface of a world. A tap and a spoken name open the channel. Spacers take it for granted, like the air they breathe. When it goes silent, people feel suddenly very far from home.
