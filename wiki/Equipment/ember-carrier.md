# Ember carrier

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: stone tools · any*

A horn, shell or clay pot packed with smouldering punk or fungus, with a live coal nursed inside. It keeps a fire alive through a day's march, so the evening fire can be lit from it without any spark or friction at all. Wandering peoples carried fire this way for thousands of years, and letting it go out was a disgrace.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Fire-Making relighting from carried embers
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.4 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** fire horn, slow-match box

## Not to be confused with

*[Tinderbox](tinderbox.md)*, which makes a new fire from sparks; an ember carrier keeps the old fire alive.
