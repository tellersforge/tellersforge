# Slide rule

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: printing · any*

Two long rulers marked with scales that slide against each other, with a clear cursor to line up the marks. Engineers, navigators and scientists carry one in a leather case, and in their hands it multiplies, divides and takes roots in seconds. It gives an answer to three figures, not more, and the user has to know where the decimal point goes. Old engineers still keep theirs polished long after machines replaced them.
