# Guild badge

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: iron · any*

A badge of enamel, cast metal or embroidered cloth bearing the arms of a trade guild: hammers for smiths, scales for merchants, a ship for mariners. It admits the wearer to guild halls and marks them as trusted in the trade. Fellow members deal fairly with them. Rival guilds and thieves see a target.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 Trading & Business among guild members
- **Penalty:** −1 Streetwise among rival guilds or thieves
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** guild token, craft badge

## Not to be confused with

*[Faction colours](faction-colours.md)*, which mark political or gang loyalty; the guild badge marks trade membership.
