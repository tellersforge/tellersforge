# Reading spectacles

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: printing · any*

A pair of ground glass lenses set in a wire or horn frame, perched on the nose or held by a handle. They let an ageing scholar, clerk or merchant read small writing again after years of squinting at arm's length. The world beyond the page swims into a blur while they are on, so most wearers peer over the top of them at anyone across the room.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +1 Research reading small print when long-sighted
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception at a distance while worn
- **Used for:** —

*[Magnifying Lens](magnifying-lens.md)*, a single handheld glass for looking closely at one object rather than reading whole pages.
