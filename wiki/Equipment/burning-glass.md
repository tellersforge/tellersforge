# Burning glass

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: printing · any*

A convex lens of polished glass or crystal that gathers the sun into a single burning point. Held steady over tinder, it raises a curl of smoke and then a flame. It needs no fuel and never wears out. It also needs sunlight, and on cloudy days or at night it is only a pretty piece of glass.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Fire-Making in bright sun
- **Penalty:** −2 Fire-Making on cloudy days or at night
- **Used for:** [Fire-Making](../Skills/fire-making.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** magnifying lens

## Not to be confused with

*[Magnifying lens](magnifying-lens.md)*, the same kind of glass used for reading and close work rather than fire.
