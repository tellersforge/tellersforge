# Headscarf

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: agriculture · any*

A square or length of cloth drawn over the hair and tied beneath the chin or behind the neck. In many faiths and customs it is required for women in temples and in public, and for men in others. It also keeps off dust and sun. Wearing one in the right place shows respect; forgetting it can close doors.
