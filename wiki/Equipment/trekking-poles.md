# Trekking poles

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: computing · modern*

A pair of telescoping poles of light alloy with padded grips, wrist straps and small baskets near the tips. The walker plants them with each step, taking some of the load off the knees on steep descents and helping to push up hills. They steady the stride across scree and streams. They look strange to people who have never tried them.
