# Shawl

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: pottery · any*

A rectangle of wool, silk or fine cotton drawn around the shoulders, knotted at the breast or held by one hand. It is the small comfort of cool evenings, of draughty halls and early mornings at the market. Some are plain grey, some are heirlooms worked with flowers and fringes that took a winter to make.
