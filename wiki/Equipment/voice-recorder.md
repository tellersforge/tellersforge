# Voice recorder

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: computing · modern*

A slim pocket device that records hours of speech at the touch of a button, from across a table or through a thin wall. Journalists put it on the table at interviews, investigators leave it running in rooms, and forgetful people talk into it all day. The recordings can be copied, sent and searched. People who notice it tend to say less, which is sometimes the point.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Investigation recording interviews
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Media Production](../Skills/media-production.md), [Shadowing & Surveillance](../Skills/shadowing-and-surveillance.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** pocket recorder

## Not to be confused with

*[Tape Recorder](tape-recorder.md)*, which is heavier and records on reels; the voice recorder is small and stores its recordings digitally.
