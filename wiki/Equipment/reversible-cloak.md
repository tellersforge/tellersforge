# Reversible cloak

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · spies and thieves*

A cloak with a lining in a different colour, sewn so that either side can be worn out. Brown one moment, blue the next: a thief ducks round a corner, turns the cloak in two movements, and walks back out as someone else. Spies and pickpockets swear by it. It works best in a crowd, where pursuers are looking for a colour rather than a face.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +1 Disguise changing colour mid-chase; +1 Shadowing & Surveillance changing appearance
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** two-faced cloak

## Not to be confused with

*[Hood](hood.md)*, a simple garment that hides the face, where the reversible cloak changes the whole look of the wearer.
