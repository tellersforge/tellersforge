# Electronic translator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: computing · modern*

A small handheld box that listens to speech, pauses, and then speaks the words back in another language it knows. It lets travellers order meals, ask directions and bargain in markets with strangers. It is slow, flat and literal. Jokes, flattery and careful lies are lost in its dull voice, and the pause before each answer gives everyone time to stare.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Language (per language) basic exchanges in a known tongue
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA stilted, delayed speech; −1 Deception cannot carry subtle meaning
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** phrase translator
