# Electronics toolkit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: computing · modern*

A hard case that opens to reveal rows of tiny screwdrivers, probes, tweezers, spare parts and a small analyser. It holds nearly everything needed to take apart and repair a radio, a computer or a damaged robot. Technicians carry it on every job. Every piece has its place, and the missing piece is always the one needed.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Electronics complex repairs; +1 Robotics repairing robots
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Electronics](../Skills/electronics.md), [Robotics](../Skills/robotics.md)
- **Weight:** 4 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 15 S

## Not to be confused with

the *[Soldering Kit](soldering-kit.md)*, which joins wires but lacks the tools for full repairs.
