# Focus crystal

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: far future · far future*

A clear, tuned crystal, often set in a band or held in the palm, that hums faintly at the edge of hearing. It steadies the mind, sharpens concentration and helps the user hold a thought or a discipline without wavering. Far-future mystics and psionic adepts carry one, and scientists argue about whether it works or simply convinces the user that it does.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Meditation & Concentration mental disciplines
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Meditation & Concentration](../Skills/meditation-and-concentration.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 50 S
- **Also known as:** psionic focus

## Not to be confused with

*[Singing Bowl](singing-bowl.md)*, a bronze bowl whose tone settles the mind by sound rather than by resonance.
