# Mechanic's tools

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A steel box of spanners, screwdrivers, pliers, hammers, feeler gauges and oily rags. A mechanic with these tools keeps engines, pumps and machines working, and can often get a broken one running again well enough to limp home. Mechanics are practical, blunt and indispensable. Their hands are never quite clean.
