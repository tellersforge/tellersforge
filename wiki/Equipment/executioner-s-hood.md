# Executioner's hood

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · any*

A close hood of black or red cloth pulled over the whole head, with only two holes for the eyes. It hides the face of the one who carries out the sentences of the law, so that they may go home and live as a neighbour. On the scaffold, it makes a person into an instrument of justice. In any gathering, it makes everyone draw away.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +2 Intimidation on the scaffold; +2 Disguise hiding your identity
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA in company
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** headsman's hood

*[Hood](hood.md)*, the plain hood of cloaks and travellers, which shades the face without hiding it completely.
