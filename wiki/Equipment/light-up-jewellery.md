# Light-up jewellery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: computing · modern*

Rings, earrings, collars and hair clips lit by tiny lamps, glowing dyes or flickering strips, made for dance floors and night markets. They pulse with the music and make the wearer a moving spark in a dark room. That is the whole point at a party, and the whole problem anywhere else: in a dark alley, the wearer is the brightest thing for streets around.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 Carousing at clubs and parties
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth in the dark
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** LED jewellery, glow jewellery
