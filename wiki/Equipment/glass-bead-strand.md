# Glass bead strand

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · any*

A string of beads of coloured glass or glazed clay: blue, red, yellow and striped. They are bright, cheap to make in bulk and eagerly wanted where glass is unknown, which makes them a handy currency along trade routes. A traveller with a few strands in the pack can buy food, favours and goodwill in many a market.
