# Spirit board

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: steam · any*

A wooden board printed with the alphabet, numbers and the words yes, no and goodbye, and a small heart-shaped pointer on felt feet. Sitters rest their fingers lightly on the pointer and ask questions of the dead, and it slides from letter to letter to spell out answers. It is a parlour game to some and a door to something darker to others.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Otherworldly Dealings contacting the dead
- **Penalty:** 0

*[Divination Cards](divination-cards.md)*, which reveal the future through symbols rather than spelling out messages from the dead.
