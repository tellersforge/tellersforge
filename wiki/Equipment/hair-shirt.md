# Hair shirt

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · ascetic orders*

A shirt of coarse goat or horse hair worn against the bare skin, hidden under ordinary clothes. The constant itching and scratching is the point: it is a discipline of body and will for those who seek holiness through suffering. The wearer learns to ignore pain. The skin, however, does not learn, and it itches and blisters and never heals.

## Game block

- **Slot:** torso
- **Bonus:** +1 Pain Tolerance through mortification practice
- **Penalty:** −1 END itching and sores
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** penance shirt
