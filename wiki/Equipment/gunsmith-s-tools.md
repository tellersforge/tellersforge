# Gunsmith's tools

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: gunpowder · any*

A wooden case of files, drifts, springs, screwdrivers, small vices and bottles of oil. A gunsmith uses them to strip, clean, repair and adjust firearms, fitting new springs and straightening bent parts. In frontier towns, the gunsmith is as important as the doctor and gets paid more promptly. A firearm neglected too long in the wrong hands will fail when it matters most.
