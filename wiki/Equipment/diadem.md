# Diadem

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · imperial courts*

A slim band or tiara of gold, silver or jewelled metal worn across the brow, the mark of a noble, a high priest or a royal consort. It speaks of rank without the weight of a crown, and courtiers read it and adjust their manners accordingly. In poorer streets, however, it marks the wearer as rich prey for thieves and kidnappers.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +1 Court Etiquette & Protocol at court; +1 CHA among nobles
- **Penalty:** −1 Streetwise marks the wearer as rich prey
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 40 G
- **Also known as:** circlet, coronet, tiara

## Not to be confused with

*[Crown](crown.md)*, the heavier headpiece that belongs to a ruler; the diadem marks lesser nobility and priesthood.
