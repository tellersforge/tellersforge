# Searchlight

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: electricity · modern*

A heavy mounted lamp with a great polished mirror, throwing a beam that reaches hundreds of metres. Prison towers, warships, coastguards and air defences sweep the dark with them. It finds anything moving on open ground at night, and it can send signals by flashing. It also shows everyone exactly where it is.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Searching at night over open ground
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth while lit
- **Used for:** [Signals](../Skills/signals.md)
- **Weight:** 15 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 40 S
- **Also known as:** spotlight

## Not to be confused with

*[Signal lamp](signal-lamp.md)*, which is smaller and made for flashing messages rather than sweeping for intruders.
