# Wooden xylophone

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: iron · forest tribes*

A row of hardwood bars, each shaved to its note, laid across a frame with a hollow gourd hanging beneath every bar to make it boom. The player strikes them with rubber- or fibre-tipped mallets, fast and complex, often with a second player on the same instrument. Forest tribes build them for festivals and courts. They are too large to travel lightly, and too loud to ignore.
