# Grooming kit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: agriculture · any*

A satchel of stiff and soft brushes, a curry comb, a mane comb and a hoof pick. Grooming keeps the coat clean and the skin healthy, and it lets the rider check for sores, stones and swellings every day. It also builds trust: a horse that is groomed daily by the same hands is calmer, steadier and easier to handle.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Animal Handling keeping mounts calm
- **Penalty:** 0
