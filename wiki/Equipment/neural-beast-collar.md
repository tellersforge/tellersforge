# Neural beast collar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: far future · far future*

A slim band with sensors pressed against the skull, reading an animal's fear, hunger and temper and sending commands straight into its brain. A handler wearing the matching headset feels what the animal feels and directs it almost as if it were an extra limb. Hunting beasts and guard animals obey it with eerie precision. People who love animals tend to find it disturbing, and they say so to anyone wearing one.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +3 Animal Handling directing an animal
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA among animal lovers
- **Used for:** [Animal Handling](../Skills/animal-handling.md), [Exotic Beasts](../Skills/exotic-beasts.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 30 S
- **Also known as:** mind collar

## Not to be confused with

*[Remote Beast Collar](remote-beast-collar.md)*, which only tracks the animal and sends a tone or shock; the neural collar gives it orders from inside its own head.
