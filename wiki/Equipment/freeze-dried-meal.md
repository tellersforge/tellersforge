# Freeze-dried meal

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: computing · modern*

A light foil bag of cooked food with all the water taken out. Pour in boiling water, fold the top and wait, and it becomes a hot, decent meal. Hikers and mountaineers carry a week of them in the space of a few days' fresh food.
