# Dowsing rod

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: agriculture · any*

A forked hazel or willow twig held loosely in both hands, or a weighted pendulum on a string, carried slowly over the ground. When the dowser passes over water, buried metal or a lost thing, the rod twitches down or the pendulum swings, or so they say. Well-diggers and prospectors hire dowsers, and some swear by them. Others note the dowser is paid either way.
