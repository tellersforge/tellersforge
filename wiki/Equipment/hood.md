# Hood

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · western kingdoms*

A loose hood of wool or linen sewn to a short shoulder cape, worn apart from any cloak. The point at the back often runs out into a long tail that can be wound round the neck or head. Pulled up, it keeps rain off the neck and throws the face into shadow, which suits travellers, pilgrims and anyone who would rather not be recognised. It also narrows the world to a tunnel and muffles the footstep behind.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +1 END against rain; +1 Disguise hood pulled forward
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception hood up
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** chaperon, cowl

## Not to be confused with

*[Woollen cloak](woollen-cloak.md)*, which covers the whole body; the hood covers only head and shoulders and can be worn under a cloak or without one.
