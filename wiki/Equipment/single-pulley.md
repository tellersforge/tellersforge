# Single pulley

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: bronze · any*

A single grooved wheel turning in a wooden or bronze block, with a hook to hang it by. A rope run over it lets a worker pull downward to raise a load upward, which is easier and safer than hauling straight up. Wells, cranes, ship masts and market hoists have used it since the age of bronze. It moves the load no more easily, only more comfortably.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Lifting & Carrying hoisting with a fixed anchor
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Cargo Handling](../Skills/cargo-handling.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** sheave block

## Not to be confused with

*[Block and Tackle](block-and-tackle.md)*, which uses several wheels to multiply the pull.
