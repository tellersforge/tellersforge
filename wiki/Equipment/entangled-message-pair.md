# Entangled message pair

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: far future · far future*

Two matched devices, each small enough to carry, linked so that whatever is written into one appears instantly in the other at any distance and nowhere else. Nothing travels between them that can be caught, jammed or overheard. Spymasters, admirals and rulers keep them close. If one is destroyed, its twin falls silent forever, and no substitute can ever be made.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +3 Cryptography messages cannot be intercepted
- **Penalty:** 0

- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 200 G

## Not to be confused with

*[Laser Communicator](laser-communicator.md)*, which needs a clear line of sight; the entangled pair works across any distance and cannot be intercepted.
