# Key impression kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: iron · any*

A flat tin of soft wax or clay, a few key blanks, and a set of fine files. Someone gets hold of the key for a moment, a servant's apron, a gaoler's belt, a drunk's pocket, and presses it into the wax. Later, at leisure, the copy is filed to match the print. The owner still has the key and never knows a second one exists.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Locksmithing copying a key
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Locksmithing](../Skills/locksmithing.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** wax key kit

## Not to be confused with

*[Skeleton Key](skeleton-key.md)*, a single key filed to fool many simple locks, rather than a faithful copy of one particular key.
