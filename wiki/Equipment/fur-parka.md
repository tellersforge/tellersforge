# Fur parka

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

A hooded coat of caribou or seal fur, often two coats in one: an inner layer with the hair against the skin and an outer layer with the hair facing the wind. The hood is ringed with long fur that breaks the gale before it reaches the face. Inside it, a hunter can wait at a breathing hole through a polar night. Outside the cold, it becomes an oven, and thick fur sleeves make any delicate work with the fingers clumsy.
