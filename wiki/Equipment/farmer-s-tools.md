# Farmer's tools

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: agriculture · any*

A hoe, a sickle, a digging stick and a few sacks of seed, perhaps with a small hand plough. They are the tools of the oldest trade that feeds all the rest. With them, a family clears ground, plants it, weeds it and brings in the harvest. They are plain and worn, and in hard times the sickle and the hoe have made their way into more than one peasant army.
