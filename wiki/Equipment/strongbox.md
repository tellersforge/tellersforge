# Strongbox

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: iron · any*

A small chest of thick oak bound in iron straps, with a heavy lock and sometimes a second hasp for a padlock. Tax collectors, merchants and paymasters keep coin, deeds and seals in one, and often chain it to the floor or the wagon. It is small enough to carry under an arm, which is the whole of its virtue and most of its weakness. A thief who cannot open it can usually walk away with it.
