# Nano-cosmetic spray

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: far future · far future*

A fine mist of tiny machines that settle on the skin and lay down any look the wearer chooses: flawless make-up, a new skin tone, a scar, freckles, an older face. The machines lift away again on a word. It does in a moment what a stage artist does in an hour, and it is equally at home in a courtier's dressing room or a spy's pocket.

- **Bonus:** +2 Disguise altering skin and face; +1 Grooming & Style instant styling
- **Penalty:** 0
