# Masquerade mask

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · festival cities*

A full or three-quarter mask of painted leather, papier-mache or silk, gilded, feathered and trimmed with ribbons. In the festival cities, during carnival season, half the town goes masked, and the mask lets a merchant dance with a duchess and a husband flirt with a stranger. At the ball it hides the wearer and invites mischief. Worn anywhere else it is a plain confession of bad intent, and the narrow eyeholes hide the edges of the world.

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +2 Disguise at a masquerade; +1 Charm & Seduction at a masked ball
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA outside a festival (suspicious); −1 Perception narrow eyeholes
- **Used for:** —
