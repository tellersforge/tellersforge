# Stoker's gear

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A coverall of thick wool faced with leather, with heavy gauntlets and a flap that hangs over the neck, worn by the men who feed coal to boilers and furnaces. In the roaring heat of an engine room or a foundry pit it keeps the skin from blistering and the lungs from searing. It is stained black and smells of smoke and sweat, and anywhere outside the engine room its wearer is plainly out of place.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 END against furnace heat
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA outside the engine room
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 4 S
- **Also known as:** boiler suit, fireman's rig

## Not to be confused with

*[Fire proximity suit](fire-proximity-suit.md)*, a later, sealed and reflective suit for walking close to open flame; stoker's gear only lets a worker bear a furnace's heat.
