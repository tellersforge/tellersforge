# Antiseptic flask

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A stoppered flask of carbolic solution or strong clear spirit, poured over wounds, hands and instruments before and after surgery. It stings like fire and smells like a hospital corridor. Once surgeons learned that dirt kills more patients than the knife, the flask became as important as the scalpel. Old soldiers sometimes drink from it, which is a waste of good medicine and bad for the stomach.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Surgery against infection afterward; +1 Field Medicine against wound infection
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Field Medicine](../Skills/field-medicine.md)
- **Weight:** 0.4 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** carbolic flask, wound spirit

## Not to be confused with

*[Honey Salve Jar](honey-salve-jar.md)*, which keeps a cut clean as it heals; the antiseptic flask sterilises wounds, hands and tools on the spot.
