# Arming doublet

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · western kingdoms*

A close-fitted padded doublet worn beneath a suit of plate. Laces called points hang from the shoulders, elbows and hips, and the armour is tied to them piece by piece so it hangs right and moves with the body. A knight who wears one can bear his armour from dawn to dusk without the steel rubbing him raw. In the summer sun, though, it turns the whole harness into an oven.

## Game block

- **Slot:** torso
- **Bonus:** +1 END wearing armour for long hours
- **Penalty:** −1 END in heat
- **Used for:** —

## Not to be confused with

*[Doublet](doublet.md)*, the ordinary fashionable garment; the arming doublet is thicker, plainer, and made to carry armour rather than to impress.
