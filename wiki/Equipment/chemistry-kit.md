# Chemistry kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A wooden case of glass tubes, flasks, a spirit burner, bottles of reagents and a small balance. With it a chemist tests water, identifies poisons, analyses ore and mixes useful compounds. It is the practical heir to the alchemist's chest, and its users are fond of saying so. Spilled reagents burn holes in cloth and fingers alike.
