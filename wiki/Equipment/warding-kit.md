# Warding kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: iron · any*

A satchel of coarse salt, white chalk, iron nails, dried herbs and a small hammer, all the supplies needed to seal a room against spirits. Salt lines the doorway and window, chalk draws the signs, and iron nails go into the frame. It is old knowledge, handed down in families, and anyone who spends a night in a haunted house wishes they had brought one.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Exorcism & Curse-Breaking warding a room
- **Penalty:** 0

- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** salt and chalk
