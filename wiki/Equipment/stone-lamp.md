# Stone lamp

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

A shallow bowl of carved soapstone filled with seal or whale fat, with a wick of moss along one edge. Arctic peoples cook, dry clothes and warm their snow houses over it, and its low, steady flame keeps a family alive through the long dark. Tending it well is a skill passed down carefully.

- **Bonus:** +1 END warming a snow house
- **Penalty:** 0
