# Felt cap

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · any*

A soft, brimless cap of felted wool, shaped to the head and sometimes folded forward at the crown. Sailors, labourers, shepherds and craftsmen wear it everywhere, because it is cheap, warm and hard to lose. In some lands a freed slave puts one on as the first sign of liberty, and rebels have waved it on a pole. It keeps the ears warm and asks for nothing.
