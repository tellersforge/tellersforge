# Poultice kit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: fire · any*

A roll of coarse cloths, a small clay pot and a packet of dried mash of herbs, bran or clay. The healer wets the mash, heats it in the pot and binds it hot over a sprain, a boil or an angry wound. The heat draws out swelling and the herbs fight the rot. It is messy, smells of a wet barn and is loved by grandmothers everywhere, often with good reason.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 First Aid against swelling and infection
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [First Aid](../Skills/first-aid.md), [Remedies & Pharmacy](../Skills/remedies-and-pharmacy.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** fire · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** plaster cloths

## Not to be confused with

*[Honey Salve Jar](honey-salve-jar.md)*, which seals a clean cut; the poultice is a hot pack for swelling and infection already setting in.
