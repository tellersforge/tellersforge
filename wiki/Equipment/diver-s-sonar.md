# Diver's sonar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: computing · modern*

A chunky handheld unit with a grip and a small glowing screen, swung slowly in front of a diver like a torch in fog. In silted harbours and flooded caves where a hand in front of the mask cannot be seen, it draws the outline of walls, wreckage and lost objects. Salvagers and rescue divers rely on it. Its quiet pings can still be heard by anyone underwater who knows the sound.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Searching underwater in murky water
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth its pings can be heard
- **Used for:** [Diving](../Skills/diving.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** handheld sonar
