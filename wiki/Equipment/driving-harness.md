# Driving harness

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: wheel and roads · any*

A tangle of leather straps, collars, traces and reins that joins horses, mules or oxen to a cart or wagon. A good harness spreads the load across the animal's chest or shoulders so it can pull all day without injury. Getting a team harnessed correctly takes practice, and a wrongly fitted strap can cause sores that lame an animal for weeks.
