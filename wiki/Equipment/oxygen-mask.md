# Oxygen mask

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: electricity · modern*

A soft mask over nose and mouth, fed from a small bottle of oxygen by a thin tube. High on mountains and in open-cockpit aircraft, the air grows thin enough to steal thought and strength, and a few sips of pure oxygen bring both back. Climbers ration their bottles like gold on the last push to the summit. Talking through it is muffled and awkward, so most learn to use hand signs instead.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +2 END at high altitude; +1 Mountaineering above the death zone
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA speaking through it
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 5 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** oxygen bottle

## Not to be confused with

the *[Breather Mask](breather-mask.md)*, which filters and enriches strange alien air; the oxygen mask simply feeds bottled oxygen where the air is too thin.
