# Typewriter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A compact machine of keys, levers and a rolling carriage that strikes inked letters onto paper, with carbon sheets between the pages for extra copies. It produces clean, even text that anyone can read. Clerks, reporters and novelists work on them, and offices fill with their clatter. There is no way to type quietly: the keys clack, the bell rings and the carriage thumps back with every line.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Administration clean copies
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth while typing
- **Used for:** [Administration](../Skills/administration.md), [Literacy (per script)](../Skills/literacy-per-script.md)
- **Weight:** 5 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 30 S
- **Also known as:** portable typewriter

## Not to be confused with

*[Hand Press](hand-press.md)*, which prints many copies at once; the typewriter writes one page, with perhaps a few carbon copies.
