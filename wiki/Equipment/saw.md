# Saw

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: bronze · any*

A thin toothed blade on a wooden handle or in a frame, pulled or pushed across wood to cut it. It is slower than an axe but far neater, and carpenters prefer it for anything that needs to fit. A saw needs sharpening and setting, and a blade that binds in the cut can take the skin off the user's knuckles.
