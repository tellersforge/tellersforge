# Linen tunic

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: agriculture · any*

A simple knee-length tunic of linen or cotton, sleeveless or short-sleeved, the everyday garment of hot countries. It lets air pass, dries quickly and keeps the sun off the shoulders. Rich and poor wear the same cut; the difference is in the fineness of the thread and whether it has been bleached white.
