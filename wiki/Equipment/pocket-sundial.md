# Pocket sundial

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: printing · western kingdoms*

A small folding dial of ivory, wood or brass, or a metal ring with a pinhole, carried on a cord round the neck or in a purse. Opened and turned to north by the little compass set in its base, it gives the hour wherever the sun shines. Merchants and scholars carried them before watches were common. They are pretty things, and often the first thing a pickpocket takes.
