# Broad straw hat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: agriculture · farming villages*

A wide hat of woven straw, rice stalk or palm leaf, broad-brimmed or shaped like a shallow cone, the hat of farmers who spend all day bent in the fields. It shades the face and neck from the sun and sheds rain like a small roof. The broad brim also hides whatever is going on overhead.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +1 END against sun and rain
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception brim blocks the view above
