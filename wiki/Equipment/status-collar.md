# Status collar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: far future · far future*

A slim collar of polished material that glows softly and broadcasts the wearer's house and exact rank to anyone equipped to read it. At a far-future court it smooths every greeting, because everyone knows precisely whom to bow to. It also makes lying about one's standing nearly impossible, and in the halls of a rival house it is a badge of the enemy.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +2 Court Etiquette & Protocol at a far-future court
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA among those of a rival house; −1 Deception reveals exact rank
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 20 S
- **Also known as:** rank ring, caste collar

## Not to be confused with

*[Rank insignia](rank-insignia.md)*, which show military or official rank on a uniform; a status collar announces house and court standing.
