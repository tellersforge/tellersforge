# Divination sticks

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: writing · eastern empire*

A tall cup of numbered bamboo sticks, or a bundle of dried stalks, shaken or sorted until one falls out or a pattern forms. The number leads to a verse in an old book of oracles, and the verse is read for its meaning. Temples in eastern empires keep them by the door, and the faithful shake them before weddings, voyages and exams.
