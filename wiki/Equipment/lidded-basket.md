# Lidded basket

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: agriculture · any*

A basket woven tight and fitted with a lid that sits snug, keeping food, cloth or small goods clean and out of reach of mice and flies. Households store bread and linen in them, and travellers carry lunch or a snake charmer's snake. Good ones last a generation.
