# Ox yoke

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: agriculture · any*

A heavy wooden beam shaped to rest on the necks of two oxen, with wooden bows or straps to hold them in place. A pole or chain from its centre pulls a plough, cart or log. It is one of the oldest farming tools and still used where oxen do the work. A yoked pair must learn to walk in step, and a mismatched pair is miserable for everyone.
