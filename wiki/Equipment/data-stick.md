# Data stick

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: computing · modern*

A small stick of plastic or metal, not much bigger than a thumb, that holds as much writing as a great library. It plugs into computers and carries files, pictures and secret records from one place to another. People lose them, find them and forget what is on them. Spies smuggle them in buttons and heels, because so much can hide in so little.
