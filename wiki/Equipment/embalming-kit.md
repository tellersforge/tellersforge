# Embalming kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: bronze · river kingdoms*

A chest of bronze hooks and knives, jars of natron salt and aromatic resin, bundles of linen bandages, and little pots of oils and spices. The embalmer drains, dries and wraps the dead so they will last for the long journey after life. It is slow, meticulous work in the river kingdoms, done with prayer at every step, and it smells powerfully of cedar and myrrh.
