# Thermal underwear

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · modern*

Close-fitting long-sleeved vests and long johns of fine wool or synthetic knit, worn under ordinary clothes. They trap a thin layer of warm air against the skin and wick away sweat, turning a cold day into a bearable one. Soldiers, skiers and outdoor workers wear them through the winter. When the weather warms or the wearer comes indoors, they swelter quietly underneath.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 END against cold
- **Penalty:** −1 END in heat
- **Used for:** —

- **Also known as:** long johns, base layer

## Not to be confused with

*[Fleece layer](fleece-layer.md)*, which is worn as a middle layer over other clothes; thermal underwear goes directly against the skin.
