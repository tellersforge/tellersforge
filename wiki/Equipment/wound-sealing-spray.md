# Wound-sealing spray

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A small aerosol can that sprays a fine sterile mist over a cut or burn. In seconds the mist sets into a clear flexible film that closes the wound, stops the bleeding and keeps out dirt and air. It flexes with the skin and peels away once the wound has healed. It is one of the first things anyone packs for a long voyage, and one of the first things they run out of.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 First Aid closing cuts and burns
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [First Aid](../Skills/first-aid.md), [Field Medicine](../Skills/field-medicine.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** spray skin, liquid bandage

## Not to be confused with

*[Regeneration Gel](regeneration-gel.md)*, which regrows lost flesh over hours; the spray only seals the wound shut.
