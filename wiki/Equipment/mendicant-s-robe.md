# Mendicant's robe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · wandering monks*

A robe sewn from patches of cast-off cloth and dyed a single colour with plant dyes, the garment of wandering monks who own nothing and live on what they are given. The devout see it and fill the begging bowl. It is thin and patched, and gives little comfort against a mountain wind.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Persuasion asking alms; +1 CHA before the devout
- **Penalty:** −1 END against cold
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** saffron robe, alms robe

## Not to be confused with

*[Monk's Habit](monk-s-habit.md)*, the robe of the monastery, heavier and warmer, worn by those who stay behind the walls.
