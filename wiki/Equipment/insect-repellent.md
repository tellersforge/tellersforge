# Insect repellent

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: electricity · modern*

A small bottle of pungent oil, a jar of salve or a pressurised spray, rubbed onto exposed skin and clothes. It smells strongly of citronella, herbs or chemicals, and for a few hours the mosquitoes, midges and ticks keep their distance. Travellers in hot, wet lands reapply it constantly. It stings in cuts and eyes, and it keeps off some of the fevers as well as the itching.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 END against insect-borne fever
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** bug spray, citronella oil

## Not to be confused with

*[Mosquito Net](mosquito-net.md)*, which protects a sleeper through the night; repellent protects the skin while awake and moving.
