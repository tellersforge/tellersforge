# Fire drill

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: stone tools · any*

A straight wooden spindle spun hard between the palms, or with a bow, against a notch in a flat hearth board, until the friction dust smoulders into an ember. It takes skill, dry wood and sore hands. Every people who has lived without steel has known some version of it, and those who master it can make fire almost anywhere.
