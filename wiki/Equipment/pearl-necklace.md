# Pearl necklace

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · any*

One or two strands of matched pearls, each carefully graded by size and lustre, fastened with a small clasp of gold. Pearls are costly to gather and hard to match, and a good necklace is the pride of a wealthy family. In high society it says the wearer belongs. On the street it says the wearer is worth robbing, and thieves take note.
