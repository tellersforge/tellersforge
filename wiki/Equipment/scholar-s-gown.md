# Scholar's gown

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · scholars*

A dark gown of black or deep-coloured wool, with wide, open sleeves and often a hood in the colour of the wearer's faculty. It marks a graduate of an academy, a lawyer or a judge. In a court of law or a learned debate, it lends its wearer the weight of the institutions that granted it. It is long and awkward to run in.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Oratory & Debate in academies and courts of law; +1 Law in court
- **Penalty:** −1 Running long gown
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 12 S
- **Also known as:** academic robe, judge's robe

## Not to be confused with

*[Merchant's Gown](merchant-s-gown.md)*, a fur-trimmed gown that shows wealth rather than learning.
