# War saddle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · western kingdoms*

A heavy wood-and-leather saddle with a tall arched front and a high back that wraps around the rider's hips. It locks a knight into place so that the shock of a lance strike goes into the horse instead of knocking the rider out of the seat. It is uncomfortable for long rides and awkward to climb into, especially in armour. Squires spend a great deal of time helping their masters mount.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Mounted Combat lance charges; +1 Riding staying seated
- **Penalty:** −1 Riding mounting quickly
- **Used for:** [Riding](../Skills/riding.md), [Mounted Combat](../Skills/mounted-combat.md)
- **Weight:** 12 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 40 S
- **Also known as:** knight's saddle

## Not to be confused with

the *[Stock Saddle](stock-saddle.md)*, which also holds the rider deep but is made for herding cattle rather than charging.
