# Rush light

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: agriculture · western kingdoms*

A peeled rush dipped in kitchen fat and clipped into a holder, burning with a small, flickering light for a short while. It is the poor household's candle, made at home for nothing. It is enough to eat supper or mend a shirt by, and not much more.
