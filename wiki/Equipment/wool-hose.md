# Wool hose

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · western kingdoms*

Leg coverings cut from woven wool on the bias so they stretch, fitted close to the leg, and tied to a belt or to the hem of the tunic with laces. Early pairs are separate stockings; later they are joined into a single garment. They keep the legs warm under a short tunic and show off a good calf, which some wearers care about a great deal.

## Game block

- **Slot:** legs
