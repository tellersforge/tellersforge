# Ritual candles

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: bronze · any*

A bundle of tall beeswax or tallow candles, often coloured, carved with signs or anointed with oil and blessed for rites. Their light marks the edge of a sacred circle, a vigil for the dead or the hours of a night ritual. They burn slowly and steadily, and a flame that gutters suddenly in still air is always taken as a sign of something.
