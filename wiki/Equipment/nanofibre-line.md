# Nanofibre line

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: far future · far future*

A spool of feather-light line that responds to a spoken word or a tap on its reel. At command it stiffens into a rod, ties itself into a named knot round an anchor, or lets go and slides free for the owner to reel in. Climbers use it to set anchors out of reach and haul gear in the same breath. It is easy to become dependent on it, and it can be told to unknot by the wrong person.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Climbing anchoring and hauling; +1 Knots & Rope Work self-tying on command
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md), [Knots & Rope Work](../Skills/knots-and-rope-work.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** smart rope

## Not to be confused with

*[Monofilament Rope](monofilament-rope.md)*, which is stronger but inert; nanofibre line obeys commands.
