# Powdered wig

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: printing · western kingdoms*

A curled wig of human hair, horse hair or goat hair, dusted white with scented powder and tied at the back with a ribbon. It is the badge of courtiers, judges, barristers and gentlemen. At a court or in a court of law, it shows the wearer knows the forms and deserves a hearing. Among commoners and rebels it is a symbol of the powerful, and in summer it is hot and itchy.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +1 Etiquette at a western court or law court; +1 Law in court
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA among commoners or rebels; −1 END in heat
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.4 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** periwig, peruke

## Not to be confused with

*[Disguise wig](disguise-wig.md)*, which is made to change the wearer's looks; the powdered wig is a formal mark of station.
