# Pitons

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: iron · mountain folk*

A bundle of iron spikes, each with an eye at the head, hammered into cracks in rock so a rope can be clipped through. Every ring of the hammer tells the climber how well the spike is biting. On a long route, pitons turn an impossible face into a slow ladder. The ringing carries far across a quiet valley, and leaves scars behind in the rock.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing on cracked rock
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth while hammering
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md), [Mountaineering](../Skills/mountaineering.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** rock pegs

## Not to be confused with

*[Climbing Cams](climbing-cams.md)*, which grip a crack silently and come out cleanly.
