# Printed map

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: printing · any*

A folded sheet of paper showing a region from above: roads in red or black, rivers in blue, towns as dots and the capital as a star. Printing made maps cheap enough for merchants, pilgrims and soldiers, and suddenly anyone could find the next town without asking. A printed map is only as honest as its surveyor and only as new as its date. Roads move, bridges fall, and the ferry marked so clearly may have sunk years ago.
