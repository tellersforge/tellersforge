# Whistle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: stone tools · any*

A small whistle of carved bone, reed or metal, hung on a cord round the neck. A sharp blast cuts through wind and noise and carries a surprising distance. Watchmen blow it to call for help, referees to stop a game, guards to raise an alarm and lost walkers to be found. It needs no skill to use, which is exactly why it is so useful.
