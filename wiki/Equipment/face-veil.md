# Face veil

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · desert peoples*

A long cloth wound around the head and drawn across the nose and mouth, leaving only the eyes. In the desert it keeps out sand, dust and sun, and the air behind it stays a little damper. It also hides the face, which is useful on some errands. In lands where honest people show their faces, the veiled stranger is mistrusted.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +1 END against sand and sun; +1 Disguise hiding the face
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA where faces are shown to earn trust
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** litham, face wrap

## Not to be confused with

*[Full-Body Veil](full-body-veil.md)*, a great cloth covering the wearer from head to foot, not the face alone.
