# Safecracker's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A heavy leather bag that clanks: hand drills and hardened bits, a doctor's listening tube for hearing the wheels inside a lock fall, dial wrenches, steel wedges, chalk for marking numbers and a small jar of grease. Patient crackers listen to the dial one click at a time; impatient ones drill. The kit is built for combination safes, and owning one outside the trade is a confession in itself.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Lockpicking combination safes
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Lockpicking](../Skills/lockpicking.md)
- **Weight:** 6 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** cracksman's bag

## Not to be confused with

*[Lockpicks](lockpicks.md)*, a pocket roll for key locks that is no help against a combination dial.
