# Electric tattoo machine

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: electricity · modern*

A small powered machine that drives a needle up and down many times a second, with a range of inks, disposable needles and sterile supplies. It lets an artist work quickly and precisely, producing fine lines, shading and bright colour that would take days by hand. Tattoo parlours and travelling artists alike rely on it, and it hums like an angry insect.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Tattooing & Body Art speed and fine detail
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Tattooing & Body Art](../Skills/tattooing-and-body-art.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** tattoo gun

## Not to be confused with

*[Tattoo kit](tattoo-kit.md)*, the older set of hand needles and ink; the electric tattoo machine is faster and finer.
