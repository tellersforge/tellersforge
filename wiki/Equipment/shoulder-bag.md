# Shoulder bag

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: agriculture · any*

A flat bag on a single strap worn across the body, sitting at the hip where a hand can reach it without stopping. Travellers keep in it the things they need along the way: a knife, bread, a purse, a letter. It is the everyday bag of every age, from herders to scholars.
