# Transfusion kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A case of rubber tubes, needles, sealed bags and cards for testing whose blood can safely go into whom. With it, blood flows from a healthy donor into a patient who has lost too much. It turned wounds that were once certain death into wounds that could be survived. It needs a donor, a steady hand and the right match; the wrong blood can kill as surely as none.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Surgery after heavy blood loss
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Medicine](../Skills/medicine.md), [Surgery](../Skills/surgery.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** blood-giving set

## Not to be confused with

*[Medical Injector](medical-injector.md)*, which is a quick dose of drugs; the transfusion kit replaces the blood itself.
