# Toga

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · city states*

A great semicircle of white wool, several paces across, wound around the body and thrown over the left shoulder in heavy, carefully arranged folds. Only a citizen may wear it, and in the assembly or the law court, nothing else will do. It is magnificent and impractical: one arm is always busy holding the folds in place, and if the wearer runs, the whole thing slides to the ground.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Oratory & Debate in the assembly; +1 Etiquette among citizens
- **Penalty:** −1 DEX one arm holds the folds; −1 Running folds fall loose
