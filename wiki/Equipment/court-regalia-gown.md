# Court regalia gown

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: far future · far future*

A towering garment of drifting, glowing fabric with ornaments that hover about the wearer's shoulders and head, each proclaiming a precise rank in the court. It is worn at great ceremonies before an emperor or council, and anyone watching knows exactly who outranks whom. Lesser courtiers bow low. It is useless for anything but standing and walking in state, and among rebels or the poor it provokes open hatred.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +3 Etiquette at a far-future court; +2 Intimidation over lesser courtiers
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA among rebels or the poor; −2 Running while worn; −2 Stealth while worn
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 30 S
- **Also known as:** status gown, luminous court dress

## Not to be confused with

*[Field-draped robe](field-draped-robe.md)*, a lighter court garment held up by force fields; the regalia gown is heavier, grander and announces exact rank.
