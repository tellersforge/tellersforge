# Wetsuit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: computing · modern*

A snug suit of foam rubber, zipped up the back or chest, that lets a thin layer of water seep in and holds it against the skin, where the body warms it. Divers, surfers and rescue swimmers wear one to stay in cold water far longer and swim with more ease. Out of the water on a sunny day it becomes a sweating trap, and peeling it off is a struggle.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 END in cold water; +1 Swimming in cold water
- **Penalty:** −2 END in heat out of the water
- **Used for:** [Diving](../Skills/diving.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** dive skin

## Not to be confused with

*[Swimsuit](swimsuit.md)*, the ordinary bathing garment with no warmth; and *[Diver's dress](diver-s-dress.md)*, a sealed suit with a helmet for walking the seabed.
