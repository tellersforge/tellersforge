# Snow goggles

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

A band of bone, antler or driftwood carved to fit the face, with two narrow slits to look through, tied on with sinew. On bright snow and ice the sun comes up from the ground as well as down from the sky, and it can burn the eyes blind in a single day. The slits cut that glare to a thin, bearable line. They also narrow the world, and a hunter wearing them must turn his whole head to see what is beside him.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +2 END against snow glare
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception to the sides
- **Used for:** —

*[Sunglasses](sunglasses.md)*, which cut glare with tinted glass rather than slits, and hide the eyes rather than simply shading them.
