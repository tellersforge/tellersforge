# Sackcloth

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: agriculture · temple faiths*

A rough garment of goat hair or coarse sacking, shapeless and scratchy, worn to show grief, penitence or humility before the gods. Often ashes are added. It moves hearts: the priest sees true penance and the offended see a humbled enemy. It will never be welcome at court.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Ritual & Liturgy rites of penance; +1 Persuasion begging forgiveness
- **Penalty:** −2 Etiquette at court
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** penitent's robe

*[Hair Shirt](hair-shirt.md)*, worn secretly under clothes as a private discipline, where sackcloth is a public display of grief.
