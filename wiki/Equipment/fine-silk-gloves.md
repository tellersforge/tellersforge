# Fine silk gloves

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · western courts*

Thin, pale gloves of silk or soft kid leather, fitted close to every finger, worn at court, in church and anywhere the hands should be seen but not touched. Removing one is a gesture; offering one is a promise. They are useless against cold and make the fingers slippery for anything delicate or dishonest.
