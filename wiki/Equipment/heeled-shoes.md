# Heeled shoes

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · western courts*

Fashionable shoes raised on a block of wood or stacked leather, fastened with a silver buckle or a bright ribbon. Gentlemen and ladies alike wear them to stand a little taller at court and to show a well-turned leg in the dance. They look splendid in a ballroom. On wet cobbles they wobble, and anyone who tries to run in them does so with great care and little speed.
