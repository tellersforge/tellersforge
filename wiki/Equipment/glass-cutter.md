# Glass cutter

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: steam · any*

A small tool with a hard steel wheel or diamond chip for a tip, paired with a suction cup on a handle. The burglar scores a circle on the pane, taps it, and lifts the disc of glass free before it can fall and shatter. A hand goes through the hole to the window latch. Done well, the only sound is a faint squeak, and the only trace is a neat round hole for the morning to find.
