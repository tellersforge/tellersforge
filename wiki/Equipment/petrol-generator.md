# Petrol generator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A heavy engine and dynamo on a wheeled frame, running on fuel and roaring as it turns. It powers lights, tools and heaters for a camp, a worksite or a small village after a disaster. It needs fuel, oil and the occasional kick. It also stinks, smokes and can be heard far away, so it is no friend to anyone trying to stay unnoticed.
