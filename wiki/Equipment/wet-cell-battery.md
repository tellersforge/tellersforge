# Wet-cell battery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A row of glass jars or a heavy box of lead plates standing in acid, joined by wires. It gives a steady trickle of current for telegraphs, lamps and early radios. It is heavy, awkward and dangerous; the acid eats cloth and skin, and tipping it over is a small disaster. Early electricians learned to move them with slow, careful hands.
