# Letter-opening kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: printing · any*

A slim case of the spy's and the censor's trade: thin ivory blades, a little kettle spout for steaming glue, tweezers, fine brushes and sticks of coloured wax. The seal is softened or lifted whole, the letter read or copied, and everything closed again so neatly that the recipient never knows a stranger has been inside. Postmasters of suspicious governments keep rooms full of such tools and quiet clerks to use them.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Tradecraft opening and resealing letters unseen
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Tradecraft](../Skills/tradecraft.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** flap-and-seal kit

## Not to be confused with

*[Seal and Sealing Wax](seal-and-sealing-wax.md)*, which close a letter honestly; this kit exists to open one and pretend it was never touched.
