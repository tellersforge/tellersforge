# Beaded collar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · river kingdoms*

A broad collar of many rows of faience, glass and precious stone beads strung together so that it spreads across the chest and shoulders like a jewelled bib. In the river kingdoms such collars are worn by nobles, priests and honoured servants at court. They shimmer with every movement, and they clink softly, which makes them a poor choice for anyone trying to go unheard.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +2 CHA at a river-kingdom court
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth clinks when moving
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.6 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** broad collar, wesekh

## Not to be confused with

*[Torc](torc.md)*, a solid metal neck ring; the beaded collar is a wide spread of strung beads over the shoulders.
