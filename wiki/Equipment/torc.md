# Torc

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · northern clans*

A heavy, open-ended ring of twisted gold, silver or bronze, worn close around the neck with the thick ends meeting at the throat. Among the northern clans it is the badge of a free warrior or a chief, and men have fought naked but for their torc. Clan warriors follow a man who wears one, and enemies see a fighter of rank to be feared. Enemies of the clans also see a prize.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 Leadership among clan warriors; +1 Intimidation in battle
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA among the clans' enemies
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.8 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 10 G
- **Also known as:** neck ring, twisted collar

*[Arm ring](arm-ring.md)*, a lord's gift worn on the upper arm; the torc marks free rank at the neck.
