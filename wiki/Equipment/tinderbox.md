# Tinderbox

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: iron · western kingdoms*

A small tin box holding flint, steel and a supply of dry char cloth, with a lid to keep it all together and damp out. It sits on every mantelpiece and in every soldier's pack in the western kingdoms. Keeping the tinder dry makes the difference on a wet morning.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Fire-Making in damp weather
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Fire-Making](../Skills/fire-making.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 8 C
- **Also known as:** tinder tin

## Not to be confused with

*[Flint and steel](flint-and-steel.md)*, the striking pair on their own, without the box and dry tinder.
