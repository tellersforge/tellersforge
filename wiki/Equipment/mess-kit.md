# Mess kit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: steam · modern*

A small pot, a lidded pan, a cup and a folding spoon or fork, all nested together into one tin bundle that rattles in the bottom of a pack. It is what soldiers, prospectors and wandering students eat from for months at a time. Every dent in it has a story. It cooks a meal for one over any fire, and a clean one is the first sign that a traveller has learned to look after themselves.
