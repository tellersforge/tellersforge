# Safe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A squat steel box with walls a hand thick, a hinged door and a polished combination dial, usually painted with gilt lettering to reassure the customers. Banks, shops and wealthy households bolt one into a back room and sleep better. Nobody walks away with it. Getting into one without the combination means patience, drills, a stethoscope ear, or enough explosive to wake the street and ruin whatever was inside.
