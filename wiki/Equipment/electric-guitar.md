# Electric guitar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: electricity · modern*

A solid wooden guitar body fitted with electric pickups, played through an amplifier and speaker. Turned up, it snarls, howls and roars, making a sound no wooden instrument ever could. It is the heart of a loud show, where the crowd does as much singing as the band. It needs power, cables and a speaker, and no one within a city block misses it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Carousing at a show
- **Penalty:** −3 Stealth while playing
