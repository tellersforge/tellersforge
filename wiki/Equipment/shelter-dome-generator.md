# Shelter dome generator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: far future · far future*

A small drum or disc that, planted in the ground and switched on, throws up a curved dome of faintly shimmering force over a camp. Rain streams off it, sand bounces from it, and the wind howls around without getting in. Inside it is quiet and still. It needs a power source and does not stop weapons, only weather, but after a week in a storm, travellers sleep under it like children.
