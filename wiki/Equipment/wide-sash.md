# Wide sash

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · eastern empire*

A long, wide band of silk or stiff cotton wound several times around the waist and tied behind in a knot whose shape depends on the wearer's age, rank and the occasion. It holds a robe closed, carries a fan, a pouch or a short blade, and at formal gatherings the wrong knot is a small disgrace.

- **Penalty:** 0
