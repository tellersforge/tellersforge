# Pavilion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: iron · imperial courts*

A great tent of painted and embroidered cloth, with walls, a peaked roof, pennants, curtains dividing the rooms and rugs underfoot. It takes a wagon and several servants to move and an hour to raise. It is the field palace of a commander or a noble, where maps are spread, captains summoned, envoys received and plans made. Enemies know exactly which tent to aim at, of course.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Leadership holding a war council in the field
- **Penalty:** 0

*[Canvas Tent](canvas-tent.md)*, the plain shelter of the rank and file; the pavilion is a mobile hall for command and display.
