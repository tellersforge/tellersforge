# Solar still

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: electricity · modern*

A clear or dark plastic sheet stretched over a pit, weighted with a stone in the middle above a cup. The sun warms the damp soil and any cut plants thrown in, the vapour condenses on the underside of the sheet and runs down into the cup. On a good day it yields a mouthful or two, enough to keep a stranded person alive but not comfortable. Digging the pit costs sweat, which is the joke every survival instructor makes.
