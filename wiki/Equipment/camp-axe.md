# Camp axe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: iron · any*

A short-handled axe with a small head and a flat poll, carried in a sheath on a pack or a saddle. It splits kindling, fells a small tree, trims poles for a shelter and drives tent pegs with the back of the head. Every experienced camper keeps it sharp and dry and never lends it. It can be a weapon, but it is a poor one.
