# Walk-through detector

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: computing · modern*

A doorway frame of metal and plastic that people walk through one by one. If anyone carrying metal passes through, a light flashes and an alarm sounds, and guards come with hand wands. Airports, courts, prisons and embassies use them to screen for weapons. Anyone hoping to smuggle a blade past one needs to think of something other than metal.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Security Systems screening people for weapons
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Security Systems](../Skills/security-systems.md)
- **Weight:** 80 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 40 S
- **Also known as:** metal detector arch
