# Trepanning kit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

A flint scraper, a small bow drill and slivers of bone for pins, wrapped in hide. With them a healer cuts a round hole in the skull to let out pressure, blood or evil spirits. It is one of the oldest operations known, and skulls with healed holes show that many patients lived. The kit does one thing, and does nothing well for any other kind of surgery.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** 0
- **Penalty:** −1 Surgery for anything but skull work
- **Used for:** [Surgery](../Skills/surgery.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 1 S 5 C
- **Also known as:** skull drill

## Not to be confused with

*[Surgeon's Instrument Roll](surgeon-s-instrument-roll.md)*, which holds tools for general surgery; the trepanning kit is only for opening the skull.
