# Stone bead necklace

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: agriculture · any*

Beads of amber, jade, carnelian, turquoise or lapis, each drilled painstakingly by hand and polished smooth, strung together on a cord. Early farming peoples traded such beads over long distances, and a good necklace shows wealth and good connections. Traders and farmers alike admire it, and it can be broken up and spent a bead at a time in hard years.
