# Electronic keypad lock

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: computing · modern*

A plastic or steel panel beside a door, with a keypad, a card slot or a glass pad that reads a fingertip. Behind it, a small bolt or magnet holds the door until the right code arrives. Offices, hotels and laboratories fit them by the hundred because codes can be changed in a moment and lost cards cancelled. Most quietly keep a record of every opening, which is a comfort to owners and a nuisance to anyone who was never there.
