# Trapper's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: stone tools · any*

A bag of snares, coils of thin wire, sharpened stakes, bait and a few heavy spring traps. Set along game trails and checked each morning, they catch rabbits, foxes and birds while the trapper sleeps. Trappers know animal habits the way a merchant knows prices. A careless trapper can catch the wrong thing, and more than one walker has stepped into a snare.
