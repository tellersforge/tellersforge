# Colony work suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A layered outfit of tough fabric, patched and repatched, with a filter hood that pulls over the mouth and nose. Settlers on raw frontier worlds wear it for clearing ground, herding strange stock and riding out storms of grit. It shields the wearer from dust, weather and thorny growth. On a wealthy central world, it marks the wearer as a rough frontier colonist.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 END against dust and harsh weather; +1 armour against scrapes and thorns
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA in core-world society
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** settler coverall, frontier suit

## Not to be confused with

*[Shipboard jumpsuit](shipboard-jumpsuit.md)*, the clean, close-fitting dress of starship crews; the colony work suit is built for open ground and bad weather.
