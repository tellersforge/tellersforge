# Tourniquet

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: agriculture · any*

A broad strap and a short stick. The strap goes round a limb above a spurting wound, the stick twists it tight until the bleeding stops, and the healer writes down the time. Soldiers have carried them since armies began. It saves the life and risks the limb, because a tourniquet left on too long kills what lies below it. Only a surgeon can safely take it off.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 First Aid stopping heavy bleeding from a limb
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [First Aid](../Skills/first-aid.md), [Field Medicine](../Skills/field-medicine.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** windlass strap

## Not to be confused with

*[Trauma Kit](trauma-kit.md)*, which bundles a modern quick tourniquet with dressings and seals; this is the strap and stick alone.
