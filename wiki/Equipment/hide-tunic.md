# Hide tunic

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · any*

A simple shirt of tanned hide, two pieces laced together at the sides and shoulders with thongs. It is the first proper garment of the hunting peoples, tougher than any cloth they can make, and it shrugs off brambles and grit that would shred bare skin. It keeps some warmth in. After a season it takes the shape of the body that wears it.
