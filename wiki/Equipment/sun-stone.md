# Sun stone

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: iron · northern seafarers*

A chip of clear, pale crystal that northern seafarers held up to a grey sky. Turned slowly, it brightens and darkens, and where it glows strongest lies the hidden sun, even through thick cloud or sea fog. With the sun found, a steersman can hold his course across open water where no coast is visible. It looks like nothing much, a pebble a child might pick up on the shore, and it is worth a ship.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Sea Navigation cloudy days
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Sea Navigation](../Skills/sea-navigation.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** sky crystal

## Not to be confused with

the *[Lodestone](lodestone.md)*, which points north by magnetism rather than finding the sun in the sky.
