# Climbing cams

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: computing · modern*

Spring-loaded devices with toothed, curved lobes on a trigger bar. Squeezed and pushed into a crack, they spread out and lock tighter the harder they are pulled. They go in silently, hold a falling climber and come out with a squeeze of the trigger, leaving no scar. They are costly, and each one fits only a narrow range of cracks.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing on cracked rock
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md), [Mountaineering](../Skills/mountaineering.md)
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** spring anchors, nuts and cams

## Not to be confused with

*[Pitons](pitons.md)*, which are cheap and fit any crack but must be hammered in, loudly.
