# Exotic mount saddle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: far future · far future*

A saddle of smart materials that shifts its frame and padding to fit whatever it is placed on: a six-legged grazer, a giant bird, or something with scales. Straps adjust themselves, pressure points are cushioned and the creature is spared the discomfort of a badly fitted harness. It helps rider and mount understand each other, which is half of riding any strange beast.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Exotic Beasts riding strange creatures
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Riding](../Skills/riding.md), [Exotic Beasts](../Skills/exotic-beasts.md)
- **Weight:** 10 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 40 S
- **Also known as:** beast saddle

## Not to be confused with

the *[Stock Saddle](stock-saddle.md)*, which fits a horse well and nothing else.
