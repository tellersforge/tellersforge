# Oilskin smock

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · fishing villages*

A long, loose smock of canvas or linen soaked again and again in linseed oil or tallow until it is stiff and yellow and sheds water like a duck. Fishers pull it on over their warm clothes before going to sea, and it keeps them dry through a day of rain and spray. If the wearer goes overboard, it fills and drags.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +2 END against rain and spray; +1 Fishing in foul weather
- **Penalty:** −1 Swimming if you go overboard
