# Thigh boots

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · western cavalry*

Tall boots of heavy, stiffened leather that climb past the knee and flare open at the top, where a rider's leg meets the saddle. They are made for sitting a horse, not for walking. In the saddle they grip the flanks and turn the careless blade that swings low at a trooper's thigh. On foot they clump and chafe, and a man who falls into a river in them learns quickly how much water a boot can hold.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 Riding in the saddle; +1 armour against leg cuts on horseback
- **Penalty:** −2 Swimming they fill with water; −1 Running heavy and stiff
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2.5 kg · **Needs:** gunpowder · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** jackboots, cavalry boots

## Not to be confused with

*[Riding boots](riding-boots.md)*, which are lower, softer and easier to walk in, but give the leg far less protection from a cut.
