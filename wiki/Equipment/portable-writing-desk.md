# Portable writing desk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: printing · any*

A hinged wooden box that opens into a sloped writing surface covered in leather or baize, with little drawers and wells inside for paper, ink, pens and sealing wax. Officers, diplomats and travelling writers carry one on campaigns and long journeys. It turns any table, bed or carriage seat into a proper place to write. A good one also has a hidden drawer, which everyone knows about.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Calligraphy & Scribing writing on the road
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Calligraphy & Scribing](../Skills/calligraphy-and-scribing.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** lap desk, writing box

## Not to be confused with

*[Ink and Quills](ink-and-quills.md)*, which are the writing tools alone; the desk gives a proper surface and storage to write on the move.
