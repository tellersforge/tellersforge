# Juggling balls

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: agriculture · any*

A set of leather or cloth balls filled with sand or grain, sometimes clubs or rings, painted in bright colours. A juggler keeps three, four, five of them turning in the air, adding a joke here and a near-miss there. It gathers a crowd on a market day in moments. It is also good training for quick hands, which is why some jugglers are thieves.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Carousing entertaining a crowd
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Acrobatics](../Skills/acrobatics.md), [Sleight of Hand](../Skills/sleight-of-hand.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** juggling clubs

## Not to be confused with

*[Puppet Set](puppet-set.md)*, another street performer's way of drawing a crowd, with figures and voices in place of flying balls.
