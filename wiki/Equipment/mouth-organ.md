# Mouth organ

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: steam · any*

A small metal-and-wood box of reeds, no bigger than a closed hand, played by blowing and drawing breath through a row of holes. It wails, it chugs, it imitates a train whistle, and it fits in a shirt pocket. Soldiers, drifters, cowhands and sailors carry one because it weighs nothing and turns a long night into something bearable.
