# Acupuncture needles

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: bronze · eastern empire*

A slim lacquered case of very fine needles in bronze, silver or steel, with a chart of the body's points folded inside the lid. The practitioner places them in the skin at chosen spots and twists them gently to ease pain, stiffness and illness. Patients feel a dull tingle rather than a stab. Its masters spend years learning the points, and argue with each other about them for the rest of their lives.
