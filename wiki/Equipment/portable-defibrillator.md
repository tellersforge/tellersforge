# Portable defibrillator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: computing · modern*

A small box with two sticky pads on cords. Once the pads are on a collapsed person's chest, the box reads the heart, tells the helper aloud what to do, and gives a shock if the heart has stopped beating properly. It hangs on walls in airports and stations, waiting. Almost anyone can use it, and every second of hesitation lessens the chance it will work.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Medicine restarting a stopped heart
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [First Aid](../Skills/first-aid.md), [Medicine](../Skills/medicine.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 30 S
- **Also known as:** heart-shock box

## Not to be confused with

*[Medical Scanner](medical-scanner.md)*, which only reads the body; the defibrillator acts, shocking a stopped heart back into rhythm.
