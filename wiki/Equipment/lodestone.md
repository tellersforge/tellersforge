# Lodestone

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: iron · eastern empire*

A lump of dark, heavy ore that pulls at iron and, left to hang on a thread or float on a wooden chip, turns itself to point along the north-south line. An eastern empire's diviners used it long before sailors did, carving it into spoons that spun on bronze plates. It works under a grey sky when sun and stars are hidden, but it wavers near iron, near certain rocks, and in any place where the ground itself seems to pull.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Wayfinding overcast skies
- **Penalty:** −1 Wayfinding near iron or strange ground
- **Used for:** [Wayfinding](../Skills/wayfinding.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** south-pointer, magnet stone

## Not to be confused with

the *[Floating Compass](floating-compass.md)*, a magnetised needle in water that points more steadily and is easier to read.
