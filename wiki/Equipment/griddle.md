# Griddle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: iron · any*

A flat round plate of iron, or a smooth slab of stone, set on stones or a trivet above a low fire. Flatbreads, pancakes and oatcakes are slapped onto it, blister for a moment and are flipped with the fingers or a wooden paddle. A seasoned griddle is black and slick, and good cooks never let it touch soap. It also fries eggs, warms yesterday's bread and, in a pinch, makes a passable shield against a spitting pot.
