# Crown

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · imperial courts*

A jewelled circlet or tall headpiece of gold, sometimes studded with gems and topped with crosses, spikes or leaves. Only a ruler wears one. Loyal subjects bow and obey without question, and commoners tremble. To rebels and enemy courts the crown is a target and an insult. Nobody can hide in a crowd with a crown on their head.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +2 Leadership among loyal subjects; +2 Intimidation over commoners
- **Penalty:** −3 CHA among rebels or enemy courts; −2 Stealth impossible to hide in a crowd
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 200 G
- **Also known as:** royal crown

## Not to be confused with

*[Diadem](diadem.md)*, a slimmer band worn by nobles and priests; the crown belongs to the ruler alone.
