# Brewer's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: pottery · any*

Crocks, strainers, a sack of grain or dried fruit, a jar of living yeast and sometimes a small copper still. Given time, clean water and patience, a brewer turns them into beer, wine or something much stronger. In many lands the brewer's work is safer to drink than the water, and a camp that can brew its own is a happy one, at least for a while.
