# Ink and quills

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: iron · western kingdoms*

A horn or small pot of black ink, a handful of goose quills or cut reeds, and a small, very sharp knife for trimming nibs. It is the scribe's everyday kit, kept on the desk or in a leather case for travel. A well-cut quill writes a clean line; a worn one spatters and blots. Scribes wear out quills quickly, and the ink stains fingers for days.
