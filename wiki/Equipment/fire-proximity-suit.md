# Fire proximity suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: computing · modern*

A bulky suit coated in shining foil, with a hood and a gold-tinted visor that reflects heat like a mirror. It lets its wearer walk up to a roaring furnace, a burning fuel spill or a crashed aircraft and work close to flames that would blister bare skin at twenty paces. Airport crews and foundry workers wear it. It is stiff and heavy, and the gold visor tints the world in odd, dim shades.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 END near great heat; +1 armour against fire
- **Penalty:** −2 DEX stiff suit; −1 Perception through the gold visor
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 10 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 20 S
- **Also known as:** heat suit, silver suit

## Not to be confused with

the *[Smoke Helmet](smoke-helmet.md)*, which helps the wearer breathe in smoke but offers no protection against heat.
