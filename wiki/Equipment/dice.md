# Dice

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: bronze · any*

A set of small cubes of bone, wood, ivory or clay, each face marked with pips, or long flat sticks marked on one side. They are thrown from the hand or a cup and read by what lands uppermost. Taverns, barracks and back alleys echo with their rattle. They settle bets, decide disputes, and ruin whole families in a single evening.
