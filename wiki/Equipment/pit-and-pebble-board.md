# Pit-and-pebble board

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: agriculture · any*

A carved wooden plank or a row of holes scooped in the ground, with two rows of cup-shaped pits and a bag of seeds, pebbles or shells. Players lift all the seeds from one pit and sow them one by one around the board, capturing when the count falls right. It is quick to learn and endless to master, and it teaches children to count faster than any schoolroom.
