# Motion sensor kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: computing · modern*

A box of small white sensors, each the size of a matchbox, that stick to walls or stake into the ground. They watch for moving warmth or broken beams across a room, corridor or campsite, and sound a chirp or send an alert to a handset when something crosses. Nothing opens or shatters; the intruder simply walks into view of something they never noticed. They are hard to fool, and very easy to set up.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Perception noticing intruders in a watched area
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Security Systems](../Skills/security-systems.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** movement alarm

## Not to be confused with

*[Burglar Alarm](burglar-alarm.md)*, a wired system that triggers only when a door or window is opened.
