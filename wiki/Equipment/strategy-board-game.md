# Strategy board game

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: iron · any*

A chequered or lined board and two armies of carved pieces, each with its own way of moving. There is no luck: every win is earned and every loss is the player's own fault. Generals and nobles play it to sharpen the mind, and some claim the patience and foresight it teaches carry straight onto the battlefield. Others simply enjoy beating the general.
