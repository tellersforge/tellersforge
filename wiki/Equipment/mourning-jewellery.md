# Mourning jewellery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: printing · western kingdoms*

Black jet, dark enamel and small lockets holding a lock of the dead one's hair, worn in the months after a death. In the western kingdoms the rules are strict: how long, how black, what may be worn when grief begins to lift. Wearing it properly earns sympathy and respect at funerals. Wearing it to a party casts a shadow over the room that no amount of cheer will lift.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 Etiquette at funerals and during mourning
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA at parties
- **Used for:** —
