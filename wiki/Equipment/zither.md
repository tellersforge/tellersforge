# Zither

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: iron · eastern empire*

A long, shallow wooden box, strung along its full length with many silk or wire strings over movable bridges. The player sits or kneels with it laid across the knees or a low table, plucking with one hand and pressing the strings with the other to bend the notes. It is the scholar's instrument in eastern empires, played alone in a quiet room or a garden.
