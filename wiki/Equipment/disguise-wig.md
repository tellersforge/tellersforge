# Disguise wig

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: printing · any*

A well-made wig of real or fine artificial hair in any colour, cut and style, fitted with a cap and pins so it stays on during a chase. It is chosen to change the wearer's looks, not to impress. A new hair colour and a different parting can make an old friend walk straight past. Actors, spies and fugitives keep one handy.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +1 Disguise changing appearance
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Disguise](../Skills/disguise.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** false hair, hairpiece

## Not to be confused with

*[Powdered wig](powdered-wig.md)*, which advertises rank; the disguise wig is meant not to be noticed at all.
