# Message stick

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: stone tools · desert peoples*

A short stick carved with lines, dots and notches that remind the carrier of every part of a message: who sends it, to whom, what is asked and when to meet. Handed over at the end of a journey, it also proves that the bearer really comes from the sender. Messengers carrying one are allowed to cross lands that would otherwise turn them back. The marks mean little to anyone else.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Diplomacy as a recognised messenger
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Signals](../Skills/signals.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 1 C
- **Also known as:** carved message token

## Not to be confused with

*[Tally Stick](tally-stick.md)*, which counts things; the message stick carries meaning and proves who sent the bearer.
