# Herald's tabard

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · western kingdoms*

A short coat, open at the sides, bearing a lord's coat of arms on the front, the back and on both short sleeves. It makes the herald unmistakable across a battlefield. By old custom, a herald crossing between camps is to be treated as an envoy, not an enemy, and the tabard is the proof. That brightly coloured cloth makes hiding impossible.
