# Ghillie suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A net or loose smock tied thick with strips of burlap, twine and cloth in earth colours, and stuffed with grass, leaves and twigs picked on the spot. Lying still in the right undergrowth, a sniper or gamekeeper in one becomes part of the ground; people have stepped on them without noticing. It is hot, heavy and snags on everything, and it hides only a wearer who keeps still or moves at a crawl.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +3 Stealth lying still in matching vegetation; +2 Camouflage in matching vegetation
- **Penalty:** −2 END in heat; −2 Running while worn; −1 Stealth while moving quickly
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** sniper suit, yowie suit

## Not to be confused with

*[Leaf-and-grass cloak](leaf-and-grass-cloak.md)*, a simpler ancient version of the same idea; the ghillie suit covers the body fully and is built for long, still watches.
