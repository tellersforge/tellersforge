# Inflatable sleeping pad

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: computing · modern*

A tough tube of air-tight fabric, sometimes with a foam core, that is blown up by mouth or with a small bag pump. It spreads under a sleeping bag and keeps the cold of frozen ground or stone from stealing the sleeper's heat, which a thin blanket never manages. It rolls up small. It also punctures, and hissing slowly flat in the small hours is its signature failure.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 END sleeping on cold ground
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —

## Not to be confused with

*[Sleeping Mat](sleeping-mat.md)*, which is woven and simple, and keeps off damp but not the deep cold of frozen ground.
