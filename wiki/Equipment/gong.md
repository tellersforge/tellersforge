# Gong

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: bronze · eastern empire*

A great bronze disc, hammered thin and hung from a wooden frame, struck with a padded mallet. One blow fills a hall with a shimmering roar that seems to go on long after it should stop. Temples use it to call to prayer, palaces to announce a guest, and ships to warn each other in fog. Anyone hoping to go unnoticed after it sounds has left it too late.
