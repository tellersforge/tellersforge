# Climbing harness

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: computing · modern*

A set of stitched webbing loops round the waist and each thigh, with a strong front ring for tying in and gear loops round the sides. It spreads the shock of a fall across the hips and legs rather than crushing the ribs, and lets a climber hang for hours in comfort. It carries the clanking rack of cams and clips.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing hanging or belaying
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md), [Mountaineering](../Skills/mountaineering.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** sit harness

## Not to be confused with

*[Rope Sling Seat](rope-sling-seat.md)*, an older, simpler way to sit on a rope; the harness is made to hold a falling body safely.
