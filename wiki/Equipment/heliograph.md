# Heliograph

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: steam · any*

A mirror on a tripod with a sighting rod and a shutter that opens and closes to break the sun's reflection into long and short flashes. With a trained operator, it sends coded messages across deserts and mountain ranges, from peak to peak. Colonial armies used chains of them where wires could not be laid. It is silent and hard to intercept, but useless under cloud or after dark.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Signals long range in sunlight
- **Penalty:** −2 Signals under cloud or at night
- **Used for:** [Signals](../Skills/signals.md)
- **Weight:** 5 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 25 S
- **Also known as:** sun telegraph

## Not to be confused with

*[Signal Mirror](signal-mirror.md)*, which only flashes to attract attention; the heliograph spells out full coded messages.
