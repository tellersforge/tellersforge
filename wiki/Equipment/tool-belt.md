# Tool belt

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: printing · any*

A heavy belt of leather or canvas with loops, hooks and pockets for hammer, nails, chisel and measure. Carpenters, roofers and builders wear it on ladders and scaffolds, where every trip down for a forgotten tool is time and a risk. It sags, it jangles, and it keeps both hands free for work.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Construction working on a ladder or roof
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** work belt

## Not to be confused with

*[Carpenter's tools](carpenter-s-tools.md)*, the kit itself; the tool belt is only the way to carry it about the job.
