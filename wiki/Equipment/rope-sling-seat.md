# Rope sling seat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: iron · any*

A short plank or a canvas seat slung from a rope, sometimes with a loop for the feet. Painters, sailors working aloft, cliff-egg gatherers and builders on walls sit in it and work with both hands free while hanging in space. It is lowered or hauled up by a partner, or by the worker with a friction hitch. It is uncomfortable, but less uncomfortable than holding on.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing working while hanging
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md), [Sailing & Seamanship](../Skills/sailing-and-seamanship.md)
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** bosun's chair, boatswain's seat

## Not to be confused with

*[Climbing Harness](climbing-harness.md)*, which catches falls; the sling seat is for working while hanging still.
