# Flashlight

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: electricity · modern*

A tube of metal or plastic holding batteries, a bulb and a mirror, throwing a beam wherever it is pointed. It lights a path, a keyhole or a face in the dark. It is the simple, ordinary modern light, and it always seems to need new batteries at the worst possible moment.
