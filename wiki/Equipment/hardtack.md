# Hardtack

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: iron · western kingdoms*

A hard, dry biscuit of flour, water and a little salt, baked and baked again until it keeps for years if kept dry. Sailors and soldiers eat it soaked in coffee or crumbled into stew, and they joke about breaking teeth on it. After weeks of nothing else, the jokes stop being funny and tempers fray.
