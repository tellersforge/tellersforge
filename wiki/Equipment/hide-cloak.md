# Hide cloak

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · any*

The oldest coat there is: a hide scraped clean, dried, softened with fat or brains, and tied at the throat with a thong. It keeps the wind off the back and the rain off the shoulders, and at night it becomes a blanket. It smells of smoke and animal, it stiffens when wet, and nobody has ever called it handsome. Every people that hunts has made one.
