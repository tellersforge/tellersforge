# Wooden chest

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: iron · any*

A strong wooden box with a hinged lid, iron corners and a hasp for a lock, holding clothes, papers, silver or treasure. It sits at the foot of a bed, in a ship's cabin or in a lord's strongroom. It is heavy, sturdy and hard to carry off quietly, which is exactly why people trust it.
