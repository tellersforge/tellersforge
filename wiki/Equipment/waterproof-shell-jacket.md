# Waterproof shell jacket

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: computing · modern*

A light hooded jacket of layered fabric that sheds rain on the outside and lets sweat escape as vapour from within. Hikers, soldiers and climbers stuff one into a pack for when the weather turns. It folds small, weighs little, and keeps the wearer dry in wind and rain alike. In the quiet of a hide or a forest at night, though, its slick fabric rustles with every movement.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +2 END against rain and wind
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth rustles in quiet places
- **Used for:** —
