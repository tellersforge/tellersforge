# Jester's motley

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · noble courts*

A suit sewn from patches of bright, clashing colours, with a hood ending in points tipped with little bells. It is the uniform of the court fool, and it carries a strange licence: the fool may say what no courtier dares, as long as it is funny. It makes a wonderful stage. Nobody dressed in it will ever be taken seriously.
