# Signal mirror

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: bronze · any*

A small, polished mirror of bronze, silver or glass, often with a hole in the middle for aiming. Tilted at the sun, it flashes a spot of light that can be seen from a great distance, even from the air. Shepherds, scouts and castaways all use them to signal. It needs sunlight: under cloud, at dusk or at night, it is only a mirror.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Signals in sunlight
- **Penalty:** −2 Signals under cloud or at night
- **Used for:** [Signals](../Skills/signals.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** flash mirror

## Not to be confused with

*[Heliograph](heliograph.md)*, which is a larger mirror on a stand with a shutter for coded messages; the signal mirror only flashes.
