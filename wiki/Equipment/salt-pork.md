# Salt pork

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: iron · any*

Pork packed tightly in salt inside a barrel, where it keeps for months at sea or in camp. Cooks soak and boil it before serving, and even so it is salty enough to raise a raging thirst. Without plenty of water to go with it, it makes the eater miserable and sick.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** 0
- **Penalty:** −1 END if eaten without water
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** salt beef

## Not to be confused with

*[Dried meat](dried-meat.md)*, which keeps by drying rather than salting and is easier to eat on the march.
