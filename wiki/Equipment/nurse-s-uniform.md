# Nurse's uniform

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: steam · modern*

A clean, washable dress or tunic and trousers in white or pale colours, often with a starched cap, an apron and a watch pinned at the breast. It marks the wearer as hospital staff, someone patients trust with their pain and fears. Doctors nod past it and porters hold doors for it. Anyone wearing one can walk the corridors of a hospital with few questions asked, whether or not they have ever nursed.
