# Animal crate

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · any*

A box of slatted wood or wire mesh with a latched door and a handle on top, big enough for a dog, a pair of hens or a sulking cat. It keeps an animal safe and contained on a cart, a ship or a long journey. Straw on the floor and a cloth over the top calm most passengers. None of them enjoy it, and all of them make that clear at every stop.
