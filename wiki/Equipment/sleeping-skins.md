# Sleeping skins

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: stone tools · any*

A pile of furs and hides, some laid under the sleeper to keep off the cold of the ground, others heaped on top. Reindeer, bear, sheep or whatever the hunt provided. They are heavy, they smell of animal and smoke, and they are the warmest bed anyone had for many thousands of years. People in cold lands still know to sleep naked in them, with the fur side in.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 END sleeping in cold
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 4 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** fur robe, hide bedding

## Not to be confused with

*[Wool Blanket](wool-blanket.md)*, which is woven cloth; sleeping skins are hides taken straight from the hunt.
