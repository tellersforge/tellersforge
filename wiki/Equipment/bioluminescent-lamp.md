# Bioluminescent lamp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A sealed vessel of engineered glowing microbes, fed with nutrient gel, giving off a soft blue or green light for weeks. Spacers use them on long voyages to save power, and colonists light their first huts with them. The light is gentle and steady and faintly alive.
