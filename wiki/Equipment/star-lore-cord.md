# Star lore cord

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: stone tools · island peoples*

A knotted cord, or a ring of wood or shell with marks around it, recording the points on the horizon where the steering stars rise and set through the year. The navigator uses it to learn the sky, and at night finds the bearing of home by the star rising over the right knot. It is a memory aid more than a map, and useless to someone not taught to read it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Wayfinding by night

## Not to be confused with

*[Star Chart](star-chart.md)*, which draws the constellations; the star lore cord remembers where stars rise for steering.
