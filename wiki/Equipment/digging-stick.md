# Digging stick

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: stone tools · any*

A straight, stout branch with one end sharpened and hardened in the fire. It is the forager's plough: jabbed into the earth, it loosens soil and levers up roots, tubers and bulbs. Some are weighted with a pierced stone for extra force. It is simple, light and almost indestructible, and many peoples depend on one for most of their food.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Foraging roots and tubers
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Foraging](../Skills/foraging.md), [Farming](../Skills/farming.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 1 C
