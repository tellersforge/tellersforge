# Smelling salts

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: steam · any*

A tiny glass bottle with a tight stopper, holding crystals that give off a sharp, choking reek when opened. Waved under the nose of someone who has fainted, it jolts them awake with a gasp and streaming eyes. Chaperones, stage managers and boxing seconds carry them. They do nothing for the cause of the faint, but they bring the patient back to deal with it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 First Aid reviving the fainted or stunned
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [First Aid](../Skills/first-aid.md)
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** hartshorn, sal volatile

## Not to be confused with

*[Antidote Kit](antidote-kit.md)*, which treats poisons; smelling salts simply wake someone who has fainted or been stunned.
