# Loin belt

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · wrestling schools*

A long, thick band of heavy cloth wound tightly around the waist and between the legs, knotted at the back. It is the only garment of the wrestlers of certain schools, and in a bout the opponent grips it to lift, turn and throw. It is designed to be seized. Off the fighting ground, it is considered indecent.

## Game block

- **Slot:** legs
- **Bonus:** +1 Wrestling & Grappling in a bout
- **Penalty:** −2 Etiquette off the fighting ground
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** wrestler's belt, fighting belt

*[Loincloth](loincloth.md)*, a simple everyday garment of hot lands, not built to be seized in a bout.
