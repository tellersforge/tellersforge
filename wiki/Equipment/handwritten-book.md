# Handwritten book

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: iron · any*

A text copied by hand onto leaves of parchment or paper and bound between boards, sometimes decorated with coloured letters and small painted scenes. Each copy takes a scribe months. Before printing, a single book could cost as much as a house, and a library of a few dozen was a treasure. Every copy is different, and every copyist makes mistakes that the next copyist faithfully repeats.
