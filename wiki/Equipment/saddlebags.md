# Saddlebags

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · any*

A pair of leather bags joined by a strap and hung across the back of a saddle, one on each side of the horse. They hold a rider's food, spare clothes, tools and anything else needed for the journey. They are handy and sturdy, but they add weight to the horse and a thief can cut them free in a moment.
