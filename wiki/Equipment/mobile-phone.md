# Mobile phone

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: computing · modern*

A flat pocket device with a glowing screen that makes calls, sends messages, takes pictures, shows maps and looks up almost anything in moments, wherever there is a signal. Nearly everyone carries one, and many people could not find their way home without it. It also tells anyone with the right access exactly where its owner is. Away from towns, or with a flat battery, it goes quiet.
