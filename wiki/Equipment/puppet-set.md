# Puppet set

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: iron · any*

A trunk of hand puppets with carved wooden heads, string puppets with jointed limbs, or cut and painted leather figures that throw coloured shadows on a lit screen. A puppeteer can play a king, a devil and a clever servant all in one show, voices and all. Children shriek, and adults laugh at jokes that would get a living actor arrested.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Storytelling & Conversation with children or crowds
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Acting](../Skills/acting.md), [Storytelling & Conversation](../Skills/storytelling-and-conversation.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** marionettes, shadow puppets

## Not to be confused with

*[Theatre Mask](theatre-mask.md)*, which lets a living actor become a character, where the puppeteer makes one out of wood.
