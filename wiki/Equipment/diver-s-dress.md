# Diver's dress

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A suit of rubberised canvas bolted to a round copper helmet with little glass windows, with lead boots and a weighted belt. Air is pumped down a hose from a crew on the surface. In it a diver can walk the seabed in cold, dark water for hours, patching hulls and salvaging wrecks. Out of the water the whole rig is a clumsy burden, and a diver in it cannot swim at all, only sink and walk.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 END under cold water
- **Penalty:** −2 DEX out of the water; −2 Swimming while worn
- **Used for:** [Diving](../Skills/diving.md)
- **Weight:** 80 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 30 S
- **Also known as:** standard diving dress, hard-hat dress

## Not to be confused with

*[Hard diving suit](hard-diving-suit.md)*, an armoured metal shell for great depths; the diver's dress is flexible canvas and copper for shallower work.
