# Stretcher

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: stone tools · any*

Two long poles with a bed of hide, canvas or woven rope slung between them. Two bearers carry a wounded person out of danger, one at each end, keeping in step. It turns a friend who cannot walk into a load that can be moved. It is also slow and awkward: the bearers cannot run, and on a steep path the whole party moves at the speed of the worst foothold.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** 0
- **Penalty:** −1 Running for the bearers
- **Used for:** [Caregiving](../Skills/caregiving.md), [Field Medicine](../Skills/field-medicine.md)
- **Weight:** 6 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** litter, travois litter

## Not to be confused with

*[Travois](travois.md)*, which drags a load behind a person or animal; the stretcher is carried level and gently by two bearers.
