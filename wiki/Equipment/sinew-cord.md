# Sinew cord

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: stone tools · any*

Thin cord made from the dried sinews and guts of animals, split, chewed soft and twisted into strands. It is astonishingly strong for its thickness and is used to sew hides, bind arrowheads, string bows and lash tools together. As it dries it shrinks and grips tight. Its weakness is water: soaked, it stretches, slackens and slips, and knots tied in it come loose.
