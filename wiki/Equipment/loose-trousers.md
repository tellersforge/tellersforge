# Loose trousers

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · desert peoples*

Full trousers of light cotton or linen, gathered at the waist and ankle, billowing in between. Air moves freely through them, which keeps the legs cool under a hot sun, and they never bind when the wearer squats, climbs or runs. In the dry lands both men and women wear them beneath a long shirt or robe.
