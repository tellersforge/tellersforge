# Hazmat hood

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: computing · modern*

A sealed hood of treated fabric or plastic with a clear visor and a small filter, pulled down over the head and fastened at the collar. It keeps chemicals, germs and dust away from the eyes and lungs, and it goes on in seconds. Nurses, lab workers and emergency crews keep one at hand for spills and outbreaks. Inside it, the world goes quiet and muffled, and every voice seems far away.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head

- **Penalty:** −1 Perception muffled hearing

the *[Hazmat Suit](hazmat-suit.md)*, which seals the whole body and carries its own air; the hood protects only the head and lungs.
