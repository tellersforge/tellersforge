# Climbing claws

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: iron · shadow guilds*

Iron bands with sharp spikes, strapped round the palm and over the sole of the boot. With them, a climber can scale a tree trunk, a timber palisade or a rough stone wall like a cat. Hunters use them to reach honey and fruit, and raiders use them on stockades. While wearing them, fine handwork of any kind is clumsy and slow.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing on wood, bark or rough walls
- **Penalty:** −1 DEX fine handwork
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** hand claws, tiger claws

## Not to be confused with

*[Crampons](crampons.md)*, which grip ice; climbing claws bite into wood and rough walls.
