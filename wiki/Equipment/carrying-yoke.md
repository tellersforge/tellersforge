# Carrying yoke

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: agriculture · any*

A springy pole of wood or bamboo, shaped to sit across one or both shoulders, with a basket, a bucket or a bundle hung from each end. The bearer trots along with a rolling gait that matches the bounce of the pole, carrying loads that would otherwise be impossible. Market sellers, water carriers and porters use it. Loaded up, the bearer can hardly duck or twist.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Lifting & Carrying two balanced loads
- **Penalty:** −1 Dodge while loaded
- **Used for:** [Lifting & Carrying](../Skills/lifting-and-carrying.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** shoulder pole, milkmaid's yoke

## Not to be confused with

*[Ox Yoke](ox-yoke.md)*, which harnesses a pair of animals to a plough or cart; the carrying yoke is for a human porter.
