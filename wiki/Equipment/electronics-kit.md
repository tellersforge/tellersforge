# Electronics kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: computing · modern*

A small hard case holding a soldering iron, a meter, magnifying glasses, fine tweezers, spare components and a reel of solder. With it a technician repairs circuit boards, builds small devices and modifies equipment for purposes its makers never intended. The work is delicate and precise, and a burnt thumb is a rite of passage.
