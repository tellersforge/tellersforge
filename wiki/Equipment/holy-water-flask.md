# Holy water flask

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: iron · any*

A small flask of glass, clay or silver, stoppered tight and filled with water blessed by a priest. It is sprinkled over the sick, the dying and the newly born, and dashed at anything that should not walk the earth. It burns the unholy, some say, or at least makes them flinch. Hunters of the dead never travel without one.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Exorcism & Curse-Breaking against the unholy
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Exorcism & Curse-Breaking](../Skills/exorcism-and-curse-breaking.md)
- **Weight:** 0.4 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** blessed water
