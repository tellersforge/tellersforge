# Security camera

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: computing · modern*

A small camera on a bracket, pointing at a doorway, a till, a car park or a street corner, sending its picture to a screen somewhere or to a recorder that keeps weeks of footage. It never blinks, never gets bored and never takes a bribe, though it can be pointed the wrong way. Burglars wear hoods and caps. Investigators spend long nights watching grey shapes cross the frame, looking for the one that matters.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Shadowing & Surveillance watching a fixed spot
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Security Systems](../Skills/security-systems.md), [Shadowing & Surveillance](../Skills/shadowing-and-surveillance.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 5 S
