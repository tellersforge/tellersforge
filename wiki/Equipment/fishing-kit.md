# Fishing kit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: stone tools · any*

A pouch or small box of lines, hooks of bone or bent wire, sinkers, floats and a folded net. Tied to a pole or cast by hand from a boat, it turns a river or a shore into a meal. Patience is its main ingredient. Many a traveller has survived a lean season with a fishing kit and not much else.
