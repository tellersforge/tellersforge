# Sewing kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: stone tools · any*

A small pouch or case with needles of bone or steel, a few hanks of thread, an awl and a thimble. Soldiers, sailors and travellers carry one, because torn clothes and split packs happen far from any tailor. A good sewing kit is the difference between arriving in rags and arriving in patched but decent clothes. A few also carry a needle for stitching wounds, if they must.
