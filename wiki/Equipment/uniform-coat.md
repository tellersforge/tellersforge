# Uniform coat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · standing armies*

A tailored coat in a regiment's chosen colour, faced at cuffs and collar with a second one, so that in the smoke of battle everyone knows which side a man is on. Officers wear it to command and civilians step aside when they see it. The same colours make the wearer a target at night or in cover, and a very poor envoy on the enemy's side of the line.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +1 Leadership commanding troops; +1 Intimidation before civilians
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth bright colours; −1 Diplomacy behind enemy lines
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** gunpowder · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** regimental coat

## Not to be confused with

*[Military Service Uniform](military-service-uniform.md)*, the drab, practical dress of later armies, which is made to hide rather than to be seen.
