# Leaf-and-grass cloak

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

A cape of knotted fibre netting with fresh leaves, grass and twigs tucked into every mesh, remade wherever the hunter goes so it always matches the ground. Lying flat in the brush, the wearer becomes one more hummock of vegetation, and deer walk past within a spear's throw. It is light and quick to make. Once the rain gets into it, it turns into a cold, dripping heap that clings to the skin.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +2 Camouflage lying still in brush; +1 Stealth in grass or woodland
- **Penalty:** −1 END once soaked by rain
