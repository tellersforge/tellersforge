# Animal treats

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: agriculture · any*

A pouch of small rewards: strips of dried meat for a dog or a hawk, lumps of sugar for a horse, a handful of grain for anything that pecks or nibbles. Trainers keep it at the belt and pay out a morsel for every task done right. A stranger with treats in hand makes friends with a nervous animal far faster than one with a stick. The pouch smells, and every dog in the village knows exactly who is carrying it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Animal Training any; +1 Animal Handling winning over an animal
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Animal Training](../Skills/animal-training.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 1 C
- **Also known as:** titbits

## Not to be confused with

*[Scent Lure](scent-lure.md)*, which draws wild animals to a place; treats win over and reward an animal already at hand.
