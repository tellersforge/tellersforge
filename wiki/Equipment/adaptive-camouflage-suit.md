# Adaptive camouflage suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: far future · far future*

A close-fitting suit whose skin is covered in countless light-bending cells, each bending the light behind the wearer around to the front. Standing still, the wearer simply vanishes into whatever surrounds them, against any background. Even moving, they are hard to follow with the eye. The suit is warm to wear, because the cells shed heat slowly, and in hot places that becomes a trial.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +3 Stealth standing still; +3 Camouflage any terrain; +1 Stealth while moving
- **Penalty:** −1 END in heat
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 60 S
- **Also known as:** active camo suit, mimetic suit

## Not to be confused with

*[Adaptive camouflage cloak](adaptive-camouflage-cloak.md)*, a looser garment that is easier to throw on but less complete when the wearer stands still.
