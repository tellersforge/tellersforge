# Travois

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: stone tools · plains peoples*

Two long poles joined near one end, with a frame of netting or a stretched hide lashed between them. The joined end rests on the shoulders of a person or the back of a dog or horse, and the other ends drag along the ground. It carries loads, the sick and the old across open plains where there are no roads for wheels. It also carves two deep lines across the grass that anyone can follow.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Lifting & Carrying over open ground
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth leaves a clear trail
- **Used for:** [Lifting & Carrying](../Skills/lifting-and-carrying.md)
- **Weight:** 5 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** drag frame

## Not to be confused with

*[Hand Sledge](hand-sledge.md)*, which runs on snow and ice; the travois drags over open ground.
