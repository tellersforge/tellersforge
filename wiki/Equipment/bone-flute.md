# Bone flute

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: stone tools · any*

One of the oldest instruments in the world: a hollow bird bone, reed or bamboo stem with a few holes cut along its length and a notch at the end to blow across. It is small enough to carry in a pouch and sounds thin, breathy and haunting, carrying far across still water or a valley at dusk. Herders play it to pass the hours, and shamans to call something that might listen.
