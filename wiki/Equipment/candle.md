# Candle

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: writing · any*

A wick set in wax or tallow, burning with a steady, familiar flame. Tallow candles are cheap and smelly; beeswax ones are clean and costly and belong to churches and the rich. A candle on a sheltered table lights reading, writing and late suppers, but it needs protection from draughts.
