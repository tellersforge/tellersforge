# Potter's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: pottery · any*

Wooden paddles, smooth anvil stones, bone scrapers, pots of coloured slip and a small wheel head that can be set on a peg in any flat ground. With good clay, water and a fire, a potter makes cooking pots, storage jars and fine bowls. Potters mark their wares with thumbprints or stamps, and a single shard can tell a scholar centuries later who made it and where.
