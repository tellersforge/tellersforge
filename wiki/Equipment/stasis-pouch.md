# Stasis pouch

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: far future · far future*

A body-length bag of silvery fabric that seals round a dying patient and slows their body almost to a stop. Blood barely moves, the heart ticks only now and then, and death is held off for hours or days until a proper surgeon can be reached. Rescue teams carry it rolled up in a pack. Opening it too early, or in the wrong place, undoes all the good it has done.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +3 Field Medicine keeping a dying patient alive
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Field Medicine](../Skills/field-medicine.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 20 G
- **Also known as:** life-hold bag

## Not to be confused with

*[Stasis Pod](stasis-pod.md)*, which is a large fixed chamber; the pouch is portable and made for carrying the dying to help.
