# Straw snow boots

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: agriculture · mountain villages*

Tall boots plaited from rice or grain straw, packed inside with dry grass and bound to the leg with cord. Mountain villagers weave them by the fire over the winter evenings, and wear them out by spring. The rough straw bites into packed snow where leather would slide, and the grass stuffing holds the warmth well. Once the straw is soaked through, though, the boots turn cold and heavy, and a sensible walker carries a dry pair.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 END against snow; +1 Snow & Ice Travel grip on packed snow
- **Penalty:** −1 END once soaked
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** snow shoes of straw

## Not to be confused with

*[Snowshoes](snowshoes.md)*, which are strapped under ordinary footwear to spread the weight over soft snow; straw snow boots keep feet warm and grip the packed trail.
