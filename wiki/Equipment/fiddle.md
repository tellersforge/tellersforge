# Fiddle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: iron · any*

A small wooden body with a waist, four strings and a bow of horsehair drawn across them. Held under the chin or propped on the knee, it can weep, jig or shriek, and a good fiddler can keep a whole barn dancing till dawn. It is light, it travels well, and it has been blamed by preachers for more sin than strong drink. Every village wedding needs one.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Carousing at a dance
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Instrument (per instrument)](../Skills/instrument-per-instrument.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S

## Not to be confused with

*[Lute](lute.md)*, which is plucked and gentle, where the fiddle is bowed and drives a dance.
