# Dried fish

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: stone tools · coastal peoples*

Fish split open and dried on racks in the wind, or salted until hard as board. It feeds coastal villages through winter and sailors on long voyages, and it trades far inland. It must be soaked and cooked before it is pleasant. The smell travels further than the fish.
