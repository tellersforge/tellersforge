# Gravity boots

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: far future · far future*

Sleek boots with softly glowing soles that create their own small pull, pinning their wearer to whatever surface they step on. With them, a person can walk up a wall, across a ceiling or along a ship's hull in open space as easily as crossing a kitchen. They take some getting used to; the first time the ceiling becomes the floor, most people are sick.
