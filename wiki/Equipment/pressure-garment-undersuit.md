# Pressure-garment undersuit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A tight suit of elastic fabric that squeezes the legs and belly to keep blood in the head when a ship pulls hard acceleration. Pilots and crew wear it under their clothes and under space suits, which it also makes easier to put on and wear for long hours. It is uncomfortably warm to wear and exhausting to pull on, but it keeps a pilot awake through a hard burn.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Endurance under high acceleration
- **Penalty:** −1 END in heat
- **Used for:** [Environment Suits](../Skills/environment-suits.md)
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 25 S
- **Also known as:** counter-pressure layer, compression suit

## Not to be confused with

*[Pressure suit](pressure-suit.md)*, a full sealed suit for vacuum; the undersuit only keeps blood from pooling and makes wearing such a suit easier.
