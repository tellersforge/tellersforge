# Riding trousers

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: bronze · steppe riders*

Close-cut trousers of wool or soft leather, invented by the people who live on horseback. They let a rider grip the horse with the knees all day without rubbing the legs raw, and keep the wind out on the open plain. People of the draped-robe lands think them barbaric, which amuses the horse peoples greatly.

## Game block

- **Slot:** legs
- **Bonus:** +1 Riding on long rides; +1 END against chafing and cold
- **Penalty:** −1 Etiquette in lands of draped robes
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.8 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 4 S
- **Also known as:** breeches, trousers

## Not to be confused with

*[Loose Trousers](loose-trousers.md)*, full and gathered, cool for walking in heat but less good at gripping a saddle.
