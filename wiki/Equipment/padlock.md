# Padlock

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: iron · any*

A portable lock with a curved shackle that snaps shut through a hasp, a ring or a length of chain. It secures chests, gates, cellars and prisoners' chains, and can be moved from one to another as needed. Cheap ones rattle and break under a sharp blow; good ones are heavy and stubborn. Its key is small and easily lost, which is how many padlocks meet their end.
