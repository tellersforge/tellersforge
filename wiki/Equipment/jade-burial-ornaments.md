# Jade burial ornaments

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · eastern empire*

Pierced discs, carved plaques and small figures of jade, worn in life at rites and laid with the dead in the tomb. In the eastern empire jade stands for virtue, endurance and the favour of ancestors, and a family's finest pieces are handed down or buried with the most honoured. Scholars and nobles know their meaning at a glance and treat the wearer accordingly.
