# Water purification tablets

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: electricity · modern*

A small bottle or foil strip of chalky tablets, each one dropped into a canteen of doubtful water. The water is shaken and left for a while, and then it tastes faintly of a swimming bath but won't give the drinker a fever or the flux. Soldiers and aid workers carry them everywhere. They do nothing about mud, salt or poison, only about the things too small to see.
