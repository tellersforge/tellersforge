# Multitool

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: electricity · modern*

A palm-sized steel frame that folds out into pliers, a knife blade, several screwdrivers, a file, a saw and a bottle opener. It fixes loose screws, bends wire, cuts cable and gets small jobs done wherever they appear. It won't replace a real toolbox, but it's there when the toolbox isn't. Nearly every soldier and technician carries one in a belt pouch.
