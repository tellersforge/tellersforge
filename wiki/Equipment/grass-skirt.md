# Grass skirt

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · island peoples*

A skirt of dried grass, palm leaves or strips of bark fibre hung thick from a cord at the waist. On warm islands it is everyday dress, and in the dances it sways and flicks with every movement of the hips, making the steps visible from the far side of the fire. It rustles with every step.
