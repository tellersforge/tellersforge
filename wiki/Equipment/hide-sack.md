# Hide sack

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: stone tools · any*

A bag of scraped animal hide, sewn at the sides and drawn shut with a leather thong. It is one of the oldest containers there is: tough, a little smelly, and very hard to tear. Hunters carry meat home in it, gatherers fill it with nuts, and anyone with a knife and a skin can make another.
