# Fur boots

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

Tall, soft boots of caribou or seal fur with the hair outside, with thick soles of tough hide, sometimes with a fur lining turned inward. The wearer walks on snow as others walk on grass, and the feet stay warm through a day on the ice. In a warm room they are unbearable.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +2 END against cold; +1 Snow & Ice Travel on snow
- **Penalty:** −1 END in heat
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** mukluks, snow boots

## Not to be confused with

*[Felt Boots](felt-boots.md)*, the thick pressed-wool boots of colder farming lands, cheaper and warm but useless when wet.
