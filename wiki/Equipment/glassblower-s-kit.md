# Glassblower's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: iron · any*

Long iron blowpipes, pincers, shears and wooden paddles, used at a furnace so hot it glows white. The glassblower gathers a blob of molten glass, blows it into a bubble, spins it and shapes it into bottles, bowls and windows. It is a hot, delicate art, prized by merchants and scholars alike. Glassblowers have strong lungs and scarred forearms.
