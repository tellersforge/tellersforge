# Leather jerkin

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · western kingdoms*

A sleeveless coat of thick leather, rubbed with oil or tallow until it shines and sheds rain. Soldiers wear it over a shirt, hunters over wool, woodcutters and hedgers because brambles and flying chips cannot get through. It will turn a glancing cut or a thorn, though a determined blade goes straight in. It is comfortable, long-lasting and plain, the sort of garment that is handed down and patched rather than replaced.
