# Seal and sealing wax

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: writing · any*

A carved stamp of stone, metal or jade, and a stick of coloured wax. The wax is melted over a folded letter and the seal pressed into it, leaving the owner's mark. A broken seal shows the letter has been opened; an intact one shows it came from the right hand. Officials and nobles guard their seals carefully, since whoever holds one can speak in the owner's name.
