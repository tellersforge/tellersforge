# Thermal viewer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: computing · modern*

A handheld camera that sees warmth, not light, and shows it as a picture of colours: hot bodies glow white or red against a cool dark background. It finds people hiding in darkness, smoke or undergrowth, and even shows warm footprints and the engine of a car that was recently driven. Fine detail and faces, though, are a blur.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Searching finding warm bodies; +1 Tracking following fresh heat traces
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception reading fine detail or faces
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 20 S
- **Also known as:** heat camera

## Not to be confused with

*[Night-Vision Goggles](night-vision-goggles.md)*, which amplify faint light rather than showing heat.
