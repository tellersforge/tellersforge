# Feather fan

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · river kingdoms*

A large fan of ostrich or peacock feathers mounted on a tall pole or a short handle. In the river kingdoms, servants carry them beside rulers and priests to fan them and shade them from the sun, and the fans themselves became symbols of high rank. At court or at the temple, anyone with such a fan draws respect.
