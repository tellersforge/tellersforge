# Linen bandages

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: agriculture · any*

Rolls of clean linen torn into long strips, kept wrapped in a cloth so they stay dry and free of dirt. They bind wounds, pad blisters, strap sprains and hold splints and poultices in place. Every healer carries some and every household keeps a supply. Clean ones are worth more than people expect: a dirty bandage can cause more harm than the wound it covers.
