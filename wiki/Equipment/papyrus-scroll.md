# Papyrus scroll

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: writing · river kingdoms*

A long roll of sheets made from the pressed pith of a river reed, pasted end to end and wound round a rod. It takes ink well and is light and cheap in the lands where the reed grows. Libraries of scrolls held the learning of the old world. The pages grow brittle with age, and damp or rough handling cracks and rots them, so old scrolls must be unrolled with great care.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** 0
- **Penalty:** −1 Research after damp or rough handling
- **Used for:** [Calligraphy & Scribing](../Skills/calligraphy-and-scribing.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** reed-paper roll

## Not to be confused with

*[Parchment Roll](parchment-roll.md)*, which is made of skin and lasts far longer; papyrus is cheaper and more fragile.
