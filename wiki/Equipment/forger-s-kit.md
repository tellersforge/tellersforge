# Forger's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: writing · any*

A flat case of fine pens, inks in many shades, blank paper and parchment of various ages, seal blanks and coloured waxes. With it and a steady hand, a forger copies letters, deeds and passes. The best forgers study handwriting the way a painter studies faces, and they know that the seal convinces more people than the words.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Forgery copying seals
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Forgery](../Skills/forgery.md), [Calligraphy & Scribing](../Skills/calligraphy-and-scribing.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** forgery kit

## Not to be confused with

the *[Disguise Kit](disguise-kit.md)*, which forges a face rather than a document.
