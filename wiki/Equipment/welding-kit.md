# Welding kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: electricity · modern*

A gas torch or an electric arc welder, a dark-visored mask, heavy gloves and a bundle of filler rods. It melts steel and joins it seamlessly, or cuts through it like a hot knife through tallow. Welding fills the air with a hissing roar and a blinding, blue-white light that can be seen far away, so a welder at work is anything but discreet.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** 0
