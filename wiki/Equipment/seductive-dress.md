# Seductive dress

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A close-fitting, revealing garment of silk, satin or stretch fabric, cut low and short to draw every eye in a dim room. It is made for nightclubs, private rooms and late meetings where charm opens more doors than money. At a funeral, a temple or a council of war it causes offence rather than admiration. It also offers almost no warmth when the night turns cold.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 Charm & Seduction in a private meeting or nightclub
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA at a temple, funeral or war council; −1 END against cold
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** slinky dress, revealing outfit

## Not to be confused with

*[Evening dress](evening-dress.md)*, the formal attire of galas and dinners; the seductive dress breaks the dress code on purpose to attract attention.
