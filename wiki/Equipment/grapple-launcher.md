# Grapple launcher

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: electricity · modern*

A stubby gun that fires a grapnel on a thin line with a bang of compressed gas, up to the top of a tall building or across a gorge. The prongs spring open on impact and bite. It reaches anchors no arm could throw to. It is also loud, and the thump of its firing turns heads for a long way.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Climbing reaching a high anchor
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth when fired
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** line thrower, grapnel gun

## Not to be confused with

*[Grappling Hook](grappling-hook.md)*, which is thrown by hand and reaches only as far as a strong arm.
