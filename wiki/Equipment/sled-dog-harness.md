# Sled dog harness

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

Lines, collars and padded harnesses of hide or rope that join a team of dogs to a sled. Each dog has its place, from the leaders at the front to the strong wheel dogs nearest the sled. A well-fitted harness lets each animal pull hard without chafing. Tangled lines are a constant fight, and a driver spends a great deal of time sorting them out.
