# Life jacket

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: steam · seafaring peoples*

A vest of cork blocks, foam panels or inflatable bladders, buckled tight around the chest. It holds the wearer's head above water whether they can swim or not, and keeps a little warmth in when the sea is cold. Sailors grumble about wearing it and are glad of it the moment they go over the side. It is bulky and awkward, and anyone wearing one cannot dive under the surface at all.

## Game block

- **Slot:** torso
- **Bonus:** +2 Swimming staying afloat; +1 END surviving cold water
- **Penalty:** −2 Diving cannot go under; −1 Dodge bulky
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** cork jacket, life vest

## Not to be confused with

the *[Survival Suit](survival-suit.md)*, which covers the whole body against freezing water as well as keeping it afloat.
