# Swim fins

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: electricity · modern*

A pair of long rubber blades worn over the feet like oversized slippers. Kicked slowly and steadily, they drive a swimmer through the water with much less effort than bare feet, and they leave the hands free for a light, a spear or a line. Divers and lifeguards rarely go without them. On land they are ridiculous; their wearer waddles and slaps along, and a quick getaway on foot means taking them off first.
