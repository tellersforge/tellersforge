# Linen shift

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: agriculture · any*

A plain, loose garment of linen worn next to the skin under everything else. It is the one piece of clothing that is washed often, which is why it is linen. Under a wool tunic it stops the itching; under armour and a padded coat it stops the chafing that turns a day's march into a day of misery.

## Game block

- **Slot:** torso
- **Bonus:** +1 END against chafing under wool or armour
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** chemise, undershirt

## Not to be confused with

*[Silk Undershirt](silk-undershirt.md)*, the costly underlayer of later soldiers and nobles, smoother and said to help when an arrowhead has to be drawn.
