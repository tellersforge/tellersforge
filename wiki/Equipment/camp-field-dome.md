# Camp field dome

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Fields and far-future devices · Needs: far future · far future*

A small disc set in the middle of a campsite that raises a faint, weatherproof dome of force over tents and fire. Rain runs off it, wind breaks against it and snow piles up on its curve. It chimes softly whenever anything crosses its edge, so sleepers wake before a visitor arrives. Travellers on harsh worlds sleep far better under one.
