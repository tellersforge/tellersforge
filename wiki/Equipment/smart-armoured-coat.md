# Smart armoured coat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: far future · far future*

An elegant coat of fabric that behaves like silk until something strikes it, when the weave stiffens at once into a hard shell. It is protection disguised as fashion, worn by nobles, executives and anyone who has enemies but does not want to look worried. It turns a sudden blow, a thrown object or a surprise stab. Among the wealthy it is admired as much as tailored clothing.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +2 armour against sudden impacts; +1 CHA among the wealthy
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
