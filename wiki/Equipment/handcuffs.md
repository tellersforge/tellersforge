# Handcuffs

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A pair of light steel cuffs on a short chain, each with a toothed arm that ratchets shut with a snap and tightens with a squeeze. One small key opens them all. Constables and police carry a pair on the belt, and the click of them closing has ended a great many chases. They are quick and humane compared with the old irons, and a practised escape artist can slip them with a hairpin.
