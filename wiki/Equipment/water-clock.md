# Water clock

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: writing · river kingdoms*

A clay or bronze vessel with a tiny hole near the base, marked inside with lines. Water drips out at a steady rate and the falling surface shows the hours. River kingdoms timed night watches with them, courts limited speeches with them and astronomers kept the sky's schedule by them. The water must be clean and the hole kept clear. Cold makes it slow, and a cracked pot makes it useless.
