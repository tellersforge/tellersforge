# Magnifying lens

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: iron · any*

A disc of polished rock crystal or clear glass, set in a ring of horn or brass with a handle. Held over a page, a coin, a seal or a splinter, it makes small things large and hidden details plain. Scholars, jewellers and moneylenders keep one in a drawer, and investigators never leave home without it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Searching examining small things close up
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Appraisal](../Skills/appraisal.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** reading stone, hand lens

## Not to be confused with

*[Jeweller's Loupe](jeweller-s-loupe.md)*, a small cupped lens held in the eye for close work with both hands free.
