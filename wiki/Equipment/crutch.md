# Crutch

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: agriculture · any*

A stout wooden prop, padded where it fits under the arm, that lets a person with a hurt leg or foot get about without help. Taverns and hospitals have a few leaning in the corner. Using one is slow and awkward at first and becomes quick with practice. A veteran on crutches can still move surprisingly fast, though anything requiring both hands, or nimble footwork, becomes a struggle.
