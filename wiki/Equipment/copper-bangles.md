# Copper bangles

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: copper · any*

Stacks of thin bangles of copper, brass or bronze worn by the dozen on the wrist or climbing the forearm. They are cheap, bright and cheerful, and at festivals and markets they catch the light and the eye. They jingle with every gesture, which is part of their charm and entirely incompatible with sneaking anywhere.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 CHA at festivals and markets
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth jingle when moving
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** copper · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** arm rings, bracelets

## Not to be confused with

*[Arm ring](arm-ring.md)*, a single heavy ring worn on the upper arm as a gift and pledge; copper bangles are cheap ornament worn in stacks.
