# Silk undershirt

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · steppe riders*

A close-fitting shirt of raw, tightly woven silk worn under armour or a coat. Steppe horse-archers trusted it for a simple reason: an arrow that gets through often fails to cut the silk, and instead drives it into the wound like a sock. Pulling gently on the cloth then draws the arrowhead out cleanly, with less tearing and less dirt left behind. It is costly, light and quietly lifesaving.

## Game block

- **Slot:** torso
- **Bonus:** +1 armour against arrows; +1 First Aid pulling arrows out
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —

*[Linen shift](linen-shift.md)*, an ordinary underlayer that is cheaper and cooler, but tears when an arrow strikes and does nothing to help pull it out.
