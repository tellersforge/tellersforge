# Belay device

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: computing · modern*

A small piece of shaped metal through which the rope runs in a tight bend. With it, one climber can hold another's fall with a flick of the wrist, or slide down a rope in a smooth, controlled descent instead of a burning plunge. It is simple, cheap and saves lives every day. Clipping it in the wrong way round is a mistake one makes only once.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing abseiling or belaying
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** descender, figure-eight

## Not to be confused with

*[Rope Ascenders](rope-ascenders.md)*, which go up a rope; the belay device holds a fall or goes down.
