# Lyre

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: bronze · river kingdoms*

A small wooden frame with two curved arms and a crossbar, often built on a tortoise shell or sound box, strung with a handful of gut strings and plucked with the fingers or a plectrum. It is the bard's instrument in river kingdoms, played to carry a long tale of kings and gods from one verse to the next. A teller with a lyre in hand holds a room longer than one without.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Storytelling & Conversation accompanying a tale
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Instrument (per instrument)](../Skills/instrument-per-instrument.md)
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** small harp

## Not to be confused with

*[Harp](harp.md)*, a larger instrument with many more strings, played seated and used more for music than for telling stories.
