# Snow camouflage overwhites

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A loose white smock and over-trousers, often with a hood, pulled over the soldier's ordinary clothes and gear. Against snowfields and frosted forests, the wearer seems to vanish, leaving only a moving shadow. Ski troops and winter scouts rely on them. Once the snow melts or the wearer crosses into dark woods or a town, the bright white stands out worse than any other colour.
