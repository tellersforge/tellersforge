# Monofilament rope

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A near-invisible line of single-strand synthetic crystal, so thin it seems to vanish at a few paces and strong enough to lift a groundcar. It pays out from a heavy reel with a guarded handle. Over a long haul it is a wonder. Handled with bare skin, it slices like a razor, and more than one careless user has lost a finger to it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing long hauls
- **Penalty:** −1 DEX handling without gloves
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md), [Knots & Rope Work](../Skills/knots-and-rope-work.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** fibre line

## Not to be confused with

*[Nylon Climbing Rope](nylon-climbing-rope.md)*, which is thick, soft and forgiving to the hands.
