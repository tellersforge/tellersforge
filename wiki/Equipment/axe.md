# Axe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: bronze · any*

A wedge of bronze or iron on a long wooden handle, balanced for felling trees and splitting logs. Woodcutters, soldiers and settlers all carry one. With it, a traveller in forest country can build a shelter, cut firewood and clear a trail. A dull axe is dangerous and a sharp one is a joy, and anyone who uses one learns to keep the edge keen.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Survival: Forest (Woodcraft) building shelter or fire
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Hand Tools](../Skills/hand-tools.md), [Carpentry & Joinery](../Skills/carpentry-and-joinery.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** hatchet, felling axe

## Not to be confused with

the *[Saw](saw.md)*, which cuts wood more cleanly but more slowly.
