# Perimeter alarm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: computing · modern*

A kit of small sensors, beams or tripwires, planted around a camp's edge, that chime, flash or buzz a handheld unit when something crosses the line. The sleeper wakes before the intruder reaches the tent. Rain, wandering animals and the careless companion answering a call of nature set it off too, which is why few who use it sleep particularly well.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Danger Sense against intruders at camp
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** tripwire alarm, motion sensor

## Not to be confused with

*[Bell Tripwire](bell-tripwire.md)*, which does the same job with string and bells; the perimeter alarm is quieter to the intruder and wider in reach.
