# Steel-toed boots

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · industrial cities*

Laced work boots with a cap of pressed steel hidden in the toe and a thick sole that resists oil and nails. Builders, dockers and factory workers wear them, because sooner or later something heavy falls. When it does, the foot survives. The boots are heavy and loud on concrete and metal floors, and after a long march in them the wearer's feet are sore in quite another way.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 armour against crushing and falling objects on the feet
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth on hard floors; −1 Running long distances
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.8 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 4 S
- **Also known as:** safety boots, toe-capped boots

## Not to be confused with

*[Mountaineering boots](mountaineering-boots.md)*, which are stiff and insulated for snow and ice; steel-toed boots guard against crushing on a work site.
