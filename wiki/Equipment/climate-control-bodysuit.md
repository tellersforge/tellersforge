# Climate-control bodysuit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A thin, close-fitting undersuit woven with tiny channels and heating threads, powered by a small cell at the hip. It warms the skin in bitter cold and cools it in fierce heat, adjusting itself without the wearer thinking. Spacers, colonists and explorers wear it under their clothes on worlds where the weather swings from frost to furnace. It is so comfortable that most forget they have it on.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 END against heat; +2 END against cold
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 30 S
- **Also known as:** thermal bodysuit, regulating suit

## Not to be confused with

*[Heated suit](heated-suit.md)*, which only warms, and *[Cooling vest](cooling-vest.md)*, which only cools; the bodysuit does both.
