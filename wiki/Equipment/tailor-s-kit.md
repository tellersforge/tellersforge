# Tailor's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: iron · any*

Heavy shears, a lump of chalk, a knotted measuring cord, pins and fine needles, rolled in a cloth or packed in a case. With them a tailor measures, cuts and fits clothes that hang properly and last. A good tailor can make a nobody look like a courtier, and a bad one can make a courtier look like a scarecrow. Tailors hear a great deal of gossip while pinning hems.
