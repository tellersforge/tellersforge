# Webbing harness

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: electricity · modern*

A system of straps, belts and clip-on pouches worn around the chest, shoulders and hips, spreading a load of water, ammunition and kit evenly across the body. Soldiers and field workers live in them. A well-fitted harness lets its wearer march all day and still reach a canteen or a spare magazine without looking.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Lifting & Carrying spreading a field load
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** load-bearing vest, chest rig

## Not to be confused with

*[Bandolier](bandolier.md)*, a single belt across the chest; the harness spreads a whole field load.
