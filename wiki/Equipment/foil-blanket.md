# Foil blanket

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: computing · modern*

A sheet of thin, shiny foil that crumples into a packet the size of a fist. Wrapped around a body, it holds in warmth that would otherwise bleed away into the night. Rescue teams hand them out to survivors, and hikers keep one in every pack. It is almost weightless and nearly indestructible. It is also bright as a mirror and crackles at every movement, so it is no friend to anyone trying to hide.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 END against cold when resting
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth crackles and shines
