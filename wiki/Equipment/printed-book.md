# Printed book

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: printing · any*

A text printed from set type or plates, bound between boards or paper covers and sold by the hundred. It may be an almanac, a book of sermons, a treatise on horses or a novel. Printing put books in the hands of merchants, artisans and eventually everyone. A well-chosen book can teach a whole trade; a badly chosen one can start an argument lasting generations.
