# Chemical sniffer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: computing · modern*

A slim wand with a nose of fine mesh, passed over luggage, clothing and door handles, or fed a cloth swab wiped across a suspect's hands. It tastes the air for the ghost of chemicals: the oily sweetness of explosives, the traces of drugs, the bitter edge of poison. Checkpoint guards wave it like a priest's censer, and smugglers go to great lengths with soap, gloves and perfume to beat it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Searching finding explosives, drugs or poisons
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Security Systems](../Skills/security-systems.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** explosive detector

## Not to be confused with

the *[Gas Detector](gas-detector.md)*, which warns its wearer of dangerous air; the sniffer hunts for faint traces someone has tried to hide.
