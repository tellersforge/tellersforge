# Carved bone pendant

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: stone tools · any*

A small figure carved from bone, antler, shell or ivory, hung from the neck on a cord: an animal, a spirit, a fish hook, a spiral. Such pendants are often ancestral treasures, handed down from grandmother to grandchild. Among the wearer's own people, the carving says who they are and where they come from, and earns the kindness due a kinsman.
