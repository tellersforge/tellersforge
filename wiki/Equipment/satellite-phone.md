# Satellite phone

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: spaceflight · modern*

A chunky phone with a thick fold-out aerial that talks to satellites overhead instead of towers on the ground. It works at sea, in the desert, on a glacier or anywhere else with open sky. Explorers, reporters and aid workers carry one when nothing else will reach. Calls are costly, there is a short delay in every conversation, and under a roof or in a canyon it falls silent.

*[Mobile Phone](mobile-phone.md)*, which needs a local signal; the satellite phone works almost anywhere under open sky.
