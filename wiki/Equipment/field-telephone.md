# Field telephone

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: electricity · modern*

A telephone in a sturdy leather or metal case, with a crank on the side that rings the other end. Laid cables connect it to others in trenches, command posts and outposts. It carries voices clearly and cannot easily be overheard from the air. Its weakness is the wire: shellfire, careless boots and enemy scouts all cut it, and someone must crawl out to find and mend the break.
