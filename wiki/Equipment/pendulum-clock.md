# Pendulum clock

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: printing · western kingdoms*

A wooden case, a painted face and a long swinging pendulum that ticks off the seconds with steady patience. The pendulum made clocks accurate enough to trust with minutes. Wealthy homes, observatories and counting houses keep them in the hall, where they become part of the family. They must stand straight and still, and a clock carried in a cart arrives sulking and needs setting again.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Counting & Measuring exact timing
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Clockwork & Instruments](../Skills/clockwork-and-instruments.md)
- **Weight:** 15 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 20 G
- **Also known as:** mantel clock, longcase clock

the *[Tower Clock](tower-clock.md)*, which rings the hours for a whole town but keeps much rougher time.
