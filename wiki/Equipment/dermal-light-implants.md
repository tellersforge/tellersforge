# Dermal light implants

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

Tiny lights or luminous inks placed under the skin, tracing lines, stars or patterns that glow when the wearer wills it. In fashionable circles among the stars they are a mark of daring and good taste, and the patterns can be changed with the mood or the occasion. The wearer must remember to turn them off, because a softly glowing cheek is a fine target in the dark.
