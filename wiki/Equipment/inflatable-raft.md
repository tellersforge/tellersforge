# Inflatable raft

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: electricity · modern*

A boat of rubberised cloth, folded into a bag and inflated by a hand pump or a gas bottle. It carries several people and their gear across a river or a lake, and it bounces off rocks that would stove in a wooden boat. It is paddled rather than rowed, and it is slow and clumsy in any wind, which pushes it about like a leaf.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Rowing & Paddling any
- **Penalty:** −1 Rowing & Paddling in wind
- **Used for:** [Rowing & Paddling](../Skills/rowing-and-paddling.md)
- **Weight:** 12 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** rubber boat

## Not to be confused with

*[Folding Kayak](folding-kayak.md)*, which is sleeker, faster and handles wind better.
