# Handheld computer

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: computing · modern*

A flat slab of glass and metal small enough for a pocket, with a touch screen that answers to the tap and swipe of a finger. It holds maps, notes, books and pictures, and can find its owner's place in a strange city in a moment. People look at them constantly. When its battery dies, many owners feel strangely lost.
