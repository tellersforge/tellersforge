# Fire plough

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: stone tools · island peoples*

A hardwood stick pushed hard and fast along a groove in a softer board until the dust at the end of the groove smokes and glows. Island peoples use it where good drilling wood is scarce. It is exhausting work, done kneeling, and the fire-maker must know just when to stop and nurse the ember.
