# Radiation shot

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A single-use injector of cloudy drug, pressed to the thigh before or during heavy exposure. It floods the body with compounds that shield the cells and patch the worst of the damage as it happens. Soldiers and rescuers in poisoned zones carry them, grateful and wary in equal measure. The price is paid later: the rest of the day is spent grey-faced and queasy, with every meal a gamble.

- **Bonus:** +2 END against radiation
- **Penalty:** −1 END nausea for the rest of the day
