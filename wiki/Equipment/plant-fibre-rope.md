# Plant-fibre rope

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: stone tools · any*

Rope twisted from whatever fibre the land offers: inner bark, grass, reed, palm leaf, the husk of coconuts. A coil is a day's patient work for a skilled twister. It ties bundles, lashes rafts, hauls loads and hangs hammocks. It is rough and a little uneven, and under a great weight, a climber's full weight on a long drop, it can fray and part without much warning.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** 0
- **Penalty:** −1 Climbing bearing heavy loads
- **Used for:** [Knots & Rope Work](../Skills/knots-and-rope-work.md), [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md)
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 8 C
- **Also known as:** bast rope, coir rope, grass rope

## Not to be confused with

*[Hemp Rope](hemp-rope.md)*, the evenly laid rope of farming peoples; plant-fibre rope is older, rougher and less reliable under heavy loads.
