# Medals and decorations

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: printing · any*

Medals, stars, crosses and ribbons pinned to the breast, each marking a campaign, an act of valour, long service or royal favour. At official ceremonies and among veterans, they speak of courage and sacrifice without a word, and old soldiers will listen to the wearer. Pacifists and former enemies see them differently.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 Leadership among veterans; +1 CHA at official ceremonies
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA among pacifists or former enemies
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** orders, campaign ribbons

## Not to be confused with

*[Rank insignia](rank-insignia.md)*, which show authority; medals and decorations show deeds done and honours given.
