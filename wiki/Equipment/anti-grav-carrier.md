# Anti-grav carrier

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: far future · far future*

A flat lifting plate that floats a load at hip height and follows its owner at a steady distance, like a well-trained dog. It can carry freight that would take a team of porters, and it goes where its owner goes, across rough ground, over water and up stairs. It needs no rest and never complains.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +3 Lifting & Carrying hauling freight
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 30 G
- **Also known as:** float pack, hover sled

## Not to be confused with

*[Pack cart](pack-cart.md)*, which rolls a heavy load on wheels and cannot leave the road.
