# Dried meat

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: stone tools · any*

Thin strips of lean meat dried in wind, sun or smoke until they are hard, dark and nearly weightless. They keep for months and can be chewed on the move or stewed into something softer. Hunters, herders and soldiers of every age carry it. It tastes of salt, smoke and hard work.
