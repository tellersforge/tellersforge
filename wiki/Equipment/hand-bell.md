# Hand bell

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: bronze · any*

A bronze or iron bell with a wooden handle, swung to make a loud, clear clang. Town criers ring it before calling out news, schoolteachers ring it to call children in, and street sellers, lepers and watchmen each have their own purpose for it. A few strokes turn every head in a market square. It is hard to ignore, and just as hard to muffle once it starts swinging.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Oratory & Debate gathering a crowd
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Signals](../Skills/signals.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** crier's bell

## Not to be confused with

*[Signal Horn](signal-horn.md)*, which carries far over open country; the hand bell calls a crowd close by.
