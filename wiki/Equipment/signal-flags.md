# Signal flags

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: gunpowder · sailors*

A set of bright cloth flags in many colours and patterns, each standing for a letter, a number or a whole message. Hoisted in a line up a ship's mast or waved by a signaller on a ridge, they spell out orders and news for anyone who can read them. Navies developed whole languages of flags. They need daylight, a line of sight and someone at the other end with a book and a good spyglass.
