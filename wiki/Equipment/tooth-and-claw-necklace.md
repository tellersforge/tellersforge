# Tooth and claw necklace

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

Teeth, claws and tusks from animals the wearer has killed, strung on sinew or hide thong with a few beads between. Each one is a story: a bear taken in the spring, a big cat in the reeds. Hunters and warriors read it at a glance and give the wearer the respect it proclaims. City folk see only a savage trinket and a reason to keep their distance.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 Intimidation among hunters and warriors
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA among city folk
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 3 C
- **Also known as:** trophy necklace, fang string

## Not to be confused with

*[War trophy scalp belt](war-trophy-scalp-belt.md)*, which boasts of enemies killed rather than beasts; the tooth and claw necklace is the hunter's boast.
