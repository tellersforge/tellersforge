# Elephant goad

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · river kingdoms*

A stout wooden staff capped with a metal point and a curved hook, used by the rider or handler of an elephant. It is less a weapon than a vocabulary: a touch behind the ear, a pull at the leg, a tap to stop. Elephant handlers train from childhood and are often bonded to one animal for life. An elephant that respects its handler barely needs the goad at all; one that doesn't will not be stopped by it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Animal Handling elephants
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Animal Handling](../Skills/animal-handling.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** ankus

## Not to be confused with

*[Herding Crook](herding-crook.md)*, which catches and guides sheep and goats; the goad directs a single enormous animal.
