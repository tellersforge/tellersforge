# Hydrophone

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: electricity · modern*

A waterproof microphone on a long cable, lowered over the side of a boat or a pier into the dark. Headphones on, the listener hears a world that looked silent: the thrash of distant propellers, the clicks and songs of great sea creatures, the bubbling breath of a diver underneath the hull. Fishermen, naval watchers and scientists all use them. Storms, surf and a nearby engine drown out everything else.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Perception hearing vessels and creatures underwater
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Communications & Sensors](../Skills/communications-and-sensors.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** underwater microphone

## Not to be confused with

the *[Sonar Set](sonar-set.md)*, which sends out its own sound and listens for echoes; the hydrophone only listens, and so gives nothing away.
