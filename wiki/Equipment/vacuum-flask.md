# Vacuum flask

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: electricity · modern*

A tall bottle with an inner wall of silvered glass or steel and nothing but empty space between that and the outer case. Poured in hot, tea or soup comes out hot at the end of the day; poured in cold, water stays cold in the desert. On a freezing night watch, a hot drink from the flask warms the hands and the belly and lifts the spirits. Dropped hard, the old glass kind shatters with a sad tinkle.
