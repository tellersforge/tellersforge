# Wrapped robe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · eastern empire*

An ankle-length robe with wide sleeves, crossed left over right at the breast and closed with a sash. It is the proper dress for the house of an imperial people, and the pattern, colour and fabric of the robe tell visitors much about the wearer. The skirt is cut narrow, so the wearer walks in short, dignified steps, and cannot run far.
