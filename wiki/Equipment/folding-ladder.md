# Folding ladder

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: electricity · modern*

A ladder of light alloy that telescopes or hinges into a compact bundle and opens to reach an upper window or the top of a wall. Painters, window cleaners, firefighters and burglars all use it. It is quick and sturdy, but even folded it is a long, awkward object, and carrying one through a city street at night draws exactly the wrong sort of attention.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing walls up to five metres
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth carrying through streets
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md)
- **Weight:** 10 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** extension ladder

## Not to be confused with

*[Rope Ladder](rope-ladder.md)*, which is lighter and hides more easily, but needs something above to hang from.
