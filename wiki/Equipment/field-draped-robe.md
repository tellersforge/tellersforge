# Field-draped robe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: far future · far future*

A robe whose long train and folds are held aloft by gentle force fields, so the fabric floats and ripples as if underwater while weighing almost nothing. It is worn by nobles and high officials at far-future courts to show their wealth and grace. Among the high born it is admired. Among labourers and workers it looks like arrogance.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 CHA at a far-future court; +1 Etiquette among the high born
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA among labourers
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 20 S
- **Also known as:** light-woven robe, hover-train robe

## Not to be confused with

*[Court regalia gown](court-regalia-gown.md)*, the heavier ceremonial garment that marks exact rank; the field-draped robe is simply elegant fashion.
