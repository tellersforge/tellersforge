# Clay oil lamp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: pottery · any*

A small fired lamp shaped like a closed dish with a spout for a wick, burning olive or other vegetable oil. It sits in wall niches, on tables and in tombs across the ancient world. Its light is small and warm and it smokes a little, but it is cheap and it burns for hours.
