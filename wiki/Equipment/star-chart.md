# Star chart

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: writing · any*

A drawn map of the sky on parchment, paper or a painted panel, showing the fixed stars, the constellations, the path of the sun and the moon's stations. Astronomers use it to track the seasons and set calendars, and navigators use it to find their latitude and the bearing of a star. A careful copy is valued, and passed from teacher to student.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Astronomy & Calendars any; +1 Sea Navigation at night
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Astronomy & Calendars](../Skills/astronomy-and-calendars.md), [Sea Navigation](../Skills/sea-navigation.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** sky map, celestial atlas

## Not to be confused with

*[Star Lore Cord](star-lore-cord.md)*, a knotted memory aid for steering; the star chart maps the whole sky.
