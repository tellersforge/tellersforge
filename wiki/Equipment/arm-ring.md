# Arm ring

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · northern raiders*

A heavy spiral or twisted ring of silver, gold or bronze, worn on the upper arm. Among the northern raiders, a lord gives such rings to his warriors as payment and as a pledge: the ring is the bond between them. A warrior wearing one is known as sworn to a lord, and the lord's other followers treat him as a brother.

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 Leadership among a lord's sworn followers
- **Penalty:** 0

- **Also known as:** oath ring, armlet

## Not to be confused with

*[Torc](torc.md)*, which is worn around the neck as a badge of freedom and chiefly rank; the arm ring is a lord's gift to a sworn follower.
