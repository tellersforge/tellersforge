# Lock bypass kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: computing · modern*

A zippered case of clips, needle probes, a code reader with a small screen, loops of wire and a stack of blank cards. The thief pops the cover off a keypad or card reader, finds the wires that matter, and either feeds the lock a borrowed code or tells it the door is already open. It needs a steady hand and some knowledge of circuits. Keypads and card readers are what it was made for.

- **Bonus:** +1 Security Systems bypassing keypads and card readers

## Not to be confused with

*[Keycard Cloner](keycard-cloner.md)*, which only copies a card someone else is carrying; the bypass kit attacks the lock itself.
