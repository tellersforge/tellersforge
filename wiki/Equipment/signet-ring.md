# Signet ring

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: writing · any*

A heavy ring whose face is carved in reverse with a personal or family seal, pressed into warm wax to sign and close letters and documents. Nobles, merchants and officials wear it constantly, and it acts as a signature that anyone can recognise. It proves rank and house at court. It also makes a false identity dangerous, because anyone who knows the seal knows exactly whose hand it belongs to.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 Administration to seal documents; +1 Court Etiquette & Protocol to prove rank or house
- **Penalty:** −2 Deception if seen by those who know the seal
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.02 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** seal ring

## Not to be confused with

*[Seal and sealing wax](seal-and-sealing-wax.md)*, the stamp and wax kept on a desk; the signet ring carries the seal on the finger and proves its wearer.
