# Power pack

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A dense, sealed energy cell about the size of a book, with a sturdy clip and a small readout of its charge. It powers suits, scanners, tools and small weapons for weeks. Explorers and soldiers of the far future carry spares and guard them carefully. A power pack is heavy for its size, and damaged ones can grow hot or crack.
