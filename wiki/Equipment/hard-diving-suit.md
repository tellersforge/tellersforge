# Hard diving suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: computing · modern*

A hulking armoured suit of steel or cast metal with great ball joints at the shoulders, elbows and knees, and heavy clamps instead of hands. Inside, the air stays at the same pressure as the surface, so the diver can descend where the sea would crush a normal body. It walks slowly on the bottom or hangs from a winch cable like a spider. Delicate work is impossible; everything must be gripped and turned by clamp.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 Diving very deep dives
- **Penalty:** −3 DEX clamp hands only; −2 Swimming walks or is winched
- **Used for:** [Diving](../Skills/diving.md), [Environment Suits](../Skills/environment-suits.md)
- **Weight:** 250 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 60 S
- **Also known as:** atmospheric diving suit

## Not to be confused with

the *[Diving Helmet](diving-helmet.md)*, a flexible canvas suit for shallower water, where the diver can still use their own hands.
