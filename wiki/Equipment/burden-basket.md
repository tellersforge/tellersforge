# Burden basket

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: agriculture · any*

A deep, conical basket carried on the back, held by a band across the forehead or the shoulders. Farmers carry harvests, firewood and market goods in it for miles along steep paths, bent forward under the load. It is excellent for walking and awful for climbing, where the weight pulls backward at every handhold.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Lifting & Carrying long hauls on foot
- **Penalty:** −1 Climbing while worn
- **Used for:** —

- **Also known as:** tumpline basket, back basket
