# Wooden clogs

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · farming villages*

Shoes carved from a single block of willow or poplar, hollowed to fit the foot and sometimes padded with straw. They cost almost nothing, last for years and keep the feet dry in muddy yards and wet fields. They also clatter on every stone, and running in them is an exercise in keeping one's ankles.
