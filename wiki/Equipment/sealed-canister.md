# Sealed canister

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A metal canister with a screw-down lid and a seal tight enough to hold air in or keep vacuum out, for years if need be. Spacers keep samples, spare air and precious food in them, and survey crews bring back soil from strange worlds sealed inside. Whatever goes in comes out unchanged, apart from time.
