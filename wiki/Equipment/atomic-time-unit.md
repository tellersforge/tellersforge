# Atomic time unit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A sealed module no larger than a matchbox, holding a clock that counts the steady vibrations of atoms. It loses less than a blink over years. Spacecraft use it to time engine burns, to synchronise with distant stations and to measure position by the delay of signals. Ordinary people never see one, but every navigation system in a star-faring civilisation depends on it.
