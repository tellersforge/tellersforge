# Loaded dice

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: iron · any*

A set of gaming cubes that look ordinary but carry a drop of lead or mercury under one face, or edges sanded so they land one way more often than chance allows. A cheat who throws them carefully wins slowly and steadily. One who wins too fast, or lets a sharp-eyed opponent handle them, will have a great deal of explaining to do, often in an alley.
