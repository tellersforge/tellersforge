# Weaving kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: agriculture · any*

A spindle weighted with a clay whorl, a distaff for the raw wool or fibre, and a backstrap loom that rolls up small enough to carry. The weaver ties one end to a tree and the other round her waist, and cloth grows between them. Spinning and weaving fill every spare hour in most households, because every tunic, sail and sack has to come from somewhere.
