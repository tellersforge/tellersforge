# Utility knife

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: stone tools · any*

A short, plain blade of stone, bronze or steel, often with a wooden or bone handle and a leather sheath. It cuts rope, carves meat, whittles pegs, opens letters and scrapes hides. Nearly everyone carries one, and nobody thinks of it as a weapon until they need it to be one.
