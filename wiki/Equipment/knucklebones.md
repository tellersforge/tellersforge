# Knucklebones

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: stone tools · any*

A handful of small ankle bones from sheep or goats, polished smooth by years of fingers. They are tossed up and caught on the back of the hand, or thrown to see which side lands up and scored accordingly. Children play for pebbles, soldiers for coin, and oracles for the favour of the gods. They are the oldest game in many camps.
