# False beard

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · any*

A beard of real hair, wool or fibre, fixed to the chin with gum or tied on with straps behind the ears. Rulers of old wore ritual beards as symbols of authority, and actors and spies wear them to change a face at a stroke. From across a room, a false beard can turn a young woman into an old man. Up close, a good eye can spot the edges and the glue.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +1 Disguise changing appearance
- **Penalty:** −1 Disguise under close inspection
- **Used for:** [Disguise](../Skills/disguise.md)
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** stage beard, ceremonial beard

## Not to be confused with

*[Disguise wig](disguise-wig.md)*, which changes the hair on the head; the false beard changes the chin and jaw.
