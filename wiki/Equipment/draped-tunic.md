# Draped tunic

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · city states*

A single rectangle of wool or linen folded around the body, pinned at each shoulder with brooches or pins and belted at the waist, leaving the arms bare. There is no sewing, no cutting, no tailoring at all: it goes on in a moment and comes off as fast. Citizens of hot city-states wear it at home, at work and in the gymnasium.
