# Running shoes

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: computing · modern*

Light laced shoes with a cushioned sole of foam and rubber, made for running and walking far on roads, pavements and tracks. They make long distances easier and faster, and they are comfortable enough to wear all day. The mesh uppers let air in and water too: in rain, puddles or wet grass, the wearer's feet soak through at once and stay cold.
