# White face paint

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: printing · imperial courts*

A thick white powder or paste smoothed over the face and neck, often with bright lips and painted brows. Courtiers wear it to show they never labour in the sun, and actors wear it so the audience can read their expressions by lamplight. At court and on stage it marks the wearer as refined. Among ordinary folk it seems strange and haughty, and old recipes made with lead slowly poisoned the wearer.
