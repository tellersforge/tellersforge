# Turnshoes

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · western kingdoms*

Thin-soled shoes of soft leather, sewn inside out and then turned the right way so the seams are hidden inside. They are light, flexible and quiet, and the wearer feels the ground through the sole. They are cheap to make and quick to mend. In the wet they let water straight in.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 Stealth quiet soles
- **Penalty:** −1 END in the wet
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** soft leather shoes
