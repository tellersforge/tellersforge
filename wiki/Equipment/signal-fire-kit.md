# Signal fire kit

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: fire · any*

A bundle of dry wood, a sack of damp green leaves, some oil and a heavy blanket. On a hilltop, the fire is built and lit, green leaves thrown on for thick white smoke by day, or oil for tall flames at night. A blanket held over the fire and snatched away breaks the smoke into puffs that can be counted. Chains of such fires have carried news across a kingdom in a single night.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Signals from high ground

- **Used for:** [Signals](../Skills/signals.md), [Fire-Making](../Skills/fire-making.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** fire · **Price:** 1 S

## Not to be confused with

*[Signal Flares](signal-flares.md)*, which burn brightly for a short time; the signal fire can be kept going and shaped into a message.
