# Advanced multi-scanner

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: far future · far future*

A slim, elegant device hardly thicker than a letter, which quietly takes in everything within a room's reach. It maps walls down to their studs, bodies down to their bones, and powders down to their molecules, then lays it all out as a shifting picture the user can turn with a finger. Investigators, surveyors and first-contact teams treat it as a second set of senses, and feel half blind the day it is lost.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Investigation analysing a scene; +2 Searching finding hidden or small things; +2 Physical Sciences identifying substances and energies
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Communications & Sensors](../Skills/communications-and-sensors.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 50 G
- **Also known as:** deep scanner

## Not to be confused with

the *[Handheld Scanner](handheld-scanner.md)*, the older and cruder all-purpose device that reads a scene but cannot map it in depth.
