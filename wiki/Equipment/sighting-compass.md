# Sighting compass

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A brass compass with a hinged lid, a narrow sighting slit and a small mirror set so the user can watch a distant peak and the needle at the same time. Surveyors, artillery officers and careful explorers use it to take exact bearings: two landmarks sighted, two lines drawn on the map, and where they cross is where you stand. It rewards patience and a steady hand, and punishes anyone who reads it upside down.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Wayfinding taking bearings; +1 Surveying quick bearings
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Wayfinding](../Skills/wayfinding.md), [Surveying](../Skills/surveying.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** prismatic compass, lensatic compass

## Not to be confused with

the *[Dry Compass](dry-compass.md)*, which shows north well enough but can't take a precise bearing on a landmark.
