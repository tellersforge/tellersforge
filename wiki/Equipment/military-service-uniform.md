# Military service uniform

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: steam · modern*

A tailored tunic and trousers in regimental colours, with rank badges, buttons polished bright and a belt and boots shined to a mirror. It is the soldier's formal dress for parades, barracks and duty in the streets. Among fellow soldiers it binds the unit together, and civilians tend to step aside. Among rebels or the enemy it makes the wearer a target, and in any crowd it stands out.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Intimidation over civilians; +1 Leadership among troops who share it
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA among the enemy or rebels; −1 Stealth in a crowd
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** dress uniform, service dress

## Not to be confused with

*[Field combat uniform](field-combat-uniform.md)*, the drab working clothes of the same soldier; the service uniform is for show and authority, not for weeks in the mud.
