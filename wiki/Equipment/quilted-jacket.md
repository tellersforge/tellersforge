# Quilted jacket

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · any*

A jacket built up from many layers of linen or cotton, stitched through in rows so the stuffing cannot shift. It is the soldier's armour when he cannot afford any other, and it is better than it looks: the layers soak up a blow and catch the edge of a blade. It can be worn alone or under mail. In summer it is a sweating misery, and it takes days to dry after rain.

## Game block

- **Slot:** torso
- **Bonus:** +1 armour against blows and cuts
- **Penalty:** −1 END in heat
