# Kohl

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · river kingdoms*

A dark mineral powder or paste kept in a small pot or tube, applied along the eyelids and lashes with a slim stick. In the river kingdoms and the deserts, both men and women wear it daily. It makes the eyes look large and striking, and it also cuts the glare of the sun on sand and water. It is a small thing, and it is found in nearly every grave.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +1 CHA in desert and river-kingdom society; +1 END against sun glare
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Grooming & Style](../Skills/grooming-and-style.md)
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** eye paint, antimony liner, surma

## Not to be confused with

*[Cosmetics case](cosmetics-case.md)*, a full kit of powders and colours; kohl is the single eye paint.
