# Slate and chalk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: printing · any*

A thin slab of dark slate in a wooden frame and a few sticks of white chalk. Writing wipes away with a rag or a sleeve, so it can be used again and again. Schoolrooms use them by the dozen, and teachers write the day's lesson on a large one on the wall. Tavern keepers chalk up debts on them, and wipe them clean only when paid.
