# Divination cards

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: printing · any*

A deck of painted picture cards, each bearing a symbol, a figure or a scene: the tower, the traveller, the moon. The reader lays them out in a pattern and reads the meaning from their order and position. A good reader watches the face of the person across the table as closely as the cards, and learns a great deal from both.
