# Hand sledge

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

A low, narrow sled of wooden or bone runners and a slatted bed, pulled by a single person on a harness across their chest. It carries more over snow and ice than anyone could carry on their back: tent, fuel, food, a day's catch. Hunters on the sea ice and explorers on long journeys pull them for weeks. On soft snow, it sinks and drags.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Lifting & Carrying over snow or ice
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Snow & Ice Travel](../Skills/snow-and-ice-travel.md)
- **Weight:** 8 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** pulk, toboggan

## Not to be confused with

*[Dog Sled](dog-sled.md)*, which is larger and drawn by a team of dogs; the hand sledge is pulled by its owner.
