# Power drill

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: electricity · modern*

A hand-held electric motor with a chuck for drill bits and screwdriver heads. It bores holes in wood, masonry and metal in seconds and drives screws faster than anyone can with a hand tool. Builders, mechanics and handy householders all rely on one. It needs power or a charged battery, and its whine is audible several rooms away.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Construction any
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth while in use
- **Used for:** [Hand Tools](../Skills/hand-tools.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** cordless drill

## Not to be confused with

the *[Hand Drill](hand-drill.md)*, which does the same job by muscle power, slowly and silently.
