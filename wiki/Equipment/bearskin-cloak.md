# Bearskin cloak

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

The pelt of a bear, wolf or great cat, taken whole, with the head worn as a hood so the beast's jaws frame the warrior's face. It is warm, it is heavy, and it announces that the wearer killed something most people run from. Enemies notice it first. The weight drags at the shoulders, the fur catches on branches, and nobody sneaks anywhere dressed as a bear.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +2 END against cold; +1 Intimidation worn by a warrior
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth heavy and rustling; −1 Acrobatics heavy pelt
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 6 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 6 S
- **Also known as:** wolf pelt, lion pelt

## Not to be confused with

*[Hide Cloak](hide-cloak.md)*, a plain scraped skin worn for warmth alone, lighter and quieter and impressing no one.
