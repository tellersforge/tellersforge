# Remote beast collar

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: computing · modern*

A sturdy collar fitted with a locator and a small receiver that sounds a tone, buzzes or gives a mild shock at the press of a button on the handler's remote. Search teams, herders and security handlers use it to direct a dog across open ground and to find it again if it runs off. Animals learn its signals quickly. Some handlers swear by it; others think a dog should come because it wants to.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Animal Handling directing an animal; +1 Tracking finding a tagged animal
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Animal Handling](../Skills/animal-handling.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** shock collar, tracking collar

## Not to be confused with

*[Neural Beast Collar](neural-beast-collar.md)*, which speaks straight to the animal's brain; the remote collar only signals and tracks, and the animal must still be trained.
