# Hand drill

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: stone tools · any*

A wooden shaft with a stone, bone or metal point, spun by a bowstring wrapped round it or by a pump weight that bobs up and down. It bores holes through wood, bone, shell and soft stone, and with a bit of tinder it can also start a fire. It is slow but steady work, and a good one has been in the same family for generations.
