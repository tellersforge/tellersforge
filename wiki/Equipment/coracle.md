# Coracle

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: stone tools · river peoples*

A small, round, bowl-shaped boat made of hide or tarred cloth stretched over a frame of woven wicker. It holds one person and a little cargo and is paddled with a single paddle, sculling from the front. It is so light that its owner carries it home on their back. On calm water it is handy; in a current or a breeze it spins helplessly.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Rowing & Paddling calm water
- **Penalty:** −1 Rowing & Paddling currents and wind
- **Used for:** [Rowing & Paddling](../Skills/rowing-and-paddling.md)
- **Weight:** 15 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 3 S
