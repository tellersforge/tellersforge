# Casting bones

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: stone tools · any*

A leather bag of small bones, shells, stones and odd carved pieces, each with its own meaning. The diviner shakes the bag and throws them onto a mat, then reads how they fall: which lies near which, which points where, which is face up. The pattern tells of illness, enemies, journeys or luck. Every diviner's set is unique, and never lent.
