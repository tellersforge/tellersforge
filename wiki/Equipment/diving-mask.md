# Diving mask

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: electricity · modern*

A glass or clear plastic plate set in a soft rubber skirt that seals over the eyes and nose. Underwater, the naked eye sees only blur; behind the mask, the world snaps into focus, colourful and strange. It is the first thing a diver buys and the last thing they forget. Pearl divers and spear fishers have used simpler versions of wood and glass for centuries.
