# Sheer silk gown

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: writing · wealthy households*

A long, clinging gown of silk gauze so fine it floats, falling in layers that reveal as much as they hide. In wealthy households it is worn for private evenings, feasts and quiet meetings where one wants to be noticed and remembered. At a temple or before a war council it is an insult, and it is no use at all against a chill.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 Charm & Seduction at a private meeting; +1 Carousing at a feast
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA at a temple or war council; −1 END against cold
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** gauze robe

## Not to be confused with

*[Low-Cut Bodice Gown](low-cut-bodice-gown.md)*, the tailored evening gown of western balls, which charms by shape and style rather than transparency.
