# Sandglass

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: iron · any*

Two bulbs of glass joined by a narrow waist and held in a wooden frame. Turned over, the sand trickles from top to bottom in a set time, then someone turns it again. Ships' watches, sermons, lessons and boiled eggs are all measured by it. It can't tell the hour of day, only how long something has taken, and it only works if someone remembers to turn it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Counting & Measuring timing intervals
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Sea Navigation](../Skills/sea-navigation.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** hourglass, minute glass

## Not to be confused with

the *[Water Clock](water-clock.md)*, which runs continuously through the hours rather than measuring one fixed span.
