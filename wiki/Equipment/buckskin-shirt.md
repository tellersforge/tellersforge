# Buckskin shirt

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

A soft shirt of deerskin tanned with brains and smoked until it is supple and brown. Long fringes hang from the sleeves and seams, and they carry rain off the body drop by drop. It is quiet in brush where cloth would scratch and rustle, and it keeps brambles and thorns off the arms. Hunters of the deep woods treasure a good one.
