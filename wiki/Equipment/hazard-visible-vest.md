# Hazard-visible vest

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: computing · modern*

A sleeveless vest in fluorescent yellow or orange, crossed with strips of reflective tape that blaze in headlights. Road crews, rail workers and site staff wear it so drivers and machines see them. The strange side of it is well known: a person in a bright vest with a purposeful walk is taken for staff almost anywhere, and waved through doors and gates. It is, of course, the worst possible thing to wear while hiding.
