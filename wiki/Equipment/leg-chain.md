# Leg chain

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: iron · any*

A pair of iron cuffs locked around the ankles, joined by a chain short enough that a prisoner can shuffle and work but never run. Galleys, quarries and chain gangs are built around them. The chain drags and clanks, marking the wearer wherever they go. Escape means a file, a smith, or a friend with a key, and a great deal of luck.
