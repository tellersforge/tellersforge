# Life-sign detector

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A boxy detector with a sweeping antenna that counts heartbeats, breath and body heat within its reach, and shows each one as a small warm point of light. Rescue teams use it to find people trapped in rubble; soldiers use it to count who is waiting behind a wall. It is honest but not wise. A friend and an enemy, a child and a large dog, look very much the same to it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Searching finding living beings
- **Penalty:** −1 Searching telling friend from foe
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** spaceflight · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** bio-sign detector

## Not to be confused with

the *[Motion Tracker](motion-tracker.md)*, which sees only what moves; the life-sign detector finds the living even when they hold still.
