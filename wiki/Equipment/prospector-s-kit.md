# Prospector's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A shallow pan for washing gravel, a rock hammer, small bottles of acid, a lens and a notebook, all fitting in a saddlebag. A prospector wanders the hills tapping rocks, panning streams and squinting at the glitter. Most find nothing. A few find fortunes, and then the miners and the claim-jumpers arrive.
