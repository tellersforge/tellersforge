# Cartographer's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: printing · any*

A case of drawing pens, inks, rulers, dividers and rolled sheets of paper or vellum. A cartographer uses it to draw maps from surveys, travellers' tales and his own journeys. Good maps are precious and dangerous, because they show armies where to go and merchants where the money is. Many a cartographer has been accused of spying.
