# Tinned food

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

Cooked meat, beans, fruit or soup sealed in a tin can and heated to keep it safe for years. It changed how armies, explorers and cities ate. It is heavy and needs an opener or a knife and some patience, but inside the tin, dinner waits unchanged.
