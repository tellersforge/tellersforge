# Fuel cell

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A quiet, compact box that turns stored fuel into electricity through a slow chemical reaction, with only clean water dripping out as waste. It hums softly and gives steady power for hours or days. Scientists, soldiers and remote stations like it because it makes no smoke and very little noise. It needs its own special fuel, which can be hard to find.
