# Belt pouch

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: stone tools · any*

A small leather or cloth pouch on a drawstring, hung from the belt. It holds the handful of things a person wants within reach: coins, flints, tinder, a fishhook, a lucky charm. Almost everyone in every age has worn one, and every cutpurse knows exactly where it hangs.
