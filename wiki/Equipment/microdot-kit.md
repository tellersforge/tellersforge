# Microdot kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: electricity · modern*

A small case holding a special camera, reducing lenses, chemicals and fine tools. It shrinks a whole photographed page to a dot no bigger than a full stop, which can be hidden on a letter, under a stamp or in the eye of a painted flower. Spies use it to send plans and lists through the ordinary post. Reading it back takes a microscope and knowing where to look.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Smuggling & Concealment hiding messages
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Tradecraft](../Skills/tradecraft.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** micro-photo kit

## Not to be confused with

*[Codebook](codebook.md)*, which disguises what a message says; the microdot kit hides that there is a message at all.
