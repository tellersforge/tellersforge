# Plumed hat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: gunpowder · western kingdoms*

A broad-brimmed felt hat, often cocked up on one side, with a great sweeping ostrich plume. It is the crown of musketeers, duellists and swaggering courtiers, and it makes a flourish of every bow. Among soldiers it earns respect, and in a tavern or on a duelling ground it lends the wearer's sharp tongue a certain authority. It is not a hat for skulking.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +1 CHA among soldiers and gallants; +1 Wit & Mockery at a duel or tavern
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth while worn
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.4 kg · **Needs:** gunpowder · **Price:** 15 S
