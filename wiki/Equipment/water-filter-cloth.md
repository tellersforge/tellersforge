# Water filter cloth

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: agriculture · any*

A square of clean, tightly woven cloth through which drinking water is poured into a jar or a cupped hand. It strains out grit, weed, tiny worms and the water fleas that carry sickness, and it can be washed and dried on a rock. It does nothing against the invisible sickness in foul water, but it is better than drinking a pond as it comes. Some travellers wear it as a headscarf between uses.
