# Smart tattoo

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A tattoo of programmable pigment that can shift from one design to another, scroll a line of text, or fade until the skin looks bare. Spacers use them for identification, lovers for private messages, and people with something to hide for obvious reasons. A distinctive marking that vanishes on command makes it much harder for witnesses to describe who they saw.
