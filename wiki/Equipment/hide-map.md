# Hide map

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: stone tools · any*

A tanned hide, scratched or painted with charcoal and ochre, showing rivers, trails, springs, mountains and landmarks. Distances are rough and the scale changes, but the important things are there, and the traveller who carries one can find the way through land others have walked. It can be rolled up and carried, or pegged out beside a fire to plan a journey.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Wayfinding in mapped land
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Geography](../Skills/geography.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** painted skin map

## Not to be confused with

*[Printed Map](printed-map.md)*, which is accurate, detailed and to scale; the hide map is a rough sketch of known landmarks.
