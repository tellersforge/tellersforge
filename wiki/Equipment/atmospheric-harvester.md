# Atmospheric harvester

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: far future · far future*

A folding device of fins and cold plates that draws moisture from the air and drips it into a reservoir, even in air so dry that lips crack. It hums quietly all night and by morning there is enough to drink for the day. Desert travellers of the far future carry one and are rarely thirsty. Scavengers covet them, and caravans that carry several are never short of friends.
