# Messenger bag

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: electricity · modern*

A tough shoulder bag with a wide flap and a strap cut to sit high on the back, made for couriers who ride through traffic all day. It swings round to the front for quick access and back again out of the way. Students, clerks and cyclists all carry one, and it is always slightly too full.
