# Hand quern

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: agriculture · any*

Two round stones, one lying flat, one on top with a hole in the middle and a wooden peg for a handle. Grain is fed into the hole and the top stone turned, round and round, until flour spills from the edges. It is slow, back-aching work done every day in every farming household, usually by the women and the old. The flour always has a little grit in it, and the grinder's arms are always strong.
