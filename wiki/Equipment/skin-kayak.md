# Skin kayak

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: stone tools · arctic peoples*

A long, slim boat of seal or other hide stretched over a light frame, closed over the top except for a hole where the paddler sits. It is fast, seaworthy and built for the icy open sea, where a skilled hunter can roll it upright after a capsize. Its narrow hull is tippy, though, and getting in and out without falling into freezing water is a skill in itself.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Rowing & Paddling open cold water
- **Penalty:** −1 Balance getting in or out
- **Used for:** [Rowing & Paddling](../Skills/rowing-and-paddling.md), [Hunting](../Skills/hunting.md)
- **Weight:** 18 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** hunting kayak

## Not to be confused with

*[Bark Canoe](bark-canoe.md)*, which is open and roomy and made for calmer rivers and lakes.
