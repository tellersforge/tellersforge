# Gas detector

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: computing · modern*

A small meter the size of a deck of cards, clipped to a collar or a belt, that sniffs the air all day and says nothing until something is wrong. Then it shrieks and flashes. It knows the gases that choke, the gases that burn and the quiet absence of air that simply lets a person fall asleep and not wake. Miners, sewer crews and tank cleaners wear them, and learn to hate the false alarms and trust every one of them.
