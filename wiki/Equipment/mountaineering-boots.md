# Mountaineering boots

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · modern*

Stiff, high boots of leather or plastic, insulated against cold and fitted with a sturdy welt so crampons can be clamped on. Climbers wear them on glaciers, snowfields and icy ridges, where a soft boot would slip or freeze. They grip hard snow and ice and keep toes alive in the cold. They are heavy and loud on hard floors, and walking any distance on flat ground in them is a chore.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 Mountaineering on snow and ice; +1 END against cold feet
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth on hard floors; −1 Running while worn
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** climbing boots, crampon boots

## Not to be confused with

*[Crampons](crampons.md)*, the steel spikes clamped onto these boots; the boots alone give warmth and a firm platform, the crampons give bite on ice.
