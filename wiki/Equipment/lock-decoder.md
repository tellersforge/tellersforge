# Lock decoder

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: far future · far future*

A smooth device no larger than a palm, pressed against a door panel or a lock housing. It listens to the lock's circuits, tries possibilities faster than any mind can count, and a moment later the light turns and the door slides aside. On far-future ships and stations, where almost every door is electronic, it opens a great deal. Security officers regard owning one much as earlier ages regarded carrying a crowbar at midnight.
