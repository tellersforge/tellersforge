# Travel trunk

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A large trunk of wood and canvas with metal corners, strong locks and handles at each end, packed with everything a traveller of means brings on a long voyage. It goes by wagon, steamer and railway, heaved about by porters, and arrives battered but whole. No one carries one alone.
