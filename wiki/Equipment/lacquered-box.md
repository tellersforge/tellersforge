# Lacquered box

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: writing · eastern empire*

A light wooden box coated with many layers of lacquer, each dried and polished before the next, until it is glossy, hard and sealed against damp and insects. In the eastern empire such boxes hold documents, brushes, jewellery and tea. Fine ones are painted or inlaid and are gifts in their own right.
