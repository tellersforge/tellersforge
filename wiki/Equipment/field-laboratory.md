# Field laboratory

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: computing · modern*

A stack of rugged cases that open into a working lab: analysers, a microscope, sample kits and sealed reagents. Investigators use one to test blood, soil, water and fibres at the scene instead of waiting weeks for results. It needs power and a clean surface, and it is too heavy to carry far on foot, but it answers questions while the trail is still warm.
