# Singing bowl

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: bronze · mountain monasteries*

A thick bronze bowl, hand-hammered and heavy, that sings when a wooden stick is rubbed around its rim or when it is struck gently. The note swells and hangs in the air long after the sound should have died. Mountain monks use it to begin and end meditation, and anyone listening closely finds their breathing slow and their thoughts settle.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Meditation & Concentration settling the mind
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Meditation & Concentration](../Skills/meditation-and-concentration.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** standing bell

## Not to be confused with

*[Gong](gong.md)*, a great bronze disc struck to make a loud, announcing crash, where the bowl gives a quiet, steady tone.
