# Canary cage

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A little wicker or wire cage with a yellow songbird inside, hung from a hook at the end of the tunnel where the miners are working. The bird breathes faster than a man and feels bad air sooner. While it sings and hops, the air is good. When it goes quiet and fluffs up, sensible miners walk out; when it drops from its perch, they run. Crews grow fond of their birds, and the best of them are carried out first.
