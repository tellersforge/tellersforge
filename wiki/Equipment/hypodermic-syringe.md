# Hypodermic syringe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: steam · any*

A slim barrel of glass or later plastic, a plunger and a hollow steel needle, for putting medicine under the skin or straight into a vein. It made modern medicine possible, delivering drugs quickly and in exact doses. It also made new dangers possible, and in the wrong hands it delivers poison as easily as a cure. Needles must be boiled or thrown away, or they spread sickness from one patient to the next.
