# Stage make-up kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: steam · modern*

A box of greasepaints, powders, nose putty, spirit gum, crepe hair and brushes, used by actors to build a new face for each role. A skilled artist can add twenty years, a broken nose or a new jaw, and make the change hold up under stage lights. Spies and swindlers have found the same kit just as useful off the stage.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Disguise altering the face; +1 Acting on stage
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Disguise](../Skills/disguise.md), [Acting](../Skills/acting.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** greasepaint kit, theatrical make-up

## Not to be confused with

*[Cosmetics case](cosmetics-case.md)*, which makes a face look its best; the stage make-up kit makes a face look like someone else's.
