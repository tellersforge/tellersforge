# Notched pole ladder

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: stone tools · any*

A single log or stout pole with steps chopped into its side, leaned against a wall, a cliff, a storage platform or a rooftop entrance. It is the oldest ladder there is. Climbing it is easy for those who grew up with it and alarming for everyone else. It is also easy to pull up after you, leaving intruders standing below.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing short walls
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md)
- **Weight:** 6 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** notched log

## Not to be confused with

*[Rope Ladder](rope-ladder.md)*, which rolls up for carrying; the notched pole is solid, heavy and made on the spot.
