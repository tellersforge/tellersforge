# Homespun clothes

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: agriculture · farming villages*

Plain garments of undyed or plant-dyed cloth, spun and woven at home and cut to last. They are hard-wearing, easily mended and utterly unremarkable, which is their hidden virtue: a person in homespun disappears among a market crowd of farmers. The same clothes at court make every head turn in disdain.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Disguise blending in among commoners
- **Penalty:** −1 Court Etiquette & Protocol at court
- **Used for:** —
