# Brush and ink stone

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: writing · eastern empire*

A set of fine brushes of animal hair, a solid stick of pressed soot ink, and a flat stone with a shallow well. A few drops of water and slow circles of the stick grind fresh ink, dark or pale as the writer chooses. The brush writes flowing characters and paints pictures with the same stroke. In the lands where it is used, writing well is a mark of the educated and an art in itself.

- **Bonus:** +1 Calligraphy & Scribing brush scripts

## Not to be confused with

*[Ink and Quills](ink-and-quills.md)*, which write with a cut nib from ready-made ink; the brush set grinds its own ink and paints the letters.
