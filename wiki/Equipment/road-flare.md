# Road flare

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: electricity · modern*

A stick that burns with a fierce red flame and thick smoke for a quarter of an hour, seen for miles on a dark road. Drivers set them out to warn of a wreck, and rescuers wave them to be found. Animals keep their distance. Anyone trying to stay hidden should not light one.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Intimidation against animals
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth while lit
- **Used for:** [Signals](../Skills/signals.md)
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** fusee, hand flare

## Not to be confused with

*[Signal flare pistol](signal-flare-pistol.md)*, which throws a flare high into the sky rather than burning on the ground.
