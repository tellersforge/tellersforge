# Water recycler

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: spaceflight · space-faring*

A boxy unit of pumps, membranes and filters, part of a ship's systems or carried on a crawler, that takes in sweat, urine and grey wash-water and returns clean drinking water. The water tastes of nothing and nobody talks about where it has been. On a long voyage or in a sealed habitat, it is the thing everyone knows the location of. When it fails, rationing begins that same hour.
