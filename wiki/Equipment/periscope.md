# Periscope

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: electricity · industrial cities*

A tall tube with angled mirrors at the top and bottom, letting the user look over a wall, a trench parapet or the surface of the water while staying safely hidden. Soldiers in trenches and submarine crews rely on them. The view is narrow and flat, though, and the user sees little beyond what the tube is pointed at.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Shadowing & Surveillance watching from cover; +1 Stealth looking out without showing yourself
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception narrow field of view
