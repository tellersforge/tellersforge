# Storm matches

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: electricity · modern*

Long matches with a thick coating that burns fiercely, spits and refuses to go out even in a gale, or after being dunked in water and pulled out again. Sailors, mountaineers and survival kits carry them for the one night when nothing else will work.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Fire-Making in wind or rain
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Fire-Making](../Skills/fire-making.md)
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 2 C
- **Also known as:** lifeboat matches

## Not to be confused with

*[Matches](matches.md)*, the ordinary kind, cheap and handy but easily beaten by wind and damp.
