# Bark cloth wrap

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

Inner bark stripped from certain trees, soaked, and beaten out with grooved mallets until it is broad, thin and soft as cloth. It is wrapped around the body and often painted with bold black and red patterns. In steamy forests it is cool and airy. In a downpour it soaks through, goes limp and begins to tear.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 END in humid heat
- **Penalty:** −1 END in rain (it soaks and tears)
