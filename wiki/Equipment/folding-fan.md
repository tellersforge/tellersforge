# Folding fan

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: printing · eastern empire*

A pleated fan of paper or silk on slender ribs of bamboo, wood or ivory, opening with a flick of the wrist. At court, a fan speaks a silent language: half-opened, snapped shut, held to the lips, each movement carries a message. It also cools the wearer on hot days, and courtiers of the eastern empire rarely go without one.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Court Etiquette & Protocol signalling at court; +1 END against heat
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Sign Language & Gesture](../Skills/sign-language-and-gesture.md)
- **Weight:** 0.1 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** court fan, hand fan

## Not to be confused with

*[Feather fan](feather-fan.md)*, a large fan carried for dignitaries; the folding fan is small, personal and used to signal.
