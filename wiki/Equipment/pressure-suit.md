# Pressure suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Environment and survival gear · Needs: computing · modern*

A tight, sealed flight suit of layered fabric and rubber, with a helmet and gloves locked on at the neck and wrists. High above the clouds, where the air is too thin to breathe and blood would boil, it swells and holds the pilot's body together. Test pilots and high-altitude flyers wear them, strapped into cramped cockpits. Once inflated, the suit is stiff and every movement becomes a struggle.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +2 END at extreme altitude
- **Penalty:** −2 DEX stiff when inflated
- **Used for:** [Environment Suits](../Skills/environment-suits.md)
- **Weight:** 12 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 40 S
- **Also known as:** high-altitude suit

## Not to be confused with

the *[Environment Suit](environment-suit.md)*, which protects against hostile air on the ground but cannot keep its wearer alive in near-vacuum.
