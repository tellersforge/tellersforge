# Linen kilt

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: copper · river kingdoms*

A short skirt of fine white linen wrapped around the hips and tied at the waist. Labourers wear a plain one. The rich wear linen so fine it is almost sheer, pressed into crisp pleats that take a servant a morning to set. It is perfect in the dry heat of a river valley and worth very little when the night turns cold.
