# Leather belt

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: bronze · any*

A strap of stout leather with a buckle or a ring, holding up trousers or cinching in a tunic. Its real work is carrying: a knife, a purse, a pouch of flint and steel, a water skin, a hatchet, a set of keys. A traveller with a good belt has both hands free and everything they need within reach.

- **Slot:** torso
- **Bonus:** +1 Lifting & Carrying hanging pouches and tools
- **Penalty:** 0
