# Silk rope

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: writing · imperial courts*

Rope twisted from strands of silk: thin, smooth, pale and far stronger than its weight suggests. A long coil folds into a bundle that fits inside a sleeve or wraps round the waist under a sash. Thieves, spies and escaping prisoners have always prized it. It is costly, and it slips in knots unless they are tied with care.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Smuggling & Concealment hiding a coil
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Knots & Rope Work](../Skills/knots-and-rope-work.md), [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** silk cord
