# Hacking deck

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: computing · modern*

A battered portable computer, often built into a small case and covered in stickers and scratches, loaded with software for breaking into networks and systems. Its owner knows every button and trick by heart. In the right hands, it can open locked doors, read secret records and turn off alarms from across a city. Carrying one draws suspicion; being caught with one is usually worse.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Hacking breaking into systems
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Hacking](../Skills/hacking.md)
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 25 S
- **Also known as:** intrusion deck, cyberdeck

## Not to be confused with

the *[Data Spike](data-spike.md)*, which delivers a single pre-loaded attack through a port; the deck is a full workshop for breaking in.
