# Robotics kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: computing · modern*

A hard case of fine tools, spare servos, sensors, circuit boards and a diagnostic tablet that talks to the robot's brain. With it a technician can mend a drone's broken rotor, recalibrate a robot's arm or coax a faulty machine into obeying orders. Robots are patient patients but deeply unhelpful ones, and they never describe their symptoms.
