# Stirrups

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · any*

A pair of loops of iron, wood or leather hung from the saddle on straps, so the rider can brace their feet. They make mounting easier, long rides less tiring and fighting from horseback far more effective, because a rider can stand, lean and strike without falling off. Such a simple idea changed warfare for centuries. Riders who get a foot caught in one when they fall rarely forget it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Mounted Combat any; +1 Riding staying seated
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Riding](../Skills/riding.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** footrests

## Not to be confused with

the *[War Saddle](war-saddle.md)*, which grips the rider's hips but does nothing for the feet.
