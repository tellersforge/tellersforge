# Pad saddle

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: agriculture · steppe riders*

A thick cushion of felt, leather or stuffed hide, girthed onto a horse's back with no rigid frame and no stirrups. The steppe peoples who first rode long distances used these, and they are comfortable for horse and rider over a long day. They offer little to brace against, so the rider stays on by balance and grip, and a sudden shy of the horse can unseat anyone.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Riding long rides
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Riding](../Skills/riding.md)
- **Weight:** 3 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** riding pad
