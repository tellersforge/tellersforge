# Bookbinder's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: printing · any*

A small wooden press, a sewing frame, awls, needles, strong thread, glue, boards and leather. With them a binder gathers loose pages into books that last for centuries. Binders are careful, quiet craftspeople who sometimes read what they bind and sometimes wish they hadn't. A good binding outlives the author, the reader and often the kingdom.
