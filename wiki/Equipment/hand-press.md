# Hand press

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: printing · any*

A small iron screw press on a wooden frame, with cases of metal type, ink, rollers and a stack of paper. With it, a printer can turn out hundreds of handbills, pamphlets or broadsheets in a night. Rebels, preachers and pamphleteers have used such presses to spread ideas that the powerful would rather suppress. It is heavy, noisy and smells of ink, and it is almost impossible to hide when the authorities come looking.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Media Production runs of pamphlets
- **Penalty:** −2 Smuggling & Concealment hiding it
- **Used for:** [Media Production](../Skills/media-production.md), [Bookcraft](../Skills/bookcraft.md)
- **Weight:** 40 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 40 G
- **Also known as:** portable printing press

## Not to be confused with

*[Typewriter](typewriter.md)*, which writes one copy at a time; the hand press prints many identical copies for distribution.
