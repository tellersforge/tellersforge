# Wedding dress

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: bronze · any*

The finest garment a family can make, borrow or buy, for a single day: often the brightest colour the family can afford, sometimes pure white, sometimes red for luck, embroidered or beaded, and sometimes passed down through generations of brides. For that day, the bride is the one everyone watches, and no other outfit could make her more noticeable.
