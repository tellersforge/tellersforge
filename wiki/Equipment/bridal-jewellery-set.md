# Bridal jewellery set

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: iron · any*

A full matched set of necklaces, earrings, bangles, rings and headpieces, heavy with gold and gems. In many lands it is the bride's own wealth, a family fortune she can carry away on her body if the marriage fails. At a wedding it makes the wearer glow. Anywhere else it clinks with every step and draws every thief within a mile.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +2 CHA at a wedding or feast
- **Penalty:** −2 Streetwise draws thieves; −2 Stealth clinks when moving
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 80 G
- **Also known as:** wedding jewels, dowry jewels

## Not to be confused with

*[Coin headdress](coin-headdress.md)*, which also turns savings into ornament but is worn by dancers and at festivals.
