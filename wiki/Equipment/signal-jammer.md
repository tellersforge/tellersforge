# Signal jammer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: computing · modern*

A dull box with a few stubby antennas, which fills the air around it with a wall of radio noise. Phones fall silent, radios hiss, and remote-controlled devices stop answering. Soldiers use it to cut off enemy messages; thieves use it to stop alarms calling for help. The trouble is that a jammer is loud in its own way, and anyone with the right tools can follow the noise back to its source.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Electronic Warfare blocking radio and signals nearby
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth its noise can be traced
- **Used for:** [Electronic Warfare](../Skills/electronic-warfare.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** jammer

## Not to be confused with

the *[Radio Set](radio-set.md)*, which sends and receives messages; the jammer only drowns them out.
