# Wrap skirt

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: agriculture · island peoples*

A length of woven cloth, sometimes sewn into a tube, wrapped around the waist and tucked in at the side or front. Men and women wear it alike on warm islands and coasts. It is cool in the damp heat, dries fast after rain or a swim, and can be refolded into a sling, a headcloth or a bag in a moment.
