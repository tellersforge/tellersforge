# Wooden pin lock

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: writing · river kingdoms*

A heavy wooden bolt sliding through a wooden housing on a door, held shut by pegs that drop into holes in the bolt. The key is a long wooden stick with pegs of its own, pushed in to lift the pins and let the bolt slide free. It is one of the oldest locks known, and it secures granaries and storehouses in lands without iron. It is big and clumsy but surprisingly hard to pick.
