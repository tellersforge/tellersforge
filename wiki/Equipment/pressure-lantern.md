# Pressure lantern

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: electricity · modern*

A lantern whose paraffin or gas is pumped under pressure through a white mantle, which glows so brightly it lights a whole camp or workshop. It hisses constantly and throws a hard glare that can be seen from far off. It is excellent for working and useless for hiding.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Perception lighting a whole camp
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth hissing and glare
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** mantle lantern

## Not to be confused with

*[Oil lantern](oil-lantern.md)*, whose simpler wick gives less light but does not hiss or blaze.
