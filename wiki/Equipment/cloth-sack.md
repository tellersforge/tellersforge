# Cloth sack

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: agriculture · any*

A simple woven sack of hemp, jute or linen, tied at the neck. Farmers carry grain in it, millers flour, thieves loot, and every port in the world is stacked with them. It is cheap, light when empty, and good for anything that will not leak. It is a poor home for anything sharp, fragile or wet.
