# Combination lock

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Security and thievery · Needs: printing · any*

A lock with no keyhole at all: a row of lettered rings or a numbered dial that must be turned to the right sequence before the shackle lets go. Merchants like it because there is no key to lose, steal or copy, only a word or a string of figures to keep in the head. Travellers like it on trunks and chests. Its weakness is the owner, who writes the combination inside a hat or picks a word anyone could guess.

*[Padlock](padlock.md)*, which hangs from a hasp in the same way but opens with a key, and so can be picked where a combination lock must be puzzled out.
