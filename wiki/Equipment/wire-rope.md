# Wire rope

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

Cable of many steel wires twisted into strands and the strands twisted together. It holds weights that would snap any fibre rope, hauls ships, lifts engines and hangs bridges. It is heavy, stiff and springy, it bites through gloves when it frays, and it refuses almost every knot a sailor knows. It is fixed with clamps, thimbles and shackles instead.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Lifting & Carrying hauling heavy loads
- **Penalty:** −1 Knots & Rope Work tying knots
- **Used for:** [Cargo Handling](../Skills/cargo-handling.md), [Heavy Machinery](../Skills/heavy-machinery.md)
- **Weight:** 6 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** steel cable

## Not to be confused with

*[Hemp Rope](hemp-rope.md)*, which ties easily and is kind to the hands, but will never haul what wire can.
