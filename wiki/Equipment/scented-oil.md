# Scented oil

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: agriculture · any*

A small jar of oil infused with herbs, flowers or resin, rubbed onto the skin and hair after bathing. In hot lands it softens sun-dried skin and hides the smell of sweat, and it is expected at social gatherings and feasts. It is milder than perfume, but animals can still scent a wearer approaching.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 CHA in hot climates at social events
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth against animals by scent
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** anointing oil, hair oil

## Not to be confused with

*[Perfume](perfume.md)*, which is stronger and carries farther; scented oil is gentler and mainly soothes the skin.
