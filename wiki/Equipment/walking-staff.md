# Walking staff

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: stone tools · any*

A straight staff of seasoned wood, shod with iron or left plain, reaching about to the shoulder. It steadies the walker on steep or broken ground, probes the depth of a ford or a bog, fends off dogs and, in hard times, serves as a stout club. Pilgrims, shepherds and old wanderers all carry one. After years of use it knows the owner's hand.
