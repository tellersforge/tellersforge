# Pemmican

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: stone tools · plains peoples*

Dried meat pounded to a powder and mixed with melted fat and dried berries, then pressed into dense cakes or packed into hide bags. It keeps for years and gives more strength per mouthful than almost anything else. The plains peoples made it, and every traveller in the cold who tries it understands why.
