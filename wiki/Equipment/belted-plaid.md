# Belted plaid

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · highland clans*

A great length of tartan wool, laid out on the ground, gathered into pleats, belted around the waist and thrown over the shoulder. By day it is a kilt and a cloak. By night the clansman unbuckles it and rolls himself up in it on the heather. It keeps out rain and cold in the hills, and it is the dress a mountain people are proud of.
