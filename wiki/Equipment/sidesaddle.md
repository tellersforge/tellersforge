# Sidesaddle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: printing · imperial courts*

A saddle built so that the rider sits with both legs on one side, hooked round padded horns. It lets a rider in long skirts ride with modesty and grace, which is what courts require. A skilled rider can hunt and jump in one, but it is harder to stay on and much harder to fall off safely. Ladies of rank ride this way to show that they can.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Etiquette formal rides
- **Penalty:** −1 Riding hard riding or jumps
- **Used for:** [Riding](../Skills/riding.md)
- **Weight:** 10 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 30 S
- **Also known as:** lady's saddle

## Not to be confused with

the *[War Saddle](war-saddle.md)*, which grips the rider astride for hard riding and fighting.
