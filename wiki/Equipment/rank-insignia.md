# Rank insignia

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · any*

Stars, bars, stripes, braid or a chain of office, worn on the shoulder, collar or breast to show a person's rank. Troops of the same force read it instantly and obey, and subordinates snap to attention. Enemies read it too, and a high rank makes the wearer a prize. It is hard to pass as someone else when the rank is sewn on.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +2 Leadership over troops of the same force; +1 Intimidation over subordinates
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA among the enemy; −1 Deception hard to pass as someone else
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** badge of office, shoulder boards

## Not to be confused with

*[Medals and decorations](medals-and-decorations.md)*, which mark deeds and honours; rank insignia mark position and authority.
