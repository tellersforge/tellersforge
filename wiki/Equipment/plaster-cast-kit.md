# Plaster cast kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

Rolls of gauze and a tin of plaster powder. Once the bone is set, the gauze is soaked in wet plaster and wound round the limb, and within the hour it hardens into a white shell that holds everything still. It is heavier and hotter than splints, and it itches terribly, but it lets a clean break heal straight. Friends traditionally write rude messages on it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Medicine healing clean fractures
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Medicine](../Skills/medicine.md), [First Aid](../Skills/first-aid.md)
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** casting bandages

## Not to be confused with

*[Bone-Setting Splints](bone-setting-splints.md)*, which bind slats round the limb; the cast wraps it in a hard shell that holds far better.
