# Camp stove

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Food, water and cooking · Needs: electricity · modern*

A compact burner that runs on paraffin, spirit or a screw-in canister of gas, with folding legs to hold a pot. It lights with a hiss and a blue flame and boils a pot of water in the time it takes to unroll a bed. Where no fire can be lit, on bare rock, in a snowfield, in a shelter or where smoke would give a camp away, it is the difference between a hot meal and a cold one. The fuel runs out, of course.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Cooking where no fire can be lit
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Cooking](../Skills/cooking.md)
- **Weight:** 0.8 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** pressure stove, gas stove

## Not to be confused with

*[Folding Stove](folding-stove.md)*, which burns fuel tablets and heats barely a mug; the camp stove cooks proper meals.
