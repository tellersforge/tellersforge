# Colour-shifting garment

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: far future · far future*

Clothing whose fibres change colour, pattern and even cut on command, reshaping from sober grey to rich court finery with a word or a gesture. Fashionable folk use it to keep up with the latest style without buying anything, and spies use it to change appearance on the run. At any fashionable gathering it shows the wearer is up to date.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Disguise quick changes; +1 CHA at fashionable gatherings
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Disguise](../Skills/disguise.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** chroma fabric, mood cloth

## Not to be confused with

*[Holographic disguise](holographic-disguise.md)*, which projects a false image over the whole person; the colour-shifting garment changes only the clothes themselves.
