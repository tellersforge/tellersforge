# Wool scarf

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · any*

A long strip of knitted or woven wool wound around the neck, and pulled up over the mouth and nose when the wind turns cruel. It weighs almost nothing, fits in a pocket, and on a cold hillside it makes more difference than any other single garment. Many are the gift of a mother, a sweetheart or a grandmother.

## Game block

- **Slot:** neck
- **Bonus:** +1 END against cold wind
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** muffler

## Not to be confused with

*[Shawl](shawl.md)*, a broader cloth draped around the shoulders rather than wound close about the throat.
