# Faction colours

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: iron · any*

A ribbon, armband, cockade, sash or scarf in the colours of a political party, noble house or street gang. Among supporters it is a password and a promise, and it makes them listen and follow. Worn in the wrong quarter or among the wrong people, it is an invitation to a beating or worse.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 Persuasion among the faction; +1 Leadership among its supporters
- **Penalty:** −3 CHA where it marks the wrong faction
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** favour, armband, cockade, sash

## Not to be confused with

*[Guild badge](guild-badge.md)*, which marks trade membership; faction colours mark allegiance in politics or the street.
