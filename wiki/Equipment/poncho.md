# Poncho

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: agriculture · highland peoples*

A woven blanket, often striped in bright colours, with a slit in the middle for the head. It hangs loose over whatever else is worn, keeps off wind and mountain rain, and doubles as a bedroll or a saddle cloth. Herders on high plateaus live in them. Its loose edges and corners catch on rocks and branches whenever the wearer has to scramble up anything.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
