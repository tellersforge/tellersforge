# Felt boots

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: iron · steppe riders*

Boots made in one piece from wool beaten and pressed into thick felt, with no seams for the wind to find. Steppe herders pull them on for the long winter, when the frost is hard and the snow squeaks underfoot. In that dry, bitter cold nothing keeps the toes alive better. Thaw is another matter: in slush and meltwater the felt drinks the wet like a sponge, and the warmth goes out of it at once.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +2 END against dry cold
- **Penalty:** −1 END in wet slush
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** felted boots

## Not to be confused with

*[Fur boots](fur-boots.md)*, which are sewn from pelts, shed wet snow a little better, and are the hunter's choice rather than the herder's.
