# Rope ascenders

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: computing · modern*

A pair of handled clamps with toothed cams that slide up a fixed rope freely and lock when weight hangs from them. Clipped to the harness with foot loops, they let a climber inch up a hanging rope with steps rather than arm strength. Cavers climbing out of deep shafts and riggers on towers use them. They chew up muddy ropes.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Climbing climbing a fixed rope
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md), [Survival: Caves & Underground](../Skills/survival-caves-and-underground.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 4 S
- **Also known as:** jumars

## Not to be confused with

*[Belay Device](belay-device.md)*, which controls a descent or holds a partner; ascenders climb up a rope.
