# Hologram recorder

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: far future · far future*

A palm-sized device with a ring of tiny lenses that records a scene as a moving three-dimensional image. Played back, the recording rises from the device as a glowing miniature, or life-size in a room, which viewers can walk around. It records testimonies, weddings, briefings and last messages. Seeing a dead friend speak in full shape is comforting to some and deeply unsettling to others.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Media Production capturing scenes
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Media Production](../Skills/media-production.md), [Photography & Film](../Skills/photography-and-film.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 25 S
- **Also known as:** 3d recorder

## Not to be confused with

*[Holographic Projector](holographic-projector.md)*, which displays images; the recorder captures a scene so it can be played back later.
