# Oilskin coat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: steam · modern*

A long coat of cotton soaked in linseed oil until it is stiff and waterproof, usually worn with a broad-brimmed hat that pours water down the back. Fishermen, sailors and coach drivers wear it in the worst weather, and it keeps them dry while waves break over the rail. It is hot and clammy in warm weather, and it creaks and rustles with every step.

## Game block

- **Slot:** outer
- **Bonus:** +2 END against rain and sea spray
- **Penalty:** −1 END in heat; −1 Stealth rustles when moving
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 6 S
- **Also known as:** sou'wester coat, slicker

## Not to be confused with

*[Waterproof shell jacket](waterproof-shell-jacket.md)*, its modern, lighter successor that lets sweat escape; the oilskin keeps out water but traps the wearer's heat.
