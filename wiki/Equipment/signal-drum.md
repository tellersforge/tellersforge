# Signal drum

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Communication and signalling · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

A hollow log or tall skin drum whose different tones, beaten in patterns, imitate the rise and fall of speech. Drummers relay messages from village to village across forest and river, faster than any runner. A skilled drummer can announce a birth, a death, a meeting or a warning, and name the person concerned. Everyone within hearing knows something is happening, and those who understand the drum know exactly what.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Signals across forest and valleys
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth once played
- **Used for:** [Signals](../Skills/signals.md), [Instrument (per instrument)](../Skills/instrument-per-instrument.md)
- **Weight:** 6 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** talking drum, log drum

## Not to be confused with

*[Hand Drum](hand-drum.md)*, which is played for music; the signal drum carries coded messages over long distances.
