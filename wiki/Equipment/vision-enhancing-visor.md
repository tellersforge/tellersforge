# Vision-enhancing visor

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: far future · far future*

A light, curved visor that fits over the eyes and blends night vision, heat vision and zoom into one clear picture. It picks out warm bodies in darkness or smoke, magnifies distant details and scans a scene for anything out of place. Far-future soldiers and explorers wear it as a matter of course.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +3 Perception in darkness, smoke or at distance; +1 Searching scanning a scene
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 20 G
- **Also known as:** smart visor
