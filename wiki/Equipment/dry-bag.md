# Dry bag

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: computing · modern*

A roll-top bag of coated fabric that seals when the top is folded down several times and clipped. Paddlers, swimmers and divers trust their phones, papers and dry socks to it. It can be dunked, dragged through surf or left floating in a river and the inside stays dry. It also floats, which has saved more than one careless owner.
