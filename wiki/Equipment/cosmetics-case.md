# Cosmetics case

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · any*

A small box of wood, lacquer or leather holding powders, rouges, lip colour, eye paints and fine brushes, with a mirror set into the lid. It lets the owner turn themselves out beautifully wherever they are. A skilled hand can also alter the face with it: shadows to age a cheek, colour to hide a scar, a new mouth for a new name.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Grooming & Style with the case; +1 Disguise altering the face
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Grooming & Style](../Skills/grooming-and-style.md), [Disguise](../Skills/disguise.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** make-up box, vanity case

## Not to be confused with

*[Stage make-up kit](stage-make-up-kit.md)*, which is bolder and includes putty and false hair for building a whole new face.
