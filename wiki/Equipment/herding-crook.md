# Herding crook

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: agriculture · any*

A shepherd's staff as tall as its owner, with a broad hook at the top just wide enough to catch a sheep or goat by the neck or the hind leg. It is a walking stick on the hills, a lever on rough ground and a sign of the trade in every pastoral country. Shepherds lean on it for hours while the flock grazes. Bishops and kings have carried gilded copies to show that they, too, keep a flock.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Herding & Husbandry sheep and goats
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Herding & Husbandry](../Skills/herding-and-husbandry.md)
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** agriculture · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** shepherd's crook

## Not to be confused with

*[Walking Staff](walking-staff.md)*, which only helps a traveller walk; the crook also catches and steers animals.
