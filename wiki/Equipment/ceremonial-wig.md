# Ceremonial wig

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · river kingdoms*

A heavy wig of braided human hair or wool, sometimes dyed and beaded, worn over a shaved head by nobles and priests of the river kingdoms on formal days. It shows rank and elegance at court, and it also shields the bare scalp from the fierce sun. It is hot and heavy, but those who wear it rarely go out in the full sun without it.

## Game block

- **Slot:** head
- **Bonus:** +1 CHA at a river-kingdom court; +1 END against sun on a shaved head
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** braided wig

## Not to be confused with

*[Powdered wig](powdered-wig.md)*, the white, curled wig of western courts and law courts; the ceremonial wig is braided and belongs to the river kingdoms.
