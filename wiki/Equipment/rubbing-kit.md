# Rubbing kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: writing · any*

Rolls of thin, tough paper, sticks of dark wax crayon and lumps of charcoal. Pressed over a carved stone, a brass plate or an inscription and rubbed gently, the paper picks up every line and letter as a clean copy to study later. Scholars, antiquarians and treasure hunters carry one to ruins and graveyards. It works best on dry days, and badly in the rain.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Archaeology copying inscriptions
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Archaeology](../Skills/archaeology.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** tracing kit

## Not to be confused with

*[Notebook](notebook.md)*, which records what the owner sees and writes; the rubbing kit copies a carving exactly as it is.
