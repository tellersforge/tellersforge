# Midwife's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Medicine and healing · Needs: bronze · any*

A cloth bag packed with everything a birth needs: clean linen, cord ties, a small sharp blade, warming oils and bundles of herbs to ease pain and bleeding. The midwife carries it from house to house, often at night, often through bad weather. She is welcomed at every door and feared a little too, since she has seen every family at its most frightened. A difficult birth goes better in skilled hands with the right tools ready.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Midwifery difficult births
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Midwifery](../Skills/midwifery.md)
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** birthing kit

## Not to be confused with

*[Doctor's Bag](doctor-s-bag.md)*, which carries a physician's general supplies; the midwife's kit is made for childbirth.
