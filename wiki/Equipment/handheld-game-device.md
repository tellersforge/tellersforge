# Handheld game device

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: computing · modern*

A small plastic case with a bright screen and a few buttons, holding games that beep and flash and never end. It passes the time on long journeys, in waiting rooms and during dull watches. It also swallows attention whole, and a player deep in a game will not hear footsteps behind them or notice the lights change.
