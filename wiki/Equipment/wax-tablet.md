# Wax tablet

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Writing and records · Needs: writing · western kingdoms*

A pair of hinged wooden boards, each hollowed out and filled with a thin layer of dark wax. The writer scratches letters with the point of a stylus and smooths them away with its flat end to start again. Students, merchants and poets carry them for notes, sums and drafts. In hot sun the wax softens and smears, so wise scribes keep theirs in the shade.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Calligraphy & Scribing quick notes that can be erased
- **Penalty:** −1 Calligraphy & Scribing in hot sun
- **Used for:** [Calligraphy & Scribing](../Skills/calligraphy-and-scribing.md)
- **Weight:** 0.4 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 1 S
- **Also known as:** wax board, writing diptych

## Not to be confused with

*[Slate and Chalk](slate-and-chalk.md)*, which also wipes clean; the wax tablet is scratched rather than chalked and folds shut to protect the writing.
