# Earrings

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · any*

Hoops, studs or dangling drops of metal, glass and gems, worn in pierced ears by sailors, dancers, merchants and queens alike. A good pair catches candlelight and draws the eye to the face, which is exactly what they are for at a feast or a gathering. In a brawl they are a liability: a hoop is an easy thing to grab, and a torn ear is a lesson learnt once.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 CHA in social settings
- **Penalty:** −1 Wrestling & Grappling hoops can be grabbed
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.02 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** ear hoops, studs

## Not to be confused with

*[Ear spools](ear-spools.md)*, which fill a stretched lobe and cannot be torn out by a grasping hand.
