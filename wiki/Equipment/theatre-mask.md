# Theatre mask

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: writing · any*

A mask of carved wood, linen or leather that fixes one character's face: a laughing fool, a weeping widow, a fierce demon. Exaggerated features let an audience at the back of a large theatre see who is on stage and what they feel. Actors in stylised theatre learn to perform with their whole body behind it. The mask cuts off side vision.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +1 Acting in stylised theatre
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception side vision
- **Used for:** [Acting](../Skills/acting.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** writing · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** stage mask, character mask

## Not to be confused with

*[Festival mask](festival-mask.md)*, which is worn in processions and dances; the theatre mask is made to be read by an audience.
