# Leatherworker's kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: stone tools · any*

A roll of knives, awls, punches, stitching needles, a mallet and a lump of waxed thread. With tanned hide it makes belts, bags, boots, harness, scabbards and armour. Leather is tough and forgiving, and a patient worker can repair almost anything made from it. Leatherworkers smell faintly of oil and tannin and have hands like the stuff they work.
