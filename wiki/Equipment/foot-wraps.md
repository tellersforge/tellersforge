# Foot wraps

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: stone tools · any*

Pieces of hide, fur or cloth wound around the feet and tied at the ankle with cord, the footwear of people who have nothing better. They keep bare feet off frozen ground and sharp stones. They also come loose, slip, bunch, and trip up anyone who breaks into a run.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 END against cold ground
- **Penalty:** −1 Running they slip loose
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 1 C
- **Also known as:** hide wrappings

## Not to be confused with

*[Moccasins](moccasins.md)*, proper shoes of hide sewn to shape, which stay on and move quietly.
