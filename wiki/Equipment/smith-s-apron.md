# Smith's apron

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: copper · smiths*

A heavy apron of thick leather hanging from chest to shin, scorched, pitted and blackened by years of work. It shields the smith from sparks, flying scale and the occasional dropped piece of glowing iron. Its pockets hold tongs, a punch or a file. Most smiths feel undressed at the anvil without it.

## Game block

- **Slot:** torso
- **Bonus:** +1 armour against sparks and hot metal; +1 Blacksmithing at the forge
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2.5 kg · **Needs:** copper · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** leather apron, forge apron

## Not to be confused with

*[Leather Work Apron](leather-work-apron.md)*, the general tradesman's apron of later workshops, lighter and made for tools rather than fire.
