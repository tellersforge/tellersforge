# Dancer's costume

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: bronze · temple and court dancers*

A light, bright costume of silk or fine cotton hung with little bells, tassels, coins or ribbons that swing and sound with every turn. Temple and court dancers wear it to perform, and the sound becomes part of the dance. Offstage, it looks immodest and out of place, and it is too thin to keep anyone warm.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Dance performing; +1 Charm & Seduction after a performance
- **Penalty:** −2 Etiquette outside the performance; −1 END against cold
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** dance dress

## Not to be confused with

*[Jester's Motley](jester-s-motley.md)*, the bright costume of another court performer, whose licence is mockery rather than dance.
