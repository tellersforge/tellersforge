# Lip plug

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: stone tools · forest tribes*

A disc or plug of polished wood, stone or shell, set in a hole pierced below the lip and widened over years. Among the forest peoples who wear it, a large plug marks an adult, a warrior or a respected elder, and a bare lip looks childish. Strangers tend to stare, and some cannot hide their unease. It changes the way the wearer speaks and eats, and nobody who has one forgets it is there.

## Game block

- **Slot:** face
- **Bonus:** +1 CHA among the wearer's people
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA among strangers
- **Used for:** —
