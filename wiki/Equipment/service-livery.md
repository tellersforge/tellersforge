# Service livery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: printing · western kingdoms*

A tailored coat and breeches, or a neat dress and apron, in the colours of a great house or a grand hotel, with brass buttons bearing a crest. Footmen, porters, maids and doormen wear it. The livery is designed to make them useful and invisible at once: guests hand them coats and speak freely in front of them. The same clothes make it very hard for a servant to give orders to the gentry.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Service & Hospitality in a great house or hotel; +1 Stealth to pass unnoticed among the wealthy
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA when giving orders to the gentry
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** footman's livery, doorman's uniform

## Not to be confused with

*[Servant's livery](servant-s-livery.md)*, the older household dress of a single noble family; service livery is its broader form, worn in hotels and grand establishments too.
