# Training whistle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · any*

A short metal whistle on a cord, blown in a pattern of long and short notes that a trained dog learns to obey. Some are pitched so high that people hear nothing but a faint hiss while every hound in the yard pricks up its ears. Shepherds, gamekeepers and handlers of guard dogs carry one, and a good handler can work a dog across a whole hillside without raising her voice. It works only on animals that have been taught what the notes mean.
