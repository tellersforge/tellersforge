# Hammer

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: bronze · any*

A head of iron, bronze or hardwood on a short handle, swung to drive nails, flatten metal and knock things into place. Every trade uses one and every household has one. It is the simplest tool after the rock that came before it, and it bruises more thumbs than any enemy.
