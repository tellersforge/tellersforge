# Wool tunic

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: agriculture · any*

A knee-length tunic of woven wool with long sleeves, belted at the waist, the common dress of cold and temperate lands for centuries. It is warm, it keeps warm even when damp, and it wears well. A farmer, a soldier and a lord's servant might all wear one, differing only in the colour of the dye and the quality of the weave.
