# Muzzle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Riding and animal gear · Needs: iron · any*

A cage of leather straps or metal wires fitted around an animal's jaws to stop it biting, while still letting it breathe and drink. Handlers use it on guard dogs in public, on frightened animals being treated and on beasts too dangerous to trust. The animal usually hates it, and removing it is sometimes as risky as fitting it.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Animal Handling dangerous animals
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Animal Handling](../Skills/animal-handling.md)
- **Weight:** 0.3 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** dog muzzle

## Not to be confused with

the *[Leash and Collar](leash-and-collar.md)*, which controls where an animal goes but not what it bites.
