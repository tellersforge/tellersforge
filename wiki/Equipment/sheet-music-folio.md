# Sheet music folio

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Music, games and entertainment · Needs: printing · any*

A cloth or leather folder of printed and hand-copied music: dances, hymns, ballads, parts for a small band, and the lyrics of songs everybody knows the first verse of. A musician who can read notation can pick up a piece they have never heard and play it through. Composers carry their own work in one, marked up with corrections and coffee rings.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Instrument (per instrument) playing unfamiliar pieces
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Musical Composition](../Skills/musical-composition.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** printing · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** songbook

## Not to be confused with

*[Printed Book](printed-book.md)*, a general volume to read; the folio holds music to play.
