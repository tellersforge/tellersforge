# Street fashion

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: computing · modern*

A hooded top, loose jeans or cargo trousers, well-worn trainers, perhaps a cap and a backpack. It is what the young of any modern city wear, and it lets the wearer vanish into a crowd on a platform or a street corner. Among city youth it earns a nod and a conversation. In a courtroom, embassy or formal dinner it earns disapproving looks.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Streetwise among city youth; +1 Stealth to blend into an urban crowd
- **Penalty:** −1 Etiquette in formal settings
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.2 kg · **Needs:** computing · **Price:** 2 S
- **Also known as:** streetwear, hoodie and jeans

## Not to be confused with

*[Athletic kit](athletic-kit.md)*, which is made for running and sport; street fashion is made for blending in and looking right among the young.
