# Jerrycan

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: electricity · modern*

A flat can of stamped steel or later hard plastic, with a spout, a handle and a shape that stacks neatly in a truck. It carries fuel or water and pours without spilling. Armies made them famous, and every overland traveller since has strapped a few to the back of a vehicle.
