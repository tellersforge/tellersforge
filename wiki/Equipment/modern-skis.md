# Modern skis

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: electricity · modern*

Shaped skis of laminated wood, metal and plastic, with steel edges, waxed bases and bindings that lock the boot and release it in a fall. With a pair of poles they glide across country or carve down steep mountainsides at speed. Soldiers of mountain units, rescuers and pleasure-seekers alike use them. A good skier on them flies.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Snow & Ice Travel any snow; +1 Balance downhill
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Snow & Ice Travel](../Skills/snow-and-ice-travel.md)
- **Weight:** 5 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 10 S
- **Also known as:** touring skis

## Not to be confused with

*[Wooden Skis](wooden-skis.md)*, which glide over flat snowfields but are hard to steer downhill.
