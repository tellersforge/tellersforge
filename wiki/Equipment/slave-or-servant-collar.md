# Slave or servant collar

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: bronze · any*

A plain metal collar, riveted or locked around the neck, marking the wearer as someone else's property. It is a grim thing, and the respect of others withers in its presence: nobody listens to a collared person, and nobody cuts them into any schemes. Its one strange use is that masters stop seeing the collared at all, and a wearer can pass among them unnoticed.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +1 Stealth to go unnoticed among masters
- **Penalty:** −2 CHA in any company; −2 Intrigue & Politics while worn
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** bronze · **Price:** 5 S
- **Also known as:** bondsman's collar

## Not to be confused with

*[Status collar](status-collar.md)*, a far-future badge of rank or allegiance worn by choice; the slave or servant collar marks the wearer as unfree.
