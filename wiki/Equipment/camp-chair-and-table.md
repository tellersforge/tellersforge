# Camp chair and table

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Camp and shelter · Needs: steam · any*

A folding chair of canvas and wood or light metal, and a small folding table that sets up on scissored legs. They add nothing to survival and weigh a great deal, and yet officers, explorers and wealthy travellers carry them everywhere. A camp with a chair and a table becomes a room where letters are written, cards played and meals eaten with dignity.
