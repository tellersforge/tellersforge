# Carpenter's tools

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Tools and trade kits · Needs: iron · any*

A chest or leather roll of saws, chisels, an adze, an auger, a plane, a mallet and a square. With them and good timber, a carpenter builds anything from a stool to a roof truss to a wagon bed. The tools are sharpened lovingly and lent to no one. A carpenter's chest is a family treasure, passed down with the handles worn smooth by generations of hands.
