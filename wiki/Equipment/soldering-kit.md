# Soldering kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A small case with a heated iron, a reel of soft metal wire, a pot of flux and a stand to rest the hot iron on. With it, a careful hand can join wires, fix broken circuits and build new devices from parts. It smells of hot metal and resin, and it leaves many small burns on the careless.
