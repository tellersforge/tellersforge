# Carrying frame

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Carrying and containers · Needs: stone tools · any*

A frame of lashed poles or bent wood worn on the back with straps, to which firewood, a haunch of meat, a bundle of hides or a sick child can be tied. It carries awkward loads that no bag could hold. Loaded, it is wide and heavy, and its wearer turns slowly and moves like a laden mule.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Lifting & Carrying awkward loads
- **Penalty:** −1 Dodge while loaded
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 2 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** pack board, A-frame carrier

## Not to be confused with

*[Burden basket](burden-basket.md)*, which holds loose loads inside a woven cone instead of lashing them to a frame.
