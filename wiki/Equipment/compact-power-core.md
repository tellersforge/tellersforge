# Compact power core

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: far future · far future*

A sealed, heavy core of dark metal about the size of a keg, humming faintly, which can power a vehicle, a camp or a small outpost for years without refuelling. Its workings are beyond most engineers, and its casing is designed never to be opened. Colonists guard their core like a heart, because when it stops, everything else does too.
