# Ritual mask

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: stone tools · any*

A carved and painted mask of wood, bark or hide, often hung with feathers, hair or shells, worn to become a spirit, an ancestor or a god during a rite. Once it is on, the wearer is no longer themselves; the dancers treat them as the being they represent. The eye holes are narrow, and seeing the world around through them is hard.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Trance & Spirit Work during masked rites
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception while worn
- **Used for:** [Ritual & Liturgy](../Skills/ritual-and-liturgy.md), [Acting](../Skills/acting.md)
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** stone tools · **Price:** 5 C
- **Also known as:** spirit mask
