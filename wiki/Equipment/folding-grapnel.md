# Folding grapnel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Rope, climbing and travel · Needs: iron · any*

A grappling hook whose prongs fold flat against the shank and lock open with a twist. Folded, it hides in a satchel or under a coat; opened, its tines are wrapped in cloth to deaden the clang when it lands. Burglars and boarding parties prefer it. It is still noisy on stone, despite the padding, and it is not quite as strong as a solid hook.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Smuggling & Concealment carrying hidden
- **Penalty:** −1 Stealth when thrown onto stone
- **Used for:** [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md), [Throwing](../Skills/throwing.md)
- **Weight:** 1.2 kg · **Needs:** iron · **Price:** 4 S
- **Also known as:** collapsible grapnel

## Not to be confused with

*[Grappling Hook](grappling-hook.md)*, which is heavier and simpler, and has no way to hide.
