# Wide-brimmed felt hat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · western kingdoms*

A hat of felted wool with a broad brim, often turned up on one side or pinned up on three, sometimes finished with a feather. It keeps rain off the back of the neck and the sun out of the eyes. Lifting it to a lady, a priest or a magistrate is the gesture of a gentleman, and it is noticed when forgotten.
