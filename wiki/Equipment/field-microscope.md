# Field microscope

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A small folding microscope in a wooden or leather case, carried by naturalists and doctors on the move. It lets them look at samples where they find them: a leaf in the jungle, a drop of blood in a clinic, a crushed insect in a tent. It is less powerful than a laboratory microscope, but a great deal easier to carry.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +1 Life Sciences studying samples in the field
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** [Life Sciences](../Skills/life-sciences.md)
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** pocket microscope

## Not to be confused with

*[Microscope](microscope.md)*, the full benchtop instrument, more powerful but too heavy to take into the field.
