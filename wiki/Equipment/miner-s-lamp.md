# Miner's lamp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Light and fire · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A lamp for underground work, its flame shut behind fine metal gauze, or burning carbide gas behind a reflector. It lights the way through tunnels safely, and when bad air seeps in, the flame changes colour or flares up, warning the miners to run. Many owe their lives to watching it.
