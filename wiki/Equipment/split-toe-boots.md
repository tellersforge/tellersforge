# Split-toe boots

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · eastern islands*

Soft ankle boots of cloth or thin leather, divided between the big toe and the rest of the foot like a mitten for the feet. Labourers, porters and roofers of the eastern islands wear them, and so, in the tales, do spies and night-climbers. The thin sole lets the wearer feel every twig before putting weight on it, and the split toe can pinch a beam or a rope like a clumsy thumb.

## Game block

- **Slot:** feet
- **Bonus:** +1 Stealth creeping; +1 Climbing gripping with the toes
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.4 kg · **Needs:** gunpowder · **Price:** 2 S
