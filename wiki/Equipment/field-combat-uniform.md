# Field combat uniform

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · modern*

Shirt and trousers of tough drab or mottled cloth, reinforced at the knees and elbows and covered in pockets for maps, dressings and spare rounds. It is made for weeks in the field: it dries fast, shrugs off wind and thorns, and can be slept in. Nobody mistakes it for anything but soldier's clothing. It does not belong at a dinner table, and a spy who tries to pass as a civilian in it will not get far.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 END against rough weather in the field
- **Penalty:** −1 CHA in polite society; −2 Deception to pass as a civilian
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 8 S
- **Also known as:** battledress, fatigues

## Not to be confused with

*[Military service uniform](military-service-uniform.md)*, the polished parade dress of the same army; the field uniform is built for weather and mud, not for show.
