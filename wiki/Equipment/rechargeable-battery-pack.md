# Rechargeable battery pack

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: computing · modern*

A flat, compact pack with a cable or two, that stores enough power to keep phones, radios and sensors running for days. When it runs low, it can be filled again from a wall socket, a generator or a solar panel. Travellers, soldiers and field workers treat it as essential. It is light enough to forget about, until the moment it is flat and nothing else will charge.
