# Hand-crank generator

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Power, electronics and computing · Needs: electricity · modern*

A small box with a folding handle, holding a dynamo that turns motion into electricity. Wind the crank steadily and it charges a battery or runs a radio; stop cranking and the power stops too. It is slow, tiring work, but it never runs out of fuel. Its whirring hum carries farther than its user might like.
