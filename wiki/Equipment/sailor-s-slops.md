# Sailor's slops

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: gunpowder · seafarers*

Loose, wide-legged trousers of canvas or duck, cut off short at the calf, the working clothes of ordinary sailors. They do not snag on the rigging, they dry fast after a soaking, and they roll up out of the way on deck. Any sailor ashore in a pair can be spotted from the far side of a port town.

## Game block

- **Slot:** legs
- **Bonus:** +1 Sailing & Seamanship working the rigging
- **Penalty:** 0
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg · **Needs:** gunpowder · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** wide sailor trousers

## Not to be confused with

*[Loose Trousers](loose-trousers.md)*, full desert trousers gathered at the ankle, made for heat rather than for climbing rigging.
