# Binoculars

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Optics, sensing and scanners · Needs: steam · industrial cities*

A pair of telescopes joined side by side, with prisms to fold the light and a wheel to focus both at once. They give a steady, clear view of things far away, and are easier to use for long periods than a spyglass. Soldiers, birdwatchers, hunters and private detectives all use them. While looking through them, the world close by is invisible.

## Game block

- **Bonus:** +2 Perception spotting things far away; +1 Shadowing & Surveillance watching from afar
- **Penalty:** −1 Perception noticing anything close by while looking
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 1 kg · **Needs:** steam · **Price:** 15 S
- **Also known as:** field glasses

*[Spyglass](spyglass.md)*, the older single-tube telescope, lighter but harder to hold steady.
