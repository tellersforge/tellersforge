# Prayer rug

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Faith, ritual and occult · Needs: iron · desert peoples*

A small, finely woven rug, often with a pattern pointing the way to face, rolled up and carried by desert peoples wherever they go. At the hour of prayer it is unrolled on sand, stone or a merchant's floor, making a small clean place to kneel. The best are heirlooms worth a fortune, and the humblest are worn through at the knees.
