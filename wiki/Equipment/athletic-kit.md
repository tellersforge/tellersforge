# Athletic kit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Clothing · Needs: electricity · modern*

Light stretch shorts or leggings, a vest or thin top, and cushioned running shoes, all made to move freely and dry fast. Athletes, gymnasts and dancers wear it, and so does anyone who expects to run, jump or climb. It gives no warmth, so the wearer chills quickly once they stop moving, and in a formal setting it looks as if they came from the gym.

## Game block

- **Slot:** full body
- **Bonus:** +1 Running while worn; +1 Acrobatics while worn
- **Penalty:** −1 END against cold when resting; −1 CHA in formal settings
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.6 kg · **Needs:** electricity · **Price:** 3 S
- **Also known as:** tracksuit, gym clothes, running kit

## Not to be confused with

*[Running shoes](running-shoes.md)*, only the footwear; athletic kit is the whole outfit made for free movement.
