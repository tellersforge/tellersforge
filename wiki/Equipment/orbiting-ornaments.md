# Orbiting ornaments

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Jewellery and adornment · Needs: far future · far future*

Small real gems that circle slowly around the wearer's head, held in orbit by tiny gravity fields. Only the very highest ranks of a far-future court wear them, and their presence settles arguments before they start. Lesser courtiers lower their eyes. The poor and the rebellious see them as everything wrong with the world, and no one wearing them can hope to go unnoticed.

## Game block

- **Slot:** jewellery
- **Bonus:** +2 Etiquette at a far-future court; +1 Intimidation over lesser courtiers
- **Penalty:** −2 Stealth while worn; −1 CHA among the poor or rebels
- **Used for:** —
- **Weight:** 0.05 kg · **Needs:** far future · **Price:** 40 S
- **Also known as:** floating jewels, halo stones

## Not to be confused with

*[Hologram jewellery](hologram-jewellery.md)*, which only projects light; orbiting ornaments are solid, costly and a mark of rank, not mere fashion.
