# Tower clock

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Navigation and timekeeping · Needs: printing · western kingdoms*

A great iron clockwork mounted high in a church, town hall or gate tower, driven by heavy weights that a keeper winds up each day. Its bell strikes the hours across fields and rooftops, and in time a painted face shows them to the whole square. It rules work, markets and prayer for everyone within earshot. It is a town's pride and a thief's worst neighbour, and it is often wrong.
