# Linguistics

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

How languages work: their sounds, their grammar, their family trees. The linguist studies tongues rather than simply speaking them, and can often pick up a new one fast because they know the patterns it is likely to follow. Their great work is decipherment: facing an unknown script or a dead language, and slowly, sign by sign, making it speak again.

The skill belongs to scholars in dusty archives, explorers copying inscriptions from cliff faces, and diplomats facing a people no one has ever met. In the far future it may meet languages that aren't made of sound at all. A linguist is often the first to understand what the strangers are actually saying.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** writing · **Covers:** how languages work, deciphering unknown and dead scripts
- **Why these attributes:** heavy reasoning, then intuition
- **What failure looks like:** the pattern is not found, and the text stays silent. Spectacular failure: a plausible translation is published, and it is wrong in some important way.
