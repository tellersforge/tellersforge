# Glassmaking

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Sand, ash and fire. The glassmaker melts them together in a furnace hotter than a smith's forge and gathers the glowing mass on the end of a pipe. A breath, a turn, a pinch of tongs, and it becomes a bottle, a bowl or a sheet of window glass. Coloured glass, beads, mirrors and at last lenses for spectacles and telescopes come from the same furnace, each with its own guarded secrets.

It is hot, delicate and beautiful work, and in many lands its masters are kept close, even forbidden to leave, so that their knowledge does not travel. A glassworker knows the melt by its colour and the moment to shape it by its glow. One breath too many and the piece is gone.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/END/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** bronze · **Covers:** melting, blowing and casting glass; lenses
- **Why these attributes:** handwork, heat, knowledge
- **What failure looks like:** The glass is cloudy, bubbled or cracks in cooling. Spectacular failure: the piece shatters, or the furnace spills and burns.

## Not to be confused with

*[Pottery & Brickmaking](pottery-and-brickmaking.md)*, which fires clay; Glassmaking melts and blows glass, and eventually grinds the lenses that change the world.
