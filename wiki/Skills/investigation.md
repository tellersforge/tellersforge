# Investigation

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Investigation*

*aka: ______________________________*

Clues are only marks and objects until someone thinks about them. Investigation is that thinking: putting the muddy boot print, the missing key and the widow's nervous smile together into a story that explains them all. It covers crime scenes, interviews, timelines and the slow sorting of what matters from what merely distracts.

Magistrates, thief-takers, detectives and inquisitors all practise it, some fairly and some not. A good investigator distrusts the obvious answer and keeps asking who gained. They are the one at the end who gathers everyone in the library and explains, and the one who, just before that, notices the single detail that changes everything.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/PER/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** clues, deduction, crime scenes, profiling
- **Why these attributes:** reasoning, then insight
- **What failure looks like:** the clues don't come together, or point in a plausible wrong direction. Spectacular failure: an innocent person is accused, and the real culprit learns they are being hunted.

## Not to be confused with

*[Forensics](forensics.md)*, which analyses physical evidence with tools; and *[Trained Observation](trained-observation.md)*, which notices details. Investigation reasons about what the details mean.
