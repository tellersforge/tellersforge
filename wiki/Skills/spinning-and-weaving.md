# Spinning & Weaving

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A spindle turns, wool or flax twists into thread, and the thread goes onto the loom. Spinning and weaving make cloth, and cloth is the great hidden labour of history: every sail, blanket, cloak and bandage began as fibre in someone's hands. The craft includes felting wool into mats and hats, beating bark into cloth and steeping fabrics in dyes of root, berry and shell.

It is patient, rhythmic and endless. In many lands it is the work of every household, done in the evenings, and in others it is a guild trade with great workshops and jealous secrets. A weaver can read a cloth like a letter: where it was made, how well, and how rich its owner is.
