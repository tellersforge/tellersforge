# Diving

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Going down into the water and coming back up. Pearl divers on a held breath, salvagers working a wreck, children diving for coins thrown from a boat, and in later ages divers with tanks of air on their backs and weights on their belts. Coastal peoples prize the skill, and some villages have lived by it for centuries.

Diving is breath control and calm. The pressure builds, the light fades, the cold bites, and the diver has to keep working without panic. Good divers know their limits and the dangers of the depths: currents, snagging lines, sharp coral and the slow sickness that comes from rising too fast.
