# Scavenging

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Finding what is still useful among what is broken. The scavenger picks through ruins, wrecks and rubbish heaps and comes out with tools, parts, food, cloth and wire that others walked past. They know what lasts, where people used to keep things, and how to tell a salvageable engine from a dead one.

In ages of plenty it is the trade of rag-pickers and junk dealers. After the fall of a civilisation it can be the only trade that matters. A scavenger's eye sees value in a heap of scrap, and their hands know how to get it out without bringing the rest of the heap down.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** finding usable things in ruins, wrecks and junk
- **Why these attributes:** eye for value, then knowing what it is
- **What failure looks like:** nothing worth taking is found. Spectacular failure: the ruin collapses, a hazard is disturbed, or someone else's cache is looted, and they notice.
