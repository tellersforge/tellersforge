# Law

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The codes, customs and precedents by which a society settles its quarrels. A lawyer knows what the law says, what the judges think it means, and where the gaps are. Law might be a chieftain's memory of old rulings, a scroll of temple edicts, or a library of statutes, but in every age someone gets paid to know it better than everyone else.

Advocacy is the sharp end of it: standing before a court and making the law bend in a client's favour. Loopholes are the delight of it. A good lawyer can make a signed contract worthless, find the ancient right that lets a peasant graze on a lord's land, or keep a guilty man from the gallows on a point of procedure.
