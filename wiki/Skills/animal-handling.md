# Animal Handling

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Animal Handling*

*aka: ______________________________*

Getting the goat into the pen, the ox under the yoke and the dog off the dinner table. Animal handling is the plain skill of living and working alongside the creatures of the farmyard and the road: horses, mules, cattle, sheep, pigs, dogs and fowl. Farm children learn it young, often from the wrong end of a kick.

It is calm first and firmness second. A good handler moves slowly, speaks low and never stands where they might be trampled. Animals know who is frightened. A skilled handler can soothe a panicking horse in a fire or get a stubborn mule across a bridge. They can't teach an animal new tricks.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** calming and managing common domestic animals
- **Why these attributes:** patience, then presence
