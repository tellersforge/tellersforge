# First Aid

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Healing*

*aka: ______________________________*

Keeping someone alive until they can be properly treated. Pressing on a wound to stop the bleeding, cleaning it, binding it, splinting a broken arm with two sticks and a belt, cooling a fever with wet cloths. Mothers, farmers, sergeants and village elders all learn some of it, because help is often far away.

First aid isn't medicine. It doesn't heal; it keeps things from getting worse. A good first-aider is calm, quick and practical, and knows the limits of what they are doing. The wounded remember those hands for the rest of their lives.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** binding wounds, splints, fever care
- **Why these attributes:** knowing what to do, then doing it
- **What failure looks like:** The treatment doesn't help and the patient's condition stays as it was. Spectacular failure: the bandage slips, the wound fouls, or the splint sets the bone crooked.

## Not to be confused with

*[Field Medicine](field-medicine.md)*, which is the trained soldier's craft of treating serious wounds under fire; First Aid is simple care anyone can learn.
