# Wayfinding

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Survival*

*aka: ______________________________*

Knowing where you are and how to get where you're going, overland and without a road. The wayfinder reads the sun and the stars, the lean of trees, the flow of water, and the shape of hills, and keeps a running count of distance and direction in the head. Landmarks are remembered, routes are chosen, and the long way round is taken when the short way is a swamp.

Guides, scouts, herders and caravan masters live by it. Those who lack it walk in circles and find themselves, three days later, back at the same broken tree. A good wayfinder can bring a party through fog, forest or featureless plain and still arrive where they meant to.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** overland direction, landmarks, route-finding
- **Why these attributes:** sense of place, then reckoning
- **What failure looks like:** the party strays from the route and loses time. Spectacular failure: they are thoroughly lost, and do not realise it until far too late.

## Not to be confused with

*[Sea Navigation](sea-navigation.md)*, which finds the way at sea; and *[Cartography](cartography.md)*, which makes and reads the maps. Wayfinding works from the land itself.
