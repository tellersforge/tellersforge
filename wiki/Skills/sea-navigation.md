# Sea Navigation

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Finding the way across open water where there are no roads, only horizon. Some peoples learned it from the stars and the swells, feeling the pattern of waves bounced from an island they couldn't yet see, or watching which way the evening birds flew. Later sailors had compass, charts and the endless small sums of dead reckoning: speed, heading and time written in the log each turn of the glass.

A navigator is the ship's quiet authority. The captain decides where to go; the navigator knows where the ship actually is. Mistakes are measured in weeks of hunger or a reef in the dark. Pilots who know a harbour by heart, fishermen who never lose their grounds and ocean voyagers crossing uncharted seas all depend on it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **PER**/INT/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** star, swell and bird navigation, compass, charts, dead reckoning
- **Why these attributes:** reading nature, heavy calculation
- **What failure looks like:** the ship drifts off course, and the error grows with every day. Spectacular failure: the navigator is sure of the position, and completely wrong, until land appears where it shouldn't.
