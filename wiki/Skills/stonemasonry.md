# Stonemasonry

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Stone does not forgive. The mason splits blocks from the quarry face, dresses them with chisel and mallet until each is square and true, then lays them in courses that will stand for a thousand years. Arches, vaults, columns and carved window tracery all come from the same hands, which learn to feel the stone's bed and grain and to follow them.

Masons travel. The great building projects of any age draw them from far away, and they carry their marks, their tools and their lodge secrets with them. Their work is slow, dusty and loud, and their joints are fine enough that a knife blade cannot enter. A castle, a cathedral or a city wall is their memorial, even when no one remembers their names.

- **What failure looks like:** The stone is cut wrong and wasted, or the course is out of true. Spectacular failure: a block cracks under load, or an arch collapses when the frame is taken away.
