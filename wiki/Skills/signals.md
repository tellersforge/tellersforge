# Signals

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Messages sent over distances no voice can cross. Flags run up a mast, drums beaten in a rhythm, smoke by day and fire by night, beacons chained from hilltop to hilltop, chalk marks on a doorpost, and in later ages the clipped procedure of the radio operator. Signals is knowing the codes, sending cleanly and reading accurately.

Armies, fleets, smugglers and secret societies all depend on it. A good signaller can pass word of an invasion across a kingdom in a night, or tell a ship's captain what the admiral wants without a word spoken. A bad one can send a whole fleet in the wrong direction.

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2

- **Why these attributes:** knowing the code, then sending it
- **What failure looks like:** the message is garbled or missed. Spectacular failure: the wrong message goes out, or the enemy reads it as easily as the friends do.
