# Survival: Arctic

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Living through cold that kills. Arctic survival is dressing in layers and never sweating, building a snow shelter that is warmer than any tent, finding fuel where there are no trees, and knowing the difference between ice that holds and ice that doesn't. It is also knowing the signs of frostbite and cold sickness and treating them before they turn deadly.

Polar peoples have refined this over countless generations, and outsiders are wise to copy them. The cold is patient; it waits for a wet sock, a lost mitten or a moment's carelessness. The long dark, the whiteout and the sea that freezes overnight all belong to this knowledge too.
