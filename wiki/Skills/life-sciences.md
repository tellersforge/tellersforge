# Life Sciences

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The systematic study of living things. Biology, botany, anatomy, the workings of cells and inheritance, and in far-flung futures the strange chemistry of creatures from other worlds. A life scientist dissects, classifies, cultures and compares, and knows why a creature is built the way it is rather than just where it lives.

In a story, the one who has it can say what a beast eats from the shape of its teeth, how a disease spreads, why this crop is failing, and whether the alien thing in the jar is dead or merely waiting. It is the learning of naturalists with notebooks, laboratory workers in white coats, and ship's scientists far from home.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** printing · **Covers:** biology, botany, genetics, xenobiology
- **Why these attributes:** heavy knowledge, then observation
- **What failure looks like:** the organism is misunderstood or wrongly classified. Spectacular failure: a specimen is badly mishandled, and it spreads, escapes or dies with its secrets.

## Not to be confused with

*[Nature Lore](nature-lore.md)*, which is practical, traditional knowledge of the wild; Life Sciences studies living things by theory, experiment and instruments.
