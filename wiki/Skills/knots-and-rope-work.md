# Knots & Rope Work

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Crafting & Repair*

*aka: ______________________________*

The right knot for the right job. A bowline that never slips, a hitch that frees with one pull, a lashing that holds a raft together through a flood. Sailors, riggers, herders, climbers and hangmen all learn their own knots, and a sailor who meets another will often compare them before trading any other news.

Rope work is handwork and memory. It is splicing a broken line, rigging a pulley, tying a load onto a mule so it doesn't shift, and binding a prisoner so they stay bound. A good knot holds under strain and comes loose when asked. A bad one does the opposite, usually at the worst time.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** tying, lashing, rigging, binding captives
- **Why these attributes:** handwork, then knowing the right knot
- **What failure looks like:** The knot slips, the lashing loosens or the rigging sags. Spectacular failure: it gives way under load, or jams so tight it has to be cut.
