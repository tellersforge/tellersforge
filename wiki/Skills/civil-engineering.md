# Civil Engineering

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Roads that last centuries, bridges across rivers that swallow boats, aqueducts carrying water over valleys, harbours that hold back storms. Civil engineering is the design of works too large for one building: the arteries and bones of a civilisation. The engineer surveys, calculates, plans, and then directs armies of labourers through years of work.

It is a slow and serious art. Its practitioners think in slopes, loads, flows and the patience of stone and water. Their successes are so ordinary that nobody notices them, and their failures are catastrophes that everyone remembers. Empires are measured in their roads, and cities live or die on their water.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** wheel and roads · **Covers:** roads, bridges, aqueducts, canals, dams, harbours
- **Why these attributes:** heavy design, heavy judgement
- **What failure looks like:** The design is costly, slow or flawed. Spectacular failure: a bridge, dam or aqueduct fails under strain and takes lives and years of work with it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Architecture](architecture.md)*, which designs buildings; Civil Engineering designs roads, bridges, canals, dams and the great works between them.
