# Medicine

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Healing*

*aka: ______________________________*

The physician's art: finding what is wrong and making it right. The doctor listens to the patient, takes the pulse, looks at tongue and eyes and skin, and weighs what they see against years of study. Then comes the treatment: rest, diet, medicine, surgery if needed, and the long, patient care of the chronically ill. In early ages medicine is part lore and part guesswork; in later ones, laboratories and machines lend a hand.

Good physicians are trusted, well paid and sometimes feared, since they hold life in their hands. Their bedside manner matters as much as their knowledge. The best of them know when to act, when to wait, and when there is nothing more to be done.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** diagnosing and treating illness, long-term care

- **What failure looks like:** The illness is misdiagnosed or the treatment does little good. Spectacular failure: the cure makes the patient worse, or the true illness is missed until too late.

## Not to be confused with
