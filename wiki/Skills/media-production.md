# Media Production

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Making the sounds and pictures that reach a crowd through machines. The engineer in a recording studio balancing voices and instruments, the broadcaster keeping a radio show on air, the designer shaping posters, advertisements and screens. Media Production covers sound recording, broadcasting and digital design.

It is technical and creative at once. Knowing the equipment matters, but so does knowing what makes listeners stay and viewers look. Radio stations, record companies, newsrooms, advertisers and propaganda offices all need it. In the far future the skill builds entire illusions of news and entertainment beamed across worlds. A broadcast at the right moment can calm a panicking city, or set it on fire.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/PER/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** electricity · **Covers:** sound recording, broadcasting, digital design
- **Why these attributes:** technique, judgement, handling
- **What failure looks like:** the recording is poor, the broadcast drops out, the design falls flat. Spectacular failure: the wrong message goes out live, to everyone.

## Not to be confused with

*[Photography & Film](photography-and-film.md)*, which captures pictures and moving images. Media Production records sound and designs and broadcasts content.
