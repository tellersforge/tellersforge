# Fishing

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Survival*

*aka: ______________________________*

Pulling food from the water. Fishing covers the hook and line, the net cast from a boat, the spear thrust into a shallow stream, and the woven trap left overnight in the reeds. Fishers know where fish gather, when they feed, what bait tempts them and how the weather changes their mood.

It feeds whole coasts and river valleys, and it is also the quiet refuge of those who want an afternoon alone. A patient fisher can feed a camp from a river others walked past. Seafaring fishers know the sea's tempers as well as any sailor and speak of it with the same wary respect.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** line, net, spear and trap fishing
- **Why these attributes:** reading the water, then technique
- **What failure looks like:** nothing bites, and the day is wasted. Spectacular failure: gear is lost, the boat is swamped, or the catch turns out to be something dangerous.

## Not to be confused with

*[Hunting](hunting.md)*, which takes game on land; Fishing takes it from the water, mostly by patience and the right bait.
