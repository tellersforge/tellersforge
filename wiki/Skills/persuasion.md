# Persuasion

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Persuasion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Convincing someone by reason and goodwill. Talking a guard into letting you pass, persuading the council to fund the bridge, getting a friend to lend you their horse. Persuasion finds common ground, offers good arguments and makes the other side want to agree. Merchants, envoys and parents all practise it daily.

It relies on honesty and understanding. The persuader listens first, finds what the other wants, and shows how agreeing gets them there. It won't make anyone do what they fundamentally oppose, but it can tip the undecided, soften a refusal and turn an enemy into a reluctant ally.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** convincing by argument and goodwill
- **Why these attributes:** presence, then knowing what they need
- **What failure looks like:** The listener isn't convinced and holds their position. Spectacular failure: they are offended, hardened in their refusal, or suspicious of the persuader's motives.
