# Oratory & Debate

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Speaking to be heard and argued with. A sermon from the pulpit, a speech in the forum, a debate before the council, a battle cry before the charge. Oratory moves crowds; debate wins arguments in public. Priests, politicians, lawyers and rabble-rousers all practise it.

It is voice, structure and presence. The orator builds an argument, uses rhythm and emotion, and knows the audience. The debater listens, spots weaknesses and answers quickly. Both can change minds, stir passions and bring down rulers. Both can also be heckled into silence.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/COU/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** speeches, sermons, public argument, swaying crowds
- **Why these attributes:** presence, nerve before a crowd, argument
- **What failure looks like:** The speech falls flat or the argument is lost. Spectacular failure: the crowd turns hostile or laughs the speaker off the stage.

*[Persuasion](persuasion.md)*, which convinces one person or a small group; and *[Storytelling & Conversation](storytelling-and-conversation.md)*, which entertains. Oratory & Debate addresses crowds and public argument.
