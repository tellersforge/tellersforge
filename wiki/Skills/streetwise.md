# Streetwise

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Knowing how the city really works. Where to buy stolen goods, who to bribe, which alleys to avoid, which tavern keeper hears everything. Streetwise is the knowledge of the underworld and the art of surviving in it. Thieves, beggars, watchmen and journalists pick it up, some from birth.

It is contacts, rumour and reading the street. The streetwise can find a fence, hear news before it spreads and spot a pickpocket at work. They speak the slang and know the unwritten rules. It works in any city, but the specifics must be learnt afresh, and an outsider asking questions draws attention.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** underworld contacts, rumours, fences, bribes, city survival
- **Why these attributes:** street sense, dealing, nerve
- **What failure looks like:** The character finds no contacts or only stale rumours. Spectacular failure: they draw attention from the wrong people, or get robbed.
