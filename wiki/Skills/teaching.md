# Teaching

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Knowing a thing and passing it on are different gifts, and the second is rarer. A teacher breaks a craft into pieces a beginner can hold, shows the hard part slowly, then watches the pupil's hands and face to see what has actually gone in. Village schoolmistresses, drill sergeants, master craftsmen with apprentices and monks with novices all practise it, some with patience and some with a stick.

Good teaching is mostly listening. The pupil who nods is not always the one who understood, and the one who argues is often the one who will surpass the master. A great teacher leaves behind people who know things they were never told outright.
