# Astronomy & Calendars

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The sky is the oldest clock. Astronomy is knowing the paths of sun, moons and stars, the wandering lights, and the rhythm of seasons they mark. Calendar-keepers use it to tell farmers when to sow and priests when to hold the great feasts; navigators use it to find their way; astrologers use it to tell princes what they want to hear.

The one who has it can predict an eclipse, date an ancient event from the sky it describes, and read a star chart drawn by a vanished people. In later ages it becomes the science of telescopes and orbits, and in the far future the stars are destinations as well as signs.

## Not to be confused with
