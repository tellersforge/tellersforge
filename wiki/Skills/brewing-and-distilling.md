# Brewing & Distilling

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Grain, fruit or honey, water, yeast and time. The brewer steeps, mashes and ferments, and the result is ale, wine, mead, cider or any of a hundred sour and sparkling drinks that people have made since they first stored grain. The distiller goes further, boiling the brew in a still and catching its spirit, strong enough to burn and to preserve.

Brewing is as much judgement as recipe. The brewer smells the vat, tastes the wort, and knows when to stop. Monasteries, farm wives, taverns and great breweries all practise it, and in many places a clean ale is safer to drink than the water. A good brewer has friends everywhere; a bad distiller can blind a whole village.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** agriculture · **Covers:** beer, wine, mead, spirits, fermented drinks
- **Why these attributes:** judgement, knowledge, handling
- **What failure looks like:** The batch sours, goes flat or tastes of the barrel. Spectacular failure: the still bursts or the spirit comes out poisonous, and someone drinks it before anyone notices.

## Not to be confused with
