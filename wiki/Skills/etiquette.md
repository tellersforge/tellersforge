# Etiquette

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Persuasion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Knowing how things are done at home. Who sits where at the table, whom you greet first, when to take off your hat, which subjects are for family and which are for strangers. Every culture has its manners, and most of them are learnt without anyone noticing, from parents, neighbours and the sharp look of an aunt.

Good etiquette is invisible. The well-mannered guest is simply pleasant to have around, and nobody remembers why. Bad etiquette is remembered for years. The skill covers the customs of your own people and your own station: weddings, funerals, feasts, markets and the proper way to refuse a second helping.
