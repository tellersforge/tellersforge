# Heraldry & Genealogy

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Who is who, and who descends from whom. Heraldry reads the colours and beasts painted on shields, banners and seals; genealogy traces the lines of birth and marriage that make one cousin a duke and another a stable hand. Heralds, clerks of noble houses and lawyers in inheritance disputes all depend on it.

On a battlefield it tells a captain which knight to ransom and which to avoid. At court it tells a newcomer whose sister married whose rival. In a dispute over land it can win or lose everything, because a claim is only as good as the lineage behind it. In later ages the banners become corporate crests and the family trees become records, but the questions stay the same.
