# Carpentry & Joinery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Wood is the most forgiving material and the easiest to ruin. The carpenter frames houses and barns, raises roof trusses and builds bridges of timber; the joiner makes doors, tables, chests and cabinets, fitting pieces together so well that no nail is needed. Wheelwrights and coopers belong to the same family, bending staves into barrels and building wheels that will take a cart across rough country.

The work is read in the grain. A good woodworker knows which timber will twist, which will split and which will last for centuries, and chooses accordingly. Their tools are simple and their standards are not. Carvers turn the same skill to beauty, filling beams and screens with leaves, faces and beasts.
