# Service & Hospitality

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Making guests feel welcome. Setting a table, pouring wine without spilling a drop, finding a room for a late arrival, keeping an inn running smoothly through a busy market day. Servants, stewards, innkeepers and hosts of every kind practise it, and in many cultures the duty to host guests is sacred.

It is attentiveness and tact. The good servant anticipates needs before they are voiced, stays invisible when needed and knows every guest's rank and preferences. It is hard work that is rarely noticed when done well. It also gives access to private rooms, overheard conversations and the trust of the powerful.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** hosting, serving, stewarding guests, innkeeping
- **Why these attributes:** anticipating needs, then grace
- **What failure looks like:** The service is clumsy or a guest is neglected. Spectacular failure: a serious blunder that embarrasses the host and offends the guests.

## Not to be confused with

*[Etiquette](etiquette.md)*, which is personal manners; Service & Hospitality is the work of looking after others.
