# Survival: Jungle & Swamp

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Staying alive where everything grows, rots and bites. Jungle and swamp survival is keeping dry when nothing is dry, finding water that won't sicken you, building a sleeping platform above the mud, and knowing which leeches, snakes, fevers and fungi are merely unpleasant and which will kill. It is slow travel, sharp blades and constant vigilance.

Those who grow up in such country move through it with an easy patience that outsiders find baffling. Everyone else sweats, stumbles and itches, and learns that boots rot, maps lie and the river is never quite where it should be. Armies have been lost in such places without ever meeting an enemy.
