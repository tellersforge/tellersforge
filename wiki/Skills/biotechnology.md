# Biotechnology

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Medicine at the scale of cells. The biotechnologist grows tissue in dishes, reads and edits the code of life, cultures bacteria to make drugs, and uses laboratory machines to diagnose diseases no physician could see. Later ages turn this knowledge into engineered crops, custom organisms and treatments tailored to a single patient's blood.

It is precise and powerful work, done with pipettes, incubators and computers, and it raises questions no one has fully answered. Biotechnologists work in hospitals, companies, universities and secret laboratories. The best can make wonders; the careless can make disasters that grow on their own.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** computing · **Covers:** genetic engineering, cell culture, lab medicine
- **Why these attributes:** heavy science, judgement, technique
- **What failure looks like:** The culture fails or the results are unclear. Spectacular failure: contamination, a dangerous mutation, or something escapes the laboratory.

## Not to be confused with

*[Life Sciences](life-sciences.md)*, which studies living things; Biotechnology manipulates them in the lab for medicine and industry.
