# Haggling

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Bartering*

*aka: ______________________________*

The market-stall dance of price and counter-price. The seller names a figure, the buyer laughs, the seller clutches their heart, and somewhere between the two a fair price is found, or an unfair one that both can live with. In much of the world nobody pays the first price for anything, and not haggling is faintly insulting.

Haggling is presence first: looking like you might walk away and meaning it. Then comes reading the other side, spotting when they need the sale more than you need the goods. It is simple trade, one deal at a time, with a handshake or a spit in the palm at the end.

## Game block

- **Why these attributes:** presence, then reading the other side
- **What failure looks like:** The seller won't budge, or the price stays where it started. Spectacular failure: the character offends the trader, who refuses to deal with them at all.
