# Starship Engineering

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Keeping a ship alive between the stars. The starship engineer knows the drive, the reactor, the power grid, the air and water systems, and how they talk to each other. They patch hull breaches, reroute power around damage, coax more speed from a tired engine and keep the reactor from doing what reactors most want to do. They live half in the ship's belly, among humming machines, and can tell from a change in vibration that something is wrong.

Captains give orders; engineers make them possible. On long voyages the engineer is the most important person aboard and knows it. In an emergency, everyone else waits for them to say whether they will live.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/DEX/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **Needs:** spaceflight · **Covers:** drives, reactors, power, keeping a ship alive
- **Why these attributes:** heavy knowledge, diagnosis, hands, long shifts under strain
- **What failure looks like:** The system stays down, or the fix drains power elsewhere. Spectacular failure: a reactor, drive or life-support failure puts the whole ship at risk.

## Not to be confused with

*[Aerospace Engineering](aerospace-engineering.md)*, which designs new craft; Starship Engineering runs and repairs a ship already in space.
