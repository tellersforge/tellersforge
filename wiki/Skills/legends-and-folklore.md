# Legends & Folklore

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The tales grandmothers tell by the fire, the songs sung at harvest, and the strange rhymes children skip to without knowing why. Legends and folklore is knowing them, and more than that, knowing what they might mean. The ballad of the drowned bride may remember a real flood. The rhyme about the three grey stones may be directions.

Bards, wandering priests and curious scholars collect such things, and treasure hunters learn to listen to them. Folklore is rarely true in its details and rarely false in its heart. A character who knows it well can hear an old verse and say, with a chill, that they have heard this story before, somewhere far away, told a little differently.
