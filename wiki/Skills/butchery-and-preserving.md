# Butchery & Preserving

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A carcass is a season's food if it is handled right and a heap of spoiled meat if it is not. The butcher kills cleanly, bleeds, skins and joints the animal, and knows where every cut belongs. Then comes the harder part: keeping it. Salt, smoke, wind and sun, brine and vinegar, sealed jars and packed barrels all hold back rot for weeks or months, if the work is done well.

Every farmstead has someone who can do it, and every town has a shambles that smells of it. Armies, ships and expeditions live on what the preserver has packed. A good hand wastes nothing: hides go to the tanner, bones to the stock-pot, fat to candles, and the rest into sausages.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** slaughter, jointing, smoking, salting, drying, pickling, canning
- **Why these attributes:** knife work, then knowing when it's right
- **What failure looks like:** The meat is badly cut or the preserved food goes off sooner than expected. Spectacular failure: a whole store spoils unnoticed, and those who eat it fall sick.

## Not to be confused with

*[Cooking](cooking.md)*, which prepares a meal for today; Butchery & Preserving turns animals and harvests into food that lasts through winter or a long voyage.
