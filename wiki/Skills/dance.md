# Dance

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Moving to music, or to a rhythm older than music. The court dance with its careful steps and bows, the village circle round the fire at harvest, the ritual dance that calls rain or honours the dead, the performer on a stage who makes the whole audience forget to breathe. Dance covers social, ritual and performance forms.

Dancing well asks for grace, stamina and memory. It also asks for the courage to be watched. In many cultures a good dancer is admired, courted and trusted with sacred duties. At a noble ball, stepping on a partner's foot is a small disaster; in a temple, a misstep in the sacred dance may be taken as an evil omen. Dancers who master the art can say things with their bodies that words cannot.
