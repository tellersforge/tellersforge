# Folk Magic ✦

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The small, stubborn magic of ordinary people. A charm of red thread tied round a baby's wrist, a horseshoe over the door, a curse scratched on a lead tablet and dropped down a well, a rune carved on a spear shaft, a line of salt across a threshold. Folk magic covers charms, curses, wards, sigils, rune-carving and the reading of the land's hidden lines.

It is taught at the hearth, not in schools, and every village does it a little differently. The wise woman, the hedge-witch, the cunning man and the grandmother who knows a word or two all practise it. In some worlds it is harmless tradition; in others, it works. Either way, neighbours are careful not to offend those who know it.
