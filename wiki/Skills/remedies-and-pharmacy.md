# Remedies & Pharmacy

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Willow bark for fever, poppy for pain, moss on a wound and a bitter tea to settle the stomach. The herbalist knows which plants heal and which kill, when to gather them and how to dry, steep, grind and mix them. Apothecaries go further, compounding pills, tinctures and ointments by recipe, and later pharmacists work with measured doses and chemical drugs.

It is the most widespread of the healing crafts. Village wise women, monastery gardeners, shop apothecaries and ship's surgeons all practise some of it. Remedies are cheaper than doctors and often work as well. The difference between medicine and poison, as every good herbalist knows, is mostly the dose.
