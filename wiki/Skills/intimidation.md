# Intimidation

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Intimidation*

*aka: ______________________________*

Getting your way by making people afraid. A looming figure in a doorway, a quiet threat with a hand on a hilt, a stare that doesn't blink. Bullies, guards, bailiffs and warlords use it, and sometimes heroes do too. Intimidation works fast, but it breeds resentment and usually lasts only as long as the fear does.

It relies on presence, confidence and making the threat believable. Size and weapons help, but the best intimidators are often calm and small. They find what their target fears and lean on it. It fails against the brave, the desperate and those with nothing left to lose.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **COU**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** compliance through threat or presence
- **Why these attributes:** nerve to mean it, then presence (a strong body earns a circumstance bonus)
- **What failure looks like:** The target isn't impressed, or stands their ground. Spectacular failure: they laugh, call the bluff, or turn hostile and fight back.
