# Plumbing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Water in, waste out. The plumber lays pipes of clay, lead, copper or steel, joins them tight, and makes them run downhill where they should. Drains, sewers, fountains, cisterns and bathhouses all depend on this unglamorous knowledge. The trade is old: early cities had covered drains, and the rich had water brought to their courtyards long before the poor did.

Plumbers work in the wet, the dark and the smell, and they know every buried path beneath a city. They are called when things go wrong and blamed when they do not come quickly. Thieves, rebels and spies sometimes wish they had asked one first which tunnels lead where.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** wheel and roads · **Covers:** pipes, drains, sewers, fittings
- **Why these attributes:** handwork, then layout
- **What failure looks like:** The joint leaks or the drain backs up. Spectacular failure: a burst, a flood, or a sewer that empties where it should not.

## Not to be confused with

*[Civil Engineering](civil-engineering.md)*, which designs aqueducts and sewer systems; Plumbing builds and fixes the pipes and drains themselves.
