# Rowing & Paddling

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The oldest way to cross water that isn't swimming. A hunter kneels in a bark canoe and slips through reeds without a ripple; a fisherman rows out past the breakers before dawn; a hundred galley oarsmen pull together to the beat of a drum. Every coast and river in every age has known it.

Paddling is balance and reading the water. Rowing is rhythm and back muscle, and the knowledge that the boat goes where the stroke tells it, not where the rower looks. The skilled know how to cross a current without being swept away, when to rest in an eddy, how to bring a small boat through surf and how to land it without being rolled. Messengers, ferrymen, traders and raiders all learn it, and a river-people's children often paddle before they walk.
