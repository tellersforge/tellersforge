# Tradecraft

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The quiet professional habits of espionage. The spy lives inside a cover story, with papers, habits and friends to match. Messages are left in a hollow brick or a newspaper at the café, meetings are arranged with chalk marks on a lamppost, and informants are recruited, paid and kept from betraying each other. Tradecraft is the discipline that keeps a network alive.

It depends on writing, codes and patience, and on never doing the obvious thing twice. Good handlers can read a nervous agent and know when to pull them out. Intelligence services, rebels, merchant houses and royal courts all use it. A single mistake in tradecraft can unravel years of careful work and leave friends dead.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/CHA/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** writing · **Covers:** espionage: covers, dead drops, agent handling
- **Why these attributes:** planning, judgement, manipulation, nerve
- **What failure looks like:** the drop is missed, the cover is thin, the contact doesn't show. Spectacular failure: the network is compromised, and someone else now knows who the agents are.

## Not to be confused with

*[Shadowing & Surveillance](shadowing-and-surveillance.md)*, which follows and watches a target. Tradecraft runs the spy's whole secret life and network.
