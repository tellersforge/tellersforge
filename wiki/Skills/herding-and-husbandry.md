# Herding & Husbandry

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The raising and tending of livestock. Husbandry is breeding the best animals, keeping them fed and healthy, bringing them through birthing and winter, milking, shearing and keeping bees. Herding is moving animals: the shepherd with dog and crook, the drover pushing cattle to market, the nomad following the grass with the whole herd.

For most of history it is wealth itself. Dowries are paid in cattle and wars are fought over grazing rights. Herders know each animal, its temper and its worth, and read the flock's mood as a sailor reads the sea. A good herder brings the herd home fatter and larger than it went out.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** agriculture · **Covers:** raising, breeding and herding livestock; dairying, beekeeping
- **Why these attributes:** care and judgement, then long days
- **What failure looks like:** animals sicken, stray or fail to thrive. Spectacular failure: the herd stampedes, a disease sweeps through, or a season's breeding is lost.

## Not to be confused with

*[Animal Training](animal-training.md)*, which teaches individual animals tasks; Herding & Husbandry raises and moves them in numbers.
