# Electronic Warfare

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The invisible fight over the air itself. Before the guns speak, one side jams the other's radios with a howl of noise, fills their screens with false echoes, and listens to every word they say. Electronic Warfare is the art of blinding and deafening an enemy who depends on signals, while keeping one's own voice clear.

It is a quiet, intense skill practised by hunched technicians in the back of aircraft, in armoured vans and in starship sensor bays. They know which frequencies matter, how to hide a signal in another, and when to go silent. A good one can make a missile lose its target or an entire fleet think it faces a phantom squadron. A bad one gives away their own position and has only a few moments to regret it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** electricity · **Covers:** jamming, spoofing, interception
- **Why these attributes:** heavy knowledge, judgement, speed
- **What failure looks like:** the jamming doesn't bite, the deception is seen through, the message isn't caught. Spectacular failure: the operator's own signal gives away their position or scrambles their allies instead.

## Not to be confused with

*[Communications & Sensors](communications-and-sensors.md)*, which sends and reads signals honestly. Electronic Warfare jams, fakes and intercepts them.
