# Hacking

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Getting into a system that was built to keep you out. The hacker probes a network for a forgotten door, guesses at passwords, tricks a clerk into giving one up, or slips a small program past the guards and waits for it to open things from inside. Then they read, copy, change or wreck what they find, and try to leave no footprints behind.

It is a patient craft dressed up as a fast one. Most of the work is study: learning how a system is laid out, what software it runs and who looks after it. The other side of the skill is defence: spotting an intruder, closing the gap, laying a false trail. Criminals, spies, bored teenagers and corporate security teams all practise it, and they spend a lot of time watching one another.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** computing · **Covers:** breaking into systems and networks, countermeasures
- **Why these attributes:** heavy logic, intuition, speed
- **What failure looks like:** the system stays shut, and the attempt may be logged. Spectacular failure: the intruder is traced, locked out, or leaves something that points straight back to them.
