# Tattooing & Body Art

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Art on living skin. The tattooist pricks soot or ink beneath the skin in lines that will last a lifetime; the painter covers a warrior in patterns of clay and ochre before battle; the scar-maker cuts ritual marks that heal into raised designs of rank or courage. In many cultures such marks tell the story of a life: family, deeds, faith and oaths.

The work demands a steady hand, clean tools and knowledge of how skin heals. Mistakes are permanent, and infection is a real danger. Tattooists are artists, healers and keepers of meaning at once, and sailors, soldiers, priests and gangs all seek them out. A skilled one can hide a secret map in a sleeve of flowers; a careless one can mark a customer as something they never meant to be.
