# Arcana

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The theory of magic, studied as knowledge rather than worked as power. An arcanist knows the schools and traditions, how a ward is built, what a summoning circle is for, why certain sigils are carved on certain doors. They can identify a spell by its traces and guess at its maker from its style, much as a scholar recognises a writer by their hand.

It is entirely possible to know arcana and never cast so much as a spark. Many of the best theorists are like that, and many great casters understand remarkably little of what they do. In settings where magic is rare or secret, arcana is fragmentary and dangerous learning, kept in locked books and whispered between trusted friends.
