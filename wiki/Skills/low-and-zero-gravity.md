# Low & Zero Gravity

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Moving where down barely exists. Pushing off a wall and floating the length of a corridor, working on a spacecraft's hull with only a tether to hold you, fighting on a drifting island, or walking upside down across a ceiling in a strange realm. Those raised on the ground find it baffling and nauseating at first.

It is a whole new set of reflexes. Everything keeps moving until something stops it. A thrown tool pushes the thrower backward. A hard kick sends one spinning helplessly. Experts move with small, deliberate pushes, always keep a handhold, and never lose track of which way the nearest wall is.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/END/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — (the setting decides) · **Covers:** moving, working and fighting in low or no gravity: spacewalks, floating islands, falling fights, walking upside down
- **Why these attributes:** control, tolerance, spatial judgement
- **What failure looks like:** The character drifts, tumbles or can't get where they meant to go. Spectacular failure: they spin out of control, lose hold of their line or crash hard into something.
