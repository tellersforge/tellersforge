# Acrobatics

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

The body as an instrument. Tumbling, cartwheels, flips, springing off walls, rolling out of a fall and coming up on your feet. Travelling players and circus folk make a living from it; rooftop thieves and fleet-footed messengers find it equally useful. Some fighting traditions build their training around it.

Acrobatics is precision and nerve. It combines balance, jumping and timing into flowing movement. A good acrobat treats the world as a playground: a railing is a handhold, a wall a step, and a long drop merely something to roll out of. Heavy armour and packs put an end to all of it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/COU/STR · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** tumbling, flips, breaking falls, rooftop running
- **Why these attributes:** agility, nerve to commit, strength to carry it
- **What failure looks like:** The move doesn't come off; the character stumbles or stops. Spectacular failure: a hard fall, a sprain, or crashing into the thing they meant to vault.

## Not to be confused with

*[Balance](balance.md)*, which keeps one steady on a narrow surface; and *[Jumping](jumping.md)*, which is one leap. Acrobatics links flips, rolls and falls into flowing movement.
