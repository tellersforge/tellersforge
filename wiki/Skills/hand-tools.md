# Hand Tools

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Crafting & Repair*

*aka: ______________________________*

The common tools that every household owns. A knife for whittling and cutting cord, an axe for firewood, a hoe for the garden, a hammer and nails, a saw for a new shelf. Using them well is so ordinary that people only notice when someone does it badly, usually with a yelp.

Hand tools are the foundation of every craft and the first thing a child learns to hold in many cultures. A handy person can put up a fence, fix a door, split kindling and sharpen their own blade. They can't build a house or forge a sword, but they can keep one in good order.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/STR · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** knife, axe, hoe, hammer, saw
- **Why these attributes:** handling, then force
- **What failure looks like:** The job is slow, rough or has to be redone. Spectacular failure: the tool breaks, the work is ruined, or the user cuts or hits themselves.

## Not to be confused with

*[Carpentry & Joinery](carpentry-and-joinery.md)*, which is the skilled trade of building with wood; Hand Tools is everyday use of common tools for simple jobs.
