# Nature Lore

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Lore*

*aka: ______________________________*

Knowing the living world as a whole. Which bird calls before rain, which tree grows only near water, what it means when the deer leave a valley early. Nature lore is the knowledge of herders, druids, hunters and farmers, built up over lifetimes of watching: the habits of beasts, the seasons of plants, and the countless small signs by which the wild tells what it is about to do.

It is practical knowledge, not a catalogue. The one who has it can name a plant and also say what eats it, what it cures and where its cousins grow. They read a landscape the way a scholar reads a page, and they notice at once when something in it is wrong.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** plants, animals, ecology, natural signs
- **Why these attributes:** knowledge, then observation
- **What failure looks like:** the sign is missed or the creature misidentified. Spectacular failure: the character confidently declares something harmless that very much is not.
