# Mortuary Arts

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The dead need care too. The mortician washes, dresses and prepares bodies for burial or burning, embalms them to slow decay, and makes them presentable for grieving families. Some traditions keep the dead for centuries with salts, resins and linen wrappings. In later ages, the same knowledge supports autopsy: opening a body carefully to find out how and why it died.

The work is quiet, solemn and misunderstood. People are uneasy around those who handle the dead, yet they seek them out in times of loss. A good mortician is patient, respectful and unshaken, and notices small details on the body that others would miss, which makes them useful to investigators as well as mourners.
