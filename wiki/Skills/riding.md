# Riding

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Animal Handling*

*aka: ______________________________*

Staying on, and going where you mean to. Riding is the partnership of rider and mount: horse, camel, reindeer, mule or stranger beasts in stranger worlds. It is knowing the animal's pace and temper, handling it on rough ground, urging it on without breaking it, and keeping one's seat when it shies at a flapping cloak.

In many ages the rider is a class apart, raised above those on foot. Nomads ride before they walk, messengers ride through the night, knights and herders both spend half their lives in the saddle. A good rider and a good mount together can cover ground and do things neither could alone.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** agriculture · **Covers:** riding horses, camels, reindeer and other mounts
- **Why these attributes:** balance, then feel for the animal
- **What failure looks like:** the mount balks, stumbles or goes its own way. Spectacular failure: the rider is thrown, or the mount bolts with them.
