# Veterinary Care

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The lame horse, the bloated cow, the hound with a thorn in its paw and a fever behind its eyes. Animals cannot say where it hurts, so the healer of beasts learns to read them: the way they stand, what they refuse to eat, the heat of a hoof and the colour of a gum. Much of the work is dirty, some of it is dangerous, and all of it depends on hands steady enough to set a leg while the patient tries to kick.

Farriers belong here too, trimming and shoeing hooves and knowing the difference between a bad shoe and a bad foot. In farming country the animal doctor is as valued as any physician, since a dead plough-ox can ruin a family as surely as a fever.

## Not to be confused with

*[Medicine](medicine.md)*, which treats people; Veterinary Care works on patients who cannot explain themselves and would often rather bite than be helped.
