# Aerospace Engineering

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Designing things that fly: aircraft, rockets, gliders, orbital craft. The aerospace engineer balances weight, lift, thrust, fuel and structure, and knows that a mistake measured in grams or seconds can bring the whole thing down. They work with wind tunnels, test flights, models and calculations, and then watch nervously as someone brave climbs aboard.

It is one of the most demanding crafts in any age, and the history of flight is written in the wreckage of its lessons. The engineers who succeed are cautious, stubborn and a little romantic. A new wing shape or engine design can change warfare, trade and exploration in a single generation.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 5 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/INT/WIS/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 5
- **Needs:** electricity · **Covers:** aircraft and spacecraft design
- **Why these attributes:** extreme design load, judgement, building
- **What failure looks like:** The design is sluggish, unstable or impossible to build. Spectacular failure: a flaw appears in flight, and the craft comes apart.

## Not to be confused with

*[Starship Engineering](starship-engineering.md)*, which keeps an existing starship's systems alive; Aerospace Engineering designs aircraft and spacecraft in the first place.
