# Construction

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The practical business of putting up buildings and moving earth. Construction covers the labourer's knowledge: digging footings, raising walls of whatever the land provides, getting a roof on before the rains, plastering, building scaffolds and hauling everything up them. It is heavy work that rewards strength, but also a sense of order, since a job done in the wrong sequence has to be done twice.

Every village has people who can build a barn with their neighbours, and every army has soldiers who can throw up a rampart overnight. They are not architects and do not pretend to be. They make what the plan says, find a way when the plan is wrong, and know which shortcuts will hold and which will fall down in a year.

## Not to be confused with
