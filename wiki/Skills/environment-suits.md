# Environment Suits

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Living inside a small, sealed world. The hard-hat diver clumping along the seabed in a copper helmet, the rescue worker in a rubber suit walking into a gas cloud, the spacer drifting outside the hull on a tether. Each depends on a suit that keeps out what would kill them, and on knowing how to move, work and breathe in it.

Suits are clumsy, hot and loud with the sound of one's own breathing. Fingers go numb in heavy gloves, a visor fogs, a hose kinks. The skill is in the small habits: checking seals twice, pacing the air, moving so the suit doesn't fight back, and staying calm when an alarm starts to chime. Those who master it can work for hours where others would last minutes. Those who panic tend not to get a second lesson.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** steam · **Covers:** vacuum suits, hazmat, hard-hat diving gear
- **Why these attributes:** movement, then tolerance
- **What failure looks like:** the wearer tires quickly, fumbles the work or uses up air too fast. Spectacular failure: a seal fails, a line tangles, or the suit itself becomes the danger.

## Not to be confused with

*[Powered Armour](powered-armour.md)*, which moves in a suit that amplifies strength. An environment suit only protects; it gives no extra power.
