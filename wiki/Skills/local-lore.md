# Local Lore

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Lore*

*aka: ______________________________*

Knowing your own patch. Which well is sweet and which is foul, who owns the mill and who really runs it, where the ford is shallow in summer, which old woman to ask about the lights on the hill. Everyone who grew up somewhere has some of it, and the ones who stayed longest have the most.

Local lore is lived experience before it is memory. It is news, rumour, family feuds, saints' days and the price of eggs. It is worthless a few valleys away, where different people know different things, and priceless at home, where it can find a friend, avoid a quarrel or name a stranger in the crowd.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** your home area, its people, news and customs
- **Why these attributes:** lived experience, then memory
- **What failure looks like:** The character doesn't know, or remembers something out of date. Spectacular failure: they pass on an old rumour as fact and send everyone the wrong way.
