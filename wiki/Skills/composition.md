# Composition

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The craft of putting words together well. Composition covers prose and verse, songs and epics, reports and speeches, letters and newspaper columns. The composer chooses the words, orders them, and makes them do what they are meant to do: persuade, entertain, inform or wound. Poets, chroniclers, playwrights and journalists all practise it.

Good writing looks easy. It is not. Behind a clear report or a stirring ballad are many drafts, much crossing out, and a feel for rhythm and for what the reader needs. A well-composed piece can outlast the one who wrote it by centuries, and a vicious one can end a career overnight.
