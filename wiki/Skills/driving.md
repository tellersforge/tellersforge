# Driving

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Vehicles*

*aka: ______________________________*

Hands on the wheel, eyes on the road, and a sense of what every other driver is about to do wrong. Driving covers motor cars, lorries and motorcycles, from a gentle Sunday outing to a delivery through a crowded city to a chase through narrow streets with someone shooting from the passenger window.

Most people who drive do it well enough, which is why the skilled stand out. They know how a heavy van leans on a bend, how a motorcycle behaves on wet cobbles, how long it takes a car to stop at speed. At its finest the skill becomes stunt work: handbrake turns, jumps, driving on two wheels, slipping through gaps no wider than a coat of paint. Couriers, taxi drivers, getaway drivers and police all have it, and all of them think they are better at it than they are.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/PER/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** electricity · **Covers:** cars, trucks, motorcycles; pursuit and stunt driving at higher levels
- **Why these attributes:** control, then judgement
- **What failure looks like:** the vehicle stalls, skids or loses the pursuit. Spectacular failure: a crash, a rollover, or a chase that ends in the wrong kind of wall.
