# Intrigue & Politics

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The game played in corridors, in letters that are burned after reading, and in the whispers before a vote. Intrigue is knowing who owes whom, who hates whom, and who can be made to need something. A courtier who has it hears of a plot before it is hatched and makes sure to be standing on the right side when it breaks. A candidate who has it wins elections without seeming to fight them.

It takes nerve as much as cunning. Rumours must be started without fingerprints, alliances made with people who would sell you tomorrow, and a smile kept on a face that has just learned it was betrayed. Every era has its factions, and every faction needs someone who plays this game well.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/CHA/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** factions, leverage, court politics, elections, whispering campaigns
- **Why these attributes:** scheming, influence, reading people, nerve to play
- **What failure looks like:** the scheme goes nowhere, and a rival quietly gains ground. Spectacular failure: the plot is exposed and traced straight back to the schemer.

## Not to be confused with

*[Diplomacy](diplomacy.md)*, which seeks agreement openly between groups; Intrigue works in the shadows to win within one.
