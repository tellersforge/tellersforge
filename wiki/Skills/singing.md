# Singing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The oldest music, carried in the body. A mother hums over a cradle, a work gang chants to keep the rhythm of the hammers, a choir fills a stone hall until the dust seems to shiver. Singing covers song, chant and choral work, from a drinking song in a crowded inn to a temple hymn sung the same way for a thousand years.

A trained voice carries, holds a note steadily, and moves listeners in ways they can't explain. Singers learn breath, pitch and the words of a great many songs. They are welcome at feasts, useful at funerals and sometimes dangerous at rallies. In many places a good voice is a passport: a wandering singer is fed and housed wherever people want to hear the old songs again.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2

- **What failure looks like:** the voice cracks, the pitch wanders, the audience talks through it. Spectacular failure: the singer forgets the words in front of everyone, or sings something that deeply offends.
