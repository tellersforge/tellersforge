# Shipwrighting

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Building a vessel that will float, sail and survive a storm. The shipwright might hollow a log into a canoe, sew planks into a trading boat, lay down the keel of a war galley or, in later ages, rivet steel plates onto a hull. Each age has its methods, but the work is always the same in spirit: shaping the hull to the water, making it strong without making it heavy, and sealing every seam.

Shipyards are loud, busy places full of carpenters, smiths and ropemakers, and the shipwright directs them all. The good ones have an eye for the line of a hull that no plan fully captures. A badly built ship can sink on its first voyage, and a good one can outlast its crew.
