# Literacy (per script)

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Lore*

*aka: ______________________________*

The ability to read and write one system of letters, signs or characters. In many ages it is a rare and guarded thing, held by priests, clerks and the children of the rich, and a peasant who can read a contract is a peasant who can't be cheated with one. In other ages nearly everyone has it, and the one who lacks it hides the fact carefully.

Each script must be learned on its own. Knowing one alphabet gives a head start on its cousins, but a reader of flowing temple characters stands helpless before a merchant's tally marks from across the sea. Literacy opens every book, ledger, notice and letter, which is why tyrants have so often tried to keep it scarce.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **INT** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **Needs:** writing · **Covers:** reading and writing one script
- **Why these attributes:** learning
- **What failure looks like:** the text is misread or only half understood. Spectacular failure: the reader is sure it says something it plainly does not.

## Not to be confused with

*[Language (per language)](language-per-language.md)*, which is speaking and understanding a tongue; one can speak a language fluently and still not read a word of it.
