# Balance

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Keeping your feet where there is little to keep them on. A narrow ledge above a drop, a beam across a gorge, the rolling deck of a ship in a squall, an icy log over a stream. Sailors have it, roofers need it, rope-walkers make a living from it and drunkards temporarily lose it.

It is a quiet steadiness of body and mind. Good balance means knees soft, eyes forward and weight low, with small corrections that no one else notices. Wind, a heavy pack, a blow from an enemy or a slippery surface make it harder, and the worst thing a balancing person can do is look down and think about it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** ledges, beams, rolling decks
- **Why these attributes:** body control, then nerve
- **What failure looks like:** The character wobbles, freezes or has to retreat. Spectacular failure: they fall, and whatever is beneath is now a problem.

## Not to be confused with

*[Acrobatics](acrobatics.md)*, which is tumbling, flipping and breaking falls; Balance keeps you upright and still on a narrow or moving surface.
