# Sign Language & Gesture

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Speaking with the hands. Some sign languages are full tongues, used by communities of the deaf, with grammar as rich as any spoken speech. Others are working codes: hunters signalling across a clearing, soldiers clearing a building in silence, traders haggling across a noisy market with fingers alone. All of them let people talk where voices cannot or must not be heard.

The one who has the skill can hold a conversation across a crowded room, warn a friend without a sound, and pick up the gist of a stranger's signs by watching closely. It is invaluable to thieves, sentries and anyone who shares a secret in the presence of enemies.

- **What failure looks like:** the message is misread, or only partly understood. Spectacular failure: a sign is misunderstood at the crucial moment, and someone acts on the wrong message.

## Not to be confused with

*[Signals](signals.md)*, which sends messages over distance with flags, fires or instruments; sign language is close, fast and personal.
