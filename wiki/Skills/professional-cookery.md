# Professional Cookery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Feeding a family is one thing. Feeding a hall of guests, a regiment or a lord who has eaten everything is another. The professional cook plans days ahead, runs a kitchen full of boys, fires and noise, and keeps a hundred dishes moving so that each arrives hot and in its turn. Pastry, sauces, roasts, sugar work and the showpiece swan in its feathers all belong to the trade.

Great cooks are artists and quartermasters at once. They taste constantly, bargain with suppliers, and know that a feast can make an alliance or ruin one. In palaces they are quietly powerful, and in inns their name brings travellers back. A banquet is a small campaign, and the kitchen is its field headquarters.
