# Musical Composition

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Writing music that others will play. The composer hears a melody in their head, catches it on paper or holds it in memory, and builds it into a song, a hymn, a march or a long work for many players. Arranging is part of the craft too: taking an old tune and fitting it for a choir, a band of pipes or an orchestra.

It is solitary, stubborn work, done by candle or by lamp with an instrument close by. Composers are hired to celebrate a coronation, mourn a ruler, give a festival its tune or give an army its marching song. The best works outlive their makers by centuries, hummed by people who have never heard the composer's name.
