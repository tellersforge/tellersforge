# Carousing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The art of the long night out. Drinking deep without losing your wits, joining in the songs, making friends at every table and waking up with more information than a headache. Soldiers, sailors, merchants and spies all know the tavern is where news travels fastest, and where the careless tell their secrets.

Carousing is stamina, sociability and a hard head. The good carouser keeps up with the crowd while staying sharp, knows when to buy a round and when to slip away, and remembers who said what. The bad one ends up in a ditch, or in debt, or married.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** drinking without losing your wits, tavern gossip
- **Why these attributes:** holding your liquor, then being good company
- **What failure looks like:** The character hears nothing useful and wakes with a sore head. Spectacular failure: they get drunk, start a brawl, spill their own secrets or lose their purse.

## Not to be confused with

*[Streetwise](streetwise.md)*, which works the underworld for contacts and rumours; Carousing gathers gossip by drinking and socialising.
