# Trapping

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Survival*

*aka: ______________________________*

Letting the forest do the hunting. The trapper sets snares on game trails, builds deadfalls weighted with stones, weaves fish traps and places them in the current, and returns later to see what the night has caught. It takes knowledge of animal habits, a light touch that leaves no human scent, and patience.

Trappers are often solitary folk, working long lines through wild country and trading furs in the spring. The skill feeds families through hard winters and is a living in itself where pelts fetch good prices. A careless trapper catches nothing, or catches the neighbour's dog, or catches a bear that is still very much alive when he returns.
