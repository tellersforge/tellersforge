# Climbing

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Athletics*

*aka: ______________________________*

Up a tree to look for smoke, over a garden wall at night, up a knotted rope to a ship's rigging or a crumbling cliff to a bird's nest full of eggs. Climbing is pulling your own weight upward and trusting each hold before you lean on it. Children do it for fun. Thieves, sailors, egg-gatherers and besiegers do it for a living.

A good climber moves slowly and looks bored. They test every handhold, keep three points on the rock and never look down for longer than they need to. A bad climber is fast, cheerful and briefly airborne. Ropes, spikes and a friend below all make it easier, and wet stone, darkness and someone shooting at you make it much harder.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/STR · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** trees, walls, ropes, rocks
- **Why these attributes:** pulling your weight, placing hands and feet
- **What failure looks like:** The climber stalls, can't find the next hold and has to go back down. Spectacular failure: they fall, drop their gear, or bring loose rock down on whoever is below.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mountaineering](mountaineering.md)*, which is the technical craft of ice, altitude and rope teams on real mountains; Climbing gets you over the wall or up the tree.
