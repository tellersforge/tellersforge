# Camouflage

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Hiding things and places, rather than oneself on the move. Camouflage builds a hunter's hide from branches and grass, a sniper's nest in rubble, a camp that looks like nothing from the next ridge, a gun position that blends into the hedge. It is knowing how eyes scan a landscape and what makes them stop.

The craft uses whatever is to hand: mud, leaves, netting, paint, shadows, the smoke of a fire kept small. Soldiers, hunters, bandits and fugitives all learn it. A good hide can hold a lookout for days. A poor one has a straight edge or a gleam of metal, and the enemy sees it at once.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** hides, concealed camps and positions
- **Why these attributes:** eye for terrain, then making it
- **What failure looks like:** the hide or camp is easy to spot. Spectacular failure: the concealment itself draws the eye, and the enemy knows exactly where to look.

## Not to be confused with

*[Stealth](stealth.md)*, which is moving and staying unseen personally; Camouflage makes the position or object blend in.
