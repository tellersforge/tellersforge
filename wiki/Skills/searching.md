# Searching

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Investigation*

*aka: ______________________________*

Room by room, pocket by pocket, crate by crate. Searching is the patient, methodical hunt for something hidden: tapping the floorboards, turning out the mattress, running a hand along the underside of a table, checking the cargo hold. Customs officers, thieves, soldiers and bailiffs learn it, and so do anxious parents looking for lost keys.

A good searcher works in order and misses nothing. Speed costs thoroughness, and thoroughness costs time. The best know where people hide things, which tends to be in the same places the world over. They can leave a room as they found it, or leave it in pieces.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** methodically searching a room, body or cargo
- **Why these attributes:** method, then noticing
- **What failure looks like:** The hidden thing stays hidden. Spectacular failure: the search is noticed, leaves obvious traces, or sets off whatever was guarding it.

## Not to be confused with

*Perception (attribute)*, which is noticing without looking; and *[Investigation](investigation.md)*, which works out what the clues mean.
