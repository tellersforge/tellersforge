# Mathematics

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Technology*

*aka: ______________________________*

The art of number, shape and proof. Mathematics begins with the land-measurers who needed to restore fields after the flood and the builders who needed right angles, and grows into geometry, algebra, the calculus of change, and the statistics of whole nations. A mathematician can work out what everyone else can only guess.

In a story it is quiet power. It calculates the fall of a shot, the strength of a beam, the odds in a rigged game, and the orbit of a ship. Most people find it cold. The few who love it speak of it the way others speak of music, and cannot quite explain why everyone else doesn't hear it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** writing · **Covers:** geometry, algebra, calculus, statistics
- **Why these attributes:** reasoning twice over, then insight
- **What failure looks like:** the sum is wrong, and the error goes unnoticed. Spectacular failure: the whole plan rests on the mistake, and it shows only when the plan is tested.
