# Survival: Caves & Underground

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Living in the dark beneath the earth. Underground survival is rationing light, marking the way back, finding water that drips from stone, and listening for the change in the air that means a shaft ahead or a flood coming. It is knowing bad air by the flicker of a flame, loose rock by its sound, and the rising river that fills a passage in minutes.

Miners, cavers, hermits and those who fled below all learn it. The dark plays tricks: distances lie, echoes mislead, and time drifts. Those who know the underground keep calm, move carefully and always know how to get out. Those who don't are rarely seen again.
