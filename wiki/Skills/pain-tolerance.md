# Pain Tolerance

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Holding on while it hurts. Walking on a twisted ankle, keeping still while a wound is stitched without anything for the pain, gritting one's teeth through a burn, refusing to cry out under the lash. Soldiers and labourers learn it the hard way, and some cultures test their young by it.

Pain tolerance is not bravery, though they help each other. It is the ability to keep thinking and moving while the body screams to stop. Those who have it are often quiet about their injuries, which makes them hard to nurse. Those who push it too far pay later, when the adrenaline fades.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** enduring pain, injury or torture without breaking
- **Why these attributes:** toughness, then will
- **What failure looks like:** The pain wins for a moment: the character cries out, flinches or has to stop. Spectacular failure: they black out or break completely.

## Not to be confused with

*[Composure](composure.md)*, which is keeping one's head under fear and stress; and *Endurance (attribute)*, which is lasting through hunger and fatigue. Pain Tolerance is for injury.
