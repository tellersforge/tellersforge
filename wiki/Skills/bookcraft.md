# Bookcraft

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Everything that turns writing into books. Rags or bark are pulped and pressed into paper; hides are scraped into parchment; soot, gall and gum become ink. Then come copying or printing, with movable type or carved blocks, and at last the binding: sewing the gatherings, fitting the boards, covering them in leather and tooling the title in gold.

Bookmakers serve scholars, priests, lawyers and kings, and later publishers and propagandists. The workshop smells of glue, ink and damp paper. A fine book is a small fortune; a cheap pamphlet can start a revolt. Bookmakers know how to make an old volume look older than it is, and how to hide something inside the spine.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** writing · **Covers:** papermaking, printing, bookbinding, ink
- **Why these attributes:** handwork, process, care
- **What failure looks like:** The pages smear, the paper tears or the binding falls apart. Spectacular failure: a press breaks, or an error is printed a thousand times.

## Not to be confused with

*[Calligraphy & Scribing](calligraphy-and-scribing.md)*, which is the art of the written hand; Bookcraft makes paper, ink, printed pages and the book that holds them.
