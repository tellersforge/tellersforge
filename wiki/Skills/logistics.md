# Logistics

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Armies march on their stomachs, and so do households, caravans and expeditions. Logistics is the art of having enough of everything in the right place at the right time: grain, fodder, water, boots, arrows, fuel, spare wheels. It is sums on slates, carts on roads, depots set up weeks before they are needed, and the grim arithmetic of how long a siege can last.

The quartermaster is rarely a hero in the songs. But the general who ignores one loses, and the expedition that forgets one starves in the snow. A good logistician looks at a plan and asks the only question that matters: what will they eat on the fourth day?

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** supply, rationing, provisioning a household, expedition or army
- **Why these attributes:** planning, then foresight
- **What failure looks like:** supplies run short, or pile up where nobody needs them. Spectacular failure: a critical shortage is discovered at the worst moment, far from any help.
