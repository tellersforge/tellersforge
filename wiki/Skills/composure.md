# Composure

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Insight*

*aka: ______________________________*

Keeping your head when everything is going wrong. Staying calm in a fire, keeping a straight face while lying at cards, refusing a tempting bribe without flinching, standing firm when a monster roars. Composure is the quiet strength that keeps panic, rage or greed from taking the reins.

It is self-control and a steady mind. Those who have it are trusted in crises and feared across a gaming table. It can be learnt through discipline or hardship, and it shows in small ways: a steady voice, still hands, the pause before answering. It does not stop fear or temptation. It stops them from showing, and from deciding.

## Game block
