# Metallurgy

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Before anyone can forge a sword, someone must make the metal. The metallurgist roasts and smelts ore in a furnace, drives off slag, mixes copper and tin into bronze or iron and carbon into steel, and pours the molten result into moulds. Assaying belongs here too: testing a coin or an ingot to know what it is truly made of and whether it has been cut with something cheaper.

The work is half fire and half knowledge. Old metallurgists knew their furnaces by smell, colour and sound; later ones by tables and gauges. Each age has its own secrets, and cities grow rich on the ones they keep. A good metallurgist can tell by a glance whether a blade will hold an edge or shatter.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/END/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** copper · **Covers:** smelting, alloying, casting, assaying
- **Why these attributes:** knowledge, heat, judgement
- **What failure looks like:** The melt is impure or the alloy wrong. Spectacular failure: the furnace bursts, or metal that looks sound fails once it is worked or used.

## Not to be confused with

*[Blacksmithing](blacksmithing.md)*, which shapes the metal into tools; Metallurgy makes the metal itself from ore and assays its quality.
