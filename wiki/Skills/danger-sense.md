# Danger Sense

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Survival*

*aka: ______________________________*

The prickle at the back of the neck. A path that is too quiet, a door slightly ajar, an innkeeper who smiles a little too much. Danger sense is the gut feeling that something is wrong before the mind can say what. Old soldiers, hunters and thieves swear by it, and those who ignore it rarely get old.

It is not magic, though some believe it. It is a lifetime of small signals, the missing birdsong, the too-fresh footprints, noticed without thinking. It gives no details, only a warning: stop, look, go back. Those who trust it lose some opportunities and keep all their limbs.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** gut feeling for ambushes and traps
- **Why these attributes:** instinct
- **What failure looks like:** No warning comes. Spectacular failure: the character feels danger in the wrong place, and leads everyone away from safety straight into trouble.

## Not to be confused with

*Perception (attribute)*, which notices specific details; and *[Traps](traps.md)*, which finds and disarms devices. Danger Sense is a vague warning without knowing why.

## Relations

- **Close to:** Perception (attribute), [Traps](traps.md)
