# Fire-Making

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Survival*

*aka: ______________________________*

Coaxing a flame from nothing. Flint and steel over dry tinder, a bow drill spinning until the dust smokes, a match struck in the wind with one hand cupped around it, or simply getting a damp stove to light at dawn. Once lit, a fire has to be fed, banked, sheltered and kept from spreading, which is a skill of its own.

It is knowing conditions first and technique second. Which wood burns hot, which smokes, which spits. How to build the fire so the air gets in. The fire-maker is the first friend of every cold, wet and hungry traveller, and in older times the keeper of a village's hearth was an honoured post.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** lighting and tending fires, stoves, heaters
- **Why these attributes:** knowing conditions, then technique
- **What failure looks like:** The fire won't catch, or smoulders and dies. Spectacular failure: it gets away, catching a tent, a roof or a hillside of dry grass.
