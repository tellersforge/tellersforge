# Electronics

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Circuits, components and the small, precise flows of current that carry signals rather than power. The electronics expert designs, builds and repairs radios, computers, sensors, alarms and every device that thinks or listens. They read diagrams like prose, solder with steady hands, and can make a broken set talk again with a few spare parts and a patient afternoon.

The craft belongs to modern and future ages, and it changes faster than any other. Yesterday's genius is today's apprentice. A skilled practitioner is useful everywhere: repairing a ship's sensors, building a hidden listening device, or finding out exactly what a strange gadget does before someone switches it on.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/DEX/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** electricity · **Covers:** circuits, devices, repair and design
- **Why these attributes:** heavy knowledge, fine hands, diagnosis
- **What failure looks like:** The device does not work, or does something unexpected. Spectacular failure: it burns out, destroys other equipment, or broadcasts when it should be silent.

## Not to be confused with

*[Electrical Work](electrical-work.md)*, which installs wiring and power; Electronics designs and repairs the circuits and devices that run on it.
