# Gunsmithing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A gun is a tube that has to survive an explosion inside it, thousands of times. The gunsmith bores barrels, fits locks and triggers, shapes stocks, and makes sure each part works with the others. Early smiths hammer barrels around a mandrel; later ones cut rifling, machine bolts and assemble magazines. Repair, cleaning and quiet improvement are the daily bread.

Soldiers, hunters and duellists all depend on them. A gunsmith can make a weapon shoot straighter, fire faster or hide inside a walking stick. Their workshops are careful places, and the careless ones do not practise long. Good gunsmiths can also look at a broken weapon and tell exactly what happened to it and who has been careless.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX/DEX/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** gunpowder · **Covers:** building, repairing and modifying firearms
- **Why these attributes:** design, then very fine handwork, judgement
- **What failure looks like:** The gun misfires, jams or shoots wide. Spectacular failure: it bursts in the shooter's hands.

## Not to be confused with

*[Weaponsmithing & Armouring](weaponsmithing-and-armouring.md)*, which makes blades and armour; Gunsmithing builds and repairs firearms and their mechanisms.
