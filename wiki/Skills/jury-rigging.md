# Jury-Rigging

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Fixing it with what you have, now, while it is on fire. The jury-rigger does not have the right part, the right tool or the time, and gets it working anyway: a broken mast spliced with a spar and rope, an engine held together with wire, a leaking hull plugged with a blanket and a prayer. The repair will not last, but it only needs to last long enough.

Soldiers, sailors, mechanics and scavengers learn it the hard way. It rewards cool heads, quick hands and a willingness to try something foolish. The best of them have pockets full of string, wire and odd parts, and a look of calm that only makes sense once the machine starts.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** quick fixes with the wrong parts under pressure
- **Why these attributes:** hands, then improvisation
- **What failure looks like:** The fix does not hold or does not work at all. Spectacular failure: it holds just long enough to fail at the worst moment, perhaps taking something else with it.
