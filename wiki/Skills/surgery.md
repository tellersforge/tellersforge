# Surgery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The knife and the nerve to use it. Surgeons cut into living bodies to fix what cannot be fixed from the outside: they remove arrows and bullets, amputate shattered limbs, stitch wounds, set bones, pull teeth and in later ages open the chest to repair the heart. The work needs anatomical knowledge, steady hands, a strong stomach and courage.

For most of history surgery is brutal, fast and frightening, with the patient awake and held down. Later it becomes precise, clean and quiet, with sleep drugs and sterile instruments. In every age, a surgeon's decisions come quickly and cannot be undone. Many people owe their lives to one, and some owe their deaths.
