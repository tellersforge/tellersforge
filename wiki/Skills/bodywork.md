# Bodywork

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Hands that know where the pain lives. Bodywork eases stiff muscles, loosens knotted backs, puts a dislocated shoulder gently back where it belongs, and in some traditions uses needles placed along the body's hidden pathways. It covers the bathhouse masseur, the wrestler's rubber, the bonesetter's gentler cousin and the physical therapist who teaches an injured soldier to walk again.

The skill is intimate and practical. A good practitioner reads a body by touch, notices where it guards itself and works patiently toward relief. Athletes, dancers, labourers and aching nobles all seek them out, and more secrets are told on their tables than anywhere else in the city.

*[Medicine](medicine.md)*, which diagnoses and treats illness; Bodywork relieves pain and stiffness through touch and movement.
