# Cargo Handling

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Moving goods without breaking them, losing them or letting them shift in a storm. The stevedore knows which crates go in first and which last, how to balance a hold so the ship sits true, and how to lash a load so it survives rough seas. The caravan master knows how much a camel will carry, and how to pack it so that it doesn't rub raw on the third day.

It is heavy, practical and underrated. A clever packer fits a third more into the same space; a careless one sinks the ship. Dockers, porters, muleteers, warehouse keepers and freight crews on starships all practise it, and all of them can tell at a glance when someone else has packed badly. In ports it comes with its own slang, its own guilds and its own opinions about who deserves to work the next ship.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/STR · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2

- **What failure looks like:** the load is slow to stow, badly balanced or damaged in transit. Spectacular failure: it shifts, spills or brings down the vessel, animal or wagon carrying it.
