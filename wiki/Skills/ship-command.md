# Ship Command

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A large ship is not sailed by one person; it is sailed by a hundred people who have to be told what to do, at the right time, in the right order, without a moment's doubt. Ship Command is the art of handling a vessel through its crew: giving the orders, reading how they are carried out, and knowing when to trust the officers and when to take over.

A captain stands on deck or bridge and must hold the whole ship in mind: wind, sea, crew, cargo, enemy, shore. They have to judge what the ship can do and what the crew can bear. Merchant masters, navy captains, pirate chiefs and starship commanders all practise it. The good ones are remembered as calm voices in the storm; the bad ones as the reason for mutinies.
