# Trading & Business

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Running a venture that makes more than it costs. The merchant buys spices where they are cheap and carries them where they are dear. The shopkeeper knows which goods sell in winter and which gather dust. The mill owner juggles wages, suppliers, buyers and rivals, and wakes each morning hoping the balance still leans the right way.

Trading & Business is broader than a single deal. It covers choosing what to sell, finding partners, keeping customers, hiring the right help and spotting a market before others do. It is practised by caravan traders, market-stall families, guild masters, factory owners and interstellar merchant houses alike. Success brings wealth and influence; failure brings debt, and sometimes a hurried departure by night.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/CHA/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** merchant ventures, shops, sales, running an enterprise
- **Why these attributes:** planning, dealing, reading markets
- **What failure looks like:** the venture stalls, loses money or misses its moment. Spectacular failure: the business collapses, or a partner walks off with the profits.
