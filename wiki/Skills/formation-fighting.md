# Formation Fighting

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Fighting as one body instead of many. The shield wall locks together, rims overlapping; spears bristle from the second rank; the whole line steps forward on the call. Formation Fighting covers drill, the discipline of holding a place in the ranks and the skill of moving and striking with the soldiers on either side.

It is less glamorous than single combat and far more decisive. A trained formation can stop a charge that would scatter a crowd of braver warriors. Soldiers drill until turning, closing ranks and bracing become instinct. Militias, legions, pike squares and riot police all practise it. The worst sin is to break ranks: once one person runs, the whole line may follow.

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/COU/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
