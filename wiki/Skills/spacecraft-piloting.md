# Spacecraft Piloting

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The hands on the controls when a ship leaves a world or comes home to one. Launching through the thickening air, matching speed with a station until the docking clamps catch, dropping onto a landing field in the dark, twisting through a fight between fighters with thrusters flaring. It is flying without up or down, where nothing slows down on its own.

A good pilot feels the ship's mass and the timing of every burn. They know that a moment of haste costs fuel they may need later, that a planet's pull is always waiting, and that most accidents happen in the last few metres. In the far future pilots are prized and a little spoiled, known for their loud boasting and their quiet checks before every launch.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/PER/INT/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** spaceflight · **Covers:** launch, docking, landing, dogfights
- **Why these attributes:** control, judgement, systems, nerve
- **What failure looks like:** the manoeuvre wastes fuel, overshoots or misses the dock. Spectacular failure: a collision, a tumble out of control, or a hard landing that no one walks away from easily.
