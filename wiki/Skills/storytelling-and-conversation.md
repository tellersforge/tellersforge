# Storytelling & Conversation

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Performance*

*aka: ______________________________*

Keeping people listening. The tale told by the fire while the wind rises outside, the gossip passed across a fence, the small talk that fills a long wagon ride without anyone noticing the time. Grandparents, travelling merchants, soldiers home from the war and innkeepers are all good at it, or they are nothing but tiresome.

It is presence first and wit second. A good storyteller knows when to pause, when to lower their voice and when to leave the ending unsaid. A good conversationalist asks questions and remembers the answers. Both make strangers into friends, pass the hours, and quietly learn a great deal about the people who are enjoying themselves.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** holding attention, telling tales, small talk
- **Why these attributes:** presence, then wit
- **What failure looks like:** The audience drifts off, the tale falls flat or the conversation dies. Spectacular failure: the character says exactly the wrong thing and the room goes cold.
