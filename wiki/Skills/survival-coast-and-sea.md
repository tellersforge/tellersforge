# Survival: Coast & Sea

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Living on the edge of the sea, or adrift on it. Coastal survival is finding shellfish at low tide, fresh water behind the dunes, and shelter from the salt wind; knowing the tides that cut off a headland and the rocks that trap the unwary. At sea it is catching rain, rationing water, fishing from a raft, and reading the swell and the birds for the nearness of land.

Fishing folk, castaways, beachcombers and wreck survivors all have need of it. The sea gives generously and takes suddenly. Those who know it are never quite easy with it, and those who don't learn fast or not at all.
