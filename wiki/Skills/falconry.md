# Falconry

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A hooded hawk on a gloved fist, bells at its feet, waiting. Falconry is the old art of taking a bird of prey that could simply fly away and persuading it, every single time, to come back. The falconer traps or raises the bird, mans it through long nights of sitting still together, trains it to the lure, and learns to read the tiny signs of hunger, temper and weather in its feathers.

It is a sport of nobles in some lands and a working trade in others, where a trained bird keeps a family fed through winter. In the field it looks effortless: a stoop, a burst of feathers, a return to the glove. Behind that moment stand months of quiet labour and a bond more trust than obedience.

## Not to be confused with

*[Animal Training](animal-training.md)*, which schools beasts of every kind; Falconry is the narrow, demanding art of birds of prey and the hunt they fly.
