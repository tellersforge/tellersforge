# Painting & Drawing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Making pictures that others want to look at. A charcoal sketch of a face, a mural across a temple wall, a map illustrated with sea monsters, a portrait of a queen that flatters just enough. Painting and drawing cover all the ways of putting images on a surface: pigment on plaster, ink on paper, oil on canvas, and in later ages digital brushes on a screen.

Skill here is part eye and part hand. The painter sees light, shadow and proportion, and knows how to mix colours from earth and plants or buy them in tubes. Artists are commissioned by rulers, priests and rich merchants, and sometimes starve in attics. A good drawing can serve as evidence, a wanted poster, a record of an expedition or a way to remember a lost friend.
