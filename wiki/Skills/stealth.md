# Stealth

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Stealth*

*aka: ______________________________*

Moving without being heard and staying where you won't be seen. Stepping on the edge of the stair, waiting for the wind in the trees to cover a footfall, holding still in a shadow while a lantern passes a hand's width away. Hunters, burglars, scouts and anyone who has ever crept home late know the feeling of holding their breath.

Stealth is body control and patience in equal measure. Knowing when to move matters as much as how. A good sneak reads the guard's attention like weather, moves in the gaps and freezes the instant it turns. Soft shoes, dark clothes and a quiet belt help. A cough, a loose buckle or a nervous companion undo it all.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** moving quietly, hiding
- **Why these attributes:** body control, then judging when to move
- **What failure looks like:** The character is heard or glimpsed and the watcher grows wary. Spectacular failure: they are caught in plain sight, or knock something over in the loudest possible way.

## Not to be confused with

*[Camouflage](camouflage.md)*, which is preparing a hiding place or disguise against a background in advance; Stealth is moving and hiding in the moment.
