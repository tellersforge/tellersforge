# Trance & Spirit Work ✦

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Leaving the body, or letting something else in. The shaman drums until the world falls away and walks the spirit roads to bargain for a sick child's soul. The medium sits in a darkened room while the dead speak through her mouth. The devotee dances until a god rides him and speaks to the crowd. This skill covers journeying, mediumship and possession.

It is dangerous and exhausting work. The spirit world has its own rules, its own guardians and its own hungers, and the one who enters it must know the way back. Some peoples honour these travellers as healers and guides; others fear them. Afterward, the practitioner often remembers little, and the others must explain what happened.

- ✦ Magic-flavoured: a learned procedure; any real supernatural effect belongs to a power.
