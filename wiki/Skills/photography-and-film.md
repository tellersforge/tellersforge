# Photography & Film

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Catching light and keeping it. The early photographer stands behind a box on a tripod, head under a black cloth, while the sitters hold still and hardly breathe. Later come handheld cameras, moving pictures, newsreels, feature films and the editing bench where hours of footage become a story. Photography and film cover taking pictures, shooting films and cutting them together.

A good photographer understands light, framing and timing: the moment a face shows the truth, or a crowd tips into panic. Film-makers add movement, sound and sequence. Reporters, portraitists, spies, propagandists and artists all rely on the skill. A single picture can topple a politician, prove a crime, or preserve a moment long after everyone in it is gone.
