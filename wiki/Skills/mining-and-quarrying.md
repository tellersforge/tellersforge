# Mining & Quarrying

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Going into the earth and coming back with something worth having. Miners sink shafts, drive tunnels, prop the roof with timber and dig ore, coal or salt, while quarrymen split building stone from the open face. Both know which rock will hold and which will slip, how to keep a working dry, and when a creak in the timbers means it is time to run.

It is hard, dark and dangerous work, and those who do it are proud of it. They listen to the rock, watch the lamps for bad air and trust their partners with their lives. Mining towns rise fast and fall faster. A skilled miner is useful to armies too, for tunnels under walls or through mountains.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **STR**/END/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3

- **What failure looks like:** The seam is lost or the working goes slowly. Spectacular failure: a collapse, a flood or foul air traps or kills those below.
