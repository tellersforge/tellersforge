# Field Medicine

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Saving lives where there is no hospital, no quiet and no time. The field medic crawls under fire to reach the wounded, decides in a glance who can be saved, stops bleeding with whatever is to hand and sets a broken leg against a rifle stock. Battlefield surgeons, ship's mates with a sea chest of instruments, and survivors in a ruined city all learn the craft.

It is rough, fast and brave. The medic's hands shake later, not now. Every army values them, and every soldier who has been carried from the line remembers their face. Field medicine does not cure; it keeps someone alive long enough for a proper healer to try.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** triage and wound care under fire, bonesetting

- **What failure looks like:** The patient's condition does not improve. Spectacular failure: a wrong choice under pressure makes the wound worse, or the medic is hit while working.

## Not to be confused with

*[First Aid](first-aid.md)*, which is basic care anyone can learn; Field Medicine is triage and trauma care under danger and pressure.
