# Court Etiquette & Protocol

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The formal manners of power. Who bows to whom, how to address a queen or an envoy, where each guest sits at a state feast, which gift insults and which honours, how to challenge an insult without starting a war. Courtiers, heralds, diplomats and ambitious servants live by it.

It is knowledge first and grace second. The protocol expert knows the ranks, titles, codes of honour and rules of guest-right, both at home and abroad. Foreign courts differ, so the best study them too. A slip can cost a treaty; a well-judged gesture can win a kingdom.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** precedence, formal address, honour codes, guest-right, foreign manners
- **Why these attributes:** grace, knowledge of the rules, judgement
- **What failure looks like:** The character makes a slip that is noticed and costs them standing. Spectacular failure: a serious breach that offends the host, dishonours the character or sparks a feud.

## Not to be confused with

*[Etiquette](etiquette.md)*, which covers everyday manners at home; and *[Diplomacy](diplomacy.md)*, which negotiates between powers. Court Etiquette & Protocol is formal ceremony and rank.
