# Snow & Ice Travel

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Getting across a white country without dying in it. Skis over a frozen plain, snowshoes through a forest after a blizzard, a sledge behind a team of dogs or reindeer, careful steps across a lake that may or may not hold. The peoples of the far north and the high passes learn it as children, and outsiders learn it from them or not at all.

It is reading the snow and the ice. Knowing which crust will bear, which slope will slide, which grey patch on the lake is thin. It is also knowing how to fall, how to get up with skis on, and how to keep toes and fingers from freezing on the trail.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/AGI · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** skis, snowshoes, sledges, crossing ice
- **Why these attributes:** stamina, then footing
- **What failure looks like:** Progress is slow and exhausting, or the route has to be changed. Spectacular failure: the ice breaks, the slope slides or the sledge overturns.
