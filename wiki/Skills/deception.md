# Deception

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Deception*

*aka: ______________________________*

Making someone believe what is not true. A lie told with a straight face, a bluff at cards, a forged letter delivered with confidence, a whole false identity built for a con. Deception is the swindler's stock in trade, but spies, diplomats and parents protecting a surprise use it too.

It relies on presence and nerve, a plausible story and knowing what the mark wants to believe. Good liars keep things simple, stay close to the truth and never overexplain. They remember their lies. Bad liars embellish, contradict themselves and look away.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/INT/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** lying, bluffing, cons, false fronts
- **Why these attributes:** delivery, keeping the story straight, nerve
- **What failure looks like:** The target doubts the story or sees through it. Spectacular failure: the lie is exposed completely, and the target knows exactly what the character tried to do.

## Not to be confused with

*[Acting](acting.md)*, which is performing a role on stage; and *[Disguise](disguise.md)*, which changes appearance. Deception is telling lies.
