# Forgery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Making a false thing that passes as true. A royal writ in the right hand, sealed with the right wax; a letter of credit from a bank that never issued it; a painting by an old master that dried last week; a stack of coins or banknotes good enough to pay a tavern bill. Forgery covers documents, seals, signatures, art and money.

The forger needs a steady hand, a sharp eye for detail and the right materials: old paper, period ink, a carved seal. They study originals endlessly. The best work is never flashy; it hides in plain sight, among piles of real papers. Criminals, spies, desperate petitioners and even governments in wartime have practised it, and the punishment for being caught is rarely mild.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/DEX/INT/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** writing · **Covers:** documents, seals, signatures, art, counterfeit money
- **Why these attributes:** very fine hands, knowledge, eye for detail
- **What failure looks like:** the forgery is clumsy or the materials are wrong. Spectacular failure: it is caught by an expert, with the forger's own mark somehow on it.
