# Interrogation

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Getting answers out of someone who doesn't want to give them. The patient questioning in a quiet room, the friendly cup of wine, the sudden hard stare, the repeated question from a new angle. Interrogators work for magistrates, armies and secret services, and some work for themselves.

It is a blend of insight and pressure. The skilled interrogator builds rapport, spots evasions and pushes where the story is weakest. Threats may play a part, but patience and cleverness usually work better. Torture makes people talk but rarely tell the truth, which the best interrogators know.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** questioning, pressure, rapport to extract information
- **Why these attributes:** reading the subject, presence, nerve
- **What failure looks like:** The subject gives nothing useful or feeds misleading answers. Spectacular failure: they clam up permanently, or the interrogator reveals more than they learn.

## Not to be confused with

*[Intimidation](intimidation.md)*, which frightens for compliance; and *[Insight](insight.md)*, which reads people. Interrogation extracts information through questioning.
