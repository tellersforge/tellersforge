# Finance

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Money that works while its owner sleeps. The banker lends at interest and judges who will repay. The investor buys a share in a voyage and waits for the ship. The treasurer of a kingdom balances taxes, loans and war debts and hopes no one looks too closely. Finance is the skill of credit, capital and the great invisible tides of economies.

It needs writing and records, because it depends on promises kept on paper. The skilled understand risk, interest, currency and confidence, and know that a rumour can empty a bank faster than any thief. Moneylenders, merchant bankers, court treasurers and corporate officers all practise it. They are respected, resented and occasionally chased out of town.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** writing · **Covers:** banking, credit, investment, economics
- **Why these attributes:** heavy calculation, then judgement
- **What failure looks like:** the investment loses money or the loan goes bad. Spectacular failure: a ruinous collapse, a run on the bank, or debts that come back to haunt the financier.
