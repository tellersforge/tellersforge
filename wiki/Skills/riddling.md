# Riddling

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Questions with hidden answers. A riddle asked at a gate, a puzzle carved on a tomb, a game played by the fire on long winter nights, a contest of wits with a strange creature who will not let you pass. Riddling is the art of posing and solving these puzzles, and in many old tales it saves lives.

It needs lateral thinking, wordplay and a store of old riddles. Good riddlers see double meanings and ignore obvious answers. They can also invent new riddles, fair but hard. It is a respected skill in bardic traditions, an amusing pastime in taverns and a desperate measure in strange places.
