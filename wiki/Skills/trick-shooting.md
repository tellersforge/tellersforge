# Trick Shooting

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The shot that makes a crowd gasp. Splitting an arrow already in the target, shooting an apple off a volunteer's head, knocking a coin out of the air, hitting a candle flame without touching the wick. Trick Shooting covers called shots for show and the uncanny precision behind them.

Showmen at fairs make a living from it; hunters and soldiers use the same precision when a single shot has to go exactly where it's needed. It takes a steady hand, careful breath and endless practice with the same weapon. A trick shooter knows exactly how an arrow or bullet flies. Audiences love them, and volunteers are usually harder to find than applause.

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/DEX/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** called shots for show, splitting arrows
- **What failure looks like:** the shot misses the showy mark. Spectacular failure: it hits something it shouldn't, including, occasionally, the volunteer.
