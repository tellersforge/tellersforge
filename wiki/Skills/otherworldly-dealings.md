# Otherworldly Dealings ✦

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Bargaining with beings that aren't human and don't play fair. The fae who trade gifts for names, the spirit who guards a spring, the devil at the crossroads with a contract and a smile. Otherworldly Dealings covers the etiquette, oaths and bargains of such encounters: what to offer, what never to eat, how to hold them to their word and how to find the loophole in theirs.

The skill is part lore and part nerve. The dealer knows that true names have power, that promises bind both sides, and that every clause has a second meaning. Witches, exorcists, desperate farmers and ambitious sorcerers all try it. The wisest never give away more than they can afford, and never assume they have won.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** bargaining with spirits, fae and devils; oaths; true names; spotting loopholes

- **What failure looks like:** the bargain fails, or the terms are worse than they seemed. Spectacular failure: the dealer is bound to something they did not understand, and it will come to collect.
- ✦ Magic-flavoured: a learned procedure; any real supernatural effect belongs to a power.
