# Electrical Work

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Wires, switches, fuses and power. The electrician runs cables through walls, connects lamps and motors, fits generators and batteries, and keeps the current where it belongs. Early practitioners string telegraph lines and light the first streets; later ones wire whole cities, ships and space stations. Much of the work is careful routine, and the rest is finding out why a building has gone dark.

The trade rewards respect for what cannot be seen. A careless electrician is lucky to be burned, and a careless job can set a house on fire weeks later. Good ones carry a tester, a roll of tape and a healthy suspicion of anything someone else has wired.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** electricity · **Covers:** wiring, power distribution, generators
- **Why these attributes:** knowledge, handwork, care
- **What failure looks like:** The circuit fails, shorts or blows its fuse. Spectacular failure: a shock, a fire, or power lost across a whole building or ship.
