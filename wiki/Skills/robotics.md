# Robotics

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Building machines that move, sense and decide for themselves. The roboticist designs the body, fits the motors and sensors, and writes the instructions that let a robot walk, grasp, fly or talk. The work ranges from factory arms and clumsy drones to synthetic beings that pass for people in dim light.

It is a meeting of every other technical skill: mechanics, electronics and programming woven together, and never quite finished. Roboticists are often fond of their creations and worried about them in equal measure. The best understand not only how to make a robot obey, but what it will do when its orders stop making sense.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/DEX/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** computing · **Covers:** building and programming robots and synthetic beings
- **Why these attributes:** heavy design, handwork, debugging
- **What failure looks like:** The robot moves wrongly, stalls or misunderstands its orders. Spectacular failure: it behaves in a way nobody intended and nobody can stop at once.

## Not to be confused with

*[Electronics](electronics.md)*, which works on devices and circuits; Robotics builds whole machines that move, sense and act on their own.
