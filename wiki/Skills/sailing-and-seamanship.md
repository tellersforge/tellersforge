# Sailing & Seamanship

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The whole trade of living and working on a ship under sail. Hauling a line at the right moment, going aloft to reef in a gale, splicing rope, keeping watch, knowing the name of every line and block aboard. It is a hard, cramped life with its own language, customs and superstitions, and landsmen who join a crew spend weeks being laughed at.

The skilled sailor reads the wind in the canvas and the sea in the swell. They know how a ship behaves close-hauled, how to bring her into harbour under shortened sail, and what to do when the storm arrives before the captain has decided what to do about it. From small coastal traders to tall ships with vast clouds of canvas, the same work keeps them afloat.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/DEX/STR · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** copper · **Covers:** sail handling, rigging, ship routine, harbours, storms
- **Why these attributes:** reading wind and sea, handling, labour
- **What failure looks like:** the ship is slow, the sail badly set, the manoeuvre fumbled. Spectacular failure: rigging parts, a spar falls, or the vessel is driven toward rocks.
