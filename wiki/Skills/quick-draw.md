# Quick-Draw

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The weapon is in the hand before anyone else has thought about it. The gunfighter's pistol clears the holster in an eye-blink; the swordsman's blade leaves its scabbard in the same motion as the cut; the knife-thrower's dagger appears from a sleeve. Quick-Draw is the art of readying a weapon instantly and striking first.

It is practised for hours in private and used for heartbeats in public. The best quick-drawers stay calm as the tension grows, watching an opponent's eyes and shoulders rather than their hands. Duellists, gunslingers, bodyguards and assassins all prize it. In a showdown at noon, the one who draws first often has no need to draw twice.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** readying a weapon instantly, showdowns
- **What failure looks like:** the weapon catches, fumbles or comes out too slowly. Spectacular failure: it is dropped, or goes off early into the dust, or into a foot.

## Not to be confused with

*[Trick Shooting](trick-shooting.md)*, which makes showy, precise shots. Quick-Draw is about speed in getting a weapon ready.
