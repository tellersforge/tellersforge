# Religion & Theology

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The study of the gods and of what people believe about them. It covers doctrines and heresies, holy days and fasts, the orders of priests and the quarrels between them, the sacred law that says what may be eaten, worn and done. A theologian can explain why two temples on the same street hate each other over a single word in a creed.

It is knowledge, not faith. An unbeliever may know more of a religion than its most devout follower, and a zealot may know almost nothing. For a traveller, it is the difference between a warm welcome and a stoning. For a scholar, it is the key to half of every ancient text, which was written by priests and for them.
