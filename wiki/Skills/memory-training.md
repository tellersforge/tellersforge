# Memory Training

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The art of remembering on purpose. Bards who carry epics in their heads, lawspeakers who recite the whole code from memory, priests who know every verse of the liturgy, and families who can name their ancestors back to the founding. Before writing was common, this was how knowledge survived.

It uses techniques: rhymes, repeated patterns, imagined palaces with a memory in every room. With training, one can recall long texts word for word, remember every face at a feast or learn a map at a glance. It is hard work, and those who have it are both treasured and slightly unsettling.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** memory techniques, reciting epics, genealogies and law
- **Why these attributes:** recall, then attention
- **What failure looks like:** The memory is incomplete or muddled. Spectacular failure: the character remembers something wrong with total confidence.

## Not to be confused with

*[Trained Observation](trained-observation.md)*, which fixes a scene in the mind as it happens; Memory Training learns and recalls texts, lists and lore.
