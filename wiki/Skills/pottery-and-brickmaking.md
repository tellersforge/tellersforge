# Pottery & Brickmaking

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Dig clay, clean it, knead it, shape it and put it in the fire. Pots, jars, lamps, pipes, roof tiles and bricks all come from the same mud, and each asks something different of the hands and the kiln. The potter shapes by coil or wheel; the brickmaker by the mould, thousands at a time, then builds the clamp or kiln and tends it through a long, hot night. Burning limestone for mortar and plaster belongs to the same yard.

Pottery is so common that people forget it, until they need to cook, store grain or keep the rain out. Broken sherds outlive everything else, which is why diggers of old ruins love them. A good kiln-master reads the colour of the flame the way a smith reads iron.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** pottery · **Covers:** clay, kilns, bricks, tiles, lime
- **Why these attributes:** hands, then feel for fire and clay
- **What failure looks like:** The clay cracks in drying or the kiln load comes out soft and crumbling. Spectacular failure: the kiln itself bursts or collapses, and a whole firing is lost.
