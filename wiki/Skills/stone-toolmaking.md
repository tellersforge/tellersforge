# Stone Toolmaking

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Before metal, there was the stone and the one who knew how to break it. The knapper holds a nodule of flint or glassy black stone and strikes it with a hammerstone or antler, flake by flake, until a blade, a scraper or a spearhead emerges with an edge sharper than steel. Other tools are ground rather than struck: axes and adzes polished against sandstone for days until they are smooth and heavy.

It looks crude to those who have never tried it. It is not. The stone has a grain and hidden flaws, and the maker must read them before each blow. In the stone age this is the craft that keeps everyone alive, and even in later ages a castaway who remembers it will not go unarmed.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** knapping flint and obsidian, ground-stone tools
- **Why these attributes:** precise striking, then reading the stone
- **What failure looks like:** The stone snaps wrong and the piece is wasted. Spectacular failure: a flying shard cuts the maker's hand or eye, or a tool breaks the first time it is really needed.

## Not to be confused with

*[Weaponsmithing & Armouring](weaponsmithing-and-armouring.md)*, which forges metal arms; Stone Toolmaking shapes flint and stone by striking and grinding, and needs no fire at all.
