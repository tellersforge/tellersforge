# Tailoring

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Cloth becomes clothing. The tailor measures a body, cuts the fabric to a pattern, and sews a coat that fits across the shoulders and lets the arms move. It is quiet, precise work, done with shears, chalk, needle and a good eye for how a garment will hang. Uniforms, gowns, doublets, suits and costumes for the stage all come from the same bench.

Clothes speak before their wearer does, and tailors know it. A good one can make a merchant look like a lord or a spy look like a servant. In cities they are confidants of the rich, who stand still and talk while being pinned. A skilled tailor can also hide a pocket, a blade or a letter where no one will look.

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
