# Leadership

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Making others follow you. Rallying a broken line, keeping a crew working through a storm, holding a village together through famine, giving the orders that people obey without question. Leadership is presence, judgement and the trust of those led. Captains, chieftains, foremen and saints have it.

It is not just giving orders. A good leader knows their people, shares their hardships, makes decisions and takes responsibility. They inspire courage, keep up spirits and hold a group together when it wants to split. A bad leader can do all the talking in the world and still be alone when the fighting starts.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/COU/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** inspiring, commanding, rallying, keeping morale
- **Why these attributes:** presence, leading by example, judgement
- **What failure looks like:** The followers hesitate or drift. Spectacular failure: they ignore, mock or desert the leader.

## Not to be confused with

*[Oratory & Debate](oratory-and-debate.md)*, which sways a crowd with speeches; and *[Ship Command](ship-command.md)*, which leads a vessel's crew. Leadership inspires and holds any group together.
