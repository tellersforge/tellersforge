# Swimming

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Athletics*

*aka: ______________________________*

Keeping your head above water and getting somewhere while you do it. In some places everyone swims from infancy; in others, sailors refuse to learn so that a wreck will be quick. Fishers, pearl divers, river raiders and castle attackers crossing a moat all rely on it, and in a flood it is the difference between the living and the drowned.

Calm water in summer is a pleasure. Cold water steals the strength out of arms in minutes, currents carry swimmers where they don't want to go, and clothes, armour and packs drag like anchors. A strong swimmer knows when to float and rest, when to fight the stream and when to let it take them to the bank.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/STR · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** staying afloat, swimming distances
- **Why these attributes:** stamina in water, then stroke power
- **What failure looks like:** The swimmer makes no headway, tires or is swept along. Spectacular failure: they go under and start to drown, or lose everything they were carrying.

## Not to be confused with

*[Diving](diving.md)*, which is going beneath the surface on a held breath or breathing gear; Swimming keeps you on top of the water and moving across it.
