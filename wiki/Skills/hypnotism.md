# Hypnotism

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A swinging watch, a low voice, a candle flame, and a mind drifting down into stillness. The hypnotist induces trance through ordinary means: rhythm, suggestion, focus and the subject's own willingness. In trance, people remember what they had forgotten, lose their fear, feel less pain or accept suggestions they might otherwise resist.

It is used by physicians, stage performers, interrogators and charlatans. Some treat it as a science, others as theatre, others as something closer to sorcery. Those who know it best say it is none of these: only a subtle, powerful way of speaking to the parts of the mind that rarely listen.
