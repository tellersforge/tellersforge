# Exotic Beasts

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Some creatures are too big, too strange or too hungry for any ordinary stable. The war elephant, the riding lizard the size of a barn, the great flightless bird, the winged thing that should not carry a rider and does. Handling them takes nerve first of all, since the beast knows at once whether you are afraid, and then everything a good animal handler knows, scaled up until a mistake can crush a man.

Those with this skill are rare and well paid: mahouts, menagerie keepers, beast-riders of desert and sky, and the few who tend the monsters that kings keep to impress their guests. Most carry scars and speak of their charges with a mix of love and wary respect, the way sailors speak of the sea.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **COU**/WIS/CHA/AGI · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** handling and riding dangerous, huge or fantastic creatures
- **Why these attributes:** nerve first, then patience, presence, handling
- **What failure looks like:** The creature ignores its handler, grows restless or refuses to be mounted. Spectacular failure: it panics or rages, and everyone nearby, rider included, is in real danger.

## Not to be confused with

*[Animal Handling](animal-handling.md)*, which covers ordinary domestic beasts; Exotic Beasts is for creatures so large, wild or unnatural that nerve matters more than knack.
