# Mechanics

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Grease under the nails and an ear for the wrong noise. The mechanic lifts the bonnet, listens to the engine cough, and knows from the sound whether it wants a new belt, a cleaned valve or a merciful end. It is the skill of keeping machines alive: steam engines, motor cars, tractors, pumps, generators and, in later ages, the drive units of starships.

A good mechanic works in a set order. First find what's wrong, then take it apart without losing the small pieces, then put it back together stronger than it was. Strength helps, because bolts seize and parts are heavy, but patience helps more. Every garage, depot, airfield and engine room has one, and in the far corners of the world a travelling mechanic is welcomed like a doctor.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX/STR · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** steam · **Covers:** repairing engines, vehicles and machines
- **Why these attributes:** diagnosis, handwork, force
- **What failure looks like:** the fault isn't found, or the repair lasts only a short while. Spectacular failure: a new fault appears, a part breaks, or the machine fails later at the worst moment.
