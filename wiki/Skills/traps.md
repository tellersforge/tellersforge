# Traps

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The deadly art of the hidden mechanism. A pressure plate in a tomb corridor, a spring-loaded blade in a chest lid, a pit covered with reeds, a door that opens only when the right stones are pressed in the right order. The skill covers spotting such things, building them and disarming them without losing a finger.

A trap-expert thinks like the one who built it: where a victim would step, what they would touch, what they would take for granted. They move slowly and test every surface. Tomb robbers, soldiers clearing fortifications, paranoid lords and treasure hunters all rely on it. Most people meet traps only once; the ones who live to tell of it tend to be very cautious for the rest of their lives.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** finding, setting and disarming traps and puzzle locks
- **Why these attributes:** working out the mechanism, handwork, spotting it
- **What failure looks like:** the trap goes unnoticed, or is not disarmed. Spectacular failure: it springs, catching the one who was trying to handle it.
