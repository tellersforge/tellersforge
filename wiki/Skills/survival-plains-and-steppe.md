# Survival: Plains & Steppe

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Living in the great open. On the plains and the steppe the sky is enormous, the wind never stops, and there is nowhere to hide. Survival here is finding water in a land that looks the same for days, keeping fires going with dung or grass, reading the horizon for dust that means riders or storms, and knowing that the herds and the grass tell you where to go.

Horse peoples and herders have always ruled such country, travelling light and moving fast. Outsiders feel exposed and tiny under all that sky. The dangers are lightning, prairie fire, blizzards that come in hours, and the simple difficulty of getting anywhere on foot.
