# Resisting Interrogation

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Holding a story together while someone tries to break it. The spy sticking to a cover, the prisoner giving only name and rank, the smuggler sticking to the same tale about the cargo for the third time. Resisting interrogation is keeping calm, consistent and closed while questions come from every angle.

It is discipline and preparation. The practised know to keep answers simple, to repeat themselves and to never argue. They recognise tricks such as the friendly questioner or the false claim that a companion has already talked. It cannot stop pain, but it can keep secrets from slipping through fear or fatigue.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/END/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** holding a cover story under pressure
- **Why these attributes:** will, endurance, keeping the story straight
- **What failure looks like:** The character lets slip a detail or looks suspicious. Spectacular failure: their story collapses and they reveal the secret.
