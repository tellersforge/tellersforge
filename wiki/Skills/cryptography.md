# Cryptography

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Hiding words in plain sight, and prying them out again. Cryptography makes codes that replace words with other words and ciphers that scramble letters by a secret rule; it also breaks them, which takes patience, a head for patterns and a great deal of scrap paper. Kings, spies, merchants and lovers have all sent secrets this way, and someone has always been trying to read them.

In simpler ages it is shifted alphabets and code books; later it becomes cipher machines and mathematics so deep that only computers can follow it. The cryptographer's joy is the moment when a page of nonsense suddenly begins to speak.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** writing · **Covers:** making and breaking codes and ciphers
- **Why these attributes:** heavy reasoning, then pattern sense
- **What failure looks like:** the code holds, or the new cipher is easily broken. Spectacular failure: the character's own secret is read by the enemy without anyone realising.

## Not to be confused with

*[Linguistics](linguistics.md)*, which deciphers unknown languages; Cryptography deals with messages deliberately hidden inside known ones.
