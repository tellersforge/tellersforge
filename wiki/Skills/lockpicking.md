# Lockpicking

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Thievery*

*aka: ______________________________*

Opening what someone wanted closed. With a pick, a tension wrench and a quiet hand, the lockpicker feels the pins inside a lock and lifts them one by one until the bolt slides. Padlocks, door locks, strongboxes and at last the heavy combination dials of safes and vaults all have their weaknesses, and they know them.

It is delicate, patient work, often done by feel in the dark with listening ears. Thieves, spies, rescuers and locksmiths all learn it, and the good ones can tell the quality of a lock by a glance at its face. In ages of iron and brass, fortunes hang on a few minutes' fiddling, and a skilled lockpicker is worth more than a band of armed men.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/PER/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** iron · **Covers:** mechanical locks, safes, vaults
- **Why these attributes:** fine hands, mechanism, feel
- **What failure looks like:** the lock won't yield, and time runs out. Spectacular failure: a pick snaps in the keyhole, jamming the lock or leaving clear evidence of tampering.
