# Aircraft Piloting

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Flying something heavier or lighter than air and bringing it back down in one piece. The balloonist rides the wind with a bag of sand and a prayer; the airship captain trims a great silver hull against a storm; the biplane pilot banks over a field of grass; the helicopter pilot hovers above a cliff while a rope goes down to a stranded climber.

Flying is simple when the weather is kind and the engines are healthy. The skill shows in everything else: fog, crosswinds, failing fuel, a landing strip that is shorter than the chart claims. Pilots think in three dimensions and plan their way out of trouble before they are in it. Airmail flyers, crop dusters, war pilots and rescue crews all practise it, and all of them keep an eye on the clouds.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/PER/INT/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** electricity · **Covers:** planes, helicopters, airships, balloons
- **Why these attributes:** control, judgement, systems, nerve
- **What failure looks like:** the flight is rough, the landing hard, the course lost. Spectacular failure: a stall, a spin, or a crash landing.
