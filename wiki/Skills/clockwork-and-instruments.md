# Clockwork & Instruments

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Gears no bigger than a fingernail, springs wound tight, and a patience that borders on obsession. The instrument-maker builds clocks that strike the hours, astrolabes that map the heavens, compasses, scales, sextants and later microscopes. Musical instruments belong here too: a lute's belly, an organ's pipes, a violin varnished to a glow. At the far edge of the craft are automata, mechanical birds that sing and figures that write their name.

These makers live between art and science. Their workshops are full of tiny files, magnifying lenses and half-finished marvels, and their customers are astronomers, navigators, musicians and kings with a taste for wonders. One slipped tooth and a year's work stops.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/INT/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** iron · **Covers:** clocks, automata, astrolabes, scientific and musical instruments
- **Why these attributes:** fine hands, heavy design, judgement
- **What failure looks like:** The mechanism runs wrong or the instrument plays or reads false. Spectacular failure: it fails in the middle of its purpose, or breaks beyond repair.

## Not to be confused with

*[Mechanical Engineering](mechanical-engineering.md)*, which designs large machines; Clockwork & Instruments makes small, precise mechanisms and instruments by hand.
