# Mimicry

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The voice of someone else in your throat. The mimic calls like a hunting owl so well that a real owl answers. They copy the merchant's accent, the captain's bark, the child crying outside the door. Some can throw their voice so that it seems to come from a box, a statue or a dark corner of the room.

It is a playful skill with serious uses. Hunters lure game with it; scouts signal each other with bird calls; entertainers make crowds laugh with impressions of local lords; tricksters send guards running after a voice that isn't there. It needs a sharp ear and hours of practice. A perfect imitation is uncanny, and some folk still believe that only spirits can do it well.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **PER**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** imitating voices and animal calls, ventriloquism
- **Why these attributes:** a good ear, then delivery
- **What failure looks like:** the voice sounds wrong, and listeners notice. Spectacular failure: the mimicry is exposed as fake at exactly the moment it was meant to fool.
