# Survival: Desert

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Living where water is life and the sun is the enemy. Desert survival is travelling at dawn and dusk and resting through the killing heat, wrapping up rather than stripping off, finding water under a dry riverbed or in the shade of a certain rock, and reading the sky for the storm that turns day into choking night.

Desert peoples carry this knowledge like a birthright and share it grudgingly. Outsiders learn it the hard way, or not at all. It also means cold nights, scorpions in boots, mirages that look exactly like water, and the hard discipline of rationing a waterskin when the next well may be dry.
