# Calligraphy & Scribing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Fine writing, done by hand, meant to last. The scribe copies books, draws up charters, writes out decrees and letters for those who cannot write. Calligraphy raises it to an art: letters of a beauty that make a page worth framing, and in many cultures a sign of a cultivated mind. Scribes are clerks, monks, court secretaries and the market-square letter-writers who read and write for a coin.

The craft demands a steady hand, good ink and endless patience. A scribe can match a style, lay out a page, and spot a copying error from across the desk. In ages of print their work becomes a luxury, and in ages of screens a curiosity.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** writing · **Covers:** fine writing, copying, formal documents
- **Why these attributes:** hand, then knowledge
- **What failure looks like:** the page is blotted or the copy flawed. Spectacular failure: a crucial word is miscopied, and the document means something quite different.

## Not to be confused with

*[Composition](composition.md)*, which is choosing the words; and *[Forgery](forgery.md)*, which copies a hand to deceive. The scribe writes the words well, and honestly.
