# Archaeology

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Reading the past from what it left in the ground. The archaeologist digs carefully, one layer at a time, and knows that where a thing lies says as much as what it is. Broken pots date a floor, ash marks a burning, the shape of a doorway tells whose builders made it. Ruins that look like tumbled stones to anyone else become streets, temples and kitchens.

In older ages it is a gentleman's hobby or a grave robber's trade; in later ones it is a science with trowels, brushes and patient notebooks. Either way, the one who has it can walk into a ruin and tell who built it, roughly when, and where the interesting rooms probably are.

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/PER/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3

- **Why these attributes:** knowledge, judgement, careful hands
- **What failure looks like:** the site is misread or the find wrongly dated. Spectacular failure: careless digging destroys the very thing that mattered, or wakes something that should have stayed buried.

## Not to be confused with

*[History](history.md)*, which works from written records; Archaeology works from what was left behind when the writing stopped, or before it began.
