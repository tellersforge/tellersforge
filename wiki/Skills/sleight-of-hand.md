# Sleight of Hand

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Thievery*

*aka: ______________________________*

Quick fingers and a straight face. The pickpocket brushes past in a crowd and a purse changes owners. The conjurer shows an empty hand, then produces a coin from a child's ear. The card sharp deals himself the queen he needs from the bottom of the deck without anyone seeing a thing. Sleight of hand is about moving small objects where no one is looking.

It is built on misdirection as much as speed: the eye follows the showy hand while the quiet one does the work. Street thieves, stage magicians, gamblers and spies all practise it for hours in front of mirrors. Some use it to amuse, some to steal, some to plant evidence on others. Onlookers who spot it rarely forgive it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/COU/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** pickpocketing, palming, planting items, card cheating, stage conjuring
- **Why these attributes:** fine hands, nerve, timing
- **What failure looks like:** the object is fumbled, dropped or never taken. Spectacular failure: the trick is caught in the act, in front of the victim and everyone else.
