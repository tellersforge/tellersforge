# Hunting

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Survival*

*aka: ______________________________*

Getting close to game and killing it cleanly. The hunter knows where animals feed and rest, moves downwind, waits, and places the shot where it will do its work quickly. After the kill comes the less glorious part: gutting, skinning and quartering in the field, and carrying the meat home before it spoils or draws scavengers.

In early ages hunting feeds the band; later it feeds farm families in lean years or entertains lords with horns and hounds. Every era has its hunters, and they share a respect for the animals they take. The shot itself relies on the weapon, but everything before and after is the hunter's craft.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **PER**/AGI/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** stalking, killing cleanly, field-dressing game (the shot itself uses the weapon)
- **Why these attributes:** patience and reading game, stalk, stamina
- **What failure looks like:** the quarry catches the scent and is gone. Spectacular failure: the beast is only wounded and turns, or the hunter becomes the hunted.

## Not to be confused with

*[Trapping](trapping.md)*, which catches game without being there; and *[Tracking](tracking.md)*, which follows the trail. Hunting is the stalk, the kill and the dressing of the carcass.
