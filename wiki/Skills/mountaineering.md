# Mountaineering

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Climbing real mountains. Ice axes and crampons, ropes between climbers, the long slog up snowfields in thin air, the camp pitched on a ledge. It is much more than climbing: it is choosing routes, reading avalanche slopes, handling altitude sickness, rigging belays and bringing the whole team home.

Mountain guides, highland herders, border patrols and those who climb for the glory of it all learn it. It is slow and cautious work, and the best mountaineers are famous for turning back. The summit can wait; a storm or a crevasse doesn't.
