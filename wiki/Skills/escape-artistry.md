# Escape Artistry

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Thievery*

*aka: ______________________________*

Getting out of what is meant to hold you. Wriggling free of tied ropes, slipping a hand out of a manacle, squeezing through a gap in the bars or a chimney barely wide enough for a child. Stage magicians perform it, captured spies depend on it, and some thieves claim no gaol can keep them.

It is flexibility, patience and a cool head. Tensing the muscles while being tied, then relaxing to get slack. Dislocating a thumb and resetting it. Finding the weak point in the knot or the cuff. It is slow, painful work, and it goes much better when nobody is watching.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/DEX/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** slipping bonds, cuffs and tight spaces
- **Why these attributes:** flexibility, working out the trick, enduring the strain
- **What failure looks like:** The bonds hold and the character stays put. Spectacular failure: they get stuck halfway, hurt themselves, or are caught in the act.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lockpicking](lockpicking.md)*, which opens locks with tools; Escape Artistry frees the body from bonds and tight spaces by flexibility and skill.
