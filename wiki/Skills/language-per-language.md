# Language (per language)

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Lore*

*aka: ______________________________*

Speaking and understanding one foreign tongue. A character's own language comes free; every other one must be learned, from a tutor, a trade partner, years of travel or a childhood in a border town. At first it is phrases and gestures and a great deal of pointing. Later it is jokes, arguments and dreams.

Knowing a language opens doors that gold cannot. Market prices fall, suspicion softens, and conversations meant to be private become suddenly public. Speaking it badly marks a stranger at once. Speaking it with the right accent can pass a spy as a native. Each language is learned separately, though related tongues come more easily to one who already knows their cousins.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** speaking and understanding one foreign tongue (your native tongue is automatic)
- **Why these attributes:** learning, then feel for meaning
- **What failure looks like:** the meaning is garbled, or only half understood. Spectacular failure: the character says something rude, alarming or disastrously different from what they meant.

## Not to be confused with

*[Literacy (per script)](literacy-per-script.md)*, which is reading and writing; one can speak a language well and be unable to read it.
