# Creature & Occult Lore

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

What lurks outside the lamplight, and how to deal with it. This is the knowledge of monster hunters, exorcists and wary old villagers: which beasts come out at the dark of the moon, what a spirit wants, whether salt or silver or running water will keep a thing at bay. It is part bestiary, part rumour, and part hard-won experience from those who came back.

Much of what passes for such lore is nonsense, and telling the true from the false is half the skill. A character who knows it can look at claw marks on a barn door and say what made them, what it eats, and what will make it leave. The other half of the skill is knowing when to run.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** monsters, spirits and unknown beings; their habits and weaknesses
- **Why these attributes:** knowledge, then judgement
- **What failure looks like:** the creature can't be identified, or its weakness is forgotten. Spectacular failure: the character is certain of a weakness the creature does not have.
