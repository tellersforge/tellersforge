# Meditation & Concentration

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Stilling the mind and holding it where it's put. A monk sitting in silence through a long night, a scholar working through a noisy market, an archer blocking out the crowd before the shot. Concentration lets someone focus despite pain, noise, fear or fatigue; meditation brings calm and clarity.

It takes practice and patience, usually taught by a master or learnt through faith. The breath slows, the thoughts settle, the world narrows. Those who master it can rest deeply in a short time, think clearly under pressure and resist distraction. It also underlies many mystical traditions.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** focus through distraction, calming the mind
- **Why these attributes:** inner discipline, then stamina
- **What failure looks like:** The mind wanders and the task is interrupted. Spectacular failure: the character is utterly distracted and loses the thread entirely.
