# History

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Lore*

*aka: ______________________________*

The record of what has happened: wars and treaties, the rise and fall of dynasties, plagues, migrations and inventions. A historian knows which king followed which, why the old empire fell, and what happened the last time two cities quarrelled over that river. They know too that every account was written by someone with a reason.

History is useful in ways that surprise the unlearned. It tells a diplomat what grudges lie beneath the smiles, a general how this pass was forced before, and a traveller why the locals spit at the name of a certain family. Those who know it well tend to be gloomy about the future, and they are often right.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** past events, eras, records
- **Why these attributes:** heavy knowledge, then interpretation
- **What failure looks like:** the relevant history is unknown or hopelessly muddled. Spectacular failure: the character recalls a stirring version of events that proves to be propaganda.
