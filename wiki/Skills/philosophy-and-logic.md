# Philosophy & Logic

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The discipline of thinking about thinking. Philosophy asks what is good, what is real and what can be known; logic is its sharpest tool, the art of following an argument step by step and seeing where it breaks. Philosophers argue in porticos and academies, in monasteries and coffee houses, and occasionally in prisons, since rulers do not always enjoy their questions.

In play it lets a character spot the flaw in a clever argument, recognise which school of thought an opponent follows, and reason carefully through a moral tangle. It will not win a fight. It may, now and then, prevent one, or show that the fight is about something other than what everyone thinks.
