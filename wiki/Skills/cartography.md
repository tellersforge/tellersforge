# Cartography

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The making and reading of maps. A cartographer takes notes, bearings and travellers' sketches and turns them into a picture of the land that others can follow. It is a craft of ink, patience and careful measurement, and in older ages a map was worth more than its weight in silver, because it told where the roads went and the enemies were.

Reading maps well is its own talent. Old charts are full of guesswork, flattery and deliberate lies, and a cartographer can tell a sound map from a fanciful one. Sea charts, city plans, military maps and star charts all belong to the craft, each with its own symbols and habits.
