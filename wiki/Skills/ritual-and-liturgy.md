# Ritual & Liturgy

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The right words, in the right order, at the right time. The priest lifts the cup, the drum keeps the beat it has always kept, the elders walk the boundary of the fields, the dead are washed, wrapped and carried out feet first. Ritual & Liturgy covers ceremonies of every kind: sacrifice, blessing, marriage, coronation, burial and the daily observances that keep the gods and the ancestors content.

The skill is knowing the forms and performing them with dignity. A good officiant moves a congregation to awe; a bad one makes them shuffle. In some worlds a rite is only a comfort. In others, a rite performed exactly has real weight, and a mistake can anger powers better left calm. Priests, shamans, heralds and heads of families all practise it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** ceremonies, sacrifice, funerary rites, observances
- **Why these attributes:** devotion and judgement, knowing the steps, leading them
- **What failure looks like:** the ceremony falls flat and leaves the people unmoved. Spectacular failure: a grave mistake is made in front of everyone, and it is taken as an ill omen.
