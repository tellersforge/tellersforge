# Forensics

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Making the evidence speak. Forensics studies a scene with instruments: the residue on a glove, the angle of a wound, the poison hidden in a teacup, the fibre caught on a window latch. It arrives in the steam age with microscopes and chemical tests and grows into a whole laboratory science, able to tell who touched what and when.

The forensic scientist does not guess. They test, compare, and write it down, and then they hand the results to someone else to argue about in court. The work is careful and often grim, and the best of it lets the dead testify against the living.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/PER/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** steam · **Covers:** lab analysis of residues, wounds, evidence
- **Why these attributes:** heavy science, judgement, careful handling
- **What failure looks like:** the test is inconclusive or the evidence contaminated. Spectacular failure: a mistake points firmly at the wrong suspect.
