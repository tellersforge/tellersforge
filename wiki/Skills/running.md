# Running

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Athletics*

*aka: ______________________________*

Feet on the ground, one after the other, faster than anyone chasing you. Running is the oldest way to escape, to carry a message and to catch dinner. Messengers ran between hill forts, hunters once ran game down over a whole day until it simply lay down, and every child who ever stole an apple learnt the short, desperate version.

There are two kinds of runner. The sprinter explodes off the mark and is spent within a street. The distance runner starts slow, breathes steadily and is still going when the sprinter is bent double against a wall. Most people have a little of each, and the lungs give out long before the legs do.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/AGI · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** sprinting, long runs
- **Why these attributes:** staying power is what gives out first
- **What failure looks like:** The runner flags, falls behind or has to stop for breath. Spectacular failure: a turned ankle, a stitch that drops them to their knees, or a headlong fall in front of everyone.

## Not to be confused with

*Endurance (attribute)*, which is lasting through hunger, cold and long marches at a walking pace; Running is about speed and getting there first.
