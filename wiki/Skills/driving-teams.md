# Driving Teams

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Controlling animals that pull. The teamster handles oxen before a plough, horses before a coach, mules before a supply wagon, dogs before a sled, and in the grander ages, horses before a racing chariot. It is reins and voice and whip, and knowing how each animal in the team is feeling.

The skill looks simple and is not. A team must pull together, turn together, stop together, and not panic together when a wheel drops into a rut. Carters, coachmen, farmers and sled drivers learn it young. A master can take a heavy wagon through a crowded market at a trot without touching a single stall.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** wheel and roads · **Covers:** carts, wagons, chariots, dog sleds, ox teams
- **Why these attributes:** managing animals, then handling the reins
- **What failure looks like:** the team balks or pulls unevenly, and progress is slow. Spectacular failure: the vehicle overturns, runs off the road or loses its team.
