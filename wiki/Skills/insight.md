# Insight

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Insight*

*aka: ______________________________*

Reading people. The tightness in a smile, the hand that goes to a purse when money is mentioned, the answer that comes a heartbeat too quickly. Insight tells you what someone feels, what they want and whether they believe what they are saying. Innkeepers have it, card players cultivate it, and good mothers seem to be born with it.

It is intuition first and social feel second. The insightful don't catch every lie, but they notice when a story doesn't sit right, and they notice when a stranger is frightened rather than rude. It works best on people you can watch for a while, and worst on the practised, the calm and the genuinely strange.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** reading moods, motives and lies
- **Why these attributes:** intuition, then social feel
- **What failure looks like:** The character reads nothing useful, or reads the mood but not the reason. Spectacular failure: they confidently misjudge someone, trusting a liar or accusing an honest friend.

## Not to be confused with

*[Interrogation](interrogation.md)*, which applies pressure to pull out answers; Insight simply reads what a person is feeling and hiding without asking anything.
