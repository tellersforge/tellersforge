# Machining & Welding

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Metal cut, shaped and joined to the thickness of a fingernail. The machinist sets a bar spinning in the lathe and feeds the cutter in slowly until the shaft is exactly the size the drawing asks. The welder lowers a dark visor, strikes an arc brighter than the sun, and runs a bead of molten metal along a seam that must hold under pressure. In later ages, printers build parts layer by layer from a design.

This is the workshop that every factory, shipyard and repair bay depends on. The work is loud, hot and full of fumes, and the best practitioners are known by their calm hands and their scars. They read drawings, measure twice, and know how metal warps when it cools. A precise part from their bench can bring a broken machine back to life.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/INT/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** steam · **Covers:** lathes, precision parts, welding, fabrication, 3D printing
- **Why these attributes:** handwork, planning, heat and fumes
- **What failure looks like:** the part is out of true, the weld is weak, the stock is wasted. Spectacular failure: a burn, a flying chip, or a join that looks sound and gives way under load.

## Not to be confused with

*[Blacksmithing](blacksmithing.md)*, which shapes metal by fire and hammer. Machining & Welding works with powered tools and much finer precision.
