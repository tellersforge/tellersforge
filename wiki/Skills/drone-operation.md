# Drone Operation

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Flying, driving or crawling a machine that you are not inside. The operator sits in a van, a trench or a comfortable chair, watching the world through a camera on a small aircraft, a wheeled rover or a crawler in a pipe. It asks for a steady hand and a strange double awareness: knowing where the drone is while the body stays somewhere else entirely.

Farmers use drones to count sheep, surveyors to map cliffs, police to watch crowds, armies to hunt from the sky. On distant worlds, rovers scout ground no one has yet dared to walk on. The best operators have an instinct for the delay between command and movement and for the blind spots of their cameras. The worst learn about trees, wires and wind the expensive way.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** computing · **Covers:** remote vehicles, drones, rovers
- **Why these attributes:** control, then spatial judgement
- **What failure looks like:** the drone drifts, loses its target or has to be recalled. Spectacular failure: it crashes, is lost out of range, or lands somewhere it gives its operator away.

## Not to be confused with

*[Aircraft Piloting](aircraft-piloting.md)*, which flies a machine from inside the cockpit. Drone Operation controls it from a distance, through a screen.
