# High-Gravity Conditioning

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Living where everything weighs more. Every step is a climb, every fall is a disaster, and lifting a bucket feels like carrying a sack of stones. Some worlds, strange realms or cursed places press down this way, and those raised there are short, stocky and strong. Visitors struggle.

The skill is conditioning and technique: moving carefully, never jumping, breathing deeply, sleeping flat and resting often. It trains the body to cope rather than giving up by midday. It also means knowing how dangerous a small tumble can be, and treating every ladder and stair with suspicion.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **END**/STR · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — (the setting decides) · **Covers:** moving and working when everything weighs more
- **Why these attributes:** endurance, then strength
- **What failure looks like:** The character tires quickly and has to rest. Spectacular failure: they collapse, faint or injure themselves in a fall.
