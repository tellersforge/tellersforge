# Smuggling & Concealment

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Getting goods where they aren't allowed to go. A false bottom in a wine barrel, coins sewn into a hem, a hidden locker behind the ship's water tanks, a knife in a boot. Smuggling is the art of hiding things from people whose job is to find them, and staying relaxed while they look.

The smuggler knows how inspectors work, what they check, when they tire and how they can be distracted or bribed. They build compartments, choose routes and plan cover stories. Border folk, pirates, rebels and ordinary traders facing heavy taxes all practise it. In some places it is honest work in all but name, done openly while the law looks elsewhere; in others it gets people hanged.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** hidden compartments, holdout items, running goods past inspectors
- **Why these attributes:** planning, then hiding it
- **What failure looks like:** the hidden goods are found, or the hiding place is too obvious to use. Spectacular failure: the smuggler is caught red-handed with an audience watching.
