# Sculpture

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Shaping figures that stand in the world. The sculptor carves a saint from limestone, models a warrior in clay and casts him in bronze, whittles a small god from a piece of driftwood. Sculpture covers statues, busts, reliefs cut into temple walls and figures of every size, from a charm that fits in the hand to a colossus guarding a harbour.

The work is slow and physical. Stone forgives nothing; a single wrong blow can crack a year's work. Clay is kinder but must be fired or cast. A sculptor needs strength, patience and a sense of how a figure will look from every side and in every light. Rulers pay them to make themselves immortal, temples to give their gods a face, and families to remember their dead.
