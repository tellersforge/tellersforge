# Charm & Seduction

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Persuasion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Winning people over with charm. A smile, a compliment, a lingering glance, laughter at the right joke. Charm makes people like you; seduction makes them want you, whether as a friend, lover or confidant. Courtiers, entertainers, con artists and romantics all practise it, some honestly, some not.

It relies on presence, attentiveness and reading what the other person wants. Flattery works on the vain, sincerity on the lonely, mystery on the curious. Charm opens doors, loosens tongues and smooths quarrels. It fails with the suspicious and the proud, and it can make powerful enemies when discovered.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** winning people over, courtship, flattery
- **Why these attributes:** charisma, then timing
- **What failure looks like:** The target is unmoved or finds the attempt awkward. Spectacular failure: they are offended, disgusted, or see straight through the act.
