# Disguise

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Deception*

*aka: ______________________________*

Becoming someone else to the eye. A beard glued on, a scar painted, a hunched back padded in, a soldier's coat borrowed from a line, a limp practised in the alley. Disguise covers make-up, costume, posture and the small details that tell others who a person is: the hands of a labourer, the shoes of a clerk, the way a noble ignores servants.

The best disguises are simple and believable. A spy who looks like a baker is more convincing than one dressed as a duke. Actors, spies, thieves and fugitives all rely on it, along with the occasional prince who wants to walk the streets unseen. The skill cannot make someone pass a close friend's scrutiny forever, but it can buy a crucial hour.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/CHA/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** make-up, costume, posture, looking like someone else
- **Why these attributes:** planning, application, carrying it off
- **What failure looks like:** the disguise looks false or doesn't fit the part. Spectacular failure: it falls apart at the worst moment, a beard slipping or a wig coming off in front of the guards.
