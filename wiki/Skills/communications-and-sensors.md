# Communications & Sensors

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The ears and eyes of an age that has learned to listen past the horizon. The telegraph key clicking in a lonely station, the radio operator bent over a crackle of static, the sonar watcher counting the beat of a distant screw, the officer staring at a green sweep for the blip that shouldn't be there. At its simplest, the skill sends and receives messages. At its best, it reads meaning into noise.

That second half matters more than it looks. Anyone can switch on a scanner; it takes training to know that a faint echo is a whale and not a submarine, that a weather front is hiding a ship, or that a garbled signal is a code rather than interference. Ships, aircraft, starships and outposts all keep someone at the set, and those people are often the first to know when something is terribly wrong.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** electricity · **Covers:** radio, telegraph, radar, sonar, scanners, reading anomalies
- **Why these attributes:** operation, then interpretation
- **What failure looks like:** the signal is lost or misread, and the contact is missed. Spectacular failure: the operator is confidently wrong, reporting an enemy that isn't there or clearing one that is.

## Not to be confused with

*[Signals](signals.md)*, which uses flags, lights, drums and smoke. Communications & Sensors needs powered equipment and reads what the machines detect.
