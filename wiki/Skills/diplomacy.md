# Diplomacy

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The slow art of getting two groups who distrust each other to put their names to the same page. Diplomats carry messages between chieftains, sit through feasts they would rather avoid, and spend days on the wording of a single clause. They learn what each side truly needs, what it only says it needs, and what it will never admit to wanting.

Diplomacy is patient and formal. It is played in tents between armies, in the halls of rival guilds, and at tables where every seat was argued over before anyone sat down. A good envoy can end a war without either side feeling it lost, which is harder than winning one.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** treaties, mediation, formal negotiation between groups
- **Why these attributes:** presence, judgement, understanding interests
- **What failure looks like:** talks stall, and both sides go home with nothing. Spectacular failure: an insult is given or taken, and the parties leave angrier than they came.
