# Bowyery & Fletching

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A bow is a tree limb persuaded to store the strength of an arm. The bowyer chooses the stave, seasons it, and shapes it slowly, tillering it until both limbs bend evenly; composite bows add horn, sinew and glue, and months of drying. The fletcher makes the arrows: straight shafts, points fitted true, feathers split and glued so that each flies like its fellows. Crossbows, with their stocks, locks and strings, share the same workshop.

The craft is patient, finicky and deeply practical. A good bow lasts years; a bad one breaks in the archer's hands. Armies of archers depend on bowyers and fletchers working through the winter, and hunting peoples teach the art to every child.
