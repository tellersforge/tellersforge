# Mounted Combat

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Fighting from a moving animal. The knight lowers his lance and charges; the steppe rider turns in the saddle and looses arrows behind him; the cavalry trooper slashes down at infantry with a sabre. Mounted Combat covers fighting and shooting from horseback, camelback or any beast that will carry a warrior into battle.

It demands more than riding well. The rider must strike while balancing, guide the mount with knees and weight, and keep the animal calm among screams and blood. Horse and rider must trust each other entirely. Mounted warriors dominated battlefields for centuries, and in many cultures the right to fight from the saddle is a mark of nobility. Falling off in the middle of a charge is not just embarrassing; it is often fatal.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/COU/STR · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** agriculture · **Covers:** fighting and shooting from the saddle
- **What failure looks like:** the rider misses, loses balance or can't control the mount. Spectacular failure: the rider falls, or the frightened animal bolts into danger.

## Not to be confused with
