# Counting & Measuring

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Technology*

*aka: ______________________________*

Sums, weights and distances in everyday life. Counting sheep through a gate, working out how much grain a family needs until harvest, pacing the length of a field, knowing that the shopkeeper's scale is short, estimating how long before the tide turns. Most people do a little of this without thinking; some are notably better at it than others.

It is simple reasoning, practised. Merchants' children learn it early, and so do builders, cooks and quartermasters. It is not learned mathematics, only the practical head for quantities that keeps people from being cheated and makes sure there is enough rope, bread or lamp oil for the trip.
