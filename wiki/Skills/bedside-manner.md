# Bedside Manner

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The voice that tells a frightened patient the truth without breaking them. Bedside manner is sitting down rather than standing, saying the hard word plainly and then staying in the room. It is the calm hand on the shoulder of a man screaming in the field hospital, the quiet explanation to a family, the steady tone that makes a patient hold still for the needle.

Healers who have it are asked for by name. Those who lack it can be brilliant and still lose patients to fear, refusal and flight. Priests, nurses, midwives and old soldiers who have sat with the dying learn it the same way: one terrible conversation at a time.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/WIS/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** calming patients, delivering bad news, steadying the panicking (designer's original)
- **Why these attributes:** warmth, empathy, the nerve to say hard things
- **What failure looks like:** the patient stays frightened, stiff or uncooperative. Spectacular failure: the words land wrong, and the patient panics, refuses treatment or bolts.
