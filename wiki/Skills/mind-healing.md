# Mind Healing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Wounds that do not bleed. The mind healer listens, patiently and without judgement, to people haunted by grief, fear, despair or memories that return unbidden. They help soldiers who cannot sleep, widows who cannot stop weeping and people who have seen what should not be seen. Some work through conversation, some through ritual, rest and routine, some through modern medicine.

It is quiet work, slow and uncertain, and its successes are often invisible. Many ages barely recognise it, and practitioners may be priests, wise women or friends who simply know how to listen. In later ages it becomes a science of its own. Everywhere, it demands trust and a great deal of patience.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/INT/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** counselling, trauma care, psychiatry
- **Why these attributes:** empathy, knowledge, trust
- **What failure looks like:** The patient does not improve or refuses to talk. Spectacular failure: an old wound is reopened without healing it, and the patient is shaken worse than before.

## Not to be confused with

*[Caregiving](caregiving.md)*, which offers comfort and daily help; Mind Healing works deliberately to treat lasting harm to the mind.
