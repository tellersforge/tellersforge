# Alchemy ✦

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Steaming flasks, bubbling retorts and a room that smells of sulphur. The alchemist grinds, distils and combines, seeking potions that heal, poisons that kill, elixirs that promise long life and the reagents that other crafts need. In worlds where magic runs, the work can create wonders. In the sciences of later ages, the same careful habits become practical chemistry.

The alchemist needs patience, precision and a healthy fear of explosions. Mistakes blind, burn and poison. Their notebooks are full of symbols, recipes and warnings, often written so that rivals can't steal them. Physicians, court sorcerers, dyers, apothecaries and treasure seekers all have reason to consult them, though few want to live next door.
