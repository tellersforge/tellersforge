# Farming

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The oldest labour and the one every other rests on. The farmer reads the soil by its colour and crumble, the sky by its clouds and the season by the first birds, and decides what to sow, when to plough, and when to trust the rain. Fields, kitchen gardens, orchards and vineyards all answer to the same patience. Water must be led where it is needed, pests driven off, and the harvest brought in before the weather turns.

It is backbreaking, repetitive and never finished, and it makes the difference between a village that eats and one that starves. Farmers are rarely heroes in songs, yet every hero eats their bread. In hard times a stranger who knows how to coax a crop from poor ground is welcome anywhere.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/END/STR · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** agriculture · **Covers:** fields, gardens, orchards, vines, irrigation
- **Why these attributes:** reading soil and season, long labour, hard work
- **What failure looks like:** The crop is thin, late or blighted. Spectacular failure: a wrong choice about planting, water or storage ruins the harvest outright.

## Not to be confused with

*[Herding & Husbandry](herding-and-husbandry.md)*, which raises livestock; Farming works the land itself, growing grain, fruit and vegetables.
