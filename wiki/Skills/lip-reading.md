# Lip Reading

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Understanding speech without hearing it. Watching a conversation across a crowded hall, reading a whispered order through a window, following a friend's words when noise drowns them out. Deaf people often learn it young; spies, guards and gossips learn it for their own reasons.

It is careful watching and knowing the language well. Many sounds look alike on the lips, so the reader guesses from context and expression. It works best face on, in good light, with someone who speaks clearly. Beards, masks, turned heads and foreign accents make it much harder.
