# Acting

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Being someone you aren't, convincingly. On a stage the actor becomes a king, a ghost or a fool, and a whole theatre believes it for an evening. Off the stage the same skill lets a spy play a grieving widow, a con artist a lost heir, and a deserter a respectable traveller. Acting covers playing a role, holding it under pressure and, at its most demanding, impersonating a particular real person.

It asks for voice, posture, timing and nerve. The actor studies how people speak, stand and hesitate, and borrows those details. Travelling companies, temple players, court entertainers and tricksters all practise it. A good performance can move a crowd to tears, or into a riot; a bad one can get the performer pelted with fruit, or worse.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/INT/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** theatre, playing a role, impersonating a specific person
- **Why these attributes:** presence, understanding the role, nerve
- **What failure looks like:** the role is unconvincing, and the audience stays unmoved. Spectacular failure: the actor breaks character at the worst moment, revealing who they really are.
