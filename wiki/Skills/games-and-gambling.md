# Games & Gambling

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Knucklebones in the barracks, cards in the back room of a tavern, a board game carved into the steps of a temple, bets on a cockfight or a horse. Games of chance and skill are found in every culture, and so are the people who are suspiciously good at them. Soldiers, sailors and idle nobles play most, and lose most.

The skill is knowing the odds, reading the other players and recognising when to stop. A good player wins more than they lose, and knows when the table is rigged. It is also a social passport: sitting down to play is the quickest way to make friends, enemies and useful acquaintances in a strange town.
