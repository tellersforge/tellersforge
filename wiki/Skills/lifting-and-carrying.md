# Lifting & Carrying

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Getting a heavy thing off the ground and keeping it there. A sack of grain onto a cart, a fallen beam off a trapped friend, a stuck door forced open with a shoulder. Porters, dockhands, farmhands and stonemasons' labourers live by it, and so does anyone who has ever had to carry a wounded comrade out of trouble.

There is more craft in it than it seems. Good lifters use their legs, keep the load close and know when to ask for a second pair of hands. Bad lifters are brave for a moment and sore for a month. A strong back is admired everywhere, and in hard times it is also the most honest wage there is.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **STR** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** lifting, hauling, forcing, carrying loads
- **Why these attributes:** raw strength
- **What failure looks like:** The load won't budge, or has to be put down short of where it was going. Spectacular failure: it is dropped on something fragile, or someone's foot, or the lifter wrenches their back.
