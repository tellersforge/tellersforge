# Physical Sciences

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

How the world works at its roots: force and motion, heat and light, the combining of substances, the flow of energy. The physical scientist measures, tests and theorises, and trusts nothing that hasn't been shown twice. The subject begins with natural philosophers rolling balls down slopes and grows into laboratories, reactors and the physics of starships.

It answers questions no craftsman needs to ask but every inventor does. Why does this lens focus light? What will happen if these two substances meet? How much energy is in that cell, and what will happen if it is released all at once? The one who knows can often say what a strange device is for before anyone dares touch it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** printing · **Covers:** physics, chemistry theory, optics, energy
- **Why these attributes:** heavy knowledge, then insight
- **What failure looks like:** the theory is misapplied, and the prediction fails. Spectacular failure: an experiment goes wrong in the loudest, hottest or most poisonous way possible.
