# Research

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Investigation*

*aka: ______________________________*

Finding the one record that matters among ten thousand that don't. The researcher knows how a library is ordered, how archives were catalogued by clerks long dead, which indexes lie and which librarians know more than their catalogues. In later ages the same patience turns to databases and search engines, and the same instinct separates the useful result from the flood.

Most of research is method: start broad, follow references, note where everything came from. The rest is judgement, knowing when a dusty letter is a curiosity and when it is the key to everything. Scholars, lawyers, spies and journalists all live by it, and all of them know the particular joy of the page that answers the question.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** writing · **Covers:** libraries, archives, databases, finding the one record that matters
- **Why these attributes:** method, then knowing what matters
- **What failure looks like:** hours pass among the shelves and nothing useful turns up. Spectacular failure: the researcher finds something convincing, and it is wrong.

## Not to be confused with

*[Investigation](investigation.md)*, which reasons from clues in the world; Research digs the answer out of what others have already written down.
