# Tracking

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Survival*

*aka: ______________________________*

Following the signs a creature leaves as it moves. A bent stem, a scuffed stone, a hair caught on bark, a print half filled with rain. The tracker reads them all and knows how old they are, how fast the quarry was moving, whether it was hurt, and where it was likely going. The trail is a story, and the tracker reads it one mark at a time.

Hunters use it on game, soldiers on enemy patrols, and lawmen on fugitives. It is slow, careful and often boring, until the tracks suddenly become fresh. A great tracker can follow a trail across rocky ground days after others have lost it, and knows when the one being tracked has doubled back.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **PER**/WIS/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** following people or animals, judging the age of signs
- **Why these attributes:** reading signs, deduction, staying on the trail
- **What failure looks like:** the trail is lost. Spectacular failure: the tracker follows the wrong trail with confidence, or walks straight into the quarry's ambush.

## Not to be confused with

*[Hunting](hunting.md)*, which is the stalk and the kill; and *Perception (attribute)*, which notices things in passing. Tracking deliberately follows a trail.
