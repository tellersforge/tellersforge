# Implants & Prosthetics

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Replacing what the body has lost or improving what it has. The practitioner fits artificial limbs, eyes, ears and organs, connects them to nerves and flesh, and keeps them working. In early ages this means a carved leg or a hook; in later ones a mechanical hand that feels, a camera eye or a chip in the brain.

The work mixes surgery, engineering and patient care. Implants must fit the body, not provoke it, and be kept clean and maintained. Soldiers, accident victims and the curious or ambitious all seek it out. A skilled practitioner can give someone a new life, while a poor one can leave them with a hardware problem in their own body.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/INT/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** computing · **Covers:** installing and maintaining artificial and cybernetic body parts
- **Why these attributes:** fine hands, heavy technical knowledge, judgement
- **What failure looks like:** The implant fits poorly, glitches or is rejected. Spectacular failure: it fails dramatically, harms the patient or behaves unpredictably.

## Not to be confused with

*[Surgery](surgery.md)*, which operates on the body; Implants & Prosthetics installs and maintains artificial parts in it.
