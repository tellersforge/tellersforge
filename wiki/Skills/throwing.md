# Throwing

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A stone at a crow, a rope to a sinking boat, a ball through a hoop, a lantern up to a friend on the wall. Throwing is aim first and force second, and humans are unusually good at it. Shepherds keep wolves off with it, children settle arguments with it, and soldiers in every age have lobbed whatever came to hand over walls and into trenches.

A good thrower judges weight and distance by feel. They know that a long throw is an arc, not a line, and that a wind or a rolling deck changes everything. Plenty of people can throw hard. Far fewer can put the thing exactly where it needs to land, and then do it again.
