# Military Tactics

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

How to win the fight in front of you. Tactics is placing a handful of soldiers where they can see without being seen, choosing the ground for an ambush, timing a charge, and knowing when to withdraw before a skirmish becomes a massacre. It is the craft of sergeants, raiders, captains and, in some stories, thieves planning a robbery like a small war.

Good tactics looks simple afterwards. The enemy walked into the crossfire, the gate was opened from inside, the reinforcements arrived at the right moment. None of it was luck. The tactician had looked at the ground, counted the doors, and thought about what the other side would most likely do.
