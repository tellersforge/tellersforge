# Survival: Ruins & Wasteland

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Living in the bones of a fallen world. Ruins and wasteland survival is finding shelter in buildings that might collapse, clean water where everything is fouled, and a safe path through rubble, poisoned ground and whatever else now lives there. It is reading the signs of danger: stained earth, dead birds, fresh tracks in dust that should have been undisturbed.

In many settings this is ancient ruins overgrown by forest; in others it is a shattered city after a war or a catastrophe. The survivors who know such places move carefully, trust nothing that looks too easy, and never drink from a pool that nothing else drinks from.
