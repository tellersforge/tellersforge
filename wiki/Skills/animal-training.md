# Animal Training

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Handling an animal gets it through the day; training changes what it is. The trainer takes a wild colt, a half-grown dog or a sulking ox and, over weeks of patience, turns it into something that comes when called, stands when told and does not bolt at the first drum. It is slow work built on repetition, reward and a calm that the animal can feel through a rope or a hand on its neck.

Horse-breakers are the famous ones, bruised and grinning in the paddock, but the skill belongs just as much to shepherds with clever dogs, to circus folk, to war-stable masters and to the old woman whose goose guards the gate better than any man. Good trainers are remembered by their animals long after the lesson is over.

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3

- **What failure looks like:** The animal learns nothing, or learns the wrong thing. Spectacular failure: it turns on the trainer, bolts, or picks up a bad habit that will take months to undo.

## Not to be confused with

*[Animal Handling](animal-handling.md)*, which calms and directs an animal in the moment; Animal Training teaches it lasting behaviour, over weeks, that it keeps without you.
