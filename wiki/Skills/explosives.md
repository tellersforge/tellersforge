# Explosives

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Powder, charges, fuses and nerve. The explosives expert knows how much is enough to open a door and how much would bring down the house, how to shape a charge so it cuts in one direction and how to fix the timing so everyone is clear. Miners, sappers, demolition crews and saboteurs use the craft to break rock, walls and bridges. The other half is the reverse: making bombs safe, often with little time and an audience waiting outside.

Everyone who works with explosives has a story, and the careful ones live to tell it. The work rewards calculation, steady fingers and courage in equal measure. A good hand can open a vault quietly; a bad one opens the building, the street and themselves.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/DEX/COU · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** gunpowder · **Covers:** blasting, demolition, breaching, bomb disposal
- **Why these attributes:** knowledge, steady hands, nerve
- **What failure looks like:** The charge fizzles or blows too little or in the wrong direction. Spectacular failure: it goes off early, too big, or while someone is still holding it.
