# Instrument (per instrument)

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

One instrument, learned properly. The bone flute, the drum, the lyre, the bagpipe, the violin, the piano, the electric guitar: each is its own world, and a character learns one at a time, or one family of close relations. The drummer and the harpist share a love of music but not a single technique.

Mastery takes years. Fingers learn the fret or keys until they no longer need thinking about; the ear learns to hear a note before it is played. Musicians are hired for weddings, taverns, courts, armies and funerals, and good ones are always in demand. In battle a war drum or horn can steady frightened soldiers; at a feast, a well-played tune can soften a hard bargain.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** playing one instrument or instrument family
- **Why these attributes:** technique, then musical ear
- **What failure looks like:** the notes are wrong, the rhythm falters, the tune loses its audience. Spectacular failure: a string snaps, the instrument breaks, or the music draws exactly the wrong reaction.

## Not to be confused with

*[Singing](singing.md)*, which makes music with the voice alone; and *[Musical Composition](musical-composition.md)*, which writes the music rather than playing it.
