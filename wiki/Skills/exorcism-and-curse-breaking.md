# Exorcism & Curse-Breaking ✦

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Undoing what the unseen has done. A house where the doors slam at night, a child who speaks in a voice not their own, a family whose firstborn sons have all died young. The exorcist comes with candles, salt, holy words or the drum, finds the spirit and makes it leave. The curse-breaker digs into the past to learn what was done, and what will undo it.

It is detective work mixed with danger. Every haunting and every curse has a cause, and finding it matters as much as the rite. Priests, shamans, wise women and a few hardened specialists practise it. The spirits resist, and the people involved often lie. Success brings peace; failure can make the trouble far worse.

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **COU**/WIS/INT/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4

- ✦ Magic-flavoured: a learned procedure; any real supernatural effect belongs to a power.
