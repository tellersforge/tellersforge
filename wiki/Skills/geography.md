# Geography

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Lore*

*aka: ______________________________*

Knowledge of the wider world: which mountains lie between the river kingdoms and the sea, which peoples live beyond the desert, where the passes close in winter and which roads the caravans prefer. A geographer carries a map of the world in the head, rough at the edges but sound at the centre, and knows how its parts connect.

Merchants value it for routes, generals for terrain, and rulers for knowing what lies over the border. It is book learning and travellers' tales stitched together, and it changes as kingdoms rise and fall. A good geographer can tell you where a stranger probably comes from by the cut of his coat and the spices he asks for.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** lands, regions, peoples, routes
- **Why these attributes:** memory, then context
- **What failure looks like:** the facts are vague, out of date or simply mistaken. Spectacular failure: the character confidently sends the party the wrong way across the world.
