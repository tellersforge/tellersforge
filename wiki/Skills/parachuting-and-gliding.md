# Parachuting & Gliding

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Stepping out of an aircraft into empty air, or launching from a hilltop on wings of cloth and wire. Parachutists fall until the canopy opens and then steer it down; glider pilots ride rising air without an engine. Soldiers jump behind enemy lines, adventurers jump for joy, and the lost jump because the aircraft is on fire.

It is nerve, timing and feel for the wind. Knowing when to pull the cord, how to spill air to turn, how to land without breaking a leg. A glider pilot reads warm fields and cliffs for lift. Both know that the ground always wins in the end, and the trick is making it a gentle win.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **COU**/AGI/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** electricity · **Covers:** jumping from aircraft, gliders, controlled descent
- **Why these attributes:** nerve first, then control and judgement
- **What failure looks like:** The landing is rough or far off target. Spectacular failure: the canopy tangles, the glider stalls, or the jumper lands in trees, water or among enemies.

## Not to be confused with

*[Aircraft Piloting](aircraft-piloting.md)*, which is flying powered aircraft; Parachuting & Gliding is the unpowered descent.
