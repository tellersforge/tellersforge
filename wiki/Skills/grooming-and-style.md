# Grooming & Style

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Hair cut and dressed, a beard trimmed, a face painted, a costume chosen to make an impression. Grooming and style is the art of making people look how they want to look, or how someone else needs them to look. Barbers, hairdressers, cosmetic artists, valets and fashion advisers all practise it, and in courts and cities it can make or break a reputation.

It seems trivial until an audience matters. A good stylist knows the fashions of the season, the rules of rank and the small signs that mark someone as an outsider. They can make a tired soldier presentable for court or a rich heir look like a dock worker. Barbers, besides, hear everything.
