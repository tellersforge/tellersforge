# Wit & Mockery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The sharp tongue. A clever comeback, a biting joke, a string of insults that leaves the victim speechless and the crowd roaring. Some cultures hold formal flyting contests in rhymed insult; jesters and satirists make a living from it; everyone else just enjoys a good taunt.

Wit is quick thinking and timing. The mocker notices weaknesses and exploits them, turns an opponent's words against them and knows when to stop before a joke turns into a fight. It can win respect, deflate pomposity and goad an enemy into a mistake. It can also make lifelong enemies.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **CHA**/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** verbal duels, taunting, insult contests, comedy
- **Why these attributes:** delivery, then quick thinking
- **What failure looks like:** The joke falls flat or the insult misses. Spectacular failure: the character becomes the butt of the joke, or offends someone dangerous.
