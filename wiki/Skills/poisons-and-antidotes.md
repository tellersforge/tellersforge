# Poisons & Antidotes

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The dark twin of healing. The poisoner knows which berries stop the heart, which mushrooms kill slowly, which powders dissolve without taste and which venoms act in moments. They know how to gather, refine and hide them. The same knowledge runs the other way: recognising the signs of poisoning in a victim, naming the cause and finding the antidote before it is too late.

Courtiers fear it, food tasters depend on it, assassins and healers both study it. Some poisoners are famous; the best are never suspected. Those who treat poisonings are just as quiet, and just as necessary, in any palace where people die of convenient illnesses.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3

- **What failure looks like:** The poison is weak or the antidote fails to take hold. Spectacular failure: the poisoner doses themselves, or the wrong person is affected.
