# Geology & Prospecting

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Reading rock. The prospector looks at a hillside and sees what lies beneath it: the vein of ore that shows as rusty stains, the layer of good building stone, the spring waiting under a certain kind of clay. Geology is the wider understanding of how the land was laid down, worn away and folded over ages beyond counting.

Prospectors are a hardy, secretive breed, wandering wild country with a hammer, a pan and a mule, and telling no one what they find. Engineers and mine owners want the same knowledge with more paperwork. Either way, the one who has it can say where to dig, where not to build, and why the mountain rumbles.
