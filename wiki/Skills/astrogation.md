# Astrogation

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Plotting a path between worlds. Nothing in space sits still: planets swing round their stars, moons round their planets, and a ship leaving one must aim for where the other will be, weeks or months ahead. Astrogation is the mathematics of orbits and the art of route planning, whether that means a slow, fuel-saving arc between moons or a jump across vast distances by some means the setting allows.

The astrogator lives among charts, calculations and long lists of hazards: drifting rock, radiation belts, the pull of heavy worlds. They choose the route; the pilot flies it. A good astrogator saves time, fuel and lives. A careless one sends a ship somewhere it has no hope of returning from, and has a very long journey to think about the error.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** spaceflight · **Covers:** orbital mechanics, jump and route plotting
- **Why these attributes:** extreme calculation, then judgement
- **What failure looks like:** the route is slow, wasteful or slightly off. Spectacular failure: the ship arrives somewhere unintended, or not at all.
