# Public Health

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Keeping a whole town healthy rather than one patient. Public health is the knowledge of how sickness spreads through water, air, rats, crowds and trade, and what to do about it. Its practitioners plan sewers and clean wells, isolate the sick, burn infected bedding, set quarantines at ports and teach people to wash their hands. In plague years they are hated for closing markets and loved afterwards when the town survives.

The skill is part science, part organisation and part persuasion, for no plan works unless people follow it. Officials, physicians, priests and later scientists practise it. A town that ignores them learns the lesson the hard way.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** quarantine, sanitation, infection control, epidemics
- **Why these attributes:** knowledge, judgement, getting people to comply
- **What failure looks like:** The measures fail to slow the sickness. Spectacular failure: panic, riots, or a quarantine that spreads the disease instead of containing it.

## Not to be confused with

*[Medicine](medicine.md)*, which treats individual patients; Public Health protects whole populations from disease.
