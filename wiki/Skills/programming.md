# Programming

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Instructions written for a thing that does exactly what it is told. The programmer sits with a cold drink and a warm screen, building worlds of logic line by line: the accounts system, the navigation routine, the guidance for a missile, the patient voice that answers questions aboard a ship. Every line is simple. Getting thousands of them to work together is not.

Most of the job is finding one's own mistakes. The program runs, does something strange, and the programmer hunts through the logic until the misplaced word turns up. Administrators keep systems running through the night; researchers teach machines to learn. In the far future the finest programmers are treated like priests of an obscure faith, consulted gravely by people who have no idea what they actually do.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** computing · **Covers:** writing software, systems administration, AI
- **Why these attributes:** heavy logic, then debugging judgement
- **What failure looks like:** the code doesn't run, or runs and does the wrong thing. Spectacular failure: it seems to work, and the hidden flaw surfaces later, when it matters most.
