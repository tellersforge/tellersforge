# Locksmithing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Wards, tumblers, springs and a key cut to match. The locksmith builds locks, from the simple padlock of a peasant's chest to the many-bolted door of a treasury, and makes and copies the keys that open them. They repair what is broken, open what is stuck for the rightful owner, and fit new locks when a key has gone missing in suspicious circumstances.

A good locksmith understands locks better than any thief, which is why the rich hire them and the careful watch them. Their workshops are full of tiny files and brass shavings. Some keep a ledger of every key they have ever cut, and a few, quietly, keep a copy of each one.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** iron · **Covers:** making and servicing locks and keys
- **Why these attributes:** handwork, mechanism, judgement
- **What failure looks like:** The lock jams or the key does not fit. Spectacular failure: the mechanism breaks and seals the lock for good, or the key opens more than it should.

## Not to be confused with

*[Lockpicking](lockpicking.md)*, which opens a lock without its key; Locksmithing builds and repairs locks, and the keys that belong to them.
