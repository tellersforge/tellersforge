# Computer Use

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The everyday knack of making a machine with a screen do what you want. Finding the file someone saved in the wrong place, sending the message, filling in the form, reading the map on a terminal, persuading a stubborn printer that it has paper after all. In the eras that have computers, nearly everyone has some of it, and the ones who don't are quietly helped by a younger relative.

It is not the art of building software or breaking into it. It is knowing where things usually live, what a warning means, and which button not to press. Clerks, students, soldiers at a console and ship's crew on a bridge all lean on it. A traveller from an older age, faced with a glowing panel and no one to ask, learns quickly how much of the modern world hides behind it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 1 · **Attributes:** **INT** · **Mastered at:** +5 | 1
- **Needs:** computing · **Covers:** operating everyday computers and software
- **Why these attributes:** learning
- **What failure looks like:** the task goes nowhere: the file isn't found, the form is rejected, the screen stays stubborn. Spectacular failure: something important is deleted, sent to the wrong person, or locked up for good.
