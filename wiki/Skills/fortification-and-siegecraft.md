# Fortification & Siegecraft

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Walls, ditches, bunkers and the art of taking them. The fortifier knows where to put a rampart so an enemy must climb it under fire, how to angle a tower so no ground is hidden, and how to barricade a farmhouse into a refuge in a single night. The besieger knows the reverse: where to dig, where to mine, where to batter and when a wall is ripe for breaching.

Both sides of the craft are old, slow and grim. They are learned by soldiers, engineers and survivors, and practised with spades more often than swords. A sound fortification changes a battle before it begins; a well-planned siege ends it without anyone climbing a ladder.

- **What failure looks like:** The defence has a gap or the siege work goes slowly. Spectacular failure: a mine collapses, a breach opens in the wrong wall, or the defenders are trapped by their own works.
