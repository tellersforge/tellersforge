# Survival: Forest (Woodcraft)

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Survival*

*aka: ______________________________*

The craft of living well in the woods. Woodcraft is choosing a dry campsite under good trees, building a shelter from branches and bark, finding water and deadwood, and knowing which berries the birds leave alone. It is also moving quietly without getting lost, keeping a fire small and hidden, and knowing the forest's dangers, from falling limbs to wolves in winter.

Foresters, charcoal burners, outlaws and hunters have it, and to them the forest is a larder and a home. To everyone else it is dark, confusing and full of noises. Old woodcrafters speak of the forest almost as a person, one that tolerates those who respect it.
