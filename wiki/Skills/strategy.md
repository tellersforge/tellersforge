# Strategy

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The general's art: deciding which battles to fight, where, and whether to fight at all. Strategy looks at the whole map, the whole season, the whole war, and weighs roads, harvests, alliances and the morale of distant capitals. A strategist can lose every skirmish and still win the war, or win every battle and lose it.

It is also the art of rulers, merchant houses and anyone with a long game to play. A good strategist thinks in months and years, asks what the enemy wants and what they fear, and is rarely surprised. They are also, in most histories, the ones who are blamed when things go wrong.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** campaigns, generalship, grand strategy
- **Why these attributes:** heavy planning, then judgement
- **What failure looks like:** the plan misjudges the enemy or the situation. Spectacular failure: the campaign plays right into the opponent's hands.
