# Blacksmithing

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Fire, iron and a hammer. The smith heats bar stock until it glows and beats it into the hardware of daily life: nails, hinges, hoops for barrels, chains, pothooks, horseshoes, axes, hoes and the tools of every other trade. The forge roars, the anvil rings, and the smith reads the heat by the colour of the metal and judges a weld by eye and ear.

A village without a smith is a village that waits for a travelling one. The smithy is where news is traded and repairs are done, and where people gather in winter for the warmth. Smiths are strong, respected and sometimes a little feared, and in old tales they are suspected of knowing more than iron.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **STR**/DEX/END · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** copper · **Covers:** forging tools, hardware, horseshoes
- **Why these attributes:** power, precision, endurance at the forge
- **What failure looks like:** The piece is spoiled, a weld is weak, or the smith is burned. Spectacular failure: a tool or fitting breaks at the worst possible moment.
