# Basketry & Cordage

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Reeds, willow, grass, bark and fibre, twisted, plaited and woven into the things that hold a world together. Baskets carry grain and fish, mats cover floors and walls, nets catch birds and fish, and cordage lashes rafts, hafts axes and hangs meat out of reach of wolves. The craft is older than pottery and still in use in every age.

The skill lies in choosing the right material, cutting it at the right season and knowing how to keep it supple while the hands do their work. Most of it is done while talking, by the fire or in the shade, by people who could do it in the dark. A well-made basket lasts a lifetime and a good rope can be trusted with one.
