# Administration

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Making an organisation actually function. The steward runs a great house so that the meals appear, the servants are paid and the roof gets fixed. The clerk in a government office knows which form must be stamped by whom. The magistrate's secretary knows how to hurry a petition, lose one, or bury it under other papers for a year.

This skill is invisible when done well and maddening when done badly. It covers offices, estates, households, tax collection and every kind of bureaucracy, and it depends on writing, because records are its bones. Good administrators are the reason a realm keeps running when its rulers are busy fighting. Characters who master it can find their way through any tangle of officialdom, or make one for others.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** writing · **Covers:** running offices, estates and households; tax; handling bureaucracy
- **Why these attributes:** organisation, judgement, dealing with officials
- **What failure looks like:** the paperwork goes astray, the request is delayed, the household falls behind. Spectacular failure: the matter lands on the wrong desk and causes a scandal, or an official grudge.

## Not to be confused with
