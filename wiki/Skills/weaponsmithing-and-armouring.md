# Weaponsmithing & Armouring

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The finest and hardest work of the forge. A weaponsmith makes blades that are hard at the edge and springy at the core, heads for spears and axes that stay on their hafts, and helmets beaten from a single sheet. Armourers link mail ring by ring and shape plate that turns a blow and still lets the wearer move. In later ages the same trade makes steel helmets and the stiff plates of modern body armour.

Their work is life and death, and it shows. They test every piece, argue about steel and tempering, and pass secrets only to trusted apprentices. A master armourer measures a knight like a tailor and fits them like a second skin; a lord will wait years for the right smith.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/STR/END/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** copper · **Covers:** blades, polearms, helmets, mail, plate, body armour
- **Why these attributes:** precision, power, endurance, design
- **What failure looks like:** The blade is brittle or the armour poorly fitted. Spectacular failure: a flaw goes unseen until the weapon breaks or the armour fails in battle.

## Not to be confused with

*[Blacksmithing](blacksmithing.md)*, which makes tools and hardware; Weaponsmithing & Armouring shapes blades and armour, harder and less forgiving work.
