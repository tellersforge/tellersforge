# Mechanical Engineering

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Wheels, levers, gears, pistons and the power to drive them. The mechanical engineer designs machines: water mills and windmills, cranes that lift stone blocks, pumps that drain mines, siege engines that hurl rocks, and later steam engines, turbines and motors. They work with drawings, models and calculations, and hand the plans to smiths and machinists to build.

A good engineer understands how force travels through a machine and where it will try to break it. Rulers and merchants hire them to build things that multiply labour, and armies to build things that throw fire. Their workshops are cluttered with prototypes and failures, and the successes change how people live.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** wheel and roads · **Covers:** designing mills, cranes, pumps, engines, siege engines
- **Why these attributes:** heavy design, judgement, building
- **What failure looks like:** The design jams, wastes power or cannot be built as drawn. Spectacular failure: the machine breaks violently, often at its first real use.
