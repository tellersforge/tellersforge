# Divination ✦

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Asking the world what will come, and reading its answer. The augur watches the flight of birds; the seer casts bones, sticks or lots; the card reader lays out a spread by lamplight; the astrologer charts the heavens at a child's birth; the dream-reader listens to a king's nightmare and chooses words very carefully. Divination covers all the arts that read signs and prophecy.

In some worlds it is superstition, a way of guiding choices and comforting the anxious. In others, where the unseen is real, the signs truly speak. Either way, the skill lies in knowing the systems and interpreting them wisely. Rulers keep diviners close and fear what they might say. A clever diviner learns that a vague prophecy is often the safest one.

- ✦ Magic-flavoured: a learned procedure; any real supernatural effect belongs to a power.
