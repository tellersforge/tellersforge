# Jewellery & Gem Cutting

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Gold drawn into wire, silver hammered thin, a stone ground until light breaks inside it. The jeweller works small and exact, with tiny tools and a lamp, setting gems into rings, brooches, crowns and chains of office. The gem-cutter cleaves raw stones along their hidden planes and grinds facets or polishes cabochons, and a carver works jade and other hard stones into seals and ornaments over months.

Their clients are the rich, the proud and the newly married. A jeweller learns to keep secrets and to spot a false stone at a glance. Their shops are heavily locked, and their hands are steady enough that a single slip, which can ruin a fortune, rarely happens.
