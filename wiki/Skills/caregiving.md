# Caregiving

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Healing*

*aka: ______________________________*

Looking after those who can't look after themselves. Minding children, feeding an invalid, washing the old and making sure they drink enough water, sitting up through a fever. In most places it is unpaid, unsung and done by whoever happens to be at home, and without it every household would fall apart.

Caregiving is wisdom and warmth. Knowing when a child is ill and when they are only sulky, keeping a sickroom clean and cheerful, getting a stubborn patient to take their broth. Nurses, nannies, grandparents and older siblings all have it. It is not medicine, but good care has saved more lives than most physicians.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** minding children, the sick and the elderly
- **Why these attributes:** attentiveness, then warmth
- **What failure looks like:** The patient or child is fractious, unwell or neglected for a while. Spectacular failure: something important is missed, and someone in the character's care comes to harm.

## Not to be confused with

*[Bedside Manner](bedside-manner.md)*, which is a healer's knack for putting patients at ease; Caregiving is the daily work of feeding, washing and minding.
