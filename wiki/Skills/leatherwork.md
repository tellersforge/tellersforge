# Leatherwork

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A hide from the butcher is not yet leather. First comes tanning, the long, foul-smelling work of soaking skins in bark or brine until they stop rotting and start lasting. Tanneries are always downwind of the town for a reason. Then the currier softens and oils the leather, and the leatherworker cuts, stitches and tools it into shoes, belts, saddles, harness, bottles, bags and jerkins.

Leather is tough, flexible and mendable, and every age uses it. The good worker knows which part of the hide is strong and which is soft, and matches it to the job. A well-made saddle fits both horse and rider, and a good boot walks a soldier across a continent without failing him.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/STR/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** tanning, leather goods, shoes, saddles, harness
- **Why these attributes:** handwork, force, judging the hide
- **What failure looks like:** The leather cracks or the stitching gives. Spectacular failure: a strap or saddle girth breaks just when the rider needs it most.

## Not to be confused with

*[Tailoring](tailoring.md)*, which works in cloth; Leatherwork prepares hides and makes tough goods like saddles, harness and boots.
