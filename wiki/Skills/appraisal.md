# Appraisal

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Knowing what a thing is worth, and whether it is what it claims to be. The appraiser turns a ring in the light, weighs a coin, sniffs a bolt of silk, runs a thumbnail down the back of a painting. They tell good wine from bad, true gold from gilding, old masters from last year's copies.

The skill is equal parts knowledge and suspicion. A good appraiser has seen a great many things and remembers what each was worth, where it came from and how it was faked. Pawnbrokers, auction houses, customs officers, collectors and thieves all rely on it. A clever appraiser can make a fortune by recognising treasure in a pile of junk, and another by recognising junk dressed up as treasure.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/PER/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** value, quality and authenticity of goods, art and antiques
- **Why these attributes:** knowledge, then a trained eye
- **What failure looks like:** the value is badly guessed or the fake goes unnoticed. Spectacular failure: the appraiser pays a fortune for a worthless copy, or sells a treasure for a song.
