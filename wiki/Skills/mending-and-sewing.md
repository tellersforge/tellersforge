# Mending & Sewing

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Crafting & Repair*

*aka: ______________________________*

Needle, thread and patience. Sewing a button back on, patching the knee of a pair of trousers, darning a sock, stitching a torn tent flap by lantern light or restitching the strap of a pack. In a world where cloth is costly, almost everyone mends, and a well-kept patch is a point of pride.

Mending is careful handwork and knowing what will hold. The right thread for the job, small even stitches, a double knot at the end. It keeps travellers clothed and gear working far from any tailor. It is also the quiet work of long evenings, when talk flows easily between people with their heads bent over their laps.
