# Powered Armour

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Walking in a machine shaped like a person. The exoskeleton lifts crates that would break a docker's back; the full combat suit lets a soldier run through fire and tear open a door. It is a heavy, humming, marvellous thing, and learning to move inside it is harder than it looks. The suit answers every twitch, which means every clumsy twitch becomes a clumsy stride twice as strong.

Operators talk about the first week, when they smash cups, walk into doorframes and fall over trying to turn. After that comes a strange grace, a sense of the suit as a second body. The best move in it lightly, climb in it, fight in it, and remember to plug it in at night. Miners, loaders, rescuers and shock troops in the far future all train with it.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **AGI**/STR/WIS · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** spaceflight · **Covers:** moving and working in exoskeletons and powered suits
- **Why these attributes:** control, strength, spatial sense
- **What failure looks like:** the wearer moves stiffly, breaks what they meant to hold, or tires the suit's power. Spectacular failure: the suit topples, locks up, or throws its wearer into something hard.

## Not to be confused with

*[Environment Suits](environment-suits.md)*, which only seal out danger. Powered Armour amplifies strength and movement.
