# Bookkeeping

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Keeping count of what is owned, owed and spent. In early ages it is notches on a stick split between buyer and seller, or cords knotted by colour and position in a record only the keeper can read. Later come clay tablets, ledgers in neat columns, and double entries that must always agree. The bookkeeper knows where the money went, and can prove it.

It is patient, careful and often dull work, with moments of great excitement when the numbers don't match. Temple stores, merchant houses, estates, guilds and armies all need it. A good audit can uncover a thief, a lost shipment or a noble's secret debts. Bookkeepers are rarely admired, often needed, and sometimes feared by those who have something to hide.
