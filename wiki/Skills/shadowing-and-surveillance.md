# Shadowing & Surveillance

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Watching without being watched. The shadow drifts through a crowd a dozen steps behind their target, changing coats, pausing at shop windows, never meeting their eyes. The watcher sits in a rented room across the street for three days, noting who comes and goes. The counter-agent sweeps a hotel room for hidden listening devices before saying a word.

It is patient, tedious and full of tension. Good watchers are forgettable: ordinary clothes, ordinary faces, nothing to draw a second glance. They know when to close in and when to let go before they're noticed. Detectives, spies, private agents and thieves casing a house all practise it. The other side of the skill is spotting when one is being followed, and knowing how to lose a tail.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **PER**/AGI/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** tailing, stakeouts, counter-surveillance, finding bugs
- **Why these attributes:** watchfulness, moving unseen, planning
- **What failure looks like:** the target is lost, or the stakeout sees nothing useful. Spectacular failure: the watcher is spotted, and the target now knows someone is interested.

## Not to be confused with

*[Stealth](stealth.md)*, which is moving unseen and unheard. Shadowing & Surveillance is following and watching over time, often in plain sight.
