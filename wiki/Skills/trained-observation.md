# Trained Observation

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The detective's eye. Walking into a room and taking it all in: the muddy boots by the door, the half-drunk cup, the chair pushed back in haste. Later, the trained observer can describe the whole scene as if they were still standing there. Investigators, artists, spies and some scholars build this into a discipline.

It differs from simple noticing because it is deliberate and complete. The observer scans methodically, commits details to memory and can recall them clearly hours or days later. It is tiring and needs focus, but a good observer can solve a mystery from details everyone else walked past.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **PER**/INT/INT · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** total recall of a scene; the detective's eye
- **Why these attributes:** beyond Perception: noticing, then heavy reasoning
- **What failure looks like:** The character remembers only a general impression. Spectacular failure: they recall key details wrongly and build a false picture on them.

## Not to be confused with

*Perception (attribute)*, which notices without effort; and *[Investigation](investigation.md)*, which reasons from the clues. Trained Observation records the scene completely.
