# Architecture

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

The art of deciding what a building should be before anyone lifts a stone. The architect draws plans, calculates loads, chooses materials and makes sure the walls will carry the roof and the roof will keep out the weather. They think in rooms, stairs, light and movement, and in how a hall should make a visitor feel small or welcome. Great architects also design for secrets: hidden doors, priest holes, passages behind the panelling.

An architect sees a building as a whole, so they also know its weak points, where a wall is thin or a vault depends on a single pillar. Kings hire them for palaces and temples; burglars and besiegers would give much to read their drawings.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 4 · **Attributes:** **INT**/INT/WIS/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 4
- **Needs:** writing · **Covers:** designing buildings, plans, secret passages, weak points
- **Why these attributes:** heavy design, judgement, drafting
- **What failure looks like:** The design is flawed, ugly or impractical. Spectacular failure: a hidden weakness brings part of the building down, or a secret passage turns out to be no secret.
