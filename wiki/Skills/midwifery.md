# Midwifery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Bringing new life safely into the world. The midwife cares for pregnant women, knows the signs that all is well and the ones that are not, and stays through the long hours of labour. She turns a baby that lies wrong, eases a difficult birth, cuts the cord and makes sure both mother and child survive the first dangerous days. In many places she is also the one who knows herbs, women's ailments and the troubles of new mothers.

Midwives are trusted in every village and sometimes feared, for they are present at the moments of greatest danger and hold knowledge others do not. Their skill is practical and calm: steady hands, patience and a voice that tells a frightened woman she can do this.
