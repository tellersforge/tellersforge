# Foraging

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Survival*

*aka: ______________________________*

Living off what the land offers. Berries, nuts, roots and greens; mushrooms that are safe and the ones that are not; the hollow where water collects, the reed that can be chewed, the bark that can be boiled. Foragers know where to look in each season and how much to take without leaving the patch bare.

In early ages it is simply how people eat. In later ones it is the knowledge of country folk, runaways, soldiers cut off from supply and anyone lost too long in the wild. A good forager can keep a small party fed through a lean week. A careless one can poison them all with a single handful.
