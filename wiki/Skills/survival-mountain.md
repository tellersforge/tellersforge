# Survival: Mountain

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Living on the heights. Mountain survival is knowing the weather that comes over the ridge faster than a horse can gallop, finding shelter from wind that strips heat from the body, reading snow for avalanche and rock for falls, and pacing the climb so the thin air doesn't bring on sickness. It is also finding water, fuel and a safe place to sleep on ground that is never flat.

Shepherds, hermits, smugglers and mountain folk have it. They move slowly and never argue with the mountain. Those who do argue are found in the spring, if at all. The high places are beautiful and utterly indifferent, and this knowledge is how one stays among them.
