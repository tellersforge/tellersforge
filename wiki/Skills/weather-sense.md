# Weather Sense

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Survival*

*aka: ______________________________*

Reading what the sky will do next. A red dawn, a halo round the moon, swallows flying low, the ache in an old shoulder, the wind backing round to the east. Farmers, sailors, shepherds and travellers watch the weather because their lives and livelihoods hang on it, and the best of them are rarely caught without a cloak.

It is pure experience: years of looking up and remembering what happened next. The weatherwise know the seasons of their country, when the first frost usually comes and how long a dry spell can last. Far from home the signs change, and even an old hand has to learn the new sky.
