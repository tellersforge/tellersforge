# Cooking

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Crafting & Repair*

*aka: ______________________________*

Turning raw food into a meal that is safe, filling and, with luck, pleasant. A pot of stew over a camp fire, flatbread on a hot stone, porridge in the morning, a hare skinned and roasted. Everyone eats, and in most households someone has to cook every day, whether or not they have a knack for it.

Good everyday cooking is judgement and taste before handwork. Knowing when meat is done, how long beans need soaking, what to throw out and what can still be saved. A good camp cook keeps a party cheerful. A bad one keeps them close to the latrine.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **WIS**/DEX · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** everyday meals, preparing food safely
- **Why these attributes:** judgement and taste, then handwork
- **What failure looks like:** The meal is bland, burnt or undercooked, but edible. Spectacular failure: something spoiled gets through, and everyone who eats it falls ill.
