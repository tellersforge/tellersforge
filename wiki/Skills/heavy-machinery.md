# Heavy Machinery

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Iron that weighs as much as a house, moving because one person tells it to. The crane driver swings a girder across a building site, the bulldozer shoves a hillside aside, the engineer on the footplate eases a train of wagons into a siding without spilling a drop from the cups in the dining car. It is the skill of great weight and small movements.

The work demands a light hand and a long view. A lever pulled too hard throws a load into a wall; a moment's inattention crushes a foot or topples the whole machine. Operators learn to feel the machine through the seat and the controls, to hear when a motor is straining and to know how far a load will swing before it stops. On a busy site they are watched carefully, because everyone knows what happens when they get it wrong.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **DEX**/PER · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** steam · **Covers:** cranes, bulldozers, factory machines, locomotives
- **Why these attributes:** control, then judgement
- **What failure looks like:** the load swings, the blade digs in the wrong place, the train overruns its mark. Spectacular failure: the machine tips, collides or drops its load on something, or someone, below.

## Not to be confused with
