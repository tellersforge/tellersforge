# Jumping

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Athletics*

*aka: ______________________________*

Across the ditch, over the fence, from one rooftop to the next with the city watch shouting behind. Jumping is a single moment of power followed by a much longer moment of hoping. Herders leap streams, dancers leap for joy, and warriors in some cultures prove themselves by vaulting over bulls or blades.

There is a knack to it. The run-up has to be judged, the take-off timed, and the landing is where most jumpers actually get hurt. Bent knees, a forward roll and a little humility turn a bad landing into merely an undignified one. A standing jump is harder than a running one, and a jump into the dark is the hardest of all.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 2 · **Attributes:** **STR**/AGI · **Mastered at:** +5 | 2
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** leaping gaps and heights
- **Why these attributes:** power, then landing it
- **What failure looks like:** The jumper falls short, balks at the edge or lands badly. Spectacular failure: they miss entirely and fall, or land hard enough to hurt themselves.

## Not to be confused with

*[Acrobatics](acrobatics.md)*, which strings flips, rolls and falls into a flowing whole; Jumping is one clean leap across a gap or up to a ledge.
