# Cultures & Customs

*Set: Core · Basic skill: Lore*

*aka: ______________________________*

How other peoples live, and how not to insult them. Cultures and customs covers greetings and gifts, taboos and table manners, what a nod means and what an open hand means, who may speak to whom and who must not be looked in the eye. It is the knowledge of traders, envoys, missionaries and explorers who have learned, often painfully, that their own ways are not the only ones.

At its best it becomes the art of first meetings: understanding strangers quickly enough to avoid disaster, and respecting them well enough to be welcome again. In far-future settings the strangers may not even be human, and the stakes of a misread gesture are all the higher.

## Game block

- **Dice:** 3 · **Attributes:** **INT**/WIS/CHA · **Mastered at:** +5 | 3
- **Needs:** — · **Covers:** foreign customs and societies, first contact
- **Why these attributes:** knowledge, understanding, rapport
- **What failure looks like:** the custom is unknown and the character fumbles through. Spectacular failure: an innocent gesture turns out to be a deadly insult.

## Not to be confused with

*[Etiquette](etiquette.md)*, which is good manners in one's own society; Cultures & Customs deals with other societies.
