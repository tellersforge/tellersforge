# Stations


Creation step 3, second half: **your family's place in its culture.** Rank only means something inside a society, so station comes after culture and is read through it. A commoner of a free republic knows the law and argues in the assembly; a commoner under a king owes service and has no say. A queen knows court and duty, and little of the market or the street.

## How to use it

1. **Pick a station** from the table below. Take its **+1** and **−1** lines.
2. **Look up your culture's society and schooling** (Culture Catalogue, or table 16.10) in the two tables under "How your society changes your station". Take the lines for your station's **tier** (low, middle or high).
3. **Starting wealth:** your station's wealth, one step poorer in a **poor** land and one step richer in a **rich** land (never below none or above rich). It sets what you own at the start (step 9).

- **Shape:** every station has two **+1** lines. Stations that start **comfortable** or **rich** take more **−1** lines: money and privilege shelter you from ordinary life.
- The names fit any era: a medieval serf and a modern debt-bound worker are both **Bonded**; the gentry of a feudal kingdom and a modern professional family are both **Gentry**.
- A band or tribe has no real ranks: use **Commoner**, **Chief's or elder's kin** or **Outcast**.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## How your society changes your station

What belongs where: the **culture** says how the whole people lives: its political system, its schools, how rich its land is. The **station** says where your family stands in that. These tables are where the two meet.

### Political system

| Society | What it's like | Low | Middle | High |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Band or tribe** | everyone shares the work and the stories; there are no ranks to speak of | Legends & Folklore +1 | Legends & Folklore +1 | Legends & Folklore +1 |
| **Chiefdom or clan** | a leading family and its kin decide; everyone else follows and fights for the clan | Lifting & Carrying +1 | Heraldry & Genealogy +1 | Oratory & Debate +1 |
| **Feudal monarchy** | a ruler and lords own the land; commoners owe service and have no say | Law −1 | Etiquette +1 | Heraldry & Genealogy +1 |
| **Bureaucratic empire** | officials run everything by rule and record; everyone deals with paperwork | Administration +1 | Administration +1 | Administration +1; Law +1 |
| **Theocracy** | the priests rule in the gods' name; faith shapes every rank | Ritual & Liturgy +1 | Ritual & Liturgy +1 | Ritual & Liturgy +1; Religion & Theology +1 |
| **Republic** | free citizens vote, argue and serve; laws bind rulers too | Law +1 | Law +1 | Law +1; Oratory & Debate +1 |
| **Corporate rule** | companies own the land, the law and the people; money is rank | Streetwise +1 | Bookkeeping +1 | Finance +1 |
| **Lawless** | no one rules for long; strength and nerve decide who leads | Intimidation +1 | Intimidation +1 | Intimidation +1 |

### Schooling

| Schooling | What it's like | Literacy (your script) +1 for |
|---|---|---|
| **None** | no schools and no writing, or writing only for outsiders | no one |
| **Temple and noble tutors** | only the powerful and the temples teach reading | every high station, Temple family |
| **Apprenticeship** | guilds and trading houses teach their own to read and reckon | every middle station, every high station |
| **Public schooling** | most children go to school for some years | every middle station, every high station, Working poor |
| **Schooling for all** | everyone learns to read, and many go on to study | every low station, every middle station, every high station; also Research +1 for every middle and high station |

### Wealth of the land

| Land | Starting wealth |
|---|---|
| **poor** | one step poorer than the station's |
| **middling** | as the station's |
| **rich** | one step richer than the station's |

### Examples

- **Commoner, Feudal kingdom culture** (feudal monarchy, temple and noble tutors, middling land): Local Lore +1; Haggling +1; Court Etiquette & Protocol −1; from society and schooling: Etiquette +1. Starting wealth: modest.
- **Commoner, Trading city-states culture** (republic, apprenticeship, rich land): Local Lore +1; Haggling +1; Court Etiquette & Protocol −1; from society and schooling: Law +1; Literacy (per script) +1. Starting wealth: comfortable.
- **High nobility, Feudal kingdom culture** (feudal monarchy, temple and noble tutors, middling land): Court Etiquette & Protocol +1; Leadership +1; Streetwise −1; Haggling −1; Hand Tools −1; from society and schooling: Heraldry & Genealogy +1; Literacy (per script) +1. Starting wealth: rich.
- **Working poor, Modern city culture** (republic, schooling for all, rich land): Lifting & Carrying +1; Hand Tools +1; Etiquette −1; from society and schooling: Law +1; Literacy (per script) +1. Starting wealth: modest.
- **Chief's or elder's kin, Forest hunter-gatherers culture** (band or tribe, none, poor land): Leadership +1; Legends & Folklore +1; Haggling −1; Stealth −1; from society and schooling: Legends & Folklore +1. Starting wealth: modest.

## Starting wealth

| Wealth | What you start with |
|---|---|
| none | the clothes you stand in and one tool or weapon; starting money 1d6 C |
| poor | plain clothing, one more weapon, one work kit; starting money 1d6 S |
| modest | decent clothing, one more weapon and one piece of armour your profession would wear; starting money 3d6 S |
| comfortable | good clothing, a better weapon and armour, a riding animal or vehicle of your era; starting money 3d6 × 5 S |
| rich | fine clothing, your kit in the best quality, weapons and armour of choice, a mount or vehicle, servants or staff; starting money 3d6 × 5 G |

Your profession's starting kit is always free; this line adds what your family gives you and the money you start with (chapter 3, step 9; prices in tables 16.5 to 16.7 and 16.13).
