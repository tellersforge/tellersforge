# Downloads


Every Teller’s Forge book as a PDF, grouped from the top down: Teller’s Forge Original or adapted, then the game it was written for, then its world or setting, then its campaign, then the kind of book. Click a title to open or save it. The Core books add to the Basic Book and never change it; where an older draft and a newer book differ, the newer book wins.
