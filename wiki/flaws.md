# Flaws


Every flaw a character can take, on one page. Pick seven flaws from this list and write them on your sheet. Choose carefully. A flaw can hurt you badly, or it can make the whole table laugh, and most land somewhere in between. Fear of Heights is harmless in a swamp and deadly on a castle wall. Curiosity is a joke until it opens the wrong door. Think about who your character is, where they are likely to go, and which flaws will make their story worth telling. A flaw you play well is rewarded.

**Two different rolls.** At creation, roll each flaw's score dice dice **once** to set its **score**, and write it down. In play, when the flaw's moment comes, roll **1d20**: at or under the score, the flaw fires. Example: Fear of Heights (moderate) rolls 1d4+3 = 6 at creation; at a rope bridge you roll 1d20 and get 4, so the fear fires.

**Score dice and starting score:** every flaw has a score dice, which sets its starting score. **Light** (comedic, awkward, harmless in the long run): 1d4+5, 6 to 9, fires 30% to 45% of the time. **Moderate** (real trouble, but survivable): 1d4+3, 4 to 7, 20% to 35%. **Grave** (can get someone killed or break the party): 1d4+1, 2 to 5, 10% to 25%. Fun flaws come up often; ruinous ones rarely.

**How a flaw works** ([Rules, chapter 5](Rules/05-flaws.md)): each flaw has a score. When its moment comes, the player rolls 1d20 at or under the score; if it fires, the character acts on it, whether the player likes it or not. A flaw that fires and is played out well earns a little XP (about 1 to 5, the Game Master's call); one played brilliantly earns the top of that range.

- **Chosen flaws:** some are taken for something specific you name, like a Fear of an Animal, an Addiction, a Prejudice or an Obsession. Each choice counts as its own flaw.
- **Not flaws:** physical drawbacks such as poor eyesight or a bad leg are permanent injuries, low attributes or species drawbacks, not flaws.
- **Jinxed** and **Weird Luck** oppose no attribute: when they fire, the Game Master adds a mishap or an oddity.
