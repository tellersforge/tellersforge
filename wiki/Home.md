# Teller's Forge


Teller's Forge is an open tabletop roleplaying game for every era, from the stone age to the far future: a 3d20 roll-under system where you play a person, not a pile of numbers. This wiki is its complete home. Every rule, skill, power, weapon, piece of gear, species and creature has its own page, and every page's numbers come from the same data the Rules' stat tables are built from.

## Where to start

- **New to the game?** Read the [Rules](Rules/01-before-you-begin.md), starting with chapter 1.
- **Making a character?** Follow [Rules, chapter 3](Rules/03-making-a-character.md) step by step: first choose a [species](choose-a-species.md), a [culture](cultures.md), a [station](stations.md) and a [profession](professions.md), then roll attributes and choose seven [flaws](flaws.md).
- **Running a game?** The [Bestiary](bestiary.md) has every people as an NPC and every creature.
- **Spotted a problem or have an idea?** Use [Suggest a change](suggest.html), on every page. No account needed.- **Want the books?** Every booklet is on the [Downloads](downloads.md) page as a PDF.
