# Draconic


The dragons and everything that carries their blood. At the top stand the true dragons: vast, clever, long-lived and proud, hoarding gold or knowledge or storms, and speaking to lesser peoples as a lord speaks to a stray dog. Below them come world-coiling serpents old enough to be landscape, feral wyverns and drakes with more teeth than thought, and folk of mixed blood who carry scales and a spark of that ancient fire. Scales, great strength and a sense of their own importance unite them. Kingdoms pay dragons, pray to them or hire people to kill them, and the dragons remember which.

## Family layer (defaults for every species below)

- **Attributes:** STR Mod+2; COU Mod+1; AGI Mod−1; WIS Mod−1
- **Skills:** Intimidation Mod+3; Appraisal Mod+2; Composure Mod+1; Creature & Occult Lore Mod+1; Stealth Mod−2; Disguise Mod−2
- **Powers:** Resist Element Set +2; Fire Blast Mod+1 (affinity)
- **Weapon groups:** Brawling Mod+1
- **Natural attacks:** claws 1d6+1; bite 1d6+1; tail 1d6+1
- **Note:** Resist Element is the family's own element (the species names it). Breath weapons are Set at species level; Fire Blast affinity stands in for any breath element.

How the layers work, and every rule for species numbers: [Species rules](../species-rules.md).
