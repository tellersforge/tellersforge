# Cultures


Creation step 3, first half: **where your character grew up.** Each culture is a set of skill lines you simply take. Cultures are ways of life, not nations: pick the one closest to the place your character comes from, and give it any name that suits your world.

## How to use it

- **Pick a culture whose eras include your era** (step 2). The Game Master can stretch a band for their world.
- **Every culture has the same shape**, so none is simply better: its **signature** skill **+2**; three skills **+1** (the last is the people's **weapon tradition**); two skills they neglect **−1**.
- **Weapon tradition:** use the latest weapon in the list whose era you have reached (for example, steppe riders: Bows; from steam, Long Guns).
- **A line for a skill your era doesn't have does nothing:** the skill stays at Foreign Concept (−6).
- **Choice lines** ("one of") let you pick one skill from the list.
- **Non-human characters** choose a culture the same way. What their body does is already in the species step.
- **Your native language is automatic**; other languages come from the Language skill.
- **Society, schooling and land** are the culture's usual political system, who gets taught to read, and how rich the land is. They don't give lines by themselves: they change what your **station** means (Station Catalogue, "How your society changes your station"). The Game Master can change them for their world: a Farming village can sit under a king or in a republic.
