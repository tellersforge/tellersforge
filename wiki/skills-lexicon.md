# Skills lexicon


Every skill in Teller's Forge, each listing its attributes from the ten (STR DEX AGI END INT WIS PER CHA COU LUCK; whole-body movement is Agility, fine handwork is Dexterity). Endurance and Perception are attributes now, not skills. **Dice** is how many d20s a check rolls (more is harder); the **bold** attribute is pivotal and gets the fate die when you roll the check in play (it plays no part in making a character); **Needs** is the earliest Era point; ✦ marks magic-flavoured skills. Weapon groups and their techniques, and the Minor and Great Works, have their own sections.
