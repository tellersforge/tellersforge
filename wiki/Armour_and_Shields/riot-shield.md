# Riot shield

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A tall panel of clear, tough plastic with a grip and an arm strap behind it. The bearer can see the crowd through it, and the crowd can see the bearer, which matters to those in charge of keeping order. It stops bricks, bottles, sticks and fists, and turns a small-calibre pistol round at close range.

In a line, riot shields make a wall that can advance, halt or push. Alone, it is a defence, not a weapon, though a hard shove with the edge ends many arguments.

## Game block

- **Parry bonus:** +2 (a shield is not armour; it adds to Parry and parries missiles)
- **Cover:** heavy (-4) against thrown objects and pistols
- **Needs:** computing
- **Weight:** 3 kg
- **Price:** 3 S
- **Notes:** modern; clear, so you can see through it

## Not to be confused with

*[Ballistic shield](ballistic-shield.md)*, which is opaque, much heavier and made to stop gunfire rather than thrown objects.
