# Mail glove or diving mail suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

Steel mail never went away. A butcher or an oyster shucker wears a mail glove on the hand that holds the meat, so a slip of the knife ends in a clink instead of a hospital. Divers who film sharks wear full suits of it, and let the sharks bite to learn how they feed.

It is the same armour a knight would recognise, made by machine in stainless steel. It is heavy, cold and noisy, and it stops teeth and blades very well. Against a gunshot or a hammer, it does very little.

## Game block

- **Value:** 2
- **Zones:** arms (glove), or all but head (suit)
- **Wear:** 30
- **Dodge penalty:** −1
- **Weight:** 1–8 kg
- **Needs:** electricity
- **Price:** 5 S
- **Notes:** still used today by butchers and shark divers; stops cuts and bites

## Not to be confused with

*[Mail shirt](mail-shirt.md)*, the old battlefield version; this is modern stainless mail made for work rather than war.
