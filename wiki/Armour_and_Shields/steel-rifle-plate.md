# Steel rifle plate

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A thick, curved slab of hardened steel the size of a large book, slipped into a pocket on the front or back of a vest. It stops rifle rounds, again and again, and it lasts for years without complaint. It is also heavy enough that a soldier wearing two of them will feel it at the end of a long march.

Bullets hitting steel can shatter and spray fragments at the wearer's throat and arms, so most plates are coated in a rubbery layer to catch them. Cheap, tough and dependable, it is the choice of those who can't afford better.
