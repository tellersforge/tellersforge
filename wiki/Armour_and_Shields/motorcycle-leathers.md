# Motorcycle leathers

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A thick leather jacket and trousers, often with pads or hard cups sewn in at the shoulders, elbows and knees. They are made to stop skin being scraped off on a road at speed. By accident, they are also decent protection against a knife, a broken bottle or a dog bite.

Bikers, couriers and street gangs wear them, and they look the part in any bar fight. They do nothing against a bullet, but they keep the wearer warm and dry on a long ride, and they last for years.

## Game block

- **Value:** 1
- **Zones:** torso, arms, legs
- **Wear:** 8
- **Dodge penalty:** 0
- **Weight:** 4 kg
- **Needs:** electricity
- **Price:** 4 S
- **Notes:** stops scrapes and knives, not bullets

## Not to be confused with

*[Stab vest](stab-vest.md)*, which is made to stop a knife on purpose; leathers stop one only by accident, but cover the arms and legs.
