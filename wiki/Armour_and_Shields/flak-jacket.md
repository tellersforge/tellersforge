# Flak jacket

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A bulky vest of layered nylon, fibre or small steel plates, made to catch the splinters of shells and grenades. Soldiers in the trenches of great industrial wars and helicopter crews on later battlefields wore it for the same reason: most wounds on a battlefield come from flying metal, not aimed bullets.

It does that job well. A rifle bullet fired straight at it, though, goes through. It is hot, heavy and stiff, and troops in hot climates often leave it in the truck and pray.
