# Bomb suit

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A thick, bulky suit of layered armour plates and blast-resistant fabric, with a massive helmet and a clear visor. It is made for a single person to walk up to a bomb and take it apart. It won't save the wearer from a large explosion, but it can turn a fatal blast from a small one into a survivable one.

It is suffocatingly hot, so heavy that the wearer moves like a diver on the sea bed, and nearly impossible to fight in. Those who wear it are respected, and slightly feared, by everyone who watches them walk away.
