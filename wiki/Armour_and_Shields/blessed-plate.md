# Blessed plate

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A full harness of plate consecrated by a priest or a holy order, engraved with prayers and sacred signs and often washed in holy water before battle. It is as heavy and as strong as any other plate. Against creatures of the grave and the pit, it is much more: their claws and blows fail on it as if it burned them.

Holy knights wear it on crusades into haunted lands. It must be kept clean, polished and reblessed, and a wearer who breaks their vows may find it no better than steel.

- **Wear:** 40
