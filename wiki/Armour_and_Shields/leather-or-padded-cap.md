# Leather or padded cap

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A snug cap of stiff leather or quilted cloth, tied under the chin. It won't stop a sword, but it takes the edge off a stone, a club or a fall from a horse. Slingers, peasant levies, miners and brawlers wear it. It is also worn under a metal helmet, to keep the steel from ringing against the skull.

It is cheap, warm and light, and doesn't block the ears. Its wearer can hear a shout from across the field. Its real advantage is that it is better than a bare head.
