# Warding charm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A small token worn on a cord, sewn into a hem or pinned inside a collar: a carved bone, a knotted thread, a stone with a hole, a prayer on a scrap of paper. It isn't armour, but it makes whatever the wearer has on work better. Blows slide off at the last instant, arrows turn aside in the wind.

Its power is limited. After a certain number of close calls, the charm cracks, frays or goes cold, and must be replaced. Soldiers carry them without believing, and are glad of them anyway.

## Game block

- **Value:** +1
- **Zones:** total only
- **Wear:** a set number of uses
- **Dodge penalty:** 0
- **Needs:** —
- **Weight:** —
- **Price:** 50 G
- **Notes:** a worn charm that adds to the total armour box

## Not to be confused with

*[Enchanted mail](enchanted-mail.md)*, which is armour with magic in it; a warding charm is only a token that helps whatever is worn.
