# Soft body armour (concealable)

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A vest of many layers of woven synthetic fibre, stitched together and slipped into a thin cotton cover. Worn under a shirt, it is almost invisible. Police officers, bodyguards, reporters and nervous politicians wear it every day. When a pistol round hits it, the fibres catch and spread the bullet and it stops.

The wearer is still knocked flat, bruised badly, and often cracks a rib. A knife point can slide between the fibres, and a rifle round goes straight through. It is warm to wear, and the fibre degrades if left wet or in the sun.
