# Heavy clothing, furs

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Layers of wool, felt and fur, belted tight and piled on until the wearer looks twice their size. Nobody puts it on for battle. They put it on because the wind off the ice will kill them faster than any enemy. Herders, trappers and winter raiders live in it for months at a time.

It turns a glancing knife and softens a club, mostly by accident. A determined blow goes straight through. It is warm, it smells of smoke and animal, and in summer it is simply a bundle to carry.

*[Hide](hide.md)*, which is a single tough skin cut and shaped to protect, where furs are layers worn mainly to keep warm.
