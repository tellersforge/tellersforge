# Stab vest

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A slim vest of tightly woven fibre or thin metal plates, worn under or over a uniform shirt. It is made for police officers, prison guards and security staff who face knives, needles and broken glass more often than guns. It is light enough to wear all day and slim enough to go unnoticed.

A knife that would have gone deep skids off it. A bullet, however, goes through as if it weren't there. Those who wear it know the difference, and hope the person they're approaching doesn't have a gun.
