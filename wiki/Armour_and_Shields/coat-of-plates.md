# Coat of plates

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A sleeveless jacket of canvas or leather with a handful of large iron plates riveted to the inside, covering the chest, belly and back. It came when smiths learned to hammer bigger pieces of iron and armies began to want more than mail. Worn over a mail shirt, it made its owner very hard to wound.

It is stiff, it doesn't bend at the waist as well as it should, and it hides no secrets: everyone can see the rivets. But the plates stop a lance point that would burst the rings of mail. It is the first step on the road to full plate.
