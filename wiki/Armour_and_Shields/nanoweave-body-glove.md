# Nanoweave body glove

![Nanoweave body glove](../Images/armour-nanoweave-body-glove.jpg){.illus}

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A skin-tight suit of smart fabric, thinner than a wetsuit, that hardens where it is struck and knits itself back together afterwards. It can be worn under ordinary clothes, and often is. Spies, bodyguards and wealthy travellers on dangerous worlds swear by it.

When a blade or bullet hits, the fibres lock rigid for an instant and spread the shock across the body. Afterwards, tiny machines in the weave repair the damage slowly while the wearer rests. It is expensive, fussy about cleaning and rather too revealing for some tastes.
