# Quilted cotton armour

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A thick, sleeveless or short-sleeved coat of cotton padding, sometimes as deep as a hand, quilted tight and soaked in brine to stiffen it. In warm lands where metal is scarce or the heat makes it unbearable, it is the armour of whole armies. Jungle warriors, desert guards and river-kingdom soldiers have all trusted it.

It swallows arrows and darts, catches obsidian and stone blades, and holds up well against a swung club. Foreign soldiers in steel have been known to throw away their own armour and put it on instead. It is light enough to march in, and cool enough not to kill the wearer.

*[Padded jack / gambeson](padded-jack-gambeson.md)*, which is thinner and usually worn under mail; quilted cotton is made to stand on its own.
