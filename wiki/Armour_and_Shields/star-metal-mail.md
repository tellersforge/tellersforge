# Star-metal mail

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A shirt of mail made from a metal that falls from the sky, or is dug from the deepest mines, or is said to be the tears of a god. Nobody agrees. What all accounts say is that it is as light as silk, as bright as silver and harder than any steel. It can be worn under a shirt, and nobody notices.

It doesn't slow the wearer at all, never rusts, and never wears. Kings would trade kingdoms for it, and the tales are full of those who did. Owning one is a good reason to keep quiet.

## Game block

- **Value:** 3
- **Zones:** torso, arms
- **Wear:** never wears
- **Dodge penalty:** 0
- **Needs:** —
- **Weight:** 3 kg
- **Price:** 1000 G
- **Notes:** impossibly light metal from legend; no Dodge penalty, can be worn under clothes

## Not to be confused with

*[Enchanted mail](enchanted-mail.md)*, which is ordinary mail with a spell; star-metal mail is made of a legendary material that needs no magic.
