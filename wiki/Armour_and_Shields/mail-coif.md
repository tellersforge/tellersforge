# Mail coif

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

A hood of mail that covers the head, neck and throat, leaving an opening for the face. It drapes over the shoulders and is often joined to the hauberk. Worn over a padded cap and under a metal helmet, it makes the whole head a hard target. Alone, it is better than nothing, but a heavy blow to the crown still rings the skull.

It is cold in winter, hot in summer, and it pulls hair out by the root when taken off in a hurry.

## Game block

- **Value:** 2
- **Zones:** head
- **Wear:** 30
- **Needs:** iron
- **Weight:** 2 kg
- **Price:** 60 S
- **Notes:** worn under or with a helmet (+1 with a helmet)

## Not to be confused with

*[Open metal helmet](open-metal-helmet.md)*, which is rigid and protects the crown better; the coif guards the neck and throat instead.
