# Starship


*aka: ______________________________*

A ship that can cross space. It is designed from nothing, its hull built and sealed, its drives and power plant installed, its systems wired and its ship's mind programmed. Then come the flight trials, when every error is found the hard way. A starship takes a shipyard, a fortune and many hands. When it lifts off for the first time, the whole yard stops work to watch.

## Phases

*A ship that can cross space. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design it | [Aerospace Engineering](../Skills/aerospace-engineering.md) | 6 | T |
| Build the hull | [Machining & Welding](../Skills/machining-and-welding.md) | 5 | T |
| Install drives and power | [Starship Engineering](../Skills/starship-engineering.md) | 6 | T |
| Wire the systems | [Electronics](../Skills/electronics.md) | 4 | T |
| Program the ship | [Programming](../Skills/programming.md) | 3 | T |
| Flight trials | [Spacecraft Piloting](../Skills/spacecraft-piloting.md) | 2 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Great Ship](great-ship.md)*, the ocean-going vessel of earlier ages; a Starship sails between worlds.
