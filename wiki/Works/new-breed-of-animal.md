# New Breed of Animal


*aka: ______________________________*

A line of animals bred for a purpose: a faster horse, a hardier sheep, a hound with a better nose. The breeder chooses the stock with care, then breeds over generations, keeping the best and culling the rest, all while keeping the herd healthy. It is a lifetime's work, often two. The result bears the breeder's name, or the name of their valley, for centuries afterwards.

## Phases

*A line of animals bred for a purpose. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Choose the stock | [Life Sciences](../Skills/life-sciences.md) | 2 | T |
| Breed over generations | [Herding & Husbandry](../Skills/herding-and-husbandry.md) | 8 | T |
| Keep them healthy | [Veterinary Care](../Skills/veterinary-care.md) | 3 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Trained Companion](trained-companion.md)*, which shapes one animal for one owner; a New Breed of Animal shapes a whole bloodline.
