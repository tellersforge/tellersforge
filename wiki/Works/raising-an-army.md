# Raising an Army


*aka: ______________________________*

Recruiting, training and supplying a fighting force. Speeches bring the recruits in, drill turns them into a body that moves as one, supply keeps them fed and armed, and leadership holds them together when the fighting starts. An army can be raised in a season if the cause is strong, but it can melt away in a week if the food runs out. Generals know the second half better than the first.
