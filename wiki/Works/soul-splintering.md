# Soul-Splintering


*aka: ______________________________*

Dividing one's own soul into pieces kept in separate places. The seeker studies forbidden texts, prepares the vessels, usually jewels or small precious objects, and then splits the soul and hides each piece. While any shard survives, the splinterer cannot be wholly destroyed. It is an unspeakable act, and it changes those who do it: each piece taken leaves less of a person behind.

## Phases

*Dividing one's soul into pieces kept in separate places. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Study the forbidden texts | [Research](../Skills/research.md) | 4 | T |
| Prepare the vessels | [Jewellery & Gem Cutting](../Skills/jewellery-and-gem-cutting.md) | 2 | T |
| Split the soul | [Soul Craft](../Powers/soul-craft.md) | 5 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Becoming Deathless](becoming-deathless.md)*, which goes further and withholds death itself; Soul-Splintering is its first dark step.
