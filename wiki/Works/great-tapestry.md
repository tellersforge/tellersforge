# Great Tapestry


*aka: ______________________________*

A woven picture of a story, long enough to cover the wall of a hall. The design is drawn in full, the thread spun and dyed in dozens of colours, and then the weaving begins, a hand's width at a time, often by a whole workshop. It records a battle, a wedding or a founding legend, keeps a draughty room warm, and can be rolled up and carried away when the castle falls.

## Phases

*A woven picture of a story. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design it | [Painting & Drawing](../Skills/painting-and-drawing.md) | 2 | B |
| Spin and dye the thread | [Spinning & Weaving](../Skills/spinning-and-weaving.md) | 3 | T |
| Weave it | [Spinning & Weaving](../Skills/spinning-and-weaving.md) | 8 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Masterpiece Painting or Fresco](masterpiece-painting-or-fresco.md)*, which is painted; a Great Tapestry is woven, and can travel.
