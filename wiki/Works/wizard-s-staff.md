# Wizard's Staff


*aka: ______________________________*

A staff that makes its owner stronger in the arts. The wood must be found and cut in the right place, at the right season, from the right tree, then carved, sanded and treated until it is smooth to the hand. Incantations are inscribed along its length, and finally the staff is bound to its owner. From then on it answers them and no one else. Wizards lean on it, point with it, and sleep with it within reach.

## Phases

*A staff that strengthens its owner's powers. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Find and cut the right wood | [Survival: Forest (Woodcraft)](../Skills/survival-forest-woodcraft.md) | 1 | B |
| Carve the staff | [Carpentry & Joinery](../Skills/carpentry-and-joinery.md) | 3 | T |
| Sand and treat the wood | [Carpentry & Joinery](../Skills/carpentry-and-joinery.md) | 2 | T |
| Inscribe the incantations | [Rune & Sigil Magic](../Powers/rune-and-sigil-magic.md) | 3 | B |
| Bind the staff to its owner | [Focus Bonding](../Powers/focus-bonding.md) | 2 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Enchanted Item](enchanted-item.md)*, which can be almost any object with lasting magic; a Wizard's Staff exists to strengthen its owner's own powers.
