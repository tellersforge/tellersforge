# Healing Potion


*aka: ______________________________*

A small flask of something cloudy and bitter that closes a wound as it goes down. The alchemist first prepares a clean base of distilled spirits, salts and simples; then the brewer works the healing power into it over a low flame, until the draught takes on a faint glow or warmth. Soldiers carry one sewn into a belt, and wealthy travellers never leave home without two. It tastes terrible, and nobody has ever complained.

## Phases

*A draught that closes wounds at once. Size: Minor.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare the base | [Alchemy](../Skills/alchemy.md) | 1 | T |
| Brew in the power | [Potion Brewing](../Powers/potion-brewing.md) | 1 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Elixir of Life](elixir-of-life.md)*, a far greater brew that halts ageing; a Healing Potion only mends the body's present hurts.
