# Blessed Charm


*aka: ______________________________*

A little thing to hang at the throat or tie into a child's hair: a pierced coin, a carved bead, a polished stone in a silver cage. The jeweller makes it well, because a blessing sits poorly in shoddy work, and then a priest or holy person lays the blessing on it with prayer and touch. The charm carries a modest grace for its wearer. Mothers buy them, sailors swear by them, and sceptics wear one anyway.

## Phases

*A small charm that carries a blessing. Size: Minor.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Make the charm | [Jewellery & Gem Cutting](../Skills/jewellery-and-gem-cutting.md) | 1 | T |
| Bless it | [Blessing](../Powers/blessing.md) | 1 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Permanent Clerical Blessing](permanent-clerical-blessing.md)*, the great and lasting consecration of an object; a Blessed Charm is a small, humbler favour.
