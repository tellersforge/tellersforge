# Power Armour Suit


*aka: ______________________________*

A walking machine worn like a coat of plate. The frame is fabricated and welded, the systems wired, and the motors and joints built to carry far more weight than any human could. Finally the suit is fitted and calibrated to its wearer, so that it moves when they move and not a moment later. Inside one, a soldier can lift a vehicle, shrug off gunfire and run for hours. Outside one, they are ordinary again.

## Phases

*A powered suit built for one wearer. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fabricate the frame | [Machining & Welding](../Skills/machining-and-welding.md) | 5 | T |
| Wire the systems | [Electronics](../Skills/electronics.md) | 4 | T |
| Build the motors and joints | [Robotics](../Skills/robotics.md) | 3 | T |
| Fit and calibrate to the wearer | [Robotics](../Skills/robotics.md) | 1 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Fitted Plate Armour](fitted-plate-armour.md)*, the steel harness of the knight, which has no power of its own.
