# Exorcism


*aka: ______________________________*

Something has taken up residence where it does not belong: in a child who speaks in another voice, a house where the doors will not stay shut, a well that whispers. The exorcist prepares the rite with salt, water, bells and old words, performs it in full, and then the real contest begins, as power is set against the intruder and it is ordered out. It rarely goes quietly. Afterwards, the family is grateful, and the exorcist sleeps badly for a week.

## Phases

*Driving a spirit out of a person or place. Size: Minor.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare and perform the rite | [Exorcism & Curse-Breaking](../Skills/exorcism-and-curse-breaking.md) | 1 | T |
| Cast the spirit out | [Exorcism](../Powers/exorcism.md) | 1 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Spirit Consultation](spirit-consultation.md)*, which calls a spirit to answer a question; an Exorcism drives one out.
