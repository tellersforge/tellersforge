# Robot or Artificial Mind


*aka: ______________________________*

A thinking machine. The body is built, with joints, sensors and hands; the electronics are laid in; and then the mind is written, line by line, until it can learn, choose and reply. What emerges may be a patient servant, a curious child or something no one expected. Those who make one soon find themselves asking questions they did not plan for, about duty, rights and what it means to be alive.

## Phases

*A thinking machine. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build the body | [Robotics](../Skills/robotics.md) | 6 | T |
| Build the electronics | [Electronics](../Skills/electronics.md) | 3 | T |
| Write the mind | [Programming](../Skills/programming.md) | 6 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Stone Guardian](stone-guardian.md)*, a body given life by runes; a Robot or Artificial Mind is given life by code and circuitry.
