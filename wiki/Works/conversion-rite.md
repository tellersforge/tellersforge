# Conversion Rite


*aka: ______________________________*

A rite that installs a hollow, tethered devotion to a master. The rite is prepared with care, the convert is broken down with words, isolation and fear, the devotion is installed in the emptiness that remains, and a tether ties them to the master. The convert looks and speaks as before, but the will is gone, and they serve with a smile. Their families often notice first.

## Phases

*A rite that installs a hollow, tethered devotion to a master. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Founding a Faith or Temple Order](founding-a-faith-or-temple-order.md)*, which gathers willing believers; a Conversion Rite hollows out the unwilling.
