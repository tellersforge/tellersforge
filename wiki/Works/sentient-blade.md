# Sentient Blade


*aka: ______________________________*

A weapon that thinks. The blade is forged and tempered like any fine sword, then binding runes are inscribed along it, and finally a mind is woken inside the steel. What wakes is not always what the maker intended. Some such blades are loyal, some proud, some sulk, and a few have ambitions of their own. Their owners learn to listen to them, and to argue with them, often out loud.

## Phases

*A weapon with a mind of its own. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forge the blade | [Blacksmithing](../Skills/blacksmithing.md) | 5 | T |
| Temper and finish it | [Weaponsmithing & Armouring](../Skills/weaponsmithing-and-armouring.md) | 3 | T |
| Inscribe the binding runes | [Rune & Sigil Magic](../Powers/rune-and-sigil-magic.md) | 3 | T |
| Awaken its mind | [Awaken Object](../Powers/awaken-object.md) | 5 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Master Sword](master-sword.md)*, the finest blade a smith can make, but with no mind of its own.
