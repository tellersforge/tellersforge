# True Reading


*aka: ______________________________*

Anyone can lay out cards or cast bones and tell a hopeful customer what they want to hear. A true reading is the rarer thing: the diviner sets out the signs with proper care, and then, for a moment, the pattern opens and something real looks back. The seeker leaves with a glimpse of what is actually coming, not a comfort. Few readers can manage it on demand, and the honest ones admit how often they fail.

## Phases

*A reading that really shows the future. Size: Minor.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lay out and read the signs | [Divination](../Skills/divination.md) | 1 | T |
| See what is truly coming | [Augury](../Powers/augury.md) | 1 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Drawn Prophecy](drawn-prophecy.md)*, which keeps a foretelling on paper for others to study; a True Reading is spoken once, to the one who asked.
