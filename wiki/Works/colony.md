# Colony


*aka: ______________________________*

A settlement on a hostile world. It is planned from orbit, shelters are built against the air, the cold or the heat, food is grown in soil that has never known a seed, and the whole community is run so that it survives its first hard years. Colonists are brave, desperate or both. Their grandchildren will call the place home and forget it was ever hostile.

## Phases

*A settlement on a hostile world. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plan it | [Civil Engineering](../Skills/civil-engineering.md) | 4 | T |
| Build shelters | [Construction](../Skills/construction.md) | 6 | T |
| Grow food | [Farming](../Skills/farming.md) | 4 | T |
| Run it | [Administration](../Skills/administration.md) | 3 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Expedition into the Unknown](expedition-into-the-unknown.md)*, which explores and comes back; a Colony stays and builds.
