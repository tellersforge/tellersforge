# Master Firearm


*aka: ______________________________*

A gun built from the ground up for one shooter. Its parts are machined by hand to fine tolerances, the action and barrel assembled and finished by a gunsmith, and the stock shaped from chosen wood to fit a particular cheek and shoulder. Last comes the fitting and sighting, done at the range with the owner. The result is quieter, smoother and more accurate than anything from a factory, and irreplaceable when lost.

## Phases

*A custom gun built for one shooter. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Machine the parts | [Machining & Welding](../Skills/machining-and-welding.md) | 3 | T |
| Build the action and barrel | [Gunsmithing](../Skills/gunsmithing.md) | 5 | T |
| Shape the stock | [Carpentry & Joinery](../Skills/carpentry-and-joinery.md) | 2 | T |
| Fit and zero it to the owner | [Gunsmithing](../Skills/gunsmithing.md) | 1 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Master Bow](master-bow.md)*, the bowyer's equivalent; a Master Firearm belongs to the age of powder and machined steel.
