# Cure for a Disease


*aka: ______________________________*

A treatment for an illness that no one has yet been able to cure. The physician studies the sick, often at risk to themselves, searches the records for anything similar, and tests remedy after remedy until one works. Then the cure must be spread, taught and made, and people persuaded to take it. Those who succeed are remembered for centuries. Many who try are remembered only by the disease.

## Phases

*A treatment for an illness no one can cure. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Study the sick | [Medicine](../Skills/medicine.md) | 4 | T |
| Search the records | [Research](../Skills/research.md) | 3 | T |
| Test remedies | [Remedies & Pharmacy](../Skills/remedies-and-pharmacy.md) | 5 | T |
| Spread the cure | [Public Health](../Skills/public-health.md) | 2 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Healing Potion](healing-potion.md)*, which closes one person's wounds; a Cure for a Disease defeats an illness across a whole people.
