# Scientific Discovery


*aka: ______________________________*

A new law of nature, worked out and proven. The scientist does the mathematics, runs the experiments that show it holds, and then publishes it for others to test and argue over. The work is lonely, the doubters are loud, and the discovery is often stolen, ignored or disputed for a generation. Once accepted, it becomes so obvious that nobody can imagine not knowing it.

## Phases

*A new law of nature, proven. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Do the mathematics | [Mathematics](../Skills/mathematics.md) | 5 | T |
| Run the experiments | [Physical Sciences](../Skills/physical-sciences.md) | 5 | T |
| Publish it | [Composition](../Skills/composition.md) | 2 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Invention](invention.md)*, which builds a new device; a Scientific Discovery explains why things work.
