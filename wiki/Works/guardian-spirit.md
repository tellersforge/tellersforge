# Guardian Spirit


*aka: ______________________________*

A spirit bound to protect a place or a family. The shrine is prepared with offerings and care, the spirit is called, and then it is bound, by agreement or by force, to guard what it is given. A guardian spirit watches the doorways, warns of danger and drives off intruders. It must be honoured, and if it is neglected it may leave, or stay and become something much less friendly.

## Phases

*A spirit bound to protect a place or family. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare the shrine | [Ritual & Liturgy](../Skills/ritual-and-liturgy.md) | 3 | T |
| Call the spirit | [Summon Creature](../Powers/summon-creature.md) | 2 | B |
| Bind it as guardian | [Spirit Allies](../Powers/spirit-allies.md) | 4 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Stone Guardian](stone-guardian.md)*, a made body that obeys; a Guardian Spirit is a living spirit bound by rite.
