# Sworn Oath


*aka: ______________________________*

A promise made so formally, and bound so firmly, that breaking it brings harm on the breaker. First the words must be framed exactly: who swears, to whom, for what, and until when, because an oath with loose wording is a trap for both sides. Then the binding is laid on it. Kings demand them from vassals, merchants from partners, and lovers, now and then, from each other. Nobody takes one lightly twice.

## Phases

*A promise that punishes the one who breaks it. Size: Minor.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

## Not to be confused with

*[Treaty](treaty.md)*, a great work of peace between whole peoples; a Sworn Oath binds individuals, and punishes the one who breaks it.
