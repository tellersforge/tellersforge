# Enchanted Arrows


*aka: ______________________________*

A quiver of well-made shafts, fletched and headed by a skilled hand, then charged with power for a single battle or hunt. The charge might make them fly truer, burn on impact or pass through a shield as if it were paper. It does not last: the working fades within days, and an arrow once loosed is spent. Archers who know a hard fight is coming pay dearly for a quiver, and count every shaft back afterwards.

## Phases

*A quiver of arrows charged with power. Size: Minor.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Make the arrows | [Bowyery & Fletching](../Skills/bowyery-and-fletching.md) | 1 | T |
| Charge them | [Temporary Enchantment](../Powers/temporary-enchantment.md) | 1 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Enchanted Item](enchanted-item.md)*, which makes a lasting magic object; Enchanted Arrows carry a short-lived charge.
