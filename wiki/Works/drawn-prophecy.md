# Drawn Prophecy


*aka: ______________________________*

A foretelling kept on paper. A vision of the future will not hold in a memory stone; it slips out like water through fingers. So the seer describes what they see, while an artist draws it as the words come, quickly and faithfully. The drawings are strange, full of symbols and half-glimpsed faces. Scholars argue over them for generations, and sometimes understand them only afterwards.

## Phases

*Keeping a foretelling, which won't hold in a stone, by having it drawn. Size: Minor.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| See what is coming | [Future Seeing (Precognition)](../Powers/future-seeing-precognition.md) | 1 | B |
| Draw it as the seer describes it | [Painting & Drawing](../Skills/painting-and-drawing.md) | 2 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[True Reading](true-reading.md)*, which gives one seeker a spoken glimpse; a Drawn Prophecy keeps the vision for later study.
