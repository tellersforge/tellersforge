# Pocket Realm


*aka: ______________________________*

A hidden world of one's own. The maker designs it as carefully as any architect designs a palace, planning rooms, gardens, sky and weather, then creates it with a great working of power. Entry is by a door, a mirror or a word. Some pocket realms are sanctuaries, some libraries, some prisons. Most of them are strange in small ways, because the maker forgot something the real world takes for granted.

## Phases

*A hidden world of your own. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design the realm | [Architecture](../Skills/architecture.md) | 2 | B |
| Create the realm | [Pocket Realm](../Powers/pocket-realm.md) | 6 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Standing Portal](standing-portal.md)*, which opens a way to a real place; a Pocket Realm creates a new one.
