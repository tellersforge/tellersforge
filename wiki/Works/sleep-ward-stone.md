# Sleep-Ward Stone


*aka: ______________________________*

A stone that holds a sleep-working, ready to use. The token is shaped, runes and wards engraved, the working stored in it with care, and then made permanent so that it never fades. Set by a bed, it brings deep, safe sleep to the troubled and the wounded. Set by a door, it can put a watchman out for the night. Those who make them choose their buyers carefully.

## Phases

*A stone that holds a sleep-working, ready to use. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shape the token | [Stone Toolmaking](../Skills/stone-toolmaking.md) | 1 | T |
| Engrave the runes and wards | [Rune & Sigil Magic](../Powers/rune-and-sigil-magic.md) | 2 | T |
| Store the sleep-working | [Calibrated Sleep](../Powers/calibrated-sleep.md) | 2 | B |
| Make it permanent | [Permanency](../Powers/permanency.md) | 2 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Memory Stone](memory-stone.md)*, which holds a memory; a Sleep-Ward Stone holds a working of sleep.
