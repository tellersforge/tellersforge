# Cybernetic Implant


*aka: ______________________________*

An artificial part built for one body: an eye, a hand, a heart, or something no natural body ever had. The implant is built with care, the connection to nerves and flesh designed to fit its owner exactly, and the whole thing installed by surgery. When it works, the owner can hardly remember life without it. When it does not, the body fights it, and the owner suffers.

## Phases

*An artificial part built for one body. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build the implant | [Electronics](../Skills/electronics.md) | 3 | T |
| Design the connection | [Implants & Prosthetics](../Skills/implants-and-prosthetics.md) | 4 | T |
| Install it in the owner | [Surgery](../Skills/surgery.md) | 2 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Power Armour Suit](power-armour-suit.md)*, worn over the body; a Cybernetic Implant goes inside it.
