# Castle or Fortress


*aka: ______________________________*

A stronghold built to hold out when everything outside has fallen. The defences are designed first, with walls, towers and killing grounds, then the buildings within are planned. Stonemasons raise the walls, labourers dig the ditches and throw up the earthworks, and at last the place is stocked and staffed with a garrison, stores and a well. A good castle changes the map of a region simply by standing there.

## Phases

*A stronghold that can hold a siege. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

## Not to be confused with

*[Warding a City](warding-a-city.md)*, which protects with magic rather than walls; a Castle or Fortress is stone, earth and soldiers.
