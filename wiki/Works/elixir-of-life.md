# Elixir of Life


*aka: ______________________________*

A potion that halts ageing. The seeker searches the oldest texts for hints, works the alchemy through long and dangerous stages, and at last brews the power into the draught. Those who drink it stop growing older. They do not stop being hurt, and they do not stop losing everyone they love. Many who have sought it spent the lives it would have saved in the searching.

## Phases

*A potion that halts ageing. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search the old texts | [Research](../Skills/research.md) | 4 | T |
| Work the alchemy | [Alchemy](../Skills/alchemy.md) | 8 | T |
| Brew in the power | [Potion Brewing](../Powers/potion-brewing.md) | 5 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Healing Potion](healing-potion.md)*, which closes wounds; an Elixir of Life stops the years themselves.
