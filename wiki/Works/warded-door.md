# Warded Door


*aka: ______________________________*

Signs are carved into the lintel or painted along the frame, neat and exact, and then the ward is set upon them. From then on the door knows who belongs. An intruder may find the handle will not turn, or that every bell in the house rings at once. Merchants ward their counting rooms, scholars their libraries, and more than one jealous husband has found himself locked out of his own bedroom.

## Phases

*A door that alarms or bars intruders. Size: Minor.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carve or paint the signs | [Calligraphy & Scribing](../Skills/calligraphy-and-scribing.md) | 1 | T |
| Set the ward | [Ward](../Powers/ward.md) | 1 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Warding a City](warding-a-city.md)*, which raises protection over whole districts and walls; a Warded Door guards a single entrance.
