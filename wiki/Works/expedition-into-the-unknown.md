# Expedition into the Unknown


*aka: ______________________________*

A journey to a place no one has mapped. It is planned and supplied down to the last nail and biscuit, then the party crosses the land, finds a way where there is none, and maps what they find so others can follow. Many expeditions come home thin and quarrelling. Some come home with a new world. A few do not come home, and are searched for for years.

## Phases

*Reaching a place no one has mapped. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plan and supply it | [Logistics](../Skills/logistics.md) | 3 | T |
| Cross the land | [Survival: Forest (Woodcraft)](../Skills/survival-forest-woodcraft.md) | 5 | T |
| Find the way | [Wayfinding](../Skills/wayfinding.md) | 4 | T |
| Map what was found | [Cartography](../Skills/cartography.md) | 2 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Great Map](great-map.md)*, which sets down known country precisely; an Expedition into the Unknown goes where there is no map yet.
