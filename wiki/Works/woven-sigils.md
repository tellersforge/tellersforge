# Woven Sigils


*aka: ______________________________*

Small workings sewn into clothing so that they pass for embroidery. The tailor stitches the design, the sigils are shaped correctly within it, and a working is stored in the threads. To the eye it is a pretty border on a cuff or collar. To the wearer, it is protection, luck or a hidden tool. Travellers, courtiers and spies wear them, and never let anyone else do their laundry.

## Phases

*Small workings sewn into clothing that pass for embroidery. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Embroider the sigils | [Tailoring](../Skills/tailoring.md) | 2 | T |
| Shape the signs correctly | [Rune & Sigil Magic](../Powers/rune-and-sigil-magic.md) | 2 | T |
| Store the working in them | [Lasting Enchantment](../Powers/lasting-enchantment.md) | 2 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Scroll of Power](scroll-of-power.md)*, which holds a working in writing to be spent once; Woven Sigils hide theirs in clothing and last.
