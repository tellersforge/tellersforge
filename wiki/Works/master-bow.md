# Master Bow


*aka: ______________________________*

A war bow made for one archer's draw. The bowyer chooses a stave and seasons it for as long as it takes, then shapes it and tillers it with a scraper, flexing and testing until both limbs bend as one. A string is laid and twisted to match. Finally the bow is tuned to its archer, who must draw it again and again while the maker watches. In the right hands it is astonishingly quick and true.

## Phases

*A war bow made for one archer. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|

## Not to be confused with

*[Master Sword](master-sword.md)*, the same care given to a blade; a Master Bow is shaped for a single archer's reach and strength.
