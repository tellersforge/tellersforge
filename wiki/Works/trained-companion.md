# Trained Companion


*aka: ______________________________*

A war mount, a hound or a hunting bird raised and trained for one owner. It is reared from birth, trained patiently to its work, and at last bonded with its master, who must learn it as well as it learns them. A trained companion is more than a tool. It knows its owner's moods, and it will charge, track, fetch or guard on a word or a gesture. Its loss is grieved like a friend's.

## Phases

*A war mount, hound or hunting bird trained for one owner. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raise it | [Herding & Husbandry](../Skills/herding-and-husbandry.md) | 2 | T |
| Train it | [Animal Training](../Skills/animal-training.md) | 5 | T |
| Bond with the owner | [Animal Handling](../Skills/animal-handling.md) | 2 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Taming a Great Beast](taming-a-great-beast.md)*, which wins over a creature far wilder and more powerful; a Trained Companion is raised from the start.
