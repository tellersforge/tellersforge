# Wind Knot


*aka: ______________________________*

A length of cord tied with a row of careful knots, into which a fair wind has been bound. When the sails hang limp and the ship lies becalmed, the captain unties a knot and the breeze returns. Coastal wise-women sell them on the quays, and sailors pay without haggling. The wise ones untie them one at a time. Legend is full of greedy crews who loosed them all at once and met a gale.

## Phases

*A cord holding a fair wind for later. Size: Minor.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

## Not to be confused with
