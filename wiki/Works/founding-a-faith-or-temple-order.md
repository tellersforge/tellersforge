# Founding a Faith or Temple Order


*aka: ______________________________*

A community gathered around a belief. The founder sets the rites that will bind the faithful together, preaches until people listen, and organises them into something that can survive the founder's death. It begins in a small room or an open field. If it lasts, it will build temples, write scriptures, split into rival schools and outlive every kingdom that tried to stamp it out.

## Phases

*A community gathered around a belief. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).
