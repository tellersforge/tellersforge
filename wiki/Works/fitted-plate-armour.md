# Fitted Plate Armour


*aka: ______________________________*

A full harness of steel plates, shaped and articulated to one body. The metal is prepared, then beaten into dozens of pieces that must overlap and slide as the wearer moves. Straps, lining and padding follow, and finally the armourer fits the whole suit to its owner, piece by piece, until it moves like clothing. A fitted harness costs as much as a farm. A knight in one can run, climb and even mount unaided.

## Phases

*A full harness made to measure. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare the steel | [Metallurgy](../Skills/metallurgy.md) | 3 | T |
| Shape the plates | [Weaponsmithing & Armouring](../Skills/weaponsmithing-and-armouring.md) | 6 | T |
| Straps, lining and padding | [Leatherwork](../Skills/leatherwork.md) | 2 | T |
| Fit it to the wearer | [Weaponsmithing & Armouring](../Skills/weaponsmithing-and-armouring.md) | 2 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Power Armour Suit](power-armour-suit.md)*, a machine that moves under its own power; Fitted Plate Armour is steel, leather and the wearer's own strength.
