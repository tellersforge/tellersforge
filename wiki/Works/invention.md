# Invention


*aka: ______________________________*

A device no one has built before. The inventor first works out the principle, then designs a machine to use it, builds a prototype in the workshop and tests it again and again, fixing what breaks. Most prototypes fail in some interesting way. The one that works changes something forever: how people travel, fight, cook, count or speak across distances.

## Phases

*A device no one has built before. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work out the principle | [Physical Sciences](../Skills/physical-sciences.md) | 4 | T |
| Design it | [Mechanical Engineering](../Skills/mechanical-engineering.md) | 4 | T |
| Build a prototype | [Machining & Welding](../Skills/machining-and-welding.md) | 3 | T |
| Test and fix it | [Jury-Rigging](../Skills/jury-rigging.md) | 2 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Scientific Discovery](scientific-discovery.md)*, which proves a law of nature; an Invention puts known principles into a working machine.
