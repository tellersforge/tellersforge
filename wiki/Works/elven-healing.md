# Elven Healing


*aka: ______________________________*

The herb-and-hand craft of the forest elves. The healer cleanses and dresses the wound with forest simples: moss, bark, sap and leaf, gathered at the right time. Then the mending continues at the body's own pace, gently, but far better than herbs alone could manage. The elves call it skill, and teach it as such. Anyone watching closely can see that part of it is magic, and that the elves simply do not know it.

## Phases

*Forest elves' herb-and-hand craft; the elves call it skill, but the mending past the herbs is magic (unknowing magic). Size: Minor.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Healing Potion](healing-potion.md)*, a brewed draught that closes wounds at once; Elven Healing works slowly, by hand and herb.
