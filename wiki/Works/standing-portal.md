# Standing Portal


*aka: ______________________________*

A permanent gateway to another place or another world. An arch is built in stone, heavy and exact, the runes are carved into it, and then the way is opened. From then on, a step through leads somewhere far away. Portals change the fortunes of cities and draw the attention of everything on the other side. A wise builder puts a strong door on it, and a guard.

## Phases

*A permanent gateway to another place or world. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build the arch | [Stonemasonry](../Skills/stonemasonry.md) | 4 | T |
| Carve the runes | [Rune & Sigil Magic](../Powers/rune-and-sigil-magic.md) | 4 | T |
| Open the way | [Portal](../Powers/portal.md) | 5 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Pocket Realm](../Powers/pocket-realm.md)*, which creates a hidden world of one's own; a Standing Portal only opens a way to somewhere that already exists.
