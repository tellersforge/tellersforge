# Scroll of Power


*aka: ______________________________*

A sheet of fine vellum or paper, written in a careful hand, which holds a working ready to be let loose. The scribe prepares the page with clean lines and exact characters, and the caster then pours the power into the writing, where it waits. Anyone who knows how to read it aloud can release it later, once. Wizards stock them before a journey, and apprentices steal them, usually with memorable results.

## Phases

*A power written down to be released later. Size: Minor.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Write the scroll | [Calligraphy & Scribing](../Skills/calligraphy-and-scribing.md) | 1 | T |
| Store the power in it | [Store Power](../Powers/store-power.md) | 1 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Woven Sigils](woven-sigils.md)*, which hold small workings in embroidered cloth; a Scroll of Power is written, and spent when it is read.
