# Printing Workshop


*aka: ______________________________*

A press, a case of type and a room full of ink and paper. The press is built from heavy timber, the type or plates cast in metal, and the whole workshop set up to turn out pages by the hundred. Where a scribe copies one book in a season, a press makes a whole edition in weeks. Once one exists, ideas travel faster than any ruler can stop them, and rulers know it.

## Phases

*A press that makes books in numbers. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build the press | [Carpentry & Joinery](../Skills/carpentry-and-joinery.md) | 3 | T |
| Cast the type or plates | [Metallurgy](../Skills/metallurgy.md) | 3 | T |
| Set up the workshop | [Bookcraft](../Skills/bookcraft.md) | 2 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Chronicle](chronicle.md)*, a single written history; a Printing Workshop is the means of copying books in numbers.
