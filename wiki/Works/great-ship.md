# Great Ship


*aka: ______________________________*

An ocean-going vessel, big enough to carry a crew across the world and back. The hull is designed and then built plank by plank, masts and fittings are made and stepped, the rigging laid out, the sails woven and sewn. Then comes the test: sea trials, where every flaw shows itself. A great ship takes a shipyard and a year. A harbour full of them is the sign of a people who mean to go somewhere.

## Phases

*An ocean-going vessel. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |

| Build the hull | [Shipwrighting](../Skills/shipwrighting.md) | 6 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Ocean Voyage](ocean-voyage.md)*, the crossing itself; a Great Ship is the vessel that makes it possible.
