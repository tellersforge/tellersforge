# Warding a City


*aka: ______________________________*

Protecting a whole city with wards. Boundary stones are marked with runes around the walls, each is consecrated in turn, and the great ward is raised over them all. Afterwards, the city is harder to attack by magic, spirit or sometimes plain force. It takes a guild of casters and the goodwill of priests. It must be tended, and a neglected ward fails just when it is needed most.

## Phases

*Protecting a whole city with wards. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mark the boundary stones | [Rune & Sigil Magic](../Powers/rune-and-sigil-magic.md) | 6 | T |
| Consecrate them | [Ritual & Liturgy](../Skills/ritual-and-liturgy.md) | 2 | T |
| Raise the ward | [Ward](../Powers/ward.md) | 6 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Warded Door](warded-door.md)*, which guards a single entrance; Warding a City guards everything within its walls.
