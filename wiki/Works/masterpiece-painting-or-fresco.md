# Masterpiece Painting or Fresco


*aka: ______________________________*

A painting that people travel to see. The artist makes studies and sketches by the dozen, the wall or canvas is prepared with plaster or ground, and then the painting itself is done, sometimes over years, on scaffolding or in a cold studio. When it is finished, crowds come. Some weep in front of it. Others argue about it for centuries, which is its own kind of success.

## Phases

*A painting people travel to see. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studies and sketches | [Painting & Drawing](../Skills/painting-and-drawing.md) | 2 | B |
| Prepare the wall or canvas | [Construction](../Skills/construction.md) | 1 | T |
| Paint it | [Painting & Drawing](../Skills/painting-and-drawing.md) | 6 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Great Tapestry](great-tapestry.md)*, which tells a picture in thread; a Masterpiece Painting or Fresco is worked in paint on wall or canvas.
