# Mill or Great Machine


*aka: ______________________________*

A water mill, a windmill, a crane that lifts stone to the top of a cathedral, or an engine that does the work of a hundred hands. The works are designed, the frame built in heavy timber, the iron parts forged, and the whole thing set up and run until it turns smoothly. A mill feeds a district, and the miller, often, grows rich and unpopular. A great machine changes what a town can make.

## Phases

*A water mill, windmill, crane or engine. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design the works | [Mechanical Engineering](../Skills/mechanical-engineering.md) | 4 | T |
| Build the frame | [Carpentry & Joinery](../Skills/carpentry-and-joinery.md) | 4 | T |
| Forge the iron parts | [Blacksmithing](../Skills/blacksmithing.md) | 3 | T |
| Set it up and run it in | [Construction](../Skills/construction.md) | 2 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Invention](invention.md)*, which creates a device no one has built before; a Mill or Great Machine builds a known design on a grand scale.
