# Spirit Consultation


*aka: ______________________________*

A question asked of something older and wiser than any living person. The priest or shaman performs the rite in full, with its incense, chants and offerings, because spirits and gods do not answer the careless. Then comes the listening: a voice in the smoke, a sign in the entrails, a certainty that settles in the chest. The answer is true, but it is rarely simple, and it is never quite the answer to the question that was asked.

## Phases

*Asking a spirit or god a question. Size: Minor.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Perform the rite | [Ritual & Liturgy](../Skills/ritual-and-liturgy.md) | 1 | T |
| Hear the answer | [Commune](../Powers/commune.md) | 1 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[True Reading](true-reading.md)*, which looks into the future through signs; a Spirit Consultation asks a spirit or god directly and hears its reply.
