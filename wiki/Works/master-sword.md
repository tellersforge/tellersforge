# Master Sword


*aka: ______________________________*

A blade made for one owner and no other. The steel is smelted and folded until it is clean and even, forged into shape, then tempered and balanced with endless care. The hilt and grip are wrapped to suit a particular hand, and at last the smith fits the finished sword to the owner, adjusting weight and length until it feels like part of the arm. Such swords are handed down, fought over and given names.

## Phases

*A blade made for one owner. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smelt and fold the steel | [Metallurgy](../Skills/metallurgy.md) | 3 | T |
| Forge the blade | [Blacksmithing](../Skills/blacksmithing.md) | 5 | T |
| Temper and balance it | [Weaponsmithing & Armouring](../Skills/weaponsmithing-and-armouring.md) | 4 | T |
| Make the hilt and grip | [Leatherwork](../Skills/leatherwork.md) | 2 | T |
| Fit it to the owner's hand | [Weaponsmithing & Armouring](../Skills/weaponsmithing-and-armouring.md) | 1 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Sentient Blade](sentient-blade.md)*, which is awakened to think for itself; a Master Sword is simply the finest steel a smith can make.
