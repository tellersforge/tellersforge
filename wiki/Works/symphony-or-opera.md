# Symphony or Opera


*aka: ______________________________*

A great musical work, written and brought to life. The composer writes it, sometimes in a fever and sometimes over years, the players are rehearsed until they can play it together, and then comes the performance itself. The first night may end in cheers or a riot. Either way, the music goes on being played long after the composer, the players and the audience are gone.

## Phases

*A great musical work, performed. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compose it | [Musical Composition](../Skills/musical-composition.md) | 6 | B |
| Rehearse the players | [Teaching](../Skills/teaching.md) | 3 | T |
| Perform it | [Instrument (per instrument)](../Skills/instrument-per-instrument.md) | 2 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Epic or Great Novel](epic-or-great-novel.md)*, the writer's great work; a Symphony or Opera lives only when it is performed.
