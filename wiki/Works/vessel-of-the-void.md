# Vessel of the Void


*aka: ______________________________*

A made thing that pours emptiness into a mind until the will drowns. The maker studies forbidden texts, merges two crafts that were never meant to meet, fashions the vessel as a jewel or ornament, draws the Void into it and makes it unchangeable. The final step is the price: the maker's own soul is split to seal it. Those who hold such a thing are hollowed out, slowly, and never notice.

## Phases

*A made thing that pours emptiness into a mind until the will drowns. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Study the forbidden texts | [Research](../Skills/research.md) | 6 | T |
| Merge the two crafts | [Arcana](../Skills/arcana.md) | 4 | B |
| Make the vessel | [Jewellery & Gem Cutting](../Skills/jewellery-and-gem-cutting.md) | 3 | T |
| Draw the Void into it | [Void Drawing](../Powers/void-drawing.md) | 5 | B |
| Make it immutable | [Permanency](../Powers/permanency.md) | 3 | B |
| Pay the price: split the soul | [Soul Craft](../Powers/soul-craft.md) | 2 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Soul-Splintering](soul-splintering.md)*, which divides the soul for the maker's own sake; a Vessel of the Void spends a piece of it to empty others.
