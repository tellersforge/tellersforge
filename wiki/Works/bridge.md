# Bridge


*aka: ______________________________*

A crossing where there was none: over a river too wide to ford, a gorge too deep to climb. The site is surveyed with care, the bridge designed for the load it must carry and the floods it must survive, the piers and arches built in stone, and the deck laid across them. Trade follows a bridge the way water follows a channel. A town at a bridge grows rich, and a town that loses one withers.

## Phases

*A bridge across a river or gorge. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survey the site | [Surveying](../Skills/surveying.md) | 2 | T |
| Design it | [Civil Engineering](../Skills/civil-engineering.md) | 4 | T |
| Build the piers and arches | [Stonemasonry](../Skills/stonemasonry.md) | 5 | T |
| Build the deck | [Construction](../Skills/construction.md) | 4 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Aqueduct or Canal](aqueduct-or-canal.md)*, which carries water across the land; a Bridge carries people and carts over it.
