# Great Map


*aka: ______________________________*

An accurate map of a coast, a range of mountains or a whole region. The mapmaker travels the land, taking bearings, then surveys it properly with chain and instrument, and at last draws it with care and a good deal of ink. A true map is worth more than gold to a general, a merchant or a smuggler. A false one has lost more armies than any enemy.

## Phases

*An accurate map of a region or coast. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Travel and take bearings | [Wayfinding](../Skills/wayfinding.md) | 4 | T |
| Measure the land | [Surveying](../Skills/surveying.md) | 4 | T |
| Draw the map | [Cartography](../Skills/cartography.md) | 4 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Expedition into the Unknown](expedition-into-the-unknown.md)*, which reaches places no one has been; a Great Map sets down land already reached, precisely.
