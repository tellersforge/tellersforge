# School or Order


*aka: ______________________________*

A place that trains others in a discipline: a fighting school, a college of scholars, a lodge of mystics. The founder gathers teachers and students, sets up the place with its rules, rooms and money, and teaches the first generation, who will teach the next. A good school outlives its founder by centuries. Its graduates recognise one another across the world by a word, a stance or a ring.

## Phases

*A place that trains others in a discipline. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).
