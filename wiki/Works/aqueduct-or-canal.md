# Aqueduct or Canal


*aka: ______________________________*

Water carried where it is needed, sometimes over great distances. The route is surveyed so the fall is gentle and constant, the works designed, the channel dug and built, and stone channels and arches raised where the land dips. When it is finished, a dry city drinks, a field blooms, or barges float heavy loads across country. It is patient, unglamorous engineering, and it outlasts empires.

## Phases

*Water carried across miles. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survey the route | [Surveying](../Skills/surveying.md) | 3 | T |
| Design the works | [Civil Engineering](../Skills/civil-engineering.md) | 5 | T |
| Dig and build | [Construction](../Skills/construction.md) | 8 | T |
| Stone channels and arches | [Stonemasonry](../Skills/stonemasonry.md) | 5 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Bridge](bridge.md)*, which spans a gap for travellers; an Aqueduct or Canal moves water, often across a bridge of its own.
