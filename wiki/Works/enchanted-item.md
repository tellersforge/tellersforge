# Enchanted Item


*aka: ______________________________*

A made thing with magic that lasts: a ring, a cloak, a lamp, a pair of boots. First the item is crafted properly by whoever has the trade. Runes are inscribed on it, the enchantment is laid into them, and the finished piece is attuned to the one who will use it. It is the classic undertaking of the working enchanter, slow and expensive, and every piece is different. The best are passed down for generations.

## Phases

*A lasting magic item of any kind. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Craft the item | (the craft skill that makes it) | 4 | T |
| Inscribe the runes | [Rune & Sigil Magic](../Powers/rune-and-sigil-magic.md) | 3 | T |
| Lay the enchantment | [Lasting Enchantment](../Powers/lasting-enchantment.md) | 5 | B |
| Attune it to the owner | [Focus Bonding](../Powers/focus-bonding.md) | 1 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Permanent Arcane Enchantment](permanent-arcane-enchantment.md)*, which fixes a single named spell into an object forever; an Enchanted Item carries a broader, crafted magic.
