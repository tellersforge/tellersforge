# Taming a Great Beast


*aka: ______________________________*

Winning over a dragon, a great cat of the high mountains or some other creature that could kill its would-be friend in an instant. The tamer must first learn its ways from lore and observation, then earn its trust, slowly and at great risk, and finally seal a bond between them. Most who try are eaten. Those who succeed gain an ally no army can match, and a companion with opinions.

## Phases

*Winning over a dragon or other great creature. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learn its ways | [Creature & Occult Lore](../Skills/creature-and-occult-lore.md) | 2 | T |
| Earn its trust | [Exotic Beasts](../Skills/exotic-beasts.md) | 8 | B |
| Seal the bond | [Beast Bonding](../Powers/beast-bonding.md) | 3 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Trained Companion](trained-companion.md)*, an ordinary animal raised for one owner; Taming a Great Beast wins over something wild and dangerous.
