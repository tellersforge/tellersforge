# Long Con


*aka: ______________________________*

A swindle played out over months. The con artist studies the mark from a distance, builds a false identity that will stand up to scrutiny, forges the papers to back it, and then plays the part, day after day, until the moment comes to take everything. The best cons leave the victim unaware that anything happened. The worst leave a very angry, very rich enemy.

## Phases

*A swindle played out over months. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Study the mark | [Shadowing & Surveillance](../Skills/shadowing-and-surveillance.md) | 3 | T |
| Build the false identity | [Disguise](../Skills/disguise.md) | 2 | T |
| Forge the papers | [Forgery](../Skills/forgery.md) | 2 | T |
| Play the part | [Deception](../Skills/deception.md) | 5 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Treaty](treaty.md)*, which wins agreement honestly; a Long Con wins trust in order to betray it.
