# Great Statue


*aka: ______________________________*

A monument in stone or bronze, larger than life and meant to stay. The sculptor models it first, in clay or wax; then the material is quarried or cast; and the figure is carved or finished until it seems about to move. A great statue can mark a harbour, a city square or a hilltop tomb. Long after the person it shows is forgotten, travellers give directions by it.

## Phases

*A monument in stone or bronze. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Model it | [Sculpture](../Skills/sculpture.md) | 3 | B |
| Quarry or cast the material | [Stonemasonry](../Skills/stonemasonry.md) | 3 | T |
| Carve or finish it | [Sculpture](../Skills/sculpture.md) | 6 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Stone Guardian](stone-guardian.md)*, a carved figure that moves and obeys; a Great Statue only stands and is admired.
