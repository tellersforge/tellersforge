# Becoming Deathless


*aka: ______________________________*

Withholding one's own death. The soul is splintered, death itself is withheld by power, and the body is sustained on life stolen from others. The result is not immortality in any pleasant sense: the body slowly decays, but does not die. Those who have done it grow grey, then withered, then something worse, and must keep feeding to go on. They are feared, hunted and, above all, patient.

## Phases

*Withholding one's own death; the body slowly decays but will not die. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Splinter the soul | [Soul Craft](../Powers/soul-craft.md) | 3 | B |
| Withhold death | [Cheat Death](../Powers/cheat-death.md) | 5 | B |
| Sustain the body on stolen life | [Life Drain](../Powers/life-drain.md) | 3 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Elixir of Life](elixir-of-life.md)*, which halts ageing in a living body; Becoming Deathless keeps a decaying body from dying.
