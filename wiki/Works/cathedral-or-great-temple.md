# Cathedral or Great Temple


*aka: ______________________________*

A house of worship built over a lifetime or several. It is designed by a master builder, then raised stone by stone, roofed with great timbers, glazed with coloured windows and filled with paintings and carvings. Only when all that is done is it consecrated. Whole towns grow up around such buildings, living off the work. The people who laid the foundations rarely see the last stone set, and they build anyway.

## Phases

*A house of worship built over years. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
