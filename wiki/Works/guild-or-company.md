# Guild or Company


*aka: ______________________________*

A lasting organisation of traders or crafters. A charter is drawn up to say who may join and what they may do, money is raised, the company is run from day to day, and its members are led and kept together. A strong guild sets prices, trains apprentices, looks after widows and argues with the crown. Some grow richer and more powerful than the kingdoms they trade in.

## Phases

*A lasting organisation of traders or crafters. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Draw up the charter | [Law](../Skills/law.md) | 2 | T |
| Raise the money | [Finance](../Skills/finance.md) | 3 | T |
| Run it | [Administration](../Skills/administration.md) | 4 | T |
| Lead the members | [Leadership](../Skills/leadership.md) | 3 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[School or Order](school-or-order.md)*, which exists to teach; a Guild or Company exists to trade and protect its members.
