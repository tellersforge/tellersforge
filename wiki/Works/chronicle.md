# Chronicle


*aka: ______________________________*

A written history of a people, a war or a realm. The chronicler gathers sources, from letters and ledgers to the tales of old soldiers, then sorts truth from legend, writes it all out in order, and has it copied and bound. A good chronicle outlives the kingdom it describes. A partial one outlives it too, and it is the version later ages believe.

## Phases

*A history of a people, war or realm. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gather sources | [Research](../Skills/research.md) | 4 | T |
| Sort truth from legend | [History](../Skills/history.md) | 4 | T |
| Write it | [Composition](../Skills/composition.md) | 4 | T |
| Copy and bind it | [Bookcraft](../Skills/bookcraft.md) | 2 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Epic or Great Novel](epic-or-great-novel.md)*, which tells a story that need not be true; a Chronicle aims to record what really happened.
