# Epic or Great Novel


*aka: ______________________________*

A work of words that outlives its author. The writer gathers old tales and new ones, shapes them into something larger, writes it through to the end, and has it copied and bound. People recite it by the fire, quote it in court and teach it to children. The author may die poor and unknown. The characters, somehow, never do.

## Phases

*A work of words that outlives its author. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gather the tales | [Legends & Folklore](../Skills/legends-and-folklore.md) | 2 | T |
| Write it | [Composition](../Skills/composition.md) | 8 | B |
| Copy and bind it | [Bookcraft](../Skills/bookcraft.md) | 2 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Chronicle](chronicle.md)*, which records what happened; an Epic or Great Novel tells what might have, or should have.
