# Vineyard or Great Garden


*aka: ______________________________*

Land made to bear its best. The ground is prepared, the vines or trees planted and tended over many seasons, and at last the harvest is gathered and turned into wine, cider or preserves worth the waiting. A great vineyard takes a generation to mature and a family to keep. Its name goes on the bottle, and a good vintage can be remembered as well as a battle.

## Phases

*Land made to bear its best. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare the land | [Farming](../Skills/farming.md) | 4 | T |
| Grow it over seasons | [Farming](../Skills/farming.md) | 6 | T |
| Make the wine or harvest | [Brewing & Distilling](../Skills/brewing-and-distilling.md) | 3 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[New Breed of Animal](new-breed-of-animal.md)*, which improves a bloodline over generations; a Vineyard or Great Garden improves the land.
