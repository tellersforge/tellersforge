# Trade Route


*aka: ______________________________*

A lasting, profitable route between peoples who did not trade before. The merchant must learn the other side's customs, win agreements from chiefs and officials, organise the caravans or ships that will carry the goods, and then make the whole thing pay. When it works, wealth flows both ways, along with ideas, foods and diseases. Cities grow up at its crossings, and wars are fought over them.

## Phases

*A lasting, profitable route between peoples. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

## Not to be confused with

*[Treaty](treaty.md)*, a formal peace between peoples; a Trade Route is a commercial link that may or may not need one.
