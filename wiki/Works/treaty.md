# Treaty


*aka: ______________________________*

A lasting peace or alliance between peoples who have reason to distrust each other. The envoy learns the other side's customs and fears, negotiates, often for months, writes the terms so that both sides can live with them, and wins over the doubters at home. A good treaty holds for generations. A bad one holds until the first excuse to break it, and is remembered as the cause of the next war.
