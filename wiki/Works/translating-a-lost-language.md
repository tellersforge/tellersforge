# Translating a Lost Language


*aka: ______________________________*

Making a dead script speak again. The scholar gathers every inscription they can find, on temple walls, broken pots and buried tablets, then hunts through related languages for echoes and clues. At last, through patience and a flash of insight, the language breaks open. Suddenly the dead have names, laws, jokes and grievances. The scholar's reward is to read, first of anyone in ages, what they wrote.

## Phases

*Reading a language no one has read for ages. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|

## Not to be confused with

*[Chronicle](chronicle.md)*, which writes history from sources that can already be read; this work makes a lost source readable in the first place.
