# Ocean Voyage


*aka: ______________________________*

Crossing an ocean to a far shore. The ship is provisioned for months, the navigator plots the course by stars and currents, and the crew handles her through storms, calms and the long grinding middle where nothing happens but weather. A successful voyage links two worlds that did not know each other existed. A failed one becomes a song, if anyone lives to sing it.

## Phases

*Crossing an ocean to a far shore. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Provision the ship | [Logistics](../Skills/logistics.md) | 3 | T |
| Navigate | [Sea Navigation](../Skills/sea-navigation.md) | 5 | T |
| Handle the ship through storms | [Sailing & Seamanship](../Skills/sailing-and-seamanship.md) | 5 | T |

## Not to be confused with

*[Great Ship](great-ship.md)*, which builds the vessel; an Ocean Voyage is the crossing made in it.
