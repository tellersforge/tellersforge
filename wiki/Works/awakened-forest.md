# Awakened Forest


*aka: ______________________________*

A forest that grows and guards on command. The druid first learns the land, its soil, water and old roots, then grows the forest by power, faster than nature ever could, and at last awakens the trees so that they move, listen and obey. Travellers who enter uninvited find paths that close behind them. Armies have been lost in such woods, and so have careless woodcutters.

## Phases

*A forest that grows and guards on command. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Know the land | [Nature Lore](../Skills/nature-lore.md) | 2 | T |
| Grow the forest | [Plant Growth](../Powers/plant-growth.md) | 6 | T |
| Awaken the trees | [Plant Animation](../Powers/plant-animation.md) | 4 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Vineyard or Great Garden](vineyard-or-great-garden.md)*, which cultivates land by skill and patience; an Awakened Forest is grown and woken by power.
