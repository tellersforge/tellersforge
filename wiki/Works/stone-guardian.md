# Stone Guardian


*aka: ______________________________*

A figure of carved stone that walks and obeys its maker. The sculptor carves the body, heavy and patient, and command runes are cut into it where they will not be worn away. Then comes the working that gives it life, and the binding that ties it to a master. It does not tire, eat or sleep. It stands at gates for centuries, and it will do exactly what it was told, which is not always what was meant.

## Phases

*A golem that obeys its maker. Size: Great.* T = transferable (anyone with the skill can do it); B = bound (must be done by the owner or the one with the power).

| Phase | Skill or power | Passes needed | T/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carve the body | [Sculpture](../Skills/sculpture.md) | 6 | T |
| Inscribe the command runes | [Rune & Sigil Magic](../Powers/rune-and-sigil-magic.md) | 4 | T |
| Give it life | [Animate Object](../Powers/animate-object.md) | 4 | B |
| Bind it to its master | [Lasting Enchantment](../Powers/lasting-enchantment.md) | 2 | B |

## Not to be confused with

*[Guardian Spirit](guardian-spirit.md)*, which binds a spirit to protect a place; a Stone Guardian is a made body driven by runes.
