# Gear lexicon


Everything a character can carry. Combat gear first (weapons, then armour and shields), then general equipment grouped by use, in the order a player kits out. Every item lists its weight and its price at the era it first appears (1 G = 10 S = 100 C; 1 S is an ordinary worker's pay for a day; see [Rules, chapter 11](Rules/11-equipment-and-carrying.md)). Ammunition is in the Weapon List and the Rules' table 16.5; vehicles and mounts have their own [page](vehicles.md). General equipment always shows a bonus and a penalty, 0 when there is none.
