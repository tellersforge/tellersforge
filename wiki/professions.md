# Professions


Creation step 4: **what your character became.** It comes last and has the final word.

## How to use it

- **Every profession has the same shape:** four **core** skills **+3** (Practiced), two **supporting** skills **+1**, and two **neglected** skills **−1**: what that life leaves no room for.
- **Station and profession together tell the story:** an aristocratic scholar is **Gentry** + **Scholar** (book learning, reading from childhood, no fighting sense); a common-born field officer is **Commoner** or **Warrior family** + **Military officer** (reads people and ground, knows by experience how things stand and break, without the mathematics).
- **Life bonus:** **+6** if it fights for a living, **+4** if it works with its body or travels rough, **+2** otherwise. Add it to Life (step 10).
- **Needs** is the earliest era the profession exists in. Choose one your era has reached.
- **Choice lines** ("one of") let you pick one; for weapons, pick one your era has.
- **Starting kit** is what the profession carries; station decides its quality (starting wealth).
