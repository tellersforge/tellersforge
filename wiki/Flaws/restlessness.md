# Restlessness

*Set: Core*

A flaw of the *Habits and body* kind. It fires when can't sit still, wait or keep watch.

## Game block

- **Score:** roll 1d4+5 once when the flaw is taken; in play, roll 1d20 at or under the score
- **When it fires:** can't sit still, wait or keep watch
- **Opposes:** WIS
- **How flaws work:** the player rolls 1d20 at or under the flaw's score; if it fires, the character acts on it, whether the player likes it or not (Rules, chapter 5).
