# Superstition

*Set: Core*

A flaw of the *Mind and belief* kind. It fires when trusts lucky charms and bad-luck signs; won't act on a bad omen.

## Game block

- **Score:** roll 1d4+5 once when the flaw is taken; in play, roll 1d20 at or under the score
- **When it fires:** trusts lucky charms and bad-luck signs; won't act on a bad omen
- **Opposes:** PER (a standard flaw)
- **How flaws work:** the player rolls 1d20 at or under the flaw's score; if it fires, the character acts on it, whether the player likes it or not (Rules, chapter 5).
