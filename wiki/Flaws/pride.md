# Pride

*Set: Core*

A flaw of the *Pride and ego* kind. It fires when can't swallow their pride: won't apologise, ask for help or accept charity.

## Game block

- **Score:** roll 1d4+3 once when the flaw is taken; in play, roll 1d20 at or under the score
- **When it fires:** can't swallow their pride: won't apologise, ask for help or accept charity
- **Opposes:** WIS
- **How flaws work:** the player rolls 1d20 at or under the flaw's score; if it fires, the character acts on it, whether the player likes it or not (Rules, chapter 5).
