# Addiction (chosen substance)

*Set: Core*

A flaw of the *Appetites and vices* kind. It fires when craves it and goes looking for it; gets sick and desperate without it.

## Game block

- **Score:** roll 1d4+1 once when the flaw is taken; in play, roll 1d20 at or under the score
- **When it fires:** craves it and goes looking for it; gets sick and desperate without it
- **Opposes:** END
- **How flaws work:** the player rolls 1d20 at or under the flaw's score; if it fires, the character acts on it, whether the player likes it or not (Rules, chapter 5).
