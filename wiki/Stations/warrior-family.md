# Warrior family

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Who it covers: a line of soldiers, guards or sworn fighters.

## Station lines

- **Tier:** middle
- **+1:** any one weapon group of your era; END (attribute)
- **−1:** [Diplomacy](../Skills/diplomacy.md)
- **Starting wealth:** modest: decent clothing, one more weapon and one piece of armour your profession would wear; starting money 3d6 S (one step poorer in a poor land, richer in a rich one)
- **Read it through your culture:** your culture's society and schooling add lines for this station's tier; see [Stations](../stations.md), "How your society changes your station".
