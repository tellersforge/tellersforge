# Craft family

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

Who it covers: a family that lives by a skilled craft: smiths, weavers, builders, engineers.

## Station lines

- **Tier:** middle
- **+1:** any one skill from group 10 (Crafts) or 11 (Building and engineering) in table 16.1, or Mechanics; [Appraisal](../Skills/appraisal.md)
- **−1:** [Riding](../Skills/riding.md)
- **Starting wealth:** modest: decent clothing, one more weapon and one piece of armour your profession would wear; starting money 3d6 S (one step poorer in a poor land, richer in a rich one)
- **Read it through your culture:** your culture's society and schooling add lines for this station's tier; see [Stations](../stations.md), "How your society changes your station".

How to use a station: [Rules, chapter 3, step 3](../Rules/03-making-a-character.md); every station on one page: [Stations](../stations.md).
