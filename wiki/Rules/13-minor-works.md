# 13. Minor Works

*Set: Core*

Some tasks are too big for one roll. They are broken into phases; each phase names a skill or power and how many passes it needs. Core carries the small kind, the Minor Work. The large kind, the Great Work (forging a magic sword, building a cathedral, finding a cure over months or years, with phases anyone may do and phases only the future owner may do), is in the Expansions.

### Minor Works

A skill and a power together, done in a scene or a day. First the skill phase (the method), then the power phase (the magic). Each phase is one check. If the skill phase fails, the power phase is at −3; if the power phase fails, the skill's result stands on its own.

**A true fortune:** Divination (the skill: laying out and reading the signs), then Augury (the power: seeing what is really coming). A charlatan with only the skill gives a convincing but ordinary reading. A raw talent with only the power sees true, but handles it poorly.

Any pair the player can argue for is a Minor Work: Healing then Healing Touch for a wound that will not close; Crafting then Mend for a broken blade; Performance then Sleep for a lullaby that really works. The Game Master may ask for a third phase when the thing is hard.
