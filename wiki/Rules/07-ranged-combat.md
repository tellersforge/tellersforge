# 7. Ranged Combat

*Set: Core*

Ranged attacks use the Ranged Attack value (or the weapon group's Attack in Core), one d20. The target can only Dodge; you cannot parry an arrow with a sword. A shield can block arrows and thrown weapons (chapter 8).

### Range, unchanged

Range changes the shooter's Attack. This is Basic's table and it stands at every layer.

| Target is at     | Point blank | Short | Medium | Long | Extreme |
|------------------|-------------|-------|--------|------|---------|
| Change to Attack | +2          | 0     | −2     | −4   | −8      |

Each weapon lists its own distances in metres. Thrown spear or knife 2 / 5 / 10 / 15 / 25; sling 3 / 20 / 40 / 70 / 120; bow 5 / 30 / 60 / 100 / 200; pistol 5 / 15 / 30 / 50 / 100; rifle 5 / 100 / 300 / 600 / 1,000; laser blaster 5 / 50 / 150 / 300 / 500. Gear / Core has the rest. Energy weapons halve range penalties.

### Core adds: cover

| Cover            | Partial (a tree, a doorframe) | Heavy (a wall, a vehicle) | Full                       |
|------------------|-------------------------------|---------------------------|----------------------------|
| Change to Attack | −2                            | −4                        | cannot be hit, only pinned |

### Core adds: reloading

Each weapon lists how many rounds a reload takes. A bow shoots every round; a crossbow every second or third; a musket every fourth or fifth; a magazine weapon fires until it is empty and then takes a round. Power cells work exactly like ammunition.

### Core adds: how you fire

- **Aimed at one target:** normal roll, full damage.
- **At a group or position:** +3 to hit, but the weapon's fixed damage is halved. A rifle doing 2d6+2 would do 2d6+1.
- **Pinning fire:** no roll. Everyone in the area stays in cover, or takes −2 to everything they do that round.
- **Deeper step, stray rounds:** each exposed person in the area rolls a d20; a natural 20 means they are hit. Tables that use this accept that someone may die to nobody's decision.

### Core adds: bursts and recoil

Roll each shot of a burst separately. The first uses your normal Attack; each after it is 4 worse than the one before. Braced on a hard surface: 3 worse each. Bipod or tripod: 2 worse each.

**Example.** Attack 13, three-round burst: standing 13, 9, 5. Braced 13, 10, 7. Bipod 13, 11, 9.

Guns are deadly and hard to hit with. Firefights are about not getting shot: cover, pinning fire and flanking.

### Thrown weapons

A spear, a knife or a grenade is thrown with the Slings and Thrown group (or Basic's Ranged Attack). Strength sets how far: the listed ranges are for Strength 11; add 1 metre to each band for every point above, take 1 off for every point below, never under half. A grenade has no damage attribute: its damage depends only on how close the target is to the blast (Gear / Core).

### The narrow escape

When a hit would kill a player character, the Game Master may turn it into a lasting mark instead: a stone chip from a near miss takes an eye, a graze leaves a scar, the bullet stops in the armour plate and the soldier keeps it. The dice said dead; the story says survived, and the character carries the mark. See Permanent injuries, chapter 10.
