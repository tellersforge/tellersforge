# 15. Cheat Sheets

*Set: Core*

> ### Checking a skill or power (Core)
>
> - Roll one d20 per attribute listed. The fate die goes on the bold one.
> - Each target = attribute + Lvl/Mod + everything else. No upper limit; lowest is 1.
> - Every die at or under its target: success. Any die over: failure.
> - Spend rerolls on failed dice, one each.
> - Fate die natural 1: success. Natural 20: failure, no reroll. The impossible result: roll Luck.
> - Basic style: one die against the bold attribute + Lvl/Mod, −4 if other attributes are listed.

> ### Combat round
>
> - Highest Courage goes first.
> - Attacker rolls d20 under Attack.
> - Defender rolls under Parry (one per weapon or shield, each against a different attack) or Dodge.
> - Hit: weapon damage + bonus, minus armour, comes off Life. Armour is never used up.
> - Weak point −4, armour half. Vital gap −8, no armour.
> - Natural 1 to hit: critical. Natural 20: miss, and maybe a fumble.
> - Two rounds with no damage: Strength or Endurance contest.
> - Tired: the penalty comes off damage.

> ### Shooting
>
> - Range: point blank +2, short 0, medium −2, long −4, extreme −8.
> - Cover: partial −2, heavy −4, full: pinned only.
> - At a group: +3 to hit, half the fixed damage.
> - Pinning fire: no roll; targets stay down or take −2.
> - Burst: each shot 4 worse (braced 3, bipod 2).
> - Target can only Dodge (or block with a shield, not bullets).

> ### Magic
>
> - Max Energy = INT + WIS. Cast: roll the power's value. Success: pay the cost. Failure: pay half.
> - Level makes a power reliable; a bigger effect is the next version, after mastery.
> - Damaging powers: target Dodges, shield halves, armour applies. Lightning ignores both.

> ### Level Up
>
> - Attribute roll: d20 against one attribute. Over it: +1.
> - Flaw roll: d20 against one flaw. At or under it: −1.
> - Two points: each raises a skill or power one level, or adds a reroll after +5. Tip: spread them.
> - Skill level: Lvl/Mod +1 to +5, then rerolls up to the number of dice. Both full: mastered.

> ### End of session
>
> - Each player says what their character learned.
> - Game Master gives XP (plus extras for good play and well-played flaws).
> - BONUS roll if allowed: d20 at or under 10 − the skill's level: +1 level.
