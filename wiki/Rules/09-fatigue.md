# 9. Fatigue

*Set: Core*

Hard effort wears you down. This chapter is Core only; Basic has no fatigue. Fatigue follows whichever attribute is working hardest: muscles, mind, or nerve.

| Stage    | Effect                                                                    |
|----------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Warning  | No penalty. The Game Master tells you you are tiring.                     |
| Winded   | −1                                                                        |
| Spent    | −3                                                                        |
| Breaking | Before every action, pass an Endurance check (or the strained attribute). |

- In a fight, the penalty comes off damage, not your chance to hit. A tired fighter hits as often, but weaker.
- Mental strain lands on thinking skills; strain on your charm lands on social skills.
- Step out of the fight for a round: drop one stage. A short rest clears physical fatigue. Mental fatigue needs quiet rest or sleep.
- A tiring fighter may switch from parrying (strength) to dodging (agility).
- A fumble (chapter 6) may push you one stage down.

Fatigue is the Game Master's call. It only comes when the effort is really hard for that character: a blacksmith hammers all day without tiring; a scholar tires in minutes. Beginners tire fast; experts hardly tire at all under ordinary conditions. For harsh conditions, the Game Master can lower a skill a level or start a character further down the ladder.
