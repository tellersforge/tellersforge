# 16. The Tables

*Set: Core*

This book holds the rules. The numbers, every skill, power, weapon, piece of armour, item, flaw, culture, social standing, profession, species and creature, live in the five Core lexicon books, each an entry per line with its game block: the attributes it rolls, its dice, its cost, its damage, its era, its weight and price.

| Book              | What is in it                                                                                                                              | Entries                    |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Characters / Core | Making a character, step by step; the Basic-to-Core upgrade; 54 species; 40 professions; cultures and social standings; the flaw catalogue | 54 species, 40 professions |
| Skills / Core     | Every Core skill, with the Basic skill it belongs to                                                                                       | 55                         |
| Powers / Core     | Every Core power and its versions, Basic's twelve first                                                                                    | 63                         |
| Gear / Core       | Weapon groups; weapons, armour and shields, equipment, vehicles, by era                                                                    | 145                        |
| Bestiary / Core   | Creatures and opponents, with the same numbers a character has                                                                             | 215                        |

A table that plays with only the Basic lists loses nothing: the Basic booklet's skill table, weapon table, armour table and twelve powers are complete in themselves, and every one of them appears unchanged in its Core book.
