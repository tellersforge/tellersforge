# 10. Healing and Injury

*Set: Core*

### Life, unchanged

Current Life can never go above Max Life. At 0 Life your character falls down, out of the fight. What happens next is up to the Game Master. A Healer's successful Healing check, or the power Healing Touch, saves them. Or the table uses the dying roll: each round the player rolls the d20, Endurance or lower to hang on; higher, they die; a successful Healing check stops the dying, and the character wakes after a night's sleep.

### Recovery

How much Life a night's sleep gives back is a table setting, chosen before characters are made. Basic's two choices stand, and Core adds a third:

- **Gritty:** 1 Life per night. Weeks, doctors, lasting scars.
- **Standard:** 1d6 Life per night ★. A good night's rest does real work.
- **Heroic:** all Life after a night's sleep. Back on your feet by morning.

**Healers.** A character with the Healing skill (or Core's First Aid or Medicine) can treat a wounded friend. A successful check gives back another 1d6 Life, once for each injury.

**Quick heals.** Scarce items that heal at once and skip the waiting: a healing draught, a trauma kit, an injector, a healing power. The world decides what they look like; Gear / Core and Powers / Core give the numbers.

### Core adds: permanent injuries

Whether lasting injuries happen at all is the table's choice. When one does:

- The character gains 10 XP at once.
- Player and Game Master pick one or two skills that make up for it. A one-eyed scout: Perception by hearing, Insight.
- While the injury lasts, any session where the character truly relies on one of those skills, the Game Master may allow an extra BONUS roll for it.
- The injured attribute gets a second attribute roll at every Level Up.
- The injury may be written as a flaw gained in play (chapter 5), with a score, if it can take over at a bad moment: a lamed leg on a long march, a ruined hand in a fight.

The reward always goes to what compensates, never to the thing lost. A mauled face costs Charisma, but Charisma grows back faster, as long as the character keeps facing people.

### Core adds: poison, disease, cold and hunger

These attack Endurance. The Game Master names a strength for the threat (a mild poison 10, a plague 16) and the character rolls a d20 under Endurance, with the difference between the threat and 11 as a modifier; failure costs Life or a stage of fatigue, as the Game Master judges, and the roll repeats each day or hour until the threat is treated or ends. Healing, Medicine and the right powers end it. The Bestiary gives numbers for venoms; Gear / Core for cures.
