# Chair, stool, bottle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Any era · Improvised (Improvised Weapons)*

Whatever comes to hand when the talking stops. A stool swung by the legs, a chair broken over a back, a bottle smashed on the table edge and held by the neck. Every inn fight in every age reaches this point sooner or later, and innkeepers price their furniture accordingly.

These weapons have one great virtue: they are already there. Nobody searches a man for a chair. They are also unreliable. A stool is clumsy, a bottle is short, and all of them fly apart on the first good blow, leaving the wielder holding a leg or a jagged neck of glass. A broken bottle is nastier than the whole one, and much more likely to bring the watch.
