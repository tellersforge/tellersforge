# Pocket Pistol

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Steam (needs steam) · Frontier and industrial nations*

A tiny handgun, small enough to fit in a waistcoat, a garter, a muff or a hollowed-out book. It carries only a couple of shots and has barely any barrel to speak of, so it is useless at a distance and uncertain even at a few paces. Its virtue is that nobody knows it is there.

Gamblers carry it up a sleeve, ladies in their handbags, clerks in a desk drawer. It is the weapon of a last resort and a sudden surprise, drawn across a card table or pressed into a man's side in a crowd. Police and doormen learn to watch hands rather than faces. A pocket pistol rarely wins a fight. It ends ones that the other person thought they had already won.
