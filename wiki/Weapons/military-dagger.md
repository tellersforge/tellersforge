# Military Dagger

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Iron (needs iron) · Mediterranean empire · Legion drill*

A short, broad, double-edged blade with a sturdy grip and a pointed tip, carried on the belt of every soldier of the legion. It is the knife of last resort, used when a man falls or when the fighting comes too close for a sword. It is also a tool, cutting rope and meat and doing all the small jobs of camp life.

It is strong enough to stab through leather and cut through a belt, and it leaves no one in doubt about its purpose. Officers often wear more ornate versions, with decorated scabbards, as a mark of rank. A soldier who loses his dagger is teased; one who loses his sword and fights on with it is praised.
