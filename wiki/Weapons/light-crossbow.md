# Light Crossbow

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Iron (needs iron) · Western kingdoms · Knightly and common warfare*

A short, stiff bow fixed crosswise on a wooden stock, with a trigger that releases the string. The archer pulls it back with a belt hook or a lever, drops a short, heavy bolt in the groove, and aims along the stock like a gun. It takes an afternoon to learn and a lifetime to find a use it won't serve.

It shoots hard and straight, and a soldier can hold it drawn while waiting for a target. Its weakness is speed: loading takes several times as long as nocking an arrow. Town militias, ships' crews and castle garrisons love it, because it turns ordinary men into dangerous shots behind a wall or a large shield.
