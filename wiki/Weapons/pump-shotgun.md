# Pump Shotgun

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Electricity (needs electricity) · Modern armies and police*

A long gun with a tube of shells under the barrel and a sliding fore-end: pull it back and forward and the next shell is chambered with a sound that everyone in the house recognises. It sprays pellets at close range, or fires a single heavy slug when something needs stopping at a greater distance. Police cars, farmhouses, shops and soldiers clearing buildings all keep one close.

It is simple, sturdy and brutally effective at the distances where most real fights happen. It is also heavy, slow to top up and loud enough to ring the ears for a day. Its greatest strength may be that sound of the slide, which often ends an argument before a shot is fired.
