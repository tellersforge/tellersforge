# Smallsword

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Gunpowder (needs gunpowder) · Duellists and courts*

A short, light, stiff sword with a triangular blade and a small shell guard, worn by gentlemen as part of their dress. It is the rapier refined for a quieter age: no edge worth mentioning, only a point that moves faster than the eye can follow. Worn at the hip, it signals rank. Drawn, it signals that someone's honour has been wounded and will shortly be avenged.

The smallsword is fought close and fast, with tiny movements of the wrist and fingers. Duels are brief and often end with a single wound that looks small and proves fatal later. Military officers wear it with uniform, although on campaign most of them carry something heavier. Its best masters rarely need to use it at all.
