# Fist

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Any era · Unarmed (Brawling, Martial Arts, Wrestling & Grappling)*

The oldest weapon there is, and the one nobody can confiscate. A closed hand swung hard settles tavern quarrels, schoolyard feuds and a surprising number of wars that started as either. It needs no smith, no licence and no practice to use badly, and a lifetime of practice to use well. Boxers, dockhands, monks and street children all rely on it.

A fist is honest about its limits. It bruises, cuts lips and breaks noses, but it breaks its own knuckles just as easily on a skull or a helmet. Against anyone in real armour it does nothing at all, so the wise brawler goes for the throat, the jaw and the soft place under the ribs, or finds something heavier to hold.
