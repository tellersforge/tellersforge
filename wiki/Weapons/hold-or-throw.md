# Hold or throw

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Any era · Unarmed (Brawling, Martial Arts, Wrestling & Grappling)*

Not a blow at all, but a grip. The wrestler closes in, takes a wrist, a collar or a waist, and either locks the other body in place or turns its own weight against it and sends it to the ground. Farm lads learn it at fairs, soldiers learn it for the moment when weapons are lost in the press, and some schools make a whole art of it.

A hold hurts less than a punch, but it lasts longer. Once caught, the victim can't run, can't draw and can't reach for help until they tear themselves free, and a strong wrestler rarely lets that happen. A throw onto stone or into a table can end a fight on its own. Armour makes little difference: a knight on his back is still on his back.
