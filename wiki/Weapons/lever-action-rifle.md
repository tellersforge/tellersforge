# Lever-Action Rifle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Steam (needs steam) · Frontier and industrial nations*

A rifle whose owner works a lever under the stock to throw out the spent case and feed a new round from a tube beneath the barrel. It fires fast for its day, so fast that those on the wrong end swore it never needed reloading. On the frontier it is the rifle of the rider, the settler and the scout: short enough to fit a saddle scabbard, quick enough to keep a war party at bay.

Its rounds are lighter than a soldier's rifle, and it is less accurate at long distances. It shines in the middle distance, in quick, rolling fights around wagons, ridges and farmhouses. A family often owns just one, and it hangs above the door where anyone can reach it.
