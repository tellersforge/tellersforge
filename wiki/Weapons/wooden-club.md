# Wooden Club

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Stone and fire (—) · Hunter peoples · Hunting and skirmish*

A length of hardwood, thicker at one end, sometimes with a root knot left on for weight. It is among the first weapons anyone ever made, and it has never quite gone away. A hunter can shape one in an afternoon with a stone blade and a fire to harden it.

The club does not cut or pierce; it crushes. It stuns prey caught in a snare, breaks the leg of a wounded animal and ends quarrels between rival bands with brutal finality. It is cheap, tough and quick to replace. A good one is passed from parent to child, the grip polished smooth by years of hands, and sometimes carved with marks that tell whose it was.
