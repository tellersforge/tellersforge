# Parrying Dagger

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Gunpowder (needs gunpowder) · Duellists and courts*

A long dagger held in the off hand, with wide quillons and sometimes a side ring or a spring-loaded blade that splits into three to trap a sword. It is the companion of the rapier. The duellist catches or turns the enemy's blade with the dagger and answers with the sword, or presses in close and stabs with the dagger itself.

Fighting with two weapons is a separate art, and fencing masters charge extra to teach it. The dagger makes the swordsman harder to hit and more dangerous at close range, but it also asks a lot of the hand that is not used to leading. Without its partner sword it is merely a long knife with a broad crossguard, useful enough in a tavern.
