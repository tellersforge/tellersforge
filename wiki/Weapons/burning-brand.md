# Burning brand

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Any era · Improvised (Improvised Weapons)*

A stick pulled from the fire, a torch snatched from a wall bracket, a rag tied round a pole and set alight. It is a poor club and a frightening weapon. Animals shy from it, crowds part before it, and even hardened fighters flinch when a flame swings at their face.

The real danger is what it touches. Hair, cloth, thatch, spilled oil and dry hay all catch, and a fight with a brand can become a burning house in moments. Hunters have used fire to hold off wolves since the first campfire; mobs have used it to settle grudges for almost as long. It burns down, of course, and it lights up the person holding it for every archer in the dark.
