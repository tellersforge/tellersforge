# Short Companion Blade

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Iron (needs iron) · Island warrior houses · Retainer's code*

A short, curved, single-edged blade, a smaller partner to the warblade, carried in the same sash. Together the pair marks a man as a member of the warrior houses. The short blade stays at his side even indoors, when the long one has been left at the door.

It serves in cramped rooms, narrow corridors and the grapple, where the longer blade is too long to swing. Some schools teach fighting with both at once, long in one hand and short in the other. It is also the blade of last resort and of honour, used in rituals that the warrior houses keep to themselves, and it is handed down with as much care as the warblade itself.
