# Knuckle-duster

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Any era · Unarmed (Brawling, Martial Arts, Wrestling & Grappling)*

A band of cast metal that slips over the fingers, with a bar that sits snug in the palm. Closed in a fist, it turns an ordinary punch into something that splits skin and cracks bone. It fits in a pocket, costs little and looks like nothing much until it's used. Dockside gangs, bouncers, sailors on shore leave and back-alley toughs favour it; respectable people pretend not to know what it is.

It protects the hand as much as it hurts the target, which is half its value. The brawler can hit a jaw or a doorframe without fear of breaking their own fingers. It is still a fist, though, with a fist's reach, and it still does little against a helmet or a breastplate.
