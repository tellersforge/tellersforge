# Lasso

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Iron (needs iron) · Steppe riders · Horse archery*

A long rope of plaited hide or horsehair, with a running loop at one end. The steppe rider swings it overhead and lets it fly, and the loop settles over a horse's neck, a steer's horns or a fleeing man's shoulders. Then the rider pulls, and the horse leans back, and whatever was caught goes down.

It is a herder's tool before it is a weapon. On the open plains it catches stray animals, breaks wild horses and drags carts from mud. In war it pulls riders from the saddle and takes captives for ransom. It needs space to swing and a steady mount, and a strong victim can turn the contest into a tug of war.
