# Long Knife

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Iron (needs iron) · Northern tribes · Raiding and shield wall*

A long, heavy, single-edged blade, straight-backed and pointed, with a simple grip. It began as a working knife for butchering, cutting wood and clearing brush, and grew until it became a weapon in its own right. Every free man among the northern tribes carries one, and some carry them longer than a short sword.

It cuts well, thrusts adequately and can be used in the crush of a shield wall where a longer weapon would be trapped. The spine is thick, so it takes hard knocks. It is a weapon of everyday life and sudden violence, handed down and repaired for generations, and a man's knife often goes into the ground with him.
