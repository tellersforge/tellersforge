# Stone-Thrower

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Iron (needs iron) · Sieges (any iron-age culture)*

A siege engine built around a great twisted skein of sinew or hair rope. A crew winds the arm back against the skein, loads a round stone into the sling or cup, and releases it in a high arc over walls, ditches and anything else between the engine and its target. It can't hit a single man except by accident. It hits roofs, crowds, walls and morale.

Every iron-age army that besieges towns has some version of it, and every town has learned what a day of stones falling does to the people inside. The skein is its weak point: wet weather slackens it, heat dries it, and a crew who lets it go wrong can lose an arm. Good crews wet it and tighten it every morning.
