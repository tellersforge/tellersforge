# Sling-Mace

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Iron (needs iron) · Jungle and highland empires · Capture warfare*

A heavy stone carved into a star, or ringed with points, tied to the end of a braided cord a little longer than an arm. The fighter whirls it and lets it fly out to crack against a shield, a knee or a skull, then hauls it back for another blow. It is cheap, light and easy to make, and in the capture wars of the jungle and highland empires it was a fine way to stun a man who was wanted alive.

It is an awkward weapon to learn. A cord that wraps the wrong way brings the stone back into the wielder's own shins, and there is no guarding with it at all. In a crowded press it tangles. In an open skirmish it hits harder than it has any right to.
