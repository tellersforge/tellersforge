# Curved Polearm

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Iron (needs iron) · Island warrior houses · Retainer's code*

A long wooden haft, lacquered and bound, ending in a broad curved blade like a sword that has decided to become a spear. In the houses of an island warrior class it is the weapon of the hall and the household: retainers carry it on the march, and the women of the house are taught it to defend the gate while the men are away. It hangs over doorways as much as it stands in armouries.

In the hand it sweeps rather than stabs. The long curve cuts at legs, at the heads of horses, at anyone who tries to close the distance, and the butt of the shaft comes round to knock a man flat. It wants room. In a narrow corridor it is a liability, which is why the same houses build their corridors narrow.
