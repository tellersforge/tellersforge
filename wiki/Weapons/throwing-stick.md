# Throwing Stick

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Stone and fire (—) · Hunter peoples · Hunting and skirmish*

A flat, curved length of hard wood, shaped so it spins as it flies. Thrown low across open ground, it skims and whirls into the legs of running birds, hares and small game. Some are made to fly back to a thrower who misses; the heavier hunting sticks are not, because they are meant to hit and stay down.

It looks like a toy and works like a club that flies. Hunters carry several, and a good throw breaks a leg or a neck. Against a person it bruises and trips more often than it kills. It is quiet, cheap and easy to carve, and children learn with smaller copies long before they are trusted on a hunt.
