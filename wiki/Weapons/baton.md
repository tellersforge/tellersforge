# Baton

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Electricity (needs electricity) · Modern armies and police*

A stick of hardwood, rubber or metal, the length of a forearm, sometimes with a side handle or a telescopic body that snaps out with a flick of the wrist. It is the tool of police and guards whose job is to control people, not kill them. Strikes are aimed at the arms and thighs, where they hurt and numb without cracking a skull.

It keeps a crowd back, breaks up a fight and makes a point without drawing a gun. It can also do terrible harm in the wrong hands, and every force has stories of that. Carried openly on a belt, it tells people what behaviour is expected of them, which is most of what it is for.
