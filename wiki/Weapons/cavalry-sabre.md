# Cavalry Sabre

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Steam (needs steam) · Frontier and industrial nations*

A long, slightly curved sword with a heavy basket or stirrup guard, issued to horse soldiers of the industrial nations. It is the last of the great military swords, drilled into recruits with endless exercises on the parade ground: cut to the left, cut to the right, point, guard. A charge of sabre-armed cavalry, sun on the steel, is still one of the grandest sights a battlefield can offer.

It is also increasingly a ceremonial one. Against rifles and machine guns, the charge rarely arrives. Officers keep their sabres polished for parades and mess dinners, and troopers use them to cut firewood and open tins. But in a sudden skirmish with other riders, a well-kept sabre still does exactly what it has always done.
