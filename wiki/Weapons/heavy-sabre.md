# Heavy Sabre

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Iron (needs iron) · Eastern empire · Imperial armies*

A single-edged, gently curved blade that broadens towards the tip, with a disc or cup guard and a grip for one hand. It is the common soldier's sword of the eastern empire, carried by cavalry and infantry alike. It is heavy enough to chop and simple enough to teach in a season.

The weight at the tip gives each cut great force, and the curve makes it slide through as it strikes. Horsemen slash down from the saddle; foot soldiers hack in the press of battle. It is not elegant, and scholars look down on it, but in war it does its work well. Its broad back also makes it a decent tool for clearing brush.
