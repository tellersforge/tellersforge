# Kick

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Any era · Unarmed (Brawling, Martial Arts, Wrestling & Grappling)*

A foot driven into a knee, a belly or a man already on the floor. The legs are stronger than the arms, and a kick lands with the whole weight of the body behind it. Every brawler knows the stamp and the shove; trained fighters in some traditions learn to kick high, spin and strike with the heel, the shin or the ball of the foot.

The kick has a price. For a moment the kicker stands on one leg, and anyone quick enough to catch the ankle can put them on their back. Heavy boots make it crueller. Bare feet on stone make it a gamble. It is at its best as a surprise, low and fast, when the other fighter is watching the hands.
