# Battle Rifle

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Electricity (needs electricity) · Modern armies and police*

A long, heavy automatic rifle that fires the same powerful cartridges as the old bolt-action service rifles, but reloads itself. It was the first generation of self-loading infantry rifle, designed by armies that still expected to fight across open fields at long distances, and its rounds go through cover that stops lighter bullets.

The price is weight and kick. Fired in bursts, it bucks and climbs so hard that most armies lock it to single shots. Soldiers complain about carrying it and its ammunition, and then praise it the first time they need to reach an enemy across a valley. Many still serve as marksmen's weapons long after their armies have moved to lighter rifles.
