# Submachine Gun

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Electricity (needs electricity) · Modern armies and police*

A compact automatic weapon that fires pistol ammunition in rapid bursts. Short, boxy and often with a folding stock, it is made for close quarters: trenches, stairwells, corridors, cars. It was first given to soldiers who needed to clear enemy positions quickly, and it was later adopted by gangsters, bodyguards and police tactical teams for the same reason.

Held down on the trigger, it empties its magazine in a couple of seconds and climbs toward the ceiling as it goes. Trained users fire in short, controlled bursts. Its pistol rounds are weak against body armour and lose their punch quickly over distance, so it rarely matters beyond a street's width, but inside a building it is a deadly tool.
