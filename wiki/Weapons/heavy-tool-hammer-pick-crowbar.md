# Heavy tool (hammer, pick, crowbar)

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Any era · Improvised (Improvised Weapons)*

A tool made to break stone, drive spikes or prise open crates will break a skull just as well. Miners, labourers, smiths and railway gangs carry them all day, and when trouble comes there is no need to reach for anything else. A hammer crushes, a pick punches deep and a crowbar is simply an iron bar with a purpose.

They are heavy and clumsy compared with a real weapon, and not balanced for quick recovery, but they don't break and nobody questions a worker for carrying one. In riots and strikes they have been the weapon of whole crowds. A pick in the right hands can even find its way through a gap in armour, which is roughly what the war hammer was invented to do on purpose.
