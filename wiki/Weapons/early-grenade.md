# Early Grenade

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Gunpowder (needs gunpowder) · Pike-and-shot armies*

An iron or clay ball the size of a fist, stuffed with black powder and fitted with a fuse. The thrower lights it from a slow match, counts, and throws it hard. Special troops are chosen for the work: tall, strong men who can throw far, and who have the nerve to hold a burning bomb for exactly as long as it takes.

It is a siege weapon first, rolled into trenches or lobbed over walls into crowded defences, and a boarding weapon at sea. Fuses are unreliable. Some burn too long and the grenade is thrown back. Some burn too short. The blast scatters iron fragments in all directions, and nobody near it cares which side threw it.
