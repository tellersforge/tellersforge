# Weighted Net

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Iron (needs iron) · Arena · Spectacle*

A round net of knotted cord with small lead or stone weights sewn around its edge, thrown with a flat spinning motion so that it opens in the air and falls over its target like a closing hand. In the arena it is the partner of the trident, the tool of the fighter who has no armour and must win by cleverness. Out in the world, fishermen, hunters and slavers use the same thing for the obvious reasons.

It does no harm on its own. What it does is worse: arms pinned, feet tangled, a sword caught in the mesh, and a few long moments spent cutting free while the thrower closes in. A net that misses is a problem, because it has to be gathered and folded before it is any use again.
