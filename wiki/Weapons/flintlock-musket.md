# Flintlock Musket

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Gunpowder (needs gunpowder) · Pike-and-shot armies*

A long smoothbore gun fired by a piece of flint held in a spring-loaded jaw. Pull the trigger, the flint strikes a steel plate, the sparks fall into the pan and the charge goes off with a flash, a bang and a wall of white smoke. It is the weapon of the line: soldiers standing shoulder to shoulder, loading in unison, firing on command into another line doing the same.

It is not accurate. A single shot at any distance is a matter of luck, which is why armies fire by the volley and then close with the bayonet. It is simpler and more dependable than the matchlock before it, though a worn flint or damp powder still gives only a click and a flash in the pan, and a very embarrassed soldier.
