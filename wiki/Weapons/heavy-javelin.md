# Heavy Javelin

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Iron (needs iron) · Mediterranean empire · Legion drill*

A slim iron shank with a small barbed head, set on a wooden shaft. Each soldier of the legion carries one or two, and throws them just before the lines meet. The weight gives it enough weight to punch through a shield and into the arm or the body behind.

The cunning is in the metal. The shank is soft iron, so when the javelin sticks in a shield, it bends under its own weight. The enemy can't pull it free or throw it back, and a shield with a bent spear hanging from it is too heavy and awkward to use. Many men simply drop their shields and meet the charge that follows with nothing but a sword.

## Not to be confused with

the *[Throwing Axe](throwing-axe.md)*, a thrown weapon that strikes to cut; the javelin is thrown to pierce and disable shields.
