# Socket Bayonet

*Set: Expansion*

*aka: ______________________________*

*Era: Gunpowder (needs gunpowder) · Pike-and-shot armies*

A long, narrow triangular blade on a hollow sleeve that slides over the muzzle of a musket and locks with a twist. Unlike the older plug bayonet jammed down the barrel, it lets the soldier keep loading and firing with the blade fixed. With it, every musketeer becomes his own pikeman, and the pike begins to disappear from the battlefield.

Fighting with it is spear work: thrust, parry with the barrel, drive in with the butt. Drill-masters teach it with sacks of straw and a great deal of shouting. Many battles are decided by a bayonet charge that never actually lands, because one side's nerve breaks before the lines meet. When they do meet, the killing is close and very ugly.
