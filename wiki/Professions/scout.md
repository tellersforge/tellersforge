# Scout

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A pathfinder or ranger who goes ahead, reads the land and brings back word.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** stone tools
- **Life bonus:** +4
- **Core +3:** [Wayfinding](../Skills/wayfinding.md); [Stealth](../Skills/stealth.md); PER (attribute); any one Survival skill
- **Supporting +1:** one of [Bows](../Weapon_Groups/bows.md), [Spears](../Weapon_Groups/spears.md), [Long Guns](../Weapon_Groups/long-guns.md); [Signals](../Skills/signals.md)
- **Neglected −1:** [Etiquette](../Skills/etiquette.md); [Bookkeeping](../Skills/bookkeeping.md)
- **Starting kit:** a light weapon, knife, rope, travel pack, a map if your era has them
