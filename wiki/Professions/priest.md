# Priest

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A servant of a faith, who leads its rites and cares for its people.

- **Neglected −1:** [Deception](../Skills/deception.md); [Carousing](../Skills/carousing.md)
- **Starting kit:** robes, holy symbol, a book or relic of your faith, a staff
