# Pirate

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A sea robber (or a space one) who chases ships, boards them and takes their cargo.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** copper
- **Life bonus:** +6
- **Core +3:** one of [Sailing & Seamanship](../Skills/sailing-and-seamanship.md), [Spacecraft Piloting](../Skills/spacecraft-piloting.md); one of [Swords](../Weapon_Groups/swords.md), [Axes, Maces & Clubs](../Weapon_Groups/axes-maces-and-clubs.md), [Black-Powder Guns](../Weapon_Groups/black-powder-guns.md), [Handguns](../Weapon_Groups/handguns.md), [Energy Weapons](../Weapon_Groups/energy-weapons.md); [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md); [Intimidation](../Skills/intimidation.md)
- **Supporting +1:** [Knots & Rope Work](../Skills/knots-and-rope-work.md); [Carousing](../Skills/carousing.md)
- **Neglected −1:** [Law](../Skills/law.md); [Riding](../Skills/riding.md)
- **Starting kit:** a cutlass or boarding axe, a pistol if your era has one, sea clothing, a share in a ship

How to use a profession: [Rules, chapter 3, step 4](../Rules/03-making-a-character.md); every profession on one page: [Professions](../professions.md).
