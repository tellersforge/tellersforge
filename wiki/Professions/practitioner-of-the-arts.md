# Practitioner of the arts

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A student of hidden powers: wizard, witch, psychic adept or the like, as the world allows.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** stone tools
- **Life bonus:** +2
- **Core +3:** [Arcana](../Skills/arcana.md); [Meditation & Concentration](../Skills/meditation-and-concentration.md); [Creature & Occult Lore](../Skills/creature-and-occult-lore.md); [Literacy (per script)](../Skills/literacy-per-script.md)
- **Supporting +1:** [Staves](../Weapon_Groups/staves.md); [Alchemy](../Skills/alchemy.md)
- **Neglected −1:** [Brawling](../Weapon_Groups/brawling.md); [Lifting & Carrying](../Skills/lifting-and-carrying.md)
- **Starting kit:** a staff, a book or tokens of your art, robes. Only where the world has powers; the power itself comes as the Game Master's world allows (step 8)
