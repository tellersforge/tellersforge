# Engineer

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A designer of roads, machines, circuits or starships.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** wheel and roads
- **Life bonus:** +2
- **Core +3:** one of [Civil Engineering](../Skills/civil-engineering.md), [Mechanical Engineering](../Skills/mechanical-engineering.md), [Electronics](../Skills/electronics.md), [Starship Engineering](../Skills/starship-engineering.md); [Mathematics](../Skills/mathematics.md); [Hand Tools](../Skills/hand-tools.md); [Jury-Rigging](../Skills/jury-rigging.md)
- **Supporting +1:** [Mechanics](../Skills/mechanics.md); [Literacy (per script)](../Skills/literacy-per-script.md)
- **Neglected −1:** [Insight](../Skills/insight.md); [Brawling](../Weapon_Groups/brawling.md)
- **Starting kit:** an engineer's tool kit of your era, drawing materials or a computer
