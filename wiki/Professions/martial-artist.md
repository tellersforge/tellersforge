# Martial artist

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A monk or fighter trained in an unarmed fighting style, who turns their own body into a weapon.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** agriculture
- **Life bonus:** +6
- **Core +3:** [Martial Arts](../Weapon_Groups/martial-arts.md); [Acrobatics](../Skills/acrobatics.md); [Meditation & Concentration](../Skills/meditation-and-concentration.md); one of [Staves](../Weapon_Groups/staves.md), [Polearms](../Weapon_Groups/polearms.md), [Knives & Daggers](../Weapon_Groups/knives-and-daggers.md), [Swords](../Weapon_Groups/swords.md)
- **Supporting +1:** [Balance](../Skills/balance.md); [Composure](../Skills/composure.md)
- **Neglected −1:** [Carousing](../Skills/carousing.md); [Haggling](../Skills/haggling.md)
- **Starting kit:** plain clothes or robes, a staff or pair of fighting sticks, no armour
