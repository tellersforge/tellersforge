# Tribal warrior

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A fighter of a tribe or clan, raised to raid, defend the people and prove their courage.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** stone tools
- **Life bonus:** +6
- **Core +3:** one of [Spears](../Weapon_Groups/spears.md), [Axes, Maces & Clubs](../Weapon_Groups/axes-maces-and-clubs.md), [Bows](../Weapon_Groups/bows.md), [Thrown Weapons](../Weapon_Groups/thrown-weapons.md); [Stealth](../Skills/stealth.md); END (attribute); [Tracking](../Skills/tracking.md)
- **Supporting +1:** [Intimidation](../Skills/intimidation.md); [Hunting](../Skills/hunting.md)
- **Neglected −1:** [Diplomacy](../Skills/diplomacy.md); [Haggling](../Skills/haggling.md)
- **Starting kit:** two weapons of your people, a hide or padded shirt, a shield
