# Guard

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A watchman, gate guard, bodyguard or police officer, paid to keep the peace and stop trouble.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** agriculture
- **Life bonus:** +6
- **Core +3:** one of [Spears](../Weapon_Groups/spears.md), [Polearms](../Weapon_Groups/polearms.md), [Axes, Maces & Clubs](../Weapon_Groups/axes-maces-and-clubs.md), [Handguns](../Weapon_Groups/handguns.md); PER (attribute); [Intimidation](../Skills/intimidation.md); [Wrestling & Grappling](../Weapon_Groups/wrestling-and-grappling.md)
- **Supporting +1:** [Law](../Skills/law.md); [Local Lore](../Skills/local-lore.md)
- **Neglected −1:** [Stealth](../Skills/stealth.md); [Research](../Skills/research.md)
- **Starting kit:** a weapon, a club or baton, the light armour your post issues, a light source, a whistle or horn
