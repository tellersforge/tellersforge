# Scholar

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A sage, scientist or professor who studies one field deeply.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** writing
- **Life bonus:** +2
- **Core +3:** [Literacy (per script)](../Skills/literacy-per-script.md); [Research](../Skills/research.md); [History](../Skills/history.md); one of [Mathematics](../Skills/mathematics.md), [Philosophy & Logic](../Skills/philosophy-and-logic.md), [Physical Sciences](../Skills/physical-sciences.md), [Life Sciences](../Skills/life-sciences.md), [Linguistics](../Skills/linguistics.md)
- **Supporting +1:** [Language (per language)](../Skills/language-per-language.md); [Teaching](../Skills/teaching.md)
- **Neglected −1:** [Brawling](../Weapon_Groups/brawling.md); [Military Tactics](../Skills/military-tactics.md)
- **Starting kit:** writing materials, three books or records, a scholar's robe or clothes
