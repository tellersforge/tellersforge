# Merchant

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A buyer and seller of goods, from market trader to trading-house owner.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** agriculture
- **Life bonus:** +2
- **Core +3:** [Trading & Business](../Skills/trading-and-business.md); [Haggling](../Skills/haggling.md); [Appraisal](../Skills/appraisal.md); [Bookkeeping](../Skills/bookkeeping.md)
- **Supporting +1:** [Persuasion](../Skills/persuasion.md); [Language (per language)](../Skills/language-per-language.md)
- **Neglected −1:** [Brawling](../Weapon_Groups/brawling.md); [Hunting](../Skills/hunting.md)
- **Starting kit:** a purse of trade goods, scales or a ledger, good clothes, a pack animal or cart
