# Duellist

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A blade or gun for hire who fights one on one, with style and nerve.

- **Neglected −1:** END (attribute); [Hand Tools](../Skills/hand-tools.md)
- **Starting kit:** a fine blade or pistol, a second weapon, good clothes, no armour
