# Crafter

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A maker who works in one craft: smith, carpenter, weaver, potter, jeweller.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** stone tools
- **Life bonus:** +2
- **Core +3:** any one skill from group 10 (Crafts) in table 16.1; [Hand Tools](../Skills/hand-tools.md); [Appraisal](../Skills/appraisal.md); [Haggling](../Skills/haggling.md)
- **Supporting +1:** a second craft skill; [Lifting & Carrying](../Skills/lifting-and-carrying.md)
- **Neglected −1:** [Diplomacy](../Skills/diplomacy.md); [Riding](../Skills/riding.md)
- **Starting kit:** the full tool kit of your craft, a work apron, a small workshop share

How to use a profession: [Rules, chapter 3, step 4](../Rules/03-making-a-character.md); every profession on one page: [Professions](../professions.md).
