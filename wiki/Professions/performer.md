# Performer

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A singer, dancer, actor, acrobat, musician, bard or minstrel who lives by entertaining.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** stone tools
- **Life bonus:** +4
- **Core +3:** one of [Singing](../Skills/singing.md), [Dance](../Skills/dance.md), [Acting](../Skills/acting.md), [Acrobatics](../Skills/acrobatics.md), [Instrument (per instrument)](../Skills/instrument-per-instrument.md); [Storytelling & Conversation](../Skills/storytelling-and-conversation.md); [Carousing](../Skills/carousing.md); [Charm & Seduction](../Skills/charm-and-seduction.md)
- **Supporting +1:** [Sleight of Hand](../Skills/sleight-of-hand.md); [Knives & Daggers](../Weapon_Groups/knives-and-daggers.md)
- **Neglected −1:** END (attribute); [Bookkeeping](../Skills/bookkeeping.md)
- **Starting kit:** an instrument or costume, bright clothes, a knife
