# Healer

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A village healer, herbalist or medic who treats wounds and sickness.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** stone tools
- **Life bonus:** +2
- **Core +3:** [First Aid](../Skills/first-aid.md); [Remedies & Pharmacy](../Skills/remedies-and-pharmacy.md); [Bedside Manner](../Skills/bedside-manner.md); [Field Medicine](../Skills/field-medicine.md)
- **Supporting +1:** [Foraging](../Skills/foraging.md); [Midwifery](../Skills/midwifery.md)
- **Neglected −1:** [Brawling](../Weapon_Groups/brawling.md); [Intimidation](../Skills/intimidation.md)
- **Starting kit:** a healer's kit, bandages, herbs or medicines of your era
