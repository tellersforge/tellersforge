# Explorer

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A traveller who maps the unknown and comes back to tell of it.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** stone tools
- **Life bonus:** +4
- **Core +3:** [Wayfinding](../Skills/wayfinding.md); [Cartography](../Skills/cartography.md); [Climbing](../Skills/climbing.md); any one Survival skill
- **Supporting +1:** [Cultures & Customs](../Skills/cultures-and-customs.md); [First Aid](../Skills/first-aid.md)
- **Neglected −1:** [Etiquette](../Skills/etiquette.md); [Local Lore](../Skills/local-lore.md)
- **Starting kit:** rope, travel pack, a light weapon, a journal (writing era and later)
