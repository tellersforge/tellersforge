# Herder

*Set: Core*

*aka: ______________________________*

A shepherd, cowboy or herdsman who drives and guards flocks and herds across open country.

## Profession lines

- **Needs:** agriculture
- **Life bonus:** +4
- **Core +3:** [Herding & Husbandry](../Skills/herding-and-husbandry.md); [Animal Handling](../Skills/animal-handling.md); [Riding](../Skills/riding.md); any one Survival skill
- **Supporting +1:** [Slings](../Weapon_Groups/slings.md); [Veterinary Care](../Skills/veterinary-care.md)
- **Neglected −1:** [Swimming](../Skills/swimming.md); [Streetwise](../Skills/streetwise.md)
- **Starting kit:** a crook or staff, a sling, a riding animal, a herding dog
